\[1\] Pricing spread option with liquidity adjustments

标题 : 带流动性调整的价差期权定价

作者 : Kevin Shuai Zhang, Traian Pirvu , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00223

说明 : Comments: 21 pages, 7 figures

Abstract : We study the pricing and hedging of European spread options on correlated assets when, in contrast to the standard framework and consistent with imperfect liquidity markets, the trading in the stock market has a direct impact on stocks prices. We consider a partial impact and a full impact model in which the price impact is caused by every trading strategy in the market. The generalized Black Scholes pricing partial differential equations (PDEs) are obtained and analysed. We perform a numerical analysis to exhibit the illiquidity effect on the replication strategy of the European spread option. Compared to the Black Scholes model or a partial impact model, the trader in the full impact model buys more stock to replicate the option, and this leads to a higher option price.

摘要 : 与标准框架相反，在不完全流动性市场条件下，股票市场的交易对股票价格有直接影响，我们研究了相关资产上欧式价差期权的定价和套期保值问题。我们考虑了一个部分影响和完全影响模型，其中价格影响是由市场中的每个交易策略引起的。得到并分析了广义Black-Scholes定价偏微分方程。本文通过数值分析，揭示了非流动性对欧式价差期权复制策略的影响。与Black-Scholes模型或部分冲击模型相比，完全冲击模型中的交易者购买更多的股票来复制期权，这导致了更高的期权价格。

\[2\] Forward indifference valuation and hedging of basis risk under partial information

标题 : 部分信息下基差风险的远期无差异估值与套期保值

作者 : Mahan Tahvildari , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00251

说明 : Comments: 63 pages, 1 figure, MSc dissertation

Abstract : We study the hedging and valuation of European and American claims on a non traded asset Y , when a traded stock S is available for hedging, with S and Y following correlated geometric Brownian motions. This is an incomplete market, often called a basis risk model. The market agent s risk preferences are modelled using a so called forward performance process (forward utility), which is a time decreasing utility of exponential type. Moreover, the market agent (investor) does not know with certainty the values of the asset price drifts. This market setting with drift parameter uncertainty is the partial information scenario. We discuss the stochastic control problem obtained by setting up the hedging portfolio and derive the optimal hedging strategy. Furthermore, a (dual) forward indifference price representation of the claim and its PDE are obtained. With these results, the residual risk process representing the basis risk (hedging error), pay off decompositions and asymptotic expansions of the indifference price in the European case are derived. We develop the analogous stochastic control and stopping problem with an American claim and obtain the corresponding forward indifference price valuation formula.

摘要 : 我们研究了当交易股票S可进行套期保值，且S和Y遵循相关的几何布朗运动时，非交易资产Y上欧美债权的套期保值和估值。这是一个不完全市场，通常称为基差风险模型。市场主体的风险偏好是使用所谓的远期绩效过程（远期效用）来建模的，这是一种指数型的时间递减效用。此外，市场代理人（投资者）并不确定资产价格漂移的价值。这种带有漂移参数不确定性的市场设置是部分信息场景。讨论了通过建立套期保值组合得到的随机控制问题，并给出了最优套期保值策略。进一步，得到了该索赔的（对偶）前向无差异价格表示及其偏微分方程。利用这些结果，导出了欧式情形下表示基差风险（套期保值误差）的剩余风险过程、无差异价格的支付分解和渐近展开式。我们发展了一个类似于随机控制和停止问题的美式索赔，并得到了相应的前向无差异价格估值公式。

\[4\] Extreme Strike Comparisons and Structural Bounds for SPX and VIX Options

标题 : SPX和VIX期权的极端走向比较和结构界限

作者 : Andrew Papanicolaou , 类别 ：Pricing of Securities (q-fin.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00299

说明 : Comments: Special Thank You to Roger Lee for you help in this paper

Abstract : This article explores the relationship between the SPX and VIX options markets. High strike VIX call options are used to hedge tail risk in the SPX, which means that SPX options are a reflection of the extreme strike asymptotics of VIX options, and vice versa. This relationship can be quantified using moment formulas in a model free way. Comparisons are made between VIX and SPX implied volatilities along with various examples of stochastic volatility models.

摘要 : 本文探讨了SPX和VIX期权市场之间的关系。高行使VIX看涨期权用于对冲SPX中的尾部风险，这意味着SPX期权反映了VIX期权的极端行使渐近性，反之亦然。这种关系可以用无模型的矩公式来量化。比较了VIX和SPX隐含波动率以及随机波动率模型的各种例子。

\[5\] The 2020 Global Stock Market Crash

标题 : 2020年全球股市崩盘

作者 : Ruiqiang Song, Min Shu, Wei Zhu , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00327

说明 : Comments: 25 pages, 4 figures

Abstract : Starting on February 20, 2020, the global stock markets began to suffer the worst decline since the Great Recession in 2008, and the COVID 19 has been widely blamed on the stock market crashes. In this study, we applied the log periodic power law singularity (LPPLS) methodology based on multilevel time series to unravel the underlying mechanisms of the 2020 global stock market crash by analyzing the trajectories of 10 major stock market indexes from both developed and emergent stock markets, including the S&P 500, DJIA, NASDAQ, FTSE, DAX, NIKKEI, CSI 300, HSI, BSESN, and BOVESPA. In order to effectively distinguish between endogenous crash and exogenous crash, we proposed using the LPPLS confidence indicator as a classification proxy. The results show that the apparent LPPLS bubble patterns of the super exponential increase, corrected by the accelerating logarithm periodic oscillations, have indeed presented in the price trajectories of the seven indexes S&P 500, DJIA, NASDAQ, DAX, CSI 300, BSESN, and BOVESPA, indicating that the large positive bubbles have formed endogenously prior to the 2020 stock market crash, and the subsequent crashes for the seven indexes are endogenous, stemming from the increasingly systemic instability of the stock markets, while the well known external shocks such as the COVID 19 pandemic etc. only acted as sparks during the 2020 global stock market crash. In contrast, the obvious signatures of the LPPLS model have not been observed in the price trajectories of the three remaining indexes FTSE, NIKKEI, and HSI, signifying that the crashes in these three indexes are exogenous, stemming from external shocks. The novel classification method of crash types proposed in this study can also be used to analyze regime changes of any price trajectories in global financial markets.

摘要 : 从2020年2月20日开始，全球股市开始遭受自2008年大萧条以来最严重的下跌，covid19被广泛归咎于股市崩盘。在本研究中，我们运用基于多层次时间序列的对数周期幂律奇异性（LPPLS）方法，通过分析标准普尔500指数、道琼斯工业平均指数、纳斯达克指数、富时指数等10个发达和新兴股市指数的运行轨迹，揭示了2020年全球股市崩盘的内在机制，DAX指数、日经指数、沪深300指数、恒生指数、BSESN指数和BOVESPA指数。为了有效区分内生崩溃和外生崩溃，我们提出使用LPPLS置信度指标作为分类代理。结果表明，标准普尔500指数、道琼斯工业平均指数、纳斯达克指数、DAX指数、沪深300指数、BSESN指数和BOVESPA指数的价格轨迹中确实出现了明显的对数周期振荡修正后的超指数增长LPPLS泡沫模式，表明巨大的正泡沫是内生形成的在2020年股市崩盘之前，以及随后七个指数的崩盘都是内生的，源于股市系统性不稳定的加剧，而众所周知的外部冲击，如covid19大流行等，只是在2020年全球股市崩盘期间起到了火花的作用。相比之下，在富时指数、日经指数和恒生指数其余三个指数的价格轨迹中，没有观察到LPPLS模型的明显特征，这意味着这三个指数的崩溃是外生的，源于外部冲击。本文提出的崩盘类型分类方法也可用于分析全球金融市场中任何价格轨迹的制度变迁。

\[6\] Dynamics, behaviours, and anomaly persistence in cryptocurrencies and equities surrounding COVID 19

标题 : COVID 19周围加密货币和股票的动态、行为和异常持续性

作者 : Nick James , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00576

说明 : Comments: 32 pages

Abstract : This paper uses new and recently introduced methodologies to study the similarity in the dynamics and behaviours of cryptocurrencies and equities surrounding the COVID 19 pandemic. We study two collections 45 cryptocurrencies and 72 equities, both independently and in conjunction. First, we examine the evolution of cryptocurrency and equity market dynamics, with a particular focus on their change during the COVID 19 pandemic. We demonstrate markedly more similar dynamics during times of crisis. Next, we apply recently introduced methods to contrast trajectories, erratic behaviours, and extreme values among the two multivariate time series. Finally, we introduce a new framework for determining the persistence of market anomalies over time. Surprisingly, we find that although cryptocurrencies exhibit stronger collective dynamics and correlation in all market conditions, equities behave more similarly in their trajectories, extremes, and show greater persistence in anomalies over time.

摘要 : 本文使用新的和最近引入的方法来研究围绕covid19大流行的加密货币和股票的动力学和行为的相似性。我们研究了45种加密货币和72种股票的两个集合，既有独立的，也有联合的。首先，我们研究加密货币和股票市场动态的演变，特别关注它们在covid19大流行期间的变化。在危机时期，我们表现出明显更为相似的动态。接下来，我们应用最近介绍的方法来对比两个多元时间序列的轨迹、不稳定行为和极值。最后，我们引入了一个新的框架来确定市场异常的持续性。令人惊讶的是，我们发现，尽管加密货币在所有市场条件下都表现出更强的集体动力和相关性，但股票在其轨迹、极端情况下的表现更为相似，并且随着时间的推移表现出更大的异常持续性。

\[7\] What does the consumer know about the environmental damage caused by the disposable cup and the need to replace it

标题 : 消费者对一次性杯子造成的环境损害以及更换的必要性了解多少

作者 : Guillermo José Navarro del Toro , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00625

说明 : Comments: in Spanish

Abstract : The objective of this work was to know the amount and frequency with which the people of Arandas in the Altos de Jalisco region use disposable cups and then know how willing they are to use edible cups made with natural gelatin. In this regard, it is worth commenting that these can not only be nutritious for those who consume them (since gelatin is a fortifying nutrient created from the skin and bone of pigs and cows), but they could also be degraded in a few days or be ingested by animals. To collect the information, a survey consisting of six questions was used, which was applied to 31 people by telephone and another 345 personally (in both cases they were applied to young people and adults). The results show that the residents of that town considerably use plastic cups in the different events that take place each week, which are more numerous during the patron saint festivities or at the end of the year. Even so, these people would be willing to change these habits, although for this, measures must be taken that do not affect the companies in that area, which work mainly with plastics and generate a high percentage of jobs.

摘要 : 这项工作的目的是了解阿尔托斯德哈利斯科地区的阿兰达斯人使用一次性杯子的数量和频率，然后了解他们有多愿意使用天然明胶制成的可食用杯子。在这方面，值得评论的是，这些不仅对食用它们的人有营养（因为明胶是从猪和牛的皮肤和骨头中产生的强化营养素），而且它们也可能在几天内被降解或被动物摄入。为了收集信息，使用了一项由六个问题组成的调查，通过电话对31人进行了调查，另外345人亲自进行了调查（在这两种情况下，调查都适用于年轻人和成年人）。结果显示，该镇居民在每周举行的不同活动中大量使用塑料杯，而在守护神庆典期间或年底使用塑料杯的人数较多。即便如此，这些人还是愿意改变这些习惯，尽管为此，必须采取不影响该领域公司的措施，因为这些公司主要从事塑料行业，并创造了大量就业机会。

\[8\] Governmental incentives for green bonds investment

标题 : 政府鼓励绿色债券投资

作者 : Bastien Baldacci, Dylan Possamaï , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00648

说明 : -

Abstract : Motivated by the recent studies on the green bond market, we build a model in which an investor trades on a portfolio of green and conventional bonds, both issued by the same governmental entity. The government provides incentives to the bondholder in order to increase the amount invested in green bonds. These incentives are, optimally, indexed on the prices of the bonds, their quadratic variation and covariation. We show numerically on a set of French governmental bonds that our methodology outperforms the current tax incentives systems in terms of green investments. Moreover, it is robust to model specification for bond prices and can be applied to a large portfolio of bonds using classical optimisation methods.

摘要 : 基于最近对绿色债券市场的研究，我们建立了一个模型，在这个模型中，投资者对由同一个政府实体发行的绿色债券和传统债券的投资组合进行交易。政府向债券持有人提供激励措施，以增加绿色债券的投资额。最理想的情况是，这些激励是以债券价格、二次变化和协变量为指标的。我们在一组法国政府债券上的数字显示，我们的方法在绿色投资方面优于现行的税收激励制度。此外，它对债券价格的模型规范具有鲁棒性，并且可以应用于使用经典优化方法的大型债券投资组合。

\[9\] Analysis and Design of Markets for Tradable MobilityCredit Schemes

标题 : 可交易流动性贷款方案的市场分析与设计

作者 : Siyu Chen, Ravi Seshadri, Carlos Lima Azevedo, Arun P. Akkinepally, Renming Liu, Andrea Araldo, Yu Jiang, Moshe E. Ben-Akiva , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00669

说明 : -

Abstract : Tradable mobility credit (TMC) schemes are an approach to travel demand management that have received significant attention in the transportation domain in recent years as a promising means to mitigate the adverse environmental, economic and social effects of urban traffic congestion. In TMC schemes, a regulator provides an initial endowment of mobility credits (or tokens) to all potential travelers. In order to use the transportation system, travelers need to spend a certain amount of tokens (tariff) that could vary with their choice of mode, route, departure time etc. The tokens can be bought and sold in a market that is managed by and operated by a regulator at a price that is dynamically determined by the demand and supply of tokens. This paper proposes and analyzes alternative market models for a TMC system (focusing on market design aspects such as allocation expiration of credits, rules governing trading, transaction costs, regulator intervention, price dynamics), and develops a methodology to explicitly model the disaggregate behavior of individuals within the market. Extensive simulation experiments are conducted within a departure time context for the morning commute problem to compare the performance of the alternative designs relative to congestion pricing and a no control scenario. The simulation experiments employ a day to day assignment framework wherein transportation demand is modeled using a logit mixture model and supply is modeled using a standard bottleneck model. The paper addresses a growing and imminent need to develop methodologies to realistically model TMCs that are suited for real world deployments and can help us better understand the performance of these systems and the impact in particular, of market dynamics.

摘要 : 可交易流动性信用（Tradable mobility credit，TMC）是近年来交通领域备受关注的一种出行需求管理方法，是缓解城市交通拥堵对环境、经济和社会等方面不利影响的一种有效手段。在TMC计划中，监管机构向所有潜在旅客提供流动性信用（或代币）的初始捐赠。为了使用交通系统，旅客需要花费一定数量的代币（关税），这些代币可能随他们选择的交通方式、路线、出发时间等而变化。代币可以在监管机构管理和运营的市场上买卖，价格由代币的需求和供应动态决定。本文提出并分析了TMC系统的替代市场模型（重点关注市场设计方面，如信贷分配到期、交易规则、交易成本、监管机构干预、价格动态等），并发展了一种方法来明确模拟市场中个人的非聚集行为。在早上通勤问题的出发时间背景下进行了大量的模拟实验，比较了不同设计方案相对于拥挤收费和无控制方案的性能。模拟实验采用了一种日常分配框架，其中运输需求采用logit混合模型建模，供给采用标准瓶颈模型建模。本文提出了一个日益迫切的需求，即开发适合于实际部署的tmc实际建模方法，可以帮助我们更好地了解这些系统的性能，特别是市场动态的影响。

\[10\] Bankruptcy prediction using disclosure text features

标题 : 基于披露文本特征的破产预测

作者 : Sridhar Ravula , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00719

说明 : Comments: 36 pages, 14 figures

Abstract : A public firm s bankruptcy prediction is an important financial research problem because of the security price downside risks. Traditional methods rely on accounting metrics that suffer from shortcomings like window dressing and retrospective focus. While disclosure text based metrics overcome some of these issues, current methods excessively focus on disclosure tone and sentiment. There is a requirement to relate meaningful signals in the disclosure text to financial outcomes and quantify the disclosure text data. This work proposes a new distress dictionary based on the sentences used by managers in explaining financial status. It demonstrates the significant differences in linguistic features between bankrupt and non bankrupt firms. Further, using a large sample of 500 bankrupt firms, it builds predictive models and compares the performance against two dictionaries used in financial text analysis. This research shows that the proposed stress dictionary captures unique information from disclosures and the predictive models based on its features have the highest accuracy.

摘要 : 上市公司破产风险预测是一个重要的研究课题。传统的方法依赖于会计指标，这些指标存在着诸如粉饰和回顾性聚焦等缺点。虽然基于披露文本的指标克服了其中一些问题，但当前的方法过分关注披露基调和情绪。要求将披露文本中的有意义信号与财务结果联系起来，并量化披露文本数据。这项工作提出了一个新的苦恼字典的基础上使用的句子经理解释财务状况。破产企业与非破产企业在语言特征上存在显著差异。进一步，以500家破产企业为样本，建立预测模型，并与财务文本分析中使用的两种词典进行比较。研究表明，所提出的压力字典能够从披露中获取独特的信息，基于其特征的预测模型具有最高的准确性。

\[11\] The Value Added of Machine Learning to Causal Inference

标题 : 机器学习对因果推理的附加价值

作者 : Anna Baiardi, Andrea A. Naghi , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00878
