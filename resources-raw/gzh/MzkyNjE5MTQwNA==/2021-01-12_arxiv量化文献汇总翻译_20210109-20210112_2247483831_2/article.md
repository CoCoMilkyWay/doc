\[2\] Exploring the association between R&D expenditure and the job quality in the European Union

标题 : 欧盟国家R&D支出与工作质量的关系研究

作者 : Fernando Almeida, Nelson Amoedo , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.03214

说明 : Comments: 18 pages, 1 figure, 5 tables

Abstract : Investment in research and development is a key factor in increasing countries competitiveness. However, its impact can potentially be broader and include other socially relevant elements like job quality. In effect, the quantity of generated jobs is an incomplete indicator since it does not allow to conclude on the quality of the job generated. In this sense, this paper intends to explore the relevance of R&D investments for the job quality in the European Union between 2009 and 2018. For this purpose, we investigate the effects of R&D expenditures made by the business sector, government, and higher education sector on three dimensions of job quality. Three research methods are employed, i.e. univariate linear analysis, multiple linear analysis, and cluster analysis. The findings only confirm the association between R&D expenditure and the number of hours worked, such that the European Union countries with the highest R&D expenses are those with the lowest average weekly working hours.

摘要 : 研发投资是提高国家竞争力的关键因素。然而，它的影响可能更广泛，包括其他与社会相关的因素，如工作质量。实际上，产生的工作数量是一个不完整的指标，因为它不允许对产生的工作质量作出结论。从这个意义上说，本文旨在探讨2009-2018年间欧盟研发投资对就业质量的相关性。为此，我们研究了企业、政府和高等教育部门的研发支出对工作质量三个维度的影响。研究方法主要有三种：单变量线性分析、多元线性分析和聚类分析。研究结果只证实了研发支出与工作时数之间的关联，因此研发支出最高的欧盟国家是那些平均每周工作时数最低的国家。

\[3\] Quantum credit loans

标题 : 量子信用贷款

作者 : Ardenghi Juan Sebastian , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.03231

说明 : Comments: 15 pages

Abstract : Quantum models based on the mathematics of quantum mechanics (QM) have been developed in cognitive sciences, game theory and econophysics. In this work a generalization of credit loans is introduced by using the vector space formalism of QM. Operators for the debt, amortization, interest and periodic installments are defined and its mean values in an arbitrary orthonormal basis of the vectorial space give the corresponding values at each period of the loan. Endowing the vector space of dimension M, where M is the loan duration, with a SO(M) symmetry, it is possible to rotate the eigenbasis to obtain better schedule periodic payments for the borrower, by using the rotation angles of the SO(M) transformation. Given that a rotation preserves the length of the vectors, the total amortization, debt and periodic installments are not changed. For a general description of the formalism introduced, the loan operator relations are given in terms of a generalized Heisenberg algebra, where finite dimensional representations are considered and commutative operators are defined for the specific loan types. The results obtained are an improvement of the usual financial instrument of credit because introduce several degrees of freedom through the rotation angles, which allows to select superposition states of the corresponding commutative operators that enables the borrower to tune the periodic installments in order to obtain better benefits without changing what the lender earns.

摘要 : 基于量子力学（QM）数学的量子模型已经在认知科学、博弈论和经济物理学中得到发展。本文利用QM的向量空间形式，对信用贷款进行了推广。定义了债务、分期偿还、利息和定期分期付款的算符，其在向量空间的任意正交基上的平均值给出了贷款各期的相应值。赋予M维向量空间，其中M是贷款期限，具有SO（M）对称性，通过使用SO（M）变换的旋转角，可以旋转特征基以获得更好的借款人定期付款计划。假设旋转保持向量的长度，总摊销、债务和定期分期付款不变。对于形式主义的一般描述，loan算子关系用广义Heisenberg代数给出，其中考虑了有限维表示，并定义了特定loan类型的交换算子。所得结果是对通常的金融信贷工具的改进，因为通过旋转角度引入了多个自由度，它允许选择相应交换算子的叠加状态，使借款人能够调整定期分期付款，以便在不改变贷款人收入的情况下获得更好的收益。

Abstract : According to the World Health Organization, development of the COVID 19 vaccine is occurring in record time. Administration of the vaccine has started the same year as the declaration of the COVID 19 pandemic. The United Nations emphasized the importance of providing COVID 19 vaccines as a global public good , which is accessible and affordable world wide. Pricing the COVID 19 vaccines is a controversial topic. \[10\] The COVID Crash of the 2020 U.S. Stock Market

标题 : 2020年美国股市的COVID崩盘

作者 : Min Shu, Ruiqiang Song, Wei Zhu , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.03625

说明 : Comments: 19 pages, 3 figures. arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:2101.00327

Abstract : We employed the log periodic power law singularity (LPPLS) methodology to systematically investigate the 2020 stock market crash in the U.S. equities sectors with different levels of total market capitalizations through four major U.S. stock market indexes, including the Wilshire 5000 Total Market index, the S&P 500 index, the S&P MidCap 400 index, and the Russell 2000 index, representing the stocks overall, the large capitalization stocks, the middle capitalization stocks and the small capitalization stocks, respectively. During the 2020 U.S. stock market crash, all four indexes lost more than a third of their values within five weeks, while both the middle capitalization stocks and the small capitalization stocks have suffered much greater losses than the large capitalization stocks and stocks overall. Our results indicate that the price trajectories of these four stock market indexes prior to the 2020 stock market crash have clearly featured the obvious LPPLS bubble pattern and were indeed in a positive bubble regime. Contrary to the popular belief that the COVID 19 led to the 2020 stock market crash, the 2020 U.S. stock market crash was endogenous, stemming from the increasingly systemic instability of the stock market itself. We also performed the complementary post mortem analysis of the 2020 U.S. stock market crash. Our analyses indicate that the 2020 U.S. stock market crash originated from a bubble which began to form as early as September 2018 and the bubbles in stocks with different levels of total market capitalizations have significantly different starting time profiles. This study not only sheds new light on the making of the 2020 U.S. stock market crash but also creates a novel pipeline for future real time crash detection and mechanism dissection of any financial market and or economic index.

摘要 : 我们采用对数周期幂律奇异性（LPPLS）方法，通过威尔希尔5000总市值指数、标准普尔500指数等四大美国股市指数，系统研究了总市值水平不同的美国股市板块在2020年的崩盘情况，标普中盘400指数和罗素2000指数，分别代表股票总体、大盘股、中盘股和小盘股。在2020年美国股市崩盘期间，四大股指在5周内的跌幅均超过三分之一，而中小盘股的跌幅都远大于大盘股和整体股票。研究结果表明，这四个股指在2020年股市崩盘前的价格轨迹具有明显的LPPLS泡沫模式，且确实处于正泡沫状态。与人们普遍认为的covid19导致2020年股市崩盘相反，2020年美国股市崩盘是内生的，源于股市本身日益系统化的不稳定性。我们还对2020年美国股市崩盘进行了补充事后分析。我们的分析表明，2020年美国股市崩盘源于泡沫，泡沫早在2018年9月就开始形成，不同总市值水平的股票泡沫的开始时间分布有显著差异。这项研究不仅为2020年美国股市崩盘的形成提供了新的线索，而且为未来任何金融市场和经济指数的实时崩盘检测和机制剖析创造了一条新的管道。

\[11\] On the RND under Heston s stochastic volatility model

标题 : Heston随机波动模型下的RND研究

作者 : Ben Boukai , 类别 ：Pricing of Securities (q-fin.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.03626

说明 : -

Abstract : We consider Heston s (1993) stochastic volatility model for valuation of European options to which (semi) closed form solutions are available and are given in terms of characteristic functions. We prove that the class of scale parameter distributions with mean being the forward spot price satisfies Heston s solution. Thus, we show that any member of this class could be used for the direct risk neutral valuation of the option price under Heston s SV model.
