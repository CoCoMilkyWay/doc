\[1\] Early Human Capital Accumulation and Decentralization

标题 : 早期人力资本积累与分权

作者 : Guy Tchuente , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.12902

说明 : -

Abstract : Decentralization is a centerpiece in Cameroonian s government institutions design. This chapter elaborates a simple hierarchy model for the analysis of the effects of power devolution. The model predicts overall positive effects of decentralization with larger effects when the local authority processes useful information on how to better allocate the resources. The estimation of the effects of the 2010 s power devolution to municipalities in Cameroon suggests a positive impact of decentralization on early human capital accumulation. The value added by decentralization is the same for Anglophone and Francophone municipalities the effects of decentralization are larger for advanced levels of primary school.

摘要 : 权力下放是喀麦隆政府机构设计的核心。本章阐述了一个简单的层次模型来分析权力下放的影响。该模型预测了权力下放的总体积极影响，当地方当局处理关于如何更好地分配资源的有用信息时，影响更大。对2010年喀麦隆市政府权力下放影响的估计表明，权力下放对早期人力资本积累有积极影响。权力下放的附加值对于讲英语和讲法语的市镇来说是相同的。权力下放对小学高年级学生的影响更大。

\[2\] An Empirical Assessment of Characteristics and Optimal Portfolios

标题 : 特征与最优投资组合的实证分析

作者 : Christopher G. Lamoureux, Huacheng Zhang , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.12975

说明 : -

Abstract : We analyze characteristics joint predictive information through the lens of out of sample power utility functions. Linking weights to characteristics to form optimal portfolios suffers from estimation error which we mitigate by maximizing an in sample loss function that is more concave than the utility function. While no single characteristic can be used to enhance utility by all investors, conditioning on momentum, size, and residual volatility produces portfolios with significantly higher certainty equivalents than benchmarks for all investors. Characteristic complementarities produce the benefits, for example momentum mitigates overfitting inherent in other characteristics. Optimal portfolios returns lie largely outside the span of traditional factors.

摘要 : 通过样本外的幂函数分析联合预测信息的特征。将权重与特征联系起来形成最优投资组合会受到估计误差的影响，我们通过最大化比效用函数更凹的样本内损失函数来减小估计误差。虽然没有一个单一的特征可以用来提高所有投资者的效用，但以动量、规模和剩余波动率为条件的投资组合产生的确定性远远高于所有投资者的基准。特征互补性产生了一些好处，例如动量缓解了其他特征固有的过度拟合。最佳投资组合的回报在很大程度上超出了传统因素的范围。

\[4\] (When) should you adjust inferences for multiple hypothesis testing

标题 : （什么时候）你应该调整多重假设检验的推论

作者 : Davide Viviano, Kaspar Wuthrich, Paul Niehaus , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.13367

说明 : -

Abstract : The use of multiple hypothesis testing adjustments varies widely in applied economic research, without consensus on when and how it should be done. We provide a game theoretic foundation for this practice. Adjustments are often appropriate in our model when research influences multiple policy decisions. While control of classical notions of compound error rates can emerge in special cases, the appropriate adjustments generally depend on the nature of scale economies in the research production function and on economic interactions between policy decisions. When research examines multiple outcomes this motivates their aggregation into sufficient statistics for policy making rather than multiple testing adjustments.

摘要 : 在应用经济学研究中，多重假设检验调整的使用差异很大，在何时以及如何进行调整方面没有达成共识。我们提供了一个博弈论的基础，这种做法。在我们的模型中，当研究影响多个决策时，调整通常是适当的。虽然在特殊情况下可以对复合错误率的经典概念进行控制，但适当的调整通常取决于研究生产函数中规模经济的性质以及政策决策之间的经济互动。当研究检验多个结果时，这会促使它们聚合成足够的统计数据，以供决策，而不是多次测试调整。

\[5\] Search and Competition with Flexible Investigations

标题 : 灵活调查的搜索和竞争

作者 : Vasudha Jain, Mark Whitmeyer , 类别 ：Theoretical Economics (econ.TH)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.13159

说明 : -

Abstract : We modify the standard model of price competition with horizontally differentiated products, imperfect information, and search frictions by allowing consumers to flexibly acquire information about a product s match value during their visits. We characterize a consumer s optimal search and information acquisition protocol and analyze the pricing game between firms. Notably, we establish that in search markets there are fundamental differences between search frictions and information frictions, which affect market prices, profits, and consumer welfare in markedly different ways. Although higher search costs beget higher prices (and profits for firms), higher information acquisition costs lead to lower prices and may benefit consumers. We discuss implications of our findings for policies concerning disclosure rules and hidden fees.

摘要 : 通过允许消费者在访问过程中灵活地获取产品匹配价值的信息，我们修改了具有水平差异化产品、不完全信息和搜索摩擦的价格竞争标准模型。我们刻画了消费者的最优搜索和信息获取协议，并分析了企业之间的定价博弈。值得注意的是，我们发现在搜索市场中，搜索摩擦和信息摩擦之间存在着根本性的差异，它们以显著不同的方式影响市场价格、利润和消费者福利。虽然较高的搜索成本会导致较高的价格（以及公司的利润），但较高的信息获取成本会导致较低的价格，并可能使消费者受益。我们讨论了我们的发现对有关披露规则和隐藏费用的政策的影响。

\[6\] Estimating Future VaR from Value Samples and Applications to Future Initial Margin

标题 : 从价值样本估计未来VaR及其在未来初始保证金中的应用

作者 : Narayan Ganesan, Bernhard Hientzsch , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.11768

说明 : Comments: 26 pages

Abstract : Predicting future values at risk (fVaR) is an important problem in finance. They arise in the modelling of future initial margin requirements for counterparty credit risk and future market risk VaR. One is also interested in derived quantities such as i) Dynamic Initial Margin (DIM) and Margin Value Adjustment (MVA) in the counterparty risk context and ii) risk weighted assets (RWA) and Capital Value Adjustment (KVA) for market risk. This paper describes several methods that can be used to predict fVaRs. We begin with the Nested MC empirical quantile method as benchmark, but it is too computationally intensive for routine use. We review several known methods and discuss their novel applications to the problem at hand. The techniques considered include computing percentiles from distributions (Normal and Johnson) that were matched to parametric moments or percentile estimates, quantile regressions methods, and others with more specific assumptions or requirements. We also consider how limited inner simulations can be used to improve the performance of these techniques. The paper also provides illustrations, results, and visualizations of intermediate and final results for the various approaches and methods.

摘要 : 预测未来风险价值（fVaR）是金融学中的一个重要问题。它们出现在交易对手信用风险和未来市场风险VaR的未来初始保证金要求的建模中。人们还对衍生数量感兴趣，如i）交易对手风险背景下的动态初始保证金（DIM）和保证金价值调整（MVA）以及ii）风险加权资产（RWA）和资本价值调整（KVA）市场风险。本文介绍了几种预测fVaRs的方法。我们首先以嵌套MC经验分位数法作为基准，但它对于常规使用来说计算量太大。我们回顾了几种已知的方法，并讨论了它们在当前问题中的新应用。所考虑的技术包括从与参数矩或百分位估计相匹配的分布（正态分布和约翰逊分布）计算百分位、分位数回归方法以及其他具有更具体假设或要求的方法。我们还考虑如何使用有限的内部模拟来提高这些技术的性能。本文还提供了插图，结果和可视化的中间和最终结果的各种方法和方法。

\[7\] Using Satellite Imagery and Deep Learning to Evaluate the Impact of Anti Poverty Programs

标题 : 利用卫星图像和深度学习评估扶贫项目的影响

作者 : Luna Yue Huang, Solomon Hsiang, Marco Gonzalez-Navarro , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.11772

说明 : -

Abstract : The rigorous evaluation of anti poverty programs is key to the fight against global poverty. Traditional evaluation approaches rely heavily on repeated in person field surveys to measure changes in economic well being and thus program effects. However, this is known to be costly, time consuming, and often logistically challenging. Here we provide the first evidence that we can conduct such program evaluations based solely on high resolution satellite imagery and deep learning methods. Our application estimates changes in household welfare in the context of a recent anti poverty program in rural Kenya. The approach we use is based on a large literature documenting a reliable relationship between housing quality and household wealth. We infer changes in household wealth based on satellite derived changes in housing quality and obtain consistent results with the traditional field survey based approach. Our approach can be used to obtain inexpensive and timely insights on program effectiveness in international development programs.

摘要 : 对反贫困项目的严格评估是与全球贫困作斗争的关键。传统的评估方法严重依赖于反复的实地调查来衡量经济状况的变化，从而衡量项目的效果。然而，这是众所周知的昂贵，耗时，往往后勤挑战。在这里，我们提供的第一个证据表明，我们可以进行这样的项目评估完全基于高分辨率卫星图像和深入的学习方法。我们的应用程序在肯尼亚农村最近的一个反贫困项目的背景下估计了家庭福利的变化。我们使用的方法是基于大量文献记录的可靠关系，住房质量和家庭财富。我们根据卫星得出的住房质量变化推断家庭财富的变化，并获得与传统实地调查方法一致的结果。我们的方法可以用来获得廉价和及时的见解，在国际发展计划的方案有效性。

\[8\] Hermite Polynomial based Valuation of American Options with General Jump Diffusion Processes

标题 : 基于Hermite多项式的一般跳扩散美式期权定价

作者 : Li Chen, Guang Zhang , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.11870

说明 : -

Abstract : We present a new approximation scheme for the price and exercise policy of American options. The scheme is based on Hermite polynomial expansions of the transition density of the underlying asset dynamics and the early exercise premium representation of the American option price. The advantages of the proposed approach are threefold. First, our approach does not require the transition density and characteristic functions of the underlying asset dynamics to be attainable in closed form. Second, our approach is fast and accurate, while the prices and exercise policy can be jointly produced. Third, our approach has a wide range of applications. We show that the proposed approximations of the price and optimal exercise boundary converge to the true ones. We also provide a numerical method based on a step function to implement our proposed approach. Applications to nonlinear mean reverting models, double mean reverting models, Merton s and Kou s jump diffusion models are presented and discussed.

摘要 : 本文提出了一种新的美式期权价格和行权策略的近似方案。该方案基于标的资产动态转移密度的Hermite多项式展开和美式期权价格的早期行使溢价表示。这种方法的优点有三个。首先，我们的方法不需要转换密度和特征函数的基础资产动态是可以实现的封闭形式。第二，我们的方法是快速和准确的，而价格和行使政策可以联合产生。第三，我们的方法有广泛的应用。我们证明了所提出的价格和最优行权边界的近似值收敛于真实值。我们也提供了一个基于阶跃函数的数值方法来实现我们提出的方法。讨论了在非线性均值回复模型、双均值回复模型、Merton和Kou跳扩散模型中的应用。

\[9\] Re investigating the oil food price co movement using wavelet analysis

标题 : 基于小波分析的粮油价格联动再研究

作者 : Loretta Mastroeni, Greta Quaresima, Pierluigi Vellucci , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.11891

说明 : -

Abstract : In this article we analyse the oil food price co movement and its determinants in both time and frequency domains, using the wavelet analysis approach. Our results show that the significant local correlation between food and oil is only apparent. This is mainly due to the activity of commodity index investments and, to a lower extent, to the increasing demand from emerging economies. Furthermore, we employ the wavelet entropy to assess the predictability of the time series under consideration. We find that some variables share with both food and oil a similar predictability structure. These variables are those that mostly co move with both oil and food. Some policy implications can be derived from our results, the most relevant being that the activity of commodity index investments is able to increase correlation between food and oil. This activity generates highly integrated markets and an increasing risk of joint price movements which is potentially dangerous in periods of economic downturn and financial stress. In our work we suggest that governments should also provide subsidy packages based on the commodity traded in commodity indices to protect producers and consumers from adverse price movements due to financial activity rather than lack of supply or demand.

摘要 : 本文利用小波分析方法，从时域和频域两个方面分析了油粮价格的协动及其影响因素。我们的结果表明，食物和石油之间的显著的局部相关性只是很明显的。这主要是由于大宗商品指数投资的活跃，以及新兴经济体不断增长的需求。此外，我们利用小波熵来评估时间序列的可预测性。我们发现一些变量与食物和石油具有相似的可预测性结构。这些变量主要与石油和食物共同运动。从我们的结果中可以得出一些政策含义，最相关的是商品指数投资活动能够增加粮食和石油之间的相关性。这种活动产生了高度一体化的市场和日益增加的联合价格变动风险，这在经济低迷和金融压力时期具有潜在的危险性。在我们的工作中，我们建议各国政府还应根据商品指数中所交易的商品提供一揽子补贴，以保护生产者和消费者免受由于金融活动而造成的不利价格变动，而不是供应或需求不足。

Abstract : How does an entrepreneur s social capital improve small informal business productivity Although studies have investigated this relationship, we still know little about the underlying theoretical mechanisms driving these findings. Using a unique Zambian Business Survey of 1,971 entrepreneurs administered by the World Bank, we find an entrepreneur s social capital facilitates small business productivity through the mediating channels of firm financing and customer relationships. \[11\] Weathering the Storm

标题 : 经受风暴

作者 : Christopher Boudreaux, Anand Jha, Monica Escaleras , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.12008

说明 : Comments: 38 pages, 3 figures, 4 tables

Abstract : This study examines how foreign aid and institutions affect entrepreneurship activity following natural disasters. We use insights from the entrepreneurship, development, and institutions literature to develop a model of entrepreneurship activity in the aftermath of natural disasters. First, we hypothesize the effect of natural disasters on entrepreneurship activity depends on the amount of foreign aid received. Second, we hypothesize that natural disasters and foreign aid either encourages or discourages entrepreneurship activity depending on two important institutional conditions the quality of government and economic freedom. The findings from our panel of 85 countries from 2006 to 2016 indicate that natural disasters are negatively associated with entrepreneurship activity, but both foreign aid and economic freedom attenuate this effect. In addition, we observe that foreign aid is positively associated with entrepreneurship activity but only in countries with high quality government.
