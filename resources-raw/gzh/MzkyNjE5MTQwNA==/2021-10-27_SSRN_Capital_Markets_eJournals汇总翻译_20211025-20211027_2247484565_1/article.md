\[1\] Covid, Work-from-Home, and Securities Misconduct

标题:新冠病毒、在家工作和证券不当行为

作者:Douglas J. Cumming,Chris Firth,John Gathergood,Neil Stewart

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3936059

Abstract : We consider whether traders are more likely to commit securities violations when trading at home, a new form of working induced by the COVID pandemic. We examine data pre- and post-COVID, during which some traders were unexpectedly forced to work at home. The data indicate the presence of both a treatment and a selection effect, where work at home exhibits fewer misconduct cases. Work at home is associated with fewer cases of trading misconduct, although no difference in communications misconduct. The economic significance of working from home on trading misconduct is large for both the treatment and selection effects.

Keywords : Market Manipulation, Trading, Surveillance, Securities Regulation

Abstract :我们考虑交易者在国内交易时是否更容易发生证券违规行为，这是由大流行引起的一种新的工作形式。我们检查了新冠病毒感染前后的数据，在此期间，一些交易员意外地被迫在家工作。数据表明存在治疗和选择效应，在家工作的不当行为案例较少。在家工作与较少的交易不当行为相关，尽管在沟通不当行为方面没有差异。在家里处理交易不当行为的经济意义对于处理和选择效果都非常重要。

Keywords :市场操纵、交易、监督、证券监管

\[2\] The Price Effect of Temporary Short-selling Bans: Theory and Evidence

标题:临时卖空禁令的价格效应：理论与证据

作者:Haoshu Tian,Xuemin Sterling Yan,Lingling Zheng

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3949039

Abstract : We develop a model of temporary short-selling bans by extending the infinite-horizon speculative bubble model of Scheinkman and Xiong (2003). Our model predicts that a short-selling ban leads to a price inflation that is the highest at the beginning of the ban and gradually converges to zero at the end of the ban. Examining the 2008 short-selling ban of financial stocks in the U.S., we find evidence consistent with this prediction. The innovation of our empirical design is to use the financial segments of non-banned stocks as a control group for the banned financial stocks.

Keywords : None

Abstract :通过扩展Scheinkman和Xiong（2003）的无限期投机泡沫模型，我们建立了一个临时卖空禁令模型。我们的模型预测，卖空禁令会导致价格通胀，在禁令开始时最高，在禁令结束时逐渐收敛到零。研究2008年美国金融股卖空禁令，我们发现证据与这一预测一致。我们的实证设计的创新之处在于将非禁售金融股的金融板块作为禁售金融股的控制组。

Keywords :None

\[3\] Market Feedback and Access to Buy-side Research: Evidence From Monthly Mutual Fund Portfolio Disclosures

标题:市场反馈和买方研究：来自每月共同基金投资组合披露的证据

作者:Kai Du,Jingzhi Huang,Henock Louis,Phong Truong

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3948716

Abstract : Using shifts in mutual funds’ holdings disclosures frequency from quarterly to monthly to proxy for increased access to buy-side research, we find evidence indicating that greater access to buy-side research reduces the sensitivity of corporate investment to stock prices. Consistent with a crowding-out mechanism, the evidence suggests that monthly portfolio disclosures discourage information production activities by investors and, consequently, corporate managers’ ability to learn from prices. This effect increases with managers’ incentives to learn from prices and investors’ potential use of monthly fund disclosures. Our findings highlight the potential cost of more frequent portfolio disclosures for the real economy.

Keywords : mutual funds, portfolio holdings, market feedback, investment-q sensitivity

Abstract :利用共同基金持股披露频率从季度到月度的变化，以增加购买方研究的机会，我们发现证据表明，增加购买方研究的机会会降低公司投资对股价的敏感性。与挤出机制一致的是，证据表明，月度投资组合披露会抑制投资者的信息生产活动，从而抑制公司经理从价格中学习的能力。这一效应随着经理人从价格中学习的动机以及投资者可能使用每月基金披露而增加。我们的研究结果突出了实体经济更频繁披露投资组合的潜在成本。

Keywords :共同基金、投资组合持有量、市场反馈、投资q敏感性

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3947994

Abstract : Møller and Rangvid (2015) report that economic growth at the end of the year is a strong predictor of future stock returns for the post-WWII period, whereas economic growth during the rest of the year does not. 作者:Martijn Adriaan Boermans,Ian A. Cooper,Piet Sercu,Rosanne Vanpee

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3947763

Abstract : We use the European Central Bank's new and detailed database of European equity holdings by households to test two competing theories of international biases in equity portfolios, that they reflect either informational advantages or familiarity bias. The database allows look-through handling of investments via mutual funds etc, and reveals that home bias is smaller than usually believed. 出处:-

Abstract : A special purpose acquisition company (SPAC) allows sponsors to directly access public capital markets to raise funds to conduct acquisitions. Traditionally, such sponsors would raise capital by first tapping private markets to initiate a venture capital (VC) or private equity (PE) fund. \[8\] The Deadweight Loss of Active Management

标题:主动管理的无谓损失

作者:Moshe Levy

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3947150

Abstract : As most investors hold only one or two funds, the Sharpe ratio is the performance measure relevant for them. Employing this measure, we find that only 13% of active U.S. equity funds outperform the market. We estimate the aggregate annual loss to investors in U.S. active equity funds at $235 Billion. This loss can be decomposed into wealth destruction component of $186 Billion, and a $49 Billion wealth transfer from investors to funds. \[13\] The Debt-Equity Spread

标题:债务-股权利差

作者:Hui Chen,Zhiyao Chen,Jun Li

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3944082

Abstract : We propose the debt-equity spread (DES) as a measure of the valuation gap between debt and equity at firm level. DES strongly predicts stock and bond returns in opposite directions. A strategy that takes long positions in firms with low DES, whose stocks are cheap relative to bonds, and short those with high DES generates an average stock return of 6% per annum and bond return of -3.3% per annum. The return predictability is consistently stronger among small, illiquid, and difficult-to-short stocks and bonds. In addition, higher debt-equity spreads are associated with (i) higher probability for negative revision of long-term earnings growth forecasts; (ii) more equity issuance and debt repurchase, resulting in a low leverage ratio; (iii) stronger preferences for stock payments in acquisitions; and (iv) more insider stock selling. Together, these results suggest that the return prediction of DES is likely driven by mispricing rather than risk exposures.
