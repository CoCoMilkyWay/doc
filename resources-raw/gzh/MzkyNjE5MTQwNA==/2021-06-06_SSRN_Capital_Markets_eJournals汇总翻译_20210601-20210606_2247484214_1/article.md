\[2\] Extracting Implied Stock Returns from Options Prices

标题:从期权价格中提取隐含股票收益

作者:Nikhil Jaisinghani

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3855124

Abstract : This paper proposes a new method for extracting the market’s expected return of a stock from options prices while also calculating option-specific risk discounts (calls) and premiums (puts). However, first, I revisit the variable μ (expected return of a stock) as it relates to stock prices in the Black-Scholes formula derivation. I postulate that μ is itself a function of time and therefore the partial derivative equation Black, Scholes, and Merton solved was incomplete. Importantly, this undermines the conclusion Black, Scholes, and Merton came to, that an option’s price is not a function of the expected return of the underlying stock. To extract the expected return from options prices, I begin by proposing formulas for call and put prices introducing variables for strike price specific discounts and premiums. Known qualities of options, required to satisfy the no arbitrage assumption, are then used to solve for these discounts and premiums as a function of the implied expected price of a stock and σ. Finally, implied expected price and σ are solved for using numerical analysis.

Keywords : asset pricing, options pricing, options implied expected return, market expected return

Abstract :本文提出了一种从期权价格中提取股票市场预期收益的新方法，同时还计算了期权特有的风险折扣（看涨期权）和溢价（看跌期权）。但是，首先，我要重新讨论变量μ (股票的预期收益率），因为它与股票价格在布莱克-斯科尔斯公式推导。我假设μ 它本身是时间的函数，因此布莱克、斯科尔斯和默顿解的偏导数方程是不完整的。重要的是，这破坏了布莱克、斯科尔斯和默顿得出的结论，即期权的价格不是标的股票预期收益的函数。为了从期权价格中提取预期收益，我首先提出了买入和卖出价格的公式，并引入了执行价特定折扣和溢价的变量。为了满足无套利假设，期权的已知性质被用来求解这些折价和溢价，作为股票和股票的隐含预期价格的函数σ. 最后，隐含预期价格和σ 用数值分析方法求解。

Keywords :资产定价，期权定价，期权隐含预期收益，市场预期收益

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3859175

Abstract : This study examines the cross-sectional relation between return range and future returns for the first time in literature. We show that the return range can serve as a very practical measure of total volatility instead of standard deviation due to the range’s high correlation with standard deviation and strong predictive ability. Range, standard deviation, and idiosyncratic volatility are cross-sectionally linked to future returns on indexes of small size, while earnings-to-price ratio and net share issuance predict returns of mid-cap and large-cap indexes, respectively. \[12\] Quantized Portfolio Theory

标题:量化投资组合理论

作者:Benjamin Chung

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3853181

Abstract : The Kelly Criterion provides a promising starting point for a portfolio theory. J.L Kelly revealed a link between capital growth and information. Ed Thorp then demonstrated how this equivalence can be used to determine optimal allocations for stock portfolios, which coincidentally led straight to the mean-variance optimization procedure (MVO) introduced by Harry M. Markowitz. But mean-variance is as far as Kelly-Criterion (KC) reaches in Markowitz Portfolio Theory. To proceed any further, a step back must be taken to re-examine Kelly’s equivalence of growth and information rate. The embedded information rate reveals something unintuitive–all trading signals must be binary for capital growth to be maximized via KC/MVO.
