\[1\] Size and Value Premium in International Portfolios: Evidence from 15 European Countries

标题:国际投资组合的规模与价值溢价：来自15个欧洲国家的证据

作者:Ayesha Afzal,Nawazish Mirza

出处:Czech Journal of Economics and Finance 2011

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3878295

Abstract : The current study evaluates the performance of the Fama and French three-factor model in a global setting with stocks selected from 15 European countries. We employed the multivariate regression approach after sorting six portfolios according to size and book-to-market. The constituent stocks were selected to represent each country of our sample. In order to homogenize the returns we used the spot exchange rates of non-euro-area countries to convert prices into euros. Since we were analyzing on a global portfolio level we used the MSCI EMU index as the proxy for the market portfolio. Daily returns were employed for a period of five years from January 2002 to December 2006. The results were not very encouraging for the three-factor model. Except for one portfolio, the three-factor model failed to explain the variations in returns, and even in the single portfolio that demonstrated size and value premiums, the market premium was insignificant. Our findings are consistent with Griffin (2002), who suggested that the three-factor model is domestic in nature and performs poorly for global portfolios.

Keywords : international asset pricing; Fama and French factor model

Abstract :目前的研究评估了Fama和French三因素模型在全球范围内的表现，选取了15个欧洲国家的股票。在根据规模和账面市值对六个投资组合进行分类后，我们采用了多元回归方法。选择了代表我们样本中每个国家的成份股。为了使收益均匀化，我们使用非欧元区国家的即期汇率将价格换算成欧元。由于我们在全球投资组合层面进行分析，因此我们使用摩根士丹利资本国际EMU指数作为市场投资组合的代理。从2002年1月到2006年12月的五年期间，采用了日收益率。三因素模型的结果不太令人鼓舞。除一个投资组合外，三因素模型未能解释收益率的变化，即使在表现出规模溢价和价值溢价的单一投资组合中，市场溢价也微不足道。我们的发现与Griffin（2002）一致，Griffin认为三因素模型本质上是国内的，在全球投资组合中表现不佳。

Keywords :国际资产定价；Fama与法国因子模型

\[2\] Some Preliminary Evidence on Stock Price Bubbles in an Emerging Market

标题:新兴市场股价泡沫的初步证据

作者:Ayesha Afzal,Nawazish Mirza

出处:The Romanian Economic Journal 2012

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3878287

Abstract : This paper analyzes the presence of a speculative component during the extra ordinary upsurge in Karachi Stock Exchange. We implement cointegration tests, between 1997 and 2008, on price and dividends of various market and sectoral indices. The no bubble hypothesis could not be rejected for market level indices establishing the presence of a speculative factor. Among sectoral indices, banking sector depicted a speculative component, however, the price level of Oil and Gas sector did not diverge from the related dividends. These results remained robust with evidence of persistent volatility shocks for the sample period.

Keywords : Karachi Stock Exchange, Speculative Bubbles, Cointegration, Unit Root, Dividend Yield

Abstract :本文分析了卡拉奇证券交易所在超常交易热潮中投机成分的存在。在1997年至2008年间，我们对各种市场和部门指数的价格和股息进行协整检验。对于确定存在投机因素的市场级指数而言，不存在泡沫的假设不能被拒绝。在行业指数中，银行业表现出投机成分，然而，石油和天然气行业的价格水平并未偏离相关股息。这些结果在样本期持续波动性冲击的证据下仍然稳健。

Keywords :卡拉奇证券交易所，投机泡沫，协整，单位根，股息率

\[3\] Can Current Earnings Predict Future Cash Flows? A Literature Survey

标题:当前收益能预测未来现金流吗？文献综述

作者:Ayesha Afzal,Nawazish Mirza,Syed Kumail Abbas Rizvi,Bushra Naqvi

出处:Research Journal of Recent Sciences 2013

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3878268

Abstract : Investors consider cash flows to be more value relevant than profitability disclosures. This value relevance emerges from low discretionary control of managers on cash flows compared to net income. The accrual accounting is based on recognizing revenues and expenses as they occur and not when the cash transaction takes place. This will result in a variance of net income from cash flows. However, given the nature of accounting system, in general it is assumed that earnings and cash flows are co integrated. As a result, an exhaustive literature exists that attempts to explore the capacity of current earnings in predicting future cash flows of a firm. The findings on the subject are mixed with some studies validating the hypothesis that earnings can be a good predictor of future cash flows, while others refute it questioning the value relevance of earnings. In this literature survey, we present and discuss some notable findings on this important financial issue.

Keywords : Earnings, cash flows, value relevance

Abstract :投资者认为现金流比盈利能力披露更具价值相关性。与净收入相比，管理者对现金流的自由裁量权较低，由此产生了这种价值相关性。权责发生制会计的基础是在收入和费用发生时确认，而不是在现金交易发生时确认。这将导致现金流净收入的差异。然而，鉴于会计制度的性质，一般假定收益和现金流量是共同综合的。因此，一个详尽的文献存在，试图探讨目前的盈利能力，预测未来的现金流量的公司。关于这一主题的研究结果与一些研究相混合，这些研究证实了收益可以很好地预测未来现金流的假设，而另一些研究则反驳了对收益价值相关性的质疑。在这篇文献综述中，我们提出并讨论了一些关于这一重要财务问题的显著发现。

Keywords :收益、现金流、价值相关性

\[4\] Pandemic Waves, Government Response, and Bank Stock Returns: Evidence from 36 Countries

标题:大流行浪潮、政府反应与银行股收益：来自36个国家的证据

作者:Stephan Bales,Hans-Peter Burghof

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3886541

Abstract : This paper examines the impact of COVID-19 on bank stock returns over various time scales and frequencies. Considering FTSE banking sector returns in 36 countries, wavelet coherency analysis indicates that the number of confirmed COVID-19 cases negatively impacts bank stock returns during different waves of the pandemic in the medium-run. However, there is only little dependence in the very short-run. Moreover, fixed effects panel regression shows that the bank returns positively react to domestic COVID-19 policy. This ultimately demonstrates that governmental interventions not only reduce the spread of COVID-19 but thereby are also able to calm the financial markets.

Keywords : COVID-19, Wavelet analysis, Bank return, Government response

Abstract :本文考察了COVID-19在不同时间尺度和频率下对银行股票收益率的影响。考虑到36个国家的富时银行业回报，小波一致性分析表明，在中期大流行的不同浪潮中，确诊的COVID-19病例数量对银行股票回报产生负面影响。然而，在很短的时间内，依赖性很小。此外，固定效应面板回归显示，银行收益率对国内COVID-19政策有正向反应。这最终表明，政府的干预不仅减少了COVID-19的传播，而且能够使金融市场平静下来。

Keywords :COVID-19，小波分析，银行收益，政府反应

作者:Chuan-Yang Hwang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3890536

Abstract : We classify institutions into socially responsible investors (SRI) and not socially responsible investors (NSRI) using the value weighted Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) scores of their portfolio holdings. We find that firms that exhibit increases in SRI ownership tend to increase future CSR scores. Our analysis of stock price responses to the revelation of SRI ownership changes indicates that the revelation of higher SRI ownership is associated with negative stock returns. \[6\] Stock Price Crash Risk and the Managerial Rhetoric Channel: Evidence from Narrative R&D Disclosure

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3891736

Abstract : This study proposes the managerial rhetoric channel, which is exploited as a vital conduit through which managers convey information to the investment community. Managerial narrative in corporate reports featuring technology and innovation disclosure activities is positively associated with one year ahead stock price crash risk. Moreover, the positive relation between managerial narrative and stock price crash risk is more pronounced for firms with powerful, more able, younger CEOs and CEOs with higher industry tournament incentives. \[7\] Local Religiosity and Insider Trading Activity

标题:当地宗教信仰与内幕交易活动

作者:Avishek Bhandari,Md Nazmul Hasan Bhuyan

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3891720

Abstract : Based on the organizational psychology and social norms theories, we provide empirical evidence on the effect of local religiosity on insider trading activity. We find that local religiosity has a negative and statistically significant impact on insider trading activity, which is robust to alternative model specifications, selection bias, reverse causality, omitted variable bias, and measurement errors. Local religiosity negatively affects both insider purchase and sale activity, supporting the predictions of the morality and risk-aversion traits of local religiosity. However, the effect of the risk-aversion trait dominates the morality trait since the negative effect of local religiosity on insider sale transactions is higher. We also show that the level of information asymmetry, a key driver of insider trading activity, magnifies the effect of local religiosity on insider trading activity. Overall, local religiosity works as a compensatory control mechanism moderating the effect of managerial rent extraction motives on insider trading activity.

Keywords : Local religiosity, Insider trading activity, Information asymmetry, Agency costs

Abstract :本文以组织心理学和社会规范理论为基础，实证研究了地方宗教信仰对内幕交易活动的影响。我们发现，当地宗教信仰对内幕交易活动有负面的和统计上显著的影响，这对替代模型规范、选择偏差、反向因果关系、遗漏变量偏差和测量误差都是稳健的。当地宗教信仰对内部人买卖活动都有负面影响，支持当地宗教信仰的道德和风险厌恶特性的预测。然而，由于当地宗教信仰对内幕交易的负面影响更大，因此风险厌恶特质对道德特质的影响占主导地位。我们还发现，作为内幕交易活动关键驱动因素的信息不对称程度放大了当地宗教信仰对内幕交易活动的影响。总的来说，地方宗教作为一种补偿控制机制，调节了管理层租金提取动机对内幕交易活动的影响。

Keywords :地方宗教信仰、内幕交易、信息不对称、代理成本

\[8\] Financial Statement Adequacy and Firms’ MD&A Disclosures

标题:财务报表的充分性与公司的MD&A披露

作者:Stephen V. Brown,Lisa A. Hinson,Jenny Wu Tucker

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3891572

Abstract : Firms are required to provide financial information via the financial statements and the MD&A—a narrative explanation of the financial statements. Our study examines how firms use the MD&A channel when their financial statement channel is inadequate. We proxy for the adequacy of the financial statement channel by the value relevance of book value and earnings. We use several approaches to extract MD&A disclosure attributes: (1) keyword searches to identify non-GAAP disclosure, (2) supervised deep learning models to identify forward-looking statements, and (3) unsupervised topic models as well as text segmentation techniques to identify topics and topic locations. We find that firms with lower value relevance of financial statements (1) are more likely to provide non-GAAP disclosure in the MD&A, (2) include more forward-looking statements in the MD&A, and (3) use larger proportions of the MD&A to discuss intangibles and discuss them more prominently. Our findings suggest that managers use the MD&A, a relatively more flexible channel, to a greater extent to provide information when their financial statement channel is less adequate.

Keywords : deep learning, machine learning, topic analysis, text segmentation, disclosure.

Abstract :公司需要通过财务报表和MD&A（财务报表的叙述性解释）提供财务信息。我们的研究探讨了当财务报表渠道不足时，企业如何使用MD&A渠道。我们通过账面价值和收益的价值相关性来代理财务报表渠道的充分性。我们使用几种方法来提取MD&A披露属性：（1）关键字搜索来识别非GAAP披露；（2）有监督的深度学习模型来识别前瞻性陈述；（3）无监督的主题模型以及文本分割技术来识别主题和主题位置。我们发现，财务报表价值相关性较低的公司（1）更有可能在MD&A中提供非GAAP披露，（2）在MD&A中包含更多前瞻性报表，以及（3）使用较大比例的MD&A来讨论无形资产，并更突出地讨论它们。我们的研究结果表明，当管理者的财务报表渠道不充分时，他们会在更大程度上使用MD&A这一相对更灵活的渠道来提供信息。

Keywords :深度学习，机器学习，主题分析，文本分割，信息披露。

\[9\] Social Media Management: Evidence on Seasoned Equity Offerings

标题:社交媒体管理：经验丰富的股票发行的证据

作者:Henry Leung,Ruiqi Mao,Buhui Qiu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3891354

Abstract : We find that SEO firms receive more favorable messages on the financial social media platform, StockTwits, around the periods 62 trading days up to 10 trading days prior offerings and from 10 trading days after offerings to the expiration of lockup agreements, compared to their non-SEO matching controls. On average, evidence shows that SEO firms ranking in the top quartile of abnormal pre-SEO bullishness on social media reduce their underpricing by 1.12%. Moreover, insiders from active SEO firms exhibit higher net sales in the 90 days following the expiration of lockup agreements.
