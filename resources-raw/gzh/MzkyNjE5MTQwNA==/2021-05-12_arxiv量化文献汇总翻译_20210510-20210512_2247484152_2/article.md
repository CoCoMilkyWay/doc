\[1\] Can an Agency Role Reversal Lead to an Organizational Collapse A Study Proposal

标题 : 研究建议中的代理角色转换会导致组织崩溃吗

作者 : Yossi Haimberg , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.04667

说明 : -

Abstract : The Principal Agent Theory model is widely used to explain governance role where there is a separation of ownership and control, as it defines clear boundaries between governance and executives. However, examination of recent corporate failure reveals the concerning contribution of the Board of Directors to such failures and calls into question governance effectiveness in the presence of a powerful and charismatic CEO. This study proposes a framework for analyzing the relationship between the Board of Directors and the CEO, and how certain relationships affect the power structure and behavior of the Board, which leads to a role reversal in the Principal Agent Theory, as the Board assumes the role of the CEO s agent. This study s results may help create a red flag for a board and leader s behavior that may result in governance failure.

摘要 : 委托代理理论模型被广泛用于解释所有权和控制权分离时的治理角色，因为它明确了治理和高管之间的界限。然而，对最近公司失败的研究揭示了董事会对此类失败的贡献，并对一位有权势和魅力的首席执行官在场时的治理有效性提出了质疑。本研究提出了一个分析董事会与CEO之间关系的框架，以及某些关系如何影响董事会的权力结构和行为，从而导致委托代理理论中的角色逆转，因为董事会承担CEO的代理人的角色。这项研究的结果可能有助于为董事会和领导可能导致治理失败的行为制造危险信号。

\[2\] Economic analysis of tidal stream turbine arrays

标题 : 潮流涡轮阵列的经济性分析

作者 : Zoe Goss, Daniel Coles, Matthew Piggott , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.04718

说明 : Comments: 29 pages, 4 figures

Abstract : This tidal stream energy industry has to date been comprised of small demonstrator projects made up of one to a four turbines. However, there are currently plans to expand to commercially sized projects with tens of turbines or more. As the industry moves to large scale arrays for the first time, there has been a push to develop tools to optimise the array design and help bring down the costs. This review investigates different methods of modelling the economic performance of tidal stream arrays, for use within these optimisation tools. The different cost reduction pathways are discussed from costs falling as the global installed capacity increases, due to greater experience, improved power curves through larger diameter higher rated turbines, to economic efficiencies that can be found by moving to large scale arrays. A literature review is conducted to establish the most appropriate input values for use in economic models. This includes finding a best case, worst case and typical values for costs and other related parameters. The information collated in this review can provide a useful steering for the many optimisation tools that have been developed, especially when cost information is commercially sensitive and a realistic parameter range is difficult to obtain.

摘要 : 迄今为止，潮汐流能源产业由一到四台涡轮机组成的小型示范项目组成。然而，目前有计划扩大到商业规模的项目与几十个涡轮机或更多。随着业界首次转向大规模阵列，开发工具以优化阵列设计并帮助降低成本已成为一大趋势。本综述研究了潮汐流阵列经济性能建模的不同方法，以用于这些优化工具。讨论了不同的成本降低途径，从随着全球装机容量的增加而降低的成本（由于更多的经验）、通过更大直径、更高额定功率的涡轮机改善的功率曲线，到通过转移到大规模阵列可以发现的经济效率。通过文献综述，确定了经济模型中最合适的输入值。这包括找到成本和其他相关参数的最佳情况、最坏情况和典型值。本综述中整理的信息可为已开发的许多优化工具提供有用的指导，尤其是在成本信息具有商业敏感性且难以获得实际参数范围的情况下。

\[3\] Exploring the Antecedents of Consumer Confidence through Semantic Network Analysis of Online News

标题 : 基于网络新闻语义网络分析的消费者信心前因研究

作者 : A. Fronzetti Colladon, F. Grippa, B. Guardabascio, F. Ravazzolo , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.04900

说明 : -

Abstract : This article studies the impact of online news on social and economic consumer perceptions through the application of semantic network analysis. Using almost 1.3 million online articles on Italian media covering a period of four years, we assessed the incremental predictive power of economic related keywords on the Consumer Confidence Index. We transformed news into networks of co occurring words and calculated the semantic importance of specific keywords, to see if words appearing in the articles could anticipate consumers judgements about the economic situation. Results show that economic related keywords have a stronger predictive power if we consider the current households and national situation, while their predictive power is less significant with regards to expectations about the future. Our indicator of semantic importance offers a complementary approach to estimate consumer confidence, lessening the limitations of traditional survey based methods.

摘要 : 本文运用语义网络分析方法研究了网络新闻对社会经济消费者感知的影响。利用意大利媒体上近130万篇四年的在线文章，我们评估了经济相关关键词对消费者信心指数的增量预测能力。我们将新闻转化为共现词网络，并计算特定关键词的语义重要性，以观察文章中出现的词是否能够预测消费者对经济形势的判断。结果表明，考虑当前家庭和国情，经济相关关键词具有较强的预测能力，而对未来的预测能力不显著。我们的语义重要性指标提供了一种评估消费者信心的补充方法，减少了传统调查方法的局限性。

\[4\] Winterization of Texan power system infrastructure is profitable but risky

标题 : 德州电力系统基础设施的过冬是有利可图的，但风险很大

作者 : Katharina Gruber, Tobias Gauster, Peter Regner, Gregor Laaha, Johannes Schmidt , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05148

说明 : -

Abstract : We deliver the first analysis of the 2021 cold spell in Texas which combines temperature dependent load estimates with temperature dependent estimates of power plant outages to understand the frequency of loss of load events, using a 71 year long time series of climate data. The expected revenue from full winterization is 11.74bn over a 30 years investment period. We find that large scale winterization, in particular of gas infrastructure and gas power plants, would be profitable, as related costs for winterization are substantially lower. At the same moment, the associated investment risks are high due to the low frequency of events the 2021 event was the largest and we observe only 8 other similar ones for the simulated 71 years. As risks to investors are considerable, regulatory measures may be necessary to enforce winterization.

摘要 : 我们利用71年的气候数据时间序列，首次对德克萨斯州2021年的寒潮进行了分析，将与温度相关的负荷估计与与与温度相关的电厂停运估计相结合，以了解失负荷事件的频率。在30年的投资期内，全面过冬的预期收入为117.4亿英镑。我们发现，大规模越冬，特别是天然气基础设施和天然气发电厂，将是有利可图的，因为越冬的相关成本大大降低。同时，由于事件频率较低，相关投资风险较高2021年的事件是最大的，我们在模拟的71年中仅观察到8个其他类似事件。由于投资者面临的风险相当大，可能需要采取监管措施来实施过冬。

\[5\] Investigating Socio spatial Differences between Solo Ridehailing and Pooled Rides in Diverse Communities

标题 : 调查不同社区中单独驾驶和集合驾驶的社会空间差异

作者 : Jason Soria, Amanda Stathopoulos , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.03512

说明 : Comments: Submitted to journal of Transport Geography

Abstract : Transformative mobility services present both considerable opportunities and challenges for urban mobility systems. Increasing attention is being paid to ridehailing platforms and connections between demand and continuous innovation in service features one of these features is dynamic ride pooling. To disentangle how ridehailing impacts existing transportation networks and its ability to support economic vitality and community livability it is essential to consider the distribution of demand across diverse communities. In this paper we expand the literature on ridehailing demand by exploring community variation and spatial dependence in ridehailing use. Specifically, we investigate the diffusion and role of solo requests versus ride pooling to shed light on how different mobility services, with different environmental and accessibility implications, are used by diverse communities. This paper employs a Social Disadvantage Index, Transit Access Analysis, and a Spatial Durbin Model to investigate the influence of both local and spatial spillover effects on the demand for shared and solo ridehailing. The analysis of 127 million ridehailing rides, of which 15 are pooled, confirms the presence of spatial effects. Results indicate that density and vibrancy variables have analogue effects, both direct and indirect, on demand for solo vs pooled rides. Instead, our analysis reveals significant contrasting effects for socio economic disadvantage, which is positively correlated with ride pooling and negatively with solo rides. Additionally, we find that higher rail transit access is associated with higher demand for both solo and pooled ridehailing along with substantial spatial spillovers. We discuss implications for policy, operations and research related to the novel insight on how pooled ridesourcing relate to geography, living conditions, and transit interactions.

摘要 : 变革性的交通服务为城市交通系统带来了巨大的机遇和挑战。越来越多的人开始关注行车平台以及需求与服务功能持续创新之间的联系，其中一个功能是动态行车共用。为了弄清公路行车道对现有交通网络的影响及其支持经济活力和社区宜居性的能力，有必要考虑不同社区的需求分布。在这篇论文中，我们扩展了有关脊帆需求的文献，探讨了脊帆使用中的群落变异和空间依赖性。具体而言，我们调查了单独请求与乘车合用的传播和作用，以阐明不同社区如何使用具有不同环境和可访问性影响的不同移动服务。本文采用社会劣势指数、公交通道分析和空间杜宾模型，研究了局部溢出效应和空间溢出效应对共享和独行铁路需求的影响。这项对1.27亿架桥机的分析证实了空间效应的存在。结果表明，密度和振动变量有类似的影响，无论是直接和间接的，对需求的单独或集合乘坐。相反，我们的分析揭示了社会经济劣势的显著对比效应，这与拼车正相关，与单独乘车负相关。此外，我们发现，更高的轨道交通接入与更高的单独和合并的脊海航需求以及大量的空间溢出有关。我们讨论的政策，运营和研究的影响，有关的新见解如何汇集乘车资源与地理，生活条件和交通互动。

\[6\] Global Index on Financial Losses due to Crime in the United States

标题 : 美国犯罪造成的经济损失全球指数

作者 : Thilini Mahanama, Abootaleb Shirvani, Svetlozar Rachev , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.03514

说明 : -

Abstract : Crime can have a volatile impact on investments. Despite the potential importance of crime rates in investments, there are no indices dedicated to evaluating the financial impact of crime in the United States. As such, this paper presents an index based insurance portfolio for crime in the United States by utilizing the financial losses reported by the Federal Bureau of Investigation for property crimes and cybercrimes. Our research intends to help investors envision risk exposure in our portfolio, gauge investment risk based on their desired risk level, and hedge strategies for potential losses due to economic crashes. Underlying the index, we hedge the investments by issuing marketable European call and put options and providing risk budgets (diversifying risk to each type of crime). We find that real estate, ransomware, and government impersonation are the main risk contributors. We then evaluate the performance of our index to determine its resilience to economic crisis. The unemployment rate potentially demonstrates a high systemic risk on the portfolio compared to the economic factors used in this study. In conclusion, we provide a basis for the securitization of insurance risk from certain crimes that could forewarn investors to transfer their risk to capital market investors.

摘要 : 犯罪会对投资产生不稳定的影响。尽管犯罪率在投资中具有潜在的重要性，但在美国没有专门用于评估犯罪对金融影响的指数。因此，本文利用联邦财产犯罪和网络犯罪调查局报告的经济损失，提出了一种基于指数的美国犯罪保险组合。我们的研究旨在帮助投资者预见我们投资组合中的风险敞口，根据他们期望的风险水平衡量投资风险，并为经济崩溃造成的潜在损失制定对冲策略。在该指数的基础上，我们通过发行可销售的欧洲看涨期权和看跌期权以及提供风险预算（使每种犯罪的风险多样化）来对冲投资。我们发现房地产、勒索软件和政府假冒是主要的风险因素。然后，我们评估指数的表现，以确定其对经济危机的抵御能力。与本研究中使用的经济因素相比，失业率可能显示出投资组合的高系统性风险。综上所述，本文为保险风险证券化提供了一定的依据，可以预警投资者将其风险转移给资本市场投资者。

\[7\] Deeply decarbonizing residential and urban central districts through photovoltaics plus electric vehicle applications

标题 : 通过光伏和电动汽车应用对住宅区和城市中心区进行深度脱碳

作者 : Takuro Kobashi, Younghun Choi, Yujiro Hirano, Yoshiki Yamagata, Kelvin Say , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.03562

说明 : Comments: 40 pages

Abstract : With the costs of renewable energy technologies declining, new forms of urban energy systems are emerging that can be established in a cost effective way. The SolarEV City concept has been proposed that uses rooftop Photovoltaics (PV) to its maximum extent, combined with Electric Vehicle (EV) with bi directional charging for energy storage. Urban environments consist of various areas, such as residential and commercial districts, with different energy consumption patterns, building structures, and car parks. The cost effectiveness and decarbonization potentials of PV EV and PV ( battery) systems vary across these different urban environments and change over time as cost structures gradually shift. To evaluate these characteristics, we performed techno economic analyses of PV, battery, and EV technologies for a residential area in Shinchi, Fukushima and the central commercial district of Kyoto, Japan between 2020 and 2040. We found that PV EV and PV only systems in 2020 are already cost competitive relative to existing energy systems (grid electricity and gasoline car). In particular, the PV EV system rapidly increases its economic advantage over time, particularly in the residential district which has larger PV capacity and EV battery storage relative to the size of energy demand. Electricity exchanges between neighbors (e.g., peer to peer or microgrid) further enhanced the economic value (net present value) and decarbonization potential of PV EV systems up to 23 percent and 7 percent in 2030, respectively. These outcomes have important strategic implications for urban decarbonization over the coming decades.

摘要 : 随着可再生能源技术成本的下降，新形式的城市能源系统正在出现，可以以成本效益高的方式建立。SolarEV城市概念已被提出，最大限度地利用屋顶光伏（PV），结合具有双向充电的电动汽车（EV）进行储能。城市环境包括不同的区域，如住宅区和商业区，具有不同的能源消耗模式、建筑结构和停车场。在这些不同的城市环境中，PV-EV和PV（电池）系统的成本效益和脱碳潜力各不相同，并且随着成本结构的逐渐变化而变化。为了评估这些特征，我们对2020年至2040年间日本福岛新町和京都中央商业区的居民区进行了光伏、电池和电动汽车技术的技术经济分析。我们发现，与现有能源系统（电网电力和汽油车）相比，2020年的光伏-电动汽车和纯光伏系统已经具有成本竞争力。特别是，随着时间的推移，光伏-电动汽车系统的经济优势迅速增加，特别是在住宅区，相对于能源需求的规模，光伏容量和电动汽车电池的存储量都较大。邻居之间的电力交换（如点对点或微电网）进一步提高了光伏-电动汽车系统的经济价值（净现值）和脱碳潜力，到2030年分别达到23%和7%。这些成果对未来几十年的城市脱碳具有重要的战略意义。

\[8\] MCTG

标题 : 味精

作者 : Zhishun Wang, Wei Lu, Kaixin Zhang, Tianhao Li, Zixi Zhao , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.03625

说明 : -

Abstract : Making profits in stock market is a challenging task for both professional institutional investors and individual traders. With the development combination of quantitative trading and reinforcement learning, more trading algorithms have achieved significant gains beyond the benchmark model Buy&Hold (B&H). There is a certain gap between these algorithms and the real trading decision making scenarios. On the one hand, they only consider trading signals while ignoring the number of transactions. On the other hand, the information level considered by these algorithms is not rich enough, which limits the performance of these algorithms. Thus, we propose an algorithm called the Multi frequency Continuous share Trading algorithm with GARCH (MCTG) to solve the problems above, which consists of parallel network layers and deep reinforcement learning. The former is composed of three parallel network layers, respectively dealing with different frequencies (five minute, one day, one week) data, and day level considers the volatilities of stocks. The latter with a continuous action space of the reinforcement learning algorithm is used to solve the problem of trading stock shares. Experiments in different industries of Chinese stock market show our method achieves more extra profit comparing with basic DRL methods and bench model.

摘要 : 无论是对专业机构投资者还是个人交易员来说，在股票市场上盈利都是一项具有挑战性的任务。随着定量交易与强化学习相结合的发展，越来越多的交易算法取得了超越基准模型Buy&Hold（B&H）的显著收益。这些算法与真实的交易决策场景有一定的差距。一方面，他们只考虑交易信号，而忽略了交易数量。另一方面，这些算法所考虑的信息层次不够丰富，限制了算法的性能。为此，本文提出了一种基于GARCH的多频连续股票交易算法（MCTG），该算法由并行网络层和深度强化学习组成。前者由三个平行网络层组成，分别处理不同频率（5分钟、1天、1周）的数据，日层考虑股票的波动性。后者用连续动作空间的强化学习算法来解决股票交易问题。在中国股票市场不同行业的实验结果表明，与基本DRL方法和bench模型相比，该方法获得了更多的额外收益。

\[9\] Estimates of the social cost of carbon have not changed over time

标题 : 对碳的社会成本的估计并没有随着时间的推移而改变

作者 : Richard S.J. Tol , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.03656

说明 : Comments: arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:2003.09276

Abstract : Some claim that as knowledge about climate change accumulates, the social cost of carbon increases. A meta analysis of published estimates shows that this is not the case. Correcting for inflation and emission year and controlling for the discount rate, kernel density decomposition reveals a stationary distribution. Actual carbon prices are almost everywhere below the estimated social cost of carbon.
