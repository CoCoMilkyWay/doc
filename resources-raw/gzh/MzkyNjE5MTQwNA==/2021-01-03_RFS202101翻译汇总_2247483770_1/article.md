## \[1\] CAPM-Based Company (Mis)valuations

**标题****:****基于****CAPM****的公司（****Mis****）估值**

**作者****:Olivier Dessaint, Jacques Olivier,Clemens A Otto, David Thesmar**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/1/5836312;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa049**

Abstract : There is a discrepancy between CAPM-impliedand realized returns. Using the CAPM in capital budgeting—as recommended intextbooks—should thus have real effects. For instance, low beta projects shouldbe valued more by CAPM users than by the market. We test this hypothesis usingM&amp;A data and show that bids for low-beta private targets entail lowerbidder returns. We provide further support by testing several ancillarypredictions. Our analyses suggest that using the CAPM when valuing targetsleads to valuation errors (relative to the market’s view) corresponding onaverage to 12% to 33% of the deal values.

Abstract:CAPM隐含收益和实现收益之间存在差异。因此，在资本预算中使用教科书中建议的资本资产定价模型应该会产生实际效果。例如，低测试项目应该更受CAPM用户的重视，而不是市场的重视。我们利用并购数据检验了这一假设，并表明对低贝塔私人目标的出价会带来较低的出价回报。我们通过测试几个辅助预测来提供进一步的支持。我们的分析表明，在评估目标时使用CAPM会导致估值错误（相对于市场的观点），平均相当于交易价值的12%至33%。

## \[2\] Models or Stars: The Role of Asset Pricing Modelsand Heuristics in Investor Risk Adjustment

**标题****:****模型还是明星：资产定价模型和启发式在投资者风险调整中的作用**

**作者****:Richard B Evans, Yang Sun**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/67/5816603;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa043**

Abstract : We examine the role of factor models andsimple performance heuristics in investor decision-making using Morningstar’s2002 rating methodology change. Before the change, flows strongly correlatedwith CAPM alphas. After, when funds are ranked by size and book-to-marketgroups, flows become more sensitive to 3-factor alphas (FF3). Flows to amatched institutional sample (same managers/strategies) follow FF3 before andafter the change but are unrelated to the CAPM. Placebo tests with sector fundsand other factor loadings show no effects. Our results imply that improvementsin simple performance heuristics can result in more sophisticated riskadjustment by retail investors.

Abstract:我们使用晨星2002年的评级方法变化来检验因素模型和简单的绩效启发式在投资者决策中的作用。在变化之前，流量与CAPMalphas密切相关。之后，当按规模和账面市值分组对基金进行排名时，资金流对3因子α（FF3）更为敏感。流向匹配机构样本（相同的管理者/策略）的流动在变更前后遵循FF3，但与CAPM无关。用部门基金和其他因子负荷进行的安慰剂试验没有显示出效果。我们的研究结果表明，改进简单的性能启发式可以导致更复杂的风险调整由散户投资者。

## \[3\] What Do Fund Flows Reveal about Asset PricingModels and Investor Sophistication?

**作者****:Narasimhan Jegadeesh, ChandraSekhar Mangipudi**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/108/5820526;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa045**

Abstract : Recent evidence indicates that market modelalphas are stronger predictors of mutual fund flows than alphas with othermodels. Some recent papers have interpreted this evidence to mean that CAPM isthe best asset pricing model, but some others have interpreted it as evidenceagainst investor sophistication. We evaluate the merits of these mutuallyexclusive interpretations. We show that no tenable inference about the validityof any asset pricing model can be drawn from this evidence. Rejecting theinvestor sophistication hypothesis is tenable, but the appropriate benchmark tojudge sophistication is different from that used in this literature.

Abstract:最近的证据表明，市场模型alphas比其他模型alphas更能预测共同基金的流动。最近的一些论文将这一证据解释为CAPM是最好的资产定价模型，但也有一些论文将其解释为不利于投资者成熟度的证据。我们评估这些相互排斥的解释的优点。我们证明，从这些证据中无法得出任何关于资产定价模型有效性的合理推论。拒绝投资者成熟度假设是站得住脚的，但判断成熟度的适当基准与本文中使用的不同。

## \[4\] Out-of-Sample Performance of Mutual Fund Predictors

**标题****:****共同基金预测因子的样本外绩效**

**作者****:Christopher S Jones, Haitao Mo**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/149/5781971;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa026**

Abstract : We analyze the out-of-sample performance ofvariables shown to forecast future mutual fund alphas. The degree of predictability,as measured by alpha spreads from quintile sorts or cross-sectional regressionslopes, falls by at least half post-sample. These declines appear to beprimarily the result of changes in the level of arbitrage activity in themarket, with mutual fund competition appearing to play a secondary role. Wefind no evidence that the declines are the result of data snooping or learningby investors or fund managers. Finally, we show that corporate bond fundperformance exhibits similar dependence on measures of bond market arbitrageactivity.

Abstract:我们分析了用于预测未来共同基金alphas的变量的样本外表现。可预测性的程度，如从五分位排序或横截面回归斜率的阿尔法扩散来衡量的，在抽样后至少下降了一半。这些下跌似乎主要是市场套利活动水平变化的结果，共同基金竞争似乎起次要作用。我们没有发现证据表明下跌是投资者或基金经理窥探或学习数据的结果。最后，我们发现，公司债券基金表现出类似的依赖性措施的债券市场套利活动。

## \[5\] Portfolio Pumping and Managerial Structure

**标题****:****投资组合和管理结构**

**作者****:Saurin Patel, Sergei Sarkissian**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/194/5766141;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa027**

Abstract : Using U.S. equity mutual fund data, we showthat portfolio pumping—an illegal trading activity that artificially inflatesyear- and quarter-end portfolio returns—is more pronounced among single-managedfunds compared with team-managed ones. The return inflation by team-managedfunds is 45% lower than by single-managed funds at year-ends. Also, portfoliopumping decreases as team size increases. These results are driven by peereffects among teams and, sometimes, amplified by less convex flow-performancerelation in team-managed funds. Our findings are robust to differences in fund governance,manager career concerns, local networks, fund family policies, and changes inthe SEC’s enforcement policies.

Abstract:利用美国股票共同基金的数据，我们发现，与团队管理的基金相比，单一管理的基金中，投资组合泵送非法交易活动（人为抬高年终和季末投资组合回报）更为明显。年底，团队管理基金的回报通胀率比单一管理基金低45%。此外，随着团队规模的增加，投资组合的泵送也会减少。这些结果是由团队间的同侪效应驱动的，有时，团队管理基金的流动绩效关系不那么凸。我们的研究结果对基金治理、经理人职业关注、本地网络、基金家族政策以及美国证交会执法政策的变化等方面的差异具有显著性。

## \[6\] Best Buys and Own Brands: Investment Platforms’Recommendations of Mutual Funds

**作者****:Gordon Cookson, Tim Jenkinson,Howard Jones, Jose Vicente Martinez**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/227/5841239;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa057**

Abstract : Individuals increasingly buy mutual fundsvia online platforms, whose “best-buy” recommendations heavily influence flows.As intermediaries of mutual funds, platforms provide none of the unobservableinteraction or intangible benefits of brokers, and so allow clean tests of thedeterminants, influence, and value of their fund recommendations. Using uniqueU.K. data, we find that platforms favor “own-brand” funds and those paying thema higher commission share. **作者****:Neil D Pearson, Zhishu Yang, QiZhang**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/264/5811576;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa037**

Abstract : We use brokerage account records to studytrading during the Chinese put warrants bubble and find evidence consistentwith extrapolative theories of speculative asset price bubbles. We identify theevent that started the bubble and show that investors engaged in a form offeedback trading based on their own past returns. The interaction of feedbacktrading with the precipitating event caused additional buying and priceincreases in a feedback loop, and estimates of the trading volume due to thismechanism explain prices and returns during the bubble.

Abstract:我们使用经纪账户记录来研究中国认沽权证泡沫期间的交易，并找到与投机资产价格泡沫外推理论相一致的证据。我们确定了引发泡沫的事件，并表明投资者根据自己过去的回报进行了某种形式的反馈交易。反馈交易与沉淀事件的相互作用在反馈回路中引起了额外的买入和价格上涨，由于这种机制对交易量的估计解释了泡沫期间的价格和回报。

## \[9\] Momentum and Reversals When Overconfident InvestorsUnderestimate Their Competition

**作者****:Jiang Luo, Avanidhar Subrahmanyam,Sheridan Titman**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/351/5736564;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa016**

Abstract : We develop a model in which overconfidentinvestors overestimate their own signal quality but are skeptical of others’Investors who are initially uninformed believe that early-informed investorshave learned little, leading the former investors to provide excess liquidity,which, in turn, causes underreaction and short-run momentum. Skepticalinvestors can also react to stale information, causing momentum, followed byreversals. Hence, skepticism generates both momentum and reversals; the latterare amplified if investors overassess their own signal precision. We explainhow long-run reversals can disappear while shorter-term momentum prevails,provide empirical implications, and link momentum to liquidity and priceefficiency.

Abstract:我们建立了一个模型，在这个模型中，过度自信的投资者高估了自己的信号质量，但对其他投资者持怀疑态度，这些投资者最初并不知情，他们认为早期知情的投资者学到的东西很少，导致前投资者提供了过剩的流动性，进而导致反应不足和短期动力不足。持怀疑态度的投资者也会对陈旧的信息做出反应，造成动量，然后出现反转。因此，怀疑论会产生动量和反转；如果投资者过度估计自己的信号精度，后者就会被放大。我们解释了当短期动量占上风时，长期反转是如何消失的，提供了经验启示，并将动量与流动性和价格效率联系起来。

## \[11\] Untangling the Value Premium with Labor Shares

**标题****:****用劳动股解构价值溢价**

**作者****:Andres Donangelo**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/451/5753963;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa022**

Abstract : This paper quantifies the relativeimportance of labor-induced operating leverage at explaining the value premium.I extend a traditional variance decomposition methodology using labor shares todisentangle labor leverage from the value premium and from the value spread andfrom the variation in profitability levels and growth. My extendeddecomposition shows that labor leverage explains approximately 50% of the valuepremium, whereas profitability and growth-based mechanisms explain the other50%. Abstract:本文量化了劳动诱导经营杠杆在解释价值溢价中的相对重要性。我扩展了一种传统的方差分解方法，使用劳动力份额将劳动力杠杆从价值溢价、价值差价以及盈利水平和增长的变化中分离出来。我的扩展分解表明，劳动力杠杆解释了大约50%的价值溢价，而盈利能力和增长机制解释了其他50%。我提出了一个易于处理的基于生产的资产定价模型，定性和定量地解释了这一发现，以及账面市盈率、贴现率、盈利能力和未来增长之间的关系。

## \[12\] Heterogeneous Taxes and Limited Risk Sharing:Evidence from Municipal Bonds

**标题****:****异质税收与有限风险分担：来自市政债券的证据**

**作者****:Tania Babina, ChotibhakJotikasthira, Christian Lundblad, Tarun Ramadorai**

**链接****:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/1/509/5803088;https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa028**

Abstract : We evaluate the impacts of tax policy onasset returns using the U.S. municipal bond market. In theory, tax-inducedownership segmentation limits risk sharing, creating downward-sloping regionsof the aggregate demand curve for the asset. In the data, cross-state variationin tax privilege policies predicts differences in in-state ownership of localmunicipal bonds; the policies create incentives for concentrated localownership. High tax privilege states have muni bond yields that are moresensitive to variations in supply and local idiosyncratic risk. The effects arestronger when local investors face correlated background risk and/ordiminishing marginal nonpecuniary benefits from holding local assets.

Abstract:我们使用美国市政债券市场评估税收政策对资产回报的影响。理论上，税收导致的所有权分割限制了风险分担，造成了资产总需求曲线的向下倾斜区域。在数据中，税收优惠政策的跨州差异预测了地方市政债券的国家所有权差异；这些政策为集中的地方所有权创造了激励。高税收优惠州的市政债券收益率对供给变化和当地特殊风险更为敏感。当本地投资者面临相关的背景风险和/或持有本地资产的边际非金融收益递减时，这种影响更大。
