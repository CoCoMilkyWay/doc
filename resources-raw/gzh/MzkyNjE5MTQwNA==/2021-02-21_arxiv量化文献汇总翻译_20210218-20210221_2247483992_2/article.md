\[1\] A Core of E Commerce Customer Experience based on Conversational Data using Network Text Methodology

标题 : 基于网络文本方法的会话数据的电子商务客户体验核心

作者 : Andry Alamsyah, Nurlisa Laksmiani, Lies Anisa Rahimi , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09107

说明 : Comments: 9 pages, 1 figure, 4 tables

Abstract : E commerce provides an efficient and effective way to exchange goods between sellers and customers. E commerce has been a popular method for doing business, because of its simplicity of having commerce activity transparently available, including customer voice and opinion about their own experience. Those experiences can be a great benefit to understand customer experience comprehensively, both for sellers and future customers. This paper applies to e commerces and customers in Indonesia. Many Indonesian customers expressed their voice to open social network services such as Twitter and Facebook, where a large proportion of data is in the form of conversational data. By understanding customer behavior through open social network service, we can have descriptions about the e commerce services level in Indonesia. Thus, it is related to the government s effort to improve the Indonesian digital economy ecosystem. A method for finding core topics in large scale internet unstructured text data is needed, where the method should be fast but sufficiently accurate. Processing large scale data is not a straightforward job, it often needs special skills of people and complex software and hardware computer system. We propose a fast methodology of text mining methods based on frequently appeared words and their word association to form network text methodology. This method is adapted from Social Network Analysis by the model relationships between words instead of actors.

摘要 : 电子商务为买卖双方提供了一种高效的商品交换方式。电子商务已经成为一种流行的商务方式，因为它简单明了，使商务活动透明可用，包括客户的意见和对自己体验的看法。这些体验对于全面了解客户体验，无论是对于卖家还是未来的客户，都是一大好处。本文适用于印度尼西亚的电子商务和客户。许多印尼客户对Twitter和Facebook等开放的社交网络服务表达了自己的意见，其中很大一部分数据是以会话数据的形式提供的。通过对开放式社交网络服务中顾客行为的了解，我们可以对印尼的电子商务服务水平进行描述。因此，这与政府改善印尼数字经济生态系统的努力有关。在大规模互联网非结构化文本数据中寻找核心主题需要一种快速而准确的方法。处理大规模数据并不是一项简单的工作，它往往需要专门的技术人员和复杂的软硬件计算机系统。提出了一种基于频繁出现词及其词间关联的快速文本挖掘方法，形成网络文本挖掘方法。这种方法是从社会网络分析中通过建立词与词之间的关系模型而不是通过参与者来实现的。

\[2\] Sustainable and Resilient Systems for Intergenerational Justice

标题 : 可持续和有弹性的代际司法制度

作者 : Sahar Zandi , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09122

说明 : -

Abstract : Rawls theory of justice aims at fairness. He does not only think of justice between exiting parties in existing society, but he also thinks of it between generations intergenerational justice problem. Rawls solution to this problem is the saving principle, and he says that we are responsible for being just with the next generations. Wolf thinks of our responsibility for future generations as a kind of financial debt that we have to pay. He also develops the meaning of saving and says that it is not restricted to the monetary one. Wolf extends the definition of saving such that it includes investment on behalf of the next generations as well. In this paper, I want to extend the meaning of saving to using the resources for sustainable and resilient systems. By referring to the problem of time, I show that our decision on behalf of the next generations will be rational only if we entirely use the natural resources and wealth to produce sustainable and resilient systems.

摘要 : 罗尔斯的正义理论以公平为目标。他不仅思考了现存社会中存在的政党之间的正义问题，而且还思考了代际正义问题。罗尔斯对这个问题的解决办法是拯救原则，他说，我们有责任与下一代保持公正。沃尔夫认为我们对后代的责任是一种我们必须偿还的财政债务。他还发展了储蓄的含义，并说它不仅限于货币意义。沃尔夫扩展了储蓄的定义，因此它也包括了代表下一代的投资。在这篇论文中，我想把节约的意义扩展到利用资源来建立可持续的、有弹性的系统。通过提到时间问题，我表明，只有当我们完全利用自然资源和财富来生产可持续和有弹性的系统时，我们代表下一代作出的决定才是合理的。

\[3\] Understanding algorithmic collusion with experience replay

标题 : 利用经验回放理解算法合谋

作者 : Bingyan Han , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09139

说明 : Comments: Work in progress. Comments are welcome

Abstract : In an infinitely repeated pricing game, pricing algorithms based on artificial intelligence (Q learning) may consistently learn to charge supra competitive prices even without communication. Although concerns on algorithmic collusion have arisen, little is known on underlying factors. In this work, we experimentally analyze the dynamics of algorithms with three variants of experience replay. Algorithmic collusion still has roots in human preferences. Randomizing experience yields prices close to the static Bertrand equilibrium and higher prices are easily restored by favoring the latest experience. Moreover, relative performance concerns also stabilize the collusion. Finally, we investigate the scenarios with heterogeneous agents and test robustness on various factors.

摘要 : 在一个无限重复的定价博弈中，基于人工智能（Q学习）的定价算法即使在没有通信的情况下也可以持续地学习超竞争价格。虽然对算法合谋的关注已经出现，但对潜在因素知之甚少。在这项工作中，我们实验分析了三种不同的经验回放算法的动态性。算法合谋仍然源于人类的偏好。随机经验产生的价格接近静态伯特兰均衡，更高的价格很容易恢复青睐最新的经验。此外，相对性能的关注也稳定了合谋。最后，我们研究了具有异构代理的场景，并测试了对各种因素的鲁棒性。

\[4\] The Gender Pay Gap Revisited with Big Data

标题 : 用大数据重新审视性别工资差距

作者 : Anthony Strittmatter, Conny Wunsch , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09207

说明 : -

Abstract : The vast majority of existing studies that estimate the average unexplained gender pay gap use unnecessarily restrictive linear versions of the Blinder Oaxaca decomposition. Using a notably rich and large data set of 1.7 million employees in Switzerland, we investigate how the methodological improvements made possible by such big data affect estimates of the unexplained gender pay gap. We study the sensitivity of the estimates with regard to i) the availability of observationally comparable men and women, ii) model flexibility when controlling for wage determinants, and iii) the choice of different parametric and semi parametric estimators, including variants that make use of machine learning methods. We find that these three factors matter greatly. Blinder Oaxaca estimates of the unexplained gender pay gap decline by up to 39 when we enforce comparability between men and women and use a more flexible specification of the wage equation. Semi parametric matching yields estimates that when compared with the Blinder Oaxaca estimates, are up to 50 smaller and also less sensitive to the way wage determinants are included.

摘要 : 绝大多数现有的研究，估计平均未解释的性别工资差距使用不必要的限制线性版本的盲目瓦哈卡分解。利用瑞士170万名雇员的大量数据，我们调查了这些大数据可能带来的方法改进如何影响对无法解释的性别工资差距的估计。我们研究了估计值的敏感性，包括i）可观察比较的男性和女性的可用性，ii）控制工资决定因素时的模型灵活性，以及iii）不同参数和半参数估计值的选择，包括使用机器学习方法的变体。我们发现这三个因素非常重要。当我们加强男女之间的可比性并使用更灵活的工资公式时，Blinder Oaxaca对无法解释的性别工资差距的估计下降了39%。半参数匹配收益率估计，当与盲瓦哈卡估计相比，是多达50小，也不太敏感的方式工资决定因素包括在内。

标题 : 公司社会责任与公司治理

作者 : Rania Béji (UM, MRM), Ouidad Yousfi (UM, MRM), Abdelwahed Omri , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09218

说明 : Comments: World Scientific Publisher. Financial and Economic Systems: Transformations & New Challenges, 2020

Abstract : This chapter aims to critically review the existing literature on the relationship between corporate social responsibility (CSR) and corporate governance features. Drawn on management and corporate governance theories, we develop a theoretical model that makes explicit the links between board diversity, CSR committees attributes, CSR and financial performance. Particularly, we show that focusing on the cognitive and demographic characteristics of board members could provide more insights on the link between corporate governance and CSR. We also highlight how the functioning and the composition of CSR committees, could be valuable to better understand the relationship between corporate governance and CSR.

\[6\] Integrating prediction in mean variance portfolio optimization

标题 : 均值-方差组合优化中的集成预测

作者 : Andrew Butler, Roy H. Kwon , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09287

说明 : -

Abstract : Many problems in quantitative finance involve both predictive forecasting and decision based optimization. Traditionally, predictive models are optimized with unique prediction based objectives and constraints, and are therefore unaware of how those predictions will ultimately be used in the context of their final decision based optimization. We present a stochastic optimization framework for integrating regression based predictive models in a mean variance portfolio optimization setting. Closed form analytical solutions are provided for the unconstrained and equality constrained case. For the general inequality constrained case, we make use of recent advances in neural network architecture for efficient optimization of batch quadratic programs. To our knowledge, this is the first rigorous study of integrating prediction in a mean variance portfolio optimization setting. We present several historical simulations using global futures data and demonstrate the benefits of the integrated approach in comparison to the decoupled alternative.

摘要 : 定量金融中的许多问题都涉及预测和基于决策的优化。传统上，预测模型是用唯一的基于预测的目标和约束进行优化的，因此不知道这些预测最终将如何用于基于最终决策的优化。我们提出了一个随机优化框架，用于在均值-方差投资组合优化设置中集成基于回归的预测模型。对于无约束和等式约束的情况，给出了封闭形式的解析解。对于一般的不等式约束情形，我们利用神经网络结构的最新进展来有效地优化批处理二次规划。据我们所知，这是第一次在均值-方差投资组合优化设置中集成预测的严格研究。我们使用全球期货数据进行了几次历史模拟，并与解耦方案进行了比较，说明了集成方法的优点。

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09102

Abstract : The internet s rapid growth stimulates the emergence of start up companies based on information technology and telecommunication (ICT) in Indonesia and Singapore. As the number of start ups and its investor growth, the network of its relationship become larger and complex, but on the other side feel small. Everyone in the ICT start up investment network can be reached in short steps, led to a phenomenon called small world phenomenon, a principle that we are all connected by a short chain of relationships. We investigate the pattern of the relationship between a start up with its investor and the small world characteristics using network analysis methodology. \[8\] A maximum entropy model of bounded rational decision making with prior beliefs and market feedback

标题 : 具有先验信念和市场反馈的有限理性决策的最大熵模型

作者 : Benjamin Patrick Evans, Mikhail Prokopenko , 类别 ：Information Theory (cs.IT)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09180

说明 : Comments: 32 pages

Abstract : Bounded rationality is an important consideration stemming from the fact that agents often have limits on their processing abilities, making the assumption of perfect rationality inapplicable to many real tasks. We propose an information theoretic approach to the inference of agent decisions under Smithian competition. The model explicitly captures the boundedness of agents (limited in their information processing capacity) as the cost of information acquisition for expanding their prior beliefs. The expansion is measured as the Kullblack Leibler divergence between posterior decisions and prior beliefs. When information acquisition is free, the textit homo economicus agent is recovered, while in cases when information acquisition becomes costly, agents instead revert to their prior beliefs. The maximum entropy principle is used to infer least biased decisions, based upon the notion of Smithian competition formalised within the Quantal Response Statistical Equilibrium framework. The incorporation of prior beliefs into such a framework allowed us to systematically explore the effects of prior beliefs on decision making, in the presence of market feedback. We verified the proposed model using Australian housing market data, showing how the incorporation of prior knowledge alters the resulting agent decisions. Specifically, it allowed for the separation (and analysis) of past beliefs and utility maximisation behaviour of the agent.

摘要 : 有限理性是一个重要的考虑因素，因为代理人的处理能力往往受到限制，使得完全理性的假设不适用于许多实际任务。我们提出了一个信息论的方法来推断史密斯竞争下的代理决策。该模型明确地捕获了代理的有界性（其信息处理能力有限）作为扩展其先验信念的信息获取成本。扩展被测量为后验决策和先验信念之间的Kullblack-Leibler散度。当信息获取是免费的，textit人经济主体被恢复，而在情况下，当信息获取变得昂贵，代理人反而恢复到他们以前的信念。基于量子反应统计平衡框架下形式化的史密斯竞争概念，利用最大熵原理推导了最小偏差决策。将先验信念纳入这样一个框架，使我们能够系统地探讨在存在市场反馈的情况下，先验信念对决策的影响。我们使用澳大利亚的住房市场数据验证了所提出的模型，显示了先验知识的结合如何改变最终的代理决策。具体来说，它允许分离（和分析）过去的信念和效用最大化的行为的代理人。

\[9\] The economic dependency of the Bitcoin security

标题 : 比特币安全的经济依赖性

作者 : Pavel Ciaian, d'Artis Kancs, Miroslava Rajcaniova , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.08378

说明 : Comments: arXiv admin note: substantial text overlap with arXiv:2102.08107

Abstract : We study to what extent the Bitcoin blockchain security permanently depends on the underlying distribution of cryptocurrency market outcomes. We use daily blockchain and Bitcoin data for 2014 2019 and employ the ARDL approach. We test three equilibrium hypotheses (i) sensitivity of the Bitcoin blockchain to mining reward (ii) security outcomes of the Bitcoin blockchain and the proof of work cost and (iii) the speed of adjustment of the Bitcoin blockchain security to deviations from the equilibrium path. Our results suggest that the Bitcoin price and mining rewards are intrinsically linked to Bitcoin security outcomes. The Bitcoin blockchain security s dependency on mining costs is geographically differenced it is more significant for the global mining leader China than for other world regions. After input or output price shocks, the Bitcoin blockchain security reverts to its equilibrium security level.

摘要 : 我们研究比特币区块链的安全性在多大程度上永久取决于加密货币市场结果的潜在分布。我们使用2014-2019年的每日区块链和比特币数据，并采用ARDL方法。我们测试了三个均衡假设（i）比特币区块链对挖掘报酬的敏感性（ii）比特币区块链的安全结果和工作成本证明，以及（iii）比特币区块链安全对偏离均衡路径的调整速度。我们的结果表明，比特币的价格和开采回报与比特币的安全结果有着内在的联系。比特币区块链安全对采矿成本的依赖在地理上是不同的，这对全球采矿业领头羊中国来说比世界其他地区更为重要。在输入或输出价格冲击之后，比特币区块链安全性恢复到其均衡安全水平。

\[10\] Deep Learning for Market by Order Data

标题 : 基于订单数据的市场深度学习

作者 : Zihao Zhang, Bryan Lim, Stefan Zohren , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.08811

说明 : Comments: 16 pages, 6 figures

Abstract : Market by order (MBO) data a detailed feed of individual trade instructions for a given stock on an exchange is arguably one of the most granular sources of microstructure information. While limit order books (LOBs) are implicitly derived from it, MBO data is largely neglected by current academic literature which focuses primarily on LOB modelling. In this paper, we demonstrate the utility of MBO data for forecasting high frequency price movements, providing an orthogonal source of information to LOB snapshots. We provide the first predictive analysis on MBO data by carefully introducing the data structure and presenting a specific normalisation scheme to consider level information in order books and to allow model training with multiple instruments. Through forecasting experiments using deep neural networks, we show that while MBO driven and LOB driven models individually provide similar performance, ensembles of the two can lead to improvements in forecasting accuracy indicating that MBO data is additive to LOB based features.

摘要 : 市场订单（MBO）数据交易所对给定股票的详细交易指令，可以说是微观结构信息最细粒度的来源之一。虽然限制订单书（LOB）是隐含地从中衍生出来的，但MBO数据在很大程度上被当前主要关注LOB建模的学术文献所忽略。在本文中，我们展示了MBO数据在预测高频价格变动方面的效用，为LOB快照提供了一个正交的信息源。我们通过仔细介绍数据结构和提出一个具体的标准化方案，对MBO数据进行了第一次预测分析，以考虑订单中的水平信息，并允许使用多个工具进行模型训练。通过使用深度神经网络的预测实验，我们发现，虽然MBO驱动和LOB驱动的模型各自提供相似的性能，但两者的集成可以提高预测精度，这表明MBO数据是基于LOB的特征的加法。
