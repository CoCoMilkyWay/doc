\[1\] The Active vs Passive: Smart Factors, Market Portfolio or Both?

标题:主动与被动：聪明因素、市场组合还是两者兼而有之？

作者:Matus Padysak(Quantpedia.com)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3745517

Abstract : While there may be debates about passive and active investing, and even blogs about the numbers of active funds that were outperformed by the market, the history taught us that the outperformance of active or passive investing is cyclical. As a proxy for the active investing, the paper examines factor strategies and their smart allocation using fast or slow time-series momentum signals, the relative weights based on the strength of the signals and even blending the signals. While the performance can be significantly improved, using those smartapproaches, the factors still got beaten by the market in both US and EAFE sample. However, the passive approach did not show to be superior. The factor strategies and market aresignificantly negatively correlated and impressively complement each other. The combined Smart Factors and market portfolio vastly outperforms both factors and market throughout the sample in both markets. With the combined approach, the ever-present market falls can be at least mitigated or profitable thanks to the factors.

Keywords : passive investing, active investing, market timing, smart beta, factors, factor allocation, momentum, factor momentum

Abstract :虽然可能会有关于被动和主动投资的争论，甚至有关于主动基金的数量超过市场表现的博客，但历史告诉我们，主动或被动投资的表现是周期性的。作为主动投资的代表，本文利用快或慢时间序列动量信号、基于信号强度的相对权重，甚至混合信号，研究了因子策略及其智能配置。虽然使用这些智能方法可以显著提高性能，但在美国和EAFE样本中，这些因素仍然被市场击败。然而，被动方法并没有显示出优越性。要素策略与市场之间存在显著的负相关关系，且具有显著的互补性。在这两个市场的整个样本中，组合的智能因素和市场投资组合的表现远远优于因素和市场。通过这种组合方法，由于这些因素，市场持续下跌至少可以得到缓解或盈利。

Keywords :被动投资，主动投资，市场时机，智能贝塔，因素，要素配置，动量，要素动量

\[2\] Leverage Constraints Affect Portfolio Choice: Evidence from Closed-End Funds

标题:杠杆约束影响投资组合选择：来自封闭式基金的证据

作者:Petri Jylha(Aalto University ),Paul Rintamäki(Aalto University - School of Business)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722010

Abstract : The market for auction rate securities collapsed following a series of failed auctions in February 2008, significantly constraining some closed-end funds’ access to leverage. We use this exogenous shock to study empirically how leverage constraints affect investors’ portfolio choice. Our main finding is that tightened leverage constraints result in an increased appetite for systematic risk: in the months following the shock, the affected funds bought significantly more high-beta stocks, and sold significantly more low-beta stocks, than their unaffected peers. Our results are consistent with the theoretical predictions of Black (1972) and Frazzini and Pedersen (2014), and provide causal evidence of the central mechanism underlying asset pricing under leverage constraints.

Keywords : leverage constraints, portfolio choice, closed-end funds

Abstract :在2008年2月一系列拍卖失败后，拍卖利率证券市场崩溃，严重限制了一些封闭式基金获得杠杆的机会。我们利用这种外生冲击来实证研究杠杆约束如何影响投资者的投资组合选择。我们的主要发现是，收紧杠杆约束导致对系统性风险的偏好增加：在冲击发生后的几个月里，受影响的基金买入的高贝塔股明显多于未受影响的基金，卖出的低贝塔股也明显多于未受影响的基金。我们的结果与Black（1972）、Frazzini和Pedersen（2014）的理论预测一致，并为杠杆约束下资产定价的核心机制提供了因果证据。

Keywords :杠杆约束、投资组合选择、封闭式基金

\[3\] The Complementarity Effect in the Venture Capital Contract: A Generalized Formulation and New Insights

标题:风险投资契约中的互补效应：一个广义公式和新见解

作者:Menghui Qiu(Santa Monica College)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3725712

Abstract : The moral hazard problem plagues the start-ups and even mature enterprises when they incorporate venture capital to pursue business upgrade or expansion. Especially in the value chain where the venture capitalist can synergize with the entrepreneur to contribute to the firm value. This paper proposes a game theoretic approach to solve the entrepreneur's optimization problem in a venture capital financing scheme and generalizes the formulation without a specific revenue function. Furthermore, this study generates managerial insights for venture capital market and relevant sustainable supply chain management. The key results also show that the complementarity effect can incentivize both the entrepreneur and the venture capitalist to exert more effort, thus achieving larger enterprise value when it is relatively significant, and the inefficiency caused by the allocation of equity share could be mitigated.

Keywords : equity share; venture capital; sequential decisions; game theory; supply chain management

Abstract :创业企业乃至成熟企业在引入风险投资进行业务升级或扩张时，道德风险问题一直困扰着它们。特别是在价值链中，风险资本家可以与企业家协同为企业价值做出贡献。本文提出了一种博弈论方法来解决风险投资融资方案中的企业家优化问题，并在没有特定收益函数的情况下推广了该公式。此外，本研究也为风险投资市场及相关的可持续供应链管理提供管理启示。关键结果还表明，互补效应可以激励企业家和风险资本家做出更大的努力，从而在互补效应相对显著的情况下获得更大的企业价值，并可以缓解股权配置的无效性。

Keywords :股权分置；风险投资；序贯决策；博弈论；供应链管理

\[4\] Multi-Asset Market Impact and Order Flow Commonality

标题:多资产市场影响与订单流共性

作者:Francesco Capponi(Imperial College London ),Rama Cont(University of Oxford)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3706390

Abstract : The empirical finding that market movements in stock prices may be correlated with the order flow of other stocks has led to the notion of "cross-impact" and has prompted the development of multivariate models of market impact. These models are parametrized by a matrix of impact coefficients whose off-diagonal elements are meant to capture how trades in one asset influence the price of other assets, leading to a large number of 'cross-impact' parameters which may not be identified solely based on the covariance of returns with order flow. Moreover, empirical evidence suggests that these cross-impact terms are unstable and change sign randomly over time, which poses a problem for their interpretation and use. We reexamine this empirical evidence from a causal standpoint and offer a simpler explanation for the observed correlation between the returns of an asset and the order flow imbalance of other assets, in terms of common components in order flow across stocks which may naturally arise from multi-asset trading strategies.We provide empirical evidence from order flow and price changes of NASDAQ-100 stocks to support this explanation. Our results show the main determinants of impact to be idiosyncratic order flow imbalance as well as a market order flow factor common across stocks. Additional ‘cross-impact’ terms account for less than 1% of market impact. This leads to a parsimonious approach for causal modelling of multi-asset impact, which does not require introducing any concept of "cross-impact".

Keywords : market impact, cross-impact, liquidity, volatility

Abstract :股票价格的市场运动可能与其他股票的订单流相关的实证研究结果导致了“交叉影响”的概念，并推动了市场影响的多元模型的发展。这些模型通过影响系数矩阵进行参数化，其非对角元素旨在捕捉一种资产的交易如何影响其他资产的价格，从而产生大量的“交叉影响”参数，这些参数可能无法仅基于订单流回报的协方差进行识别。此外，经验证据表明，这些交叉影响项是不稳定的，并且随着时间的推移随机改变符号，这给它们的解释和使用带来了问题。我们从因果关系的角度重新审视了这一经验证据，并为观察到的资产收益率与其他资产订单流失衡之间的相关性提供了更简单的解释，在多资产交易中自然产生的股票顺序流动的共同组成部分方面战略。我们从纳斯达克100指数成分股的订单流和价格变化提供实证证据来支持这一解释。我们的结果表明，影响的主要决定因素是特殊的订单流不平衡以及市场订单流因素共同的股票。额外的“交叉影响”条款占市场影响的比例不到1%。这导致多资产影响因果模型的简化方法，不需要引入任何“交叉影响”概念。

Keywords :市场影响、交叉影响、流动性、波动性

\[5\] Identification of Information Networks in Stock Markets

标题:证券市场信息网络的识别

作者:Margarita Baltakienė(Tampere University),Juho Kanniainen(Tampere University),Kęstutis Baltakys(Tampere University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3750035

Abstract : Existing studies have addressed the significance of social influence and private communication in decision making in stock markets. However, the estimation of investor information networks remains an important and challenging task because the existing network inference methodologies lack the ability to explicitly account for the impact of public information on investor trading decisions. We address this gap by proposing a new framework to estimate private information channels in stock markets. In our approach, the impact of public information on investors' trading events is filtered out from investors' transactions. This allows us to reveal their co-behavior driven by the transfer of private information. Our results show that taking public information into account when inferring investor networks significantly changes their topology and strengthens the relationship between investor's network centrality and returns. Therefore, we believe that our approach leads to a more precise representation of the information network. Moreover, we find that the association between centrality and returns becomes statistically insignificant when network links are validated. The latter observation provides evidence of the importance of weak ties in information diffusion.

Keywords : information channels, information transfer, investor network, network inference, private information, public information

Abstract :已有研究探讨了社会影响和私人沟通在股票市场决策中的意义。然而，投资者信息网络的估计仍然是一项重要而富有挑战性的任务，因为现有的网络推理方法缺乏明确说明公共信息对投资者交易决策影响的能力。我们提出了一个新的框架来估计股票市场中的私人信息渠道，以此来弥补这一差距。在我们的方法中，公开信息对投资者交易事件的影响是从投资者的交易中过滤出来的。这使我们能够揭示他们的共同行为所驱动的私人信息的转让。我们的研究结果表明，在推断投资者网络时考虑公共信息会显著改变其拓扑结构，并加强投资者网络中心性与收益之间的关系。因此，我们相信我们的方法可以更精确地表示信息网络。此外，我们发现当网路连结被验证时，中心性与回报之间的关联在统计上变得不显著。后一种观察结果证明了弱联系在信息传播中的重要性。

Keywords :信息渠道、信息传递、投资者网络、网络推理、私人信息、公共信息
