Abstract : In this paper, we empirically investigate the relationship between the convenience yield of government bonds and the real exchange rates using monthly data spanning from 1999 to 2018. \[6\] Decomposing the Effects of Crowd-Wisdom Aggregators: The Bias-Information-Noise (BIN) Model

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3781405

Abstract : Aggregating predictions from multiple judges often yields more accurate predictions than relying on a single judge: the “wisdom-of-the-crowd” effect. This aggregation can be conducted by different methods, from simple averaging to complex techniques, like Bayesian estimators and prediction markets. \[9\] Signal-precision Uncertainty and Trading Volume

标题:信号精度不确定性与交易量

作者:Chris Armstrong,Mirko Stanislav Heinle,Irina Luneva

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3780824

Abstract : Traditional models of market reactions to signals about a firm's value predict that trading volume is a V-shaped function of the signal. These models also imply that investors' posterior beliefs converge after the signal is released. However, empirical and anecdotal evidence suggest that trading volume is not V-shaped and that investors' posterior beliefs seem to diverge following the release of certain types of information. We reconcile the conflicting theoretical predictions and empirical evidence by developing a generalized model in which investors have both heterogeneous beliefs about a firm's prospects and uncertainty about the signal-precision. In contrast to traditional models, we show that some signals cause investors' beliefs to diverge and trading volume, in turn, becomes an M-shaped function of the signal. We document empirical evidence that corroborates this prediction: we show that trading volume is indeed an M-shaped function. We also develop a measure of signal-precision uncertainty and show that trading volume's M-shaped pattern is more pronounced in an environment with high signal-precision uncertainty.

Keywords : Signal-presicion, trading volume, uncertainty, disclosure

Abstract :传统的市场反应模型预测交易量是信号的V形函数。这些模型还表明，投资者的后验信念在信号释放后趋于一致。然而，经验和轶事证据表明，交易量不是V形的，投资者的后验信念似乎在某些类型的信息发布后出现分歧。我们通过建立一个广义模型来调和相互矛盾的理论预测和经验证据，在这个模型中，投资者对公司前景既有不同的信念，又对信号精度有不确定性。与传统模型不同的是，我们发现一些信号会导致投资者的信念发生偏离，成交量也会变成信号的M形函数。我们的文件实证证据证实了这一预测：我们表明，交易量确实是一个M形函数。我们还提出了信号精度不确定性的度量方法，并证明了在高信号精度不确定性环境下，交易量的M形格局更为明显。

Keywords :信号预测、交易量、不确定性、披露

\[10\] Information Absorption in Stocks with Short-Selling Constraints

标题:卖空约束下股票的信息吸收

作者:Ioannis V. Floros,Ajai K. Singh,Katsushi Suzuki

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3780820

Abstract : In an efficient market with unrestricted trading, information is expected to be absorbed into stock prices without delay. However, we document a phenomenon under short-sales restrictions, where the market reacts again to publicly available adverse information, to which it has already responded before. It is best explained by a heterogeneity of investors, where pessimistic investors’ response is initially stifled due to short-sales constraints. To identify and scrutinize the phenomenon, we employ a Japanese dataset endowed with distinctive regulatory features pertaining to trading restrictions for a subset of stocks, and unique regulations governing seasoned equity offers (SEOs). Specifically, a Japanese SEO’s issue date, by regulation, is a minimum of five trading days from its pricing date. Further, in Japan, unlike the U.S., both the price and quantity are already known prior to the offering’s issue date. We posit that when an SEO is announced for short-sales restricted stocks, pessimistic investors are initially kept at abeyance and restricted from responding freely to the offering-related adverse information, until the issue date. When the influx of additional shares occurs, pessimistic investors are finally engaged on the SEO’s issue date, and they trade at their lower reservation prices. The price reaction manifested on the issue date, to the now stale information to which the market has already reacted at its announcement, is akin to a “supply effect”. The phenomenon is attributable to a delayed reaction to publicly available information, manifested only when additional shares are introduced.

Keywords : Short-sales Constraints, Stale Information, Increased Supply, Relative Offer Size

Abstract :在一个无限制交易的有效市场中，信息被迅速地吸收到股票价格中。然而，我们记录了卖空限制下的一个现象，即市场对公开的不利信息再次作出反应，而此前市场已经对这些信息作出了反应。最好的解释是投资者的异质性，悲观投资者的反应最初由于卖空限制而受到抑制。为了识别和审视这一现象，我们使用了一个日本数据集，该数据集具有与股票子集交易限制相关的独特监管特征，以及管理季节性股票发行（SEO）的独特监管规定。具体来说，按照规定，日本SEO的发行日期至少为定价日后的五个交易日。此外，在日本，与美国不同的是，价格和数量在发行日期之前就已经知道了。我们假设，当宣布卖空限制性股票的SEO时，悲观的投资者最初被搁置，并被限制对发行相关的不利信息作出自由反应，直到发行日期。当增发股大量涌入时，悲观的投资者最终会在增发日参与增发，并以较低的保留价进行交易。在发行日表现出来的价格反应，对于现在已经过时的信息，市场已经在其公告中作出反应，类似于“供给效应”。这一现象可归因于对公开信息的延迟反应，只有在引入增发股份时才表现出来。

Keywords :卖空限制、陈旧信息、供应增加、相对报价规模
