作者:Gbenga Ibikunle, Ben Moews, Dmitriy Muravyev, Khaladdin Rzayev

来源:ARXIV\_20260804

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.00858

Abstract : We introduce data driven measures of high frequency trading (HFT) that distinguish between liquidity supplying and liquidity demanding strategies. We train machine learning models on a proprietary dataset with observed HFT activity, then apply these models to public intraday data to generate HFT measures across all U.S. stocks during 2010 2023. Our measures outperform conventional proxies, which struggle to capture the temporal dynamics of HFT. Consistent with theory, our measures respond to a quasi exogenous speed bump introduction and a data feed upgrade. The measures help uncover the differential impact of HFT on information acquisition. Liquidity supplying HFT improves price informativeness around earnings announcements, while liquidity demanding HFT impedes it.

\[2\] Predicting Startup Exit from Textual Descriptors   A Computational Linguistics Framework

标题:从文本描述符预测启动退出——一个计算语言学框架

作者:Alberto M.G. Saruggia, Sebastien Germano

来源:ARXIV\_20260804

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.00045

Abstract : This study shows that textual descriptors alone can predict early stage startup success, defined as Exit, without relying on contextual, financial, or human capital variables. Using venture capital curated datasets covering 7,419 startups over 20 years, the research isolates text based framing variables and engineers 850 features through startup narrative mapping. Data subsets and vector embeddings are evaluated for statistical significance, followed by supervised machine learning experiments across six models. LightGBM achieved the highest predictive performance (F1   0.48), while textual descriptors alone achieved F1   0.30, confirming the standalone predictive value of founder narratives. Feature analysis shows that optimized densities of hyping markers, including adjectives, jargon, and buzzwords, are associated with higher Exit probability, whereas excessive statement or name length reduces it. The study also introduces a quantifiable Hyping Score for venture capital applications, demonstrating that startup framing provides measurable signals for predicting Exit under conditions of high information asymmetry.

\[4\] Neural Networks with Local Converging Inputs for Efficient Options Pricing Models

标题:具有局部收敛输入的神经网络用于高效期权定价模型

作者:Harris Cobb, Wenbo Hao, Yingjie Liu

来源:ARXIV\_20260805

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02778

Abstract : We present a novel application of Neural Networks with Local Converging Inputs (NNLCI) to improve the efficiency of existing numerical methods for pricing multi asset options. The most concise input format for NNLCI has been introduced, offering substantial convenience and efficiency. NNLCI uses a neural network to locally correct solutions from a coarse mesh and a refined mesh (relative to the coarse one), requiring only a minimal amount of high fidelity training data. We demonstrate this approach on cash or nothing options under the Black Scholes equation in one, two, and three spatial dimensions, and on single asset down and out barrier call options under the Heston stochastic volatility model (whose pricing PDE is two dimensional in the spot price  S  and the instantaneous variance  v ). In each case, NNLCI reduces the root mean square error (RMSE) of the refined mesh numerical solution by a factor of approximately 4 12 on test sets, even when the neural network is trained on only a small subset of parameter combinations. These results demonstrate that NNLCI significantly reduces computational requirements for high dimensional problems in real time options trading and risk management, offering low training costs and strong generalization ability.

Abstract :我们提出了一种具有局部收敛输入的神经网络（NNLCI）的新应用，以提高现有多资产期权定价数值方法的效率。介绍了NNLCI最简洁的输入格式，提供了极大的便利和效率。NNLCI使用神经网络对粗网格和精细网格（相对于粗网格）的解进行局部校正，只需要最少量的高保真训练数据。我们在一、二和三个空间维度上对Black-Scholes方程下的现金或无现金期权以及Heston随机波动率模型下的单资产下下限看涨期权（其定价PDE在现货价格S和瞬时方差v中是二维的）演示了这种方法。在每种情况下，NNLCI在测试集上将精细网格数值解的均方根误差（RMSE）降低了约4.12倍，即使神经网络仅在一小部分参数组合上进行训练。这些结果表明，NNLCI显著降低了实时期权交易和风险管理中高维问题的计算要求，提供了低培训成本和强大的泛化能力。

\[6\] Cross Sectional Heterogeneity in LSTM Networks for Financial Time Series

标题:金融时间序列LSTM网络中的横截面异质性

作者:Julius Döbelt

来源:ARXIV\_20260807

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05755

Abstract : Predicting financial asset returns remains one of the most difficult challenges in empirical finance, driven by the low signal to noise ratio and the semi strong form of market efficiency. While deep learning models, especially LSTM networks, have shown promise in capturing temporal dependencies, standard architectures often struggle to account for the cross sectional heterogeneity of asset returns. This paper proposes a novel architectural extension to the basic LSTM model designed to improve both predictive accuracy and model interpretability. The framework integrates macro financial covariates to capture broader economic signals and learnable sector embeddings to encompass heterogeneity by sector. The trading strategy involves constructing a long short portfolio based on daily directional forecasts for each S&P 500 constituent, targeting stocks expected to under  or outperform the cross sectional median return of the S&P 500. Model Performance is evaluated against three competitive benchmarks  a basic LSTM, a Random Forest model and a traditional market buy and hold strategy. The empirical results demonstrate that the LSTM with sector embeddings outperforms all benchmarks across key risk and return metrics. By utilizing sector embeddings, the model explicitly incorporates cross sectional heterogeneity, allowing it to adapt to varying industry dynamics within the market. To address the black box nature of deep learning, I use latent space visualizations to analyse how the model differentiates between sectors, providing insights into the internal representation of the sectors in the LSTM. The impact of the sector information can be quantified using a novel contribution metric by inspecting the weights of the LSTM. The predictive signal is driven by a short term reversal factor and an industry momentum factor.

Keywords : 

Abstract :由于低信噪比和半强形式的市场效率，预测金融资产回报仍然是实证金融中最困难的挑战之一。虽然深度学习模型，特别是LSTM网络，在捕捉时间依赖性方面显示出了希望，但标准架构往往难以解释资产回报的横截面异质性。本文对基本LSTM模型提出了一种新的架构扩展，旨在提高预测精度和模型可解释性。该框架整合了宏观金融协变量，以捕捉更广泛的经济信号和可学习的部门嵌入，以涵盖各部门的异质性。交易策略包括根据标准普尔500指数各成分股的每日方向预测构建长短组合，目标是预期低于或超过标准普尔500的横截面中值回报的股票。模型性能根据三个竞争基准进行评估，即基本LSTM、随机森林模型和传统的市场买入并持有策略。实证结果表明，具有行业嵌入的LSTM在关键风险和回报指标上优于所有基准。通过利用行业嵌入，该模型明确地结合了横截面异质性，使其能够适应市场内不同的行业动态。为了解决深度学习的黑箱性质，我使用潜在空间可视化来分析模型如何区分部门，从而提供对LSTM中部门内部表示的见解。通过检查LSTM的权重，可以使用新的贡献度量来量化部门信息的影响。预测信号由短期反转因素和行业动量因素驱动。

\[7\] Large Market Discipline in Combinatorial Double Auctions

标题:组合式双拍卖中的大市场纪律

作者:Konstantinos E. Zachariadis, Yongxin Yang

来源:ARXIV\_20260807

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.06134

Abstract : We study double auctions for markets in which goods are valuable in bundles, such as data, model weights, and fine tuned AI assets. A key friction in such markets is No Assembly  a platform may be unable, for legal or technical reasons, to combine components supplied by different sellers into a single bundle. We formulate a combinatorial buyer s bid double auction under this constraint. Under explicit stability and price influence conditions (maintained in general, and for two goods derived from local price taking and a feedback bound), each bundle submarket inherits the large market discipline of single good double auctions  bid shading vanishes, and clearing prices concentrate on competitive levels and track the common value (price discovery). The key incentive step, that bidding on a bundle creates no first order strategic distortion beyond the single good logic, is proved for two goods  for larger item sets it remains a maintained condition. Multi agent reinforcement learning simulations decompose the welfare loss and indicate that No Assembly, not strategic shading, is the binding finite market friction, with both losses small in moderately thick markets and declining with complementarity amongst goods.

Keywords : 

Abstract :我们研究了捆绑商品有价值的市场的双重拍卖，如数据、模型权重和微调的人工智能资产。此类市场中的一个关键摩擦是，由于法律或技术原因，平台可能无法将不同卖家提供的组件组合成一个捆绑包。在此约束下，我们提出了一个组合买家出价双拍卖。在显式稳定性和价格影响条件下（一般保持不变，对于从局部价格获取和反馈约束中得出的两种商品），每个捆绑子市场都继承了单商品双拍卖的大市场规律——出价阴影消失，清算价格集中在竞争水平并跟踪共同价值（价格发现）。关键的激励步骤是，对捆绑包的投标不会产生超出单一良好逻辑的一阶战略扭曲，这一点在两种商品的较大商品集上得到了证明，它仍然是一个维持的条件。多智能体强化学习模拟分解了福利损失，并表明无组装，而不是战略阴影，是具有约束力的有限市场摩擦，在中等规模的市场中损失很小，并且随着商品之间的互补性而下降。

\[8\] Counterfactual Analysis via Large Language Models

标题:基于大型语言模型的反事实分析

作者:Zonghao Yang

来源:ARXIV\_20260807

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05367

Abstract : Counterfactual analysis aims to predict potential outcomes under hypothetical scenarios, offering valuable insights for decision making. This paper investigates the application of large language models (LLMs), specifically the GPT 3.5 model, for counterfactual analysis. We focus on the online lending context, where the counterfactual return on investment (ROI) is crucial for evaluating different interest rate schemes. We begin by assessing the predictive performance of GPT and comparing it with advanced machine learning algorithms. The results show that prompt engineering can significantly enhance GPT s predictions, with the R squared increasing from 1.97  to 2.84 , closely approaching the 3.48  achieved by gradient boosted regression. Subsequently, we utilize GPT to generate counterfactual ROIs under a set of alternative interest rates. GPT exhibits logical coherence and causal reasoning in its responses. The findings underscore the potential of LLMs as effective tools for counterfactual analysis in online lending, suggesting broader applications for LLMs in various predictive and decision making contexts.

Keywords : 

Abstract :反事实分析旨在预测假设情景下的潜在结果，为决策提供有价值的见解。本文研究了大型语言模型（LLM），特别是GPT 3.5模型在反事实分析中的应用。我们专注于在线借贷环境，其中反事实投资回报率（ROI）对于评估不同的利率方案至关重要。我们首先评估GPT的预测性能，并将其与先进的机器学习算法进行比较。结果表明，快速工程可以显著提高GPT的预测，R平方从1.97增加到2.84，接近梯度增强回归的3.48。随后，我们利用GPT在一组替代利率下生成反事实ROI。GPT在其反应中表现出逻辑连贯性和因果推理。研究结果强调了LLM作为在线借贷反事实分析的有效工具的潜力，表明LLM在各种预测和决策环境中具有更广泛的应用。
