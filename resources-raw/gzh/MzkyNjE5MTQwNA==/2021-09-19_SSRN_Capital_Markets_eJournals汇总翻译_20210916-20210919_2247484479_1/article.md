\[2\] Integrating Factor Models

标题:积分因子模型

作者:Doron Avramov,Si Cheng,Lior Metzker,Stefan Voigt

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3924337

Abstract : This paper proposes a comprehensive framework to address uncertainty about the correct factor model. Asset pricing inferences draw on a composite model that integrates over candidate models using posterior probabilities as weights. While the integrated model is weighted against deviations from the CAPM, evidence shows that (i) considerable time-varying mispricing exists, (ii) unconditional models record near-zero probabilities, and (iii) the post-earnings announcement drift, quality-minus-junk, and intermediary capital factors are incremental to the market. Moreover, equities appear considerably riskier in the presence of uncertainty about the correct model and underlying parameters. Out-of-sample, the integrated model delivers stable and outperforming efficient portfolios.

Keywords : Asset pricing models, Model integration, Posterior probability, Stock return predictability, Mispricing

Abstract :本文提出了一个全面的框架来解决关于正确因子模型的不确定性。资产定价推断基于一个组合模型，该模型使用后验概率作为权重，集成了多个候选模型。虽然综合模型根据与CAPM的偏差进行加权，但有证据表明：（i）存在相当大的时变错误定价，（ii）无条件模型记录了接近零的概率，以及（iii）盈利公告后的漂移、质量减去垃圾以及中介资本因素对市场的影响是递增的。此外，在正确的模型和基本参数存在不确定性的情况下，股票似乎风险更大。在样本之外，集成模型提供了稳定且优于高效的投资组合。

Keywords :资产定价模型、模型集成、后验概率、股票收益可预测性、错误定价

\[3\] Anxiety, Excitement, and Asset Prices

标题:焦虑、兴奋和资产价格

作者:Shehub Bin Hasan,Alok Kumar,Richard Taffler

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3902654

Abstract : This study examines the impact of integral emotions on portfolio decisions and asset prices. Using a new dictionary of anxiety- and excitement-related keywords, we measure the emotional state of the market and compute firm-level sensitivity to changes in market-level emotions (i.e., emotion beta). We find that stocks with high emotion betas outperform low emotion beta firms and this performance differential is corrected in about four months. During the 1990-2018 sample period, a Long-Short investment strategy with high-emotion beta stocks in the Long portfolio and low-emotion beta stocks in the Short generates an alpha of 4.92%. This evidence of emotion-based predictability is distinct from the known pricing effects of mood, sentiment, economic and policy uncertainty, and tone. Abstract : This study examines the behavior of sovereign bonds to COVID-19 related news in 24 countries most affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. \[5\] Delayed price adjustment and the estimation of systematic risk – empirical evidence from European stock markets

标题:延迟价格调整与系统风险估计——来自欧洲股市的经验证据

作者:Andrea Jacob

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3889915

Abstract : Even though technological advancement and global interconnected markets lead to persistent information flows and higher trading activity, price adjustment delays still arise as frictions in the financial market. This paper demonstrates that price adjustment delays influence the statistical properties of European daily stock returns. Cross-correlations in returns lead to biased estimation of systematic risk, i.e. the market beta. Traditional adjustment techniques for nonsynchronous trading effects take account of this shortcoming and effectively improve beta estimation. Especially portfolios sorted on stock characteristics known to be sensitive towards price adjustment delays profit from a more defined beta estimation technique. This study demonstrates how to derive an accurate estimation of systematic risk and thus enables more informed decision making in risk and portfolio management.

Keywords : price adjustment delays, nonsynchronous trading, systematic risk, beta adjustments

Abstract :尽管技术进步和全球互联市场导致持续的信息流动和更高的交易活动，但由于金融市场的摩擦，价格调整延迟仍然存在。本文证明了价格调整延迟影响欧洲股票日收益率的统计特性。回报的交叉相关性导致对系统风险的偏差估计，即市场贝塔。传统的非同步交易效应调整技术考虑了这一缺点，有效地改进了贝塔估计。特别是根据股票特征分类的投资组合，已知对价格调整敏感，延迟了更明确的贝塔估计技术带来的利润。本研究展示了如何得出系统风险的准确估计，从而在风险和投资组合管理中实现更明智的决策。

\[6\] The effect of labor unions on municipal bonds

标题:工会对市政债券的影响

作者:Yunhui Han

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3923674

Abstract : I present three findings on the effects of local unions on the municipal bond market. First, public- and private-sector unions play differential roles in capital markets: municipal bond issuers in areas with higher public-sector union density incur higher issuance costs and lower credit ratings. Private-sector unions only affect municipal bond issuance costs if bonds are backed by tax revenues, if bonds are not insured, or if bond issuers are exposed to strong private-sector union power. Second, by employing a regression discontinuity design using local variation in the vote share of union elections, I find that closely won union elections lead to significantly higher yields on the secondary market. Third, the issuance costs of municipal bonds are further affected by state-level legislation that restricts the collective bargaining power and impedes the possibility of a strike. Overall, I conclude that union exposure is viewed as a risk factor by municipal bond market participants.

Keywords : Municipal bonds, Public-sector unions, Private-sector unions, Cost of debt

Abstract :我提出了三个关于地方工会对市政债券市场影响的调查结果。首先，公共部门和私营部门的工会在资本市场中发挥着不同的作用：公共部门工会密度较高地区的市政债券发行人会产生较高的发行成本和较低的信用评级。只有在债券有税收支持、债券没有保险或债券发行人面临强大的私营部门工会力量的情况下，私营部门工会才会影响市政债券发行成本。其次，通过采用回归不连续设计，利用工会选举投票份额的局部变化，我发现获胜的工会选举导致二级市场收益率显著提高。第三，市政债券的发行成本进一步受到限制集体谈判能力和阻止罢工可能性的国家级立法的影响。总的来说，我的结论是，市政债券市场参与者将工会风险敞口视为一个风险因素。

Keywords :市政债券、公共部门工会、私营部门工会、债务成本

\[8\] Green portfolios

标题:绿色投资组合

作者:Thomas Fischer,Frederik Lundtofte

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3923578

Abstract : We develop a rational, long-horizon model in which investments may damage future production/consumption possibilities through their impact on the climate. Within this framework, we show that the optimal investment consists of four funds: i) the risk-free asset; ii) a myopic portfolio; iii) a portfolio that accounts for pollution externalities; iv) a hedging portfolio, which hedges the climate impact of investments. \[9\] Empirical Deep Hedging

标题:经验深度套期保值

作者:Oskari Mikkilä,Juho Kanniainen

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3923529

Abstract : The existing delta hedging strategies are model-based: either the strategies are explicitly based on a given option pricing model or reinforcement learning is used to train an agent based on synthetic data generated by a given volatility model. In this paper, we empirically train an agent for the hedging of derivative securities in a model-free manner using deep reinforcement learning (DRL) with continuous action domain. The training data consists of intra-day option price observations on S&P 500 index over six years, and top of that, we use other data periods for validation and testing. We have two important results. First, a DRL agent trained using synthetic data generated from a calibrated stochastic volatility model outperforms the practitioners delta hedging strategy. Second, and more importantly, we find that an empirically trained DRL agent has superior performance, even if no specifications about volatility jumps models were provided in the DRL procedure. Abstract :现有的增量套期保值策略是基于模型的：要么这些策略明确基于给定的期权定价模型，要么使用强化学习根据给定波动率模型生成的合成数据来训练代理。在本文中，我们使用具有连续作用域的深度强化学习（DRL）以无模型的方式对衍生证券套期保值代理进行了经验培训。培训数据包括标准普尔500指数六年内的日内期权价格观察，除此之外，我们还使用其他数据周期进行验证和测试。我们有两个重要的结果。首先，使用校准随机波动率模型生成的合成数据训练的DRL代理的表现优于对冲策略。第二，也是更重要的一点，我们发现，经过经验培训的DRL代理具有优异的性能，即使DRL程序中没有提供关于波动率跳跃模型的规范。这意味着DRL可以捕捉标准普尔500指数的动态，并自学如何有效对冲实际期权。

Keywords :期权，套期保值，期权，随机波动，深度强化学习

Abstract : In this paper, I test the prediction that green bonds sell for lower yields than equivalent non-green bonds. \[11\] Do Short Sellers Anticipate Future Market Returns? International Evidence

标题:卖空者是否预期未来的市场回报？国际证据

作者:Arseny Gorbenko

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3923111

Abstract : I show that short interest is a strong negative predictor of stock market returns internationally. Short interest significantly and negatively predicts returns in 24 out of 32 countries examined; this predictability survives out-of-sample tests, persists outside recessions, and is not subsumed by other well-known return predictors. The predictive power of short interest varies across regions and increases when short selling is constrained by local short sale regulations or the availability of shares in the equity lending market.
