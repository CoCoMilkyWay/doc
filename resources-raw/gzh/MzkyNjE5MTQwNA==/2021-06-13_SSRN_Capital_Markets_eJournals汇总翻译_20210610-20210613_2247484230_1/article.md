\[1\] Arrow-Debreu Meets Kyle

标题:阿罗·德布鲁遇见凯尔

作者:Christian Keller,Michael Tseng

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3862047

Abstract : We consider an extension of the Kyle (1985) setting where Arrow-Debreu securities are traded and the informed trader has private information regarding arbitrary higher moments of the asset payoff distribution. In this setting we analyze price discovery of Arrow-Debreu securities---equivalently, options. Our model admits a simple equilibrium solution. The informed trader strategy in our model is consistent with options trading strategies used to trade on higher moments in practice. We find that information efficiency of Arrow-Debreu prices increases with respect to the dispersion of the informed trader's private signals.

Keywords : Arrow-Debreu, Kyle, Option Price Discovery, Volatility Straddle.

Abstract :我们考虑了Kyle（1985）设置的一个扩展，其中Arrow-Debreu证券被交易，知情交易者拥有关于资产收益分布任意高阶矩的私人信息。在这个背景下，我们分析了阿罗-德布鲁证券的价格发现——相当于期权。我们的模型允许一个简单的平衡解。在我们的模型中，知情交易者的策略与期权交易策略是一致的，期权交易策略通常在高阶矩上进行交易。我们发现，随着知情交易者私人信号的分散，价格的信息效率会提高。

Keywords :Arrow Debreu，Kyle，期权价格发现，波动性跨越。

Abstract : A property's location is often considered to be the ultimate determinant of its investment performance. But how exactly does a property's location influence its risk and return? We focus on the effects of location density on the risk and return of commercial real estate investments. We do this by studying the geographical characteristics of the property portfolios of U.S. equity REITs. \[7\] Cross-Section of Option Returns and the Volatility Risk Premium

作者:Simon Fritzsch,Felix Irresberger,Gregor N. F. Weiss

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3864131

Abstract : This paper presents a robust new finding that delta-hedged equity option returns include a volatility risk premium. To separate volatility risk premia from confounding effects, we estimate conditional quantile curves of implied volatilities using machine learning. We find that a zero-cost trading strategy that is long (short) in the portfolio with low (high) implied volatility -- conditional on the options' moneyness and realized volatility -- produces an economically and statistically significant average monthly return. Using conditional quantile curves not only helps in distinguishing volatility risk premia from other effects, most notably realized volatility, it also leads to returns that are higher than those reported in previous work on similar volatility strategies.

Abstract : We use the COVID-19 pandemic as a natural experiment to study the effects of childcare and household duties on analysts. \[10\] Stock Liquidity and Algorithmic Market Making During the COVID-19 Crisis

标题:COVID-19危机期间的股票流动性与算法做市

作者:Bidisha Chakrabarty,Roberto Pascual

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3857463

Abstract : As the role of designated market makers in liquidity supply has diminished over time, algorithmic traders (ATs) have stepped up to fill that gap in modern equity markets. Prompted by concerns of fragility introduced by such voluntary market making, we examine ATs’ liquidity-provision role during the COVID-19 crisis. Amidst the turmoil as overall market liquidity declined, we find that ATs did not (disproportionately) withdraw liquidity supply. Stocks with the highest algorithmic trading (AT) activity experienced lower liquidity reduction compared to stocks with low AT activity, although profits from market making in high AT stocks fell more. High AT stocks did not experience greater reduction in either competition for liquidity provision or price improvements than low AT stocks. Multiple tests indicate that high AT activity did not associate with any greater deterioration in price efficiency vis-à-vis low AT stocks. Stocks in industries hardest hit by COVID-19 did not see any less AT competition for liquidity supply or price efficiency than stocks in the least affected ones. Overall, our results allay some recent concerns that the current levels of AT have made markets more susceptible to liquidity withdrawal in times of crises.

Keywords : COVID-19, algorithmic trading, liquidity, efficiency, competition

Abstract :随着时间的推移，指定做市商在流动性供应中的作用逐渐减弱，算法交易员（ATs）已加快步伐，填补现代股市的这一空白。出于对这种自愿做市带来的脆弱性的担忧，我们考察了ATs在COVID-19危机期间的流动性供给作用。在整体市场流动性下降的动荡中，我们发现ATs没有（不成比例地）收回流动性供应。算法交易（AT）活跃度最高的股票与AT活跃度较低的股票相比流动性减少较少，尽管AT活跃度较高的股票的做市利润下降较多。在流动性供应或价格改善的竞争中，高市值股票并没有经历比低市值股票更大的减少。多项测试表明，高AT活动与价格效率的任何更大恶化没有关联-à-股票价格很低。受COVID-19影响最严重的行业的股票在流动性供应或价格效率方面的竞争并不比受影响最小的行业的股票少。总的来说，我们的研究结果消除了近期的一些担忧，即当前的AT水平使市场在危机时期更容易出现流动性撤出。

Keywords :COVID-19，算法交易，流动性，效率，竞争
