\[1\] Identifying Signals of the Cross Section of Stock Returns

标题:股票收益横截面的识别信号

作者:Tengjia Shu,Ashish Tiwari

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3898282

Abstract : The proliferation of anomalies and the resulting \`factor zoo' has challenged finance researchers to identify firm characteristics that are genuinely related to the cross-sectional variation in expected stock returns. We address this challenge using a Bayesian ensemble of trees approach, namely, Bayesian Additive Regression Trees (BART), which combines the advantages of machine learning with a Bayesian inference framework. Applying the methodology to U.S. stock returns and a large set of characteristics, we find a firm's market value is the sole consistently relevant characteristic. A BART framework that exploits the information content of a sparse set of characteristics identified in real time, offers substantial gains over competing models in both statistical and economic terms. We further confirm that the stochastic discount factor based on a sparse set of factors can successfully explain most of the variation in expected returns. A key reason for the sparsity of the factor set identified by BART is the methodology’s ability to incorporate non-linearities and high order interaction effects in a non-parametric manner. Our findings suggest that the vast majority of the documented anomalies are redundant, and in this sense our results offer a more optimistic view of the state of affairs in asset pricing.

Keywords : None

Abstract :异常现象的激增以及由此产生的“因子动物园”让金融研究人员面临着挑战，他们必须确定与预期股票回报的横截面变化真正相关的公司特征。我们使用贝叶斯树集成方法，即贝叶斯加性回归树（BART），将机器学习的优点与贝叶斯推理框架相结合，来解决这一挑战。将该方法应用于美国股票收益率和一系列特征，我们发现公司的市场价值是唯一一致相关的特征。BART框架利用实时识别的稀疏特征集的信息内容，在统计和经济方面都比竞争模型有很大的优势。我们进一步证实，基于稀疏因子集的随机贴现因子可以成功地解释预期收益的大部分变化。BART确定的因子集稀疏的一个关键原因是，该方法能够以非参数方式将非线性和高阶相互作用效应结合起来。我们的研究结果表明，绝大多数记录在案的异常现象都是多余的，从这个意义上说，我们的结果为资产定价的现状提供了一个更为乐观的观点。

Keywords :None

\[2\] Two Investors, Two Trees, Two Goods

标题:两个投资者，两棵树，两种商品

作者:Maxime Sauzet

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3898027

Abstract : I characterize the global solution to the portfolio problem of two heterogeneous investors with general preferences, in a two-tree, two-good environment. Investors have recursive preferences and a bias in consumption towards a preferred good. The framework highlights the role of the allocation of wealth across investors for portfolios, asset prices, and risk sharing, an aspect that had received little emphasis in such a setting. The influence of the allocation of wealth grows especially as markets become imperfectly integrated, and as investor heterogeneity rises -- be it through a larger bias in consumption, the introduction of labor income, or asymmetries in preferences -- to the point where it can match or surpass the impact of fundamentals. The framework lends itself to several applications and extensions, e.g. in international or environmental contexts.

Keywords : Portfolio Choice, Asset Pricing, Wealth Allocation, Heterogeneous Investors, International Financial System, Environmental Finance

Abstract :在两棵树、两个良好的环境中，我描述了两个具有一般偏好的异质投资者的投资组合问题的全局解。投资者有递归偏好，消费倾向于偏好商品。该框架强调了投资组合、资产价格和风险分担在投资者之间分配财富的作用，而在这样的背景下，这一点很少受到重视。财富分配的影响尤其随着市场的不完全整合，以及投资者异质性的上升——无论是通过更大的消费偏好、劳动力收入的引入，还是偏好的不对称——达到可以匹配或超越基本面影响的程度。该框架适用于多种应用和扩展，例如在国际或环境环境中。

Keywords :投资组合选择、资产定价、财富分配、异质投资者、国际金融体系、环境金融

\[4\] Naïve Earnings Growth Extrapolation

标题:天真的收益增长外推

作者:Chenyu Cui,Frank Weikai Li,Xinyi Zhang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3897761

Abstract : Exploiting the unique financial reporting format in China, we document that stocks with the strongest past year-to-date earnings growth experience a significant price run-up of 1.2% during the five trading days before their quarterly earnings announcements and a significant return reversal of -1.35% in the five trading days afterward. This inverted V-shaped pattern on cumulative return spreads is more pronounced among smaller firms with lower institutional ownership and fewer analyst coverage, and it is less pronounced among foreign B-share. Consistent with investor excess demand driving the price run-up, we find retail investor sentiment and buy-sell order imbalance rise ahead of earnings announcements for firms with high past earnings growth. Our findings support models of fundamental extrapolation and suggest investors naively extrapolate the salient but not-so-informative year-to-date earnings growth when forming expectations about the upcoming earnings.

Keywords : Extrapolation, Earnings Announcements, Speculative Trading, Financial Reporting

Abstract :利用中国独特的财务报告格式，我们记录了过去一年迄今盈利增长最强劲的股票在季度盈利公告前的五个交易日内价格大幅上涨1.2%，随后的五个交易日内回报率大幅反转-1.35%。累积收益利差的倒V型模式在机构所有权较低、分析师覆盖率较低的小公司中更为明显，而在外国B股中则不太明显。与推动价格上涨的投资者过度需求一致，我们发现，对于过去盈利增长率较高的公司，在发布盈利公告之前，散户投资者情绪和买卖订单失衡上升。我们的研究结果支持基本面推断模型，并表明投资者在形成对未来收益的预期时，天真地推断了今年迄今为止显著但信息量不大的收益增长。

Keywords :外推、收益公告、投机交易、财务报告

\[5\] Is Corporate Governance Priced in the Option Market? Evidence from Shareholder Proposals

标题:公司治理是否在期权市场中定价？股东提案的证据

作者:Jimmy Chengyuan Qu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3897217

Abstract : By exploiting the local randomness in close-call votes on governance-related shareholder proposals, this paper finds a negative effect of passing a governance proposal on firms’ ex-ante tail risk measured by the cost of option protection against downside tail risks, which suggests that corporate governance is priced in the option market. In a local regression discontinuity (RD) analysis, firms that narrowly pass the majority threshold show a lower ex-ante tail risk measured by implied volatility smirk and model-free implied skewness than those that narrowly fail. This effect is stronger for firms with weaker corporate governance and higher information transparency and is attenuated when firms perform better. Evidence from a global RD analysis confirms the external validity of results in the local RD analysis. Overall, this paper observes a causal impact of corporate governance on the option market and sheds new light on the cross-sectional determinants of option prices.

Keywords : Corporate Governance; Shareholder Proposals; Option Prices; Ex-ante Tail Risk; Implied Volatility Smirk; Model-free Implied Skewness

Abstract :通过利用与治理相关的股东提案的近距离看涨表决中的局部随机性，本文发现通过治理提案对公司的事前尾部风险产生了负面影响，其衡量方法是针对下行尾部风险的期权保护成本，这表明公司治理是在期权市场中定价的。在局部回归不连续性（RD）分析中，勉强通过多数阈值的公司与勉强失败的公司相比，通过隐含波动率傻笑和无模型隐含偏度测量的事前尾部风险更低。对于公司治理较弱、信息透明度较高的公司，这种效应更强，而当公司表现更好时，这种效应会减弱。来自全球研发分析的证据证实了本地研发分析结果的外部有效性。总体而言，本文观察了公司治理对期权市场的因果影响，并对期权价格的横截面决定因素提供了新的解释。

Keywords :公司治理；股东提案；期权价格；事前尾部风险；隐含波动性假笑；无模型隐含偏度

\[10\] An Insight into SPACs and their Valuation Conundrum

标题:对SPAC及其估值难题的洞察

作者:Raghav Agarwal

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3882261

Abstract : There has been a great deal of SPAC activity in the last year and a half, but there are still a lot of unknowns in the dynamics of a SPAC deal. In this paper, I explore Special Purpose Acquisition Companies (SPACs), their origins and evolution, their origins and evolution, guaranteed returns for sponsors, investor protections, and investor risks. A recent SPAC acquisition – ARBE Robotics’ acquisition by ITAC, a publicly-traded SPAC is used as an example of the valuation conundrum faced by SPAC investors. I analyze recent SEC guidance on warrants and how that will limit future SPAC deals and potential returns. The findings summarized in this paper are arrived from analyzing publicly available data. I perform a valuation analysis on the ITAC deal and try to reconcile the values used in the investor presentation to two commonly used valuation methodologies - perpetual growth rate and the enterprise exit multiple, which reinforce the lack of adequate due diligence. It is important to note that the majority of business combinations associated with SPACs have no requirement for independent valuation opinions during M&A. I believe that this lack of requirement is going to lead to more failures and losses for investors in SPACs and therefore public investors in SPACs should refrain from investing in their IPOs. Public investors are advised to wait for changes in public policy or wait till after the business combination is announced.
