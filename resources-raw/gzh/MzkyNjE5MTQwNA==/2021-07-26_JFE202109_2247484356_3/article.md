\[1\] Network risk and key players: A structural analysis of interbank liquidity

标题:网络风险与关键参与者：银行间流动性的结构分析

作者:Edward Denbee,Christian Julliard,Ye Li,Kathy Yuan

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001926

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.010

Abstract : Using a structural model, we estimate the liquidity multiplier of an interbank network and banks’ contributions to systemic risk. To provide payment services, banks hold reserves. Their equilibrium holdings can be strategic complements or substitutes. The former arises when payment velocity and multiplier are high. The latter prevails when the opportunity cost of liquidity is large, incentivising banks to borrow neighbors’ reserves instead of holding their own. Consequently, the network can amplify or dampen shocks to individual banks. Empirically, network topology explains cross-sectional heterogeneity in banks’ systemic-risk contributions while changes in the equilibrium type drive time-series variation.

Keywords : Financial networks;Liquidity;Interbank market;Payment systems;Payment velocity;Payment multiplier;Key players;Systemic risk;Spatial models

Abstract :利用结构模型，我们估计了银行间网络的流动性乘数以及银行对系统性风险的贡献。为了提供支付服务，银行持有准备金。它们的均衡持有量可以是战略的补充或替代。前者出现在支付速度和乘数较高时。当流动性的机会成本很大时，后者就占了上风，促使银行借入邻国的储备，而不是持有自己的储备。因此，网络可以放大或抑制对个别银行的冲击。从经验上看，网络拓扑结构解释了银行系统性风险贡献的横截面异质性，而均衡类型的变化驱动时间序列的变化。

Keywords :金融网络；资产变现能力银行间市场；支付系统；支付速度；支付乘数；关键参与者；系统性风险；空间模型

\[2\] Central bank communication and the yield curve

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001665

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.036

Abstract : In this paper, we argue that monetary policy in the form of central bank communication can shape long-term interest rates by changing risk premia. Using high-frequency movements of default-free rates and equity, we show that monetary policy communications by the European Central Bank on regular announcement days led to a significant yield spread between peripheral and core countries during the European sovereign debt crisis by increasing credit risk premia. 链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002105

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.028

Abstract : We provide direct evidence that governments selectively default on debt when they can identify creditors. Analyzing a comprehensive data set of subnational debt, we show that Chinese local governments choose to default on banks with weaker political power. A reduction in a bank's political power relative to other banks increases the likelihood of selective default by local governments. Such default selections are driven by banks’ influence over politician promotion. When local politicians are highly ranked or connected to national leaders, they engage less in selective default as their promotion is less affected by bank loan defaults. \[5\] The short duration premium

标题:短期保险费

作者:Andrei S. Gonçalves

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X2100146X

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.019

Abstract : Stocks of firms with cash flows concentrated in the short term (i.e., short duration stocks) pay a large premium over long duration stocks. I empirically demonstrate that this premium (i) is long-lived and strong even among large firms, (ii) subsumes the value and profitability premia, and (iii) exposes investors to variation in expected returns, especially in times when the premium is high. These facts are consistent with an intertemporal model in which the marginal (long-term) investor dislikes expected return declines as they lead to lower expected wealth growth. \[6\] Long-term discount rates do not vary across firms

标题:不同公司的长期贴现率没有差异

作者:Matti Keloharju,Juhani T. Linnainmaa,Peter Nyberg

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001616

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.031

Abstract : Long-term expected returns do not appear to vary in the cross section of stocks. We show that even negligible persistent differences in expected returns, if they existed, would be easy to detect. Markers of such differences, however, are absent from actual stock returns. Our results are consistent with behavioral models and production-based asset pricing models in which firms’ risks change over time. Consistent with the lack of long-term differences in expected returns, persistent differences in firm characteristics do not predict the cross section of stock returns. Our results imply that stock market anomalies have only a limited effect on firm valuations.

Keywords : Factors;Return predictability;Market efficiency;Production-based asset pricing models;Time-varying risks

Abstract :长期预期收益在股票的横截面上似乎没有变化。我们表明，即使预期收益中存在微不足道的持续差异，也很容易被发现。然而，实际股票回报率中却没有这种差异的标记。我们的结果与行为模型和基于生产的资产定价模型是一致的，在这些模型中，企业的风险随着时间的推移而变化。与预期收益缺乏长期差异相一致，企业特征的持续差异并不能预测股票收益的横截面。我们的研究结果表明，股市异常对公司估值的影响有限。

Keywords :因素；收益可预测性；市场效率；基于生产的资产定价模型；时变风险

\[7\] Who provides liquidity, and when?

标题:谁提供流动性，何时提供？

作者:Sida Li,Xin Wang,Mao Ye

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001501

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.020

Abstract : We model competition for liquidity provision between high-frequency traders (HFTs) and slower execution algorithms (EAs) designed to minimize investors’ transaction costs. Under continuous pricing, EAs dominate liquidity provision by using aggressive limit orders to stimulate HFTs’ market orders. Under discrete pricing, HFTs dominate liquidity provision if the bid-ask spread is binding at one tick. If the tick size (minimum price variation) is not binding, EAs choose between stimulating HFTs and providing liquidity to non-HFTs. Transaction costs increase with the tick size but can be negatively correlated with the bid-ask spread when all traders can provide liquidity.

Keywords : High-frequency trading;Algorithmic trading;Tick size;Liquidity;Bid-ask spread

Abstract :我们模拟了高频交易者（hft）和慢执行算法（EAs）之间的流动性供应竞争，后者旨在最小化投资者的交易成本。在连续定价下，东亚银行通过积极的限制指令刺激高频交易的市场指令来主导流动性供应。在离散定价下，如果买卖价差在一个点上具有约束力，高频交易将主导流动性供给。如果刻度大小（最小价格变化）不具有约束力，东亚峰会将在刺激高频交易和向非高频交易提供流动性之间做出选择。交易成本随着交易量的增加而增加，但当所有交易者都能提供流动性时，交易成本可能与买卖价差负相关。

Keywords :高频交易；算法交易；刻度大小；资产变现能力买卖价差

\[9\] What drove the 2003–2006 house price boom and subsequent collapse? Disentangling competing explanations

标题:是什么推动了2003-2006年的房价繁荣和随后的崩溃？解开相互矛盾的解释

Abstract : Ten years after the financial crisis, the central question of what explains the rise and fall in house prices remains unresolved. \[10\] Debt relief and slow recovery: A decade after Lehman

标题:债务减免与缓慢复苏：雷曼破产后的十年

作者:Tomasz Piskorski,Amit Seru

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20303482

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.12.008

Abstract : We follow a representative panel of millions of consumers in the United States from 2007 to 2017 and document new facts on the long-term effects of the Great Recession. There were about six million foreclosures over this period. Only a quarter of foreclosed households regained homeownership, taking an average four years to do so. This persistent loss of homeownership accounts for most of the decline in the U.S. homeownership rate. Despite massive stimulus and debt relief policies, house prices, consumption, and unemployment remained below precrisis levels in about half of the zip codes in the United States Regions that recovered to precrisis levels took on average four to five years. Keywords : Great Recession;Slow recovery;Debt relief;Refinancing;Consumption;House prices;Default;Foreclosures;Unemployment;Mortgages;Government intervention;Monetary policy pass-through

Keywords :大萧条；恢复缓慢；债务减免；再融资；消费；房价；违约取消抵押品赎回权；失业；抵押贷款；政府干预；货币政策传导

\[11\] Diagnostic bubbles

标题:诊断气泡

作者:Pedro Bordalo,Nicola Gennaioli,Spencer Yongwook Kwon,Andrei Shleifer

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20301860

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.06.019

Abstract : We introduce diagnostic expectations into a standard setting of price formation in which investors learn about the fundamental value of an asset and trade it. We study the interaction of diagnostic expectations with learning from prices and speculation (buying for resale). With diagnostic (but not with rational) expectations, these mechanisms lead to price paths exhibiting three phases: initial underreaction, then overshooting (the bubble), and finally a crash. When investors speculate, even mild diagnostic distortions generate substantial bubbles.

Keywords : Bubble;Speculation;Diagnostic expectations

Abstract :我们将诊断性预期引入价格形成的标准设置中，投资者在其中了解资产的基本价值并进行交易。我们研究诊断期望与从价格和投机（转售购买）中学习的相互作用。在诊断性（但不是理性）预期下，这些机制导致价格路径呈现出三个阶段：最初反应不足，然后过度（泡沫），最后崩溃。通过从价格中学习，该模型产生了价格外推，作为基本面信念的副产品，只在泡沫形成时持续。当投资者投机时，即使是轻微的诊断扭曲也会产生大量泡沫。

\[12\] Intraday arbitrage between ETFs and their underlying portfolios

标题:ETF与其基础投资组合之间的日内套利

作者:Travis Box,Ryan Davis,Richard Evans,Andrew Lynch

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001537

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.023

Abstract : Prior research suggests that nonfundamental exchange-traded fund (ETF) price shocks are transmitted to their portfolios through an arbitrage mechanism.
