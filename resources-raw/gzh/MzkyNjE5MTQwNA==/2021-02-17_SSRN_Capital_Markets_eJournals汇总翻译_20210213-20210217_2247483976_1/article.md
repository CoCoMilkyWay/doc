\[1\] COVID-19 Vaccines: Saving Lives and the Global Stock Markets

标题:COVID-19疫苗：拯救生命与全球股市

作者:Kam Fong Chan,Zhuo Chen,Yuanji Wen,Tong Xu

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3785533

Abstract : Global stock markets react positively when different phases of human clinical trials on COVID-19 vaccine candidates begin. The average increase in stock market returns on day one of the trials is 15.2 basis points, and this estimate is both economically and statistically significant. There are also cross-phase and cross-country variations in the market reactions. We use a simple capital budgeting framework to explain the finding: stock markets convey important information about the market-wide expectation on the development of vaccines that evolves along with the pandemic.

Keywords : COVID-19, pandemics, stock markets, vaccines

Abstract :当COVID-19候选疫苗人体临床试验的不同阶段开始时，全球股市反应积极。在试验的第一天，股票市场回报的平均增长是15.2个基点，这个估计在经济上和统计上都是显著的。市场反应也存在跨阶段和跨国家的差异。我们使用一个简单的资本预算框架来解释这一发现：股票市场传达了有关市场对疫苗开发的重要信息，这些疫苗随着大流行而发展。

Keywords :COVID-19，流行病，股票市场，疫苗

\[2\] Hedging, Hedge Accounting, and Earnings Predictability

标题:套期保值、套期会计和收益可预测性

作者:Tharindra Ranasinghe,Shiva Sivaramakrishnan,Lin Yi

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3770487

Abstract : Studies suggest that pursuant to the implementation of SFAS No. 133, even sophisticated users of financial statements find it difficult to comprehend earnings implications of hedging derivatives. Moreover, due to stringent hedge accounting requirements under these standards, many economic hedges do not qualify for hedge accounting and are deemed “ineffective” for financial reporting purposes. Motivated by these considerations, we investigate the impact of hedging on earnings predictability by analyzing hand-collected hedging data from two industries that extensively use derivatives to manage price risks: the oil and gas exploration and production industry and the airline industry. In contrast to extant evidence, we find that overall, hedging derivatives improve income predictability and increase (decrease) analysts’ forecast accuracy (dispersion). We also show that ineffective hedges significantly impair earnings predictability. Our evidence also indicates that ineffective hedges sometimes make earnings even less predictable than if there were no hedges in the first place, which lends support to concerns expressed by some corporate managers and industry experts against stringent hedge accounting requirements.

Keywords : Financial analysts, earnings forecasts, derivatives, hedging, hedge ineffectiveness

Abstract :研究表明，根据财务会计准则第133号的实施，即使是财务报表的高级使用者也很难理解对冲衍生工具的收益含义。此外，由于这些标准下严格的对冲会计要求，许多经济对冲不符合对冲会计的条件，并被视为“无效”的财务报告目的。基于这些考虑，我们通过分析从两个广泛使用衍生工具管理价格风险的行业（油气勘探与生产行业和航空业）收集的对冲数据，研究对冲对收益可预测性的影响。与现有证据相比，我们发现，总体而言，对冲衍生品提高了收入的可预测性，并增加（减少）分析师的预测准确性（离散度）。我们还表明，无效的对冲显着损害收益的可预测性。我们的证据还表明，无效的对冲有时会使收益的可预测性甚至低于当初没有对冲的情况，这支持了一些公司经理和行业专家对严格的对冲会计要求表示的担忧。

Keywords :财务分析师、盈利预测、衍生品、对冲、对冲无效性

\[3\] How Elon Musk's Twitter Activity Moves Cryptocurrency Markets

作者:Lennart Ante

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3778844

Abstract : Elon Musk, the richest person in the world, is considered a technological visionary and has over 44.7 million Twitter followers. We analyze to what extent Musk’s Twitter activity affects short-term cryptocurrency returns and volume. Based on six recent cryptocurrency-related Twitter activities, we identify highly significant abnormal trading volume following each event. Furthermore, we discover significantly abnormal returns of up to 18.99% for Bitcoin and 17.31% for Dogecoin across different time frames. \[4\] Taking the Heat: (Non)Disclosure of Climate Change Risks in India

标题:冒着酷暑：（不）披露印度气候变化风险

作者:Anik Bhaduri

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3777390

Abstract : Following the bankruptcy of Pacific Gas and Electric in California, a number of academic commentators, central banks and regulators are coming to the realization that climate change poses a massive threat to the existing financial markets. While numerous legislative proposals to combat the emerging threat are being discussed across the world, there has been a massive rise in securities litigation involving climate change issues, particularly in the US. As early as in 2007, some US states had begun investigating companies for deliberately misleading their shareholders through non-disclosure of climate change risks. A number of similar suits have followed, increasingly binging into question the meaning and extent of ‘material information’ in the context of capital markets. In India, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has identified climate change as a potential risk to the stability of the financial system, but there has been little legislative action in this regard. The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has a provision similar to that in the US requiring all listed companies to disclose ‘material’ facts regarding their assets and value on the market, while Indian courts have long recognized that corporations as well as intermediaries on the securities market are bound by a fiduciary duty to disclose all material information to investors. Drawing a comparison with the rising trend of climate change related securities litigation in the US, this paper explores whether a company failing to disclose risks associated with climate change would be in violation of the current Indian regulatory framework, and what kind of legislative changes, if any, are required to safeguard the interests of Indian investors.

Keywords : climate change, disclosure, securities regulation, corporate governance, financial bubble

Abstract :在加州太平洋天然气和电力公司破产后，一些学术评论员、央行和监管机构开始意识到，气候变化对现有金融市场构成了巨大威胁。尽管世界各地都在讨论应对这一新兴威胁的众多立法建议，但涉及气候变化问题的证券诉讼却大幅上升，尤其是在美国。早在2007年，美国一些州就开始调查公司，因为这些公司故意通过不披露气候变化风险误导股东。一系列类似的诉讼也接踵而至，越来越多地对资本市场背景下“重大信息”的含义和范围提出质疑。在印度，印度储备银行（RBI）已将气候变化确定为金融体系稳定的潜在风险，但在这方面几乎没有立法行动。印度证券交易委员会（SEBI）有一项类似于美国的规定，要求所有上市公司披露有关其资产和市场价值的“重大”事实，尽管印度法院早就认识到，证券市场上的公司和中介机构有义务向投资者披露所有重要信息。本文通过与美国气候变化相关证券诉讼上升趋势的比较，探讨了一家公司如果不披露与气候变化相关的风险，是否会违反印度现行的监管框架，以及需要进行什么样的立法变革来维护其利益印度投资者。

Keywords :气候变化、信息披露、证券监管、公司治理、金融泡沫

Abstract : Abstract: In the recent time, the relation between accounting information and stock market value has been an internationally popular subject for research. The important is that value relevance is a measure of investor perception of the reliability of corporate financial accounting information. This study investigates the value relevance of accounting information and stock market prices on listed companies in Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) Sri Lanka. The study was identified 24 Companies listed in the CSE for the period from year 2006/2007 to 2010/2011.The changes in Earning Per Share ( EPS ) and changes in Net Asset Value Per Share ( NAVPS ) are the independent variables and changes in Share Price as dependent variable for this study. The present study used the correlation coefficient and linear regression to measure the value relevance between share prices and two core accounting variables. It is also found that there is 30% relationship between share price and EPS and found that there is a negative relationship between EPS and NAVPS. According to the present study, investors tend to focus on information other than the corporate financial accounting information, such as short term capital gains and tend to be speculative or irrational for unusual events.

Keywords : Earning Per Share, Net Asset Value Per Share, Share Price, CSE

Abstract :摘要：近年来，会计信息与股票市场价值的关系一直是国际上研究的热门课题。重要的是，价值相关性是衡量投资者对企业财务会计信息可靠性认知的一种尺度。本研究探讨斯里兰卡科伦坡证券交易所上市公司会计信息与股票市场价格的价值相关性。本研究选取了2006/2007年至2010/2011年期间在中国证监会上市的24家公司为研究对象，以每股收益（EPS）和每股净资产（NAVPS）的变动为自变量，以股价变动为因变量。本研究利用相关系数与线性回归来衡量股价与两个核心会计变量之间的价值相关性。研究发现，只有9.3%的股价变动可以解释为已定义会计变量的变动；另一方面，考虑到样本公司股价与资产净值之间存在负相关关系。研究还发现，股票价格与每股收益之间存在30%的相关关系，每股收益与资产净值之间存在负相关关系。根据目前的研究，投资者倾向于关注公司财务会计信息以外的信息，如短期资本利得，并倾向于对异常事件的投机性或非理性。

Keywords :每股收益、每股资产净值、股价、综合股价

\[6\] Diversity, Disagreement, and Stock Price Crash Risk

标题:多样性、分歧和股价暴跌风险

作者:Yen-Cheng Chang,Yu-Siang Su,Kevin Tseng,Na Wang

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3780961

Abstract : We provide plausibly causal evidence for the role of cultural and linguistic diversity in stock price crash risk. Using unique data from China, we show that firms headquartered in linguistically diverse areas, instrumented by the extent of geographical isolation, have higher stock price crash risk, but not higher jump risk. The effect is more pronounced for stocks that are more likely to be held by local investors. Our evidence further shows that linguistic diversity is associated with investor disagreement, and not with companies’ fundamentals, propensities to hoard bad news, or innovation activities. Our results are consistent with models of investor disagreement.

Keywords : linguistic diversity, investor disagreement, stock price crash risk

Abstract :我们为文化和语言多样性在股价暴跌风险中的作用提供了合理的因果证据。利用来自中国的独特数据，我们发现，总部位于语言多样性地区的公司，受地理隔离程度的影响，有较高的股价暴跌风险，但没有较高的跳跃风险。对于更可能由本地投资者持有的股票，这种影响更为明显。我们的证据进一步表明，语言多样性与投资者意见分歧有关，而与公司的基本面、囤积坏消息的倾向或创新活动无关。我们的结果与投资者意见分歧的模型是一致的。

Keywords :语言多样性、投资者意见分歧、股价暴跌风险

\[7\] Central Bank Communication Choices: Adverse Selection, Volatility and Liquidity in a Market With Fast and Slow Traders

标题:央行沟通选择：快速和慢速交易者市场中的逆向选择、波动性和流动性

作者:Hedi Benamar,Alain Chaboud,Clara Vega

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3779912

Abstract : We study the impact of different central bank communication practices on the trading behavior and profitability of fast and slow traders in the foreign exchange market. \[8\] Value Investing: Requiem, Rebirth or Reincarnation?

标题:价值投资：安魂曲，重生还是轮回？

作者:Bradford Cornell,Aswath Damodaran

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3779481

Abstract : For much of the last century, value investors considered themselves to be the winners in the investment world, a result they attributed to their patience, maturity and good sense. That view, at least on the surface, was backed up by evidence that “value” stocks, defined as stocks that trade at low multiples of earnings and book value, earned higher returns than “growth” stocks, defined loosely as companies that trade at high multiples of earnings or book value. It was reinforced by the mythology of great value investors, with Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger taking center stage, as deep thinkers, with profound insights on how markets work. In the last two decades, value investing lost its edge, and a debate has revolved around whether this is a temporary phase, and the result of an unusual macro environment, or a reflection of a permanent change in economies and markets. In this paper, we argue that value investing, at least as practiced today, has become rigid and ritualistic, and that while some of its failures can be attributed to external factors, many can be traced back to practices and rules of thumb that have outlived their usefulness. We argue that if value investing is to be successful in the future, it needs to develop a more dynamic view of value and a greater willingness to live with and invest in the face of uncertainty.

Keywords : Value Investing, Growth Investing, Active Investing

Abstract :在上个世纪的大部分时间里，价值投资者都认为自己是投资界的赢家，这一结果归功于他们的耐心、成熟和良好的判断力。这一观点至少在表面上得到了证据的支持，即“价值型”股票（定义为以低收益和账面价值倍数交易的股票）比“成长型”股票（定义为以高收益或账面价值倍数交易的公司）获得更高的回报。沃伦·巴菲特和查理·芒格作为深邃的思想者，以对市场运作方式的深刻洞察，登上了舞台的中心，这一伟大价值投资者的神话进一步强化了这一点。在过去的二十年里，价值投资失去了它的优势，一场争论围绕着这是一个暂时的阶段，是不寻常的宏观环境的结果，还是经济和市场永久性变化的反映。在本文中，我们认为，价值投资，至少在今天的实践中，已经变得僵化和仪式化，虽然它的一些失败可以归因于外部因素，但许多可以追溯到实践和经验法则，它们已经过时了。我们认为，如果价值投资要在未来取得成功，就需要形成一种更具活力的价值观，以及在面对不确定性时更愿意接受和投资。

Keywords :价值投资、成长投资、主动投资

\[9\] Trade Sentiment and the Stock Market: New Evidence Based on Big Data Textual Analysis of Chinese Media

标题:交易情绪与股市：基于中国媒体大数据文本分析的新证据

作者:Marlene Amstad,Leonardo Gambacorta,Chao He,Fan Dora Xia

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3783897

Abstract : Trade tensions between China and US have played an important role in swinging global stock markets but effects are difficult to quantify. We develop a novel trade sentiment index (TSI) based on textual analysis and machine learning applied on a big data pool that assesses the positive or negative tone of the Chinese media coverage, and evaluates its capacity to explain the behaviour of 60 global equity markets. We find the TSI to contribute around 10% of model capacity to explain the stock price variability from January 2018 to June 2019 in countries that are more exposed to the China-US value chain. Most of the contribution is given by the tone extracted from social media (9%), while that obtained from traditional media explains only a modest part of stock price variability (1%). No equity market benefits from the China-US trade war, and Asian markets tend to be more negatively affected. In particular, we find that sectors most affected by tariffs such as information technology related ones are particularly sensitive to the tone in trade tension.

Keywords : None

Abstract :中美之间的贸易紧张关系在全球股市波动中发挥了重要作用，但影响难以量化。我们开发了一个基于文本分析和机器学习的新型贸易情绪指数（TSI），应用于一个大数据池，评估中国媒体报道的积极或消极基调，并评估其解释60个全球股市行为的能力。我们发现，TSI贡献了大约10%的模型容量，可以解释2018年1月至2019年6月在中美价值链风险较大的国家的股价波动。大部分贡献来自社交媒体（9%），而从传统媒体获得的信息只能解释股价波动的一小部分（1%）。中美贸易战没有给股市带来好处，亚洲市场受到的负面影响往往更大。特别是，我们发现，受关税影响最大的部门，如与信息技术有关的部门，对贸易紧张局势的基调特别敏感。

Keywords :None

\[10\] COVID-19: Fear of Pandemic and Short-Term IPO Performance

标题:COVID-19：大流行恐惧与短期IPO表现

作者:Sharif Mazumder,Pritam Saha

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3784013

Abstract : This study analyzes the relationship between COVID-19 related fear and short-term IPO performance. Though the average market-adjusted initial return of IPOs in the year 2020 is higher than that of the last four decades, it decreases if fear of pandemic increases. The evidence is robust when we use matching firm-adjusted initial returns. Next, we analyze the persistence of performance after the IPO date. \[11\] Financial Contagion During Stock Market Bubbles

标题:股市泡沫期间的金融传染

作者:Diego Escobari,Shahil Sharma

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3747334

Abstract : We investigate the role of bubbles on financial contagion using a set of developed economies. First, using the recursive flexible window right-tailed ADF-based procedure, we date stamp bubble periods in stock index series. Second, we capture contagion with a DCC multivariate GARCH framework. In a third step, we construct a panel by pooling across the time-series dynamic conditional correlations and bubbles to estimate various dynamic panel specifications that consider the endogenous nature of bubbles. We find statistically significant decreases in the dynamic correlations during periods of bubbles, which shows that the financial contagion between pair of countries diminishes when any of the two countries in the pair is going through a bubble period. This implies that during bubble periods investors are looking for an investment opportunity within their economy and rely less on international diversification. However, decrease in contagion between two economies could provide ample diversification opportunities for portfolio managers.

Keywords : Financial contagion; Stock markets; Bubbles; Dynamic correlations, Dynamic panels
