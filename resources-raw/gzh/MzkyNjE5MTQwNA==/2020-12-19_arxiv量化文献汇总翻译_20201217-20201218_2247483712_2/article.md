\[1\] Model of cunning agents

标题 : 狡猾的代理人模型

作者 : Mateusz Denys , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08517

说明 : Comments: 20 pages, 10 figures

Abstract : A numerical agent based spin model of financial markets, based on the Potts model from statistical mechanics, with a novel interpretation of the spin variable (as regards financial market models) is presented. In this model, a value of the spin variable is only the agent s opinion concerning current market situation, which he communicates to his nearest neighbors. Instead, the agent s action (i.e., buying, selling, or staying inactive) is connected with a change of the spin variable. Hence, the agents can be considered as cunning in this model. That is, these agents encourage their neighbors to buy stocks if the agents have an opportunity to sell them, and the agents encourage their neighbors to sell stocks if the agents have a reversed opportunity. Predictions of the model are in good agreement with empirical data from various real life financial markets. The model reproduces the shape of the usual and absolute value autocorrelation function of returns as well as the distribution of times between superthreshold losses.

摘要 : 基于统计力学中的Potts模型，提出了一种基于数值代理的金融市场自旋模型，并对自旋变量（金融市场模型）进行了新的解释。在这个模型中，自旋变量的值仅仅是代理人对当前市场形势的看法，并将其传达给最近的邻居。相反，代理人的行为（即买入、卖出或保持不活跃）与自旋变量的变化有关。因此，在这个模型中，代理可以被认为是狡猾的。也就是说，如果代理有机会出售股票，这些代理人会鼓励他们的邻居购买股票，如果代理人有相反的机会，代理人会鼓励他们的邻居出售股票。模型的预测结果与实际金融市场的经验数据吻合得很好。该模型再现了收益率的常值自相关函数和绝对值自相关函数的形状以及超阈值损失之间的时间分布。

\[2\] Exploring Narrative Economics

标题 : 探索叙事经济学

作者 : Kenneth Lomas, Dave Cliff , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08840

说明 : Comments: To be presented at the 13th International Conference on Agents and Artificial Intelligence (ICAART2021), Vienna, 4th--6th February 2021. 18 pages; 14 figures

Abstract : In seeking to explain aspects of real world economies that defy easy understanding when analysed via conventional means, Nobel Laureate Robert Shiller has since 2017 introduced and developed the idea of Narrative Economics, where observable economic factors such as the dynamics of prices in asset markets are explained largely as a consequence of the narratives (i.e., the stories) heard, told, and believed by participants in those markets. Shiller argues that otherwise irrational and difficult to explain behaviors, such as investors participating in highly volatile cryptocurrency markets, are best explained and understood in narrative terms people invest because they believe, because they have a heartfelt opinions, about the future prospects of the asset, and they tell to themselves and others stories (narratives) about those beliefs and opinions. In this paper we describe what is, to the best of our knowledge, the first ever agent based modelling platform that allows for the study of issues in narrative economics. We have created this by integrating and synthesizing research in two previously separate fields opinion dynamics (OD), and agent based computational economics (ACE) in the form of minimally intelligent trader agents operating in accurately modelled financial markets. We show here for the first time how long established models in OD and in ACE can be brought together to enable the experimental study of issues in narrative economics, and we present initial results from our system. The program code for our simulation platform has been released as freely available open source software on GitHub, to enable other researchers to replicate and extend our work

摘要 : 诺贝尔奖获得者罗伯特·希勒（Robert Shiller）自2017年起引入并发展了叙事经济学的概念，试图解释现实世界经济中难以理解的方面，在这种情况下，可观察到的经济因素，如资产市场价格的动态，在很大程度上是由于这些市场参与者听到、讲述和相信的故事（即故事）来解释的。Shiller认为，其他非理性和难以解释的行为，例如投资者参与高度动荡的加密货币市场，最好用叙述性的术语来解释和理解人们的投资，因为他们相信，因为他们对资产的未来前景有着真挚的看法，他们向自己和他人讲述关于这些信仰和观点的故事（叙述）。在这篇论文中，我们描述了，据我们所知，第一个基于代理的建模平台，允许研究叙事经济学中的问题。我们通过整合和综合以前两个独立领域的研究，即意见动力学（OD）和基于代理的计算经济学（ACE），以在精确建模的金融市场中运行的最小智能交易员代理的形式创建了这一模型。我们在这里首次展示了在OD和ACE中建立了多久的模型能够结合在一起，从而能够对叙事经济学中的问题进行实验研究，并且我们展示了我们系统的初步结果。我们的模拟平台的程序代码已经在GitHub上作为免费的开源软件发布，以使其他研究人员能够复制和扩展我们的工作

\[4\] Disentangling the socio ecological drivers behind illegal fishing in a small scale fishery managed by a TURF system

标题 : 破除草皮系统管理的小规模渔业非法捕捞背后的社会生态驱动力

作者 : Silvia de Juan, Maria Dulce Subida, Andres Ospina-Alvarez, Ainara Aguilar, Miriam Fernandez , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08970

说明 : Comments: 24 pages, 6 figures, 2 tables, supplementary information

Abstract : A substantial increase in illegal extraction of the benthic resources in central Chile is likely driven by an interplay of numerous socio economic local factors that threatens the success of the fisheries management areas (MA) system. To assess this problem, the exploitation state of a commercially important benthic resource (i.e., keyhole limpet) in the MAs was related with socio economic drivers of the small scale fisheries. The potential drivers of illegal extraction included rebound effect of fishing effort displacement by MAs, level of enforcement, distance to surveillance authorities, wave exposure and land based access to the MA, and alternative economic activities in the fishing village. The exploitation state of limpets was assessed by the proportion of the catch that is below the minimum legal size, with high proportions indicating a poor state, and by the relative median size of limpets fished within the MAs in comparison with neighbouring OA areas, with larger relative sizes in the MA indicating a good state. A Bayesian Belief Network approach was adopted to assess the effects of potential drivers of illegal fishing on the status of the benthic resource in the MAs. Results evidenced the absence of a direct link between the level of enforcement and the status of the resource, with other socio economic (e.g., alternative economic activities in the village) and context variables (e.g., fishing effort or distance to surveillance authorities) playing important roles. Scenario analysis explored variables that are susceptible to be managed, evidencing that BBN is a powerful approach to explore the role of multiple external drivers, and their impact on marine resources, in complex small scale fisheries.

摘要 : 智利中部非法开采底栖资源的大量增加很可能是由于许多社会经济当地因素的相互作用，这些因素威胁到渔业管理区系统的成功。为了评估这一问题，MAs中一种具有商业价值的底栖资源（即锁孔帽贝）的开发状况与小规模渔业的社会经济驱动因素有关。非法开采的潜在驱动因素包括渔业资源管理局转移捕鱼努力的反弹效应、执法水平、与监测当局的距离、海浪暴露和陆地进入海洋资源管理局以及渔村的替代经济活动。根据低于最低法定尺寸的渔获物比例评估帽贝的开发状况，高比例表示状态较差，而MAs内捕捞的帽贝与邻近OA区域相比的相对中值大小，MA中相对大小较大表示状态良好。采用贝叶斯信念网络方法，评估潜在的非法捕捞驱动因素对海洋生态系统底栖资源状况的影响。结果表明，执法水平与资源状况之间没有直接联系，而其他社会经济（如村里的替代经济活动）和环境变量（如捕鱼量或与监测当局的距离）起着重要作用。情景分析探讨了易于管理的变量，证明了BBN是探索复杂小规模渔业中多种外部驱动因素的作用及其对海洋资源的影响的有力方法。

\[5\] Estimating real world probabilities

标题 : 估计真实世界的概率

作者 : Ricardo Crisóstomo , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.09041

说明 : -

Abstract : We show that disentangling sentiment induced biases from fundamental expectations significantly improves the accuracy and consistency of probabilistic forecasts. Using data from 1994 to 2017, we analyze 15 stochastic models and risk preference combinations and in all possible cases a simple behavioral transformation delivers substantial forecast gains. Our results are robust across different evaluation methods, risk preference hypotheses and sentiment calibrations, demonstrating that behavioral effects can be effectively used to forecast asset prices. Further analyses confirm that our real world densities outperform densities recalibrated to avoid past mistakes and improve predictive models where risk aversion is dynamically estimated from option prices.

摘要 : 我们发现，基本面预测的一致性和概率偏差显著提高了。利用1994年至2017年的数据，我们分析了15个随机模型和风险偏好组合，在所有可能的情况下，一个简单的行为转换就能带来可观的预测收益。我们的结果在不同的评估方法、风险偏好假设和情绪校正上都是稳健的，证明行为效应可以有效地用于预测资产价格。进一步的分析证实，我们的现实世界密度优于重新校准的密度，以避免过去的错误，并改进预测模型，其中风险规避是根据期权价格动态估计的。

\[7\] Towards robust and speculation reduction real estate pricing models based on a data driven strategy

作者 : Vladimir Vargas-Calderón, Jorge E. Camargo , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.09115

说明 : -

Abstract : In many countries, real estate appraisal is based on conventional methods that rely on appraisers abilities to collect data, interpret it and model the price of a real estate property. With the increasing use of real estate online platforms and the large amount of information found therein, there exists the possibility of overcoming many drawbacks of conventional pricing models such as subjectivity, cost, unfairness, among others. In this paper we propose a data driven real estate pricing model based on machine learning methods to estimate prices reducing human bias. \[8\] A Khasminskii Type Averaging Principle for Non autonomous Slow fast Stochastic Differential Equations and an Application to a Local Stochastic Volatility Model

标题 : 非自治慢-快随机微分方程的Khasminskii型平均原理及其在局部随机波动率模型中的应用

作者 : Filippo de Feo , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.09082

说明 : -

Abstract : In this work we study the averaging principle for non autonomous slow fast systems of stochastic differential equations. In particular in the first part we generalize Khasminskii s averaging principle to the non autonomous case, assuming the sublinearity, the Lipschitzianity and the Holder s continuity in time of the coefficients, an ergodic hypothesis and an mathcal L 2 bound of the fast component. In this setting we prove the weak convergence of the slow component to the solution of the averaged equation. Moreover we provide a suitable dissipativity condition under which the ergodic hypothesis and the mathcal L 2 bound of the fast component, which are implicit conditions, are satisfied. In the second part we propose a financial application of this result we apply the theory developed to a slow fast local stochastic volatility model. First we prove the weak convergence of the model to a local volatility one. Then under a risk neutral measure we show that the prices of the derivatives, possibly path dependent, converge to the ones calculated using the limit model.

摘要 : 在这项工作中，我们研究非自治的慢-快随机微分方程组的平均原理。特别是在第一部分中，我们将哈斯明斯基的平均原理推广到非自治情形，假设系数的次线性、lipschitz性和Holder在时间上的连续性，一个遍历假设和快速分量的数学L2界。在这种情况下，我们证明了慢分量对平均方程解的弱收敛性。此外，我们还提供了一个合适的耗散条件，在这个条件下，快分量的遍历假设和数学l2界都是隐式条件。在第二部分，我们提出了一个金融应用的结果，我们将发展的理论应用于一个慢-快的局部随机波动率模型。首先，我们证明了该模型的弱收敛性。然后，在风险中性度量下，我们证明了衍生工具的价格，可能是路径依赖的，收敛于使用极限模型计算的价格。

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.09306

说明 : -

Abstract : In this paper, we analyze various Decentralized Finance (DeFi) protocols in terms of their token distributions. We propose an iterative mapping process that allows us to split aggregate token holdings from custodial and escrow contracts and assign them to their economic beneficiaries. This method accounts for liquidity , lending , and staking pools, as well as token wrappers, and can be used to break down token holdings, even for high nesting levels. We compute individual address balances for several snapshots and analyze intertemporal distribution changes. 摘要 : 在本文中，我们分析了不同的分散金融（DeFi）协议的令牌分布。我们提出了一个迭代映射过程，它允许我们从托管和托管合同中分离出总代币持有量，并将其分配给其经济受益人。这种方法考虑了流动性、借贷和赌注池，以及代币包装，可以用来分解代币持有量，即使是在高嵌套水平下也是如此。我们计算了几个快照的地址平衡，并分析了跨时间分布的变化。此外，我们还研究了重新分配和协议使用数据，并提出了一个用于度量令牌依赖性和生态系统集成的代理。本文提供了新的见解DEFI互操作性和令牌所有权分配，可以作为进一步研究的基础。

\[10\] Levelling Down and the COVID 19 Lockdowns

标题 : 调平和COVID 19锁定

作者 : John Gathergood, Fabian Gunzinger, Benedict Guttman-Kenney, Edika Quispe-Torreblanca, Neil Stewart , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.09336

说明 : Comments: arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:2010.04129

Abstract : We show the recovery in consumer spending in the United Kingdom through the second half of 2020 is unevenly distributed across regions. We utilise Fable Data a real time source of consumption data that is a highly correlated, leading indicator of Bank of England and Office for National Statistics data. The UK s recovery is heavily weighted towards the home counties around outer London and the South. We observe a stark contrast between strong online spending growth while offline spending contracts. The strongest recovery in spending is seen in online spending in the commuter belt areas in outer London and the surrounding localities and also in areas of high second home ownership, where working from home (including working from second homes) has significantly displaced the location of spending. Year on year spending growth in November 2020 in localities facing the UK s new tighter Tier 3 restrictions (mostly the midlands and northern areas) was 38.4 lower compared with areas facing the less restrictive Tier 2 (mostly London and the South). These patterns have been further exacerbated during November 2020 when a second national lockdown was imposed.
