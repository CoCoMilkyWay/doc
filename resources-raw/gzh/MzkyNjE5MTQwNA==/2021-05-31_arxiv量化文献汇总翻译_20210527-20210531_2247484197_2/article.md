\[1\] Learning about latent dynamic trading demand

标题 : 了解潜在的动态交易需求

作者 : Xiao Chen, Jin Hyuk Choi, Kasper Larsen, Duane J. Seppi , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.13401

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper presents an equilibrium model of dynamic trading, learning, and pricing by strategic investors with trading targets and price impact. Since trading targets are private, rebalancers and liquidity providers filter the child order flow over time to estimate the latent underlying parent trading demand imbalance and its expected impact on subsequent price pressure dynamics. We prove existence of the equilibrium and solve for equilibrium trading strategies and prices in terms of the solution to a system of coupled ODEs. We show that trading strategies are combinations of trading towards investor targets, liquidity provision for other investors demands, and front running based on learning about latent underlying trading demand imbalances and future price pressure.

摘要 : 本文提出了一个考虑交易目标和价格影响的战略投资者动态交易、学习和定价的均衡模型。由于交易目标是私人的，再平衡者和流动性提供者过滤随时间推移的子订单流，以估计潜在的潜在母公司交易需求失衡及其对后续价格压力动态的预期影响。我们证明了均衡的存在性，并用耦合常微分方程组的解来求解均衡交易策略和均衡价格。我们发现，交易策略是针对投资者目标的交易、为其他投资者需求提供流动性、以及基于了解潜在潜在交易需求失衡和未来价格压力的前期运作的组合。

\[3\] Behavior of Liquidity Providers in Decentralized Exchanges

标题 : 分散交易中流动性提供者的行为

作者 : Ye Wang, Lioba Heimbach, Roger Wattenhofer , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.13822

说明 : -

Abstract : Decentralized exchanges (DEXes) have introduced an innovative trading mechanism, where it is not necessary to match buy orders and sell orders to execute a trade. DEXes execute each trade individually, and the exchange rate is automatically determined by the ratio of assets reserved in the market. Therefore, apart from trading, financial players can also liquidity providers, benefiting from transaction fees from trades executed in DEXes. Although liquidity providers are essential for the functionality of DEXes, it is not clear how liquidity providers behave in such markets.In this paper, we aim to understand how liquidity providers react to market information and how they benefit from providing liquidity in DEXes. We measure the operations of liquidity providers on Uniswap and analyze how they determine their investment strategy based on market changes. We also reveal their returns and risks of investments in different trading pair categories, i.e., stable pairs, normal pairs, and exotic pairs. Further, we investigate the movement of liquidity between trading pools. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work that systematically studies the behavior of liquidity providers in DEXes.

摘要 : 分散化交易所（DEXes）引入了一种创新的交易机制，在这种机制中，执行交易不需要匹配买入和卖出指令。指数单独执行每笔交易，汇率由市场储备资产的比率自动决定。因此，除了交易，金融参与者还可以提供流动性，受益于DEXes交易的交易费用。尽管流动性提供者对DEXes的功能至关重要，但目前尚不清楚流动性提供者在此类市场中的行为。本文旨在了解流动性提供者如何对市场信息做出反应，以及他们如何从DEXes提供流动性中获益。我们衡量了Uniswap上流动性提供商的运营情况，并分析了他们如何根据市场变化来确定投资策略。我们还揭示了它们在不同交易对类别（稳定对、正常对和奇异对）中的投资收益和风险。此外，我们还研究了交易池之间流动性的变化。据我们所知，这是第一部系统研究DEXes流动性提供者行为的著作。

Abstract : Yield farming has been an immensely popular activity for cryptocurrency holders since the explosion of Decentralized Finance (DeFi) in the summer of 2020. \[6\] Three Remarks On Asset Pricing

标题 : 关于资产定价的三点意见

作者 : Victor Olkhov , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.13903

说明 : Comments: 29 pages

Abstract : We make three remarks to the main CAPM equation presented in the well known textbook by John Cochrane (2001). First, we believe that any economic averaging procedure implies aggregation of corresponding time series during certain time interval  Delta and explain the necessity to use math expectation for both sides of the main CAPM equation. Second, the first order condition of utility max used to derive main CAPM equation should be complemented by the second one that requires negative utility second derivative. Both define the amount of assets xi max that delivers max to utility. Expansions of the utility in a Taylor series by price and payoff variations give approximations for xi max and uncover equations on price, payoff, volatility, skewness, their covariance s and etc. We discuss why market price volume positive correlations may prohibit existence of xi max and main CAPM equation. Third, we argue that the economic sense of the conventional frequency based price probability may be poor. To overcome this trouble we propose new price probability measure based on widely used volume weighted average price (VWAP). To forecast price volatility one should predict evolution of squares of the value and the volume of market trades aggregated during averaging interval Delta . The forecast of the new price probability measure may be the main tough puzzle for CAPM and finance. However investors are free to chose any probability measure they prefer as ground for their investment strategies but should be ready for unexpected losses due to possible distinctions with real market trade price dynamics.

摘要 : 我们对John Cochrane（2001）在著名教科书中提出的主要CAPM方程作了三点评述。首先，我们认为任何经济平均过程都意味着在一定的时间间隔增量内相应的时间序列的聚集，并解释了对主CAPM方程两边使用数学期望的必要性。其次，用效用最大值的一阶条件导出CAPM主方程，并用需要负效用二阶导数的二阶条件加以补充。两者都定义了席max，它将最大值交付给公用事业。通过价格和收益变化的效用在泰勒级数中的扩展给出了价格、收益、波动、偏斜、协方差S等的Xi max和揭方程的近似。我们讨论了为什么市场价格正相关可能禁止席席max和主CAPM方程的存在。第三，我们认为传统的基于频率的价格概率的经济意义可能很差。为了克服这一问题，我们提出了一种新的基于广泛使用的量加权平均价格（VWAP）的价格概率测度。为了预测价格波动，我们应该预测价值平方和市场交易量在平均间隔Delta期间的变化。新价格概率测度的预测可能是CAPM和金融界面临的主要难题。然而，投资者可以自由选择他们喜欢的任何概率指标作为他们投资策略的依据，但由于与真实市场交易价格动态可能存在的差异，投资者应该准备好应对意外损失。

\[7\] A Note on Optimal Fees for Constant Function Market Makers

标题 : 关于常函数做市商最优费用的注记

作者 : Robin Fritsch, Roger Wattenhofer , 类别 ：Computer Science and Game Theory (cs.GT)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.13510

说明 : Comments: 11 pages, 5 figures

Abstract : We suggest a framework to determine optimal trading fees for constant function market makers (CFMMs) in order to maximize liquidity provider returns. In a setting of multiple competing liquidity pools, we show that no race to the bottom occurs, but instead pure Nash equilibria of optimal fees exist. We theoretically prove the existence of these equilibria for pools using the constant product trade function used in popular CFMMs like Uniswap. We also numerically compute the equilibria for a number of examples and discuss the effects the equilibrium fees have on capital allocation among pools. Finally, we use our framework to compute optimal fees for real world pools using past trade data.

摘要 : 我们建议一个框架，以确定最佳交易费用的恒定功能做市商（CFMM），以最大限度地提高流动性供应商的回报。在多个相互竞争的流动性池的情况下，我们证明了不存在竞争到底，而是存在最优费用的纯纳什均衡。利用Uniswap等常用的cfmm中的常积交易函数，从理论上证明了这些均衡的存在性。我们还数值计算了一些例子的均衡，并讨论了均衡费用对池间资本配置的影响。最后，我们使用我们的框架，使用过去的贸易数据来计算现实世界中资金池的最佳费用。

Abstract : In e commerce platforms, sponsored and non sponsored content are jointly displayed to users and both may interactively influence their engagement behavior. A burning issue for e commerce platform design is how to blend advertising with content in a way that respects these interactions and balances these multiple business objectives. \[9\] Slow Momentum with Fast Reversion

标题 : 慢动量与快逆转

作者 : Kieran Wood, Stephen Roberts, Stefan Zohren , 类别 ：Machine Learning (stat.ML)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.13727

说明 : -

Abstract : Momentum strategies are an important part of alternative investments and are at the heart of commodity trading advisors (CTAs). These strategies have however been found to have difficulties adjusting to rapid changes in market conditions, such as during the 2020 market crash. In particular, immediately after momentum turning points, where a trend reverses from an uptrend (downtrend) to a downtrend (uptrend), time series momentum (TSMOM) strategies are prone to making bad bets. To improve the response to regime change, we introduce a novel approach, where we insert an online change point detection (CPD) module into a Deep Momentum Network (DMN) 1904.04912 pipeline, which uses an LSTM deep learning architecture to simultaneously learn both trend estimation and position sizing. Furthermore, our model is able to optimise the way in which it balances 1) a slow momentum strategy which exploits persisting trends, but does not overreact to localised price moves, and 2) a fast mean reversion strategy regime by quickly flipping its position, then swapping it back again to exploit localised price moves. Our CPD module outputs a changepoint location and severity score, allowing our model to learn to respond to varying degrees of disequilibrium, or smaller and more localised changepoints, in a data driven manner. Using a portfolio of 50, liquid, continuous futures contracts over the period 1990 2020, the addition of the CPD module leads to an improvement in Sharpe ratio of 33 .
