\[1\] A General Framework to Forecast the Adoption of Novel Products

标题 : 预测新产品采用的一般框架

作者 : Subodh Dubey, Ishant Sharma, Sabyasachee Mishra, Oded Cats, Prateek Bansal , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.06169

说明 : -

Abstract : Due to the unavailability of prototypes, the early adopters of novel products actively seek information from multiple sources (e.g., media and social networks) to minimize the potential risk. The existing behavior models not only fail to capture the information propagation within the individual s social network, but also they do not incorporate the impact of such word of mouth (WOM) dissemination on the consumer s risk preferences. Moreover, even cutting edge forecasting models rely on crude synthetic consumer behavior models. We propose a general framework to forecast the adoption of novel products by developing a new consumer behavior model and integrating it into a population level agent based model. Specifically, we extend the hybrid choice model to estimate consumer behavior, which incorporates social network effects and interplay between WOM and risk aversion. The calibrated consumer behavior model and synthetic population are passed through the agent based model for forecasting the product market share. We apply the proposed framework to forecast the adoption of autonomous vehicles (AVs) in Nashville, USA. The consumer behavior model is calibrated with a stated preference survey data of 1,495 Nashville residents. The output of the agent based model provides the effect of the purchase price, post purchase satisfaction, and safety measures regulations on the forecasted AV market share. With an annual AV price reduction of 5 at the initial purchase price of 40,000 and 90 of satisfied adopters, AVs are forecasted to attain around 85 market share in thirty years. These findings are crucial for policymakers to develop infrastructure plans and manufacturers to conduct an after sales cost benefit analysis.

摘要 : 由于原型的不可用，新产品的早期采用者积极地从多个来源（如媒体和社交网络）寻求信息，以最大限度地降低潜在风险。现有的行为模型不仅无法捕捉个人社交网络中的信息传播，而且也没有考虑口碑传播对消费者风险偏好的影响。此外，即使是最前沿的预测模型也依赖于粗糙的综合消费者行为模型。我们通过开发一个新的消费者行为模型，并将其集成到一个基于群体级代理的模型中，提出了一个预测新产品采用率的通用框架。具体而言，我们扩展了混合选择模型来估计消费者行为，该模型结合了社会网络效应以及口碑和风险规避之间的相互作用。校准后的消费者行为模型和综合人口通过基于代理的模型进行预测，以预测产品的市场份额。我们应用所提出的框架来预测美国纳什维尔的自动驾驶汽车（AVs）采用情况。消费者行为模型用1495名纳什维尔居民的规定偏好调查数据进行校准。基于代理的模型的输出提供了购买价格、购买后满意度和安全措施法规对预测AV市场份额的影响。在初始购买价为40000和90%的用户满意的情况下，AVs的年度价格下降了5%，预计在30年内AVs将达到85%左右的市场份额。这些发现对于决策者制定基础设施计划和制造商进行售后成本效益分析至关重要。

\[2\] A consumption investment model with state dependent lower bound constraint on consumption

标题 : 一类消费下限约束依赖于状态的消费投资模型

作者 : Chonghu Guan, Zuo Quan Xu, Fahuai Yi , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.06378

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper considers a life time consumption investment problem under the Black Scholes framework, where the investor s consumption rate is subject to a lower bound constraint that linearly depends on the investor s wealth. Due to the state dependent control constraint, the standard stochastic control theory cannot be directly applied to our problem. We overcome this obstacle by examining an equivalent problem that does not impose state dependent control constraint. It is shown that the value function is a third order continuously differentiable function by using differential equation approaches. The feedback form optimal consumption and investment strategies are given. According to our findings, if the investor is more concerned with long term consumption than short term consumption, then she should, regardless of her financial condition, always consume as few as possible otherwise, her optimal consumption strategy is state dependent consuming optimally when her financial condition is good, and consuming at the lowest possible rate when her financial situation is bad.

摘要 : 本文在Black-Scholes框架下考虑了一个终生消费投资问题，其中投资者的消费率服从一个与投资者财富成线性关系的下限约束。由于依赖于状态的控制约束，标准的随机控制理论不能直接应用于我们的问题。我们通过研究一个不施加状态相关控制约束的等价问题来克服这个障碍。利用微分方程方法证明了该值函数是一个三阶连续可微函数。给出了最优消费和投资策略的反馈。根据我们的研究结果，如果投资者更关注长期消费而不是短期消费，那么无论她的财务状况如何，她都应该尽可能少地消费，否则，她的最佳消费策略是在财务状况良好时进行依赖于国家的最佳消费，当她的经济状况不好时，以尽可能低的速度消费。

\[3\] Vaccination strategies and transmission of COVID 19

标题 : 疫苗接种策略与新冠病毒19的传播

作者 : Dongwoo Kim, Young Jun Lee , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.06453

说明 : -

Abstract : Vaccination has been perceived as a key to reaching herd immunity in the current COVID 19 pandemic. This paper examines effectiveness of different vaccination strategies. We investigate the effects of two key elements in mass vaccination, which are allocations and timing of first and second doses and types of vaccines, on the spread of COVID 19. Amid limited supply of approved vaccines and constrained medical resources, the choice of a vaccination strategy is fundamentally an economic problem. We employ standard time series and panel data models commonly used in economic research with real world data to estimate the effects of progress in vaccination and types of vaccines on health outcomes. Potential confounders such as government responses and people s behavioral changes are also taken into account. Our findings suggest that the share of people vaccinated with at least one dose is significantly negatively associated with new infections and deaths. Conditioning on first dose progress, full vaccination offers no further reductions in new cases and deaths. For vaccines from China, however, we find weaker effects of vaccination progress on health outcomes. Our results support the extending interval between first and second dose policy adopted by Canada and the UK among others for mRNA based vaccines. As vaccination progressed, people s mobility increased and it offset the direct effects of vaccination. Therefore, public health measures are still important to contain the transmission by refraining people from being more mobile after vaccinated.

摘要 : 在当前的新冠病毒19大流行中，疫苗接种被视为实现群体免疫的关键。本文研究了不同疫苗接种策略的有效性。我们调查了大规模疫苗接种中的两个关键因素，即第一和第二剂量的分配和时间以及疫苗类型对新冠病毒19传播的影响。在核准疫苗供应有限和医疗资源有限的情况下，疫苗接种策略的选择从根本上说是一个经济问题。我们利用经济研究中常用的标准时间序列和面板数据模型以及真实世界的数据来估计疫苗接种进展和疫苗类型对健康结果的影响。潜在的混杂因素，如政府的反应和人们的行为变化也被考虑在内。我们的研究结果表明，接种至少一剂疫苗的人群比例与新感染和死亡显著负相关。在第一次剂量进展的条件下，完全接种疫苗不会进一步减少新病例和死亡。然而，对于来自中国的疫苗，我们发现疫苗接种进展对健康结果的影响较弱。我们的结果支持加拿大和英国以及其他国家对基于mRNA的疫苗采取的延长第一次和第二次剂量政策的间隔。随着疫苗接种的进展，人们的流动性增加，从而抵消了疫苗接种的直接影响。因此，公共卫生措施对于控制传播仍然很重要，方法是避免人们在接种疫苗后移动。

\[4\] Wrapping trust for interoperability. A study of wrapped tokens

标题 : 包装互用性信任。包装代币研究

作者 : Giulio Caldarelli , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.06847

说明 : Comments: 14 pages, 4 figures and 1 table. Oriented to a conference

Abstract : As known, blockchains are traditionally blind to the real world. This implies the reliance on third parties called oracles when extrinsic data is needed for smart contracts. However, reintroducing trust and single point of failure, oracles implementation is still controversial and debated. The blindness to the real world makes blockchains also unable to communicate with each other preventing any form of interoperability. An early approach to the interoperability issue is constituted by wrapped tokens, representing blockchain native tokens issued on a non native blockchain. Similar to how oracles reintroduce trust, and single point of failure, the issuance of wrapped tokens involves third parties whose characteristics need to be considered when evaluating the advantages of crossing chains. This paper provides an overview of the wrapped tokens and the main technologies implemented in their issuance. Advantages, as well as limitations, are also listed and discussed.

摘要 : 众所周知，区块链传统上对现实世界视而不见。这意味着当智能合约需要外部数据时，需要依赖称为oracles的第三方。然而，重新引入信任和单点失败，oracles的实现仍然存在争议和争论。对现实世界的盲目性使得区块链也无法相互通信，从而阻止了任何形式的互操作性。互操作性问题的早期方法由包装的令牌构成，表示在非本地区块链上发布的区块链本地令牌。与oracles如何重新引入信任和单点故障类似，包装令牌的发行涉及第三方，在评估跨链优势时需要考虑其特征。本文概述了包装令牌及其发行中实现的主要技术。还列出并讨论了其优点和局限性。

\[5\] Stability of the Weak Martingale Optimal Transport Problem

标题 : 弱鞅最优运输问题的稳定性

作者 : Mathias Beiglböck, Benjamin Jourdain, William Margheriti, Gudmund Pammer , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.06322

说明 : -

Abstract : While many questions in (robust) finance can be posed in the martingale optimal transport (MOT) framework, others require to consider also non linear cost functionals. Following the terminology of Gozlan, Roberto, Samson and Tetali this corresponds to weak martingale optimal transport (WMOT). In this article we establish stability of WMOT which is important since financial data can give only imprecise information on the underlying marginals. As application, we deduce the stability of the superreplication bound for VIX futures as well as the stability of stretched Brownian motion and we derive a monotonicity principle for WMOT.

摘要 : 虽然在鞅最优运输（MOT）框架中可以提出（稳健）金融中的许多问题，但其他问题也需要考虑非线性成本泛函。按照Gozlan、Roberto、Samson和Tetali的术语，这对应于弱鞅最优传输（WMOT）。在本文中，我们建立了WMOT的稳定性，这一点很重要，因为财务数据只能提供关于潜在边际的不精确信息。作为应用，我们推导了VIX期货的超复制界的稳定性以及拉伸布朗运动的稳定性，并推导了WMOT的单调性原理。

\[6\] The impact of the COVID 19 pandemic on academic productivity

标题 : 新冠病毒19大流行对学术生产力的影响

作者 : Andrew R. Casey, Ilya Mandel, Prasun K. Ray , 类别 ：Digital Libraries (cs.DL)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.06591

说明 : Comments: Submitted to RSOS

Abstract : Publish or perish is an expression describing the pressure on academics to consistently publish research to ensure a successful career in academia. With a global pandemic that has changed the world, how has it changed academic productivity Here we show that academics are posting just as many publications on the arXiv pre print server as if there were no pandemic 168,630 were posted in 2020, a 12.6 change from 2019 and 1.4 sigma deviation above the predicted 162,577 pm 4,393. However, some immediate impacts are visible in individual research fields. Conference cancellations have led to sharp drops in pre prints, but laboratory closures have had mixed effects. Only some experimental fields show mild declines in outputs, with most being consistent on previous years or even increasing above model expectations. The most significant change is a 50 increase ( 8 sigma ) in quantitative biology research, all related to the COVID 19 pandemic. Some of these publications are by biologists using arXiv for the first time, and some are written by researchers from other fields (e.g., physicists, mathematicians). While quantitative biology pre prints have returned to pre pandemic levels, 20 of the research in this field is now focussed on the COVID 19 pandemic, demonstrating a strong shift in research focus.

摘要 : “发布或消亡”是一个表达，描述了学术界为确保在学术界取得成功而不断发布研究成果的压力。随着一场全球大流行改变了世界，它如何改变了学术生产力？我们在此表明，学术界在arXiv打印前服务器上发布的出版物数量与2020年没有发布的168630大流行一样多，与2019年相比变化了12.6，比预测的162577 pm 4393偏差了1.4 sigma。然而，一些直接影响在个别研究领域是显而易见的。会议的取消导致预印品数量急剧下降，但实验室的关闭产生了好坏参半的影响。只有一些试验田的产出略有下降，大部分与前几年持平，甚至高于模型预期。最显著的变化是数量生物学研究增加了50（8西格玛），所有这些都与新冠病毒19大流行有关。这些出版物中有些是生物学家首次使用arXiv，有些是其他领域的研究人员（如物理学家、数学家）撰写的。虽然定量生物学预印已经恢复到大流行前的水平，但该领域的20项研究现在集中于新冠病毒19大流行，表明研究重点发生了重大转变。

\[7\] Financial Trading with Feature Preprocessing and Recurrent Reinforcement Learning

标题 : 基于特征预处理和循环强化学习的金融交易

作者 : Lin Li , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.05283

说明 : Comments: accepted for publication by IEEE ISKE21

Abstract : Financial trading aims to build profitable strategies to make wise investment decisions in the financial market. It has attracted interests in the machine learning community for a long time. This paper proposes to trade financial assets automatically using feature preprocessing skills and Recurrent Reinforcement Learning (RRL) algorithm. The strategy starts from technical indicators extracted from assets market information. Then these technical indicators are preprocessed by Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) and eventually inputted to the RRL algorithm to do the trading. The extensive empirical evidence shows that the proposed strategy is not only effective and robust in its performance, but also can mitigate the drawbacks underlying the initial trading using RRL.

摘要 : 金融交易旨在制定有利可图的策略，以便在金融市场上做出明智的投资决策。长期以来，它吸引了机器学习界的兴趣。本文提出利用特征预处理技术和递归强化学习（RRL）算法实现金融资产的自动交易。该战略从从资产市场信息中提取的技术指标开始。然后通过主成分分析（PCA）和离散小波变换（DWT）对这些技术指标进行预处理，最终输入RRL算法进行交易。大量的经验证据表明，该策略不仅有效且稳健，而且可以缓解使用RRL进行初始交易的缺点。

\[8\] Estimating the Environmental Cost of Shrimp Farming in Coastal Areas of Chittagong and Coxs bazaar in Bangladesh

标题 : 估算孟加拉国吉大港和柯斯巴扎沿海地区养虾的环境成本

作者 : Mohammad Nur Nobi, Dr. A N M Moinul Islam , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.05416

说明 : Comments: 28 pages

Abstract : During the last three decades, shrimp has remained one of the major export items in Bangladesh. It contributes to the development of this country by enhancing export earnings and promoting employment. However, coastal wetlands and agricultural lands are used for shrimp culture, which reduces agricultural opportunity and peasants income, and destroys the mangroves and coastal eco system. These are the external environmental costs that are not reflected in farmers price and output decisions. This study has aimed to estimate those external environmental costs through the contingent valuation method. The calculated environmental cost of shrimp farming is USD 13.66 per acre per year. Findings suggest that current shrimp production and shrimp price will no longer be optimal once the external costs are internalized. Thus alternative policy recommendations have been proposed so that shrimp farming becomes a sustainable and equitable means of aquaculture.

摘要 : 在过去三十年中，虾一直是孟加拉国的主要出口商品之一。它通过增加出口收入和促进就业为该国的发展作出了贡献。然而，沿海湿地和农田被用于养虾，这减少了农业机会和农民收入，破坏了红树林和沿海生态系统。这些外部环境成本并未反映在农民的价格和产出决策中。本研究旨在通过条件估值法估算这些外部环境成本。虾养殖的计算环境成本为每年每英亩13.66美元。研究结果表明，一旦外部成本内部化，当前的虾产量和虾价将不再是最优的。因此，提出了替代政策建议，使养虾成为可持续和公平的水产养殖手段。

\[9\] Cost Benefit Analysis of Kaptai Dam in Rangamati District, Chittagong, Bangladesh

标题 : 孟加拉国吉大港Rangamati区Kaptai大坝的成本效益分析

作者 : Mohammad Nur Nobi , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.05419

说明 : Comments: 10 pages

Abstract : This study aims to assess the net benefit of the kaptai dam on the Karnafuli river in Kaptai, Chittagong, Bangladesh. Kaptai Dam, the only hydroelectricity power source in Bangladesh, provides only 5 electricity demand of Bangladesh. The Dam is located on the Karnafuli River at Kaptai in Rangamati District, 65 km upstream from Chittagong. It is an earth fill or embankment dam with a reservoir with a water storage capacity of 11,000 skm. Though the Dam s primary purpose is to generate electricity, it became a reservoir of water used for fishing and tourism. To find the net benefit value and estimate the environmental costs and benefits, we considered the environmental net benefit from 1962 to 1997. We identify the costs of Kaptai Dam, including its establishment cost, operational costs, the costs of lives that have been lost due to conflicts, and environmental costs, including loss of biodiversity, loss of land uses, and loss of human displacements. Also, we assess the benefits of electricity production, earnings from fisheries production, and gain from tourism to Kaptai Lake. The findings show that the Dam contributes tremendous value to Bangladesh. As a source of hydroelectricity, the Kaptai Dam is a source of clean energy, and its value might have been worthy of this Dam produced a significant portion of the electricity.
