\[1\] Selfish Shareholders

标题 : 自私的股东

作者 : Michele Fioretti, Victor Saint-Jean, Simon C. Smith , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.12138

说明 : -

Abstract : During the onset of the COVID 19 pandemic, conflicting incentives caused most stakeholders to dislike corporate social responsibility (CSR) measured by firms charitable donations  since it would further burden firms already strained finances. Those stakeholders that did favor donations, large individual investors, did so only to bolster their own images as they are typically synonymous with the donating firms. Image gains do not pass through to institutional shareholders, who instead preferred to donate themselves rather than having the firms in their portfolios donate. Our results cast doubts on large corporations willingness to enforce CSR measures for firms in their portfolio.

摘要 : 在covid19流感大流行期间，相互冲突的激励机制导致大多数利益相关者不喜欢企业的社会责任（CSR），因为这将进一步加重企业已经紧张的财政负担。那些支持捐赠的利益相关者，大型个人投资者，这样做只是为了提升自己的形象，因为他们通常是捐赠公司的代名词。形象收益不会传递给机构股东，他们更愿意自己捐赠，而不是让投资组合中的公司捐赠。我们的研究结果让人怀疑大公司是否愿意对其投资组合中的公司实施企业社会责任措施。

\[2\] Mental Health and Abortions among Young Women

标题 : 年轻妇女的心理健康和堕胎

作者 : Lena Janys, Bettina Siflinger , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.12159

说明 : -

Abstract : In this paper, we provide causal evidence on abortions and risky health behaviors as determinants of mental health development among young women. Using administrative in and outpatient records from Sweden, we apply a novel grouped fixed effects estimator proposed by Bonhomme and Manresa (2015) to allow for time varying unobserved heterogeneity. We show that the positive association obtained from standard estimators shrinks to zero once we control for grouped time varying unobserved heterogeneity. We estimate the group specific profiles of unobserved heterogeneity, which reflect differences in unobserved risk to be diagnosed with a mental health condition. We then analyze mental health development and risky health behaviors other than unwanted pregnancies across groups. Our results suggest that these are determined by the same type of unobserved heterogeneity, which we attribute to the same unobserved process of decision making. We develop and estimate a theoretical model of risky choices and mental health, in which mental health disparity across groups is generated by different degrees of self control problems. Our findings imply that mental health concerns cannot be used to justify restrictive abortion policies. Moreover, potential self control problems should be targeted as early as possible to combat future mental health consequences.

摘要 : 在这篇论文中，我们提供了有关堕胎和危险健康行为的因果证据，作为年轻女性心理健康发展的决定因素。利用瑞典的行政住院和门诊记录，我们采用Bonhomme和Manresa（2015）提出的一种新的分组固定效应估计器，以考虑时变的未观察到的异质性。我们证明，当我们控制分组时变的未观察到的异质性时，从标准估计得到的正关联将收缩到零。我们估计了未观察到的异质性的组特异性，这反映了被诊断为精神健康状况的未观察到的风险的差异。然后，我们分析心理健康发展和危险的健康行为以外的意外怀孕跨群体。我们的结果表明，这是由相同类型的未观察到的异质性决定的，我们将其归因于相同的未观察到的决策过程。我们发展并评估了一个风险选择与心理健康的理论模型，其中不同程度的自我控制问题导致了不同群体的心理健康差异。我们的研究结果表明，心理健康问题不能被用来证明限制性堕胎政策的合理性。此外，应尽早针对潜在的自我控制问题，以应对未来的心理健康后果。

\[3\] Understanding Factors that Influence Upskillng

标题 : 了解影响提高技能的因素

作者 : Monica Bhole, Eduardo Laguna-Muggenburg, Michael Meaney , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.12193

说明 : Comments: 11 pages, 4 tables, 3 figures

Abstract : We investigate the motivation and means through which individuals expand their skill set by analyzing a survey of applicants from the Facebook Jobs product. Individuals who report being influenced by their networks or local economy are over 29 more likely to have a postsecondary degree, but peer effects still exist among those who do not acknowledge such influences. Users with postsecondary degrees are more likely to upskill in general, by continuing coursework or applying to higher skill jobs, though the latter is more common among users across all education backgrounds. These findings indicate that policies aimed at connecting individuals with different educational backgrounds can encourage upskilling. Policies that encourage users to enroll in coursework may not be as effective among individuals with a high school degree or less. Instead, connecting such individuals to opportunities that value skills acquired outside of a formal education, and allow for on the job training, may be more effective.

摘要 : 我们通过分析Facebook招聘产品对求职者的调查，调查个人拓展技能的动机和方式。报告受到人际网络或当地经济影响的人中，29岁以上的人更有可能获得高等教育学位，但在那些不承认这种影响的人中，同伴效应仍然存在。拥有高等教育学位的用户通常更有可能通过继续学习课程或申请更高技能的工作来提高技能，尽管后者在所有教育背景的用户中更为常见。这些发现表明，旨在将不同教育背景的个人联系起来的政策可以鼓励提高技能。鼓励用户注册课程的政策在高中或以下学历的个人中可能没有那么有效。相反，将这些人与重视在正规教育之外获得的技能并允许在职培训的机会联系起来可能更有效。

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.12503

说明 : Comments: 38 pages, 3 tables, 3 figures

Abstract : Using the method of Haberis and Lipinska (2020), this paper explores the effect of forward guidance (FG) in a two country New Keynesian (NK) economy under the zero lower bound (ZLB). \[6\] Valuing Exotic Options and Estimating Model Risk

作者 : Jay Cao, Jacky Chen, John Hull, Zissis Poulos , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.12551

说明 : Comments: 20 pages, 2 figures

Abstract : A common approach to valuing exotic options involves choosing a model and then determining its parameters to fit the volatility surface as closely as possible. We refer to this as the model calibration approach (MCA). This paper considers an alternative approach where the points on the volatility surface are features input to a neural network. We refer to this as the volatility feature approach (VFA). We conduct experiments showing that VFA can be expected to outperform MCA for the volatility surfaces encountered in practice. Once the upfront computational time has been invested in developing the neural network, the valuation of exotic options using VFA is very fast. VFA is a useful tool for the estimation of model risk. We illustrate this using S&P 500 data for the 2001 to 2019 period.

摘要 : 评估奇异期权的一种常见方法是选择一个模型，然后确定其参数，以尽可能接近波动率表面。我们称之为模型校准方法（MCA）。本文考虑另一种方法，将波动面上的点作为神经网络的特征输入。我们称之为波动特征法（VFA）。我们进行的实验表明，VFA可以预期优于MCA的波动面在实践中遇到。一旦前期的计算时间已经投入到开发神经网络中，使用VFA对奇异期权的估值就非常快。VFA是模型风险估计的有用工具。我们用标准普尔500指数2001年至2019年的数据来说明这一点。

Abstract : An unknown number of people around the world are earning income by working through online labour platforms such as Upwork and Amazon Mechanical Turk. \[9\] Historical Evolution of Global Inequality in Carbon Emissions and Footprints versus Redistributive Scenarios

标题 : 全球碳排放和碳足迹不平等的历史演变与再分配情景

作者 : Gregor Semieniuk, Victor M. Yakovenko , 类别 ：Physics and Society (physics.soc-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.00111

说明 : Comments: 26 pages, 6 figures, accepted to Journal of Cleaner Production

Abstract : Ambitious scenarios of carbon emission redistribution for mitigating climate change in line with the Paris Agreement and reaching the sustainable development goal of eradicating poverty have been proposed recently. They imply a strong reduction in carbon footprint inequality by 2030 that effectively halves the Gini coefficient to about 0.25. This paper examines feasibility of these scenarios by analyzing the historical evolution of both weighted international inequality in CO2 emissions attributed territorially and global inequality in carbon footprints attributed to end consumers. For the latter, a new dataset is constructed that is more comprehensive than existing ones. In both cases, we find a decreasing trend in global inequality, partially attributed to the move of China from the lower to the middle part of the distribution, with footprints more unequal than territorial emissions. These results show that realization of the redistributive scenarios would require an unprecedented reduction in global inequality far below historical levels. Moreover, the territorial emissions data, available for more recent years up to 2017, show a saturation of the decreasing Gini coefficient at a level of 0.5. This observation confirms an earlier prediction based on maximal entropy reasoning that the Lorenz curve converges to the exponential distribution. This saturation further undermines feasibility of the redistributive scenarios, which are also hindered by structural tendencies that reinforce carbon footprint inequality under global capitalism. One way out of this conundrum is a fast decarbonization of the global energy supply in order to decrease global carbon emissions without relying crucially on carbon inequality reduction.

摘要 : 最近提出了雄心勃勃的碳排放再分配设想，以根据《巴黎协定》减缓气候变化，实现消除贫穷的可持续发展目标。这意味着，到2030年，碳足迹不平等现象将大大减少，有效地将基尼系数减半至0.25左右。本文通过分析地区二氧化碳排放加权国际不平等和最终消费者碳足迹全球不平等的历史演变，探讨了这些情景的可行性。对于后者，构造了一个比现有数据集更全面的新数据集。在这两种情况下，我们发现全球不平等的趋势都在减少，部分原因是中国从分布的较低部分向中间部分移动，足迹比领土排放更不平等。这些结果表明，要实现再分配的设想，就需要将全球不平等程度空前地降低到远远低于历史水平的水平。此外，截至2017年的近几年区域排放数据显示，基尼系数在0.5的水平上趋于饱和。这一观察证实了基于最大熵推理的早期预测，即Lorenz曲线收敛于指数分布。这种饱和状态进一步削弱了再分配方案的可行性，而在全球资本主义条件下，这种方案也受到了加剧碳足迹不平等的结构性趋势的阻碍。走出这一难题的一个办法是迅速减少全球能源供应的碳排放，以减少全球碳排放，而不必严重依赖减少碳不平等。

\[10\] The Success of AdaBoost and Its Application in Portfolio Management

标题 : AdaBoost的成功及其在投资组合管理中的应用

作者 : Yijian Chuan, Chaoyi Zhao, Zhenrui He, Lan Wu , 类别 ：Machine Learning (stat.ML)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.12345

说明 : -
