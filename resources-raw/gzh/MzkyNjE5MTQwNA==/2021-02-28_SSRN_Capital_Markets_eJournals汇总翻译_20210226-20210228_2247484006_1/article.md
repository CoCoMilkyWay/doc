\[1\] Institutional Investors and Granularity in Equity Markets

标题:机构投资者与股票市场的粒度

作者:Eric Ghysels,Hanwei Liu,Steve Raymond

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3758615

Abstract : The U.S. equity markets are largely driven by actions of institutional investors. Using quarterly 13-F holdings, we construct the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index of institutional investor concentration as a measure of granularity. We study how granularity affects: the cross-section of returns, conditional variances and downside risk. Next, we study the impact of granularity in a demand-driven asset pricing model introduced by Koijen and Yogo (2019). We derive a decomposition of expected returns in terms of equally weighted asset demands and granularity residuals. Using this decomposition, we revisit the empirical stylized facts pertaining to granularity and asset pricing.

Keywords : Granularity, Institutional investors

Abstract :美国股市主要由机构投资者的行为推动。利用季度13-F持股量，我们构建了机构投资者集中度的Herfindahl-Hirschman指数作为粒度度量。我们研究粒度如何影响：回报的横截面、条件方差和下行风险。接下来，我们研究了Koijen和Yogo（2019）提出的需求驱动资产定价模型中粒度的影响。我们将期望收益分解为平均加权资产需求和粒度残差。通过这种分解，我们重新审视了与粒度和资产定价相关的经验风格化事实。

Keywords :机构投资者

\[2\] Deep Learning, Text, and Patent Valuation

标题:深度学习、文本和专利评估

作者:Po-Hsuan Hsu,Dokyun Lee,Prasanna Tambe,David H. Hsu

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3758388

Abstract : This paper uses deep learning and natural language processing (NLP) methods on the US patent corpus to evaluate their predictive power in estimating two measures of patent value: (i) investor reaction to patent announcements as measured in Kogan et al., 2017 and (ii) forward citations. While forward citations have traditionally been used as measures of economic value in the literature, their utility is mainly retrospective. Contemporaneous predictions of patent value, as embodied in investor reactions to patent grants, can be important for managers and policy-makers for prospective decision making. We compare the prediction performance of models using the structured features of the patent (number of citations, technology class, etc.) to deep learning and NLP methods. Relative to linear regression models using the same features, deep learning models reduce mean absolute error (MAE) by approximately 32%. Incorporating patent text further lowers the MAE by 13%.

Keywords : patent value; deep learning; natural language processing

Abstract :本文利用美国专利语料库中的深度学习和自然语言处理（NLP）方法，评估了它们在估计专利价值两个指标时的预测能力：（i）Kogan等人，2017年测量的投资者对专利公告的反应和（ii）前向引用。虽然前向引文传统上被用来衡量文献的经济价值，但它们的效用主要是回顾性的。同时预测专利价值，体现在投资者对专利授予的反应中，对管理者和决策者的未来决策可能很重要。我们比较了利用专利结构特征（引用次数、技术等级等）的模型与深度学习和自然语言处理方法的预测性能。与使用相同特征的线性回归模型相比，深度学习模型将平均绝对误差（MAE）降低了约32%。合并专利文本进一步降低了13%的MAE。

Keywords :专利价值；深度学习；自然语言处理

\[3\] Stochastic Asset Flow Equations: Interdependence of Trend and Volatility

标题:随机资产流动方程：趋势与波动的相互依赖性

作者:Carey Caginalp,Gunduz Caginalp,David Swigon

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3757759

Abstract : The interaction between the volatility and price dynamics is explored. We model stochastic asset prices using the asset flow model with randomness arising directly from supply and demand. We show that the volatility is smallest at the extrema of the price. Linearizing the stochastic differential equation (SDE) about equilibrium, we obtain an exact relation for the autocovariance function, and relate it to the (3 by 3) Jacobian of the linearized SDE. In particular, we find the conditions under which one has a pair of complex conjugate eigenvalues of the Jacobian resulting in oscillations. The frequency of the oscillations depends only on the imaginary part of the complex pair, while the decay rate depends only on the real eigenvalue and the real parts of the complex pair. For the deterministic system, oscillations typically decay rapidly. However, randomness induces oscillations to continue indefinitely with a frequency that depends on the parameters of the deterministic system. The computations and analytical results presented here demonstrate that volatility increases when traders place greater emphasis on trend, confirming a generally held belief among practitioners.

Keywords : autocovariance, random supply, demand, stochastic differential equations, behavioral effects, price oscillations

Abstract :探讨了波动性与价格动态之间的相互作用。我们利用直接由供求关系产生随机性的资产流模型来建立随机资产价格模型。我们证明了在价格的极值处波动最小。将随机微分方程（SDE）线性化，得到了自协方差函数的精确关系，并将其与线性化SDE的（3×3）雅可比矩阵联系起来。特别地，我们找到了一对雅可比矩阵的复共轭特征值产生振荡的条件。振荡的频率只取决于复对的虚部，而衰减率只取决于实特征值和复对的实部。对于确定性系统，振荡通常迅速衰减。然而，随机性会导致振荡无限期地持续下去，其频率取决于确定性系统的参数。本文的计算和分析结果表明，当交易者更加重视趋势时，波动性增加，证实了从业者普遍持有的信念。

Keywords :自协方差，随机供给，随机需求，随机微分方程，行为效应，价格振荡

\[4\] Disclosure and the Cost-of Capital: Evidence from FOMC Announcements

标题:信息披露与资本成本：来自联邦公开市场委员会公告的证据

作者:Michael Dambra,Mihail Velikov,Joseph Weber

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3757513

Abstract : We investigate the effect of shocks to firms’ cost of capital on firms’ disclosure decisions. Specifically, we examine the extent to which firms issue press releases and/or Form 8-K disclosures following unexpected changes to the Fed funds rate. We find consistent evidence that unexpected decreases in the cost of capital lead to larger increases in disclosure by firms that are more sensitive to shocks to their cost of capital. Furthermore, the disclosure response to cost-of-capital surprises appears concentrated in Form 8-K items that require a materiality-based decision. Extended tests show that Fed funds rate shocks lead to capital markets being more responsive to earnings information for firms that are more affected by Fed funds rate changes and that our results are not attributable to changes in the cost of capital leading to increased disclosure because firms are raising capital or hyping their stock. Overall, these results are consistent with concerns raised in recent research papers that changes in cost of capital likely affect disclosure decisions.

Keywords : Disclosure, Materiality, Cost of Capital, Monetary Policy

Abstract :研究了企业资本成本冲击对企业信息披露决策的影响。具体而言，我们考察了美联储基金利率意外变动后，企业发布新闻稿和/或8-K表披露的程度。我们发现一致的证据表明，资本成本的意外下降会导致对资本成本冲击更敏感的公司披露的大幅增加。此外，对资本成本意外的披露反应似乎集中在表8-K项目中，这些项目需要基于重要性的决策。扩展测试表明，联邦基金利率冲击导致资本市场对受联邦基金利率变化影响较大的公司的盈利信息反应更灵敏，我们的结果不能归因于资本成本的变化导致披露增加，因为公司正在筹集资本或炒作股票。总的来说，这些结果与最近研究论文中提出的资本成本变化可能影响披露决策的担忧是一致的。

Keywords :披露、重要性、资本成本、货币政策

\[5\] Private Conversation Matters: Evidence from Sell-side Analyst Reports after Private Meetings

标题:私下谈话很重要：私下会谈后卖方分析师报告的证据

作者:Huan Cai,Zhen QI

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3732509

Abstract : Using a hand-collected database of Shenzhen Stock Exchange listed firms from 2013 to 2015, we examine the key characteristics of firms’ private meetings and their effects on analysts’ reports. We find that the short-term earnings forecast is more accurate and less optimistic during hosting periods after these meetings in general, but recommendations are still biased upwardly, even after the Fair Disclosure Regulation change in 2012. These results are robust regardless of whether the analyst attend meetings or whether the meeting is hold onsite so that analysts can observe the firm’s operations. We also investigate various aspects of private conversations in meetings, and show that the presence of managers, a smaller number of meeting participants, and discussing questions in more categories are associated with more accurate and less optimistic short-term forecasts during the hosting periods following the meeting. Our results suggest that private and small group conversations between managers and analysts can be significant information channels in these meetings.

Keywords : Private meetings, Private conversations, Meeting characteristics, Analyst forecast, Information channels, Management access.

Abstract :本文以2013-2015年深交所上市公司为样本，考察了非公开会议的主要特征及其对分析师报告的影响。我们发现，在这些会议之后的主持期间，短期盈利预测总体上更为准确，也不太乐观，但建议仍然向上倾斜，即使在2012年公平披露监管发生变化之后。无论分析师是否出席会议或会议是否在现场举行，这些结果都是可靠的，以便分析师能够观察公司的运营情况。我们还调查了会议中私人谈话的各个方面，结果表明，在会议之后的主持期间，经理在场、会议参与者人数较少以及讨论更多类别的问题与更准确、更不乐观的短期预测相关。我们的研究结果表明，在这些会议中，经理和分析师之间的私人和小组对话可以成为重要的信息渠道。

Keywords :私人会议，私人谈话，会议特点，分析师预测，信息渠道，管理访问。

\[6\] Nonlinearity in the Cross-Section of Stock Returns: Evidence from China

标题:股票收益横截面的非线性：来自中国的证据

作者:Dongxu Chen,Guoshi Tong,Jianqiu Wang,Ke Wu

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3757315

Abstract : We study which characteristics provide incremental predictive information for the cross-section of expected returns in the Chinese stock market. Our results provide empirical evidence for strong nonlinear relations between expected returns and selected characteristics, especially in the Trading Friction category. While a four-factor model of Liu, Stambaugh, and Yuan (2019) explains a majority of anomalous characteristics-sorted portfolio returns, we find significant alphas when exploring these characteristics jointly using flexible predictive functions. A long-short spread portfolio based on out-of-sample predicted returns by a nonlinear model delivers higher Sharpe ratio than that by a linear model.

Keywords : Cross-sectional return predictability, characteristics, adaptive group LASSO

Abstract :我们研究了哪些特征为中国股票市场预期收益的横截面提供了增量预测信息。我们的结果提供了经验证据，强烈的非线性关系之间的预期收益和选定的特征，特别是在贸易摩擦类别。虽然Liu、Stambaugh和Yuan（2019）的四因素模型解释了大多数按投资组合收益排序的异常特征，但我们在联合使用灵活的预测函数探索这些特征时发现了显著的alphas。非线性模型基于样本外预测收益的长-短利差投资组合比线性模型具有更高的夏普比率。

\[7\] Managerial Multi-tasking, Team Diversity and Mutual Fund Performance

作者:Jean Jinghan Chen,Li Xie,Si Zhou

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3757195

Abstract : This study examines the impact of multi-tasking teams on fund performance. We find that while managerial multi-tasking has a negative impact on fund performance, teamwork can mitigate the adverse effect associated with managerial multi-tasking, which is indicative of superior performance of funds managed by multi-tasking teams. \[8\] What Drives Intraday Reversal? Illiquidity or Liquidity Oversupply?

标题:是什么推动了盘中反转？流动性不足还是流动性过剩？

作者:Junqing Kang,Shen Lin,Xiong Xiong

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3756630

Abstract : We document and compare the cross-section intraday reversal patterns between Chinese and U.S. market, and further study the driving forces for each of them. Different from U.S. market, there is no significant dependence of intraday reversal on stock liquidity for Chinese market. Hence, the illiquid based explanation, which has been long documented in U.S. data, could not be automatically applied. Instead, we show there is a significant resilience pattern for accumulated returns after intraday reversal: the negative correlation between previous intraday return and future return in Chinese market is reversed afterwards. These results speak for liquidity oversupply explanation. Moreover, intraday reversal is significantly stronger for stocks with higher participation of retailed investors, providing further supporting evidence. Our results shed some new light on economic drivers for intraday return behaviors, which has important implications for empirical asset pricing tests and provides understandings for the limits of market efficiency.

Keywords : Intraday reversal, liquidity, Chinese market

Abstract :我们记录并比较了中国和美国市场的横截面日内反转模式，并进一步研究了它们各自的驱动力。与美国市场不同，中国股市日内反转对股票流动性的依赖性不显著。因此，美国数据中长期记录的基于非流动性的解释无法自动适用。相反，我们发现在日内反转之后，累积收益率有一个显著的弹性模式：中国市场先前的日内收益率和未来收益率之间的负相关在日内反转之后被反转。这些结果说明了流动性过剩的原因。此外，散户投资者参与度较高的股票日内反转明显较强，提供了进一步的佐证。我们的研究结果为日内收益行为的经济驱动因素提供了一些新的线索，这对实证资产定价测试具有重要的意义，并为理解市场效率的极限提供了重要的依据。

Keywords :日内反转，流动性，中国市场

\[9\] Bond Yield Changes and the Cross-Section of Global Equity Returns

标题:债券收益率变化与全球股票收益率横截面

作者:Adam Zaremba,Nusret Cakici,Robert J. Bianchi,Huaigang Long

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3756047

Abstract : We document a new cross-sectional anomaly linking international government bond and equity markets. Using a unique long-run dataset of 61 countries for the years 1900–2019, we demonstrate that past bond yield changes predict future stock index returns in the cross-section. The quintile of countries with the largest decline (or smallest increase) in government bond yields outperforms the quintile of countries with the smallest decline (or largest increase) by 0.63% per month. The anomaly survives various robustness tests. Our findings support the behavioral roots of this effect and suggest that investors underreact to yield changes, and limits to arbitrage prevent investors from eliminating the anomaly.
