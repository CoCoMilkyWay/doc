\[1\] Inspecting the mechanism of quantitative easing in the euro area

标题:考察欧元区量化宽松机制

作者:Ralph S.J. Koijen,François Koulischer,Benoît Nguyen,Motohiro Yogo

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20303123

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.11.006

Abstract : Using security-level holdings for all euro-area investors, we study portfolio rebalancing during the quantitative easing program from March 2015 to December 2017. Foreign investors outside the euro area accommodated most of the Eurosystem’s purchases. Duration, government credit, and corporate credit risk did not get concentrated in particular regions or investor sectors. We estimate a demand system for government bonds by instrumental variables to relate portfolio rebalancing to yield changes. Government bond yields decreased by 65 basis points on average, and this estimate varies from 38 to 83 basis points across countries.

Keywords : Portfolio rebalancing;Quantitative easing;Risk concentration;Unconventional monetary policy

Abstract :利用欧元区所有投资者持有的证券水平，我们研究了2015年3月至2017年12月量化宽松计划期间的投资组合再平衡。欧元区以外的外国投资者接受了欧元体系的大部分购买。期限、政府信贷和企业信贷风险并未集中在特定地区或投资者行业。我们通过工具变量来估计政府债券的需求系统，将投资组合再平衡与收益率变化联系起来。政府债券收益率平均下降了65个基点，这一估计值在各国从38个基点到83个基点不等。

Keywords :投资组合再平衡；量化宽松；风险集中；非常规货币政策

\[2\] Voluntary disclosure with evolving news

标题:随新闻发展而自愿披露

作者:Cyrus Aghamolla,Byeong-Je An

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X2030310X

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.11.004

Abstract : We study a dynamic voluntary disclosure setting where the manager’s information and the firm’s value evolve over time. The manager is not limited in her disclosure opportunities, but disclosure is costly. The results show that the manager discloses even if this leads to a price decrease in the current period. The manager absorbs this price drop in order to increase her option value of withholding disclosure in the future. That is, by disclosing today, the manager can improve her continuation value. The results provide a number of novel empirical predictions regarding asset prices and disclosure patterns over time. These include, among others, that disclosures are negatively correlated in time, and stock return skewness is negatively correlated with lagged returns for firms with low uncertainty over their future profitability, in more competitive industries, and in industries with less informative public news.

Keywords : Voluntary disclosure;Evolving information;Dynamic disclosure;Communication;Price drop;Return skewness

Abstract :我们研究了一个动态的自愿披露环境，其中经理的信息和公司的价值随着时间的推移而演变。经理的信息披露机会不受限制，但信息披露的成本很高。结果表明，即使这导致当期价格下降，管理者也会披露。经理人吸收这一价格下跌，以增加其在未来拒绝披露的期权价值。也就是说，通过今天的披露，经理人可以提高自己的持续价值。结果提供了一系列关于资产价格和披露模式的新的经验预测。其中包括，披露在时间上呈负相关，对于未来盈利能力不确定的公司、竞争性较强的行业和公共新闻信息较少的行业，股票收益偏斜与滞后收益呈负相关。

Keywords :自愿披露；演化信息；动态披露；沟通；价格下跌；收益偏斜

\[3\] What to expect when everyone is expecting: Self-fulfilling expectations and asset-pricing puzzles

标题:当每个人都在期待的时候，期待什么：自我实现的期望和资产定价的困惑

作者:Nicolae Gârleanu,Stavros Panageas

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302828

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.10.007

Abstract : We study an economy without bubbles in which expectations about future discount rates can become self-fulfilling because asset valuations redistribute wealth across different investor cohorts. For such redistribution to take place, the wealth of arriving and existing cohorts must react differently to discount rates, and in addition only the existing agents are marginal in financial markets. The self-fulfilling nature of discount rate expectations means that the economy can address several well-documented empirical asset-pricing facts (excessive volatility, return predictability, low interest rate level and volatility), while all real quantities (aggregate consumption and dividend growth) are smooth.

Keywords : Asset pricing;Self-fulfilling expectations;Sunspot equilibria;Equity premium puzzle;Excess volatility puzzle;Inequality

Abstract :我们研究的是一个没有泡沫的经济体，在这个经济体中，对未来贴现率的预期可能会自我实现，因为资产估值会在不同的投资者群体中重新分配财富。为了实现这种再分配，到达的和现有的群体的财富必须对贴现率作出不同的反应，此外，只有现有的代理人在金融市场上处于边缘地位。贴现率预期的自我实现性质意味着，经济体可以解决几个有据可查的经验资产定价事实（过度波动、回报可预测性、低利率水平和波动性），而所有实际数量（总消费和股息增长）都是平稳的。

Keywords :资产定价；自我实现预期；黑子均衡；股票溢价之谜；过度波动之谜；不平等

\[4\] Benchmark interest rates when the government is risky

标题:政府有风险时的基准利率

作者:P. Augustin,M. Chernov,L. Schmid,D. Song

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302841

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.10.009

Abstract : Since the global financial crisis, interest rate swap rates, which represent future uncollateralized interbank borrowing, have fallen below maturity-matched Treasury rates. This is surprising, because US Treasuries, which are deemed expensive because of superior liquidity and safety, should produce yields that are lower than those of swap rates. We show, by no-arbitrage, that sovereign default risk explains negative swap spreads even without frictions such as balance sheet constraints, convenience yield, and hedging demand. We support this explanation with an equilibrium model that jointly accounts for macroeconomic fundamentals and the term structures of interest and US credit default swap rates.

Keywords : Sovereign credit risk;Negative swap rates;Recursive preferences;Term structure

Abstract :自全球金融危机以来，代表未来无抵押银行间借款的利率互换利率已降至与到期日匹配的国债利率以下。这是令人惊讶的，因为美国国债因其优越的流动性和安全性而被视为昂贵，其收益率应该低于互换利率。通过无套利，我们发现主权违约风险解释了负掉期息差，即使没有资产负债表约束、便利收益率和对冲需求等摩擦。我们用一个均衡模型支持这一解释，该模型综合考虑了宏观经济基本面、利率期限结构和美国信用违约互换利率。

Keywords :主权信用风险；负互换利率；递推偏好；期限结构

\[5\] Estimating the anomaly base rate

标题:估计异常基准率

作者:Alex Chinco,Andreas Neuhierl,Michael Weber

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20303305

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.12.003

Abstract : The anomaly zoo has caused many to question whether researchers are using the right tests of statistical significance. But even if researchers are using the right tests, they will still draw the wrong conclusions from their econometric analyses if they start out with the wrong priors (i.e., if they start out with incorrect beliefs about the ex ante probability of encountering a tradable anomaly, the “anomaly base rate”). We propose a way to estimate it by combining two key insights: Empirical Bayes methods capture the implicit process by which researchers form priors about the likelihood that a new variable is a tradable anomaly based on their past experience, and under certain conditions, a one-to-one mapping exists between these prior beliefs and the best-fit tuning parameter in a penalized regression. The anomaly base rate varies substantially over time, and we study trading-strategy performance to verify our estimation results.

Keywords : Return predictability;Data mining;Empirical Bayes;Penalized regressions;C12;C52;G11

Abstract :这一反常现象让许多人质疑研究人员是否使用了正确的统计显著性检验。但是，即使研究人员使用了正确的测试，如果他们从错误的先验出发（即，如果他们从错误的信念出发，对遇到可交易异常的事前概率，即“异常基准利率”）的话，他们仍然会从经济计量分析中得出错误的结论。我们提出了一种通过结合两个关键的见解来估计它的方法：经验贝叶斯方法捕捉了研究人员根据他们过去的经验，在一定条件下，形成关于新变量是可交易异常可能性的先验的隐式过程，在惩罚回归中，这些先验信念和最佳拟合调整参数之间存在一对一的映射。异常基准利率随时间变化很大，我们通过研究交易策略的表现来验证我们的估计结果。

Keywords :收益可预测性；数据挖掘；经验贝叶斯；惩罚回归；C12；C52；G11

\[6\] Implied volatility duration: A measure for the timing of uncertainty resolution

标题:隐含波动持续时间：不确定性解决时机的度量

作者:Christian Schlag,Julian Thimme,Rüdiger Weber

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302877

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.11.003

Abstract : We introduce implied volatility duration (IVD) as a new measure for the timing of uncertainty resolution, with a high IVD corresponding to late resolution. Portfolio sorts on a large cross-section of stocks indicate that investors demand, on average, more than 5% return per year as a compensation for a late resolution of uncertainty. In a general equilibrium model, we show that “late” stocks can only have higher expected returns than “early” stocks if the investor exhibits a preference for early resolution of uncertainty. Our empirical analysis thus provides a purely market-based assessment of the timing preferences of the marginal investor.

Keywords :不确定性的早期解决偏好；隐含波动率；预期股票收益横截面；资产定价

\[7\] Macroprudential FX regulations: Shifting the snowbanks of FX vulnerability?

标题:宏观审慎外汇监管：转移外汇脆弱性的雪堆？

作者:Toni Ahnert,Kristin Forbes,Christian Friedrich,Dennis Reinhardt

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302804

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.10.005

Abstract : We use a new data set on macroprudential foreign exchange (FX) regulations to evaluate their effectiveness and unintended consequences. Our results support the predictions of a model in which banks and markets lend in different currencies, but only banks can screen firm productivity. Regulations significantly reduce bank FX borrowing, and firms respond by increasing FX debt issuance. Moreover, regulations reduce bank sensitivity to exchange rates but are less effective at reducing the sensitivity of the broader economy. Therefore, FX regulations mitigate bank vulnerability to currency fluctuations and the global financial cycle, but appear to partially shift the snowbanks of vulnerability elsewhere.

Keywords : Macroprudential;FX regulations;Currency mismatch;Capital flows

Abstract :我们使用一组新的宏观审慎外汇（FX）监管数据来评估其有效性和意外后果。我们的结果支持一个模型的预测，在这个模型中，银行和市场以不同的货币放贷，但只有银行可以筛选企业生产率。监管措施大大减少了银行外汇借款，而企业则通过增加外汇债券发行来应对。此外，监管降低了银行对汇率的敏感性，但在降低更广泛经济的敏感性方面效果较差。因此，外汇监管减轻了银行对货币波动和全球金融周期的脆弱性，但似乎部分地将脆弱性雪堆转移到了其他地方。

Keywords :宏观审慎；外汇管制；货币错配；资本流动

\[8\] Extrapolative beliefs in the cross-section: What can we learn from the crowds?

标题:横截面外推信念：我们能从人群中学到什么？

作者:Zhi Da,Xing Huang,Lawrence J. Jin

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302786

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.10.003

Abstract : Using novel data from a crowdsourcing platform for ranking stocks, we investigate how investors form expectations about stock returns over the next week. We find that investors extrapolate from stocks’ recent past returns, with more weight on more recent returns, especially when recent returns are negative, salient, or from a dispersed cross-section. Such extrapolative beliefs are stronger among nonprofessionals and large stocks. Moreover, consensus rankings negatively predict returns over the next week, more so among stocks with low institutional ownership and a high degree of extrapolation. A trading strategy that sorts stocks on investor beliefs generates an economically significant profit.

Keywords : Return extrapolation;Beliefs in the cross-section;Expectation formation

Abstract :利用众包平台提供的新数据对股票进行排名，我们调查了投资者对下周股票收益的预期。我们发现，投资者从股票最近的过去回报推断，更重视最近的回报，特别是当最近的回报是负的，显着的，或从一个分散的横截面。这种推断在非专业人士和大型股票中更为强烈。此外，普遍的排名对下周的回报率有负面预测，机构持股比例低、外推程度高的股票更是如此。一种根据投资者信念对股票进行分类的交易策略会产生经济上显著的利润。

Keywords :回归外推；横截面信念；期望形成

\[9\] Real effects of share repurchases legalization on corporate behaviors

标题:股份回购法律化对公司行为的现实影响

作者:Zigan Wang,Qie Ellie Yin,Luping Yu

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X2030283X

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.10.008

Abstract : We use staggered share repurchases legalization from 1985 to 2010 across the world to examine its impact on corporate behaviors. We find that share-repurchasing firms do not cut dividends as a substitution. The cash for repurchasing shares comes more from internal cash than external debt issuance, leading to reductions in capital expenditures and R&D expenses. While this strategy boosts stock prices, it results in lower long-run Tobin's Q, profitability, growth, and innovation, accompanied by lower insider ownership. Tax benefits and paying out temporary earnings are two primary reasons that firms repurchase.

Keywords : Share repurchases;Payout;Investment;Innovation;Firm performance

Abstract :本文利用1985年至2010年全球范围内的交错式股份回购合法化来考察其对公司行为的影响。我们发现，股票回购公司不削减股息作为替代。回购股份的现金来源更多的是内部现金，而不是发行外债，从而减少了资本支出和研发费用。虽然这一策略会推高股价，但它会导致长期托宾Q、盈利能力、增长和创新能力降低，同时内部持股比例也会降低。税收优惠和支付临时收益是企业回购的两个主要原因。

Keywords :股份回购；支付；投资；创新；公司绩效

\[10\] Competition among liquidity providers with access to high-frequency trading technology

标题:获得高频交易技术的流动性提供者之间的竞争

作者:Dion Bongaerts,Mark Van Achter

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302865

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.11.002

Abstract : We model endogenous technology adoption and competition among liquidity providers with access to High-Frequency Trading (HFT) technology. HFT technology provides speed and information advantages. Information advantages may restore excessively toxic markets. Speed advantages may reduce resource costs for liquidity provision. Both effects increase liquidity and welfare. However, informationally advantaged HFTs may impose a winner’s curse on traditional market makers, who in response reduce their participation. This increases resource costs and lowers the execution likelihood for market orders, thereby reducing liquidity and welfare. This result also holds when HFT technology dominates traditional technology in terms of costs and informational advantages.

Keywords : Adverse selection;Liquidity;Latency;Informed trading;Trading technology

Abstract :我们模型内生技术采用和流动性供应商之间的竞争，获得高频交易（HFT）技术。HFT技术提供了速度和信息优势。信息优势可能会恢复过度有毒的市场。速度优势可能会降低流动性供应的资源成本。这两种效应都增加了流动性和福利。然而，信息优势的高频交易可能会给传统做市商带来赢家的诅咒，而传统做市商相应地减少了参与。这增加了资源成本，降低了市场订单的执行可能性，从而降低了流动性和福利。当HFT技术在成本和信息优势方面超过传统技术时，这一结果也成立。

Keywords :逆向选择；流动性；潜伏期；知情交易；交易技术

\[11\] Investors’ appetite for money-like assets: The MMF industry after the 2014 regulatory reform

标题:投资者对货币类资产的偏好：2014年监管改革后的货币市场基金业

作者:Marco Cipriani,Gabriele La Spada

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20303111

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.11.005

Abstract : This paper uses a quasi-natural experiment to estimate the premium for money-likeness. The 2014 Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) reform of the money market fund (MMF) industry reduced the money-likeness of prime MMFs by increasing their information sensitivity, while leaving government MMFs unaffected. Investors fled from prime to government MMFs, with total outflows exceeding one trillion dollars. Using a difference-in-differences design, we estimate the premium for money-likeness to be between 20 and 30 basis points (bps). These premiums are not due to changes in investors’ risk tolerance or funds’ risk taking. Our results support recent developments in monetary theory identifying information insensitivity as a key feature of money.

Keywords : Money-like assets;Information sensitivity;Money market funds;Money market funds reform

Abstract :本文采用准自然实验方法对货币相似性溢价进行了估计。2014年美国证券交易委员会（SEC）对货币市场基金（MMF）行业的改革，通过提高其信息敏感性，降低了主要MMF的货币相似性，同时使政府MMF不受影响。投资者从优质货币基金逃往政府货币基金，总流出额超过1万亿美元。使用差异设计中的差异，我们估计货币相似性溢价在20到30个基点（bps）之间。这些溢价并非由于投资者风险承受能力或基金风险承担能力的变化。我们的研究结果支持了货币理论的最新发展，认为信息不敏感是货币的一个重要特征。

Keywords :货币性资产；信息敏感性；货币市场基金；货币市场基金改革

\[12\] What is the impact of introducing a parallel OTC market? Theory and evidence from the chinese interbank FX market

标题:引入平行场外交易市场有何影响？中国银行间外汇市场的理论与实证研究

作者:Craig W. Holden,Dong Lu,Volodymyr Lugovskyy,Daniela Puzzello

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302798

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.10.004
