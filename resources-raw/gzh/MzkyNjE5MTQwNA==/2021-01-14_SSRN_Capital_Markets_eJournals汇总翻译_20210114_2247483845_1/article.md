\[1\] Intraday Market Predictability: A Machine Learning Approach

标题:日内市场可预测性：一种机器学习方法

作者:Dillon Huddleston(University of Western Ontario - Department of Economics),Fred Liu(University of Western Ontario, Department of Economics),Lars Stentoft(Department of Economics, University of Western Ontario; Center for Interuniversity Research and Analysis on Organization (CIRANO); Aarhus University - CREATES)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3726765

Abstract : This paper analyses and demonstrates the predictability of intraday market returns. Conducting, to our knowledge, the largest study ever of five-minute market returns using state-of-the-art machine learning models trained on lagged returns to forecast five-minute equity market index returns, we show that regularized linear models such as lasso and elastic nets along with nonlinear tree based models such as random forests yield significant predictability. Ensemble models that combine individual model predictions perform the best across time and their return predictability translates into economically significant profits with Sharpe ratios after transaction costs of 0.98. This strong predictability of intraday market returns provides evidence against market efficiency over short time horizons. Consistent with the hypothesis that predictability is driven by trader inefficiency, predictability decreased after decimalization, and market returns are more predictable during the middle of the day, on days with high volatility or high illiquidity, and in years of financial crisis.

Keywords : Machine Learning, Return Prediction, High-Frequency, Equity Market, Big Data, Lasso, Elastic Net, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Deep Neural Networks, Fintech

Abstract :本文分析并论证了日内市场收益的可预测性。据我们所知，利用最先进的机器学习模型对五分钟市场收益率进行了有史以来最大规模的研究，对滞后收益率进行了培训，以预测五分钟股市指数收益率，我们证明了正则化的线性模型（如套索和弹性网）以及非线性树模型（如随机森林）具有显著的可预测性。集合模型结合了单个模型的预测，在整个时间段内表现最好，其收益可预测性转化为具有经济意义的利润，夏普比率在交易成本为0.98之后。日内市场收益的这种强可预测性为短期内的市场效率提供了证据。与可预测性是由交易者的低效率驱动的假设相一致，可预测性在小数化后下降，市场回报在中午、高波动或高流动性的日子以及金融危机的年份更可预测。

Keywords :机器学习，收益预测，高频，股市，大数据，套索，弹性网，随机森林，梯度推进，深度神经网络，金融科技

\[2\] Long-Term Investing in Triple Leveraged Exchange Traded Funds

标题:长期投资于三杠杆交易所买卖基金

作者:Lewis A. Glenn(Independent)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722318

Abstract : The phrase long-term investing in triple leveraged ETFs is somewhat of an anathema for investment academicians. As a result of daily rebalancing and so-called beta slippage or “the constant leverage trap” it is highly likely over the long term to significantly deviate from the targeted leverage and, in so doing, generate wipeout losses. In fact, the data show that many 3X leveraged ETFs have performed poorly over the long term in the period of their existence. However, there are some significant exceptions. The 3X leveraged Nasdaq ETF, TQQQ, for example has delivered a total return exceeding 10,000% (CAGR of 54.4%) over the 10+ years of its existence, more than 13 times the total return of its underlying ETF (QQQ). Similar, but less impressive numbers have been achieved with triple leveraged ETFs that target the S&P 500 and the Dow Industrials.These results, however, are accompanied by excessive volatility and very large maximum end-of-month (EOM) draw downs, in excess of 49% for TQQQ, and with maximum daily drawdown of almost 70%. Many retail investors would be unwilling to sustain such (paper) losses. Here we propose a strategy to mitigate these large draw downs while still retaining much of the upside performance of triple leveraging. We show that by simply establishing a portfolio consisting of an equal dollar amount of TQQQ and TMF (the triple leveraged ETF linked to the 20+ year treasury bond), and performing bimonthly rebalancing, a total return, after transaction costs, in excess of 5,800% (CAGR of 44.9%) would have been achieved over the 10+ years of TQQQ existence, with the maximum EOM draw down less than 25%.

Keywords : Triple leveraging, strategy, draw down

Abstract :“长期投资于三杠杆ETF”这句话多少让投资学者深恶痛绝。由于每日的重新平衡和所谓的贝塔下滑或“持续杠杆陷阱”，长期来看，很有可能显著偏离目标杠杆，并在这样做的过程中产生彻底的损失。事实上，数据显示，许多3倍杠杆ETF在存续期间长期表现不佳。然而，也有一些重要的例外。例如，3倍杠杆率的纳斯达克ETF TQQ在成立10多年来的总回报率超过10000%（复合年增长率为54.4%），是其基础ETF（QQQ）总回报率的13倍多。以标普500指数和道琼斯指数为目标的三杠杆etf也取得了类似但不太令人印象深刻的数据工业。这些然而，结果伴随着过度的波动和非常大的月底（EOM）提取，TQQ超过49%，最大日提取近70%。许多散户投资者不愿意承受这种（纸面）损失。在这里，我们提出了一个战略，以减轻这些巨大的下降，同时仍保留了三重杠杆的大部分上行业绩。我们表明，只要建立一个由TQQ和TMF（与20年期以上国债挂钩的三杠杆ETF）等额组成的投资组合，并进行双月再平衡，在TQQ存在的10多年时间内，交易成本后的总回报率将超过5800%（复合年增长率为44.9%），并且最大EOM下降低于25%。

Keywords :三重杠杆、战略、缩减

\[3\] Cash Heterogeneity and the Payout Channel of Monetary Policy

标题:现金异质性与货币政策的支付渠道

作者:Altan Pazarbasi(Frankfurt School of Finance & Management )

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3758539

Abstract : This paper investigates the role of corporate cash heterogeneity in the transmission of monetary policy to equity payouts and cross-sectional risk premia. Compared to cash-poor firms, cash-rich firms not only pay out persistently more but also earn higher returns in response to expansionary monetary policy news surprises. I develop a heterogeneous firm New-Keynesian model with cash holdings and costly equity issuance to interpret the empirical findings. In the model, cash-rich firms have weaker cash flow and investment responses to monetary policy, however they disburse excess savings as precautionary cash demand declines due to monetary easing. Nominal rigidities endogenously produce procyclical payouts, rendering payout claims of cash-rich firms risky and generating cross-sectional risk premia. The paper supports a central role for the expected path of the monetary policy in affecting corporate outcomes and asset prices.

Keywords : Monetary Policy, Cash Holdings, Equity Payout, Risk Premia, General Equilibrium

Abstract :本文研究了企业现金异质性在货币政策向股票支付和横截面风险溢价传导中的作用。与现金匮乏的公司相比，现金充裕的公司不仅持续支付更多，而且在应对扩张性货币政策的意外消息时获得更高的回报。我发展了一个异质企业的新凯恩斯模型与现金持有和昂贵的股票发行来解释实证结果。在该模型中，现金充裕的企业对货币政策的现金流和投资反应较弱，但由于货币宽松导致预防性现金需求下降，它们会支付超额储蓄。名义刚性内在地产生顺周期的支付，使得现金充裕的公司的支付要求具有风险，并产生横截面风险溢价。本文支持货币政策预期路径在影响企业业绩和资产价格方面的核心作用。

Keywords :货币政策，现金持有，股票支付，风险溢价，一般均衡

\[4\] Barriers to Global Capital Allocation

标题:全球资本配置障碍

作者:Bruno Pellegrino(University of Maryland - Robert H. Smith School of Business),Enrico Spolaore(Tufts University - Department of Economics),Romain T. Wacziarg(UCLA Anderson School of Management; National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER))

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3752485

Abstract : We quantify the impact of barriers to international investment, using a novel multi-country over- lapping generations model with heterogeneous investors and imperfect capital mobility. Our model yields a gravity equation for foreign asset demand. We estimate this gravity equation using recently- developed foreign investment data that has been restated to account for the presence of offshore investment and financing vehicles. We show that a parsimonious implementation of the model, with four barriers (capital controls, geographic distance, institutional distance and cultural distance) can account for a large share of the observed variation in bilateral Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) and Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI) positions. Our model predicts a significant home bias and higher rates of return on capital in emerging markets. In our benchmark calibration, we estimate that capital misallocation induced by these barriers reduces World GDP by 8.8%, compared to a situation without barriers. We also find that barriers to global capital allocation contribute significantly to cross-country inequality: the standard deviation of log capital per employee is 70% higher than it would be in a world without barriers to international investment, while the dispersion in output per employee is 39% higher.

Keywords : Capital Flows, Foreign Investment, Culture, Institutions, Geography, Gravity, Interna- tional Macroeconomics, International Finance, Open Economy

Abstract :我们使用一个新的多国重叠世代模型，在异质投资者和不完全资本流动的情况下，量化了障碍对国际投资的影响。我们的模型得出了外国资产需求的引力方程。我们估计这个重力方程使用最近开发的外国投资数据，这些数据已被重新表述，以说明存在离岸投资和融资工具。我们发现，在双边外国直接投资（FDI）和外国证券投资（FPI）头寸中，有四个障碍（资本管制、地理距离、制度距离和文化距离）的模型的节省实施可以解释观察到的很大一部分变化。我们的模型预测，新兴市场将出现明显的国内偏好和较高的资本回报率。在我们的基准校准中，我们估计，与没有障碍的情况相比，这些障碍导致的资本配置不当会使世界GDP减少8.8%。我们还发现，全球资本配置的障碍对跨国不平等有显著贡献：在没有国际投资障碍的世界中，每位员工的对数资本的标准差比没有国际投资障碍的世界高出70%，而每位员工的产出离差则高出39%。

Keywords :资本流动、外国投资、文化、制度、地理、引力、国际宏观经济、国际金融、开放经济

\[5\] Machine Learning, Regularization, and Reporting Bias

标题:机器学习、规则化和报告偏差

作者:Yi Chen(Cornell University),Kai Du(Pennsylvania State University),Shuyang Wang(University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign),Zhe Wang(Pennsylvania State University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3742212

Abstract : We study how investors' use of machine learning methods affects corporate financial reporting decisions. Our model consists of a representative investor who employs a regularized regression (e.g., lasso) to infer value from earnings reports, along with a firm manager who may bias earnings reports. The investor's use of regularized regression serves as a commitment to underutilize earnings reports, and consequently, induces a lower price-earnings association, a smaller reporting bias, but greater price efficiency. The two-account version of our model suggests that regularization gives rise to a spillover effect: factors that influence the reporting bias in one account indirectly impact the manager's incentive to manipulate the other account. The model offers policy implications on algorithmic transparency and provides testable predictions.

Keywords : machine learning, regularization, lasso, reporting bias

Abstract :本文研究了投资者使用机器学习方法对公司财务报告决策的影响。我们的模型由一个代表性投资者和一个可能对收益报告产生偏见的公司经理组成，代表性投资者使用正则回归（如套索）从收益报告中推断价值。投资者使用正则化回归作为对未充分利用收益报告的承诺，从而导致较低的市盈率关联，较小的报告偏差，但更高的价格效率。我们模型的两个账户版本表明，正规化会产生溢出效应：影响一个账户报告偏差的因素会间接影响管理者操纵另一个账户的动机。该模型提供了算法透明度的政策含义，并提供了可测试的预测。

Keywords :机器学习，正则化，套索，报告偏差

\[6\] Greed and Individual Trading Behavior in Experimental Asset Markets

标题:实验性资产市场中的贪婪与个人交易行为

作者:Karlijn Hoyer(Tilburg University),Stefan Zeisberger(Radboud University, Institute for Management Research; University of Zurich, Department of Banking and Finance),Seger Breugelmans(Tilburg University),Marcel Zeelenberg(Tilburg University - Faculty of Social and Behavioural Sciences)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3740473

Abstract : Greed has been shown to be an important economic motive. Both the popular press as well as scientific papers have mentioned questionable practices by greedy bankers and investors as one of the root causes of the 2008 global financial crisis. In spite of these suggestions, there is as of yet no substantive empirical evidence for a contribution of greed to individual trading behavior. This paper presents the result of 15 experimental asset markets in which we test the influence of greed on trading behavior. We do not find empirical support for the idea that greedier investors trade fundamentally different from their less greedy counterparts in markets. These findings shed light on the role of greed in trading and the emergence of asset market bubbles in specific, and of the financial crisis in general. Directions for future research are discussed.

Keywords : dispositional greed, experimental finance, experimental asset markets, bubbles, mispricing

Abstract :贪婪已被证明是一个重要的经济动机。大众媒体和科学论文都提到贪婪的银行家和投资者的可疑行为是2008年全球金融危机的根源之一。尽管有这些建议，但迄今为止还没有实质性的经验证据表明贪婪对个人交易行为的贡献。本文给出了15个实验性资产市场的结果，检验了贪婪对交易行为的影响。我们没有发现实证支持的观点，即贪婪的投资者交易从根本上不同于他们在市场上不那么贪婪的对手。这些发现揭示了贪婪在交易中的作用，特别是资产市场泡沫的出现，以及整个金融危机。讨论了今后的研究方向。

Keywords :处置性贪婪，实验性金融，实验性资产市场，泡沫，错误定价

\[7\] Market Selection and the Absence of Arbitrage

标题:市场选择与无套利

作者:Sabine Elmiger(University of Zurich)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3746988

Abstract : A central conjecture of behavioural finance is that arbitrage opportunities appear as a result of systematic irrational investment behaviour and persist since real-world arbitrage trades actually involve costs and risks due to market frictions and non-fundamental risk. This paper shows that the no-arbitrage condition can emerge from the market selection process even if systematic irrational trading behaviour occurs permanently and there are no strategic arbitrage trades. The model consists of two types of agents and two assets. Dividends are independently and identically distributed over time. Both types of agents invest positive amounts of wealth into each asset and keep portfolio weights constant. Arbitrage opportunities naturally occur in the short and medium term depending on how both types invest but disappear in the long run if both types survive the market selection process - regardless of both types' portfolios and initial wealth distribution. A necessary condition for arbitrage opportunities to persist is that one type of agents drives the other one out of the market.

Keywords : market selection, arbitrage, evolutionary finance

Abstract :行为金融学的一个核心猜想是，套利机会是系统性非理性投资行为的结果，而且由于市场摩擦和非基本面风险，现实世界的套利交易实际上涉及成本和风险，因此套利机会仍然存在。本文表明，即使系统性非理性交易行为永久发生，并且不存在战略性套利交易，市场选择过程中也会产生无套利条件。该模型由两类代理和两种资产组成。随着时间的推移，股息是独立且相同的分配。这两种类型的代理都将正数量的财富投资到每种资产中，并保持投资组合权重不变。套利机会自然发生在短期和中期，这取决于这两种类型的投资方式，但从长远来看，如果这两种类型都在市场选择过程中幸存下来，无论这两种类型的投资组合和初始财富分布如何，套利机会都会消失。套利机会持续存在的一个必要条件是，一类代理人将另一类代理人赶出市场。

Keywords :市场选择、套利、演化金融

\[8\] S-Shaped Consumption Utility: Empirical Evidence and Implications

标题:S型消费效用：经验证据与启示

作者:Gaosheng Ju(China Center for Economic Studies, School of Economics, Fudan University),Qi Li(Texas A&M University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3730422

Abstract : We find that common macro factors generate a “big bang/crunch” effect on micro consumption. Genreally speaking, when the aggregate effect of the common factors on the consumption in low consumption-growth states is negative (resp. positive), this effect in high consumption-growth states is positive (resp. negative). The big bang/crunch suggests that consumption utility functions are not concave-shaped but S-shaped. S-shaped consumption utility accounts for the unsatisfactory empirical performance of traditional consumption-based asset pricing models. Moreover, the S-shaped utility helps explain the negative elasticity of intertemporal substitution reported in the literature.

Keywords : S-Shaped Consumption Utility, Common Factors, Consumption-Based Asset Pricing, Elasticity of Intertemporal Substitution

Abstract :我们发现，共同的宏观因素对微观消费产生了“大爆炸/紧缩”效应。实际上，当低消费增长状态下公共因素对消费的总效应为负时（分别为。积极的），这种影响在高消费增长状态是积极的（分别。阴性）。大爆炸/危机表明，消费效用函数不是凹形的，而是S形的。S型消费效用是传统消费型资产定价模型实证效果不理想的原因。此外，S型效用有助于解释文献报道的跨期替代的负弹性。

Keywords :S型消费效用、公共因素、基于消费的资产定价、跨期替代弹性

\[9\] Asset Pricing Based on Micro Consumption

标题:基于微观消费的资产定价

作者:Gaosheng Ju(China Center for Economic Studies, School of Economics, Fudan University),Qi Li(Texas A&M University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3741867

Abstract : Using micro data, we find that the top quantiles of consumption growth are generally positively correlated with asset returns; at low quantiles, the correlations are negative-valued for many individuals. The findings support our asset pricing model based on an S-shaped consumption utility. In this model, individuals are risk-seeking (resp. risk-averse) in certain states, and their consumption growth varies negatively (resp. positively) with risky returns. Therefore, consumption growth has a small covariance with risky returns.
