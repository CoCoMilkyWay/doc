\[2\] Blockchain without Waste: Proof-of-Stake

标题:无浪费区块链：利益证明

作者:Fahad Saleh

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/3/1156/5868423; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa075

Abstract : Permissionless blockchains require a protocol to generate consensus. Many prominent permissionless blockchains employ Proof-of-Work (PoW) for that purpose, but PoW possesses significant shortcomings. Various alternatives have been proposed. This paper provides the first formal economic model of the most famous alternative, Proof-of-Stake (PoS), and establishes conditions under which PoS generates consensus. A sufficiently modest reward schedule not only implies the existence of an equilibrium in which consensus obtains as soon as possible but also precludes a persistent forking equilibrium. The latter result arises because PoS, unlike PoW, requires that validators are also stakeholders.

Abstract:无权限区块链需要一个协议来产生共识。许多著名的无许可区块链为此目的使用工作证明（PoW），但PoW具有显著的缺点。已经提出了各种备选方案。本文给出了最著名的替代方案的第一个形式经济模型，即股权证明（PoS），并建立了PoS产生共识的条件。一个足够适度的奖励计划不仅意味着一个均衡的存在，在这个均衡中，共识尽快获得，而且还排除了一个持久的分叉均衡。出现后一种结果是因为PoS与PoW不同，它要求验证器也是涉众。

\[3\] Decentralized Mining in Centralized Pools

标题:集中池分散开采

作者:Lin William Cong, Zhiguo He, Jiasun Li

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/3/1191/5815571; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa040

Abstract : The rise of centralized mining pools for risk sharing does not necessarily undermine the decentralization required for blockchains: because of miners’ cross-pool diversification and pool managers’ endogenous fee setting, larger pools better internalize their externality on global hash rates, charge higher fees, attract disproportionately fewer miners, and grow more slowly. Instead, mining pools as a financial innovation escalate miners’ arms race and significantly increase the energy consumption of proof-of-work-based blockchains. Empirical evidence from Bitcoin mining supports our model’s predictions. The economic insights inform other consensus protocols and the industrial organization of mainstream sectors with similar characteristics but ambiguous prior findings.

Abstract:集中化采矿池用于风险分担的兴起并不一定会破坏区块链所需的分散化：由于采矿者的跨池多样化和池经理的内生费用设置，较大的池更好地将其外部性内在化到全球散列率上，收取更高的费用，吸引的资金也相应减少矿工们，成长得更慢。取而代之的是，采矿池作为一种金融创新，加剧了矿工之间的军备竞赛，并显著增加了基于工作证明的区块链的能耗。来自比特币挖掘的经验证据支持我们的模型预测。经济洞察为其他共识协议和具有类似特征但先前发现不明确的主流行业的产业组织提供了信息。

\[4\] Uncertainty, Investor Sentiment, and Innovation

标题:不确定性、投资者情绪和创新

作者:David Dicks, Paolo Fulghieri

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/3/1236/5858144; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa065

Abstract : We develop a theory of innovation waves, investor sentiment, and merger activity based on Knightian uncertainty. Uncertainty-averse investors are more optimistic on an innovation when they can make contemporaneous investments in multiple uncertain projects. Innovation waves occur when there is a critical mass of innovative companies, and are characterized by stronger investor sentiment, higher equity valuation, and hot initial public offering markets. Our approach to investor sentiment is not based on erroneous beliefs disjoint from economic fundamentals, but depends on uncertainty on the fundamentals. Our model can explain sector-specific booms uncorrelated with aggregate economic activity and the overall stock market.

Abstract:我们发展了一个基于奈特不确定性的创新浪潮、投资者情绪和并购活动理论。厌恶不确定性的投资者对一项创新更为乐观，因为他们可以同时投资于多个不确定的项目。当创新型公司达到临界数量时，就会出现创新浪潮，其特点是投资者情绪更加强烈、股票估值更高、首次公开发行（ipo）市场火热。我们对待投资者情绪的方法不是基于与经济基本面脱节的错误信念，而是取决于基本面上的不确定性。我们的模型可以解释特定行业的繁荣与总体经济活动和整体股市不相关。

\[6\] Do Foreign Institutional Investors Improve Price Efficiency?

标题:外国机构投资者是否提高了价格效率？

作者:Marcin Kacperczyk, Savitar Sundaresan, Tianyu Wang

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/3/1317/5866988; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa076

Abstract : We study the impact of foreign institutional investors on price efficiency with firm-level international data. Using additions to the MSCI index and the U.S. Jobs and Growth Tax Relief Reconciliation Act as exogenous shocks to foreign ownership, we show that greater foreign ownership increases stock price informativeness, especially in developed economies. This increase arises from new information that foreign investors bring in and displacement of less-informed domestic retail investors. Finally, we show that foreign ownership, particularly from active investors, increases market liquidity, reduces firms’ cost of equity, and increases firms’ real investment growth.

Abstract:本文利用企业层面的国际数据研究了外国机构投资者对价格效率的影响。通过增加摩根士丹利资本国际指数和美国就业与增长税收减免调节作为对外资所有权的外生冲击，我们表明，外资所有权的增加增加了股票价格的信息性，特别是在发达经济体。这一增长源于外国投资者带来的新信息以及不太了解情况的国内散户投资者的取代。最后，我们表明，外国所有权，特别是来自积极投资者的所有权，增加了市场流动性，降低了企业的股权成本，增加了企业的实际投资增长。

作者:George O Aragon, Vikram Nanda, Haibei Zhao

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/3/1368/5828944; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa051

Abstract : We find that capital flows to hedge funds in different countries are influenced by the strength and the enforcement of investor protection laws. Hedge funds located in weak investor protection countries exhibit greater sensitivity of investor outflow to poor performance, relative to funds in countries with strong protection. Our findings suggest that in countries with weaker investor protection, poor fund performance exposes investors to a greater risk of fraud and legal jeopardy, thus triggering a larger outflow of capital.

\[9\] Taper Tantrums: Quantitative Easing, Its Aftermath, and Emerging Market Capital Flows

标题:逐渐缓和的情绪：量化宽松、其后果和新兴市场资本流动

作者:Anusha Chari, Karlye Dilts Stedman, Christian Lundblad

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/3/1445/5817323; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa044

Abstract : This paper examines the spillover effects of U.S. unconventional monetary policy (UMP) on emerging market capital flows and asset prices. Affine term structure model estimates show that U.S. monetary policy shocks, identified with high-frequency Treasury futures data, represent revisions to expected short-term yields and term premia, especially during the UMP period. The policy shocks exhibit sizable effects on U.S. holdings of emerging market assets. These effects disproportionately manifest through valuation changes versus physical flows, are more pronounced for equity relative to bond markets, and are asymmetric between the quantitative easing and tapering periods, with flows more important during the unwinding.

Abstract:本文考察了美国非常规货币政策对新兴市场资本流动和资产价格的溢出效应。仿射期限结构模型估计显示，美国货币政策冲击（通过高频国债期货数据确定）代表对预期短期收益率和期限溢价的修正，特别是在货币政策联盟期间。政策冲击对美国持有的新兴市场资产产生了相当大的影响。这些影响不成比例地表现在估值变化与实物流动之间，相对于债券市场而言，股票的影响更为明显，而且在量化宽松和逐步缩减期间是不对称的，流动在平仓期间更为重要。

\[11\] Carbon Tail Risk

标题:碳尾风险

作者:Emirhan Ilhan, Zacharias Sautner, Grigory Vilkov

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/3/1540/5864038; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa071

Abstract : Strong regulatory actions are needed to combat climate change, but climate policy uncertainty makes it difficult for investors to quantify the impact of future climate regulation. We show that such uncertainty is priced in the option market. The cost of option protection against downside tail risks is larger for firms with more carbon-intense business models. For carbon-intense firms, the cost of protection against downside tail risk is magnified at times when the public’s attention to climate change spikes, and it decreased after the election of climate change skeptic President Trump.

Abstract:应对气候变化需要强有力的监管行动，但气候政策的不确定性使得投资者难以量化未来气候监管的影响。我们证明了这种不确定性是在期权市场中定价的。对于碳密集型商业模式的公司来说，针对下行尾部风险的期权保护成本更大。对于碳密集型企业而言，当公众对气候变化的关注度飙升时，防范下行尾部风险的成本有时会被放大，而在气候变化怀疑论者特朗普总统当选后，这种成本会下降。

\[12\] The Skewness of the Stock Market over Long Horizons

标题:股市长期以来的偏斜

作者:Anthony Neuberger, Richard Payne

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/3/1572/5825293; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa048

Abstract : Higher moments of long-horizon returns are important for asset pricing but are hard to measure accurately using standard techniques. We provide theory showing that short-horizon (e.g., daily) returns can be used to construct precise estimates of long-horizon (e.g., annual) moments without making strong assumptions about the data-generating process. Skewness comprises two components: skewness of short-horizon returns and a leverage effect, that is, covariance between variance and lagged returns. We provide similar results for kurtosis. An application to U.S. stock index returns shows that skew is large and negative and attenuates only slowly as one moves from monthly to multiyear horizons.

Abstract:长期回报的高阶矩对资产定价很重要，但很难用标准技术准确衡量。我们提供的理论表明，短期（如每日）回报率可用于构建长期（如每年）矩的精确估计，而无需对数据生成过程作出强有力的假设。偏态包括两个组成部分：短期回报的偏态和杠杆效应，即方差和滞后回报之间的协方差。我们为峰度提供了类似的结果。对美国股指收益率的一个应用表明，偏差很大，而且是负的，当一个人从月度水平移动到多年水平时，这种偏差只会缓慢衰减。
