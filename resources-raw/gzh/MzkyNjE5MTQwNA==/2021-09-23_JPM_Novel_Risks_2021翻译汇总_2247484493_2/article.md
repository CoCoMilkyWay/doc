\[2\] Novel Risks: A Research and Policy Overview

标题:新风险：研究和政策概述

作者:Ahmet K. Karagozoglu

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/9/11;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.287

Abstract : In a broad sense, novel risks arise from environmental-, governance-, healthcare-, social responsibility–, sustainability-, and technology-related shortcomings of or challenges faced by firms, as well as the uncertainty caused by potential domestic and global regulatory policy responses. Recent academic literature suggests that there are parallels among environmental, social, and governance (ESG) risk, climate change risk, cybersecurity risk, and geopolitical risk in terms of measurement challenges, including but not limited to emerging data and measurement methods; the similarities in terms of their insufficient, noncomparable, less-specific, and non–decision-useful disclosures; and the potential interaction between these risks. Establishment of consistent disclosure policy and reporting requirements as well as improvement in measuring the impact of these novel risks on asset prices, volatility, and global financial stability is at the forefront of contemporary financial economics and portfolio management.

TOPICS: Risk management, tail risks, ESG investing, legal/regulatory/public policy

Key Finding

\- ▪ A broad characterization of novel risks is presented, and environmental, social, and governance (ESG) risk, climate change risk, cybersecurity risk, and geopolitical risk specifically are defined.

\- ▪ An overview of the recent academic literature suggests that there are parallels among these novel risks in terms of measurement challenges and disclosure regulations.

\- ▪ Measures based on novel applications of text and news analytics are identified as proxies for novel risks as investigated in the recent academic literature.

Abstract:从广义上讲，新的风险来自企业面临的环境、治理、医疗、社会责任、可持续性和技术相关的缺陷或挑战，以及潜在的国内和全球监管政策响应所造成的不确定性。最近的学术文献表明，环境、社会和治理（ESG）风险、气候变化风险、网络安全风险和地缘政治风险在计量挑战方面存在相似之处，包括但不限于新兴数据和计量方法；相似之处在于披露的信息不充分、不可比喻、不太具体和非决策有用；以及这些风险之间的潜在相互作用。建立一致的披露政策和报告要求，以及改进衡量这些新风险对资产价格、波动性和全球金融稳定的影响，是当代金融经济学和投资组合管理的前沿。

主题：风险管理、尾部风险、ESG投资、法律/监管/公共政策

Key Finding

\- ▪ 提出了新风险的广泛特征，并具体定义了环境、社会和治理（ESG）风险、气候变化风险、网络安全风险和地缘政治风险。

\- ▪ 对近期学术文献的综述表明，这些新风险在计量挑战和披露法规方面存在相似之处。

\- ▪ 基于文本和新闻分析新应用的措施被确定为最新学术文献中调查的新风险的代理。

\[3\] Foundations of Climate Investing: How Equity Markets Have Priced Climate-Transition Risks

标题:气候投资的基础：股票市场如何定价气候变化风险

作者:Guido Giese, Zoltán Nagy and Bruno Rauis

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/9/35;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.283

Abstract : Countries have set varying targets to reduce greenhouse gas emissions in line with the Paris Agreement’s goal of keeping the increase in global average temperatures to well below 2°C. In this article, the authors examine to what extent climate risk has been priced into equity markets and whether climate change can be modeled using a typical risk model structure. They develop the fundamental economic transmission channels to explain the potential impact of climate change on equity prices, including empirical evidence for climate policies and green technology as financial risk drivers. They also study the impact of climate-transition risk on valuation levels and trends. They conclude with a discussion of how to measure and categorize companies’ climate-risk exposures and how to integrate climate-transition risks into risk models.

TOPICS: ESG investing, security analysis and valuation, tail risks

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors identified economic transmission channels showing how regulatory policies and green technology influence financial markets. In developed markets outside the United States, more carbon-efficient companies experienced stronger performance over a seven-year study period. They found companies’ green revenue share was clearly associated with higher earnings growth and relatively better stock performance within a given sector.

\- ▪ Next, they looked at the relationship between companies’ climate-transition risk profiles and their valuation levels, finding that carbon-intensive companies experienced declining valuation in terms of price-to-book ratios than their less carbon-intensive sector peers—suggesting that markets have discounted the book value of carbon-intensive companies during the study period. In contrast, companies with significant green revenues saw their price-to-earnings ratios increase relative to their sector peers.

\- ▪ Companies’ earnings growth and stock performance was directly related to their greenhouse gas emissions. Using five MSCI low carbon transition categories, the authors found that the riskiest category (stranded assets) had the weakest performance and the solutions category the strongest during the study period. Although most performance differences were explained by the industry factor, there was a significant stock-specific return that showed a strong correlation to companies’ climate transition-risk profile.

Abstract:各国根据《巴黎协定》将全球平均气温增幅控制在2°C以下的目标，制定了不同的减排目标。在本文中，作者研究了气候风险在多大程度上已被定价到股票市场，以及气候变化是否可以使用典型的风险模型结构进行建模。他们开发了基本的经济传导渠道来解释气候变化对股票价格的潜在影响，包括气候政策和绿色技术作为金融风险驱动因素的经验证据。他们还研究气候变化风险对估值水平和趋势的影响。他们最后讨论了如何衡量和分类公司的气候风险敞口，以及如何将气候变化风险整合到风险模型中。

主题：ESG投资、证券分析和估值、尾部风险

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者确定了经济传播渠道，展示了监管政策和绿色技术如何影响金融市场。在美国以外的发达市场，碳效率更高的公司在七年的研究期间表现更强。他们发现，公司的绿色收入份额显然与特定行业内较高的盈利增长和相对较好的股票表现有关。

\- ▪ 接下来，他们研究了公司气候变化风险状况与其估值水平之间的关系，研究发现，碳密集型公司在价格与账面比率方面的估值比碳密集度较低的行业同行有所下降，这表明市场在研究期间对碳密集型公司的账面价值进行了贴现。相比之下，拥有大量绿色收入的公司，其市盈率相对于行业同行有所上升。

\- ▪ 公司的盈利增长和股票表现与其温室气体排放量直接相关。使用五个MSCI低碳转型类别，作者发现，在研究期间，风险最高的类别（搁浅资产）表现最差，而解决方案类别表现最强。尽管大多数业绩差异是由行业因素解释的，但存在显著的股票特定回报率，这与公司的气候变化风险状况密切相关。

\[4\] The Market Measure of Carbon Risk and its Impact on the Minimum Variance Portfolio

标题:碳风险的市场测度及其对最小方差投资组合的影响

作者:Théo Roncalli, Théo Le Guenedal, Frédéric Lepetit, Thierry Roncalli and Takaya Sekine

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/9/54;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.285

Abstract : Like environment, social, and governance investing, climate change is an important concern for asset managers and owners and a new challenge for portfolio construction. Until now, investors have mainly measured carbon risk using fundamental approaches, such as with carbon intensity metrics. Nevertheless, it has not been proven that asset prices are directly affected by these fundamental-based measures. In this article, the authors focus on another approach, which consists of measuring the sensitivity of stock prices with respect to a carbon risk factor. In the authors’ opinion, carbon betas are market-based measures that are complementary to carbon intensities or fundamental-based measures when managing investment portfolios; carbon betas may be viewed as an extension or forward-looking measure of the current carbon footprint. In particular, they show how this new metric can be used to build minimum variance strategies and how it affects portfolio construction.

TOPICS: ESG investing, portfolio construction, tail risks, fundamental equity analysis

Key Finding

\- ▪ Measuring carbon risk is different if we consider a fundamental-based approach by using carbon intensity metrics or a market-based approach by using carbon betas.

\- ▪ Managing relative carbon risk implies overweighting of green firms, whereas managing absolute carbon risk implies having zero exposure to the carbon risk factor. The first approach is an active management bet, whereas the second is an immunization investment strategy.

\- ▪ Both specific and systematic carbon risks are important when building a minimum variance portfolio and justify combining fundamental and market approaches to carbon risk.

Abstract:与环境、社会和治理投资一样，气候变化也是资产管理者和所有者关注的一个重要问题，也是投资组合构建的一个新挑战。到目前为止，投资者主要使用基本方法（如碳强度指标）来衡量碳风险。然而，尚未证明资产价格直接受到这些基本面指标的影响。在这篇文章中，作者关注的是另一种方法，包括测量股票价格对碳风险因素的敏感性。作者认为，在管理投资组合时，碳beta是与碳强度或基本面指标互补的基于市场的指标；碳beta可被视为当前碳足迹的延伸或前瞻性度量。特别是，他们展示了如何使用这个新指标来构建最小方差策略，以及它如何影响投资组合的构建。

主题：ESG投资、投资组合构建、尾部风险、基本权益分析

Key Finding

\- ▪ 测量碳风险是不同的，如果我们考虑一个基本的方法，通过使用碳强度度量或基于市场的方法，使用碳贝塔。▪ 管理相对碳风险意味着绿色企业的权重过高，而管理绝对碳风险意味着零碳风险因素。第一种方法是积极的管理赌注，而第二种方法是免疫投资策略

\- ▪ 在构建最小方差投资组合时，具体和系统的碳风险都很重要，并证明将基本方法和市场方法结合起来应对碳风险是合理的。

\[5\] Top-Down Portfolio Implications of Climate Change

标题:气候变化自上而下的投资组合影响

作者:Yesim Tokat-Acikel, Marco Aiolfi, Lorne Johnson, John Hall and Jessica (Yiwen) Jin

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/9/69;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.279

Abstract : This article reviews the significant progress in academic research on economic impact of climate change and explores the implications for expected returns and strategic portfolio allocation across major public asset classes. There have been numerous efforts to measure the environmental impact within a broader environment, social, and governance framework with a focus on microeconomic and firm-level implications. In this article, the authors assess the impact of climate change on long-term expected returns across asset classes from a top-down macroeconomic perspective. They use well-accepted climate risk scenarios to assess the potential impact of alternative climate scenarios on economic growth, inflation, and asset returns for major asset classes. Finally, they design hypothetical portfolios given these top-down assumptions and explore portfolio allocation implications.

TOPICS: ESG investing, legal/regulatory/public policy, tail risks, portfolio construction

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors assess the top-down cross-asset impact of climate change for strategic asset allocation in both optimistic and pessimistic scenarios.

\- ▪ The top-down analysis suggests that growth-oriented assets, such as equities, would be directly affected by climate change. Their return would decline in the pessimistic scenario, with significant differences across countries.

\- ▪ Using these strategic return expectations, a top-down climate risk–aware portfolio would tilt away from regions and assets that are expected to be adversely affected, particularly certain emerging markets, for better risk-adjusted returns.

Abstract:本文回顾了气候变化对经济影响的学术研究的重大进展，并探讨了预期收益和跨主要公共资产类别的战略投资组合分配的影响。为了在更广泛的环境、社会和治理框架内衡量环境影响，已经做出了许多努力，重点关注微观经济和企业层面的影响。在本文中，作者从自上而下的宏观经济角度评估了气候变化对不同资产类别长期预期收益的影响。他们使用公认的气候风险情景来评估替代气候情景对主要资产类别的经济增长、通货膨胀和资产回报的潜在影响。最后，他们根据这些自上而下的假设设计假设投资组合，并探索投资组合分配的含义。

主题：ESG投资、法律/监管/公共政策、尾部风险、投资组合构建

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者在乐观和悲观情景下评估了气候变化对战略资产配置的自上而下的交叉资产影响。

\- ▪ 自上而下的分析表明，以增长为导向的资产，如股票，将直接受到气候变化的影响。在悲观的情况下，他们的回报率将下降，各国之间存在显著差异。

\- ▪ 利用这些战略回报预期，自上而下的气候风险意识投资组合将远离预计将受到不利影响的地区和资产，特别是某些新兴市场，以获得更好的风险调整回报。

\[6\] Geopolitical Risk in Investment Research: Allies, Adversaries, and Algorithms

标题:投资研究中的地缘政治风险：盟友、对手和算法

作者:Joseph Simonian

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/9/92;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.284

Abstract : Geopolitical risk is a driver of just about every type of investment portfolio. However, in practice, most geopolitical research published by investment firms is not informed by international relations theory, giving it a less rigorous, editorial flavor. This article is an attempt to address the latter shortcoming by providing a theoretically grounded framework for analyzing geopolitical risk in an investment context. The first half of the article presents a qualitative framework for analyzing geopolitical risk. The framework uses conceptual tools from international relations theory that can be easily adapted to portfolio management. The second half of the article explores the analysis of geopolitical risk from a quantitative standpoint. The focus of this section is the application of game-theoretic, machine learning, and algorithmic techniques to the study of international relations. The last section of the article briefly addresses the topic of portfolio construction and provides a simple framework for incorporating geopolitical views into the portfolio selection process.

TOPICS: Risk management, global markets, portfolio management/multi-asset allocation, big data/machine learning

Key Finding

\- ▪ Analyses of geopolitical risk in an investment context are most effective when formulated in a clear and precisely expressed conceptual framework.

\- ▪ Considerations of geopolitical risk often act as defeaters, overriding potentially compelling market-related reasons for or against a particular investment.

\- ▪ Quantitative approaches to analyzing geopolitical risk are most useful in the analysis and simulation of the structural relationships between global actors.

Abstract:地缘政治风险是几乎所有类型投资组合的驱动因素。然而，在实践中，投资公司发表的大多数地缘政治研究都没有国际关系理论的指导，这使其不那么严谨，带有编辑色彩。本文试图通过提供一个在投资背景下分析地缘政治风险的理论基础框架来解决后一个缺陷。文章的前半部分提出了一个分析地缘政治风险的定性框架。该框架使用国际关系理论中的概念工具，这些工具可以很容易地应用于投资组合管理。文章的后半部分从定量的角度探讨了地缘政治风险的分析。本节的重点是博弈论、机器学习和算法技术在国际关系研究中的应用。文章的最后一部分简要介绍了投资组合构建的主题，并提供了一个将地缘政治观点纳入投资组合选择过程的简单框架。

主题：风险管理、全球市场、投资组合管理/多资产配置、大数据/机器学习

Key Finding

\- ▪ 在投资环境中对地缘政治风险的分析，如果是在清晰、准确的概念框架中制定的，则最为有效。

\- ▪ 对地缘政治风险的考虑往往是失败的因素，压倒了支持或反对某项投资的潜在强制性市场相关理由。

\- ▪ 分析地缘政治风险的定量方法在分析和模拟全球行为者之间的结构关系时最为有用。

\[7\] Firm-Level Cybersecurity Risk and Idiosyncratic Volatility

标题:企业层面的网络安全风险和特质波动

作者:Nazli Sila Alan, Ahmet K. Karagozoglu and Tianpeng Zhou

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/9/110;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.286

Abstract : The authors propose a measure of firm-level cybersecurity risk developed by employing pattern-based sequence-classification method from computational linguistics to determine the proportion of time devoted to issues related to cybersecurity risk during earnings conference calls. Using their measure, they investigate the effect of cybersecurity risk on firm-level return volatility; they examine both idiosyncratic volatility and implied volatility and find that firm-level cybersecurity risk is positively correlated to idiosyncratic volatility on the days on which earnings calls are held. This suggests that the discussion of issues related to cybersecurity risk during earnings calls is related to an increase in the component of the volatility that responds only to firm-specific news. That positive relationship is robust to alternative measurements of the language in earnings call discussions and to industry classifications.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, risk management, big data/machine learning

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors propose a new measure of firm-level cybersecurity risk that applies textual analysis to earnings conference call transcripts

\- they use that measure to investigate the effect of cybersecurity risk on firm-level return volatility.

\- ▪ They find that discussion of issues related to cybersecurity risk during earnings calls is associated with an increase in the component of volatility that responds only to firm-specific news.

\- ▪ The authors show that the impact of cybersecurity risk on firm-level volatility is robust to alternative measurements of the language in earnings call discussions and to different industry classifications.

Abstract:作者提出了一种企业层面网络安全风险的度量方法，该方法采用计算语言学中基于模式的序列分类方法来确定收益电话会议期间用于网络安全风险相关问题的时间比例。利用他们的测量方法，他们调查了网络安全风险对公司层面回报波动性的影响；他们对特质波动率和隐含波动率进行了研究，发现公司层面的网络安全风险与持有收益通知日的特质波动率正相关。这表明，盈利电话会议期间对网络安全风险相关问题的讨论与仅对公司特定新闻作出反应的波动性成分的增加有关。这种积极的关系对财报电话会议和行业分类中的语言替代测量具有很强的鲁棒性。

主题：安全分析和评估、风险管理、大数据/机器学习

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者提出了一种新的企业级网络安全风险度量方法，将文本分析应用于收益电话会议记录

\- 他们利用这一指标来调查网络安全风险对公司层面回报波动性的影响。

\- ▪ 他们发现，在盈利电话会议期间对网络安全风险相关问题的讨论与仅对公司特定新闻作出反应的波动性成分的增加有关。

\- ▪ 作者表明，网络安全风险对企业级波动的影响是稳健的替代测量的财报电话会议和不同行业分类。

\[8\] Investment Management Post Pandemic, Post Global Warming, Post Resource Depletion

标题:大流行后、全球变暖后、资源枯竭后的投资管理

作者:Frank J. Fabozzi, Sergio Focardi and Zenu Sharma

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/9/141;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.280

Abstract : Environmental issues including mitigating climate change, reducing pollution, and halting exhaustion of natural resources are no longer marginal cultural issues but have become parts of serious government plans with substantial funding in both the United States and Europe.
