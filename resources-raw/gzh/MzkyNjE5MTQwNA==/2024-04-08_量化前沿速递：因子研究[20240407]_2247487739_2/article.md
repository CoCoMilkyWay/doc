标题:寻找阿尔法：比肉眼所见更复杂

作者:Duo Selina Pei,Abhinav Anand,Xing Huan

来源:SSRN\_20240406

Abstract : This study investigates the information content of articles from the crowd-sourced investment advice platform Seeking Alpha (SA), focusing on its timeliness and relevance for more sophisticated investors. Textual analysis is employed to extract positive and negative sentiment from economic events in SA documents.
