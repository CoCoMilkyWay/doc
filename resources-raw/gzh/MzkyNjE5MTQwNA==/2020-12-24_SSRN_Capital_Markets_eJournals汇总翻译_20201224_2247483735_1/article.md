\[1\] The Pricing and Risk of Dividends by Maturity: Theory and Evidence from the COVID-19 Pandemic

标题:到期股利的定价与风险：来自COVID-19大流行的理论与证据

作者:Niels Joachim Gormsen(University of Chicago - Booth School of Business),Ralph S. J. Koijen(University of Chicago - Booth School of Business; Centre for Economic Policy Research (CEPR); National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER)),Ian Martin(London School of Economics & Political Science (LSE) - Department of Finance)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3753691

Abstract : A large literature tries to estimate the conditional expectation and riskiness of economic growth across horizons. The COVID-19 crisis underscores the importance of this literature as households, market participants, and policymakers try to assess the impact of the pandemic on the distribution of economic growth over the next few years.A recent literature shows the benefits of using dividend futures or index option prices for forecasting economic growth . We extend this literature by using options on index-level dividends. These options allow us to measure the implied volatility of aggregate dividends in a particular year. We construct a term structure of volatilities that characterizes how riskiness varies across dividends with different maturities. We study how this term structure developed over the COVID-19 crisis, documenting a substantial increase in the volatility of near-future dividends that has lingered even as the volatility of the overall market portfolio has started to fall.In addition to introducing this market, we also provide new theoretical results that show how these data can be used to derive lower bounds on expected dividend returns and on expected growth rates, by maturity. This provides an alternative to methods used in the literature using vector autoregressions or survey expectations

Keywords : None

Abstract :大量文献试图从不同的角度评估经济增长的条件预期和风险。COVID-19危机强调了这一文献的重要性，因为家庭、市场参与者和政策制定者试图评估这一流行病对未来几年经济增长分布的影响。最近的一篇文献显示了使用股息期货或指数期权价格预测经济增长的好处。我们通过使用指数级股息的期权来扩展这一文献。这些期权使我们能够衡量特定年份总股息的隐含波动性。我们构造了一个波动率的期限结构，它描述了不同期限的股息的风险如何变化。我们研究这一期限结构在COVID-19危机期间是如何发展的，记录了近期股息波动性的大幅增加，尽管整体市场投资组合的波动性已经开始波动掉下去，进去除了介绍这个市场之外，我们还提供了新的理论结果来说明这些数据是如何做到的以及预期收益率的下限。这为文献中使用向量自回归或调查期望的方法提供了一种替代方法

\[2\] Investable and Interpretable Machine Learning for Equities

标题:股票的可投资和可解释机器学习

作者:Yimou Li(State Street Associates),Zachary Simon(State Street Associates),David Turkington(State Street Associates)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3747203

Abstract : We propose three principles for evaluating the practical efficacy of machine learning for stock selection, and we compare the performance of various models and investment goals using this framework. The first principle is investability. To this end, we focus on portfolios formed from highly liquid US stocks, and we calibrate models to require a reasonable amount of trading. The second principle is interpretability. Investors must understand a model’s output well enough to trust it and extract some general insight from it. To this end, we choose a concise set of predictor variables, and we apply a novel method called the Model Fingerprint to reveal the linear, nonlinear, and interaction effects that drive a model’s predictions. The third principle is that a model’s predictions should be interesting, by which we mean they should convincingly outperform simpler models. To this end, we evaluate performance out-of-sample compared to linear regressions. In addition to these three principles, we also consider the important role people play by imparting domain knowledge and preferences to a model. We argue that adjusting the prediction goal is one of the most powerful ways to do this. We test random forest, boosted trees and neural network models for multiple calibrations which we conclude are investable, interpretable, and interesting.

Keywords : Machine learning, Stocks, Equity, Factors, Neural network, Boosted machine, Random forest

Abstract :我们提出了评估机器学习在股票选择中实际有效性的三个原则，并在此框架下比较了各种模型和投资目标的表现。第一个原则是可投资性。为此，我们专注于由高流动性的美国股票组成的投资组合，并校准模型，以要求合理的交易量。第二个原则是可解释性。投资者必须充分理解一个模型的输出，以信任它，并从中提取一些一般性的见解。为此，我们选择一组简洁的预测变量，并应用一种称为模型指纹的新方法来揭示驱动模型预测的线性、非线性和交互作用效应。第三个原则是，模型的预测应该是有趣的，我们的意思是它们应该令人信服地优于更简单的模型。为此，我们对样本外的性能进行了评估，并与线性回归进行了比较。除了这三个原则外，我们还考虑了人们通过向模型传授领域知识和偏好所起的重要作用。我们认为调整预测目标是实现这一目标的最有力的方法之一。我们测试随机森林，增强树和神经网络模型的多重校准，我们得出结论是可投资的，可解释的，有趣的。

Keywords :机器学习，股票，股票，因素，神经网络，提升机，随机森林

\[3\] Capital Markets and Law: Nigeria As a Case Study

标题:资本市场与法律：以尼日利亚为例

作者:David Ademola Olajide(Independent)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3744995

Abstract : The dynamism of law cannot be undermined rather it extends to new frontiers even as existence continues to unfold. In line with the aforesaid, it is no gainsaying that the continuum of law as extended into the distinctive aspects of financial markets most notably, capital markets. Undoubtedly, in the emerging capital markets Nigeria has a history and it strongly represents a growing proportion in the country’s economy. On this note, this paper seeks to review the evolution of the capital markets institution in tandem with law and regulation in Nigeria. As an objective, it further seeks to give a report on the legal and regulatory framework governing capital markets in Nigeria with attention to the inadequacies and possible solutions to improve the quality of regulations which will contribute to the growth and development of the capital markets and its legal regime. With this background, it is necessary to consider the legal regulations that oversees the Nigerian capital markets. Albeit, it is considerable to understand the nitty-gritties of capital markets in order to establish clearly its synergy with law. Sequel on these findings, this paper makes recommendations for the way forward.

Keywords : Capital market, Equity, Operators in the Market, Securities, Secondary Market, Primary Markets

Abstract :法律的活力不能被削弱，而是延伸到新的领域，即使存在继续展开。与上述一致，不可否认的是，法律的连续性延伸到了金融市场的独特方面，尤其是资本市场。毫无疑问，在新兴资本市场中，尼日利亚有着悠久的历史，它在该国经济中所占的比例日益增长。在此基础上，本文试图回顾尼日利亚资本市场制度与法律法规的演变。作为一项目标，本报告还试图就尼日利亚资本市场的法律和监管框架提出一份报告，并注意其中的不足之处和可能的解决办法，以提高监管质量，这将有助于资本市场及其法律制度的增长和发展。在这种背景下，有必要考虑监管尼日利亚资本市场的法律法规。尽管如此，了解资本市场的本质，以明确其与法律的协同作用是相当重要的。根据这些发现，本文提出了今后的建议。

Keywords :资本市场、股权、经营者市场、证券、二级市场、一级市场

\[4\] Does Sustainable Investing Deprive Unsustainable Firms from Fresh Capital?

标题:可持续的投资会让不可持续的公司失去新的资本吗？

作者:David Blitz(Robeco Quantitative Investments),Laurens Swinkels(Erasmus University Rotterdam (EUR); Robeco Quantitative Investments),Jan Anton van Zanten(Erasmus University Rotterdam (EUR) - Rotterdam School of Management (RSM); Robeco Asset Management)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3728025

Abstract : This paper examines the sustainability characteristics of listed firms that raise fresh capital by issuing stocks or bonds. Issuance, i.e. the primary market, should be of paramount importance to sustainable investors since this is where the demand for and supply of capital meet, contrary to the secondary market where ownership of existing stocks and bonds is merely exchanged between investors. We find no evidence that fresh capital is flowing more towards sustainable than to unsustainable firms. The sustainability profile of equity issuers is generally similar to the broad market, while debt issuers even tend to have a below-average sustainability profile. Thus, unsustainable firms appear to have no problems in obtaining funding in public markets. Our results suggest that the current level of sustainable investing does not deprive unsustainable firms from fresh capital, although it cannot be disproved that sustainable investing may have prevented such firms from raising even more capital. It remains an open question whether a more widespread adoption of sustainable investing would have a more noticeable impact on capital flows.

Keywords : Sustainable investing, Socially Responsible Investing (SRI), Environmental Social and Governance (ESG), Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), Exclusion, Divestment; Issuance; Capital flows

Abstract :本文考察了通过发行股票或债券筹集新资本的上市公司的可持续性特征。发行，即一级市场，对可持续的投资者来说应该是最重要的，因为这是资本需求和供应的地方，而不是二级市场，即现有股票和债券的所有权只在投资者之间交换。我们没有发现任何证据表明，新资本流向可持续发展的公司比流向不可持续发展的公司要多。股票发行人的可持续发展概况通常与大市场相似，而债务发行人的可持续性状况甚至往往低于平均水平。因此，不可持续的公司在公开市场获得资金方面似乎没有问题。我们的结果表明，目前的可持续投资水平并没有剥夺不可持续的企业从新的资本，虽然它不能否认，可持续投资可能阻止了这些企业筹集更多的资本。更广泛地采用可持续投资是否会对资本流动产生更显著的影响，仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。

Keywords :可持续投资、社会责任投资（SRI）、环境社会和治理（ESG）、可持续发展目标（SDG）、排斥、撤资；发行；资本流动
