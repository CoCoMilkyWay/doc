\[1\] What Matters in a Characteristic?

标题:特征中什么是重要的？

作者:Hugues Langlois

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3848587

Abstract : We investigate how expected returns and comovements in international stock markets depend on different components of firm characteristics. We use cross-sectional regressions to decompose each characteristic into country, industry, and adjusted components. We then use these characteristic components to capture time-varying alphas and factor exposures in an asset pricing model with latent factors. We show that systematically decomposing characteristics into components is crucial to model jointly expected returns and comovements; country and adjusted components capture systematic risk exposures and alphas, respectively, and increase model fit compared to benchmark models. Contrary to the U.S. market, alphas in other developed and emerging markets are significant. However, trading strategies using predicted alphas do not generate significant out-of-sample, risk-adjusted, and net of transaction costs returns, indicating that estimated alphas are related to limits-to-arbitrage.

Keywords : IPCA, characteristics, country, industry, alpha, systematic risk.

Abstract :我们研究了国际股票市场的预期收益和协动如何依赖于企业特征的不同组成部分。我们使用横截面回归将每个特征分解为国家、行业和调整后的组成部分。然后，我们使用这些特征成分来捕捉带有潜在因素的资产定价模型中随时间变化的α和因子敞口。我们表明，系统地将特征分解为成分是建立联合预期收益和协动模型的关键；国家和调整后的组成部分分别捕获系统风险敞口和alpha，并与基准模型相比增加模型拟合度。与美国市场相反，其他发达国家和新兴市场的alpha市场意义重大。然而，使用预测alpha的交易策略不会产生显著的样本外、风险调整和交易成本净回报，这表明估计alpha与套利限制有关。

Keywords :IPCA、特征、国家、行业、阿尔法、系统风险。

\[2\] Option Pricing using High-frequency Futures Prices

标题:基于高频期货价格的期权定价

作者:Stavros Degiannakis,Christos Floros,Thomas Poufinas,George Filis,Konstantinos Gkillas

出处:Journal of Risk, Vol. 23, No. 4

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3850088

Abstract : Option pricing depends heavily on the volatility measure used. We examine two potential routes to improve the outcome of option pricing: extracting the variance from futures prices instead of the underlying asset prices, and calculating the variance in different frequencies with intraday data instead of daily closing prices. We perform a valuation of call and put options for six volatility measures, namely unconditional volatility, historical volatility, conditional volatility, realized volatility and implied volatility, to examine which one gives the optimal result versus the (market) price. We show that the implied volatility exhibits the lowest deviation from the market price, as judged by the mean and the standard deviation of the relative difference of the option value from the market price (as calculated using each of the volatility measures). Also, using econometric models, we find the determinants of the deviation of the calculated option value from the actual market price. We show that the time to expiration, the spread and the moneyness, as well as their squares, are all statistically significant at all levels for all volatility measures. Moreover, the time-to-expiration coefficient implies that all volatility measures yield option values closer to the observed market price for shorter times to expiration.

Keywords : high-frequency data; volatility measures; futures prices; option pricing; moneyness; Standard & Poor’s 500 (S&P 500); time to expiration.

Abstract :期权定价在很大程度上取决于所使用的波动率度量。我们研究了两种改进期权定价结果的可能途径：从期货价格中提取方差而不是从标的资产价格中提取方差，以及用日内数据而不是日收盘价计算不同频率的方差。我们对六个波动率指标，即无条件波动率、历史波动率、条件波动率、已实现波动率和隐含波动率进行了看涨期权和看跌期权的估值，以检验哪一个指标相对于（市场）价格给出了最优结果。我们表明，隐含波动率表现出最低的偏离市场价格，作为判断的平均值和标准偏差的相对差异的期权价值从市场价格（作为计算使用每一个波动性措施）。同时，利用计量经济学模型，我们发现了计算出的期权价值偏离实际市场价格的决定因素。我们表明，到期时间、价差和货币性，以及它们的平方，在所有波动性度量的所有水平上都具有统计显著性。此外，到期时间系数意味着，所有波动率指标在较短的到期时间内产生的期权价值更接近观察到的市场价格。

Keywords :高频数据；波动性度量；期货价格；期权定价；金钱；标准普尔500指数；过期时间。

\[3\] The Value of Marriage to Family Firms

标题:婚姻对家族企业的价值

作者:Yupana Wiwattanakantang,Pramuan Bunkanwanicha,Joseph P. H. Fan

出处:Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis, 2013, 48 (2), 611-636

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3849189

Abstract : This paper presents the first empirical evidence showing that the marriage of a member of the controlling family adds value to public corporations. The results, based on a uniquely comprehensive data set from Thailand, show that the family firm’s stock price increases when the partner is from either a prominent business or a political family. Abnormal returns tend to be higher for firms whose operation depends on extensive networks. In contrast, marriages to ordinary citizens are not associated with any abnormal returns. These findings are generally supportive of the value of networks in general and marriage in particular.

Keywords : Family firm, Succession, Corporate Governance, M&A, Thailand

Abstract :本文提出的第一个实证证据表明，婚姻的一个成员的控制家庭增加了价值的上市公司。研究结果基于泰国独特的综合数据集，显示当合伙人来自显赫企业或政治家族时，家族企业的股价会上涨。对于那些依靠广泛网络运作的公司来说，超额收益往往更高。与此相反，与普通公民结婚并不意味着任何异常回报。这些发现大体上支持了网络的价值，尤其是婚姻的价值。

Keywords :家族企业，继承，公司治理，并购，泰国

\[4\] Investing in Crises

标题:在危机中投资

作者:Matthew Baron,Luc Laeven,Julien Pénasse,Yevhenii Usenko

出处:ECB Working Paper No. 2021/2548

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3845170

Abstract : We investigate asset returns around banking crises in 44 advanced and emerging economies from 1960 to 2018. In contrast to the view that buying assets during banking crises is a profitable long-run strategy, we find returns of equity and other asset classes generally underperform after banking crises. While prices are depressed during crises and partially recover after acute stress ends, consistent with theories of fire sales and intermediary-based asset pricing, we argue that investors do not fully anticipate the consequences of debt overhang, which result in lower long-run dividends. Our results on bank stock underperformance suggest that government-funded bank recapitalizations can often lead to substantial taxpayer losses.

Keywords : None

Abstract :我们调查了从1960年到2018年44个发达经济体和新兴经济体在银行业危机前后的资产回报率。与在银行业危机期间购买资产是一种有利可图的长期战略的观点不同，我们发现在银行业危机后，股票和其他资产类别的回报率普遍表现不佳。虽然在危机期间，价格受到抑制，在严重压力结束后部分恢复，这与火售和基于中介机构的资产定价理论一致，但我们认为，投资者没有充分预期债务超支的后果，从而导致长期股息较低。我们对银行股表现不佳的研究结果表明，政府资助的银行资本重组往往会导致纳税人蒙受巨大损失。

Keywords :None

\[5\] Does Volatility Harvesting Really Work?

标题:收获真的有用吗？

作者:Magnus Pedersen

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3847692

Abstract : A large number of academic papers claim that there is a consistent advantage to rebalancing an investment portfolio relative to a simple Buy&Hold strategy, while other academic papers claim the opposite. The papers on both sides are typically based on unclear mathematical arguments with only few real-world examples. This paper does not try to give a mathematical explanation but is entirely empirical. We use daily stock-returns for more than 900 U.S. stocks between the years 2007 and 2021, where we generate thousands of random portfolios of different sizes and start/end-dates, to compare the Rebalanced and Buy&Hold strategies on different performance metrics. Although the Rebalanced portfolios may seem to perform better than the Buy&Hold portfolios in terms of e.g. the arithmetic mean daily return, it actually depends on which period we consider. We also make preliminary experiments with intraday data, where it does appear that Rebalancing is consistently better than Buy&Hold, although these data-sets are so small that we cannot conclude this advantage holds in general.

Keywords : portfolio rebalancing, volatility harvesting

Abstract :大量学术论文认为，相对于简单的买入&持有策略，重新平衡投资组合具有一致的优势，而其他学术论文则持相反的观点。双方的论文通常都是基于不明确的数学论证，只有很少的现实例子。本文不试图给出一个数学解释，但完全是经验的。我们使用2007年至2021年间900多只美国股票的每日股票回报率，生成数千个不同规模和起止日期的随机投资组合，以比较不同业绩指标的再平衡和买入持有策略。虽然重新平衡的投资组合似乎比买入和持有投资组合表现好，例如算术平均日收益率，但实际上取决于我们考虑的是哪个时期。我们还对日内数据进行了初步实验，结果显示，再平衡始终优于买入&持有，尽管这些数据集非常小，我们无法得出结论，这种优势总体上是成立的。

Keywords :投资组合再平衡，波动性收获

\[6\] Do Chinese Investors Underact to Goodwill

标题:中国投资者对商誉的行为有失公允吗

作者:Yongqing Li

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3847088

Abstract : This paper mainly studies goodwill as an intangible asset, and considers that goodwill and sales contain information about enterprise value. The results show that goodwill can predict the future return rate of China's stock market, indicating that investors in China’s stock market underact to information contained in goodwill. In addition, this paper also finds that the organizational structure of the company not only affects the merger behavior of the company, but also affects the predictability of the company's future stock returns. Specifically, in China, goodwill can only predict the future stock returns of non-state-owned enterprises, but not the future stock returns of state-owned enterprises. The difference is not due to the size of the company.

Keywords : Goodwill, Cross-sectional Stock Return Predictability, China’s Market, State-owned Enterprises

Abstract :本文将商誉作为一种无形资产进行研究，认为商誉和销售额都包含企业价值信息。研究结果表明，商誉能够预测中国股票市场的未来收益率，表明中国股票市场的投资者对商誉所包含的信息反应不足。此外，本文还发现公司的组织结构不仅影响公司的并购行为，而且影响公司未来股票收益的可预测性。具体而言，在我国，商誉只能预测非国有企业未来的股票收益，而不能预测国有企业未来的股票收益。这种差异并不是因为公司的规模。

Keywords :商誉，横截面股票收益可预测性，中国市场，国有企业

\[7\] Interest Rates Forecasting: Between Hull and White and the CIR#. How to Make a Single Factor Model Work

标题:利率预测：介于赫尔和怀特以及CIR之间。如何使单因素模型发挥作用

作者:Giuseppe Orlando,Michele Bufalo

出处:Orlando, G, Bufalo, M. Interest rates forecasting: between Hull and White and the CIR#. How to make a single factor model work. Journal of Forecasting. 2021. https://doi.org/10.1002/for.2783

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3847157

Abstract : In this work we present our findings of the so‐called CIR#, which is a modified version of the Cox, Ingersoll & Ross (CIR) model, turned into a forecasting tool for any term structure. The main feature of the CIR# model is its ability to cope with negative interest rates, cluster volatility and jumps. By considering a dataset composed of money market interest rates during turmoil and calmer periods, we show how the CIR# performs in terms of directionality of rates and forecasting error. Comparison is carried out with a revamped version of the CIR model (denoted CIRadj), the Hull and White model and the EWMA which is often adopted whenever no structure in data is assumed. Testing and validation is performed on both historical and had hoc data with different metrics and clustering criteria to confirm the analysis.

Keywords : Interest rate forecasting, Hull and White model, CIR model, ARIMA, cluster volatility and jumps fitting

Abstract :在这项工作中，我们提出了所谓的CIR的发现，它是Cox，Ingersoll&Ross（CIR）模型的一个修正版本，变成了任何期限结构的预测工具。CIR模型的主要特点是能够应对负利率、集群波动和跳跃。通过考虑一个由动荡和平静时期货币市场利率组成的数据集，我们展示了CIR在利率方向性和预测误差方面的表现。与改进版的CIR模型（表示为CIRadj）、Hull-and-White模型和EWMA模型进行了比较，当假设数据中没有结构时，通常采用EWMA模型。对具有不同度量和聚类标准的历史数据和had-hoc数据进行测试和验证，以确认分析。

Keywords :利率预测、Hull-and-White模型、CIR模型、ARIMA、集群波动率和跳跃拟合

\[8\] ESG, Risk, and (Tail) Dependence

标题:ESG、风险和（尾部）依赖

作者:Karoline Bax,Özge Sahin,Claudia Czado,Sandra Paterlini

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3846739

Abstract : While environmental, social, and governance (ESG) trading activity has been a distinctive feature of financial markets, the debate if ESG scores can also convey information regarding a company's riskiness remains open. Regulatory authorities, such as the European Banking Authority (EBA), have acknowledged that ESG factors can contribute to risk. Therefore, it is important to model such risks and quantify what part of a company's riskiness can be attributed to the ESG ratings. This paper aims to question whether ESG scores can be used to provide information on (tail) riskiness. By analyzing the (tail) dependence structure of companies with a range of ESG scores, using high-dimensional vine copula modelling, we are able to show that risk can also depend on and be directly associated with a specific ESG rating class. Empirical findings on real-world data show positive not negligible dependencies between clusters determined by ESG scores, especially during the 2008 crisis.

Keywords : ESG scores, Risk, Dependence, Tail dependence, Vine Copula models

Abstract :虽然环境、社会和治理（ESG）交易活动一直是金融市场的一个显著特征，但ESG得分是否也能传达有关公司风险的信息，这一争论仍然悬而未决。欧洲银行管理局（EBA）等监管机构已经承认，ESG因素可能会导致风险。因此，对此类风险进行建模并量化ESG评级对公司风险的贡献是非常重要的。本文旨在探讨ESG评分是否可以用来提供尾部风险的信息。通过使用高维vine-copula模型分析具有一系列ESG评分的公司的（尾部）依赖结构，我们能够证明风险也可以依赖于特定的ESG评级类别，并与之直接相关。对真实世界数据的实证研究结果表明，由ESG得分确定的集群之间存在着不可忽视的正相关性，特别是在2008年危机期间。

Keywords :ESG评分、风险、依赖性、尾部依赖性、Vine Copula模型

\[9\] Investment in Human Capital and External Reporting Quality

标题:人力资本投资与外部报告质量

作者:Ruby Lee,Gwen Yu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3846394

Abstract : Ensuring that firms devote sufficient resources to the reporting process is important for quality reporting. To explore the effects of resources invested in the reporting function, we use a regulatory intervention in South Korea that led to an increase in human capital invested in the reporting process. The new regulation required firms to file internally prepared, pre-audited financial statements before the start of the year-end field audit process. Using the staggered adoption of this regulation, we confirm that the regulation leads to an increase in internal accounting employees at firms, and that the increase in rank-and-file accounting employees is more pronounced than the increase in top executives. Using difference-in-differences tests, we find improvement in external reporting quality (i.e., fewer restatements) for treatment firms relative to similar-size control firms that were not subject to the regulation. The effects are greater when an increase in accounting employees is accompanied by an increase in external audit effort, measured using audit hours. Treatment firms also show a drop in their hiring of non-accounting employees suggesting reallocation of labor inside the firm. The findings suggest that regulatory efforts to increase investment in internal accounting employees lead to improved reporting quality but can reduce labor forces in other non-accounting functions.

Keywords : Human Capital; Financial Reporting Quality; Auditing

Abstract :确保公司在报告过程中投入足够的资源对于高质量的报告非常重要。为了探讨在报告功能中投入的资源的影响，我们在韩国采用了一种监管干预措施，导致在报告过程中投入的人力资本增加。新规定要求各公司在年终实地审计程序开始前，提交内部编制、预审计的财务报表。通过交错采用这一规定，我们确认，这一规定导致了公司内部会计人员的增加，普通会计人员的增加比高管人员的增加更为明显。利用差异中的差异检验，我们发现相对于不受监管的类似规模控制公司，治疗公司的外部报告质量（即更少的重述）有所提高。当会计从业人员的增加伴随着外部审计工作的增加时，这种影响会更大，用审计时间来衡量。待遇公司雇佣非会计员工的数量也有所下降，这表明公司内部劳动力的重新分配。研究结果表明，监管部门加大对内部会计人员的投资，可以提高报告质量，但可以减少其他非会计职能部门的劳动力。

Keywords :人力资本；财务报告质量；审计
