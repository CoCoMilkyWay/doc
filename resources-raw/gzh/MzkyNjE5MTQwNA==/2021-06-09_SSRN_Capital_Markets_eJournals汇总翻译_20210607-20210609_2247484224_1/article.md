\[1\] Price Informativeness with Equity Market Factors

标题:股票市场因素下的价格信息性

作者:Roger G Clarke,Harindra de Silva,Steven Thorley

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3861918

Abstract : Price informativeness measures how and when information is aggregated into asset prices. We study the price informativeness of realized earnings growth for U.S. stocks with a focus on exposures to factors that have historically outperformed the market index. Our study includes the largest one thousand stocks from 1975 to 2019 and approximately 180 thousand individual corporate net income observations aligned by report date. Capitalization-weighted cross-sectional regressions of monthly stock returns on past, concurrent, and future reports of earnings growth produce coefficients that are averaged over time. Stock returns are sensitive to concurrent and realized earnings growth reports up to 15 months into the future, but not to old earnings reports. The decomposition of the Value, Momentum, Small Size, Low Beta, and Profitability factor active returns into components that are explained and unexplained by earnings helps in understanding the anomalous nature of their positive market-relative performance.

Keywords : Price Informativeness, Factor Investing, Earnings Growth

Abstract :价格信息性衡量信息如何以及何时聚合为资产价格。我们研究美国股票已实现收益增长的价格信息性，重点关注历史上表现优于市场指数的因素。我们的研究包括1975年至2019年最大的1000只股票，以及按报告日期排列的约18万个个人企业净收入观察值。资本化加权横截面回归的月度股票收益率在过去，同时，和未来的报告收益增长产生的系数是平均的时间。股票回报率对未来15个月内的同期和已实现盈利增长报告敏感，但对旧盈利报告不敏感。将价值、动量、小规模、低贝塔系数和盈利因素的积极回报分解为由收益解释和解释的组成部分，有助于理解其正市场相对表现的反常性质。

Keywords :价格信息性、要素投资、收益增长

\[2\] Do You Feel Lucky? Lottery Jackpot Winnings and Retail Trading around Neighborhoods

标题:你觉得幸运吗？彩票中奖和社区零售交易

作者:Dien Giau Bui,Yu-Ju Chan,Chih-Yung Lin,Tse-Chun Lin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3860620

Abstract : Using lottery jackpot winnings within neighborhoods as exogenous shocks, we examine whether feeling lucky affects retail stock purchases. We find that retail investors who trade through brokerage branches located close to stores that sell jackpot-winning tickets buy more stock shares than their counterparts. More importantly, these retail buys tend to lose money. We also find that these retail investors tend to purchase lottery-like stocks, linking the luck-driven trading motive to the traded targets under the common theme of gambling. We substantiate our results via several sets of falsification tests and robustness checks. Overall, feeling lucky induces retail investors to trade aggressively and leads to welfare losses.

Keywords : Lottery winnings, feeling lucky, trading turnover rate, investment performance, retail investors

Abstract :以社区内的彩票头奖奖金作为外生冲击，我们检验了幸运感是否影响零售股票购买。我们发现，散户投资者通过靠近出售头奖彩票的商店的经纪机构进行交易，他们购买的股票比他们的同行多。更重要的是，这些零售购买往往会赔钱。我们还发现，这些散户倾向于购买彩票类股票，将运气驱动的交易动机与赌博这一共同主题下的交易对象联系起来。我们通过几组伪造测试和稳健性检查来证实我们的结果。总的来说，感到幸运会促使散户投资者积极交易，并导致福利损失。

Keywords :彩票中奖，幸运感，交易周转率，投资表现，散户

\[3\] Trade Policy Uncertainty and Global Stock Returns: Evidence from the 2016 US Presidential Election

标题:贸易政策不确定性与全球股市收益：来自2016年美国总统大选的证据

作者:Dien Giau Bui,Iftekhar Hasan,Chih-Yung Lin,Chris Vaike

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3860623

Abstract : Pástor and Veronesi (2012) develop a general equilibrium model to examine the relation between policy uncertainty and asset prices. Extending to their study, we develop a novel measure of firms’ uncertainty about the change in bilateral trade flows between each country and the US. We investigate the effect of trade policy uncertainty on the stock returns of firms in 52 countries around the 2016 US presidential election. Our findings show that firms with greater uncertainty about trade policy experience more negative stock returns during the election. Our results further show that this effect is more prominent for riskier firms or firms with more firm-specific information in their stock prices. Additionally, this effect becomes stronger in countries with closer social, economic, and political integration with the US or with stronger investor protection.

Keywords : Trade policy uncertainty, stock return, bilateral trade flow, information asymmetry, and globalization

Abstract :Pástor和Veronesi（2012）发展了一个一般均衡模型来检验政策不确定性与资产价格之间的关系。扩展到他们的研究，我们开发了一种新的衡量企业对中美双边贸易流量变化的不确定性的方法。我们研究了2016年美国总统大选前后52个国家贸易政策不确定性对公司股票收益的影响。我们的研究结果显示，在选举期间，贸易政策不确定性较大的公司会经历更多的负股票收益。我们的研究结果进一步显示，对于风险较高的公司或股价中含有更多公司特定信息的公司，这种效应更为显著。此外，在与美国社会、经济和政治一体化程度更高或投资者保护力度更大的国家，这种影响会更大。

Keywords :贸易政策不确定性、股票收益率、双边贸易流动、信息不对称与全球化

\[4\] Transition versus physical climate risk pricing in euro area financial markets: A text-based approach

标题:欧元区金融市场过渡期与实际气候风险定价：基于文本的方法

作者:Giovanna Bua,Daniel Kapp,Federico Ramella,Lavinia Rognone

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3860234

Abstract : This study analyses the pricing of climate risk in equity markets. To this end, we first collect authoritative and scientific texts on the topic of physical and transition risk and build two novel vocabularies. Following, we apply the cosine-similarity approach suggested by Engle et al. 2020 to compare both vocabularies with a corpus of European daily news and construct two novel physical and transition climate risk indices covering the period 2005-2021. Finally, these time series are integrated into an asset pricing model to test the sensitivity of daily equity returns to climate shocks, controlling for several climate exposure metrics. Our results suggest that news on physical risk and transition risk carry relevant information which is reflected in asset prices. Firms with poor environmental and Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) performances, as well as firms with high Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions underperform when transition risk rises. Analogously, excess returns of firms with low environmental and ESG scores decline in the event of physical risk news. While investors appear to penalise high climate risk exposure, no evidence of outperformance of less exposed firms can be found, suggesting negative screening as a predominant investment strategy.

Keywords : Climate risk, Transition risk, Physical risk, Pricing of climate risk, Textual analysis

Abstract :本文分析了股票市场中气候风险的定价问题。为此，我们首先收集了有关物理和过渡风险的权威和科学文本，并构建了两个新颖的词汇表。接下来，我们应用Engle et al.2020提出的余弦相似方法，将这两个词汇与欧洲每日新闻语料库进行比较，并构建了两个涵盖2005-2021年期间的新的物理和过渡气候风险指数。最后，这些时间序列被整合到一个资产定价模型中，以测试每日股票收益对气候冲击的敏感性，并控制几个气候暴露指标。我们的研究结果表明，关于实物风险和过渡风险的新闻携带了相关的信息，这些信息反映在资产价格中。当过渡风险上升时，环境和环境、社会和治理（ESG）表现不佳的公司以及温室气体（GHG）排放量高的公司表现不佳。类似地，当出现物理风险消息时，环境和ESG得分较低的公司的超额收益也会下降。虽然投资者似乎在惩罚高气候风险敞口，但没有证据表明风险敞口较小的公司表现优异，这表明负面筛选是一种主要的投资策略。

Keywords :气候风险、过渡风险、物理风险、气候风险定价、文本分析

\[6\] The Effect of Credit, Liquidity and Rollover Risk on Bondholder Wealth in Mergers and Acquisitions

标题:信用、流动性和展期风险对并购中债券持有人财富的影响

作者:Rainer Jankowitsch,Florian Pauer

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3859921

Abstract : We analyze M&A announcements and focus on the potential impact of these deals on bond prices in the US corporate bond market. In particular, we investigate the effect of changes in credit, liquidity and rollover risk. This is important, as especially target firms are often small with rather illiquid bonds and show maturity concentrations. M&A transactions can significantly change the debt maturity structure and liquidity risk of these bonds. We find the size of the average announcement return of target bonds is 40 bp and increases by around 50% for target firms with a low debt dispersion and illiquid bonds. Furthermore, we document a permanent increase of 15% in the liquidity of these bonds. We find only small negative returns for acquirer bonds, which can be explained by the difference in size. Overall, we provide important new insights concerning bond price effects around M&A announcements.

Keywords : bond price, acquisition, merger, event study, co-insurance, rollover risk

Abstract :我们分析并购公告，并关注这些交易对美国公司债券市场债券价格的潜在影响。特别是，我们调查的影响，在信贷，流动性和展期风险的变化。这一点很重要，尤其是目标公司通常规模较小，债券流动性较差，且到期日集中。并购交易可以显著改变这些债券的债务期限结构和流动性风险。我们发现，目标公司债券的平均公告收益率为40bp，对于债务分散度较低且债券流动性较差的目标公司，其平均公告收益率约为50%。此外，我们还记录了这些债券的流动性永久性增长15%。我们发现收购方债券只有很小的负收益，这可以用规模的差异来解释。总体而言，我们提供了有关并购公告前后债券价格效应的重要新见解。

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3858730

Abstract : Since the early beginning of investing as it was commonly seen as a form of gambling for the rich and wealthy, the idea of Harry Markowitz was revolutionising the way of thinking and how portfolios should be constructed. However, today the traditional mean-variance portfolios are still not fully adopted by practitioners. After the financial crisis of 2008, a type of portfolio called risk parity arise and attracted the attention of numerous investors. In this paper, a risk parity portfolio named equal risk contribution portfolio is constructed based on a rolling window of 300 days. Keywords :风险平价，等风险贡献，资产配置，投资组合构建，期货，COVID-19

出处:-

Abstract : We propose a loglinear present-value identity in which investment ("scale"), profitability ("yield"), and discount rates determine a firm’s market-to-book ratio. \[10\] Does CSR Engender Trust? Evidence From Investor Reactions to Corporate Disclosures

标题:企业社会责任产生信任吗？投资者对公司披露的反应证据

作者:Jonathan Berkovitch,Doron Israeli,Atanu Rakshit,Suhas A. Sridharan

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3858135

Abstract : We investigate whether a firm’s corporate social responsibility (CSR) activities engender investor trust. Motivated by the observation that investor trust facilitates greater informational efficiency of security prices, we address our question by examining the relation between CSR and three dimensions of stock price discovery: (1) the speed with which stock prices reflect earnings news, (2) the level of investor uncertainty before and during earnings announcements, and (3) earnings response coefficients. We find robust evidence in support of the hypothesis that CSR enhances investor trust in firms. Specifically, we find that firms with higher levels of CSR enjoy greater intraperiod timeliness and efficiency of reported earnings, lower investor uncertainty before and during earnings announcements, and higher earnings response coefficients. Using regression discontinuity design, we identify a causal effect of the passage of CSR-related initiatives on the speed with which stock prices reflect earnings news. Our inferences are robust to controls for characteristics of reported earnings, firm information environment, and alternative measures of CSR. Our study provides direct evidence that CSR affects stock price informativeness and sheds light on a desirable capital market consequence of CSR.

Keywords : Corporate social responsibility (CSR), investor trust, price discovery, informational efficiency, corporate disclosures

Abstract :我们调查企业的企业社会责任（CSR）活动是否会产生投资者信任。基于投资者信任有助于提高证券价格的信息效率这一观察结果，我们通过研究企业社会责任与股价发现的三个维度之间的关系来解决我们的问题：（1）股价反映收益消息的速度，（2）盈余公告前及期间投资者的不确定性程度，以及（3）盈余反应系数。我们发现强有力的证据支持企业社会责任增强投资者对企业信任的假设。具体而言，我们发现，企业社会责任水平越高的企业，其报告盈余的内期及时性和有效性越高，在盈余公告之前和期间投资者的不确定性越低，盈余反应系数越高。利用回归不连续性设计，我们确定了企业社会责任相关举措的通过对股价反映盈利新闻速度的因果影响。我们的推论对报告盈利特征、企业信息环境和企业社会责任的替代性衡量标准的控制是稳健的。我们的研究提供了企业社会责任影响股价信息性的直接证据，并揭示了企业社会责任对资本市场的一个理想后果。

Keywords :企业社会责任、投资者信任、价格发现、信息效率、企业披露
