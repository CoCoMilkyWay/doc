\[4\] Dual theory of choice with multivariate risks

标题 : 多元风险下的对偶选择理论

作者 : Alfred Galichon, Marc Henry , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.02578

说明 : Comments: 22 pages

Abstract : We propose a multivariate extension of Yaari s dual theory of choice under risk. We show that a decision maker with a preference relation on multidimensional prospects that preserves first order stochastic dominance and satisfies comonotonic independence behaves as if evaluating prospects using a weighted sum of quantiles. Both the notions of quantiles and of comonotonicity are extended to the multivariate framework using optimal transportation maps. Finally, risk averse decision makers are characterized within this framework and their local utility functions are derived. Applications to the measurement of multi attribute inequality are also discussed.

摘要 : 我们提出了风险下Yaari对偶选择理论的多元推广。我们证明了在多维前景上具有偏好关系且保持一阶随机优势且满足共单调独立性的决策者，其行为就如同使用加权分位数和来评估前景一样。利用最优运输图，将分位数和共单调性的概念推广到多元框架。最后，在此框架下刻画了风险规避决策者的特征，并导出了其局部效用函数。讨论了在多属性不等式度量中的应用。

\[5\] The housing problem and revealed preference theory

标题 : 住房问题与显性偏好理论

作者 : Ivar Ekeland, Alfred Galichon , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.02593

说明 : Comments: 19 pages

Abstract : This paper exhibits a duality between the theory of Revealed Preference of Afriat and the housing allocation problem of Shapley and Scarf. In particular, it is shown that Afriat s theorem can be interpreted as a second welfare theorem in the housing problem. Using this duality, the revealed preference problem is connected to an optimal assignment problem, and a geometrical characterization of the rationalizability of experiment data is given. This allows in turn to give new indices of rationalizability of the data, and to define weaker notions of rationalizability, in the spirit of Afriat s efficiency index.

摘要 : 本文展示了一个二元的理论之间的揭示偏好理论和住房分配问题的夏普利和围巾。特别地，证明了Afriat定理可以解释为住房问题中的第二福利定理。利用这种对偶性，将显示偏好问题与最优分配问题联系起来，给出了实验数据合理化的几何刻画。这反过来又允许给出数据合理化的新指数，并根据Afriat的效率指数定义合理化的较弱概念。

\[6\] Insurance Business and Sustainable Development

标题 : 保险业与可持续发展

作者 : Dietmar Pfeifer, Vivien Langen , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.02612

说明 : Comments: 10 pages

Abstract : In this study, we will discuss recent developments in risk management of the global financial and insurance business with respect to sustainable development. So far climate change aspects have been the dominant aspect in managing sustainability risks and opportunities, accompanied by the development of several legislative initiatives triggered by supervisory authorities. However, a sole concentration on these aspects misses out other important economic and social facets of sustainable development goals formulated by the UN. Such aspects have very recently come into the focus of the European Committee concerning the Solvency II project for the European insurance industry. Clearly the new legislative expectations can be better handled by larger insurance companies and holdings than by small and medium sized mutual insurance companies which are numerous in central Europe, due to their historic development starting in the late medieval ages and early modern times. We therefore also concentrate on strategies within the risk management of such small and medium sized enterprises that can be achieved without much effort, in particular those that are not directly related to climate change.

摘要 : 在这项研究中，我们将讨论最近的发展，在风险管理方面的全球金融和保险业务的可持续发展。到目前为止，气候变化方面一直是管理可持续性风险和机遇的主要方面，伴随着监管当局发起的若干立法倡议的发展。然而，仅仅关注这些方面，就忽略了联合国制定的可持续发展目标的其他重要经济和社会方面。这些方面最近成为欧洲委员会关于欧洲保险业偿付能力II项目的重点。显然，与中欧众多的中小型互助保险公司相比，大型保险公司和控股公司能够更好地处理新的立法期望，因为它们在中世纪晚期和现代早期就开始了历史性的发展。因此，我们还将重点放在这些中小企业的风险管理战略上，这些战略不需要付出太多努力就可以实现，特别是那些与气候变化没有直接关系的战略。

\[7\] On the stability of the martingale optimal transport problem

标题 : 鞅最优运输问题的稳定性

作者 : Ariel Neufeld, Julian Sester , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.02718

说明 : -

Abstract : Continuity of the value of the martingale optimal transport problem on the real line w.r.t. its marginals was recently established in Backhoff Veraguas and Pammer 2 and Wiesel 19 . We present a new perspective of this result using the theory of set valued maps. In particular, using results from Beiglb ock, Jourdain, Margheriti, and Pammer 4 , we show that the set of martingale measures with fixed marginals is continuous, i.e., lower and upper hemicontinuous, w.r.t. its marginals. Moreover, we establish compactness of the set of optimizers as well as upper hemicontinuity of the optimizers w.r.t. the marginals.

摘要 : 实线w.r.t.上鞅最优运输问题值的连续性最近在Backhoff-Veraguas，Pammer 2和Wiesel 19中建立了它的边值。我们利用集值映射理论给出了这个结果的一个新的观点。特别地，利用Beiglb-ock，Jourdain，Margheriti和Pammer 4的结果，我们证明了具有固定边缘的鞅测度集是连续的，即下半连续和上半连续，w.r.t.它的边缘。此外，我们建立了优化器集的紧性以及优化器w.r.t.的上半连续性。
