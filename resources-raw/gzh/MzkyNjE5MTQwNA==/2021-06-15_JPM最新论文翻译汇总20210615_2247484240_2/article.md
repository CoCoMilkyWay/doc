\[1\] Global Risk Premiums on Direct Office Real Estate Returns

标题:直接办公房地产回报的全球风险溢价

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Real Estate 2021, jpm.2021.1.266

作者:Ivo de Wit and Christopher Mayer

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/06/15/jpm.2021.1.266;

Abstract : This article empirically examines the magnitude of risk premiums for direct real estate investments on a global basis. As this article analyzes ex-ante risk premiums over more than 25 years consistently across the world, it enhances current knowledge about the regional differences between risk premiums and helps long-term investors with their global portfolio allocation over time. On a global level, the authors find a risk premium of 4.1% for Gordon’s growth and 3.7% for two-stage growth model. The periodic growth model shows a slightly lower risk premium of 3.1%. As this study is analyzing a long time period, the article extends the literature by finding evidence for convergence between risk premiums across continents both over time and in cross-sectional variance. However, this is happening slowly, thereby making differences in risk premiums very important in making a global direct real estate investment allocation.

TOPICS: Real estate, analysis of individual factors/risk premia, portfolio construction, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors analyze ex-ante risk premiums over more than 25 years across the world and use three alternative valuation models to estimate ex-ante risk premiums.

\- ▪ They find that the regional risk premiums are consistent with previous research on the determinants of risk premiums, but these studies were done over shorter time periods.

\- ▪ The authors find evidence for convergence between risk premiums across continents both over time and in cross-sectional variance.

Abstract:本文实证研究了全球范围内房地产直接投资的风险溢价的大小。由于本文对全球25年以上的事前风险溢价进行了一致性分析，它增强了当前对风险溢价地区差异的了解，并帮助长期投资者在一段时间内进行全球投资组合配置。在全球范围内，作者发现Gordon增长模型的风险溢价为4.1%，两阶段增长模型的风险溢价为3.7%。周期增长模型显示风险溢价略低，为3.1%。由于这项研究分析了很长一段时间，文章扩展了文献，找到了各大洲风险溢价在时间和横截面方差上趋同的证据。然而，这种情况正在缓慢发生，因此，在全球直接房地产投资配置中，风险溢价的差异非常重要。

主题：房地产，个体因素/风险溢价分析，投资组合构建，绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者分析了全球超过25年的事前风险溢价，并使用三种不同的估值模型来估计事前风险溢价。

\- ▪ 他们发现，区域风险溢价与以往关于风险溢价决定因素的研究一致，但这些研究是在较短的时间内进行的

\- ▪ 作者发现了各大洲风险溢价在时间和横截面方差上趋同的证据。

\[2\] A Time Series Analysis and Forecast of CAPE

标题:海角的时间序列分析与预测

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management August 2021, jpm.2021.1.265

作者:Marc Fandetti

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/06/12/jpm.2021.1.265;

Abstract : Expanding stock market valuation multiples add to expected returns, and contracting multiples subtract from them. But how to forecast valuation measures, like the cyclically adjusted price earnings (CAPE) ratio? Using Robert Shiller’s CAPE, the author first shows that forecasts based on mean reversion are almost certainly wrong—CAPE appears to be nonstationary, indicating no tendency toward mean reversion. The author then provides forecasts of CAPE using time series analysis—an approach that requires no theory, and that accommodates a range of views about factors that influence valuation. The author suggests that practitioners use these time series forecasts to help inform capital market assumptions.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, fundamental equity analysis, quantitative methods, statistical methods, financial crises and financial market history

Key Finding

\- ▪ CAPE may be nonstationary and as such have no tendency to revert to its long-term or any other mean—forecasts of CAPE based on mean regression may be wrong.

\- ▪ Considering the range of outcomes for CAPE values suggested by time series models reduces the chances of a big surprise in either direction.

\- ▪ Practitioners factoring valuation changes into their capital market assumptions may wish to assume no or small changes as a base case, rather than reversion to a long-term mean value.

Abstract:不断扩大的股市估值倍数增加了预期收益，而收缩倍数则减少了预期收益。但如何预测估值指标，比如周期性调整的市盈率（CAPE）？作者首先利用罗伯特·希勒的CAPE证明，基于均值回归的预测几乎肯定是错误的CAPE似乎是非平稳的，表明没有均值回归的趋势。然后，作者使用时间序列分析方法对CAPE进行预测，这种方法不需要理论，并且可以容纳关于影响估值的因素的各种观点。作者建议从业者使用这些时间序列预测来帮助提供资本市场假设。

主题：证券分析和估值，基本股票分析，定量方法，统计方法，金融危机和金融市场历史

Key Finding

\- ▪ CAPE可能是非平稳的，因此没有恢复到长期的趋势，或者基于平均回归的CAPE的任何其他平均预测可能是错误的。

\- ▪ 考虑到时间序列模型所建议的CAPE值的结果范围，在任何一个方向上都减少了大惊喜的机会。

\- ▪ 将估值变化纳入资本市场假设的从业者可能希望假设没有或很小的变化作为基本情况，而不是回归到长期平均值。

\[3\] Momentum Information Propagation through Global Supply Chain Networks

标题:动量信息在全球供应链网络中的传播

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management August 2021, jpm.2021.1.264

作者:Rei Yamamoto, Naoya Kawadai and Hiroki Miyahara

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/06/10/jpm.2021.1.264;

Abstract : Customer momentum has been demonstrated to be effective in the US and Japanese stock markets but has not been examined using a large global supply chain network. In this article, the authors propose a new customer momentum strategy based on global supply chain network data combined with prior research. They apply (1) network theory, (2) medium-term momentum propagation, and (3) higher-layer customer information based on the characteristics of global supply chain network data. Empirical analysis in global stock markets confirms that the proposed strategy has 4.5% annual return based on a long–short analysis. This strategy is more effective than standard momentum and customer momentum in former studies.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, developed markets, analysis of individual factors/risk premia, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors propose a new customer momentum strategy based on global supply chain network data.

\- ▪ They apply (1) network theory, (2) medium-term momentum propagation, and (3) higher-layer customer information based on the characteristics of global supply chain network data.

\- ▪ Empirical analysis in global stock markets confirms that the proposed strategy has 4.5% annual return based on a long–short analysis.

Abstract:客户动力在美国和日本股市已被证明是有效的，但尚未使用大型全球供应链网络进行检验。本文结合前人的研究成果，提出了一种基于全球供应链网络数据的顾客动量策略。它们应用了（1）网络理论，（2）中期动量传播，和（3）基于全球供应链网络数据特征的更高层客户信息。在全球股票市场的实证分析证实，基于长期-短期分析，该策略的年回报率为4.5%。这种策略比以往研究中的标准动量和顾客动量更有效。

主题：证券分析和估值，发达市场，个别因素/风险溢价分析，业绩衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 提出了一种基于全球供应链网络数据的顾客动量策略

\- ▪ 他们应用了（1）网络理论，（2）中期动量传播，以及（3）基于全球供应链网络数据特征的更高层客户信息。

\- ▪ 在全球股票市场的实证分析证实，基于长期-短期分析，该策略的年回报率为4.5%。

\[4\] Portfolio Upside and Downside Risk—Both Matter!

标题:投资组合上行和下行风险都很重要！

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Real Estate 2021, jpm.2021.1.263

作者:Jeffrey D. Fisher and Joseph D’Alessandro

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/06/05/jpm.2021.1.263;

Abstract : Traditional performance measures do not distinguish between upside risk and downside risk. Downside risk is based on returns that are below a target rate, below zero, below average, or below a benchmark, and vice versa for upside risk. Downside risk measures and the “duality” of beta have been discussed extensively in literature related to public markets but have not been applied to the analysis of private equity real estate in the academic literature. This article examines the measures that can distinguish between upside and downside risk that are commonly used in public markets, such as upside and downside beta and the Sortino ratio for downside analysis, and applies them to different property sectors and Core-Based Statistical Areas in the NCREIF Property Index. In addition, these measures are used to analyze gateway versus nongateway markets’ historical performance on both an upside and downside risk basis. The same techniques are applied to perform an attribution analysis of a portfolio’s alpha into upside and downside components.

TOPICS: Real estate, risk management, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors show that downside risk measures such as the Sortino ratio and Downside Beta can provide insights into what drives returns for different property sectors and Core-Based Statistical Areas, as well as gateway versus nongateway markets.

\- ▪ Using the upside to downside beta ratio, the authors show how property sectors and markets vary as to whether beta is magnifying return on the upside and/or minimizing declines on the downside.

\- ▪ By using the duality of beta, the authors show how to decompose the risk-adjusted overperformance or underperformance into upside and downside alpha earned by a portfolio, a complement to traditional Brinson attribution analysis.

Abstract:传统的业绩衡量标准没有区分上行风险和下行风险。下行风险基于低于目标利率、低于零、低于平均水平或低于基准的回报，反之亦然。下行风险度量和贝塔系数的“二元性”在与公开市场相关的文献中已被广泛讨论，但在学术文献中尚未应用于私募股权房地产的分析。本文考察了公共市场中常用的区分上行和下行风险的指标，如上行和下行贝塔和下行分析的索蒂诺比率，并将其应用于NCREIF房地产指数中不同的房地产行业和基于核心的统计领域。此外，这些指标还用于分析网关市场和非网关市场在上行和下行风险基础上的历史表现。同样的技术被应用于对投资组合的α进行归因分析，分为上行和下行两部分。

主题：房地产，风险管理，绩效评估

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者表明，下行风险指标，如索蒂诺比率和下行贝塔系数，可以为不同的房地产行业和基于核心的统计领域，以及门户市场和非门户市场的回报驱动提供洞察。

\- ▪ 利用上下行贝塔比率，作者展示了房地产行业和市场在贝塔是否放大上行回报和/或最小化下行下跌方面的变化。

\- ▪ 利用beta的对偶性，作者展示了如何将风险调整后的表现优异或表现不佳分解为投资组合的上下α，这是对传统Brinson归因分析的补充。

\[5\] Long-Term Investing and the Frequency of Investment Decisions

标题:长期投资与投资决策频率

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management August 2021, jpm.2021.1.262

作者:Ronald J. M. van Loon

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/06/03/jpm.2021.1.262;

Abstract : This article analyzes the impact of the frequency of investment decisions on long-term investment results. Long-term investment returns are shown to be a nonlinear function of skill, transaction costs, volatility, and frequency. The author uses compound returns to show the minimum skill level that is required to result in a positive expected return after costs and shows the skill level required to outperform the buy-and-hold investor. These can be high for markets that have high transaction costs per unit of volatility. The optimum frequency of investment decisions is derived as that frequency that maximizes the expected terminal value of wealth. Empirical estimates from the US equity and fixed-income markets show that this optimum frequency is considerably different between these markets, even when the investor has an equal amount of skill in each. The author also shows how long-horizon value-at-risk varies with the frequency of investment decision-making.

TOPICS: Wealth management, equity portfolio management, fixed income portfolio management, risk management, portfolio construction, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The author derives the success rate that is required to result in a positive expected return after cost and applies it to directional strategies in the US equity and fixed-income markets. For fixed income, the investor requires a success rate of more than 51%, regardless of the frequency of those decisions. For directional strategies in the US equity market, this minimum success rate varies more with frequency, but it is higher than 53%.

\- ▪ The optimum frequency of investment decisions is derived as that frequency that maximizes expected terminal wealth. The article shows that this frequency is equal to skill times volatility over costs, squared. Because neither of these parameters is likely to be constant through time, the optimum frequency of investment decision-making is also likely to change through time, which has important implications for how an investment process is to be structured. Empirical estimates show very different results for different markets.

\- ▪ Even for highly skilled investment processes, downside risk is meaningful when frequency is low. Downside risks are meaningfully lower only for high-skill investment processes at high frequency. This is an important consideration for investors who place high importance on downside risks at long investment horizons.

Abstract:本文分析了投资决策频率对长期投资效果的影响。长期投资回报率是技能、交易成本、波动率和频率的非线性函数。作者使用复合回报来显示最低的技能水平，这是需要导致一个积极的预期回报后的成本，并显示了技能水平所需的表现优于购买和持有投资者。对于每单位波动率的交易成本较高的市场而言，这可能会很高。投资决策的最佳频率是指财富的预期终值最大化的频率。来自美国股票市场和固定收益市场的经验估计表明，这两个市场之间的最佳频率相差很大，即使投资者在这两个市场上的技能相同。作者还展示了长期风险价值随投资决策频率的变化。

主题：财富管理，股票投资组合管理，固定收益投资组合管理，风险管理，投资组合构建，绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者导出了在成本后产生正预期收益所需的成功率，并将其应用于美国股票和固定收益市场的定向策略。对于固定收益，投资者要求成功率超过51%，无论这些决定的频率如何。对于美国股市的定向策略，这个最小成功率随频率的变化更大，但高于53%。

\- ▪ 投资决策的最佳频率被导出为期望最终财富最大化的频率。文章表明，这个频率等于技能乘以成本波动率的平方。因为这两个参数都不可能随着时间的推移而保持不变，投资决策的最佳频率也可能随着时间的推移而变化，这对投资过程的结构有着重要的影响。对于不同的市场，经验估计显示出非常不同的结果。

\- ▪ 即使是高技能的投资过程，下行风险是有意义的，当频率较低。只有在高技能、高频率的投资过程中，下行风险才会显著降低。对于那些在长期投资中高度重视下行风险的投资者来说，这是一个重要的考虑因素。

\[6\] Hedge Funds and Their Prime Brokers: Favorable IPO Allocations

标题:对冲基金及其主要经纪人：有利的IPO配置

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management August 2021, jpm.2021.1.261

作者:Xiaohui Yang, Hossein B. Kazemi and Mila Getmansky Sherman

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/06/03/jpm.2021.1.261;

Abstract : Do hedge funds receive special treatment from their prime brokers in initial public offering (IPO) allocations? Using a comprehensive dataset, the authors examine investment banks’ allocations of IPOs to their hedge fund clients. They find that investment banks/prime brokers that have business relationships with hedge funds tend to allocate more IPOs to those hedge funds when the banks are the lead underwriters. Moreover, the size of allocation to hedge funds is larger when IPOs are underpriced, and allocations are larger during bear equity markets. The authors document that younger funds or funds with recent poor performance tend to receive relatively larger allocations of underpriced IPOs. The size of these allocations is determined by the strength of the funds’ relationships with their prime brokers, rather than by the level of their managers’ skill. This is supported by the finding that, even after receiving relatively large allocations of underpriced IPOs, these funds fail to generate significant alphas.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, exchanges/markets/clearinghouses, real assets/alternative investments/private equity, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ Investment banks that act as prime brokers for hedge funds and are lead underwriters of IPOs tend to allocate larger amounts of underpriced IPOs to their hedge fund clients.

\- ▪ Investment banks tend to allocate a larger amount to their clients during bear equity markets, with younger funds and those with poor recent performance receiving relatively larger allocations.

\- ▪ Investment banks reward those clients that have stronger relationship with them. The skills of hedge fund managers do not drive the favorable allocations.

Abstract:对冲基金在首次公开发行（IPO）配置中是否从主要经纪人处获得特殊待遇？利用一个全面的数据集，作者研究了投资银行对对冲基金客户ipo的分配情况。他们发现，与对冲基金有业务关系的投资银行/主要经纪商往往会在银行担任主承销商时，向这些对冲基金分配更多的ipo。此外，当ipo定价过低时，对冲基金的配置规模更大，而在熊市时，配置规模更大。作者指出，较年轻的基金或近期表现不佳的基金往往会获得相对较大的低估ipo配置。这些配置的规模是由基金与其主要经纪人的关系强度决定的，而不是由基金经理的技能水平决定的。这一发现得到了支持，即即使在获得相对较大的低价ipo配置后，这些基金也未能产生显著的alpha。

主题：证券分析和估值、交易所/市场/票据交换所、实物资产/另类投资/私募股权、业绩衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作为对冲基金的主要经纪商和ipo主承销商的投资银行，往往会向对冲基金客户分配大量定价偏低的ipo。

\- ▪ 在熊市期间，投资银行倾向于向客户分配更多的资金，较年轻的基金和近期表现不佳的基金获得的资金相对较多。

\- ▪ 投资银行奖励那些与他们关系更密切的客户。对冲基金经理的技能不会推动有利的配置。

\[7\] Tokenization—The Future of Real Estate Investment?

标题:房地产投资的未来？

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Real Estate 2021, jpm.2021.1.260

作者:Andrew Baum

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/06/02/jpm.2021.1.260;

Abstract : This article examines the mechanisms now available to tokenize real asset ownership and create active secondary markets in tokenized or fractionalized units. Real estate is lumpy and illiquid, and tokenization would allow participation in the market by a broader group of investors and the ability to build diversified portfolios with modest sums of money. However, two developments are necessary for the digital tokenization of single real estate assets: (1) an expressed demand for the fractionalization of single real estate assets, and (2) market participants need to be comfortable with blockchain, the digital underpinning of tokenization. Further, in many land markets fractionalization requires an intermediate structure to be established because the direct ownership of land cannot be shared amongst many, increasing the cost of tokenization. Larger assets already held in fund structures may eventually be tokenized successfully; there may also be an alternative market for tokenized residential, social impact, or community assets where investment regulation and risk/return are not the main drivers of behavior. The mass market for the tokenization of single commercial real estate assets, however, may be some way down the road.

TOPICS: Real estate, exchanges/markets/clearinghouses, currency, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The author examines the mechanisms now available to tokenize real asset ownership.

\- ▪ The author presents new survey-based evidence of real estate tokenization deals.

\- ▪ The author concludes that an intermediate structure is likely to be both necessary and convenient when fractionalizing a single asset. For that reason, funds are more natural targets for tokenization.

Abstract:本文探讨了目前可用的机制，以标记化实际资产所有权和创造活跃的二级市场的标记化或分拆单位。房地产是不稳定和流动性差的，标记化将允许更广泛的投资者群体参与市场，并有能力用适度的资金建立多样化的投资组合。然而，单一房地产资产的数字标记化需要两个方面的发展：（1）单一房地产资产分拆的明确需求；（2）市场参与者需要熟悉区块链，即标记化的数字基础。此外，在许多土地市场，分割需要建立一个中间结构，因为土地的直接所有权不能在许多人之间共享，增加了标记化的成本。基金结构中已经持有的较大资产最终可能会成功地标记化；在投资监管和风险/回报不是行为的主要驱动因素的情况下，也可能有一个替代性的住宅、社会影响或社区资产市场。然而，单一商业房地产资产标记化的大众市场可能还有一段路要走。

主题：房地产、交易所/市场/票据交换所、货币、绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者研究了目前可用于标记真实资产所有权的机制。

\- ▪ 作者提出了新的基于调查的房地产标记化交易证据。

\- ▪ 作者的结论是，在对单一资产进行分拆时，中间结构可能既必要又方便。因此，基金更自然地成为标记化的目标。
