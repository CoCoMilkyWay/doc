\[1\] Employee Satisfaction and Stock Returns during the COVID-19 Pandemic

标题:新冠疫情期间的员工满意度和股票回报

作者:Mary J Becker,Alexander Cardazzi,Zachary McGurk

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3931528

Abstract : The COVID-19 Pandemic has had an unprecedented impact on how employees and employers operate. Employees, directly affected by workplace changes, may provide information regarding future efficiencies. As a result, crowdsourced employee satisfaction (ES) reviews mentioning the COVID-19 Pandemic may contain useful information regarding the future profitability of these firms. We utilize crowdsourced COVID-19 Pandemic specific ES obtained from Glassdoor.com to determine the impact on abnormal stock returns for public firms from March-December 2020. We find evidence that higher COVID-19 ES is related to higher abnormal stock returns. While non-COVID ES is found not to be related to abnormal stock returns.

Keywords : COVID-19 Pandemic, Glassdoor, Employee Satisfaction, Abnormal Re-turns

Abstract :新冠病毒-19大流行对雇员和雇主的经营方式产生了前所未有的影响。直接受工作场所变化影响的员工可能会提供有关未来效率的信息。因此，提及新冠疫情的众包员工满意度（ES）评估可能包含有关这些公司未来盈利能力的有用信息。我们利用从Glassdoor.com获得的众包新冠病毒-19大流行特异性ES来确定从2020年3月至12月对上市公司异常股票回报的影响。我们发现证据表明，较高的新冠病毒-19 E与较高的异常股票回报率有关。而非新冠病毒ES被发现与异常股票收益无关。

Keywords :新冠病毒-19大流行、玻璃门、员工满意度、异常复发

\[2\] Banks’ Equity Stakes in Firms: A Blessing or Curse in Credit Markets?

标题:银行在企业中的股权：信贷市场的福还是祸？

作者:Falko Fecht,José-Luis Peydró,Gunseli Tumer-Alkan,Yuejuan Yu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3932233

Abstract : We analyze how banks’ equity stakes in firms influence their credit supply in crisis times. For identification, we exploit the 2008 Global Financial Crisis and merge unique supervisory data from the German credit register on individual bank-firm credit exposures with the security register data that include banks’ equity holdings. We find that a large and ex-ante persistent equity position held by a bank in a firm is associated with a larger credit provision from the respective bank to that firm. In crisis times, however, equity stakes only foster credit supply to ex-ante riskier firms especially from relatively weak banks. This ex-ante risk-taking may be due to better (insider) information by the bank, including a traditional lending relationship over the crisis. However, this ex-ante riskier lending translates also into higher ex-post loan defaults, worse firm-level stock market returns and even more firm bankruptcy or restructuring cases. Our results therefore suggest that banks’ equity stakes in their borrowers do not mitigate debt overhang problems of distressed firms in crisis times, but rather foster evergreening of banks’ outstanding credit to those (zombie) firms.

Keywords : Universal banks; Credit supply; Bank equity holdings; Debt overhang; Evergreening.

Abstract :我们分析了银行在企业中的股权如何影响危机时期的信贷供应。为了进行识别，我们利用2008年全球金融危机，将德国信贷登记册中关于单个银行公司信贷风险的独特监管数据与证券登记册中包括银行股权的数据合并。我们发现，一家银行在一家公司持有的大量且事先持续的股权头寸，与该银行向该公司提供的更大的信贷相关。然而，在危机时期，股权只会促进对事前风险较高的公司的信贷供应，尤其是相对较弱的银行。这种事前冒险行为可能是由于银行提供了更好的（内部）信息，包括危机期间的传统借贷关系。然而，这种事前风险更高的贷款也会导致更高的事后违约率、更差的公司层面股票市场回报率以及更多的公司破产或重组案例。因此，我们的研究结果表明，银行持有借款人的股权并不能缓解危机时期陷入困境的企业的债务积压问题，而是促进了银行对这些（僵尸）企业的未偿信贷的持续增长。

Keywords :全能银行；信贷供应；银行股权控股；债务积压；常青树。

\[3\] Social Media Analyst Coverage of Socially Responsible Firms

标题:社会媒体分析师对社会责任公司的报道

作者:Matthew D. DeAngelis,Danya Mi,James Moon,Dana Wallace

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3918531

Abstract : We examine the influence of corporate social responsibility on the information dissemination behavior of social media analysts (SMAs). We find that SMAs give greater coverage to the earnings announcements of socially responsible firms (SRFs) compared to other firms, particularly for good news announcements. We further investigate whether the difference in coverage for SRFs and non-SRFs is due to superior disclosure practices by SRFs or to positive affective reactions by SMAs. Consistent with an affective response, we find that articles for SRFs appear less tethered to firm fundamentals—the sentiment in SMA articles more poorly predicts future earnings surprises and is less responsive to earnings announcement news for SRFs. Market-based tests similarly suggest that SMA analyses at earnings announcements are of lower quality for SRFs and potentially impair price efficiency. Overall, we attribute our results to SMAs’ desire to view SRFs in a favorable light no matter the disclosure news.

Keywords : Social media, CSR, Social responsibility, Social media analyst, Seeking Alpha

Abstract :我们研究了企业社会责任对社交媒体分析师（SMA）信息传播行为的影响。我们发现，与其他公司相比，SMA对社会责任公司（SRF）的收益公告的覆盖率更高，尤其是对好消息的公告。我们进一步调查SRF和非SRF覆盖范围的差异是否是由于SRF的优越披露实践或SMA的积极情感反应。与情感反应一致，我们发现SRF的文章似乎与公司基本面联系较少。SMA文章中的情感更难预测未来的收益惊喜，并且对SRF的收益公告消息反应较少。基于市场的测试同样表明，收益公告中的SMA分析对于SRF而言质量较低，并可能损害价格效率。总的来说，我们将我们的结果归因于SMA希望从有利的角度看待SRF，无论披露消息如何。

Keywords :社交媒体，企业社会责任，社会责任，社交媒体分析师，寻找阿尔法

\[4\] Analoging the digital: Designing better binary option contracts

标题:模拟数字：设计更好的二进制期权合约

作者:Yisong S. Tian

出处:Financial Markets, Institutions & Instruments, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/fmii.12151

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3931376

Abstract : Binary option pays a fixed dollar amount if it matures in the money and nothing otherwise. While this cash-or-nothing payoff structure is very attractive to speculators, it also creates incentives to manipulate the underlying asset price in order to gain extra payoff. In this paper, we propose better designs for binary options that disincentivize market manipulation and highlight two key features of such designs. We demonstrate the effectiveness of the new contract designs using numerical examples.

\[5\] Mutual Fund Risk Shifting and Risk Anomalies

标题:共同基金风险转移和风险异常

作者:Xiao Han,Nikolai L. Roussanov,Hongxun Ruan

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3931449

Abstract : Risk-shifting by underperforming funds increases their demand for risky stocks. We investigate its contribution to the well known risk anomalies: the apparent overvaluation of stocks with high beta, idiosyncratic volatility, and skewness. We show that these anomalies are concentrated among stocks mainly held by laggard funds. Exploiting the Morningstar methodology change in 2002, whereby its “star” ratings became based on relative performance within a style category rather than across the entire fund universe, we show that the beta anomaly is significant only when beta is measured against the S&P 500 index for the pre-2002 period and against the relevant category index for the post-2002 period. Using a demand system approach we find that removing holdings of the bottom performance quintile of funds substantially reduces the beta anomaly returns.

Keywords : Beta Anomaly, Morningstar Ratings, Mutual Funds, Risk Shifting, Idiosyncratic Volatility, Skewness, Demand System

Abstract :表现不佳的基金的风险转移增加了它们对高风险股票的需求。我们调查了它对众所周知的风险异常的贡献：高贝塔系数、特殊波动性和偏斜的股票的明显高估。我们发现，这些异常现象集中在主要由落后基金持有的股票中。利用Morningstar方法在2002年的变化，即其“明星”评级基于某一风格类别内的相对表现，而不是整个基金领域，我们表明，只有当贝塔指数与2002年前的标准普尔500指数以及2002年后的相关类别指数进行比较时，贝塔异常才会显著。使用需求系统方法，我们发现，移除持有业绩最低的五分之一的基金，会显著降低贝塔异常回报率。

Keywords :贝塔异常、晨星评级、共同基金、风险转移、特殊波动、偏斜、需求系统

\[6\] Common Analyst and Convergence in Accounting Policies

标题:共同分析师与会计政策趋同

作者:Abdul Mohi Khizer

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3930921

Abstract : This paper presents novel evidence of a convergence in accounting policies among firms due to common analysts. The findings are robust to two exogenous shocks: Brokerage closures and Regulation Fair Disclosure (Reg FD). The convergence manifests in policies related to depreciation, acquisition/sales valuation, inventory valuation, and PP&E (plant, property, and equipment) valuation. In cross-sectional tests, I document that the convergence is stronger for firms characterized by higher financial constraints, poorer information environment, lower agency conflicts, lesser sophisticated analysts' coverage, and lower performance. In addition, the convergence in accounting policies improves analyst forecast quality, firm information environment, and firm's financial constraints. This paper documents the impact of analysts on accounting policy choices and managers catering to analyst expectations via alteration of accounting policies. Overall, this paper provides evidence of the role played by analysts in shaping managerial reporting decisions.

Keywords : Accounting Policies; Common Analyst; Policy Convergence

Abstract :本文提出了一个新的证据，证明了由于普通分析师的存在，公司之间的会计政策趋于一致。研究结果对两种外部冲击具有鲁棒性：经纪机构关闭和监管公平披露（Reg FD）。这种趋同体现在与折旧、收购/销售估价、存货估价和PP&E（厂房、物业和设备）估价相关的政策中。在横截面测试中，我证明，对于具有较高财务约束、较差信息环境、较低代理冲突、较低复杂分析师覆盖率和较低绩效的公司，趋同性更强。此外，会计政策的趋同改善了分析师的预测质量、公司的信息环境和公司的财务约束。本文记录了分析师对会计政策选择的影响，以及管理者通过改变会计政策来满足分析师的期望。总体而言，本文提供了分析师在塑造管理层报告决策中所起作用的证据。

Keywords :会计政策；普通分析员；政策趋同

\[8\] Noisy Factors

标题:噪声因素

作者:Pat Akey,Adriana Robertson,Mikhail Simutin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3930228

Abstract : The Fama-French factors are ubiquitous in empirical finance, industry, and law. We find that factor returns differ substantially depending on when the data were downloaded. The effects of these retroactive changes are large. Holding the sample period constant and varying only factor vintages, we show this in three contexts. First, in cross-sectional equity pricing, unconditional alphas of a third of long-short ‘anomaly’ portfolios lose statistical significance. Second, in mutual fund analyses, we show that annual alphas of almost half of individual funds and even portfolios of funds change by more than 1%. Third, in the context of testing asset pricing models, F-statistics from GRS tests of the three-factor model on standard test portfolios vary by up to 40% due only to changes in factor vintages. Our results do not suggest that any particular factor vintage is dominant but point to a source of latent noise that is ignored in conventional tests. We provide suggestions to empiricists on dealing with the noise. Our findings have significant implications for the replicability and robustness of finance research and have a direct bearing on a variety of empirical contexts.

Keywords : Fama French factors, asset pricing, performance evaluation, equities, mutual funds, model fit

Abstract :Fama-French因素在实证金融、工业和法律中无处不在。我们发现，因子回报率因数据下载时间的不同而有很大差异。这些追溯性变化的影响很大。保持采样周期不变，只改变因子年份，我们在三种情况下展示了这一点。首先，在横截面股票定价中，三分之一的长短“异常”投资组合的无条件阿尔法失去了统计意义。其次，在共同基金分析中，我们显示，几乎一半的单个基金甚至基金组合的年度alpha变化超过1%。第三，在测试资产定价模型的背景下，标准测试投资组合的三因素模型GRS测试的F-统计数据仅因因素年份的变化而变化高达40%。我们的结果并不表明任何特定的因素是主要的，但指出了一个潜在的噪声源，在传统的测试中被忽略。我们为经验主义者提供处理噪音的建议。我们的研究结果对金融研究的可复制性和稳健性具有重要意义，并直接影响到各种经验背景。

Keywords :Fama French factors、资产定价、绩效评估、股票、共同基金、模型拟合

\[9\] The Quant Cycle

标题:量子周期

作者:David Blitz

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3930006

Abstract : Traditional business cycle indicators do not capture much of the large cyclical variation in factor returns. Major turning points of factors seem to be caused by abrupt changes in investor sentiment instead. We infer a Quant Cycle directly from factor returns, which consists of a normal stage that is interrupted by occasional drawdowns of the value factor and subsequent reversals. Value factor drawdowns can occur in bullish environments due to growth rallies and in bearish environments due to crashes of value stocks. For the reversals we also distinguish between bullish and bearish subvariants. Empirically we show that our simple 3-stage model captures a considerable amount of time variation in factor returns. We conclude that investors should focus on better understanding the quant cycle as implied by factors themselves, rather than adhering to traditional frameworks which, at best, have a weak relation with actual factor returns.

Keywords : asset pricing, factor models, business cycle, value, momentum, low volatility, quality, behavioral finance

Abstract :传统的商业周期指标并没有捕捉到要素收益率的大量周期性变化。这些因素的主要转折点似乎是由投资者情绪的突然变化引起的。我们直接从因子收益率推断出一个数量周期，该周期由一个正常阶段组成，该阶段被价值因子的偶尔下降和随后的反转所中断。价值因素下降可能发生在增长反弹导致的看涨环境中，也可能发生在价值股崩溃导致的看跌环境中。对于反转，我们也区分看涨和看跌的子变量。从经验上看，我们的简单三阶段模型捕捉到了要素收益的大量时间变化。我们的结论是，投资者应该专注于更好地理解要素本身所隐含的数量周期，而不是坚持传统的框架，因为传统框架充其量与实际要素收益率的关系很弱。

Keywords :资产定价、要素模型、商业周期、价值、动量、低波动性、质量、行为金融学

\[10\] Expected Returns and Informed Demand: Implication of two competing information effects

标题:预期收益和知情需求：两种竞争信息效应的含义

作者:Moonsoo Kang,Eric N. Hughson

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3929703

Abstract : We analyze joint dynamics of informed demand and expected returns by developing a theoretical measure for information-driven demand. Our analysis shows that informed demand relates to expected returns through two competing effects: the 'information risk effect' and the 'information transmission effect'. The former effect suggests that higher informed demand leads to higher expected returns because of information asymmetry among investors. However, the latter effect implies that high informed demand lowers expected returns by revealing more information. Overall, this study provides a balanced understanding of the information effect on asset prices by highlighting the dual implication of informed demand.

Keywords : Informed Demand, Expected Returns, Information Risk, Information Transmission

Abstract :我们通过开发信息驱动需求的理论度量来分析知情需求和预期收益的联合动态。我们的分析表明，知情需求通过两种竞争效应与预期收益相关：“信息风险效应”和“信息传递效应”。前者的效应表明，由于投资者之间的信息不对称，更高的知情需求导致更高的预期收益。然而，后一种效应意味着高知情需求通过披露更多信息降低了预期回报。总的来说，本研究通过强调知情需求的双重含义，平衡地理解了信息对资产价格的影响。

Keywords :知情需求、预期收益、信息风险、信息传递

\[11\] Going by the Book: Valuation Ratios and Stock Returns

标题:按部就班：估值比率和股票回报

作者:Ki-Soon Choi,Eric C. So,Charles C. Y. Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3854022

Abstract : We study the use of book-to-market ratios (B/M) in value investing and its implications for comovements in firms' stock returns and trading volumes. We show B/M has increasingly detached from alternative valuation ratios while becoming worse at forecasting returns and growth in an absolute and relative sense. Despite these trends, major U.S. stock indexes and institutional funds continue relying on B/M when identifying value stocks.
