\[1\] Fundamental Analysis of XBRL Data: A Machine Learning Approach

标题:XBRL数据的基本分析：一种机器学习方法

作者:Xi Chen(New York University (NYU) - Leonard N. Stern School of Business),Yang Ha Cho(New York University (NYU) - Department of Accounting),Yiwei Dou(New York University (NYU) - Department of Accounting),Baruch Lev(New York University - Stern School of Business)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3741015

Abstract : Since 2012, all U.S. public companies must tag quantitative amounts in financial statements and footnotes of their 10-K reports using the eXtensible Business Reporting Language (XBRL). We conduct a fundamental analysis of this large set of detailed financial information to predict earnings. Using machine learning methods, we combine the XBRL data into a summary measure for the direction of one-year-ahead earnings changes. Hedge portfolios are formed based on this measure during the period 2015-2018. The annual size-adjusted returns to the hedge portfolios range from 5.02 to 9.7 percent. These returns persist after accounting for transaction costs and risk. Our strategies outperform those of Ou and Penman (1989), who extract the summary measure from 65 accounting variables using logistic regressions. Additional analyses suggest that the outperformance stems from both nonlinear predictor interactions missed by regressions and more detailed financial data in XBRL documents.

Keywords : Fundamental Analysis, XBRL, Machine Learning

Abstract :自2012年以来，所有美国上市公司都必须在财务报表和10-K报告脚注中使用可扩展商业报告语言（XBRL）标记定量金额。我们对这一大套详细的财务信息进行基本面分析，以预测收益。使用机器学习方法，我们将XBRL数据组合成一个总结指标，用于预测未来一年的收益变化方向。2015-2018年期间，对冲投资组合是基于这一衡量标准形成的。对冲投资组合的年度规模调整回报率从5.02%到9.7%不等。在考虑了交易成本和风险之后，这些回报仍然存在。我们的策略优于Ou和Penman（1989），他们使用logistic回归从65个会计变量中提取汇总指标。额外的分析表明，表现优异源于回归遗漏的非线性预测交互作用和XBRL文件中更详细的财务数据。

Keywords :基本分析，XBRL，机器学习

\[2\] On (r, g) and the Identity ROE = EP\*MTB

标题:在（r，g）上，等式ROE=EP\*MTB

作者:James A. Ohlson(Hong Kong Polytechnic University - School of Accounting and Finance),Sophia Weihuan Zhai(Hong Kong Polytechnic University - School of Accounting and Finance)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3741088

Abstract : The paper concerns concepts of equity valuation. Three primary financial ratios -- (forward) return on equity (ROE), (forward) earnings to price ratio (EP), and the (current) market-to-book ratio (MTB) – are connected to the standard valuation parameters, r = cost of equity (discount factor), and g = growth. The framework relies on a Gordon-Williams type of PVD model and combines it with an add-on steady-state growth requirement: Subject to clean surplus accounting, (the expected) earnings, dividends, and book values all grow at the same rate. This condition is adapted from “The Second Fundamental Law of Capitalism”, articulated by Piketty (2014). Applying these ideas result in a benchmark model: (i) a weighted average representation, EP = BTM\*r + (1- BTM) \*Div.Yield , and (ii) the inequalities 0 < BTM < 1 and Div.Yield < EP < r < ROE. Additional analysis relaxes the steady-state growth condition: the g-parameter is now replaced by forecasts of near future earnings growth which get updated as time passes. An empirical part of the paper evaluates whether the steady-state growth concept holds as an average for S&P 500 firms. Results are generally supportive.

Keywords : None

Abstract :本文涉及股权评估的概念。三个主要财务比率——（远期）股本回报率（ROE），（远期）市盈率（EP）和（当前）市盈率（MTB）——与标准估值参数相关，r=权益成本（贴现因子），g=增长。该框架依赖于Gordon-Williams类型的PVD模型，并将其与附加的稳态增长要求相结合：根据净盈余会计，（预期）收益、股息和账面价值都以相同的速度增长。这一条件改编自Piketty（2014）阐述的“资本主义第二基本定律”。将这些思想应用于基准模型：（i）加权平均表示法，EP=BTM\*r+（1-BTM）\*部门收益率和（ii）不等式0<BTM<1和部门收益率<EP<r<ROE。额外的分析放宽了稳态增长条件：g参数现在被对近期收益增长的预测所取代，这些预测随着时间的推移而更新。本文的实证部分评估了稳态增长概念是否适用于标准普尔500指数成分股的平均水平。结果总体上是支持的。

Keywords :None

\[3\] Aggressive Boards and CEO Turnover

标题:积极的董事会和CEO更替

作者:Cyrus Aghamolla(University of Minnesota - Twin Cities),Tadashi Hashimoto(Yeshiva University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3760170

Abstract : This study investigates a communication game between a CEO and a board of directors where the CEO's career concerns can potentially impede value-increasing informative communication. By adopting a policy of aggressive boards (excessive replacement), shareholders can facilitate communication between the CEO and the board. The results are in contrast to the multitude of models which generally find that management-friendly boards improve communication, and help to explain empirical results concerning CEO turnover. The results also provide the following novel predictions concerning variation in CEO turnover: (i) there is greater CEO turnover in firms or industries where CEO performance is relatively more difficult to assess; (ii) the board is more aggressive in their replacement of the CEO in industries or firms where the board's advisory role is more salient; and (iii) there is comparatively less CEO turnover in firms or industries where the variance of CEO talent is high.

Keywords : Corporate Governance, Advising, CEO Replacement, Communication, CEO Turnover, Board Independence

Abstract :本研究探讨了CEO与董事会之间的沟通博弈，其中CEO的职业顾虑可能会阻碍信息沟通的价值提升。通过采取积极的董事会政策（过度更换），股东可以促进首席执行官和董事会之间的沟通。研究结果与众多模型形成对比，这些模型普遍发现，管理友好型董事会能够改善沟通，并有助于解释有关CEO离职的实证结果。研究结果还提供了以下有关CEO更替变化的新预测：（i）在CEO绩效相对较难评估的公司或行业中，CEO更替更大；（ii）在董事会的咨询作用更为突出的行业或公司，董事会更积极地更换首席执行官；以及（iii）在首席执行官人才差异较大的公司或行业，首席执行官更替相对较少。

Keywords :公司治理、咨询、首席执行官更换、沟通、首席执行官更替、董事会独立性

\[4\] Underpricing in the Cryptocurrency World: Evidence from Initial Coin Offerings

标题:加密货币市场的抑价：来自首次发行硬币的证据

作者:Thijn Felix(University of Groningen),J. Henk von Eije(University of Groningen - Faculty of Economics and Business)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3753845

Abstract : We analyze underpricing in listed Initial Coin Offerings (ICOs) by using a sample of 247 ICOs from September 2015 till January 2018. The results show an average level of underpricing of ICOs of 123% in the USA and 97% in the other countries. The results for the USA ICOs are significantly higher than for USA IPOs on average, and also significantly higher than USA IPOs at the beginning of the dot.com bubble. We also study the determinants of ICO underpricing. We use proxies based on asymmetric information from the IPO literature as well as ICO related variables. First-day trading volume and a good sentiment on the ICO market go together with more ICO underpricing. Moreover, hot markets make first day investors to benefit less. Finally, companies that use a large issue size or a pre-ICO (a sale of cryptocurrencies before the ICO) leave less money on the table.

Keywords : Initial Coin Offerings, ICOs, IPOs, Underpricing, Hot issue market, issue size, Pre-ICO, Cryptocurrencies, Tokens, Bitcoin, Blockchain

Abstract :我们使用2015年9月至2018年1月的247个ICO样本，分析了上市初始硬币发行（ICO）的抑价情况。结果表明，美国ICOs的平均抑价水平为123%，其他国家为97%。美国ICOs的业绩显著高于美国ipo的平均业绩，也显著高于美国ipo上市之初的业绩互联网泡泡。我们还研究了ICO抑价的决定因素。我们使用基于IPO文献中的不对称信息以及ICO相关变量的代理。首日成交量和ICO市场的良好人气伴随着更多的ICO抑价。此外，火爆的市场使得首日投资者受益较少。最后，使用大发行规模或预ICO（在ICO之前出售加密货币）的公司在桌上留下的资金较少。

Keywords :首次硬币发行、ICO、IPO、抑价、热点发行市场、发行规模、ICO前、加密货币、代币、比特币、区块链

\[5\] A Prospect Theory Model for Predicting Cryptocurrency Returns

标题:预测加密货币收益的前景理论模型

作者:Alexander Thoma(University of Zurich - Department of Banking and Finance)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3753530

Abstract : This paper investigates the risk and return properties of a trading strategy for the cryptocurrency market. The main predictive power for portfolio formation comes from a simple prospect theory model that only uses price information readily available. The dataset consists of a large body of cryptocurrencies from 2014 to 2020. I find a strong outperformance over the market, even after controlling for known predictors. Factor regressions with a cryptocurrency three-factor model further reveal significant alphas. Robustness test emphasize the legitimacy of the strategy. On average, cryptocurrencies with a high (low) prospect theory value earn low (high) subsequent returns. Interestingly, traders in the cryptocurrency market seem to assess the attractiveness of cryptocurrency in a way described by prospect theory. Mechanical tests of the model show that probability weighting is a main driver behind this assessment. Cryptocurrencies with a high prospect theory value tend to be highly positively skewed. This skewness could be the reason why the cryptocurrency seems attractive to traders, similar to lottery-like gambles.

Keywords : Asset Pricing, Behavioral Finance, Cryptocurrencies, Cryptocurrency Markets, Cryptocurrency Trading, Herd Behavior, Loss Aversion, Portfolio, Portfolio Choice, Prospect Theory

Abstract :本文研究了加密货币市场交易策略的风险和收益性质。投资组合形成的主要预测能力来自一个简单的前景理论模型，该模型只使用现成的价格信息。该数据集由2014年至2020年的大量加密货币组成。我发现，即使在控制了已知的预测因素之后，仍有强劲的表现优于市场。密码货币三因子模型的因子回归进一步揭示了显著的alphas。稳健性测试强调策略的合法性。平均而言，具有高（低）前景理论价值的加密货币可获得低（高）后续回报。有趣的是，加密货币市场的交易者似乎用前景理论描述的方式来评估加密货币的吸引力。模型的机械试验表明，概率权重是这一评估背后的主要驱动力。具有高前景理论价值的加密货币倾向于高度正偏态。这种偏斜可能是加密货币对交易者具有吸引力的原因，类似于彩票赌博。

Keywords :资产定价，行为金融学，加密货币，加密货币市场，加密货币交易，羊群行为，损失厌恶，投资组合，投资组合选择，前景理论

\[6\] Dose Macroeconomic Factors Influence Cryptocurrencies Return?

标题:宏观经济因素影响加密货币收益吗？

作者:Kei Nakagawa(Innovatioin Lab),Ryuta Sakemoto(Okayama University; Keio University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3749918

Abstract : This study investigates the relationship between expected returns on cryptocurrencies and macroeconomic fundamentals. We employ a dynamic factor model and summarize information as common factors. We observe that the common factors are strongly linked to the cryptocurrency expected returns at a quarterly frequency while we do not see that relationship using a few macroeconomic indicators such as inflation and money supply. This suggests that the macroeconomic information matters in a longer term, which contrasts to the study of Liu & Tsyvinski (2020) who explore a short-term relationship.

Keywords : Cryptocurrencies, Macroeconomic Factor, Factor Model

Abstract :本研究探讨加密货币预期收益与宏观经济基本面之间的关系。我们采用一个动态因素模型，将信息归纳为共同因素。我们观察到，共同因素以季度频率与加密货币的预期收益紧密相关，而使用通货膨胀和货币供应量等少数宏观经济指标，我们看不到这种关系。这表明宏观经济信息在一个较长的时期内是重要的，这与Liu和Tsyvinski（2020）的研究形成了对比，他们探讨了短期关系。

Keywords :加密货币，宏观经济因素，因素模型

\[7\] Can Sustainability and Financial Returns Be Derived in the Cryptocurrency Markets?

标题:加密货币市场能否带来可持续性和财务回报？

作者:Elise Alfieri(University Grenoble Alpes - Center for Studies and Applied Research in Management (CERAG); University Grenoble Alpes),Yann Ferrat(affiliation not provided to SSRN)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3745049

Abstract : ‘Cryptocurrencies’ and ‘sustainability’ are among the most common buzzwords in the financial press. Cryptocurrencies are trending due to the exponential returns derived as well as the blockchain technology which revolution numerous business sectors. On the other hand, environmental, social and governance (ESG) receives important coverage due to the significant growth of assets managed responsibly and increasing public scrutiny of firm’s corporate social responsibility (CSR) practices. However, very little has been written combining cryptocurrencies and ESG together. Given the significant share of intangibles in the valuation of cryptocurrencies, we believe that these markets may exhibit an ESG-related risk premium. Using an unbalanced sample of cryptocurrencies historically, we empirical test this hypothesis. Preliminary results support this assumption. Indeed, an environmental risk premium does exist in the cryptocurrency markets. Using a measure for energetical efficiency, our findings demonstrate that a portfolio of high ‘eco-efficient’ cryptocurrencies significantly outperforms the low ‘eco-efficiency’ one. Further assessing should be made by controlling for risk factors that explain excess returns, as presented by Shen, Urquhart and Wang (2020). The skeptical readers may suggest that this premium is obtained including the Bitcoin in high ‘eco-efficiency’ portfolios. However, the extent of returns is unaffected when controlling for the Bitcoin’s market share. While environmental concerns of the mining protocol and governance issues related to market regulation have been heavily expressed, the ethical perspective considers also social externalities. We believe the CSR profile of a cryptocurrency should be defined similarly to a traditional debt and equity issuers. In this spirit, we propose a grid that enables a socially conscious investor to evaluate a cryptocurrency’s environmental, social and governance performance. The work in progress attempts to translate ESG indicators into CSR ratings. This scoring may enable researchers to test the hypothesis that the cryptocurrency markets exhibit a three-dimensional CSR risk premium.

Keywords : Cryptocurrencies; ESG; CSR; Portfolio Simulation; Market Efficiency

Abstract :“加密货币”和“可持续性”是金融界最常见的流行语之一。加密货币的发展趋势得益于指数回报率以及区块链技术，区块链技术革命了许多商业领域。另一方面，环境、社会和治理（ESG）由于负责任管理的资产的显著增长以及公众对企业社会责任（CSR）实践的日益严格的监督而受到重要关注。然而，将加密货币和ESG结合起来的研究很少。鉴于无形资产在加密货币估值中所占份额很大，我们认为这些市场可能会出现与ESG相关的风险溢价。利用历史上不平衡的加密货币样本，我们对这一假设进行了实证检验。初步结果支持这一假设。事实上，加密货币市场确实存在环境风险溢价。通过对能量效率的衡量，我们的研究结果表明，高“生态效率”的加密货币组合显著优于低“生态效率”的加密货币组合。应通过控制解释超额收益的风险因素进行进一步评估，如沈、厄克哈特和王（2020）所述。持怀疑态度的读者可能会认为，这种溢价是通过将比特币纳入高“生态效率”投资组合获得的。然而，当控制比特币的市场份额时，回报的程度不受影响。虽然采矿协议的环境问题和与市场监管相关的治理问题已经被大量表达，但伦理观点也考虑了社会外部性。我们认为，加密货币的企业社会责任概况的定义应类似于传统的债务和股权发行人。本着这种精神，我们提出了一个网格，使有社会意识的投资者能够评估加密货币的环境、社会和治理绩效。正在进行的工作试图将ESG指标转化为企业社会责任评级。这一评分可能使研究人员能够检验加密货币市场表现出三维CSR风险溢价的假设。

Keywords :加密货币；ESG；企业社会责任；投资组合模拟；市场效率

\[8\] Cinderella's Slipper: A Better Approach to Regulating Cryptoassets as Securities

标题:灰姑娘的拖鞋：将加密资产作为证券进行监管的更好方法

作者:Carol R. Goforth(University of Arkansas - School of Law)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3744254

Abstract : The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) seeks both to protect investors and to promote efficient capital formation, but in the context of cryptoassets these goals sometimes collide. The SEC vigorously reacts to fraudulent offerings of cryptoassets but has had to do so by forcing crypto into an antiquated framework designed with very different interests in mind. Even worse than the convoluted and complex arguments needed to force crypto into the existing category of “investment contracts,” once crypto is treated as a security, a host of onerous and inapt disclosure requirements and regulations follow. Developers, promoters, exchanges, and others who might assist in the sale of such assets are all forced into a regime that was never intended to cover this new class of assets. This Article therefore suggests changes to the existing regulatory regime to more fairly apportion duties and responsibilities between regulators, issuers, promoters, and purchasers. This Article suggests that the SEC is the appropriate agency to oversee transactions in cryptoassets, but the underlying legislation should be amended to create a new category of securities, with different disclosure requirements and exemptions tailored to the informational needs of potential crypto purchasers. Maintaining the current anti-fraud rules will protect the public while allowing for innovation in this rapidly moving space. It will avoid wasting assets of both regulators and the regulated by eliminating the debate over whether crypto is or is not a security and will avoid duplication of efforts between the SEC and other federal regulators. It will also improve the relevance of available information for potential purchasers. This approach has the dual advantage of facilitating both parts of the SEC’s mission: protection of investors while supporting innovative capital formation for legitimate crypto enterprises.
