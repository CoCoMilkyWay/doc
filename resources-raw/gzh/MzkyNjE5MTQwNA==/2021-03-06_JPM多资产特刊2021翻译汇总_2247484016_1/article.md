\[2\] Deep Value

标题:深度价值

作者:Cliff Asness, John Liew, Lasse Heje Pedersen and Ashwin Thapar

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/4/11;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.215

Abstract : The authors define deep value as episodes in which the valuation spread between cheap and expensive securities is especially wide relative to its history. Examining global individual equities, equity index futures, currencies, and global bonds, the authors find that deep value is (1) highly compensated; (2) related to worsening fundamentals; (3) associated with higher risk but not fully explained by known risk factors; and (4) characterized by selling pressure related to overextrapolation of past returns and, although arbitrageurs take the other side, they face elevated trading costs and risk. These findings support a theory of return extrapolation driving the value risk premium over other behavioral and rational explanations.

Key Finding

\- ▪ The evolution of asset returns and fundamentals around deep value episodes—defined as periods with an especially wide valuation spread between cheap and expensive securities—offers a new window to understand markets and differentiate competing theories of the value premium.

\- ▪ Deep value episodes have historically been characterized by poor past performance of value investing and high future returns to value investing.

\- ▪ During these episodes, value stocks experience worsening earnings fundamentals, negative sentiment in news stories, selling pressure, and higher limits to arbitrage

\- nevertheless, sophisticated arbitrageurs pursue these opportunities.

\- TOPICS: Global markets, portfolio construction, risk management, quantitative methods

Abstract:作者将深度价值定义为相对于历史而言，廉价和昂贵证券之间的估值价差特别大的时期。通过对全球个股、股指期货、货币和全球债券的考察，作者发现，深度价值具有（1）高补偿；（2）与基本面恶化相关；（3）与高风险相关，但未被已知风险因素充分解释；（4）其特点是与过去收益过度剥离有关的抛售压力，尽管套利者选择了另一边，但他们面临着高昂的交易成本和风险。这些发现支持了一种收益外推理论，它推动了价值风险溢价高于其他行为和理性解释。

Key Finding

\- ▪ 资产收益率和基本面围绕深度价值阶段的演变，深度价值阶段定义为在廉价和昂贵证券之间具有特别广泛的估值差的时期，为理解市场和区分价值溢价的竞争理论提供了一个新的窗口。

\- ▪ 历史上，深度价值事件的特点是价值投资的过去表现不佳，价值投资的未来回报率较高。

\- ▪ 在这些事件中，价值股经历了盈利基本面恶化、新闻报道中的负面情绪、抛售压力和套利的更高限制

\- 然而，成熟的套利者追求这些机会

\- 主题：全球市场、投资组合构建、风险管理、定量方法

\[4\] Asset Allocation and Private Market Investing

标题:资产配置与私人市场投资

作者:Junying Shen, Ding Li, Grace (Tiantian) Qiu, Vishv Jeet, Michelle (Yu) Teng and Ki Cheong Wong

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/4/71;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.211

Abstract : Investors have been increasing their allocations to private assets, seeking higher returns and better portfolio diversification. However, as this allocation increases, the liquidity characteristics of their portfolios change. The authors create a framework that links bottom-up private asset investing with top-down asset allocation. Private asset cash flows are consistently modeled together with public asset returns and risk that, in turn, drive portfolio construction. This helps investors analyze how allocations to illiquid private assets, in combination with their commitment strategy, may affect their portfolio’s ability to respond to various liquidity demands. By measuring the potential trade-off among asset allocations, total portfolio performance, and the frequency of certain liquidity events with different severities, this framework can help investors quantify the interaction between their portfolio structure and performance and formalize their decision making around portfolio liquidity choices.

TOPICS: Real assets/alternative investments/private equity, portfolio construction, performance measurement, tail risks

Key Finding

\- ▪ We present a framework to systematically address various liquidity issues for multi-asset portfolios.

\- ▪ Liquidity requirements have a clear impact on portfolio structure and expected performance.

\- ▪ Commitment pacing of private investments plays a crucial role in the trade-off between liquidity and performance.

Abstract:投资者一直在增加对私人资产的配置，寻求更高的回报和更好的投资组合多样化。然而，随着这种配置的增加，其投资组合的流动性特征也会发生变化。作者创建了一个框架，将自下而上的私人资产投资与自上而下的资产配置联系起来。私人资产现金流与公共资产回报和风险一起建模，而公共资产回报和风险反过来又推动投资组合的构建。这有助于投资者分析如何配置非流动性私人资产，结合他们的承诺策略，可能会影响他们的投资组合对各种流动性需求的反应能力。通过衡量资产配置、总投资组合绩效和不同严重程度的流动性事件频率之间的潜在权衡，该框架可以帮助投资者量化其投资组合结构和绩效之间的相互作用，并使其围绕投资组合流动性选择的决策形式化。

主题：实物资产/另类投资/私募股权、投资组合构建、绩效衡量、尾部风险

Key Finding

\- ▪ 我们提出了一个框架来系统地解决多资产组合的各种流动性问题。

\- ▪ 流动性要求对投资组合结构和预期业绩有明显影响。

\- ▪ 私人投资的承诺步调在流动性和绩效之间的权衡中起着至关重要的作用。

\[5\] Forecasting Long-Horizon Volatility for Strategic Asset Allocation

标题:战略资产配置的长期波动预测

作者:Mirko Cardinale, Narayan Y. Naik and Varun Sharma

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/4/83;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.212

Abstract : Long-term volatility is a key forecasting input for strategic asset allocation analysis, yet most studies on volatility models have focused on short horizons. The authors use a large sample of global equity and bond indexes since 1934 to test the predictive power of different long-horizon volatility models. Their findings suggest that the best approach to forecasting long-horizon volatility is to use a long historical window and capture both long-term mean reversion and short-term volatility clustering properties. The results show that the authors’ model specification does a better job of reducing forecasting errors than does a naïve model based on the simple extrapolation of historical volatility.

TOPICS: Portfolio construction, volatility measures, quantitative methods, statistical methods, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ This study tests the predictive power of different long-horizon volatility models using a large sample of global equity and bond indexes since 1934.

\- ▪ The best approach to forecasting long-horizon volatility is to use a long historical window and capture both long-term mean reversion and short-term volatility clustering properties.

\- ▪ The results show that the proposed model specification does a better job of reducing forecasting errors than does a naïve model based on the simple extrapolation of historical volatility.

Abstract:长期波动性是战略资产配置分析的一个重要预测输入，然而大多数波动性模型的研究都集中在短期。作者使用1934年以来全球股票和债券指数的大样本来检验不同长期波动模型的预测能力。他们的研究结果表明，预测长期波动性的最佳方法是使用一个长期的历史窗口，并捕捉长期均值回归和短期波动聚类特性。结果表明，作者的模型规范在减少预测误差方面比基于历史波动率简单外推的天真模型做得更好。

主题：投资组合构建，波动性度量，定量方法，统计方法，绩效度量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 本文以1934年以来全球股票和债券指数为样本，检验了不同长周期波动率模型的预测能力。

\- ▪ 预测长期波动性的最佳方法是使用一个长期历史窗口，并捕捉长期均值回归和短期波动性聚类特性。;▪ 结果表明，与基于历史波动率简单外推的简单模型相比，所提出的模型规范在减少预测误差方面做得更好。

\[6\] Managing Portfolio Volatility

标题:管理投资组合波动性

作者:Michael Stamos and Thomas Zimmerer

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/4/99;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.207

Abstract : The authors revisit asset allocation strategies that aim at actively managing the volatility of multi-asset-class portfolios in response to time-varying volatility forecasts. They use the historical data of 29 major market indexes covering global equities, bonds, currencies, and commodities and apply a common set of exponentially weighted volatility estimates to them. The authors find that active volatility management is beneficial for most of these asset classes and for mixed asset portfolios, leading to more consistent wealth accumulation over time. In cross-validations, they find that fast-moving volatility forecasts seem beneficial because they have better forecasting accuracy and produce economic gains in terms of risk accuracy and performance. The authors also find significant reduction of tail risks for most assets, except for bonds, where the reduction is minor.

TOPICS: Portfolio construction, volatility measures, risk management, tail risks, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ We find that active volatility management is beneficial for many major asset classes and for mixed asset portfolios, leading to more consistent wealth accumulation over time.

\- ▪ In cross-validations, we find that fast-moving volatility forecasts seem beneficial because they have better forecasting accuracy and produce economic gains in terms of risk accuracy and performance.

\- ▪ We also find significant reduction of tail risks for most assets, except for bonds, where the reduction is minor.

Abstract:作者重新审视了旨在积极管理多资产类别投资组合波动性以应对时变波动性预测的资产配置策略。他们使用涵盖全球股票、债券、货币和大宗商品的29个主要市场指数的历史数据，并对这些指数应用一套通用的指数加权波动率估计。作者发现，主动波动性管理对这些资产类别和混合资产组合中的大多数都是有益的，随着时间的推移，会导致更一致的财富积累。在交叉验证中，他们发现快速波动率预测似乎是有益的，因为它们具有更好的预测准确性，并在风险准确性和绩效方面产生经济收益。作者还发现，大多数资产的尾部风险显著降低，债券除外，债券的尾部风险降低幅度较小。

主题：投资组合构建，波动性度量，风险管理，尾部风险，绩效度量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 我们发现，积极的波动率管理有利于许多主要资产类别和混合资产组合，导致随着时间的推移，财富积累更加一致。

\- ▪ 在交叉验证中，我们发现快速波动率预测似乎是有益的，因为它们具有更好的预测精度，并在风险准确性和绩效方面产生经济收益。

\- ▪ 我们还发现大多数资产的尾部风险显著降低，债券除外，债券的尾部风险降低幅度较小。

\[7\] Fuzzy Factors and Asset Allocation

标题:模糊因素与资产配置

作者:Alexander Rudin and Daniel Farley

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/4/110;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.214

Abstract : In this article, the authors discuss asset allocation through the factor exposure lens. They argue that a factor dimension brings novel challenges, including ambiguity in investor objectives, a multitude of suitable factor definitions, and the time-varying nature of asset/factor correlations. Such new classes of uncertainties do not fit neatly into the probabilistic framework that is at the core of modern portfolio theory. As a remedy, the authors suggest leveraging fuzzy set theory, which has been specifically created to deal with ambiguities in process objectives, component definitions, and relationships. The authors review basics of the theory, illustrate how to apply it to portfolio optimization problems, and discuss practical application of such a fuzzy asset allocation framework.

TOPICS: Factor-based models, portfolio theory, quantitative methods

Key Finding

\- ▪ Most existing factor-based asset allocation frameworks imply quasi-deterministic linkage between assets and factors. At the same time, a broad body of evidence supports the existence of a broad-stroke relationship.

\- ▪ Introducing factor considerations into portfolio construction also brings genuinely novel challenges, including vagueness of related investor objectives, time-varying asset/factor relationships, and ambiguity of factor definitions.

\- ▪ We show that using the so-called fuzzy set theory may serve as a simple and entirely intuitive framework for accommodating such challenges. Fuzzy set theory was originally introduced in the 1960s and since then has grown into a well-developed branch of applied mathematics and artificial intelligence. We illustrate the basics of the theory and the benefits of applying it to both factor asset allocation tasks and portfolio construction problems more generally.

Abstract:在这篇文章中，作者讨论了资产配置的因素暴露镜头。他们认为，因子维度带来了新的挑战，包括投资者目标的模糊性、多种合适的因子定义以及资产/因子相关性的时变性。这类新的不确定性并不完全符合现代投资组合理论核心的概率框架。作为补救措施，作者建议利用模糊集理论，这是专门用来处理模糊的过程目标，组件定义和关系。作者回顾了该理论的基础知识，说明了如何将其应用于投资组合优化问题，并讨论了这种模糊资产配置框架的实际应用。

主题：基于因素的模型，投资组合理论，定量方法

Key Finding

\- ▪ 大多数现有的基于要素的资产配置框架暗示着资产和要素之间的准确定性联系。同时，大量的证据支持广泛中风关系的存在。

\- ▪ 在投资组合构建中引入因子考虑也带来了真正新颖的挑战，包括相关投资者目标的模糊性、时变资产/因子关系以及因子定义的模糊性。

\- ▪ 我们表明，使用所谓的模糊集理论可以作为一个简单和完全直观的框架，以适应这种挑战。模糊集理论最初是在20世纪60年代提出的，此后发展成为应用数学和人工智能的一个成熟分支。我们说明了该理论的基础知识，以及将其应用于更普遍的要素资产配置任务和投资组合构建问题的好处。

\[8\] Climate Change and Asset Allocation: A Distinction That Makes a Difference

Abstract : It has become generally accepted that climate change affects economic variables such as growth and inflation. Via these economic channels, it is likely that climate change will also affect asset returns and risks. The authors provide evidence-based estimates of what these effects are and discuss how to incorporate climate change to build portfolios that are robust to a variety of climate change scenarios.

\- ▪ A simple example is used to show how to incorporate various climate change scenarios into asset allocation decisions.

主题：投资组合构建，ESG投资，风险管理，尾部风险

Key Finding

\- ▪ 气候变化可能会降低经济增长和股票回报率，同时加剧股市波动。

\- ▪ 用一个简单的例子说明如何将各种气候变化情景纳入资产配置决策中。

\- ▪ 基于传统和高评级环境、社会和治理指数的经验风险和回报，使用约束优化实现具有可持续性目标或约束的投资结果是一个简单的问题。

\[9\] Measuring Investment Skill in Multi-Asset Strategies: An Empirical Study of the Information Coefficient as Weighted Rank Correlation

标题:多资产投资策略中投资技巧的度量：基于信息系数加权秩相关的实证研究

作者:Steve Q. Xia and Joseph Simonian

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/4/135;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.208

Abstract : Over the past decade, the investment industry has come to appreciate the importance of asset allocation and its role in achieving clients’ financial objectives. The result has been a profound change in the landscape for the industry in terms of both product design and distribution. Increasingly, the asset flow to investment managers has been heavily driven by multi-asset funds and balanced funds rather than individual stock or bond funds. With the growing prominence of multi-asset investing, the role of active asset allocation has also become more important. For investors who are evaluating multi-asset funds and for asset allocators trying to set expected asset allocation alpha, the measurement of asset allocation skill is a crucial task. To the latter end, this article provides an intuitive methodology to measure asset allocation skill within the formal context of the fundamental law of active management, a well-known characterization of portfolio managers’ alpha generation process. Specifically, the authors show how weighted rank correlation provides an intuitive and transparent version of the information coefficient. Their study is framed within a novel simulation-based framework that they use to analyze the impact of asset allocation skill and its implications for estimating reasonable expectations of asset allocation alpha.

TOPICS: Portfolio construction, exchange-traded funds and applications, performance measurement, quantitative methods, statistical methods

Key Finding

\- ▪ Weighted rank correlation provides an intuitive and nuanced way to express the information coefficient—the relationship between ex ante and realized returns.

\- ▪ The bespoke simulation framework described in the article can provide insight into the relationship between different skill levels and expected asset allocation alpha.
