\[1\] Local Crowding‐Out in China

标题:中国的局部挤出

作者:YI HUANG,MARCO PAGANO,UGO PANIZZA

首次发表时间:26 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12966

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12966

Abstract : In China, between 2006 and 2013, local public debt crowded out the investment of private firms by tightening their funding constraints while leaving state‐owned firms' investment unaffected. We establish this result using a purpose‐built data set for Chinese local public debt. Private firms invest less in cities with more public debt, with the reduction in investment larger for firms located farther from banks in other cities or more dependent on external funding. Moreover, in cities where public debt is high, private firms' investment is more sensitive to internal cash flow.

Abstract :在中国，2006年至2013年间，地方公债收紧了民营企业的融资约束，同时不影响国有企业的投资，从而挤占了民营企业的投资。我们利用一个专门针对中国地方公债的数据集来建立这个结果。私人企业在公共债务较多的城市投资较少，而距离其他城市银行较远或更依赖外部资金的企业投资减少幅度更大。此外，在公共债务高企的城市，私营企业的投资对内部现金流更为敏感。

\[2\] Every Cloud Has a Silver Lining: Fast Trading, Microwave Connectivity, and Trading Costs

标题:每个云都有一线希望：快速交易、微波连接和交易成本

作者:ANDRIY SHKILKO,KONSTANTIN SOKOLOV

首次发表时间:27 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12969

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12969

Abstract : Modern markets are characterized by speed differentials, with some traders being fractions of a second faster than others. Theoretical models suggest that such differentials may have both positive and negative effects on liquidity and gains from trade. We examine these effects by studying a series of exogenous weather episodes that temporarily remove the speed advantages of the fastest traders by disrupting their microwave networks. The disruptions are associated with lower adverse selection and lower trading costs. In additional analysis, we show that the long‐term removal of speed differentials results in similar effects and also increases gains from trade.

Abstract :现代市场的特点是速度差，有些交易者比其他人快几分之一秒。理论模型表明，这种差异可能对流动性和贸易收益产生积极和消极的影响。我们通过研究一系列外生的天气事件来检验这些影响，这些事件通过中断最快的交易者的微波网络来暂时消除他们的速度优势。这种干扰与较低的逆向选择和较低的交易成本有关。在附加分析中，我们表明，长期消除速度差异会产生类似的效果，同时也会增加贸易收益。

\[3\] Credit Rating Inflation and Firms' Investments

标题:信用评级通货膨胀与企业投资

作者:ITAY GOLDSTEIN,CHONG HUANG

首次发表时间:10 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12961

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12961

Abstract : We analyze credit rating effects on firm investments in a rational bond financing game that features a feedback loop. The credit rating agency (CRA) inflates the rating, providing a biased but informative signal to creditors. Creditors' response to the rating affects the firm's investment decision and thus its credit quality, which is reflected in the rating. The CRA might reduce ex ante economic efficiency, which results solely from its strategic effect: the CRA assigns more firms high ratings and allows them to gamble for resurrection. We derive empirical predictions on the determinants of rating standards and inflation and discuss policy implications.

Abstract :在一个具有反馈回路的理性债券融资博弈中，我们分析了信用评级对企业投资的影响。信用评级机构（CRA）夸大了评级，向债权人提供了一个有偏见但信息丰富的信号。债权人对评级的反应影响企业的投资决策，进而影响企业的信用质量，信用质量反映在评级中。CRA可能会降低事前的经济效率，而这完全是由于它的战略效应：CRA给更多的公司分配高评级，并允许它们为复苏而赌博。我们对评级标准和通货膨胀的决定因素进行了实证预测，并讨论了政策含义。

\[4\] Safety Transformation and the Structure of the Financial System

标题:金融体系的安全与转型

作者:WILLIAM DIAMOND

首次发表时间:27 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12967

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12967

Abstract : This paper studies how a financial system that is organized to efficiently create safe assets responds to macroeconomic shocks. Financial intermediaries face a cost of bearing risk, so they choose the least risky portfolio that backs their issuance of riskless deposits: a diversified pool of nonfinancial firms' debt. Nonfinancial firms choose their capital structure to exploit the resulting segmentation between debt and equity markets. Increased safe asset demand yields larger and riskier intermediaries and more levered firms. Quantitative easing reduces the size and riskiness of intermediaries and can decrease firm leverage, despite reducing borrowing costs at the zero lower bound.

Abstract :本文研究的是一个金融体系如何有效地创造安全资产，以应对宏观经济冲击。金融中介机构面临承担风险的成本，因此他们选择风险最小的投资组合来支持其无风险存款的发行：一个多元化的非金融企业债务池。非金融企业选择自己的资本结构来利用由此产生的债务和股票市场之间的分割。安全资产需求的增加会产生规模更大、风险更高的中介机构和杠杆率更高的公司。量化宽松政策降低了中介机构的规模和风险，并降低了公司杠杆率，尽管借贷成本降低到了零下限。

\[5\] The Forced Safety Effect: How Higher Capital Requirements Can Increase Bank Lending

标题:强制安全效应：提高资本金要求如何增加银行贷款

作者:SALEEM BAHAJ,FREDERIC MALHERBE

首次发表时间:10 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12958

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12958

Abstract : Government guarantees generate an implicit subsidy for banks. A capital requirement reduces this subsidy, through a simple liability composition effect. However, the guarantees also make a bank undervalue loans that generates surplus in states of the world in which it defaults. Raising the capital requirement makes the bank safer, which alleviates this problem. We refer to this mechanism, which we argue is empirically relevant, as the forced safety effect.

Abstract :政府担保给银行带来了隐性补贴。资本要求通过一个简单的负债构成效应来减少这种补贴。然而，这些担保也使银行低估了贷款的价值，这些贷款在其违约的国家产生了盈余。提高资本金要求可以使银行更加安全，从而缓解了这一问题。我们将这种机制称为强制安全效应，我们认为这一机制在经验上是相关的。

\[6\] Monetary Policy and Global Banking

标题:货币政策与全球银行业

作者:FALK BRÄUNING,VICTORIA IVASHINA

首次发表时间:10 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12959

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12959

Abstract : When central banks adjust interest rates, the opportunity cost of lending in local currency changes, but—absent frictions—there is no spillover effect to lending in other currencies. However, when equity capital is limited, global banks must benchmark domestic and foreign lending opportunities. We show that, in equilibrium, the marginal return on foreign lending is affected by the interest rate differential, with lower domestic rates leading to an increase in local lending, at the expense of a reduction in foreign lending. We test our prediction in the context of changes in interest rates in six major currency areas.

Abstract :当中央银行调整利率时，本币贷款的机会成本会发生变化，但在没有摩擦的情况下，不会对其他货币贷款产生溢出效应。然而，当股本有限时，全球银行必须以国内外贷款机会为基准。我们发现，在均衡状态下，国外贷款的边际收益受利率差的影响，国内利率降低导致本地贷款增加，而以减少外国贷款为代价。我们在六个主要货币区利率变化的背景下检验我们的预测。

\[7\] Sovereign Debt Portfolios, Bond Risks, and the Credibility of Monetary Policy

标题:主权债务组合、债券风险和货币政策的可信度

作者:WENXIN DU,CAROLIN E. PFLUEGER,JESSE SCHREGER

首次发表时间:26 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12965

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12965

Abstract : We document that governments whose local currency debt provides them with greater hedging benefits actually borrow more in foreign currency. We introduce two features into a government's debt portfolio choice problem to explain this finding: risk‐averse lenders and lack of monetary policy commitment. A government without commitment chooses excessively countercyclical inflation ex post, which leads risk‐averse lenders to require a risk premium ex ante. This makes local currency debt too expensive from the government's perspective and thereby discourages the government from borrowing in its own currency.

Abstract :我们的文件表明，那些本币债务为它们提供了更大的对冲利益的政府实际上借入了更多的外币。我们在政府债务组合选择问题中引入了两个特征来解释这一发现：厌恶风险的贷款人和缺乏货币政策承诺。一个没有承诺的政府事后会选择过度的反周期通胀，这会导致厌恶风险的贷款人在事前要求风险溢价。从政府的角度看，这使得本币债务过于昂贵，从而阻碍了政府以本币借款。

\[8\] The Employment Effects of Faster Payment: Evidence from the Federal Quickpay Reform

标题:快速支付对就业的影响：来自联邦快速支付改革的证据

作者:JEAN‐NOËL BARROT,RAMANA NANDA

首次发表时间:02 June 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12955

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12955

Abstract : We study the impact of Quickpay, a reform that permanently accelerated payments to small business contractors of the U.S. government. We find a strong direct effect of the reform on employment growth at the firm level. However, we document substantial crowding out of nontreated firms' employment within local labor markets. While the overall net employment effect is positive, it is close to zero in tight labor markets. Our results highlight an important channel for alleviating financing constraints in small firms, but emphasize the general‐equilibrium effects of large‐scale interventions, which can lead to lower aggregate outcomes depending on labor market conditions.

Abstract :我们研究Quickpay的影响，这项改革永久性地加速了对美国政府小企业承包商的付款。我们发现改革对企业层面的就业增长有着强烈的直接影响。然而，我们发现，在当地劳动力市场上，未经处理的公司的就业大量被挤出。虽然总的净就业效应是正面的，但在劳动力市场紧张的情况下，净就业效应接近于零。我们的结果强调了缓解小企业融资约束的一个重要渠道，但强调了大规模干预的一般均衡效应，根据劳动力市场状况，这可能导致较低的总产出。

\[9\] Stock Market Returns and Consumption

标题:股市收益与消费

作者:MARCO DI MAGGIO,AMIR KERMANI,KAVEH MAJLESI

首次发表时间:27 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12968

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12968

Abstract : This paper employs Swedish data on households' stock holdings to investigate how consumption responds to changes in stock market returns. We instrument the actual capital gains and dividend payments with past portfolio weights. Unrealized capital gains lead to a marginal propensity to consume of 23% for the bottom 50% of the wealth distribution and about 3% for the top 30% of the wealth distribution. Household consumption is significantly more responsive to dividend payouts across all parts of the wealth distribution. Our findings are consistent with households treating capital gains and dividends as separate sources of income.

Abstract :本文利用瑞典家庭持股数据，研究消费对股市回报率变化的反应。我们用过去的投资组合权重来衡量实际资本收益和股息支付。未实现的资本收益导致财富分配中最底层的50%的边际消费倾向为23%，而财富分配最高的30%的人的边际消费倾向约为3%。在财富分配的各个部分，家庭消费对股息支付的反应明显更大。我们的研究结果与将资本收益和股息作为独立收入来源的家庭是一致的。
