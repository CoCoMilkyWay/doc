\[1\] Determinants of the Bitcoin Performance during the COVID-19 Pandemic Period: How Can We Measure the Fear?

标题:COVID-19大流行期间比特币表现的决定因素：我们如何衡量恐惧？

作者:Evangelos Vasileiou

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3791745

Abstract : The purpose of this paper is to present a behavioral analysis on the impact of the pandemic on BitCoin (BTC) prices. How fear of COVID-19 influenced risk aversion on the part of the investors and BTC performance during the period 31.12.2019-30.09.2020. Most early studies that examine the influence of the pandemic on asset prices use as an explanatory variable the number of new cases. In this study, we show that the number of cases is not the appropriate variable to explain the BTC prices. In order to quantitatively include a fear index into our analysis, we employ a code which is based on the Google searches which include the term “Coronavirus”. The higher the index, the more the fear due to the pandemic. The empirical findings confirm our assumptions that the newly suggested fear index explains the BTC performance with increase statistical significance, and that the number of cases is not the most appropriate variable to explain the BTC prices during the examined period. The methodology and the outcome of our study could be useful to investors, practitioners and scholars whose intention is to examine how asset prices, BTC prices in our case, perform during periods of high stress.

Keywords : Behavioral Finance; COVID-19; Health Risk; Risk Aversion; Google Trends

Abstract :本文的目的是提出一个行为分析的影响，大流行比特币（BTC）的价格。2019年12月31日至2020年9月30日期间，投资者对COVID-19的恐惧如何影响风险规避和BTC表现。大多数早期的研究都是研究流感大流行对资产价格的影响，将新病例的数量作为解释变量。在这项研究中，我们发现案例的数量不是解释BTC价格的合适变量。为了定量地将恐惧指数纳入我们的分析，我们使用了一个基于Google搜索的代码，其中包括“冠状病毒”一词。指数越高，对流感大流行的恐惧就越大。实证结果证实了我们的假设，即新提出的恐惧指数解释了BTC的表现，具有更高的统计显著性，并且案例数量不是解释研究期间BTC价格的最合适变量。我们的研究方法和结果可能对投资者、从业者和学者有用，他们的目的是研究资产价格，即我们案例中的BTC价格，在高压力时期的表现。

Keywords :行为金融学；COVID-19；健康风险；风险规避；Google趋势

\[2\] An Empirical Study on Influence of Economic Fundamentals on Indian Stock Market

标题:经济基本面对印度股市影响的实证研究

作者:Baranidharan S

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3748921

Abstract : The study investigates the dynamic effect of macroeconomic variables on BSE returns. In recent phenomena, stock market is a leading indicator of an economy growth and economic fundamentals are determinants of the stock market movements. The study results found that selected macroeconomic variables were weakly correlated with BSE and each other variable. The macroeconomic variables influenced approximately 93 percent of the variation on BSE return, which indicated the high impact of macroeconomic variables on BSE return. The results of Durbin Wastan test exhibited that no auto correlation in the selected sample of the study. The estimated coefficients of CMR, GP, IIP, INF R, MKT TO BSE and TB recorded negative impact on BSE. The coefficient of EXCR, M3 and MKT CAP BSE exercised positive effect on BSE. In cointegration values of selected macroeconomic variables of likelihood ratio were higher than critical values at 99, 95 and 90 percent and it indicated that there was long run relation. The VAR results revealed that the shock of macroeconomic variables administered to BSE was very flat and not statistically significant. The stock market was not affected in the short term by the macroeconomic variables. The CUSUM Test exhibited the existence of stability in the long run coefficient. There were only call money rate and inflation rate caused BSE sensex returns. The study concludes that there was stability in the long run cointegration and high impact of macroeconomic variable on BSE sensex but absence of short term relation during the study period. EXCR, GP, MKT CAP BSE and MKT TO BSE were highly residual plotted. the study would suggest the investor community should aware and to understand the movement of CMR, IIP, INF R, GP, MKT TO BSE and TB. These variables cause negative effect and which may be irrespective of the price fluctuation. The study advised that investor community should study the changes of macro-eocnomic variables regime to enhance their returns and investments in capital market. The policy makers needs to improve the economy policy and in terms of reduce the inflation rate, call money rate volatility, high interest rate, enhance the growth of money supply, appreciation of exchange rate, increase the net FIIs, increases the growth of IIP and control the gold price fluctuation, from these, variables shows glossiness of stock market development and the growth of economy of a country.

Keywords : Macroeconomic, Stock Market, Economy, Investment, Inflation, Price, Institutional Investor, Investment Decision, Policy

Abstract :研究了宏观经济变量对疯牛病收益的动态影响。在最近的现象中，股市是经济增长的主要指标，经济基本面是股市走势的决定因素。研究结果发现，所选取的宏观经济变量与疯牛病及其他变量的相关性较弱。宏观经济变量对疯牛病收益的影响约为93%，表明宏观经济变量对疯牛病收益的影响较大。Durbin-Wastan检验的结果显示，在所选的研究样本中没有自相关。CMR、GP、IIP、INF-R、MKT对BSE和TB的估计系数对BSE有负面影响。EXCR、M3和MKT-CAP-BSE系数对BSE有正向影响。在协整分析中，所选宏观经济变量的似然比均高于临界值，分别为99%、95%和90%，表明两者之间存在长期的关系。VAR结果显示，BSE宏观经济变量的冲击非常平缓，没有统计学意义。股市短期内不受宏观经济变量的影响。CUSUM检验表明长期系数存在稳定性。只有看涨期权利率和通货膨胀率引起了BSE敏感指数的收益。研究结果表明，长期协整关系是稳定的，宏观经济变量对BSE-sensex的影响较大，但短期内不存在协整关系。EXCR、GP、MKT-CAP-BSE和MKT-TO-BSE是高度残差图。这项研究将建议投资者群体应该意识到并了解CMR、IIP、INF R、GP、MKT向BSE和TB的转移。这些变量会造成负面影响，这可能与价格波动无关。研究建议投资者群体应研究宏观经济变量制度的变化，以提高其在资本市场的投资收益。政策制定者需要完善经济政策，从降低通货膨胀率、呼吁货币利率波动、提高利率、促进货币供应量增长、汇率升值、提高净金融投资收益率、提高国际投资头寸增长率、控制金价波动等方面，从这些方面着手，变量反映了一个国家股市发展和经济增长的光泽度。

Keywords :宏观经济，股票市场，经济，投资，通货膨胀，价格，机构投资者，投资决策，政策

\[3\] Intangible Capital in Factor Models

标题:要素模型中的无形资本

作者:Huseyin Gulen,Dongmei Li,Ryan H. Peters,Morad Zekhnini

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3725002

Abstract : We study the impact of increasingly substantial intangible capital on popular empirical factor models. Conceptually, we explicitly incorporate intangible investment/capital into the dividend discount model in Fama and French (2015) and the classical q-factor model in Hou, Xue, and Zhang (2015) and illustrate the distinctive effects of intangibles on expected stock returns via well-known return predictors such as the book-to-market ratio, investment, and profitability. Our theoretical frameworks highlight the importance of separating tangible from intangible investments and addressing the effects of intangible investment on profitability and other valuation measures. Empirically, we show that incorporating intangibles significantly improves the Fama-French three- and five-factor models and the q-factor model, especially over recent decades during which intangible investments become even more crucial to firms' growth and success.

Keywords : Intangible Investment/Capital, Factors, Factor Models

Abstract :我们研究了日益庞大的无形资本对流行的实证因素模型的影响。在概念上，我们明确将无形投资/资本纳入Fama和French（2015）的股息贴现模型和Hou、Xue和Zhang（2015）的经典q因子模型，并通过众所周知的收益预测因子（如账面市盈率）说明无形资产对预期股票收益的显著影响，投资和盈利能力。我们的理论框架强调了将有形投资与无形投资分开的重要性，并强调了无形投资对盈利能力和其他估值指标的影响。实证研究表明，无形资产的引入显著改善了Fama-French三因子和五因子模型以及q因子模型，特别是在近几十年中，无形资产投资对企业的成长和成功更为重要。

Keywords :无形投资/资本、要素、要素模型

\[4\] Testing the Bulk Volume Classification Algorithm

标题:批量分类算法的测试

作者:Allen Carrion,Madhuparna Kolay

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3746731

Abstract : We document that the existing evidence that bulk volume trade classification (BVC) measures informed trading arises largely due to mis-specified tests. Simulations show that these tests detect spurious relationships in data containing only uninformed liquidity trades. We also assess the performance of BVC order imbalances in the NASDAQ HFT dataset, showing that BVC order imbalances underperform conventional order imbalance measures in detecting informed trading. The component of order flow designated by BVC as passive informed trading fails to predict returns with the correct sign. On balance, our evidence supports the use of conventional order imbalance measures to identify informed trading.

Keywords : Market Microstructure, Informed Trading, High-frequency Trading, Bulk Volume Classification, Trade classification, Order Imbalances, Simulation, Estimator Properties

Abstract :我们的文件，现有的证据表明，大宗交易分类（BVC）措施知情交易产生很大程度上是由于错误指定的测试。模拟结果显示，这些测试在只包含未知情流动性交易的数据中检测到虚假关系。我们还评估了纳斯达克HFT数据集中BVC指令不平衡的表现，显示BVC指令不平衡在检测知情交易方面不如传统的指令不平衡度量。英属维尔京群岛指定为被动知情交易的订单流的组成部分无法用正确的符号预测收益。总的来说，我们的证据支持使用传统的订单不平衡度量来识别知情交易。

Keywords :市场微观结构，知情交易，高频交易，批量分类，交易分类，订单不平衡，模拟，估计属性
