\[2\] Active Factor Completion Strategies

标题:主动因素完成策略

作者:Hubert Dichtl, Wolfgang Drobetz, Harald Lohre and Carsten Rother

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/2/9;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.193

Abstract : Embracing the concept of factor investing, the authors design a flexible framework for building out different factor completion strategies for traditional multi-asset allocations. Their notion of factor completion comprises a maximally diversified reference portfolio anchored in a multi-asset multi-factor risk model that acknowledges market factors such as equity, duration, and commodity, as well as style factors such as carry, value, momentum, and quality. The specific nature of a given factor completion strategy varies with investor preferences and constraints. The authors tailor a select set of factor completion strategies that include factor-based tail hedging, constrained factor completion, and a fully diversified multi-asset multi-factor proposition. Their framework is able to organically exploit tactical asset allocation signals without sacrificing the notion of maximum diversification. To illustrate, the authors additionally embed the common trend style that permeates many asset classes, and they also include the notion of style factor momentum.

TOPICS: Factor-based models, portfolio management/multi-asset allocation, style investing

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors design a flexible framework for building out different factor completion strategies for traditional multi-asset allocations using a maximally diversified reference portfolio anchored in a multi-asset multi-factor risk model comprising market as well as style factors.

\- ▪ The authors tailor a select set of factor completion strategies that include factor-based tail hedging, constrained factor completion, and a fully diversified multi-asset multi-factor proposition.

\- ▪ Their framework is able to organically exploit tactical asset allocation signals without sacrificing the notion of maximum diversification, demonstrated by embedding the common trend style in asset classes as well as style factor momentum.

Abstract:引入要素投资的概念，作者设计了一个灵活的框架，为传统的多资产配置制定不同的要素完成策略。他们的要素完成概念包括一个最大化多元化的参考投资组合，该投资组合锚定在一个多资产多要素风险模型中，该模型承认市场因素，如权益、期限和商品，以及风格因素，如套利、价值、动量和质量。给定因子完成策略的具体性质因投资者偏好和约束条件而异。作者定制了一套精选的因子完成策略，包括基于因子的尾部套期保值、约束因子完成和完全多样化的多资产多因子命题。他们的框架能够有机地利用战术资产配置信号，而不牺牲最大化多样化的概念。为了说明这一点，作者还嵌入了渗透到许多资产类别中的通用趋势风格，并且还包含了风格因素动量的概念。

主题：基于因素的模型、投资组合管理/多资产配置、风格投资

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者设计了一个灵活的框架，为传统的多资产配置建立不同的因素完成策略，使用一个最大化多样化的参考投资组合，锚定在一个包含市场和风格因素的多资产多因素风险模型中。

\- ▪ 作者定制了一套精选的因子完成策略，包括基于因子的尾部套期保值、约束因子完成和完全多样化的多资产多因子命题。

\- ▪ 他们的框架能够有机地利用战术性资产配置信号，而不牺牲最大多样化的概念，这通过在资产类别中嵌入共同趋势风格以及风格因素动量来证明。

\[3\] Is (Systematic) Value Investing Dead?

标题:（系统的）价值投资死了吗？

作者:Ronen Israel, Kristoffer Laursen and Scott Richardson

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/2/38;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.194

Abstract : Value investing involves buying securities that appear cheap relative to some fundamental anchor. For equity investors, that anchor is typically a measure of intrinsic value linked to financial statements. Recently, much has been written about the death of value investing. Although undoubtedly many approaches to value investing have suffered recently, the authors find the suggestion that value investing is dead to be premature. Both from a theoretical and empirical perspective, expectations of fundamental information have been and continue to be an important driver of security returns. The authors also address critiques leveled at value investing and find them generally lacking in substance.

TOPICS: Analysis of individual factors/risk premia, factor-based models, fundamental equity analysis

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors address a series of possible (ex post) rationalizations of the recent underperformance of value strategies. They find little empirical evidence or theoretical foundation to support the criticisms.

\- ▪ Building on prior academic research, the authors find strong evidence that fundamental information is relevant for stock prices.

\- ▪ However, the authors find that there are periods in which stock prices become less connected to fundamental information

\- when value underperforms significantly, it is primarily attributable to a widening gap between prices and fundamentals.

Abstract:价值投资包括购买相对于某些基本面锚而言似乎便宜的证券。对于股票投资者来说，这种锚定通常是与财务报表相关的内在价值的衡量标准。最近，关于价值投资的死亡有很多文章。尽管毫无疑问，许多价值投资的方法最近都受到了影响，但作者发现，认为价值投资已死的说法还为时过早。从理论和实证的角度来看，对基本面信息的预期一直是并将继续是证券收益的重要驱动力。作者还提出了价值投资的批评，发现它们普遍缺乏实质内容。

主题：个人因素/风险溢价分析、基于因素的模型、基本权益分析

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者提出了一系列可能的（事后）合理化的最近表现不佳的价值战略。他们很少有经验证据或理论基础来支持这些批评。▪ 在先前学术研究的基础上，作者发现了基本面信息与股价相关的有力证据。;▪ 然而，作者发现，在某些时期，股价与基本面信息的关联性降低

\- 当价值表现显著不佳时，主要是由于价格与基本面之间的差距扩大所致。

\[4\] Resurrecting the Value Premium

标题:重振价值溢价

作者:David Blitz and Matthias X. Hanauer

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/2/63;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.188

Abstract : The prolonged poor performance of the value factor has led to doubts about whether the value premium still exists. Some have noted that the observed returns still fall within statistical confidence intervals, but such arguments do not restore full confidence in the value premium. This article adds to the literature by showing that the academic value factor, HML, has not only suffered setbacks in recent years but has, in fact, been weak for decades already. However, the authors show that the value premium can be resurrected using insights that are well documented in the literature or common knowledge among practitioners. In particular, they include more powerful value metrics, apply some basic risk management, and make more effective use of the breadth of the liquid universe of stocks. Their enhanced value strategy also suffers in recent years, but this is largely explained by an extreme widening of valuation multiples similar to the late 1990s. The authors conclude that a solid value premium is still clearly present in the cross-section of stock returns.

TOPICS: Analysis of individual factors/risk premia, developed markets, emerging markets, factor-based models

Key Finding

\- ▪ The standard academic value factor has been weak for decades already, but we show that the value premium can be resurrected with a more sophisticated approach.

\- ▪ Our key enhancements are using more powerful value metrics, applying some basic risk management, and making more efficient use of opportunities in liquid stocks.

\- ▪ The enhanced value strategy also suffers in recent years, but this is largely explained by an extreme widening of valuation multiples similar to the late 1990s.

Abstract:价值因素长期表现不佳，导致人们怀疑价值溢价是否仍然存在。有人指出，观察到的回报率仍在统计置信区间内，但这些论点并不能恢复对价值溢价的完全信心。这篇文章补充了文献，表明学术价值因素，HML，不仅在最近几年遭受了挫折，但事实上，已经薄弱了几十年。然而，作者表明，价值溢价可以复活使用的见解，在文献中有很好的记录或共同的知识从业者。特别是，它们包括更强大的价值度量，应用一些基本的风险管理，并更有效地利用股票流动性的广度。近年来，他们的强化价值策略也受到了影响，但这主要是由于估值倍数的极端扩大（类似于上世纪90年代末）造成的。作者得出结论，股票回报率的横截面中仍然明显存在稳定的价值溢价。

主题：个体因素/风险溢价分析、发达市场、新兴市场、基于因素的模型

Key Finding

\- ▪ 标准学术价值因素已经弱了几十年，但我们表明，价值溢价可以用更复杂的方法复活。

\- ▪ 我们的主要改进是使用更强大的价值指标，应用一些基本的风险管理，以及更有效地利用流动性股票的机会。

\- ▪ 近年来，增强型价值策略也受到了影响，但这主要是由于估值倍数的极端扩大造成的，类似于上世纪90年代末的情况。

\[5\] Implementing Value and Momentum Strategies in Credit Portfolios

标题:在信贷组合中实施价值和动量策略

作者:Simon Polbennikov, Albert Desclée and Mathieu Dubois

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/2/82;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.190

Abstract : Implementing quantitative signals in a corporate bond portfolio can be challenging owing to high transaction costs, large variations of liquidity across individual bonds, and the requirement to follow a benchmark index. The authors design realistic simulations that illustrate the performance and characteristics of quantitatively managed portfolios of liquid corporate bonds with attractive value and momentum characteristics. They show how to implement a turnover budget to explicitly control rebalancing volumes. Such realistic strategy portfolios have significantly outperformed the Bloomberg Barclays US Corporate Bond Index after transaction costs, with information ratios above 1 since 2007. The results of this study support the case for style factor investing in credit.

TOPICS: Performance measurement, portfolio construction, simulations, style investing

Key Finding

\- ▪ Relative value and equity momentum styles can be successfully implemented in corporate bond portfolios net of transaction costs.

\- ▪ We explain how to overcome challenges related to identifying liquid tradable securities with attractive value and momentum characteristics, keeping transaction costs in check, and following a benchmark index at the same time.

\- ▪ Index-tracking portfolios that match key exposures of the US Corporate index, but also overweight bonds with attractive value and momentum characteristics, significantly outperformed the benchmark index after transaction costs, with information ratios above 1 since 2007.

Abstract:由于交易成本高、单个债券的流动性差异大以及需要遵循基准指数，在公司债券投资组合中实施量化信号可能具有挑战性。作者设计了真实的模拟来说明流动性公司债券的价值和动量特征的定量管理投资组合的性能和特点。它们展示了如何实施营业额预算来明确控制再平衡量。这种现实的策略投资组合在扣除交易成本后的表现明显优于彭博-巴克莱美国公司债券指数（Bloomberg-Barclays-US Corporate Bond Index），自2007年以来，信息比率一直高于1。本文的研究结果支持了风格因素投资在信贷中的应用。

主题：绩效评估，投资组合构建，模拟，风格投资

Key Finding

\- ▪ 在扣除交易成本后的公司债券投资组合中，相对价值和股票动量风格可以成功地实施。

\- ▪ 我们解释了如何克服与识别具有吸引力价值和动量特征的流动性可交易证券、控制交易成本以及同时遵循基准指数相关的挑战。

\- ▪ 与美国企业指数关键敞口相符的指数跟踪投资组合，以及具有吸引力价值和动量特征的增持债券，在扣除交易成本后的表现明显优于基准指数，自2007年以来，信息比率一直高于1。

\[6\] Settling the Size Matter

标题:解决尺寸问题

作者:David Blitz and Matthias X. Hanauer

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/2/99;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.187

Abstract : The size premium has failed to materialize since its discovery almost 40 years ago, but is seemingly revived when controlling for quality-versus-junk exposures. This article aims to resolve whether there exists a distinct size premium that can be captured in reality. For the United States, the authors confirm that a highly significant alpha emerges in regressions of size on quality, but for international markets, they find that the size premium remains statistically indistinguishable from zero. Moreover, the US size premium appears to be beyond the practical reach of investors because the alpha that is observed ex post in regressions cannot be captured by controlling for quality exposures ex ante. The authors also find that the significant regression alpha in the United States is entirely driven by the short side of quality. Altogether, these results imply that size only adds value in conjunction with a short position in US junk stocks. However, the authors also show that small-cap exposure is vital for unlocking the full potential of other factors, such as value and momentum. They conclude that size is weak as a stand-alone factor, but a powerful catalyst for other factors.

TOPICS: Analysis of individual factors/risk premia, factor-based models, portfolio construction

Key Finding

\- ▪ The size premium in the United States becomes significant in regressions that control for quality versus junk, but for international markets, it remains small and insignificant.

\- ▪ The US size premium appears to be beyond the reach of investors because it cannot be captured by controlling for quality-versus-junk exposures ex ante.

\- ▪ Size is weak as a stand-alone factor but a vital catalyst for unlocking the full potential of other factors, such as value and momentum.

Abstract:自近40年前发现以来，规模溢价一直未能实现，但在控制质量与垃圾敞口时，规模溢价似乎又复活了。本文旨在解决是否存在一个独特的规模溢价，可以捕捉到在现实中。对于美国，作者证实了在规模与质量的回归中出现了一个非常显著的alpha，但是对于国际市场，他们发现规模溢价在统计学上仍然无法与零区分开来。此外，美国规模溢价似乎超出了投资者的实际承受能力，因为通过事前控制质量风险敞口，无法捕捉到事后在回归中观察到的阿尔法。作者还发现，美国的显著回归alpha完全是由质量的短边驱动的。总而言之，这些结果意味着，规模只是在美国垃圾股空头头寸的情况下增加了价值。然而，作者还表明，小盘股敞口对于释放价值和动量等其他因素的全部潜力至关重要。他们得出结论，规模作为一个独立的因素是薄弱的，但对其他因素是一个强大的催化剂。

主题：个体因素/风险溢价分析，基于因素的模型，投资组合构建

Key Finding

\- ▪ 美国的规模溢价在控制质量与垃圾的回归中变得显著，但在国际市场上，规模溢价仍然很小且微不足道。

\- ▪ 美国规模的溢价似乎超出了投资者的承受能力，因为无法通过事前控制质量与垃圾风险敞口来获取。

\- ▪ 规模作为一个独立的因素是薄弱的，但却是释放价值和动力等其他因素全部潜力的重要催化剂。

\[7\] Factor Investing with Black–Litterman–Bayes: Incorporating Factor Views and Priors in Portfolio Construction

标题:Black-Litterman-Bayes的因子投资：在投资组合构建中纳入因子观点和先验

作者:Petter N. Kolm and Gordon Ritter

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/2/113;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.196

Abstract : The authors propose a general framework referred to as Black–Litterman–Bayes (BLB) for constructing optimal portfolios for factor-based investing. In the spirit of the classical Black–Litterman model, the framework allows for the incorporation of investor views and priors on factor risk premiums, including data-driven and benchmark priors. Computationally efficient closed-form formulas are provided for the (posterior) expected returns and return covariance matrix that result from integrating factor views into an arbitrage pricing theory multi-factor model. In a step-by-step procedure, the authors show how to build the prior and incorporate the factor views, demonstrating in a realistic empirical example and using a number of well-known cross-sectional US equity factors, that the BLB approach can add value to mean–variance-optimal multi-factor risk premium portfolios.

TOPICS: Factor-based models, factors, risk premia, portfolio construction, portfolio theory

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors propose a general framework referred to as Black–Litterman–Bayes (BLB) for constructing optimal portfolios for factor-based investing.

\- ▪ The framework allows for the incorporation of investor views and priors on factor risk premiums, including data-driven and benchmark priors.

\- ▪ The authors provide computationally efficient closed-form formulas for the (posterior) expected returns and return covariance matrix.

\- ▪ In a realistic empirical example, using a number of well-known cross-sectional US equity factors, they demonstrate that the BLB approach can add value to mean–variance-optimal multi-factor risk premium portfolios.

Abstract:作者提出了一个被称为Black-Litterman-Bayes（Black-Litterman-Bayes）的总体框架，用于构建基于因子的投资的最优投资组合。在经典的Black-Litterman模型的精神下，该框架允许将投资者的观点和因子风险溢价的prior（包括数据驱动的prior和基准prior）结合起来。将因子观点整合到套利定价理论的多因子模型中，给出了后验期望收益和收益协方差矩阵的闭式计算公式。在一步一步的过程中，作者展示了如何建立先验和合并因子的观点，在一个现实的实证例子中，并使用一些著名的美国股票横截面因子，证明BLB方法可以增加均值-方差最优多因子风险溢价投资组合的价值。

主题：基于因素的模型，因素，风险溢价，投资组合构建，投资组合理论

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者提出了一个被称为Black-Litterman-Bayes（Black-Litterman-Bayes）的一般框架，用于构建基于因子的投资的最优投资组合。

\- ▪ 该框架允许纳入投资者对因子风险溢价的看法和先验，包括数据驱动和基准先验。

\- ▪ 本文给出了后验期望收益和收益协方差矩阵的闭式计算公式。

\- ▪ 在一个现实的实证例子中，他们使用了许多著名的美国股票横截面因子，证明了BLB方法可以为均值-方差最优多因子风险溢价投资组合增加价值。

\[8\] Waiting for the Next Factor Wave: Daily Rebalancing around Market Cycle Transitions

标题:等待下一个因素浪潮：围绕市场周期转换的每日再平衡

作者:Kevin Khang and Antonio Picca

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/2/127;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.47.2.127

Abstract : To deliver historically observed factor premiums, long-only factor investing relied heavily on a small number of periods, when factors realized outsized returns in the midst of changing market leadership. This article shows that by rebalancing factor funds more frequently during these periods—rebalancing on a daily basis instead of monthly or biannually—investors would have achieved significantly higher factor premiums, effectively doubling the historically observed premiums of many factors. These findings indicate that to harvest factor premiums to their maximal potential, skill is needed on the part of the fund manager—an ability to tell the right moment to aggressively rebalance.

TOPICS: Analysis of individual factors/risk premia, factor-based models, Factors, risk premia

Key Finding

\- ▪ Historically, long-only factor investing relied heavily on a small number of periods with outsized returns to deliver factor premiums. These periods coincided with market cycle transitions when the makeup of market leadership changed dramatically.

\- ▪ By rebalancing frequently—daily instead of every month or six months—during market cycle transitions, a factor investor would have achieved significantly higher factor premiums, effectively doubling the historically observed premiums. This finding holds true even after accounting for much higher transaction costs driven by the higher turnover.

\- ▪ Our findings indicate that an ability to tell the right moment to rebalance more frequently may be central to harvesting factor premiums to their maximal potential.

Abstract:为了提供历史上观察到的要素溢价，仅长期要素投资在很大程度上依赖于少数时期，即要素在不断变化的市场领导地位中实现了巨大的回报。本文表明，在这些时期，通过更频繁地重新平衡因子基金，投资者可以实现更高的因子溢价，有效地将历史上观察到的许多因子溢价翻一番。这些研究结果表明，为了最大限度地挖掘因子溢价的潜力，基金经理需要具备一种能力，即判断恰当时机，积极进行再平衡。

主题：个体因素/风险溢价分析，基于因素的模型，因素，风险溢价

Key Finding

\- ▪ 从历史上看，长期纯要素投资严重依赖于少数回报率过高的时期来提供要素溢价。这些时期恰逢市场周期转换之际，市场领导层的构成发生了巨大变化。
