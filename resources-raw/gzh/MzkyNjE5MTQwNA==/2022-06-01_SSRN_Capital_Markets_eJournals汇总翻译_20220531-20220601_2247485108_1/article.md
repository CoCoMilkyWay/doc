\[1\] Search Steering in Two-Sided Platforms

标题:在双边平台上搜索转向

作者:Pedro Vaissman Guinsburg,Gustavo Saraiva

出处:-

Abstract : We study the incentives from a two-sided platform to segment the market by providing personalized search results. In our environment, a monopolistic platform is in charge of matching sellers with buyers. Upon being matched, each pair of buyer and seller negotiate prices. If they choose to transact, the platform receives a commission fee proportional to the value of the transaction. The platform is assumed to have full information over customers' and sellers' outside options. We show that in this environment the platform may have incentives to prioritize finding feasible matches to more expensive products so as to inflate market prices and, thus, the commissions it receives from transactions. By doing this, the platform maximizes the number of transactions, which can generate excess liquidity.

Keywords : Market Segmentation, Information Design,  Two-sided markets

\[2\] Equity Market Structure and the Persistence of Unsolved Problems: A Microstructure Perspective

标题:股票市场结构与未解决问题的持续性：微观结构视角

作者:Robert Schwartz,James Ross,Deniz Ozenbas

出处:Journal of Portfolio Management, Forthcoming

Abstract : This paper presents a brief historical perspective on the evolution of equity market structure and considers implications microstructure analysis has had for improving market structure.  Since the 1975 Securities Acts Amendments mandated the development of a National Market System, the robust growth of institutional investing and the impact of striking technological change have been transformative.  Yet, market impact costs and price discovery noise continue to accentuate short-period returns variance, and the paper presents empirical evidence of how sizable that accentuation is.  In dealing with this problem, the authors focus on the rules that govern how orders are integrated together and turned into trades and transaction prices.

Keywords : None

\[3\] The Illusion of the Metaverse and Meta-Economy

标题:元宇宙与元经济的幻觉

作者:David Vidal-Tomás

出处:-

Abstract : Based on the metaverse framework described in the literature, this study  aimed to assess  the current state of the meta-economy by analysing (i) economic governance and (ii) metaverse commerce. To do so, on the one hand, we analysed whether economic governance is based on tokens that can be considered reliable means of exchange by studying their price dynamics and volatile behaviour. On the other hand, after providing an empirical description of the evolution of non-fungible tokens, we evaluated the possible connectedness between economic governance (fungible tokens) and metaverse commerce (non-fungible tokens). Our results showed that (i) the meta-economy includes metaverse tokens that cannot be defined as reliable means of exchange due to their explosive behaviour and unstable performance; (ii) the metaverse is characterised by a failed meta-economy in which economic governance drives metaverse commerce; and (iii), as particular case studies, The Sandbox and Decentraland are metaverses with an unsuitable meta-economy. Therefore, at present, the current metaverse can only be defined as a speculative digital world. These outcomes underline the questions of who will be building the metaverse—crypto-start-ups or incumbents—and how the metaverse will be built—as private  or public. Consequently, in future research, developers, scholars, and companies must consider and analyse the different platforms and infrastructures that can make the metaverse a persistent reality.

Keywords : Metaverse, Meta-economy, non-fungible token, Cryptocurrency

\[4\] Turn of the Month Effect

标题:月初效应

作者:Marta Vidal,Javier Vidal-García

出处:-

Abstract : It is common to find calendar anomalies in stock markets, which have been the subject of various studies in recent decades, even showing that some of them have been disappearing through time. In this context, this article examines one of these anomalies, the end of the month effect, both in returns and in volatility in the UK stock market using capitalization-weighted indices in the period 1990-2021. The objective of this study is to analyze whether the month-end effect has been disappearing or still persists. The evidence of this research, which is carried out according to the three different periods defined in the literature to analyze the impact of the end of the month effect, shows that all stock indices under analysis obtain positive abnormal returns on the days associated to the change of the month and also show anomalies in volatility. This research not only analyzes the most relevant stock indices for the UK market, but also the stocks included in each one, obtaining a much larger sample to be able to conclude on the existence of the end of the month phenomenon. Additionally, we also examine the transaction volume to verify certain hypotheses of the end of the month effect associated to a larger cash flow.

Keywords : London Stock Exchange, Calendar Anomalies, FTSE Indices, Change of the Month Effect

\[5\] An Evolutionary Theory of Overconfidence and Loss Aversion in Adaptive Markets

标题:适应性市场中过度自信和损失厌恶的进化理论

作者:Zhongzhi Lawrence He

出处:-

Abstract : This paper develops an agent-based model of learning process that systematically organizes actual choice behavior along the path to equilibrium. The model relies on expected utility maximization to provide directions for economic agents to make choices at the margin toward their ends in adaptive markets. Thus, every action is driven by an intention but adapts to individual experience. The model provides a unified evolutionary explanation for overconfidence and loss aversion that are rationally priced in terms of the gap between willingness-to-accept (WTA) and willingness-to-pay (WTP). Specifically, inexperienced agents exhibit overconfidence for which they are willing to pay a premium to discover dominant strategies, whereas the stabilization motive drives loss aversion for which they demand a compensation for making risky choices. Market experience eventually eliminates behavioral biases by the law of diminishing marginal utility, making agents behave as if they were fully rational. The model is formulated within a reinforcement learning framework in which a simulated call market demonstrates how choice uncertainty is resolved through mutual coordination of action plans.

Keywords : Overconfidence; Loss Aversion; Adaptive Markets; Procedural Rationality; Agent-based Modeling; Reinforcement Learning

\[6\] Look Who’s Talking: An Analysis of Market Responses to Executive Earnings Call Communication

标题:看谁在说话：高管电话财报会议沟通的市场反应分析

作者:Nathan C.  Goldman,Yuan Zhang

出处:-

Abstract : We examine capital market responses to CEO communication versus other executives in the Q&A sections of earnings calls. While CEO tone is more optimistic than other executives, we find significantly stronger market responses to CEO tone than to non-CEO tone or CFO tone. This result is more pronounced for firms with weaker information environments or higher information uncertainty. Relative to non-CEO communication, CEO communication also significantly increases earnings response coefficients. Additional analyses show that CEO tone has greater predictive ability for future earnings growth than does non-CEO tone, providing a rational explanation for our main results. We observe no significant return reversals or drifts associated with executive tone, suggesting investors efficiently incorporate the information in executives’ earnings call communication. Our results underscore the importance of CEO communication with investors in corporate disclosure strategies.

Keywords : Corporate Disclosure; Earnings Calls; CEO Communication; Corporate Executives.

\[7\] VIX Modeling for a Market Insider

标题:面向市场内幕人士的VIX建模

作者:Markus Hess

出处:-

Abstract : In this paper, we extend the popular Barndorff-Nielsen Shephard stochastic volatility model to the case of a pure-jump Ornstein-Uhlenbeck equation with non-vanishing stochastic mean-reversion level. Based on this setup, we derive representations for the squared VIX process and related VIX futures prices. Having these results at hand, we introduce an initially enlarged filtration which models the view of a VIX market insider who has knowledge about the future behavior of the stochastic mean-reversion level of the squared volatility process available. In this enlarged filtration framework, we infer an explicit representation for the anticipative VIX process and obtain the associated time dynamics. We finally investigate the pricing of variance swaps under both backward- and forward-looking information.

Keywords : VIX index, VIX futures, variance swap, realized volatility, information premium, stochastic volatility model, Lévy process, enlarged filtration, stochastic differential equation,  inverse Fourier pricing

\[8\] Scarce, Abundant, or Ample? A Time-Varying Model of the Reserve Demand Curve

标题:稀缺、充裕还是充裕？储备需求曲线的时变模型

作者:Gara Afonso,Domenico Giannone,Gabriele La Spada,John C. Williams

出处:FRB of New York Staff Report No. 1019, 2022

Abstract : Does the federal funds rate respond to shocks when aggregate reserves are in the trillions of dollars? Has banks’ demand for reserves moved over time? We provide a structural time-varying estimate of the slope of the reserve demand curve over 2010-21. We estimate a time-varying vector autoregressive model at daily frequency with an instrumental variable approach to address endogeneity. Consistent with economic theory, our estimates show a nonlinear demand function that exhibits a negative slope in 2010-11 and 2018-19 but is flat over 2012-17 and after mid-2020. We also find that the curve has moved outward, both vertically and horizontally.

Keywords : demand for reserves, federal funds market, monetary policy

\[9\] Predicting Returns Using Behavioral Market Barometers

标题:使用行为市场晴雨表预测回报

作者:C. Thomas Howard

出处:Journal of Investment Consulting, Vol. 21, No. 1, pp. 53-64, 2022

Abstract : This paper introduces market barometers that are based on measurable and persistent investor behavior. I test the ability of U.S. market, international market, and capitalization barometers to predict S&P 500, MSCI EAFE, and Russell 2000 returns, respectively. The empirical results for January 1981–December 2020 are statistically and economically significant and cannot be explained by trailing equity returns or the Institute of Supply Management Purchasing Managers’ Index,1 one of the best measures of economic activity. Barometers are used to develop a set of trading rules that show evidence of superior performance when compared with relevant benchmarks over the period evaluated.

Keywords : behavioral finance, behavioral investing, investment strategy, sentiment index, expected market return

\[10\] Firm Value and the Use of Financial Derivatives: Evidence from Developed Countries

标题:公司价值与金融衍生品的使用：来自发达国家的证据

作者:Mohamed Ayadi,Don Cyr,Skander Lazrak,Zhangwei Lu

出处:-

Abstract : Firms use derivatives to achieve various stated goals ranging from enhancing risk management, alleviating under-investment, and information asymmetry, reducing the financial distress costs and tax burden, as well as maximizing firm value. Using a sample of 47,191 year-firm observations spanning 7 developed countries and accounting for potential reverse causality, we test whether financial derivatives usage impacts firm value. Empirical evidence shows firms that use derivatives do not see any positive effect on their valuation. When we distinguish the firms adopting derivatives usage from those abandoning their use, we observe an asymmetric effect for some specific countries. For instance, US firms abandoning the use of derivatives experience no significant effect on their valuation while those adopting their use observe a negative impact in terms of the valuation measures employed. Similarly, UK firms showed no significant valuation effect when abandoning the use of derivatives while adopting derivatives, more specifically interest rate instruments, to lower industry adjusted values.

Keywords : Firm value, Currency, commodity, and interest rate derivatives, endogeneity, difference-in-differences
