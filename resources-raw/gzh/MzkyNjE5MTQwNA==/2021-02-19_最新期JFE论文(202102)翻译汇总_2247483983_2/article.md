\[1\] What’s wrong with Pittsburgh? Delegated investors and liquidity concentration

标题:匹兹堡怎么了？委托投资者与流动性集中

作者:Andra C. Ghent

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302439

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.015

Abstract : What makes an asset institutional quality? This paper proposes that one reason is the existing concentration of delegated investors in a market through a liquidity channel. Consistent with this intuition, it documents differences in investor composition across US cities and shows that delegated investors concentrate their investments in cities with higher turnover. It then estimates a search model showing how heterogeneity in liquidity preferences makes some markets more liquid, even when assets have identical cash flows. The paper provides evidence for clientele equilibria arising in frictional asset markets and suggests that a liquidity channel may explain divergent paths in city development.

Keywords : Alternative asset classes;Delegated asset management;Liquidity

Abstract :是什么造就了资产的制度品质？本文认为，一个原因是委托投资者通过流动性渠道集中于市场。与这种直觉相一致，它记录了美国各城市投资者构成的差异，并表明委托投资者将投资集中在营业额较高的城市。然后，它估计了一个搜索模型，该模型显示了流动性偏好的异质性如何使一些市场更具流动性，即使资产拥有相同的现金流。本文为摩擦性资产市场的顾客均衡提供了证据，并提出流动性渠道可以解释城市发展的不同路径。

Keywords :另类资产；委托资产管理；流动性

\[2\] Sticking to your plan: The role of present bias for credit card paydown

标题:坚持你的计划：目前的偏见对信用卡还款的作用

作者:Theresa Kuchler,Michaela Pagel

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302300

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.002

Abstract : We use data from an online financial service to show that many consumers fail to stick to their self-set debt paydown plans. This behavior is best explained by present bias. Our empirical approach is informed by a parsimonious model showing that the sensitivity of spending to paycheck receipt reflects a present-biased agents short-run impatience, and that this sensitivity is reduced by available resources only for agents who are aware (sophisticated) of their future impatience. Classifying users accordingly, we find that (i) sophisticated users debt paydown decreases with short-run impatience, and that (ii) planned paydown is most predictive of actual paydown for sophisticated users.

Keywords : Credit card paydown;Present bias;Hyperbolic discounting;Sophistication;Naivete

Abstract :我们利用一家在线金融服务机构的数据显示，许多消费者未能坚持自己制定的债务偿还计划。这种行为最好用现在的偏见来解释。我们的实证方法是由一个节俭模型提供的，该模型表明，支出对工资收入的敏感性反映了当前有偏见的代理人的短期不耐烦，并且这种敏感性仅因知道（成熟的）他们未来不耐烦的代理人的可用资源而降低。根据用户分类，我们发现（i）复杂用户的债务偿还随着短期不耐烦而减少，并且（ii）计划偿还最能预测复杂用户的实际偿还。

Keywords :信用卡还款；目前偏向；双曲线贴现；复杂度；天真

\[3\] A dynamic theory of multiple borrowing

标题:多重借款的动态理论

作者:Daniel Green,Ernest Liu

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302440

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.016

Abstract : Multiple borrowing—when borrower obtains overlapping loans from multiple lenders—is a common phenomenon in many credit markets. We build a tractable, dynamic model of multiple borrowing and show that, because overlapping creditors can impose default externalities on each other, expanding financial access by introducing more lenders can backfire. Capital allocation is distorted away from the most productive uses. Entrepreneurs choose inefficient endeavors with low returns to scale. These problems are exacerbated when investments become more pledgeable or when borrowers have access to more lenders, explaining why increased access to finance does not always improve outcomes.

Keywords : Commitment;Common agency;Investment;Misallocation

Abstract :当借款人从多个贷款人处获得重叠贷款时，多次借款是许多信贷市场的常见现象。我们建立了一个易于处理的多重借款动态模型，并表明，由于重叠的债权人可以相互施加违约外部性，通过引入更多的贷款人来扩大融资渠道可能适得其反。资本配置偏离了最具生产性的用途。企业家选择低效的努力和低回报的规模。当投资变得更具可抵押性，或者当借款人能够接触到更多的贷款人时，这些问题就会加剧，这就解释了为什么增加融资渠道并不总能改善结果。

Keywords :承诺；共同代理；投资；错配

\[4\] Signaling safety

标题:信号安全

作者:Roni Michaely,Stefano Rossi,Michael Weber

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302415

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.013

Abstract : Contrary to signaling models’ central predictions, changes in the level of cash flows do not empirically follow changes in dividends. We use the Campbell (1991) decomposition to construct cash-flow and discount-rate news from returns and find the following: (1) both dividend changes and repurchase announcements signal changes in cash-flow volatility (in opposite directions); (2) larger cash-flow volatility changes come with larger announcement returns; and (3) neither discount-rate news, nor the level of cash-flow news, nor total stock return volatility change following dividend changes. We conclude cash-flow news—and not discount-rate news—drive payout policy, and payout policy conveys information about future cash-flow volatility.

Keywords : Dividends;Payout policy;Cash-flow volatility;Signaling model

Abstract :与信号模型的核心预测相反，现金流水平的变化在经验上并不跟随股息的变化。我们使用Campbell（1991）分解从收益中构造现金流和折现率新闻，发现：（1）股利变化和回购公告都表明现金流波动性的变化（方向相反）；（2）现金流波动性变化越大，公告收益越大；贴现率、现金流水平和总收益波动率均不随股利变化而变化。我们得出结论：现金流消息而非贴现率消息驱动支付策略，而支付策略传递的是未来现金流波动的信息。

Keywords :股利；支付政策；现金流波动；信号模型

\[5\] Time-varying state variable risk premia in the ICAPM

标题:ICAPM中的时变状态变量风险溢价

作者:Pedro Barroso,Martijn Boons,Paul Karehnke

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302269

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.016

Abstract : We find that the relation between state variables, such as the t-bill rate and term spread, and consumption growth is time-varying. In the cross-section of U.S. stocks, risk premia for exposure to state variables vary over time accordingly. When a state variable predicts consumption strongly relative to its own history, its annualized risk premium increases by 6% (0.4 in Sharpe ratio). This effect implies that risk premia can switch signs and are increasing in the conditional variance of the state variable. These common drivers of time-varying risk premia are consistent with the Intertemporal CAPM. Benchmark factors contain the same conditional expected return effects as state variable risk premia.

Keywords : Conditional asset pricing models;State variables;Intertemporal CAPM;Consumption predictability;Time-varying equity risk premia

Abstract :我们发现，国库券利率、期限利差等状态变量与消费增长之间的关系是时变的。在美国股市的横截面中，暴露于国家变量的风险溢价随着时间的推移而相应变化。当一个状态变量相对于其自身历史强烈预测消费时，其年化风险溢价将增加6%（夏普比率为0.4）。这种效应意味着风险溢价可以转换信号，并且在状态变量的条件方差中增加。这些时变风险溢价的共同驱动因素与跨期CAPM是一致的。基准因子包含与状态变量风险溢价相同的条件预期收益效应。

Keywords :条件资产定价模型；状态变量；跨期CAPM；消费可预测性；时变股权风险溢价

\[6\] Bank monitoring: Evidence from syndicated loans

标题:银行监管：来自银团贷款的证据

作者:Matthew T. Gustafson,Ivan T. Ivanov,Ralf R. Meisenzahl

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302452

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.017

Abstract : We directly measure banks’ monitoring of syndicated loans. Banks typically demand borrower information on at least a monthly basis. About 20% of loans involve active monitoring (i.e., site visits or third-party appraisals). Monitoring increases with the lead bank’s incentives and the value of information and is negatively associated with loan spreads and maturity. The monitoring captured by our measures can either complement or substitute for covenant-based monitoring, depending on whether the monitoring informs covenant compliance. Banks increase monitoring following deteriorations in borrower financial condition and credit line drawdowns. Finally, monitoring is positively related to future covenant violations and loan renegotiations.

Keywords : Bank monitoring;Syndicated loans

Abstract :我们直接衡量银行对银团贷款的监控。银行通常至少每月要求借款人提供信息。约20%的贷款涉及主动监测（即实地考察或第三方评估）。随着牵头银行的激励措施和信息价值的增加，监测工作也在增加，并且与贷款利差和到期日呈负相关。我们的措施所捕获的监测可以补充或替代基于契约的监测，这取决于监测是否告知契约遵守情况。随着借款人财务状况恶化和信贷额度下降，银行加强了监控。最后，监测与未来违反契约和贷款重新谈判正相关。

Keywords :银行监管；银团贷款

\[7\] The Sources of Financing Constraints

标题:融资约束的来源

作者:Boris Nikolov,Lukas Schmid,Roberto Steri

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302282

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.018

Abstract : Which financial frictions drive firms’ financing constraints? We structurally estimate dynamic firm financing models embedding many financial frictions, on panels of public firms and private firms. We focus on limited enforcement, moral hazard, and trade-off models and assess which models rationalize best observed corporate policies across various samples. Our tests, based on empirical policy function benchmarks, favor trade-off models for larger public firms, limited commitment models for smaller public firms, and moral hazard models for Private firms. Our estimates suggest significant financing constraints due to agency frictions and highlight the importance of identifying their sources for firm valuation.

Keywords :金融摩擦；道德风险；有限执行；权衡；动态契约

\[8\] Rejected stock exchange applicants

标题:被拒绝的证券交易所申请人

作者:Sturla L. Fjesme,Neal E. Galpin,Lyndon Moore

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302336

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.005

Abstract : We examine listing applications by firms to the London Stock Exchange between 1891 and 1911. The exchange rejected 82 (13.1%) of the 628 applicants to its main board. Accepted applicants were twice as likely to pay dividends (and to pay twice as much) and had longer firm lives than rejected applicants. Rejected applicants were more likely to file for liquidation than successful applicants. These results remain even after we control for the primary benefits of the listing itself: liquidity and future capital inflows. In this era, the London Stock Exchange could screen applicants for listing.

Keywords : Financial intermediaries;Securities markets;Initial public offering

Abstract :我们研究了1891年至1911年间公司在伦敦证券交易所的上市申请。该交易所拒绝了628名申请者中的82人（13.1%）进入主板。与被拒绝的申请人相比，被接受的申请人支付股息的可能性是被拒绝的申请人的两倍（而且支付的金额是被拒绝的申请人的两倍），并且拥有更长的公司寿命。被拒绝的申请人比成功的申请人更有可能申请清算。即使我们控制了上市本身的主要好处：流动性和未来资本流入，这些结果仍然存在。在这个时代，伦敦证券交易所可以筛选上市申请人。

Keywords :金融中介机构；证券市场；首次公开发行

\[9\] Can unpredictable risk exposure be priced?

标题:不可预测的风险敞口能否定价？

作者:Ricardo Barahona,Joost Driessen,Rik Frehen

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302348

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.006

Abstract : We study the link between beta predictability and the price of risk. An investor who desires exposure to a certain risk factor needs to predict what next period’s beta will be. We use a simple model to show that an ambiguity averse agent’s demand is lower when betas are hard to predict, leading to a reduction in risk premiums. We test the implications for downside betas and VIX betas. We find that they have economically and statistically small prices of risk once we account for the fact that an investor cannot observe ex-post realized betas when determining asset demand.

Keywords : Ambiguity aversion;Beta uncertainty;Hedging demand;Price of risk

Abstract :我们研究贝塔预测性和风险价格之间的联系。希望暴露于某一风险因素的投资者需要预测下一阶段的贝塔值。我们使用一个简单的模型来说明当beta难以预测时，模糊厌恶代理的需求较低，从而导致风险溢价的降低。我们测试了下行beta和VIX beta的含义。我们发现，一旦我们考虑到投资者在确定资产需求时不能观察事后实现的beta这一事实，它们在经济上和统计上的风险价格都很小。

Keywords :模糊厌恶；贝塔不确定性；套期保值需求；风险价格

\[10\] Funding liquidity shocks in a quasi-experiment: Evidence from the CDS Big Bang

标题:准实验中的流动性冲击融资：CDS大爆炸的证据

作者:Xinjie Wang,Yangru Wu,Hongjun Yan,Zhaodong (Ken) Zhong

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302324

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.004

Abstract : We use the advent of new credit default swap (CDS) trading conventions in April 2009—the CDS Big Bang—to study how a shock to funding liquidity impacts market liquidity. After the Big Bang, traders are required to pay upfront fees to execute CDS transactions, with the size of the fees depending on the level of CDS spreads. While CDS bid-ask spreads decline in aggregate after the Big Bang, they do so less for contracts that require larger fees. Furthermore, the funding effect is stronger for smaller and riskier firms and for noncentrally cleared contracts. The effect also becomes stronger after Deutsche Bank's exit.

Keywords : Funding liquidity;CDS Big Bang;CDS Small Bang;Standardization;Central clearing

Abstract :我们利用2009年4月新的信用违约互换（CDS）交易惯例CDS大爆炸来研究融资流动性冲击如何影响市场流动性。大爆炸之后，交易者需要支付执行CDS交易的前期费用，费用的大小取决于CDS价差的水平。虽然CDS的买卖价差在大爆炸之后总体上有所下降，但对于需要更高费用的合约，它们的降幅较小。此外，对于规模较小、风险较高的公司和非中央结算合约，融资效应更强。德意志银行退出后，这种影响也变得更加强烈。

Keywords :融资流动性；CDS大爆炸；CDS小爆炸；标准化；中央结算

\[11\] Can investors time their exposure to private equity?

标题:投资者能否确定他们在私募股权投资中的时间？

作者:Gregory Brown,Robert Harris,Wendy Hu,Tim Jenkinson,Steven N. Kaplan,David T. Robinson

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302427

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.014

Abstract : Private equity performance, both for buyouts and venture capital, has been highly cyclical: periods of high fundraising have been followed by periods of low performance. Despite this seemingly predictable variation, we find modest gains, at best, to pursuing realistic, investable strategies that time capital commitments to private equity. This occurs, in part, because investors can only time their commitments to funds; they cannot time when commitments are called or when investments are exited. There is a high degree of time-series correlation in net cash flows even across commitment strategies that allocate capital in a very different manner over time.

Keywords : Private equity;Buyouts;Venture capital;Market timing

Abstract :无论是收购还是风险投资，私募股权的表现都具有高度的周期性：高筹资期之后是低业绩期。尽管存在这种看似可预测的变化，但我们发现，追求现实的、可投资的战略，即对私人股本的资本承诺，最多只能带来微薄的收益。出现这种情况的部分原因是，投资者只能对其对基金的承诺进行计时；他们不能对何时调用承诺或何时退出投资进行计时。在净现金流中存在高度的时间序列相关性，即使是在以非常不同的方式分配资本的承诺策略中也是如此。

Keywords :私募股权；收购；风险投资；市场时机

\[12\] Friends with bankruptcy protection benefits

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302312

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.003

Abstract : We show information spillovers limit the effectiveness of targeted debt relief programs. We study individuals who learn about the likelihood of debt relief from the recent experiences of workplace peers filing for bankruptcy protection. Peers granted bankruptcy can discharge debts, while peers facing dismissal lose all protections. Exploiting the random assignment of judges to bankruptcy cases, we determine that individuals with a “dismissed peer” are significantly less likely to file for bankruptcy or enter foreclosure. \[13\] The hidden costs of being public: Evidence from multinational firms operating in an emerging market

标题:上市的隐性成本：来自新兴市场跨国公司的证据

作者:Pablo Slutzky

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302294

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.001

Abstract : I study how firms deal with business regulations that limit their operations. Abstract : Long-term reversals in corporate bonds are economically and statistically significant in a comprehensive sample spanning the period 1977 to 2017.
