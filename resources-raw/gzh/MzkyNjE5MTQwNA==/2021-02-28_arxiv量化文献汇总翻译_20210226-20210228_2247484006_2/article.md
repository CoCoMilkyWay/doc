\[1\] Optimal Dynamic Futures Portfolios Under a Multiscale Central Tendency Ornstein Uhlenbeck Model

标题 : 多尺度中心趋势Ornstein-Uhlenbeck模型下的最优动态期货组合

作者 : Tim Leung, Yang Zhou , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12601

说明 : -

Abstract : We study the problem of dynamically trading multiple futures whose underlying asset price follows a multiscale central tendency Ornstein Uhlenbeck (MCTOU) model. Under this model, we derive the closed form no arbitrage prices for the futures contracts. Applying a utility maximization approach, we solve for the optimal trading strategies under different portfolio configurations by examining the associated system of Hamilton Jacobi Bellman (HJB) equations. The optimal strategies depend on not only the parameters of the underlying asset price process but also the risk premia embedded in the futures prices. Numerical examples are provided to illustrate the investor s optimal positions and optimal wealth over time.

摘要 : 研究了标的资产价格服从多尺度中心趋势Ornstein-Uhlenbeck（MCTOU）模型的多品种期货的动态交易问题。在此模型下，我们得到了期货合约的闭式无套利价格。应用效用最大化方法，通过研究Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman（HJB）方程的关联系统，求解不同投资组合配置下的最优交易策略。最优策略不仅取决于标的资产价格过程的参数，而且还取决于期货价格中蕴含的风险溢价。数值例子说明了投资者的最优头寸和最优财富随时间的变化。

\[2\] Deep Equal Risk Pricing of Financial Derivatives with Multiple Hedging Instruments

标题 : 多重对冲金融衍生工具的深度等风险定价

作者 : Alexandre Carbonneau, Frédéric Godin , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12694

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper studies the equal risk pricing (ERP) framework for the valuation of European financial derivatives. This option pricing approach is consistent with global trading strategies by setting the premium as the value such that the residual hedging risk of the long and short positions in the option are equal under optimal hedging. The ERP setup of Marzban et al. (2020) is considered where residual hedging risk is quantified with convex risk measures. The main objective of this paper is to assess through extensive numerical experiments the impact of including options as hedging instruments within the ERP framework. The reinforcement learning procedure developed in Carbonneau and Godin (2020), which relies on the deep hedging algorithm of Buehler et al. (2019b), is applied to numerically solve the global hedging problems by representing trading policies with neural networks. Among other findings, numerical results indicate that in the presence of jump risk, hedging long term puts with shorter term options entails a significant decrease of both equal risk prices and market incompleteness as compared to trading only the stock. Monte Carlo experiments demonstrate the potential of ERP as a fair valuation approach providing prices consistent with observable market prices. Analyses exhibit the ability of ERP to span a large interval of prices through the choice of convex risk measures which is close to encompass the variance optimal premium.

摘要 : 本文研究了欧洲金融衍生品的等风险定价（ERP）框架。这种期权定价方法与全球交易策略是一致的，通过设定溢价作为价值，使得在最优套期保值下，期权中多头和空头头寸的剩余套期保值风险相等。Marzban等人（2020年）的ERP设置考虑了剩余对冲风险用凸风险度量进行量化。本文的主要目的是通过大量的数值实验来评估在ERP框架下将期权作为套期保值工具的影响。Carboneau和Godin（2020）开发的强化学习程序依赖于Buehler等人（2019b）的深度套期保值算法，通过用神经网络表示交易政策来数值求解全球套期保值问题。数值结果表明，在存在跳变风险的情况下，与只交易股票相比，使用短期期权对冲长期看跌期权可以显著降低等风险价格和市场不完全性。montecarlo实验证明了ERP作为一种公平估价方法的潜力，它提供了与可观察市场价格一致的价格。分析表明，企业资源规划的能力，以跨越一个大的价格区间，通过选择凸风险措施，这是接近包含方差最优溢价。

\[3\] Incorporating Financial Big Data in Small Portfolio Risk Analysis

标题 : 金融大数据在小资产组合风险分析中的应用

作者 : Donggyu Kim, Seunghyeon Yu , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12783

说明 : -

Abstract : When applying Value at Risk (VaR) procedures to specific positions or portfolios, we often focus on developing procedures only for the specific assets in the portfolio. However, since this small portfolio risk analysis ignores information from assets outside the target portfolio, there may be significant information loss. In this paper, we develop a dynamic process to incorporate the ignored information. We also study how to overcome the curse of dimensionality and discuss where and when benefits occur from a large number of assets, which is called the blessing of dimensionality. We find empirical support for the proposed method.

摘要 : 在将风险价值（VaR）程序应用于特定头寸或投资组合时，我们通常只专注于为投资组合中的特定资产开发程序。然而，由于这种小型投资组合风险分析忽略了来自目标投资组合以外资产的信息，因此可能存在重大的信息损失。在本文中，我们发展了一个动态的过程来整合被忽略的信息。我们还研究了如何克服维度的诅咒，并讨论了何时何地从大量资产中获益，这被称为维度的祝福。我们发现实证支持所提出的方法。

\[4\] The Stochastic Balance Equation for the American Option Value Function and its Gradient

标题 : 美式期权价值函数的随机平衡方程及其梯度

作者 : Malkhaz Shashiashvili , 类别 ：Pricing of Securities (q-fin.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12800

说明 : Comments: 19 pages

Abstract : In the paper we consider the problem of valuation and hedging of American options written on dividend paying assets whose price dynamics follow the multidimensional diffusion model. We derive a stochastic balance equation for the American option value function and its gradient. We prove that the latter pair is the unique solution of the stochastic balance equation as a result of the uniqueness in the related adapted future supremum problem.

摘要 : 本文研究了价格动态服从多维扩散模型的支付股利资产上美式期权的定价和套期保值问题。导出了美式期权价值函数及其梯度的随机平衡方程。我们证明了后一对是随机平衡方程的唯一解，这是由于相关的适应未来上确界问题的唯一性。

\[5\] Vector quantile regression and optimal transport, from theory to numerics

标题 : 向量分位数回归与最优传输，从理论到数值

作者 : Guillaume Carlier, Victor Chernozhukov, Gwendoline De Bie, Alfred Galichon , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12809

说明 : Comments: 35 pages, 19 figures, 4 tables. arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:1610.06833

Abstract : In this paper, we first revisit the Koenker and Bassett variational approach to (univariate) quantile regression, emphasizing its link with latent factor representations and correlation maximization problems. We then review the multivariate extension due to Carlier et al. (2016, 2017) which relates vector quantile regression to an optimal transport problem with mean independence constraints. We introduce an entropic regularization of this problem, implement a gradient descent numerical method and illustrate its feasibility on univariate and bivariate examples.

摘要 : 在本文中，我们首先回顾了（单变量）分位数回归的Koenker和Bassett变分方法，强调了它与潜在因子表示和相关最大化问题的联系。然后，我们回顾了Carlier等人（2016、2017）提出的多元扩展，该扩展将向量分位数回归与具有平均独立性约束的最优运输问题联系起来。我们引入了一种熵正则化方法，实现了一种梯度下降的数值方法，并用一元和二元算例说明了该方法的可行性。

\[6\] Matching with Trade offs

标题 : 与权衡相匹配

作者 : Alfred Galichon, Bernard Salanié , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12811

说明 : Comments: 62 pages, 7 figures

Abstract : We investigate in this paper the theory and econometrics of optimal matchings with competing criteria. The surplus from a marriage match, for instance, may depend both on the incomes and on the educations of the partners, as well as on characteristics that the analyst does not observe. Even if the surplus is complementary in incomes, and complementary in educations, imperfect correlation between income and education at the individual level implies that the social optimum must trade off matching on incomes and matching on educations. Given a flexible specification of the surplus function, we characterize under mild assumptions the properties of the set of feasible matchings and of the socially optimal matching. Then we show how data on the covariation of the types of the partners in observed matches can be used to test that the observed matches are socially optimal for this specification, and to estimate the parameters that define social preferences over matches.

摘要 : 本文研究了竞争条件下最优匹配的理论和计量经济学。例如，婚姻配对的盈余可能既取决于双方的收入和教育程度，也取决于分析者没有观察到的特征。即使盈余在收入上是互补的，在教育上是互补的，但个人层面上收入与教育的不完全相关意味着社会最优必须在收入匹配和教育匹配之间进行权衡。给出了剩余函数的一个灵活的描述，我们在温和的假设下刻画了可行匹配集和社会最优匹配集的性质。然后，我们展示了如何使用观察到的匹配中的合作伙伴类型的协变量数据来检验观察到的匹配对于该规范是社会最优的，并估计定义社会偏好的参数。

\[7\] Deep Stochastic Volatility Model

标题 : 深度随机波动模型

作者 : Xiuqin Xu, Ying Chen , 类别 ：Machine Learning (cs.LG)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12658

说明 : -

Abstract : Volatility for financial assets returns can be used to gauge the risk for financial market. We propose a deep stochastic volatility model (DSVM) based on the framework of deep latent variable models. It uses flexible deep learning models to automatically detect the dependence of the future volatility on past returns, past volatilities and the stochastic noise, and thus provides a flexible volatility model without the need to manually select features. We develop a scalable inference and learning algorithm based on variational inference. In real data analysis, the DSVM outperforms several popular alternative volatility models. In addition, the predicted volatility of the DSVM provides a more reliable risk measure that can better reflex the risk in the financial market, reaching more quickly to a higher level when the market becomes more risky and to a lower level when the market is more stable, compared with the commonly used GARCH type model with a huge data set on the U.S. stock market.

摘要 : 金融资产收益率的波动性可以用来衡量金融市场的风险。基于深层潜变量模型的框架，提出了一种深层随机波动率模型（DSVM）。它利用灵活的深度学习模型自动检测未来波动率对过去收益率、过去波动率和随机噪声的依赖性，从而提供了一个灵活的波动率模型，无需手动选择特征。提出了一种基于变分推理的可伸缩推理学习算法。在实际数据分析中，DSVM优于几种流行的替代波动率模型。此外，DSVM的预测波动率提供了一个更可靠的风险度量，可以更好地反映金融市场的风险，当市场变得更具风险时，它能更快地到达一个较高的水平，而当市场更稳定时，它能更快地到达一个较低的水平，这与市场上通常使用的具有巨大数据集的GARCH型模型相比美国股市。

\[8\] Social Welfare Maximization and Conformism via Information Design in Linear Quadratic Gaussian Games

标题 : 基于线性二次高斯博弈信息设计的社会福利最大化与一致性

作者 : Furkan Sezer, Hossein Khazaei, Ceyhun Eksin , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.13047

说明 : -

Abstract : We consider linear quadratic Gaussian (LQG) games in which players have quadratic payoffs that depend on the players actions and an unknown payoff relevant state, and signals on the state that follow a Gaussian distribution conditional on the state realization. An information designer decides the fidelity of information revealed to the players in order to maximize the social welfare of the players or reduce the disagreement among players actions. Leveraging the semi definiteness of the information design problem, we derive analytical solutions for these objectives under specific LQG games. We show that full information disclosure maximizes social welfare when there is a common payoff relevant state, when there is strategic substitutability in the actions of players, or when the signals are public. Numerical results show that as strategic substitution increases, the value of the information disclosure increases. When the objective is to induce conformity among players actions, hiding information is optimal. Lastly, we consider the information design objective that is a weighted combination of social welfare and cohesiveness of players actions. We obtain an interval for the weights where full information disclosure is optimal under public signals for games with strategic substitutability. Numerical solutions show that the actual interval where full information disclosure is optimal gets close to the analytical interval obtained as substitution increases.

摘要 : 我们考虑了线性二次高斯（LQG）对策，在该对策中，参与者具有依赖于参与者行为的二次支付和未知支付相关状态，并且状态上的信号遵循高斯分布，条件是状态实现。信息设计者决定向参与者透露的信息的保真度，以最大限度地提高参与者的社会福利或减少参与者之间的分歧。利用信息设计问题的半确定性，我们在特定的LQG对策下导出了这些目标的解析解。我们发现，当存在共同的收益相关状态，当参与者的行为具有战略替代性，或者当信号是公开的时，完全信息披露可以最大限度地提高社会福利。数值结果表明，随着战略替代的增加，信息披露的价值增加。当目标是诱导玩家行为一致时，隐藏信息是最优的。最后，我们考虑了信息设计的目标，即社会福利和参与者行为的内聚性的加权组合。对于具有战略替代性的博弈，我们得到了在公共信号下完全信息披露最优的权重区间。数值结果表明，当替代率增加时，完全信息披露最优的实际区间与解析区间接近。
