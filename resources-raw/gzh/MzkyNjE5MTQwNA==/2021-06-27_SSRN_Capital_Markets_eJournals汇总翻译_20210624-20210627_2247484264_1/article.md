\[1\] Examining the Drivers of Stock Prices of Private Sector Banks in India

标题:印度私营银行股价驱动因素研究

作者:Rajveer Rawlin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3867898

Abstract : Indian private sector banking stocks have been rebounding from a massive selloff. Private sector banks which have traditionally maintained their asset quality and profitability when compared to their public-sector counterparts, have recently seen their asset quality erode. Given that private sector banks play an influential role in the Indian economy it would be helpful to understand the key drivers of private bank stock prices. This paper studied the influence of some key micro and macro determinants on the stock prices of all listed private sector banks in India over the 12 years from 2008-2019. Panel regression models were fit to study the relationship between the variables. Liquidity, profitability, growth, inflation and exchange rate indicators proved to be important influencers of the stock prices of private sector banks in India. The study provides evidence for the Fisher effect suggesting that private bank stocks can provide good inflation-adjusted returns over time. Stakeholders of these banks can focus on these determinants which contribute to shareholder value over time.

Keywords : Indian Private Banks, Stock Market Capitalization, Liquidity, Exchange Rates, GDP, Panel Regression

Abstract :印度私人银行股已经从大规模抛售中反弹。与公共部门的银行相比，传统上保持资产质量和盈利能力的私营部门银行最近的资产质量有所下降。鉴于私营银行在印度经济中发挥着重要作用，了解私营银行股价的主要驱动力将是有帮助的。本文研究了2008-2019年12年间，一些关键的微观和宏观决定因素对印度所有上市私营银行股价的影响。采用面板回归模型研究变量之间的关系。流动性、盈利能力、增长、通货膨胀和汇率指标被证明是印度私营银行股价的重要影响因素。这项研究为费希尔效应提供了证据，表明私人银行股可以随着时间的推移提供良好的通胀调整后回报。这些银行的利益相关者可以关注这些随着时间推移对股东价值做出贡献的决定因素。

Keywords :印度私人银行，股票市值，流动性，汇率，GDP，面板回归

\[2\] Scenario Analysis with the DD-PD Mapping Approach: Stock Market Shocks and U.S. Corporate Default Risk

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3867829

Abstract : This paper introduces the quantile regression- based Distance-to-Default to Probability of Default (DD-PD) mapping, which links individual firms’ DD to their real world PD. Since changes in the DD depend on a handful of parameters, the mapping easily accommodates shocks arising from quantitative and narrative scenarios informed by expert judgment. \[3\] The Evolution of Environmental Discourse: Evidence from Conference Calls

标题:环境话语的演变：来自电话会议的证据

作者:Elaine Henry,Xi Jiang,Andrea Rozario

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3863354

Abstract : We examine the evolution of environmental discourse in conference calls and whether and how this discourse affects firm valuation. We measure the amount of environmental discourse as the frequency count of environmentally-related topics in the transcripts of conference calls. We find that environmental discourse increased substantially from 2006 to 2020 and that discourse is incrementally informative – beyond environmental performance metrics and environmental disclosures – for firm valuation. Specifically, we find that greater environmental discourse in conference calls is associated with higher cost of capital and lower share price, which we interpret as suggesting that discussion of environmental topics increases uncertainty. We also find that the amount of discourse in the prepared remarks portion of conference calls dominates that in the Q&A portion as an explanatory factor for firm value. We complement our analysis by examining the value-relevance of general ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) discussion and show that more general ESG discourse is associated with lower cost of capital and higher share price, implying a more favorable valuation impact of general discussions of social and governance issues.

Keywords : textual analysis, environmental disclosure, firm valuation, conference calls

Abstract :我们考察了电话会议中环境话语的演变，以及这种话语是否以及如何影响企业价值。我们以电话会议记录中与环境相关的话题的频率计数来衡量环境话语的数量。我们发现，从2006年到2020年，环境话语大幅增加，而且这种话语对于公司估值来说，除了环境绩效指标和环境披露之外，信息量也在不断增加。具体来说，我们发现电话会议中更多的环境议题与更高的资本成本和更低的股价相关，我们认为这表明环境议题的讨论增加了不确定性。我们还发现，作为企业价值的一个解释因素，电话会议准备评论部分的话语量占问答部分的主导地位。我们通过检查一般ESG（环境、社会和治理）讨论的价值相关性来补充我们的分析，并表明更一般的ESG讨论与更低的资本成本和更高的股价相关，这意味着对社会和治理问题的一般讨论会产生更有利的估值影响。

Keywords :文本分析，环境披露，公司估值，电话会议

\[5\] Unrecognized Expected Credit Losses and Bank Share Prices

标题:未确认的预期信贷损失和银行股价

作者:Barrett Wheeler

出处:Journal of Accounting Research, Volume 59, Issue 3

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3867752

Abstract : Accounting for credit losses under U.S. GAAP is transitioning from an incurred to an expected loss model. The model change was motivated by concerns that reporting only incurred losses does not provide investors with sufficient and timely information about banks’ credit risk. In this paper, I develop a measure of lifetime expected credit losses using vintage analysis to examine whether stock prices reflect information about unrecognized expected credit losses in an incurred loss regime. Consistent with investors being able to obtain information about expected losses that are not recognized in the financial statements, I find that unrecognized expected credit losses are negatively associated with bank stock prices. The pricing of these losses is stronger for larger banks, consistent with lower costs of obtaining this information for banks with better information environments. I also find that recorded allowances were less than estimated expected losses, on average, consistent with concerns that implementing the expected loss model will adversely impact regulatory capital adequacy.

Keywords : banking; allowance for loan losses; loan loss provisions; incurred loss model; current expected credit loss model; standard setting

Abstract :根据美国公认会计准则，信贷损失的会计处理正在从已发生损失模式过渡到预期损失模式。这种模式的改变是出于这样一种担忧，即只报告已发生的损失并不能向投资者提供有关银行信贷风险的充分和及时的信息。在本文中，我发展了一个衡量终身预期信用损失使用年份分析，以检查股票价格是否反映了未确认的预期信用损失的信息，在已发生的损失制度。与投资者能够获得财务报表中未确认的预期损失信息相一致，我发现未确认的预期信用损失与银行股价负相关。对于大型银行来说，这些损失的定价更高，这与信息环境更好的银行获得这些信息的成本更低是一致的。我还发现，平均而言，记录的备抵低于预计损失，这与实施预期损失模型将对监管资本充足率产生不利影响的担忧相一致。

Keywords :银行业务；贷款损失准备金；贷款损失准备金；发生损失模型；当前预期信用损失模型；标准制定

\[6\] The Asymmetric Effect of Reporting Flexibility on Priced Risk

标题:报告灵活性对定价风险的不对称影响

作者:Matthew J. Bloomfield

出处:Journal of Accounting Research, Volume 59, Issue 3, 2021

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3867597

Abstract : Most firms covary more positively with downmarkets than upmarkets—a phenomenon I refer to as “risk asymmetry.” I predict and find that risk asymmetry is caused, at least in part, by a firm's ability to selectively obfuscate poor performance. Risk asymmetry decreases significantly when firms are required to adhere to the more stringent auditing standards mandated under Section 404 of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act, however this decrease is more muted for firms with weak internal controls. Consistent with my predictions, these patterns are stronger for more market-sensitive firms and weaker for firms that include relative performance evaluation in their CEOs' pay packages. Taken together with prior literature (which documents that risk asymmetry is priced), my results suggest that a firm can lower its cost of capital by credibly reducing its ability to obfuscate value-relevant information.

Keywords : reporting quality; obfuscation; asymmetric risk; Sarbanes-Oxley; internal controls

Abstract :大多数公司与低端市场的合作比与高端市场的合作更积极，我把这种现象称为“风险不对称”。我预测并发现，风险不对称至少在一定程度上是由公司有选择地混淆糟糕业绩的能力造成的。当公司被要求遵守《萨班斯-奥克斯利法案》第404节规定的更严格的审计标准时，风险不对称性显著降低，但是对于内部控制薄弱的公司，这种降低更为平缓。与我的预测一致，这些模式对于市场敏感度较高的公司来说更强，而对于将相对绩效评估纳入CEO薪酬体系的公司来说较弱。结合以前的文献（这些文献表明风险不对称是定价的），我的结果表明，企业可以通过可信地降低其混淆价值相关信息的能力来降低其资本成本。

Keywords :报告质量；混淆；不对称风险；萨班斯-奥克斯利法案；内部控制

\[7\] The Information Role of the Media in Earnings News

标题:盈利新闻中媒体的信息角色

作者:Nicholas M. Guest

出处:Journal of Accounting Research, Volume 59, Issue 3, Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3867592

Abstract : I reexamine whether media articles with substantive editorial content inform the market's reaction to firms' earnings news. Using variation in earnings announcement coverage because of restructuring at The Wall Street Journal (WSJ), my analyses suggest that WSJ earnings articles improve price discovery and increase trading volume at S&P 500 earnings announcements. Additionally, textual analysis suggests media articles that differ more from the firm's earnings release increase trading volume, and that the differences speed up (slow down) price discovery when they corroborate (contradict) the tone of the firm's news. Such high difference articles are slightly longer, are more readable and specific, include more references to the industry and economy, repeat less “stale” news published in previous WSJ articles, and quote more investor and expert sources. Overall, my paper contributes to research on the role of the media in earnings news by providing evidence that journalists' editorial content helps investors understand firms' earnings, instead of simply entertaining or increasing awareness.

Keywords : business journalism; media; price discovery; volume; returns; earnings

Abstract :我重新审视了具有实质性编辑内容的媒体文章是否会告知市场对公司盈利消息的反应。利用《华尔街日报》（Wall Street Journal，WSJ）重组后收益公告覆盖率的变化，我的分析表明，《华尔街日报》（WSJ）的收益文章改善了标准普尔500指数（S&p500）收益公告的价格发现，增加了交易量。此外，文本分析表明，媒体文章与公司公布的收益差异较大，会增加交易量，当这些差异证实（反驳）公司新闻的基调时，会加速（减缓）价格发现。这种差异较大的文章篇幅略长，可读性和具体性更强，更多地提及行业和经济，较少重复《华尔街日报》先前文章中发表的“陈腐”新闻，并引用更多投资者和专家的资料。总的来说，我的论文有助于研究媒体在盈利新闻中的作用，通过提供证据表明，记者的编辑内容有助于投资者了解公司的盈利，而不是简单地娱乐或提高认识。

Keywords :商业新闻；媒体；价格发现；体积；回报；收益

\[8\] In Art We Trust

标题:我们信任艺术

作者:Yuexin Li,Marshall Xiaoyin Ma,Luc Renneboog

出处:CentER Discussion Paper Series No. 2021-016

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3871007

Abstract : While trust is the cornerstone in the functioning of any market, it is of particular importance in the markets that are unregulated, illiquid, and opaque, such as the art market. We therefore examine the role of authenticity, as captured by the provenance information, on the sales probability of auctioned paintings, their price formation and returns. Auction catalogues include four authenticity dimensions: pedigree, exhibition history, literature coverage, and certification. We find that provenance information increases sales probability by up to 4%, leads to price premiums up to 54%, and increases annualized returns by 5% to 16%. As high dimensional fixed effects may induce estimation concerns, we perform LASSO estimations. To address potential endogeneity problems between the provision of provenance and price expectations, we perform quasi-natural experiments in difference-in-differences (DiD) settings on auction houses’ provenance policy changes, and the discovery of fakes and forgeries. We also perform robustness tests on subsamples less affected by past prices such as those estate sales following the death of a collector.

Keywords : auction; hedonic pricing; art investment; art returns; pedigree; auction house; cultural economics

Abstract :尽管信托是任何市场运作的基石，但在不受监管、流动性不足和不透明的市场（如艺术品市场）中，信托尤为重要。因此，我们研究了来源信息所捕捉到的真实性对拍卖画的销售概率、价格形成和回报的作用。拍卖目录包括四个真实性维度：血统、展览历史、文献覆盖率和认证。我们发现，来源信息增加销售概率高达4%，导致价格溢价高达54%，并增加5%至16%的年化收益率。由于高维固定效应可能引起估计问题，我们进行了套索估计。为了解决货源提供和价格预期之间潜在的内生性问题，我们在差异中差异（DiD）环境下对拍卖行的货源政策变化以及赝品和赝品的发现进行了准自然实验。我们还对受过去价格影响较小的子样本进行稳健性测试，例如收藏家去世后的房地产销售。

Keywords :拍卖；享乐定价；艺术品投资；艺术回报；血统；拍卖行；文化经济学

\[9\] On the Unification of Centralized and Decentralized Clearing Mechanisms in Financial Networks

标题:论金融网络集中与分散清算机制的统一

作者:M.W. Ketelaars,Peter Borm

出处:CentER Discussion Paper Series No. 2021-015

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3868871

Abstract : We analyze clearing mechanisms in financial networks in which agents may have both monetary individual assets and mutual liabilities. A clearing mechanism prescribes mutual payments between agents to settle their mutual liabilities. The corresponding payments, summarized in a payment matrix, are made in accordance with agent specific claims rules that stem from the vast literature on claims problems. We show that large classes of centralized and decentralized clearing mechanisms all prescribe the same payment matrix under the condition that the underlying claims rules satisfy composition; a property satisfied by the proportional rule that is often applied in insolvency proceedings. This payment matrix is the one that contains the minimal amount of payments required to clear the network. In fact, we show that composition guarantees unification of clearing mechanisms in which agents pay simultaneously and clearing mechanisms in which agents pay sequentially in any arbitrary order. Therefore, for a given financial network, each clearing mechanism gives rise to the same transfer allocation. Moreover, we provide an axiomatic characterization of the corresponding mutual claims rule on the basis of five axioms: scale invariance, equal treatment of equals, composition, path independence and consistency.
