\[1\] Measurement Error and Bias in Causal Models in Accounting Research

标题:会计研究因果模型中的计量误差与偏差

作者:Jared N. Jennings(Washington University in St. Louis),Jung Min Kim(University of Pennsylvania - The Wharton School),Joshua A. Lee(Brigham Young University),Daniel J. Taylor(The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3731197

Abstract : “Measurement error biases against \[finding results\]” is an often-repeated phrase used to dismiss validity threats arising from measurement error. As a general rule, this phrase is false. We provide examples of commonly encountered circumstances where the variable of interest is exogenous––the gold standard for causal inference––but where measurement error in empirical proxies nonetheless bias in favor of rejecting a true null hypothesis. In addition, we show that the common practice of including high-dimensional fixed effects, specifically firm fixed effects, can exacerbate this bias and lead researchers to spuriously estimate a causal effect when none exists. Finally, we show that measurement error pervades the accounting literature, and illustrate the effect of measurement error on causal inferences in a popular quasi-natural experimental setting where we can observe the measurement error in the treatment variable. We encourage researchers to triangulate inferences across multiple empirical proxies and to report results from specifications with and without high-dimensional fixed effects.

Keywords : measurement error, fixed effects, causal models, accounting research

Abstract :“测量误差对\[发现结果\]的偏见”是一个经常重复的短语，用于消除测量误差引起的有效性威胁。一般来说，这个短语是错误的。我们提供了一些常见情况的例子，其中兴趣变量是外生的（因果推断的黄金标准），但经验代理中的测量误差仍然偏向于拒绝真正的无效假设。此外，我们还表明，将高维固定效应（特别是固定效应）包括在内的常见做法会加剧这种偏见，并导致研究人员在不存在因果效应时错误地估计因果效应。最后，我们证明了会计文献中普遍存在计量误差，并在一个流行的准自然实验环境中说明了计量误差对因果推断的影响，在这个实验环境中我们可以观察到处理变量中的计量误差。我们鼓励研究人员在多个经验性代理之间进行三角化推断，并报告有无高维固定效应的规范结果。

Keywords :计量误差，固定效应，因果模型，会计研究

\[2\] Market Liquidity and Price Disparity: Evidence from Chinese Cross-listed Firms

标题:市场流动性与价格差异：来自中国交叉上市公司的证据

作者:Jun Liu(Tilburg University - TIAS School for Business and Society),Kai Wu(Central University of Finance and Economics (CUFE) - School of Finance),Fuwei Jiang(Central University of Finance and Economics (CUFE)),Botao Fan(China Securities Co., Ltd.)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3731149

Abstract : In this study, we investigate the association between stock liquidity and the H-share discount using a sample of Chinese cross-listed stocks in A- and H-shares markets. We examine the liquidity hypothesis by utilizing depth and trading activity and our results suggest that stocks with higher level in depth (active trading) of A-shares relative to H-shares are associated with less (more) H-share discount. Such effect is more pronounced in stocks that are non-state-owned and with low product market competition and ownership concentration. Moreover, we provide supportive evidence that the Stock Connect Program introduced in 2015 significantly dampens the association between stock liquidity and H-share discount. Overall, our findings highlight that the liquidity differences of cross-listed stocks explain large proportion of variation in price disparity.

Keywords : market liquidity, price disparity, the law of one price

Abstract :本文以中国a股和H股交叉上市股票为样本，研究了股票流动性与H股折价之间的关系。我们利用深度和交易活动来检验流动性假设，结果表明，相对于H股，A股的深度较高（活跃交易）的股票与H股的折价较少（较多）相关。这种效应在非国有、产品市场竞争和股权集中度较低的股票中更为明显。此外，我们还提供了支持性证据，证明2015年推出的“沪港通”计划显著抑制了股票流动性与H股折价之间的关联。总的来说，我们的研究结果强调了交叉上市股票的流动性差异在很大程度上解释了价格差异的变化。

Keywords :市场流动性，价格差异，一价定律

\[3\] Disagreement, Speculation, and the Idiosyncratic Volatility Puzzle

标题:分歧、投机和特殊波动性之谜

作者:Ying Jiang(Renmin University of China - Hanqing Institute; Renmin University of China),Jiening Pan(Nankai University),Jianqiu Wang(Capital University of Economics and Business),Ke Wu(Renmin University of China)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3731011

Abstract : We propose that disagreement and speculative trading play an important role in explaining the idiosyncratic volatility (IVOL) puzzle, the correlation among IVOL, market beta and trading volume, and the co-movement of IVOL. The empirical tests show that the return spread between high and low IVOL quintile portfolios is closely related to both aggregate and stock-level disagreement. The IVOL common factor is strongly correlated to aggregate disagreement. We provide a mechanism that high aggregate disagreement can lead to more firm level speculative trading, which is consistent with a factor structure in IVOL and a positive correlation among stock beta, the IVOL effect and the trading volume.

Keywords :分歧、投机性交易、学习、特质波动性难题

\[4\] The Bright Side of Financial Fragility

标题:金融脆弱的光明面

作者:Massimo Massa(INSEAD - Finance),David Schumacher(McGill University),Yan Wang(McMaster University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3730193

Abstract : We highlight an important but overlooked characteristic of financial fragility: “fragile” stocks are more liquid because they are sensitive to non-fundamental liquidity shocks. This makes them less sensitive to corporate actions with price impact and therefore affects firms’ incentives to engage in those actions. We show that fragile firms have lower share repurchases but invest more, the effects stronger for financially constrained firms. We establish causality by relying on exogenous changes in fragility induced by mergers of asset managers with portfolio overlap in the stocks. Our results suggest that financial fragility has direct but unexpected real implications for corporate actions.

Keywords : Financial Fragility, Liquidity, Share Repurchases, Corporate Investment.

Abstract :我们强调了金融脆弱性的一个重要但被忽视的特征：“脆弱”的股票流动性更强，因为它们对非基本面流动性冲击敏感。这使得他们对具有价格影响的企业行为不那么敏感，因此影响了企业参与这些行为的动机。研究表明，脆弱企业的股票回购率较低，但投资越多，对财务约束企业的影响越大。我们通过依赖于股票中投资组合重叠的资产管理公司合并引起的脆弱性的外生变化来建立因果关系。我们的研究结果表明，金融脆弱性对企业行为具有直接但意想不到的实际影响。

Keywords :金融脆弱性，流动性，股票回购，公司投资。

Abstract : It is widely acknowledged that asset returns are driven by common sources of risk, especially in challenging times when the benefits from traditional portfolio diversification fail to realize. \[6\] Interpretable Machine Learning for Diversified Portfolio Construction

作者:Markus Jaeger(Munich Reinsurance Company, Financial Solutions),Stephan Krügel(Munich Reinsurance Company, Financial Solutions),Dimitri Marinelli(Munich Re),Jochen Papenbrock(Firamis),Peter Schwendner(Zurich University of Applied Sciences)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3730144

Abstract : In this paper, the authors construct a pipeline to benchmark Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP) relative to Equal Risk Contribution (ERC) as examples of diversification strategies allocating to liquid multi-asset futures markets with dynamic leverage ("volatility target"). \[8\] Decarbonizing Everything: Climate Data, Industry Returns, and Portfolio Construction

标题:去碳化一切：气候数据、行业回报和投资组合构建

作者:Alexander Cheema-Fox(State Street Associates),Bridget Realmuto LaPerla(State Street Associates),George Serafeim(Harvard University - Harvard Business School),David Turkington(State Street Associates),Hui (Stacie) Wang(State Street Associates)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3693941

Abstract : We evaluate the relative merits and applicability of various measures of corporate climate risk, and we investigate how these measures can be combined to enhance risk-adjusted returns of equity portfolios. Metrics that are directly observable and easily quantified are limited by their relatively narrow scope, while metrics that are broader in scope are limited by their qualitative and potentially subjective construction. Across 43 industries we examine the performance of long-short investment strategies based on three metrics: carbon emissions from operations, carbon emissions from operations plus estimated upstream and downstream emissions, and analyst ratings of a firm’s climate related risk and opportunities management. Investment strategies that weight each industry by the variation of carbon outcomes across firms exhibit superior performance compared to market capitalization-weighted industry portfolios. We synthesize these findings into a framework that combines qualitative and quantitative measures to produce targeted strategies for managing climate risk while increasing risk-adjusted returns.

Keywords : climate change, investments, factor investing, ESG, environment, climate finance

Keywords :气候变化、投资、要素投资、ESG、环境、气候金融
