作者 : Jackie Shen , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08813

说明 : -

Abstract : This editorial article partially informs the algorithmic trading community about launching of the new journal Algorithmic Trading and Controls (ATC). ATC is an online open access journal that publishes novel works on algorithmic trading and its control methodologies. In this inaugural article, we discuss nine major challenges that contemporary Algo trading faces. There is nothing superstitiously magical about the number nine, but so is any other one. Several of these challenges are at the strategy level, including for example, trading of illiquid securities or optimal portfolio execution. Others are more at the level of risk management and controls, such as on how to develop automated controls, testing and simulations. The editorial views could be inevitably personal and biased, but have been explored with the most innocent intention of contributing to this important field in modern financial services and technologies.

\[2\] A Contextualist Decision Theory

标题 : 语境决策理论

作者 : Saleh Afroogh , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08914

说明 : -

Abstract : Decision theorists propose a normative theory of rational choice. Traditionally, they assume that they should provide some constant and invariant principles as criteria for rational decisions, and indirectly, for agents. They seek a decision theory that invaribably works for all agents all the time. They believe that a rational agent should follow a certain principle, perhaps the principle of maximizing expected utility everywhere, all the time. As a result of the given context, these principles are considered, in this sense, context independent. Furthermore, decision theorists usually assume that the relevant agents at work are ideal agents, and they believe that non ideal agents should follow them so that their decisions qualify as rational. These principles are universal rules. I will refer to this context independent and universal approach in traditional decision theory as Invariantism. This approach is, implicitly or explicitly, adopted by theories which are proposed on the basis of these two assumptions.

摘要 : 决策理论家提出了理性选择的规范理论。传统上，他们假设他们应该提供一些恒定不变的原则，作为理性决策的标准，并间接地为代理人。他们寻求一种决策理论，这种理论一直适用于所有代理。他们认为一个理性的代理人应该遵循一定的原则，也许是在任何地方、任何时候都要最大化预期效用的原则。作为给定语境的结果，这些原则在这个意义上被认为是与语境无关的。此外，决策理论家通常假设工作中的相关主体是理想主体，他们认为非理想主体应该遵循理想主体，这样他们的决策才符合理性。这些原则是普遍的规则。我将传统决策理论中这种与上下文无关的通用方法称为不变性。基于这两个假设提出的理论，隐式或显式地采用了这种方法。

\[3\] How does COVID 19 change insurance and vaccine demand Evidence from short panel data in Japan

标题 : covid19如何改变保险和疫苗需求来自日本短面板数据的证据

作者 : Eiji Yamamura, Yoshiro Tsutsui , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08922

说明 : -

Abstract : In this study, we explored how the coronavirus disease (COVID 19) affected the demand for insurance and vaccines in Japan from mid March to mid April 2020. Through independent internet surveys, respondents were asked hypothetical questions concerning the demand for insurance and vaccines for protection against COVID 19. Using the collected short panel data, after controlling for individual characteristics using the fixed effects model, the key findings, within the context of the pandemic, were as follows (1) Contrary to extant studies, the demand for insurance by females was smaller than that by their male counterparts (2) The gap in demand for insurance between genders increased as the pandemic prevailed (3) The demand for a vaccine by females was higher than that for males and (4) As COVID 19 spread throughout Japan, demand for insurance decreased, whereas the demand for a vaccine increased.

摘要 : 在这项研究中，我们探讨了2020年3月中旬至4月中旬日本冠状病毒病（covid19）对保险和疫苗需求的影响。通过独立的互联网调查，受访者被问到了一些假设性的问题，这些问题涉及到对疫苗和保险的需求，以预防covid19。使用收集的短面板数据，在使用固定效应模型控制个体特征后，在大流行背景下的主要发现如下（1）与现有研究相反，女性对保险的需求小于男性（2）随着流感大流行，性别之间的保险需求差距增大（3）女性对疫苗的需求高于男性（4）随着COVID 19在日本的传播，保险需求减少，而对疫苗的需求则减少疫苗增加了。

\[4\] Generalized BN S Model Application

标题 : 广义bns模型的应用

作者 : Xianfei Hui, Baiqing Sun, Indranil SenGupta, Hui Jiang , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08984

说明 : -

Abstract : We use the superposition of the Levy processes to optimize the classic BN S model. Considering the frequent fluctuations of price parameters difficult to accurately estimate in the model, we preprocess the price data based on fuzzy theory. The price of S&P500 stock index options in the past ten years are analyzed, and the deterministic fluctuations are captured by machine learning methods. The results show that the new model in a fuzzy environment solves the long term dependence problem of the classic model with fewer parameter changes, and effectively analyzes the random dynamic characteristics of stock index option price time series.

摘要 : 我们利用Levy过程的叠加来优化经典bns模型。考虑到模型中价格参数的频繁波动难以准确估计，本文基于模糊理论对价格数据进行了预处理。对近10年来标普500指数期权的价格进行了分析，并用机器学习方法捕捉了确定性波动。结果表明，新模型在模糊环境下以较少的参数变化解决了经典模型的长期依赖问题，有效地分析了股指期权价格时间序列的随机动态特性。

\[5\] Extensive networks would eliminate the demand for pricing formulas

标题 : 广泛的网络将消除对定价公式的需求

作者 : Jaegi Jeon, Kyunghyun Park, Jeonggyu Huh , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.09064

说明 : Comments: 18 pages,5 figures, 3 tables

Abstract : In this study, we generate a large number of implied volatilities for the Stochastic Alpha Beta Rho (SABR) model using a graphics processing unit (GPU) based simulation and enable an extensive neural network to learn them. This model does not have any exact pricing formulas for vanilla options, and neural networks have an outstanding ability to approximate various functions. Surprisingly, the network reduces the simulation noises by itself, thereby achieving as much accuracy as the Monte Carlo simulation. Extremely high accuracy cannot be attained via existing approximate formulas. Moreover, the network is as efficient as the approaches based on the formulas. When evaluating based on high accuracy and efficiency, extensive networks can eliminate the necessity of the pricing formulas for the SABR model. Another significant contribution is that a novel method is proposed to examine the errors based on nonlinear regression. This approach is easily extendable to other pricing models for which it is hard to induce analytic formulas.

摘要 : 在这项研究中，我们使用图形处理单元（GPU）为随机α-β-ρ（SABR）模型模拟产生大量隐含的波动率，并使用广泛的神经网络来学习它们。该模型没有精确的香草期权定价公式，神经网络对各种函数的逼近能力很强。令人惊讶的是，网络本身减少了模拟噪声，从而达到了与蒙特卡罗模拟一样的精度。现有的近似公式无法达到极高的精度。此外，该网络与基于公式的方法同样有效。在基于高精度和高效率的评估时，扩展网络可以消除SABR模型定价公式的必要性。另一个重要贡献是提出了一种基于非线性回归的误差检测方法。这种方法很容易推广到其他定价模型中，很难导出解析公式。

\[6\] Probabilistic Framework For Loss Distribution Of Smart Contract Risk

标题 : 智能合约风险损失分布的概率框架

作者 : Petar Jevtic, Nicolas Lanchier , 类别 ：Discrete Mathematics (cs.DM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08964

说明 : Comments: 26 pages, 2 figures

Abstract : Smart contract risk can be defined as a financial risk of loss due to cyber attacks on or contagious failures of smart contracts. Its quantification is of paramount importance to technology platform providers as well as companies and individuals when considering the deployment of this new technology. That is why, as our primary contribution, we propose a structural framework of aggregate loss distribution for smart contract risk under the assumption of a tree stars graph topology representing the network of interactions among smart contracts and their users. Up to our knowledge, there exist no theoretical frameworks or models of an aggregate loss distribution for smart contracts in this setting. To achieve our goal, we contextualize the problem in the probabilistic graph theoretical framework using bond percolation models. We assume that the smart contract network topology is represented by a random tree graph of finite size, and that each smart contract is the center of a random star graph whose leaves represent the users of the smart contract. We allow for heterogeneous loss topology superimposed on this smart contract and user topology and provide analytical results and instructive numerical examples.

摘要 : 智能合约风险可以定义为由于网络攻击或智能合约的传染性失败而造成损失的财务风险。它的量化对于技术平台提供商以及公司和个人在考虑部署这项新技术时至关重要。这就是为什么，作为我们的主要贡献，我们提出了一个结构框架的总损失分布的智能合约风险的假设下，树星图拓扑结构表示网络的互动智能合约和他们的用户。据我们所知，在这种情况下，不存在智能合约总损失分布的理论框架或模型。为了达到我们的目标，我们在概率图理论框架下使用键渗流模型来解决这个问题。我们假设智能合约网络拓扑由一个有限大小的随机树形图表示，每个智能合约是一个随机星形图的中心，其叶子代表智能合约的用户。我们考虑了叠加在智能合约和用户拓扑上的异构损耗拓扑，并给出了分析结果和有指导意义的数值例子。

\[7\] Graphical Models for Financial Time Series and Portfolio Selection

标题 : 金融时间序列和投资组合选择的图形模型

作者 : Ni Zhan, Yijia Sun, Aman Jakhar, He Liu , 类别 ：Machine Learning (cs.LG)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.09214

说明 : Comments: Published at ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF '20)

Abstract : We examine a variety of graphical models to construct optimal portfolios. Graphical models such as PCA KMeans, autoencoders, dynamic clustering, and structural learning can capture the time varying patterns in the covariance matrix and allow the creation of an optimal and robust portfolio. We compared the resulting portfolios from the different models with baseline methods. In many cases our graphical strategies generated steadily increasing returns with low risk and outgrew the S&P 500 index. This work suggests that graphical models can effectively learn the temporal dependencies in time series data and are proved useful in asset management.

摘要 : 我们研究了各种图形模型来构建最优投资组合。图形模型，如PCA-KMeans、自动编码器、动态聚类和结构学习可以捕获协方差矩阵中的时变模式，并允许创建一个最优和健壮的投资组合。我们将不同模型的投资组合与基线方法进行了比较。在许多情况下，我们的图形化策略产生了稳定增长的低风险回报，并超过了标准普尔500指数。这项工作表明，图形模型可以有效地学习时间序列数据的时间依赖性，并被证明是有用的资产管理。

\[8\] Where does the Stimulus go Deep Generative Model for Commercial Banking Deposits

标题 : 刺激政策将何去何从商业银行存款的深层次生成模式

作者 : Ni Zhan , 类别 ：Machine Learning (cs.LG)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.09230

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper examines deposits of individuals ( retail ) and large companies ( wholesale ) in the U.S. banking industry, and how these deposit types are impacted by macroeconomic factors, such as quantitative easing (QE). Actual data for deposits by holder are unavailable. We use a dataset on banks financial information and probabilistic generative model to predict industry retail wholesale deposit split from 2000 to 2020. Our model assumes account balances arise from separate retail and wholesale lognormal distributions and fit parameters of distributions by minimizing error between actual bank metrics and simulated metrics using the model s generative process. We use time series regression to forward predict retail wholesale deposits as function of loans, retail loans, and reserve balances at Fed banks. We find increase in reserves (representing QE) increases wholesale but not retail deposits, and increase in loans increase both wholesale and retail deposits evenly. The result shows that QE following the 2008 financial crisis benefited large companies more than average individuals, a relevant finding for economic decision making. In addition, this work benefits bank management strategy by providing forecasting capability for retail wholesale deposits.

摘要 : 本文考察了美国银行业个人（零售）和大公司（批发）的存款，以及这些存款类型如何受到宏观经济因素（如量化宽松）的影响。持有人存款的实际数据不可用。本文利用银行财务信息数据集和概率生成模型对2000-2020年行业零售批发存款分割进行了预测。我们的模型假设账户余额来自单独的零售和批发对数正态分布，并通过使用模型的生成过程最小化实际银行指标和模拟指标之间的误差来拟合分布参数。我们使用时间序列回归预测零售批发存款作为贷款、零售贷款和美联储银行准备金余额的函数。我们发现，准备金的增加（代表量化宽松）增加了批发存款，但没有增加零售存款，贷款的增加使批发存款和零售存款都平均增加。结果显示，2008年金融危机后的量化宽松政策对大公司的好处大于对普通个人的好处，这是一个与经济决策相关的发现。此外，这项工作提供零售批发存款的预测能力，有利于银行的管理策略。
