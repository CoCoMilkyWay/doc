\[1\] Big Data in Finance

标题:金融大数据

作者:Itay Goldstein, Chester S Spatt, Mao Ye

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/7/3213/6210658; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab038

Abstract : Big data is revolutionizing the finance industry and has the potential to significantly shape future research in finance. This special issue contains papers following the 2019 NBER-RFS Conference on Big Data. In this introduction to the special issue, we define the “big data” phenomenon as a combination of three features: large size, high dimension, and complex structure. Using the papers in the special issue, we discuss how new research builds on these features to push the frontier on fundamental questions across areas in finance—including corporate finance, market microstructure, and asset pricing. Finally, we offer some thoughts for future research directions.

Abstract:大数据正在给金融业带来革命性的变革，并有可能对未来的金融研究产生重大影响。本期特刊包含2019年NBER-RFS大数据会议之后的论文。在本期专刊的导言中，我们将“大数据”现象定义为三个特征的结合：大数据、高维度、结构复杂。利用特刊中的论文，我们讨论了新的研究如何建立在这些特征的基础上，推动金融领域基本问题的前沿，包括公司金融、市场微观结构和资产定价。最后，对未来的研究方向提出了一些思考。

\[2\] Selecting Directors Using Machine Learning

标题:利用机器学习选择控制器

作者:Isil Erel, Léa H Stern, Chenhao Tan, Michael S Weisbach

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/7/3226/6239715; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab050

Abstract : Can algorithms assist firms in their decisions on nominating corporate directors? Directors predicted by algorithms to perform poorly indeed do perform poorly compared to a realistic pool of candidates in out-of-sample tests. Predictably bad directors are more likely to be male, accumulate more directorships, and have larger networks than the directors the algorithm would recommend in their place. Companies with weaker governance structures are more likely to nominate them. Our results suggest that machine learning holds promise for understanding the process by which governance structures are chosen and has potential to help real-world firms improve their governance.

Abstract:算法能帮助公司做出提名公司董事的决定吗？与样本外测试中现实的候选人库相比，算法预测的执行较差的控制器确实执行较差。可以预见的是，糟糕的董事更可能是男性，积累更多的董事职位，拥有比算法推荐的董事更大的人脉。治理结构较弱的公司更有可能提名他们。我们的研究结果表明，机器学习有希望了解治理结构的选择过程，并有可能帮助现实世界中的公司改善其治理。

\[3\] Measuring Corporate Culture Using Machine Learning

标题:用机器学习测量企业文化

作者:Kai Li, Feng Mai, Rui Shen, Xinyan Yan

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/7/3265/5869446; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa079

Abstract : We create a culture dictionary using one of the latest machine learning techniques—the word embedding model—and 209,480 earnings call transcripts. We score the five corporate cultural values of innovation, integrity, quality, respect, and teamwork for 62,664 firm-year observations over the period 2001–2018. We show that an innovative culture is broader than the usual measures of corporate innovation – R&D expenses and the number of patents. Moreover, we show that corporate culture correlates with business outcomes, including operational efficiency, risk-taking, earnings management, executive compensation design, firm value, and deal making, and that the culture-performance link is more pronounced in bad times. Finally, we present suggestive evidence that corporate culture is shaped by major corporate events, such as mergers and acquisitions.

Abstract:我们使用最新的机器学习技术——单词嵌入模型和209480个财报电话会议记录创建一个文化词典。我们对2001-2018年62664个企业年度的观察结果，对创新、诚信、质量、尊重和团队合作这五种企业文化价值观进行了评分。我们发现，创新文化比通常衡量企业创新的指标——研发费用和专利数量更广泛。此外，我们还发现，企业文化与经营成果（包括经营效率、风险承担、盈余管理、高管薪酬设计、企业价值和交易决策）相关，在经济不景气时期，文化与绩效的关联更为显著。最后，我们提出了企业文化是由重大企业事件，如兼并和收购形成的建议性证据。

\[4\] Microstructure in the Machine Age

标题:机械时代的微观结构

作者:David Easley, Marcos López de Prado, Maureen O’Hara, Zhibai Zhang

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/7/3316/5868424; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa078

Abstract : Understanding modern market microstructure phenomena requires large amounts of data and advanced mathematical tools. We demonstrate how machine learning can be applied to microstructural research. We find that microstructure measures continue to provide insights into the price process in current complex markets. Some microstructure features with high explanatory power exhibit low predictive power, while others with less explanatory power have more predictive power. We find that some microstructure-based measures are useful for out-of-sample prediction of various market statistics, leading to questions about market efficiency. We also show how microstructure measures can have important cross-asset effects. Our results are derived using 87 liquid futures contracts across all asset classes.

Abstract:了解现代市场微观结构现象需要大量的数据和先进的数学工具。我们演示了如何将机器学习应用于微观结构研究。我们发现，微观结构的措施继续提供洞察价格过程中，目前复杂的市场。一些解释力高的微观结构特征的预测力较低，而解释力低的微观结构特征的预测力较大。我们发现，一些基于微观结构的指标对于各种市场统计数据的样本外预测是有用的，这导致了有关市场效率的问题。我们还展示了微观结构指标如何具有重要的交叉资产效应。我们的结果是使用所有资产类别的87个流动性期货合约得出的。

\[5\] Institutional Order Handling and Broker-Affiliated Trading Venues

标题:机构订单处理和经纪人附属交易场所

作者:Amber Anand, Mehrdad Samadi, Jonathan Sokobin, Kumar Venkataraman

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/7/3364/6123760; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab004

Abstract : Using detailed order handling data, we find that institutional brokers who route more orders to affiliated alternative trading systems (ATSs) are associated with lower execution quality (i.e., lower fill rates and higher implementation shortfall costs). To separate clients’ preference for ATSs from brokers’ routing decisions, we confirm these results for orders where brokers have more order handling discretion, matched broker analysis that accounts for ATS usage, matched child orders that account for client intent, and based on an exogenous constraint on ATS venue choice. Our results suggest that increased transparency of order routing practices will improve execution quality.

Abstract:使用详细的订单处理数据，我们发现机构经纪人将更多的订单发送到附属替代交易系统（ATS）与较低的执行质量相关（即，较低的填充率和较高的执行短缺成本）。为了将客户对自动交易系统的偏好与经纪人的路由决策分开，我们对经纪人有更多订单处理自由裁量权的订单、解释自动交易系统使用情况的匹配经纪人分析、解释客户意图的匹配子订单以及基于自动交易系统场地选择的外部约束的订单确认了这些结果。我们的结果表明，增加订单路由实践的透明度将提高执行质量。

\[6\] Demand for Information, Uncertainty, and the Response of U.S. Treasury Securities to News

标题:信息需求、不确定性与美国国债对新闻的反应

作者:Hedi Benamar, Thierry Foucault, Clara Vega

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/7/3403/5862643; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa072

Abstract : We use clickstream data to show that investors’ demand for information about macroeconomic factors affecting the path of future interest rates is a measure of their uncertainty about this path. In particular, an increase in information demand ahead of influential economic announcements affecting investors’ beliefs about future interest rates predicts a stronger reaction of U.S. Treasury note yields to these announcements, as it should if information demand positively covaries with uncertainty. This relationship does not vanish after using standard measures of uncertainty as predictors, suggesting that clickstream data contain unique information about investors’ uncertainty.

Abstract:我们使用clickstream数据显示，投资者对影响未来利率走势的宏观经济因素信息的需求，是他们对这一走势不确定性的一种度量。特别是，在影响投资者对未来利率的信心的有影响力的经济公告之前，信息需求的增加，预示着美国国债收益率对这些公告的反应会更强烈，如果信息需求与不确定性正相关，这是应该的。在使用标准的不确定性度量作为预测因子后，这种关系并没有消失，这表明clickstream数据包含关于投资者不确定性的独特信息。

\[7\] Thousands of Alpha Tests

标题:成千上万的阿尔法测试

作者:Stefano Giglio, Yuan Liao, Dacheng Xiu

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/7/3456/5911131; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa111

Abstract : Data snooping is a major concern in empirical asset pricing. We develop a new framework to rigorously perform multiple hypothesis testing in linear asset pricing models, while limiting the occurrence of false positive results typically associated with data snooping. By exploiting a variety of machine learning techniques, our multiple-testing procedure is robust to omitted factors and missing data. We also prove its asymptotic validity when the number of tests is large relative to the sample size, as in many finance applications. To improve the finite sample performance, we also provide a wild-bootstrap procedure for inference and prove its validity in this setting. Finally, we illustrate the empirical relevance in the context of hedge fund performance evaluation.

Abstract:数据窥探是实证资产定价中的一个主要问题。我们开发了一个新的框架，在线性资产定价模型中严格执行多假设检验，同时限制通常与数据窥探相关的假阳性结果的发生。通过利用各种机器学习技术，我们的多重测试程序对遗漏因素和缺失数据具有鲁棒性。我们还证明了它的渐近有效性时，测试的数量是相对大的样本大小，在许多金融应用。为了提高有限样本的性能，我们还提供了一个用于推理的野生引导过程，并证明了它在这种情况下的有效性。最后，我们在对冲基金绩效评估的背景下说明了实证相关性。
