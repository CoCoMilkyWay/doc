\[1\] Can the Changes in Fundamentals Explain the Attenuation of Investment-Related Anomalies

标题:基本面变化能否解释投资相关异常的减弱

作者:Siu Kai Choy,Craig M. Lewis,Yongxian Tan

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3903888

Abstract : The existing literature attributes the recent decay of stock market anomalies to increased arbitrage (e.g., Chordia, Subrahmanyam and Tong, 2014; Engelberg, McLean and Pontiff, 2016; Green, Hand and Zhang, 2016). In this paper, we present evidence that the apparent demise of several prominent classes of stock market anomalies is better explained by changes in underlying fundamentals. The differences in subsequent fundamental performance between the long- and short-leg portfolios, as measured by standardized seasonally differenced return on average assets (SDROA), decrease substantially after 2003 for the financing, investment and accruals anomalies we examine. We find that quarterly stock return can predict SDROAs up to four quarters ahead and that the decline in SDROA spreads is accompanied by a decline in the investment spreads between the long- and short-leg portfolios. These results are consistent with the q-theory of investment, which attributes the attenuation of these anomalies to time variation in discount rates. We provide evidence that the time variation in discount rates is associated with increased uncertainty. We also show that neither changes in analyst forecast errors nor proxies for increased arbitrage can explain the attenuation of these anomalies.

Keywords : Stock Market Anomalies; Q-Theory; Arbitrage; Uncertainty

Abstract :现有文献将最近股市异常现象的衰退归因于套利增加（例如，Chordia、Subrahmanyam和Tong，2014；Engelberg、McLean和Pontiff，2016；Green、Hand和Zhang，2016）。在这篇论文中，我们提出证据表明，基本面变化更好地解释了几类显著的股市异常现象的明显消亡。根据标准化的季节性平均资产回报率（SDROA）衡量，长线和短线投资组合之间的后续基本面表现差异在2003年后大幅减少，因为我们检查了融资、投资和应计项目异常情况。我们发现，季度股票收益率可以预测未来四个季度的SDROA，并且SDROA利差的下降伴随着长短段投资组合之间的投资利差的下降。这些结果与投资的q理论一致，该理论将这些异常的衰减归因于贴现率的时间变化。我们提供证据证明贴现率的时间变化与不确定性增加有关。我们还表明，分析师预测误差的变化和套利增加的代理都不能解释这些异常的减弱。

Keywords :股市异常；Q理论；套汇不确定性

\[2\] Volatility Contagion between Crude Oil and G7 Stock Markets in the Light of Trade Wars and COVID-19: An Application Based on the TVP-VAR Extended Joint Connectedness Approach

标题:从贸易战和新冠病毒19看原油和G7股票市场之间的波动传染：基于TVP-VAR扩展联合连通性方法的应用

作者:Ioannis Chatziantoniou,Christos Floros,David Gabauer

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3929734

Abstract : We investigate volatility contagion across G7 stock markets and the market for crude oil for the period between 2007 and 2021. Following the work of Balcilar et al. (2021), we utilise the TVP-VAR extended joint connectedness method and compare results to the standard TVP-VAR method that predicates upon the normalisation approach by Diebold and Yilmaz (2009, 2012, 2014). Both methods provide qualitatively similar results. Overall, findings suggest that connectedness in this network is highly responsive to events that greatly affect international financial markets and assumes large values across the sample period. Prominent among our results is that the crude oil market is an important net transmitter of volatility shocks across the network during the 2014 oil collapse period. In addition, the period from the beginning of 2018 and until mid-2019 is a rather turbulent period for stock markets a fact which is reflected upon the net receiving character of the market for crude oil. Finally, we show that the Canadian stock market is a persistent net transmitter of volatility in recent years, following developments in international trade and the outbreak of the COVID-19 crisis.

Keywords : Crude oil; stock market indices; G7; COVID-19; TVP-VAR; dynamic connectedness; volatility spillovers.

Abstract :我们调查了2007年至2021年间七国集团股票市场和原油市场的波动传染。继Balcilar等人（2021年）的工作之后，我们使用TVP-VAR扩展联合连通性方法，并将结果与标准TVP-VAR方法进行比较，该方法以Diebold和Yilmaz（2009、2012、2014）的归一化方法为基础。两种方法都提供了定性相似的结果。总体而言，研究结果表明，该网络中的连通性对严重影响国际金融市场的事件具有高度响应性，并在整个样本期内假设了较大的价值。我们的结果中最突出的是，原油市场是2014年石油崩溃期间整个网络波动性冲击的重要净传递者。此外，从2018年初到2019年年中这段时间是股市相当动荡的时期，这一事实反映在原油市场的净收货特征上。最后，我们表明，随着国际贸易的发展和新冠疫情的爆发，加拿大股市是近年来波动性的持续净传递者。

Keywords :原油股票市场指数；七国集团；2019冠状病毒疾病TVP-VAR；动态连通性；波动溢出。

\[6\] Intangible assets and the cross-section of stock returns

标题:无形资产与股票收益的横截面

作者:Dion Bongaerts,Xiaowei Kang,Mathijs A. Van Dijk

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3927990

Abstract : We examine whether intangible assets are priced in the cross-section of stock returns. We find that intangible asset intensity has more explanatory power than size, value, profitability, and investment. An intangibles-based long-short factor has a higher Sharpe ratio than these established factors. Adding the intangible factor to the Fama-French five-factor model improves the description of average returns and makes the investment factor redundant. The intangible factor is distinct from traditional growth strategies, provides a hedge to value and quality strategies, and expands investors’ opportunity sets. Intangible intensity as characteristic is more important than as risk factor, consistent with intangibles-based mispricing.

Keywords : Intangible Assets, Knowledge Capital, Organization Capital, Asset Pricing, Factor Models, Information Complexity, Mispricing

Abstract :我们检查无形资产是否在股票回报的横截面中定价。我们发现无形资产强度比规模、价值、盈利能力和投资更具解释力。基于无形资产的长短因子比这些既定因子具有更高的夏普比率。将无形因素添加到Fama-French五因素模型中改进了对平均回报的描述，并使投资因素变得多余。无形因素有别于传统的增长战略，为价值和质量战略提供了对冲，并扩大了投资者的机会集。无形强度作为特征比作为风险因素更重要，与基于无形资产的错误定价一致。

Keywords :无形资产、知识资本、组织资本、资产定价、因素模型、信息复杂性、错误定价

\[7\] Multi-Asset Option Pricing Using Normal Tempered Stable Processes with Stochastic Correlation

标题:基于随机相关正态稳定过程的多资产期权定价

作者:Young Shin Kim,Hyangju Kim,Jaehyung Choi,Frank J. Fabozzi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3927399

Abstract : We present a new model of normal tempered stable (NTS) processes with stochastic correlation for multi-asset option pricing. The model is constructed by extending the constant correlation term in the NTS model to the stochastic correlation making use of the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process. As the proposed model captures the stylized empirical facts of heavy tails, kurtosis, skewness, and stochastic correlation, multi-asset option pricing is one of the various applications. In particular, we discuss its application to quanto option pricing as a bivariate example and derive its closed-form solution under the risk-neutral measure. Building on the calibration results, we identify the presence of stochastic correlation in the risk-neutral world.

Keywords : Normal tempered stable process, Levy process, Stochastic correlation, Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process, Multi-asset option pricing, Quanto option pricing

Abstract :针对多资产期权定价问题，提出了一种新的随机相关正态回火稳定（NTS）过程模型。该模型是利用Ornstein-Uhlenbeck过程将NTS模型中的常数相关项扩展为随机相关项而建立的。由于所提出的模型捕捉了厚尾、峰度、偏度和随机相关性等典型的经验事实，因此多资产期权定价是各种应用之一。特别地，作为一个二元例子，我们讨论了它在量子期权定价中的应用，并在风险中性测度下导出了它的闭式解。基于校准结果，我们确定风险中性世界中存在随机相关性。

Keywords :正态稳定过程、Levy过程、随机相关性、Ornstein-Uhlenbeck过程、多资产期权定价、Quanto期权定价

Abstract : We provide novel asymptotic tools for tests of asset pricing models and factor model comparisons when portfolios trade dynamically using lagged information. \[9\] Macro-Financial Trends in a Model with Concentrated Ownership of Capital

标题:资本所有权集中模型中的宏观金融趋势

作者:Francesco Saverio Gaudio

出处:WBS Finance Group Research Paper

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3929331

Abstract : This paper investigates the macroeconomic and asset pricing consequences of the upward trend in financial market participation observed in the U.S. since the late 1980s. In a limited participation two-agent Real Business Cycle model where stockholders feature external habit preferences, higher participation produces higher equity premium and stock market volatility, while reducing the risk-free rate and the standard deviation of aggregate consumption. When coupled with a lower volatility of aggregate shocks, this mechanism helps rationalizing a period characterized by milder aggregate fluctuations but increased perceived risk in asset markets, such as the Great Moderation. I show that these results stem from a novel mechanism whereby an increase in the participation rate improves risk-sharing but raises the representative investor's average risk-aversion, with the latter channel dominating the former.
