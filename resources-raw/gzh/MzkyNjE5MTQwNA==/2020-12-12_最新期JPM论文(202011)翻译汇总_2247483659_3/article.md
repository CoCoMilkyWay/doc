\[3\] Monetization Matters: Active Tail Risk Management and the Great Virus Crisis

标题:货币化问题：积极的尾部风险管理和重大病毒危机

作者:Vineer Bhansali, Linda Chang, Jeremie Holdom and Marcy Rappaport

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/1/16;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.181

Abstract : The authors discuss monetization strategies for both left- and right-tail risk hedging to illustrate the potential benefits of active management of hedges. In particular, by including actual data from the sharp COVID-19 pandemic-related market correction and subsequent rebound of 2020, the authors quantify how monetization strategies have the ability to improve the performance of portfolio hedges. This extends previous work on active tail risk hedging published in this journal. The authors conclude that active management of tail hedging can result in significant increases in the efficacy of tail hedging.

TOPICS: Options, performance measurement, portfolio construction, tail risks

Key Finding

\- Simple monetization rules designed as a function of the option price can result in the full or partial sale of a hedging position that has appreciated in price.

\- Monetization strategies for both left-tail and right-tail risk hedging have the ability to improve the performance of portfolio hedges.

\- Active management of tail hedging can result in significant increases in the efficacy of tail hedging.

Abstract:作者讨论了左尾和右尾风险套期保值的货币化策略，以说明积极管理套期保值的潜在好处。特别是，通过纳入夏普COVID-19大流行相关市场修正和随后的2020年反弹的实际数据，作者量化了货币化策略如何能够改善投资组合对冲的表现。这扩展了先前在本期刊上发表的关于主动尾部风险对冲的研究。作者认为，积极管理尾部套期保值可以显著提高尾部套期保值的有效性。

主题：期权，业绩衡量，投资组合构建，尾部风险

Key Finding

\- 作为期权价格函数设计的简单货币化规则可以导致价格上涨的对冲头寸的全部或部分出售

\- 左尾和右尾风险对冲的货币化策略都能够提高组合套期保值的绩效。

\- 积极管理尾部套期保值可以显著提高尾部套期保值的有效性。

\[4\] Is Smart Beta Still Smart under the Lens of the Diversification Return?

标题:在多元化回报的镜头下，Smart Beta是否依然聪明？

作者:Wenguang Lin and Gary C. Sanger

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/1/29;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.180

Abstract : The diversification return is known to explain a significant portion of portfolio growth rates. Separately, results from backtesting have created significant interest in smart beta portfolio strategies. Extant research shows that smart beta strategies outperform the passive value-weighted benchmark because of their implicit risk factor tilts, such as value and size. This article reexamines the performance of a subset of smart beta strategies, known as fundamental indexing, focusing on the diversification return. The authors demonstrate that the outperformance of these strategies is completely explained by the diversification returns embedded in the portfolio rebalancing inherent in all such strategies. Efficient factor tilts explain none of the outperformance.

TOPICS: Factors, risk premia, factor-based models, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- The article confirms past results that fundamental indexes outperform the market-cap-weighted index.

\- Importantly, the article shows that all outperformance is due to the diversification return of Fernholz and Shay and Booth and Fama.

\- Standard explanations for outperformance (factor tilts) do not appear to hold up in factor models after accounting for the diversification return.

Abstract:众所周知，分散投资回报率可以解释投资组合增长率的很大一部分。另外，回溯测试的结果也引起了人们对智能beta投资组合策略的浓厚兴趣。现有研究表明，智能贝塔策略优于被动价值加权基准，因为其隐含的风险因素倾斜，如价值和规模。本文重新检查了智能beta策略子集（称为基本面指数）的性能，重点关注多样化回报。作者证明，这些策略的优异表现完全是由投资组合再平衡所蕴含的多样化回报来解释的。有效因子倾斜并不能解释这些优异表现。

主题：因素，风险溢价，基于因素的模型，绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- 本文证实了过去基本面指数表现优于市值加权指数的结论

\- 重要的是，本文证明了所有的跑赢都是由于费恩霍兹和谢伊以及布斯和法马的多元化收益。

\- 在考虑了多元化收益后，因子模型中对超额收益（因子倾斜）的标准解释似乎并不成立。

\[5\] Sharpe Ratios, Target Ratios, and Return Goals

标题:夏普比率、目标比率和回报目标

作者:Martin L. Leibowitz and Stanley Kogelman

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/1/41;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.179

Abstract : Some form of success estimation is present in virtually all decision-making processes. In most cases, estimations are implicit and judgmental. However, in certain data-rich areas, success prospects can be sharpened into probabilities. Although funds may settle for an expected return that equals some fixed target return, that match results in only a 50% probability of success. However, important goals may require a higher success probability, such as 60%. In this article, the authors present an approach that facilitates calculation of success probabilities for many common investment situations. The key success factor turns out to be the target ratio (T-ratio), a generalization of the standard Sharpe ratio. In addition to fixed return targets, the T-ratio can be applied to a wide range of market-dependent targets such as policy portfolios, benchmark indexes, and/or peer group percentiles. Moreover, within the typically relevant range, a simple approximation can directly map T-ratio values into success probabilities. The structure of the T-ratio underscores the importance of more tightly integrating risk control considerations and success probabilities into the return-seeking process.

TOPICS: Performance measurement, risk management, volatility measures

Key Finding

\- The common practice of matching the expected portfolio return to some fixed target return may prove insufficient for critically important goals that require a higher than 50% probability of success.

\- To obtain a success probability above 50%, a fund’s risk–return structure must provide a sufficiently high T-ratio, a generalization of the Sharpe ratio. A simple formula, based on this T-ratio, can be applied to estimate success probability.

\- To preserve a fund’s return advantage and desired probability of success, the fund also must achieve a level of risk control that results in the T-ratio value associated with that probability.

Abstract:在几乎所有的决策过程中都存在某种形式的成功评估。在大多数情况下，估计是隐含的和判断性的。然而，在某些数据丰富的领域，成功的前景可以被锐化为概率。虽然基金可能会满足于一个预期的回报率，相当于某个固定的目标回报率，匹配的结果只有50%的成功概率。然而，重要的目标可能需要更高的成功概率，比如60%。在这篇文章中，作者提出了一种方法，方便计算许多常见投资情况下的成功概率。成功的关键因素是目标比率（T-ratio），它是标准夏普比率的推广。除了固定收益目标，T比率还可以应用于广泛的市场依赖目标，如政策投资组合、基准指数和/或同行群体百分位数。此外，在典型的相关范围内，一个简单的近似值可以直接将T比率值映射为成功概率。T比率的结构强调了将风险控制考虑因素和成功概率更紧密地结合到寻求回报过程中的重要性。

主题：绩效衡量，风险管理，波动性度量

Key Finding

\- 将预期投资组合回报率与某个固定目标回报率相匹配的常见做法可能不足以满足要求成功概率高于50%的关键重要目标

\- 要获得高于50%的成功概率，基金的风险回报结构必须提供足够高的T比率，夏普比的推广。基于这个T比率的一个简单公式可以用来估计成功概率

\- 为了保持基金的回报优势和期望的成功概率，基金还必须实现一定程度的风险控制，以获得与该概率相关的T比率值。

\[6\] Optimal Beats Naive Diversification: Asset Allocation Using High-Frequency Data

标题:最优策略胜过幼稚的多元化：利用高频数据进行资产配置

作者:Nuria Alemany, Vicent Aragó and Enrique Salvador

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/1/51;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.185

Abstract : This article evaluates the usefulness of high-frequency data in optimal portfolio choice. The authors use a comprehensive list of major stock indexes and different frequencies of observations. Furthermore, they consider the impact of economic cycles, microstructure noise, and seasonality on performance. Their results show the ability of high-frequency data–based strategies to beat both monthly and daily based strategies and the benchmark equally weighted portfolio, even in presence of transaction costs. The authors also find that the outperformance arises from the reduction in the estimation error of the covariance matrix, which offsets the increase in transaction costs.

TOPICS: Performance measurement, portfolio construction, portfolio theory

Key Finding

\- The authors evaluate the usefulness of high-frequency data in optimal portfolio choice.

\- They employ several stock indexes and different frequencies of observations.

\- The authors consider the impact of economic cycles, microstructure noise, and intraday seasonality on performance.

Abstract:本文评估了高频数据在最优投资组合选择中的有用性。作者使用了主要股票指数的综合列表和不同的观察频率。此外，他们还考虑了经济周期、微观结构噪声和季节性对绩效的影响。他们的研究结果表明，即使存在交易成本，基于高频数据的策略也能够击败基于月度和每日的策略以及基准等权重投资组合。作者还发现，表现优异是由于协方差矩阵的估计误差减小，从而抵消了交易成本的增加。

主题：绩效评估，投资组合构建，投资组合理论

Key Finding

\- 作者评估了高频数据在最优投资组合选择中的有用性

\- 他们使用了多个股票指数和不同的观察频率

\- 作者考虑了经济周期、微观结构噪声和日内季节性对业绩的影响。

\[7\] Divergent ESG Ratings

标题:不同的ESG评级

作者:Elroy Dimson, Paul Marsh and Mike Staunton

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/1/75;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.175

Abstract : Responsible investors require data to underpin their stock and sector selections. Regardless of the rating agency, bond ratings for a particular issuer are broadly similar. This is not the case for ESG ratings. Companies with a high score from one rater often receive a middling or low score from another rater. This article examines the extent of, and reasons for, disagreement among the leading suppliers of ESG ratings. The weightings given to each pillar of an ESG rating also vary across agencies. Many asset managers contend that ESG ratings can help investors to select assets with superior financial prospects, and the authors therefore review the investment performance of portfolios and of indexes screened for their ESG credentials. In the authors’ opinion, ESG ratings, used in isolation, are unlikely to make a material contribution to portfolio returns.

TOPICS: Equity portfolio management, ESG investing, foundations and endowments, performance measurement, wealth management

Key Finding

\- Data are essential for making investment decisions, and most institutions rely wholly or partly on external providers of ESG data. However, there is minimal correlation between ESG ratings from alternative agencies.

\- This article explains why different raters’ appraisals diverge. The article also examines whether ESG is associated with subsequent fund or index outperformance.

\- Over the longest available period, ESG indexes did not outperform the “parent” index from which they are derived. Equally, however, for the two indexes that most closely represent what ESG investors actually do (FTSE4GOOD and MSCI-ESG) there is no evidence of underperformance.

Abstract:负责任的投资者需要数据来支持他们的股票和行业选择。不管评级机构如何，特定发行人的债券评级大体相似。ESG评级并非如此。一个评级机构的高分公司通常会从另一个评级机构得到中等或较低的评分。本文探讨了ESG评级的主要供应商之间的分歧程度和原因。ESG评级的每个支柱所赋予的权重也因机构而异。许多资产管理公司认为，ESG评级可以帮助投资者选择具有良好财务前景的资产，因此，作者审查了投资组合的投资业绩以及根据ESG资格筛选的指数。在作者看来，单独使用ESG评级不太可能对投资组合的回报做出实质性贡献。

主题：股票投资组合管理，ESG投资，基金会和捐赠基金，业绩衡量，财富管理

Key Finding

\- 数据对投资决策至关重要，大多数机构完全或部分依赖外部ESG数据提供商。然而，不同评级机构的ESG评级之间的相关性很小。本文还考察了ESG是否与后续基金或指数的跑赢有关：在最长的可用期内，ESG指数的表现没有超过衍生出来的“母”指数。然而，同样，对于最接近ESG投资者实际表现的两个指数（FTSE4GOOD和MSCI-ESG），没有证据表明表现不佳。

\[8\] The Low-Risk Anomaly: How Much Is a Good Risk Estimate Worth?

标题:低风险异常：一个好的风险评估值多少钱？

作者:Tony Barchetto, Razvan Pascalau and Ryan Poirier

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/1/88;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.183

Abstract : Many academics and practitioners rely on standard, relatively basic methods to estimate and manage portfolio risk. This can affect an investment manager’s ability to accurately target lower volatility stocks designed to exploit the well-documented low-risk anomaly. This article finds a hybrid risk estimate that mixes short-, medium-, and long-term variances leads to superior ex post information ratios and alphas by properly aligning securities in the correct order (low risk to high risk). This risk estimate may be worth between $420 million and $1.9 billion annually, calculated from the overall size ($75 billion) of the market. The significance of this estimate survives transaction costs, various time periods, and risk factor exposures.

TOPICS: Factors, risk premia, factor-based models, passive strategies, portfolio construction, risk management

Key Finding

\- We propose an alternative way to built portfolios designed to exploit the low-risk anomaly.

\- The alpha due to this alternative method alone is anywhere from $420 million to $1.9 billion annually, given the current size ($75 billion) of this market.

\- The significance of this approach survives transaction costs, various time periods, and risk factor exposures.

Abstract:许多学者和从业者依赖于标准的、相对基本的方法来估计和管理投资组合风险。这可能会影响投资经理准确瞄准低波动率股票的能力，这些股票旨在利用有充分记录的低风险异常。本文发现了一种混合风险评估方法，它混合了短期、中期和长期的差异，通过按照正确的顺序（从低风险到高风险）对证券进行适当调整，可以获得更高的事后信息比率和字母表。根据市场的总体规模（750亿美元）计算，这一风险估计每年可能价值4.2亿至19亿美元。这一估计的重要性不受交易成本、不同时间段和风险因素敞口的影响。

主题：因子，风险溢价，基于因子的模型，被动策略，投资组合构建，风险管理

Key Finding

\- 我们提出了一种构建投资组合的替代方法，旨在利用低风险异常

\- 考虑到目前这个市场的规模（750亿美元），仅此一种替代方法产生的α值就在4.2亿美元到19亿美元之间

\- 这种方法的重要性不受交易成本、不同时间段和风险因素的影响风险敞口。

\[9\] Selecting Computational Models for Asset Management: Financial Econometrics versus Machine Learning—Is There a Conflict?

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/1/107;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.184

Abstract : Successful forecasting requires integrating financial theory, market behavior, exploding sources of data, and computational innovation. Building accurate computational models can be achieved by assembling the most comprehensive toolbox. Both financial econometrics and machine learning approaches help to achieve this objective. Machine learning tools provide the ability to make more accurate predictions by accommodating nonlinearities in data, understanding complex interaction among variables, and allowing the use of large, unstructured datasets. The tools of financial econometrics remain critical in answering questions related to inference among the variables describing economic relationships in finance; when properly applied, their role has not diminished with the introduction of machine learning.

TOPICS: Big data/machine learning, simulations, statistical methods

Key Finding

\- Successful forecasting requires integration of financial theory, market behavior, exploding sources of data, and computational innovation.

\- The well-known documented stylized statistical factors associated with financial market variables and their importance in choosing and applying the proper statistical techniques and machine learning algorithms are discussed.

\- Guidelines are provided for integrating the characteristics of data, inference features of financial econometrics, and prediction capabilities from machine learning.

Abstract:成功的预测需要整合金融理论、市场行为、爆炸式的数据来源和计算创新。建立精确的计算模型可以通过组装最全面的工具箱来实现。金融计量经济学和机器学习方法都有助于实现这一目标。机器学习工具通过适应数据中的非线性、理解变量之间的复杂交互作用以及允许使用大型非结构化数据集，提供了进行更准确预测的能力。金融计量经济学的工具在回答与金融中描述经济关系的变量之间的推断有关的问题时仍然至关重要；如果应用得当，它们的作用并没有随着机器学习的引入而减弱。

主题：大数据/机器学习，模拟，统计方法

Key Finding

\- 成功的预测需要金融理论、市场行为、爆炸性数据源和计算创新的整合

\- 与金融市场变量相关的著名文档化统计因素及其在选择和应用适当的统计技术和机器学习算法中的重要性为整合数据特征、金融计量经济学的推断特征和机器学习的预测能力提供了指南。

\[10\] Better Betas

标题:更好的贝塔

作者:Lisa R. Goldberg, Alex Papanicolaou, Alex Shkolnik and Simge Ulucam

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/1/119;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.47.1.119

Abstract : The authors introduce the data-driven Goldberg, Papanicolaou, and Shkolnik (GPS) adjustment for estimated betas, which leads to material improvements in the accuracy of weights and risk forecasts of minimum variance portfolios. Like the widely used Blume 2/3 rule and Vasicek adjustment developed in the 1970s, the GPS adjustment for estimated betas shrinks raw beta estimates toward one. Unlike its antecedents, the GPS adjustment operates on the dominant factor of a sample covariance matrix, and this adjustment adapts dynamically to varying levels of beta dispersion. The authors illustrate the power of the GPS adjustment in a simulation that is calibrated to calm and stressed market regimes.

TOPICS: Factor-based models, portfolio construction, risk management, simulations

Key Finding

\- Betas play a central role in determining optimized portfolios, and principal component analysis (PCA) is an effective tool to estimate betas.

\- More accurate PCA betas can be achieved with the Goldberg, Papanicolaou, and Shkolnik (GPS) adjustment, which is analogous to standard beta shrinkage adjustments applied to betas obtained from time-series regression.

\- Substantial improvements to the accuracy of minimum variance portfolio weights and risk forecasts may be realized when applying the GPS adjustment, which leads to better betas in any market regime.
