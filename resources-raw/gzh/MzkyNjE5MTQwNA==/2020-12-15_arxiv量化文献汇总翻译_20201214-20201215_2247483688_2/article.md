\[1\] A Sentiment Analysis Approach to the Prediction of Market Volatility

标题 : 市场波动性预测的情绪分析方法

作者 : Justina Deveikyte, Helyette Geman, Carlo Piccari, Alessandro Provetti , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.05906

说明 : -

Abstract : Prediction and quantification of future volatility and returns play an important role in financial modelling, both in portfolio optimization and risk management. Natural language processing today allows to process news and social media comments to detect signals of investors confidence. We have explored the relationship between sentiment extracted from financial news and tweets and FTSE100 movements. We investigated the strength of the correlation between sentiment measures on a given day and market volatility and returns observed the next day. The findings suggest that there is evidence of correlation between sentiment and stock market movements the sentiment captured from news headlines could be used as a signal to predict market returns the same does not apply for volatility. Also, in a surprising finding, for the sentiment found in Twitter comments we obtained a correlation coefficient of 0.7, and p value below 0.05, which indicates a strong negative correlation between positive sentiment captured from the tweets on a given day and the volatility observed the next day. We developed an accurate classifier for the prediction of market volatility in response to the arrival of new information by deploying topic modelling, based on Latent Dirichlet Allocation, to extract feature vectors from a collection of tweets and financial news. The obtained features were used as additional input to the classifier. Thanks to the combination of sentiment and topic modelling our classifier achieved a directional prediction accuracy for volatility of 63 .

摘要 : 未来波动率和收益的预测和量化在金融模型中发挥着重要作用，无论是在投资组合优化还是风险管理方面。如今，自然语言处理允许处理新闻和社交媒体评论，以检测投资者信心的信号。我们探讨了从金融新闻和推文中提取的情绪与富时100指数走势之间的关系。我们调查了某一天的情绪指标与第二天观察到的市场波动和回报之间的相关性强度。研究结果表明，有证据表明，市场情绪与股市走势之间存在相关性。从新闻头条中捕捉到的情绪可以作为预测市场回报的信号，但这一点并不适用于波动性。另外，一个令人惊讶的发现是，对于Twitter评论中发现的情绪，我们得到了一个0.7的相关系数，p值低于0.05，这表明在给定的一天从tweets捕捉到的积极情绪和第二天观察到的波动之间有很强的负相关。我们开发了一个准确的分类器，用于预测市场波动性以应对新信息的到来，通过部署基于潜在Dirichlet分配的主题建模，从tweet和财经新闻集合中提取特征向量。所获得的特征被用作分类器的附加输入。多亏了情感和主题建模的结合，我们的分类器对波动率的定向预测精度达到了63。

\[2\] Portfolio optimization with two quasiconvex risk measures

标题 : 两个拟凸风险测度下的投资组合优化

作者 : Çağın Ararat , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.06173

说明 : Comments: 21 pages

Abstract : We study a static portfolio optimization problem with two risk measures a principle risk measure in the objective function and a secondary risk measure whose value is controlled in the constraints. This problem is of interest when it is necessary to consider the risk preferences of two parties, such as a portfolio manager and a regulator, at the same time. A special case of this problem where the risk measures are assumed to be coherent (positively homogeneous) is studied recently in a joint work of the author. The present paper extends the analysis to a more general setting by assuming that the two risk measures are only quasiconvex. First, we study the case where the principal risk measure is convex. We introduce a dual problem, show that there is zero duality gap between the portfolio optimization problem and the dual problem, and finally identify a condition under which the Lagrange multiplier associated to the dual problem at optimality gives an optimal portfolio. Next, we study the general case without the convexity assumption and show that an approximately optimal solution with prescribed optimality gap can be achieved by using the well known bisection algorithm combined with a duality result that we prove.

摘要 : 研究了一个静态投资组合优化问题，目标函数中的主风险度量和值受控于约束的二次风险测度。当有必要同时考虑投资组合经理和监管者等双方的风险偏好时，这个问题就很有趣了。作者最近在一项联合工作中研究了这个问题的一个特例，即假设风险度量是一致的（正齐次的）。本文通过假设这两个风险度量仅为拟凸，将分析扩展到更一般的情况。首先，我们研究了主风险测度是凸的情形。我们引入了一个对偶问题，证明了投资组合优化问题与对偶问题之间存在零对偶差距，并最终确定了与对偶问题相关的拉格朗日乘子给出最优投资组合的条件。其次，我们研究了不带凸性假设的一般情形，并证明了利用已知的二分法和我们所证明的对偶结果，可以得到具有给定最优性间隔的近似最优解。

\[4\] The Deep Parametric PDE Method

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.06211

说明 : Comments: Some examples can be reproduced in our Jupyter Notebook: this https URL

Abstract : We propose the deep parametric PDE method to solve high dimensional parametric partial differential equations. A single neural network approximates the solution of a whole family of PDEs after being trained without the need of sample solutions. As a practical application, we compute option prices in the multivariate Black Scholes model. After a single training phase, the prices for different time, state and model parameters are available in milliseconds. We evaluate the accuracy in the price and a generalisation of the implied volatility with examples of up to 25 dimensions. Abstract : I propose a new tool to characterize the resolution of uncertainty around FOMC press conferences. \[8\] An approximate closed formula for European Mortgage Options

标题 : 欧式抵押期权的一个近似闭式公式

作者 : Manuel Lopez Galvan , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.06645

说明 : -

Abstract : The aim of this paper is to investigate the use of close formula approximation for pricing European mortgage options. Under the assumption of logistic duration and normal mortgage rates the underlying price at the option expiry is approximated by shifted lognormal or regular lognormal distribution by matching moments. Once the price function is approximated by lognormal distributions, the option price can be computed directly as an integration of the distribution function over the payoff at the option expiry by using Black Scholes Merton close formula. We will see that lower curvature levels correspond to positively skewness price distributions and in this case lognormal approximation leads to close parametric formula representation in terms of all model parameters. The proposed methodologies are tested against Monte Carlo approach under different market and contract parameters and the tests confirmed that the close form approximation have a very good accuracy.

摘要 : 本文的目的是研究在欧式抵押贷款期权定价中使用闭式近似。在假设logistic久期和正态抵押贷款利率的情况下，期权到期时的标的价格通过匹配矩近似为位移对数正态分布或正态对数正态分布。一旦价格函数被对数正态分布逼近，期权价格就可以通过Black-Scholes-Merton-close公式直接计算为期权到期时收益的分布函数的积分。我们将看到，较低的曲率水平对应于正偏度价格分布，在这种情况下，对数正态近似导致了所有模型参数的密切参数公式表示。在不同的市场参数和合约参数下，用蒙特卡罗方法对所提出的方法进行了检验，结果表明闭式近似具有很好的精度。

\[10\] Non fundamental Home Bias in International Equity Markets

标题 : 国际股市中的非基本面国内偏好

作者 : Gyu Hyun Kim , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.06716

说明 : -

Abstract : This study investigates the relationship of the equity home bias with 1) the country level behavioral unfamiliarity, and 2) the home foreign return correlation. We set the hypotheses that 1) unfamiliarity about foreign equities plays a role in the portfolio set up and 2) the correlation of return on home and foreign equities affects the equity home bias when there is a lack of information about foreign equities. For the empirical analysis, the proportion of respondents to the question How much do you trust People you meet for the first time is used as a proxy measure for country specific unfamiliarity. Based on the eleven developed countries for which such data are available, we implement a feasible generalized linear squares (FGLS) method. Empirical results suggest that country specific unfamiliarity has a significant and positive correlation with the equity home bias. When it comes to the correlation of return between home and foreign equities, we identify that there is a negative correlation with the equity home bias, which is against our hypothesis. Moreover, an excess return on home equities compared to foreign ones is found to have a positive correlation with the equity home bias, which is consistent with the comparative statics only if foreign investors have a sufficiently higher risk aversion than domestic investors. We check the robustness of our empirical analysis by fitting alternative specifications and use a log transformed measure of the equity home bias, resulting in consistent results with ones with the original measure.

摘要 : 本研究探讨了股权母国偏好与1）国家层面的行为不熟悉度，以及2）国内外回报率的相关性。我们假设：1）对外国股票的不熟悉程度在投资组合的建立中起作用；2）在缺乏外国股票信息的情况下，国内外股票收益率的相关性会影响股票母国偏好。在实证分析中，你对第一次见面的人有多信任这一问题的受访者比例，被用作衡量各国对具体国家不熟悉程度的一种替代性指标。基于11个发达国家的数据，我们实现了一个可行的广义线性平方（FGLS）方法。实证结果显示，特定国家的不熟悉程度与股权母国偏好显著正相关。当涉及到国内外股票收益率的相关性时，我们发现与股票母国偏好存在负相关关系，这与我们的假设背道而驰。此外，与外国投资者相比，本国股票的超额回报率与股票本国偏好正相关，只有当外国投资者的风险厌恶程度足够高时，这与比较静态理论一致。我们通过拟合替代性规范来检验实证分析的稳健性，并使用股权归属偏差的对数转换测度，结果与原始测度的结果一致。

\[11\] Monetary Risk Measures

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.06751

说明 : Comments: 21 pages

Abstract : In this paper, we study general monetary risk measures (without any convexity or weak convexity). A monetary (respectively, positively homogeneous) risk measure can be characterized as the lower envelope of a family of convex (respectively, coherent) risk measures. The proof does not depend on but easily leads to the classical representation theorems for convex and coherent risk measures. The representation of a law invariant risk measure in terms of VaR is provided.

摘要 : 本文研究了一般的货币风险度量（无凸性或弱凸性）。一个货币（分别是正齐次）风险度量可以刻画为一组凸的（分别是一致的）风险度量的下包络。这一证明不依赖于但容易导出凸和相干风险测度的经典表示定理。当涉及到法律不变性和SSD（二阶随机优势）相合性时，可以统一形式用于这类低包络特征的不是风险度量的凸性（分别是相干性），而是共单调凸性（分别是协单调相干性）。给出了一种用VaR表示法不变的风险度量。

\[12\] Dynamical Characteristics of Global Stock Markets Based on Time Dependent Tsallis Non Extensive Statistics and Generalized Hurst Exponents

标题 : 基于时间相关的Tsallis非扩展统计和广义Hurst指数的全球股市动态特征

作者 : Ioannis P. Antoniades, Leonidas P. Karakatsanis, Evgenios G. Pavlos , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.06856

说明 : Comments: 19 pages, 4 figures

Abstract : We perform time dependent extended Tsallis statistics analysis on stock market indices. Specifically, we evaluate the q triplet for particular time periods of the market with the purpose of demonstrating the temporal dependence of the extended characteristics of the underlying market dynamics.
