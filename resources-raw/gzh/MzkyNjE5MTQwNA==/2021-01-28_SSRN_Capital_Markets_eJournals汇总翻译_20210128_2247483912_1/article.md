\[1\] Can the Representativeness Heuristic Explain the Asset Growth Anomaly?

标题:代表性启发式能否解释资产增长异常？

作者:Miao He(Tulane University, A.B. Freeman School of Business),Nishad Kapadia(Tulane University - Finance & Economics),Sheri Tice(Tulane University - A.B. Freeman School of Business)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3739972

Abstract : We find that the low average returns to firms with high asset growth are consistent with two key implications of models of diagnostic investor expectations (e.g., Bordalo, Gennaioli, La Porta, and Shleifer, 2019) that formalize the representativeness heuristic of Kahneman and Tversky (1972). These models predict that investors overestimate the subjective probability of states that are more representative of a firm’s type and also neglect risk after a string of good news. We construct a measure of how representative the stereotype the ‘next Google’ is of high asset growth in the recent past. We show that this measure predicts the returns of CMA, the asset growth factor in the five factor model of Fama and French (2015). Returns to CMA are 17.5% over the 3 years after months with high representativeness and only 5.4% following low representativeness months. In the cross-section, we find evidence consistent with investors neglecting the risk of high asset growth firms. The asset growth effect is not present in portfolios with low distress risk and the interaction between distress and asset growth, rather than asset growth by itself, predicts low returns in Fama and MacBeth (1973) regressions and portfolio sorts. We also find that analysts and markets do not appreciate the importance of the interaction between asset growth and distress and are sluggish in responding to news for the ‘interaction’ portfolio. Finally, we show that our measure of representativeness predicts the returns of the interaction portfolio.

Keywords : Cross section of expected returns, Asset Growth, Representativeness

Abstract :我们发现，高资产增长公司的低平均回报率与诊断投资者预期模型的两个关键含义（例如，Bordalo、Gennaioli、La Porta和Shleifer，2019）一致，这两个模型将Kahneman和Tversky（1972）的代表性启发式形式化。这些模型预测，投资者高估了更能代表公司类型的状态的主观概率，也忽略了一系列好消息之后的风险。我们构建了一个衡量“下一个谷歌”这一刻板印象在最近几年的高资产增长中具有多大代表性的指标。我们发现，这一指标预测了资产管理公司（CMA）的收益，这是Fama和French（2015）五因素模型中的资产增长因素。在代表性较高的月份之后的3年内，CMA的回报率为17.5%，在代表性较低的月份之后，回报率仅为5.4%。在横截面上，我们发现了投资者忽略高资产增长公司风险的证据。资产增长效应并不存在于低困境风险的投资组合中，而困境与资产增长之间的相互作用，而不是资产增长本身，在Fama和MacBeth（1973）的回归和投资组合分类中预测了低回报。我们还发现，分析师和市场没有意识到资产增长和困境之间相互作用的重要性，对“相互作用”投资组合的消息反应迟钝。最后，我们证明了我们的代表性度量可以预测交互投资组合的收益。

Keywords :预期收益、资产增长、代表性的横截面

\[2\] Connecting Expected Stock Returns to Accounting Valuation Multiples: A Primer

标题:将预期股票收益与会计估值倍数联系起来：入门

作者:Akash Chattopadhyay(University of Toronto - Rotman School of Management),Matthew R. Lyle(Northwestern University - Kellogg School of Management),Charles C. Y. Wang(Harvard Business School)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3774442

Abstract : We outline a framework in which accounting “valuation anchors" could be connected to expected stock returns. Under two general conditions, expected log returns is a log- linear function of a valuation (market value-to-accounting) multiple and the expected growth in the valuation anchor. We show that the framework can: 1) allow for expected enterprise returns, 2) correct for the use of stale accounting data in estimation, and 3) accommodate differences in information quality. This analytical formulation is tractable and flexible, and provides building blocks for further innovations in accounting valuation research.

Keywords : Expected returns, Present value, Valuation multiples

Abstract :我们概述了一个框架，其中会计“估值锚”可以连接到预期的股票回报。在两种一般情况下，预期对数收益率是一个对数线性函数的估值（市值对会计）倍数和预期增长的估值锚。我们表明，该框架可以：1）允许预期的企业回报，2）正确使用陈旧的会计数据进行估计，3）适应信息质量的差异。这种分析公式是易于处理和灵活的，并为会计估值研究的进一步创新提供了基石。

Keywords :预期收益、现值、估值倍数

\[3\] Do Financial Markets Anticipate Corporate Related Decisions of the United States Supreme Court?

标题:金融市场是否预期美国最高法院的公司相关判决？

作者:Yehuda Davis(Yeshiva University -- Sy Syms School of Business),Suresh Govindaraj (Rutgers University - Rutgers Business School - Newark and New Brunswick),Kate Suslava(Bucknell University Freeman College of Management)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3761235

Abstract : This paper investigates stock and option market reactions to events in the United States Supreme Court (SC) relating to cases where at least one party involved is a public firm. Typically, cases that reach the SC level would have passed through multiple lower courts. Consequently, much of the information content of these cases would be publicly known. If the financial market had perfectly anticipated that the SC would grant the writ of certiorari (a rare event of accepting a case for review), the tone of the subsequent legal arguments, and the final decision, then there should be no reaction to any of these events, as and when they unfold. Using a comprehensive dataset of more than 500 SC cases from 1948 to 2018, we find that the stock market reacts to both the grant of certiorari and to the announcement of the final decision, suggesting that the stock market could not anticipate the SC actions. We also find that case-specific characteristics, such as parties involved, the type of legal issue, and press coverage explain some of the cross-sectional variations in the stock returns across cases. Our tests also indicate that there is no information leakage prior to the events, and no stock price drift after the events. We also find some evidence that the option market anticipates the final decision as early as the date certiorari is granted, reinforcing the theory that smart money comes early to the option market.

Keywords : abnormal stock returns; Supreme Court; media coverage; writ of certiorari

Abstract :本文研究股票和期权市场对美国最高法院（SC）中至少有一方是上市公司的案件的反应。通常，达到最高法院级别的案件会通过多个下级法院审理。因此，这些案件的许多信息内容将是众所周知的。如果金融市场完全预料到SC会批准调卷令（一个罕见的接受复审的案件）、随后法律辩论的基调和最终决定，那么当这些事件发生时，不应对任何一个事件作出反应。利用1948年至2018年500多个SC案例的综合数据集，我们发现股票市场对调取令的授予和最终决定的宣布都有反应，这表明股票市场无法预测SC的行动。我们还发现，案件的具体特征，如当事人，法律问题的类型，以及新闻报道解释了一些横截面变化的股票回报率在不同的案件。我们的测试还表明，事件发生前没有信息泄露，事件发生后没有股价漂移。我们还发现，一些证据表明，期权市场早在调取令授予之日就预期到最终决策，这加强了聪明货币早到期权市场的理论。

Keywords :股票异常收益；最高法院；媒体报道；调卷令

\[4\] The Distributional Dimension of the Resource Curse: Commodity Price Shocks and Income Inequality

标题:资源诅咒的分配维度：商品价格冲击与收入不平等

作者:Soran Mohtadi(Autonomous University of Barcelona - Department of Applied Economics),David Castells-Quintana(Autonomous University of Barcelona - Department of Applied Economics; University of Barcelona - Regional Quantitative Analysis Group (AQR-IREA))

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3742722

Abstract : How does high dependence on natural resources affect income inequality? Surprisingly little is known about the impact of high dependence on primary goods on income distribution. Building on insights from the resource curse literature, this paper studies the relationship between income shocks through changes in commodity prices and income inequality in a panel of 80 countries from 1990 to 2016. We analyze the differentiated effects of commodity price shocks depending on the type of commodity (labor vs. capital-intensive). We also study differences across world regions and explore potential mechanisms by looking at different types of inequality (pay vs. capital rents). Results show that commodity price shocks have an impact on income inequality. However, this impact depends on the type of commodity and inequality.

Keywords : resource curse, commodity price shocks, inequality, natural resources, resource booms, development

Abstract :对自然资源的高度依赖如何影响收入不平等？令人惊讶的是，人们对高度依赖初级商品对收入分配的影响知之甚少。基于资源诅咒文献的观点，本文研究了1990年至2016年80个国家通过商品价格变化产生的收入冲击与收入不平等之间的关系。我们分析了不同商品类型（劳动密集型和资本密集型）的商品价格冲击的差异效应。我们还研究了世界各地区的差异，并通过观察不同类型的不平等（工资与资本租金）来探索潜在的机制。结果表明，商品价格冲击对收入不平等有影响。然而，这种影响取决于商品类型和不平等。

Keywords :资源诅咒，商品价格冲击，不平等，自然资源，资源繁荣，发展

\[5\] Sea Level Rise and Portfolio Choice

标题:海平面上升与投资组合选择

作者:Emirhan Ilhan(Frankfurt School of Finance & Management)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3743046

Abstract : Economic theory suggests that the presence of uninsurable and unavoidable background risks influences household portfolio choices. Households face significant location-specific background risks due to sea level rise (SLR). Using detailed local variation in SLR exposure and disaggregated geographic information on households in the United States, I show that SLR exposed homeowners are less likely to participate in the stock market and invest a smaller share of their financial wealth in risky assets, compared to unexposed homeowners in the same neighborhood. Differences in risk preferences and endogenous location choices are unable to explain this effect. Using plausibly exogenous variation stemming from the adoption of state-level climate change adaptation plans that reduced households' SLR risks, I provide causal evidence of the effect of SLR risks on portfolio allocation decisions. Following the adoption of such climate adaptation plans, SLR exposed households increase their stock market participation and hold a larger risky share in their financial wealth.

Keywords : Sea level rise, climate change, background risk, portfolio choice, stock market participation

Abstract :经济理论认为，不可保险和不可避免的背景风险的存在会影响家庭投资组合的选择。由于海平面上升（SLR），家庭面临显著的特定位置背景风险。利用单反风险敞口的详细局部变化和美国家庭的分类地理信息，我表明，与同一社区的未敞口房主相比，单反风险敞口房主不太可能参与股市，将其金融财富的一小部分投资于风险资产。风险偏好和内生区位选择的差异无法解释这种效应。通过采用国家一级的气候变化适应计划，降低了家庭的SLR风险，我提供了SLR风险对投资组合分配决策影响的因果证据。在采用此类气候适应计划后，单反风险敞口家庭增加了对股市的参与，并在其金融财富中持有更大的风险份额。

Keywords :海平面上升、气候变化、背景风险、投资组合选择、股市参与

\[6\] Information Environment and Cost Asymmetry

标题:信息环境与成本不对称

作者:Nishant Agarwal(Indian School of Business (ISB), Hyderabad; The University of Western Australia - UWA Business School),Abdul Mohi Khizer(Indian School of Business),Mani Sethuraman(Cornell University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3769496

Abstract : We examine the effect of firms’ information environment on asymmetric cost behavior (cost stickiness). Using plausibly exogenous variation in firms’ information environment stemming from brokerage closures, we find that the stickiness of selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) costs reduces in response to adverse changes in the information environment. This effect is concentrated in firms characterized, ex ante, by poorer reporting quality, higher information uncertainty, lower institutional holdings, greater financial constraints, and lower resource adjustment costs. Managers respond to adverse information shocks by reducing the stickiness of SG&A costs that are less likely to create future value, thereby paving way for higher enterprise valuation. Our paper contributes to the growing literature on cost behavior research by documenting the link between firms’ external information environment and internal cost management, and presents novel evidence on managers’ altering their cost behavior to generate internal funds when access to external capital is limited.

Keywords : information environment, brokerage closure, SG&A, cost stickiness, asymmetric cost behavior, cost management

Abstract :本文考察了企业信息环境对非对称成本行为（成本粘性）的影响。利用券商倒闭导致的公司信息环境的外生变化，我们发现，随着信息环境的不利变化，销售、一般和管理（SG&A）成本的粘性降低。这种影响主要集中在事前报告质量较差、信息不确定性较高、机构持股较少、财务约束较大、资源调整成本较低的公司。管理者通过降低不太可能创造未来价值的SG&A成本的粘性来应对不利的信息冲击，从而为更高的企业估值铺平道路。本文通过记录企业外部信息环境与内部成本管理之间的联系，为越来越多的成本行为研究文献做出了贡献，并提出了在获取外部资本有限的情况下，管理者改变成本行为以产生内部资金的新证据。

Keywords :信息环境、券商关闭、SG&A、成本粘性、不对称成本行为、成本管理

\[7\] Deep Learning for Conditional Asset Pricing Models

标题:条件资产定价模型的深度学习

作者:Hongyi Liu(Washington University in St. Louis - Department of Economics)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3723237

Abstract : We propose a new pseudo-Siamese Network for Asset Pricing (SNAP) model, based on deep learning approaches, for conditional asset pricing. Our model allows for the deep alpha, deep beta and deep factor risk premia conditional on high dimensional observable information of financial characteristics and macroeconomic states, while storing the long-term dependency of the informative features through long short-term memory network. We apply this method to monthly U.S. stock returns from 1970-2019 and find that our pseudo-SNAP model outperforms the benchmark approaches in terms of out-of-sample prediction and out-of-sample Sharpe ratio. In addition, we also apply our method to calculate deep mispricing errors which we use to construct an arbitrage portfolio K-Means clustering. We find that the arbitrage portfolio has significant alphas.

Keywords : Conditional asset pricing models, deep learning, LSTM, K-Means clustering, alpha, cross-section of expected returns, arbitrage portfolios

Abstract :我们提出了一个新的伪暹罗网络资产定价（SNAP）模型，基于深度学习方法，有条件的资产定价。我们的模型考虑了以金融特征和宏观经济状态的高维可观测信息为条件的深α、深β和深因子风险溢价，同时通过长-短期记忆网络存储信息特征的长期依赖性。我们将此方法应用于1970-2019年的月度美国股票收益率，发现我们的伪SNAP模型在样本外预测和样本外夏普比率方面优于基准方法。此外，我们也应用我们的方法来计算深错定价误差，并以此来构造套利组合的K-均值聚类。我们发现套利组合具有显著的alphas。

Keywords :条件资产定价模型，深度学习，LSTM，K-均值聚类，阿尔法，预期收益横截面，套利投资组合

\[8\] The FOMC Risk Shift

标题:联邦公开市场委员会的风险转移

作者:Tim Alexander Kroencke(University of Neuchatel - Institute of Financial Analysis),Maik Schmeling(Goethe University Frankfurt - Department of Finance; Centre for Economic Policy Research (CEPR)),Andreas Schrimpf(Bank for International Settlements (BIS) - Monetary and Economic Department)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3774275

Abstract : We identify a component of monetary policy news that is extracted from high-frequency changes in risky asset prices. These surprises, which we call “risk shifts”, are uncorrelated, and therefore complementary, to risk-free rate surprises. We show that (i) risk shifts capture the lion’s share of stock price movements around FOMC announcements; (ii) that they are accompanied by significant investor fund flows, suggesting that investors react heterogeneously to monetary policy news; and (iii) that price pressure amplifies the stock market response to monetary policy news. Our results imply that central bank information effects are overshadowed by short-term dynamics stemming from investor rebalancing activities and are likely to be more difficult to identify than previously thought.

Keywords : Monetary Policy Surprises; Equity Premium; Fund Flows; Portfolio Rebalanc- ing; Price Pressures

Abstract :我们确定了货币政策新闻的一个组成部分，它是从高风险资产价格的高频变化中提取出来的。这些意外，我们称之为“风险转移”，与无风险利率意外是不相关的，因此是互补的。我们发现：（i）围绕联邦公开市场委员会的公告，风险转移占据了股票价格变动的大部分份额；（ii）伴随着大量的投资者资金流动，表明投资者对货币政策消息的反应是异质的；（iii）价格压力放大了股票市场对货币政策消息的反应。我们的结果意味着，央行信息效应被投资者再平衡活动产生的短期动态所掩盖，而且很可能比之前认为的更难以识别。

Keywords :货币政策意外；股票溢价；资金流动；投资组合再平衡；价格压力

\[9\] Do Words Hurt More Than Actions? The Impact of Trade Tensions on Financial Markets

标题:言语比行动更伤人吗？贸易紧张对金融市场的影响

作者:Massimo Minesso Ferrari(European Central Bank (ECB)),Frederik Kurcz(Berlin School of Economics and Law),Maria Sole Pagliari(Banque de France)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3774198

Abstract : In this paper, we apply textual analysis and machine learning algorithms to construct an index capturing trade tensions between US and China. Our indicator matches well-known events in the US-China trade dispute and is exogenous to the developments on global financial markets. By means of local projection methods, we show that US markets are largely unaffected by rising trade tensions, with the exception of those firms that are more exposed to China, while the same shock negatively affects stock market indices in EMEs and China. Higher trade tensions also entail: i) an appreciation of the US dollar; ii) a depreciation of EMEs currencies; iii) muted changes in safe haven currencies; iv) portfolio re-balancing between stocks and bonds in the EMEs. We also show that trade tensions account for around 15% of the variance of Chinese stocks while their contribution is muted for US markets.
