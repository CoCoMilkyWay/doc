\[1\] INTRODUCTION: Resilient Real Estate

标题:简介：弹性房地产

作者:Jim Clayton, Frank J. Fabozzi, S. Michael Giliberto, Jacques N. Gordon, Youguo Liang, Greg MacKinnon and Asieh Mansour

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/10/11;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.277

Abstract : The editors of the special real estate issue introduce the articles contained in the issue and relate them to the concept of resiliency in real estate investments. COVID-19 had a major impact on returns to real estate. However, the resilience of property returns to COVID-19 varied significantly by location, by sector, and even across individual assets of the same property type in the same city. Resilient investments are those able to withstand the effect of not only acute disruptions in the market but also chronic longer-term threats. For real estate, these threats include pandemics, climate change, changing demographics, changes in tenant preferences, changes in technology, and even factors that are unknown and unpredictable at the moment but that may become important in the future. As some of these threats may not be insurable and may not be diversifiable, much of the work of risk management in creating resilient real estate portfolios will need to be at the property level. A number of approaches can be implemented to increase resilience at a property, but flexibility in building design may be among the most important.

Key Finding

\- ▪ COVID-19 had a major impact on returns to real estate. However, the resilience of property returns to COVID-19 varied significantly by location, by sector, and even across individual assets of the same property type in the same city.

\- ▪ Resilient assets are those able to withstand the effects of not only acute disruptions in the market but also chronic longer-term threats.

\- ▪ Traditional risk management often relied on diversification and insurance to control risks, but some long-term threats may not be insurable and may be nondiversifiable. Much of the work to create resilient real estate portfolios must be at the asset level, creating properties that can withstand long-term changes through their design.

Abstract:《房地产特刊》的编辑们介绍了本期所载的文章，并将其与房地产投资中的弹性概念联系起来。新冠病毒-19对房地产收益产生了重大影响。然而，房地产回归到新冠病毒-19的弹性因地点、行业甚至同一城市相同房地产类型的个人资产而存在显著差异。弹性投资是指不仅能够承受市场严重动荡的影响，而且能够抵御长期威胁的投资。对房地产而言，这些威胁包括流行病、气候变化、人口结构变化、租户偏好变化、技术变化，甚至是目前未知和不可预测但未来可能变得重要的因素。由于其中一些威胁可能无法投保，也可能无法分散，因此在创建弹性房地产投资组合方面，风险管理的大部分工作将需要在房地产层面进行。可以采用多种方法来提高物业的弹性，但建筑设计的灵活性可能是最重要的。

Key Finding

\- ▪ 新冠病毒-19对房地产收益产生了重大影响。然而，房地产回归到新冠病毒-19的弹性因地点、行业、甚至同一城市相同房地产类型的个人资产而存在显著差异。

\- ▪ 弹性资产是指不仅能够承受市场严重动荡的影响，而且能够承受长期威胁的资产。

\- ▪ 传统的风险管理通常依靠多样化和保险来控制风险，但一些长期威胁可能不可保险，也可能不可分散。创建弹性房地产投资组合的大部分工作必须在资产层面进行，以创建能够通过其设计承受长期变化的房地产。

\[2\] Three Decades of Global Institutional Investment in Commercial Real Estate

标题:商业房地产领域三十年的全球机构投资

作者:Alexander Carlo, Piet Eichholtz and Nils Kok

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/10/25;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.275

Abstract : Alternative assets represent an increasing share of pension fund assets, and real estate is a cornerstone of that allocation. This article investigates the trends in pension fund real estate investments over the past 3 decades, both in private and in public real estate, focusing on the performance of the asset class for the ultimate asset owners. The development of pension fund allocations to real estate differs across regions, with allocations increasing in Canada, stationary in the US, and shrinking in Europe. Slightly more than 10% of the real estate exposure is through publicly listed vehicles. Within the real estate portfolio, the authors observe a continuing increase in the use of external fund managers. Investment costs are stationary, with pension funds in the US structurally paying more to their external private real estate managers than their peers in Canada and Europe. Costs relating to public real estate are more equal across regions. In terms of performance, the authors observe rather stable total returns for both private and listed real estate over the past 3 decades, contrasting volatile performance of private equity and infrastructure. Intermediated investment management for private real estate is costly, leading to disproportionately lower net returns.

Key Finding

\- ▪ Current pension fund allocation to real estate is 8.3%, on average, with a 90/10 split between private real estate and public real estate.

\- ▪ Pension funds deploy a wide range of real estate allocation strategies, with intermediation growing in popularity over the past decades.

\- ▪ Real estate has provided stable returns over the past decades, with gross returns similar to stocks, and net returns in between bonds and stocks.

Abstract:另类资产在养老基金资产中所占份额越来越大，而房地产是这一配置的基石。本文调查了过去30年养老基金房地产投资的趋势，包括私人房地产和公共房地产，重点关注最终资产所有者的资产类别表现。养老基金房地产配置的发展在不同地区有所不同，加拿大的配置增加，美国的配置保持不变，欧洲的配置减少。略多于10%的房地产敞口是通过公开上市的汽车。在房地产投资组合中，作者观察到外部基金经理的使用持续增加。投资成本是固定不变的，美国的养老基金在结构上向外部私人房地产经理支付的费用高于加拿大和欧洲的同行。与公共房地产相关的成本在各地区更为平等。在业绩方面，作者观察到，在过去30年中，私人和上市房地产的总回报相当稳定，而私募股权和基础设施的表现则不稳定。私人房地产的中间投资管理成本高昂，导致净回报不成比例地降低。

Key Finding

\- ▪ 目前养老基金分配给房地产的比例平均为8.3%，私人房地产和公共房地产的比例为90/10。

\- ▪ 养老基金部署了广泛的房地产配置策略，在过去几十年中，中介越来越受欢迎。

\- ▪ 房地产在过去几十年中提供了稳定的回报，总回报类似于股票，净回报介于债券和股票之间。

\[3\] Tokenization—The Future of Real Estate Investment?

标题:房地产投资的未来？

作者:Andrew Baum

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/10/41;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.260

Abstract : This article examines the mechanisms now available to tokenize real asset ownership and create active secondary markets in tokenized or fractionalized units. Real estate is lumpy and illiquid, and tokenization would allow participation in the market by a broader group of investors and the ability to build diversified portfolios with modest sums of money. However, two developments are necessary for the digital tokenization of single real estate assets: (1) an expressed demand for the fractionalization of single real estate assets, and (2) market participants need to be comfortable with blockchain, the digital underpinning of tokenization. Further, in many land markets fractionalization requires an intermediate structure to be established because the direct ownership of land cannot be shared amongst many, increasing the cost of tokenization. Larger assets already held in fund structures may eventually be tokenized successfully; there may also be an alternative market for tokenized residential, social impact, or community assets where investment regulation and risk/return are not the main drivers of behavior. The mass market for the tokenization of single commercial real estate assets, however, may be some way down the road.

Key Finding

\- ▪ The author examines the mechanisms now available to tokenize real asset ownership.

\- ▪ The author presents new survey-based evidence of real estate tokenization deals.

\- ▪ The author concludes that an intermediate structure is likely to be both necessary and convenient when fractionalizing a single asset. For that reason, funds are more natural targets for tokenization.

Abstract:本文探讨了目前可用于标记化真实资产所有权和在标记化或分馏单位中创建活跃二级市场的机制。房地产质量参差不齐且缺乏流动性，代币化将允许更广泛的投资者群体参与市场，并有能力用适度的资金构建多样化的投资组合。然而，单个房地产资产的数字标记化需要两个发展：（1）对单个房地产资产的分拆的明确需求，以及（2）市场参与者需要熟悉区块链，即标记化的数字基础。此外，在许多土地市场中，分割需要建立一个中间结构，因为土地的直接所有权不能在许多人之间共享，从而增加了标记化的成本。已在基金结构中持有的较大资产最终可能被成功标记化；在投资监管和风险/回报不是行为的主要驱动因素的情况下，也可能存在一个代币化住宅、社会影响或社区资产的替代市场。然而，单一商业房地产资产标记化的大众市场可能还有一段路要走。

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者研究了目前可用于标记真实资产所有权的机制。

\- ▪ 作者提出了新的基于调查的房地产标记化交易证据。

\- ▪ 作者得出结论，在对单一资产进行分拆时，中间结构可能既必要又方便。因此，基金更自然地成为标记化的目标。

\[4\] Reexamining the Real Estate Quadrants

标题:重新审视房地产象限

作者:Kieran Farrelly and Alex Moss

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/10/62;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.271

Abstract : The real estate quadrant approach for categorizing the real estate investment universe is now 20 years old. These investment conduits have developed significantly over this time, and real estate allocations are shaped by both market and regulatory forces, which are leading investors to reexamine the broadening of blended strategies across these quadrants. In this article, the performance and interrelationships between the quadrants and the way that these have evolved over time are examined. The results show that significant diversification benefits are available to investors using the quadrants. The commercial mortgage-backed securities market is found to be the main transmitter of shocks among the quadrants and private real estate the most significant receiver, with a meaningful shift in their relationships before and after the global financial crisis. Finally, blended real estate portfolios are found to reduce estimated risk and enhance the risk-adjusted performance of a purely private real estate exposure.

Key Finding

\- ▪ Significant diversification benefits are available to investors using all forms of real estate exposure, given our finding that less than one-third of the system is influenced by itself in isolation.

\- ▪ Using the dynamic connectedness methodology, the study finds that public real estate debt is the main transmitter of shocks among the quadrants and that private real estate equity is the most significant receiver of shocks among the real estate quadrants.

\- ▪ Further evidence of blended real estate portfolios enhance the risk-adjusted performance of a purely private real estate exposure.

Abstract:对房地产投资领域进行分类的房地产象限法已经有20年的历史了。这些投资渠道在这段时间内得到了显著发展，房地产配置受到市场和监管力量的影响，这促使投资者重新审视在这些象限扩大混合策略的问题。在本文中，将检查象限之间的性能和相互关系，以及它们随时间演化的方式。结果表明，使用象限，投资者可以获得显著的多元化收益。研究发现，商业抵押贷款支持证券市场是象限之间冲击的主要传递者，而私人房地产是最重要的接受者，它们在全球金融危机前后的关系发生了重大变化。最后，混合房地产投资组合被发现可以降低估计风险，提高纯私人房地产风险敞口的风险调整绩效。

Key Finding

\- ▪ 考虑到我们的发现，不到三分之一的系统是独立受自身影响的，因此使用各种形式的房地产风险敞口的投资者可以获得显著的多元化收益。

\- ▪ 运用动态关联性方法，研究发现公共房地产债务是各象限冲击的主要传递者，而私人房地产权益是各象限冲击的最重要接受者。

\- ▪ 混合房地产投资组合的进一步证据增强了纯私人房地产风险敞口的风险调整绩效。

\[5\] Climate Risk and Real Estate Prices: What Do We Know?

标题:气候风险和房地产价格：我们知道什么？

作者:Jim Clayton, Steven Devaney, Sarah Sayce and Jorn Van de Wetering

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/10/75;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.278

Abstract : There is a growing international consensus that climate change represents one of the most important structural forces and risks that long-term investors need to proactively consider in building resilient portfolios. Many institutional real estate investors have significant exposure to cities and regions that are economically important but increasingly susceptible to climate change impacts. In this article, we review and synthesize existing academic research on risk exposure to acute and chronic climate-related events and forces and their impacts on property asset values and lending practices. Evidence to date is dominated by studies focused on residential property, although some recent research has begun to examine the commercial real estate sector in a more rigorous way. We draw inferences from the residential studies for how these findings might apply to commercial real estate and highlight the more recent studies focused on commercial property. Recent research is published within both real estate and broader economics and finance journals; these articles indicate that awareness of climate risks is having a more sustained impact on pricing and on investor decision making, although the channels through which impact occurs are generally opaque and warrant further research.

Key Finding

\- ▪ Historically, property prices decline after climate events but tend to eventually recover. Recent evidence suggests that climate events in geographies that previously suffered little exposure to extreme weather can lead to a long-lasting decline in prices or liquidity.

\- ▪ Some evidence from residential markets shows that levels of belief in climate change and confidence in government-led mitigation of impacts may result in differing levels of price impacts where risks lie primarily in the future, such as sea level rise. In this respect, the nature of the marginal investor is important.

\- ▪ Commercial owners/investors in some geographies are placing a higher risk premium on all properties in metro areas affected by climate events, regardless of whether their individual properties have been directly affected.

Abstract:越来越多的国际共识认为，气候变化是长期投资者在建设有弹性的投资组合时需要积极考虑的最重要的结构性力量和风险之一。许多机构房地产投资者对经济上重要但越来越容易受到气候变化影响的城市和地区有很大的投资。在这篇文章中，我们回顾并综合了关于急性和慢性气候相关事件和力量的风险敞口及其对房地产资产价值和贷款实践的影响的现有学术研究。尽管最近的一些研究已经开始以更严格的方式研究商业房地产行业，但迄今为止的证据主要是针对住宅地产的研究。我们从住宅研究中得出这些发现如何应用于商业房地产的推论，并重点介绍了最近针对商业房地产的研究。最近的研究发表在房地产和更广泛的经济和金融杂志上；这些文章表明，对气候风险的认识正在对定价和投资者决策产生更持久的影响，尽管影响发生的渠道通常是不透明的，需要进一步研究。

Key Finding

\- ▪ 从历史上看，房地产价格在气候事件后会下降，但最终会恢复。最近的证据表明，以前很少受到极端天气影响的地区的气候事件可能导致价格或流动性的长期下降。

\- ▪ 来自住宅市场的一些证据表明，对气候变化的信心水平和对政府主导的影响缓解措施的信心水平可能会导致价格影响水平不同，而风险主要在于未来，如海平面上升。在这方面，边际投资者的性质很重要。

\- ▪ 一些地区的商业业主/投资者正在对受气候事件影响的大都市地区的所有房产支付更高的风险溢价，无论其个别房产是否受到直接影响。

\[6\] Global Risk Premiums on Direct Office Real Estate Returns

标题:直接办公室房地产回报的全球风险溢价

作者:Ivo de Wit and Christopher Mayer

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/10/91;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.266

Abstract : This article empirically examines the magnitude of risk premiums for direct real estate investments on a global basis. As this article analyzes ex-ante risk premiums over more than 25 years consistently across the world, it enhances current knowledge about the regional differences between risk premiums and helps long-term investors with their global portfolio allocation over time. On a global level, the authors find a risk premium of 4.1% for Gordon’s growth and 3.7% for two-stage growth model. The periodic growth model shows a slightly lower risk premium of 3.1%. As this study is analyzing a long time period, the article extends the literature by finding evidence for convergence between risk premiums across continents both over time and in cross-sectional variance. However, this is happening slowly, thereby making differences in risk premiums very important in making a global direct real estate investment allocation.

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors analyze ex-ante risk premiums over more than 25 years across the world and use three alternative valuation models to estimate ex-ante risk premiums.

\- ▪ They find that the regional risk premiums are consistent with previous research on the determinants of risk premiums, but these studies were done over shorter time periods.

\- ▪ The authors find evidence for convergence between risk premiums across continents both over time and in cross-sectional variance.

Abstract:本文实证研究了全球范围内直接房地产投资的风险溢价幅度。由于本文在全球范围内持续分析了超过25年的事前风险溢价，它增强了对风险溢价之间区域差异的当前认识，并帮助长期投资者在一段时间内进行全球投资组合配置。在全球层面上，作者发现Gordon增长的风险溢价为4.1%，两阶段增长模型的风险溢价为3.7%。周期增长模型显示风险溢价略低，为3.1%。由于这项研究分析了很长一段时间，本文通过寻找跨大陆风险溢价随时间和横截面方差趋同的证据来扩展文献。然而，这一过程进展缓慢，因此风险溢价的差异在全球直接房地产投资配置中非常重要。

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者分析了全球超过25年的事前风险溢价，并使用三种可选的估值模型来估算事前风险溢价。

\- ▪ 他们发现，区域风险溢价与之前关于风险溢价决定因素的研究一致，但这些研究是在较短的时间内进行的

\- ▪ 作者发现了各大洲的风险溢价在时间和横截面方差上趋于一致的证据。

\[7\] Private Equity Real Estate Fund Performance: A Comparison to REITs and Open-End Core Funds

标题:私募股权房地产基金业绩：与REITs和开放式核心基金的比较

作者:Thomas R. Arnold, David C. Ling and Andy Naranjo

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/10/107;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.276

Abstract : We provide a comprehensive examination of the return performance of closed-end, private equity real estate (PERE) funds relative to the performance of listed real estate stocks (real estate investment trusts \[REITs\]) and the NCREIF ODCE fund index. We first match each PERE fund in our sample and its realized internal rate of return and equity multiple with the return that would have been earned by an LP investor on an investment in the designated benchmark over each fund’s investment horizon. Overall, we find that closed-end PERE funds have underperformed listed REITs. In contrast, we find similar overall performance between PERE and the NCREIF ODCE fund index. We also examine the determinants of the relative performance spread between the PERE funds and the equity REIT index and find that the spread widens with interest rate environment variables (Treasury yields and default spreads) and narrows with broad macroeconomic performance indicators (growth rate of GDP).

Key Finding

\- ▪ Closed-end PERE funds underperform listed REITs—both on average and by the percentages of individual funds.
