\[1\] Credit in a Crisis: Effects of the Fed's Corporate Bond Market Intervention

标题:危机中的信贷：美联储公司债券市场干预的效果

作者:Sharjil Haque,Richard Varghese

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3870678

Abstract : We estimate the causal effects of the Federal Reserve’s Secondary Market Corporate Credit Facilities (SMCCF) on corporate bond market liquidity, cost of credit and bond price. Controlling for flight-to-quality induced by the COVID-19 pandemic, we find the SMCCF reduced trade-weighted average bid-ask spreads by 16 basis points and bond yield by 32 basis points but had negligible effect on prices of eligible bonds compared to ineligible bonds. When we refine this analysis using a credit-rating matched difference-in-differences specification, the reduction in bond yield is much larger and we also find a 1.9 percentage point increase in prices on eligible bonds. Finally, we also document that the improvements in liquidity condition and bond price was driven by the announcement of the facilities, while bond yield was driven down by actual purchases of eligible securities. Our results confirm the Fed's intervention helped restore financial stability in the U.S. corporate bond market following disruptions associated with the COVID-19 pandemic.

Keywords : Corporate bonds, COVID-19, Federal Reserve, Monetary Policy, SMCCF, Quantitative Easing, Liquidity, Bond Markets, Financial Crisis, Matched Difference-in-Differences

Abstract :我们估计了美联储二级市场企业信贷工具（SMCCF）对企业债券市场流动性、信贷成本和债券价格的因果影响。通过控制COVID-19流感大流行导致的质量下降，我们发现SMCCF将贸易加权平均买卖价差降低了16个基点，债券收益率降低了32个基点，但与不合格债券相比，对合格债券价格的影响微乎其微。当我们使用信用评级匹配差异规范来完善这一分析时，债券收益率的下降幅度要大得多，我们还发现合格债券的价格上涨了1.9个百分点。最后，我们还记录了流动性状况和债券价格的改善是由信贷安排的宣布推动的，而债券收益率是由实际购买合格证券推动的。我们的研究结果证实，美联储的干预有助于恢复美国企业债券市场在COVID-19流感爆发后的金融稳定。

Keywords :公司债券，COVID-19，美联储，货币政策，SMCCF，量化宽松，流动性，债券市场，金融危机，匹配差异

\[2\] The Impact of Containment Measures and Monetary and Fiscal Responses on US Financial Markets During the COVID-19 Pandemic

标题:COVID-19大流行期间遏制措施及货币和财政对策对美国金融市场的影响

作者:Emmanuel Joel Aikins Abakah,Guglielmo Maria Caporale,Luis A. Gil-Alana

出处:CESifo Working Paper No. 9163

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3882363

Abstract : This paper analyses the effects of containment measures and monetary and fiscal responses on US financial markets during the Covid-19 pandemic. More specifically, it applies fractional integration methods to analyse their impact on the daily S&P500, the US Treasury Bond Index (USTB), the S&P Green Bond Index (GREEN) and the Dow Jones (DJ) Islamic World Market Index (ISLAM) over the period 1/01/2020-10/03/2021. The results suggest that all four indices are highly persistent and exhibit orders of integration close to 1. A small degree of mean reversion is observed only for the S&P500 under the assumption of white noise errors and USTB with autocorrelated errors; therefore, market efficiency appears to hold in most cases. The mortality rate, surprisingly, seems to have affected stock and bond prices positively with autocorrelated errors. As for the policy responses, both the containment and fiscal measures had a rather limited impact, whilst there were significant announcement effects which lifted markets, especially in the case of monetary announcements. There is also evidence of a significant, positive response to changes in the effective Federal funds rate, which suggests that the financial industry, mainly benefiting from interest rises, plays a dominant role.

Keywords : None

Abstract :本文分析了在Covid-19流感大流行期间，遏制措施以及货币和财政应对措施对美国金融市场的影响。更具体地说，它应用分数积分方法来分析它们对每日标准普尔500指数、美国国债指数（USTB）的影响，标普绿色债券指数（Green）和道琼斯伊斯兰世界市场指数（ISLAM）在2020年1月1日至2021年3月10日期间的表现。结果表明，这四个指数都具有高度的持续性，并且呈现出接近1的整合顺序。在白噪声误差假设下，仅观察到标普500指数的小幅均值回归具有自相关误差的USTB；因此，在大多数情况下，市场效率似乎是成立的。令人惊讶的是，死亡率似乎对股票和债券价格产生了正相关的影响。就政策回应而言，遏制和财政措施的影响都相当有限，同时也有显著的公告效应提振了市场，尤其是在货币公告方面。也有证据表明，对有效联邦基金利率的变化作出了显著、积极的反应，这表明主要受益于加息的金融业发挥了主导作用。

Keywords :None

\[3\] Piotroski’s Fscore Under Varying Economic Conditions

标题:皮奥特罗斯基在不同经济条件下的核心竞争力

作者:Keith P. Anderson,Anup Chowdhury,Moshfique Uddin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3875433

Abstract : Piotroski’s (2000) Fscore has become increasingly important to investment managers and analysts as a simple measure of a company’s financial strength. However how it changes over time, and in particular how it reacts under different economic conditions, has not been considered until now. It is evident from the literature that macroeconomic condition and business cycle affect corporate valuation through affecting stock prices. Macroeconomic conditions also affect corporate liquidity, cash flow, profitability, efficiency, financing and capital structure. Therefore it is highly likely that changing macroeconomic conditions will affect the Fscore by affecting corporate profitability, liquidity and efficiency. We examine the extent to which the Fscore is affected by these macroeconomic changes or shocks. It is important that the Fscore, as a simple and powerful measure of predicting corporate financial strength, should give similar results in any state of the economy so that investors can benefit by using it. We find that macroeconomic conditions have a strong and significant effect on the Fscore; indeed during contractionary episodes they have more effect than firm-level variables. Investors should therefore be cautious in applying the Fscore equally under different macroeconomic conditions.

Keywords : None

Abstract :Piotroski（2000）的Fscore作为衡量一家公司财务实力的简单指标，对投资经理和分析师来说越来越重要。然而，它如何随着时间的推移而变化，特别是它在不同经济条件下的反应，直到现在才被考虑。从文献中可以看出，宏观经济环境和经济周期通过影响股票价格来影响企业价值。宏观经济状况也会影响公司的流动性、现金流、盈利能力、效率、融资和资本结构。因此，宏观经济环境的变化极有可能通过影响企业盈利能力、流动性和效率来影响核心竞争力。我们考察了金融稳定核心区在多大程度上受到这些宏观经济变化或冲击的影响。重要的是，作为预测企业财务实力的一个简单而有力的指标，Fscore应在任何经济状况下给出类似的结果，以便投资者能够从中受益。我们发现，宏观经济环境对金融稳定核心有着强烈而显著的影响；事实上，在收缩期，它们比公司层面的变量有更大的影响。因此，在不同的宏观经济条件下，投资者应谨慎地平等地应用Fscore。

Keywords :None

\[4\] The Informational Role of Options Trading: Evidence from the Shanghai Stock Exchange 50ETF Options in China

标题:期权交易的信息作用：来自中国上海证券交易所50ETF期权的证据

作者:Teng Ma,Xiaoyan Zhang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3874855

Abstract : This study empirically investigates the effects of options trading on future stock returns. Leveraging the Shanghai Stock Exchange 50 exchange-traded fund (50ETF) options trading data in China, we show that put-call ratios, skewness ratios, and China's Volatility Index exhibit economically and statistically significant predictability for the expected returns of the 50ETF in the time series. We also show that a simple market timing strategy based on the above indicators can effectively improve annualized returns and reduce the corresponding risk. We conclude that options trading contains useful information about the future price movements of the underlying asset.

Keywords : Put-call ratios, skewness ratios, China VIX Index, variance risk premium, stock return predictability, market timing

Abstract :本文实证研究了期权交易对未来股票收益的影响。利用中国上海证券交易所50只交易所交易基金（50ETF）的期权交易数据，我们发现看跌期权比率、偏斜度比率和中国波动率指数在时间序列中对50ETF的预期收益具有经济和统计上显著的可预测性。基于上述指标的简单市场择时策略可以有效提高年化收益率，降低相应的风险。我们得出结论，期权交易包含有关标的资产未来价格变动的有用信息。

Keywords :看跌期权比率、偏斜度比率、中国波动率指数、方差风险溢价、股票收益可预测性、市场时机

\[5\] Closet Active Management of Passive Funds

标题:被动资金的主动管理

作者:Pat Akey,Adriana Robertson,Mikhail Simutin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3874582

Abstract : Ostensibly passive index funds and ETFs are surprisingly active. A third of these funds exhibit more activeness than the median actively managed fund, as measured by conventional proxies. Using hand-collected prospectus data, we find that "passive" funds offer an increasingly wide assortment of styles and provide more extreme factor exposures than active funds. We also identify a new dimension of activeness: the use of an index that is explicitly proprietary to the index fund or ETF. In contrast with actively managed funds, more active index funds and ETFs---"closet activists"---underperform. A one-standard deviation increase in activeness is associated with a 55 basis-point decrease in annual alpha. Our results point to the increasingly blurred line between "active" and "passive" funds.

Keywords : None

Abstract :表面上被动的指数基金和etf出人意料地活跃。其中三分之一的基金表现出比传统代理衡量的平均主动管理基金更为活跃。利用手工收集的招股说明书数据，我们发现“被动”基金提供了越来越广泛的投资风格，并提供了比主动基金更极端的因子敞口。我们还发现了活跃性的一个新维度：使用指数基金或ETF明确专有的指数。与主动管理型基金相比，更为主动的指数基金和ETF表现不佳。活跃度每增加一个标准差，年α值就减少55个基点。我们的结果指出，“主动”和“被动”基金之间的界限越来越模糊。

Keywords :None

\[6\] Public Information and Capital Flows: Evidence from a Betting Market

标题:公共信息与资本流动：来自博彩市场的证据

作者:Karthik Balakrishnan,Darren Bernard

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3874155

Abstract : Using a shock to transparency in a parimutuel betting market, we show that capital flows increase in public information even in markets with largely risk-seeking participants. The evidence indicates that capital allocation decisions are partly a function of behavioral mechanisms such as the illusion of control. More broadly, our results suggest transparency can facilitate gambling behavior, a result with implications for the many settings in which decision-makers are not globally risk averse but rather exhibit local risk-seeking behavior. Our findings also inform policy debate on whether and how to use transparency to address speculative flows in financial markets.

Keywords : transparency, public information, capital flows, gambling, illusion of control, market efficiency

Abstract :利用对巴黎燃料博彩市场透明度的冲击，我们证明了即使在主要有风险寻求参与者的市场中，公开信息中的资本流动也会增加。证据表明，资本配置决策在一定程度上是控制错觉等行为机制的作用。更广泛地说，我们的研究结果表明，透明度可以促进赌博行为，这一结果对决策者不是全球风险厌恶，而是表现出局部风险寻求行为的许多环境具有影响。我们的研究结果也为是否以及如何利用透明度解决金融市场投机流动的政策辩论提供了参考。

Keywords :透明度、公开信息、资本流动、赌博、控制幻觉、市场效率

\[7\] The Race to Exploit Anomalies and the Cost of Slow Trading

标题:利用异常现象的竞赛和缓慢交易的成本

作者:Guy Kaplanski

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3873927

Abstract : Studying 71 anomalies, we show how the discovery of anomaly reshapes out-of-sample returns, thereby creating a contrarian effect to the general decay in returns. As a result, the average contribution of the first-day return to the portfolio value increases from 3% before the anomaly is discovered to 12% afterward and 30% in case of momentum anomalies. The effect exists in long-side and short-side portfolios and in the bought and sold stocks of both portfolios. The long-lasting effect indicates that arbitrage capital plays a key role in retaining market efficiency in the long run, implying a persistent mispricing component in anomalies.

Keywords : market efficiency, cross-section anomalies, arbitrage capital, asset mispricing, contrarian return effect

Abstract :通过对71个异常的研究，我们展示了异常的发现是如何重塑样本外收益率的，从而对收益率的普遍衰退产生了反转效应。因此，第一天回报对投资组合价值的平均贡献从发现异常前的3%增加到发现异常后的12%和动量异常时的30%。这种效应存在于多头和空头投资组合以及两种投资组合的买入和卖出股票中。长期效应表明，套利资本在长期维持市场效率方面起着关键作用，这意味着异常现象中存在持续的错误定价成分。

Keywords :市场效率、横截面异常、套利资本、资产错误定价、反向收益效应
