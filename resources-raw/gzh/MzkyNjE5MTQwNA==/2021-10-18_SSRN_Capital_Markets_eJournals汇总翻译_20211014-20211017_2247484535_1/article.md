\[1\] The 'Matthew Effect' in Asset Returns: Winners and Losers from Entry

标题:资产回报中的“马太效应”：进入的赢家和输家

作者:Fanis Papamichalis

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3918613

Abstract : I show that asset prices react asymmetrically to entry risk. As entry increases, competition intensifies, leading to higher aggregate output. However, aggregate price falls affecting low market power firms adversely, with less output and employees, while higher market power firms eventually expand. Since entry risk depends on aggregate conditions, the ensuing endogenous reallocation, also known as the "Matthew Effect", gives rise to several empirical phenomena, including the high equity premium and the size effect. Conversely, as entry rates in the US have been declining since the early 80’s, an "Anti-Matthew Effect" has prevailed in more recent years, establishing a positive relationship between markups and excess returns and weakening size in the data. In fact, an empirical investigation reveals that the majority of small growth low profitability firms exhibit low market power. Finally, a strategy exploiting the "Anti-Matthew Effect" generates positive - and statistically significant - excess returns of 7.8% per annum.

Keywords : Matthew Effect, Markups, Size Effect, Market Structure

Abstract :我证明了资产价格对进入风险的反应是不对称的。随着进入的增加，竞争加剧，导致总产出增加。然而，总价格下跌对低市场力企业产生不利影响，产出和员工减少，而高市场力企业最终会扩张。由于进入风险取决于总体条件，因此，随后的内生再分配（也称为“马太效应”）会产生若干实证现象，包括高股本溢价和规模效应。相反，由于美国的进入率自80年代初以来一直在下降，近年来“反马太效应”盛行，在加价和超额回报之间建立了积极的关系，并削弱了数据的规模。事实上，一项实证调查显示，大多数小型低盈利企业的市场影响力较低。最后，利用“反马太效应”的策略每年产生7.8%的正超额收益，且具有统计意义。

Keywords :马太效应、加价、规模效应、市场结构

\[2\] The ESG Conundrum: An Outsider’s View

作者:Anatoly B. Schmidt

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3942572

Abstract : Two suggestions are offered to address growing criticism of the ESG-based investing. First, socially responsible investors need to use portfolio performance measure that is explicitly sensitive to the portfolio ESG value. Second, since the corporate ESG ratings are not regulated and are vaguely defined, the ratings agencies should provide detailed information on how their ratings are calculated and allow their clients to implement the customized ratings of their choice.

Keywords : portfolio choice, portfolio performance measure, ESG

Abstract :本文提出了两项建议，以应对日益增长的对基于ESG的投资的批评。首先，社会责任投资者需要使用对投资组合ESG价值明确敏感的投资组合绩效指标。第二，由于企业ESG评级未受监管，且定义模糊，评级机构应提供有关其评级计算方式的详细信息，并允许其客户实施其选择的定制评级。

Keywords :投资组合选择、投资组合绩效衡量、ESG

\[3\] COVID-19 and the Financial Markets’ Volatility and Returns

标题:新冠病毒-19与金融市场的波动性和回报

作者:Habib ur Rehman,Danish Ahmed Siddiqui

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3941990

Abstract : The study is undertaken to investigate the impact of COVID-19 on the Financial Markets ‘Volatility from the period April 2020 to June 2020. Ten countries namely Pakistan, USA, China, UAE, UK, Spain, France, South Korea, Switzerland, and the Netherlands were selected. Our daily data comes from the WHO situation reports (COVID-19 statistics) and Pakistan, USA, UK, UAE, China, France, Netherlands, Switzerland’s, Spain, and South Korea Stock Exchange Indices database, respectively. We use the KSE-100, S&P 500, FTSE 100, SE 50, AEX, ADX, IBEX, FCHI, KRX, and SSMI 3-month realized volatility index as a proxy for the Pakistan financial markets’ volatility. We test a simple Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression investigating the new coronavirus impact on the financial volatility and we use a stepwise procedure. Independent variables included Global COVID-19 Confirm case, Fatality, Country COVID-19 new case, Country Fatality, and dependent variable included country’s market volatility, and market returns. Control variables of the country’s Exchange and Interest rate were also included. The study design is descriptive and analytical using the quantitative method with the application of Eviews. The results suggested that the Global COVID new case seems to have a significant negative impact on the country's market volatility, however, global fatality seems to increase market volatility. Country-specific cases seem to negatively affect that country's volatility however, country-specific fatality doesn’t seem to be significantly impacting volatility. Apart from that, interest and exchange rates seem to increase the volatility. However, the effect of these variables on returns remained insignificant. The findings of this research provide policy implications to investors and policymakers indicating that pandemics can significantly perpetrate business economics.

Keywords : COVID-19 Pandemic, Fatality Ratio, Pakistan Financial Markets' Volatility

Abstract :本研究旨在调查2020年4月至2020年6月期间新冠病毒-19对金融市场波动性的影响。选择了十个国家，即巴基斯坦、美国、中国、阿联酋、英国、西班牙、法国、韩国、瑞士和荷兰。我们的每日数据分别来自世界卫生组织形势报告（新冠肺炎-19统计数据）和巴基斯坦、美国、英国、阿联酋、中国、法国、荷兰、瑞士、西班牙和韩国股票交易所指数数据库。我们使用KSE-100、标准普尔500指数、富时100指数、SE50指数、AEX指数、ADX指数、IBEX指数、FCHI指数、KRX指数和SSMI三个月已实现波动率指数作为巴基斯坦金融市场波动率的代表。我们测试了一个简单的普通最小二乘（OLS）回归，调查了新冠病毒对金融波动性的影响，并使用了逐步过程。自变量包括全球确诊病例、病死率、国家新病例、国家病死率，因变量包括国家市场波动性和市场回报。该国汇率和利率的控制变量也包括在内。本研究设计为描述性和分析性研究，采用定量方法，并应用Eviews。结果表明，全球新冠病毒病例似乎对该国的市场波动性产生了显著的负面影响，然而，全球死亡率似乎增加了市场波动性。特定国家的病例似乎会对该国的波动性产生负面影响，但特定国家的死亡率似乎不会对波动性产生显著影响。除此之外，利率和汇率似乎增加了波动性。然而，这些变量对回报的影响仍然不显著。这项研究的结果为投资者和决策者提供了政策启示，表明流行病可以极大地影响商业经济。

Keywords :新冠病毒-19大流行、死亡率、巴基斯坦金融市场的波动性

\[4\] The Impact of Socioemotional Wealth on Corporate Reporting Readability in a Multinational Family-Controlled Firm

标题:社会情感财富对跨国家族控制企业报告可读性的影响

作者:Alonso Moreno,Martin Quinn

出处:Forthcoming, Revista de Contabilidad-Spanish Accounting Review

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3918887

Abstract : Extant research suggests that the most significant elements of a family firm’s socioemotional wealth (SEW) can drive financial reporting decisions. This paper explores this empirically by analyzing corporate disclosures of a case organization – Guinness, a multinational family brewing firm – over an extended period. We identify the presence of the SEW dimensions in the firm’s corporate disclosures and explore the relationship between the most salient SEW dimension (family identity) and readability, measured by the Bog index. The analysis finds a positive association between family identity and readability in the period when the firm under study can be defined as a family firm. Other SEW dimensions do not appear to have an influence on readability. In addition, at the end of the period of study, when the firm under study ceased to be a family firm, the SEW dimensions failed to have an effect on readability.

Keywords : corporate reporting, family business, readability, socioemotional wealth

Abstract :现有研究表明，家族企业社会情感财富（SEW）中最重要的因素可以推动财务报告决策。本文通过分析一个案例组织——吉尼斯，一家跨国家族酿酒公司——在很长一段时间内的公司披露，对这一点进行了实证研究。我们确定了SEW维度在公司披露中的存在，并探讨了最显著的SEW维度（家庭身份）与可读性之间的关系，通过Bog指数进行衡量。分析发现，在被研究企业可以被定义为家族企业的时期，家族认同与可读性之间存在正相关。其他SEW尺寸似乎对可读性没有影响。此外，在研究结束时，当被研究企业不再是家族企业时，SEW维度对可读性没有影响。

Keywords :企业报告、家族企业、可读性、社会情感财富

\[5\] Constrained Dynamic Futures Portfolios with Stochastic Basis

标题:随机基约束动态期货组合

作者:Xiaodong Chen,Tim Leung,Yang Zhou

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3942191

Abstract : We study the problem of dynamically trading multiple futures contracts on different underlying assets subject to portfolio constraints. The spreads between futures and spot prices are modeled by a multidimensional scaled Brownian bridge to account for their convergence at maturity. Under this stochastic basis model, we apply the stochastic control approach to rigorously derive the optimal trading strategies via utility maximization. This leads to the analysis of the associated system of Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equations, which are reduced to a system of ODEs. A series of numerical examples are provided to illustrate the optimal strategies and wealth distributions under different portfolio constraints.

Keywords : futures portfolio, futures basis, portfolio constraints, utility maximization

Abstract :我们研究了在投资组合约束下，在不同标的资产上动态交易多个期货合约的问题。期货和现货价格之间的价差由多维标度布朗桥建模，以说明它们在到期时的收敛性。在这个随机基础模型下，我们应用随机控制方法，通过效用最大化严格推导出最优交易策略。这导致了对关联的Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman（HJB）方程组的分析，该方程组被简化为常微分方程组。通过一系列的数值例子说明了在不同的投资组合约束下的最优策略和财富分布。

Keywords :期货组合，期货基础，组合约束，效用最大化

\[6\] Capital-Embodied Technological Progress and Obsolescence: The Role of Asset Valuation

标题:资本体现的技术进步和过时：资产评估的作用

作者:Yosuke Jin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3941848

Abstract : This study highlights the importance of technological progress embodied in capital goods in explaining business cycle fluctuations and stock market prices through asset valuation mechanisms. Such technological progress modifies the relative productivity of capital goods across different vintages, which is reflected in their market value. In estimating a DSGE model built on asset valuation, this study finds that investment and output increase significantly with such technological progress, as the initial negative wealth effects due to economic obsolescence of existing capital goods are more than offset by improved income prospects from new investments that generate higher payouts due to productivity enhancement. The investors’ technological shock perception crucially affects asset valuation, thereby altering the empirical results considerably. Different shock propagation mechanism analyses reveal possible asset price misalignments, namely extremely high stock market prices that are not aligned with actual capital accumulation dynamics.

Keywords : Technological progress, Obsolescence, Asset valuation, Wealth, Capital stock, Investment

Abstract :这项研究强调了资本货物所体现的技术进步在通过资产估值机制解释商业周期波动和股票市场价格方面的重要性。这种技术进步改变了不同年份资本品的相对生产率，这反映在它们的市场价值上。在评估基于资产估值的DSGE模型时，本研究发现，随着技术进步，投资和产出显著增加，由于现有资本货物的经济过时所产生的最初负面财富效应被新投资带来的收入前景改善所抵消，新投资因生产率提高而产生更高的支出。投资者的技术冲击感知对资产估值有着至关重要的影响，从而显著改变了实证结果。不同的冲击传播机制分析揭示了可能的资产价格偏差，即与实际资本积累动态不一致的极高股票市场价格。

Keywords :技术进步、过时、资产估值、财富、股本、投资

\[7\] Deleveraging CAPM: Asset Betas vs. Equity Betas

标题:去杠杆化CAPM：资产贝塔与权益贝塔

作者:Emilio Barone,Gaia Barone

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3941752

Abstract : The classic estimates of CAPM equity betas are notoriously unstable. We assume that this is mainly due to changes of firm’s leverage over time. In order to take leverage into account, we propose a new approach where asset correlations among firms are pairwise constant, while equity correlations change over time as a function of the stochastic evolution of firms’ asset values. The paper closes with a simulation that helps to show the model’s features.

Keywords : Asset management, derivatives, corporate finance, risk management

Abstract :CAPM权益Beta的经典估计值是出了名的不稳定。我们假设这主要是由于公司杠杆随时间的变化。为了考虑杠杆作用，我们提出了一种新的方法，即企业之间的资产相关性是成对不变的，而股权相关性随着时间的推移作为企业资产价值随机演化的函数而变化。本文以一个有助于显示模型特征的模拟结束。

Keywords :资产管理、衍生品、公司金融、风险管理

\[9\] Discount Rates, Debt Maturity, and the Fiscal Theory

标题:贴现率、债务期限和财政理论

作者:Alexandre Corhay,Thilo Kind,Howard Kung,Gonzalo Morales

出处:SAFE Working Paper No. 323

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3940955

Abstract : This paper examines how the transmission of government portfolio risk arising from maturity operations depends on the stance of monetary/fiscal policy. Accounting for risk premia in the fiscal theory allows the government portfolio to affect the expected inflation, even in a frictionless economy. The effects of maturity rebalancing on expected inflation in the fiscal theory directly depend on the conditional nominal term premium, giving rise to an optimal debt maturity policy that is state dependent. In a calibrated macro-finance model, we demonstrate that maturity operations have sizable effects on expected inflation and output through our novel risk transmission mechanism.

Keywords : erm structure of interest rates, Fiscal theory of the price level, Bond risk premia, Government debt, DSGE models, Nonlinear solution methods

Abstract :本文考察了到期操作产生的政府投资组合风险的传递如何取决于货币/财政政策的立场。财政理论中的风险溢价会计允许政府投资组合影响预期通胀，即使在无摩擦经济中也是如此。在财政理论中，到期日再平衡对预期通胀的影响直接取决于有条件的名义期限溢价，从而产生了依赖于国家的最优债务到期政策。在一个经过校准的宏观金融模型中，我们证明了到期操作通过我们新的风险传导机制对预期通胀和产出具有相当大的影响。

Keywords :利率的erm结构，价格水平的财政理论，债券风险溢价，政府债务，DSGE模型，非线性求解方法
