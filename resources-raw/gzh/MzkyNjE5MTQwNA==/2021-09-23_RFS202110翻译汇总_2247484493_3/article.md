\[1\] Fintech Borrowers: Lax Screening or Cream-Skimming?

标题:Fintech借款人：筛选松懈还是撇奶油？

作者:Marco Di Maggio, Vincent Yao

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4565/6054822; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa142

Abstract : We study the personal credit market using unique individual-level data covering fintech and traditional lenders. We show that fintech lenders acquire market share by lending first to higher-risk borrowers and then to safer borrowers, and rely mainly on hard information to make credit decisions. Fintech borrowers are significantly more likely to default than neighbor individuals with the same characteristics borrowing from traditional financial institutions. Furthermore, they tend to experience a short-lived reduction in the cost of credit, because their indebtedness increases more than non-fintech borrowers after loan origination. However, fintech lenders’ pricing strategies are likely to take this into account.

Abstract:我们使用涵盖fintech和传统贷款人的独特个人层面数据研究个人信贷市场。我们发现，fintech贷款人通过先向高风险借款人贷款，然后再向更安全的借款人贷款，从而获得市场份额，并且主要依靠硬信息来做出信贷决策。金融科技借款人明显比具有相同特征的邻居更容易违约，他们从传统金融机构借款。此外，他们往往会经历信贷成本的短期下降，因为在贷款发放后，他们的债务比非fintech借款人增加得更多。然而，fintech贷款人的定价策略可能会考虑到这一点。

\[2\] Home Equity and Labor Income: The Role of Constrained Mobility

标题:住房公平和劳动收入：流动性受限的作用

作者:Radhakrishnan Gopalan, Barton H Hamilton, Ankit Kalda, David Sovich

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4619/6055566; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa136

Abstract : Using detailed data for U.S. homeowners, we document a negative, nonlinear relation between the loan-to-value ratio (LTV) of homeowners’ primary residence and their labor income. Consistent with high LTV individuals experiencing constrained mobility, we find stronger effects among subprime, liquidity- constrained individuals and those living in regions with limited alternative local employment opportunities and strict noncompete law enforcement. Though high LTV individuals are less likely to move across MSAs, they are more likely to change jobs without changing their residence. We find no effects among similar neighboring renters employed at the same firm and with a similar job tenure.

Abstract:利用美国房主的详细数据，我们记录了房主主要住宅的贷款价值比（LTV）与其劳动收入之间的负非线性关系。与经历流动性受限的高LTV个人一致，我们发现次级抵押贷款、流动性受限的个人以及生活在当地替代就业机会有限和严格的非竞争执法地区的个人之间的影响更强。尽管LTV高的个人不太可能跨越MSA，但他们更有可能在不改变住所的情况下换工作。我们发现，在同一家公司雇佣的、具有类似工作任期的类似相邻承租人之间没有影响。

\[3\] Skilled Labor Mobility and Firm Value: Evidence from Green Card Allocations

标题:熟练劳动力流动与企业价值：来自绿卡分配的证据

作者:Mo Shen

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4663/6127186; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab014

Abstract : This paper studies how the labor market frictions of skilled workers affect corporate valuation. The analysis features immigrant workers’ mobility constraints imposed by the U.S. green card application process and exploits exogenous variations caused by imperfections in the current immigration system. The study finds that relaxing mobility constraints negatively influences firm value. This effect is stronger for firms with higher labor adjustment costs. Reductions in investments and increases in labor costs are channels through which labor mobility adversely affects firm value. The findings suggest that monopoly rent over skilled workers is an important economic determinant of corporate valuation.

Abstract:本文研究了技术工人的劳动力市场摩擦对企业价值的影响。该分析以美国绿卡申请程序施加的移民工人流动性限制为特征，并利用了当前移民制度不完善造成的外生变化。研究发现，放松流动性约束会对企业价值产生负面影响。对于劳动力调整成本较高的企业，这种影响更大。投资减少和劳动力成本增加是劳动力流动对企业价值产生不利影响的渠道。研究结果表明，技术工人的垄断租金是企业估值的一个重要经济决定因素。

\[4\] CEO Noncompete Agreements, Job Risk, and Compensation

标题:首席执行官竞业禁止协议、工作风险和薪酬

作者:Omesh Kini, Ryan Williams, Sirui Yin

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4701/5909665; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa103

Abstract : Using hand-collected data on CEO noncompete agreements (NCAs), we find that NCAs are less common when CEOs expect to incur greater personal costs from reduced job mobility and more common when firms expect to suffer greater economic harm if departing CEOs leave to work for a competitor. Additionally, turnover-performance sensitivity is stronger when CEOs have NCAs. Finally, total compensation and incentive pay are higher if CEOs have more enforceable NCAs. Our identification strategy exploits staggered state-level changes in NCA enforceability. Overall, our findings suggest that restrictions on job mobility have important implications for how CEOs are monitored and compensated.

Abstract:使用手工收集的有关CEO非竞争协议（NCA）的数据，我们发现，当CEO期望因工作流动性降低而产生更大的个人成本时，NCA不太常见，而当公司期望离职CEO为竞争对手工作时遭受更大的经济损失时，NCA更为常见。此外，CEO拥有NCA时，离职绩效敏感性更强。最后，如果首席执行官有更多可执行的NCA，总薪酬和激励性薪酬会更高。我们的识别策略利用了NCA可执行性的交错状态级变化。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，对工作流动性的限制对CEO的监控和薪酬有重要影响。

\[5\] Incentivizing Financial Regulators

标题:激励金融监管机构

作者:Joseph Kalmenovitz

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4745/6054820; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa138

Abstract : I study how promotion incentives within the public sector affect financial regulation. I assemble individual data for all SEC enforcement attorneys between 2002 and 2017, including enforcement cases, salaries, and ranks. Consistent with tournament model, attorneys with stronger promotion incentives are involved in more enforcement, especially against severe misconduct, and in tougher settlement terms. For identification, I rely on cross-sectional tests within offices and ranks and on exogenous variation in salaries resulting from a conversion to a new pay system. The findings highlight a novel link between incentives and regulation and show that the regulator’s organizational design affects securities markets.

Abstract:我研究公共部门内的晋升激励如何影响金融监管。我收集了2002年至2017年间所有SEC执法律师的个人数据，包括执法案件、工资和级别。与锦标赛模式一致，晋升激励更强的律师参与更多的执法，特别是针对严重不当行为，并以更严格的和解条款。为了确定身份，我依赖于办公室和职级内的横向测试，以及转换为新的薪酬体系所导致的薪酬的外部变化。研究结果强调了激励和监管之间的一种新联系，并表明监管机构的组织设计影响证券市场。

\[6\] Information Dispersion across Employees and Stock Returns

标题:员工之间的信息分散和股票回报

作者:Ashwini Agrawal, Isaac Hacamo, Zhongchen Hu

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4785/5906533; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa105

Abstract : Rank-and-file employees are becoming increasingly critical for many firms, yet we know little about how their employment dynamics matter for stock prices. We analyze new data from the individual CV’s of public company employees and find that rank-and-file labor flows can be used to predict abnormal stock returns. Accounting data and survey evidence indicate that workers’ labor market decisions reflect information about future corporate earnings. Investors, however, do not appear to fully incorporate this information into their earnings expectations. The findings support the hypothesis that rank-and-file employees’ entry and exit decisions reveal valuable insights into their employers’ future stock performance.

Abstract:普通员工对许多公司越来越重要，但我们对他们的就业动态对股价的影响知之甚少。我们分析了来自上市公司员工个人简历的新数据，发现普通员工的劳动力流动可以用来预测异常的股票回报。会计数据和调查证据表明，工人的劳动力市场决策反映了有关未来公司收益的信息。然而，投资者似乎没有将这些信息完全纳入他们的收益预期。这些发现支持了这样一个假设：普通员工的入职和离职决定揭示了对其雇主未来股票表现的宝贵见解。

\[7\] Private Equity Buyouts and Workplace Safety

标题:私募股权收购与工作场所安全

作者:Jonathan Cohn, Nicole Nestoriak, Malcolm Wardlaw

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4832/6081024; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab001

Abstract : This paper presents evidence of a large, persistent decline in establishment-level workplace injury rates after private equity (PE) buyouts of publicly traded U.S. firms. We find that firms experience fewer OSHA safety violations after buyouts and that a larger decline in injury rates is associated with an increased probability of exit via IPO. Employment reductions after buyouts are concentrated in relatively low-injury-risk establishments. Overall, our results suggest that buyouts improve workplace safety and that PE acquirers benefit from this improvement. We explore possible causes of these changes through interviews with executives of companies acquired in buyouts and through cross-sectional analysis.

Abstract:这篇论文展示了私募股权（PE）收购美国上市公司后，机构层面工伤率持续大幅下降的证据。我们发现，公司在收购后经历较少的OSHA安全违规行为，并且伤害率的较大下降与通过IPO退出的概率增加有关。收购后的就业减少集中在伤害风险相对较低的机构。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，收购提高了工作场所的安全性，私募股权收购方也从中受益。我们通过对收购公司高管的访谈和横断面分析，探讨这些变化的可能原因。

\[8\] International Trade and the Propagation of Merger Waves

标题:国际贸易与并购浪潮的传播

作者:Muhammad Farooq Ahmad, Eric de Bodt, Jarrad Harford

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4876/5902461; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa104

Abstract : Cross-border merger activity is growing in importance. We map the global trade network each year from 1989 to 2016 and compare it to cross-border and domestic merger activity. Trade-weighted merger activity in trading partner countries has statistically and economically significant explanatory power for the likelihood that a given country will be in a merger wave state, at both the cross-border and domestic levels, even controlling for its own lagged merger activity. The role of trade as a channel for transmitting merger waves is confirmed using import tariff cuts and trade sanctions as instruments to mitigate endogeneity. Overall, the full trade network helps our understanding of merger waves and how merger activity propagate across borders.

Abstract:跨境并购活动的重要性日益增加。从1989年到2016年，我们每年绘制全球贸易网络图，并将其与跨境和国内并购活动进行比较。贸易伙伴国家的贸易加权合并活动在统计上和经济上对某一国家在跨境和国内层面处于合并浪潮状态的可能性具有重要的解释力，甚至可以控制其自身滞后的合并活动。利用进口关税削减和贸易制裁作为缓解内生性的手段，确认了贸易作为传播合并浪潮的渠道的作用。总体而言，完整的贸易网络有助于我们了解并购浪潮以及并购活动如何跨境传播。

\[9\] Financing Corporate Growth

标题:为企业成长融资

作者:Murray Z Frank, Ali Sanati

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4926/6020280; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa133

Abstract : Considerable research tackles the aggregate impact of debt financing. We show that equity is more important for firm growth than generally understood. A dollar of equity issuance is associated with an extra |$\\$0.93$| of real assets, whereas a dollar of debt issuance is associated with an extra |$\\$0.14$|⁠. Firms issue equity first, then increase real assets, and, finally, issue debt while repurchasing equity. We explain this sequence using a model in which debt is tax preferred relative to equity but is subject to limited commitment. We use the estimated model to evaluate how several government policies affect corporate growth.

Abstract:大量研究涉及债务融资的总体影响。我们发现，股权对企业成长的重要性比人们普遍理解的要大。一美元的股权发行与额外的0.93美元的实际资产相关，而一美元的债务发行与额外的0.14美元相关$|⁠. 公司首先发行股权，然后增加实际资产，最后在回购股权的同时发行债务。我们使用一个模型来解释这个序列，在这个模型中，债务是相对于股权的税收优先，但受制于有限的承诺。我们使用估计模型来评估几种政府政策如何影响企业增长。

\[10\] Bank Concentration and Product Market Competition

标题:银行集中度与产品市场竞争

作者:Farzad Saidi, Daniel Streitz

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/4999/6124372; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab005

Abstract : This paper documents a link between bank concentration and markups in nonfinancial sectors. We exploit concentration-increasing bank mergers and variation in banks’ market shares across industries and show that higher credit concentration is associated with higher markups and that high-market-share lenders charge lower loan rates. We argue that this is due to the greater incidence of competing firms sharing common lenders that induce less aggressive product market behavior among their borrowers, thereby internalizing potential adverse effects of higher rates. Consistent with our conjecture, the effect is stronger in industries with competition in strategic substitutes where negative product market externalities are greatest.

Abstract:本文记录了银行集中度与非金融部门加价之间的联系。我们利用集中度增加的银行合并和各行业银行市场份额的变化，表明较高的信贷集中度与较高的加价相关，且较高的市场份额贷款人收取较低的贷款利率。我们认为，这是由于竞争企业共享共同贷款人的发生率较高，从而导致借款人之间的产品市场行为不那么激进，从而将更高利率的潜在不利影响内部化。与我们的推测一致，在产品市场负外部性最大的战略替代品竞争行业，这种效应更强。

\[11\] Bank Lending in the Knowledge Economy

标题:知识经济下的银行贷款

作者:Giovanni Dell’Ariccia, Dalida Kadyrzhanova, Camelia Minoiu, Lev Ratnovski

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/5036/6055563; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa144

Abstract : We study the composition of bank loan portfolios during the transition of the real sector to a knowledge economy where firms increasingly use intangible capital. Exploiting heterogeneity in bank exposure to the compositional shift from tangible to intangible capital, we show that exposed banks curtail commercial lending and reallocate lending to other assets, such as mortgages. We estimate that the substantial growth in intangible capital since the mid-1980s explains around 30% of the secular decline in the share of commercial lending in banks’ loan portfolios. We provide suggestive evidence that this reallocation increased the riskiness of banks’ mortgage lending.

Abstract:我们研究了实体经济向知识经济转型期间银行贷款组合的构成，在知识经济中，企业越来越多地使用无形资本。利用银行风险敞口的异质性，从有形资本向无形资本的构成转变，我们表明，风险敞口银行减少了商业贷款，并将贷款重新分配给其他资产，如抵押贷款。我们估计，自20世纪80年代中期以来，无形资本的大幅增长解释了商业贷款在银行贷款组合中所占份额长期下降的30%左右。我们提供了具有启发性的证据，表明这种再分配增加了银行抵押贷款的风险。

\[12\] Capital Flows, Real Estate, and Local Cycles: Evidence from German Cities, Banks, and Firms

标题:资本流动、房地产和地方周期：来自德国城市、银行和企业的证据

作者:Peter Bednarek, Daniel Marcel te Kaat, Chang Ma, Alessandro Rebucci

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/10/5077/6127185; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab017

Abstract : We study how capital flows affects German cities’ GDP growth depending on the state of their real estate markets. Identification exploits a policy framework assigning refugees to cities on a quasi-random basis and variation in nondevelopable area for the construction of an exposure measure to real estate market tightness. We estimate that the most exposed cities to real estate market tightness grew at least 1.9 percentage points more than the least exposed ones, cumulatively, from 2009 to 2014. Capital inflows shift credit to firms with more collateral, which leads firms to hire and invest more in response to these shocks.

Abstract:我们研究资本流动如何影响德国城市的GDP增长，这取决于其房地产市场的状况。识别利用了一个政策框架，将难民按准随机基础分配到城市，并在不可开发区域进行变化，以构建房地产市场紧张程度的暴露度量。我们估计，从2009年到2014年，受房地产市场紧张影响最大的城市累计增长至少比受影响最小的城市高1.9个百分点。资本流入将信贷转移到拥有更多抵押品的企业，这导致企业在应对这些冲击时雇佣和投资更多。
