\[1\] Selling options to beat the market: Further empirical evidence

标题:卖出期权以击败市场：进一步的经验证据

作者:Alejandro Balbás,Gregorio Serna

出处:-

Abstract : Brownian-motion-linked derivative pricing models predict the existenceof European options whose sale leads to value at risk and/or conditional valueat risk levels strictly lower than the obtained price. Consequently, the option sale plusthe price investment in a riskless asset becomes a self-financing portfolio with negativerisk. If this strategy is repeated over and over, then the price will be still zero but therisk will tend to minus infinity. This paper reports the results of an empirical studyabout the performance in practice of the self-financing strategies above. The AmericanSP-500 and the German DAX-30 indices will be involved. In both cases the underlyingindex is beaten by a simple buy and hold strategy involving only two securities,namely, the riskless asset and a short option. This finding may be interesting to practitioners,but it also seems relevant from a theoretical perspective. There are at leasttwo reasons. On the one hand, the market can be beaten in an orthodox way, sincetotally orthodox results of Financial Economics inspire the used methodology. Onedoes not need to assume any discrepancy between theory and empirical evidence. Themost pure theory is used to beat the benchmark. On the other hand, the risk nevertends to minus infinity in practice, so perhaps the Brownian-motion-linked models andtheir theoretical behavior in tails should be revisited and slightly improved.

Keywords : Downside risk measure, derivative market, buy and hold golden strategy, outperforming benchmarks, market efficiency.

\[2\] How Prevalent Are Financial Constraints in the U.S.?

标题:金融约束在美国有多普遍。？

作者:Joan Farre-Mensa,Alexander Ljungqvist,Enrique J. Schroth

出处:-

Abstract : We structurally estimate a set of firm- and time-varying measures of the magnitude of external financial constraints. By considering the entire capital structure—equity and debt of various maturities—we identify on which external financing margin(s) a firm is constrained at a given point in time. Using a sample of 10,902 U.S. stock market listed firms from 1989 to 2018, we find that 43% of firm-quarters are equity-constrained but far fewer are constrained to raise short-term debt (33%) or long-term debt (24%). A firm is simultaneously constrained on all three margins only 12% of the time. The prevalence of financial constraints is highly volatile, typically increasing in the runup to and during recessions. While being unconstrained is a persistent state, being constrained is not, with firms that enter a constrained state exiting it again relatively quickly.

Keywords : Financial constraints, Investment, Short-term debt, Long-term debt, Equity, Structural estimation

\[3\] Race-Related Events and Stock Prices

标题:与种族有关的事件和股票价格

作者:Matthew Denes,Duane J. Seppi

出处:-

Abstract : The role of race is being reexamined in American society driven by a series of law-enforcement-related Black deaths, Black Lives Matter protests, counter-protests, debates about Confederate monuments, and other race-related events. This raises questions about how changes in discrimination, systemic racism, and diversity affect U.S. firms. Stock prices provide a window into this question. We find that firm stock prices respond to race-related events. These responses are related to heterogeneity in managerial diversity, EEOC complaints, social media, and demographics around firms’ establishments. We also provide evidence of a structural break in these effects after the highly-visible death of George Floyd.

Keywords : Discrimination, structural racism, Black Lives Matter, asset pricing, volatility

\[4\] The Almost Unique Construction of the Zero-Coupon Curve When a Forward Curve of Tenor M Months, m ∈ {1, 3, 6, 12} Is Given

标题:当远期曲线的期限为M个月，M∈ 给出了{1,3,6,12}

作者:Flemming Lorenzen

出处:-

Abstract : Traditionally the zero-coupon yield curve is used to construct the forward rate curve. Here we will show how to do the opposite i.e. how the forward rate curve is used to construct the zero-coupon yield curve. Actually we will determine all 1B forward rates, except m−1 end of month 1B rates, by implying them from the forward rate curve. The zero-coupon yield curve is hence unique except for the freedom to choose m−1 1B forward rates arbitrarily.

Keywords : zero-coupon yield curve, yield curve, forward rate curve, curve construction, forward rate, zero-coupon rate

\[5\] The Rest Of the World's Dollar-Weighted Return on U.S. Treasurys

标题:世界其他国家对美国国债的美元加权回报

作者:Zhengyang Jiang,Arvind Krishnamurthy,Hanno N. Lustig

出处:-

Abstract : Since 1980, foreign investors have consistently earned lower returns on U.S. Treasurys than other market participants. The dollar-weighted returns or IRRs realized by the ROW 300 bps. are lower than the buy-and-hold return. The ROW's demand for U.S. Treasurys is inelastic compared to the demand of all other Treasury investors. The ROW IRRs are at least 150 bps per annum lower than the IRR of all other investors, excluding the Fed. Consistent with demand for dollar-denominated safe assets, the ROW tends to buy Treasurys when bond prices are high. During the 2021 pandemic, ROW demand for longer maturity U.S. Treasurys has weakened.

Keywords : safe assets, exorbitant privilege

\[6\] Volatility managed multi-factor portfolios

标题:波动率管理的多因素投资组合

作者:Christoph Reschenhofer,Josef Zechner

出处:-

Abstract : This paper shows that portfolio performance can be improved significantly when jointly using volatilities of past factor returns and option-implied market volatilities to determine factor exposures. Improvements are much larger in risk regimes characterized by option-implied right-skewed and/or high vola market returns. When model parameters are estimated separately for different regimes, risk-adjusted portfolio returns improve even further. The results are not driven by a specific set of factors but also achieved when principal components of a large universe of factors are used as factors. The findings are robust to transaction costs and to out-of-sample estimation.

Keywords : Volatility management, equity risk premia, portfolio management, investment strategies

\[7\] Fast and Slow Arbitrageurs: Implications for Return Predictability

标题:快速套利者和慢速套利者：对收益可预测性的影响

作者:Zhongjin Lu,Steven G. Malliaris,Zhongling Qin

出处:-

Abstract : We present a model to explain puzzling patterns surrounding cross-sectional night-minus-day return predictabilities. Heterogeneous (“fast” and “slow”) arbitrageurs with offsetting advantages endogenously become the marginal investor at different times of day. Consistent with our model, we find that predictable night-minus-day returns are associated with persistent (rather than mean-reverting) order imbalances and with price deviations at open (rather than at close). Larger expected order imbalances lead to larger night-minus-day returns, except among stocks traded overseas before the U.S. market open. Fast arbitrageurs' capital constraints predict night-minus-day returns but not close-to-close returns, while the opposite is true for slow arbitrageurs' constraints.

Keywords : Fast and slow arbitrageurs, return predictability, night-minus-day returns, endogenous limited participation, tug of war

\[8\] Do Firms Benefit from Carbon Risk Management? Evidence from the Credit Default Swaps Market

标题:企业是否从碳风险管理中获益？来自信用违约掉期市场的证据

作者:Huu Nhan Duong,Petko S. Kalev,Madhu Kalimipalli,Saurabh Trivedi

出处:-

Abstract : We examine the effect of firms’ carbon risk management practices on their credit risk. Using difference-in-differences analysis based on two quasi exogenous events involving the 2015 Paris Climate Agreement and the staggered implementation of US state climate adaptation plans, we find that better carbon risk management is associated with significantly lower credit default swap (CDS) spreads. The influence of carbon risk management performance on CDS spreads is not driven by governance factors, firm-level climate change exposures, leverage, or distress risks Firms with better carbon risk management also have enhanced future growth opportunities and cash holdings. Overall, our paper highlights the importance of carbon risk management in mitigating credit risk.

Keywords : Carbon risk management; Credit risk; Credit default swaps; The Paris climate agreement; US state climate adaptation plans.

\[9\] Recovering Missing Firm Characteristics with Attention-Based Machine Learning

标题:利用基于注意的机器学习恢复缺失的企业特征

作者:Heiner Beckmeyer,Timo Wiedemann

出处:-

Abstract : Firm characteristics are often missing. We set up an attention-based machine learning model borrowing ideas from state-of-the-art research in natural language processing to understand how characteristics relate to the cross-section of other – observed -- firm characteristics and their historical evolution. Our model reconstructs firm characteristics with high accuracy and comfortably outperforms competing approaches. Revisiting the vast literature on risk factors in financial research reveals that disregarding the influence of missing observations likely overestimates the magnitude of factor premia. We also provide the filled distribution and raw values for all characteristics for future research.

Keywords : Machine Learning, Matrix Completion, Missing Data, Attention, Big Data

\[10\] Are We Becoming Greener? Life-time Experiences and Responsible Investment

标题:我们正在变得更环保吗？终身经历和负责任的投资

作者:Milo Bianchi,Gang Wang,Zhengkai Liu

出处:-

Abstract : We study the determinants and the patterns of ESG investing by exploiting account-level data from the Shanghai Stock Exchange, which provide detailed information on individual investors’ characteristics and trading over time. We show that investors’ pro-social attitudes affect ESG demand and that these attitudes are shaped by economic and non-economic life-time experiences, such as growing up in a region with more pro-social values, being exposed to an increased level of pollution or to a natural disaster. Recent experiences tend to matter more, and non-economic experiences are particularly important to explain how investors change their attitudes during their trading life. We also show that investors display distinct trading patterns between their ESG and non-ESG stocks, which helps explaining stock market dynamics.

Keywords : Responsible investment, experience effects, pro-social attitudes, ESG trading.

\[12\] What is the Value of Being American?

标题:作为美国人的价值是什么？

作者:Wenbin Cao,Pradeep K. Yadav

出处:-

Abstract : The S&P 100 index trades both European and American options. We exploit this relatively unique feature to empirically investigate the early exercise premium (“EEP”) in American put option prices. We provide direct evidence that the American style exercise creates a transaction cost saving option, which generates a significant EEP by incentivizing and rewarding early exercise of American options that would alternatively have been “sold” in the market. After duly accounting for the market implied value of the wild card option, we find that, while jump models fit observed EEP better than other model types, even jump models consistently and significantly underprice observed EEP. We empirically estimate the EEP that arises from this transaction cost savings option, and find that it is a significant determinant of virtually all the observed overpricing of market EEPs relative to their model-based values. We further use a natural experiment – the exogenous variation of transaction costs during the Bear Stearns bailout – to show that the transaction cost saving option is the principal reason why market EEPs are larger than model-predicted EEPs and wild card premia combined.

Keywords : Early exercise premium, transaction costs, jump models

\[13\] Voluntary Disclosure of Workforce Gender Diversity

标题:自愿披露劳动力性别多样性

作者:Chuchu Liang,Ben Lourie,Alexander Nekrasov,Il Sun Yoo

出处:-

Abstract : We examine the managerial incentives to voluntarily disclose the gender diversity of a firm’s workforce. When examining voluntary disclosures, one key challenge is the difficulty of observing the underlying performance for nondisclosers. We overcome this challenge by using novel data extracted from employees’ online profiles to gauge the underlying gender diversity. After controlling for industry gender diversity, we find that firms are more likely to disclose when they are more gender diverse. This finding is consistent with managerial incentives to disclose favorable information. However, disclosure is more prevalent in industries with low gender diversity, which suggests that investor demand for disclosure is higher when firms operate in a poor gender diversity environment. Firms disclose their gender diversity when (a) their gender diversity is expected to improve, (b) the firm has more female managers, (c) institutional ownership is high, and (d) proprietary costs are low. Regarding the potential benefits firms may receive from disclosure, we find that the disclosure of gender diversity is associated with (a) increases in diversity CSR ratings, (b) improvements in the tone in media coverage of the firm’s diversity, and (c) increases in the number of CSR-oriented funds investing in the firm’s stock. Overall, our study broadens our understanding of the evolving corporate disclosure landscape by providing evidence on firms’ incentives to disclose the gender diversity of their workforce.

Keywords : voluntary disclosure; workforce gender diversity; corporate social responsibility (CSR), environmental, social, and governance (ESG); CSR ratings; CSR media tone; ESG funds

\[15\] Presidential Address: Corporate Finance and Reality

标题:总统演讲：企业融资与现实

作者:John R. Graham

出处:-

Abstract : This paper uses CFO surveys to compare corporate planning and decisions in 2022 with those from two decades prior, which allows me to identify decision-making themes that are common across policies and through time. These common elements of real-world corporate finance indicate that companies make decisions based on internal forecasts that are miscalibrated and thought to be reliable only two years out; use decision rules that are conservative, sticky, simple, and that attempt to market time; and, emphasize corporate objectives that increasingly focus on stakeholders and revenues. These themes can be used to guide and discipline academic models and tests, with the aim of better explaining outcomes. A model of satisficing decision-making aligns with many of these practice-of-finance characteristics: optimization is difficult in a complex fast-changing world, so managers use simple rules to make incremental improvements and they stick with rules that have worked well enough in the past. Non-behavioral models with costly biases can also account for some of the themes. \[16\] Stock Market in the Age of Covid19:Mere Acclimatization or Stockholm Syndrome?

标题:Covid19时代的股市：仅仅适应还是斯德哥尔摩综合征？

作者:Sarfaraz Ali Shah SYED

出处:JEA-D-21-00221

Abstract : This study investigates the behavior of stock prices to the variations in COVID19 infection rate over time. To study the inter-temporal impact of the pandemic on four major stock indexes, we apply factor model, and disaggregate the sample period in three COVID19 waves. This study brings interesting evidence on the so-called immune behavior of stock indexes. While no signs of stock market immunity to the disease were found, the opportunities created by the pandemic would help new winners and thus causing a shift in sectoral gains. We also distinguish the surges from plunges in the COVID19 infections to see the behavior of stock indexes towards different scenarios during the pandemic. While the conventional wisdom may lead to an overall pessimistic scenario, we find that diversity and speedy adjustment based on new business models, resulted in sizable inconsistencies and asymmetries in the response of stock indexes to the pandemic.

Keywords : The U.S. Stock Markets, Asymmetric Inconsistencies, COVID19, Pandemic, COVID19 Waves.
