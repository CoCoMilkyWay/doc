\[1\] Intergenerational transmission of culture among immigrants

标题 : 移民文化的代际传递

作者 : Hamid NoghaniBehambari, Nahid Tavassoli, Farzaneh Noghani , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05364

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper illustrates the intergenerational transmission of the gender gap in education among first and second generation immigrants. Using the Current Population Survey (1994 2018), we find that the difference in female male education persists from the home country to the new environment. A one standard deviation increase of the ancestral country female male difference in schooling is associated with 17.2 and 2.5 of a standard deviation increase in the gender gap among first and second generations, respectively. Since gender perspective in education uncovers a new channel for cultural transmission among families, we interpret the findings as evidence of cultural persistence among first generations and partial cultural assimilation of second generations. Moreover, Disaggregation into country groups reveals different paths for this transmission descendants of immigrants of lower income countries show fewer attachments to the gender opinions of their home country. Average local education of natives can facilitate the acculturation process. Immigrants residing in states with higher education reveal a lower tendency to follow their home country attitudes regarding the gender gap.

摘要 : 本文阐述了第一代和第二代移民教育性别差异的代际传递。利用当前的人口调查（1994-2018），我们发现，从母国到新环境，男女教育的差异依然存在。祖籍国男女受教育程度差异每增加一个标准差，第一代和第二代之间的性别差距分别增加17.2和2.5个标准差。由于教育中的性别观点为家庭间的文化传播提供了一个新的渠道，我们将这一发现解释为第一代人的文化持久性和第二代人的部分文化同化的证据。此外，将其分解为不同的国家组，可以发现这种传播的不同途径。低收入国家移民的后代对其母国的性别观点的依恋较少。当地人的平均教育可以促进文化适应过程。居住在受过高等教育的州的移民在性别差距问题上遵循母国态度的倾向较低。

\[2\] Scared into Action

标题 : 吓得行动起来

作者 : Mike Lindow, David DeFranza, Arul Mishra, Himanshu Mishra , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05365

说明 : Comments: 54 pages, 11 figures

Abstract : Differences in political ideology are increasingly appearing as an impediment to successful bipartisan communication from local leadership. For example, recent empirical findings have shown that conservatives are less likely to adhere to COVID 19 health directives. This behavior is in direct contradiction to past research which indicates that conservatives are more rule abiding, prefer to avoid loss, and are more prevention motivated than liberals. We reconcile this disconnect between recent empirical findings and past research by using insights gathered from press releases, millions of tweets, and mobility data capturing local movement in retail, grocery, workplace, parks, and transit domains during COVID 19 shelter in place orders. We find that conservatives adhere to health directives when they express more fear of the virus. In order to better understand this phenomenon, we analyze both official and citizen communications and find that press releases from local and federal government, along with the number of confirmed COVID 19 cases, lead to an increase in expressions of fear on Twitter.

摘要 : 政治意识形态的差异越来越成为地方领导层成功进行两党沟通的障碍。例如，最近的实证研究结果表明，保守派不太可能遵守covid19健康指令。这一行为与过去的研究直接矛盾，过去的研究表明保守派比自由派更遵守规则，更倾向于避免损失，更倾向于预防。我们利用从新闻稿、数百万条推特和流动性数据中收集的见解来调和最近的实证研究结果和过去的研究之间的这种脱节，这些数据捕捉了COVID 19就地安置订单期间零售、杂货店、工作场所、公园和运输领域的当地流动。我们发现，保守派在表达对病毒的更多恐惧时，会遵守卫生指令。为了更好地理解这一现象，我们分析了官方和公民的通信，发现来自地方和联邦政府的新闻稿，以及确诊的19例COVID病例的数量，导致Twitter上恐惧表达的增加。

\[3\] Dynamics of contentment

标题 : 满足的动力

作者 : Alexey A. Burluka , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05655

说明 : -

Abstract : A continuous variable changing between 0 and 1 is introduced to characterise contentment, or satisfaction with life, of an individual and an equation governing its evolution is postulated from analysis of several factors likely to affect the contentment. As contentment is strongly affected by material well being, a similar equation is formulated for wealth of an individual and from these two equations derived an evolution equation for the joint distribution of individuals wealth and contentment within a society. The equation so obtained is used to compute evolution of this joint distribution in a society with initially low variation of wealth and contentment over a long period time. As illustration of this model capabilities, effects of the wealth tax rate are simulated and it is shown that a higher taxation in the longer run may lead to a wealthier and more content society. It is also shown that lower rates of the wealth tax lead to pronounced stratification of the society in terms of both wealth and contentment and that there is no direct relationship between the average values of these two variables.

摘要 : 引入了一个在0和1之间变化的连续变量来表征一个人的满足感或对生活的满足感，并通过对可能影响满足感的几个因素的分析，提出了一个控制其演变的方程。由于满足感受到物质幸福感的强烈影响，人们对个人财富也提出了一个类似的方程，并从这两个方程中导出了个人财富和满足感在社会中共同分布的演化方程。这样得到的方程被用来计算这种联合分配在一个社会中的演变，这个社会最初的财富和满足感在很长一段时间内变化很小。为了说明这个模型的功能，我们模拟了财富税率的影响，结果表明，从长远来看，更高的税收可能会导致一个更富裕和更满足的社会。研究还表明，较低的财富税税率导致了社会在财富和满足感方面的明显分层，这两个变量的平均值之间没有直接关系。

\[4\] Should the government reward cooperation Insights from an agent based model of wealth redistribution

标题 : 政府是否应该从基于代理的财富再分配模型中奖励合作

作者 : Frank Schweitzer, Luca Verginer, Giacomo Vaccario , 类别 ：Physics and Society (physics.soc-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05580

说明 : -

Abstract : In our multi agent model agents generate wealth from repeated interactions for which a prisoner s dilemma payoff matrix is assumed. Their gains are taxed by a government at a rate alpha . The resulting budget is spent to cover administrative costs and to pay a bonus to cooperative agents, which can be identified correctly only with a probability p . Agents decide at each time step to choose either cooperation or defection based on different information. In the local scenario, they compare their potential gains from both strategies. In the global scenario, they compare the gains of the cooperative and defective subpopulations. We derive analytical expressions for the critical bonus needed to make cooperation as attractive as defection. We show that for the local scenario the government can establish only a medium level of cooperation, because the critical bonus increases with the level of cooperation. In the global scenario instead full cooperation can be achieved once the cold start problem is solved, because the critical bonus decreases with the level of cooperation. This allows to lower the tax rate, while maintaining high cooperation.

摘要 : 在我们的多代理模型中，代理从重复的交互中产生财富，对于这种交互，假设一个囚徒困境支付矩阵。他们的所得由政府按阿尔法税率征税。由此产生的预算用于支付管理费用，并向合作代理人支付奖金，只有概率p才能正确识别。代理在每个时间步根据不同的信息选择合作或叛逃。在本地场景中，他们比较两种策略的潜在收益。在全球范围内，他们比较了合作亚群体和缺陷亚群体的收益。我们推导了使合作像叛逃一样具有吸引力所需的关键红利的解析表达式。我们证明，对于局部情形，政府只能建立中等水平的合作，因为临界奖金随着合作水平的增加而增加。相反，在全局场景中，一旦冷启动问题得到解决，就可以实现充分的合作，因为关键奖金随着合作水平的降低而降低。这样可以降低税率，同时保持高度合作。

\[5\] Crisis Propagation in a Heterogeneous Self Reflexive DSGE Model

标题 : 异质自反DSGE模型中的危机传播

作者 : Federico Guglielmo Morelli, Michael Benzaquen, Jean-Philippe Bouchaud, Marco Tarzia , 类别 ：Statistical Mechanics (cond-mat.stat-mech)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05588

说明 : -

Abstract : We study a self reflexive DSGE model with heterogeneous households, aimed at characterising the impact of economic recessions on the different strata of the society. Our framework allows to analyse the combined effect of income inequalities and confidence feedback mediated by heterogeneous social networks. By varying the parameters of the model, we find different crisis typologies loss of confidence may propagate mostly within high income households, or mostly within low income households, with a rather sharp crossover between the two. We find that crises are more severe for segregated networks (where confidence feedback is essentially mediated between agents of the same social class), for which cascading contagion effects are stronger. For the same reason, larger income inequalities tend to reduce, in our model, the probability of global crises. Finally, we are able to reproduce a perhaps counter intuitive empirical finding in countries with higher Gini coefficients, the consumption of the lowest income households tends to drop less than that of the highest incomes in crisis times.

摘要 : 我们研究了一个具有异质家庭的自反DSGE模型，旨在描述经济衰退对社会不同阶层的影响。我们的框架允许分析收入不平等和异质社会网络介导的信心反馈的综合效应。通过改变模型的参数，我们发现不同的危机类型的信心丧失可能主要在高收入家庭中传播，或者主要在低收入家庭中传播，两者之间有一个相当尖锐的交叉。我们发现，对于隔离网络（信任反馈本质上是在同一社会阶层的代理人之间进行的），危机更为严重，连锁传染效应更强。基于同样的原因，在我们的模型中，更大的收入不平等倾向于降低全球危机的可能性。最后，我们能够重现一个可能与直觉相反的经验发现：在基尼系数较高的国家，危机时期，最低收入家庭的消费倾向于低于最高收入家庭的消费。

\[6\] A comparative study of scoring systems by simulations

标题 : 模拟评分系统的比较研究

作者 : László Csató , 类别 ：Other Statistics (stat.OT)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05744

说明 : Comments: 14 pages, 4 figures, 5 tables

Abstract : Scoring rules aggregate individual rankings by assigning some points to each position in each ranking such that the total sum of points provides the overall ranking of the alternatives. They are widely used in sports competitions consisting of multiple contests. We study the tradeoff between two risks in this setting (1) the threat of early clinch when the title has been clinched before the last contest(s) of the competition take place (2) the danger of winning the competition without finishing first in any contest. In particular, four historical points scoring systems of the Formula One World Championship are compared with the family of geometric scoring rules that have favourable axiomatic properties. The formers are found to be competitive or even better. The current scheme seems to be a reasonable compromise in optimising the above goals. Our results shed more light on the evolution of the Formula One points scoring systems and contribute to the issue of choosing the set of point values.

摘要 : 评分规则通过给每个排名中的每个位置分配一些分数来汇总各个排名，这样总的分数总和就提供了备选方案的总体排名。它们广泛应用于由多项比赛组成的体育比赛中。我们研究了在这种情况下两种风险之间的权衡：（1）在比赛的最后一场比赛进行之前，当冠军已经获得时，提前获得冠军的威胁；（2）在任何比赛中没有获得第一名而赢得比赛的危险。特别地，将一级方程式世界锦标赛的四个历史得分系统与具有有利公理性质的几何得分规则族进行了比较。前者被发现具有竞争力，甚至更好。目前的方案似乎是优化上述目标的合理折衷方案。我们的结果对一级方程式计分系统的演变有了更多的了解，并有助于选择一组分值的问题。
