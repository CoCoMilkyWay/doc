\[1\] The Effect of Client Appraisal on the Efficiency of Micro Finance Bank

标题 : 客户评价对小额信贷银行效率的影响

作者 : Esther Yusuf Enoch, Usman Abubakar Arabo, Abubakar Mahmud Digil , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.07679

说明 : Comments: 13 pages journal. arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:2105.10991

Abstract : One of the major problems confronting financial institutions most especially microfinance institutions is the increasing incidence of loan defaults and consequence loan losses which manifested in their financial performance with huge uncollectible loans and advances. This study assessed the effects of credit management on financial performance on microfinance institutions in Adamawa State, Nigeria. Specifically, we examine the effect of client appraisal on the efficiency of microfinance banks in Adamawa State. The methodology employed in this study is the survey method in which both primary and secondary sources were used in the collection of data. A multi stage sampling method was adopted in selecting a sample of 21 respondents from a total population of 52 credit officers. Questionnaires were used in the due collection of data from the respondents. Descriptive statistics (simple percentage) and inferential statistics (regression analysis) were used to analyze the data collected and in testing the hypotheses. The study showed that client appraisal has a positive effect on efficiency and productivity.

摘要 : 金融机构尤其是小额信贷机构面临的一个主要问题是，贷款违约和后果贷款损失的发生率越来越高，表现在它们的财务业绩上，有大量无法收回的贷款和垫款。本研究评估了信贷管理对尼日利亚阿达马瓦州小额信贷机构财务绩效的影响。具体而言，我们考察了客户评价对阿达马瓦州小额信贷银行效率的影响。本研究采用的方法是调查法，在收集数据时使用了主要和次要来源。采用多阶段抽样的方法，从52名信贷人员中抽取21名受访者。在适当收集受访者的数据时使用了问卷调查。描述性统计（简单百分比）和推断统计（回归分析）被用来分析收集的数据和检验假设。研究表明，客户评价对效率和生产率有积极影响。

\[3\] The Relationship between Foreign Direct Investment and Economic Growth

标题 : 外商直接投资与经济增长的关系

作者 : Orhan Gokmen , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.08144

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper examines the relationship between net FDI inflows and real GDP for Turkey from 1970 to 2019. Although conventional economic growth theories and most empirical research suggest that there is a bi directional positive effect between these macro variables, the results indicate that there is a uni directional significant short run positive effect of real GDP on net FDI inflows to Turkey by employing the Vector Error Correction Model, Granger Causality, Impulse Response Functions and Variance Decomposition. Also, there is no long run effect has been found. The findings recommend Turkish authorities optimally benefit from the potential positive effect of net incoming FDI on the real GDP by allocating it for the productive sectoral establishments while effectively maintaining the country s real economic growth to attract further FDI inflows.

摘要 : 本文考察了1970年至2019年土耳其FDI净流入与实际GDP之间的关系。尽管传统经济增长理论和大多数实证研究表明，这些宏观变量之间存在双向正效应，结果表明，通过向量误差修正模型、格兰杰因果关系、脉冲响应函数和方差分解，实际GDP对土耳其的FDI净流入具有单向显著的短期正效应。此外，还没有发现长期效应。调查结果表明，土耳其当局通过将净流入的外国直接投资分配给生产性部门机构，同时有效地保持该国的实际经济增长，以吸引更多的外国直接投资流入，从而从净流入的外国直接投资对实际国内生产总值的潜在积极影响中获得最佳利益。

\[4\] CeFi vs. DeFi Comparing Centralized to Decentralized Finance

标题 : CeFi与DeFi比较集中与分散金融

作者 : Kaihua Qin, Liyi Zhou, Yaroslav Afonin, Ludovico Lazzaretti, Arthur Gervais , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.08157

说明 : -

Abstract : To non experts, the traditional Centralized Finance (CeFi) ecosystem may seem obscure, because users are typically not aware of the underlying rules or agreements of financial assets and products. Decentralized Finance (DeFi), however, is making its debut as an ecosystem claiming to offer transparency and control, which are partially attributable to the underlying integrity protected blockchain, as well as currently higher financial asset yields than CeFi. Yet, the boundaries between CeFi and DeFi may not be always so clear cut. In this work, we systematically analyze the differences between CeFi and DeFi, covering legal, economic, security, privacy and market manipulation. We provide a structured methodology to differentiate between a CeFi and a DeFi service. Our findings show that certain DeFi assets (such as USDC or USDT stablecoins) do not necessarily classify as DeFi assets, and may endanger the economic security of intertwined DeFi protocols. We conclude this work with the exploration of possible synergies between CeFi and DeFi.

摘要 : 对于非专家来说，传统的集中金融（CeFi）生态系统似乎很模糊，因为用户通常不知道金融资产和产品的基本规则或协议。然而，去中心化金融（DeFi）作为一个生态系统首次亮相，声称能够提供透明度和控制，这部分归功于基础完整性保护区块链，以及目前比CeFi更高的金融资产收益率。然而，CeFi和DeFi之间的界限可能并不总是那么清晰。本文系统分析了CeFi与DeFi在法律、经济、安全、隐私和市场操纵等方面的差异。我们提供了一种结构化的方法来区分CeFi和DeFi服务。我们的研究结果表明，某些DeFi资产（如USDC或USDT stablecoins）不一定归类为DeFi资产，并且可能危及相互交织的DeFi协议的经济安全。最后，我们探讨了CeFi和DeFi之间可能的协同作用。

\[5\] Young people between education and the labour market during the COVID 19 pandemic in Italy

标题 : 意大利COVID 19大流行期间，年轻人处于教育和劳动力市场之间

作者 : Davide Fiaschi, Cristina Tealdi , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.08296

说明 : Comments: 16 pages, 4 figures, 3 tables

Abstract : We analyse the distribution and the flows between different types of employment (self employment, temporary, and permanent), unemployment, education, and other types of inactivity, with particular focus on the duration of the school to work transition (STWT). The aim is to assess the impact of the COVID 19 pandemic in Italy on the careers of individuals aged 15 34. We find that the pandemic worsened an already concerning situation of higher unemployment and inactivity rates and significantly longer STWT duration compared to other EU countries, particularly for females and residents in the South of Italy. In the midst of the pandemic, individuals aged 20 29 were less in (permanent and temporary) employment and more in the NLFET (Neither in the Labour Force nor in Education or Training) state, particularly females and non Italian citizens. We also provide evidence of an increased propensity to return to schooling, but most importantly of a substantial prolongation of the STWT duration towards permanent employment, mostly for males and non Italian citizens. Our contribution lies in providing a rigorous estimation and analysis of the impact of COVID 19 on the carriers of young individuals in Italy, which has not yet been explored in the literature.

摘要 : 我们分析了不同类型的就业（自营职业、临时性和永久性）、失业、教育和其他类型的不活动之间的分布和流动，特别关注从学校到工作的过渡期（STWT）。目的是评估意大利COVID 19大流行对15-34岁个人职业生涯的影响。我们发现，与其他欧盟国家相比，该大流行恶化了已经令人担忧的失业率和不活动率更高的情况，并且显著延长了STWT持续时间，尤其是意大利南部的女性和居民。在这场流行病期间，20至29岁的人较少（长期和临时）就业，而更多地在NLFET（既不在劳动力队伍中，也不在教育或培训中），特别是女性和非意大利公民。我们还提供了证据表明，重返学校的倾向有所增加，但最重要的是，STWT的持续时间大幅延长，主要针对男性和非意大利公民。我们的贡献在于对covid19对意大利年轻人携带者的影响进行了严格的评估和分析，这在文献中还没有探讨。

\[7\] The value of travel speed

标题 : 行驶速度值

作者 : Cornelis Dirk van Goeverden , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.06599

说明 : Comments: 14 pages, 3 figures

Abstract : Travel speed is an intrinsic feature of transport, and enlarging the speed is considered as beneficial. The benefit of a speed increase is generally assessed as the value of the saved travel time. However, this approach conflicts with the observation that time spent on travelling is rather constant and might not be affected by speed changes. The paper aims to define the benefits of a speed increase and addresses two research questions. First, how will a speed increase in person transport work out, which factors are affected Second, is the value of time a good proxy for the value of speed Based on studies on time spending and research on the association between speed and land use, we argue that human wealth could be the main affected factor by speed changes, rather than time or access. Then the value of time is not a good proxy for the value of speed the benefits of a wealth increase are negatively correlated with prosperity following the law of diminishing marginal utility, while the calculated benefits of saved travel time prove to be positively correlated. The inadequacy of the value of time is explained by some shortcomings with respect to the willingness to pay that is generally used for assessing the value of time people do not predict correctly the personal benefits that will be gained from a decision, and they neglect the social impacts.

摘要 : 旅行速度是运输的固有特征，提高旅行速度是有益的。提速的好处通常被评估为节省的行驶时间的价值。然而，这种方法与观察到的花费在旅行上的时间是相当恒定的，可能不受速度变化的影响相冲突。本文旨在界定提速的好处，并提出两个研究问题。首先，人们交通的速度增长是如何产生的，哪些因素会受到影响第二，时间价值是否是速度价值的一个很好的代表基于对时间花费的研究和对速度与土地利用之间关系的研究，我们认为人的财富可能是速度变化的主要影响因素，而不是时间或访问。那么，时间价值并不能很好地代表速度价值，财富增长的效益与繁荣呈负相关，遵循边际效用递减规律，而节省的旅行时间的计算效益则证明是正相关的。时间价值不足的原因是，人们通常用于评估时间价值的支付意愿存在一些缺陷，人们不能正确预测决策将带来的个人利益，忽视了社会影响。

\[8\] An Integration and Operation Framework of Geothermal Heat Pumps in Distribution Networks

标题 : 地热热泵在配电网中的集成与运行框架

作者 : Lei Liang, Xuan Zhang, Hongbin Sun , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.06665

说明 : -

Abstract : The application of the energy efficient thermal and energy management mechanism in Geothermal Heat Pumps (GHPs) is indispensable to reduce the overall energy consumption and carbon emission across the building sector. Besides, in the Virtual Power Plant (VPP) system, the demand response of clustered GHP systems can improve the operating flexibility of the power grid. This paper presents an integration and operation framework of GHPs in the distribution network, applying a layered communication and optimization method to coordinate multiple clustered GHPs in a community. In the proposed hierarchical operation scheme, the operator of regional GHPs collects the thermal zone information and the disturbance prediction of buildings in a short time granularity, predicts the energy demand, and transmits the information to an aggregator. Using a novel linearized optimal power flow model, the aggregator coordinates and aggregates load resources of GHP systems in the distribution network. In this way, GHP systems with thermal and energy management mechanisms can be applied to achieve the demand response in the VPP and offer more energy flexibility to the community.

摘要 : 在地热热泵系统中应用高效节能的热能和能量管理机制，对于降低整个建筑行业的能源消耗和碳排放是必不可少的。此外，在虚拟电厂（VPP）系统中，集群式GHP系统的需求响应可以提高电网的运行灵活性。提出了一种配电网GHPs的集成与运行框架，采用分层通信与优化的方法，协调小区内多个集群GHPs。在提出的分层运行方案中，区域温室气体处理站的操作员在较短的时间粒度内收集建筑物的热区信息和扰动预测，预测能源需求，并将信息传输给聚合器。采用一种新的线性化最优潮流模型，对配电网中GHP系统的负荷资源进行协调和聚合。这样，具有热能和能量管理机制的GHP系统可用于实现VPP中的需求响应，并为社区提供更大的能源灵活性。

\[9\] Quantum Portfolio Optimization with Investment Bands and Target Volatility

标题 : 带投资区间和目标波动率的量子投资组合优化

作者 : Samuel Palmer, Serkan Sahin, Rodrigo Hernandez, Samuel Mugel, Roman Orus , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.06735

说明 : Comments: 5 pages, 6 figures

Abstract : In this paper we show how to implement in a simple way some complex real life constraints on the portfolio optimization problem, so that it becomes amenable to quantum optimization algorithms. Specifically, first we explain how to obtain the best investment portfolio with a given target risk. This is important in order to produce portfolios with different risk profiles, as typically offered by financial institutions. Second, we show how to implement individual investment bands, i.e., minimum and maximum possible investments for each asset. This is also important in order to impose diversification and avoid corner solutions. Quite remarkably, we show how to build the constrained cost function as a quadratic binary optimization (QUBO) problem, this being the natural input of quantum annealers. The validity of our implementation is proven by finding the efficient frontier, using D Wave Hybrid and its Advantage quantum processor, on static portfolios taking assets from the S&P500. We use three different subsets of this index. First, the S&P100 which consists of 100 of the largest companies of the S&P500 second, the 200 best performing companies of the S&P500 and third, the full S&P500 itself. Abstract : Strategies adopted globally to mitigate the threat of COVID 19 have primarily involved lockdown measures with substantial economic and social costs with varying degrees of success. Morbidity patterns of COVID 19 variants have a strong association with age, while restrictive lockdown measures have association with negative mental health outcomes in some age groups. Reduced economic prospects may also afflict some age cohorts more than others. Motivated by this, we propose a model to describe COVID 19 community spread incorporating the role of age specific social interactions. Through a flexible parameterisation of an age structured deterministic Susceptible Exposed Infectious Removed (SEIR) model, we provide a means for characterising different forms of lockdown which may impact specific age groups differently. Social interactions are represented through age group to age group contact matrices, which can be trained using available data and are thus locally adapted. This framework is easy to interpret and suitable for describing counterfactual scenarios, which could assist policy makers with regard to minimising morbidity balanced with the costs of prospective suppression strategies. Our work originates from an Irish context and we use disease monitoring data from February 29th 2020 to January 31st 2021 gathered by Irish governmental agencies. We demonstrate how Irish lockdown scenarios can be constructed using the proposed model formulation and show results of retrospective fitting to incidence rates and forward planning with relevant what if instead of lockdown counterfactuals with uncertainty quantification. Our formulation is agnostic to a specific locale, in that lockdown strategies in other regions can be straightforwardly encoded using this model. The methods we describe are made publicly available online through an accessible and easy to use web interface.

摘要 : 全球为减轻COVID 19的威胁而采取的战略主要涉及封锁措施，这些措施带来了巨大的经济和社会代价，取得了不同程度的成功。covid19变异的发病模式与年龄密切相关，而限制性锁定措施与某些年龄组的负面心理健康结果相关。经济前景的恶化也可能对某些年龄段的人造成更大的影响。基于此，我们提出了一个模型来描述covid19的社区传播，其中包含了特定年龄的社会互动。通过一个年龄结构的确定性（SEIR）模型的灵活参数化，我们提供了一种描述不同形式的锁定的方法，这种锁定可能对特定年龄组产生不同的影响。社会互动通过年龄组与年龄组之间的接触矩阵来表示，这些矩阵可以使用可用的数据进行训练，因此是局部适应的。该框架易于解释，适合描述反事实情景，有助于决策者将发病率降至最低，并与预期抑制策略的成本相平衡。我们的工作来源于爱尔兰，我们使用了爱尔兰政府机构收集的2020年2月29日至2021年1月31日的疾病监测数据。我们展示了如何使用提出的模型公式构建爱尔兰封锁情景，并展示了对发病率进行回顾性拟合的结果，以及使用相关假设进行前瞻性规划，而不是使用不确定性量化的封锁反事实。我们的公式对一个特定的地区是不可知的，因为其他地区的封锁策略可以直接用这个模型编码。我们描述的方法是通过一个可访问且易于使用的web界面在网上公开的。
