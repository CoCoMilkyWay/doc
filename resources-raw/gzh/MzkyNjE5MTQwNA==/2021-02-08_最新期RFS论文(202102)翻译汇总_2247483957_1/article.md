\[1\] Performance-Induced CEO Turnover

标题:绩效导致的CEO离职

作者:Dirk Jenter, Katharina Lewellen

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/2/569/5857618; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa069

Abstract : This paper revisits the relationship between firm performance and CEO turnover. Instead of classifying turnovers into forced and voluntary, we introduce performance-induced turnover, defined as turnover that would not have occurred had performance been “good.” We document a close turnover-performance link and estimate that 38%–55% of turnovers are performance induced. This is significantly more than the number of forced turnovers, though the two types of turnovers are highly correlated. Compared to the predictions of Bayesian learning models, learning about CEO ability appears to be slow, and boards act as if CEO ability (or match quality) was subject to frequent shocks.

Abstract:本文重新探讨了企业绩效与CEO离职之间的关系。我们没有将离职分为强制离职和自愿离职，而是引入了绩效诱导离职，定义为如果绩效“良好”就不会发生的离职。我们记录了一个紧密的离职-绩效联系，估计38%-55%的离职是绩效诱导的。尽管这两种类型的失误高度相关，但这明显多于强迫失误的数量。与贝叶斯学习模型的预测相比，对CEO能力的学习似乎很慢，董事会的行为就好像CEO能力（或匹配质量）经常受到冲击一样。

\[3\] Weak Governance by Informed Active Shareholders

标题:知情积极股东治理不力

作者:Eitan Goldman, Wenyu Wang

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/2/661/5828945; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa052

Abstract : Do informed shareholders who can influence corporate decisions improve governance? We demonstrate this may not be generally true in a model of takeovers. The model suggests that a shareholder’s ability to collect information and trade ex post may cause him, ex ante, to support pursuing value-destroying takeovers or oppose value-enhancing takeovers. Surprisingly, we find conditions under which giving the active shareholder greater influence weakens governance and reduces firm value, even if such influence power can be used to reject bad takeovers ex post. Our model sheds light on the limitations of relying on informed, active shareholders to improve governance.

Abstract:能够影响公司决策的知情股东是否改善了公司治理？我们证明，在一个收购模型中，这种情况可能并不普遍。该模型表明，股东收集信息和事后交易的能力可能会导致他事前支持追求破坏价值的收购或反对提升价值的收购。令人惊讶的是，我们发现在何种条件下给予积极股东更大的影响力会削弱治理，降低公司价值，即使这种影响力可以用来事后拒绝不良收购。我们的模型揭示了依靠知情、积极的股东来改善治理的局限性。

\[5\] Do Minorities Pay More for Mortgages?

标题:少数民族付更多的抵押贷款吗？

作者:Neil Bhutta, Aurel Hizmo

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/2/763/5827007; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa047

Abstract : We test for racial discrimination in the prices charged by mortgage lenders. We construct a unique data set from which we observe the three dimensions of a mortgage’s price: the interest rate, discount points, and fees. Although we find statistically significant gaps by race and ethnicity in interest rates, these gaps are offset by differences in discount points. We trace out point-rate schedules and show that minorities and whites face identical schedules, but sort to different locations on the schedule. Such sorting may reflect systematic differences in liquidity or preferences. Finally, we find no differences in total fees by race or ethnicity.

Abstract:我们对抵押贷款机构收取的价格进行种族歧视测试。我们构造了一个独特的数据集，从中我们可以观察到抵押贷款价格的三个维度：利率、折扣点和费用。尽管我们发现在利率方面，种族和种族的差异在统计学上是显著的，但这些差异被贴现点的差异所抵消。我们追溯了点税率表，并显示少数民族和白人面临相同的时间表，但排序到不同的地点的时间表。这种分类可能反映了流动性或偏好的系统性差异。最后，我们发现按种族或民族划分的总费用没有差异。

\[6\] Does Borrower and Broker Race Affect the Cost of Mortgage Credit?

标题:借款人和经纪人的竞争会影响抵押贷款的成本吗？

作者:Brent W Ambrose, James N Conklin, Luis A Lopez

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/2/790/5885095; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa087

Abstract : We test for pricing disparities in mortgage contracts using a novel data set that allows us to observe the race and ethnicity of both parties to the loan. We find that minorities pay between 3% and 5% more in fees than similarly qualified whites when obtaining a loan through the same white broker. Critically, we find that the premium paid by minorities depends on the race of the broker. We also examine recent policy changes around broker compensation rules that may not only reduce these price disparities but may also limit access to credit for minorities.

Abstract:我们使用一个新的数据集测试抵押合同中的价格差异，这个数据集允许我们观察贷款双方的种族和民族。我们发现，当通过同一个白人经纪人获得贷款时，少数族裔比同样合格的白人多支付3%到5%的费用。关键的是，我们发现少数族裔支付的溢价取决于经纪人的种族。我们还研究了最近围绕经纪人薪酬规则的政策变化，这些规则不仅可能减少这些价格差异，而且可能限制少数族裔获得信贷的机会。

\[9\] The Babies of Mortgage Market Deregulation

标题:房贷市场放松管制的雏形

作者:Isaac Hacamo

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/2/907/5864039; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa073

Abstract : This paper documents that mortgage market deregulation helps mitigate the risk of population aging by affecting a foundational family-level decision: the choice to have children. Using a U.S. federal regulator ruling, I show that young households fully exposed to mortgage market deregulation increase their probability of purchasing a home and having a child by 6 percentage points. \[10\] Does Household Finance Affect the Political Process? Evidence from Voter Turnout During a Housing Crisis

标题:家庭财政是否影响政治进程？住房危机期间选民投票率的证据

作者:W Ben McCartney

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/2/949/5848015; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa064

Abstract : I examine the effect of house price declines on voter participation using a novel person-level panel data set. Contrary to what the “angry voter hypothesis” predicts, I find that a 10% decline in local house prices decreases the participation rate of the average mortgaged homeowner by 1.6 percentage points. Consistent with a financial distress channel, house price declines have no effects on renters and particularly severe effects on highly leveraged households. My findings are consistent with the existence of a feedback loop between financial distress and inequality operating through voter participation.

Abstract:我使用一个新的个人层面的面板数据集研究了房价下跌对选民参与的影响。与“愤怒的选民假说”预测的相反，我发现当地房价下降10%会使平均抵押房主的参与率降低1.6个百分点。与金融危机通道一致，房价下跌对租房者没有影响，对高负债家庭的影响尤其严重。我的研究结果与经济困境与不平等之间存在的反馈回路是一致的。

\[11\] How Important Are Inflation Expectations for the Nominal Yield Curve?

标题:通胀预期对名义收益率曲线有多重要？

作者:Roberto Gomez-Cram, Amir Yaron

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/2/985/5813734; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa039

Abstract : Macrofinance term structure models rely too heavily on the volatility of expected inflation news as a source for variations in nominal bond yield shocks. We develop and estimate a model featuring inflation nonneutrality and preference shocks. The stochastic volatility of inflation and consumption govern bond risk premiums movements, whereas preference shocks generate fluctuations in real rates. The model accounts for key bond market features without resorting to an overly dominating expected inflation channel. The estimation shows that preference shocks are strongly negatively correlated with market distress factors and that real rate news is the dominant driver of nominal yield shocks.

Abstract:宏观金融期限结构模型过于依赖预期通胀消息的波动性，将其作为名义债券收益率冲击变化的来源。我们建立并估计了一个通货膨胀非中性和偏好冲击的模型。通货膨胀和消费的随机波动控制着债券风险溢价的变动，而偏好冲击则产生实际利率的波动。该模型解释了债券市场的主要特征，而没有诉诸于过度主导的预期通胀渠道。估计结果表明，偏好冲击与市场困境因子呈显著负相关，实际利率消息是名义收益率冲击的主要驱动力。

\[12\] Bond Risk Premiums with Machine Learning

标题:基于机器学习的债券风险溢价

作者:Daniele Bianchi, Matthias Büchner, Andrea Tamoni

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/2/1046/5843806; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa062

Abstract : We show that machine learning methods, in particular, extreme trees and neural networks (NNs), provide strong statistical evidence in favor of bond return predictability. NN forecasts based on macroeconomic and yield information translate into economic gains that are larger than those obtained using yields alone. Interestingly, the nature of unspanned factors changes along the yield curve: stock- and labor-market-related variables are more relevant for short-term maturities, whereas output and income variables matter more for longer maturities. Finally, NN forecasts correlate with proxies for time-varying risk aversion and uncertainty, lending support to models featuring both channels.

Abstract:我们发现，机器学习方法，特别是极端树和神经网络（NNs），提供了强有力的统计证据，有利于债券收益率的可预测性。基于宏观经济和收益率信息的神经网络预测转化为经济收益，大于仅使用收益率获得的收益。有趣的是，非计划因素的性质沿着收益率曲线变化：股票和劳动力市场相关变量与短期到期更为相关，而产出和收入变量对长期到期更为重要。最后，神经网络预测与时变风险规避和不确定性的代理相关，为两种渠道的模型提供了支持。
