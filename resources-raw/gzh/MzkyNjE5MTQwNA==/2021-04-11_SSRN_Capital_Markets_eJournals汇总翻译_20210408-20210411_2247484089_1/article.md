\[1\] Timing, Recurrence, and Effects of Fixed Assets Revaluation: Evidence from Bangladesh

标题:固定资产重估的时机、重复性和影响：来自孟加拉国的证据

作者:Md. Tahidur Rahman,Syed Zabid Hossain,Md. Anwarul Haque

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3822822

Abstract : This study intended to explore when and how often fixed asset revaluation (FAR) is practiced in Bangladesh and explain the impacts of FAR on net asset value (NAV), stock prices, and debts of companies. Based on 175 listed companies on the Dhaka Stock Exchange, this study found increasing use of the revaluation model where the textile industry witnessed the highest number of revaluations. Most of the listed companies were irregular revaluer, and they performed FAR during the bull market in 2010. Most newly listed companies did it just before their initial public offerings (IPO). The results of the Wilcoxon signed-rank test imply that the changes in NAV, stock prices, and total debts after revaluation were statistically significant. This study found evidence of enhancing debt capacity and stock prices of several companies through improved NAV. The findings will assist regulators to recognize the consequence of revaluation and enable them to take an appropriate stance for controlling abusive and creative reporting. The study will also make investors cautious about companies with revaluation induced assets. This study suggests that companies practicing the revaluation model should perform FAR on regular intervals to reduce information asymmetry about assets’ value and thus help improve investors’ confidence.

Keywords : Fair Value Accounting, IAS 16, PP&E, Bull-bear Market, Information Asymmetry, Net Asset Value

Abstract :本研究旨在探讨孟加拉何时及多久进行一次固定资产重估，并解释固定资产重估对公司资产净值、股价及债务的影响。本研究以达卡证交所175家上市公司为研究对象，发现纺织业重估次数最多的公司越来越多地采用重估模型。大多数上市公司都是不定期的重估价，在2010年的牛市中表现很好。大多数新上市公司都是在首次公开发行（IPO）之前这样做的。Wilcoxon符号秩检验的结果表明，资产净值、股价和债务总额在重估后的变化具有统计学意义。这项研究发现，证据表明，提高债务能力和股票价格的几家公司通过提高资产净值。这些发现将有助于监管机构认识到重估的后果，并使他们能够采取适当的立场来控制滥用和创造性的报告。这项研究还将使投资者对资产重估导致的公司持谨慎态度。本研究认为，采用重估模式的公司，应在固定的时间间隔内表现良好，以减少有关资产价值的信息不对称，从而有助于提高投资者的信心。

Keywords :公允价值会计，IAS 16，PP&E，牛市熊市，信息不对称，资产净值

\[2\] Volatility Information Transfer along the Supply Chain

标题:供应链中的波动信息传递

作者:Wenli Huang,Gang Li,Shaojun Zhang

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3822362

Abstract : We investigate whether and how information about one stock’s future volatility is transferred to other related stocks along the supply chain. The supply chain setting offers an ideal setting to study the effect of cross-firm volatility information transfer because customers and suppliers are closely related. Earnings announcements can have an impact on the announcing firm's short-term, long-term, and forward expected volatility. We find that, on average, the announcing firms’ short-term expected volatility decreases substantially after earnings announcements, while the change of their forward expected volatility is close to zero. Regression analysis shows that the change in the announcing firm’s short-term (forward) volatility has a significant effect on the change in its supply chain partner’s short-term (forward) volatility. The spillover effect is economically meaningful and becomes stronger if customers and suppliers are more closely related. Our results yield new insights on the transfer of volatility related information among firms.

Keywords : Customer-supplier relationship, Model free implied volatility, Quarterly earnings announcement, Supply chain, Volatility information transfer

Abstract :我们研究一只股票的未来波动信息是否以及如何在供应链上传递给其他相关股票。供应链环境是研究跨企业波动性信息传递效应的理想环境，因为顾客和供应商是密切相关的。盈利公告会对公告公司的短期、长期和远期预期波动性产生影响。我们发现，平均而言，公告公司的短期预期波动率在盈利公告后显著下降，而其远期预期波动率的变化接近于零。回归分析表明，公告企业的短期（远期）波动性变化对其供应链合作伙伴的短期（远期）波动性变化有显著影响。溢出效应在经济上是有意义的，如果顾客和供应商的关系更密切，溢出效应就会更强。我们的研究结果为企业间波动相关信息的传递提供了新的视角。

Keywords :客户-供应商关系，无模型隐含波动率，季度收益公告，供应链，波动率信息传递

\[3\] "I Just Like the Stock" versus "Fear and Loathing on Main Street" : The Role of Reddit Sentiment in the GameStop Short Squeeze

标题:“我就是喜欢股票”与“大街上的恐惧和厌恶”：Reddit情绪在GameStop空头挤压中的作用

作者:Cheng Long,Brian M. Lucey,Larisa Yarovaya

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3822315

Abstract : This paper investigates the role, if any, played by the social media platform Reddit, in the events around the GameStop short squeeze in early 2021. In particular, we analyse the impact of discussions on the r/WallStreetBets subreddit on the price dynamics of the American online retailer GameStop. We perform textual analysis on 10.8m comments and surface the relationships between the comment sentiments and 1-min GameStop returns. Results indicate that both \\textit{tone} and number of comments influence GME intraday returns. Sentiments extracted from longer threads have a greater influence. Fear is the dominant sentiment in all comments, while comments that express a Sad sentiment show the most significant impact. While investors may just like the stock, it appears that fear and loathing also are important.

Keywords : Gamestop, Reddit, Robinhood, media sentiments, short squeeze, Herding, textual analysis

Abstract :本文调查了社交媒体平台Reddit在2021年初GameStop卖空事件中所扮演的角色（如果有的话）。特别是，我们分析了讨论对美国在线零售商GameStop价格动态的影响。我们对1080万条评论进行了文本分析，揭示了评论情绪与1分钟GameStop返回之间的关系。结果表明，评论数和评论数都会影响GME的日内收益率。从长线中提取的情感影响更大。恐惧是所有评论中的主导情绪，而表达悲伤情绪的评论则显示出最显著的影响。虽然投资者可能喜欢这只股票，但恐惧和厌恶似乎也很重要。

Keywords :Gamestop，Reddit，Robinhood，媒体情绪，短期挤压，放牧，文本分析

\[4\] Financial Intermediaries and Agency Problems With and Without Vertical Incentives

标题:有或没有纵向激励的金融中介和代理问题

作者:Yeonjoon Lee

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3821905

Abstract : I study agency problems between brokers and investors in the retail bond market. Thepresence of suitability or fiduciary regulations, which govern transactions in this setting, canlead to tensions for brokers between maximizing their own profit and acting in the best interestsof their customers. This paper measures the degree to which these conflicting incentivesinfluence brokers by examining the U.S. reverse convertible bond market. While brokers doconsider the profits of investors when making decisions, I estimate that their own profits areroughly three times more important, suggesting the existence of severe agency problems thatreduce consumer welfare.

Keywords : agency problems; vertical incentives; brokers; intermediaries; consumer finance; structured products

Abstract :我研究散户债券市场中经纪人和投资者之间的代理问题。在这种情况下，监管交易的适宜性或信托法规的存在，可能会导致经纪人在实现自身利润最大化和为客户的最佳利益行事之间产生紧张关系。本文通过考察美国反向可转换债券市场来衡量这些相互冲突的激励因素对券商的影响程度。虽然经纪人在做决定时会考虑投资者的利润，但我估计，他们自己的利润大约是投资者利润的三倍，这表明存在严重的代理问题，降低了消费者福利。

Keywords :代理问题；纵向激励；经纪人；中介机构；消费金融；结构性产品

\[5\] How Competitive is the Stock Market? Theory, Evidence from Portfolios, and Implications for the Rise of Passive Investing

标题:股市的竞争力如何？理论，来自投资组合的证据，以及对被动投资兴起的影响

作者:Valentin Haddad,Paul Huebner,Erik Loualiche

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3821263

Abstract : We develop a framework to theoretically and empirically analyze investor competition on financial markets. The classic view assumes that markets are very competitive: if a group of investors changes its behavior, other investors react such that nothing happens in equilibrium. Our framework quantifies the strength of the competitive response. We estimate a demand system of institutional investors in the US stock market accounting for two layers of equilibrium: how investors compete with each other in setting their strategies and how prices adjust to clear asset markets. We find that investors react to the behavior of others in the market: when an investor is surrounded by less aggressive traders she trades more aggressively. This reaction reduces the equilibrium consequences of changes in individual behavior by 50%. However, it also implies that the stock market is far from the competitive ideal. A consequence of this result is that the large increase in passive investing over the last 20 years has led to substantially more inelastic aggregate demand curves for individual stocks, by 15%.

Keywords : Asset pricing model, Demand system, Institutional investors, Liquidity, Information, Portfolio choice

Abstract :我们建立了一个理论和实证分析金融市场上投资者竞争的框架。经典观点认为市场是非常有竞争力的：如果一组投资者改变了行为，其他投资者的反应就是没有任何事情发生在均衡状态。我们的框架量化了竞争反应的强度。我们估计，美国股市的机构投资者需求体系包含两层均衡：投资者在制定策略时如何相互竞争，以及价格如何调整以清理资产市场。我们发现，投资者对市场上其他人的行为作出反应：当投资者被攻击性较低的交易员包围时，她交易更为积极。这种反应使个体行为改变的平衡结果减少了50%。然而，这也意味着股市离竞争的理想还很远。这一结果的一个后果是，过去20年被动投资的大幅增长导致了个股总需求曲线的非弹性大幅增加，增幅达15%。

Keywords :资产定价模型，需求系统，机构投资者，流动性，信息，投资组合选择

\[6\] Overlapping Narrative Risk Disclosures and Return Predictability

标题:重复叙述风险披露和回报可预测性

作者:John (Jianqiu) Bai,Priya Garg,Chi Wan

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3821163

Abstract : We use unsupervised machine learning techniques of natural language processing to measure the semantic relatedness of firms’ narrative risk disclosures (NRD) in the “risk factor” section of their annual reports. We find strong return predictability among firms with overlapping NRD. This momentum effect is distinct from return predictability due to existing factors such as industry momentum, complexity of firm operations, and size-based information diffusion. A long-short strategy based on the NRD relatedness yields a monthly alpha of 70 basis points. Further analyses show that this lead-lag return effect is stronger among firms with lower investor attention and higher illiquidity. Our findings highlight important asset pricing implications of textual relatedness in NRD that investors seem to be inattentive towards.

Keywords : risk factor disclosure, stock returns, limited attention, unsupervised machine learning.

Abstract :我们使用自然语言处理的无监督机器学习技术来衡量企业年度报告中“风险因素”部分的叙述性风险披露（NRD）的语义相关性。我们发现有重叠NRD的公司有很强的回报可预测性。这种动量效应不同于收益可预测性，因为存在诸如行业动量、企业运营的复杂性和基于规模的信息扩散等因素。基于NRD相关性的多空策略产生每月70个基点的阿尔法。进一步的分析表明，在投资者关注度较低、流动性较差的公司中，这种超前-滞后回报效应更为明显。我们的研究结果突出了NRD中文本相关性的重要资产定价含义，投资者似乎对此不太注意。

Keywords :风险因素披露，股票收益，有限关注，无监督机器学习。

\[7\] Pricing Climate Change Risk in Corporate Bonds

标题:企业债券的气候变化风险定价

作者:Elsa Allman

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3821018

Abstract : Using a firm’s geographic footprint to measure its exposure to sea level rise (SLR), I find that corporate bonds bear a climate risk premium upon issuance. A one standard deviation increase in firms’ SLR exposure is associated with a 7 basis point premium, representing a 3% increase in average yield spread. This effect is more pronounced for geographically concentrated firms, within industries vulnerable to extreme weather conditions, and after the Paris Agreement. I do not find evidence that credit rating agencies account for SLR exposure at bond issuance. Results are robust to placebo tests and inverse propensity weighting to address possible endogeneity.

Keywords : Climate Risk, Corporate Bonds, Sea Level Rise

Abstract :利用一家公司的地理足迹来衡量其对海平面上升（SLR）的风险敞口，我发现公司债券在发行时承担着气候风险溢价。公司单反风险敞口的一个标准差增加与7个基点的溢价相关，代表平均收益率利差增加3%。这种影响对于地理位置集中的公司、易受极端天气影响的行业内以及巴黎协议之后更为明显。我没有找到证据表明信用评级机构在债券发行时会考虑单反风险敞口。结果是稳健的安慰剂测试和反向倾向加权，以解决可能的内生性。

Keywords :气候风险、公司债券、海平面上升
