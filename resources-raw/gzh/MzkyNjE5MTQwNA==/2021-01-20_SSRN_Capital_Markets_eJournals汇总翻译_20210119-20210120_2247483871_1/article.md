\[1\] A Sensitivity Analysis of the Alpha Factor

标题:α因子的敏感性分析

作者:Michael Einemann(Deutsche Bank AG),Michael Kalkbrener(Deutsche Bank AG - Risk Management)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3769143

Abstract : In the credit risk weighted assets formula for derivatives, the exposure-at-default is scaled by a multiplier: the so-called internal alpha factor. This has been introduced by the Basel Committee to offset the model or estimation error arising from the usage of a constant exposure amount and should account for the uncertainty of counterparty exposure, the correlation between exposures and the correlation between exposure and default. In this paper, we investigate the alpha factor’s sensitivity to key model parameters under stylized portfolio assumptions in order to better understand its complex characteristics. Our analysis is based on the numerical simulation of alpha sensitivities as well as the derivation of analytical formulas under the assumption of inﬁnitely granular portfolios. We show that the relative exposure volatility and parameters that specify the link between the underlying credit portfolio model and the exposure variables (wrong-way/right-way risk parameters) have a particularly significant impact on the alpha factor. For small homogeneous portfolios the alpha factor is rather unstable with respect to the number of counterparties. We investigate an alternative deﬁnition based on the coherent risk measure expected shortfall, which reduces the alpha instability quite significantly.

Keywords : counterparty credit risk capital, wrong-way risk, counterparty exposure, Basel II, analytic methods, expected shortfall.

Abstract :在衍生工具的信用风险加权资产公式中，违约风险敞口由一个乘数来衡量：即所谓的内部α因子。巴塞尔委员会引入这一点是为了抵消因使用固定风险敞口金额而产生的模型或估计误差，并应考虑交易对手风险敞口的不确定性、风险敞口之间的相关性以及风险敞口与违约之间的相关性。在本文中，为了更好地理解α因子的复杂特性，我们研究了α因子在风格化投资组合假设下对关键模型参数的敏感性。我们的分析基于α敏感性的数值模拟以及在无限粒度投资组合假设下推导的分析公式。我们表明，相对风险波动率和参数，指定之间的联系基础信贷组合模型和风险暴露变量（错误的方式/正确的方式风险参数）有一个特别重要的影响阿尔法因素。对于小型同质投资组合，α因子相对于交易对手的数量相当不稳定。我们研究了一种基于一致风险度量预期缺口的替代定义，该定义显著降低了α不稳定性。

Keywords :巴塞尔协议二、预期资本风险敞口、错误的交易对手风险敞口、错误的交易对手风险敞口方法。

\[2\] Universalities in the Dynamics of Cryptocurrencies: Stability, Scaling and Size

标题:加密货币动力学中的普遍性：稳定性、规模和规模

作者:Andrey Pogudin(King's College London),Anindya S. Chakrabati(Indian Institute of Management (IIM), Ahmedabad),Tiziana Di Matteo(King's College London)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3768664

Abstract : Cryptocurrencies represent an asset class featuring two unique properties: they are not backed by sovereigns, and their supply is ﬁxed exogenously. This combination becomes apparent in their volatility, which is driven only by demand-side factors. In particular, cryptocurrencies represent an extreme case of the excess volatility puzzle, with asset prices moving more than the fundamentals. We explore the effects of market capitalization on the dynamics of cryptocurrencies within both returns and volatility networks and show that these cryptocurrencies exhibit scaling properties in volatility with respect to market capitalization. The dependency network suggests that currencies with a larger market share have a larger presence in the dominant eigenspectrum, and they exert more inﬂuence in the co movement network. In these regards, we ﬁnd parallels between the dynamics of cryptocurrencies and those of more traditional asset classes. Our ﬁndings have implications for both researchers and practitioners in terms of modeling and analyzing the collective behavior of ﬁnancial assets.

Keywords : cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, blockchain, network, volatility, stability, scaling

Abstract :加密货币是一种具有两个独特属性的资产类别：它们不受主权国家的支持，而且它们的供给是固定的。这种组合在它们的波动性中变得明显，而波动性只受需求方面的因素驱动。特别是，加密货币代表了过度波动性之谜的一个极端例子，资产价格的变动超过了基本面。我们探讨了市场资本化对加密货币在收益率和波动率网络中的动态性的影响，并表明这些加密货币在波动率方面表现出与市场资本化相关的标度特性。依赖网络表明，市场份额较大的货币在主导特征谱中的存在较大，它们在协动网络中的影响较大。在这些方面，我们发现加密货币的动态与更传统的资产类别之间存在相似之处。我们的发现对研究人员和从业者在金融资产集体行为建模和分析方面都有启示。

Keywords :加密货币、比特币、区块链、网络、波动性、稳定性、扩展性

\[3\] Gaussian Process Regression for Derivative Portfolio Modeling and Application to Credit Valuation Adjustment Computations

标题:基于高斯过程回归的衍生品投资组合建模及其在信用估值调整计算中的应用

作者:Stéphane Crépey(Université d'Évry - Equipe d'Analyse et Probabilites),Matthew Francis Dixon(Illinois Institute of Technology)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3768647

Abstract : Modeling counterparty risk is computationally challenging because it requires the simultaneous evaluation of all trades between each counterparty under both market and credit risk. We present a multi-Gaussian process regression approach, which is well suited for the over-the-counter derivative portfolio valuation involved in credit valuation adjustment (CVA) computation. Our approach avoids nested simulation or simulation and regression of cashflows by learning a Gaussian metamodel for the mark-to-market cube of a derivative portfolio. We model the joint posterior of the derivatives as a Gaussian process over function space, imposing the spatial covariance structure on the risk factors. Monte Carlo simulation is then used to simulate the dynamics of the risk factors. The uncertainty in portfolio valuation arising from the Gaussian process approximation is quantified numerically. Numerical experiments demonstrate the accuracy and convergence properties of our approach for CVA computations, including a counterparty portfolio of interest rate swaps.

Keywords : Gaussian processes regression, surrogate modeling, mark-to-market cube, derivatives, credit valuation adjustment, uncertainty quantification.

Abstract :交易对手风险建模在计算上具有挑战性，因为它需要同时评估市场和信用风险下每个交易对手之间的所有交易。本文提出了一种多元高斯过程回归方法，适用于信用估值调整（CVA）计算中涉及的场外衍生品组合估值。我们的方法避免了嵌套模拟或模拟和回归现金流学习高斯元模型的市值立方体的衍生工具投资组合。我们将导数的联合后验概率建模为函数空间上的高斯过程，将空间协方差结构施加在风险因子上。然后采用蒙特卡罗模拟方法对风险因素进行动态模拟。对高斯过程近似引起的投资组合估值的不确定性进行了数值量化。数值实验证明了该方法的准确性和收敛性，包括利率互换的交易对手组合。

Keywords :高斯过程回归，代理模型，按市值计价立方体，衍生工具，信用估值调整，不确定性量化。

\[4\] High-Order Approximations to Call Option Prices in the Heston Model

标题:Heston模型中看涨期权价格的高阶近似

作者:Archil Gulisashvili(Ohio University),Marc Lagunas(University of Oslo - Department of Mathematics),Raul Merino(University of Barcelona - Faculty of Mathematics),Josep Vives(University of Barcelona)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3768521

Abstract : In the present paper, a decomposition formula for the call price due to Alòs is transformed into a Taylor-type formula containing an infinite series with stochastic terms. The new decomposition may be considered as an alternative to the decomposition of the call price found in a recent paper by Als, Gatheral and Rodoičić. We use the new decomposition to obtain various approximations to the call price in the Heston model with sharper estimates of the error term than in previously known approximations. One of the formulas obtained in the present paper has five significant terms and an error estimate of the form O(v³(|P| + v)), where v and P are the volatility-of-volatility and the correlation in the Heston model, respectively. Another approximation formula contains seven more terms and the error estimate is of the form O(v⁴(1 + |P|v)). For the uncorrelated Heston model (P = 0), we obtain a formula with four significant terms and an error estimate O(v⁶) Numerical experiments show that the new approximations to the call price perform especially well in the high-volatility mode.

Keywords : computational finance, Heston model, option pricing, price approximations, stochastic volatility models, vanilla options

Abstract :本文将Alòs引起的买入价格分解公式转化为包含随机项的无穷级数的Taylor型公式。新的分解可被视为替代Als、gathereal和Rodoičić最近一篇论文中对买入价格的分解。在Heston模型中，我们使用新的分解来获得各种各样的买入价格的近似值，其误差项的估计比以前已知的近似值更精确。本文得到的一个公式有五个有效项和一个形式为O（v³（| P |+v））的误差估计，其中v和P分别是Heston模型中的波动率和相关性。另一个近似公式又包含7项，误差估计的形式为O（v⁴（1+| P|v））。对于不相关的Heston模型（P=0），我们得到了一个包含四个有效项的公式和一个误差估计O（v⁶）。数值实验表明，新的看涨期权价格近似在高波动率模式下表现得特别好。

Keywords :计算金融，赫斯顿模型，期权定价，价格近似，随机波动模型，普通期权

\[5\] Liquidity Co-Movements and Volatility Regimes in Cryptocurrencies

标题:加密货币的流动性协同运动与波动机制

作者:Hubert Anciaux(affiliation not provided to SSRN),Christophe Desagre(Catholic University of Louvain (UCL) - Louvain Finance (LFIN)),Nicolas Nicaise(affiliation not provided to SSRN),Mikael Petitjean(Catholic University of Lille - IÉSEG School of Management, Lille Campus; Lille Economie & Management (LEM) UMR 9221; Louvain School of Management (UCL); Catholic University of Louvain (UCL) - Louvain Finance (LFIN))

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3769309

Abstract : We find significant evidence of liquidity commonalities among cryptos, in particular when liquidity is estimated by relying on order-book-based proxies. Both the magnitude and pervasiveness of these co-movements are very similar to those estimated for US stocks 10 and 20 years ago. When we introduce volatility regimes based on the VCRIX volatility index on cryptocurrencies, we identify stronger liquidity co-movements in high volatility regimes across all cryptocurrencies and for all liquidity proxies. The magnitude and pervasiveness of commonalities are weaker in the low volatility regime when liquidity is arguably more idiosyncratic, affecting cryptos differently and leading to more disconnected movements relative to market liquidity. We conclude that the liquidity co-movements are not orthogonal to what was observed in the past for more centralized markets.

Keywords : Liquidity, co-movements, bitcoin, volatility, cryptos

Abstract :我们发现了密码中流动性共性的重要证据，特别是当依赖基于订单簿的代理来估计流动性时。这些共同运动的规模和普遍性与10年和20年前对美国股市的估计非常相似。当我们引入基于加密货币VCRIX波动率指数的波动率机制时，我们发现在所有加密货币和所有流动性代理的高波动率机制中存在较强的流动性协动。在低波动率制度下，当流动性可以说更具特质时，共性的规模和普遍性较弱，对加密的影响不同，并导致相对于市场流动性的更不连贯的变动。我们的结论是，流动性协同运动与过去观察到的更加集中的市场的情况并不正交。

Keywords :流动性、共同运动、比特币、波动性、加密

\[6\] The Volatility Effect in China

标题:中国的波动效应

作者:David Blitz(Robeco Quantitative Investments),Matthias X. Hanauer(Technische Universität München (TUM); Robeco Quantitative Investments),Pim van Vliet(Robeco Quantitative Investments)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3765625

Abstract : This paper shows that low-risk stocks significantly outperform high-risk stocks in the local China A shares market. The main driver of this low-risk anomaly is volatility, and not beta. A Fama-French style VOL factor is not explained by the Fama-French-Carhart factors, and has the strongest stand-alone performance among all these factors. Our findings are robust across sectors and over time, and consistent with previous empirical evidence for the US and international markets. Moreover, the VOL premium exhibits excellent investability characteristics, as it involves a low turnover and remains strong when applied to only the largest and most liquid stocks. Our results imply that the volatility effect is a highly pervasive phenomenon, and that explanations should be able to account for its presence in highly institutionalized markets, such as the US, but also in the Chinese market where private investors dominate trading.

Keywords : China A shares, low risk, low volatility, low beta, minimum variance, anomaly, value, size, momentum, profitability, investments, smart beta, low-volatility investing

Abstract :本文的研究表明，在中国A股市场，低风险股票的表现明显优于高风险股票。这种低风险异常的主要驱动力是波动性，而不是贝塔系数。Fama-French-style-VOL因子不能用Fama-French-Carhart因子来解释，在所有这些因子中，Fama-French-style-VOL因子具有最强的独立性能。我们的研究结果在各个行业和时间上都是稳健的，并且与美国和国际市场先前的经验证据一致。此外，VOL溢价表现出良好的可投资性特征，因为它涉及低换手率，并且仅适用于最大和流动性最强的股票时仍然强劲。我们的研究结果表明，波动性效应是一种非常普遍的现象，这种解释应该能够解释其存在于高度制度化的市场，如美国，但也存在于私人投资者主导交易的中国市场。

Keywords :中国A股，低风险，低波动，低贝塔，最小方差，异常，价值，规模，动量，盈利能力，投资，智能贝塔，低波动投资

\[7\] Estimating the Contagion Effect Through the Portfolio Channel Using a Network Approach

标题:利用网络方法估计投资组合渠道的传染效应

作者:Alessandro Schiavone(Bank of Italy)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3768551

Abstract : This work studies contagion risk through the portfolio investment channel using network analysis and simulation on bilateral cross-country data. The importance of the portfolio channel in the transmission of ﬁnancial shocks reﬂects the high interconnectedness of the global ﬁnancial system, which diminished in the aftermath of the global ﬁnancial crisis but has resumed in recent years. The network representing cross-country portfolio investments turns out to be highly concentrated around the main ﬁnancial centers, which act as global hubs connecting with nodes that are not linked directly. Using a network simulation model based on the assumption that international investors rebalance their portfolios after an idiosyncratic shock, reducing investments in countries to which they are overexposed, we ﬁnd that contagion effects may be significant even when the shock originates in a peripheral country. In addition, the model suggests contagion risk has risen since the global ﬁnancial crisis, owing to the greater ﬁnancial integration of emerging economies.

Keywords : contagion, portfolio investments, network, ﬁnancial integration, interconnectedness

Abstract :本文利用双边跨国数据进行网络分析和仿真，研究了证券投资渠道的传染风险。投资组合渠道在金融冲击传导中的重要性反映了全球金融体系的高度互联性，这种互联性在全球金融危机后有所减弱，但近年来又有所恢复。代表跨国投资组合的网络高度集中在主要金融中心周围，这些中心作为全球枢纽，与没有直接联系的节点相连。利用一个基于假设的网络模拟模型，即国际投资者在特殊冲击后重新平衡其投资组合，减少对过度接触国家的投资，我们发现，即使冲击源于外围国家，传染效应也可能是显著的。此外，该模型还表明，自全球金融危机以来，由于新兴经济体金融一体化程度的提高，传染风险有所上升。

Keywords :传染、组合投资、网络、金融整合、互联性

作者:Victor Olkhov(TVEL Fuel Company)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3768534

Abstract : We model interactions between ﬁnancial transactions and expectations and describe asset pricing and return disturbances. We use the risk ratings of economic agents as their coordinates, and we approximate the ﬁnancial variables, transactions and expectations of numerous separate agents by descriptions of collective variables, transactions and expectations as density functions in the economic space. We take into account ﬂows of collective ﬁnancial variables, transactions and expectations induced by the motion of separate a gents in the economic space due to the change of agents’ risk ratings and describe the impact of these ﬂows. We derive self-consistent equations on transactions and multiple kinds of expectations and propose that asset pricing ﬂuctuations may be induced by different expectations for approved transactions with particular assets. We study a model with a linear dependence between the disturbances of transactions and expectations and derive expressions that describe the explicit dependence between price and volume disturbances and the dependence between return and volume disturbances. Our results may help in further studies of price–volume and return–volume correlations.

Keywords : risk assessment, economic space, asset pricing, transactions, expectations

Abstract :我们对金融交易和预期之间的相互作用进行建模，并描述资产定价和回报扰动。我们使用经济主体的风险评级作为其坐标，并通过将集体变量、交易和期望描述为经济空间中的密度函数，来近似许多独立主体的财务变量、交易和期望。我们考虑了集体财务变量、交易和预期的流动，这些流动是由于代理人风险评级的变化而导致的单独代理人在经济空间中的流动，并描述了这些流动的影响。我们推导了交易和多种预期的自洽方程，并提出资产定价波动可能由特定资产的批准交易的不同预期引起。我们研究了一个交易扰动与预期之间具有线性依赖关系的模型，导出了描述价格扰动与成交量扰动之间显式依赖关系以及收益扰动与成交量扰动之间依赖关系的表达式。我们的结果可能有助于进一步研究价格-数量和回报-数量的相关性。

Keywords :风险评估、经济空间、资产定价、交易、预期

\[9\] What’s so Special about Time Series Momentum?
