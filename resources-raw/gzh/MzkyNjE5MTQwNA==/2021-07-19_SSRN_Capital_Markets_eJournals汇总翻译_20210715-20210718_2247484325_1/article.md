\[1\] Corporate Announcements and Market Efficiency: A Case on Indian Capital Market

标题:公司公告与市场效率：以印度资本市场为例

作者:Nagendra Marisetty,Pardhasaradhi Madasu

出处:International Journal of Business and Management, Vol. 16, No. 8; 2021

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3887149

Abstract : Capital markets being the backbone of the economy, are expected to be functioning efficiently. Efficiently-priced financial markets are considered a catalyst for the economic growth of the nations (Malkiel, 2010). Efficient markets are the reflection of security valuations. In an informationally efficient market, no one can beat the market and make abnormal returns based on the information because the information is instantaneously observed in the stock prices. The current paper analyses the market efficiency of three of the most popular corporate events, i.e., announcement of cash dividends, bonus issues, and stock split in the Indian context. The sample is 2253 pure cash dividend announcements (627 large-caps, 552 mid-caps, and 1074 small-caps), 152 bonus issue announcements (49 large-caps, 33 mid-caps, and 70 small-caps), and 181 stock split announcements (35 large-caps, 34 mid-caps, and 112 small-caps) were used for this study. Event methodology market model used to calculate Average Abnormal Returns (AAR) and Cumulative Average Abnormal Returns (CAAR).The results of the study have few findings which are contradictory to the existing literature on market efficiency. The cash dividend announcements have shown evidence for market efficiency, and results are contrary to Gupta et al. (2012), but the results are similar to Mishra (2005). Bonus issue announcements also have shown evidence for a semi-strong form of efficiency, test results identical to Dhar and Chhaochharia (2008), Kumar and Mittal (2015). Stock split announcements have not shown market efficiency, and the effect is similar to the study of Lakshmi and Roy (2012) and contrary to Chavali and Zahid (2011). Our results also support the premise that the emergingcountries depict evidence of market efficiency (Bechev, 2003). Finally, we conclude that market efficiency results differ based on corporate announcements and market capitalization.

Keywords : capital markets, corporate announcements, event study methodology, market efficiency, semi-strong form of efficiency, & S&P BSE 500 index

Abstract :资本市场作为经济的支柱，预计将有效运作。有效定价的金融市场被认为是各国经济增长的催化剂（Malkiel，2010）。有效市场是证券估值的反映。在一个信息有效的市场中，没有人能够在信息的基础上战胜市场并获得超额收益，因为信息是在股票价格中被即时观察到的。本文分析了三种最受欢迎的公司事件的市场效率，即在印度背景下宣布现金分红、红利发行和股票分割。本研究以2253份纯现金股利公告（大盘股627份，中盘股552份，小盘股1074份）、152份派息公告（大盘股49份，中盘股33份，小盘股70份）和181份股票分割公告（大盘股35份，中盘股34份，小盘股112份）为样本。事件法市场模型用于计算平均异常收益率（AAR）和累积平均异常收益率（CAAR），研究结果与现有的市场效率文献相矛盾。现金股利公告显示了市场效率的证据，结果与Gupta等人（2012）相反，但结果与Mishra（2005）相似。奖金发行公告也显示了半强式效率的证据，测试结果与Dhar和Chhaocharia（2008）、Kumar和Mittal（2015）相同。股票分割公告并未显示出市场效率，其效果类似于Lakshmi和Roy（2012）的研究，与Chavali和Zahid（2011）的研究相反。我们的结果也支持这样一个前提，即新兴国家描述了市场效率的证据（Bechev，2003）。最后，我们得出结论，市场效率的结果是不同的公司公告和市值。

Keywords :资本市场，公司公告，事件研究方法，市场效率，半强式效率，标普BSE 500指数

\[2\] Is the Value Premium Smaller Than We Thought?

标题:价值溢价是否比我们想象的要小？

作者:Mathias Hasler

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3886984

Abstract : The construction of the original HML portfolio (Fama and French, 1993) includes six seemingly innocuous decisions that could easily have been replaced with alternatives that are just as reasonable. I propose such alternatives and construct HML portfolios. In sample, the average estimate of the value premium is dramatically smaller than the original estimate of the value premium. The difference is 0.09% per month and statistically significant. Out of sample, however, this difference is statistically indistinguishable from zero. The results suggest that the original value premium estimate is upward biased because of a chance result in the original research decisions.

Keywords : Value Premium, Value Factor, HML Portfolio, Book-to-Market Equity, Chance Result, Statistical Biases

Abstract :最初的HML投资组合的构建（Fama和French，1993）包括六个看似无害的决策，这些决策很容易被同样合理的替代方案所取代。我提出了这样的替代方案，并构建了HML投资组合。在样本中，价值溢价的平均估计值明显小于价值溢价的原始估计值。差异为每月0.09%，具有统计学意义。然而，在样本之外，这种差异在统计学上与零是无法区分的。结果表明，由于原始研究决策中的偶然结果，原始价值溢价估计值向上偏移。

Keywords :价值溢价，价值因素，HML投资组合，账面市值，机会结果，统计偏差

\[3\] ETFs, Illiquid Assets, and Fire Sales

标题:ETF、非流动资产和火爆销售

作者:John J. Shim,Karamfil Todorov

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3886881

Abstract : We document several novel facts about exchange-traded funds (ETFs) holding corporate bonds. First, the portfolio of bonds that are exchanged for new or existing ETF shares (called creation or redemption baskets) often represents a small fraction of ETF holdings – a fact that we call “fractional baskets.” Second, creation and redemption baskets exhibit high turnover. Third, creation (redemption) baskets tend to have longer (shorter) durations and smaller (larger) bid-ask spreads relative to holdings. Lastly, ETFs with fractional baskets exhibit persistent premiums and discounts, which is related to the slow adjustment of NAV returns to ETF returns. We develop a simple model to show that an ETF’s authorized participants (APs) can act as a buﬀer between the ETF market and the underlying illiquid assets, and help mitigate ﬁre sales. Our ﬁndings suggest that ETFs may be more eﬀective in managing illiquid assets than mutual funds.

Keywords : Bonds, ETFs, Fire Sales, Liquidity

Abstract :我们记录了几个关于交易所交易基金（etf）持有公司债券的新事实。首先，用于交换新的或现有ETF股票的债券组合（称为创建或赎回篮子）通常只占ETF持有量的一小部分，我们称之为“部分篮子”。其次，创建和赎回篮子表现出高周转率。第三，创造（赎回）篮子的期限往往较长（较短），相对于持有量的买卖价差较小（较大）。最后，部分篮子ETF表现出持续的溢价和折价，这与资产净值回报率对ETF回报率的缓慢调整有关。我们开发了一个简单的模型来说明ETF的授权参与者（AP）可以在ETF市场和基础非流动资产之间起到缓冲作用，并有助于减少出售。我们的研究结果表明，ETF在管理非流动资产方面可能比共同基金更有效。

Keywords :债券、ETF、火爆销售、流动性

\[4\] The Structure and Degree of Dependence in Government Bond Markets

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3886486

Abstract : Our study provides new evidence on asymmetric dependencies in international government bond markets, by examining bonds from developed, emerging, and frontier countries, using a quantile regression methodology. We find that the dependence structure for emerging and frontier markets significantly changes during financial crisis periods, which we show has important implications for international diversification of investment strategies. Moreover, we also examine in detail stock-bond correlations and uncover several instances of decoupling. In contrast, developed markets exhibit a more stable dependence pattern. \[5\] Shadow Bank and Fintech Mortgage Securitization

标题:影子银行与金融科技抵押贷款证券化

作者:Yu An,Lei Li,Zhaogang Song

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3886103

Abstract : Agency MBS issuers can choose among three securitization venues: individual securitization where an issuer uses her own loans to create an MBS, collective securitization where different issuers deliver loans into a common MBS, and cash window where issuers receive immediate cash payment by selling loans to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac, who then conduct securitization. We find that issuers with greater immediate liquidity needs (e.g., smaller issuers and shadow banks) have a larger fraction of their loans securitized through cash window. The uniform pricing feature of collective securitization results in cross-subsidy from traditional banks, who have relatively high-value loans, to shadow banks, especially fintech issuers, who have relatively low-value loans; hence, shadow banks and traditional banks prefer collective and individual securitization, respectively. We further show that securitization venues affect the quality and quantity of loans that issuers securitize, using Fannie Mae's policy shock on collective securitization and the COVID-19 shock on cash window.

Keywords : Agency MBS, Bank, Fintech, Liquidity, Securitization, Shadow Bank

Abstract :机构MBS发行人可以在三种证券化场所中进行选择：个人证券化，发行人使用自己的贷款创建MBS；集体证券化，不同发行人将贷款交付给共同的MBS；现金窗口，发行人通过向房利美或房地美出售贷款获得即时现金支付，然后他们进行证券化。我们发现，具有更大即时流动性需求的发行人（例如，较小的发行人和影子银行）通过现金窗口将其大部分贷款证券化。集体证券化的统一定价特征导致了传统银行（贷款价值相对较高）对影子银行（尤其是金融科技发行人）的交叉补贴，影子银行的贷款价值相对较低；因此，影子银行和传统银行分别倾向于集体证券化和个人证券化。利用房利美对集体证券化的政策冲击和COVID-19对现金窗口的冲击，我们进一步证明了证券化场所影响发行人证券化贷款的质量和数量。

Keywords :代理MBS、银行、金融科技、流动性、证券化、影子银行

\[6\] Strategic Execution Trajectories

标题:战略执行轨迹

作者:Giuliana Bordigoni,Alessio Figalli,Anthony Ledford,Philipp Ustinov

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3885551

Abstract : We obtain the optimal execution strategy for two sequential trades in the presence of a transient price impact. We first present a novel and general solution method for the case of a single trade (a metaorder) that is executed as a sequence of sub-trades (child orders). We then analyze the case of two sequential metaorders, including the case where the size and direction of the second metaorder are uncertain at the time the first metaorder is initiated. We obtain the optimal execution strategy under two different cost functions. First, we minimize the total cost when each metaorder is benchmarked to the price at its initiation, the total separate costs approach widely used by practitioners. Although simple, we show that optimizing total separate costs can lead to a significant understatement of the real costs of trading whilst also adversely impacting order scheduling. We overcome these issues by introducing a new cost function that splits the second metaorder into two parts, one that is predictable when the first metaorder is initiated and a residual that is not. The predictable and residual parts of the second metaorder are benchmarked using the initiation prices of the first and second metaorders, respectively. We prove existence of an optimal execution trajectory for linear instantaneous price impact and positive definite decay, and derive the explicit form of the minimizer in the special case of exponentially decaying impact. Various numerical examples are included for illustration, each of which admits a unique optimal solution, however uniqueness in general remains unproven.

Keywords : Block trade, financial markets, market impact, market manipulation, optimal transport, optimal trade execution, optimization, quantitative finance, transactions costs.

Abstract :我们得到了两个连续交易在存在瞬时价格冲击时的最优执行策略。我们首先提出了一种新的和一般的解决方法的情况下，一个单一的贸易（元订单）是作为一个序列的子交易（子订单）执行。然后，我们分析了两个连续元序的情况，包括第一个元序启动时第二个元序的大小和方向不确定的情况。得到了两种不同代价函数下的最优执行策略。首先，我们最小化总成本时，每个元秩序是基准价格在其启动，总单独的成本方法广泛使用的从业者。虽然简单，我们表明，优化总分离成本可以导致一个显着低估的实际交易成本，同时也对订单调度产生不利影响。我们通过引入一个新的成本函数来克服这些问题，该函数将第二个元序分为两部分，一部分在第一个元序启动时是可预测的，另一部分则不是。第二元序的可预测部分和剩余部分分别使用第一元序和第二元序的起始价格进行基准测试。我们证明了线性瞬时价格冲击和正定衰减的最优执行轨迹的存在性，并在指数衰减冲击的特殊情况下导出了极小化的显式形式。文中给出了各种数值算例，每个算例都有一个唯一的最优解，但一般的唯一性尚未得到证实。

Keywords :大宗交易，金融市场，市场影响，市场操纵，最优运输，最优交易执行，最优化，定量金融，交易成本。

\[7\] Butterfly Implied Returns

标题:蝴蝶隐含收益

作者:Di Wu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3880815

Abstract : For each S&P 500 stock, I calculate the rolling correlation between the VIX and the premium of butterfly at different strikes. The butterfly that co-moves most positively with VIX reveals the expectation of the stock’s return in the future market crash. I call this return the Butterfly Implied Return (BIR). I construct a new strategy by shorting the vulnerable stocks with low BIR and longing the resilient stocks with high BIR. Over the sample period from 1996 to 2019, this strategy earns a statistically significant alpha, ranging from 0.26% to 0.35% per month relative to various factor models. Building on BIR, I construct a value weighted average called the Butterfly Implied Return of the Market (BIRM) which measures the severity of the future market crash. I show that BIRM is an important determinant of the time varying equity risk premium.

Keywords : Asset Pricing; Butterfly Strategy; Recovery; Aggregate Predictability

Abstract :对于每只标准普尔500指数股票，我计算了波动率指数和蝴蝶溢价在不同走向之间的滚动相关性。与波动率指数（VIX）走势最为积极的蝴蝶，揭示了该股在未来市场崩盘中的回报预期。我把这种回报称为蝴蝶隐含回报（BIR）。我构建了一个新的策略，即做空低BIR的弱势股，憧憬高BIR的弹性股。在1996年至2019年的样本期内，相对于各种因素模型，这一策略获得了具有统计意义的α，每月从0.26%到0.35%不等。在BIR的基础上，我构建了一个称为蝴蝶隐含市场收益率（BIRM）的价值加权平均值，用来衡量未来市场崩溃的严重程度。我证明了BIRM是时变股票风险溢价的一个重要决定因素。

Keywords :资产定价；蝴蝶策略；恢复；总体可预测性

\[8\] Outliers and Robust Inference in Archival Accounting Research

标题:档案会计研究中的离群值与稳健推理

作者:Joachim Gassen,David Veenman

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3880942

Abstract : This study examines the nature of outliers in archival accounting research, and evaluates the merits and limitations of robust estimators in identifying and downweighting their influence. Using simulated and actual data, we demonstrate how outliers arise non-randomly from the data generating process, research design choices such as scaling, and model misspecification. We find that robust estimators lead to more precise inferences than OLS in common archival data, but these estimators can also bias inferences due their downweighting of substantial and non-random proportions of the data. In addition, we demonstrate that a failure to account for nonlinear relations can induce biases in robust estimators that are more severe than with OLS. We advise researchers to carefully evaluate the source and nonrandom nature of outliers in their samples, to be cautious in implementing and interpreting robust estimators, and to evaluate and report the sensitivity of these methods to critical design choices. We also highlight the usefulness of combining data visualizations with robust estimators to assess both the nature of outliers and their impact on inference.

Keywords : Outliers, robust regression, MM-estimation, OLS, nonlinearities, selection bias, model specification, scaling, leverage, efficiency, skewness, kurtosis

Abstract :本研究探讨档案会计研究中离群值的性质，并评估稳健估计在识别和降低其影响时的优点和局限性。使用模拟和实际数据，我们演示了异常值是如何从数据生成过程中非随机产生的，研究设计选择（如缩放）和模型错误。我们发现，在一般档案资料中，稳健估计比OLS能得到更精确的推论，但这些估计也会因其对资料的实质性和非随机比例的加权而使推论产生偏差。此外，我们还证明了，如果不考虑非线性关系，稳健估计中会产生比OLS更严重的偏差。我们建议研究人员仔细评估样本中离群值的来源和非随机性质，谨慎地实施和解释稳健估计，并评估和报告这些方法对关键设计选择的敏感性。我们还强调了将数据可视化与稳健估计相结合的有用性，以评估异常值的性质及其对推断的影响。

Keywords :离群值、稳健回归、MM估计、OLS、非线性、选择偏差、模型规格、缩放、杠杆、效率、偏度、峰度

\[9\] The Information Quality of Complex Financial Reports: Evidence from Filing Returns and Insider Trades

标题:复杂财务报告的信息质量：来自申报表和内幕交易的证据

作者:Hyun Woong Chang,Scott Duellman,J. Philipp Klaus,Blair B. Marquardt

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3873474

Abstract : Standard setters have expressed concern over the impact of complex accounting standards on the information gap between firm insiders and financial statement users. We address this issue by examining the relationship between accounting reporting complexity (ARC) and the market response to earnings surprises at the financial report filing. We document a weaker earnings response coefficient for increasing ARC, consistent with less market reliance on complex financial reports. We then examine whether ARC is associated with executives’ ability to trade based on their residual information advantage. Consistent with this expectation, we document higher returns to executives on trades executed in the sixty days following the report filing. The higher returns are concentrated in the trades of the CEO and CFO, who likely have a more profound knowledge of U.S. GAAP. Finally, we identify decreasing earnings persistence for increasing ARC as an underlying mechanism for these effects.

Abstract :准则制定者对复杂的会计准则对公司内部人士和财务报表使用者之间的信息差距的影响表示担忧。我们通过研究会计报告复杂性（ARC）与财务报告提交时市场对意外收益的反应之间的关系来解决这个问题。我们证明，随着ARC的增加，收益反应系数变弱，这与市场对复杂财务报告的依赖程度降低一致。然后，我们检验了ARC是否与高管基于剩余信息优势的交易能力相关。与此预期一致，我们记录了报告提交后60天内执行的交易给高管的更高回报。更高的回报集中在首席执行官和首席财务官的交易中，他们可能对美国公认会计准则有更深刻的了解。最后，我们发现增加ARC的持续收益减少是这些影响的潜在机制。

Keywords :会计报告复杂性、信息不对称、盈余反应系数、内幕交易
