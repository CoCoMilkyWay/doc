\[1\] Censored EM algorithm for Weibull mixtures

标题 : 威布尔混合模型的截尾EM算法

作者 : Markus Kreer, Ayse Kizilersu, Anthony W. Thomas , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.10601

说明 : Comments: 10 pages, 5 figures

Abstract : In a previous analysis the problem of zero inflated time data (caused by high frequency trading in the electronic order book) was handled by left truncating the inter arrival times. We demonstrated, using rigorous statistical methods, that the Weibull distribution describes the corresponding stochastic dynamics for all inter arrival time differences except in the region near zero. However, since the truncated Weibull distribution was not able to describe the huge zero inflated probability mass in the neighbourhood of zero (making up approximately 50 of the data for limit orders), it became clear that the entire probability distribution is a mixture distribution of which the Weibull distribution is a significant part. Here we use a censored EM algorithm to analyse data for the difference of the arrival times of market orders, which usually have a much lower percentage of zero inflation, for four selected stocks trading on the London Stock Exchange.

摘要 : 在之前的分析中，零虚增时间数据（由电子订单中高频交易引起）的问题是通过左截断到达时间来处理的。我们用严格的统计方法证明了威布尔分布描述了所有到达时间差的随机动力学，除了在接近零的区域。然而，由于截断威布尔分布不能描述零附近的巨大的零膨胀概率质量（约占极限阶数据的50%），很明显整个概率分布是一个混合分布，其中威布尔分布是一个重要部分。在这里，我们使用一个截尾EM算法来分析伦敦证券交易所交易的四只股票的市价指令到达时间的差异，这类股票的零通货膨胀率通常要低得多。

\[2\] Power mixture forward performance processes

作者 : Levon Avanesyan, Ronnie Sircar , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.10847

说明 : Comments: 28 pages, 6 figures, submitted

Abstract : We consider the forward investment problem in market models where the stock prices are continuous semimartingales adapted to a Brownian filtration. We construct a broad class of forward performance processes with initial conditions of power mixture type, u(x) int mathbb I frac x 1 gamma 1 gamma nu( mathrm d gamma) . We proceed to define and fully characterize two power mixture forward performance processes with constant risk aversion coefficients in the interval (0,1) , and derive properties of two power mixture forward performance processes when the risk aversion coefficients are continuous stochastic processes. Finally, we discuss the problem of managing an investment pool of two investors, whose respective preferences evolve as power forward performance processes.

摘要 : 研究了股票价格服从布朗过滤的连续半鞅市场模型中的远期投资问题。我们构造了一类具有幂混合型初始条件的前向性能过程，u（x）intmathbb I frac x 1 gamma 1 gamma nu（mathrm d gamma）。在区间（0,1）中定义并充分刻画了风险厌恶系数为常数的两个幂混合正向性能过程，并推导了风险厌恶系数为连续随机过程时两个幂混合正向性能过程的性质。最后，我们讨论了管理一个由两个投资者组成的投资池的问题，两个投资者各自的偏好随着功率向前的性能过程而变化。

\[3\] High frequency dynamics of the implied volatility surface

标题 : 隐含波动率表面的高频动力学

作者 : Bastien Baldacci , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.10875

说明 : -

Abstract : We present a Hawkes modeling of the volatility surface s high frequency dynamics and show how the Hawkes kernel coefficients govern the surface s skew and convexity. We provide simple sufficient conditions on the coefficients to ensure no arbitrage opportunities of the surface. Moreover, these conditions reduce the number of the kernel s parameters to estimate. Finally, we show that at the macroscopic level, the surface is driven by a sum of risk factors whose volatility processes are rough.

摘要 : 我们提出了一个波动面高频动力学的Hawkes模型，并说明了Hawkes核系数如何控制表面的倾斜和凸性。给出了简单的无套利机会的充分条件。此外，这些条件减少了需要估计的核参数的数目。最后，我们发现在宏观层面，表面是由一系列风险因素驱动的，这些因素的波动过程是粗糙的。

\[4\] National Accounts as a Stock Flow Consistent System, Part 1

标题 : 国民账户作为一个股票流动一致性系统，第1部分

作者 : Matti Estola, Kristian Veps l inen , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11282

说明 : Comments: 29 pages, 41 figures

Abstract : The 2008 economic crisis was not forecastable by at that time existing models of macroeconomics. Thus macroeconomics needs new tools. We introduce a model based on National Accounts that shows how macroeconomic sectors are interconnected. These connections explain the spread of business cycles from one industry to another and from financial sector to the real economy. These lingages cannot be explained by General Equilibrium type of models. Our model describes the real part of National Accounts (NA) of an economy. The accounts are presented in the form of a money flow diagram between the following macro sectors Non financial firms, financial firms, households, government, and rest of the world. The model contains all main items in NA and the corresponding simulation model creates time paths for 59 key macroeconomic quantities for an unlimited future. Finnish data of NA from time period 1975 2012 is used in calibrating the parameters of the model, and the model follows the historical data with sufficient accuracy. Our study serves as a basis for systems analytic macro models that can explain the positive and negative feed backs in the production system of an economy. These feed backs are born from interactions between economic units and between real and financial markets. JEL E01, E10. Key words Stock Flow Models, National Accounts, Simulation model.

摘要 : 2008年的经济危机在当时的宏观经济模型中是无法预测的。因此，宏观经济学需要新的工具。我们引入了一个基于国民账户的模型，该模型显示了宏观经济部门是如何相互联系的。这些联系解释了商业周期从一个行业到另一个行业，从金融部门到实体经济的扩散。这些术语不能用一般均衡模型来解释。我们的模型描述了一个经济体的国民经济核算（NA）的实际部分。这些账户以货币流动图的形式呈现在以下宏观部门（非金融公司、金融公司、家庭、政府和世界其他地区）之间。该模型包含了NA中的所有主要项目，相应的模拟模型为无限未来的59个关键宏观经济量创建了时间路径。模型参数的标定采用芬兰1975-2012年的NA数据，模型遵循历史数据，具有足够的精度。我们的研究是系统分析宏观模型的基础，可以解释一个经济体生产系统中的正反馈和负反馈。这些反馈来自经济单位之间以及实体市场和金融市场之间的相互作用。凝胶E01，E10。关键词股票流动模型，国民账户，模拟模型。

\[5\] An Empirical Evaluation On The Effectiveness Of Medicaid Expansion Across 49 States

标题 : 49个州医疗补助扩展效果的实证评价

作者 : Tung Yu Marco Chan , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11286

说明 : -

Abstract : In 2014 the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (ACA) introduced the expansion of Medicaid where states can opt to expand the eligibility for those in need of free health insurance. In this paper, we attempt to assess the effectiveness of Medicaid expansion on health outcomes of state populations using Difference in Difference (DD) regressions to seek for causal impacts of expanding Medicaid on health outcomes in 49 states. We find that in the time frame of 2013 to 2016, Medicaid expansion seems to have had no significant impact on the health outcomes of states that have chosen to expand.

\[6\] Optimal ratcheting of dividends in a Brownian risk model

标题 : 布朗风险模型中股利的最优棘轮效应

作者 : Hansjoerg Albrecher, Pablo Azcue, Nora Muler , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.10632

说明 : -

Abstract : We study the problem of optimal dividend payout from a surplus process governed by Brownian motion with drift under the additional constraint of ratcheting, i.e. the dividend rate can never decrease. We solve the resulting two dimensional optimal control problem, identifying the value function to be the unique viscosity solution of the corresponding Hamilton Jacobi Bellman equation. For finitely many admissible dividend rates we prove that threshold strategies are optimal, and for any finite continuum of admissible dividend rates we establish the varepsilon optimality of curve strategies. This work is a counterpart of Albrecher et al. (2020), where the ratcheting problem was studied for a compound Poisson surplus process with drift. In the present Brownian setup, calculus of variation techniques allow to obtain a much more explicit analysis and description of the optimal dividend strategies. We also give some numerical illustrations of the optimality results.

摘要 : 在棘轮额外约束下，研究了具有漂移的布朗运动控制的盈余过程的最优股利支付问题，即股利率永远不会下降。我们解决由此产生的二维最优控制问题，辨识出相应的Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman方程的唯一粘性解。对于有限多个容许股息率，我们证明了阈值策略是最优的，对于任何有限的容许股息率连续统，我们建立了曲线策略的varepsilon最优性。这项工作与Albrecher等人（2020）的研究相对应，其中研究了具有漂移的复合泊松盈余过程的棘轮问题。在目前的布朗模型中，变分法技术使我们能够更明确地分析和描述最优股利策略。我们也给出了一些最优性结果的数值例子。

\[7\] Green governments

标题 : 绿色政府

作者 : Niklas Potrafke, Kaspar Wuthrich , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.09906

说明 : -

Abstract : We examine how Green governments influence macroeconomic, education, and environmental outcomes. Our empirical strategy exploits that the Fukushima nuclear disaster in Japan gave rise to an unanticipated change in government in the German state Baden Wuerttemberg in 2011. The incumbent rightwing government was replaced by a leftwing government led by the Green party. We use the synthetic control method to select control states against which Baden Wuerttemberg s outcomes can be compared. The results do not suggest that the Green government influenced macroeconomic outcomes. The Green government implemented education policies that caused comprehensive schools to become larger. We find no evidence that the Green government influenced CO2 emissions, particulate matter emissions, or increased energy usage from renewable energies overall. An intriguing result is that the share of wind power usage decreased relative to the estimated counterfactual. Intra ecological conflicts and realities in public office are likely to have prevented the Green government from implementing drastic policy changes.

摘要 : 我们研究绿色政府如何影响宏观经济、教育和环境结果。我们的实证策略利用了日本福岛核灾难导致2011年德国巴登-伍尔滕堡州政府发生意外变化。现任右翼政府被绿党领导的左翼政府所取代。我们使用合成控制方法来选择控制状态，并与Baden-Wuerttemberg的结果进行比较。结果并不表明绿色政府影响了宏观经济的结果。绿色政府实施的教育政策使综合性学校变得更大。我们没有发现任何证据表明绿色政府影响了二氧化碳的排放，颗粒物的排放，或者可再生能源的能源使用量的增加。一个有趣的结果是，风电使用的份额相对于估计的反事实有所下降。内部生态冲突和公共部门的现实很可能阻碍了绿色政府实施重大的政策变革。

\[8\] Heavy tailed distributions in closing auctions

标题 : 收盘拍卖中的重尾分布

作者 : M. Derksen, B. Kleijn, R. de Vilder , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.10145

说明 : -

Abstract : We study the tails of closing auction return distributions for a sample of liquid European stocks. We use the stochastic call auction model of Derksen et al. (2020a), to derive a relation between tail exponents of limit order placement distributions and tail exponents of the resulting closing auction return distribution and we verify this relation empirically. Counter intuitively, large closing price fluctuations are typically not caused by large market orders, instead tails become heavier when market orders are removed. The model explains this by the observation that limit orders are submitted so as to counter existing market order imbalance.

摘要 : 我们研究了一个流动欧洲股票的收盘拍卖收益分布的尾部。我们利用Derksen等人（2020a）的随机叫价拍卖模型，推导出了限价指令配售分布的尾指数与最终成交拍卖收益率分布的尾指数之间的关系，并对这种关系进行了实证检验。与直觉相反，大的收盘价波动通常不是由大量的市场订单引起的，相反，当市场订单被删除时，尾部会变得更重。该模型通过观察限价订单的提交来解释这一点，以应对现有的市场订单失衡。

\[9\] Trader Company Method

标题 : 贸易商公司法

作者 : Katsuya Ito, Kentaro Minami, Kentaro Imajo, Kei Nakagawa , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.10215

说明 : Comments: AAMAS 2021

Abstract : Investors try to predict returns of financial assets to make successful investment. Many quantitative analysts have used machine learning based methods to find unknown profitable market rules from large amounts of market data. However, there are several challenges in financial markets hindering practical applications of machine learning based models. First, in financial markets, there is no single model that can consistently make accurate prediction because traders in markets quickly adapt to newly available information. Instead, there are a number of ephemeral and partially correct models called alpha factors . Second, since financial markets are highly uncertain, ensuring interpretability of prediction models is quite important to make reliable trading strategies. To overcome these challenges, we propose the Trader Company method, a novel evolutionary model that mimics the roles of a financial institute and traders belonging to it. Our method predicts future stock returns by aggregating suggestions from multiple weak learners called Traders. A Trader holds a collection of simple mathematical formulae, each of which represents a candidate of an alpha factor and would be interpretable for real world investors. The aggregation algorithm, called a Company, maintains multiple Traders. By randomly generating new Traders and retraining them, Companies can efficiently find financially meaningful formulae whilst avoiding overfitting to a transient state of the market. We show the effectiveness of our method by conducting experiments on real market data.

摘要 : 投资者试图通过预测金融资产的收益率来进行成功的投资。许多定量分析师已经使用基于机器学习的方法从大量的市场数据中发现未知的盈利市场规则。然而，金融市场中存在着一些阻碍基于机器学习模型的实际应用的挑战。首先，在金融市场中，没有一个模型能够始终如一地做出准确的预测，因为市场中的交易者很快就适应了新获得的信息。相反，有许多短暂的和部分正确的模型被称为α因子。其次，由于金融市场高度不确定性，确保预测模型的可解释性对于制定可靠的交易策略非常重要。为了克服这些挑战，我们提出了交易者公司方法，这是一种新的进化模型，它模仿了金融机构和属于它的交易者的角色。我们的方法通过综合来自多个被称为交易者的弱学习者的建议来预测未来股票收益。一个交易者持有一组简单的数学公式，每一个公式都代表一个α因子的候选者，可以为现实世界的投资者解释。聚合算法称为公司，维护多个交易者。通过随机产生新的交易者并对他们进行再培训，公司可以有效地找到有财务意义的公式，同时避免过度适应市场的短暂状态。通过对实际市场数据的实验，验证了该方法的有效性。

\[10\] Matching in size

标题 : 大小匹配

作者 : Ilija I. Zovko , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.10262

说明 : -

Abstract : We show that filling an order with a large number of distinct counterparts incurs additional market impact, as opposed to filling the order with a small number of counterparts. For best execution, therefore, it may be beneficial to opportunistically fill orders with as few counterparts as possible in Large in scale (LIS) venues. This article introduces the concept of concentrated trading, a situation that occurs when a large fraction of buying or selling in a given time period is done by one or a few traders, for example when executing a large order. Using London Stock Exchange data, we show that concentrated trading suffers price impact in addition to impact caused by (smart) order routing. However, when matched with similarly concentrated counterparts on the other side of the market, the impact is greatly reduced. This suggests that exposing an order on LIS venues is expected to result in execution performance improvement.

摘要 : 我们的研究表明，与使用少量对应方填充订单相比，用大量不同的对应方填充订单会产生额外的市场影响。因此，为了达到最佳执行效果，在大型（LIS）场所，有机会地用尽可能少的对应方来完成订单可能是有益的。本文介绍集中交易的概念，集中交易是指一个或几个交易者在一定时间内进行大量买卖的情况，例如在执行一个大订单时。利用伦敦证券交易所的数据，我们发现集中交易除了受到（智能）订单路径的影响外，还受到价格的影响。然而，当与市场另一端同样集中的同行相匹配时，影响将大大降低。这表明，在LIS场馆公开订单有望提高执行绩效。
