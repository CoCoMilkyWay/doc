\[1\] Reinventing the Utility for DERs

标题 : 重新发明的实用程序

作者 : Rabab Haider, David D'Achiardi, Venkatesh Venkataramanan, Anurag Srivastava, Anjan Bose, Anuradha M. Annaswamy , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01269

说明 : -

Abstract : The increasing penetration of intermittent renewables, storage devices, and flexible loads is introducing operational challenges in distribution grids. The proper coordination and scheduling of these resources using a distributed approach is warranted, and can only be achieved through local retail markets employing transactive energy schemes. To this end, we propose a distribution level retail market operated by a Distribution System Operator (DSO), which schedules DERs and determines the real time distribution level Locational Marginal Price (d LPM). The retail market is built using a distributed Proximal Atomic Coordination (PAC) algorithm, which solves the optimal power flow model while accounting for network physics, rendering locationally and temporally varying d LMPs. A numerical study of the market structure is carried out via simulations of the IEEE 123 node network using data from ISO NE and Eversource in Massachusetts, US. The market performance is compared to existing retail practices, including demand response (DR) with no export rules and net metering. The DSO centric market increases DER utilization, permits continual market participation for DR, lowers electricity rates for customers, and eliminates the subsidies inherent to net metering programs. The resulting lower revenue stream for the DSO highlights the evolving business model of the modern utility, moving from commoditized markets towards performance based ratemaking.

摘要 : 间歇性可再生能源、存储设备和灵活负载的日益普及，给配电网带来了运营挑战。使用分布式方法对这些资源进行适当的协调和调度是必要的，并且只能通过采用交易性能源方案的本地零售市场来实现。为此，我们提出了一个由配电系统运营商（DSO）运营的配电级零售市场，该市场对配电系统进行调度，并确定配电级的实时位置边际价格（dlpm）。零售市场采用分布式近端原子协调（PAC）算法建立，该算法在考虑网络物理的前提下，求解最优潮流模型，呈现出位置和时间变化的d-lmp。利用美国马萨诸塞州isone和Eversource的数据对ieee123节点网络进行了仿真，对市场结构进行了数值研究。市场表现与现有零售实践相比较，包括没有出口规则和净计量的需求响应（DR）。以DSO为中心的市场提高了DER利用率，允许DR持续参与市场，降低了客户的电价，并消除了净计量计划固有的补贴。DSO由此产生的较低收入流凸显了现代公用事业不断发展的商业模式，从商品化市场转向基于绩效的费率制定。

\[2\] A Stochastic Time Series Model for Predicting Financial Trends using NLP

标题 : 用NLP预测金融趋势的随机时间序列模型

作者 : Pratyush Muthukumar, Jie Zhong , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01290

说明 : Comments: 16 pages, 7 figures

Abstract : Stock price forecasting is a highly complex and vitally important field of research. Recent advancements in deep neural network technology allow researchers to develop highly accurate models to predict financial trends. We propose a novel deep learning model called ST GAN, or Stochastic Time series Generative Adversarial Network, that analyzes both financial news texts and financial numerical data to predict stock trends. We utilize cutting edge technology like the Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) to learn the correlations among textual and numerical data over time. We develop a new method of training a time series GAN directly using the learned representations of Naive Bayes sentiment analysis on financial text data alongside technical indicators from numerical data. Our experimental results show significant improvement over various existing models and prior research on deep neural networks for stock price forecasting.

摘要 : 股票价格预测是一个非常复杂和非常重要的研究领域。深度神经网络技术的最新进展使研究人员能够开发出高精度的模型来预测金融趋势。我们提出了一种新的深度学习模型，称为随机时间序列生成对抗网络（ST-GAN），它分析金融新闻文本和金融数值数据来预测股票走势。我们利用尖端技术，如生成对抗网络（GAN）来学习文本数据和数字数据之间的相关性。我们开发了一种新的方法来训练一个时间序列GAN直接使用朴素贝叶斯情感分析的金融文本数据和数字数据的技术指标的学习表示。实验结果表明，与已有的各种模型和已有的深度神经网络股价预测方法相比，本文的方法有了很大的改进。

\[3\] Event Driven LSTM For Forex Price Prediction

标题 : 事件驱动的LSTM在外汇价格预测中的应用

作者 : Ling Qi, Matloob Khushi, Josiah Poon , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01499

说明 : -

Abstract : The majority of studies in the field of AI guided financial trading focus on purely applying machine learning algorithms to continuous historical price and technical analysis data. However, due to non stationary and high volatile nature of Forex market most algorithms fail when put into real practice. We developed novel event driven features which indicate a change of trend in direction. We then build long deep learning models to predict a retracement point providing a perfect entry point to gain maximum profit. We use a simple recurrent neural network (RNN) as our baseline model and compared with short term memory (LSTM), bidirectional long short term memory (BiLSTM) and gated recurrent unit (GRU). Our experiment results show that the proposed event driven feature selection together with the proposed models can form a robust prediction system which supports accurate trading strategies with minimal risk. Our best model on 15 minutes interval data for the EUR GBP currency achieved RME 0.006x10 ( 3) , RMSE 2.407x10 ( 3), MAE 1.708x10 ( 3), MAPE 0.194 outperforming previous studies.

摘要 : 人工智能引导的金融交易领域的大多数研究都集中在将机器学习算法应用于连续的历史价格和技术分析数据上。然而，由于外汇市场的非平稳性和高波动性，大多数算法在实际应用中失败。我们开发了新的事件驱动特征，表明了方向的变化趋势。然后，我们建立长期的深度学习模型，预测一个回溯点，提供一个完美的切入点，以获得最大的利润。我们使用简单的递归神经网络（RNN）作为基线模型，并与短时记忆（LSTM）、双向长短时记忆（BiLSTM）和选通递归单元（GRU）进行了比较。我们的实验结果表明，所提出的事件驱动特征选择与所提出的模型可以形成一个稳健的预测系统，以最小的风险支持准确的交易策略。我们关于欧元-英镑货币的15分钟区间数据的最佳模型达到RME 0.006x10（3），RMSE 2.407x10（3），MAE 1.708x10（3），MAPE 0.194，优于先前的研究。

\[4\] The Macroeconomic Impacts of Entitlements

标题 : 应享权利的宏观经济影响

作者 : Ateeb Akhter Shah Syed, Kaneez Fatima, Riffat Arshad , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01609

说明 : -

Abstract : The worries expressed by Alan Greenspan that the long run economic growth of the United States will fade away due to increasing burden of entitlements motivated us to empirically investigate the impact of entitlements of key macroeconomic variables. To examine this contemporary issue, we estimate a vector error correction model is used to analyze the impact of entitlements on the price level, real output, and the long term interest rate. The results show that a shock to entitlements leads to decrease in output and lends support to the assertion made by Alan Greenspan. Several robustness checks are conducted and the results of the model qualitatively remains unchanged.

摘要 : 格林斯潘（alangreenspan）所表达的对美国长期经济增长将因应享权利负担增加而消失的担忧，促使我们对关键宏观经济变量应享权利的影响进行实证研究。为了检验这个当代问题，我们估计一个向量误差修正模型被用来分析权利对价格水平、实际产出和长期利率的影响。结果表明，权利受到冲击会导致产出减少，并支持格林斯潘的主张。进行了若干鲁棒性检验，模型的结果定性地保持不变。

\[5\] Resource Availability in the Social Cloud

标题 : 社交云中的资源可用性

作者 : Pramod C. Mane, Nagarajan Krishnamurthy, Kapil Ahuja , 类别 ：Computer Science and Game Theory (cs.GT)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01071

说明 : Comments: 11 pages, 10 figures

Abstract : This paper focuses on social cloud formation, where agents are involved in a closeness based conditional resource sharing and build their resource sharing network themselves. The objectives of this paper are (1) to investigate the impact of agents decisions of link addition and deletion on their local and global resource availability, (2) to analyze spillover effects in terms of the impact of link addition between a pair of agents on others utility, (3) to study the role of agents closeness in determining what type of spillover effects these agents experience in the network, and (4) to model the choices of agents that suggest with whom they want to add links in the social cloud. The findings include the following. Firstly, agents decision of link addition (deletion) increases (decreases) their local resource availability. However, these observations do not hold in the case of global resource availability. Secondly, in a connected network, agents experience either positive or negative spillover effect and there is no case with no spillover effects. Agents observe no spillover effects if and only if the network is disconnected and consists of more than two components (sub networks). Furthermore, if there is no change in the closeness of an agent (not involved in link addition) due to a newly added link, then the agent experiences negative spillover effect. Although an increase in the closeness of agents is necessary in order to experience positive spillover effects, the condition is not sufficient. By focusing on parameters such as closeness and shortest distances, we provide conditions under which agents choose to add links so as to maximise their resource availability.

摘要 : 本文主要研究社会云的形成，即主体参与基于贴近度的有条件资源共享，并构建自己的资源共享网络。本文的目的是：（1）研究代理的链接添加和删除决策对其本地和全球资源可用性的影响；（2）从一对代理之间的链接添加对其他效用的影响的角度分析溢出效应；（3）研究代理的接近性在决定何种类型的代理中的作用这些代理在网络中体验到的溢出效应，以及（4）对代理的选择进行建模，这些代理建议他们希望与谁在社交云中添加链接。研究结果包括以下几个方面。首先，代理的链路添加（删除）决策增加（减少）了本地资源的可用性。然而，这些观察结果并不适用于全球资源可用性的情况。其次，在一个连通网络中，代理人会经历正溢出效应或负溢出效应，不存在没有溢出效应的情况。当且仅当网络断开并且由两个以上的组件（子网络）组成时，代理观察不到溢出效应。此外，如果由于新添加的链接，代理（不涉及链接添加）的接近度没有变化，那么代理将经历负溢出效应。虽然为了体验积极的溢出效应，需要增加代理的接近度，但条件是不够的。通过关注接近度和最短距离等参数，我们提供了代理选择添加链接的条件，以最大限度地提高其资源可用性。

\[6\] Limiting Value of the Kolkata Index for Social Inequality and a Possible Social Constant

标题 : 加尔各答社会不平等指数的极限值和可能的社会常数

作者 : Asim Ghosh, Bikas K Chakrabarti , 类别 ：Physics and Society (physics.soc-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01527

说明 : -

Abstract : Based on some analytic structural properties of the Gini and Kolkata indices for social inequality, as obtained from a generic form of the Lorenz function, and some more new observations on the citation statistics of individual authors (including Nobel laureates), we make a conjecture that about 14 of people or papers or social conflicts tend to earn or attract or cause about 86 of wealth or citations or deaths in very competitive situations in markets, universities or wars. This is a modified form of the (more than a) century old 80 20 law of Pareto in economy (not visible today because of various welfare and other strategies) and gives an universal value ( 0.86 ) of social (inequality) constant or number.

摘要 : 根据洛伦兹函数的一般形式得到的基尼和加尔各答社会不平等指数的一些分析结构性质，以及对个别作者（包括诺贝尔奖获得者）引文统计的一些新的观察结果，我们推测大约有14个人或论文或社会冲突倾向于在竞争激烈的市场、大学或战争中赚取、吸引或导致大约86%的财富、荣誉或死亡。这是80-20世纪帕累托经济定律的一种修正形式（由于各种福利和其他策略，今天看不见），并给出了社会（不平等）常数或数的普适值（0.86）。

\[7\] From optimal martingales to randomized dual optimal stopping

标题 : 从最优鞅到随机对偶最优停止

作者 : Denis Belomestny, John Schoenmakers , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01533

说明 : -

Abstract : In this article we study and classify optimal martingales in the dual formulation of optimal stopping problems. In this respect we distinguish between weakly optimal and surely optimal martingales. It is shown that the family of weakly optimal and surely optimal martingales may be quite large. On the other hand it is shown that the Doob martingale, that is, the martingale part of the Snell envelope, is in a certain sense the most robust surely optimal martingale under random perturbations. This new insight leads to a novel randomized dual martingale minimization algorithm that doesn t require nested simulation. As a main feature, in a possibly large family of optimal martingales the algorithm efficiently selects a martingale that is as close as possible to the Doob martingale. As a result, one obtains the dual upper bound for the optimal stopping problem with low variance.

摘要 : 本文研究了最优停止问题对偶形式中的最优鞅，并对其进行了分类。在这方面，我们区分弱最优鞅和确定最优鞅。证明了弱最优鞅族和确定最优鞅族可能是相当大的。另一方面，证明了Doob鞅，即Snell包络的鞅部分，在某种意义上是随机扰动下最稳健的确定最优鞅。这种新的见解导致了一种新的随机对偶鞅最小化算法，不需要嵌套模拟。作为一个主要特征，在一个可能很大的最优鞅族中，该算法有效地选择了一个尽可能接近Doob鞅的鞅。得到了低方差最优停止问题的对偶上界。
