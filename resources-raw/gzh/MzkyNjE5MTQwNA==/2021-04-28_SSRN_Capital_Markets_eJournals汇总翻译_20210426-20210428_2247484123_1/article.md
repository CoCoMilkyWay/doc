\[1\] COVID-19, Volatility Dynamics, and Sentiment Trading

标题:COVID-19，波动动力学和情绪交易

作者:Kose John,Jingrui (Victoria) Li

出处:Journal of Banking and Finance, Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3833059

Abstract : In this paper, we study how different categories of crucial COVID-19 information influence price dynamics in stock and option markets during the period from 01/21/20 to 01/31/21. We present a theoretical model in which the behavioral traders make perceptual errors based on the intensity of sentiment arising from different types of news. In addition to the magnitude and direction of the news and its payoff relevance to security prices, other factors such as fear, emotion, and social media can influence the sentiment level. Using Google search data, we construct novel proxies for the sentiment levels induced by five categories of news, COVID, Market, Lockdown, Banking, and Government relief efforts. If the relative presence of behavioral traders in the stock market exceeds that in the option market, different predictions obtain for the effect of sentiment indices on jump volatility of the VIX index, the S&P 500 index, and the S&P 500 Banks index. We find that the jump component in the VIX index is increasing significantly with COVID index, Market index, Lockdown index, and Banking index. However, only COVID index and Market index increase the jump component of realized volatility of the stock indices (S&P 500 index and S&P 500 Banks index). The Government relief efforts index decreases this jump component. Banking and Lockdown index reduce jump volatility in the S&P 500 index and S&P 500 Banks index, but only with a delay of 5 days. These results are consistent with the predictions of our model.

Keywords : COVID-19, coronavirus, virus, news, volatility, jumps, VIX, market, lockdown, government relief, banking, textual analysis, sentiment index, Google search index

Abstract :本文研究了20年1月21日至21年1月31日期间，不同类别的关键COVID-19信息对股票和期权市场价格动态的影响。我们提出了一个理论模型，在该模型中，行为交易者根据不同类型的新闻所引起的情绪强度而产生知觉错误。除了新闻的大小和方向以及它的收益与证券价格的相关性外，其他因素如恐惧、情绪和社交媒体也会影响情绪水平。利用Google搜索数据，我们构建了由新闻、COVID、市场、封锁、银行业和政府救济五个类别引起的情绪水平的新代理。如果股票市场中行为交易者的相对存在超过了期权市场中的行为交易者，那么情绪指数对VIX指数、S&p500指数和S&p500银行指数跳跃波动性的影响会有不同的预测。我们发现，波动率指数中的跳跃成分随着COVID指数、市场指数、锁定指数和银行指数的增加而显著增加。然而，只有COVID指数和市场指数增加了股票指数（S&p500指数和S&p500银行指数）已实现波动的跳跃分量。政府救济努力指数降低了这一跳跃成分。银行业和锁定指数降低了标准普尔500指数和标准普尔500银行指数的跳跃波动性，但只延迟了5天。这些结果与我们模型的预测是一致的。

Keywords :COVID-19，冠状病毒，病毒，新闻，波动性，跳跃，波动率，市场，锁定，政府救济，银行业，文本分析，情绪指数，谷歌搜索指数

\[3\] IPO Performance: A Cross-Country Comparison of the effect of Regulations

标题:IPO绩效：监管效果的跨国比较

作者:C. N. V. Krishnan,Yu He

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3833813

Abstract : We compare short-term and longer-term returns around IPOs, as well as post-issue performance, between US and China IPOs. Short-term abnormal returns are higher for China IPOs than US IPOs, both in univariate test (difference of means and medians tests), and in multivariate tests, controlling for other variables and fixed effects. We postulate that this is due to the different regulations between the two countries that could lead to more underpricing and constraints on reaching true value, in the short run, for China IPOs. However, the upward price pressure leading to superior stock returns does not last. In the longer-term, not only do China and US IPO firms have similar abnormal stock returns – on average negative as documented in the extant literature - but also the firms’ operating performance, on average, as well as their book-to-market ratios (a proxy for future growth options) are not significantly different, in both univariate and multivariate tests.

Keywords : Initial Public Offerings, US IPOs, China IPOs, Regulations, Abnormal Returns, Post Issue Performance, Post Issue Returns

Abstract :我们比较了中美IPO的短期和长期回报以及发行后的表现。无论是在单变量检验（均值和中位数差异检验）中，还是在控制其他变量和固定效应的多变量检验中，中国ipo的短期异常收益率都高于美国ipo。我们假设，这是由于两国不同的监管规定，可能导致中国ipo在短期内实现真正价值时出现更多的低估和限制。然而，价格上涨的压力导致股票收益率的优势并没有持续下去。从长期来看，中国和美国的IPO公司不仅具有相似的异常股票回报率（如现有文献所述，平均为负），而且公司的平均经营业绩以及账面市盈率（代表未来增长选择）也没有显著差异，在单变量和多变量检验中。

Keywords :首次公开发行（IPO）、美国IPO、中国IPO、监管、异常回报、发行后表现、发行后回报

Abstract : This work presents a disciplined convex programming framework for Kelly criterion in portfolio optimization based on exponential cone programming. This framework allows us to incorporate mean logarithmic return in problems like maximize mean logarithmic return subject to a risk constraint, maximize risk adjusted logarithmic return and constraints on mean logarithmic return. \[5\] Retail Investor Trade and the Pricing of Earnings

标题:散户交易与收益定价

作者:Jeremy Michels

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3833565

Abstract : I examine how retail investor trade is associated with the pricing of earnings by measuring retail investor trade with the number of Robinhood users holding a firm’s shares. My findings show that net retail purchasing during the earnings announcement is associated with a more pronounced market response to earnings surprises for positive earnings surprises but a more muted response for negative earnings surprises. Further intraday analysis suggests that retail investors respond to more extreme stock returns following the earnings announcement instead of the earnings news itself. Finally, in smaller firms, and for both the most positive and negative earnings surprises, returns drift upward following the earnings announcement when retail trade is high. Overall, the results show that retail trade is associated with significant changes in how the market prices earnings information, both in the immediate earnings announcement window and over longer horizons.

Keywords : Retail investor, individual investor, Robinhood, earnings announcement, post-earnings-announcement drift

Abstract :我通过测量持有公司股票的Robinhood用户的数量来检验散户投资者交易与收益定价之间的关系。我的研究结果表明，净零售购买在收益公告期间与更明显的市场反应收益惊喜积极的收益惊喜，但更沉默的反应消极的收益惊喜。进一步的盘中分析表明，散户投资者在公布财报后会对更极端的股票回报做出反应，而不是财报本身。最后，在规模较小的公司中，无论是最积极的还是最消极的盈利惊喜，当零售业处于高位时，收益会随着盈利公告而向上漂移。总体而言，研究结果显示，无论是在即时盈利公告窗口还是在较长的时间内，零售业与市场价格如何反映盈利信息的显著变化相关。

Keywords :散户投资者，个人投资者，罗比尼奥，收益公告，收益公告后漂移

\[6\] Impediments to Active Stock Selection and the Growth in Passive Fund Management

标题:主动选股障碍与被动基金管理成长

作者:Jeffrey A. Busse,Kiseo Chung,Badrinath Kottimukkalur

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3833523

Abstract : During the last decade, assets of passive funds have swelled while active funds have lost market share. This growth in passive investments coincides with a substantially more challenging environment for active stock selection, as reflected in a lower fraction of stocks with positive alpha, lower idiosyncratic volatility, and higher aggregate liquidity. As the opportunities to discover alpha decrease, the relation between alpha and fund costs turns significantly negative. To compensate, active managers reduce expenses and fund turnover. Nonetheless, the inverse relation between fund costs and performance leads to greater predictability in fund performance, a stronger inverse relation between fund costs and investor flows, and greater sensitivity of investor flows to past performance.

Keywords :共同基金、被动投资、绩效持续性、流量绩效关系

\[7\] Virtual Competition and Cost of Capital: Evidence from Telehealth

标题:虚拟竞争与资本成本：来自远程医疗的证据

作者:Kimberly Rodgers Cornaggia,Xuelin Li,Zihan Ye

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3833193

Abstract : Using the staggered implementation of telehealth parity laws in the U.S. Healthcare industry, we find causal evidence that virtual competition affects U.S. hospitals' cost of capital through a credit risk channel. Financial statements indicate that rural hospitals lose patients to urban hospitals in the same state after states require equivalent reimbursement of remote and in-person services. These effects increase rural hospital bankruptcy risk indicated by leverage and Z-Score. This increased financial stress translates into lower credit ratings and a higher cost of capital for rural hospitals. Controlling for bond characteristics, we find that affected rural hospitals' new bond costs rise by 20 - 38 bps relative to urban hospital bonds issued in the same state and in the same year. Secondary market yields of outstanding rural bonds increase by a significant 8 - 17 bps. Overall, we conclude that virtual competition in healthcare provision exacerbates healthcare inequality between rural and urban areas.

Keywords : telehealth, municipal bond, healthcare finance, healthcare inequality, bankruptcy

Abstract :通过在美国医疗行业交错实施远程医疗平价法，我们发现了虚拟竞争通过信用风险渠道影响美国医院资本成本的因果证据。财务报表显示，在各州要求对远程和当面服务给予同等补偿后，乡村医院将病人输给了同州的城市医院。这些影响增加了农村医院破产风险的杠杆率和Z评分。这种增加的财务压力转化为较低的信用评级和较高的资金成本为农村医院。通过对债券特征的控制，我们发现，受影响的农村医院的新债券成本较同期发行的城市医院债券上升了20-38个基点。未偿农村债券的二级市场收益率显著上升8-17个基点。总的来说，我们的结论是，医疗服务的虚拟竞争加剧了城乡之间的医疗不平等。

Keywords :远程医疗、市政债券、医疗金融、医疗不平等、破产

\[8\] Estimating Future VaR from Value Samples and Applications to Future Initial Margin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3832972

Abstract : Predicting future values at risk (fVaR) is an important problem in finance. They arise in the modelling of future initial margin requirements for counterparty credit risk and future market risk VaR. One is also interested in derived quantities such as: i) Dynamic Initial Margin (DIM) and Margin Value Adjustment (MVA) in the counterparty risk context; and ii) risk weighted assets (RWA) and Capital Value Adjustment (KVA) for market risk. \[9\] Which Corporate ESG News does the Market React to?

标题:市场对哪个企业ESG新闻有反应？

作者:George Serafeim,Aaron Yoon

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3832698

Abstract : Using a dataset that classifies firm-level ESG news as positive and negative, we examine how stock prices react to different types of ESG news. We analyze 111,020 firm–day observations for 3,126 companies and find that prices react only to issues identified as financially material for a given industry by sustainability accounting standards, and the reaction is larger for news that is positive, receive more attention, and that is related to social capital issues. We conclude that investors differentiate in their reactions based on whether the news is likely to affect a company’s fundamentals, and therefore their reactions are motivated by a financial rather than a nonpecuniary motive.

Keywords : ESG, information, news, market reaction

Abstract :利用一个将企业级ESG新闻分为正面和负面的数据集，我们考察了股票价格对不同类型ESG新闻的反应。我们对3126家公司的111020个公司日观察数据进行了分析，发现价格仅对可持续性会计准则确定的某一行业具有财务重要性的问题作出反应，而对积极、受到更多关注以及与社会资本问题相关的新闻的反应更大。我们的结论是，投资者的反应是根据消息是否可能影响公司的基本面来区分的，因此他们的反应是出于财务动机而不是非财务动机。

Keywords :ESG、信息、新闻、市场反应

\[10\] Jumps and Liquidity in the U.S. Stock Market

标题:美国股市的跳跃和流动性

作者:Rohit Goel,Sheheryar Malik

出处:International Monetary Fund, October 2018

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3826653

Abstract : We use a new framework to analyze the liquidity trends in the US equity markets, based on the intra-day price trend. The analysis suggests that the proportion of daily price variation explained by jumps (either small or large) is at a historical low. Furthermore while small jumps (which are generally liquidity related) are less common, but they increase significantly during the stress events. Overall, these findings suggest that liquidity has not materially deteriorated in the U.S. equity market.

Keywords : market liquidity, S&P 500, US Equities, jumps, spectral analysis

Abstract :我们使用一个新的框架，以日内价格趋势为基础，分析美国股市的流动性趋势。分析表明，由跳跃（小跳跃或大跳跃）解释的每日价格变化的比例处于历史最低水平。此外，虽然小跳跃（通常与流动性相关）不太常见，但在压力事件期间它们会显著增加。总体而言，这些调查结果表明，美国股市的流动性并未出现实质性恶化。

Keywords :市场流动性，标准普尔500指数，美国股市，跳跃，频谱分析
