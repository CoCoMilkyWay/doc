\[1\] The local innovation spillovers of listed firms

标题:上市公司的地方创新溢出

作者:Adrien Matray

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001367

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.009

Abstract : This paper provides evidence of local innovation spillovers (i.e., innovation by one firm fostering innovation by neighboring firms). First, I document that exogenous shocks to innovation by listed firms affect innovation by private firms in the same geographical area and that such local innovation spillovers decline rapidly with distance. Second, these local innovation spillovers stem from knowledge diffusing locally through two channels: learning across local firms and inventors moving from their employer to both existing firms and newly started spin-outs. Finally, I study the two-way relations between innovation spillovers and the availability of capital. I find that local innovation spillovers cause venture capital funds from outside the area to invest more in the local area, and that capital availability amplifies local innovation spillovers.

Keywords : Innovation spillovers;Knowledge diffusion;Patents;Financing of innovation

Abstract :本文提供了地方创新溢出的证据（即一个企业的创新促进了邻近企业的创新）。首先，我发现上市公司创新的外生冲击会影响同一地理区域内民营企业的创新，而且这种局部创新溢出效应会随着距离的增加而迅速下降。第二，这些本地创新溢出源于知识通过两个渠道在本地扩散：在本地公司和发明家之间的学习，从他们的雇主转移到现有的公司和新开始的螺旋输出（spin out）。最后，研究了创新溢出与资本可用性之间的双向关系。我发现，地方创新溢出会导致来自外部的风险投资基金更多地投资于地方，而资本的可获得性会放大地方创新溢出。

Keywords :创新溢出；知识扩散；专利；创新融资

\[2\] Lucky factors

标题:幸运因素

作者:Campbell R. Harvey,Yan Liu

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001410

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.014

Abstract : Identifying the factors that drive the cross-section of expected returns is challenging for at least three reasons. First, the choice of testing approach (time series versus cross-sectional) will deliver different sets of factors. Second, varying test portfolio sorts changes the importance of candidate factors. Finally, given the hundreds of factors that have been proposed, test multiplicity must be dealt with. We propose a new method that makes measured progress in addressing these key challenges. We apply our method in a panel regression setting and shed some light on the puzzling empirical result that the market factor drives the bulk of the variance of stock returns, but is often knocked out in cross-sectional tests. In our setup, the market factor is not eliminated. Further, we bypass arbitrary portfolio sorts and instead execute our tests on individual stocks with no loss in power. Finally, our bootstrap implementation, which allows us to impose the null hypothesis of no cross-sectional explanatory power, naturally controls for the multiple testing problem.

Keywords : Factors;Factor selection;Variable selection;Bootstrap;Data mining;Orthogonalization;Multiple testing;Predictive regressions;Fama–MacBeth;GRS

Abstract :确定驱动预期收益横截面的因素具有挑战性，至少有三个原因。首先，测试方法的选择（时间序列与横截面）将提供不同的因素集。第二，不同的测试组合类别改变了候选因素的重要性。最后，考虑到已经提出的数百个因素，必须处理测试多重性。我们提出了一种新方法，在应对这些关键挑战方面取得了可衡量的进展。我们将我们的方法应用于面板回归设置中，并揭示了令人费解的实证结果，即市场因素驱动了股票收益率的大部分方差，但在横截面测试中经常被剔除。在我们的设置中，市场因素并没有消除。此外，我们绕过了任意的投资组合分类，而是在没有动力损失的情况下对个股进行测试。最后，我们的bootstrap实现，允许我们强加没有横截面解释力的空假设，自然控制了多重测试问题。

Keywords :因素；因子选择；变量选择；引导；数据挖掘；正交化；多次试验；预测回归；法玛-麦克白；GRS公司

\[3\] Uncertainty, access to debt, and firm precautionary behavior

标题:不确定性、获得债务和坚定的预防行为

作者:Giovanni Favara,Janet Gao,Mariassunta Giannetti

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001379

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.010

Abstract : Better access to debt markets mitigates the effects of uncertainty on corporate policies. We establish this result using the staggered introduction of anti-recharacterization laws in US states. These laws enhanced firms’ ability to borrow by strengthening creditors’ rights to repossess collateral pledged in special purpose vehicles. After the passage of the laws, firms that face more uncertainty hoard less cash and increase payouts, leverage, and investment in intangible assets. Our findings suggest that better access to debt markets shields firms from fluctuations in uncertainty and decreases firms’ precautionary behavior, contributing to the deployment of cash and other internal resources to investment in intangible capital.

Keywords : Anti-recharacterization laws;SPVs;Creditor rights;Cash;Intangible assets;Geopolitical risk;Political uncertainty

Keywords :反再定性法；特殊目的公司；债权；现金；无形资产；地缘政治风险；政治不确定性

\[4\] Angels and venture capitalists: Substitutes or complements?

标题:天使和风险投资家：替代品还是补充品？

作者:Thomas Hellmann,Paul Schure,Dan H. Vo

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001288

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.001

Abstract : We analyze the funding of start-up companies across financing rounds, focusing on the dynamic interactions between angel investors and venture capitalists. Using unique data from British Columbia, Canada, we show that angels and venture capitalists are dynamic substitutes. This substitutes pattern applies across the performance range of companies. It is less pronounced for serial angels. An instrumental variable analysis, based on available investor tax credits, suggests that the substitutes pattern is driven by company characteristics. Overall, the evidence points to the existence of parallel streams of angel and venture capital funding, with fewer transitions between streams than is traditionally assumed.

\[5\] Macro risks and the term structure of interest rates

标题:宏观风险与利率期限结构

作者:Geert Bekaert,Eric Engstrom,Andrey Ermolov

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001252

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.03.011

Abstract : We use non-Gaussian features in U.S. macroeconomic data to identify aggregate supply and demand shocks while imposing minimal economic assumptions. Macro risks represent the variables that govern the time-varying variance, skewness, and higher-order moments of these two shocks, with ”good” (”bad”) variance associated with positive (negative) skewness. We document that macro risks significantly contribute to the variation of yields and risk premiums for nominal bonds. While overall bond risk premiums are countercyclical, an increase in aggregate demand variance significantly lowers risk premiums. Macro risks also significantly predict future realized bond return variances.

Keywords :债券收益的可预测性；定期保险费；宏观经济波动；商业周期；宏观风险因素

\[8\] Capital supply and corporate bond issuances: Evidence from mutual fund flows

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001264

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.03.012

Abstract : I examine how the investment behavior of bond mutual funds affects corporate financing decisions. Mutual funds that hold a firm’s existing bonds have a high propensity to acquire additional new issuances from the same firm. I utilize this stylized fact to construct a firm-specific bond capital supply measure by aggregating flows from a firm’s existing bondholders. Firms with a higher flow-driven capital supply are more likely to issue bonds, enjoy lower yields, and substitute away from equity financing and bank loans. 链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001306

Abstract : We propose a novel high-frequency decomposition of daily stock returns into news- and non-news-driven components, and uncover evidence of pervasive stock market underreaction to firm news. Abstract : Postcrisis regulations apply stricter liquidity rules to both money market funds (MMFs) and banks, requiring MMFs to do more overnight lending and banks to borrow longer-term. MMFs and banks resolve this dilemma by developing a “bundling” strategy across overnight and longer term markets. DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.006

Abstract : A 2006 rule change in the United States mandated that publicly traded firms provide more detailed disclosures about executives’ compensation plans. In response to the new disclosure requirements, Cournot firms with large market shares add revenue-based pay to their CEOs’ pay packages. \[12\] Out of sight no more? The effect of fee disclosures on 401(k) investment allocations

标题:再也看不见了？费用披露对401（k）投资分配的影响

作者:Mathias Kronlund,Veronika K. Pool,Clemens Sialm,Irina Stefanescu

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001355

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.008

Abstract : We examine the effects of a 2012 regulatory reform that mandated fee and performance disclosures for the investment options in 401(k) plans. We show that participants became significantly more attentive to expense ratios and short-term performance after the reform. The disclosure effects are stronger among plans with large average contributions per participant and are weaker for plans with many investment options. Additionally, these results are not driven by secular changes in investor behavior or sponsor-initiated changes to the investment menus.
