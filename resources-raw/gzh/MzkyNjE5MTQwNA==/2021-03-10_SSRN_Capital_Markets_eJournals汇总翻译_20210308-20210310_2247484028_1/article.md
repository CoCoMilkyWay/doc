\[1\] Litigation Risk, Financial Reporting Quality, and Critical Audit Matters in the Audit Report: Early U.S. Evidence

标题:诉讼风险、财务报告质量和审计报告中的关键审计事项：早期美国证据

作者:Valbona Sulcaj

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3771081

Abstract : This study contributes to the audit reporting literature by examining how disclosure of critical audit matters (CAMs) in the audit report varies with perceived litigation risk and financial reporting quality. Consistent with the litigation hypothesis (Skinner, 1994), I find a positive association between litigation risk and the number of CAMs in the audit report, suggesting that auditors try to preempt negative consequences from shareholder lawsuits by reporting more CAMs when litigation risk is higher. The results also show the number of reported CAMs increases when financial reporting quality decreases, suggesting that audit reports reflect the inherent quality of financial statements. Further, in presence of high litigation risk, the CAM language exhibits lower readability as the quality of financial reporting decreases. A detailed examination shows the lower readability is driven by the auditor response rather than CAM description, consistent with the obfuscation hypothesis (Bloomfield, 2008). Overall, these results are consistent with auditors touching upon issues they are legally obligated to without necessarily providing clarity to financial statement users. This study also contributes to audit fees literature by showing that audit fees and audit report delays are positively associated with the number of reported CAMs, suggesting that CAMs increase auditor effort and costs.

Keywords : Critical Audit Matters, Audit Report, Litigation Risk, Financial Reporting Quality, Audit Fees, Disclosure Readability.

Abstract :这项研究有助于审计报告的文献研究如何披露关键审计事项（凸轮）在审计报告中不同的感知诉讼风险和财务报告质量。与诉讼假设（Skinner，1994）一致，我发现诉讼风险与审计报告中的cam数量正相关，这表明审计人员试图通过在诉讼风险较高时报告更多cam来抢占股东诉讼的负面后果。研究结果还显示，当财务报告质量下降时，报告的审计准则数量增加，这表明审计报告反映了财务报表的内在质量。此外，在存在高诉讼风险的情况下，随着财务报告质量的下降，CAM语言的可读性降低。详细的研究表明，较低的可读性是由审核员的反应而不是CAM描述驱动的，这与模糊假设（Bloomfield，2008）是一致的。总的来说，这些结果与审计师触及法律上有义务处理的问题是一致的，而不必向财务报表使用者提供清晰的信息。这项研究也有助于审计费用的文献显示，审计费用和审计报告延误正相关的数量报告审计机关，这表明审计机关增加审计工作和成本。

Keywords :关键审计事项、审计报告、诉讼风险、财务报告质量、审计费用、披露可读性。

\[2\] Panic Selling When Disaster Strikes: Evidence in the Bond and Stock Markets

标题:灾难来临时的恐慌性抛售：债券和股票市场的证据

作者:Thanh Huynh,Ying Xia

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3767376

Abstract : This study uses disaggregated establishment-level data to identify a firm’s exposure to physical climate risk and examines investors’ reaction to natural disasters in both the U.S. corporate bond and stock markets. We find that, when a firm is exposed to disasters, investors overreact by depressing the current bond and stock prices, causing future returns to be higher. However, firms with a strong environmental profile experience lower selling pressure on their bonds and stocks, even though their fundamentals weakened following disasters. The evidence suggests that corporate investment in improving environmental profiles pays off when climate change risk is materialized.

Keywords : Physical climate risk, natural disasters, overreaction, establishment-level data

Abstract :这项研究使用分类机构层面的数据来确定一家公司的实际气候风险敞口，并在美国公司债券和股票市场考察投资者对自然灾害的反应。我们发现，当一家公司面临灾难时，投资者会过度反应，压低当前的债券和股票价格，导致未来回报率更高。然而，环境状况良好的公司债券和股票的抛售压力较低，尽管它们的基本面在灾难后有所减弱。有证据表明，当气候变化风险成为现实时，企业在改善环境状况方面的投资将获得回报。

Keywords :物理气候风险、自然灾害、过度反应、机构级数据

\[5\] Turn-of-the-Month Effect: New Evidence from an Emerging Stock Market

标题:月交效应：新兴股市的新证据

作者:Nuri Volkan Kayacetin,Senad Lekpek

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3766467

Abstract : This paper analyzes the turn-of-the-month (ToM) effect in Turkish equity returns. We show that the ToM effect is strongly significant in BIST100 index over 1988–2014, and distinct from other calendar anomalies. In particular, the mean daily index return is 0.46% in the three-day period that covers the last trading day of each month and the first two trading days of the next month, and 0.09% in the remaining days. The ToM effect is more significant following months with (a) significant information flow and (b) above average market performance, and the fraction of index returns generated within the ToM period increases secularly from 39% over 1988–1996 to 49% over 1997–2005 and to 86% over 2006–2014. A similar month-end seasonal does not exist in index trading volume or realized volatility, ruling out standard liquidity or risk-based explanations. Estimating an e-GARCH model with daily index returns, however, we link the ToM effect to a decline in expected volatility in the days leading to month-turns. These findings resonate best with a story where gradual resolution of uncertainty following high information risk periods releases a large pool of “liquid funds” accumulated during such periods into the equity market, creating an abundance of liquidity and pushing equity prices up.

Keywords : Calendar Anomalies, Turn-of-the-Month, Conditional Volatility, Information Risk

Abstract :本文分析了土耳其股票收益的月交效应。我们发现，1988-2014年BIST100指数的ToM效应非常显著，与其他日历异常不同。特别是在每个月最后一个交易日和次月前两个交易日的三天内，日均指数收益率为0.46%，其余各天为0.09%。随着（a）大量信息流和（b）高于平均水平的市场表现，ToM效应在接下来的几个月更为显著，在ToM期间产生的指数收益率从1988-1996年的39%增长到1997-2005年的49%，并在2006-2014年增长到86%。在指数成交量或已实现波动率中不存在类似的月末季节性，排除了标准流动性或基于风险的解释。然而，在估计一个具有每日指数收益率的e-GARCH模型时，我们将ToM效应与月份转换前几天预期波动率的下降联系起来。这些发现与这样一个故事最为契合：在高信息风险时期之后，不确定性的逐步解决将在这些时期积累的大量“流动资金”释放到股票市场，创造了丰富的流动性，并推动股票价格上涨。

Keywords :日历异常，月交，条件波动，信息风险

\[6\] The Financial Contagion Effect of the Subprime Crisis on Selected Developed Markets

Abstract : The purpose of this empirical study is to explore and compare the effects of subprime crisis on some of developed markets (e.g. France, Germany, the United Kingdom and Japan). The VECM model and Johansen’s cointegration approach (1988) have been used to verify the existence of potential short and long run relationships between the United States market, where the subprime crisis has been triggered, and the other markets. Abstract : We find that the order flow differential (OFD), a flight-to-quality measure constructed as the difference between large- and small-cap stock order flows, strongly and negatively forecasts output growth and interest rates in the U.S. The predictive ability of OFD for future macroeconomic fundamentals is robust to the inclusion of return factors and business cycle predictors, and it is thus a state variable candidate in the spirit of Merton (1973). \[8\] Valuing Exotic Options and Estimating Model Risk

标题:奇异期权的估值与模型风险估计

作者:Jay Cao,Jacky Chen,John C. Hull,Zissis Poulos

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3776322

Abstract : A common approach to valuing exotic options involves choosing a model and then determining its parameters to fit the volatility surface as closely as possible. We refer to this as the model calibration approach (MCA). This paper proposes an alternative approach where the points on the volatility surface are features input to a neural network. We refer to this as the volatility feature approach (VFA). We conduct experiments showing that VFA can be expected to outperform MCA for the volatility surfaces encountered in practice. Once the upfront computational time has been invested in developing the neural network, the valuation of exotic options using VFA is very fast. VFA is a useful tool for the estimation of model risk. We illustrate this using S&P 500 data for the 2001 to 2019 period.

Keywords : Exotic options, volatility surfaces, neural networks

Abstract :评估奇异期权的一种常见方法是选择一个模型，然后确定其参数，以尽可能接近波动率表面。我们称之为模型校准方法（MCA）。本文提出了另一种方法，将波动面上的点作为神经网络的特征输入。我们称之为波动特征法（VFA）。我们进行的实验表明，VFA可以预期优于MCA的波动面在实践中遇到。一旦前期的计算时间已经投入到开发神经网络中，使用VFA对奇异期权的估值就非常快。VFA是模型风险估计的有用工具。我们用标准普尔500指数2001年至2019年的数据来说明这一点。

Keywords :奇异期权，波动面，神经网络

\[9\] International Financial Contagion During the Subprime Crisis: Evidence from UK Financial Markets

标题:次贷危机期间的国际金融传染：来自英国金融市场的证据

作者:Zijun Liu,Yang Zhou,Zhiping Zhou

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3769738

Abstract : In this paper, we investigate the dynamics of contagion from the US low grade asset-backed securities (ABSs) market to UK financial markets during the 2007 subprime mortgage crisis and identify the contagion channels using both a single-state vector autoregressive (VAR) model and a Markov-switching vector autoregressive (MSVAR) model.
