\[1\] Should Defined Contribution Plans Include Private Equity Investments?

标题:设定提存计划是否应包括私募股权投资？

作者:Gregory W. Brown(University of North Carolina (UNC) at Chapel Hill - Finance Area),Keith Crouch(Burgiss),Andra C. Ghent(University of North Carolina (UNC) at Chapel Hill - Finance Area),Robert S. Harris(University of Virginia - Darden School of Business),Yael V. Hochberg(National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER); Rice University - Jesse H. Jones Graduate School of Business),Tim Jenkinson(University of Oxford - Said Business School; European Corporate Governance Institute (ECGI)),Steven N. Kaplan(University of Chicago - Booth School of Business; National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER); European Corporate Governance Institute (ECGI); University of Chicago - Polsky Center for Entrepreneurship),Richard Maxwell(University of North Carolina (UNC) at Chapel Hill - Frank Hawkins Kenan Institute of Private Enterprise),David T. Robinson(Fuqua School of Business, Duke University; National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER); Duke Innovation & Entrepreneurship Initiative)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3747684

Abstract : This paper evaluates the pros and cons of including private equity fund investments in defined contribution plans. Potential benefits include higher returns and improved diversification as well as a relatively safe method for accessing investments previously only available to institutions and the very wealthy. Despite these enticing benefits, they need to be weighed against potential challenges and costs that may arise from creating this broader access to private funds. The complicated structure and uncertainty around the mechanism to provide required liquidity backstops may bring increased fees or even disrupt the private fund model. Consequently, whether access to private investments provide a net benefit for DC plan participants will depend both on how private fund investments perform in the future as well as how institutional features around plan participation evolve.

Keywords : Private Equity, Defined Contribution Plans

Abstract :本文评估了将私募股权基金投资纳入设定提存计划的利弊。潜在的好处包括更高的回报率和更好的多样化，以及一种相对安全的方法来获取以前只有机构和非常富有的人才能获得的投资。尽管有这些诱人的好处，但它们仍需要与创造更广泛的私人资金渠道可能带来的潜在挑战和成本进行权衡。提供所需流动性支持的机制的复杂结构和不确定性可能带来费用增加，甚至扰乱私人基金模式。因此，获得私人投资是否能为DC计划参与者带来净收益，将取决于私人基金投资在未来的表现，以及围绕计划参与的制度特征如何演变。

Keywords :私募股权、固定缴款计划

\[2\] Sleep Disruption and Investor Processing of Earnings News: Evidence from Daylight Saving Time Advances

标题:睡眠中断与收益新闻的投资者处理：来自夏令时的证据

作者:Tyler J. Kleppe(University of Kentucky - Von Allmen School of Accountancy),Andrew T. Pierce(Oklahoma City University - Meinders School of Business),Zac Wiebe(University of Arkansas - Sam M. Walton College of Business),Teri Lombardi Yohn(Emory University Goizueta Business School)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3767211

Abstract : Despite extensive evidence on the effects of psychological and behavioral factors on investors’ processing of accounting information, little is known about how physiological factors affect the investor response to accounting disclosures. We use “spring forward” Daylight Saving Time (DST) phase advances, which have been shown to inhibit cognitive function by disrupting the human sleep cycle and circadian rhythm, to examine the effect of sleep disruption on investors’ reactions to earnings news. We find consistent evidence of a muted reaction to earnings news released during the week following a DST advance. We also find that this effect is stronger when transient institutional ownership is higher and, therefore, when investors are more likely to be trading on earnings news. We find no evidence of a decrease in EDGAR downloads or media attention during this period, suggesting that DST advances affect investors’ information integration, rather than information acquisition. Our inferences are strengthened through a placebo test performed during the previous (i.e., pre-2007) DST phasing regime and through alternative design choices and robustness tests. Overall, our findings suggest that sleep disruptions affect investors’ ability to integrate value-relevant earnings news into their trading decisions.

Keywords : Disclosure Processing, Earnings Response, Sleep Disruption, Daylight Saving Time

Abstract :尽管有大量的证据表明心理和行为因素对投资者会计信息处理的影响，但对生理因素如何影响投资者对会计信息披露的反应却知之甚少。我们使用“春季提前”夏令时（DST）阶段的进展，研究了睡眠中断对投资者对收益消息反应的影响，该阶段的进展已被证明通过扰乱人类睡眠周期和昼夜节律来抑制认知功能。我们发现一致的证据表明，在DST上涨后的一周内，投资者对财报消息反应平淡。我们还发现，当暂时性机构持股比例较高时，这种效应更强，因此，当投资者更有可能根据收益消息进行交易时。在此期间，我们没有发现EDGAR下载量或媒体关注度下降的迹象，这表明DST的进步影响了投资者的信息整合，而不是信息获取。我们的推论通过在先前（即2007年前）DST阶段制度期间进行的安慰剂试验以及通过替代设计选择和稳健性试验得到加强。总的来说，我们的研究结果表明睡眠中断会影响投资者将价值相关的盈利信息整合到交易决策中的能力。

Keywords :披露处理，收益反应，睡眠中断，夏令时

\[3\] Gendered Prices

标题:性别价格

作者:Renee B. Adams(University of Oxford; ABFER),Roman Kräussl(Luxembourg School of Finance; Hoover Institution, Stanford University),Marco A. Navone(Finance Discipline Group - UTS Business School; Bocconi University - CAREFIN - Centre for Applied Research in Finance; Financial Research Network (FIRN)),Patrick Verwijmeren(Erasmus University Rotterdam (EUR) - Erasmus School of Economics (ESE))

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3746124

Abstract : We provide evidence that culture is a source of pricing bias. In a sample of 1.9 million auction transactions in 49 countries, paintings by female artists sell at an unconditional discount of 42.1%. The gender discount increases with measures of country-level gender inequality — even in artist fixed effects regressions. Our results are robust to accounting for potential gender differences in art characteristics and their liquidity. Evidence from two experiments supports the argument that women's art may sell for less because it is made by women. However, the gender discount reduces over time as gender equality increases.

Keywords : Law of One Price, Bias, Art, Gender, Auction, Culture, Inequality

Abstract :我们提供的证据表明，文化是定价偏差的一个来源。在49个国家的190万宗拍卖交易中，女艺术家的作品以42.1%的无条件折扣成交。性别折扣随着国家一级性别不平等的衡量而增加，即使在固定效应回归中也是如此。我们的研究结果对于解释艺术特征及其流动性的潜在性别差异是有力的。来自两个实验的证据支持这样一种观点：女性艺术品可能卖得更低，因为它是由女性制作的。然而，随着时间的推移，性别折扣随着性别平等的增加而减少。

Keywords :一价定律，偏见，艺术，性别，拍卖，文化，不平等

\[4\] Liquidity of China’s Government Bond Market: Measures and Driving Forces

标题:我国国债市场流动性的测度与驱动力分析

作者:Gaofeng Han(Hong Kong Monetary Authority; University of California, Santa Cruz - Department of Economics),Hui Miao(International Monetary Fund (IMF)),Yabin Wang(Hong Kong Monetary Authority)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3756882

Abstract : We construct a daily liquidity index of China’s government bond market using transaction data from the national interbank market over the past twenty years. The index is a composite of popular price-based and quantity-based metrics of liquidity. The composite indexes, obtained by averaging across different metrics or by applying principal component analysis, both point to a better liquidity condition after 2010. Market liquidity swings appear to be highly correlated with domestic funding liquidity and financial market volatility, but display less correlation to global macro financial indicators. Our findings suggest that further deepening of government bond market would support domestic financial stability and monetary operations down the road.

Keywords : Government Bond, Bond Liquidity, Principal Component Analysis, Regime Switching Model

Abstract :利用全国银行间市场近20年的交易数据，构建了中国国债市场的日流动性指数。该指数综合了流行的基于价格和基于数量的流动性指标。通过对不同指标进行平均或应用主成分分析得出的综合指数都表明，2010年后流动性状况较好。市场流动性波动似乎与国内资金流动性和金融市场波动高度相关，但与全球宏观金融指标的相关性较小。我们的研究结果表明，进一步深化政府债券市场将支持国内金融稳定和货币运作的道路。

Keywords :政府债券，债券流动性，主成分分析，制度转换模型

\[5\] Bond Lending and the Law of One Price in China's Treasury Markets

标题:债券借贷与中国国债市场的一价定律

作者:Jacopo Magnani(EMLYON Business School),Yabin Wang(Hong Kong Monetary Authority)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3756891

Abstract : This paper examines how the introduction of bond lending in China's bond market has affected violations of the law of one price, measured by the yield spread between similar treasury bonds. To identify the effect of bond lending, we exploit the fact that in China identical bonds are traded on two segmented markets and bond lending has been introduced in only one of the two markets. We find that the introduction of bond lending has led to a decline in deviations from the law of one price. Consistent with an interpretation based on limits to arbitrage, a significant fraction of the deviations from the law of one price in our sample represent actual profit opportunities and the introduction of bond lending has reduced arbitrage profits.

Keywords : China’s Bond Markets, Bond Lending, Law of One Price

Abstract :本文考察了中国债券市场引入债券借贷对违反一价定律的影响，以类似国债之间的收益率差来衡量。为了确定债券借贷的影响，我们利用了这样一个事实：在中国，相同的债券在两个细分市场上交易，而债券借贷只在两个市场中的一个市场推出。我们发现，债券借贷的引入导致偏离一价定律的现象有所减少。与基于套利限制的解释一致，在我们的样本中，一个价格定律的很大一部分偏差代表了实际的利润机会，债券借贷的引入降低了套利利润。

Keywords :中国债券市场，债券借贷，一价定律
