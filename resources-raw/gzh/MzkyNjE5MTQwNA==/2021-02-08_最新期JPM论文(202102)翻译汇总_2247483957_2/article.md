\[2\] Work Harder: Diligent Rebalancing and Investment Horizon

标题:努力工作：勤奋的再平衡和投资视野

作者:Wai Lee and Pai Liu

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/3/17;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.201

Abstract : In an age of constant information flow, investors have to strike the right balance between incorporating incremental information into a portfolio and managing turnover before the end of the investment horizon. Using a tractable yet realistic model of asset and signal dynamics, the authors provide analytical rigor to justify the optimality of the common practice of updating investment views and portfolio positions as new information arrives. In analyzing different approaches to rebalancing, their results indicate that regularly updating the conditional information set achieves a higher information ratio than either waiting to rebalance until after the investment horizon is reached or using regular rebalancing based on a moving average of the past information set. The authors provide guidance on turnover management in conjunction with the information horizon of the signal.

TOPICS: Portfolio management/multi-asset allocation, portfolio construction, quantitative methods, statistical methods, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ Information quality decays according to the investment horizon of the signal.

\- ▪ Diligent updates of information and rebalancing achieve the highest information ratio.

\- ▪ Turnover and marginal improvement in the information ratio can be balanced.

Abstract:在一个信息不断流动的时代，投资者必须在将增量信息纳入投资组合和在投资期结束前管理营业额之间取得适当的平衡。利用一个易于处理但现实的资产和信号动力学模型，作者提供了严格的分析，以证明在新信息到达时更新投资视图和投资组合头寸这一常见做法的最佳性。在分析不同的再平衡方法时，他们的结果表明，定期更新条件信息集比等待达到投资期限或使用基于过去信息集移动平均值的定期再平衡获得更高的信息比率。作者结合信号的信息视界，为离职管理提供指导。

主题：投资组合管理/多资产配置，投资组合构建，定量方法，统计方法，绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 信息质量随着信号的投资期限而衰减。

\- ▪ 勤勉的信息更新和再平衡实现了最高的信息比率。

\- ▪ 流动性和边际信息比率的提高是可以平衡的。

\[3\] Portfolio Protection? It’s a Long (Term) Story…

标题:投资组合保护？这是一个长期的故事…

作者:Nicholas McQuinn, Ashwin Thapar and Dan Villalon

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/3/35;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.203

Abstract : Investors have a natural urge to protect their portfolios from sudden crashes. The authors argue that investors should instead focus on bad outcomes that unfold over longer periods because those tend to be more detrimental to the long-term goal of wealth accumulation. The authors show that options-based hedging can be effective over shorter periods but tends to weaken over time. Worse still, returns tend to be very punitive during prolonged bull markets. In contrast, risk-mitigating and diversifying strategies such as defensive equities, risk parity, alternative risk premiums, and trend-following have more consistently added value in the longer-lasting market drawdowns that matter most to investors—and, unlike puts, can profit in up as well as down markets. This latter point suggests a crucial advantage for these strategies: that unlike options-based hedging, it is never too late to consider diversifying into them.

TOPICS: Portfolio construction, tail risks, options

Key Finding

\- ▪ When it comes to drawdowns, many investors focus on depth, but length matters, too. Empirically, strategies that protect against longer-lasting tail events are more helpful to long-term wealth creation than strategies that protect against shorter-lived ones.

\- ▪ Options-based hedging strategies have been highly effective at delivering value over short-term bad outcomes for traditional portfolios, but their efficacy wanes at longer horizons—eventually underperforming 60/40 even in bad outcomes for 60/40.

\- ▪ In contrast, the authors present a range of alternative diversifying strategies that can provide more consistent and higher relative returns during longer-term bad outcomes, thus potentially benefitting investors when they need it the most.

Abstract:投资者自然有保护自己的投资组合免受突然崩盘的冲动。作者认为，投资者应该转而关注在较长时期内出现的不良结果，因为这些结果往往更不利于财富积累的长期目标。作者表明，基于期权的套期保值在较短的时间内是有效的，但随着时间的推移往往会减弱。更糟糕的是，在长期的牛市中，回报往往是非常惩罚性的。相比之下，风险缓释和多样化策略（如防御型股票、风险平价、另类风险溢价和趋势跟踪）在对投资者最为重要的长期市场下跌中具有更一致的增值作用，并且与看跌期权不同，可以在上涨和下跌市场中获利。后一点说明了这些策略的一个关键优势：与基于期权的对冲不同，考虑进行多样化投资永远不会太迟。

主题：投资组合构建，尾部风险，期权

Key Finding

\- ▪ 在资金缩减方面，许多投资者关注深度，但长度也很重要。从经验上看，防范持续时间较长的尾部事件的策略比防范持续时间较短的尾部事件的策略更有助于长期财富创造。

\- ▪ 基于期权的对冲策略在为传统投资组合提供短期不良结果的价值方面非常有效，但在较长的期限内，它们的有效性会减弱，甚至在60/40的不良结果中，最终表现也会低于60/40。

\- ▪ 与此相反，作者提出了一系列可供选择的多样化策略，在长期不良结果期间，这些策略可以提供更一致和更高的相对回报，从而在投资者最需要的时候潜在地使投资者受益。

\[4\] Model Risk in Risk Models: Quantifying Statistical Uncertainty in Active Risk

标题:风险模型中的模型风险：量化主动风险中的统计不确定性

作者:Kevin Khang

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/3/51;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.47.3.051

Abstract : Risk models commonly provide a portfolio’s ex ante active risk as a point forecast. Under the hood of risk models, this forecast relies on a bevy of statistical estimations that introduce uncertainty in the forecast. Failure to incorporate this uncertainty in the risk forecast can present an incomplete picture of the portfolio’s risk profile, particularly in periods of high volatility. This limitation may lead to an unintended risk-taking by portfolio managers. In this article, the author proposes a generalizable methodology to quantify the statistical uncertainty in an active risk forecast. This methodology can be applied to increase the precision with which portfolio managers take active risk, especially when volatility regimes change.

TOPICS: Portfolio management/multi-asset allocation, risk management, volatility measures

Key Finding

\- ▪ Active risk of a portfolio is commonly provided only as a point forecast by risk models. Statistical uncertainty in this forecast can be quantified in real time using a bootstrap approach.

\- ▪ This uncertainty is on the order of 25% of the risk forecast itself, and the amount of uncertainty generally rises in times of high volatility.

\- ▪ Real-time monitoring of the statistical uncertainty in a risk forecast can help increase the precision with which portfolio managers take risk, especially when volatility regimes change.

Abstract:风险模型通常提供投资组合的事前主动风险作为预测点。在风险模型的庇护下，这种预测依赖于一系列的统计估计，这些估计在预测中引入了不确定性。如果在风险预测中不考虑这种不确定性，可能会对投资组合的风险状况造成不完整的描述，特别是在高波动性时期。这种限制可能会导致投资组合经理的意外风险承担。在本文中，作者提出了一种通用的方法来量化的统计不确定性，在积极的风险预测。这种方法可以用来提高投资组合经理主动承担风险的精度，特别是当波动率制度发生变化时。

主题：投资组合管理/多资产配置、风险管理、波动性度量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 投资组合的主动风险通常仅作为风险模型的预测点提供。这种预测中的统计不确定性可以用bootstrap方法实时量化。

\- ▪ 这种不确定性约占风险预测本身的25%，在高波动性时期，不确定性的数量通常会增加。

\- ▪ 实时监控风险预测中的统计不确定性有助于提高投资组合经理承担风险的精度，尤其是在波动率制度发生变化时。

\[5\] The Stock-Bond Correlation

标题:股票债券相关性

作者:Megan Czasonis, Mark Kritzman and David Turkington

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/3/67;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.195

Abstract : Investors rely on the stock-bond correlation for a variety of tasks, such as forming optimal portfolios, designing hedging strategies, and assessing risk. Most investors estimate the stock–bond correlation simply by extrapolating the historical correlation of monthly returns; they assume that this correlation best characterizes the correlation of future annual or multiyear returns, but this approach is decidedly unreliable. The authors introduce four innovations for generating a reliable prediction of the stock-bond correlation. First, they show how to represent the correlation of single-period cumulative stock and bond returns in a way that captures how the returns drift during the period. Second, they identify fundamental predictors of the stock-bond correlation. Third, they model the stock–bond correlation as a function of the path of some fundamental predictors rather than single observations. Finally, they censor their sample to include only relevant observations, in which relevance has a precise mathematical definition.

TOPICS: Portfolio management/multi-asset allocation, risk management, statistical methods

Key Finding

\- ▪ The stock-bond correlation is a critical component of many investment activities, such as forming optimal portfolios, designing hedging strategies, and assessing risk.

\- ▪ Most investors estimate the correlation of longer-interval returns by extrapolating the correlation of past shorter-interval returns, but this approach is decidedly unreliable.

\- ▪ By applying recent advances in quantitative methods, it is possible to generate reliable predictions of the correlation of longer-horizon stock and bond returns.

Abstract:投资者依赖于股票与债券的相关性来完成各种任务，例如形成最优投资组合、设计对冲策略和评估风险。大多数投资者只是通过推断月收益率的历史相关性来估计股票与债券的相关性；他们认为这种相关性最能表征未来年度或多年收益率的相关性，但这种方法绝对不可靠。作者介绍了四个创新产生一个可靠的预测股票债券相关性。首先，他们展示了如何用一种方法来表示单期累积股票和债券收益的相关性，这种方法可以捕捉到收益在这一期间是如何漂移的。其次，他们确定了股票和债券相关性的基本预测因素。第三，他们将股票与债券的相关性建模为一些基本面预测因素路径的函数，而不是单个观测值。最后，他们审查他们的样本，只包括相关的观察，其中相关性有一个精确的数学定义。

主题：投资组合管理/多资产配置，风险管理，统计方法

Key Finding

\- ▪ 股票与债券的相关性是许多投资活动的重要组成部分，例如形成最佳投资组合、设计对冲策略和评估风险。

\- ▪ 大多数投资者通过推断过去较短间隔收益的相关性来估计较长间隔收益的相关性，但这种方法绝对不可靠。

\- ▪ 通过应用定量方法的最新进展，可以对长期股票和债券收益的相关性做出可靠的预测。

\[6\] Get Green or Die Trying? Carbon Risk Integration into Portfolio Management

标题:变绿了还是死了？将碳风险纳入投资组合管理

作者:Maximilian Görgen, Andrea Jacob and Martin Nerlinger

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/3/77;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.200

Abstract : Portfolio management is confronting climate change more strongly and rapidly than expected. Risks arising from the transition from a brown, carbon-based to a green, low-carbon economy need to be integrated into portfolio and risk management. The authors show how to quantify these carbon risks by using a capital markets–based approach. Their measure of carbon risk, the carbon beta, can serve as an integral part of portfolio management practices in a more comprehensive way than fundamental carbon risk measures. Apart from other studies, the authors demonstrate that both green and brown stocks are risky per se, but there is no adequate remuneration in the financial market. In addition, carbon risk exposure is correlated with exposures to other common risk factors. This requires due diligence when integrating carbon risk in investment practices. By implementing carbon risk screening and best-in-class approaches, the authors find that investors can gain a desired level of carbon risk exposure, but this does not come without well-hidden costs.

TOPICS: Portfolio construction, ESG investing, risk management

Key Finding

\- ▪ We describe a capital markets–based approach to measuring carbon risk that stems from the transition from a carbon-based to a low-carbon economy.

\- ▪ Investors can integrate carbon risk into their portfolios using carbon beta but must be aware of the associated portfolio return, risk, and factor exposures.

\- ▪ Different portfolio strategies can achieve a desired level of carbon risk exposure. However, the risk is not remunerated proportionally in the financial market.

Abstract:投资组合管理比预期更强烈、更迅速地应对气候变化。从褐色的、以碳为基础的经济向绿色的、低碳的经济过渡所产生的风险需要纳入投资组合和风险管理。作者展示了如何使用基于资本市场的方法来量化这些碳风险。他们的碳风险度量，碳贝塔，可以作为投资组合管理实践的一个组成部分，比基本的碳风险度量更全面。除其他研究外，作者还证明，绿色和棕色股票本身都有风险，但在金融市场上没有足够的报酬。此外，碳风险暴露与其他常见风险因素的暴露相关。在将碳风险纳入投资实践时，这需要尽职调查。通过实施碳风险筛选和同类最佳方法，作者发现投资者可以获得所需的碳风险敞口水平，但这并非没有良好的隐藏成本。

主题：投资组合构建、ESG投资、风险管理

Key Finding

\- ▪ 我们描述了一种基于资本市场的方法来衡量碳风险，这种方法源于从碳经济向低碳经济的转变。

\- ▪ 投资者可以使用碳贝塔系数将碳风险纳入其投资组合，但必须了解相关的投资组合回报、风险和因子敞口。

\- ▪ 不同的投资组合策略可以达到预期的碳风险敞口水平。然而，在金融市场上，风险的回报并不成比例。

\[7\] Deconstructing ESG Ratings Performance: Risk and Return for E, S, and G by Time Horizon, Sector, and Weighting

标题:分解ESG评级绩效：按时间范围、行业和权重划分的E、S和G的风险和回报

作者:Guido Giese, Zoltán Nagy and Linda-Eling Lee

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/3/94;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.198

Abstract : There are many ways to construct a company’s environmental, social, and governance (ESG) score or rating, involving different combinations of financial and nonfinancial inputs. Determining the most influential criteria for firm performance may be overlooked in the rush to “do some ESG.” In this study, the authors deconstruct ESG ratings performance at the E, S, and G pillar levels and use the most common key issues indicators that underlie ESG scores. They find that the time horizon used has an important bearing on the indicators’ significance. In the short term, they find that governance is the dominant pillar because it strongly reflects event risks, such as fraud. In the long term, however, environmental and social indicators became more important because issues such as carbon emissions tended to be more cumulative, presenting erosion risks to long-term performance. The authors also find that a more balanced and industry-specific weighting of E, S, and G issues showed better long-term relevance than the individual pillar indicators alone.

TOPICS: Equity portfolio management, ESG investing, pension funds, foundations & endowments, performance measurement, wealth management, risk management

Key Finding

\- ▪ Aggregating environmental, social, and governance pillars into a total ESG rating added value in terms of performance and risk.

\- ▪ Governance indicators showed the greatest significance in the short term because they have tended to materialize as event risks that immediately affected stock prices.

\- ▪ However, some E and S indicators have developed slowly but have had long-lasting financial effects (erosion risks).

Abstract:构建公司环境、社会和治理（ESG）评分或评级的方法有很多，涉及财务和非财务投入的不同组合。在急于“做一些ESG”的过程中，确定对公司绩效最有影响的标准可能会被忽略。在这项研究中，作者在E、S和G支柱水平上解构ESG评级绩效，并使用ESG分数背后最常见的关键问题指标。他们发现，使用的时间范围对指标的重要性有重要影响。在短期内，他们发现治理是主要支柱，因为它强烈反映了事件风险，如欺诈。然而，从长期来看，环境和社会指标变得更为重要，因为碳排放等问题往往更具累积性，给长期绩效带来侵蚀风险。作者还发现，对E、S和G问题进行更为平衡和行业特定的加权，比单独的支柱指标显示出更好的长期相关性。

主题：股票投资组合管理，ESG投资，养老基金，基金会和捐赠，绩效衡量，财富管理，风险管理

Key Finding

\- ▪ 将环境、社会和治理支柱聚合为总体ESG评级，在绩效和风险方面增加价值。

\- ▪ 治理指标在短期内显示出最大的重要性，因为它们往往成为事件风险，直接影响股价。

\- ▪ 然而，一些E和S指标发展缓慢，但具有长期的财务影响（侵蚀风险）。

\[8\] Valuing a Lost Opportunity: An Alternative Perspective on the Illiquidity Discount

标题:评估失去的机会：非流动性贴现的另一种视角

作者:Jamil Baz, Steve Sapra, Christian Stracke and Wentao Zhao

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/3/112;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2020.1.197

Abstract : The illiquidity discount is the valuation discount investors apply to investments as compensation for their lack of immediate marketability. The authors analyze the concept of the illiquidity discount in the specific context of private investments whereby investors are precluded from accessing their capital, potentially for periods as long as several years. Although many explanations have been put forth for why such discounts should exist, as well as for their magnitude, the authors posit an alternative explanation based on the concept of opportunity cost: By holding an illiquid asset, an investor forgoes the potential excess returns that could have been generated by trading the liquid markets. The authors derive a basic mathematical model to show that the illiquidity discount should be directly related to the magnitude of trading opportunities on the horizon. Investors who believe their future investment opportunities to be favorable should command commensurately large illiquidity discounts for tying up capital. This implies that more skilled investors should require larger illiquidity discounts than their less skilled counterparts. Finally, the authors conjecture that the equilibrium illiquidity discount priced into private markets at any given time is primarily a function of investors’ perceived skill rather than actual skill. This highlights an important behavioral component to the pricing of private investments.

TOPICS: Long-term/retirement investing, portfolio construction, real assets/alternative investments/private equity, theory, in portfolio management

Key Finding

\- ▪ Investors command illiquidity discounts for locking up capital in proportion to their perception of excess return opportunities on the horizon.

\- ▪ More highly skilled investors will command higher illiquidity discounts because their opportunity cost for tying up capital is greater than for average investors.

\- ▪ The equilibrium illiquidity discount for private investments will be a function of investors’ perceived skill rather than actual skill.
