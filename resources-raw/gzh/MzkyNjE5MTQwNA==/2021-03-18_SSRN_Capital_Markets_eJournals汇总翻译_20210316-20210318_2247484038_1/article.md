\[1\] Reporting Behavior and Transparency in European Banks’ Country-by-Country Reports

标题:欧洲银行国别报告中的报告行为与透明度

作者:Verena K. Dutt,Katharina Nicolay,Christoph Spengel

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3805603

Abstract : The public CbCR requirement for EU financial institutions leaves leeway to the reporting firms as regards the calculating and presentation of the data. Based on a sample of CbCRs published by EU-headquartered multinational bank groups, we analyze the reporting behavior and the degree of transparency across the reports. We observe a large heterogeneity with respect to the place of publication of the CbCR, its content, the readability of the data tables as well as the list of entities that should be published together with the by-country data. We also identify differences between headquarter countries, with CbCRs prepared by bank groups from the United Kingdom and Germany being the most transparent. Inconsistencies in reporting inhibit the interpretability and the comparability of the data. We conclude that the specification of the underlying data source and of the applicable consolidation scope as well the establishment of uniform definitions of the reportable items are essential for an appropriate consideration of the reports by all addressees. Our analyses are particularly important in light of the proposal for a public CbCR for large multinational firms in the EU.

Keywords : Country-by-Country Reporting; Financial Institutions; Public Disclosure; Reporting Behavior; Tax Transparency

Abstract :欧盟金融机构的公共CbCR要求在数据的计算和呈现方面给报告公司留有余地。本文以欧盟总部跨国银行集团发布的CBCR为样本，分析了各报告的报告行为和透明度。我们观察到，在CbCR的发布地点、内容、数据表的可读性以及应与各国数据一起发布的实体列表方面存在很大的异质性。我们还发现了总部国家之间的差异，英国和德国的银行集团编制的CBCR最为透明。报告中的不一致妨碍了数据的可解释性和可比性。我们的结论是，对基础数据源和适用合并范围的规范，以及对可报告项目的统一定义的建立，对于所有收件人对报告的适当审议至关重要。鉴于欧盟大型跨国公司的公共CbCR提案，我们的分析尤为重要。

Keywords :国别报告；金融机构；公开披露；报告行为；税收透明度

\[2\] Liquidity Spillover between ETFs and Their Constituents

标题:etf及其组成部分之间的流动性溢出

作者:Son Duy Pham,Ben R. Marshall,Nhut H. Nguyen,Nuttawat Visaltanachoti

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3806140

Abstract : ETF sponsors promote ETFs as having superior liquidity than their constituents because of ETFs' liquidity in the open market and the underlying stocks' liquidity through the creation/redemption mechanism. We find a liquidity connection between the ETF and its underlying assets suggesting the potential simultaneous liquidity dry-up in both markets. Liquidity spillover increases during the market crisis and positively relates to market volatility and funding constraints. Besides, a stock with high volatility and low trading activity exhibits higher liquidity spillover. Finally, liquidity spillover varies proportionally with ETF arbitrage activity and tends to be lower when short sales constraints exist.

Keywords : ETFs; Portfolio Liquidity; Spillover; Arbitrage; Short Sale Constraints

Abstract :由于ETF在公开市场的流动性以及标的股票通过创设/赎回机制的流动性，ETF保荐人认为ETF的流动性优于其组成部分。我们发现ETF与其标的资产之间存在流动性联系，表明两个市场可能同时出现流动性枯竭。流动性溢出在市场危机期间增加，并与市场波动和资金约束正相关。此外，波动率高、交易活跃度低的股票具有较高的流动性溢出效应。最后，流动性溢出随ETF套利活动成比例变化，当存在卖空约束时，流动性溢出往往较低。

Keywords :etf；组合流动性；溢出；套利；卖空约束

\[3\] Feature Selection in Jump Models

标题:跳跃模型中的特征选择

作者:Peter Nystrup,Petter N. Kolm,Erik Lindstrom

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3805831

Abstract : Jump models switch infrequently between states to fit a sequence of data while taking the ordering of the data into account. We propose a new framework for joint feature selection, parameter and state-sequence estimation in jump models. Feature selection is necessary in high-dimensional settings where the number of features is large compared to the number of observations and the underlying states differ only with respect to a subset of the features. We develop and implement a coordinate descent algorithm that alternates between selecting the features and estimating the model parameters and state sequence, which scales to large data sets with large numbers of (noisy) features. We demonstrate the usefulness of the proposed framework by comparing it with a number of other methods on both simulated and real data in the form of financial returns, protein sequences, and text. The resulting sparse jump model outperforms all other methods considered and is remarkably robust to noise.

Keywords : High-dimensional; sequential data; time series; clustering; unsupervised learning; regime switching

Abstract :跳跃模型很少在状态之间切换以适应数据序列，同时考虑数据的顺序。提出了一种新的跳跃模型联合特征选择、参数估计和状态序列估计框架。特征选择在高维环境中是必要的，在高维环境中，特征的数量与观察的数量相比是很大的，并且基本状态仅在特征的子集方面不同。我们开发并实现了一种坐标下降算法，该算法在选择特征和估计模型参数和状态序列之间交替进行，可扩展到具有大量（噪声）特征的大型数据集。通过与其他方法的比较，我们证明了该框架的有效性，包括模拟数据和真实数据，如财务收益、蛋白质序列和文本。由此产生的稀疏跳跃模型优于所有其他方法，并具有显著的鲁棒性噪声。

Keywords :高维；序列数据；时间序列；聚类；无监督学习；状态转换

\[4\] Do Electronic Markets Improve Execution If You Cannot Identify Yourself?

标题:如果你不能确定自己的身份，电子市场能提高执行力吗？

作者:John J. Shim,Yenan Wang

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3805830

Abstract : We study two aspects of the transition to electronic markets for large uninformed traders: (1) the increase in trading frequency, and (2) the move to anonymous trading, which challenged the implementation of reputation-based strategies. We present a model of optimal execution where a risk-neutral trader must sell over multiple trading periods before a ﬁxed deadline to several risk-averse market makers. We model trading frequency as the number of trading periods over a ﬁxed time intervial, and reputationbased strategies as the ability to commit ex-ante to a sequence of market orders, what we refer to as scheduling. While scheduling and more trading periods are both valuable, a trader typically prefers scheduling over more trading periods. Moreover, the beneﬁts of scheduling signiﬁcantly increase when coupled with more frequent trading, and outweigh the potential costs associated with predatory trading against predictable orders. Our model suggests that transparent execution algorithms, which could be used to implement scheduling in anonymous electronic markets, may have important beneﬁts for large, uninformed traders.

Keywords : Trading Frequency, Optimal Execution, Commitment Power

Abstract :我们研究了大型不知情交易者向电子市场过渡的两个方面：（1）交易频率的增加；（2）转向匿名交易，这对基于声誉的策略的实施提出了挑战。我们提出了一个最优执行模型，其中一个风险中性交易者必须在固定期限之前，在多个交易期间向几个风险规避的做市商出售股票。我们将交易频率建模为固定时间间隔内的交易周期数，将基于声誉的策略建模为对一系列市场订单进行事前承诺的能力，我们称之为调度。虽然时间安排和更多的交易周期都是有价值的，但交易者通常更喜欢安排更多的交易周期。此外，当与更频繁的交易相结合时，计划的益处显著增加，并且超过了与针对可预测订单的掠夺性交易相关的潜在成本。我们的模型表明，透明执行算法，可用于实现匿名电子市场的调度，可能对大型、不知情的交易者有重要的好处。

Keywords :交易频率，最优执行，承诺力

\[5\] The Conceptual Flaws of Constant Product Automated Market Making

标题:固定产品自动做市的概念缺陷

作者:Andreas Park

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3805750

Abstract : Blockchain-based decentralized exchanges fall into two broad categories: decentralized limit order books where an order is a smart contract registered on the blockchain, and swap exchanges where prices are set by a deterministic automated market-making rule. The most common form of the latter is the constant product rule where relative prices of crypto assets are determined by iso-liquidity curves. Although this pricing rule is simple, its use is conceptually problematic and gives rise to persistent arbitrage opportunities when there are multiple competing trading systems.It also allows intrinsically profitable front-running opportunities. Traditional market maker pricing, on the other hand, does not suffer from these flaws. Empirically, more than 10% of trades incur an excess cost of 7% or more in less liquid trading pairs.

Keywords : decentralized trading, swap exchanges, uniswap, blockchain, crypto-economics, automated market making

Abstract :基于区块链的分散式交易所分为两大类：分散式限额指令簿，其中一个指令是在区块链上注册的智能合约；以及掉期交易所，其中价格由确定性自动做市规则设定。后者最常见的形式是常数积规则，其中加密资产的相对价格由iso流动性曲线确定。尽管这个定价规则很简单，但它的使用在概念上是有问题的，当存在多个相互竞争的交易时，会产生持续的套利机会系统。It同时也提供了潜在的盈利机会。而传统做市商定价则没有这些缺陷。根据经验，在流动性较差的交易对中，超过10%的交易会产生7%或更多的超额成本。

Keywords :分散交易、掉期交易所、uniswap、区块链、加密经济学、自动化做市

\[6\] Explaining Gamestop Short Squeeze using Ιntraday Data and Google Searches.

标题:使用入门数据和谷歌搜索解释Gamestop Short squence。

作者:Evangelos Vasileiou,Eleftheria Bartzou,Polydoros Tzanakis

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3805630

Abstract : This article examines the recent short squeeze that occurred in the GameStop (GME) stock in early 2021. This event, although not the only case of short squeeze, has some idiosyncrasy that makes it extremely interesting, mainly because it was organized by non-institutional investors through social media like reddit. Using intraday data, we examine whether the market was in line with the Efficient Market Hypothesis during the period from 4/1/2021 to 4/2/2021, which includes the peak of the short squeeze, or not. Moreover, using the Granger causality approach we show that: (i) volume was a leading indicator for the GME performance, and (ii) the skyrocketing performance of the GME causes the increased interest for the GME short squeeze. Finally, using a GARCH(1,1) model, we econometrically confirm that increased trading volume leads to price increases, and that when the GME story becomes very popular the returns fall. This analysis could be very useful for scholars and practitioners who examine profitable investment strategies when such conditions emerge in the markets and it also provides some thoughts for further research on the topic of “smart and dumb money” and the impact of social networks on financial markets.

Keywords : Granger Causality; Short Squeeze; Google Searches; Smart and Dumb Money

Abstract :本文考察了最近发生在2021年初GameStop（GME）股票的空头挤压。这一事件虽然不是空头挤兑的唯一案例，但它的一些特质让它非常有趣，主要是因为它是由非机构投资者通过reddit等社交媒体组织的。利用盘中数据，我们检验了2021年4月1日至2021年4月2日期间市场是否符合有效市场假说，其中包括空头挤压的峰值。此外，使用格兰杰因果关系方法，我们表明：（i）成交量是GME表现的领先指标，以及（ii）GME的飙升表现导致对GME短期挤压的兴趣增加。最后，利用GARCH（1,1）模型，我们从计量经济学角度证实了交易量的增加会导致价格的上涨，而当GME故事变得非常流行时，回报率会下降。这一分析对于那些在市场出现盈利投资策略时研究盈利投资策略的学者和实践者非常有用，同时也为进一步研究“聪明和愚蠢的货币”以及社交网络对金融市场的影响提供了一些思路。

Keywords :格兰杰因果关系；短期挤压；谷歌搜索；聪明和愚蠢的钱
