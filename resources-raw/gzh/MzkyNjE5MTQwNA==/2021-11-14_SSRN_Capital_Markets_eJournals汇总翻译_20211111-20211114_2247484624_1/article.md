作者:Mayukh Mukhopadhyay,Kaushik Ghosh

出处:-

Abstract : Non Fungible Token (NFT) Industry has been witnessing multi-million dollar trade in recent times. With rapid innovation of the NFT market environment by technology, innovation, and decentralization, it is becoming hard to distinguish between genuine NFT from fads and scams. \[3\] Spillover Effects of Mandatory Portfolio Disclosures on Corporate Investment

标题:强制性投资组合披露对公司投资的溢出效应

作者:Jalal Sani,Nemit Shroff,Hal D. White

出处:-

Abstract : This paper examines the effect of mandatory portfolio disclosure by actively managed investment funds on investee firms’ investment decisions. Mandatory portfolio disclosures can reduce fund managers’ incentive to collect and trade on private information, and thereby reduce the stock price informativeness of their portfolio firms. Using a difference-in-differences design around the May 2004 SEC regulation requiring more frequent disclosure, we find that investment sensitivity to stock prices declines for firms with significant ownership by actively managed funds affected by the regulation. Results from cross-sectional tests indicate that the decline in investment-price sensitivity is concentrated among firms owned by funds with larger expected proprietary costs and for firms more likely to learn from the information in price. We conduct numerous placebo and robustness tests to mitigate endogeneity concerns. Overall, our results suggest that portfolio disclosure requirements have spillover effects on corporate investment by lowering managers’ opportunities to learn from price.  

Keywords : Mandatory portfolio disclosure, Proprietary cost, Information acquisition, Real effects of financial markets, Corporate investment

Abstract :本文考察了主动管理型投资基金强制披露投资组合对被投资公司投资决策的影响。强制性投资组合披露可以降低基金经理收集和交易私人信息的动机，从而降低其投资组合公司的股价信息量。利用2004年5月SEC监管要求更频繁披露的差异设计，我们发现受监管影响的积极管理基金拥有大量股权的公司对股票价格的投资敏感性下降。横截面测试的结果表明，投资价格敏感性的下降集中在预期所有权成本较高的基金所拥有的公司以及更可能从价格信息中学习的公司。我们进行了大量安慰剂和稳健性测试，以缓解内生性问题。总体而言，我们的结果表明，投资组合披露要求通过降低管理者从价格中学习的机会，对公司投资产生溢出效应。

Keywords :公司收购、投资组合的实际成本、财务影响

\[4\] Systemic Risk of the Chinese Stock Market Based on the Mobility Measures of the Marginal Expected Shortfall

标题:基于边际预期缺口流动性测度的中国股市系统性风险

作者:Xiaohang Liu,Handong Ling

出处:Journal of Risk, Vol. 24, No. 1, 2021

Abstract : This paper applies the dynamic mixture copula model method and proposes a mobility measure of the marginal expected shortfall (MES) to depict the changing systemic risk in China’s mainland stock market and Hong Kong’s stock market. The empirical analysis focuses on the rank of individual stocks’ MES and the changes in the rank. The empirical results suggest that the mobility of MES is strong in the stable stage of the market, while the MES ranking of stocks tends to be disordered. When risk events occur, the MES ranking is relatively stable and the market risk is always affected by the same specific stocks. This property gives us a new perspective on risk management and deepens our understanding of the characteristics of systemic risk. In addition, most of the MES time series have strong long-term memory, which indicates that the risks of individual stocks have persistent impacts on the market’s systemic risk.  

Keywords : system risk; dynamic mixture copula (DMC) model; marginal expected shortfall (MES); mobility measures; long memory

Abstract :本文应用动态混合Copula模型方法，提出了边际期望缺口（MES）的流动性测度，以描述中国大陆股市和香港股市的系统性风险变化。实证分析的重点是个股中小企业的排名及其变化。实证结果表明，在市场稳定阶段，中小企业的流动性较强，而股票中小企业的排名则趋于无序。当风险事件发生时，MES排名相对稳定，市场风险始终受到相同特定股票的影响。这一特性为我们提供了风险管理的新视角，加深了我们对系统性风险特征的理解。此外，大多数MES时间序列具有较强的长期记忆，这表明单个股票的风险对市场的系统性风险具有持续影响。

Keywords :系统风险；动态混合copula（DMC）模型；边际预期短缺（MES）；流动措施；长记忆

\[5\] Asset Pricing Implications of Monetary Policy Coordination

标题:货币政策协调的资产定价含义

作者:Hsuan Fu

出处:-

Abstract : This paper examines the problem of monetary coordination building on an international sticky-price model. I demonstrate that monetary coordination is associated with a welfare transfer of 0.56%--0.57% of steady-state consumption from the high-openness economy to the low-openness economy. By variance decomposition, I show that policy coordination is associated with more volatile transitory cost-push shocks, while it stabilizes long-run risks in financial markets.  

Keywords : Monetary policy coordination, Central banking, Equity risk premia, New Keynesian model, Long-run risks

Abstract :本文在国际粘性价格模型的基础上研究了货币协调问题，证明了货币协调与0.56%-0.57%的稳态消费从高开放经济向低开放经济的福利转移有关。通过方差分解，我证明了政策协调与更不稳定的暂时性成本推动冲击相关，同时稳定了金融市场的长期风险。

Keywords :货币政策协调、中央银行、股票风险溢价、新凯恩斯模型、长期风险

\[6\] Multi-asset financial bubbles in an agent-based model with noise traders’ herding described by an n-vector Ising model

标题:基于代理模型的多资产金融泡沫，噪声交易者羊群行为由n向量伊辛模型描述

作者:Davide Cividino,Rebecca Westphal,Didier Sornette

出处:Swiss Finance Institute Research Paper No. 21-76

Abstract : We present an agent-based model (ABM) of a financial market with n > 1 risky assets, whose price dynamics result from the interaction between rational fundamentalists and trend following imitative noise traders. The interactions and opinion formation of the noise traders are described by an extended O(n) vector model, which generalise the Ising model used previously in ABMs with a single risky asset. Efficient rejection-free transition probabilities are derived to describe realistic investment decisions at the micro level of individual noise traders. The ABM is validated by testing for several characteristics of financial markets such as volatility clustering and fat-tails of the distribution of returns. Furthermore, the model is able to account for the development of endogenous bubbles and crashes. We distinguish three different regimes depending on the traders’ propensity to imitate others. In the subcritical regime of the O(n) vector model, the traders’ opinions are idiosyncratic and no bubbles emerge. Around the critical value of the O(n) vector model, cross sectionally asynchronous bubbles emerge. Above the critical value, small random price fluctuations may be amplified by noise traders herding into a given asset, which then impels fundamentalists to re-equilibrate their more valuable portfolios that have become unbalanced, thus pushing the prices of the other assets upward. The resulting transient increase of the momenta of these assets triggers a reorientation of the noise traders’ portfolios that further amplifies the burgeoning bubbles. We have thus identified a mechanism by which the cautious risk-adverse contrarian rebalancing strategy of fundamentalists leads to systemic risks in the form of cascades of bubbles spreading the whole financial market.  

Keywords : financial bubbles; agent-based model; arbitrageurs; noise traders; fundamentalists; multi-assets; O(n) vector model; synchronisation

Abstract :我们提出了一个基于agent的n>1风险资产金融市场模型（ABM），其价格动态是理性原教旨主义者和趋势跟踪模仿噪声交易者相互作用的结果。噪声交易者的相互作用和意见形成由扩展的O（n）向量模型描述，该模型推广了之前在ABMs中使用的单一风险资产的伊辛模型。有效的无拒绝转移概率被导出来描述个体噪声交易者微观层面上的现实投资决策。通过测试金融市场的几个特征，如波动性集群和收益分布的厚尾，对ABM进行了验证。此外，该模型能够解释内生泡沫和崩溃的发展。我们根据交易者模仿他人的倾向来区分三种不同的制度。在O（n）向量模型的次临界区域，交易者的观点是独特的，没有泡沫出现。在O（n）矢量模型的临界值附近，出现了横截面异步气泡。高于临界值时，噪音交易者涌入某一特定资产可能会放大微小的随机价格波动，从而促使原教旨主义者重新平衡其价值更高的、变得不平衡的投资组合，从而推高其他资产的价格。由此产生的这些资产动量的短暂增加引发了噪音交易者投资组合的重新定位，进一步放大了正在萌芽的泡沫。因此，我们确定了一种机制，通过这种机制，原教旨主义者谨慎的风险逆向再平衡战略导致了系统性风险，其形式是一连串的泡沫扩散到整个金融市场。

Keywords :金融泡沫；基于agent的模型；套利者；噪音交易商；原教旨主义者；多元资产；O（n）向量模型；同步

\[7\] The Price of Money: How Collateral Policy Affects the Yield Curve

标题:货币价格：抵押品政策如何影响收益率曲线

作者:Kjell G. Nyborg,Jiri Woschitz

出处:Swiss Finance Institute Research Paper No. 21-74

Abstract : Central-bank collateral policy governs the convertibility of assets into central-bank money provided directly by the central bank. Focusing on government bonds, we develop clean identification of variation in such convertibility by exploiting differential treatment of same-country government bonds in the euro area. Combining difference-in-differences analysis with yield-curve modeling on four separate events, we show that reduced convertibility lifts yields, but with the effect tapering off at longer maturities. Our findings imply that central-bank money is priced in the market and that a central bank can move and shape the yield curve through collateral policy.  

Keywords : Yield curve, central bank, collateral policy, monetary policy, haircuts, repo, asset prices, liquidity, central-bank money, government bonds

Abstract :中央银行抵押品政策规定资产可兑换为中央银行直接提供的中央银行货币。以政府债券为重点，我们利用欧元区各国政府债券的差别待遇，对这种可兑换性的变化进行了清晰的识别。将差异分析与四个独立事件的收益率曲线建模相结合，我们表明，降低可兑换性可以提高收益率，但在较长的到期日效果逐渐减弱。我们的研究结果表明，中央银行的货币是在市场中定价的，中央银行可以通过抵押品政策调整和塑造收益率曲线。

Keywords :收益率曲线、央行、抵押品政策、货币政策、折减、回购、资产价格、流动性、央行货币、政府债券

\[8\] Herding and Anti-Herding Across ESG Funds

标题:ESG基金的羊群效应和反羊群效应

作者:Rocco Ciciretti,Ambrogio Dalò,Giovanni Ferri

出处:CEIS Working Paper No. 524

Abstract : We investigate to what extent ESG funds present an herding/anti-herding behavior, and the consequences of their investment strategies in terms of both systematic risk exposure and risk-adjusted returns. Our findings document that ESG funds pursue an anti-herding strategy that leads to higher risk-adjusted returns. Specifically, a one standard deviation increase in ESG score at the fund-level is associated with an increase in fund performance of about 3.74 basis points per year. Moreover, we document that such an enhanced performance does not come at the cost of higher systematic risk exposure but instead reduces it. A possible explanation behind our findings is that after the catching-up phase previously documented by the literature, ESG funds are now able to put to good use enhanced stock-picking skills built over the years.  

Keywords : ESG investing, Equity Funds, Herding, Anti-Herding, Risk-Adjusted Returns

Abstract :我们调查ESG基金在多大程度上呈现羊群/反羊群行为，以及其投资策略在系统风险敞口和风险调整回报方面的后果。我们的研究结果表明，ESG基金采取的是一种反羊群策略，这种策略会带来更高的风险调整回报。具体而言，基金层面ESG分数的一个标准差增加与基金绩效每年增加约3.74个基点相关。此外，我们还记录了这样一种增强的绩效不会以更高的系统风险敞口为代价，而是会降低风险敞口。我们的研究结果背后的一个可能解释是，在文献先前记录的追赶阶段之后，ESG基金现在能够很好地利用多年来建立的强化选股技能。

Keywords :ESG投资、股票基金、羊群效应、反羊群效应、风险调整收益

\[9\] Fractional Trading

作者:Zhi Da,Vivian W. Fang,Wenwei Lin

Abstract : Fractional Trading (FT)—the ability to trade less than a full share—allows low-budget retail investors to trade high-priced stocks. Since FT's introduction in late 2019, high-priced stocks have evidenced a sharp increase in retail ownership and trading. \[10\] The Value-at-Risk of Time-Series Momentum and Contrarian Trading Strategies

标题:时间序列动量和反向交易策略的风险价值

作者:Keunbae Ahn,Jihye Park,KiHoon Jimmy Hong

出处:Journal of Risk Model Validation, Vol. 15, No. 3

Abstract : This paper not only provides a theoretical model for the value-at-risk of active and passive trading strategies but also discusses the substantial implications relevant to risk management. Our results suggest that, first, passive strategies are riskier than active trading strategies based on historical returns, such as momentum and contrarian strategies. Second, momentum (contrarian) trading is riskier in a bull (bear) market. Third, the value-at-risk of momentum (contrarian) strategies has a positive relation to the absolute value of the return autocorrelation, as well as a positive (negative) relation with the state of the market. Abstract :本文不仅为主动和被动交易策略的风险价值提供了理论模型，而且还讨论了与风险管理相关的实质性含义。我们的研究结果表明，首先，被动策略比基于历史回报的主动交易策略风险更大，如动量策略和反向策略。其次，在牛市（熊市）中，动量（反向）交易的风险更大。第三，动量（反转）策略的风险价值与收益自相关的绝对值呈正相关，与市场状态呈正（负）相关。此外，与国际股票市场上的其他策略相比，动量交易策略具有更高的风险调整绩效。

Keywords :风险价值；推进力逆向的；风险管理；返回自相关。

\[11\] Segmented Arbitrage

标题:分段套利

作者:Emil Siriwardane,Aditya Sunderam,Jonathan Wallen

出处:-

Abstract : We use arbitrage activity in equity, fixed income, and foreign exchange markets to characterize the frictions and constraints facing intermediaries. The average pairwise correlation between the twenty-nine arbitrage spreads that we study is 22%. These low correlations are inconsistent with models in which an integrated intermediary sector faces a single constraint and sets all prices. We show that at least two types of segmentation drive arbitrage dynamics. First, funding is segmented --- certain trades rely on specific funding sources so arbitrage spreads are sensitive to localized funding shocks. Second, balance sheets are segmented --- intermediaries specialize in certain arbitrages so arbitrage spreads are sensitive to idiosyncratic balance sheet shocks.
