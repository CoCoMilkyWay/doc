\[1\] What do you think about climate finance?

标题:你如何看待气候金融？

作者:Johannes Stroebel,Jeffrey Wurgler

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21003494

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.08.004

Abstract : We survey 861 finance academics, professionals, and public sector regulators and policy economists about climate finance topics. They identify regulatory risk as the top climate risk to businesses and investors over the next five years, but they view physical risk as the top risk over the next 30 years. By an overwhelming margin, respondents believe that asset prices underestimate climate risks rather than overestimate them. We also tabulate opinions about the expected correlation between growth and climate change, social discount rates appropriate for projects that mitigate the effects of climate change, most influential forces for reducing climate risks, and most important research topics.

Keywords : Climate finance;Environment;ESG;SRI;Social discounting

Abstract :我们对861名金融学者、专业人士、公共部门监管机构和政策经济学家进行了气候金融专题调查。他们认为监管风险是未来五年企业和投资者面临的最大气候风险，但他们认为实体风险是未来30年的最大风险。绝大多数受访者认为，资产价格低估了气候风险，而不是高估了气候风险。我们还将增长与气候变化之间的预期相关性、缓解气候变化影响的项目适用的社会贴现率、降低气候风险的最有影响力的力量以及最重要的研究课题的观点制成表格。

Keywords :气候金融；环境ESG；斯里兰卡；社会折扣

\[2\] Corporate green bonds

标题:公司绿色债券

作者:Caroline Flammer

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000337

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.01.010

Abstract : I examine corporate green bonds, whose proceeds finance climate-friendly projects. These bonds have become more prevalent over time, especially in industries where the environment is financially material to firm operations. I show that investors respond positively to the issuance announcement, a response that is stronger for first-time issuers and bonds certified by third parties. The issuers improve their environmental performance post-issuance (i.e., higher environmental ratings and lower CO2 emissions) and experience an increase in ownership by long-term and green investors. Overall, the findings are consistent with a signaling argument—by issuing green bonds, companies credibly signal their commitment toward the environment.

Keywords : Sustainable finance;Climate change;Green bonds;Impact investing;Corporate sustainability

Abstract :我研究了公司绿色债券，其收益用于资助气候友好型项目。随着时间的推移，这些债券变得越来越普遍，尤其是在环境对公司运营至关重要的行业。我的研究表明，投资者对发行公告的反应是积极的，对于首次发行人和经第三方认证的债券，这种反应更强烈。发行人提高了发行后的环境绩效（即，更高的环境评级和更低的二氧化碳排放量），并经历了长期和绿色投资者所有权的增加。总的来说，研究结果与通过发行绿色债券发出信号的论点是一致的，公司可信地表明了他们对环境的承诺。

Keywords :可持续金融；气候变化；绿色债券；影响投资；企业可持续性

\[3\] Do investors care about carbon risk?

标题:投资者关心碳风险吗？

作者:Patrick Bolton,Marcin Kacperczyk

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001902

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.008

Abstract : We study whether carbon emissions affect the cross-section of US stock returns. We find that stocks of firms with higher total carbon dioxide emissions (and changes in emissions) earn higher returns, controlling for size, book-to-market, and other return predictors. We cannot explain this carbon premium through differences in unexpected profitability or other known risk factors. We also find that institutional investors implement exclusionary screening based on direct emission intensity (the ratio of total emissions to sales) in a few salient industries. Overall, our results are consistent with an interpretation that investors are already demanding compensation for their exposure to carbon emission risk.

Keywords : Carbon emissions;Climate change;Stock returns;Institutional investors

Abstract :我们研究碳排放是否会影响美国股票回报的横截面。我们发现，二氧化碳总排放量（以及排放量变化）较高的公司的股票获得了较高的回报，控制了规模、账面市值和其他回报预测因素。我们无法通过意外盈利能力或其他已知风险因素的差异来解释碳溢价。我们还发现，机构投资者在一些重要行业中根据直接排放强度（总排放量与销售额的比率）实施排他性筛选。总的来说，我们的结果与一种解释是一致的，即投资者已经在要求补偿他们面临的碳排放风险。

Keywords :碳排放；气候变化；股票收益；机构投资者

\[4\] Sustainable investing in equilibrium

标题:均衡中的可持续投资

作者:Ľuboš Pástor,Robert F. Stambaugh,Lucian A. Taylor

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20303512

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.12.011

Abstract : We model investing that considers environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria. In equilibrium, green assets have low expected returns because investors enjoy holding them and because green assets hedge climate risk. Green assets nevertheless outperform when positive shocks hit the ESG factor, which captures shifts in customers’ tastes for green products and investors’ tastes for green holdings. The ESG factor and the market portfolio price assets in a two-factor model. The ESG investment industry is largest when investors’ ESG preferences differ most. Sustainable investing produces positive social impact by making firms greener and by shifting real investment toward green firms.

Keywords : Sustainable investing;Socially responsible investing;ESG;Social impact

Abstract :我们建立了考虑环境、社会和治理（ESG）标准的投资模型。在均衡状态下，绿色资产的预期回报率较低，因为投资者喜欢持有它们，而且绿色资产可以对冲气候风险。然而，当正面冲击冲击ESG因素时，绿色资产的表现要好于正面冲击，ESG因素反映了客户对绿色产品的偏好和投资者对绿色持有的偏好的变化。ESG因素和市场投资组合在双因素模型中对资产进行定价。当投资者的ESG偏好差异最大时，ESG投资行业规模最大。可持续投资通过使企业更加绿色以及将实际投资转向绿色企业产生积极的社会影响。

Keywords :可持续投资；社会责任投资；ESG；社会影响

\[5\] Responsible investing: The ESG-efficient frontier

标题:负责任的投资：ESG高效前沿

作者:Lasse Heje Pedersen,Shaun Fitzgibbons,Lukasz Pomorski

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302853

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.11.001

Abstract : We propose a theory in which each stock's environmental, social, and governance (ESG) score plays two roles: (1) providing information about firm fundamentals and (2) affecting investor preferences. The solution to the investor's portfolio problem is characterized by an ESG-efficient frontier, showing the highest attainable Sharpe ratio for each ESG level. The corresponding portfolios satisfy four-fund separation. Equilibrium asset prices are determined by an ESG-adjusted capital asset pricing model, showing when ESG raises or lowers the required return. Combining several large data sets, we compute the empirical ESG-efficient frontier and show the costs and benefits of responsible investing. Finally, we test our theory's predictions using proxies for E (carbon emissions), S, G, and overall ESG.

Keywords : Portfolio choice;ESG;Socially responsible investing;Impact investing;Sustainable investing;CSR;Carbon;Governance

Abstract :我们提出了一个理论，在该理论中，每只股票的环境、社会和治理（ESG）分数都扮演着两个角色：（1）提供有关公司基本面的信息；（2）影响投资者偏好。投资者投资组合问题的解决方案以ESG有效前沿为特征，显示每个ESG水平可达到的最高夏普比率。相应的投资组合满足四个基金分离。均衡资产价格由ESG调整后的资本资产定价模型确定，显示ESG何时提高或降低所需回报。结合几个大型数据集，我们计算了经验ESG有效前沿，并展示了负责任投资的成本和收益。最后，我们使用E（碳排放量）、s、G和总ESG的代理来测试我们理论的预测。

Keywords :投资组合选择；ESG；社会责任投资；影响投资；可持续投资；企业社会责任；碳统治

\[6\] Socially responsible corporate customers

标题:对社会负责的公司客户

作者:Rui Dai,Hao Liang,Lilian Ng

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20300325

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.01.003

Abstract : Corporate customers are an important stakeholder in global supply chains. We employ several unique international databases to test whether socially responsible corporate customers can infuse similar socially responsible business behavior in suppliers. Our findings suggest a unilateral effect on corporate social responsibility (CSR) only from customers to suppliers, an evidence further supported by exogenous variation in customers’ close-call CSR proposals and by product scandals. Customers exert influence on suppliers’ CSR through positive assortative matching and their decision-making process. Enhanced collaborative CSR efforts help improve operational efficiency and firm valuation of both customers and suppliers but increase only the customers’ future sales growth.

Keywords : Corporate social responsibility;Corporate customers;Global supply chains;Economic benefits

Abstract :企业客户是全球供应链中的重要利益相关者。我们使用了几个独特的国际数据库来测试是否有社会责任感的公司客户可以在供应商中注入类似的社会责任商业行为。我们的研究结果表明，企业社会责任（CSR）只有从客户到供应商的单方面影响，这一证据进一步得到了客户近距离CSR建议和产品丑闻的外部变化的支持。顾客通过积极的匹配和决策过程对供应商的CSR产生影响。加强企业社会责任协作有助于提高运营效率和客户和供应商的公司价值，但只会增加客户未来的销售增长。

Keywords :企业社会责任；公司客户；全球供应链；经济效益

\[7\] Market expectations of a warming climate

标题:气候变暖的市场预期

作者:Wolfram Schlenker,Charles A. Taylor

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001495

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.08.019

Abstract : We compare prices of financial derivatives whose payouts are based on future weather outcomes to CMIP5 climate model predictions as well as observed weather station data across eight cities in the US from 2001 through 2020. Derivative prices respond both to short-term weather forecasts for the next two weeks and longer-term warming trends. We show that the long-term trends in derivative prices are comparable to station-level data and climate model output. The one exception is February in the northeastern US, where financial markets price in a polar vortex-induced cooling effect, a recent scientific finding that was not present in the older CMIP5 climate output. When looking at the spatial and temporal heterogeneity in trends, futures prices are more aligned with climate model output than observed weather station trends, suggesting that market participants closely align their expectations with scientific projections rather than recent observations.

Keywords : Market expectations;Belief formation;Weather markets;Climate change

Abstract :我们将金融衍生品的价格与CMIP5气候模型预测以及2001年至2020年美国八个城市的观测气象站数据进行比较，这些金融衍生品的支付基于未来天气结果。衍生品价格对未来两周的短期天气预报和长期变暖趋势都有反应。我们表明，衍生品价格的长期趋势与台站级数据和气候模型输出相当。唯一的例外是美国东北部的2月份，那里的金融市场在极涡诱导的冷却效应中定价，这是一个最近的科学发现，在旧的CMIP5气候输出中不存在。在研究趋势的空间和时间异质性时，期货价格更符合气候模型输出，而不是观测到的气象站趋势，这表明市场参与者将其预期与科学预测紧密结合，而不是与最近的观测紧密结合。

Keywords :市场预期；信仰形成；天气市场；气候变化

\[8\] Air pollution, behavioral bias, and the disposition effect in China

标题:中国的空气污染、行为偏差和处置效应

作者:Jennifer (Jie) Li,Massimo Massa,Hong Zhang,Jian Zhang

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X19302223

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.09.003

Abstract : Inspired by the recent health science findings that air pollution affects mental health and cognition, we examine whether air pollution can intensify the cognitive bias observed in the financial markets. Based on a proprietary data set obtained from a large Chinese mutual fund family consisting of complete trading information for more than 773,198 accounts in 247 cities, we find that air pollution significantly increases investors’ disposition effects. Analysis based on two plausible exogenous variations in air quality (the vast dissipation of air pollution caused by strong winds and the Huai River policy) supports a causal interpretation. Mood regulation provides a potential mechanism.

Keywords : Air pollution;The disposition effect;Mutual funds

Abstract :受最近健康科学发现空气污染影响心理健康和认知的启发，我们研究了空气污染是否会加剧金融市场中观察到的认知偏差。根据从一个大型中国共同基金家族获得的专有数据集（包括247个城市773198多个账户的完整交易信息），我们发现空气污染显著增加了投资者的处置效应。基于空气质量两种可能的外生变化（强风造成的空气污染大量消散和淮河政策）的分析支持了因果解释。情绪调节提供了一种潜在的机制。

Keywords :空气污染；处置效应；共同基金

\[9\] The Big Three and corporate carbon emissions around the world

标题:三巨头与全球企业碳排放

作者:José Azar,Miguel Duro,Igor Kadach,Gaizka Ormazabal

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001896

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.007

Abstract : This paper examines the role of the “Big Three” (i.e., BlackRock, Vanguard, and State Street Global Advisors) on the reduction of corporate carbon emissions around the world. Using novel data on engagements of the Big Three with individual firms, we find evidence that the Big Three focus their engagement effort on large firms with high CO2 emissions in which these investors hold a significant stake. Keywords :气候变化；碳排放；ESG；三巨头；股东积极主义；机构所有权

\[10\] Contracts with (Social) benefits: The implementation of impact investing

标题:具有（社会）效益的合同：实施影响投资

作者:Christopher Geczy,Jessica S. Jeffers,David K. Musto,Anne M. Tucker

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000179

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.01.006

Abstract : We draw on new data and theory to examine how private market contracts adapt to serve multiple goals, particularly the social-benefit goals that impact funds add to their financial goals. Counter to the intuition from multitasking models (Holmstrom and Milgrom, 1991), few impact funds tie compensation directly to impact, and most retain traditional financial incentives. However, funds contract directly on impact in other ways and adjust aspects of the contracts such as governance. In the cross-section of impact funds, those with higher profit goals contract more tightly around both goals.

Keywords : Impact investing;Contracts;Venture capital;Private equity;Socially responsible investing

Abstract :我们利用新的数据和理论来研究私人市场契约如何适应服务于多个目标，特别是影响基金的社会效益目标，从而增加其财务目标。与多任务模型（Holmstrom和Milgrom，1991）的直觉相反，很少有影响力基金将薪酬与影响力直接挂钩，大多数基金保留传统的财务激励。然而，基金契约直接影响其他方面，并调整契约的治理等方面。在影响力基金的横截面图中，利润目标越高的基金在这两个目标周围收缩得越紧。

Keywords :影响投资；合同；风险投资；私人股本；社会责任投资

\[11\] High-cost debt and perceived creditworthiness: Evidence from the UK

标题:高成本债务和感知信誉：来自英国的证据

作者:Andres Liberman,Daniel Paravisini,Vikram Pathania

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21003299

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.07.005

Abstract : We show that high-cost debt exacerbates financial constraints by affecting lenders’ perception of credit risk. Using data from a high-cost lender in the UK, we show that high-cost credit reduces applicants’ credit score and future bank credit even though it does not affect future debt repayment. These effects are not present among borrowers who are already tagged as high risk at application, consistent with high-cost credit affecting lenders’ beliefs about borrowers’ creditworthiness. The results highlight a novel channel through which high-cost credit can harm consumers’ financial health: a self-reinforcing stigma of high risk.

Keywords : Consumer finance;Credit scores;Credit supply

Abstract :我们发现，高成本债务通过影响贷款人对信用风险的感知而加剧了金融约束。使用来自英国一家高成本贷款机构的数据，我们表明，高成本信贷降低了申请人的信用分数和未来银行信用，即使它不会影响未来的债务偿还。这些影响在申请时已被标记为高风险的借款人中并不存在，这与影响贷款人对借款人信誉的高成本信贷一致。研究结果突出了一种新的渠道，通过这种渠道，高成本信贷可以损害消费者的财务健康：一种自我强化的高风险耻辱感。

Keywords :消费金融；信用评分；信贷供应

\[12\] Engineering lemons

标题:工程柠檬

作者:Petra Vokata

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001653

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.035

Abstract : Recent complex financial products sold to households contradict the basic premise of canonical innovation theories: Financial innovation benefits its adopters. In my 2006–2015 sample of over 28,000 yield enhancement products (YEP), the securities offer attractive yields but negative returns. The products lose money both ex ante and ex post due to their embedded fees. On average, YEPs charge 6–7% in annual fees and subsequently lose 6–7% relative to risk-adjusted benchmarks.
