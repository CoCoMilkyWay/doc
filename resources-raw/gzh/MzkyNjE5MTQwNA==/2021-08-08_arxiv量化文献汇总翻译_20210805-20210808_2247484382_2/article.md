\[1\] R&D Heterogeneity and Countercyclical Productivity Dispersion

标题 : 研发异质性与反周期生产率分散

作者 : Shuowen Chen, Yang Ming , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.02272

说明 : -

Abstract : What causes countercyclicality of industry level productivity dispersion in the U.S. Empirically, we construct an index of negative profit shocks and show that both productivity dispersion and R&D intensity dispersion enlarge at the onset of the shock and gradually dissipate. Theoretically, we build a duopolistic technology ladder model in which heterogeneous R&D costs determine firms post shock optimal behaviors and equilibrium technology gap. Quantitatively, we calibrate a parameterized model, simulate firms post shock responses and predict that productivity dispersion is due to the low cost firm increasing R&D efforts and the high cost firm doing the opposite. We provide two empirical tests for this mechanism.

摘要 : 是什么导致了美国行业层面生产率分散的反周期性？根据经验，我们构建了一个负利润冲击指数，表明生产率分散和研发强度分散在冲击开始时都会扩大，并逐渐消失。理论上，我们构建了一个双寡头技术阶梯模型，其中异质研发成本决定了企业在冲击后的最优行为和均衡技术差距。定量地，我们校准了一个参数化模型，模拟了企业的冲击后反应，并预测生产率分散是由于低成本企业增加了研发努力，而高成本企业则相反。我们为这一机制提供了两个实证检验。

\[2\] Machine Learning Classification Methods and Portfolio Allocation

标题 : 机器学习分类方法与投资组合分配

作者 : Yang Bai, Kuntara Pukthuanthong , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.02283

说明 : -

Abstract : We design a novel framework to examine market efficiency through out of sample (OOS) predictability. We frame the asset pricing problem as a machine learning classification problem and construct classification models to predict return states. The prediction based portfolios beat the market with significant OOS economic gains. We measure prediction accuracies directly. For each model, we introduce a novel application of binomial test to test the accuracy of 3.34 million return state predictions. The tests show that our models can extract useful contents from historical information to predict future return states. We provide unique economic insights about OOS predictability and machine learning models.

摘要 : 我们设计了一个新的框架，通过样本外（OOS）可预测性来检验市场效率。我们将资产定价问题视为一个机器学习分类问题，并构建分类模型来预测收益状态。基于预测的投资组合以显著的OOS经济收益击败市场。我们直接测量预测精度。对于每个模型，我们引入了一种新的二项式检验应用，以检验334万个返回状态预测的准确性。测试表明，我们的模型可以从历史信息中提取有用的内容来预测未来的回归状态。我们提供有关OOS可预测性和机器学习模型的独特经济见解。

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.02633

说明 : -

Abstract : We quantify model risk of a financial portfolio whereby a multi period mean standard deviation criterion is used as a selection criterion. In this work, model risk is defined as the loss due to uncertainty of the underlying distribution of the returns of the assets in the portfolio. The uncertainty is measured by the Kullback Leibler divergence, i.e., the relative entropy. In the worst case scenario, the optimal robust strategy can be obtained in a semi analytical form as a solution of a system of nonlinear equations. Betting exchanges act as online platforms on which bettors can find willing counterparties to a bet, and they do this in a way that is directly comparable to the manner in which electronic financial exchanges, such as major stock markets, act as platforms that allow traders to find willing counterparties to buy from or sell to the platform aggregates and anonymises orders from multiple participants, showing a summary of the market that is updated in real time. \[7\] Understanding the nature of the long range memory phenomenon in socio economic systems

标题 : 理解社会经济系统中长程记忆现象的本质

作者 : Rytis Kazakevicius, Aleksejus Kononovicius, Bronislovas Kaulakys, Vygintas Gontis , 类别 ：Physics and Society (physics.soc-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.02506

说明 : Comments: 28 pages, 9 figures, 180 references

Abstract : In the face of the upcoming 30th anniversary of econophysics, we review our contributions and other related works on the modeling of the long range memory phenomenon in physical, economic, and other social complex systems. Our group has shown that the long range memory phenomenon can be reproduced using various Markov processes, such as point processes, stochastic differential equations and agent based models. Reproduced well enough to match other statistical properties of the financial markets, such as return and trading activity distributions and first passage time distributions. Research has lead us to question whether the observed long range memory is a result of actual long range memory process or just a consequence of non linearity of Markov processes. As our most recent result we discuss the long range memory of the order flow data in the financial markets and other social systems from the perspective of the fractional L e vy stable motion. We test widely used long range memory estimators on discrete fractional L  e vy stable motion represented by the ARFIMA sample series. Our newly obtained results seem indicate that new estimators of self similarity and long range memory for analyzing systems with non Gaussian distributions have to be developed.

摘要 : 面对即将到来的经济物理学30周年，我们回顾了我们在物理、经济和其他社会复杂系统中的长程记忆现象建模方面的贡献和其他相关工作。我们的团队已经证明，长程记忆现象可以通过各种马尔可夫过程重现，如点过程、随机微分方程和基于代理的模型。其重现性足以匹配金融市场的其他统计特性，如收益率和交易活动分布以及首次通过时间分布。研究使我们质疑，观察到的长程记忆是实际长程记忆过程的结果，还是仅仅是马尔可夫过程非线性的结果。作为我们最近的结果，我们从分数维稳定运动的角度讨论了金融市场和其他社会系统中订单流数据的长程记忆。我们在由ARFIMA样本序列表示的离散分数维稳定运动上测试了广泛使用的长程记忆估计器。我们最近获得的结果似乎表明，必须开发新的自相似性和长程记忆估计量来分析非高斯分布系统。

\[8\] Optimal Consumption with Loss Aversion and Reference to Past Spending Maximum

标题 : 损失厌恶和参考过去支出最大值的最优消费

作者 : Xun Li, Xiang Yu, Qinyi Zhang , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.02648

说明 : Comments: Keywords: Loss aversion, optimal relative consumption, path-dependent reference, concave envelope, piecewise feedback control, verification arguments

Abstract : This paper studies an optimal consumption problem for a loss averse agent with reference to past consumption maximum. To account for loss aversion on relative consumption, an S shaped utility is adopted that measures the difference between the non negative consumption rate and a fraction of the historical spending peak. We consider the concave envelope of the realization utility with respect to consumption, allowing us to focus on an auxiliary HJB variational inequality on the strength of concavification principle and dynamic programming arguments. By applying the dual transform and smooth fit conditions, the auxiliary HJB variational inequality is solved in closed form piecewisely and some thresholds of the wealth variable are obtained. The optimal consumption and investment control of the original problem can be derived analytically in the piecewise feedback form. The rigorous verification proofs on optimality and concavification principle are provided.

摘要 : 本文研究了一类损失厌恶型代理的最优消费问题。为了解释对相对消费的损失厌恶，采用了S型效用来衡量非负消费率和历史消费峰值的一小部分之间的差异。我们考虑了关于消费的实现效用的凹包络，使我们能够集中在一个辅助的HJB变分不等式上，利用凹化原理和动态规划参数的强度。应用对偶变换和光滑拟合条件，分段求解辅助HJB变分不等式，得到财富变量的一些阈值。原问题的最优消费和投资控制可以用分段反馈形式解析地导出。给出了最优性和凹化原理的严格验证证明。

\[9\] The AI Economist

标题 : 人工智能经济学家

作者 : Stephan Zheng, Alexander Trott, Sunil Srinivasa, David C. Parkes, Richard Socher , 类别 ：Machine Learning (cs.LG)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.02755

说明 : Comments: Substantial Extension of this https URL SZ and AT contributed equally

Abstract : AI and reinforcement learning (RL) have improved many areas, but are not yet widely adopted in economic policy design, mechanism design, or economics at large. At the same time, current economic methodology is limited by a lack of counterfactual data, simplistic behavioral models, and limited opportunities to experiment with policies and evaluate behavioral responses. Here we show that machine learning based economic simulation is a powerful policy and mechanism design framework to overcome these limitations. The AI Economist is a two level, deep RL framework that trains both agents and a social planner who co adapt, providing a tractable solution to the highly unstable and novel two level RL challenge. From a simple specification of an economy, we learn rational agent behaviors that adapt to learned planner policies and vice versa. We demonstrate the efficacy of the AI Economist on the problem of optimal taxation. In simple one step economies, the AI Economist recovers the optimal tax policy of economic theory. In complex, dynamic economies, the AI Economist substantially improves both utilitarian social welfare and the trade off between equality and productivity over baselines. It does so despite emergent tax gaming strategies, while accounting for agent interactions and behavioral change more accurately than economic theory. These results demonstrate for the first time that two level, deep RL can be used for understanding and as a complement to theory for economic design, unlocking a new computational learning based approach to understanding economic policy.

摘要 : 人工智能和强化学习（RL）在许多领域都有所改进，但尚未广泛应用于经济政策设计、机制设计或整个经济学。与此同时，当前的经济方法受到缺乏反事实数据、过于简单的行为模型以及试验政策和评估行为反应的机会有限的限制。在这里，我们展示了基于机器学习的经济模拟是克服这些限制的强大政策和机制设计框架。AI Economist是一个两级、深层次的RL框架，培训共同适应的代理和社会规划师，为高度不稳定和新颖的两级RL挑战提供易于处理的解决方案。从一个简单的经济规范中，我们学习了理性的代理人行为，这些行为适应于所学的规划者政策，反之亦然。我们证明了人工智能经济学家在最优税收问题上的有效性。在简单的一步经济中，AI经济学家恢复了经济理论中的最优税收政策。在复杂、充满活力的经济体中，《人工智能经济学家》（AI Economist）极大地改善了功利主义社会福利以及平等和生产率之间的权衡。尽管出现了税收博弈策略，但它仍然如此，同时比经济理论更准确地解释了代理人互动和行为变化。这些结果首次表明，两个层次的深度RL可用于理解经济设计理论，并作为对经济设计理论的补充，开启了一种新的基于计算学习的经济政策理解方法。

Abstract : Deep Reinforcement learning is a branch of unsupervised learning in which an agent learns to act based on environment state in order to maximize its total reward. Deep reinforcement learning provides good opportunity to model the complexity of portfolio choice in high dimensional and data driven environment by leveraging the powerful representation of deep neural networks.
