\[1\] Effects of Bitcoin Exchange Reserves on Bitcoin Returns and Volatility

标题:比特币外汇储备对比特币收益率和波动性的影响

作者:Lai T. Hoang,Dirk G. Baur

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3902504

Abstract : This study shows that changes in bitcoin exchange reserves are negatively related to contemporaneous and future bitcoin returns, consistent with the hypothesis that the transfer of bitcoin on exchanges implies increased price pressure and vice versa. We further identify an asymmetry between positive and negative reserve changes on bitcoin returns and volatility which in turn also affect exchange reserves in extreme market conditions. The results indicate that a significant fraction of bitcoin investors store their wealth off exchanges and only use exchanges to trade. This highlights a special feature of cryptocurrency trading that does not exist in traditional markets.

Keywords : blockchain, bitcoin; exchange reserves, price pressure, on-chain transactions, off-chain transactions

Abstract :这项研究表明，比特币外汇储备的变化与同期和未来比特币回报率呈负相关，这符合比特币在交易所的转移意味着价格压力增加的假设，反之亦然。我们进一步发现，比特币收益率和波动率的正储备变化和负储备变化之间存在不对称，这反过来也会影响极端市场条件下的外汇储备。结果表明，相当一部分比特币投资者将财富储存在交易所之外，只使用交易所进行交易。这突出了加密货币交易的一个特点，这在传统市场中是不存在的。

Keywords :区块链、比特币；外汇储备、价格压力、链上交易、链外交易

\[2\] A Pattern New in Every Moment: The Temporal Clustering of Markets for Crude Oil, Refined Fuels, and Other Commodities

标题:每时每刻都有一种新模式：原油、精炼燃料和其他大宗商品市场的暂时聚集

作者:James Ming Chen,Mobeen Rehman

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3901479

Abstract : A suite of clustering methods, applied to the matrix of conditional volatility by trading days and individual assets or asset classes, can identify critical periods in markets for crude oil, refined fuels, and other commodities. Unsupervised machine learning provides a viable alternative to rules-based and subjective definitions of crises in financial markets and the broader economy. Five clustering methods—affinity propagation, mean-shift, spectral, k-means, and hierarchical agglomerative clustering—can identify anomalous periods in commodities trading. These methods identified the financial crisis of 2008–09 and the initial stages of the Covid-19 pandemic. Applied to four energy-related markets—Brent, West Texas intermediate, gasoil, and gasoline—the same methods identified additional periods connected to events such as the September 11 terrorist attacks and the 2003 Persian Gulf war. t-distributed stochastic neighbor embedding facilities the visualization of commodity trading regimes. Future applications of these methods include the definition of bull and bear markets and the identification of recessions and recoveries in the real economy.

Keywords : energy commodities, financial crises, Brent, WTI, gasoline, clustering, t-SNE, machine learning, Covid-19 pandemic

Abstract :通过交易日和单个资产或资产类别对条件波动率矩阵应用一套聚类方法，可以确定原油、精炼燃料和其他商品市场的关键时期。无监督机器学习为金融市场和更广泛经济中基于规则和主观的危机定义提供了一种可行的替代方法。五种聚类方法——亲和传播法、均值漂移法、光谱法、k-均值法和层次聚集聚类法可以识别商品交易中的异常周期。2019冠状病毒疾病的早期诊断方法是2008—09和CVID-19流行病的初始阶段。将相同的方法应用于布伦特、西德克萨斯中质、汽油和汽油这四个能源相关市场，确定了与911恐怖袭击和2003年波斯湾战争等事件相关的额外时期。t分布随机邻域嵌入工具实现了商品交易制度的可视化。这些方法的未来应用包括牛市和熊市的定义以及实体经济衰退和复苏的识别。

Keywords :能源商品、金融危机、布伦特、WTI、汽油、集群、T-SNE、机器学习、COVID-19大流行

\[3\] Responding to Diffused Stakeholders on Social Media: Connective Power and Firm Reactions to CSR-related Twitter Messages

标题:回应社交媒体上分散的利益相关者：连接力量和公司对CSR相关推特消息的反应

作者:Gregory D. Saxton,Charlotte Ren,Chao Guo

出处:Journal of Business Ethics, vol. 172, pp. 229–252 (2021)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3903541

Abstract : Social media offers a platform for diffused stakeholders to interact with firms – alternatively praising, questioning, and chastising businesses for their CSR performance and seeking to engage in two-way dialogue. In 2014, 163,402 public messages were sent to Fortune 200 firms’ CSR-focused Twitter accounts, each of which was either shared, replied to, “liked,” or ignored by the targeted firm. This paper examines firm reactions to these messages, building a model of firm response to stakeholders that combines the notions of CSR communication and stakeholder salience. Our findings show that firm response to a stakeholder on social media is positively and most significantly associated with what we refer to as the stakeholder’s connective power but negatively associated with the firm’s own connective power. To a lesser extent, firm response is positively associated with the stakeholder’s normative power but negatively associated with the firm’s own normative power. Firm response is also shown to be positively associated with stakeholder urgency in terms of both the originality of a stakeholder message and the expression of positive sentiment.

Keywords : firm response, corporate social responsibility (CSR), social media, diffused stakeholders, connective power, urgency

Abstract :社交媒体为分散的利益相关者提供了一个与企业互动的平台——或者赞扬、质疑和惩罚企业的CSR表现，并寻求进行双向对话。2014年，共有163402条公共信息被发送到《财富》200强企业以企业社会责任为中心的推特账户，每个账户要么被共享，要么回复“喜欢”，要么被目标企业忽略。本文考察了企业对这些信息的反应，建立了企业对利益相关者的反应模型，该模型结合了企业社会责任沟通和利益相关者显著性的概念。我们的研究结果表明，企业在社交媒体上对利益相关者的反应与我们所说的利益相关者的关联能力正相关，且最显著，但与企业自身的关联能力负相关。在较小程度上，企业响应与利益相关者的规范权力正相关，但与企业自身的规范权力负相关。在利益相关者信息的原创性和积极情绪的表达方面，公司反应也与利益相关者的紧迫性呈正相关。

Keywords :企业响应、企业社会责任（CSR）、社交媒体、分散的利益相关者、联结力量、紧迫性

\[4\] Company Reporting: Female Board Representation and the Use of Gender-diversity Language

标题:公司报告：女性董事会代表和性别多样性语言的使用

作者:Michael Kiely

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3904102

Abstract : It is generally understood that women are under-represented in the boards of UK firms, with homogeneous boards increasingly perceived as being unfair. An organisation’s true gender diversity values, and the influence that women on boards have upon them, may be discernible from the language used in firms’ annual reports. This study, quantitatively examining data from fifty UK FTSE-listed firms from across ten sectors, finds that there is a moderately positive correlation between the number of women on the boards of firms and the use of gender diverse terminology in those same firms’ annual reports. This study also provides descriptive statistics on the gender composition of executive boards, including the commonly reported Blau diversity index value, and of the gender diversity language used in the sampled reports. The findings support arguments that, as more women enter the boardroom, they positively affect reporting quality, and possibly the firm and its values more widely, and that their contributions are not marginalised. However, if reporting narratives are primarily used as a stakeholder impression management tactic, then the results of this study suggest that women on boards may be complicit in such strategies.

Keywords : Women, Gender diversity, Report narratives, Impression Management Theory, Social Identity Theory

Abstract :人们普遍认为，女性在英国公司董事会中的代表性不足，同质董事会越来越被认为是不公平的。一个组织真正的性别多样性价值观，以及董事会中的女性对他们的影响，可以从公司年度报告中使用的语言中辨别出来。这项研究对来自十个行业的五十家英国富时指数上市公司的数据进行了定量分析，发现公司董事会中的女性人数与这些公司年度报告中使用的性别差异术语之间存在适度的正相关关系。本研究还提供了关于执行委员会性别构成的描述性统计数据，包括通常报告的Blau多样性指数值，以及抽样报告中使用的性别多样性语言。研究结果支持这样的观点：随着更多女性进入董事会，她们会积极影响报告质量，可能会更广泛地影响公司及其价值观，而且她们的贡献不会被边缘化。然而，如果报告叙述主要用作利益相关者印象管理策略，那么本研究的结果表明，董事会中的女性可能参与了此类策略。

Keywords :女性、性别多样性、报告叙事、印象管理理论、社会认同理论

\[5\] Joint Modelling and Calibration of SPX and VIX by Optimal Transport

标题:通过优化传输对SPX和VIX进行联合建模和校准

作者:Ivan Guo,Gregoire Loeper,Jan Obłój,Shiyi Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3568998

Abstract : This paper addresses the joint calibration problem of SPX options and VIX options or futures. We show that the problem can be formulated as a semimartingale optimal transport problem under a finite number of discrete constraints, in the spirit of \[arXiv:1906.06478\]. We introduce a PDE formulation along with its dual counterpart. The solution, a calibrated diffusion process, can be represented via the solutions of Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equations arising from the dual formulation. The method is tested on both simulated data and market data. Numerical examples show that the model can be accurately calibrated to SPX options, VIX options and VIX futures simultaneously.

Keywords : Optimal Transport, Volatility calibration, S&P 500, VIX, Joint Calibration

Abstract :本文讨论了SPX期权和VIX期权或期货的联合校准问题。根据\[arXiv:1906.06478\]的精神，我们证明了该问题可以表述为有限个离散约束下的半鞅最优运输问题。我们介绍了一种偏微分方程及其对偶形式。该解是一个经过校准的扩散过程，可以通过对偶公式产生的Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman方程的解来表示。该方法在模拟数据和市场数据上都得到了验证。数值算例表明，该模型可以同时精确地标定为SPX期权、VIX期权和VIX期货。

Keywords :最佳传输、波动率校准、标准普尔500指数、波动率指数、联合校准

\[6\] From Micro to Macro: Aggregate Accruals, Mergers, and Returns. A Discussion of Heater, Nallareddy and Venkatachalam (2021)

标题:从微观到宏观：累计应计利润、合并和回报。关于加热器、Nallaredy和Venkatachalam（2021年）的讨论

作者:AJ Chen,Maria Ogneva

出处:Journal of Accounting & Economics (JAE), Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3899688

Abstract : Heater, Nallareddy, and Venkatachalam (2021), hereafter HNV, find that aggregate merger and acquisition (M&A) activity explains the ability of aggregate accruals to predict market-wide returns. In this discussion, we delineate HNV’s contribution to accounting literature and provide a review of the emerging stream of micro-to-macro accounting research. We also discuss HNV’s findings in relation to research beyond accounting, including the literature on aggregate return prediction and mergers and acquisitions. Our discussion draws parallels between HNV’s results and prior empirical evidence pertaining to aggregate investment and merger waves and identifies several puzzling inconsistencies. These inconsistencies highlight the lack of a clearly specified mechanism behind the return-predictive ability of aggregate M&A accruals. Finally, we point out the challenges posed by the small sample size and noisy measures of M&A accruals that may affect HNV’s inferences. We conclude by suggesting several directions for future research.

Keywords : Aggregate accruals, aggregate earnings, micro-to-macro, macro accounting, mergers, aggregate investment

Abstract :Heater、Nallareddy和Venkatachalam（2021）（以下简称HNV）发现，总并购（M&A）活动解释了总应计项目预测全市场回报的能力。在本次讨论中，我们描述了HNV对会计文献的贡献，并回顾了新兴的从微观到宏观的会计研究。我们还讨论了HNV与会计以外的研究相关的发现，包括关于总回报预测和并购的文献。我们的讨论将HNV的结果与之前有关总投资和合并浪潮的经验证据进行了对比，并发现了一些令人费解的不一致之处。这些不一致突出表明，在总并购应计利润的回报预测能力背后，缺乏明确规定的机制。最后，我们指出了可能影响HNV推断的并购应计项目的小样本量和嘈杂度量所带来的挑战。最后，我们提出了未来研究的几个方向。

Keywords :总应计利润、总收益、微观到宏观、宏观会计、合并、总投资

\[9\] Modeling Conditional Factor Risk Premia Implied by Index Option Returns

标题:指数期权收益隐含的条件因子风险溢价模型

作者:Mathieu Fournier,Kris Jacobs,Piotr Orłowski

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3893807

Abstract : We propose a novel factor model for option returns. Option exposures are estimated nonparametrically and factor risk premia can vary nonlinearly with states. The model is estimated using regressions, with minimal assumptions on factor and option return dynamics. Using index options, we characterize the conditional risk premia for the market return, market variance, and tail and intermediary risk factors. All average risk premia have the expected sign and meaningful magnitudes. Market and variance risk premia display pronounced time-variation, spike during crises, and always have the expected sign. Combined, market return and variance explain more than 90% of option return variation.

Keywords : Option Returns, Factor Models, Option-Implied Factor Risk Premia, Time-Varying Exposures, Machine Learning

Abstract :我们提出了一种新的期权收益因子模型。期权风险敞口是非参数估计的，因子风险溢价可能随州而非线性变化。该模型使用回归进行估计，对因素和期权回报动态进行最小假设。利用指数期权，我们描述了市场回报、市场方差以及尾部和中间风险因素的条件风险溢价。所有平均风险溢价都具有预期的符号和有意义的幅度。市场和差异风险溢价表现出明显的时间变化，在危机期间出现峰值，并且始终具有预期的迹象。综合起来，市场回报和差异解释了90%以上的期权回报变化。

Keywords :期权收益、因子模型、期权隐含因子风险溢价、时变风险敞口、机器学习

\[10\] Analyst Coverage and Syndicated Lending

标题:分析师覆盖率和银团贷款

作者:Nicholas Hallman,John S. Howe,Wei Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3896494

Abstract : We study the effects of analyst coverage on syndicated lending. We hypothesize that analyst research alleviates information asymmetries between lead arrangers and participant lenders within a syndicate, increasing the participants’ credit supply and reducing the required loan interest spread. Using exogenous shocks to firms’ analyst coverage, we find that firms pay higher loan interest spreads and participant lenders fund smaller fractions of the loans after firms experience a reduction in analyst coverage. Participants are more likely to be nonbank institutional investors and transact with familiar lead arrangers after the coverage shocks.

Keywords : Sell-side Analysts; Syndicated lending; Intra-syndicate information asymmetry; Exogenous shock

Abstract :我们研究分析师覆盖率对银团贷款的影响。我们假设，分析师研究缓解了银团内牵头行和参与者贷款人之间的信息不对称，增加了参与者的信贷供应，并降低了所需的贷款利差。通过对公司分析师覆盖率的外生冲击，我们发现，在公司经历分析师覆盖率降低后，公司支付更高的贷款利差，参与者贷款人提供的贷款比例更小。参与者更有可能是非银行机构投资者，并在承保范围冲击后与熟悉的牵头行进行交易。

Keywords :卖方分析师；银团贷款；银团内部信息不对称；外源性休克
