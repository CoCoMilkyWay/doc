\[1\] Optimal Investment and Consumption under a Habit Formation Constraint

标题 : 习惯形成约束下的最优投资与消费

作者 : Bahman Angoshtari, Erhan Bayraktar, Virginia R. Young , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.03414

说明 : Comments: 30 pages, 6 figures

Abstract : We extend the result of our earlier study Angoshtari, Bayraktar, and Young Optimal consumption under a habit formation constraint, available at arXiv 2012.02277, (2020) to a market setup that includes a risky asset whose price process is a geometric Brownian motion. We formulate an infinite horizon optimal investment and consumption problem, in which an individual forms a habit based on the exponentially weighted average of her past consumption rate, and in which she invests in a Black Scholes market. The novelty of our model is in specifying habit formation through a constraint rather than the common approach via the objective function. Specifically, the individual is constrained to consume at a rate higher than a certain proportion alpha of her consumption habit. Our habit formation model allows for both addictive ( alpha 1 ) and nonaddictive ( 0 alpha 1 ) habits. The optimal investment and consumption policies are derived explicitly in terms of the solution of a system of differential equations with free boundaries, which is analyzed in detail. If the wealth to habit ratio is below (resp. above) a critical level x , the individual consumes at (resp. above) the minimum rate and invests more (resp. less) aggressively in the risky asset. Numerical results show that the addictive habit formation requires significantly more wealth to support the same consumption rate compared to a moderately nonaddictive habit. Furthermore, an individual with a more addictive habit invests less in the risky asset compared to an individual with a less addictive habit but with the same wealth to habit ratio and risk aversion, which provides an explanation for the equity premium puzzle.

摘要 : 我们将先前研究Angoshtari、Bayraktar和Young在习惯形成约束下的最优消费（见arXiv 2012.02277，（2020））的结果扩展到一个市场设置，该市场设置包括一个价格过程为几何布朗运动的风险资产。我们建立了一个无限期最优投资与消费问题，其中一个个体根据其过去消费率的指数加权平均值形成一种习惯，并在Black-Scholes市场上进行投资。我们的模型的新颖之处在于通过约束而不是通过目标函数来指定习惯形成。具体来说，个人被限制以高于其消费习惯的一定比例α的速度消费。我们的习惯形成模型允许成瘾（α1）和非加性（0α1）习惯。利用自由边界微分方程组的解显式地导出了最优投资和消费政策，并对其进行了详细的分析。如果财富与习惯之比低于。以上）一个临界水平x，个人消耗。以上）的最低利率和投资更多（分别。减少）在风险资产中的积极性。数值结果表明，成瘾性习惯的形成需要更多的财富来支持相同的消费率相比，一个适度的非加性习惯。此外，一个更容易上瘾的个人在风险资产上的投资比一个不那么容易上瘾的个人要少，但财富与习惯比率和风险厌恶程度相同，这为股票溢价之谜提供了一个解释。

\[2\] A Modularized and Scalable Multi Agent Reinforcement Learning based System for Financial Portfolio Management

标题 : 基于模块化可扩展多Agent强化学习的金融资产组合管理系统

作者 : Zhenhan Huang, Fumihide Tanaka , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.03502

说明 : -

Abstract : Financial Portfolio Management is one of the most applicable problems in Reinforcement Learning (RL) by its sequential decision making nature. Existing RL based approaches, while inspiring, often lack scalability, reusability, or profundity of intake information to accommodate the ever changing capital markets. In this paper, we design and develop MSPM, a novel Multi agent Reinforcement learning based system with a modularized and scalable architecture for portfolio management. MSPM involves two asynchronously updated units Evolving Agent Module (EAM) and Strategic Agent Module (SAM). A self sustained EAM produces signal comprised information for a specific asset using heterogeneous data inputs, and each EAM possesses its reusability to have connections to multiple SAMs. A SAM is responsible for the assets reallocation of a portfolio using profound information from the EAMs connected. With the elaborate architecture and the multi step condensation of the volatile market information, MSPM aims to provide a customizable, stable, and dedicated solution to portfolio management that existing approaches do not. We also tackle data shortage issue of newly listed stocks by transfer learning, and validate the necessity of EAM. Experiments on 8 year U.S. stock markets data prove the effectiveness of MSPM in profits accumulation by its outperformance over existing benchmarks.

摘要 : 金融投资组合管理是强化学习（RL）中最适用的问题之一。现有的基于RL的方法虽然鼓舞人心，但往往缺乏可扩展性、可重用性，或者缺乏吸收信息的深度，以适应不断变化的资本市场。在本文中，我们设计并开发了一个新的基于多智能体强化学习的系统MSPM，该系统具有模块化和可扩展的投资组合管理体系结构。MSPM包含两个异步更新的单元：进化Agent模块（EAM）和策略Agent模块（SAM）。一个自维持的EAM使用异构的数据输入为一个特定的资产产生由信号组成的信息，并且每个EAM具有其可重用性，可以连接到多个sam。SAM负责使用来自EAMs的深刻信息重新分配投资组合的资产。通过精心设计的体系结构和对不稳定市场信息的多步骤浓缩，MSPM旨在为投资组合管理提供一个现有方法所没有的可定制、稳定和专用的解决方案。本文还利用转移学习方法解决了新上市股票的数据短缺问题，验证了EAM的必要性。在8年美国股市数据上的实验证明，MSPM在利润积累方面的有效性，其表现优于现有的基准。

\[3\] Prisoner Dilemma in maximization constrained

标题 : 最大化约束下的囚徒困境

作者 : Mahdi HajiAliAkbari, Shahin Esmaeili , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.03644

说明 : -

Abstract : David Gauthier in his article, Maximization constrained the rationality of cooperation, tries to defend the joint strategy in situations in which no outcome is both equilibrium and optimal. Prisoner Dilemma is the most familiar example of these situations. He first starts with some quotes by Hobbes in Leviathan Hobbes, in chapter 15 discusses an objection by someone is called Foole, and then will reject his view. In response to Foole, Hobbes presents two strategies (i.e. joint and individual) and two kinds of agents in such problems including Prisoner Dilemma, i.e. straightforward maximizer (SM) and constrained maximizer(CM). Then he considers two arguments respectively for SM and CM, and he will show that why in an ideal and transparent situation, the first argument fails and the second one would be the only valid argument. Likewise, in the following part of his article, he considers more realistic situations with translucency and he concludes that under some conditions, the joint strategy would be still the rational decision.

摘要 : 戴维高蒂尔在他的文章，最大化约束合作的合理性，试图捍卫联合战略的情况下，没有结果是平衡和最优的。囚徒困境是最常见的例子。他首先引用了霍布斯在《利维坦·霍布斯》一书中的一些名言，在第15章讨论了一个被称为傻瓜的人的反对，然后拒绝了他的观点。针对Foole，霍布斯提出了两种策略（联合策略和个体策略）和两种agent来解决囚徒困境问题，即直接最大化（SM）和约束最大化（CM）。然后，他分别考虑了SM和CM的两个论点，并说明为什么在理想和透明的情况下，第一个论点失败，而第二个论点是唯一有效的论点。同样，在他的文章的下一部分，他考虑了更现实的情况，并得出结论，在某些条件下，联合战略仍然是理性的决定。

\[4\] Mobility based contact exposure explains the disparity of spread of COVID 19 in urban neighborhoods

标题 : 基于流动性的接触暴露解释了covid19在城市社区传播的差异

作者 : Rajat Verma, Takahiro Yabe, Satish V. Ukkusuri , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.03698

说明 : Comments: 13 pages (overall) with 7 figures and 1 table; currently under review in another journal - Scientific Reports

Abstract : The rapid early spread of COVID 19 in the U.S. was experienced very differently by different socioeconomic groups and business industries. In this study, we study aggregate mobility patterns of New York City and Chicago to identify the relationship between the amount of interpersonal contact between people in urban neighborhoods and the disparity in the growth of positive cases among these groups. We introduce an aggregate Contact Exposure Index (CEI) to measure exposure due to this interpersonal contact and combine it with social distancing metrics to show its effect on positive case growth. With the help of structural equations modeling, we find that the effect of exposure on case growth was consistently positive and that it remained consistently higher in lower income neighborhoods, suggesting a causal path of income on case growth via contact exposure. Using the CEI, schools and restaurants are identified as high exposure industries, and the estimation suggests that implementing specific mobility restrictions on these point of interest categories are most effective. This analysis can be useful in providing insights for government officials targeting specific population groups and businesses to reduce infection spread as reopening efforts continue to expand across the nation.

摘要 : 不同的社会经济群体和商业行业经历了CoVID19在美国的快速早期传播。在这项研究中，我们研究了纽约市和芝加哥市的总体流动模式，以确定城市社区中人们之间的人际接触数量与这些群体中积极案例增长的差异之间的关系。我们引入了一个总接触暴露指数（CEI）来衡量这种人际接触的暴露程度，并将其与社会距离指标相结合来显示其对积极案例增长的影响。在结构方程模型的帮助下，我们发现接触对病例增长的影响一直是正的，而在低收入社区，这种影响一直是高的，这表明通过接触暴露，收入对病例增长的因果关系。使用CEI，学校和餐馆被确定为高风险行业，估计表明，对这些兴趣点类别实施特定的流动性限制是最有效的。这一分析有助于为政府官员提供针对特定人群和企业的见解，以减少重新开放努力在全国范围内继续扩大时的感染传播。

\[5\] Variational Autoencoders

标题 : 变分自动编码器

作者 : Maxime Bergeron, Nicholas Fung, John Hull, Zissis Poulos , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.03945

说明 : -

Abstract : A volatility surface is an important tool for pricing and hedging derivatives. The surface shows the volatility that is implied by the market price of an option on an asset as a function of the option s strike price and maturity. Often, market data is incomplete and it is necessary to estimate missing points on partially observed surfaces. In this paper, we show how variational autoencoders can be used for this task. The first step is to derive latent variables that can be used to construct synthetic volatility surfaces that are indistinguishable from those observed historically. The second step is to determine the synthetic surface generated by our latent variables that fits available data as closely as possible. As a dividend of our first step, the synthetic surfaces produced can also be used in stress testing, in market simulators for developing quantitative investment strategies, and for the valuation of exotic options. We illustrate our procedure and demonstrate its power using foreign exchange market data.

摘要 : 波动率面是衍生品定价和套期保值的重要工具。表面显示了资产期权市场价格隐含的波动性，作为期权执行价格和到期日的函数。通常，市场数据是不完整的，因此有必要估计部分观察到的表面上的缺失点。在本文中，我们将展示如何变分自动编码器可以用于这项任务。第一步是导出潜在变量，这些变量可用于构建合成波动率曲面，这些曲面与历史上观察到的曲面不可区分。第二步是确定由我们的潜在变量生成的合成曲面，该曲面与可用数据尽可能接近。作为我们第一步的红利，生成的合成表面还可以用于压力测试、市场模拟工具中，用于制定定量投资策略，以及对奇异期权进行估值。我们用外汇市场数据说明了我们的方法并证明了它的有效性。

\[6\] Increasing the price of a university degree does not significantly affect enrolment if income contingent loans are available

标题 : 如果有收入或有贷款，提高大学学位的价格不会对入学率产生重大影响

作者 : Fabio Italo Martinenghi , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.03956

说明 : -

Abstract : I provide evidence that, when income contingent loans are available, student enrolment in university courses is not significantly affected by large increases in the price of those courses. I use publicly available domestic enrolment data from Australia. I study whether large increases in the price of higher education for selected disciplines in Australia in 2009 and in 2012 was associated with changes in their enrolment growth. I find that large increases in the price of a course did not lead to significant changes in their enrolment growth for that course.

摘要 : 我提供的证据表明，在有收入或有贷款的情况下，大学课程的学生入学率不会受到这些课程价格大幅上涨的显著影响。我使用澳大利亚公开的国内入学数据。我研究了2009年和2012年澳大利亚部分学科高等教育价格的大幅上涨是否与入学率增长的变化有关。我发现课程价格的大幅上涨并没有导致该课程入学率的显著增长。

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.04137

说明 : -

Abstract : Although the share of platform work is very small compared to conventional and traditional employment system, research shows that the use of platform work is increasingly growing all over the world. Trade unions have also paid special attention to the platform work because they know that the transfer of a percentage of human resources to the platforms is undeniable. 作者 : Francesco C. De Vecchi, Elisa Mastrogiacomo, Mattia Turra, Stefania Ugolini , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.03172

说明 : -

Abstract : We establish a generalization of Noether theorem for stochastic optimal control problems. Exploiting the tools of jet bundles and contact geometry, we prove that from any (contact) symmetry of the Hamilton Jacobi Bellman equation associated to an optimal control problem it is possible to build a related local martingale. Moreover, we provide an application of the theoretical results to Merton s optimal portfolio problem, showing that this model admits infinitely many conserved quantities in the form of local martingales.

摘要 : 建立了随机最优控制问题Noether定理的推广。利用射流束和接触几何学的工具，我们证明了从与最优控制问题相关的Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman方程的任何（接触）对称性，可以建立相关的局部鞅。此外，我们还将理论结果应用于Merton最优投资组合问题，证明了该模型以局部鞅的形式存在无穷多个守恒量。

## 

\[24\] De carbonization of global energy use during the COVID 19 pandemic

标题 : 全球能源使用在covid19大流行期间的去碳化

作者 : Zhu Liu, Biqing Zhu, Philippe Ciais, Steven J. Davis, Chenxi Lu, Haiwang Zhong, Piyu Ke, Yanan Cui, Zhu Deng, Duo Cui, Taochun Sun, Xinyu Dou, Jianguang Tan, Rui Guo, Bo Zheng, Katsumasa Tanaka, Wenli Zhao, Pierre Gentine , 类别 ：Atmospheric and Oceanic Physics (physics.ao-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.03240

说明 : -

Abstract : The COVID 19 pandemic has disrupted human activities, leading to unprecedented decreases in both global energy demand and GHG emissions. Yet a little known that there is also a low carbon shift of the global energy system in 2020. Here, using the near real time data on energy related GHG emissions from 30 countries (about 70 of global power generation), we show that the pandemic caused an unprecedented de carbonization of global power system, representing by a dramatic decrease in the carbon intensity of power sector that reached a historical low of 414.9 tCO2eq GWh in 2020. Moreover, the share of energy derived from renewable and low carbon sources (nuclear, hydro energy, wind, solar, geothermal, and biomass) exceeded that from coal and oil for the first time in history in May of 2020. The decrease in global net energy demand ( 1.3 in the first half of 2020 relative to the average of the period in 2016 2019) masks a large down regulation of fossil fuel burning power plants supply ( 6.1 ) coincident with a surge of low carbon sources ( 6.2 ). Concomitant changes in the diurnal cycle of electricity demand also favored low carbon generators, including a flattening of the morning ramp, a lower midday peak, and delays in both the morning and midday load peaks in most countries. However, emission intensities in the power sector have since rebounded in many countries, and a key question for climate mitigation is thus to what extent countries can achieve and maintain lower, pandemic level carbon intensities of electricity as part of a green recovery.

摘要 : covid19大流行扰乱了人类活动，导致全球能源需求和温室气体排放量空前减少。然而鲜为人知的是，2020年全球能源系统也将发生低碳转变。在这里，我们利用30个国家（约占全球发电量的70%）与能源有关的温室气体排放的近实时数据表明，这一流行病导致了全球电力系统前所未有的去碳化，表现为电力行业的碳强度急剧下降，在2020年达到了414.9 tCO2eq GWh的历史低点。此外，2020年5月，可再生能源和低碳能源（核能、水能、风能、太阳能、地热能和生物质能）的能源份额历史上首次超过煤炭和石油。全球净能源需求的下降（2020年上半年，相对于2016年至2019年的平均水平为1.3）掩盖了化石燃料发电厂供应量的大幅下降（6.1），同时低碳能源的激增（6.2）。伴随而来的电力需求日周期变化也有利于低碳发电机，包括在大多数国家，早晨的斜坡变平，中午的峰值降低，以及上午和中午的负荷峰值都有所延迟。然而，此后许多国家的电力部门排放强度出现反弹，因此，缓解气候变化的一个关键问题是，作为绿色复苏的一部分，各国能够在多大程度上实现和保持较低的、普遍性的电力碳强度。
