## \[1\] Consumer Spending and the Cross Section of StockReturns

**标题****:****消费者支出与股票收益的横截面**

**来源****:The Journal of PortfolioManagement July 2022,  jpm.2022.1.365**

**作者****:Tarun Gupta, Edward Leung andViorel Roscovan**

**链接****:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/05/27/jpm.2022.1.365;**

Abstract: Using a unique dataset of individual transactions-level data for a universeof US consumer facing stocks, we examine the information content of consumercredit and debit card spending in explaining future stock returns. Our analysisshows that consumer spending data positively predict various measures of acompany’s future earnings surprises up to three quarters in the future. Thispredictive power remains strong in both large- and small-cap universes ofconsumer discretionary firms in our sample and is robust to the type of transactionsdata considered (credit card, debit card, or both), although the relationshipis stronger in the small-cap universe where informational asymmetries are morepronounced. Based on this empirical observation, we build a simple long–shortstrategy that takes long–short positions in the top/bottom tercile of stocksranked on our real-time sales signal. The strategy generates statistically andeconomically significant returns of 16% per annum net of transaction costs andafter controlling for the common sources of systematic factor returns. A simpleoptimization exercise to form (tangency) mean–variance-efficient portfolios offactors leads to an optimal factor allocation that assigns almost 50% weight toour long–short portfolio. Our results suggest that consumer transaction leveldata can serve as a more accurate and persistent signal of a firm’s growthpotential and future returns.

**Key Finding**

\-

Abstract:利用美国面向消费者的股票的单一交易数据集，我们检验了消费者信用卡和借记卡支出在解释未来股票回报时的信息内容。我们的分析表明，消费者支出数据积极预测了公司未来收益的各种衡量指标，在未来的三个季度里，这些指标会出人意料。这种预测能力在我们样本中的大型和小型非必需消费品公司中都很强，并且对所考虑的交易数据类型（信用卡、借记卡或两者）很稳健，尽管这种关系在信息不对称更为明显的小型股领域中更强。基于这一实证观察，我们构建了一个简单的多空策略，即在实时销售信号上排名的股票的顶部/底部三分之一中进行多空头寸。该策略在控制了系统因素回报的共同来源后，每年产生16%的统计和经济显著回报，扣除交易成本。通过一个简单的优化练习，形成（相切）均值-方差有效的因素组合，可以得到一个最优的因素分配，将近50%的权重分配给我们的多空投资组合。我们的结果表明，消费者交易水平数据可以作为企业增长潜力和未来回报的更准确和持久的信号。

## \[2\] Portfolio Factory

**标题****:****组合工厂**

**来源****:The Journal of PortfolioManagement July 2022,  jpm.2022.1.364**

**作者****:Bernd Scherer**

**链接****:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/05/26/jpm.2022.1.364;**

Abstract: The author formalizes the concept of a portfolio factory as the automatedimplementation of an active model portfolio on a wide range of clientportfolios that differ across benchmarks, regulatory constraints, orclient-specific constraints. Within this context, the article shows thatportfolio factory as an active portfolio optimization problem is equivalent totracking error minimization between an active model portfolio and active clientportfolio. This approach allows well-established portfolio optimizationframeworks and creates greater acceptance among investment professionalsbecause portfolio factories create optimal tracking portfolios.

## \[3\] Understanding the Stable Components of Seasonalityin the Size Effect

**标题****:****理解规模效应中季节性的稳定成分**

**来源****:The Journal of PortfolioManagement July 2022,  jpm.2022.1.363**

**作者****:Boris Fays, Georges Hübner andMarie Lambert**

**链接****:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/05/18/jpm.2022.1.363;**

Abstract: This article employs a sequential portfolio sorting procedure, called DSN, tofactor size characteristics into returns. This leads to narrower portfoliosthan the original Fama–French (FF) small and big SMB components. The DSNsorting uncovers a purely seasonal part of the global size premium due to aJanuary effect. The stocks that are common to the FF and DSN small-capportfolios are smaller and of lower quality than those that are only retainedby the FF procedure. This phenomenon supports a tax-loss-pruning hypothesis, inwhich investors discard small-cap stocks with the lowest quality around theyear end for tax reasons.

**Key Finding**

\-

Abstract:本文采用了一种称为DSN的顺序投资组合排序程序，将规模特征因素化为回报。这导致投资组合比最初的Fama–French（FF）小型和大型SMB组件更窄。DSN排序揭示了由于1月效应，全球规模溢价的纯季节性部分。与仅通过FF程序保留的股票相比，FF和DSN小型股投资组合中常见的股票更小，质量更低。这一现象支持一种税收损失削减假设，即投资者出于税收原因，在年底左右抛售质量最低的小盘股。

## \[4\] Expected Stock Returns When Interest Rates Are Low

**标题****:****低利率时的预期股票回报**

**来源****:The Journal of PortfolioManagement July 2022,  jpm.2022.1.362**

**作者****:David Blitz**

**链接****:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/05/14/jpm.2022.1.362;**

Abstract: The equity risk premium is generally considered to be a reward that investorsearn on top of the prevailing risk-free return, implying that, all else equal,total expected stock returns should increase with the level of the risk-freereturn. We examine whether this notion is true using long-term historical data.Our statistical tests strongly reject the hypothesis that a higher risk-freereturn implies higher total average stock returns. Instead, expected stockreturns appear to be unrelated (or perhaps even inversely related) to the levelof the risk-free return. Thus, the equity risk premium tends to be higher whenthe risk-free return is low and vice versa. This result appears to stem fromthe operating performance of firms. Our findings challenge the conventionalwisdom about expected stock returns and have important implications for assetallocation decisions, in particular when risk-free rates are at extreme levels.

**Key Finding**

\-

Abstract:股票风险溢价通常被认为是投资者在普遍无风险回报的基础上获得的回报，这意味着，在所有其他条件相同的情况下，总预期股票回报应随着无风险回报的水平而增加。我们使用长期历史数据来检验这个概念是否正确。我们的统计检验强烈否定了无风险回报率越高意味着股票总平均回报率越高的假设。相反，预期的股票回报似乎与无风险回报水平无关（甚至可能是负相关）。因此，当无风险回报率较低时，股票风险溢价往往较高，反之亦然。这一结果似乎源于公司的经营业绩。我们的发现挑战了关于预期股票回报的传统智慧，并对资产配置决策具有重要影响，尤其是在无风险利率处于极端水平时。

## \[5\] How Have ETFs Changed Market Macro Efficiency andRisk Structure?

**标题****:ETF****是如何改变市场宏观效率和风险结构的？**

**来源****:The Journal of PortfolioManagement July 2022,  jpm.2022.1.361**

**作者****:Santiago Guzman, Joseph Peteul andAmir Rezaee**

**链接****:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/05/13/jpm.2022.1.361;**

Abstract: In the late 1990s, Samuelson offered a dictum suggesting that the equitymarket, although micro efficient, is also macro inefficient. That is, at theaggregate level, the market does not accurately reflect the availableinformation. We further explore this dictum in the context of a market that hasevolved to provide larger options for trading across the whole spectrum ofinvestors. We argue that the macro efficiency of the stock market has beenincreasing over the past 20 years. We show that the development and adoption ofexchange-traded funds (ETFs) have played a major role in the transmission ofinformation to the market, resulting in an increase in market macro efficiency.Our approach is based on the R-squared of the market model explaining US stockand sector returns. First, we propose these R-squared values as a proxy formarket macro efficiency. Then, we use regression analyses at the individualstock and sector levels to observe the relationship between the rise in ETFsand market macro efficiency. Finally, we use extrafinancial data related to theawareness and adoption of ETFs to confirm their contribution.

**Key Finding**

\-

Abstract:20世纪90年代末，萨缪尔森（Samuelson）提出了一条格言，指出股票市场虽然微观有效，但宏观效率也很低。也就是说，在总体水平上，市场无法准确反映可用信息。我们将在一个不断发展的市场背景下进一步探讨这一格言，该市场为所有投资者提供了更大的交易选择。我们认为，在过去20年中，股票市场的宏观效率一直在提高。我们表明，交易所交易基金（ETF）的发展和采用在向市场传递信息方面发挥了重要作用，从而提高了市场宏观效率。我们的方法基于解释美国股票和行业回报的市场模型的R平方。首先，我们提出这些R平方值作为市场宏观效率的代表。然后，我们在个股和部门层面上进行回归分析，以观察ETF的上涨与市场宏观效率之间的关系。最后，我们使用与ETF认知和采用相关的外部财务数据来确认其贡献。

Abstract: This article examines the role of top management teams (TMTs) in the USmutual fund industry, focusing on the shared experience between fund managers.We find that there is a significant positive relation between shared experiencein TMTs and fund performance. ## \[7\] Predicting Stock Index Changes

**标题****:****预测股票指数变化**

**来源****:The Journal of PortfolioManagement July 2022,  jpm.2022.1.359**

**作者****:Sascha Wilkens**

**链接****:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/05/11/jpm.2022.1.359;**

Abstract: The addition or deletion of companies to or from a stock index has majorconsequences, with a change in demand from index-tracking investors and fundsconstituting the most obvious one. Numerous event studies since the 1980s haveevidenced the existence of abnormal returns and trading volumes (index effect)around the announcement and actual change dates for stock indexes. This articlepresents a model-driven approach to predicting changes in the index membershipitself. Index rules are combined with models for the evolution of parameterssuch as market capitalization that drive a company’s potential inclusion orexclusion. Special attention is paid to the inherent model risk. The 2021revision of Germany’s blue-chip and mid-cap indexes, DAX and MDAX, both interms of the number of index members and admission criteria, serves as a casestudy.

**Key Finding**

\-

Abstract:股票指数中增加或删除公司会产生重大后果，指数跟踪投资者和基金的需求变化是最明显的后果。自20世纪80年代以来的大量事件研究证明，在股票指数的公告和实际变动日期前后，存在异常回报和交易量（指数效应）。本文提出了一种模型驱动的方法来预测指数成员关系本身的变化。指数规则与驱动公司潜在纳入或排除的市值等参数演变模型相结合。特别注意固有的模型风险。2021修订的德国蓝筹股和中盘指数DAX和MDAX，无论是从指数成员数量还是准入标准来看，都是一个案例研究。

## \[8\] The Impact of Intangible Capital on FactorPerformance Efficacy

**标题****:****无形资本对要素绩效效能的影响**

**来源****:The Journal of PortfolioManagement July 2022,  jpm.2022.1.358**

**作者****:Andrew L. Berkin, Amitabh Dugarand Jacob Pozharny**

**链接****:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/05/11/jpm.2022.1.358;**

Abstract:  Prevalent accounting standards requirethe cost of internally created intangibles to be expensed, disproportionatelyaffecting the book values of high and low intangible intensity (II) companies.We expect this to impact the performance efficacy of various factorscontextually, depending on the extent to which intangible capital affects theircomputation. We compare return spreads for Book to Market, a measure of Value,and Momentum for companies belonging to low and high II industries and findthat for Book to Market, they have declined significantly for companies in highII industries compared to spreads for companies in low II industries. We showthat this decline is linked to the increasing intangible intensity of companiesin high II industries. In contrast, Momentum spreads have increased forcompanies in high II industries. For investors who use multifactor strategies,the implication is that weighting Book to Market and Momentum according to theII of the company’s industry may be a useful technique for incorporating theeffects of intangibles.

**Key Finding**

\-

Abstract:现行会计准则要求将内部创造的无形资产的成本支出，这对高无形强度和低无形强度（II）公司的账面价值产生了不成比例的影响。我们预计，这会影响各种因素的绩效效能，具体取决于无形资本对其计算的影响程度。我们比较了低II行业和高II行业公司的账面收益率差（一种价值衡量指标）和动量，发现高II行业公司的账面收益率差与低II行业公司的账面收益率差相比显著下降。我们表明，这种下降与高II行业公司的无形强度增加有关。相比之下，高II行业公司的动量利差有所增加。对于使用多因素策略的投资者来说，这意味着根据公司行业II对账面市值和动量进行加权可能是一种有用的技术，可以将无形资产的影响纳入其中。
