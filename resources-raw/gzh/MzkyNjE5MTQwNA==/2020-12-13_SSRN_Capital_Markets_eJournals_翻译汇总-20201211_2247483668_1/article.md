\[4\] Do Auditors Accurately Predict Litigation and Reputation Consequences of Inaccurate Accounting Estimates?

标题:审计师是否准确地预测了不准确的会计估计导致的诉讼和声誉后果？

作者:Christine Gimbar(DePaul University),Molly Mercer(DePaul University)

Suggested Citation

Gimbar, Christine and Mercer, Molly, Do Auditors Accurately Predict Litigation and Reputation Consequences of Inaccurate Accounting Estimates? (June 25, 2020). Contemporary Accounting Research, Forthcoming, Available at SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3729169

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3729169

Abstract : To effectively manage audit risk, auditors must correctly predict the potential litigation and reputation consequences associated with inaccurate accounting estimates. Accurate predictions are critical because underestimation of negative consequences leads to excess legal exposure and overestimation leads to over-auditing. Our paper examines whether auditors correctly anticipate these litigation and reputation outcomes. We provide manager- and partner-level auditors with case facts from an auditor negligence lawsuit and ask them to predict the proportion of juries that will return verdicts against their firm. We then compare auditors’ predictions to the actual verdicts we observe when we provide the same set of case facts to mock jurors who deliberate as part of juries. We find that auditors overestimate the likelihood of negligence verdicts, especially when audit quality is relatively high. Our supplemental measures help explain the reasons for this overestimation: auditors tend to underestimate jurors’ perceptions of audit quality and willingness to attribute inaccurate estimates to situational factors. Finally, we examine auditors’ predictions about how a news article about the litigation will affect their reputation with the general public. Similar to our litigation results, we find that auditors tend to overestimate the article’s negative impact on auditor reputation. Collectively, our findings suggest that auditors overestimate litigation and reputation consequences resulting from inaccurate accounting estimates. This overestimation is consequential as it leads to inefficient allocation of audit resources.

Abstract:为了有效地管理审计风险，审计师必须正确地预测与不准确的会计估计相关的潜在诉讼和声誉后果。准确的预测至关重要，因为低估负面后果会导致过度的法律风险敞口，而高估则会导致过度审计。本文检验了审计师是否正确地预测了这些诉讼和声誉结果。我们向经理和合伙人级别的审计师提供审计师过失诉讼的案件事实，并要求他们预测陪审团将对其公司作出裁决的比例。然后，我们将审计人员的预测与我们观察到的实际判决进行比较，当我们向作为陪审团一部分进行审议的模拟陪审员提供相同的案件事实集时。我们发现，审计师高估了过失裁决的可能性，尤其是在审计质量相对较高的情况下。我们的补充措施有助于解释这种高估的原因：审计师往往低估陪审员对审计质量的看法，以及将不准确估计归因于情景因素的意愿。最后，我们考察了审计人员关于一篇关于诉讼的新闻文章将如何影响他们在公众中的声誉的预测。与我们的诉讼结果相似，我们发现审计师往往高估文章对审计师声誉的负面影响。总的来说，我们的调查结果表明，审计师高估了由于不准确的会计估计而导致的诉讼和声誉后果。这种高估是必然的，因为它导致审计资源分配效率低下。

\[5\] The Differential Role of R&D and SG&A for Earnings Management and Stock Price Manipulation

标题:R&D与SG&A在盈余管理与股价操纵中的差异作用

作者:Estelle Sun(Boston University - Questrom School of Business)

Suggested Citation

Sun, Estelle Yuan, The Differential Role of R&D and SG&A for Earnings Management and Stock Price Manipulation (July 11, 2020). Contemporary Accounting Research, Forthcoming, Available at SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3733440

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3733440

Abstract : This paper documents a differential role of R&D versus SG&A for real earnings management. The distinction of these two components is important because prior studies mostly examine their combined use, but firms could manipulate them differently given the differing valuation implications: reduced SG&A is viewed positively by investors as evidence of cost reduction, while reduced R&D is viewed negatively by investors as such expenditures are critical signals of expected growth. I examine their use in the context of seasoned equity offerings (SEOs) as well as firms receiving Accounting and Auditing Enforcement Releases (AAERs). Although both groups face strong incentives to manage earnings upward by reducing expenses, I predict and find that firms will reduce SG&A but increase R&D. During the manipulation period, SEO and AAER firms exhibit lower discretionary SG&A and higher discretionary R&D, relative to control firms, and investors positively value low discretionary SG&A and high discretionary R&D. Overall, this study confirms the importance of distinguishing between R&D and SG&A in real earnings management contexts, and suggests a complementary (substitutive) relation between cutting SG&A (R&D) and accruals management.

Abstract:本文记录了R&D与SG&a在实际盈余管理中的不同作用。这两个组成部分的区别很重要，因为之前的研究大多考察了它们的组合用途，但考虑到不同的估值含义，企业可能会对它们进行不同的操纵：降低SG&A被投资者视为成本降低的积极证据，而减少的研发则被投资者视为此类支出的负面因素是预期增长的关键信号。我研究了它们在经验丰富的股票发行（seo）以及接受会计和审计执行豁免（AAERs）的公司中的使用情况。尽管这两个集团都面临着通过减少开支来管理利润的强烈动机，但我预测并发现，企业将减少SG&A，但增加研发。在操纵期间，相对于控制公司，SEO和AAER公司的可自由裁量SG&A和更高的自主研发能力，投资者对低自由裁量SG&A和高自由裁量R&D给予了正面评价。总体而言，本研究证实了在实际盈余管理背景下区分R&D和SG&A的重要性，并提出了削减SG&A（R&D）与应计利润管理之间的互补（替代）关系。

\[6\] Asymmetric Inventory Management and the Direction of Sales Changes

标题:非对称库存管理与销售变化方向

作者:Iny Hwang(Seoul National University - College of Business Administration),Taejin Jung(Shenzhen University - Shenzhen Audencia Business School),Woo-Jong Lee(Seoul National University),Daniel G. Yang(Ohio State University (OSU) - Fisher College of Business)

Suggested Citation

Hwang, Iny and Jung, Taejin and Lee, Woo-Jong and Yang, Daniel G., Asymmetric Inventory Management and the Direction of Sales Changes (July 27, 2020). Contemporary Accounting Research, Forthcoming, Available at SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3735792

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3735792

Abstract : We study manufacturing firms’ asymmetric inventory investment in response to sales changes. Focusing on the costs of resource adjustment and stockout which likely differ in sales-increasing and sales-decreasing periods, we predict and find that inventory investment declines less during periods with sales decreases than it rises during periods with sales increases. We validate this claim by showing that managers’ expectations of future demand and desire to avoid inventory stockouts are important determinants of this asymmetry. In addition, we find that asymmetric inventory investment provides useful information for predicting future sales growth, and that both managers’ and analysts’ sales forecasts are positively associated with the asymmetry. Lastly, we document that forecasts of future sales growth that incorporate asymmetric inventory investment are associated with lower absolute forecast errors than benchmark forecasts. Overall, we highlight the importance of inventory information in understanding managers’ resource adjustment and utilization decisions that have implications for forecasting future demand. Our findings on asymmetric inventory management provide new insights to fundamental analysis based on inventory signals.

Abstract:研究了制造业企业对销售变动的非对称库存投资。针对销售增长期和销售下降期可能不同的资源调整和缺货成本，我们预测并发现库存投资在销售下降期间的下降幅度小于在销售增加期间的上升。我们通过显示经理人对未来需求的预期和避免库存缺货的愿望是这种不对称性的重要决定因素，从而验证了这一说法。此外，我们发现非对称库存投资为预测未来销售增长提供了有用的信息，经理人和分析师的销售预测都与不对称性呈正相关。最后，我们记录了包含非对称库存投资的未来销售增长预测与低于基准预测的绝对预测误差相关。总的来说，我们强调库存信息在理解管理者的资源调整和利用决策中的重要性，这些决策对预测未来需求有影响。我们对非对称库存管理的发现为基于库存信号的基本面分析提供了新的见解。

\[8\] The Effect of Humanizing Robo-Advisors on Investor Judgments

标题:机器人顾问人性化对投资者判断的影响

作者:Frank D. Hodge(University of Washington - Michael G. Foster School of Business),Kim I. Mendoza(University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign - Department of Accountancy),Roshan K. Sinha(Indiana University - Kelley School of Business - Department of Accounting)

Suggested Citation

Hodge, Frank Douglas and Mendoza, Kim I. and Sinha, Roshan, The Effect of Humanizing Robo-Advisors on Investor Judgments (August 20, 2020). Contemporary Accounting Research, Forthcoming, Available at SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3736057

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3736057

Abstract : We examine the effect of humanizing (naming) robo-advisors on investor judgments, which has taken on increased importance as robo-advisors have become increasingly common and there is currently little SEC regulation governing key aspects of their use. In our first experiment, we predict and find that investors are more likely to rely on the investment recommendation of an unnamed robo-advisor, whereas they are more likely to rely on the investment recommendation of a named human advisor. Theory suggests one reason that naming a robo-advisor may have drawbacks pertains to the complexity of the task the robo-advisor performs. We explore the importance of task complexity in our second experiment. We predict and find that investors are less likely to rely on a named robo-advisor when the advisor is perceived to be performing a relatively complex task, consistent with our first experiment, and more likely to rely on a named robo-advisor when the advisor is perceived to be performing a relatively simple task, consistent with prior research on human-computer interactions. Our findings contribute to the literature examining how technology influences the acquisition and use of financial information and the general literature on human-computer interactions. Our study also addresses a call by the SEC to learn more about robo-advisors. Lastly, our study has practical implications for wealth management firms by demonstrating the potentially negative effects of making robo-advisors more humanlike in an attempt to engage and attract users.

Abstract:我们研究了人性化（命名）机器人顾问对投资者判断的影响，随着机器人顾问越来越普遍，目前美国证券交易委员会几乎没有监管机器人顾问使用的关键方面，这一点变得越来越重要。在我们的第一个实验中，我们预测并发现，投资者更可能依赖一个无名的机器人顾问的投资建议，而他们更可能依赖一个具名的人类顾问的投资建议。理论表明，命名机器人顾问可能有缺点的一个原因与机器人顾问执行的任务的复杂性有关。我们在第二个实验中探讨了任务复杂性的重要性。我们预测并发现，与我们的第一个实验一致，当被认为执行一个相对复杂的任务时，投资者不太可能依赖一个指定的机器人顾问，而当顾问被认为正在执行一个相对简单的任务时，投资者更可能依赖一个命名的机器人顾问，这与之前的研究一致人机交互。我们的发现有助于研究技术如何影响财务信息的获取和使用的文献，以及有关人机交互的一般文献。我们的研究还提到了美国证券交易委员会（SEC）的一项呼吁，希望了解更多有关机器人顾问的信息。最后，我们的研究对财富管理公司具有实际意义，通过证明让机器人顾问更人性化以吸引和吸引用户的潜在负面影响。
