\[1\] Real and Private-Value Assets

标题:不动产和私人价值资产

作者:William N Goetzmann, Christophe Spaenjers, Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3497/6218781; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab035

Abstract : Real and private-value assets—defined here as the sum of real estate, infrastructure, collectibles, and noncorporate business equity—compose an investment class worth an estimated $\\$$84 trillion in the U.S. alone. Furthermore, private values can affect pricing in many other financial markets, such as that for sustainable investments. This paper introduces the research on real assets and private values that can be found in this special issue. It also reviews recent advances and highlights new research directions on a number of topics in the real assets space that we believe to be particularly important and exciting.

Abstract:此处定义的房地产和私人价值资产是指房地产、基础设施、收藏品和非公司企业股权的总和，仅在美国就构成了一个价值约84万亿美元的投资类别。此外，私人价值可以影响许多其他金融市场的定价，例如可持续投资的定价。本文介绍了本期专刊对实物资产和私人价值的研究。它还回顾了最近的进展，并强调了新的研究方向，在一些课题在实际资产领域，我们认为是特别重要和令人兴奋的。

\[2\] Climate Change and Long-Run Discount Rates: Evidence from Real Estate

标题:气候变化与长期贴现率：来自房地产的证据

作者:Stefano Giglio, Matteo Maggiori, Krishna Rao, Johannes Stroebel, Andreas Weber

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3527/6187965; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab032

Abstract : We show that housing markets provide information about the appropriate discount rates for valuing investments in climate change abatement. Real estate is exposed to both consumption and climate risk and its term structure of discount rates is downward sloping, reaching 2.6% for payoffs beyond 100 years. We use a tractable asset pricing model that incorporates features of climate change to show that the term structure of discount rates for climate-hedging investments is thus upward sloping but bounded above by the risk-free rate. At horizons at which risk-free rates are unavailable, the estimated housing discount rates provide an upper bound.

Abstract:我们表明，房地产市场提供了有关适当的贴现率的信息，以评估在气候变化减缓方面的投资。房地产面临着消费和气候风险，其贴现率的期限结构向下倾斜，超过100年的回报率达到2.6%。我们使用一个结合了气候变化特征的易于处理的资产定价模型来说明，气候对冲投资的贴现率期限结构是向上倾斜的，但受到无风险利率的限制。在没有无风险利率的水平上，估计的住房贴现率提供了一个上限。

\[3\] The Rate of Return on Real Estate: Long-Run Micro-Level Evidence

标题:房地产收益率：长期微观证据

作者:David Chambers, Christophe Spaenjers, Eva Steiner

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3572/6187963; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab028

Abstract : Real estate—housing in particular—is a less profitable investment in the long run than previously thought. We hand-collect property-level financial data for the institutional real estate portfolios of four large Oxbridge colleges over the period 1901–1983. Gross income yields initially fluctuate around 5%, but then trend downward (upward) for agricultural and residential (commercial) real estate. Long-term real income growth rates are close to zero for all property types. Our findings imply annualized real total returns, net of costs, ranging from approximately 2.3% for residential to 4.5% for agricultural real estate.

Abstract:从长远来看，房地产尤其是住房投资的利润要低于此前的预期。我们手工收集了1901-1983年间四所牛津剑桥大学机构房地产投资组合的房地产水平财务数据。总收入收益率最初在5%左右波动，但随后农业和住宅（商业）房地产呈下降（上升）趋势。所有房地产类型的长期实际收入增长率都接近于零。我们的研究结果意味着扣除成本后的年化实际总回报率，从住宅的约2.3%到农业房地产的约4.5%不等。

\[4\] The Total Return and Risk to Residential Real Estate

标题:住宅房地产的总收益与风险

作者:Piet Eichholtz, Matthijs Korevaar, Thies Lindenthal, Ronan Tallec

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3608/6222230; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab042

Abstract : We estimate total returns to rental housing by studying over 170,000 hand-collected archival observations of prices and rents for individual houses in Paris (1809–1943) and Amsterdam (1900–1979). The annualized real total return, net of costs and taxes, is 4.0% for Paris and 4.8% for Amsterdam and entirely comes from rental yields. Our returns weakly correlate with the implied returns in Jordà et al. (2019) and are substantially lower. We decompose total return risk at the individual asset level and find that yield risk becomes an increasingly important component of property-level risk for longer investment horizons.

Abstract:我们通过研究170000多份对巴黎（1809年至1943年）和阿姆斯特丹（1900年至1979年）个别房屋的价格和租金的档案观察，估计出租房屋的总回报。扣除成本和税收后，巴黎和阿姆斯特丹的年化实际总回报率分别为4.0%和4.8%，完全来自租金收益率。我们的回报率与Jordáet al.（2019）中的隐含回报率弱相关，且显著较低。我们将总收益风险分解为单个资产层面，发现收益率风险在较长的投资期限内成为资产层面风险的一个越来越重要的组成部分。

\[5\] Asset-Level Risk and Return in Real Estate Investments

标题:房地产投资的资产风险与收益

作者:Jacob S Sagi

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3647/5941474; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa122

Abstract : In stark contrast with liquid asset returns, commercial real estate idiosyncratic return means and variances do not scale with the holding period, even after accounting for all cash flow-relevant events. This puzzling phenomenon survives controlling for vintage effects, systematic risk heterogeneity, and a host of other explanations. To explain the findings, I derive an equilibrium search-based asset-pricing model that, when calibrated, provides an excellent fit to transactions data. A structural model of transaction risk seems crucial to understanding real estate price dynamics. These insights extend to other highly illiquid asset classes, such as private equity and residential real estate.

Abstract:与流动性资产回报形成鲜明对比的是，商业地产的特殊回报手段和差异并不随持有期而扩大，即使在考虑了所有现金流相关事件之后也是如此。这种令人费解的现象在控制了年份效应、系统风险异质性和许多其他解释之后仍然存在。为了解释这些发现，我推导了一个基于均衡搜索的资产定价模型，当进行校准时，该模型能够很好地拟合交易数据。交易风险的结构模型似乎对理解房地产价格动态至关重要。这些见解延伸到其他高流动性资产类别，如私人股本和住宅房地产。

\[6\] Idiosyncratic Risk in Housing Markets

标题:房地产市场的特殊风险

作者:Marco Giacoletti

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3695/6187964; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab033

Abstract : This paper studies the idiosyncratic risk component of individual house capital gains using data on resales and intermediate capital investments. The idiosyncratic component is large; its dynamics do not follow a random walk; and its magnitude is associated with proxies of information quality and market liquidity at the level of individual properties. Accounting for idiosyncratic risk substantially changes the assessment of the risk-return trade-off for housing: it reduces Sharpe ratios and makes them holding period dependent. I use a simple quantitative portfolio model to show that homeowners may be willing to make significant payments to insure against idiosyncratic housing risk.

Abstract:本文利用转售和中间资本投资数据研究了个人住房资本收益的特殊风险成分。特质成分大；它的动力学不遵循随机游动；它的大小与信息质量和市场流动性在单个属性水平上的代理有关。特殊风险的会计处理实质上改变了对住房的风险回报权衡的评估：它降低了夏普比率，并使其与持有期相关。我使用一个简单的定量投资组合模型来表明，房主可能愿意支付大量款项，以防范特殊的住房风险。

\[7\] The Effects of a Targeted Financial Constraint on the Housing Market

标题:定向财政约束对房地产市场的影响

作者:Lu Han, Chandler Lutz, Benjamin Sand, Derek Stacey

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3742/6239712; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab047

Abstract : We study how financial constraints affect the housing market by exploiting a regulatory change that increases the down payment requirement for homes selling for $\\$$1M or more. Using Toronto data, we find that the policy causes excess bunching of homes listed at $\\$$1M and heightened bidding intensity for these homes, but only a muted response in sales. While difficult to reconcile in a frictionless market, these findings are consistent with the implications derived from an equilibrium search model with auctions and financial constraints. Our analysis points to the importance of designing macroprudential policies that recognize the strategic responses of market participants.

Abstract:我们研究了金融约束如何影响房地产市场，通过利用监管变化，提高了100万美元或以上住房的首付要求。利用多伦多的数据，我们发现这一政策导致了100万美元以上的房源过度集中，并提高了这些房源的竞价强度，但销售反应平淡。虽然在无摩擦市场中很难调和，但这些发现与拍卖和金融约束下的均衡搜索模型的含义是一致的。我们的分析指出，制定宏观审慎政策的重要性，承认市场参与者的战略反应。

\[8\] Gendered Prices

标题:性别价格

作者:Renée B Adams, Roman Kräussl, Marco Navone, Patrick Verwijmeren

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3789/6218783; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab046

Abstract : We provide evidence that culture is a source of pricing bias. In a sample of 1.9 million auction transactions in 49 countries, paintings by female artists sell at an unconditional discount of 42.1%. The gender discount increases with measures of country-level gender inequality—even in artist fixed effects regressions. Our results are robust to accounting for potential gender differences in art characteristics and their liquidity. Evidence from two experiments supports the argument that women’s art may sell for less because it is made by women. However, the gender discount reduces over time as gender equality increases.

Abstract:我们提供的证据表明，文化是定价偏差的一个来源。在49个国家的190万宗拍卖交易中，女艺术家的作品以42.1%的无条件折扣成交。即使在固定效应回归中，性别折扣也会随着国家一级的性别不平等而增加。我们的研究结果对于解释艺术特征及其流动性的潜在性别差异是有力的。来自两个实验的证据支持这样一种观点：女性艺术品可能卖得更低，因为它是由女性制作的。然而，随着时间的推移，性别折扣随着性别平等的增加而减少。

\[9\] When a Master Dies: Speculation and Asset Float

标题:大师死后：投机与资产流动

作者:Julien Pénasse, Luc Renneboog, José A Scheinkman

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3840/6124375; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab006

Abstract : An artist’s death constitutes a negative shock to his future production; death permanently decreases the artist’s float. We use this shock to test predictions of speculative trading models with short-selling constraints. As predicted in our model, we find that an artist’s premature death leads to a permanent increase in prices and turnover; this effect being larger for more famous artists. We document that premature death increases prices (by 54.7%) and secondary market volume (by 63.2%).

Abstract:艺术家的死对他未来的作品构成了负面冲击；死亡会永久地减少艺术家的浮力。我们利用这种冲击来检验带有卖空约束的投机交易模型的预测。正如我们的模型所预测的，我们发现艺术家的过早死亡会导致价格和营业额的永久性增长；这种影响对更著名的艺术家来说更大。我们记录了过早死亡会增加价格（54.7%）和二级市场交易量（63.2%）。

\[10\] Institutional Investors and Infrastructure Investing

标题:机构投资者与基础设施投资

作者:Aleksandar Andonov, Roman Kräussl, Joshua Rauh

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3880/6239714; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab048

Abstract : Institutional investors expect infrastructure to deliver long-term stable returns but gain exposure to infrastructure predominantly through finite-horizon closed private funds. The cash flows delivered by infrastructure funds display similar volatility and cyclicality as other private equity investments, and their performance similarly depends on quick deal exits. Despite weak risk-adjusted performance and failure to match the supposed characteristics of infrastructure assets, closed funds have received more commitments over time, particularly from public investors. Public institutional investors perform worse than private institutional investors. ESG preferences and regulations explain 25|$\\%$|–40|$\\%$| of their increased allocation to infrastructure and 30|$\\%$| of their underperformance.

Abstract:机构投资者预计，基础设施将带来长期稳定的回报，但主要通过有限期封闭式私人基金获得基础设施敞口。基础设施基金交付的现金流显示出与其他私人股本投资类似的波动性和周期性，它们的表现同样取决于快速交易退出。尽管风险调整后的表现疲弱，且未能与基础设施资产的预期特征相匹配，但封闭式基金随着时间的推移收到了更多的承诺，特别是来自公众投资者的承诺。公共机构投资者的表现比私人机构投资者差。ESG偏好和法规解释了25 |$\\%$|–40 |$\\%$|的基础设施配置增加和30 |$\\%$|的表现不佳。

\[11\] Personal Wealth, Self-Employment, and Business Ownership

标题:个人财富、自营职业和企业所有权

作者:Aymeric Bellon, J Anthony Cookson, Erik P Gilje, Rawley Z Heimer

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3935/6218782; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab044

Abstract : We study the effect of personal wealth on entrepreneurial decisions using data on mineral payments from Texas shale drilling to individuals throughout the United States. Large cash windfalls increase business formation by 0.8 to 2.1 percentage points, but do not affect transitions to self-employment. By contrast, cash windfalls significantly extend self-employment spells, but do not affect the duration of business ownership. Our findings help reconcile contrasting findings in prior work: liquidity constraints have different effects on entrepreneurial activity that may depend on the entrepreneur’s motivations.

Abstract:我们研究了个人财富对创业决策的影响，使用了德克萨斯州页岩钻探向全美个人支付的矿产付款数据。巨额现金暴利使企业形成率提高了0.8至2.1个百分点，但并不影响向自营职业的过渡。相比之下，现金暴利显著延长了自营职业的时间，但并不影响企业所有权的持续时间。我们的发现有助于调和先前工作中的对比发现：流动性约束对创业活动有不同的影响，这可能取决于创业者的动机。

\[12\] Get Real! Individuals Prefer More Sustainable Investments

标题:现实点！个人更喜欢可持续的投资

作者:Rob Bauer, Tobias Ruof, Paul Smeets

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/8/3976/6237929; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab037

Abstract : The United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) have created societal and political pressure for pension funds to address sustainable investing. We run two field surveys (n = 1,669, n = 3,186) with a pension fund that grants its members a real vote on its sustainable-investment policy. Two-thirds of participants are willing to expand the fund’s engagement with companies based on selected SDGs, even when they expect engagement to hurt financial performance. Support remains strong after the fund implements the choice. A key reason is participants’ strong social preferences.

Abstract:联合国的可持续发展目标给养恤基金带来了社会和政治压力，要求它们解决可持续投资问题。我们对一家养老基金进行了两次实地调查（n=1669，n=3186），该基金会授予其成员对其可持续投资政策的实际投票权。三分之二的参与者愿意根据选定的可持续发展目标扩大基金与公司的合作，即使他们预计这种合作会损害财务业绩。在基金实施这一选择后，支持力度依然强劲。一个关键原因是参与者强烈的社会偏好。
