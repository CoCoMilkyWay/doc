\[1\] Network based diversification of stock and cryptocurrency portfolios

标题:基于网络的股票和加密货币投资组合多样化

作者:Dimitar Kitanovski, Igor Mishkovski, Viktor Stojkoski, Miroslav Mirchev

来源:ARXIV\_20240822

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.11739

Abstract : Maintaining a balance between returns and volatility is a common strategy for portfolio diversification, whether investing in traditional equities or digital assets like cryptocurrencies. One approach for diversification is the application of community detection or clustering, using a network representing the relationships between assets. We examine two network representations, one based on a standard distance matrix based on correlation, and another based on mutual information. The Louvain and Affinity propagation algorithms were employed for finding the network communities (clusters) based on annual data. Furthermore, we examine building assets  co occurrence networks, where communities are detected for each month throughout a whole year and then the links represent how often assets belong to the same community. Portfolios are then constructed by selecting several assets from each community based on local properties (degree centrality), global properties (closeness centrality), or explained variance (Principal component analysis), with three value ranges (max, med, min), calculated on a maximal spanning tree or a fully connected community sub graph. We explored these various strategies on data from the S &P 500 and the Top 203 cryptocurrencies with a market cap above 2M USD in the period from Jan 2019 to Sep 2022. Moreover, we study into more details the periods of the beginning of the COVID 19 outbreak and the start of the war in Ukraine. The results confirm some of the previous findings already known for traditional stock markets and provide some further insights, while they reveal an opposing trend in the crypto assets market.

\[3\] Rooftop and Community Solar Adoption with Income Heterogeneity

标题:收入不均的屋顶和社区太阳能采用

作者:Swapnil Rayal, Apurva Jain, Matthew Lorig

来源:ARXIV\_20240823

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.11970

Abstract : Each household in a population characterized by income heterogeneity faces random demand for electricity and decides if and when it should adopt a solar product, rooftop solar or community solar. A central planner, aiming to meet an adoption level target within a set time, offers net metering and subsidy on solar products and minimizes its total cost. Our focus is on analyzing the interactions of three new features we add to the literature  income diversity, availability of community solar, and consideration of adoption timing.  Methodology and results   We develop a bilevel optimization formulation to derive the optimal subsidy policy. The upper level (planner s) problem is a constrained non linear optimization model in which the planner aims to minimize the average subsidy cost. The lower level (household s) problem is an optimal stopping formulation, which captures the adoption decisions of the households. We derive a closed form expression for the distribution of optimal adoption time of households for a given subsidy policy. We show that the planner s problem is convex in the case of homogeneous subsidy for the two products.  Managerial implications   Our results underscore the importance for planners to consider three factors   adoption level target, time target, and subsidy budget   simultaneously as they work in tandem to influence the adoption outcome. The planners must also consider the inclusion of community solar in their plans because, as we show, community and rooftop solar attract households from different sides of the income spectrum. In the presence of income inequality, the availability of community makes it easier to meet solar adoption targets.

Keywords :

Abstract :以收入异质性为特征的人口中的每个家庭都面临着随机的电力需求，并决定是否以及何时采用太阳能产品、屋顶太阳能或社区太阳能。中央规划者旨在在规定时间内达到采用水平目标，为太阳能产品提供净计量和补贴，并最大限度地降低其总成本。我们的重点是分析我们添加到文献中的三个新特征的相互作用——收入多样性、社区太阳能的可用性和采用时间的考虑。方法和结果我们开发了一个双层优化公式来推导最优补贴政策。上层（规划者）问题是一个约束非线性优化模型，其中规划者的目标是使平均补贴成本最小化。下级（家庭）问题是一个最优的停止公式，它捕捉到了家庭的收养决定。我们推导出了一个封闭形式的表达式，用于描述给定补贴政策下家庭最佳采用时间的分布。我们证明了在两种产品的同质补贴的情况下，规划者问题是凸的。管理意义我们的研究结果强调了规划者同时考虑三个因素的重要性——采用水平目标、时间目标和补贴预算，因为它们协同作用，影响采用结果。规划者还必须考虑将社区太阳能纳入他们的计划，因为正如我们所示，社区和屋顶太阳能吸引了来自不同收入阶层的家庭。在收入不平等的情况下，社区的可用性使实现太阳能采用目标变得更加容易。

\[4\] EX DRL

标题:前DRL

作者:Parvin Malekzadeh, Zissis Poulos, Jacky Chen, Zeyu Wang, Konstantinos N. Plataniotis

来源:ARXIV\_20240823

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.12446

Abstract : Recent advancements in Distributional Reinforcement Learning (DRL) for modeling loss distributions have shown promise in developing hedging strategies in derivatives markets. A common approach in DRL involves learning the quantiles of loss distributions at specified levels using Quantile Regression (QR). This method is particularly effective in option hedging due to its direct quantile based risk assessment, such as Value at Risk (VaR) and Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR). However, these risk measures depend on the accurate estimation of extreme quantiles in the loss distribution s tail, which can be imprecise in QR based DRL due to the rarity and extremity of tail data, as highlighted in the literature. To address this issue, we propose EXtreme DRL (EX DRL), which enhances extreme quantile prediction by modeling the tail of the loss distribution with a Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD). This method introduces supplementary data to mitigate the scarcity of extreme quantile observations, thereby improving estimation accuracy through QR. Comprehensive experiments on gamma hedging options demonstrate that EX DRL improves existing QR based models by providing more precise estimates of extreme quantiles, thereby improving the computation and reliability of risk metrics for complex financial risk management.

Keywords :

Abstract :分布强化学习（DRL）在模拟损失分布方面的最新进展表明，它有望在衍生品市场开发对冲策略。DRL中的一种常见方法是使用分位数回归（QR）在指定水平上学习损失分布的分位数。由于其直接的基于分位数的风险评估，如风险价值（VaR）和条件风险价值（CVaR），这种方法在期权套期保值中特别有效。然而，这些风险度量取决于对损失分布尾部极端分位数的准确估计，正如文献中强调的那样，由于尾部数据的稀有性和极端性，在基于QR的DRL中可能是不精确的。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了极限DRL（EX DRL），它通过用广义帕累托分布（GPD）对损失分布的尾部进行建模来增强极端分位数预测。该方法引入补充数据来缓解极端分位数观测值的稀缺性，从而通过QR提高估计精度。对伽马对冲期权的综合实验表明，EX DRL通过提供更精确的极端分位数估计来改进现有的基于QR的模型，从而提高了复杂金融风险管理的风险度量的计算和可靠性。
