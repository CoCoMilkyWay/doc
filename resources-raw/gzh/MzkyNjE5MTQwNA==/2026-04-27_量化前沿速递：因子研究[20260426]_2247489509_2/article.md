\[1\] Time Preference and Tax Burden Acceptance

标题:时间偏好与税负承受

作者:Eiji Yamamura, Fumio Ohtake

来源:ARXIV\_20260420

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.15349

Abstract : This paper examines the extent to which individual time preferences are associated with the willingness to accept different tax burdens. The first is an intertemporal redistribution in which a current consumption tax increase is exchanged for a proportional future reduction. The second is a contemporaneous redistribution where the tax burden borne by individuals is transferred directly to those with significantly lower incomes than their own. Using a cross sectional online survey of approximately 12,000 observations, we found through various regression analyses that higher time preference is negatively associated with acceptance in both domains. Crucially, the negative coefficient is larger in absolute value for contemporaneous redistribution than for the intertemporal one.

Abstract : Satellite data are increasingly used to measure economic activity, yet port level trade remains largely unmeasured from space. \[5\] A Theory of Covenant Accounting Adjustment

来源:ARXIV\_20260420

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.15661

Abstract : We develop an incomplete contracting model with accounting based covenants to study how covenant accounting adjustments are made and what properties they exhibit. Standard accounting rules (e.g., GAAP) can generate false alarm errors or undue optimism errors. The manager can exert costly effort to privately identify these errors and propose adjustments. If errors are not corrected, control rights may be inefficiently allocated, leading to costly renegotiation. \[7\] Optimal Insurance Menu Design under the Expected Value Premium Principle

标题:期望值保费原则下的最优保险菜单设计

作者:Xia Han, Bin Li

来源:ARXIV\_20260420

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.15881

Abstract : This paper studies optimal insurance design under asymmetric information in a Stackelberg framework, where a monopolistic insurer faces uncertainty about both the insured s risk attitude, captured by a risk aversion parameter, and the insured s risk type, characterized by the loss distribution. In particular, when the risk type is unobservable, we allow the risk aversion parameter to depend on the risk type. We construct a menu of contracts that maximizes the mean variance utilities of both parties under the expected value premium principle, subject to a truth telling constraint that ensures the truthful revelation of private information. We show that when risk attitude is private information, the optimal coverage takes the form of excess of loss insurance with linear pricing in terms of the risk loading (defined as the premium minus the expected loss), designed to screen risk preferences. In contrast, when risk type is unobserved, we restrict the coverage function to an excess of loss form and derive an ordinary differential equation that characterizes the optimal risk loading. Under mild conditions, we establish the existence and uniqueness of the solution. The results show that equilibrium contracts exhibit nonlinear pricing with decreasing risk loadings, implying that higher risk individuals face lower risk loadings in order to induce self selection. Finally, numerical illustrations demonstrate how parameter values and the distributions of unobserved heterogeneity affect the structure of optimal contracts and the resulting pricing schedule.

Keywords : 

Abstract :本文在Stackelberg框架下研究了非对称信息下的最优保险设计，其中垄断保险公司面临着由风险规避参数捕获的被保险人风险态度和以损失分布为特征的被保险风险类型的不确定性。特别是，当风险类型不可观察时，我们允许风险规避参数取决于风险类型。我们构建了一个合同菜单，在期望值溢价原则下，在保证私人信息真实披露的真实性约束下，最大化双方的均值方差效用。我们发现，当风险态度是私人信息时，最优保险范围采用超额损失保险的形式，根据风险负荷（定义为保费减去预期损失）进行线性定价，旨在筛选风险偏好。相比之下，当风险类型未被观察到时，我们将覆盖函数限制为超额损失形式，并推导出一个表征最优风险载荷的常微分方程。在温和条件下，我们建立了解的存在性和唯一性。结果表明，均衡合约表现出非线性定价，风险载荷降低，这意味着高风险个体面临较低的风险载荷，以诱导自我选择。最后，数值说明了参数值和未观察到的异质性分布如何影响最优合约的结构和由此产生的定价计划。

\[8\] Ranking Metrics

标题:排名指标

作者:Asmerilda Hitaj, Elisa Mastrogiacomo, Ilaria Peri, Marcelo Righi

来源:ARXIV\_20260421

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.16438

Abstract : This paper develops an axiomatic framework for ranking metrics, a general class of functionals for evaluating and ordering financial or insurance positions. Unlike traditional risk adjusted performance measures such as the Sharpe ratio, RAROC, or Omega that express reward per unit of risk, ranking metrics assign each position a performance level rather than a normalized return. Relying on monotonicity and a new property called cash quasiconcavity, we derive representation results linking ranking metrics to families of acceptance sets and risk measures, extending the theory of acceptability indices. Classical ratios arise as special cases, while new examples based on expected loss, Lambda quantile, and bibliometric indices illustrate the framework s flexibility. Abstract :地理背景通常被认为与汽车保险风险相关，但公共精算数据集提供的位置标识符有限，限制了如何在索赔频率模型中纳入和评估这些信息。本研究探讨了在这种约束下，如何将来自替代数据源的地理信息纳入汽车第三方责任（MTPL）索赔预测的精算模型。使用BeMTPL97数据集，我们采用区域级建模框架，并评估对看不见的邮政编码的预测性能。地理信息是通过OpenStreetMap和CORINE土地覆盖的环境指标以及比利时国家地理研究所发布的用于学术用途的正射影像这两个渠道引入的。我们评估了坐标、环境特征和图像嵌入在三个基线模型（广义线性模型（GLM）、正则化GLM和梯度增强树）中的预测贡献，而原始图像是使用卷积神经网络建模的。我们的结果表明，用构造的地理信息增加精算变量可以提高准确性。在整个实验中，线性和基于树的模型都从将坐标与5公里尺度提取的环境特征相结合中受益最大，而较小的社区也改善了基线规范。通常，当环境特征可用时，图像嵌入不会提高性能，但当这些特征不存在时，预训练的视觉变换器嵌入可以提高正则化GLM的准确性和稳定性。我们的结果表明，在区域级MTPL频率模型中，地理信息的预测价值与其说取决于模型的复杂性，不如说取决于地理的表示方式，并表明尽管个体级空间信息有限，但地理背景仍然可以被纳入。
