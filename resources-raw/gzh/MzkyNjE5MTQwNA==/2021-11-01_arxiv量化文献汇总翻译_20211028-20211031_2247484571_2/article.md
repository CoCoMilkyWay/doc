\[1\] Trading via Selective Classification

标题 : 选择性分类交易

作者 : Nestoras Chalkidis, Rahul Savani , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.14914

说明 : Comments: (8 pages, 6 figures, 4 tables, ICAIF'21)

Abstract : A binary classifier that tries to predict if the price of an asset will increase or decrease naturally gives rise to a trading strategy that follows the prediction and thus always has a position in the market. Selective classification extends a binary or many class classifier to allow it to abstain from making a prediction for certain inputs, thereby allowing a trade off between the accuracy of the resulting selective classifier against coverage of the input feature space. Selective classifiers give rise to trading strategies that do not take a trading position when the classifier abstains. We investigate the application of binary and ternary selective classification to trading strategy design. For ternary classification, in addition to classes for the price going up or down, we include a third class that corresponds to relatively small price moves in either direction, and gives the classifier another way to avoid making a directional prediction. We use a walk forward train validate test approach to evaluate and compare binary and ternary, selective and non selective classifiers across several different feature sets based on four classification approaches logistic regression, random forests, feed forward, and recurrent neural networks. We then turn these classifiers into trading strategies for which we perform backtests on commodity futures markets. Our empirical results demonstrate the potential of selective classification for trading.

摘要 : 试图预测资产价格是上涨还是下跌的二元分类器自然会产生跟随预测的交易策略，从而始终在市场中占有一席之地。选择性分类扩展了二进制或多类分类器，使其能够避免对某些输入进行预测，从而允许在结果选择性分类器的准确性与输入特征空间的覆盖率之间进行权衡。选择性分类器产生的交易策略在分类器放弃时不采取交易立场。我们研究了二元和三元选择性分类在交易策略设计中的应用。对于三元分类，除了价格上涨或下跌的分类外，我们还包括第三类，该类对应于两个方向上相对较小的价格变动，并为分类器提供了另一种避免进行方向预测的方法。基于四种分类方法logistic回归、随机森林、前馈和递归神经网络，我们使用一种前向训练验证测试方法来评估和比较多个不同特征集上的二元和三元、选择性和非选择性分类器。然后，我们将这些分类器转换为交易策略，并对商品期货市场进行回溯测试。我们的实证结果证明了选择性分类交易的潜力。

\[2\] Domestic Constraints in Crisis Bargaining

标题 : 危机谈判中的国内约束

作者 : Liqun Liu , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.14938

说明 : -

Abstract : I study how political bias and audience costs impose domestic institutional constraints that affect states capacity to reach peaceful agreements during crises. With a mechanism design approach, I derive the weighted budget constraint as necessary for the existence of peaceful agreements. The derivation has two major implications. On the one hand, if war must be averted, then audience costs do not provide incentives for political leaders to communicate private information. The rationale is that, absent the risk of war, leaders will pool on the strategy that induces the maximum bargaining gains. On the other hand, political bias matters for the scope of peace because it alters a state s expected war payoff war cannot be averted with probability one whenever two states of the highest types meet and find the war option preferable to peaceful outcomes.

摘要 : 我研究政治偏见和受众成本如何施加国内体制约束，影响各国在危机期间达成和平协议的能力。通过一种机制设计方法，我导出了存在和平协议所必需的加权预算约束。这一推导有两个主要含义。一方面，如果必须避免战争，那么受众成本并不能激励政治领导人交流私人信息。其基本原理是，在没有战争风险的情况下，领导人将集中精力制定能带来最大讨价还价收益的战略。另一方面，政治偏见对和平的范围很重要，因为它改变了一个国家预期的战争回报。每当最高类型的两个国家相遇并发现战争选择比和平结果更可取时，战争就不可能以概率1避免。

\[3\] Risk and return prediction for pricing portfolios of non performing consumer credit

标题 : 不良消费信贷定价组合的风险与收益预测

作者 : Siyi Wang, Xing Yan, Bangqi Zheng, Hu Wang, Wangli Xu, Nanbo Peng, Qi Wu , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.15102

说明 : Comments: Accepted by 2nd ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF'21)

Abstract : We design a system for risk analyzing and pricing portfolios of non performing consumer credit loans. The rapid development of credit lending business for consumers heightens the need for trading portfolios formed by overdue loans as a manner of risk transferring. However, the problem is nontrivial technically and related research is absent. We tackle the challenge by building a bottom up architecture, in which we model the distribution of every single loan s repayment rate, followed by modeling the distribution of the portfolio s overall repayment rate. To address the technical issues encountered, we adopt the approaches of simultaneous quantile regression, R copula, and Gaussian one factor copula model. To our best knowledge, this is the first study that successfully adopts a bottom up system for analyzing credit portfolio risks of consumer loans. We conduct experiments on a vast amount of data and prove that our methodology can be applied successfully in real business tasks.

摘要 : 我们设计了一个系统，用于对不良消费信贷组合进行风险分析和定价。消费者信贷业务的快速发展，增加了对逾期贷款形成的交易组合作为风险转移方式的需求。然而，这个问题在技术上并不重要，相关的研究也不多见。我们通过构建一个自下而上的体系结构来应对这一挑战，在该体系结构中，我们对每一笔贷款的还款率分布进行建模，然后对投资组合的总体还款率分布进行建模。为了解决遇到的技术问题，我们采用了同时分位数回归、R-copula和高斯单因子copula模型。据我们所知，这是第一个成功地采用自下而上系统分析消费贷款信用组合风险的研究。我们在大量数据上进行了实验，证明我们的方法可以成功地应用于实际业务任务中。

\[4\] Deep Calibration of Interest Rates Model

标题 : 利率模型的深度校准

作者 : Mohamed Ben Alaya, Ahmed Kebaier, Djibril Sarr , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.15133

说明 : -

Abstract : For any financial institution it is a necessity to be able to apprehend the behavior of interest rates. Despite the use of Deep Learning that is growing very fastly, due to many reasons (expertise, ease of use, ...) classic rates models such as CIR, or the Gaussian family are still being used widely. We propose to calibrate the five parameters of the G2 model using Neural Networks. To achieve that, we construct synthetic data sets of parameters drawn uniformly from a reference set of parameters calibrated from the market. From those parameters, we compute Zero Coupon and Forward rates and their covariances and correlations. Our first model is a Fully Connected Neural network and uses only covariances and correlations. We show that covariances are more suited to the problem than correlations. The second model is a Convulutional Neural Network using only Zero Coupon rates with no transformation. The methods we propose perform very quickly (less than 0.3 seconds for 2 000 calibrations) and have low errors and good fitting.

摘要 : 对于任何金融机构来说，都有必要了解利率的行为。尽管深度学习的使用发展非常迅速，但由于许多原因（专业知识、易用性等），经典的速率模型（如CIR或高斯族）仍在广泛使用。我们建议使用神经网络校准G2模型的五个参数。为了实现这一点，我们构建了从市场校准的参考参数集统一提取的参数合成数据集。根据这些参数，我们计算零息票和远期利率及其协方差和相关性。我们的第一个模型是完全连接的神经网络，只使用协方差和相关性。我们证明协方差比相关性更适合这个问题。第二个模型是一个仅使用零息票率且不进行转换的组合神经网络。我们提出的方法执行速度非常快（2000次校准不到0.3秒），误差小，拟合良好。

\[5\] Moral Hazard, Dynamic Incentives, and Ambiguous Perceptions

标题 : 道德风险、动态激励和模糊认知

作者 : Martin Dumav , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.15229

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper considers dynamic moral hazard settings, in which the consequences of the agent s actions are not precisely understood. In a new continuous time moral hazard model with drift ambiguity, the agent s unobservable action translates to drift set that describe the evolution of output. The agent and the principal have imprecise information about the technology, and both seek robust performance from a contract in relation to their respective worst case scenarios. We show that the optimal long term contract aligns the parties pessimistic expectations and broadly features compressing of the high powered incentives. Methodologically, we provide a tractable way to formulate and characterize optimal long run contracts with drift ambiguity. Substantively, our results provide some insights into the formal link between robustness and simplicity of dynamic contracts, in particular high powered incentives become less effective in the presence of ambiguity.

摘要 : 本文考虑的是动态道德风险环境，在这种环境下，代理人行为的后果无法准确理解。在一个新的具有漂移模糊性的连续时间道德风险模型中，主体的不可观测行为转化为描述输出演化的漂移集。代理人和委托人对技术有不精确的信息，双方都在各自的最坏情况下从合同中寻求稳健的绩效。我们证明了最优长期合同符合双方的悲观预期，并广泛体现了高动力激励的特点。在方法上，我们提供了一种易于处理的方法来制定和描述具有漂移模糊性的最优长期合同。实质上，我们的结果为动态契约的稳健性和简单性之间的正式联系提供了一些见解，特别是在存在模糊性的情况下，高功率激励变得不那么有效。

\[7\] Solving it correctly Prevalence and Persistence of Gender Gap in Basic Mathematics in rural India

标题 : 正确解决这一问题印度农村基础数学中性别差距的普遍性和持续性

作者 : Upasak Das, Karan Singhal , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.15312

说明 : -

Abstract : Mathematical ability is among the most important determinants of prospering in the labour market. Using multiple representative datasets with learning outcomes of over 2 million children from rural India in the age group 8 to 16 years, the paper examines the prevalence of gender gap in performance in mathematics and its temporal variation from 2010 to 2018. Our findings from the regressions show significant gender gap in mathematics, which is not observable for reading skills. This difference in mathematics scores remains prevalent across households of different socio economic and demographic groups. This gap is found to be persistent over time and it appears to increase as the children get older. We also find significant inter state variation with the north Indian states lagging behind considerably and the south Indian states showing a reverse gender gap. As an explanation to this, we observe evidence of a robust association between pre existing gender norms at the household and district level with higher gender gap. The findings, in light of other available evidence on the consequences of such gaps, call for the need to understand these gender specific differences more granularly and periodically to inform gender specific interventions.

摘要 : 数学能力是劳动力市场繁荣的最重要决定因素之一。本文使用多个具有代表性的数据集，对印度农村8至16岁年龄组的200多万儿童的学习成果进行了研究，考察了2010年至2018年数学成绩性别差异的普遍性及其时间变化。我们的回归结果显示，在数学方面存在显著的性别差异，这在阅读技能方面是无法观察到的。这种数学成绩的差异在不同社会经济和人口群体的家庭中仍然普遍存在。研究发现，随着时间的推移，这种差距会持续存在，而且随着孩子年龄的增长，这种差距似乎会增加。我们还发现了显著的州际差异，北印度州明显落后，南印度州显示出相反的性别差距。作为对这一点的解释，我们观察到证据表明，家庭和地区层面先前存在的性别规范与较高的性别差距之间存在着强有力的关联。根据关于这些差距后果的其他现有证据，调查结果要求更准确和定期地了解这些性别差异，以便为针对性别的干预措施提供信息。

\[10\] On the Fairness of Machine Assisted Human Decisions

标题 : 论机器辅助人工决策的公平性

作者 : Talia Gillis, Bryce McLaughlin, Jann Spiess , 类别 ：Computers and Society (cs.CY)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.15310

说明 : -

Abstract : When machine learning algorithms are deployed in high stakes decisions, we want to ensure that their deployment leads to fair and equitable outcomes. This concern has motivated a fast growing literature that focuses on diagnosing and addressing disparities in machine predictions. However, many machine predictions are deployed to assist in decisions where a human decision maker retains the ultimate decision authority. In this article, we therefore consider how properties of machine predictions affect the resulting human decisions. We show in a formal model that the inclusion of a biased human decision maker can revert common relationships between the structure of the algorithm and the qualities of resulting decisions. Specifically, we document that excluding information about protected groups from the prediction may fail to reduce, and may even increase, ultimate disparities. While our concrete results rely on specific assumptions about the data, algorithm, and decision maker, they show more broadly that any study of critical properties of complex decision systems, such as the fairness of machine assisted human decisions, should go beyond focusing on the underlying algorithmic predictions in isolation.

摘要 : 当机器学习算法被部署到高风险决策中时，我们希望确保它们的部署能够带来公平的结果。这一担忧推动了一份快速增长的文献，其重点是诊断和解决机器预测中的差异。然而，在人类决策者保留最终决策权的情况下，许多机器预测被用来辅助决策。因此，在本文中，我们考虑机器预测的性质如何影响所产生的人类决策。我们在一个正式的模型中表明，包含有偏见的人类决策者可以恢复算法结构和结果决策质量之间的共同关系。具体而言，我们记录了将受保护群体的信息排除在预测之外可能无法减少，甚至可能增加最终的差异。虽然我们的具体结果依赖于有关数据、算法和决策者的特定假设，但它们更广泛地表明，对复杂决策系统的关键属性（如机器辅助人类决策的公平性）的任何研究都应该超越孤立地关注基础算法预测。

\[11\] Fisheries Management in Congested Waters

标题 : 拥挤水域的渔业管理

作者 : Michael Macgregor Perry , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13966
