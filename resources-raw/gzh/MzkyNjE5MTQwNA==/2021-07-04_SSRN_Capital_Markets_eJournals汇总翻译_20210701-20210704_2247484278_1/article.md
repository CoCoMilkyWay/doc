\[1\] The Effects of Information Acquisition in M&As: Evidence from SEC EDGAR Web Traffic

标题:并购中的信息获取效应：来自SEC-EDGAR网络流量的证据

作者:Joanna (Xiaoyu) Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3870845

Abstract : This paper studies the effects of information acquisition in mergers and acquisitions (M&As). Information acquisition, proxied by downloads of filings on the SEC EDGAR website, improves the market’s assessment of deal synergies and the valuation of non-deal peer firms. Specifically, the information acquisition about merging firms enhances the relation between combined announcement-period abnormal returns and post-merger operating performance of the combined firm. The effects are stronger in deals that experience high institutional downloads and high trading volume. Furthermore, information acquisition in peer firms around M&A announcements strengthens the link between their short-term abnormal stock returns and long-term operating performance. Non-deal firms with greater download activity experience an increase in price informativeness and subsequent takeover probability. Overall, this paper provides supportive evidence that information acquisition improves the market’s assessment of the merger synergies and valuation on merger-related firms.

Keywords : information acquisition, stock market reaction, market efficiency, mergers and acquisitions

Abstract :本文研究了信息获取在企业并购中的作用。通过在SEC-EDGAR网站上下载文件来获取信息，可以提高市场对交易协同效应的评估和对非交易同行公司的估值。具体而言，合并企业的信息获取增强了合并公告期异常收益与合并后企业经营绩效的关系。在机构下载量高、交易量大的交易中，这种影响更为强烈。此外，同行企业围绕并购公告获取的信息加强了其短期异常股票收益与长期经营业绩之间的联系。非交易公司与更大的下载活动的经验，增加价格信息和随后的收购概率。总的来说，本文提供了支持性的证据表明，信息获取提高了市场对并购协同效应的评估和对并购相关企业的估值。

Keywords :信息获取、股市反应、市场效率、并购

\[2\] The Effects of Investors' Information Acquisition On Sell-Side Analysts Forecast Bias

标题:投资者信息获取对卖方分析师预测偏差的影响

作者:José Astaiza-Gómez

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3870796

Abstract : In this research I empirically study the effects of information acquisition by investors or traders on analysts' forecast bias. Based on the theoretical literature on sell-side analysts, I argue that forecast bias is correlated to investors' information gathering, in two opposite directions. On the one hand, higher levels of reading activities about individual firms by investors induce analysts to issue more optimistic forecasts if the potential for trading is higher. On the other hand, higher levels of reading activities about individual firms by investors help them identify opportunistic behaviors and thus to discipline analysts. I find that investors' information acquisition is positively related to analysts' optimism when the potential for trading is larger, and negatively related to optimism when investors are more likely to identify inflated forecasts. Together, these results suggest that information acquisition is not only correlated to analysts' optimism but also that its effect does not work trivially and solely in one direction but it activates two different incentives in analysts' decisions.

Keywords : trading incentives, analyst's credibility, responsive investors, naive investors.

Abstract :本文实证研究了投资者或交易者获取信息对分析师预测偏差的影响。基于卖方分析师的理论文献，我认为预测偏差与投资者的信息收集有两个相反的方向。一方面，如果交易潜力更大，投资者对个别公司的阅读活动水平越高，分析师就会发布更乐观的预测。另一方面，投资者对个别公司进行更高水平的阅读活动有助于他们识别机会主义行为，从而约束分析师。我发现，当交易潜力较大时，投资者的信息获取与分析师的乐观情绪正相关，当投资者更可能发现夸大的预测时，投资者的信息获取与乐观情绪负相关。综上所述，这些结果表明，信息获取不仅与分析师的乐观情绪相关，而且信息获取的效果并不是微不足道的，而且只在一个方向上起作用，而是在分析师的决策中激活了两种不同的激励机制。

Keywords :交易激励，分析师的信誉，反应灵敏的投资者，天真的投资者。

We also investigate the impact of asymmetric tail dependence between the portfolio returns on the out-of-sample predictability of the returns. We fit the GPD as the two margins and a variety of copula functions in the extant literature in evaluating the risk measures. We compute the VaR and ES from the benchmark Gaussian copula model and the competing copula models and confirm that the benchmark model underestimate the levels of risk regardless of the measures of dependence. We use the out-of-sample predictability test to evaluate the performance of the competing copula models. We compare the out-of-sample predictability of the three copula-based competing models by calculating the out-of-sample log-likelihood. The Gaussian and the Student’s t copula models are the representative symmetric copulas, while the Clayton copula is selected as the representative asymmetric copula model. According to the test statistics, the out-of-sample predictability of the Clayton and the Student’s t copula models are superior to that of the Gaussian copula model. Abstract :本文提出了一种新的高维资产收益率分布模型，该模型利用了混合频率数据和copula函数。收益率之间的相关性被分解为线性和非线性分量，从而能够使用高频数据准确预测线性相关性，并设计了一类新的连接函数，用于捕捉由此产生的不相关低频残差之间的非线性相关性。使用复合似然法对新的一类copula进行估计，方便了涉及数百个变量的应用。样本内和样本外检验证实了所提出模型应用于标普100指数成份股日收益率的优越性。

Keywords :复合似然，预测，高频数据，非线性依赖
