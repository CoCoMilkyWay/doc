\[1\] Impact of Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Factors on Stock Returns of Emerging Markets

标题:环境、社会和治理（ESG）因素对新兴市场股票回报的影响

作者:Dinabandhu Bag,Satyajit Mohanty

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3866655

Abstract : Empirical evidence indicates that environmental social and governance (ESG) practices are associated with firm financial performance, but little is covered about investors’ attention towards stock performance. In this paper, we conduct a test of the relationship between ESG performance disclosures and the firm’s financial or capital market performance. We choose a stratified sample of large-cap firms across 8 sectors drawn from the S & P 100 during the period 2016 to 2021. The changes in ratings, namely, governance, environment, exhibit a small but significant impact on the stock’s performance during the periods. Our results show that few of the ESG disclosures are positively related and dominate over the usual firm-level determinants of ROA (Return on Assets). We find governance practice is more significant than climate disclosures. These outcomes are robustly demonstrated on use cases of top stocks with investor safety recommendations. The results could have useful implications for investors, fund managers and regulators.

Keywords : ESG, disclosures, ROA, carbon emissions

Abstract :经验证据表明，环境、社会和治理（ESG）实践与公司财务绩效相关，但很少涉及投资者对股票绩效的关注。在本文中，我们对ESG绩效披露与公司财务或资本市场绩效之间的关系进行了测试。我们从2016年至2021年期间的标准普尔100指数中选取了8个行业的大盘股公司分层样本。评级（即治理、环境）的变化在这段时间内对股票表现产生了微小但重大的影响。我们的结果表明，ESG披露很少与ROA（资产回报率）的通常公司层面决定因素正相关并占主导地位。我们发现治理实践比气候披露更重要。这些结果在具有投资者安全建议的顶级股票的使用案例中得到了有力的证明。研究结果可能会对投资者、基金经理和监管机构产生有益的影响。

Keywords :ESG、披露、ROA、碳排放

\[2\] Do Investors Learn to Be Optimistic from SARS Experiences? Stock Market Reactions during COVID-19 in OECD Countries

标题:投资者从非典的经历中学到乐观吗？经合组织国家在新冠肺炎期间的股市反应

作者:Hao-Chang Sung,Shirley J. Ho,Yun-Shiang Huang,Hung-Wei Chang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3898370

Abstract : Various explanations have been proposed for the departure of the stock market prices from the economic realities during the COVID-19 outbreak. This paper conjectures that the SARS experiences can also have contributions to the quick recovery in stock markets. We first use an asset-pricing model to show that the stock price is affected by short-term fundamentals and the expectations about stock market. Then, we use two terms to describe "fear of missing out effect" and "resemblance effect" from the SARS experiences. Our results confirm that SARS experiences have positive contributions to the quick recovery of stock markets during the first wave of COVID-19. Both the FOMO effect and resemblance effect are positively related to stock market recovery. These positive contributions are reinforced if a country has actual experiences in treating SARS patients. We also find that the resemblance effects on stock market recovery are higher than the FOMO effects.

Keywords : COVID-19; asset pricing; expectations; SARS experiences, fear of missing out

Abstract :对于新冠肺炎爆发期间股票市场价格偏离经济现实，人们提出了各种解释。本文推测，SARS的经历也有助于股市的快速复苏。我们首先使用一个资产定价模型来说明股票价格受短期基本面和股市预期的影响。然后，我们使用两个术语来描述SARS经验中的“害怕遗漏效应”和“相似效应”。我们的研究结果证实，SARS的经验对第一波新冠肺炎期间股市的快速复苏有积极贡献。FOMO效应和相似效应都与股市复苏呈正相关。如果一个国家有治疗SARS患者的实际经验，这些积极贡献就会得到加强。我们还发现，相似效应对股市复苏的影响要高于FOMO效应。

Keywords :2019冠状病毒疾病资产定价；期望；SARS经验，害怕错过

\[3\] Ambiguity in a Pandemic Recession, Asset Prices, and Lockdown Policy

标题:大规模衰退、资产价格和封锁政策中的模糊性

作者:Keiichi Morimoto,Shiba Suzuki

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3896828

Abstract : Using an asset pricing model of a multi-sector production economy with pandemic disasters, we explain the average stock price boom and significant cross-sectional variation of stock returns in the US and Japan during the COVID-19 pandemic recession. Two features of the pandemic, ambiguity and sector-specific shocks, are crucial determinants of the unusual asset price dynamics. Extending the model, we analyze the welfare effects of lockdown policies during pandemics for heterogeneous households and obtain the following results. Enforcing a lockdown improves the welfare of the asset-holders and the household working in the sector with positive sector-specific shocks. A Pareto optimal lockdown policy controls the tightness of the lockdown so as to maximize the welfare of the household working in the sector with negative sector- specific shocks.

Keywords : Ambiguity, Asset prices, COVID-19, Heterogeneous agents, Welfare

Abstract :我们使用一个具有大流行灾难的多部门生产经济的资产定价模型，解释了美国和日本在新冠病毒-19大流行衰退期间的平均股价繁荣和股票回报的显著横截面变化。这种流行病的两个特征，模糊性和特定行业的冲击，是不寻常资产价格动态的关键决定因素。扩展该模型，我们分析了疫情期间封锁政策对异质家庭的福利效应，并得到以下结果。实施锁定可以改善资产持有人和在特定行业具有积极冲击的行业中工作的家庭的福利。帕累托最优锁定政策控制锁定的紧密性，以最大限度地提高在行业特定负面冲击下工作的家庭的福利。

Keywords :模糊性、资产价格、新冠病毒-19、异质代理人、福利
