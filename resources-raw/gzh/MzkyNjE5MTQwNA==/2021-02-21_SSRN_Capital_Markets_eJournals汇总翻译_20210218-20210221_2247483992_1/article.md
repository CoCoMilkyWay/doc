\[1\] A Short History of Value Investing and its Implications

标题:价值投资简史及其启示

作者:Bradford Cornell

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3789325

Abstract : This paper argues that what came to be called value investing was an historical accident. It arose in large part because the influential work of Graham and Dodd preceded the development of electronic spreadsheets leading them to propose short-cut estimates of value based on accounting ratios. The demise of the value premium in the last twelve years has led to doubts regarding the efficacy of this approach to value investing and an efforts to adjust the accounting ratios to make them more robust. The argument here is that this effort is misguided. Instead, it must be recognized that value investing amounts to comparing estimates of fundamental value with price and that accounting ratios, however tweaked, are not a reasonable way to estimate value – it requires a full blown DCF analysis. The paper then goes on to address some of the implications of that assertion.

Keywords : value investing, DCF

Abstract :本文认为，所谓的价值投资是一个历史的偶然。它的出现在很大程度上是因为格雷厄姆和多德的影响力工作先于电子表格的发展，导致他们提出了基于会计比率的简短价值估计。过去12年中，价值溢价的消亡，导致人们对这种价值投资方法的有效性产生怀疑，并努力调整会计比率，使其更加稳健。这里的论点是这种努力被误导了。相反，必须认识到，价值投资相当于将基本价值的估计值与价格进行比较，而会计比率无论如何调整，都不是估计价值的合理方法——它需要全面的现金流量表分析。论文接着讨论了这一论断的一些含义。

Keywords :价值投资

\[2\] Risk Sharing Within and Outside the Firm: The Disparate Effects of Wrongful Discharge Laws on Expected Stock Returns

标题:公司内外的风险分担：不当清偿法对预期股票收益的不同影响

作者:Robert Mahlstedt,Rüdiger Weber

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3746874

Abstract : We study the effect of wrongful-discharge laws (WDL) on firm-level stock returns. We find disparate effects depending on the exact design of the law. Consistent with rational, risk-based pricing, the effect on returns seems to be linked to how firms share systematic risk with their employees under the respective laws. Firms in states with WDLs prohibiting employers from acting in bad faith have more intra-firm risk sharing and lower expected returns. Vaguer legislation that prohibits discharges in retaliation for acting in accordance with public policy is associated with less intra-firm risk sharing and higher expected returns.

Keywords : labor protection, expected stock returns, risk sharing

Abstract :我们研究了不当清偿法（WDL）对公司层面股票收益率的影响。我们发现不同的效果取决于法律的精确设计。与理性的、基于风险的定价相一致，对收益的影响似乎与企业如何根据各自的法律与员工分担系统性风险有关。在WDL禁止雇主恶意行事的州，公司内部风险分担更多，预期回报更低。禁止因按照公共政策行事而进行报复性解雇的立法较为模糊，与企业内部风险分担较少和预期回报较高有关。

Keywords :劳动保护、股票预期收益、风险分担

\[3\] ESG Incidents and Shareholder Value

标题:ESG事件与股东价值

作者:Simon Glossner

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3004689

Abstract : This paper uses novel environmental, social, and governance (ESG) incident news data to study poor ESG practices. I find that firms’ past ESG incident rates predict more incidents, weaker profits, and lower risk-adjusted stock returns. When examining the cause of these abnormal returns, I find analyst forecast errors as well as lower returns around earnings announcements and subsequent incidents. Moreover, incident rates predict stronger abnormal returns in firms with higher short-term ownership, higher valuation uncertainty, and lower investor attention. Overall, these findings suggest that poor ESG practices negatively impact long-term value, which is not fully reflected in stock prices.

Keywords : ESG incidents, Corporate sustainability, Corporate social responsibility, Socially responsible investment, Managerial myopia, Limited investor attention

Abstract :本文使用新的环境、社会和治理（ESG）事件新闻数据来研究不良ESG实践。我发现，公司过去的ESG事件率预测更多的事件，较弱的利润和较低的风险调整后的股票回报。在研究这些异常回报的原因时，我发现分析师的预测错误，以及围绕盈利公告和后续事件的较低回报。此外，事件率预测，短期持股比例越高、估值不确定性越高、投资者关注度越低的公司，异常收益率越高。总的来说，这些发现表明不良的ESG实践对长期价值产生了负面影响，而长期价值并没有完全反映在股票价格中。

Keywords :ESG事件、企业可持续性、企业社会责任、社会责任投资、管理近视、投资者关注有限

\[4\] Abnormal Returns and Dispersion in Cybersecurity Exposure

标题:网络安全风险敞口中的异常收益与分散

作者:Tim Liu,Christos Makridis

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3746589

Abstract : This paper examines the dispersion in cybersecurity risk across firms. Using new, proprietary data on the Fortune 500 firms, We show that higher productivity firms exhibit abnormal returns. We subsequently document three new facts: (a) higher productivity firms have fewer cybersecurity vulnerabilities, (b) vulnerabilities are highly persistent within-firm, and (c) vulnerabilities are associated with data breaches. Our results suggest that higher productivity firms gain access to more technical human capital resources that are capable of mitigating cybersecurity vulnerabilities.

Keywords : Abnormal Returns, Asset Pricing, Cybersecurity, Data Breach, Stock Market

Abstract :本文研究了企业间网络安全风险的分散性。利用财富500强企业的最新专有数据，我们发现生产率较高的企业表现出异常回报。我们随后记录了三个新的事实：（a）生产率较高的公司有较少的网络安全漏洞，（b）漏洞在公司内部高度持久性，以及（c）漏洞与数据泄露有关。我们的研究结果表明，生产率较高的企业可以获得更多的技术人力资本资源，这些资源能够缓解网络安全漏洞。

Keywords :异常回报，资产定价，网络安全，数据泄露，股市

\[5\] Order Book Price Impact in the Chinese Soybean Futures Market

标题:中国大豆期货市场订单价格影响分析

作者:Muzhao Jin,Fearghal Kearney,Youwei Li,Yung Chiang Yang

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3747306

Abstract : We study the price impact of order flow in the world’s largest soybean meal futures markets. Our intraday results indicate that incoming orders can be used to explain price changes and to significantly predict future price changes. Our results are shown to be robust to various order flow measures, price aggregation approaches and data frequencies. We compare various order flow measures; finding that Order Flow Imbalance (OFI) is a more all-encompassing measure carrying greater information about price change relative to both Trade Imbalance (TI) and volume. Moreover, while both OFI and TI are shown to predict future price changes, this predictability diminishes over longer measure and price change frequency horizons.

Keywords : Order flow imbalance, Limit order book, Price impact, Futures markets

Abstract :我们研究了全球最大的豆粕期货市场的订单流对价格的影响。我们的盘中结果表明，来料订单可以用来解释价格变化，并显着预测未来的价格变化。我们的结果显示对各种订单流度量、价格聚合方法和数据频率具有鲁棒性。我们比较了不同的订单流测度，发现订单流不平衡（OFI）是一个更全面的测度，它包含了更多关于价格变化的信息，这些信息与贸易不平衡（TI）和贸易量有关。此外，虽然OFI和TI都能预测未来的价格变化，但这种可预测性在较长的测量和价格变化频率范围内会减弱。

Keywords :指令流不平衡、限制指令簿、价格冲击、期货市场

\[6\] The Role of Peer Events in Corporate Governance: Evidence From Data Breaches

标题:同行事件在公司治理中的作用：来自数据泄露的证据

作者:Musaib Ashraf

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3751622

Abstract : Economic theory suggests that negative peer events can result in market-wide spillovers which help unaffected firms take real actions to enhance corporate governance. Motivated by the SEC’s concern about cybersecurity, I study the role of peer events in corporate governance using the setting of data breaches. While controlling for firm-specific time-varying unobservable characteristics, I find that peer data breaches are associated with a reduction in future internal control material weaknesses for non-breached firms. The association is robust to a changes analysis and varies cross-sectionally with breach, firm, and board characteristics. Inferences remain consistent when studying IT-related material weaknesses only. Finally, non-breached firms are more likely to have a cybersecurity expert on the top management team after a peer breach. My findings have important implications for mandatory disclosure regulation in general and, in particular, suggest that regulators can help reduce market-wide exposure to cyber risk by facilitating disclosure of cyber incidents.

Keywords : peer events, corporate governance, internal controls, information technology, cybersecurity, information spillover, data breaches

Abstract :经济学理论认为，消极的同侪事件可以导致整个市场的溢出效应，帮助未受影响的企业采取实际行动，以加强公司治理。出于SEC对网络安全的关注，我通过设置数据泄露来研究同行事件在公司治理中的作用。在控制公司特定的时变不可观察特征的同时，我发现同行数据泄露与未泄露公司未来内部控制重大弱点的减少有关。该协会对变化分析是稳健的，并且随着违约、公司和董事会特征的不同而横向变化。仅在研究与信息技术相关的材料弱点时，推论保持一致。最后，未违约的公司更有可能在同行违约后，在高层管理团队中有一位网络安全专家。我的研究结果对一般的强制性披露监管具有重要意义，特别是表明监管机构可以通过促进网络事件的披露，帮助降低整个市场对网络风险的暴露。

Keywords :同行事件、公司治理、内部控制、信息技术、网络安全、信息溢出、数据泄露

\[7\] Board Governance and Investment Sensitivity to Stock Price: International Evidence

标题:董事会治理与股价投资敏感性：国际证据

作者:Hamdi Driss

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3753238

Abstract : This paper examines the effect of board governance on investment efficiency. I use the staggered enactment of board reforms in 41 countries as a shock to board structure that exogenously improves the quality of board oversight of managers. I find that investment–Q sensitivity improves by almost half post-reform. This effect is more pronounced for firms that are more exposed to the reforms or when external governance mechanisms are less likely to discipline managers. These findings suggest that increased board oversight strengthens managers’ incentives to make investment decisions that are more in line with their firms’ growth opportunities.

Keywords : Board governance, Board reforms, Investment efficiency, Investment sensitivity to price

Abstract :本文考察了董事会治理对投资效率的影响。我把41个国家交错实施的董事会改革作为对董事会结构的冲击，这种结构从外部提高了董事会对管理人员的监督质量。我发现，改革后，投资Q敏感性提高了近一半。对于那些更容易受到改革影响或外部治理机制不太可能约束管理者的公司来说，这种影响更为明显。这些研究结果表明，董事会监督的加强增强了经理人做出更符合公司增长机会的投资决策的动机。

Keywords :董事会治理、董事会改革、投资效率、投资价格敏感性

\[8\] Dealer Networks and the Cost of Immediacy

标题:经销商网络和即时成本

作者:Jens Dick-Nielsen,Thomas K. Poulsen,Obaidur Rehman

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3752881

Abstract : We show that uninformed corporate bond index trackers pay lower transaction costs when they request immediacy from more central dealers in the network. This centrality discount supports recent network models in which core dealers have a comparative advantage in carrying inventory. Core dealers provide more immediacy and revert deviations from their desired inventory faster. When dealers trade with other dealers, we find a centrality premium consistent with core dealers exploiting their comparative advantage to extract more surplus when bargaining with peripheral dealers. Using trades around index exclusions rule out alternative explanations based on adverse selection, customer bargaining power, and customer clienteles.

Keywords : Dealer network, dealer inventory, search frictions, transaction costs

Abstract :我们发现，当不知情的公司债券指数跟踪者向网络中更多的中央交易商请求即时性时，他们支付的交易成本较低。这种中心性折扣支持最近的网络模型，在这种模型中，核心经销商在进行库存方面具有比较优势。核心经销商提供更直接的和恢复偏离他们的期望库存更快。当经销商与其他经销商进行交易时，我们发现一个中心性溢价与核心经销商在与外围经销商讨价还价时利用他们的比较优势来获取更多的盈余是一致的。使用指数排除交易排除了基于逆向选择、客户议价能力和客户群的替代解释。

Keywords :经销商网络，经销商库存，搜索摩擦，交易成本

\[9\] Informational Friction, Economic Uncertainty and CDS-Bond Basis

标题:信息摩擦、经济不确定性与CDS债券基础

作者:Charlie X. Cai,Xiaoxia Ye,Ran Zhao

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3746637

Abstract : We study how macroeconomic uncertainty (EU) manifests into the cross-sectional variations of the credit default swap (CDS)-bond bases. We develop a structural model in which common EU induces informational friction affecting the pricing in the bond and CDS markets. Higher EU will lead to a larger cross-sectional divergence in the bases. Furthermore, the difference between the two markets' exposure to EU measured by the EU betas can predict cross-sectional variations in the bases, which is confirmed in our empirical study. We also study the practical implication of EU as a new basis determinant in the context of the basis arbitrage.

Keywords : uncertainty, informational friction, CDS, CDS-bond basis, uncertainty beta

Abstract :我们研究宏观经济不确定性（EU）如何体现为信用违约互换（CDS）债券基础的横截面变化。我们发展了一个结构模型，在这个模型中，共同的欧盟诱导了影响债券和CDS市场定价的信息摩擦。较高的欧盟将导致更大的横截面分歧的基础。此外，用EU-beta度量的两个市场对EU的敞口之间的差异可以预测基数的横截面变化，这在我们的实证研究中得到了证实。我们还研究了在基差套利的背景下，欧盟作为一个新的基差决定因素的实际意义。

Keywords :不确定性，信息摩擦，CDS，CDS键基，不确定性β

\[10\] Forecasting Earnings Using k-Nearest Neighbor Matching

标题:基于k近邻匹配的收益预测

作者:Peter D. Easton,Martin M. Kapons,Steven J. Monahan,Harm H. Schütt,Eric H. Weisbrod

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3752238

Abstract : We use the k-nearest neighbors (i.e., k-NN) algorithm to forecast a firm’s annual earnings by matching its recent trend in annual earnings to historical earnings sequences of “neighbor” firms. Our forecasts are more accurate than forecasts obtained from the random walk, the regression model developed by Hou, van Dijk and Zhang (2012), other regression models and the matching approach described in Blouin, Core and Guay (2010). The k-NN model is superior to these alternative models both when analysts’ forecasts are available and when they are not. Further, for firm-years with I/B/E/S earnings data available, the accuracy of k-NN forecasts of I/B/E/S earnings is similar to the accuracy of analysts’ forecasts. The k-NN model is also superior to a random forest classifier that we use to choose the best model ex-ante. Finally, we find that our forecasts of earnings changes have a positive association with future stock returns.

Keywords : earnings, forecasting, machine learning

Abstract :我们使用k-近邻（即k-NN）算法来预测一家公司的年收益，方法是将其最近的年收益趋势与“邻居”公司的历史收益序列相匹配。我们的预测比随机游走、Hou、van Dijk和Zhang（2012）开发的回归模型、其他回归模型以及Blouin、Core和Guay（2010）中描述的匹配方法得出的预测更准确。无论分析师的预测是否可用，k-NN模型都优于这些替代模型。此外，对于具有I/B/E/S收益数据的公司年份，k-NN对I/B/E/S收益的预测精度与分析师的预测精度相似。k-NN模型也优于随机森林分类器，我们用它来事先选择最佳模型。最后，我们发现我们对收益变动的预测与未来股票收益率正相关。

Keywords :收益、预测、机器学习

\[11\] Stock Return Asymmetry in China

标题:我国股票收益不对称问题研究

作者:Ke Wu,Yifeng Zhu,Dongxu Chen

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3724761

Abstract : In this paper, we find that the upside asymmetry calculated based on a new distribution-based asymmetry measure proposed by Jiang, Wu, Zhou, and Zhu (2020) is negatively related to average future returns in the cross-section of Chinese stock returns. By contrast, when using a conventional skewness measure, the relationship between asymmetry and average returns is unclear. Furthermore, the asymmetry factor constructed from the new asymmetry measure cannot be explained by the three-factor (CH-3) and four-factor (CH-4) models proposed by Liu, Stambaugh, and Yuan (2019).
