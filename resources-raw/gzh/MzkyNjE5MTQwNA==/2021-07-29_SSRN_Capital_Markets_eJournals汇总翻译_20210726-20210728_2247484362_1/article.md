\[1\] Does Firm Cloud Data Growth Predict Stock Returns?

标题:企业云数据增长预测股票收益吗？

作者:Ran Chang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3893300

Abstract : We investigate whether cloud data growth can forecast future stock returns. Using a proprietary data set on firm-level cloud data records during January 2013 to December 2020 from the leading cloud computing platform in China, we find that cloud data growth contains value-relevant information for stock return prediction. A long-short portfolio by buying (selling) stocks with the high (low) monthly growth in cloud data on firms generates a 7.2% risk-adjusted return annually. The return predictability of cloud data growth holds after controlling for firm characteristics and stays persistent in up to two quarters without any reversal. Moreover, cloud data growth positively predicts assets growth, sales growth, ROA, and earnings surprises.

Keywords : Alternative data, Cloud computing, Data growth, Expected returns, China

Abstract :我们调查云数据增长是否能预测未来股票收益。利用中国领先云计算平台2013年1月至2020年12月企业级云数据记录的专有数据集，我们发现云数据增长包含股票收益预测的价值相关信息。通过买入（卖出）公司云数据月增长率高（低）的股票的多空组合每年产生7.2%的风险调整回报。在控制了公司特征之后，云数据增长的回报可预测性保持不变，并在最多两个季度内保持不变，不会出现任何逆转。此外，云数据增长积极预测资产增长、销售增长、ROA和收益惊喜。

Keywords :替代数据、云计算、数据增长、预期收益、中国

\[2\] E-commerce Livestream Flows Growth and Cross-sectional Stock Returns

标题:电子商务Livestream流量增长和横向股票收益

作者:Ran Chang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3893295

Abstract : We study the role of E-commerce livestream flows growth in the stock price discovery process. Using a proprietary data set on firm-level E-commerce livestream flows records during July 2016 to December 2020 from the leading E-commerce livestream platform in China, we find that E-commerce livestream contains value-relevant information for stock return prediction. A long-short portfolio by buying (selling) stocks with the high (low) weekly growth in E-commerce livestream flows on firms generates a 6.864% risk- adjusted return annually. The return predictability of E-commerce livestream flows growth holds after controlling for firm characteristics and stays persistent in up to one quarter without return reversal. Moreover, livestream flows growth positively predicts firm profits growth, sales growth, ROA, and earnings surprises.

Keywords : Alternative data, E-commerce livestream, Flows growth, Expected returns, China

Abstract :我们研究了电子商务livestream流量增长在股票价格发现过程中的作用。利用中国领先的电子商务livestream平台提供的2016年7月至2020年12月企业级电子商务livestream流量记录的专有数据集，我们发现电子商务livestream包含用于股票收益预测的价值相关信息。通过买入（卖出）周增长率高（低）的公司电子商务实时流股票的长短组合，每年产生6.864%的风险调整回报。电子商务livestream流量增长的收益可预测性在控制了企业特征后仍然成立，并在一个季度内保持不变，不会出现收益逆转。此外，livestream flows的增长对公司利润增长、销售增长、ROA和意外收益有正面预测。

Keywords :替代数据、电子商务livestream、流量增长、预期收益、中国

\[3\] They Chose to Not Tell You

作者:Bruce Knuteson

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3894013

Abstract : The world's stock markets display a strikingly suspicious, decades long pattern of overnight and intraday returns that nobody (other than us) has plausibly explained and that nobody (other than us) has clearly and persistently alerted you to. We use correspondence on this topic over the past five years to show that the silence of others on this issue does not arise from their having a good reason to believe this pattern is fine. \[4\] Portfolio Selection: A Target-Distribution Approach

标题:投资组合选择：一种目标分配方法

作者:Nathan Lassance,Frédéric D. Vrins

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3893870

Abstract : We introduce a general framework to the portfolio-selection problem in which investorsaim at targeting a distribution of returns, which can accommodate a wide range of preferences.The resulting optimal portfolio has a return density that is as close as possibleto the target-return density. We study the theoretical properties of this approach fortwo classes of target distribution that allow for different first four moments. Threeresults that stand out are, first, that the fit to higher moments is controlled by theentropy of standardized portfolio returns when targeting a Gaussian distribution. Second,when targeting a specific Dirac-delta distribution, no norm-constrained portfoliocan stochastically dominate the proposed optimal portfolio. Third, if the target-returnmean and variance are located on or above the efficient frontier, the optimal portfolio ismean-variance efficient when asset returns are Gaussian. For non-Gaussian returns, theoptimal portfolio may move away from the frontier to better fit the higher moments ofthe target distribution. The empirical analysis illustrates that the proposed frameworkhelps the investor obtain portfolio returns in line with her preferences.

Keywords : portfolio selection, higher moments, tail risk, Kullback-Leibler divergence

Abstract :我们引入了一个投资组合选择问题的一般框架，在这个框架中，投资者的目标是一个收益的分布，这个分布可以容纳广泛的偏好，得到的最优投资组合的收益密度尽可能接近目标收益密度。我们研究了两类允许不同前四阶矩的目标分布的这种方法的理论性质。三个突出的结果是，第一，当以高斯分布为目标时，对高阶矩的拟合是由标准化投资组合收益的熵控制的。第二，当针对特定的Dirac-delta分布时，无范数约束的投资组合可以随机支配所提出的最优投资组合。第三，如果目标收益均值和方差都位于有效边界之上，当资产收益为高斯分布时，最优投资组合是均值方差有效的。对于非高斯收益，最优投资组合可能会远离边界，以更好地拟合目标分布的高阶矩。实证分析表明，该框架有助于投资者获得符合自己偏好的投资组合收益。

Keywords :投资组合选择，高阶矩，尾部风险，Kullback-Leibler散度

\[5\] Liquidity Provision with Adverse Selection and Inventory Costs

标题:具有逆向选择和库存成本的流动性准备

作者:Martin Herdegen,Johannes Muhle-Karbe,Florian Stebegg

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3893519

Abstract : We study one-shot Nash competition between an arbitrary number of identical dealers that compete for the order flow of a client. The client trades either because of proprietary information, exposure to idiosyncratic risk, or a mix of both trading motives. When quoting their price schedules, the dealers do not know the client's type but only its distribution, and in turn choose their price quotes to mitigate between adverse selection and inventory costs. Under essentially minimal conditions, we show that a unique symmetric Nash equilibrium exists and can be characterized by the solution of a nonlinear ODE.

Keywords : liquidity provision, Nash competition, adverse selection, inventory costs

Abstract :我们研究了一个单一的纳什竞争之间的任意数量相同的经销商竞争的订单流的客户。客户的交易要么是因为专有信息、特殊风险敞口，要么是两种交易动机的混合。在报价时，经销商不知道客户的类型，只知道客户的分布情况，然后选择他们的报价，以减轻逆向选择和库存成本之间的矛盾。在本质极小条件下，我们证明了一个唯一的对称纳什均衡存在，并且可以用非线性常微分方程的解来刻画。

Keywords :流动性供给、纳什竞争、逆向选择、库存成本

\[6\] Corporate Announcements and Market Efficiency: A Case on Indian Capital Market

标题:公司公告与市场效率：以印度资本市场为例

作者:Nagendra Marisetty,Pardhasaradhi Madasu

出处:International Journal of Business and Management; Vol. 16, No. 8; 2021

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3893377

Abstract : Capital markets being the backbone of the economy, are expected to be functioning efficiently. Efficiently-priced financial markets are considered a catalyst for the economic growth of the nations (Malkiel, 2010). Efficient markets are the reflection of security valuations. In an informationally efficient market, no one can beat the market and make abnormal returns based on the information because the information is instantaneously observed in the stock prices. The current paper analyses the market efficiency of three of the most popular corporate events, i.e., announcement of cash dividends, bonus issues, and stock split in the Indian context. The sample is 2253 pure cash dividend announcements (627 large-caps, 552 mid-caps, and 1074 small-caps), 152 bonus issue announcements (49 large-caps, 33 mid-caps, and 70 small-caps), and 181 stock split announcements (35 large-caps, 34 mid-caps, and 112 small-caps) were used for this study. Event methodology market model used to calculate Average Abnormal Returns (AAR) and Cumulative Average Abnormal Returns (CAAR).The results of the study have few findings which are contradictory to the existing literature on market efficiency. The cash dividend announcements have shown evidence for market efficiency, and results are contrary to Gupta et al. (2012), but the results are similar to Mishra (2005). Bonus issue announcements also have shown evidence for a semi-strong form of efficiency, test results identical to Dhar and Chhaochharia (2008), Kumar and Mittal (2015). Stock split announcements have not shown market efficiency, and the effect is similar to the study of Lakshmi and Roy (2012) and contrary to Chavali and Zahid (2011). Our results also support the premise that the emerging countries depict evidence of market efficiency (Bechev, 2003). Finally, we conclude that market efficiency results differ based on corporate announcements and market capitalization

Keywords : Capital Markets, Corporate Announcements, Event Study Methodology, Market Efficiency, Semi-Strong Form of Efficiency, & S&P BSE 500 Index.

Abstract :资本市场作为经济的支柱，预计将有效运作。有效定价的金融市场被认为是各国经济增长的催化剂（Malkiel，2010）。有效市场是证券估值的反映。在一个信息有效的市场中，没有人能够在信息的基础上战胜市场并获得超额收益，因为信息是在股票价格中被即时观察到的。本文分析了三种最受欢迎的公司事件的市场效率，即在印度背景下宣布现金分红、红利发行和股票分割。本研究以2253份纯现金股利公告（大盘股627份，中盘股552份，小盘股1074份）、152份派息公告（大盘股49份，中盘股33份，小盘股70份）和181份股票分割公告（大盘股35份，中盘股34份，小盘股112份）为样本。事件法市场模型用于计算平均异常收益率（AAR）和累积平均异常收益率（CAAR），研究结果与现有的市场效率文献相矛盾。现金股利公告显示了市场效率的证据，结果与Gupta等人（2012）相反，但结果与Mishra（2005）相似。奖金发行公告也显示了半强式效率的证据，测试结果与Dhar和Chhaocharia（2008）、Kumar和Mittal（2015）相同。股票分割公告并未显示出市场效率，其效果类似于Lakshmi和Roy（2012）的研究，与Chavali和Zahid（2011）的研究相反。我们的结果也支持这样一个前提，即新兴国家描述了市场效率的证据（Bechev，2003）。最后，我们得出结论，市场效率的结果是不同的公司公告和市值

Keywords :资本市场，公司公告，事件研究方法，市场效率，半强式效率，标普BSE 500指数。

\[7\] Inverse Options in a Black-Scholes World

标题:Black-Scholes世界中的逆期权

作者:Carol Alexander,Arben Imeraj

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3893037

Abstract : Most trading in cryptocurrency options is on inverse products, so called because the contract size is denominated in US dollars and they are margined and settled in crypto, typically bitcoin or ether. Their popularity stems from allowing professional traders in bitcoin or ether options to avoid transferring fiat currency to and from the exchanges. We derive new analytic pricing and hedging formulae for inverse options under the assumption that the underlying follows a geometric Brownian motion. The boundary conditions and hedge ratios exhibit relatively complex but very important new features which warrant further analysis and explanation. We also illustrate some inconsistencies, exhibited in time series of Deribit bitcoin option implied volatilities, which indicate that traders may be applying direct option hedging and valuation methods erroneously. This could be because they are unaware of the correct, inverse option characteristics which are derived in this paper.

Keywords :比特币、Delta、Deribit、Ether、Gamma、隐含波动率、定价公式、Vega

\[8\] Arbitrage-Based Recovery

标题:套利回收

作者:Ferenc Horvath

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3880921

Abstract : We develop a novel recovery theorem based on no-arbitrage principles. Our Arbitrage-Based Recovery Theorem does not require assuming time homogeneity of either the physical probabilities, the Arrow-Debreu prices, or the stochastic discount factor; and it requires the observation of Arrow-Debreu prices only for one single maturity. We perform several different density tests and mean prediction tests using 25 years of S&P 500 options data, and we find evidence that our method can correctly recover the probability distribution of the S&P 500 index level on a monthly horizon.

Keywords : recovery theorem, physical probabilities, transition state prices, stochastic discount factor, no-arbitrage

Abstract :基于无套利原理，我们提出了一个新的恢复定理。我们基于套利的恢复定理不需要假设物理概率、价格或随机贴现因子的时间齐性；而且它只需要观察一个单一到期日的价格。我们使用25年的标准普尔500指数期权数据进行了几种不同的密度检验和均值预测检验，发现我们的方法能够正确地恢复标准普尔500指数水平在月度水平上的概率分布。

Keywords :恢复定理，物理概率，过渡态价格，随机贴现因子，无套利

\[9\] The Evolution from Life Insurance to Financial Engineering

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3882190

Abstract : Since the mid-1980s, the share of household net worth intermediated by US financial institutions has shifted from defined benefit plans to life insurers and defined contribution plans. Life insurers have primarily grown through variable annuities, which are mutual funds with longevity insurance, a potential tax advantage, and minimum return guarantees. The minimum return guarantees change the primary function of life insurers from traditional insurance to financial engineering. Keywords :COVID-19危机、全球金融危机、寿险业、最低回报保证、可变年金

\[10\] Efficient Estimation of Bid-Ask Spreads from Open, High, Low, and Close Prices

标题:从开盘价、高价、低价和收盘价有效估计买卖价差

作者:David Ardia,Emanuele Guidotti,Tim Alexander Kroencke

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3892335

Abstract : We propose a novel estimation procedure of bid-ask spreads from open, high, low, and close prices. Our estimator is asymptotically unbiased and optimally combines the full set of price data to minimize the estimation variance. When quote data are not available, our estimator generally delivers the most accurate estimates of effective bid-ask spreads numerically and empirically. The estimator is derived under permissive assumptions that allow for stylized facts typically observed in real market data, is easy to implement, and can be applied to liquid and illiquid market segments, both in low and high frequency.

Keywords : market liquidity, bid-ask spread, transaction cost, asset pricing

Abstract :我们提出了一个新的估计过程的买卖价差从开放，高，低，收盘价。我们的估计器是渐近无偏的，并且最佳地组合了全套价格数据以最小化估计方差。当报价数据不可用时，我们的估计员通常会在数值和经验上提供最准确的有效买卖价差估计。估计量是在允许的假设下得出的，允许在实际市场数据中观察到典型的程式化事实，易于实现，并且可以应用于流动性和非流动性的细分市场，无论是低频率还是高频率。

Keywords :市场流动性、买卖价差、交易成本、资产定价
