\[1\] Optimal Payoff under the Generalized Dual Theory of Choice

标题:广义对偶选择理论下的最优收益

作者:Xue Dong He(The Chinese University of Hong Kong - Department of Systems Engineering and Engineering Management),Zhao Li Jiang(The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) - Department of Systems Engineering and Engineering Management)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722538

Abstract : We consider portfolio optimization under a preference model in a single-period, complete market. This preference model includes Yaari's dual theory of choice and quantile maximization as special cases. We characterize when the optimal solution exists and derive the optimal solution in closed form when it exists. The payoff of the optimal portfolio is a digital option: it yields an in-the-money payoff when the market is good and zero payoff otherwise. When the initial wealth increases, the set of good market scenarios remains unchanged while the payoff in these scenarios increases. Finally, we extend our portfolio optimization problem by imposing a dependence structure with a given benchmark payoff and derive similar results.

Keywords : Portfolio Selection, Quantile Approach, Quantile Maximization, Dual Theory of Choice

Abstract :我们考虑了在一个单期完全市场的偏好模型下的投资组合优化问题。该偏好模型将Yaari的对偶选择理论和分位数最大化作为特例。我们刻画了最优解存在的条件，并得到了最优解存在时的封闭形式。最优投资组合的收益是一个数字期权：当市场良好时，它产生一个货币内收益，反之则为零收益。当初始财富增加时，一组良好的市场情景保持不变，而这些情景中的收益增加。最后，我们将我们的投资组合优化问题扩展到一个给定基准收益的依赖结构，并得到类似的结果。

Keywords :投资组合选择，分位数法，分位数最大化，对偶选择理论

\[2\] Game in Another Town: Geography of Investor Attention and Stock Valuation

标题:另一个城市的博弈：投资者关注的地理位置与股票估值

作者:Dong Dayong(Southwest Jiaotong University),Danling Jiang(College of Business, Stony Brook University),Keke Wu(Southwest Jiaotong University),ongqHuan Zhu(Southwest Jiaotong University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3733107

Abstract : Investors have preferences for and pay attention to local stocks, as well as stocks of companies in connected cities. Using online stock watchlist data in China, we identify the investor geographic makeup of a stock by exploiting the aggregate pattern in the geographic connections of watched stocks and examine its implications for stock valuations measured by the market to book equity. We find that cross-city attention increases stock valuations in the attended city and decreases those in the unattended. The aggregate stock demand to supply in attention-connected cities also increases local stocks’ valuation. The effects of attention-connected cities are stronger for small firms, extend to stock returns, and induce excess return comovement. The findings suggest that attention-revealed city connectivity is an important factor in asset pricing.

Keywords : Attention geography, stock watchlist, local bias, city connectivity, market valuation

Abstract :投资者对本地股票以及互联城市公司的股票有偏好和关注。利用中国的在线股票观察名单数据，我们通过利用观察股票的地理联系中的总模式来确定股票的投资者地理构成，并研究其对股票估值的影响（按市值与账面净值的比率衡量）。我们发现，在有人参与的城市，跨城市注意力会增加股票估值，而在无人参与的城市，则会降低股票估值。受关注城市的总股票供需比也提高了本地股票的估值。注意关联城市对小企业的影响更大，延伸到股票收益，并诱发超额收益协动。研究结果表明，城市连通性是影响资产定价的一个重要因素。

Keywords :注意地理位置、股票观察名单、地方偏见、城市连通性、市场估值

\[3\] Bond Covenants, Bankruptcy Risk, and the Cost of Debt

标题:债券契约、破产风险和债务成本

作者:Sattar Mansi(Virginia Polytechnic Institute & State University),Yaxuan Qi(City University of Hong Kong (CityUHK) - Department of Economics & Finance),John K. Wald(University of Texas at San Antonio)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3736019

Abstract : Are all covenants equally effective at reducing the bondholder-shareholder conflict? Examining the most frequently used bond covenants, we document that four out of 24 restrictions are associated with significantly higher bankruptcy risk. The use of these Default Indicating covenants can be partly explained by faulty contract design, greater recovery in bankruptcy, or within-creditor conflicts. Firms that use In-House Counsel to help structure their bond issue and those that use Big 4 Auditors are also less likely to include Default Indicating covenants in their bonds. Further tests show that the use of these Default Indicating covenants is associated with higher bond and CDS spreads. Overall, the results help explain the prior evidence on the relation between covenant use and the cost of debt.

Keywords : Survival analysis, Bond covenants, Bankruptcy risk, Cost of debt

Abstract :所有契约在减少债券持有人与股东冲突方面是否同样有效？通过检查最常用的债券契约，我们发现24项限制中有4项与显著更高的破产风险有关。这些违约指示契约的使用可以部分解释为合同设计错误、破产时更大的恢复或债权人内部的冲突。使用内部顾问帮助构建债券发行结构的公司和使用四大审计机构的公司也不太可能在债券中包含违约指示契约。进一步的测试表明，使用这些违约指示契约与更高的债券和CDS利差有关。总的来说，研究结果有助于解释契约使用与债务成本之间关系的先前证据。

Keywords :生存分析，债券契约，破产风险，债务成本

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722492

Abstract : This paper is the first to use loan level data to investigate the relationship between equity volatility and financial leverage on the firm level. We use a comprehensive dataset of large syndicated loans with a total loan amount in excess of 12 trillion USD. This allows us to precisely identify when a company experiences a large change in leverage. In contrast to several previous studies that have relied only on accounting data, we find very clear results that increased financial leverage increases equity volatility. \[5\] ESG-Screening and Factor-Risk-Adjusted Performance: The Concentration Level of Screening Does Matter

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722485

Abstract : Constructing ESG-screened portfolios aims to reduce the aggregate ESG-risk at the portfolio level by excluding low ESG-score constituents from the selection universe. But ESG-screening imposes limits on potential diversification as well as alters risk exposures to systematic factors. To investigate ESG-screening’s impact on the factor-risk-adjusted performance of portfolios, we construct ESG-screened portfolios consisting of US equity mutual funds according to their returns-based ESG-scores. \[6\] Is ESG a Systematic Risk Factor for US Equity Mutual Funds?

标题:ESG是美国股票共同基金的系统性风险因素吗？

作者:Ick Jin(National Assembly Budget Office)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722477

Abstract : Although the environmental, social, and governance (ESG) has gained increasing attention among investors, the extent to which ESG is compensated systematically in the market remains to be investigated. On the outperformance of responsible investing (RI) which incorporates ESG into investment decisions, the empirical evidence to date is inconsistent from the viewpoint of ex-post performance. This paper tries to explain the nature of return differential between RI and conventional investing within the well-known risk-return paradigm. From the viewpoint of ex-ante equity risk premium (ERP), the five factor model of Fama and French (2015) combined with a ESG-related factor applies to returns on 1,425 US open-end equity funds for the period from April 2009 to December 2016. Empirical findings include: i) US open-end equity funds tend to hedge the ESG-related systematic risk, ii) the exposure to ESG-related systematic risk is significantly priced in the market, and iii) equity funds have become more capable to factor CSP into their investment decisions.

Keywords : ESG, CSP, Responsible Investing, Downside Protection, Systematic Risk

Abstract :尽管环境、社会和治理（ESG）越来越受到投资者的关注，但ESG在市场中得到系统补偿的程度仍有待研究。关于将ESG纳入投资决策的责任投资（RI）的表现，从事后绩效的角度来看，迄今为止的经验证据是不一致的。本文试图在著名的风险-收益范式下解释投资回报率与传统投资回报率之差的性质。从事前股权风险溢价（ERP）的角度来看，Fama和French（2015）的五因素模型结合ESG相关因素适用于2009年4月至2016年12月期间1425只美国开放式股票基金的回报。实证结果包括：1）美国开放式股票基金倾向于对冲与ESG相关的系统性风险；2）与ESG相关的系统性风险敞口在市场中的定价显著；3）股票基金已变得更有能力将CSP因素纳入其投资决策。

Keywords :ESG、CSP、负责任投资、下行保护、系统风险

\[7\] Information Overload Research in Accounting – A Systematic Review of the Literature

标题:会计信息过载研究&文献综述

作者:Maren Hartmann(University of Duesseldorf)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722423

Abstract : This paper reviews literature in the domain of information overload in accounting. The underlying psychological concepts of information load (as applied in accounting research) are summarized, and a framework to discuss findings in a structured way is proposed. This framework serves to make causes, consequences, and countermeasures transparent. Variables are further clustered into major categories from information processing research: input, process, and output. The main variables investigated are the characteristics of the information set, especially the number of information cues as an input variable; the experience of the decision-maker, the decision time, decision rule, and cue usage as process variables; and measures related to decision quality (i.e., accuracy, consensus, consistency) and related to self-insight (calibration, confidence, feeling of overload) as output variables.The contexts of the respective research papers are described, and the operationalization of variables detailed and compared. I employ the method of stylized facts to evaluate the strength of the links between variables (number of links, direction and significance of relationship). The findings can be summarized as follows: most articles focus on individual decision-making in the domain of external accounting, with financial distress predictions constituting a large part of these. Most papers focus on input and output variables with the underlying information processing receiving less attention. The effects observed are dependent on the type of information input and the task employed. Decision accuracy is likely to decrease once information load passes a certain threshold, while decision time and a feeling of overload increase with increasing information load. While experience increases decision accuracy, the results on decision time are conflicting. Most articles have not established a significant link between changes in information load and changes in decision confidence. Relative cue usage, consensus, consistency, and calibration decline with increasing information load. Based on these findings, implications for practice and future research are derived.

Keywords : information overload, literature review, accounting, decision-making, information processing

Abstract :本文回顾了有关会计信息过载的文献。总结了信息负荷的基本心理学概念（在会计研究中的应用），并提出了一个以结构化方式讨论研究结果的框架。这个框架有助于使原因、后果和对策透明化。在信息处理研究中，变量被进一步分为三大类：输入、过程和输出。研究的主要变量是信息集的特征，特别是作为输入变量的信息线索的数量；作为过程变量的决策者的经验、决策时间、决策规则和线索使用；以及与决策质量（即准确性、一致性、一致性）和与决策相关的度量自我洞察力（校准、信心、过载感）作为输出变量分别对论文的研究背景进行了描述，并对变量的可操作性进行了详细的比较。我采用程式化事实的方法来评估变量之间联系的强度（联系的数量、方向和关系的重要性）。本文的研究结果可以概括如下：大多数文献都集中在外部会计领域的个人决策，其中财务困境预测是其中的一个重要组成部分。大多数的论文关注的是输入和输出变量，而对底层信息处理的关注较少。观察到的效果取决于信息输入的类型和所采用的任务。一旦信息负载超过某个阈值，决策准确率就会降低，而决策时间和过载感则会随着信息负载的增加而增加。虽然经验提高了决策的准确性，但决策时间的结果是相互矛盾的。大多数文章都没有在信息量的变化和决策信心的变化之间建立重要的联系。相对线索的使用，共识，一致性，和校准下降，随着信息负荷的增加。在此基础上，提出了对实践和未来研究的启示。

Keywords :信息过载，文献综述，会计，决策，信息处理

\[8\] The Effects of CSR Reputation and CSR Crisis Response Strategy on Investor Judgments

标题:企业社会责任声誉与企业社会责任危机应对策略对投资者判断的影响

作者:Clarence Goh(Singapore Management University - School of Accountancy)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3732628

Abstract : I use a controlled experiment to examine, in the context of CSR crises, whether investors’ investment judgments are influenced by a firm’s CSR reputation and CSR crisis response strategy. I find that for good CSR reputation firms, the use of a rebuild or deny crisis response strategy does not lead to improvements in investment judgments. \[9\] Examining the Phenomenon of Rounding in Analysts’ EPS Forecasts: Evidence from Singapore

标题:检验分析师每股收益预测的舍入现象：来自新加坡的证据

作者:Clarence Goh(Singapore Management University - School of Accountancy)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3732634

Abstract : Prior studies have documented the phenomenon of rounding of analysts’ EPS forecasts in the US. From the outset, it is unclear if analysts following Singapore firms also similarly engage in the rounding of their EPS forecasts. This study examined a sample of analyst EPS forecasts of firms listed on the Singapore exchange and found that there is substantial rounding of EPS forecasts, with 9.59% of EPS forecasts examined ending in five or ten-cent intervals. Results from Benford’s law’s last-two digits test are also consistent with significant rounding in analysts’ EPS forecasts. In supplementary analysis, the study further found that the level of rounding was comparable across two time periods under examination, from 2011 to 2015 and from 2016 to 2019. It also found that there was substantial rounding even for forecasts of relatively large magnitudes (i.e. $1.00 and above).

Keywords : earnings per share (EPS), analyst forecasts, Singapore

Abstract :此前的研究记录了美国分析师EPS预测四舍五入的现象。从一开始，就不清楚跟踪新加坡公司的分析师是否也同样参与了EPS预测的四舍五入。本研究对新加坡交易所上市公司的分析师每股收益预测样本进行了检验，发现每股收益预测存在实质性的四舍五入，9.59%的每股收益预测以五分或十分的间隔结束。Benford's law最后两位数检验的结果也与分析师EPS预测的显著四舍五入一致。在补充分析中，该研究进一步发现，2011年至2015年和2016年至2019年两个受检时间段的舍入水平具有可比性。它还发现，即使对于相对较大的震级（即1.00美元及以上）的预测，也存在大量舍入。

Keywords :每股收益（EPS），分析师预测，新加坡

\[10\] Analysts’ EPS Forecasts: The Effects of Forecast Uncertainty and Forecast Precision on Investors’ Judgments of Forecast Reliability

标题:分析师每股收益预测：预测不确定性和预测精度对投资者预测可靠性判断的影响

作者:Clarence Goh(Singapore Management University - School of Accountancy)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3732652

Abstract : In this study, I use an experiment to investigate the joint effects of forecast precision and forecast uncertainty on investors’ judgments of the reliability of analysts’ EPS forecasts.
