\[1\] The Geometry of Market Opinions: An Introduction to Doxotaxy

标题:市场意见的几何学：多索税简介

作者:Nicolas Maloumian

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3916089

Abstract : Opinions (δόξα, doxa) have always had a generally disfavored status in philosophy and science. However, in the financial markets, contradictory opinions generate an arrangement of opinions (doxotaxy) which can mathematically be defined by analytical functions and vector geometry. Information appears to be a variable of this existing structure, hence, whilst each traded price is the result of randomness, price moves are not. The objective of this study was to present the concepts of phyllelpia and doxotaxy which show how the relationship between price, value and information is subjected by a contextual and not so unstable structure of market opinions.

Keywords : Opinions, Geometry, Price, Value, Forecasting

Abstract :观点（Δόξα，doxa）在哲学和科学中一直处于普遍不受欢迎的地位。然而，在金融市场中，相互矛盾的观点会产生一种观点的排列方式（多索学说），这种排列方式可以通过解析函数和向量几何进行数学定义。信息似乎是现有结构的一个变量，因此，虽然每个交易价格都是随机性的结果，但价格变动并非如此。本研究的目的是介绍phyllelpia和doxotaxy的概念，这表明价格、价值和信息之间的关系如何受到上下文和不太稳定的市场意见结构的影响。

Keywords :观点、几何、价格、价值、预测

\[2\] Time-Varying Consumption Risk and Stock Returns

标题:时变消费风险与股票收益

作者:Victoria Atanasov

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3936861

Abstract : This paper studies the relation between cyclical variation in the systematic consumption risk and expected stock market returns. Rather than conditioning risk on instrumental variables linked to the real economy, we tie risk directly to economic cycles. We find that high sensitivity to the aggregate consumption growth during periods of macroeconomic recessions is rewarded with a positive and statistically significant market premium. A simple model that features distinct exposures to consumption risk in high and low economic activity states explains a large fraction of the cross-sectional variation in average returns on book-to-market, size, and momentum sorted portfolios and substantially outperforms the standard unconditional model. The results hold up across different samples, model specifications, and alternative business cycle classifications.

Keywords : time-varying consumption risk, conditional consumption beta, business cycle, cross-section of expected returns

Abstract :本文研究了系统消费风险的周期性变化与股市预期收益之间的关系。我们没有将风险局限于与实体经济相关的工具变量，而是将风险直接与经济周期联系起来。我们发现，在宏观经济衰退期间，对总消费增长的高度敏感性会得到正的、统计上显著的市场溢价。一个简单的模型，以高经济活动和低经济活动状态下消费风险的不同敞口为特征，解释了账面市盈率、规模和动量排序投资组合平均回报的大部分横截面变化，并显著优于标准无条件模型。结果适用于不同的样本、模型规范和备选商业周期分类。

Keywords :时变消费风险、条件消费贝塔、商业周期、预期收益横截面

\[3\] Strategic Complexity in Disclosure

标题:披露的战略复杂性

作者:Cyrus Aghamolla,Kevin Smith

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3936805

Abstract : Extensive evidence suggests that managers strategically choose the complexity of their descriptive disclosures. However, their motives in doing so appear mixed, as complex disclosures are used to obfuscate in some cases and as a means of informative communication in others. Building on these observations, we first identify a novel stylized fact: disclosure complexity is non-monotonic in firm performance. We then develop a model of disclosure complexity that incorporates the dual roles of complexity and can explain this stylized fact. In the model, a manager discloses to investors of heterogeneous sophistication and can adjust the complexity of the disclosure to either provide more precise information or to obfuscate. In equilibrium, the manager issues a complex disclosure upon observing both highly positive and negative news. The average market reaction to complex disclosures may exceed that to simple disclosures, which is at odds with the conventional wisdom that negative news is more often complexified. We further demonstrate a policy solution to mitigate the harmful effects of obfuscation on the information environment: mandate that firms accompany complex disclosures with simplified reports.

Keywords : Financial reporting complexity, disclosure, complexity, obfuscation, disclosure informativeness, sophisticated investors, MD&A

Abstract :大量证据表明，管理者战略性地选择其描述性披露的复杂性。然而，他们这样做的动机似乎是复杂的，因为复杂的披露在某些情况下被用来混淆，在另一些情况下被用作信息交流的手段。基于这些观察，我们首先确定了一个新的程式化事实：披露复杂性在公司绩效中是非单调的。然后，我们开发了一个披露复杂性模型，该模型结合了复杂性的双重作用，可以解释这一程式化事实。在该模型中，管理者向复杂程度不同的投资者披露信息，并可以调整披露的复杂性，以提供更精确的信息或混淆信息。在均衡状态下，管理者在观察到高度积极和消极的消息后发布复杂的披露。市场对复杂信息披露的平均反应可能超过对简单信息披露的反应，这与负面消息往往更加复杂的传统观点不一致。我们进一步展示了一种政策解决方案，以减轻信息环境中模糊处理的有害影响：要求企业以简化的报告伴随复杂的披露。

Keywords :财务报告复杂性、披露、复杂性、混淆、披露信息性、成熟投资者、MD&A

\[4\] Bitcoin Blackout: Proof-of-Work and the Centralization of Mining

标题:比特币管制：工作证明和采矿集中化

作者:Stefan Scharnowski,Yanghua Shi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3936787

Abstract : Electricity constitutes the main input factor for miners of proof-of-work cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, so they gravitate towards countries with cheap energy. We analyze risks associated with this geographical centralization of mining by exploiting an exogenous shock to electricity supply in a relatively small region with heavy Bitcoin mining activity. We first document a drop of about 25% in the total computing power of the Bitcoin network during a blackout that lasts several days. Compared to a control group consisting of a proof-of-stake cryptocurrency, we find evidence of blockchain congestion as fees increase substantially while the number and value of transactions decrease. We also document an impact on exchange trading activity. Trading volume and especially exchange rate volatility increase while liquidity deteriorates during the blackout, even though returns are mostly unaffected. Additionally, market integration drops as price differences between exchanges increase considerably.

Keywords : Bitcoin, Proof-of-work, Proof-of-stake, Blackout, Mining, Centralization

Abstract :电力构成了矿工工作证明加密货币（如比特币）的主要输入因素，因此他们倾向于能源廉价的国家。我们通过在一个相对较小的比特币采矿活动频繁的地区利用对电力供应的外部冲击来分析与这种地理集中采矿相关的风险。我们首先记录了比特币网络的总计算能力在持续数天的断电期间下降了约25%。与由股权证明加密货币组成的控制组相比，我们发现了区块链拥塞的证据，因为费用大幅增加，而交易数量和价值减少。我们还记录了对交易所交易活动的影响。交易量，尤其是汇率波动性增加，而流动性在大停电期间恶化，即使回报基本不受影响。此外，随着交易所之间的价差大幅增加，市场一体化程度下降。

Keywords :比特币、工作证明、股权证明、断电、采矿、集中

\[5\] Intraday Herding and Attention Around the Clock

标题:日间放牧和全天关注

作者:Stefan Scharnowski,Yanghua Shi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3936733

Abstract : This paper analyzes return herding at the intraday level in the decentralized cryptocurrency market with its continuous, around the clock trading and dominance of retail investors. We first document substantial herding behavior that is stronger when market returns are positive. Herding is negatively related to both the level and cross-sectional dispersion of investor attention. Moreover, there are pronounced intraday variations: Market return herding exhibits similar patterns as attention and blockchain activity and is strongest during the overlap of hours when traders in major economic centers are likely awake.

Keywords : Herding, Investor Attention, Attention Dispersion, Cryptocurrency, Bitcoin

Abstract :本文分析了分散式加密货币市场的日内收益羊群效应，该市场具有连续、24小时的交易和散户投资者的主导地位。我们首先记录了大量的羊群行为，当市场回报为正时，这种行为更为强烈。羊群效应与投资者注意力的水平和横截面分散度均呈负相关。此外，还有明显的日内变化：市场回报羊群效应表现出与注意力和区块链活动类似的模式，在主要经济中心的交易者可能醒着的时间重叠期间表现最为强烈。

\[7\] The Financial Regulation of Italian SPACs

标题:意大利SPAC的金融监管

作者:Filippo Annunziata,Maria Lucia Passador,Anna Chiara Chisari

出处:in “SPACs and the Italian Capital Market: Market Practices beyond the European Legal Framework of Financial Regulation”, edited by Daniele D’Alvia and Ettore Maria Lombardi, Routledge, 2021, forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3935927

Abstract : SPACs have enjoyed, at least for some time, a fair degree of success in the Italian market. As in other jurisdictions, the relationship between the SPAC structure and financial market legislation is rather intricate: some areas of the financial regulation are potentially applicable to SPACs, while others are not. In some instances, SPACs simply fall outside the scope of certain areas of the financial and capital markets legislation; in other instances, they benefit from specific exemptions from certain provisions; and finally, they may be entirely subject to certain types of regulation. In particular, it is worth considering the following areas: (i) the relationship between SPACs and the regulation of undertakings for collective investment, which arises, in Italy, from the implementation of the AIFM Directive; (ii) the relationship between SPACs and the prospectus regulation; (iii) the regulation of trading platforms and SPACs, with particular attention to the AIM experience; (iv) the regulation of corporate governance of listed companies and SPACs; (v) the peculiarities of SPACs vis-à-vis other similar regulated vehicles (including private equity funds, club-deals, family offices, etc.). For each of these topics, this chapter will discuss how, and to what extent, SPACs are ultimately subject to the related relevant provision, or are excluded from it, and how they have actually developed their "regulatory" strategies in approaching the Italian market.Subsequently, this study will perform a quantitative analysis of Italian SPACs from January 2010 to December 2020, drawing some considerations and comparing their key characteristics with those of US SPACs.

Keywords : SPACs, blank check companies, IPO market, private equity, business combination

Abstract :SPAC在意大利市场取得了相当程度的成功，至少在一段时间内是如此。与其他司法管辖区一样，SPAC结构与金融市场立法之间的关系相当复杂：金融监管的某些领域可能适用于SPAC，而其他领域则不适用。在某些情况下，SPAC完全不属于金融和资本市场立法的某些领域；在其他情况下，他们受益于特定条款的特定豁免；最后，它们可能完全受制于某些类型的监管。特别是，值得考虑以下方面：（i）SPAC与集体投资企业监管之间的关系，这在意大利是由于实施AIFM指令而产生的；（ii）SPAC与招股章程条例之间的关系；（iii）监管交易平台和SPAC，特别注意AIM的经验；（iv）监管上市公司和SPAC的公司治理；（v） SPAC相对于其他类似监管机构（包括私募股权基金、俱乐部交易、家族理财办公室等）的特点。对于每一个主题，本章将讨论SPAC如何以及在多大程度上最终受相关条款的约束，或被排除在相关条款之外，以及他们在接近意大利市场时如何实际制定其“监管”战略。随后，本研究将对2010年1月至2020年12月期间的意大利SPAC进行定量分析，得出一些考虑因素，并将其关键特征与美国SPAC进行比较。

Keywords :SPAC、空白支票公司、IPO市场、私募股权、企业合并

\[8\] Limiting Investment Opportunity Sets, Asset Pricing, and the Roll Critique

标题:限制投资机会集、资产定价和滚动评论

作者:Bob Korkie,Harry J. Turtle

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3935363

Abstract : ABSTRACTThe theoretical construct of a limiting IOS is well-approximated by a finite proxy IOS with an upper bound on market Sharpe ratios, estimated at 0.26. The limiting Sharpe ratio is the only parameter required in the limiting IOS, there are multiple efficient risky portfolios, and all efficient portfolios generate asset pricing models with equivalent expected returns. Thus, the classic CAPM with a unique market portfolio is not descriptive of asset pricing. We find no evidence of an efficient portfolio with purely positive weights—hence, proxy market portfolios are inefficient. Keywords : Limiting Investment Opportunity Sets, Asset Pricing, Roll’s Critique, Upper Bounded Sharpe Ratios

Abstract :极限IOS的理论结构很好地近似于有限代理IOS，市场夏普比率上限估计为0.26。限制夏普比率是限制性投资组合中唯一需要的参数，存在多个有效风险投资组合，所有有效投资组合都生成具有同等预期收益的资产定价模型。因此，具有独特市场组合的经典CAPM不能描述资产定价。我们发现没有证据表明存在纯正权重的有效投资组合，因此，代理市场投资组合是低效的。利用默顿跨期CAPM，可能会产生低效的市场投资组合。

Keywords :限制投资机会集、资产定价、罗尔评论、上限夏普比率

\[9\] Dynamic ESG Equilibrium

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3935174

Abstract : This paper develops and applies an equilibrium model that accounts for ESG demand and supply dynamics. In equilibrium, ESG preference shocks represent a novel risk source characterized by diminishing marginal utility and positive premium. Expected green asset returns are negatively associated with time-varying convenience yield, while exposures to ESG preference shocks lead to positive green premium. \[10\] Identification of Factor Risk Premia

标题:因子风险溢价的识别

作者:Peter Hansen,Maziar Kazemi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3934624

Abstract : This paper a develops novel statistical test of whether individual factor risk premia are identified from return data in multi-factor models. We give a necessary and sufficient condition for population identification of individual risk premia, which we call the kernel-orthogonality condition. This condition is weaker than the standard rank condition commonly assumed for linear factor models. Under misspecification, our condition ensures point identification of the risk premium with minimal pricing error. We show how to test this restriction directly in reduced-rank models. Finally, we apply our test methodology to assess identification of risk premia associated with consumption growth and intermediary leverage.
