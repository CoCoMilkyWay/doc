\[1\] Dissecting bankruptcy frictions

标题:剖析破产摩擦

作者:Winston Wei Dou,Lucian A. Taylor,Wei Wang,Wenyu Wang

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002841

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.06.014

Abstract : How efficient is corporate bankruptcy in the United States? Two frictions, asymmetric information and conflicts of interest among creditors, can cause several inefficiencies: excess liquidation, excess continuation, and excess delay. We find large bankruptcy inefficiencies, mainly due to excess delay. Eliminating information asymmetries would increase average total payouts by 4%, and eliminating conflicts of interest would increase them by 18% more. Without these frictions, 14% more cases would be resolved pre-court, and court cases would be 73% shorter. With less delay, bankruptcy’s indirect costs would be much lower. In contrast, inefficiencies from excess liquidation and excess continuation are quite small.

Keywords : Bankruptcy;Structural estimation;Conflicts of interest;Asymmetric information

Abstract :美国企业破产的效率如何？两种摩擦，信息不对称和债权人之间的利益冲突，可能会导致一些低效率：过度清算、过度延续和过度延迟。我们发现大量破产无效，主要是由于过度延迟。消除信息不对称将使平均总支出增加4%，消除利益冲突将使其增加18%。如果没有这些摩擦，法庭前解决的案件将增加14%，法庭案件将缩短73%。如果拖延时间减少，破产的间接成本将大大降低。相比之下，过度清算和过度延续的低效率相当小。

Keywords :破产结构估计；利益冲突；信息不对称

\[2\] Failing to forecast rare events

标题:未能预测罕见事件

作者:Philip Bond,James Dow

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002981

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.06.028

Abstract : Do more talented traders prefer to bet on and against rare events or common events? Bets on rare events include out of the money options. Bets against rare events include the carry trade and investment grade bonds. In a model where traders specialize, equilibrium pricing reflects trading ability: A market with more skilled traders has a larger bid ask spread. We show that lower skill traders bet on and against rare events, while higher skill traders bet on and against frequent events, leading to higher bid-ask spreads in common event assets, and reducing financial markets’ ability to predict rare events.

Keywords : Bid ask spread;Information production;Rare event;Black swan

Abstract :更有才华的交易者是否更喜欢押注于罕见事件或普通事件？对罕见事件的押注包括赔本期权。针对罕见事件的押注包括套利交易和投资级债券。在交易者专业化的模型中，均衡定价反映了交易能力：一个拥有更多熟练交易者的市场有更大的买卖价差。我们发现，技能较低的交易者押注于罕见事件和反对罕见事件，而技能较高的交易者押注于频繁事件和反对频繁事件，导致常见事件资产的买卖价差较高，并降低金融市场预测罕见事件的能力。

Keywords :买卖价差；信息生产；罕见事件；黑天鹅

\[3\] Is there a risk-return tradeoff in the corporate bond market? Time-series and cross-sectional evidence

标题:公司债券市场是否存在风险回报权衡？时间序列和横截面证据

作者:Jennie Bai,Turan G. Bali,Quan Wen

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001835

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.003

Abstract : We provide time-series and cross-sectional evidence on the significance of a risk-return tradeoff in the bond and equity markets. We find a significantly positive intertemporal relation between expected return and risk in the bond market. We also propose novel measures of systematic and idiosyncratic risk for individual corporate bonds and find a significantly positive cross-sectional relation between systematic risk and expected bond returns, whereas there is no significant link between idiosyncratic risk and future bond returns. We provide an explanation for the significance of systematic (idiosyncratic) risk based on different investor preferences and informational frictions in the bond (equity) market.

Keywords : Corporate bonds;Systematic risk;Idiosyncratic volatility;Risk-return tradeoff

Abstract :我们提供了债券和股票市场风险收益权衡重要性的时间序列和横截面证据。我们发现，债券市场的预期收益和风险之间存在显著的跨期正相关关系。我们还提出了针对个别公司债券的系统性和特殊性风险的新措施，发现系统性风险与预期债券收益之间存在显著的正截面关系，而特殊性风险与未来债券收益之间没有显著的联系。基于债券（股票）市场中不同的投资者偏好和信息摩擦，我们解释了系统性（特殊性）风险的重要性。

Keywords :公司债券；系统性风险；特质波动性；风险回报权衡

\[4\] Optimal financing with tokens

标题:代币最优融资

作者:Sebastian Gryglewicz,Simon Mayer,Erwan Morellec

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001860

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.004

Abstract : We develop a model in which a start-up firm issues tokens to finance a digital platform, which creates agency conflicts between platform developers and outsiders. We show that token financing is preferred to equity financing unless the platform expects strong cash flows, has large financing needs, or faces severe agency conflicts. Tokens are characterized by their utility features, facilitating transactions, and security features, granting cash flow rights. While security features trigger endogenous network effects and spur platform adoption, they also dilute developers’ equity stake and incentives so that the optimal level of security features decreases with agency conflicts and financing needs.

Keywords : Coin offering;Security tokens;Optimal financing;Agency conflicts

Abstract :我们开发了一个模型，在该模型中，一家初创公司发行代币为数字平台融资，这在平台开发者和外部人员之间产生了代理冲突。我们表明，代币融资优先于股权融资，除非平台预期现金流强劲、融资需求巨大或面临严重的代理冲突。代币的特点是其实用功能、促进交易和安全功能，授予现金流权利。虽然安全特性触发了内生网络效应并刺激了平台采用，但它们也稀释了开发商的股权和激励，从而使安全特性的最佳水平随着代理冲突和融资需求而降低。

Keywords :钱币发行；安全令牌；最优融资；代理冲突

\[5\] Core earnings: New data and evidence

标题:核心收益：新数据和证据

作者:Ethan Rouen,Eric C. So,Charles C.Y. Wang

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001550

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.025

Abstract : Using a novel dataset, we show that components of firms’ GAAP earnings stemming from ancillary business activities or transitory shocks are significant in frequency and magnitude. These components have grown over time and are dispersed across various sections of the 10-K. Excluding them from GAAP earnings yields a core earnings measure that distinguishes between the recurring and non-recurring components of net income and forecasts future performance. Analysts and market participants are slow to impound these earnings components’ implications, particularly the amounts disclosed in the footnotes. Trading strategies that exploit non-core earnings produce abnormal returns of 8% per year.

Keywords : Core earnings;Transitory earnings;Non-operating earnings;Quantitative disclosures;Information processing;Fundamental analysis;Valuation

Abstract :使用一个新的数据集，我们表明，来自辅助业务活动或暂时性冲击的公司GAAP收益的组成部分在频率和幅度上都是显著的。这些组成部分随着时间的推移而增长，并分散在10-K的各个部分。将其从GAAP收益中排除，将产生一个核心收益指标，区分净收益的经常性和非经常性组成部分，并预测未来业绩。分析师和市场参与者对这些收益组成部分的影响，尤其是脚注中披露的金额，迟迟无法确定。利用非核心收益的交易策略每年产生8%的异常回报。

Keywords :核心收益；暂时收益；营业外收入；定量披露；信息处理；基础分析；估价

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002415

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.048

Abstract : We offer a model and evidence showing that private debtholders play a key role in setting the endogenous asset value threshold below which corporations declare bankruptcy. As predicted by the model, we find that the recovery rate at emergence from bankruptcy on all of the firm’s debt taken together is increasing in the pre-bankruptcy share of private debt in all debt. \[7\] Spillover effects in empirical corporate finance

标题:实证公司融资中的溢出效应

作者:Tobias Berg,Markus Reisinger,Daniel Streitz

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001690

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.039

Abstract : Despite their importance, the discussion of spillover effects in empirical research often misses the rigor dedicated to endogeneity concerns. We analyze a broad set of workhorse models of firm interactions and show that spillovers naturally arise in many corporate finance settings. This has important implications for the estimation of treatment effects: (i) even with random treatment, spillovers lead to a complicated bias; (ii) fixed effects can exacerbate the spillover-induced bias. Keywords : Spillovers;Direct vs. indirect effects;Credit supply

Abstract :尽管它们很重要，但在实证研究中对溢出效应的讨论往往忽略了内生性关注的严格性。我们分析了一系列广泛的企业互动模型，并表明溢出效应在许多企业融资环境中自然产生。这对治疗效果的估计具有重要意义：（i）即使是随机治疗，溢出效应也会导致复杂的偏差；（ii）固定效应会加剧溢出引起的偏差。我们为实证研究人员提出了简单的诊断工具，并在一个应用程序中说明了我们的指导。

\[8\] Global factor premiums

标题:全球要素溢价

作者:Guido Baltussen,Laurens Swinkels,Pim Van Vliet

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21003007

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.06.030

Abstract : We examine 24 global factor premiums across equity, bond, commodity, and currency markets via replication and out-of-sample evidence between 1800 and 2016. Replication yields ambiguous evidence within a unified testing framework that accounts for p-hacking. Out-of-sample tests reveal strong and robust presence of the large majority of global factor premiums, with limited out-of-sample decay of the premiums. We find global factor premiums to be generally unrelated to market, downside, or macroeconomic risks in the 217 years of data. These results reveal significant global factor premiums that present a challenge to traditional asset pricing theories.

Keywords : Factor premium;P-hacking;Return anomalies;Predictability;Seasonality

Abstract :我们通过复制和样本外证据研究了1800年至2016年间股票、债券、商品和货币市场的24项全球要素溢价。复制在一个统一的测试框架内产生了含糊不清的证据，该框架解释了p-hacking。样本外测试显示，绝大多数全球要素溢价存在强劲和稳健，且溢价的样本外衰减有限。我们发现，在217年的数据中，全球要素溢价通常与市场、下行或宏观经济风险无关。这些结果揭示了显著的全球要素溢价，对传统资产定价理论提出了挑战。

Keywords :要素溢价；P-hacking；回归异常；可预测性；季节性

\[9\] Does target firm insider trading signal the target's synergy potential in mergers and acquisitions?

Abstract : We find that the acquirer's (1) abnormal returns at merger and acquisition (M&A) announcements and (2) long-term abnormal returns after acquisitions increase with target firm insiders’ net purchase ratios. Abstract : We estimate a regime-switching model for the equity term structure with Bayesian methods. \[12\] Labor leverage, coordination failures, and aggregate risk

标题:劳动力杠杆、协调失败和总风险

作者:Matthieu Bouvard,Adolfo de Motta

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21003068

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.06.036

Abstract : This paper studies an economy in which demand spillovers make firms’ production decisions strategic complements. Firms choose their operating leverage trading off higher fixed costs for lower variable costs. Operating leverage governs firms’ exposures to an aggregate labor productivity shock. In equilibrium, firms exhibit excessive operating leverage because they do not internalize that an economy with higher aggregate operating leverage is more likely to fall into a recession following a negative productivity shock. Welfare losses coming from firms’ failure to coordinate production are amplified by suboptimal risk-taking, which magnifies the impact of productivity shocks onto aggregate output.

Keywords : Operating leverage;Labor leverage;Coordination failure;Aggregate risk

Abstract :本文研究了需求溢出使企业生产决策具有战略互补性的经济。企业选择经营杠杆，以较高的固定成本换取较低的可变成本。经营杠杆控制着企业对总劳动生产率冲击的风险敞口。在均衡状态下，企业表现出过度的经营杠杆，因为它们没有内在地认识到，总经营杠杆较高的经济体更可能在负生产率冲击后陷入衰退。由于企业未能协调生产而造成的福利损失因次优风险承担而放大，从而放大了生产率冲击对总产出的影响。
