\[1\] High dimensional Portfolio Optimization using Joint Shrinkage

标题 : 基于联合收缩的高维投资组合优化

作者 : Anik Burman, Sayantan Banerjee , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.13633

说明 : -

Abstract : We consider the problem of optimizing a portfolio of financial assets, where the number of assets can be much larger than the number of observations. The optimal portfolio weights require estimating the inverse covariance matrix of excess asset returns, classical solutions of which behave badly in high dimensional scenarios. We propose to use a regression based joint shrinkage method for estimating the partial correlation among the assets. Extensive simulation studies illustrate the superior performance of the proposed method with respect to variance, weight, and risk estimation errors compared with competing methods for both the global minimum variance portfolios and Markowitz mean variance portfolios. We also demonstrate the excellent empirical performances of our method on daily and monthly returns of the components of the S&P 500 index.

摘要 : 我们考虑优化金融资产组合的问题，其中资产的数量可以远远大于观察的数量。最优投资组合权重需要估计超额资产收益的逆协方差矩阵，其经典解在高维场景中表现不佳。我们建议使用基于回归的联合收缩方法来估计资产之间的偏相关。大量的模拟研究表明，与全局最小方差投资组合和Markowitz均值方差投资组合的竞争方法相比，所提出的方法在方差、权重和风险估计误差方面具有优越的性能。我们还展示了我们的方法对标准普尔500指数成份股的日收益率和月收益率的出色实证表现。

\[2\] Two step actuarial valuations

标题 : 两步精算估值

作者 : Karim Barigou (ISFA), Daniël Linders (UvA), Fan Yang , 类别 ：Pricing of Securities (q-fin.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.13796

说明 : -

Abstract : We introduce the class of actuarial consistent valuation methods for insurance liabilities which depend on both financial and actuarial risks, which imposes that all actuarial risks are priced via standard actuarial principles. We propose to extend standard actuarial principles by a new actuarialconsistent procedure, which we call two step actuarial valuations . In the coherent setting, we show that actuarial consistent valuations are equivalent to two step actuarial valuations. We also discuss the connection with two step market consistent valuations from Pelsser and Stadje (2014). In particular, we discuss how the dependence structure between actuarial and financial risks impacts both actuarial consistent and market consistent valuations.

摘要 : 我们介绍了一类保险负债的精算一致性估值方法，该方法同时依赖于财务和精算风险，规定所有精算风险均通过标准精算原则进行定价。我们建议通过一种新的精算一致性程序来扩展标准精算原理，我们称之为两步精算估值。在一致性条件下，我们证明了精算一致性估值等价于两步精算估值。我们还讨论了与Pelsser和Stadje（2014）两步市场一致性估值的联系。特别是，我们讨论了精算和财务风险之间的依赖结构如何影响精算一致性和市场一致性估值。

\[3\] Intra Day Price Simulation with Generative Adversarial Modelling of the Order Flow

标题 : 订单流生成对抗模型的日内价格模拟

作者 : Ye-Sheen Lim, Denise Gorse , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.13905

说明 : Comments: 6 pages, ICMLA 2021

Abstract : Intra day price variations in financial markets are driven by the sequence of orders, called the order flow, that is submitted at high frequency by traders. This paper introduces a novel application of the Sequence Generative Adversarial Networks framework to model the order flow, such that random sequences of the order flow can then be generated to simulate the intra day variation of prices. As a benchmark, a well known parametric model from the quantitative finance literature is selected. The models are fitted, and then multiple random paths of the order flow sequences are sampled from each model. Model performances are then evaluated by using the generated sequences to simulate price variations, and we compare the empirical regularities between the price variations produced by the generated and real sequences. The empirical regularities considered include the distribution of the price log returns, the price volatility, and the heavy tail of the log returns distributions. The results show that the order sequences from the generative model are better able to reproduce the statistical behaviour of real price variations than the sequences from the benchmark.

摘要 : 金融市场的日内价格变化是由交易员高频提交的订单序列（称为订单流）驱动的。本文介绍了序列生成对抗网络框架在订单流建模中的一个新应用，从而可以生成订单流的随机序列来模拟价格的日内变化。作为基准，从定量金融文献中选择了一个著名的参数模型。对模型进行拟合，然后从每个模型中抽取订单流序列的多条随机路径。然后，通过使用生成的序列模拟价格变化来评估模型性能，并比较生成的序列和真实序列产生的价格变化之间的经验规律。考虑的经验规律包括价格对数收益的分布、价格波动和对数收益分布的重尾。结果表明，生成模型的订单序列比基准模型的订单序列能够更好地再现真实价格变化的统计行为。

\[5\] Periodicity in Cryptocurrency Volatility and Liquidity

标题 : 加密货币波动和流动性的周期性

作者 : Peter Reinhard Hansen, Chan Kim, Wade Kimbrough , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.12142

说明 : -

Abstract : We study recurrent patterns in volatility and volume for major cryptocurrencies, Bitcoin and Ether, using data from two centralized exchanges (Coinbase Pro and Binance) and a decentralized exchange (Uniswap V2). We find systematic patterns in both volatility and volume across day of the week, hour of the day, and within the hour. These patterns have grown stronger over the years and can be related to algorithmic trading and funding times in futures markets. We also document that price formation mainly takes place on the centralized exchanges while price adjustments on the decentralized exchanges can be sluggish.

摘要 : 我们使用来自两个集中交易所（Coinbase Pro和Binance）和一个分散交易所（Uniswap V2）的数据，研究了主要加密货币比特币和以太币的波动性和交易量的循环模式。我们在一周中的每一天、每一天的每一小时和每一小时内发现波动性和成交量的系统模式。这些模式在过去几年中变得越来越强大，可能与期货市场的算法交易和融资时间有关。我们还记录了价格形成主要发生在集中交易所，而分散交易所的价格调整可能缓慢。

说明 : -

Abstract : In most real scenarios the construction of a risk neutral portfolio must be performed in discrete time and with transaction costs. \[9\] Reputation dependent pricing strategy

标题 : 声誉相关定价策略

作者 : Zehao Chen, Yanchen Zhu, Tianyang Shen, Yufan Ye , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.12477

说明 : -

Abstract : Most online markets establish reputation systems to assist building trust between sellers and buyers. Sellers reputations not only provide guidelines for buyers but may also inform sellers their optimal pricing strategy. In this research, we assumed two types of buyer informed buyers and uninformed buyers. Informed buyers know more about the reputation about the seller but may incur a search cost. Then we developed a benchmark model and a competition model. We found that high reputation sellers and low reputation sellers adapt different pricing strategy depending on the informativeness of buyers and the competition among sellers. With a large proportion of informed buyers, high reputation sellers may charge lower price than low reputation sellers, which exists a negative price premium effect, in contrast to conclusions of some previous studies. Empirical findings were in consistence with our theoretical models. We collected data of five categories of products, televisions, laptops, cosmetics, shoes, and beverages, from Taobao, a leading C2C Chinese online market. Negative price premium effect was observed for TVs, laptops, and cosmetics price premium effect was observed for beverages no significant trend was observed for shoes. We infer product value and market complexity are the main factors of buyer informativeness.

摘要 : 大多数在线市场建立声誉系统，以帮助建立卖家和买家之间的信任。卖方声誉不仅为买方提供指导，还可以告知卖方其最佳定价策略。在本研究中，我们假设了两种类型的买家：知情买家和不知情买家。知情买家更了解卖家的声誉，但可能会招致搜索成本。然后，我们开发了基准模型和竞争模型。我们发现，高声誉卖家和低声誉卖家根据买家的信息量和卖家之间的竞争情况采用不同的定价策略。与之前一些研究的结论相反，在知情买家占很大比例的情况下，高声誉卖家可能比低声誉卖家收取更低的价格，这存在负的价格溢价效应。实证结果与我们的理论模型一致。我们从中国领先的C2C在线市场淘宝网收集了五类产品的数据，包括电视机、笔记本电脑、化妆品、鞋子和饮料。电视、笔记本电脑和化妆品的价格溢价效应为负，饮料的价格溢价效应为负，鞋的价格溢价效应没有显著趋势。我们推断产品价值和市场复杂性是影响买方信息性的主要因素。

\[10\] Measuring Police Presence in US Cities using Smartphone Data

标题 : 使用智能手机数据衡量美国城市的警察存在

作者 : M. Keith Chen, Katherine L. Christensen, Elicia John, Emily Owens, Yilin Zhuo , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.12491

说明 : -

Abstract : While extensive, research on policing in America has focused on documented actions such as stops and arrests less is known about patrolling and presence. We map the movements of over ten thousand police officers across twenty three of America s largest cities by combining anonymized smartphone data with station and precinct boundaries. Police spend considerably more time in Black neighborhoods, a disparity which persists after controlling for density, socioeconomics, and crime driven demand for policing. Our results suggest that roughly half of observed racial disparities in arrests derive from disparities in exposure, and that supervisor (but not officer) diversity reduces this.

摘要 : 虽然对美国警察的研究非常广泛，但主要集中在记录在案的行动，如停车和逮捕，而对巡逻和存在的了解较少。我们通过将匿名智能手机数据与警察局和警区边界相结合，绘制了美国23个最大城市一万多名警察的行动地图。警察在黑人社区花费的时间要多得多，在控制了人口密度、社会经济和犯罪驱动的警务需求后，这种差距依然存在。我们的研究结果表明，在观察到的逮捕种族差异中，大约有一半来自于暴露的差异，而主管（而非官员）的多样性降低了这一点。

\[12\] On the nature of monetary and price inflation and hyperinflation

标题 : 论货币和价格通货膨胀与恶性通货膨胀的性质

作者 : Laurence Francis Lacey , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.12980

说明 : Comments: 24 pages, 6 figures, 2 tables, 1 appendix

Abstract : Monetary inflation is a sustained increase in the money supply than can result in price inflation, which is a rise in the general level of prices of goods and services. The objectives of this paper were to develop economic models to (1) predict the annual rate of growth in the US consumer price index (CPI), based on the annual growth in the US broad money supply (BMS), the annual growth in US real GDP, and the annual growth in US savings, over the time period 2001 to 2019 (2) investigate the means by which monetary and price inflation can develop into monetary and price hyperinflation. The hypothesis that the annual rate of growth in the US CPI is a function of the annual growth in the US BMS minus the annual growth in US real GDP minus the annual growth in US savings, over the time period investigated, has been shown to be the case.
