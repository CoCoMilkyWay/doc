\[1\] Asset Variance Risk and Compound Option Prices

标题:资产差异风险与复合期权价格

作者:Hitesh Doshi,Jan Ericsson,Mathieu Fournier,Sang Byung Seo

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3885357

Abstract : We evaluate the empirical validity of the compound option framework. In a model where corporate securities are options on a firm's assets, option contracts on these can be viewed as options on options, or compound options. We estimate a model with priced asset variance risk and find that it jointly explains the level and time variation of both equity index (SPX) and credit index (CDX) option prices well out-of-sample. This suggests that the two options markets are priced consistently, contrary to recent findings. We show that variance risk is important for establishing pricing consistency between equity, credit, and related derivatives.

Keywords : None

Abstract :我们评估了复合期权框架的实证有效性。在一个公司证券是公司资产期权的模型中，这些证券的期权合同可以被视为期权期权或复合期权。我们估计了一个有定价资产方差风险的模型，发现它能很好地解释股票指数（SPX）和信用指数（CDX）期权价格的水平和时间变化。这表明这两个期权市场的定价是一致的，与最近的研究结果相反。我们证明了方差风险对于建立股票、信贷和相关衍生品之间的定价一致性是重要的。

Keywords :None

\[2\] Yes, Virginia, there are Superstar Money Managers

标题:是的，维吉尼亚，有超级明星基金经理

作者:Valentin Dimitrov,Prem C. Jain

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3885288

Abstract : Berk and Green (2004) argue that, in equilibrium, mutual fund performance is not informative about the skill of the fund managers. Hence, instead of studying mutual fund performance, we study stock recommendations of prominent money managers made at the prestigious Barron’s Roundtable. The 3,472 buy recommendations, from 1968 to 2019 (52 years), on average, earn economically meaningful and statistically significant 4.1% excess returns over the 30 trading days immediately following the Roundtable meetings. To study the skill level across decades, we divide the 52-year-long time-series into four equal 13-year-long periods and find that excess returns are similar for each of the four periods and are statistically significant. Overall, we conclude that money managers are skillful, and that skill has not diminished over the decades.

Keywords : mutual funds, management skill, alpha, Barron's Roundtable, returns

Abstract :Berk和Green（2004）认为，在均衡状态下，共同基金的业绩并不能说明基金经理的技能。因此，我们不研究共同基金的表现，而是研究著名基金经理在著名的巴伦圆桌会议上提出的股票建议。从1968年到2019年（52年）的3472份买入建议，在圆桌会议之后的30个交易日内，平均获得了具有经济意义和统计意义的4.1%超额收益。为了研究几十年来的技能水平，我们将52年的时间序列划分为四个相等的13年时间段，发现这四个时间段的超额收益率都是相似的，并且具有统计学意义。总的来说，我们得出的结论是，基金经理很有技巧，而且这种技巧在过去几十年里没有减少。

Keywords :共同基金，管理技巧，阿尔法，巴伦圆桌会议，回报

\[3\] Firm Growth Potential and Option Returns

标题:企业成长潜力与期权收益

作者:Panayiotis C. Andreou,Turan G. Bali,Anastasios Kagkadis,Neophytos Lambertides

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3874674

Abstract : This paper shows that firm growth potential – representing a firm's yet-unexercised growth opportunities – is associated with option overpricing and low future delta-hedged option returns. We provide an explanation of this phenomenon based on the idea that retail investors exert buying pressure and tend to overpay for the call options of growth-oriented firms because they overestimate the potential profits arising from the return skewness of the underlying stocks. We further show that the effect is stronger among stocks that are more likely to exhibit high skewness, are more prone to limits-to-arbitrage and are more exposed to informational frictions.

Keywords : Option returns, Growth potential, Growth options, Idiosyncratic skewness, Skewness preference

Abstract :本文表明，企业增长潜力代表了一个企业尚未实现的增长机会，与期权定价过高和未来低delta对冲期权收益率有关。本文基于散户投资者对成长型公司的看涨期权产生购买压力并往往过高支付的观点来解释这一现象，因为散户投资者高估了标的股票收益偏斜带来的潜在利润。我们进一步表明，在更可能表现出高偏态、更容易受到套利限制以及更容易受到信息摩擦影响的股票中，这种效应更强。

Keywords :期权收益，增长潜力，增长期权，特质偏态，偏态偏好

\[4\] US Inflation and Global Asset Returns

标题:美国通胀与全球资产收益率

作者:Wei Dai,Mamdouh Medhat

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3882899

Abstract : We study the relation between US inflation and the performance of global asset classes (including bonds, stocks, industry portfolios, factor premiums, commodities, and REITs), both over a long sample period (1927–2020) and over the most recent 30 years (1991–2020). We find that most assets had positive average real returns in both low- and high-inflation years. While average real returns were lower in years with higher inflation for most assets, many of the differences are not statistically reliable, especially among non-bond assets and in more recent times. We also find mostly weak correlations over time between nominal returns and inflation, including contemporaneous, lagged, expected, and unexpected inflation. The notable exceptions are energy stocks and commodities, where there are reliably positive correlations with both expected and unexpected inflation, but our results also suggest both assets are too volatile to be an effective inflation hedge. Our results confirm the potential of most asset classes to outpace inflation over the long term and suggest that, for investors prioritizing the preservation of purchasing power, inflation-indexed securities may be a more appropriate inflation hedge than commonly suggested alternatives.

Keywords : Inflation, Asset Returns, Asset Allocation, Inflation Hedging, Inflation-Indexed Securities

Abstract :我们研究了美国通货膨胀与全球资产类别（包括债券、股票、行业投资组合、要素溢价、大宗商品和房地产投资信托基金）表现之间的关系，包括长期样本期（1927-2020年）和最近30年（1991-2020年）。我们发现，大多数资产在低通胀和高通胀年份的平均实际回报率均为正。虽然大多数资产的平均实际回报率在通货膨胀率较高的年份较低，但许多差异在统计上并不可靠，特别是在非债券资产和最近时期。我们还发现，名义收益率与通货膨胀（包括同期的、滞后的、预期的和意外的通货膨胀）之间的相关性随着时间的推移大多较弱。值得注意的例外是能源股和大宗商品，它们与预期通胀和意外通胀都存在可靠的正相关关系，但我们的结果也表明，这两种资产的波动性太大，无法有效对冲通胀。我们的结果证实了大多数资产类别在长期内超过通胀的潜力，并表明，对于优先考虑保持购买力的投资者来说，通胀指数证券可能是比通常建议的替代品更合适的通胀对冲工具。

Keywords :通货膨胀，资产回报，资产配置，通货膨胀对冲，通货膨胀指数证券

出处:-

Abstract : Beta is an important input in the cost of capital calculations. However, it is not directly observable, so an estimate needs to be made. Beta can be easily estimated using simple regression techniques. However, there is evidence that more complex estimation procedures generate superior beta forecasts in international markets. Abstract : Merton (1990, p.44) claims that the market portfolio can be constructed without the knowledge of preferences and the distribution of wealth. Abstract :默顿（1990，p.44）声称，市场投资组合可以在不了解偏好和财富分配的情况下构建。然而，CAPM市场的分析均衡解揭示了这一点。投资者的偏好和财富影响市场的均衡价格，它们不影响CAPM公式的数学形式，而是改变公式中变量的值。

\[9\] Differences of Opinion Predict Volatility

标题:意见分歧预示着波动

作者:Christian Skov Jensen,Mads Vestergaard Jensen

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3882792

Abstract : As a new test of models of differences of opinion, we study empirically how the shorting market interacts with the equity volatility. Consistent with theories of differences of opinion, a positive (negative) demand shift in the shorting market predicts higher (lower) future stock volatility: A positive demand shift predicts an increase in the next week's annualized volatility of 4.8 percentage points, which corresponds to a 10.9% increase relative to the average weekly annualized volatility. The effect remains after controlling for other proxies of differences of opinion such as bid-ask spreads and volume, suggesting that the shorting market is an important channel in which investors reveal divergence or convergence in their beliefs about the future trajectory of individual stocks.

Keywords : Differences of opinion, equity volatility, costs of shorting, short interest

Abstract :作为对意见分歧模型的一种新的检验，本文实证研究了卖空市场与股票波动的相互作用。与意见分歧理论一致，做空市场的正（负）需求变化预测未来股市波动性将上升（下降）：正需求变化预测下周的年化波动率将上升4.8个百分点，与平均周年化波动率相比，相对应的增长率为10.9%。在控制了买卖价差和成交量等其他意见分歧的指标后，这种效应仍然存在，这表明卖空市场是一个重要的渠道，投资者可以通过卖空市场发现他们对个股未来走势的看法存在分歧或趋同。

Keywords :意见分歧、股票波动性、做空成本、空头利率

\[10\] The volatility puzzle of the low-risk anomaly

标题:低风险异常的波动性之谜

作者:Pedro Barroso,Andrew L. Detzel,Paulo F. Maio

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3882108

Abstract : The volatility of betting-against-beta (BAB) and -idiosyncratic volatility (BAV) factors negatively forecasts their respective Sharpe ratios and abnormal returns. This predictability causes signicant performance gains from volatility timing these factors and provides new time-series evidence on leading theories of the low-risk anomaly. Consistent with the limits-to-arbitrage theory, we show that the abnormal returns of the volatility-managed BAV strategy are concentrated in overpriced stocks. However, controlling for mispricing, arbitrage risk, lottery demand, and multiple risk factors has no effect on the timing benefits of BAB. We further show that the leverage constraints model predicts a counterfactual positive relation between volatility and subsequent BAB Sharpe ratios, and highly active institutions shift from high- to low-beta stocks as volatility increases, suggesting their demand contributes to the abnormal returns of BAB. Overall, the predictive power of volatility challengesour current understanding of the low-risk anomaly.

Keywords : Betting-against-beta, time-varying risk, realized volatility, risk factors, scaled factors, anomalies, lottery preferences, leverage constraints.

Abstract :对贝塔（BAB）和特质波动（BAV）因子的投注波动性对其夏普比率和异常收益率具有负向预测作用。这种可预测性使波动性时间因素带来了显著的绩效收益，并为低风险异常的主导理论提供了新的时间序列证据。与套利极限理论一致，我们证明了波动率管理BAV策略的异常收益集中在定价过高的股票上。然而，控制错误定价、套利风险、彩票需求和多重风险因素对BAB的时间收益没有影响。我们进一步证明杠杆约束模型预测波动率和随后的BAB-Sharpe比率之间存在反事实的正相关关系，并且随着波动率的增加，高度活跃的机构从高贝塔股转向低贝塔股，这表明它们的需求促成了BAB的异常回报。总体而言，波动率的预测能力挑战了我们目前对低风险异常的理解。

Keywords :投注贝塔，时变风险，已实现的波动性，风险因素，规模因素，异常，彩票偏好，杠杆约束。
