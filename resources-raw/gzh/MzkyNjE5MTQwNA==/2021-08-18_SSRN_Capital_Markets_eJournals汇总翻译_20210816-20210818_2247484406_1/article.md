\[1\] Disclosure of Integrity Culture in Annual Reports of Target Firms and M&A Performance

标题:目标公司年度报告中诚信文化的披露与并购绩效

作者:Balasingham Balachandran,Robert W. Faff,Sagarika Mishra,Syed Shams

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3906290

Abstract : This study investigates whether the attribute of integrity culture (derived from annual reports of target firms) influences M&A performance. We find that the implied disclosure of integrity by target firms has a positive and significant effect on bidder market reaction. Our results hold after controlling for target firm religiosity and CSR, and sample selection bias by propensity score matching and Heckman’s two stage procedure. Further, we find that time to complete the deal is shorter for target firms with higher integrity. This study provides new insights into the role played by integrity culture of target firms in merger and acquisition decisions.

Keywords : Corporate Culture, Integrity, M&A

Abstract :本研究调查了诚信文化的属性（来源于目标公司的年度报告）是否影响并购绩效。我们发现，目标公司隐含的诚信披露对投标人的市场反应有着积极而显著的影响。我们的结果在通过倾向评分匹配和Heckman的两阶段程序控制了目标公司的宗教性和CSR以及样本选择偏差后仍然成立。此外，我们发现，对于诚信度较高的目标公司，完成交易的时间更短。本研究为目标公司的诚信文化在并购决策中的作用提供了新的见解。

Keywords :企业文化、诚信、并购

\[2\] Changing Expected Returns Can Induce Spurious Serial Correlation

标题:改变预期收益可能会导致虚假的序列相关性

作者:Kuntara Pukthuanthong,Richard Roll,Avanidhar Subrahmanyam

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3906157

Abstract : Changing expected returns can induce spurious autocorrelation in returns. We show why this happens with some simple examples and then investigate its prevalence in actual equity data. Using different measures of expected returns based on realized means, options markets, factor models, and analysts’ price targets we find evidence that supports our premise. Specifically, absolute shifts in expected returns are strongly and positively related to measured autocorrelations in the cross-section of individual stocks, as predicted by our analysis. Well-studied risk factors show no evidence of spurious components. We also show how our analysis implies spurious cross-autocorrelation and find supporting evidence for this phenomenon as well.

Keywords : Efficient Markets, Serial Correlation, Spurious Results

Abstract :改变预期收益会导致收益的虚假自相关。我们用一些简单的例子来说明为什么会发生这种情况，然后调查其在实际股票数据中的普遍性。利用基于已实现均值、期权市场、因子模型和分析师价格目标的不同预期回报指标，我们找到了支持我们假设的证据。具体而言，正如我们的分析所预测的那样，预期收益的绝对变化与单个股票横截面中测量的自相关度密切正相关。经过充分研究的风险因素没有显示虚假成分的证据。我们还展示了我们的分析如何暗示虚假的交叉自相关，并找到了支持这种现象的证据。

Keywords :有效市场、序列相关性、虚假结果

\[3\] The Disappearing Outperformance of Post-reorg Equity

标题:重组后股票的表现优势消失

作者:Wei Jiang,Wei Wang,Yan Yang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3906039

Abstract : Using trading information from both over-the-counter (OTC) markets and public exchanges of a comprehensive sample of relisted Chapter 11 firms in the past four decades, we find that newly issued equity from these firms outperforms industry- and characteristics-matched portfolios by 40--50% during the 200 trading days post emergence on average, but the median abnormal return is close to zero. The superior returns are almost exclusive to firms traded in the OTC markets. The equal-weighted calendar time portfolio generates 7.2% annualized excess returns over a five-factor benchmark, but the outperformance disappears in the recent decade. The post-emergence earnings announcement returns are positive only in the 2000s, when institutional ownership of post-reorganization equity increased significantly during the same time. The evidence suggests that the outperformance of post-reorg equity documented in earlier studies is likely due to market expectation errors for future earnings, which are corrected in recent periods by sophisticated institutional investors.

Keywords : bankruptcy, stock returns, over-the-counter (OTC) market, earnings announcement, institutional ownership

Abstract :利用过去四十年中重新上市的第11章公司的综合样本的场外交易（OTC）市场和公开交易所的交易信息，我们发现这些公司新发行的股票在出现后的200个交易日内平均比行业和特征匹配的投资组合跑赢40%-50%，但异常回报率中位数接近于零。优越的回报几乎是在场外交易市场交易的公司所独有的。与五因素基准相比，等权重日历时间投资组合产生7.2%的年化超额回报，但在最近十年中，表现出色的情况消失了。只有在2000年代，当重组后股权的机构所有权在同一时间显著增加时，出现后收益公告回报才为正。证据表明，早期研究中记录的重组后股票的表现优于预期，可能是由于市场对未来收益的预期错误，而这些错误在最近几段时间被成熟的机构投资者纠正了。

Keywords :破产、股票回报、场外交易（OTC）市场、收益公告、机构所有权

\[4\] Ownership Concentration and Performance of Deteriorating Syndicated Loans

标题:恶化银团贷款的所有权集中度和绩效

作者:Mariassunta Giannetti,Ralf Meisenzahl

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3905845

Abstract : Regulation and capital constraints may force banks and collateralized loan obligations (CLOs) to sell deteriorating loans, potentially hampering renegotiation and amplifying the initial negative shock to the borrower. We show that banks and CLOs sell downgraded loans to mutual funds and hedge funds. The reallocation of loan shares favors the syndicate's concentration increasing lenders' incentives to renegotiate. However, syndicates remain less concentrated when potential buyers experience financial constraints and subsequently loans are less likely to be amended and more likely to be downgraded even further. Our findings indicate that existing regulations may amplify shocks to credit quality during periods of generalized distress in the financial system.

Keywords : Debtor Concentration, Credit Quality, Leveraged Lending

Abstract :监管和资本约束可能会迫使银行和贷款抵押债券（CLO）出售恶化的贷款，这可能会阻碍重新谈判，并放大对借款人的最初负面冲击。我们表明，银行和CLO向共同基金和对冲基金出售降级贷款。贷款份额的重新分配有利于银团的集中，增加了贷款人重新谈判的动机。然而，当潜在买家遇到财务约束时，银团的集中度仍然较低，随后贷款不太可能被修改，更可能被进一步降级。我们的研究结果表明，在金融体系普遍陷入困境的时期，现行监管可能会放大对信贷质量的冲击。

Keywords :债务人集中度、信贷质量、杠杆贷款

\[5\] Illiquidity and the Cost of Equity Capital: Evidence from Actual Estimates of Capital Cost

标题:非流动性与权益资本成本：来自资本成本实际估计的证据

作者:Amit Goyal,Avanidhar Subrahmanyam,Bhaskaran Swaminathan

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3905830

Abstract : Illiquidity measures appear to be related to monthly realized returns but do they actually impact the long-run costs of capital for firms? We make progress on this issue by analyzing the relation between the well-known Amihud (2002) liquidity measure and actual cost-of-capital (CoC) estimates, which rely on imputing the internal rate of returns on firms’ equity cash flows. After controlling for well-known determinants of CoC, we find that illiquidity is strongly and negatively related to CoC estimates. This result obtains even as we confirm that illiquidity is positively related to monthly realized returns. While our results do not rule out that short horizon investors demand compensation for illiquidity, our results run contrary to the notion that corporations should care about illiquidity while setting discount rates for long-term projects.

Keywords : Trading Costs, Determinants of Equity Returns, Liquidity Premia

Abstract :非流动性指标似乎与月度实现回报率有关，但它们是否确实影响了企业的长期资本成本？我们通过分析著名的Amihud（2002）流动性度量与实际资本成本（CoC）估计之间的关系，在这一问题上取得了进展，后者依赖于估算公司股权现金流的内部收益率。在控制了众所周知的CoC决定因素后，我们发现流动性不足与CoC估计值呈强负相关。即使我们确认流动性不足与月度实现回报率正相关，这一结果仍然存在。虽然我们的结果不排除短期投资者要求对流动性不足进行补偿，但我们的结果与企业在设定长期项目贴现率时应关注流动性不足的理念背道而驰。

Keywords :交易成本、股票回报的决定因素、流动性溢价

\[6\] Why does Option Implied Volatility Forecast Realized Volatility? Evidence from News Events

标题:为什么期权隐含波动率预测已实现波动率？新闻事件的证据

作者:Sipeng Chen,Gang Li

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3905677

Abstract : We examine the information content of stock option implied volatility. We measure arrival intensities and magnitudes of various types of news, and find that these news measures are all positively associated with contemporaneous stock return volatility and many of them can be predicted by implied volatility. About 28% of the predictive power of implied volatility on future realized volatility is due to its ability to predict these news measures, and most of the predictive power is from predicting arrival intensities of both scheduled and unscheduled news. The predictive power is higher for fundamental news than for non-fundamental news.

Keywords : implied volatility, realized volatility, news arrival intensity, news magnitude, scheduled and unscheduled news, fundamental and non-fundamental news

Abstract :我们研究了股票期权隐含波动率的信息含量。我们测量了各种类型新闻的到达强度和大小，发现这些新闻度量都与同期股票收益率波动率正相关，其中许多可以通过隐含波动率预测。隐含波动率对未来已实现波动率的预测能力约有28%是由于其预测这些新闻指标的能力，而大部分预测能力来自预测预定和非预定新闻的到达强度。基本面新闻的预测能力高于非基本面新闻。

Keywords :隐含波动率、已实现波动率、新闻到达强度、新闻量、计划内和计划外新闻、基本面和非基本面新闻

\[7\] Know Your Customer: Relationship Lending and Bank Trading

标题:了解你的客户：关系借贷和银行交易

作者:Rainer F. H. Haselmann,Christian Leuz,Sebastian Schreiber

出处:LawFin Working Paper No. 22

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3903968

Abstract : In this study, we analyze the trading behavior of banks with lending relationships. We combine detailed German data on banks’ proprietary trading and market making with lending information from the credit register and then examine how banks trade stocks of their borrowers around important corporate events. We find that banks trade more frequently and also profitably ahead of events when they are the main lender (or relationship bank) for the borrower. Specifically, we show that relationship banks are more likely to build up positive (negative) trading positions in the two weeks before positive (negative) news events, and also that they unwind these positions shortly after the event. This trading pattern is more pronounced for unscheduled earnings events, M&A transactions, and after borrower obtain new bank loans. Our results suggest that lending relationships endow banks with important information, highlighting the potential for conflicts of interest in banking, which has been a prominent concern in the regulatory debate.

Keywords : None

Abstract :在这项研究中，我们分析了有借贷关系的银行的交易行为。我们将德国有关银行自营交易和做市的详细数据与信用记录中的贷款信息结合起来，然后研究银行如何围绕重要的公司事件交易借款人的股票。我们发现，当银行作为借款人的主要贷款人（或关系银行）时，它们的交易更频繁，而且在事件发生之前也能盈利。具体而言，我们表明，关系型银行更有可能在正面（负面）新闻事件发生前两周建立正面（负面）交易头寸，而且在事件发生后不久，它们会平仓。这种交易模式在计划外盈利事件、并购交易以及借款人获得新银行贷款后更为明显。我们的结果表明，贷款关系赋予银行重要的信息，突出了银行业利益冲突的可能性，这一直是监管辩论中的一个突出问题。

Keywords :None

\[8\] Liquidity measurement: A comparative review of the literature with a focus on high frequency

标题:流动性衡量：一项以高频为重点的文献比较综述

作者:Zeynep Cobandag Guloglu,Cumhur Ekinci

出处:Journal of Economic Surveys (https://doi.org/10.1111/joes.12440)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3905229

Abstract : This paper provides an exhaustive review and categorization of market liquidity measures that are used to quantify liquidity in empirical research. We review and discuss these measures in a comparative manner in terms of market, data features, computational ease, predictiveness, and potentiality. With a primary focus on high-frequency liquidity measurement, we highlight their advantages, limitations, and extensions. We conclude that high-frequency measures concentrate around bid–ask spread and limit order book, the latter offering a richer ground for analysis. Moreover, considering the recent developments in the industry such as market fragmentation, abundance of data, and improved technology, the practicality of these measures are challenged.

Keywords : high frequency, liquidity measures, market liquidity, market microstructure

Abstract :本文对实证研究中用于量化流动性的市场流动性指标进行了详尽的回顾和分类。我们从市场、数据特征、计算简便性、可预测性和潜力等方面对这些措施进行了比较性的审查和讨论。我们主要关注高频流动性测量，强调其优势、局限性和扩展性。我们的结论是，高频指标集中在买卖价差和限制订单簿上，后者为分析提供了更丰富的基础。此外，考虑到该行业的最新发展，如市场分割、数据丰富和技术改进，这些措施的实用性受到了挑战。

\[10\] Tomorrow Is Another Day: Stocks Active Mutual Funds Overweight Predict the Next-Day Market

标题:明天又是新的一天：股票活跃共同基金加码预测次日市场

作者:Shuaiyu Chen,Yixin Chen,Randolph B. Cohen

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3905263

Abstract : How efficient are securities markets? Do professional investors such as mutual fund managers have the ability to exploit superior information? If so, is that only micro-level information about individual firms, or are they also informed about economy-wide and market-wide prospects? These are some of the most fundamental questions in the study of finance. This paper shows that active mutual fund managers possess, and effectively incorporate, information about future short-term movements of the entire stock market into security prices, where "short term" means over the next day. Specifically, we find that when high active-mutual-fund ownership stocks outperform, the market tends to do well the next day, and vice versa. These effects are modest day by day but are quite large in the aggregate – trading the S&P 500 futures daily based on this phenomenon delivers an annualized alpha of 12% with negative market beta and an information ratio of 0.6, despite the fact that our tools are blunt: the strategy does not employ any data of mutual fund trades. Various additional tests further suggest that the novel short-term market return predictability yields from active mutual fund managers’ collective information advantage as opposed to informed fund flows or temporary price pressure.

Keywords : Market Predictability, Active Mutual Funds, Market Efficiency

Abstract :证券市场的效率如何？共同基金经理等专业投资者是否有能力利用优质信息？如果是这样的话，这仅仅是关于单个公司的微观信息，还是他们也了解整个经济和市场的前景？这些是金融学研究中最基本的问题。本文表明，积极的共同基金经理拥有并有效地将整个股票市场未来短期走势的信息纳入证券价格，其中“短期”指的是次日的价格。具体而言，我们发现，当高活跃共同基金所有权股票表现出色时，市场往往在第二天表现良好，反之亦然。这些影响日复一日是温和的，但总体而言相当大——基于这一现象的标准普尔500指数期货每日交易的年化阿尔法为12%，市场贝塔为负，信息比率为0.6，尽管我们的工具很简单：该策略不使用任何共同基金交易数据。各种附加测试进一步表明，新的短期市场回报可预测性来自积极共同基金经理的集体信息优势，而不是知情的资金流或暂时的价格压力。

Keywords :市场可预测性、积极共同基金、市场效率

\[11\] Spot Fishes at the Table: Tracking Retail Investors and Mutual Funds Return

标题:餐桌上的现货鱼：追踪散户投资者和共同基金回报

作者:Junqing Kang,Shen Lin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3905050

Abstract : The question of how mutual funds deliver excess return motivates one of the largest literature in finance. While most papers focus on whether stock picking or market timing talent exists, former studies are scare about discussion on the ability to 'spot fishes'. Hence, we propose a new definition of skill in tracking retail investors through characterizing trading synchronicity between mutual funds and retail flows. Firstly, we find robustness evidence that funds with low trading synchronicity significantly outperform other funds with high trading synchronicity. Secondly, through a noisy rational expectations model with heterogeneous skills, we demonstrate the trading synchronicity captures mutual funds' ability to explore noise retail flows, other than stock picking or market timing.
