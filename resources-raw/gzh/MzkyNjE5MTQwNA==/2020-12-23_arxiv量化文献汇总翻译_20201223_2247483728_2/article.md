\[1\] Insider trading in the run up to merger announcements. Before and after the UK s Financial Services Act 2012

标题 : 合并公告前的内幕交易。2012年英国金融服务法案前后

作者 : Rebecaa Pham, Marcel Ausloos , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11594

说明 : Comments: to be published in International Journal of Finance and Economics; 5 figures, 62 references; 36 pages

Abstract : After the 2007 2008 financial crisis, the UK government decided that a change in regulation was required to amend the poor control of financial markets. The Financial Services Act 2012 was developed as a result in order to give more control and authority to the regulators of financial markets. Thus, the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) succeeded the Financial Services Authority (FSA). An area requiring an improvement in regulation was insider trading. Our study examines the effectiveness of the FCA in its duty of regulating insider trading through utilising the event study methodology to assess abnormal returns in the run up to the first announcement of mergers. Samples of abnormal returns are examined on periods, under regulation either by the FSA or by the FCA. Practically, stock price data on the London Stock Exchange from 2008 2012 and 2015 2019 is investigated. The results from this study determine that abnormal returns are reduced after the implementation of the Financial Services Act 2012 prices are also found to be noisier in the period before the 2012 Act. Insignificant abnormal returns are found in the run up to the first announcement of mergers in the 2015 2019 period. This concludes that the FCA is efficient in regulating insider trading.

摘要 : 2007年2008年金融危机后，英国政府决定，需要改变监管，以改善对金融市场控制不力的状况。《2012年金融服务法》的制定旨在赋予金融市场监管机构更多的控制权和权力。因此，金融行为管理局（FCA）接替了金融服务管理局（FSA）。一个需要改进监管的领域是内幕交易。我们的研究透过运用事件研究法评估首次合并公告前的异常回报，来检视金融行为监管局在监管内幕交易方面的有效性。根据金融服务管理局（FSA）或金融服务监管局（FCA）的规定，异常回报率的样本在不同时期进行检验。实际上，调查了2008年、2012年和2015年至2019年伦敦证券交易所的股票价格数据。研究结果表明，2012年《金融服务法》实施后，异常收益率有所下降。2012年《金融服务法》实施前，价格波动较大。在2015年至2019年第一次宣布合并之前，发现了不显著的异常收益。这表明，FCA在监管内幕交易方面是有效的。

\[2\] Valuation Models Applied to Value Based Management. Application to the Case of UK Companies with Problems

标题 : 应用于价值管理的估值模型。英国问题公司案例的应用

作者 : Marcel Ausloos , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11595

说明 : Comments: 15 pages; 52 references; 3 Tables

Abstract : Many still rightly wonder whether accounting numbers affect business value. Basic questions are why and how I aim at promoting an objective choice on how optimizing the most suitable valuation methods under a value based management framework through some performance measurement systems. First, I present a comprehensive review of valuation methods. Three valuations methods, (i) Free Cash Flow Valuation Model (FCFVM), (ii) Residual Earning Valuation Model (REVM) and (iii) Abnormal Earning Growth Model (AEGM), are presented. I point out to advantages and limitations. As applications, the proofs of the findings are illustrated on three study cases Marks & Spencer s business pattern (size and growth prospect), which had a recently advertised valuation problem, and two comparable companies, Tesco and Sainsbury s, all three chosen for multiple based valuation. For the purpose, two value drivers are chosen, EnV EBIT (entity value earnings before interests and taxes) and the corresponding EnV Sales. Thus, the question whether accounting numbers through models based on mathematical economics truly affect business value has an answer Maybe, yes.

摘要 : 许多人仍然怀疑会计数字是否会影响企业价值。基本问题是我为什么以及如何通过一些绩效衡量系统，促进在基于价值的管理框架下如何优化最合适的估值方法的客观选择。首先，我对估值方法进行了全面的回顾。提出了三种估值方法：（i）自由现金流估值模型（FCFVM），（ii）剩余收益估值模型（REVM）和（iii）异常收益增长模型（AEGM）。我指出优点和局限性。作为应用，研究结果的证明在三个研究案例中得到了说明，即玛莎百货的商业模式（规模和增长前景），这三个案例最近有一个估值问题，还有两个可比较的公司，特易购和塞恩斯伯里s，这三个公司都选择了多重评估。为此，我们选择了两个价值驱动因素，即EnV EBIT（息税前实体价值收益）和相应的EnV销售额。因此，通过基于数理经济学的模型得出的会计数字是否真的会影响企业价值这个问题也许有一个答案，是的。

\[4\] A geometric analysis of nonlinear dynamics and its application to financial time series

标题 : 非线性动力学的几何分析及其在金融时间序列中的应用

作者 : Isao Shoji, Masahiro Nozawa , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11825

说明 : -

Abstract : A geometric method to analyze nonlinear oscillations is discussed. We consider a nonlinear oscillation modeled by a second order ordinary differential equation without specifying the function form. By transforming the differential equation into the system of first order ordinary differential equations, the trajectory is embedded in R 3 as a curve, and thereby the time evolution of the original state can be translated into the behavior of the curve in R 3 , or the vector field along the curve. We analyze the vector field to investigate the dynamic properties of a nonlinear oscillation. While the function form of the model is unspecified, the vector fields and those associated quantities can be estimated by a nonparametric filtering method. We apply the proposed analysis to the time series of the Japanese stock price index. The application shows that the vector field and its derivative will be used as the tools of picking up various signals that help understanding of the dynamic properties of the stock price index.

摘要 : 讨论了一种分析非线性振动的几何方法。我们考虑一个由二阶常微分方程模拟的非线性振动，而不指定函数形式。通过将微分方程转化为一阶常微分方程组，将轨迹作为一条曲线嵌入到r3中，从而将原始状态的时间演化转化为曲线在r3中的行为，即曲线上的矢量场。我们分析向量场来研究非线性振荡的动力学性质。当模型的函数形式未知时，向量场及其相关量可以用非参数滤波方法估计。我们将所提出的分析方法应用于日本股票价格指数的时间序列。应用表明，向量场及其导数可以作为提取各种信号的工具，有助于理解股价指数的动态特性。

\[5\] Acceptability maximization

标题 : 可接受性最大化

作者 : Gabriela Kováčová, Birgit Rudloff, Igor Cialenco , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11972

说明 : Comments: 31 pages, 3 figures

Abstract : The aim of this paper is to study the optimal investment problem by using coherent acceptability indices (CAIs) as a tool to measure the portfolio performance. We call this problem the acceptability maximization. First, we study the one period (static) case, and propose a numerical algorithm that approximates the original problem by a sequence of risk minimization problems. The results are applied to several important CAIs, such as the gain to loss ratio, the risk adjusted return on capital and the tail value at risk based CAI. In the second part of the paper we investigate the acceptability maximization in a discrete time dynamic setup. Using robust representations of CAIs in terms of a family of dynamic coherent risk measures (DCRMs), we establish an intriguing dichotomy if the corresponding family of DCRMs is recursive (i.e. strongly time consistent) and assuming some recursive structure of the market model, then the acceptability maximization problem reduces to just a one period problem and the maximal acceptability is constant across all states and times. On the other hand, if the family of DCRMs is not recursive, which is often the case, then the acceptability maximization problem ordinarily is a time inconsistent stochastic control problem, similar to the classical mean variance criteria. To overcome this form of time inconsistency, we adapt to our setup the set valued Bellman s principle recently proposed in cite KovacovaRudloff2019 applied to two particular dynamic CAIs the dynamic risk adjusted return on capital and the dynamic gain to loss ratio. The obtained theoretical results are illustrated via numerical examples that include, in particular, the computation of the intermediate mean risk efficient frontiers.

摘要 : 本文旨在研究以一致可接受指数（CAIs）为工具来衡量投资组合绩效的最优投资问题。我们称这个问题为可接受性最大化。首先，我们研究了单周期（静态）情况，并提出了一个数值算法，用一系列风险最小化问题来近似原问题。研究结果应用于损益比率、风险调整后的资本回报率和基于风险的尾部价值的CAI。在论文的第二部分中，我们研究了离散时间动态系统的可接受性最大化问题。利用动态相关风险度量（DCRMs）的稳健表示，如果相应的DCRMs族是递归的（即强时间一致的），并假设市场模型的某种递归结构，我们建立了一个有趣的二分法，然后将可接受性最大化问题归结为一个单周期问题，且最大可接受性在所有状态和时间上都是常数。另一方面，如果DCRMs族不是递归的（通常是这种情况），那么可接受性最大化问题通常是一个时间不一致的随机控制问题，类似于经典的均值-方差准则。为了克服这种形式的时间不一致，我们采用了最近在cite KovacovaRudloff2019中提出的集值Bellman原则，该原则适用于两个特定的动态CAI：动态风险调整资本回报率和动态损益率。通过数值算例说明了所得的理论结果，特别是中间平均风险有效边界的计算。

\[6\] How many people are infected A case study on SARS CoV 2 prevalence in Austria

标题 : 奥地利SARS-cov2流行的个案研究

作者 : Gabriel Ziegler , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.12020

说明 : -

Abstract : Using recent data from voluntary mass testing, I provide credible bounds on prevalence of SARS CoV 2 for Austrian counties in early December 2020. When estimating prevalence, a natural missing data problem arises no test results are generated for non tested people. In addition, tests are not perfectly predictive for the underlying infection. This is particularly relevant for mass SARS CoV 2 testing as these are conducted with rapid Antigen tests, which are known to be somewhat imprecise. Using insights from the literature on partial identification, I propose a framework addressing both issues at once. I use the framework to study differing selection assumptions for the Austrian data. Whereas weak monotone selection assumptions provide limited identification power, reasonably stronger assumptions reduce the uncertainty on prevalence significantly.

摘要 : 利用最近的自愿大规模检测数据，我提供了2020年12月初奥地利各县SARS-cov2流行率的可信界限。当估计患病率时，一个自然的数据缺失问题出现了，没有为未经测试的人生成测试结果。此外，检测并不能完全预测潜在感染。这与大规模SARS-CoV-2检测特别相关，因为这些检测是通过快速抗原检测进行的，而快速抗原检测已知有些不精确。利用文献中关于部分认同的见解，我提出了一个同时解决这两个问题的框架。我使用这个框架来研究奥地利数据的不同选择假设。弱单调选择假设提供了有限的识别能力，而相对较强的假设则显著降低了流行率的不确定性。

\[7\] Fines and progressive ideology promote social distancing

标题 : 罚款和进步的意识形态促进了社会的疏远

作者 : Edoardo Gallo, Darija Halatova, Alastair Langtry , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.12118

说明 : -

Abstract : Social distancing has been one of the core public policy measures used to mitigate the economic and health impacts of the COVID 19 pandemic. Such widespread adoption of social distancing measures is wholly unprecedented, and governments have implemented a variety of policies to encourage compliance. These typically rely on financial penalties (fines) and or informational messages (nudges). There is, however, a lack of evidence on the impact of these policies. We examine the effectiveness of fines and nudges in promoting social distancing in a web based interactive experiment. The study involves a nearrepresentative sample of 400 participants from the US population, and it was conducted in May 2020 at the height of the pandemic. Fines significantly promote distancing, but nudges only have a marginal impact. Political ideology also has a causal impact progressives are more likely to practice distancing, and are marginally more responsive to fines. Further, individuals do more social distancing when they know they may be a superspreader. Our results highlight the crucial role of web based interactive experiments in informing governments on the causal impact of policies at a time when lab and or field based experimental research is not feasible.

摘要 : 社会距离是用来减轻covid19大流行对经济和健康的影响的核心公共政策措施之一。如此广泛地采取社会距离措施是前所未有的，各国政府已实施各种政策鼓励遵守。这通常依赖于经济处罚（罚款）和/或信息消息（轻推）。然而，缺乏证据表明这些政策的影响。我们在一个基于网络的互动实验中检验了罚款和轻推在促进社会距离方面的有效性。这项研究是在2020年5月大流行最严重的时候进行的，研究对象是来自美国人口的400名参与者。罚款显著地促进了距离的疏远，但轻推的影响很小。政治意识形态也有因果关系的影响进步派更有可能实践疏远，并在边际上更响应罚款。此外，当个体知道自己可能是超级传播者时，他们会产生更多的社交距离。我们的研究结果强调了在实验室和/或实地实验研究不可行的情况下，基于网络的互动实验在向政府通报政策的因果影响方面的关键作用。

\[8\] How dark is the dark side of diversification

标题 : 多元化的黑暗面有多黑暗

作者 : Pedro Cadenas (1), Henryk Gzyl (2), Hyun Woong Park (1) ((1) Denison University, (2) IESA) , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.12154

说明 : Comments: The manuscript is currently under revision by the Journal of Risk Finance

Abstract : Against the widely held belief that diversification at banking institutions contributes to the stability of the financial system, Wagner (2010) found that diversification actually makes systemic crisis more likely. While it is true, as Wagner asserts, that the probability of joint default of the diversified portfolios is larger we contend that, as common practice, the effect of diversification is examined with respect to a risk measure like VaR. We find that when banks use VaR, diversification does reduce individual and systemic risk. This, in turn, generates a different set of incentives for banks and regulators.

摘要 : 人们普遍认为银行机构的多元化有助于金融体系的稳定，但瓦格纳（2010）发现，多元化实际上使系统性危机的可能性更大。诚如瓦格纳所言，多元化投资组合的联合违约概率更大，但我们认为，作为一种常见做法，分散投资的效果是根据风险值（VaR）这样的风险衡量指标来检验的。我们发现，当银行使用VaR时，多元化确实降低了个人和系统风险。这反过来又为银行和监管机构带来了一套不同的激励措施。

\[9\] Involuntary unemployment in overlapping generations model due to instability of the economy

标题 : 经济不稳定导致的代际非自愿失业模型

作者 : Yasuhito Tanaka , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.12199

说明 : -

Abstract : The existence of involuntary unemployment advocated by J. M. Keynes is a very important problem of the modern economic theory. Using a three generations overlapping generations model, we show that the existence of involuntary unemployment is due to the instability of the economy. Instability of the economy is the instability of the difference equation about the equilibrium price around the full employment equilibrium, which means that a fall in the nominal wage rate caused by the presence of involuntary unemployment further reduces employment. This instability is due to the negative real balance effect that occurs when consumers net savings (the difference between savings and pensions) are smaller than their debt multiplied by the marginal propensity to consume from childhood consumption.

摘要 : 凯恩斯主张的非自愿失业的存在是现代经济理论中一个非常重要的问题。利用三代重叠世代模型，我们证明非自愿失业的存在是由于经济的不稳定。经济不稳定性是指充分就业均衡点附近均衡价格的差分方程的不稳定性，这意味着非自愿失业的存在导致名义工资率的下降进一步减少了就业。这种不稳定性是由于当消费者净储蓄（储蓄和养老金之间的差额）小于他们的债务乘以儿童消费的边际消费倾向时，就会产生负的实际平衡效应。

V., Krutika Mishra, Srinidhi Murali, Aishwariya Saxena, Kasia Jakimowicz, Vivek Sharma, Rohan Iyer, Ashley Mehra, Alex Radunsky, Priyanshi Katiyar, Sunaina Anand, Shailesh Advani, Jagjit Dhaliwal, Ramesh Raskar , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.12263

说明 : Comments: 18 pages, 3 figures

Abstract : As several COVID 19 vaccine candidates approach approval for human use, governments around the world are preparing comprehensive standards for vaccine distribution and monitoring to avoid long term consequences that may result from rush to market. In this early draft article, we identify challenges for vaccine distribution in four core areas logistics, health outcomes, user centric impact, and communication. \[11\] On the Aggregation of Probability Assessments

标题 : 论概率评估的聚合

作者 : Francis X. Diebold, Minchul Shin, Boyuan Zhang , 类别 ：Econometrics (econ.EM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11649

说明 : -

Abstract : We propose methods for constructing regularized mixtures of density forecasts. We explore a variety of objectives and regularization penalties, and we use them in a substantive exploration of Eurozone inflation and real interest rate density forecasts. All individual inflation forecasters (even the ex post best forecaster) are outperformed by our regularized mixtures. The log scores of the Simplex and Best Average mixtures, for example, are approximately 7 better than that of the ex post best individual forecaster, and 15 better than that of the median forecaster. From the Great Recession onward, the optimal regularization tends to move density forecasts probability mass from the centers to the tails, correcting for overconfidence.
