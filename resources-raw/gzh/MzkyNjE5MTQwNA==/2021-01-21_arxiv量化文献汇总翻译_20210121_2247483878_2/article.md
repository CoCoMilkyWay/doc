\[1\] Modeling simultaneous supply and demand shocks in input output networks

标题 : 投入产出网络中供需同时冲击的建模

作者 : Anton Pichler, J. Doyne Farmer , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07818

说明 : -

Abstract : Natural and anthropogenic disasters frequently affect both the supply and demand side of an economy. A striking recent example is the Covid 19 pandemic which has created severe industry specific disruptions to economic output in most countries. Since firms are embedded in production networks, these direct shocks to supply and demand will propagate downstream and upstream. We show that existing input output models which allow for binding demand and supply constraints yield infeasible solutions when applied to pandemic shocks of three major European countries (Germany, Italy, Spain). We then introduce a mathematical optimization procedure which is able to determine best case feasible market allocations, giving a lower bound on total shock propagation. We find that even in this best case scenario network effects substantially amplify the initial shocks. To obtain more realistic model predictions, we study the propagation of shocks bottom up by imposing different rationing rules on firms if they are not able to satisfy incoming demand. Our results show that overall economic impacts depend strongly on the emergence of input bottlenecks, making the rationing assumption a key variable in predicting adverse economic impacts. We further establish that the magnitude of initial shocks and network density heavily influence model predictions.

摘要 : 自然灾害和人为灾害经常影响经济的供求双方。最近一个引人注目的例子是covid19大流行，它对大多数国家的经济产出造成了严重的行业性破坏。由于企业嵌入生产网络，这些对供需的直接冲击将向下游和上游传播。我们表明，现有的投入产出模型允许约束需求和供给约束，当应用于三个主要欧洲国家（德国、意大利、西班牙）的大流行冲击时，产生不可行的解决方案。然后，我们介绍了一个数学优化过程，能够确定最佳情况下可行的市场配置，给出了总冲击传播的下限。我们发现，即使在这种最好的情况下，网络效应也大大放大了初始冲击。为了得到更现实的模型预测，我们研究了冲击的传播自下而上通过施加不同的配给规则对企业，如果他们不能满足传入的需求。我们的结果表明，总体经济影响强烈依赖于投入瓶颈的出现，使得配给假设成为预测不利经济影响的关键变量。我们进一步证实了初始冲击的大小和网络密度对模型预测有很大的影响。

\[2\] Policy choices can help keep 4G and 5G universal broadband affordable

标题 : 政策选择有助于保持4G和5G通用宽带价格合理

作者 : Edward J Oughton, Niccolò Comini, Vivien Foster, Jim W Hall , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07820

说明 : -

Abstract : In recognition of the transformative opportunities that broadband connectivity presents, the United Nations Broadband Commission has committed the international community to accelerate universal access across the developing world. However, the cost of meeting this objective, and the feasibility of doing so on a commercially viable basis, are not well understood. This paper compares the global cost effectiveness of different infrastructure strategies for the developing world to achieve universal 4G or 5G mobile broadband. Utilizing remote sensing and geospatial infrastructure simulation, least cost network designs are developed for eight representative low and middle income countries (Malawi, Uganda, Kenya, Senegal, Pakistan, Albania, Peru and Mexico), the results from which form the basis for aggregation to the global level. To provide at least 2 Mbps per user, 4G is often the cheapest option, whereas a minimum 10 Mbps per user is cheapest with 5G non standalone (NSA). The cost of meeting the UN Broadband Commission target of a minimum 10 Mbps per user is estimated at USD 1.4 trillion using 5G NSA, equating to approximately 0.5 of annual GDP for the developing world over the next decade. However, by creating a favorable regulatory environment, governments can bring down these costs by as much as three quarters to USD 0.5 trillion (approximately 0.2 of annual GDP) and avoid the need for public subsidy. Providing governments make judicious choices, adopting fiscal and regulatory regimes conducive to lowering costs, broadband universal service may be within reach of most developing countries over the next decade.

摘要 : 联合国宽带委员会认识到宽带连接带来的变革性机遇，承诺国际社会加快在整个发展中世界实现普遍接入。然而，实现这一目标的成本以及在商业上可行的基础上实现这一目标的可行性尚不清楚。本文比较了发展中国家实现4G或5G移动宽带普及的不同基础设施战略的全球成本效益。利用遥感和地理空间基础设施模拟，为8个具有代表性的中低收入国家（马拉维、乌干达、肯尼亚、塞内加尔、巴基斯坦、阿尔巴尼亚、秘鲁和墨西哥）制定了成本最低的网络设计，其结果构成了向全球一级汇总的基础。要为每个用户提供至少2 Mbps的带宽，4G通常是最便宜的选择，而5G非独立网络（NSA）为每个用户提供至少10 Mbps的带宽是最便宜的。使用5G NSA，实现联合国宽带委员会（UN Broadband Commission）每个用户最低10 Mbps的目标的成本估计为1.4万亿美元，相当于未来十年发展中国家年GDP的约0.5%。然而，通过创造一个有利的监管环境，政府可以将这些成本降低四分之三，达到0.5万亿美元（约占年度国内生产总值的0.2%），并避免需要公共补贴。如果各国政府做出明智的选择，采取有利于降低成本的财政和监管制度，宽带普遍服务在未来十年可能是大多数发展中国家都能实现的。

\[3\] Willingness to Pay and Attitudinal Preferences of Indian Consumers for Electric Vehicles

标题 : 印度消费者对电动汽车的支付意愿和态度偏好

作者 : Prateek Bansal, Rajeev Ranjan Kumar, Alok Raj, Subodh Dubey, Daniel J. Graham , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08008

说明 : -

Abstract : Consumer preference elicitation is critical to devise effective policies for the diffusion of electric vehicles in India. For this purpose, we use stated preferences of over 1000 Indian consumers and present the first estimates of the additional purchase price that Indian consumers are willing to pay to improve the electric vehicle attributes (e.g., driving range). We adopt a hybrid choice modelling framework to simultaneously model the willingness to pay and the effect of consumers latent attitudes (e.g., hedonic and symbolic values) on their likelihood to adopt electric vehicles. We also account for reference dependence by considering a conventional vehicle as the reference alternative and illustrate that the resulting willingness to pay estimates are more realistic. Based on our results, Indian consumers are willing to pay additional USD 10 34 in purchase price to reduce the fast charging time by 1 minute, USD 7 40 to add a kilometre to the driving range of electric vehicle at 200 kilometres, and USD 104 692 to save USD 1 per 100 kilometres in operating cost. These estimates and the effect of attitudes on the likelihood to adopt electric vehicles provide insights about electric vehicle design, marketing strategies, and pro electric vehicle policies (e.g., specialised lanes and reserved parking for electric vehicles) to expedite the adoption of electric vehicles in India.

摘要 : 消费者偏好诱导对于制定有效的政策在印度推广电动汽车至关重要。为此，我们使用了超过1000名印度消费者的指定偏好，并给出了印度消费者愿意为改善电动汽车特性（例如，行驶里程）而支付的额外购买价格的初步估计。我们采用混合选择模型框架来同时模拟消费者的支付意愿和潜在态度（如享乐和象征价值）对他们采用电动汽车可能性的影响。我们还考虑了参考依赖性，将传统车辆作为参考替代方案，并说明由此产生的支付意愿估计更为现实。根据我们的结果，印度消费者愿意支付额外的购买价格10 34美元，以减少1分钟的快速充电时间，7 40美元，以增加200公里的电动汽车行驶里程，104 692美元，以节省每100公里1美元的运营成本。这些估计和态度对采用电动汽车可能性的影响提供了有关电动汽车设计、营销策略和支持电动汽车的政策（例如，电动汽车专用车道和预留停车场）的见解，以加速印度采用电动汽车。

\[4\] The Log Moment formula for implied volatility

标题 : 隐含波动率的对数矩公式

作者 : Vimal Raval, Antoine Jacquier , 类别 ：Pricing of Securities (q-fin.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08145

说明 : Comments: 12 pages

Abstract : We revisit the foundational Moment Formula proved by Roger Lee fifteen years ago. We show that when the underlying stock price martingale admits finite log moments E log(S) q for some positive q, the arbitrage free growth in the left wing of the implied volatility smile is less constrained than Lee s bound. The result is rationalised by a market trading discretely monitored variance swaps wherein the payoff is a function of squared log returns, and requires no assumption for the underlying martingale to admit any negative moment. In this respect, the result can derived from a model independent setup. As a byproduct, we relax the moment assumptions on the stock price to provide a new proof of the notorious Gatheral Fukasawa formula expressing variance swaps in terms of the implied volatility.

摘要 : 我们重温罗杰李15年前证明的基本矩公式。我们证明了当标的股票价格鞅对某个正q允许有限对数矩E log（S）q时，隐含波动率微笑左翼的无套利增长比Lee界的约束要小。结果是合理的市场交易离散监测方差互换，其中的回报是一个函数的平方对数回报，并没有必要假设潜在的鞅承认任何负时刻。在这方面，可以从独立于模型的设置中得出结果。作为一个副产品，我们放宽了对股票价格的矩假设，以提供一个新的证据，臭名昭著的Gatherel Fukasawa公式表示方差互换的隐含波动率。

\[5\] On Monte Carlo methods in convex stochastic optimization

标题 : 凸随机优化中的montecarlo方法

作者 : Daniel Bartl, Shahar Mendelson , 类别 ：Statistics Theory (math.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07794

说明 : Comments: Comments are welcome

Abstract : We develop a novel procedure for estimating the optimizer of general convex stochastic optimization problems of the form min x in mathcal X mathbf E F(x, xi) , when the given data is a finite independent sample selected according to xi . The procedure is based on a median of means tournament, and is the first procedure that exhibits the optimal statistical performance in heavy tailed situations we recover the asymptotic rates dictated by the central limit theorem in a non asymptotic manner once the sample size exceeds some explicitly computable threshold. Additionally, our results apply in the high dimensional setup, as the threshold sample size exhibits the optimal dependence on the dimension (up to a logarithmic factor). The general setting allows us to recover recent results on multivariate mean estimation and linear regression in heavy tailed situations and to prove the first sharp, non asymptotic results for the portfolio optimization problem.

摘要 : 在给定的数据是根据席席选取的有限独立样本的基础上，我们发展了一种估计MathCar x MathBF E F（x，Xi）中Min x形式的一般凸随机优化问题的优化器的新方法。该方法是基于均值竞赛的中值，是第一个在重尾情形下表现出最优统计性能的方法。一旦样本量超过某个可明确计算的阈值，我们就以非渐近的方式恢复由中心极限定理规定的渐近速率。此外，我们的结果适用于高维设置，因为阈值样本大小表现出对维度的最佳依赖性（高达对数因子）。一般设置允许我们恢复最近的结果多元平均估计和线性回归重尾的情况下，并证明了第一个尖锐的，非渐进的结果为投资组合优化问题。

\[6\] One dimensional game theoretic differential equations

标题 : 一维博弈论微分方程

作者 : Rafał M. Łochowski, Nicolas Perkowski, David J. Prömel , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08041

说明 : -

Abstract : We provide a very brief introduction to typical paths and the corresponding It o type integration. Relying on this robust It o integration, we prove an existence and uniqueness result for one dimensional differential equations driven by typical paths with non Lipschitz continuous coefficients in the spirit of Yamada Watanabe as well as an approximation result in the spirit of Doss Sussmann.

摘要 : 我们提供了一个非常简单的介绍典型的路径和相应的It-o型集成。基于这种稳健的It-o积分，我们以Yamada-Watanabe的精神证明了由非Lipschitz连续系数的典型路径驱动的一维微分方程的一个存在唯一性结果，并以Doss-Sussmann的精神证明了一个近似结果。

\[7\] Neural networks based algorithms for stochastic control and PDEs in finance

标题 : 金融随机控制与偏微分方程的神经网络算法

作者 : Maximilien Germain (LPSM, EDF R&D), Huyên Pham (LPSM, FiME Lab), Xavier Warin (EDF R&D, FiME Lab) , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08068

说明 : Comments: arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:2006.01496

Abstract : This paper presents machine learning techniques and deep reinforcement learningbased algorithms for the efficient resolution of nonlinear partial differential equations and dynamic optimization problems arising in investment decisions and derivative pricing in financial engineering. We survey recent results in the literature, present new developments, notably in the fully nonlinear case, and compare the different schemes illustrated by numerical tests on various financial applications. We conclude by highlighting some future research directions.

摘要 : 本文提出了基于机器学习技术和深度强化学习算法的金融工程投资决策和衍生品定价中非线性偏微分方程和动态优化问题的有效求解方法。我们回顾了文献中的最新结果，介绍了最新进展，特别是在完全非线性的情况下，并比较了各种金融应用的数值试验所说明的不同格式。最后，我们强调了一些未来的研究方向。
