\[1\] AHEAD: Ad Hoc Electronic Auction Design

标题:未来：特设电子拍卖设计

作者:Joffrey Derchu(Ecole Polytechnique, Palaiseau - CMAP CNRS-UMR 7641 and Ecole Polytechnique),Philippe Guillot(affiliation not provided to SSRN),Thibaut Mastrolia(Ecole Polytechnique, Palaiseau - CMAP CNRS-UMR 7641 and Ecole Polytechnique),Mathieu Rosenbaum(Ecole Polytechnique, Palaiseau)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3705514

Abstract : We introduce a new matching design for ﬁnancial transactions in an electronic market. In this mechanism, called ad hoc electronic auction design (AHEAD), market participants can trade between themselves at a ﬁxed price and trigger an auction when they are no longer satisﬁed with this ﬁxed price. In this context, we prove that a Nash equilibrium is obtained between market participants. Furthermore, we are able to assess quantitatively the relevance of ad hoc auctions and to compare them with periodic auctions and continuous limit order books. We show that from the investors’ viewpoint, the microstructure of the asset is usually signiﬁcantly improved when using AHEAD.

Keywords : Market microstructure, market design, ﬁnancial regulation, ad-hoc auctions, periodic auctions, limit order book, Nash equilibrium

Abstract :我们为电子市场中的金融交易引入了一种新的匹配设计。在这一机制中，称为特设电子拍卖设计（ADEAD），市场参与者可以在他们之间以固定价格进行交易，并在他们不再满意这个固定价格时触发拍卖。在此背景下，我们证明了市场参与者之间的纳什均衡。此外，我们能够定量评估特别拍卖的相关性，并将其与定期拍卖和连续限价订单簿进行比较。我们表明，从投资者的角度来看，提前使用时，资产的微观结构通常会显著改善。

Keywords :市场微观结构、市场设计、金融监管、临时拍卖、定期拍卖、限价指令簿、纳什均衡

\[2\] Stock Return Predictability and Cyclical Movements in Valuation Ratios

标题:股票收益的可预测性与估值比率的周期性变动

作者:Deshui Yu(College of Finance and Statistics, Hunan University),Difang Huang(Monash University),Li Chen(WISE and School of Economics, Xiamen University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3755710

Abstract : According to present-value models, a financial valuation ratio should predict future stock returns or cash flows but empirically shows little power. This paper develops insights about stock return predictability and reconciles the contradicting findings. We decompose a financial ratio into (1) a slow-moving component which reflects the local time-varying expected values of the financial ratio as the results of persistent shocks, and (2) a cyclical component which reflects a more rapid mean-reversion of the financial ratio toward the local time-varying mean. The cyclical component shows statistically significant power for predicting future stock returns, both in-sample and out-of-sample, and the predictability is also economically significant. Conversely, the slow-moving component fails to predict returns and therefore disguises the predictive information contained in the dividend-price ratio for future returns. To ascertain our findings, we provide a direct line linking our decomposition approach to the present-value framework and the predictive regression system.

Keywords : Stock return predictability, slow-moving and cyclical movements, present-value models, nonparametric decomposition

Abstract :一个股票价值比或现金流量比应该根据经验来预测未来。本文发展了对股票收益可预测性的见解，并调和了相互矛盾的发现。我们将金融比率分解为（1）反映金融比率局部时变预期值（作为持续冲击的结果）的缓慢移动成分，以及（2）反映金融比率向局部时变平均值更快速的均值回归的周期性成分。无论样本内还是样本外，周期性成分在预测未来股票收益方面都显示出显著的统计能力，而且其可预测性在经济上也是显著的。相反，慢动成分无法预测收益，因此掩盖了股息价格比率中包含的对未来收益的预测信息。为了确定我们的发现，我们提供了一条将我们的分解方法与现值框架和预测回归系统相联系的直线。

Keywords :股票收益的可预测性，缓慢移动和周期性运动，现值模型，非参数分解

\[3\] Time-Series Heston Model Calibration Using a Trinomial Tree

标题:基于三项式树的时间序列Heston模型校正

作者:Michael A. Clayton(Michael A. Clayton Consulting, Inc.)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3718697

Abstract : In this work a trinomial tree representing the Heston model variance process is used to estimate the parameters for the Heston stochastic volatility model using historical daily observations of the asset.The results include estimates for all Heston model parameters as well as an estimated most likely path for the latent variance process.The variance tree is constructed using a somewhat novel approach that uses a non-uniform, recombining grid, and some analysis is included to justify the approach. The probability of the observed asset path is computed as an average over all variance paths using this tree, with the asset return observations approximated as normal conditional on the values of the variance at the start and end of each observation.Calibration is achieved using a reasonably generic multidimensional optimization algorithm, with the negative of the logarithm of the asset path probability computed using the trinomial tree as the objective function.Bounds are imposed on all parameters based on the ability of the tree to estimate the parameters accurately, in particular noting that with increasing the volatility of variance the grid becomes coarser, and there is therefore a limit to how large this parameter can be in order for the tree to accurately resolve the variance distribution. Reasonable convergence with increasing number of asset return observations is demonstrated for all parameters as well as the latent variance estimation, using paths simulated from the Heston model.Results from the calibration to four foreign exchange processes are also provided, showing that the results are reasonably stable.

Keywords : Heston Model, Stochastic Volatility, Parameter estimation, Latent variables, Filtering, Trinomial tree

Abstract :在这项工作中，一个代表赫斯顿模型方差过程的三项式树被用来估计赫斯顿随机波动率模型的参数，该模型使用历史每日观测数据资产结果包括所有赫斯顿模型参数的估计以及潜在方差的估计最可能路径过程方差树是用一种新的方法构造的，这种方法使用了一种非均匀的重组网格，并进行了一些分析来证明这种方法的正确性。观察到的资产路径的概率通过使用此树作为所有方差路径的平均值来计算，资产回报观察值根据每个路径开始和结束时的方差值近似为正态条件观察、校准使用合理的通用多维优化算法，以三项式树计算的资产路径概率的对数为负为目标函数.界限基于树准确估计参数的能力对所有参数施加，特别注意，随着方差波动性的增加，网格变得更粗糙，因此，为了使树能够准确地解析方差分布，这个参数的大小是有限制的。利用Heston模型模拟的路径，证明了所有参数和潜在方差估计在资产收益率观测数增加时的合理收敛性模型.结果从校准到四个外汇过程也提供了，表明结果是相当稳定的。

Keywords :赫斯顿模型，随机波动率，参数估计，潜变量，滤波，三项式树

\[4\] Is There a Value Premium in Cryptoasset Markets?

标题:加密资产市场是否存在价值溢价？

作者:Luca Liebi(University of St. Gallen - Swiss Institute of Banking and Finance)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3718684

Abstract : Investigating a collection of 652 cryptoassets, I find that cryptoasset returns increase with increasing active addresses to network value ratio. Cryptoassets with a high active address to network value ratio yield on average 2.1 percentage points higher weekly returns compared to cryptoassets with low active addresses to network value ratio, and comparable size. Fama- Macbeth regressions indicate that the active addresses to network value ratio, combined with size, and momentum capture the cross-sectional variation of cryptoasset returns. Adding the value factor to existing factor models helps to explain average cryptoasset returns.

Abstract :通过对652个cryptoasset集合的调查，我发现cryptoasset的回报率随着活动地址与网络值比率的增加而增加。与活动地址与网络价值比率低、规模相当的加密资产相比，具有高活动地址与网络价值比率的加密资产的周回报率平均高出2.1个百分点。Fama-Macbeth回归表明，活跃地址与网络价值之比，结合大小和动量，可以捕捉到加密资产回报的横截面变化。将价值因子添加到现有因子模型中有助于解释加密资产的平均回报。

作者:Guglielmo Maria Caporale(Brunel University London - Department of Economics and Finance; London South Bank University; CESifo (Center for Economic Studies and Ifo Institute); German Institute for Economic Research (DIW Berlin)),Alex Plastun(Sumy State University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3754674

Abstract : This paper investigates the impact of abnormal returns on stock prices by using daily and hourly data for some developed (US, UK, Japan) and emerging (China, India) markets over the period 01.01.2010-01.01.2020. Average analysis, t-tests, CAR and trading simulation methods are used to test the following hypotheses: H1) abnormal returns can be detected before the end of the day; H2) there are price effects on the day after abnormal returns occur; H3) these effects are different for developed vis-Ã -vis emerging markets; H4) they can be used to generate profits from intraday trading. \[8\] Extreme Daily Returns, Lottery Mindset, Idiosyncratic Volatility and the Cross-Section of Stock Returns in a Comparatively Small Emerging Market

标题:在一个相对较小的新兴市场中，极端日收益率、彩票心态、特殊波动性和股票收益的横截面

作者:Muhammad Usman Khurram(College of economics, Zhejiang university),Fahad Ali(Zhejiang University - College of Economics),Yuexiang Jiang(Zhejiang University - College of Economics)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3718565

Abstract : The existing literature on developed and advanced emerging markets documents that the expected stock returns exhibit a positive-, negative-, and no-relationship with both idiosyncratic volatility (IVOL) and extreme daily returns (MAX or MIN). Different from developed and advanced emerging markets, the Pakistani market (PSX) is at its initial development stage with a comparatively little investment knowledge and scarcity of funds that may hinder to achieve a well-diversified portfolio. Such investment conditions may lead investors to suffer from under-diversification and behavioral biases, and therefore, provide an ideal situation to examine the IVOL, MAX and MIN effects and the relationship among these variables in the Pakistani stock market. We find a robust negative MAX effect, which is not subsumed by IVOL, MIN, and other control variables. Whereas, IVOL and MIN effects are weak and unreliable. The negative MAX-return relationship and positive MIN-return relationship indicate both preference for stocks with lottery-type features and risk-seeking behavior among the Pakistani investors. The results are robust to controls for various firm specific characteristics.

Keywords : Extreme daily returns; idiosyncratic volatility; MAX and MIN effects; gambling behavior; lottery stocks in emerging markets

\[10\] Risk-Adjusted Capital Allocation and Misallocation

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3753061

Abstract : We develop a theory linking “misallocation,” i.e., dispersion in marginal products of capital (MPK), to macroeconomic risk. Keywords :配置不当、生产力、商业周期成本、风险溢价

\[11\] Speculation and Abnormal Returns in Energy, Agriculture, and Precious Metals Futures During the COVID-19 Pandemic

标题:COVID-19流感大流行期间能源、农业和贵金属期货的投机和异常回报

作者:Imtiaz Sifat(Monash University),Abdul Ghafoor(Monash University),Abdollah Ah Mand(Sunway University Business School )

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3754672

Abstract : We report new evidence that speculation in energy and precious metal futures are more prevalent in crisis periods and even more so during the COVID-19 pandemic. 作者:Hillary Ezeaku(Caritas University - Department of Banking and Finance),Simplice Asongu(African Governance and Development Institute),Joseph Nnanna(Development Bank of Nigeria)

Abstract : This study examines the effects of oil supply and global demand shocks on the volatility of commodity prices in the metal and agricultural commodity markets using the SVAR model. The empirical evidence is based on real time daily closing international commodity prices covering the period 2 December 2019 to 1 October 2020. The findings are presented in cumulative impulse responses and variance decompositions. The former is utilized to examine the accumulated influence of structural shocks on the volatility of agricultural and metal commodities whereas the latter reflect the share of variation in the volatility of each commodity arising from each structural shock. Various patterns are provided on how metal and agricultural commodity prices have been influenced by the COVID-19 pandemic. Policy implications are discussed.
