\[1\] Evidence and Behaviour of Support and Resistance Levels in Financial Time Series

标题 : 金融时间序列中支持与阻力水平的证据与行为

作者 : Ken Chung, Anthony Bellotti , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07410

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper investigates the phenomenon of support and resistance levels (SR levels) in financial time series, which act as temporary price barriers that reverses price trends. We develop a heuristic discovery algorithm for this purpose, to discover and evaluate SR levels for intraday price series. Our simple approach discovers SR levels which are able to reverse price trends statistically significantly. Asset price entering SR levels with higher number of price bounces before are more likely to bounce on such SR levels again. We also show that the decay aspect of the discovered SR levels as decreasing probability of price bounce over time. We conclude SR levels are features in financial time series are not explained simply by AR(1) processes, stationary or otherwise and that they contribute to the temporary predictability and stationarity of the investigated price series.

摘要 : 本文研究了金融时间序列中的支持与阻力水平（SR水平）现象，SR水平是一种临时性的价格壁垒，可以逆转价格走势。为此，我们开发了一种启发式发现算法，用于发现和评估日内价格序列的SR水平。我们的简单方法发现SR水平能够显著逆转价格趋势。资产价格进入SR水平时，如果之前价格反弹次数较多，则在此类SR水平上再次反弹的可能性较大。我们还表明，随着时间的推移，发现的锶水平的衰减方面随着价格反弹的概率下降。我们得出结论，SR水平是金融时间序列中的特征，不能简单地用AR（1）过程（平稳或其他）来解释，它们有助于所研究价格序列的暂时可预测性和平稳性。

\[2\] The Elephant in the Room

标题 : 房间里的大象

作者 : Chirag Dhara, Vandana Singh , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07467

说明 : Comments: To be published in: Iyengar, R. and Kwauk, C. (Eds.), "Charting an SDG 4.7 roadmap for radical, transformative change in the midst of climate breakdown". Brill Publishers

Abstract : A transformative approach to addressing complex social environmental problems warrants reexamining our most fundamental assumptions about sustainability and progress, including the entrenched imperative for limitless economic growth. Our global resource footprint has grown in lock step with GDP since the industrial revolution, spawning the climate and ecological crises. Faith that technology will eventually decouple resource use from GDP growth is pervasive, despite there being practically no empirical evidence of decoupling in any country. We argue that complete long term decoupling is, in fact, well nigh impossible for fundamental physical, mathematical, logical, pragmatic and behavioural reasons. We suggest that a crucial first step toward a transformative education is to acknowledge this incompatibility, and provide examples of where and how our arguments may be incorporated in education. More broadly, we propose that foregrounding SDG 12 with a functional definition of sustainability, and educating and upskilling students to this end, must be a necessary minimum goal of any transformative approach to sustainability education. Our aim is to provide a conceptual scaffolding around which learning frameworks may be developed to make room for diverse alternative paths to truly sustainable social ecological cultures.

摘要 : 解决复杂社会环境问题的变革性方法需要重新审视我们关于可持续性和进步的最基本假设，包括无限经济增长的根深蒂固的必要性。自工业革命以来，我们的全球资源足迹与GDP同步增长，引发了气候和生态危机。人们普遍相信，技术最终将使资源利用与GDP增长脱钩，尽管几乎没有任何国家的经验证据表明这种脱钩。我们认为，完全的长期脱钩，事实上，几乎是不可能的基本物理，数学，逻辑，实用和行为的原因。我们建议，走向变革教育的关键第一步是承认这种不相容性，并举例说明我们的论点在哪里以及如何融入教育。更广泛地说，我们建议，将可持续发展目标12与可持续发展的功能定义相结合，并为此目的教育和提高学生的技能，必须是任何可持续发展教育变革方法的最低目标。我们的目标是提供一个概念性的脚手架，围绕这个脚手架，学习框架可以被开发出来，为通向真正可持续的社会生态文化的各种不同的替代路径腾出空间。

\[3\] The fiscal response to revenue shocks

标题 : 财政对收入冲击的反应

作者 : Simon Berset, Martin Huber, Mark Schelker , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07661

说明 : -

Abstract : We study the impact of fiscal revenue shocks on local fiscal policy. We focus on the very volatile revenues from the immovable property gains tax in the canton of Zurich, Switzerland, and analyze fiscal behavior following large and rare positive and negative revenue shocks. We apply causal machine learning strategies and implement the post double selection LASSO estimator to identify the causal effect of revenue shocks on public finances. We show that local policymakers overall predominantly smooth fiscal shocks. However, we also find some patterns consistent with fiscal conservatism, where positive shocks are smoothed, while negative ones are mitigated by spending cuts.

摘要 : 研究财政收入冲击对地方财政政策的影响。我们关注瑞士苏黎世州不动产利得税带来的非常不稳定的收入，并分析巨大且罕见的积极和消极收入冲击后的财政行为。我们应用因果机器学习策略，并实施后双选择套索估计，以确定财政收入冲击对公共财政的因果影响。我们表明，地方政策制定者总体上以平稳财政冲击为主。然而，我们也发现了一些与财政保守主义相一致的模式，即积极的冲击被平滑，而消极的冲击则被削减开支所缓解。

\[4\] Twitter Subjective Well Being Indicator During COVID 19 Pandemic

标题 : 冠状病毒19型流行期间的主观幸福感指标

作者 : Tiziana Carpi, Airo Hino, Stefano Maria Iacus, Giuseppe Porro , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07695

说明 : -

Abstract : This study analyzes the impact of the COVID 19 pandemic on the subjective well being as measured through Twitter data indicators for Japan and Italy. It turns out that, overall, the subjective well being dropped by 11.7 for Italy and 8.3 for Japan in the first nine months of 2020 compared to the last two months of 2019 and even more compared to the historical mean of the indexes. Through a data science approach we try to identify the possible causes of this drop down by considering several explanatory variables including, climate and air quality data, number of COVID 19 cases and deaths, Facebook Covid and flu symptoms global survey, Google Trends data and coronavirus related searches, Google mobility data, policy intervention measures, economic variables and their Google Trends proxies, as well as health and stress proxy variables based on big data. We show that a simple static regression model is not able to capture the complexity of well being and therefore we propose a dynamic elastic net approach to show how different group of factors may impact the well being in different periods, even over a short time length, and showing further country specific aspects. Finally, a structural equation modeling analysis tries to address the causal relationships among the COVID 19 factors and subjective well being showing that, overall, prolonged mobility restrictions,flu and Covid like symptoms, economic uncertainty, social distancing and news about the pandemic have negative effects on the subjective well being.

摘要 : 本研究通过日本和意大利的Twitter数据指标，分析了covid19大流行对主观幸福感的影响。结果表明，总体而言，与2019年最后两个月相比，2020年前9个月，意大利和日本的主观幸福感分别下降了11.7和8.3，与这些指数的历史平均值相比，下降幅度更大。通过数据科学的方法，我们试图通过考虑几个解释变量来确定这种下降的可能原因，包括气候和空气质量数据、COVID 19例病例和死亡人数、Facebook COVID和流感症状全球调查、谷歌趋势数据和冠状病毒相关搜索、谷歌移动数据，政策干预措施、经济变量及其谷歌趋势代理，以及基于大数据的健康和压力代理变量。我们发现，一个简单的静态回归模型无法捕捉幸福感的复杂性，因此我们提出了一个动态弹性网络方法来显示不同的因素组如何影响不同时期的幸福感，即使在很短的时间长度内，并进一步显示特定国家的方面。最后，一个结构方程模型分析试图解决COVID 19因素与主观幸福感之间的因果关系，结果表明，总体而言，长期的流动限制、流感和COVID样症状、经济不确定性、社会距离和有关流感大流行的新闻对主观幸福感有负面影响。

\[5\] Diagnosis of systemic risk and contagion across financial sectors

标题 : 系统性风险和金融部门传染的诊断

作者 : Sayuj Choudhari, Richard Licheng Zhu , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06585

说明 : Comments: 21 pages, 3 figures

Abstract : In normal times, it is assumed that financial institutions operating in non overlapping sectors have complementary and distinct outcomes, typically reflected in mostly uncorrelated outcomes and asset returns. Such is the reasoning behind common free lunches to be had in investing, like diversifying assets across equity and bond sectors. Unfortunately, the recurrence of crises like the Great Financial Crisis of 2007 2008 demonstrate that such convenient assumptions often break down, with dramatic consequences for all financial actors. In hindsight, the emergence of systemic risk (as exemplified by failure in one part of a system spreading to ostensibly unrelated parts of the system) has been explained by narratives such as deregulation and leverage. But can we diagnose and quantify the ongoing emergence of systemic risk in financial systems In this study, we focus on two previously documented measures of systemic risk that require only easily available time series data (eg monthly asset returns) cross correlation and principal component analysis. We apply these tests to daily and monthly returns on hedge fund indexes and broad based market indexes, and discuss their results. We hope that a frank discussion of these simple, non parametric measures can help inform legislators, lawmakers, and financial actors of potential crises looming on the horizon.

摘要 : 在正常情况下，假设在非重叠部门运营的金融机构具有互补和不同的结果，通常反映在大部分不相关的结果和资产回报中。这就是投资中常见的免费午餐背后的原因，比如在股票和债券行业实现资产多元化。不幸的是，像2007年至2008年的大金融危机这样的危机一再发生，表明这种便利的假设经常被打破，给所有金融行为体带来了戏剧性的后果。事后看来，系统性风险的出现（例如，系统某个部分的失败蔓延到系统表面上不相关的部分）已被放松管制和杠杆等叙述所解释。但是，我们能否诊断和量化金融系统中正在出现的系统性风险？在本研究中，我们将重点放在两个以前记录的系统性风险度量上，这两个度量只需要简单可用的时间序列数据（如每月资产收益率）互相关和主成分分析。我们将这些测试应用于对冲基金指数和广义市场指数的日收益率和月收益率，并讨论其结果。我们希望，坦率地讨论这些简单的、非参数化的措施，有助于向立法者、立法者和金融机构通报即将出现的潜在危机。

\[7\] A Two Population Mortality Model to Assess Longevity Basis Risk

标题 : 评估长寿风险的两种群死亡率模型

作者 : Selin Özen, Şule Şahin , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06690

说明 : -

Abstract : Index based hedging solutions are used to transfer the longevity risk to the capital markets. However, mismatches between the liability of the hedger and the hedging instrument cause longevity basis risk. Therefore, an appropriate two population model to measure and assess the longevity basis risk is required. In this paper, we aim to construct a two population mortality model to provide an effective hedge against the longevity basis risk. The reference population is modelled by using the Lee Carter model with the renewal process and exponential jumps proposed by Ozen and c S ahin (2020) and the dynamics of the book population are specified. The analysis based on the UK mortality data indicates that the proposed model for the reference population and the common age effect model for the book population provide a better fit compared to the other models considered in the paper. Different two population models are used to investigate the impact of the sampling risk on the index based hedge as well as to analyse the risk reduction regarding hedge effectiveness. The results show that the proposed model provides a significant risk reduction when mortality jumps and the sampling risk are taken into account.

摘要 : 基于指数的对冲解决方案用于将长寿风险转移到资本市场。然而，套期保值者的负债与套期工具之间的不匹配会导致长期基准风险。因此，需要一个合适的两种群模型来衡量和评估长寿基准风险。本文的目的是建立一个两种群死亡率模型，以有效地规避寿命基准风险。利用Lee-Carter模型对参考种群进行建模，采用Ozen和csahin（2020）提出的更新过程和指数跳跃，并对图书种群的动态进行了描述。基于英国死亡率数据的分析表明，与本文考虑的其他模型相比，所提出的参考人口模型和图书人口的共同年龄效应模型提供了更好的拟合。采用两种不同的总体模型研究了抽样风险对指数型套期保值的影响，并分析了风险降低对套期保值有效性的影响。结果表明，在考虑死亡率跳变和抽样风险的情况下，该模型能显著降低风险。

\[8\] Towards a more sustainable academic publishing system

标题 : 走向更可持续的学术出版体系

作者 : Mohsen Kayal, Jane Ballard, Ehsan Kayal , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

说明 : Comments: 15 pages including 1 figure

Abstract : Communicating new scientific discoveries is key to human progress. Yet, this endeavor is hindered by monetary restrictions for publishing one s findings and accessing other scientists reports. This process is further exacerbated by a large portion of publishing media owned by private, for profit companies that do not reinject academic publishing benefits into the scientific community, in contrast with journals from scientific societies. \[9\] Dynamic industry uncertainty networks and the business cycle

标题 : 动态产业不确定性网络与经济周期

作者 : Jozef Barunik, Mattia Bevilacqua, Robert Faff , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06957

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper introduces new forward looking uncertainty network measures built from the main US industries. We argue that this network structure extracted from options investors expectations is meaningfully dynamic and contains valuable information relevant for business cycles. Classifying industries according to their contribution to system related uncertainty across business cycles, we uncover an uncertainty hub role for the communications, industrials and information technology sectors, while shocks to materials, real estate and utilities do not propagate strongly across the network. We find that a dynamic ex ante network of uncertainty is a useful predictor of business cycles especially when it is based on uncertainty hubs. The uncertainty network is found to behave counter cyclically since a tighter network of industry uncertainty tends to associate with future business cycle contractions.

摘要 : 本文介绍了美国主要工业建立的新的前瞻性不确定性网络措施。我们认为，这种从期权投资者预期中提取的网络结构是有意义的动态的，包含了与商业周期相关的有价值的信息。根据各行业在整个商业周期中对系统相关不确定性的贡献对其进行分类，我们发现了通信、工业和信息技术行业的不确定性枢纽作用，而对材料、房地产和公用事业的冲击不会在整个网络中强烈传播。我们发现，一个动态的事前不确定性网络是一个有用的预测商业周期，特别是当它是基于不确定性的枢纽。不确定性网络表现出反周期性，因为更紧密的行业不确定性网络往往与未来的商业周期收缩相关联。

\[10\] Beating the Market with Generalized Generating Portfolios

标题 : 用广义生成投资组合击败市场

作者 : Patrick Mijatovic , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07084

说明 : -

Abstract : Stochastic portfolio theory aims at finding relative arbitrages, i.e. trading strategies which outperform the market with probability one. Functionally generated portfolios, which are deterministic functions of the market weights, are an invaluable tool in doing so. Driven by a practitioner point of view, where investment decisions are based upon consideration of various financial variables, we generalize functionally generated portfolios and allow them to depend on continuous path semimartingales, in addition to the market weights. By means of examples we demonstrate how the inclusion of additional processes can reduce time horizons beyond which relative arbitrage is possible, boost performance of generated portfolios, and how investor preferences and specific investment views can be included in the context of stochastic portfolio theory.
