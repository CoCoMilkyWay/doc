\[2\] Engines of Power

标题 : 动力发动机

作者 : Jeffrey Ding, Allan Dafoe , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.04338

说明 : -

Abstract : Major theories of military innovation focus on relatively narrow technological developments, such as nuclear weapons or aircraft carriers. Arguably the most profound military implications of technological change, however, come from more fundamental advances arising from general purpose technologies, such as the steam engine, electricity, and the computer. With few exceptions, political scientists have not theorized about GPTs. Drawing from the economics literature on GPTs, we distill several propositions on how and when GPTs affect military affairs. We call these effects general purpose military transformations. In particular, we argue that the impacts of GMTs on military effectiveness are broad, delayed, and shaped by indirect productivity spillovers. Additionally, GMTs differentially advantage those militaries that can draw from a robust industrial base in the GPT. To illustrate the explanatory value of our theory, we conduct a case study of the military consequences of electricity, the prototypical GPT. Finally, we apply our findings to artificial intelligence, which will plausibly cause a profound general purpose military transformation.

摘要 : 军事创新的主要理论集中在相对狭窄的技术发展上，如核武器或航空母舰。然而，可以说，技术变革最深刻的军事影响来自于通用技术的更根本的进步，如蒸汽机、电力和计算机。除了少数例外，政治学家还没有对gpt进行理论分析。从经济学文献中，我们提炼出了关于GPT如何以及何时影响军事事务的几个命题。我们称之为通用军事变革。特别是，我们认为，转基因技术对军事效能的影响是广泛的、滞后的，并且是由间接的生产力溢出形成的。此外，GMT对那些能够从GPT强大的工业基础中获益的军队有着不同的优势。为了说明我们的理论的解释价值，我们进行了一个案例研究的军事后果电力，原型GPT。最后，我们将我们的发现应用于人工智能，这似乎会引起一场深刻的通用军事变革。

\[5\] Deep Learning Statistical Arbitrage

标题 : 深度学习统计套利

作者 : Jorge Guijarro-Ordonez, Markus Pelger, Greg Zanotti , 类别 ：Machine Learning (cs.LG)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.04028

说明 : -

Abstract : Statistical arbitrage identifies and exploits temporal price differences between similar assets. We propose a unifying conceptual framework for statistical arbitrage and develop a novel deep learning solution, which finds commonality and time series patterns from large panels in a data driven and flexible way. First, we construct arbitrage portfolios of similar assets as residual portfolios from conditional latent asset pricing factors. Second, we extract the time series signals of these residual portfolios with one of the most powerful machine learning time series solutions, a convolutional transformer. Last, we use these signals to form an optimal trading policy, that maximizes risk adjusted returns under constraints. We conduct a comprehensive empirical comparison study with daily large cap U.S. stocks. Our optimal trading strategy obtains a consistently high out of sample Sharpe ratio and substantially outperforms all benchmark approaches. It is orthogonal to common risk factors, and exploits asymmetric local trend and reversion patterns. Our strategies remain profitable after taking into account trading frictions and costs. Our findings suggest a high compensation for arbitrageurs to enforce the law of one price.

摘要 : 统计套利识别并利用相似资产之间的暂时价格差异。我们提出了一个统一的统计套利概念框架，并开发了一个新的深度学习解决方案，该方案以数据驱动和灵活的方式从大型面板中发现共性和时间序列模式。首先，我们从条件潜在资产定价因子出发，构造类似资产的套利组合作为剩余组合。其次，我们使用最强大的机器学习时间序列解决方案之一，卷积变换器来提取这些剩余投资组合的时间序列信号。最后，我们利用这些信号来形成一个最优的交易策略，在约束条件下使风险调整后的收益最大化。我们对每日大盘股进行了全面的实证比较研究。我们的最佳交易策略获得了持续较高的样本外夏普比率，大大优于所有基准方法。它是正交的共同风险因素，并利用不对称的局部趋势和逆转模式。在考虑到贸易摩擦和成本后，我们的策略仍然盈利。我们的研究结果表明，套利者在执行同一价格法时会获得较高的补偿。

\[8\] Optimal Taxation of Assets

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.02861

说明 : -

Abstract : The optimal taxation of assets requires attention to two concerns 1) the elasticity of the supply of assets and 2) the impact of taxing assets on distributional objectives. The most efficient way to attend to these two concerns is to tax assets of different types separately, rather than having one tax on all assets. When assets are created by specialized effort rather than by saving, as with innovations, discoveries of mineral deposits and development of unregulated natural monopolies, it is interesting to consider a regime in which the government awards a prize for the creation of the asset and then collects the remaining value of the asset in taxes. Analytically, the prize is like a wage after taxes. In this perspective, prizes are awarded based on a variation on optimal taxation theory, while assets of different types are taxed in divergent ways, depending on their characteristics. Some categories of assets are abolished.

摘要 : 资产的最优征税需要注意两个问题：1）资产供给的弹性；2）资产征税对分配目标的影响。处理这两个问题的最有效方法是对不同类型的资产分别征税，而不是对所有资产都征收一种税。当资产是通过专业努力创造的，而不是通过储蓄创造的，比如创新、矿藏的发现和不受监管的自然垄断的发展，那么考虑一种制度是很有意思的，在这种制度中，政府对资产的创造给予奖励，然后以税收的形式收取资产的剩余价值。分析一下，奖金就像税后的工资。从这个角度来看，奖励是基于最优税收理论的变化而授予的，而不同类型的资产则根据其特点以不同的方式征税。某些类别的资产被废除。

\[12\] Dynamic Portfolio Cuts

标题 : 动态投资组合削减

作者 : Alvaro Arroyo, Bruno Scalzo, Ljubisa Stankovic, Danilo P. Mandic , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.03417

说明 : Comments: 5 pages, 3 Figures, 2 Tables

Abstract : Stock market returns are typically analyzed using standard regression, yet they reside on irregular domains which is a natural scenario for graph signal processing. To this end, we consider a market graph as an intuitive way to represent the relationships between financial assets. Traditional methods for estimating asset return covariance operate under the assumption of statistical time invariance, and are thus unable to appropriately infer the underlying true structure of the market graph. This work introduces a class of graph spectral estimators which cater for the nonstationarity inherent to asset price movements, and serve as a basis to represent the time varying interactions between assets through a dynamic spectral market graph.
