\[1\] Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency

标题:加密货币的风险与收益

作者:Yukun Liu, Aleh Tsyvinski

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/2689/5912024; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa113

Abstract : We establish that cryptocurrency returns are driven and can be predicted by factors that are specific to cryptocurrency markets. Cryptocurrency returns are exposed to cryptocurrency network factors but not cryptocurrency production factors. We construct the network factors to capture the user adoption of cryptocurrencies and the production factors to proxy for the costs of cryptocurrency production. Moreover, there is a strong time-series momentum effect, and proxies for investor attention strongly forecast future cryptocurrency returns.

Abstract:我们确定加密货币的回报是由特定于加密货币市场的因素驱动和预测的。加密货币收益受加密货币网络因素影响，但不受加密货币生产因素影响。我们构造了网络因素来捕获用户对加密货币的采用，而生产因素来代理加密货币的生产成本。此外，有一个强大的时间序列动量效应，并代理投资者的注意强烈预测未来加密货币的回报。

\[2\] The PPP View of Multihorizon Currency Risk Premiums

标题:多视野货币风险溢价的PPP视角

作者:Mikhail Chernov, Drew Creal

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/2728/5912942; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa114

Abstract : Exposures of expected future nominal depreciation rates to the current interest rate differential violate the UIP hypothesis in a pattern that is a nonmonotonic function of horizon. Forward expected nominal depreciation rates are monotonic. We explain the two patterns by simultaneously incorporating the weak form of PPP into a joint model of the stochastic discount factor, the nominal exchange rate, and domestic and foreign yield curves. Departures from PPP generate the first pattern. The risk premiums for these departures generate the second pattern. Thus, the variance of the stochastic discount factor is related to the real exchange rate.

Abstract:预期未来名义折旧率对当前利率差的敞口违反了UIP假设，其模式是地平线的非单调函数。远期预期名义折旧率是单调的。我们通过同时将购买力平价的弱形式纳入随机贴现因子、名义汇率和国内外收益率曲线的联合模型来解释这两种模式。偏离购买力平价会产生第一种模式。这些离职的风险溢价产生了第二种模式。因此，随机贴现因子的方差与实际汇率有关。

\[3\] The Yield Spread and Bond Return Predictability in Expansions and Recessions

标题:扩张和衰退中收益率差和债券收益率的可预测性

作者:Martin M Andreasen, Tom Engsted, Stig V Møller, Magnus Sander

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/2773/5902842; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa107

Abstract : This paper uncovers that expected excess bond returns display a positive correlation with the slope of the yield curve (i.e., yield spread) in expansions but a negative correlation in recessions. We use a macro-finance term structure model with different market prices of risk in expansions and recessions to show that a very accommodating monetary policy in recessions is a key driver of this switch in return predictability.

Abstract:本文揭示了预期超额收益率在扩张期与收益率曲线斜率（即收益率利差）呈正相关，而在衰退期与收益率曲线斜率呈负相关。我们使用一个宏观金融期限结构模型，在扩张期和衰退期具有不同的风险市场价格，以表明在衰退期非常宽松的货币政策是这种回报可预测性转换的关键驱动力。

\[4\] Arbitrage Portfolios

标题:套利投资组合

作者:Soohun Kim, Robert A Korajczyk, Andreas Neuhierl

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/2813/5902462; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa102

Abstract : We propose a new methodology for forming arbitrage portfolios that utilizes the information contained in firm characteristics for both abnormal returns and factor loadings. The methodology gives maximal weight to risk-based interpretations of characteristics’ predictive power before any attribution is made to abnormal returns. We apply the methodology to simulated economies and to a large panel of U.S. stock returns. The methodology works well in our simulation and when applied to stocks. Empirically, we find the arbitrage portfolio has (statistically and economically) significant alphas relative to several popular asset pricing models and annualized Sharpe ratios ranging from 1.31 to 1.66.

Abstract:我们提出了一种新的套利投资组合的形成方法，该方法利用了企业特征中包含的异常收益和因子负荷信息。在对异常收益做出任何归因之前，该方法对基于风险的特征预测能力的解释给予了最大的权重。我们将该方法应用于模拟经济体和美国股票收益的大型面板。该方法在我们的模拟和应用于股票时效果良好。在实证上，我们发现套利投资组合相对于几种流行的资产定价模型具有显著的alphas（统计上和经济上），年化Sharpe比率在1.31到1.66之间。

\[5\] Hysteresis in Price Efficiency and the Economics of Slow-Moving Capital

标题:价格效率滞后与资本流动缓慢的经济学

作者:James Dow, Jungsuk Han, Francesco Sangiorgi

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/2857/5910559; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa110

Abstract : Will arbitrage capital flow into markets experiencing shocks, mitigating adverse effects on price efficiency? Not necessarily. In a dynamic model with privately informed capital-constrained arbitrageurs, price efficiency plays a dual role, determining both the profitability of new arbitrage and the ability to close existing positions profitably. An adverse shock to efficiency lengthens arbitrage duration, effectively reducing the amount of arbitrage capital available for new positions. If this falls below a critical mass, arbitrage capital flows out, amplifying the impact on price efficiency. This creates endogenous regimes: temporary shocks can trigger “hysteresis,” a persistent shift in price efficiency.

Abstract:套利资金是否会流入遭遇冲击的市场，减轻对价格效率的不利影响？不一定。在一个有私人信息资本约束套利者的动态模型中，价格效率起着双重作用，既决定了新套利的盈利能力，也决定了平仓获利的能力。对效率的不利冲击延长了套利持续时间，有效地减少了可用于新头寸的套利资金量。如果这低于一个临界质量，套利资本就会流出，放大了对价格效率的影响。这就产生了内生机制：暂时的冲击会引发“滞后”，即价格效率的持续转变。

\[6\] Self-Fulfilling Fire Sales: Fragility of Collateralized Short-Term Debt Markets

标题:自我实现的火爆销售：抵押短期债券市场的脆弱性

作者:John Chi-Fong Kuong

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/2910/5918033; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa115

Abstract : This paper shows that collateralized short-term debt, although privately optimal for reducing borrowers’ risk-taking incentives, can induce fragility (multiple equilibria). Despite sequential-service property being absent in collateralized debt, such as repurchase agreements, a systemic run can arise, featuring large increases in default risks, fire-sale discounts of collateral, cost of credit, and amount of credit rationing. Asset price guarantees, leverage caps, and central clearing promote stability and welfare. Using global games techniques, I show that a systemic run is more likely in bad times, and a large enough asset price guarantee reduces run risks.

Abstract:本文表明，短期债务抵押虽然对降低借款人的风险承担动机是私人最优的，但它会导致脆弱性（多重均衡）。尽管有担保的债务（如回购协议）中缺少顺序服务属性，但仍可能出现系统性运行，其特征是违约风险大幅增加、抵押品的甩卖折扣、信贷成本和信贷配给量大幅增加。资产价格担保、杠杆上限和中央结算促进了稳定和福利。运用全球博弈技术，我发现在经济不景气的时候，系统性挤兑的可能性更大，而足够大的资产价格担保可以降低挤兑风险。

\[7\] Collateral Runs

标题:抵押贷款

作者:Sebastian Infante, Alexandros P Vardoulakis

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/2949/6050886; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa139

Abstract : This paper models an unexplored source of liquidity risk large broker-dealers face: a withdrawal of collateral providers. By setting different contracting terms on repurchase agreements with cash borrowers and lenders, dealers can source funds for their own activities. Cash borrowers internalize the risk of losing their collateral in case their dealer defaults, prompting them to withdraw it. This incentive creates strategic complementarities among collateral providers, reducing a dealer’s liquidity position and compromising their solvency. Collateral runs are triggered by a contraction in dealers’ assets making them markedly different than traditional wholesale funding runs. Mitigating these risks involves different policy recommendations.

Abstract:本文对大型券商面临的流动性风险的一个未经探索的来源进行了建模：抵押品提供者的退出。通过与现金借款人和贷款人就回购协议设定不同的合同条款，交易商可以为自己的活动筹集资金。现金借贷者将在交易商违约时失去抵押品的风险内在化，促使他们撤回抵押品。这种激励在抵押品提供者之间创造了战略互补性，降低了交易商的流动性状况，损害了他们的偿付能力。抵押品挤兑是由交易商资产缩水引发的，这使得它们明显不同于传统的批发融资挤兑。减轻这些风险涉及不同的政策建议。

\[8\] Mutual Fund Trading Style and Bond Market Fragility

标题:共同基金交易风格与债券市场脆弱性

作者:Amber Anand, Chotibhak Jotikasthira, Kumar Venkataraman

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/2993/5934517; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa120

Abstract : We explore the link between mutual funds and fragility risk in the corporate bond market. We classify a fund’s trading style based on its responses to signals of large dealer inventories. Trading style is persistent and the majority of funds demand liquidity. Notably, a subset of funds earn positive alpha by intentionally supplying liquidity during periods of sustained customer selling (with transitory price effects). Liquidity-supplying funds maintain their relative trading style when facing large outflows and elevated market stress, thus alleviating fragility risk. Our results add nuance to existing evidence that mutual funds pose a threat to market stability.

Abstract:我们探讨了共同基金与公司债券市场脆弱性风险之间的联系。我们根据基金对大宗交易商库存信号的反应，对基金的交易风格进行分类。交易风格持续，多数基金要求流动性。值得注意的是，一部分基金通过在持续的客户销售期间故意提供流动性（具有短暂的价格效应）来赚取正α。流动性供给型基金在面临大量资金流出和市场压力上升时保持相对的交易风格，从而缓解了脆弱性风险。我们的研究结果与现有的共同基金对市场稳定构成威胁的证据存在细微差别。

\[9\] Marketing Mutual Funds

标题:市场营销共同基金

作者:Nikolai Roussanov, Hongxun Ruan, Yanhao Wei

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/3045/5898645; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa095

Abstract : Marketing and distribution expenses are responsible for about one-third of the cost of active management in the mutual fund industry. Estimating a structural model with costly investor search and learning about fund skill, we find that marketing is nearly as important as performance and fees in determining fund size. Eliminating marketing substantially improves welfare: capital shifts toward cheaper funds and competition decreases fees; active funds shrink and capital allocation becomes more closely aligned with skill. Declining investor search costs imply a reduction in marketing expenses and management fees as well as a shift toward passive investing, as observed empirically.

Abstract:在共同基金行业，营销和分销费用约占主动管理成本的三分之一。通过对一个结构模型的估计，我们发现，在决定基金规模时，市场营销几乎与业绩和费用同等重要。取消市场营销大大提高了福利：资本转向更廉价的基金，竞争减少了费用；活跃基金缩水，资本配置与技能的关系更加密切。根据经验观察，投资者搜索成本的下降意味着营销费用和管理费用的减少，以及向被动投资的转变。

\[10\] Political Influence and the Renegotiation of Government Contracts

标题:政治影响与政府契约的重新谈判

作者:Jonathan Brogaard, Matthew Denes, Ran Duchin

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/3095/5893489; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa093

Abstract : This paper provides novel evidence that corporate political influence operates through renegotiations of existing government contracts. Using detailed data on contractual terms and renegotiations around sudden deaths and resignations of local politicians, the estimates show that politically connected firms initially bid low and successfully renegotiate contract amounts, deadlines, and incentives. The effects hold across different industries and contract types, enhance firm value, and persist around the exogenous increase in contract supply due to the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act. Overall, this paper establishes an unexplored link between political influence, ex post renegotiations, and ex ante bidding of government contracts.

Abstract:本文提供了新的证据表明，企业政治影响力通过重新谈判现有的政府合同运作。利用有关合同条款的详细数据和围绕当地政客突然死亡和辞职的重新谈判，估计表明，与政治有关的公司最初出价较低，并成功地重新谈判了合同金额、期限和激励措施。这种效应在不同的行业和合同类型中都存在，提高了企业价值，并围绕着美国复苏和再投资法案导致的合同供给的外生增长而存在。总的来说，本文建立了一个未经探索的联系之间的政治影响，事后重新谈判，和政府合同的事前招标。

\[11\] Political Cycles in Bank Lending to the Government

标题:银行向政府放贷的政治周期

作者:Michael Koetter, Alexander Popov

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/3138/5920334; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa118

Abstract : We study how political party turnover after German state elections affects banks’ lending to the regional government. We find that between 1992 and 2018, party turnover at the state level leads to a sharp and substantial increase in lending by local savings banks to their home-state government. This effect is accompanied by an equivalent reduction in private lending. A statistical association between political party turnover and government lending is absent for comparable cooperative banks that exhibit a similar regional organization and business model. Our results suggest that political frictions may interfere with government-owned banks’ local development objectives.

Abstract:我们研究德国州选举后政党更替如何影响银行对地区政府的贷款。我们发现，1992年至2018年间，州一级的政党更替导致地方储蓄银行向其所在州政府发放的贷款大幅增加。伴随着这种效应，民间借贷也相应减少。对于表现出类似区域组织和商业模式的可比合作银行，政党更替率和政府贷款之间缺乏统计关联。我们的结果表明，政治摩擦可能会干扰国有银行的地方发展目标。

\[12\] The Effect of Bank Supervision and Examination on Risk Taking: Evidence from a Natural Experiment

标题:银行监督检查对风险承担的影响：一个自然实验的证据

作者:John Kandrac, Bernd Schlusche

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/6/3181/5912025; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa090

Abstract : We exploit an exogenous reduction in bank supervision and examination to demonstrate a causal effect of supervisory oversight on financial institutions’ risk taking. The additional risk took the form of risky lending, faster asset growth, and a greater reliance on low-quality capital. This response to less oversight boosted banks’ odds of failure. Lastly, we show that the reduction in oversight capacity led to more costly failures because there were longer delays in closing insolvent institutions, and because more bad assets were passed to the government insurance fund.

Abstract:我们利用银行监管和审查的外生减少来证明监管监督对金融机构风险承担的因果影响。额外的风险表现为高风险贷款、更快的资产增长以及对低质量资本的更大依赖。这种对监管减少的反应提高了银行倒闭的几率。最后，我们表明，监督能力的降低导致了代价更高的失败，因为关闭破产机构的延迟时间更长，而且更多的不良资产转移到了政府保险基金。
