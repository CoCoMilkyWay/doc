\[1\] Multivariate tail covariance for generalized skew elliptical distributions

标题 : 广义斜椭圆分布的多元尾协方差

作者 : Baishuai Zuo, Chuancun Yin , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05201

说明 : -

Abstract : In this paper, the multivariate tail covariance (MTCov) for generalized skew elliptical distributions is considered. Some special cases for this distribution, such as generalized skew normal, generalized skew student t, generalized skew logistic and generalized skew Laplace distributions, are also considered. In order to test the theoretical feasibility of our results, the MTCov for skewed and non skewed normal distributions are computed and compared. Finally, we give a special formula of the MTCov for generalized skew elliptical distributions.

摘要 : 本文研究了广义斜椭圆分布的多元尾协方差（MTCov）。本文还讨论了这种分布的一些特殊情况，如广义斜正态分布、广义斜student分布、广义斜logistic分布和广义斜Laplace分布。为了验证我们结果的理论可行性，计算并比较了偏态和非偏态正态分布的MTCov。最后，我们给出了广义斜椭圆分布的MTCov的一个特殊公式。

\[2\] Problems with Risk Matrices Using Ordinal Scales

标题 : 基于序数尺度的风险矩阵问题

作者 : Michael Krisper , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05440

说明 : Comments: 11 pages, 9 figures

Abstract : In this paper, we discuss various problems in the usage and definition of risk matrices. We give an overview of the general process of risk assessment with risk matrices and ordinal scales. Furthermore, we explain the fallacies in each phase of this process and give hints on which decisions may lead to more problems than others and how to avoid them. Among those 24 discussed problems are ordinal scales, semi quantitative arithmetics, range compression, risk inversion, ambiguity, and neglection of uncertainty. Finally, we make a case for avoiding risk matrices altogether and instead propose using fully quantitative risk assessment methods.

摘要 : 本文讨论了风险矩阵的使用和定义中的各种问题。我们概述了风险矩阵和序数尺度的风险评估的一般过程。此外，我们还解释了这个过程中每个阶段的谬误，并给出了哪些决策可能导致比其他决策更多的问题以及如何避免这些问题的提示。在这24个讨论的问题是序数尺度，半定量算法，距离压缩，风险反演，模糊性和不确定性的忽视。最后，我们提出了一个完全避免风险矩阵的例子，并建议使用完全定量的风险评估方法。

\[3\] Portfolio risk allocation through Shapley value

标题 : 基于Shapley值的投资组合风险分配

作者 : Patrick S. Hagan, Andrew Lesniewski, Georgios E. Skoufis, Diana E. Woodward , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05453

说明 : Comments: 15 pages

Abstract : We argue that using the Shapley value of cooperative game theory as the scheme for risk allocation among non orthogonal risk factors is a natural way of interpreting the contribution made by each of such factors to overall portfolio risk. We discuss a Shapley value scheme for allocating risk to non orthogonal greeks in a portfolio of derivatives. Such a situation arises, for example, when using a stochastic volatility model to capture option volatility smile. We also show that Shapley value allows for a natural method of interpreting components of enterprise risk measures such as VaR and ES. For all applications discussed, we derive explicit formulas and or numerical algorithms to calculate the allocations.

摘要 : 我们认为，使用合作博弈理论的Shapley值作为非正交风险因素之间的风险分配方案，是解释这些因素对整体投资组合风险贡献的自然方式。我们讨论了一个Shapley值方案来分配风险的非正交希腊人在一个投资组合的衍生品。例如，当使用随机波动率模型来捕捉期权波动率时，就会出现这种情况。我们还表明，Shapley值允许一种自然的方法来解释企业风险度量的组成部分，如VaR和ES。对于所讨论的所有应用，我们导出了计算分配的显式公式和/或数值算法。

Abstract : The question of how a pure fiat currency is enforced and comes to have a non zero value has been much debated cite 10.2307 2077948 . What is less often addressed is, in the case where the enforcement is taken for granted and we ask what value (in terms of goods and services) the currency will end up taking. Establishing a decentralised mechanism for price formation has proven a challenge for economists Since no decentralized out of equilibrium adjustment mechanism has been discovered, we currently have no acceptable dynamical model of the Walrasian system (Gintis 2006). \[5\] Urban Epidemic Hazard Index for Chinese Cities

标题 : 中国城市传染病危害指数

作者 : Tianyi Li, Jiawen Luo, Cunrui Huang , 类别 ：Physics and Society (physics.soc-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05189

说明 : -

Abstract : Multiple small to middle scale cities, mostly located in northern China, became epidemic hotspots during the second wave of the spread of COVID 19 in early 2021. Despite qualitative discussions of potential social economic causes, it remains unclear how this pattern could be accounted for from a quantitative approach. Through the development of an urban epidemic hazard index (EpiRank), we came up with a mathematical explanation for this phenomenon. The index is constructed from epidemic simulations on a multi layer transportation network model on top of local SEIR transmission dynamics, which characterizes intra and inter city compartment population flow with a detailed mathematical description. Essentially, we argue that these highlighted cities possess greater epidemic hazards due to the combined effect of large regional population and small inter city transportation. 摘要 : 在2021年初第二波covid19病毒传播期间，多个主要位于中国北方的中小城市成为流行热点。尽管对潜在的社会经济原因进行了定性讨论，但仍不清楚如何从定量方法解释这一模式。通过建立城市流行病危害指数（EpiRank），我们对这一现象给出了数学解释。该指数是在当地SEIR传播动力学的基础上，通过对多层交通网络模型的流行病模拟构建的，该模型描述了城市内和城市间的区间人口流动，并给出了详细的数学描述。从本质上讲，我们认为，这些突出的城市具有更大的流行病危害，由于大区域人口和小城市间交通的共同作用。该指标具有动态性，适用于不同的疫情环境，可作为我国城市疫情风险评估和应对规划的一个有用指标。模型框架模块化，适用于其他国家。

\[6\] Portfolio Construction as Linearly Constrained Separable Optimization

标题 : 线性约束可分离优化的投资组合构造

作者 : Nicholas Moehle, Jack Gindi, Stephen Boyd, Mykel Kochenderfer , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05455

说明 : -

Abstract : Mean variance portfolio optimization problems often involve separable nonconvex terms, including penalties on capital gains, integer share constraints, and minimum position and trade sizes. We propose a heuristic algorithm for this problem based on the alternating direction method of multipliers (ADMM). This method allows for solve times in tens to hundreds of milliseconds with around 1000 securities and 100 risk factors. We also obtain a bound on the achievable performance. Our heuristic and bound are both derived from similar results for other optimization problems with a separable objective and affine equality constraints. We discuss a concrete implementation in the case where the separable terms in the objective are piecewise quadratic, and we demonstrate their effectiveness empirically in realistic tax aware portfolio construction problems.

摘要 : 均值-方差投资组合优化问题通常涉及可分离的非凸项，包括对资本收益的惩罚、整数份额约束以及最小头寸和交易规模。提出了一种基于交替方向乘子法的启发式算法。这种方法允许在大约1000个证券和100个风险因素的情况下以几十到几百毫秒的时间进行求解。我们还得到了可实现性能的一个界。我们的启发式和定界都是从类似的结果，其他优化问题的可分离的目标和仿射等式约束。我们讨论了目标中的可分离项是分段二次型的情况下的一个具体实现，并在现实的税务感知投资组合构建问题中证明了它们的有效性。

Abstract : The field of Financial Networks is a paramount example of the novel applications of Statistical Physics that have made possible by the present data revolution. As the total value of the global financial market has vastly outgrown the value of the real economy, financial institutions on this planet have created a web of interactions whose size and topology calls for a quantitative analysis by means of Complex Networks. Financial Networks are not only a playground for the use of basic tools of statistical physics as ensemble representation and entropy maximization rather, their particular dynamics and evolution triggered theoretical advancements as the definition of DebtRank to measure the impact and diffusion of shocks in the whole systems. Abstract : This paper introduces a potential application of deep learning and artificial intelligence in finance, particularly its application in hedging. The major goal encompasses two objectives. First, we present a framework of a direct policy search reinforcement agent replicating a simple vanilla European call option and use the agent for the model free delta hedging. Through the first part of this paper, we demonstrate how the RNN based direct policy search RL agents can perform delta hedging better than the classic Black Scholes model in Q world based on parametrically generated underlying scenarios, particularly minimizing tail exposures at higher values of the risk aversion parameter. \[9\] Does automation erode governments tax basis An empirical assessment of tax revenues in Europe

标题 : 自动化是否侵蚀了政府的税收基础欧洲税收的实证评估

说明 : -

Abstract : Decomposing taxes by source (labor, capital, sales), we analyze the impact of automation (1) on tax revenues, (2) the structure of taxation, and (3) identify channels of impact in 19 EU countries during 1995 2016. Robots and Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) are different technologies designed to automate manual (robots) or cognitive tasks (ICT). Until 2007, robot diffusion led to decreasing factor and tax income, and a shift from taxes on capital to goods. ICTs changed the structure of taxation from capital to labor. \[11\] The Determinants of Democracy Revisited

标题 : 重新审视民主的决定因素

作者 : Sajad Rahimian , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.04255

说明 : -

Abstract : Identifying the real causes of democracy is an ongoing debate. We contribute to the literature by examining the robustness of a comprehensive list of 42 potential determinants of democracy. We take a step forward and employ Instrumental Variable Bayesian Model Averaging (IVBMA) method to tackle endogeneity explicitly. Using the data of 111 countries, our IVBMA results mark arable land as the most persistent predictor of democracy with a posterior inclusion probability (PIP) of 0.961. 摘要 : 在以可变可再生能源（VRE）为主的未来电网中，氢（h2）用于长时间储能越来越受到人们的关注。将氢气作为电网规模储能（通常称为电-气-电（P2G2P））的作用建模时，忽略了使用已部署的氢气生产和储存资产同时为可能面临直接电气化挑战的其他最终用途部门的脱碳提供氢气所带来的成本分摊和排放效益。在这里，我们开发了一个通用的建模框架，用于在电力和运输部门以及电力和氢气供应链之间共同优化能源基础设施投资和运营，同时考虑能源需求和供应的时空变化。在一系列技术成本和碳价格情景下，将这一部门耦合框架应用于美国东北部，我们发现与P2G2P路线相比，h2（P2G）的电力价值更大。P2G提供了灵活的需求响应，而P2G2P路由的额外成本和效率惩罚使得该解决方案对网格平衡的吸引力降低。部门耦合的影响是显著的，在96种脱碳方案下，通过增加容量和减少限功率，将VRE发电量提高12 55，并将系统总成本（或能源平准化成本）降低6 14。部门耦合带来的成本节约和减排都随着其他最终用途的H 2需求而增加，在96脱碳方案中，由于H 2需求四分之一，成本节约和减排增加了一倍多。此外，我们还发现，在h2部门部署碳捕获和储存的成本更低，利用率更高，因此更具成本效益。这些发现强调了在规划能源系统脱碳路径时，使用具有多个能源向量的综合多部门能源系统框架的重要性。

\[22\] Tail forecasts of inflation using time varying parameter quantile regressions

标题 : 基于时变参数分位数回归的通货膨胀尾部预测

作者 : Michael Pfarrhofer , 类别 ：Econometrics (econ.EM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.03632

说明 : Comments: JEL: C11, C22, C53, E31; Keywords: state space models, stochastic volatility, unobserved component model, density forecasts, tail risk

Abstract : This paper proposes methods for Bayesian inference in time varying parameter (TVP) quantile regression (QR) models. We use data augmentation schemes to facilitate the conditional likelihood, and render the model conditionally Gaussian to develop an efficient Gibbs sampling algorithm. Regularization of the high dimensional parameter space is achieved via flexible dynamic shrinkage priors. A simple version of the TVP QR based on an unobserved component (UC) model is applied to dynamically trace the quantiles of the distribution of inflation in the United States (US), the United Kingdom (UK) and the euro area (EA). We conduct an out of sample inflation forecasting exercise to assess predictive accuracy of the proposed framework versus several benchmarks using metrics to capture performance in different parts of the distribution. The proposed model is competitive and performs particularly well for higher order and tail forecasts. We analyze the resulting predictive distributions and find that they are often skewed and feature heavier than normal tails.

摘要 : 提出了时变参数分位数回归模型中贝叶斯推理的方法。我们使用数据增强方案来促进条件似然，并将模型条件高斯化，以发展一个有效的Gibbs采样算法。高维参数空间的正则化是通过灵活的动收缩先验实现的。一个基于未观测分量（UC）模型的TVP-QR的简单版本被应用于动态跟踪美国（US）、英国（UK）和欧元区（EA）通货膨胀分布的分位数。我们进行了一次样本外通胀预测练习，以评估拟议框架的预测准确性，并与使用指标来捕获分布不同部分的绩效的几个基准进行比较。该模型具有很强的竞争性，特别适用于高阶和尾部预测。我们分析得到的预测分布，发现它们往往是倾斜的，特征比正常的尾巴重。
