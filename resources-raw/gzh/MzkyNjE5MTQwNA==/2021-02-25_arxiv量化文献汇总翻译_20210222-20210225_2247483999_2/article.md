\[1\] Modeling Price Clustering in High Frequency Prices

标题 : 高频价格下的价格聚类模型

作者 : Vladimír Holý, Petra Tomanová , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12112

说明 : -

Abstract : The price clustering phenomenon, i.e. an increased occurence of specific prices, is widely observed and well documented for various financial instruments in various financial markets. In the literature, however, it is rarely incorporated into price models. We consider that there are several types of agents trading only in specific multiples of the tick size resulting in an increased occurrence of these multiples in prices. For example, stocks on the NYSE and NASDAQ exchanges are traded with precision to one cent but multiples of five cents and ten cents occur much more often in prices. To capture this behaviour, we propose a discrete price model based on a mixture of double Poisson distributions with dynamic volatility and dynamic proportions of agent types. The model is estimated by the maximum likelihood method. In an empirical study of DJIA stocks, we find that higher instantaneous volatility leads to weaker price clustering at the ultra high frequency. This is in sharp contrast with results at low frequencies which show that daily realized volatility has positive impact on price clustering.

摘要 : 价格集群现象，即特定价格的增加，在各种金融市场的各种金融工具中被广泛观察和记录。然而，在文献中，它很少被纳入价格模型。我们认为，有几种类型的代理只在特定的倍数交易的刻度大小，导致增加发生这些倍数的价格。例如，纽约证交所和纳斯达克交易所的股票交易精度为1美分，但5美分和10美分的倍数在价格上出现的频率要高得多。为了捕捉这种行为，我们提出了一个基于双泊松分布的离散价格模型，该模型具有动态波动性和代理人类型的动态比例。模型采用极大似然法估计。在对道琼斯指数股票的实证研究中，我们发现，较高的瞬时波动率会导致超高频下较弱的价格聚集。这与低频率下的结果形成鲜明对比，低频率下的结果表明，每日实现波动率对价格聚类具有积极影响。

Indonesia s e commerce property business has positive trending shown by the increasing sales of more than 500 from 2011 to 2015. A prediction of the property price is important to help investors or the public to have accurate information before buying property. One of the methods for prediction is a classification based on several distinctive property industry attributes, such as building size, land size, number of rooms, and location. Today, data is easily obtained, there are many open data from E commerce sites. E commerce contains information about homes and other properties advertised to sell. \[3\] Interactions between social norms and incentive mechanisms in organizations

标题 : 社会规范与组织激励机制的互动

作者 : Ravshanbek Khodzhimatov, Stephan Leitner, Friederike Wall , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12309

说明 : -

Abstract : We focus on how individual behavior that complies with social norms interferes with performance based incentive mechanisms in organizations with multiple distributed decision making agents. We model social norms to emerge from interactions between agents agents observe other the agents actions and, from these observations, induce what kind of behavior is socially acceptable. By complying with the induced socially accepted behavior, agents experience utility. Also, agents get utility from a pay for performance incentive mechanism. Thus, agents pursue two objectives. We place the interaction between social norms and performance based incentive mechanisms in the complex environment of an organization with distributed decision makers, in which a set of interdependent tasks is allocated to multiple agents. The results suggest that, unless the sets of assigned tasks are highly correlated, complying with emergent socially accepted behavior is detrimental to the organization s performance. However, we find that incentive schemes can help offset the performance loss by applying individual based incentives in environments with lower task complexity and team based incentives in environments with higher task complexity.

摘要 : 我们关注的是在具有多个分布式决策代理的组织中，符合社会规范的个体行为如何干扰基于绩效的激励机制。我们建立社会规范的模型，从主体之间的互动中产生，主体观察其他主体的行为，并从这些观察中归纳出什么样的行为是社会可以接受的。通过遵守诱导的社会接受行为，代理人体验效用。此外，代理人从按绩效付费的激励机制中获得效用。因此，代理追求两个目标。我们将社会规范和基于绩效的激励机制之间的相互作用置于一个具有分布式决策者的组织的复杂环境中，其中一组相互依赖的任务被分配给多个代理。研究结果表明，除非所分配的任务集高度相关，否则遵从社会认可的紧急行为对组织的绩效是有害的。然而，我们发现激励方案可以通过在任务复杂度较低的环境中应用基于个人的激励和在任务复杂度较高的环境中应用基于团队的激励来抵消绩效损失。

\[8\] Optimal dynamic regulation of carbon emissions market

标题 : 碳排放市场的最优动态调节

作者 : René Aïd, Sara Biagini , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.12423

说明 : -

Abstract : We consider the problem of reducing the carbon emissions of a set of firms over a finite horizon. A regulator dynamically allocates emission allowances to each firm. Firms face idiosyncratic as well as common economic shocks on emissions, and have linear quadratic abatement costs. Firms can trade allowances so to minimise total expected costs, from abatement and trading plus a quadratic terminal penalty. Using variational methods, we exhibit in closed form the market equilibrium in function of regulator s dynamic allocation. We then solve the Stackelberg game between the regulator and the firms. Again, we obtain a closed form expression of the dynamic allocation policies that allow a desired expected emission reduction. Optimal policies are not unique but share common properties. Surprisingly, all optimal policies induce a constant abatement effort and a constant price of allowances. Dynamic allocations outperform static ones because of adjustment costs and uncertainty, in particular given the presence of common shocks. Our results are robust to some extensions, like risk aversion of firms or different penalty functions.

摘要 : 我们考虑在有限的时间范围内减少一系列公司的碳排放的问题。监管者动态地为每家公司分配排放限额。企业在排放方面既面临特殊的经济冲击，也面临共同的经济冲击，并且具有线性二次型减排成本。公司可以交易津贴，以尽量减少总预期成本，从削减和交易加上二次终端罚款。利用变分方法，给出了调节器动态配置函数下市场均衡的封闭形式。然后我们解决了监管者和企业之间的Stackelberg博弈。再次，我们得到了允许期望减排的动态分配策略的闭式表达式。最优策略不是唯一的，而是具有共同的性质。令人惊讶的是，所有的最优政策都会导致持续的减排努力和不变的补贴价格。由于调整成本和不确定性，特别是在存在共同冲击的情况下，动态配置优于静态配置。我们的结果对于一些扩展是稳健的，例如企业的风险厌恶或不同的惩罚函数。
