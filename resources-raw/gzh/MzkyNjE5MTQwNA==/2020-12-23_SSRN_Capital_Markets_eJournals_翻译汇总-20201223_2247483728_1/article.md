\[1\] Capital Share, Consumption Volatility and Long-run Risks: Theory and Evidence

标题:资本份额、消费波动与长期风险：理论与实证研究

作者:Xiaoyu Zong(Heriot-Watt University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3649112

Abstract : Pricing of capital share risks provides a novel link between macroeconomics andfinance. Our paper extends the Bansal and Yaron’s (2004) long-run risk model toderive a heterogeneous agent model that accounts for capital stock fluctuations.Our model introduce heterogeneity within stockholders and highlights the role of elevated consumption volatility of high income stockholders in the long-run.We reveal that capital share growth has strong volatility effects in the short-term market returns and propose the capital share variability as a new asset pricingfactor. The capital share variability captures large long-run cross-sectional return variations and outperforms the capital share factor.

Keywords : Asset Pricing, Capital Share, Recursive Preference, Long-run Risks

Abstract :资本份额风险定价为宏观经济学和金融学之间提供了一种新的联系。本文扩展了Bansal和Yaron（2004）的长期风险模型，得到了一个考虑资本存量的异质代理人模型波动。我们的该模型引入了股东内部的异质性，突出了长期内高收入股东消费波动加剧的作用-快跑，我们有资本增长的股票作为短期资本市场的波动性和新的定价因素，股票的收益率具有很强的波动性。资本份额的可变性捕获了较大的长期横截面收益变化，并优于资本份额因子。

Keywords :资产定价、资本份额、递归偏好、长期风险
