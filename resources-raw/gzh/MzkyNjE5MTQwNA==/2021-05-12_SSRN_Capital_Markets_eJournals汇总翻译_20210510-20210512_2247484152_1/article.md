Abstract : Market Efficiency has been the objective of many researches across the globe since the last few decades. \[4\] Is Hard and Soft Information Substitutable? Evidence from Lockdown

标题:硬信息和软信息是否可以替代？封锁的证据

作者:Jennie Bai,Massimo Massa

出处:Georgetown McDonough School of Business Research Paper ForthcomingINSEAD Working Paper Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3843782

Abstract : We study the degree of information substitutability in the financial market. Exploiting the cross-sectional and time-series variations of the pandemic-triggered lockdowns that have hampered people's physical interactions hence the ability to collect, process, and transmit soft information, we investigate how the difficulty/inability to use soft information has prompted a switch to hard information, and its implication on fund performance. We show that lockdown reduces fund investment in proximate stocks and generate a portfolio rebalancing towards distant stocks. The rebalancing has negative implications on fund performance by reducing fund raw (excess) return of an additional 0.76% (0.29%) per month during lockdown, suggesting that soft and hard information is not easily substitutable. We show that soft information originates mainly with geographic proximity and human interactions, mostly in cafe, restaurants, bars, and fitness centers. This suggests that the virtual world based on Zoom/Skype/Team has direct negative implications on the ability of collecting soft information and therefore affects strategies relying on them such as proximity investment.

Keywords : mutual funds; soft information; COVID-19; proximity investing

Abstract :研究了金融市场中信息的替代程度。利用大流行引发的封锁的横截面和时间序列变化，阻碍了人们的身体互动，从而阻碍了收集、处理和传输软信息的能力，我们研究了使用软信息的困难/能力如何促使人们转向硬信息，及其对基金绩效的影响。我们表明，锁定减少了基金对近距股票的投资，并产生了一个投资组合向远距股票的再平衡。重新平衡对基金业绩有负面影响，在锁定期间，基金原始（超额）回报率每月额外降低0.76%（0.29%），表明软信息和硬信息不易替代。我们发现，软信息主要来源于地理邻近性和人类互动，主要在咖啡馆、餐馆、酒吧和健身中心。这表明，基于Zoom/Skype/Team的虚拟世界对收集软信息的能力有直接的负面影响，因此会影响依赖软信息的策略，如近距离投资。

Keywords :共同基金；软信息；COVID-19；近距离投资

\[7\] Capital, Aggregate Risk, Insurance Prices and Regulation

标题:资本、总风险、保险价格与监管

作者:Ajay Subramanian,Jinjing Wang

出处:Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3843271

Abstract : We develop a general equilibrium model of competitive insurance and equity capital markets to show how aggregate asset and insurance liability risks affect insurance prices and regulation. In the unique equilibrium of the benchmark unregulated economy, insurers raise external capital solely by selling insurance and no outside equity. We derive the Pareto efficient allocation and implement it via multiple regulatory policies that combine risk-based capital requirements, asset risk constraints, reinsurance and bailouts. An efficient regulatory policy with a lower capital requirement must be accompanied by a more stringent asset risk constraint, and one with a capital requirement that is more sensitive to insurer assets should be less sensitive to insurer liabilities. When the aggregate asset and liability risks are below respective thresholds, insurees are fully insured in the optimally regulated economy, and the set of efficient regulatory policies does not vary with aggregate risk. Outside this region, however, insurees must bear aggregate risk with capital and asset risk constraints becoming tighter as either the aggregate asset or liability risk increases. The aggregate asset and liability risks could have opposing effects on the insurance price and insurer size in the regulated economy. Our results highlight the importance of tailoring regulatory policies to aggregate risk levels, and disentangling the impacts of aggregate asset and liability risks in analyses of how regulation influences insurance prices and insurer capitalization.

Keywords : Insurance Regulation, Aggregate Asset Risk, Aggregate Liability Risk, General Equilibrium, Insurance Prices

Abstract :我们建立了一个竞争性保险和股票资本市场的一般均衡模型，以说明总资产和保险负债风险如何影响保险价格和监管。在基准不受监管经济的独特均衡中，保险公司仅通过出售保险筹集外部资本，而没有外部股权。我们推导了帕累托有效配置，并通过多种监管政策实施，这些政策结合了基于风险的资本要求、资产风险约束、再保险和救助。资本要求较低的有效监管政策必须伴随着更严格的资产风险约束，资本要求对保险公司资产更敏感的监管政策对保险公司负债更不敏感。当总资产和负债风险低于各自的阈值时，被保险人在最优监管经济中得到充分保险，有效监管政策的设置不会随总风险而变化。然而，在该地区之外，被保险人必须承担总风险，随着总资产或负债风险的增加，资本和资产风险约束变得更加严格。在受监管的经济体中，总资产和负债风险可能对保险价格和保险公司规模产生相反的影响。我们的结果强调了调整监管政策以适应总风险水平的重要性，并在分析监管如何影响保险价格和保险公司资本化时，理清总资产和负债风险的影响。

Keywords :保险监管，总资产风险，总负债风险，一般均衡，保险价格

Abstract : This paper introduces a time series model derived from the reverse engineering of the Schrodinger equation.
