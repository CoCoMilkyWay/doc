\[1\] A Method for Predicting VaR by Aggregating Generalized Distributions  Driven by the Dynamic Conditional Score

标题 : 一种由动态条件分数驱动的广义分布聚合预测VaR的方法

作者 : Shijia Song, Handong Li , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.02953

说明 : Comments: 20 pages. arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:2110.02492

Abstract : Constructing a more effective value at risk (VaR) prediction model has long been a goal in financial risk management. In this paper, we propose a novel parametric approach and provide a standard paradigm to demonstrate the modeling. We establish a dynamic conditional score (DCS) model based on high frequency data and a generalized distribution (GD), namely, the GD DCS model, to improve the forecasts of daily VaR. The model assumes that intraday returns at different moments are independent of each other and obey the same kind of GD, whose dynamic parameters are driven by DCS. By predicting the motion law of the time varying parameters, the conditional distribution of intraday returns is determined then, the bootstrap method is used to simulate daily returns. An empirical analysis using data from the Chinese stock market shows that Weibull Pareto DCS model incorporating high frequency data is superior to traditional benchmark models, such as RGARCH, in the prediction of VaR at high risk levels, which proves that this approach contributes to the improvement of risk measurement tools.

摘要 : 构建更有效的风险价值（VaR）预测模型一直是金融风险管理的目标。在本文中，我们提出了一种新的参数化方法，并提供了一个标准的范例来演示建模。我们建立了一个基于高频数据和广义分布（GD）的动态条件分数（DCS）模型，即GD-DCS模型，以改进对日VaR的预测。该模型假设不同时刻的日内收益相互独立，服从同一种GD，其动态参数由DCS驱动。通过预测时变参数的运动规律，确定日内收益率的条件分布，采用bootstrap方法模拟日内收益率。利用中国股市数据进行的实证分析表明，包含高频数据的Weibull-Pareto DCS模型在预测高风险水平下的VaR方面优于传统的基准模型，如RGARCH，这证明该方法有助于改进风险度量工具。

\[2\] Unpacking the Black Box

标题 : 打开黑盒子

作者 : Laura Blattner, Scott Nelson, Jann Spiess , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.03443

说明 : -

Abstract : We characterize optimal oversight of algorithms in a world where an agent designs a complex prediction function but a principal is limited in the amount of information she can learn about the prediction function. We show that limiting agents to prediction functions that are simple enough to be fully transparent is inefficient as long as the bias induced by misalignment between principal s and agent s preferences is small relative to the uncertainty about the true state of the world. Algorithmic audits can improve welfare, but the gains depend on the design of the audit tools. Tools that focus on minimizing overall information loss, the focus of many post hoc explainer tools, will generally be inefficient since they focus on explaining the average behavior of the prediction function rather than sources of mis prediction, which matter for welfare relevant outcomes. Targeted tools that focus on the source of incentive misalignment, e.g., excess false positives or racial disparities, can provide first best solutions. We provide empirical support for our theoretical findings using an application in consumer lending.

摘要 : 我们描述了在这样一个世界中，代理设计了一个复杂的预测函数，但委托人可以了解的关于预测函数的信息量有限，因此对算法的最优监督是有特点的。我们表明，只要委托人和代理人偏好之间的偏差相对于世界真实状态的不确定性较小，那么将代理人限制在简单到完全透明的预测函数上是低效的。算法审计可以改善福利，但收益取决于审计工具的设计。专注于最小化总体信息损失的工具（许多事后解释工具的重点）通常效率低下，因为它们专注于解释预测函数的平均行为，而不是错误预测的来源，这关系到福利相关的结果。专注于激励失调来源的有针对性的工具，如过度误报或种族差异，可以提供最佳解决方案。我们通过在消费者贷款中的应用为我们的理论发现提供了实证支持。

\[4\] Representation of probability distributions with implied volatility and biological rationale

标题 : 具有隐含波动率和生物学原理的概率分布表示

作者 : Felix Polyakov , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.03517

说明 : -

Abstract : Economic and financial theories and practice essentially deal with uncertain future. Humans encounter uncertainty in different kinds of activity, from sensory motor control to dynamics in financial markets, what has been subject of extensive studies. Representation of uncertainty with normal or lognormal distribution is a common feature of many of those studies. For example, proposed Bayessian integration of Gaussian multisensory input in the brain or log normal distribution of future asset price in renowned Black Scholes Merton (BSM) model for pricing contingent claims. Standard deviation of log(future asset price) scaled by square root of time in the BSM model is called implied volatility. Actually, log(future asset price) is not normally distributed and traders account for that to avoid losses. Nevertheless the BSM formula derived under the assumption of constant volatility remains a major uniform framework for pricing options in financial markets. I propose that one of the reasons for such a high popularity of the BSM formula could be its ability to translate uncertainty measured with implied volatility into price in a way that is compatible with human intuition for measuring uncertainty. The present study deals with mathematical relationship between uncertainty and the BSM implied volatility. Examples for a number of common probability distributions are presented. Overall, this work proposes that representation of various probability distributions in terms of the BSM implied volatility profile may be meaningful in both biological and financial worlds. Necessary background from financial mathematics is provided in the text.

摘要 : 经济和金融理论和实践本质上是处理不确定的未来。从感觉运动控制到金融市场的动力学，人类在各种活动中都会遇到不确定性，这一直是广泛研究的主题。用正态分布或对数正态分布表示不确定性是许多研究的共同特征。例如，在著名的Black-Scholes-Merton（BSM）未定权益定价模型中，提出了大脑中高斯多传感器输入的Bayessian积分或未来资产价格的对数正态分布。在BSM模型中，以时间平方根为标度的对数（未来资产价格）的标准偏差称为隐含波动率。实际上，log（未来资产价格）不是正态分布，交易者对此进行解释以避免损失。然而，在波动率不变的假设下得出的BSM公式仍然是金融市场期权定价的主要统一框架。我认为，BSM公式如此受欢迎的原因之一可能是其将隐含波动率测量的不确定性转换为价格的能力，其方式符合人类测量不确定性的直觉。本研究探讨了不确定性与BSM隐含波动率之间的数学关系。给出了一些常见概率分布的例子。总的来说，这项工作提出，根据BSM隐含波动率曲线表示各种概率分布可能在生物和金融领域都有意义。本文提供了必要的金融数学背景。

\[5\] Physics inspired analysis of the two class income distribution in the USA in 1983 2018

标题 : 1983年至2018年美国两级收入分配的物理学启发分析

作者 : Danial Ludwig, Victor M. Yakovenko , 类别 ：Physics and Society (physics.soc-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.03140

说明 : Comments: 18 pages, 8 figures, submitted to Philosophical Transactions A for the special issue "Kinetic exchange models of societies and economies"

Abstract : The first part of this paper is a brief survey of the approaches to economic inequality based on ideas from statistical physics and kinetic theory. These include the Boltzmann kinetic equation, the time reversal symmetry, the ergodicity hypothesis, entropy maximization, and the Fokker Planck equation. The origins of the exponential Boltzmann Gibbs distribution and the Pareto power law are discussed in relation to additive and multiplicative stochastic processes. The second part of the paper analyzes income distribution data in the USA for the time period 1983 2018 using a two class decomposition. We present overwhelming evidence that the lower class (more than 90 of the population) is described by the exponential distribution, whereas the upper class (about 4 of the population in 2018) by the power law. We show that the significant growth of inequality during this time period is due to the sharp increase in the upper class income share, whereas relative inequality within the lower class remains constant. We speculate that the expansion of the upper class population and income shares may be due to increasing digitization and non locality of the economy in the last 40 years.

摘要 : 本文的第一部分简要概述了基于统计物理学和动力学理论的经济不平等性研究方法。其中包括波尔兹曼动力学方程、时间反转对称性、遍历性假设、熵最大化和福克-普朗克方程。讨论了指数Boltzmann-Gibbs分布和Pareto幂律与加性和乘性随机过程的关系。论文的第二部分采用两级分解法分析了1983-2018年期间美国的收入分配数据。我们提供了压倒性的证据，证明下层阶级（90%以上的人口）是指数分布，而上层阶级（2018年约4%的人口）是幂律分布。我们表明，这一时期不平等性的显著增长是由于上层阶级收入份额的急剧增加，而下层阶级内部的相对不平等性保持不变。我们推测，上层阶级人口和收入份额的扩大可能是由于过去40年来经济日益数字化和非地方化。

\[6\] Fake news, noise, and tenacious Bayesians

标题 : 假新闻、噪音和顽强的Bayesian人

作者 : Dorje C. Brody , 类别 ：Theoretical Economics (econ.TH)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.03432

说明 : Comments: 16 pages, 4 figures

Abstract : A modelling framework, based on the theory of signal processing, for characterising the dynamics of systems driven by the unravelling of information is outlined, and is applied to describe the process of decision makings. The model input of this approach is the specification of the flow of information. This enables the representation of (i) reliable information, (ii) noise, and (iii) disinformation, in a unified framework. Because the approach is designed to characterise the dynamics of the system under study, it is possible to quantify the impact of information control, including those resulting from the dissemination of disinformation. It is shown that if a decision maker assigns an exceptionally high weight on one of the alternative realities, then under the Bayesian logic their perception hardly changes in time even if evidences presented indicate that this alternative corresponds to a false reality. By observing the role played by noise in other areas of natural sciences, a new approach to tackle the dark forces of fake news is proposed.

摘要 : 基于信号处理理论，概述了一个描述由信息分解驱动的系统动力学的建模框架，并将其应用于描述决策过程。这种方法的模型输入是信息流的规范。这使得（i）可靠信息，（ii）噪声和（iii）虚假信息能够在统一的框架中表示。由于该方法旨在描述所研究系统的动态特征，因此可以量化信息控制的影响，包括虚假信息传播产生的影响。研究表明，如果决策者对其中一个备选现实赋予了极高的权重，那么在贝叶斯逻辑下，即使有证据表明该备选现实对应于一个虚假现实，决策者的感知也几乎不会随时间发生变化。通过观察噪音在自然科学其他领域中所起的作用，提出了一种对付虚假新闻黑暗力量的新方法。
