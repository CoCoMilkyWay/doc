\[1\] Fat Tails and Black Swans

标题 : 肥尾巴黑天鹅

作者 : Damián H. Zanette, Susanna Manrubia , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.11679

说明 : Comments: 10 pages, 6 figures

Abstract : We consider a class of multiplicative processes which, added with stochastic reset events, give origin to stationary distributions with power law tails ubiquitous in the statistics of social, economic, and ecological systems. Our main goal is to provide a series of exact results on the dynamics and asymptotic behaviour of increasingly complex versions of a basic multiplicative process with resets, including discrete and continuous time variants and several degrees of randomness in the parameters that control the process. In particular, we show how the power law distributions are built up as time elapses, how their moments behave with time, and how their stationary profiles become quantitatively determined by those parameters. Our discussion emphasizes the connection with financial systems, but these stochastic processes are also expected to be fruitful in modeling a wide variety of social and biological phenomena.

摘要 : 我们考虑了一类乘法过程，加上随机重置事件，得到了在社会、经济和生态系统统计中普遍存在的幂律尾平稳分布。我们的主要目标是提供一系列关于基本乘法过程的动力学和渐近行为的精确结果，包括离散和连续时间变量以及控制过程的参数中的若干随机度。特别地，我们展示了幂律分布是如何随着时间流逝而建立起来的，它们的矩是如何随时间变化的，以及它们的平稳分布是如何由这些参数定量决定的。我们的讨论强调了与金融系统的联系，但这些随机过程在模拟各种各样的社会和生物现象方面也有望取得丰硕的成果。

\[3\] Environmental Kuznets Curve & Effectiveness of International Policies

标题 : 环境库兹涅茨曲线与国际政策的有效性

作者 : Elvan Ece Satici, Bayram Cakir , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.11756

说明 : -

Abstract : In this article, we are presenting the relationship between environmental pollution and the income level of the selected twenty four countries. We implemented a data based research analysis where, for each country, we analyzed the related data for fifty six years, from 1960 to 2016, to assess the relationship between the carbon emission and income level. After performing the related data analysis for each country, we concluded whether the results for that country were in line with the Environmental Kuznets Curve (EKC) hypothesis. The EKC hypothesis suggests that the carbon emission per capita starts a declining trend when the country specific high level of income is reached. The results of our data analyses show that the EKC hypothesis is valid for high income countries and the declining trends of carbon emission are clearly observed when the income level reaches a specific high enough level. On the other hand, for the non high income countries, our analysis results show that it is too early to make an assessment at this growth stage of their economies because they have not reached their related high enough income per capita levels yet. Furthermore, we performed two more additional analyses on high income countries. First, we analyzed the related starting years of their carbon emission declining trends. The big variance in the starting years of the carbon emission declining trends shows that the international policies are clearly ineffective in initiating the declining trend in carbon emission. In addition, for the high income countries, we explained the differences in their carbon emission per capita levels in 2014 with their SGI indices and their dependence on high carbon emission energy production.

摘要 : 在这篇文章中，我们提出了环境污染和收入水平之间的关系选定的24个国家。我们实施了一项基于数据的研究分析，针对每个国家，我们分析了从1960年到2016年56年的相关数据，以评估碳排放和收入水平之间的关系。在对每个国家进行相关数据分析后，我们得出结论，该国的结果是否符合环境库兹涅茨曲线（EKC）假说。EKC假说认为，当达到特定国家的高收入水平时，人均碳排放量开始下降。我们的数据分析结果表明，EKC假说对高收入国家是有效的，当收入水平达到一个足够高的水平时，碳排放量的下降趋势是明显的。另一方面，对于非高收入国家，我们的分析结果表明，由于它们还没有达到相应的足够高的人均收入水平，因此现在对其经济增长阶段进行评估还为时过早。此外，我们还对高收入国家进行了另外两项分析。首先，我们分析了相关起始年份的碳排放下降趋势。碳排放下降趋势的起始年份差异较大，表明国际政策在启动碳排放下降趋势方面明显失效。此外，对于高收入国家，我们用SGI指数解释了其2014年人均碳排放水平的差异及其对高碳排放能源生产的依赖性。

\[5\] How residence permits affect the labor market attachment of foreign workers

标题 : 居住证对外来劳动力市场依恋的影响

作者 : Berno Buechel, Selina Gangl, Martin Huber , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.11840

说明 : -

Abstract : We analyze the impact of obtaining a residence permit on foreign workers labor market and residential attachment. To overcome the usually severe selection issues, we exploit a unique migration lottery that randomly assigns access to otherwise restricted residence permits in Liechtenstein (situated between Austria and Switzerland). Using an instrumental variable approach, our results show that lottery compliers (whose migration behavior complies with the assignment in their first lottery) raise their employment probability in Liechtenstein by on average 24 percentage points across outcome periods (2008 to 2018) as a result of receiving a permit. Relatedly, their activity level and employment duration in Liechtenstein increase by on average 20 percentage points and 1.15 years, respectively, over the outcome window. These substantial and statistically significant effects are mainly driven by individuals not (yet) working in Liechtenstein prior to the lottery rather than by previous cross border commuters. Moreover, we find both the labor market and residential effects to be persistent even several years after the lottery with no sign of fading out. These results suggest that granting resident permits to foreign workers can be effective to foster labor supply even beyond the effect of cross border commuting from adjacent regions.

摘要 : 我们分析了获得居留许可对外籍工人劳动力市场和居住依附的影响。为了克服通常严重的选择问题，我们利用了一种独特的移民彩票，随机分配访问列支敦士登（位于奥地利和瑞士之间）其他限制居住许可。使用工具变量法，我们的结果表明，彩票编辑（其移民行为符合其第一次彩票中的分配）在结果期（2008年至2018年）内，由于获得许可证，其在列支敦士登的就业概率平均提高了24个百分点。与此相关的是，他们在列支敦士登的活动水平和就业期限在结果窗口期内分别平均增加了20个百分点和1.15年。这些实质性的和统计上显著的影响主要是由彩票发行前没有（尚未）在列支敦士登工作的个人推动的，而不是由以前的跨境通勤者推动的。此外，我们发现劳动力市场和居住效应即使在彩票发行几年后仍然持续存在，而且没有消失的迹象。这些结果表明，向外籍工人发放居住许可证可以有效地促进劳动力供应，甚至超越了从邻近地区跨境通勤的影响。

\[7\] Conditional Non Lattice Integration, Pricing and Superhedging

标题 : 条件非格积分、定价与超边缘化

作者 : Christian Bender, Sebastian E. Ferrando, Alfredo L. Gonzalez , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.12072

说明 : Comments: 44 pages

Abstract : Closely motivated by financial considerations, we develop an integration theory which is not classical i.e. it is not necessarily associated to a measure. The base space, denoted by mathcal S and called a trajectory space, substitutes the set Omega in probability theory and provides a fundamental structure via conditional subsets mathcal S (S,j) that allows the definition of conditional integrals. The setting is a natural by product of no arbitrage assumptions that are used to model financial markets and games of chance (in a discrete infinite time framework). The constructed conditional integrals can be interpreted as required investments, at the conditioning node, for hedging an integrable function, the latter characterized a.e. and in the limit as we increase the number of portfolios used. The integral is not classical due to the fact that the original vector space of portfolio payoffs is not a vector lattice. In contrast to a classical stochastic setting, where price processes are associated to conditional expectations (with respect to risk neutral measures), we uncover a theory where prices are naturally given by conditional non lattice integrals. One could then study analogues of classical probabilistic notions in such non classical setting, the paper stops after defining trajectorial martingales the study of which is deferred to future work.

摘要 : 在财务考虑的密切推动下，我们发展了一个非经典的整合理论，即它不一定与度量相关联。用数学S表示的基空间称为轨迹空间，它代替概率论中的集合欧米茄，并通过允许定义条件积分的条件子集数学S（S，j）提供基本结构。这种设置是无套利假设的自然副产品，用于模拟金融市场和机会博弈（在离散无限时间框架内）。构造的条件积分可以被解释为在条件节点上对冲可积函数所需的投资，后者的特征是a.e.并且随着我们增加所使用的投资组合的数量而处于极限。由于投资组合收益的原始向量空间不是向量格，所以积分不是经典的。与经典的随机设置（价格过程与条件期望（关于风险中性度量）相关）不同，我们揭示了一个理论，其中价格自然由条件非格积分给出。在这样的非经典背景下，我们可以研究经典概率概念的类似物，本文在定义了轨迹鞅之后就停止了，对它的研究将推迟到以后的工作。

Abstract : Quantum mechanics is well known to accelerate statistical sampling processes over classical techniques. In quantitative finance, statistical samplings arise broadly in many use cases. \[9\] Deprivation, Crime, and Abandonment

作者 : Scott W. Hegerty , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.10567

说明 : -

Abstract : Both within the United States and worldwide, the city of Detroit has become synonymous with economic decline, depopulation, and crime. Is Detroit s situation unique, or can similar neighborhoods be found elsewhere This study examines Census block group data, as well as local crime statistics for 2014, for a set of five Midwestern cities. Roughly three percent of Chicago s and Milwaukee s block groups all of which are in majority nonwhite areas exceed Detroit s median values for certain crimes, vacancies, and a poverty measure. This figure rises to 11 percent for St. Louis, while Minneapolis has only a single Detroit like block group.
