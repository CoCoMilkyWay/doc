\[1\] General Compound Hawkes Processes for Mid Price Prediction

标题 : 中等价格预测的一般复合Hawkes过程

作者 : Myles Sjogren (1), Timothy DeLise (2) ((1) University of Calgary, (2) Université de Montréal) , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.07075

说明 : Comments: 20 pages, 21 figures

Abstract : High frequency financial data is burdened by a level of randomness that is unavoidable and obfuscates the task of modelling. This idea is reflected in the intraday evolution of limit orders book data for many financial assets and suggests several justifications for the use of stochastic models. For instance, the arbitrary distribution of inter arrival times and the subsequent dependence structure between consecutive book events. This has lead to the development of many stochastic models for the dynamics of limit order books. In this paper we look to examine the adaptability of one family of such models, the General Compound Hawkes Process (GCHP) models, to new data and new tasks. We further focus on the prediction problem for the mid price within a limit order book and the practical applications of these stochastic models, which is the main contribution of this paper. To this end we examine the use of the GCHP for predicting the direction and volatility of futures and stock data and discuss possible extensions of the model to help improve its predictive capabilities.

摘要 : 高频金融数据的随机性是不可避免的，并且混淆了建模任务。这一想法反映在许多金融资产的限额订单账面数据的日内演变中，并提出了使用随机模型的若干理由。例如，到达时间的任意分布以及连续书本事件之间的后续依赖结构。这导致了许多随机模型的发展，为动态的限价订单书籍。在本文中，我们将考察一系列此类模型，即通用复合霍克斯过程（GCHP）模型对新数据和新任务的适应性。本文的主要贡献是进一步研究了限价订单中的中间价预测问题以及这些随机模型的实际应用。为此，我们研究了GCHP在预测期货和股票数据的方向和波动性方面的应用，并讨论了模型的可能扩展，以帮助提高其预测能力。

\[2\] ETF Risk Models

标题 : ETF风险模型

作者 : Zura Kakushadze, Willie Yu , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.07138

说明 : Comments: 20 pages; to appear in Bulletin of Applied Economics (in press)

Abstract : We discuss how to build ETF risk models. Our approach anchors on i) first building a multilevel (non )binary classification taxonomy for ETFs, which is utilized in order to define the risk factors, and ii) then building the risk models based on these risk factors by utilizing the heterotic risk model construction of https ssrn.com abstract 2600798 (for binary classifications) or general risk model construction of https ssrn.com abstract 2722093 (for non binary classifications). We discuss how to build an ETF taxonomy using ETF constituent data. A multilevel ETF taxonomy can also be constructed by appropriately augmenting and expanding well built and granular third party single level ETF groupings.

摘要 : 我们讨论如何建立ETF风险模型。我们的方法基于i）首先构建ETF的多级（非）二元分类法，用于定义风险因素；ii）然后利用https ssrn.com摘要2600798的杂合风险模型构建基于这些风险因素的风险模型（用于二元分类）或https ssrn.com摘要2722093（非二进制分类）的一般风险模型构建。我们将讨论如何使用ETF成分数据构建ETF分类。多层次ETF分类法也可以通过适当地增加和扩展构建良好的细粒度第三方单层次ETF分组来构建。

\[3\] An Empirical Analysis of how Internet Access Influences Public Opinion towards Undocumented Immigrants and Unaccompanied Children

标题 : 互联网接入对无证移民和孤身儿童舆论影响的实证分析

作者 : Muhammad Hassan Bin Afzal , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.07489

说明 : Comments: 20 pages, 2 Figures, 2 Tables

Abstract : This research adds to the expanding field of data driven analysis, scientific modeling, and forecasting on the impact of having access to the Internet and IoT on the general US population regarding immigrants and immigration policies. More specifically, this research focuses on the public opinion of undocumented immigrants in the United States and having access to the Internet in their local settings. The term Undocumented Immigrants refers to those who live in the United States without legal papers, documents, or visas. Undocumented immigrants may have come into the country unlawfully or with valid documentation, but their legal status has expired. Using the 2020 American National Election Studies (ANES) time series dataset, I investigated the relationship between internet access (A2I) and public perception of undocumented immigrants. According to my research and analysis, increasing internet access among non Hispanic whites with at least a bachelors degree with an annual household income of less than 99K is more likely to oppose the deportation of undocumented immigrants and separating unaccompanied children from their families in borderland areas. The individuals with substantial Republican political ideology exhibit significantly lower opposing effects in deporting undocumented immigrants or separating unaccompanied children from their families. The evidence from multiple statistical models is resilient to a variety of factors. The findings show that increased internet access may improve undocumented immigrants social integration and acceptability. During health emergencies, it may be especially beneficial to make them feel safe, included, and supported in their local settings.

摘要 : 这项研究为数据驱动分析、科学建模和预测接入互联网和物联网对普通美国人口移民和移民政策影响的扩展领域增添了新的内容。更具体地说，这项研究侧重于美国无证移民的公众意见，以及在当地环境中访问互联网的情况。“无证件移民”一词是指那些居住在美国而没有法律文件、文件或签证的人。无证件移民可能非法入境或持有有效证件，但其法律地位已过期。利用2020年美国全国选举研究（ANES）时间序列数据集，我调查了互联网接入（A2I）与公众对无证移民的认知之间的关系。根据我的研究和分析，在拥有至少学士学位、家庭年收入低于99K的非西班牙裔白人中增加互联网接入，更有可能反对驱逐无证移民和将无人陪伴的儿童与边境地区的家庭分离。具有浓厚共和党政治意识形态的个人在驱逐无证移民或将孤身儿童与家人分离方面表现出明显较低的反对效果。来自多个统计模型的证据对各种因素具有弹性。调查结果表明，增加互联网接入可能会改善无证移民的社会融合和可接受性。在突发卫生事件期间，让他们在当地环境中感到安全、包容和支持可能特别有益。

\[4\] Ordinal Synchronization and Typical States in High Frequency Digital Markets

标题 : 高频数字市场中的顺序同步和典型状态

作者 : Mario López Pérez, Ricardo Mansilla , 类别 ：Adaptation and Self-Organizing Systems (nlin.AO)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.07047

说明 : Comments: 29 pages, 22 figures

Abstract : In this paper we show, through the study of ordinal patterns, information theoretic and network measures and clustering algorithms, the presence of typical states in automated high frequency records during a one year period in the US stock market, characterized by their degree of centralized or descentralized synchronicity. We also find two whole coherent seasons of highly centralized and descentralized synchronicity, respectively.

摘要 : 在本文中，我们通过对顺序模式、信息论和网络度量以及聚类算法的研究，展示了美国股市在一年期间自动高频记录中存在的典型状态，其特征是集中或分散的同步性程度。我们还发现了两个完整的相干季节，分别具有高度集中和去中心的同步性。

\[5\] ESG and Sovereign Risk

标题 : ESG与主权风险

作者 : Raphaël Semet, Thierry Roncalli, Lauren Stagnol , 类别 ：Pricing of Securities (q-fin.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.06617

说明 : Comments: 102 pages, 22 figures, 69 tables

Abstract : In this paper, we examine the materiality of ESG on country creditworthiness from a credit risk and fundamental analysis viewpoint. We first determine the ESG indicators that are most relevant when it comes to explaining the sovereign bond yield, after controlling the effects of traditional fundamental variables such as economic strength and credit rating. We also emphasize the major themes that are directly useful for investors when assessing the country risk premium. At the global level, we notice that these themes mainly belong to the E and G pillars. Those results confirm that extra financial criteria are integrated into bond pricing. However, we also identify a clear difference between high and middle income countries. Indeed, whereas the S pillar is lagging for the highest income countries, it is nearly as important as the G pillar for the middle income ones. Second, we determine which ESG metrics are indirectly valuable for assessing a country s solvency. More precisely, we attempt to infer credit rating solely from extra financial criteria, that is the ESG indicators that are priced in by credit rating agencies. We find that there is no overlap between the set of indicators that predict credit ratings and those that directly explain sovereign bond yields. The results also highlight the importance of the G and S pillars when predicting credit ratings. The E pillar is lagging, suggesting that credit rating agencies are undermining the impact of climate change and environmental topics on country creditworthiness. This is consistent with the traditional view that social and governance issues are the main drivers of the sovereign risk, because they are more specific and less global than environmental issues. Finally, taking these different results together, this research shows that opposing extra financial and fundamental analysis does not make a lot of sense.

摘要 : 在本文中，我们从信用风险和基本分析的角度考察了ESG对国家信用的重要性。在控制经济实力和信用评级等传统基本变量的影响后，我们首先确定在解释主权债券收益率时最相关的ESG指标。我们还强调了在评估国家风险溢价时对投资者直接有用的主要主题。在全球一级，我们注意到这些主题主要属于E和G支柱。这些结果证实了额外的财务标准被整合到债券定价中。然而，我们也发现了高收入国家和中等收入国家之间的明显差异。事实上，尽管S支柱对最高收入国家来说相对滞后，但对中等收入国家来说，它几乎与G支柱同等重要。其次，我们确定哪些ESG指标对评估一个国家的偿付能力具有间接价值。更准确地说，我们试图仅从额外的财务标准推断信用评级，即信用评级机构定价的ESG指标。我们发现，预测信用评级的指标集与直接解释主权债券收益率的指标集之间没有重叠。该结果还突出了G和S支柱在预测信用评级时的重要性。E支柱滞后，表明信用评级机构正在削弱气候变化和环境议题对国家信誉的影响。这与传统观点一致，即社会和治理问题是主权风险的主要驱动因素，因为它们比环境问题更具体，更不具全球性。最后，把这些不同的结果放在一起，这项研究表明，反对额外的财务和基本面分析没有多大意义。

\[6\] Interpreting the Caste based Earning Gaps in the Indian Labour Market

标题 : 解读印度劳动力市场中基于种姓的收入差距

作者 : Pallavi Gupta (BSE Institute Ltd., Mumbai, INDIA), Satyanarayan Kothe (Mumbai School of Economics and Public Policy, University of Mumbai, INDIA) , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.06822

说明 : Comments: Keywords: inequality, wage discrimination, Theil index, Theil decomposition, Oaxaca Three-Fold decomposition, NSSO-EUS 68th round JEL Classification: J01, J08, J15, J30, J31, J71

Abstract : The UN states that inequalities are determined along with income by other factors gender, age, origin, ethnicity, disability, sexual orientation, class, and religion. India, since the ancient period, has socio political stratification that induced socio economic inequality and continued till now. There have been attempts to reduce socio economic inequality through policy interventions since the first plan, still there are evidences of social and economic discrimination. This paper examines earning gaps between the forward castes and the traditionally disadvantaged caste workers in the Indian labour market using two distinct estimation methods. First, we interpret the inequality indicator of the Theil index and decompose Theil to show within and between group inequalities. Second, a Threefold Oaxaca Decomposition is employed to break the earnings differentials into components of endowment, coefficient and interaction. Earnings gaps are examined separately in urban and rural divisions. Within group, inequalities are found larger than between groups across variables with a higher overall inequality for forward castes. A high endowment is observed which implies pre market discrimination in human capital investment such as nutrition and education. Policymakers should first invest in basic quality education and simultaneously expand post graduate diploma opportunities, subsequently increasing the participation in the labour force for the traditionally disadvantaged in disciplines and occupations where the forward castes have long dominated.

摘要 : 联合国指出，不平等与收入一起由性别、年龄、出身、种族、残疾、性取向、阶级和宗教等其他因素决定。印度自古以来就存在社会政治分层，导致社会经济不平等，并一直持续到现在。自第一个计划以来，一直有人试图通过政策干预减少社会经济不平等，但仍有证据表明存在社会和经济歧视。本文采用两种不同的估计方法，考察了印度劳动力市场上的前级种姓和传统弱势种姓工人之间的收入差距。首先，我们解释了泰尔指数的不平等指标，并对泰尔进行分解，以显示组内和组间的不平等。其次，采用三重瓦哈卡分解将收入差异分解为禀赋、系数和相互作用三个部分。收入差距分别在城市和农村部门进行检查。在群体内部，不同变量之间的不平等性比群体之间的不平等性更大，且向前种姓的总体不平等性更高。观察到高捐赠，这意味着在营养和教育等人力资本投资方面存在市场前歧视。政策制定者应该首先投资于基础素质教育，同时扩大研究生文凭的机会，从而增加传统上处于不利地位的人在前沿种姓长期占据主导地位的学科和职业中的劳动力参与。

\[7\] Appointments

标题 : 约会

作者 : Laura Derksen, Jason Kerwin, Natalia Ordaz Reynoso, Olivier Sterck , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.06876

说明 : -

Abstract : Health behaviors are plagued by self control problems, and commitment devices are frequently proposed as a solution. We show that a simple alternative works even better appointments. We randomly offer HIV testing appointments and financial commitment devices to high risk men in Malawi. Appointments are much more effective than financial commitment devices, more than doubling testing rates. In contrast, most men who take up financial commitment devices lose their investments. Appointments address procrastination without the potential drawback of commitment failure, and also address limited memory problems. Appointments have the potential to increase demand for healthcare in the developing world.

摘要 : 健康行为受到自我控制问题的困扰，承诺装置经常被提出作为解决方案。我们证明了一个简单的替代方案甚至可以更好地工作。我们随机为马拉维的高危男性提供艾滋病毒检测预约和财务承诺设备。预约比财务承诺手段更有效，检测率翻了一番还多。相比之下，大多数使用金融承诺工具的男性会失去投资。预约解决了拖延问题，但没有承诺失败的潜在缺点，也解决了记忆有限的问题。预约有可能增加发展中国家对医疗保健的需求。

\[8\] Data driven distributionally robust risk parity portfolio optimization

标题 : 数据驱动的分布稳健风险平价投资组合优化

作者 : Giorgio Costa, Roy H. Kwon , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.06464

说明 : -

Abstract : We propose a distributionally robust formulation of the traditional risk parity portfolio optimization problem. Distributional robustness is introduced by targeting the discrete probabilities attached to each observation used during parameter estimation. Instead of assuming that all observations are equally likely, we consider an ambiguity set that provides us with the flexibility to find the most adversarial probability distribution based on the investor s desired degree of robustness. This allows us to derive robust estimates to parametrize the distribution of asset returns without having to impose any particular structure on the data. The resulting distributionally robust optimization problem is a constrained convex concave minimax problem. Our approach is financially meaningful and attempts to attain full risk diversification with respect to the worst case instance of the portfolio risk measure. We propose a novel algorithmic approach to solve this minimax problem, which blends projected gradient ascent with sequential convex programming. By design, this algorithm is highly flexible and allows the user to choose among alternative statistical distance measures to define the ambiguity set. Moreover, the algorithm is highly tractable and scalable. Our numerical experiments suggest that a distributionally robust risk parity portfolio can yield a higher risk adjusted rate of return when compared against the nominal portfolio.

摘要 : 我们提出了传统风险平价投资组合优化问题的分布稳健公式。分布稳健性是通过针对参数估计过程中使用的每个观测值的离散概率引入的。而不是假设所有的观测是相同的可能性，我们认为一个模糊集，为我们提供了灵活性，找到最敌对的概率分布的基础上，投资者的期望程度的鲁棒性。这使我们能够得出稳健的估计，以参数化资产回报的分布，而不必对数据施加任何特定的结构。由此产生的分布鲁棒优化问题是一个约束凸凹极大极小问题。我们的方法在财务上是有意义的，并试图在投资组合风险度量的最坏情况下实现完全的风险分散。我们提出了一种新的算法来解决这个极小极大问题，它将投影梯度上升和序列凸规划相结合。通过设计，该算法具有高度的灵活性，允许用户选择其他统计距离度量来定义模糊度集。此外，该算法具有高度的可处理性和可扩展性。我们的数值实验表明，与名义投资组合相比，分布稳健的风险平价投资组合可以产生更高的风险调整回报率。

\[9\] Towards a fully RL based Market Simulator

标题 : 走向完全基于RL的市场模拟器

作者 : Leo Ardon, Nelson Vadori, Thomas Spooner, Mengda Xu, Jared Vann, Sumitra Ganesh , 类别 ：Multiagent Systems (cs.MA)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.06829

说明 : Comments: Published at ICAIF'21: ACM International Conference on AI in Finance

Abstract : We present a new financial framework where two families of RL based agents representing the Liquidity Providers and Liquidity Takers learn simultaneously to satisfy their objective. Thanks to a parametrized reward formulation and the use of Deep RL, each group learns a shared policy able to generalize and interpolate over a wide range of behaviors. This is a step towards a fully RL based market simulator replicating complex market conditions particularly suited to study the dynamics of the financial market under various scenarios.

摘要 : 我们提出了一个新的财务框架，其中代表流动性提供者和流动性接受者的两个基于RL的代理家族同时学习以满足其目标。由于参数化的奖励公式和深度RL的使用，每个小组学习一个共享策略，能够概括和插值广泛的行为。这是向完全基于RL的市场模拟器迈出的一步，该模拟器复制了复杂的市场条件，特别适合研究各种情景下的金融市场动态。
