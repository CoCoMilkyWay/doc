\[1\] And the children shall lead: Gender diversity and performance in venture capital

标题:孩子们将成为领导者：风险投资中的性别多样性和绩效

作者:Sophie Calder-Wang,Paul A. Gompers

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001483

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.06.026

Abstract : Given overall lack of gender diversity in the venture capital and entrepreneurship industry shown in Calder-Wang and Gompers (2017) we ask: What promotes greater gender diversity in hiring? Does diversity lead to better firm performance and higher investment returns? In this paper, using a unique dataset of the gender of venture capital partners’ children, we find strong evidence that when partners have more daughters, the propensity to hire female partners increases. Moreover, our instrumental variable results suggest that increased gender diversity improves deal and fund performance. Keywords : Venture capital;Gender diversity

Abstract :鉴于Calder Wang和Gompers（2017）中所示的风险投资和创业行业总体上缺乏性别多样性，我们问：是什么促进了招聘中更大的性别多样性？多元化是否会带来更好的公司业绩和更高的投资回报？在本文中，我们使用一个独特的风险投资合伙人子女性别数据集，发现强有力的证据表明，当合伙人有更多的女儿时，雇用女性合伙人的倾向增加。此外，我们的工具变量结果表明，增加性别多样性可以改善交易和基金绩效。最后，影响主要由高级伙伴子女的性别决定。

\[2\] Hedging macroeconomic and financial uncertainty and volatility

标题:对冲宏观经济和金融不确定性和波动性

作者:Ian Dew-Becker,Stefano Giglio,Bryan Kelly

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002476

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.053

Abstract : We study the pricing of shocks to uncertainty and volatility using a wide-ranging set of options contracts covering a variety of different markets. If uncertainty shocks are viewed as bad by investors, they should carry negative risk premiums. Empirically, however, uncertainty risk premiums are positive in most markets. Instead, it is the realization of large shocks to fundamentals that has historically carried a negative premium. In other words, we find that the return premium for gamma is negative, while that for vega is positive. These results imply that it is jumps, for which exposure is measured by gamma, not forward-looking uncertainty shocks, measured by vega, that drive investors’ marginal utility. In further support of the jump interpretation, the return patterns are more extreme for deeper out-of-the-money options.

Keywords : -

Abstract :我们使用涵盖各种不同市场的一系列广泛的期权合同来研究对不确定性和波动性的冲击的定价。如果投资者认为不确定性冲击是坏的，那么它们应该带来负风险溢价。然而，从经验上看，不确定性风险溢价在大多数市场都是正的。相反，对基本面的巨大冲击的实现在历史上带来了负溢价。换句话说，我们发现gamma的回报溢价为负，而vega的回报溢价为正。这些结果表明，驱动投资者边际效用的是跳跃，其风险敞口由伽马测量，而不是前瞻性不确定性冲击（由vega测量）。为了进一步支持跳跃式解释，收益模式对于更深层次的货币期权来说更为极端。

\[3\] Anatomy of a liquidity crisis: Corporate bonds in the COVID-19 crisis

标题:流动性危机剖析2019冠状病毒疾病公司债券

作者:Maureen O'Hara,Xing (Alex) Zhou

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002464

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.052

Abstract : We examine the microstructure of liquidity provision in the COVID-19 corporate bond liquidity crisis. During the two weeks leading up to Federal Reserve System interventions, volume shifted to liquid securities, transaction costs soared, trade-size pricing inverted, and dealers, particularly non-primary dealers, shifted from buying to selling, causing dealers’ inventories to plummet. Liquidity provisions in electronic customer-to-customer trading increased, though at prohibitively high costs. By improving dealer funding conditions and providing a liquidity backstop, the Primary Dealer Credit Facility and the Secondary Market Corporate Credit Facility (SMCCF) stabilized trading conditions. Most of the impact of SMCCF on bond liquidity seems to have materialized following its announcement. We argue that the Federal Reserve's actions reflect a new role as market maker of last resort.

Abstract : We examine whether the concern about insurers selling similar assets due to an overlap in holdings is justified. \[5\] Should information be sold separately? Evidence from MiFID II

标题:信息应该单独出售吗？来自MiFID II的证据

作者:Yifeng Guo,Lira Mota

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002300

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.037

Abstract : Information production is key to the efficiency of financial markets. Does selling information separately improve its production? We investigate this question using MiFID II, a European regulation that unbundles research from transactions. We show that unbundling causes fewer research analysts to cover a firm. This decrease does not come from small- or mid-cap firms but is concentrated in large firms. Surprisingly, the reduction in analyst coverage is accompanied by a decrease in forecast error. Further analyses suggest that analyst competition enhancement could drive the results: inaccurate analysts drop out, and analysts who stay produce more accurate research.

Keywords : Analyst research;Information production;MiFID II;Unbundling

Abstract : Labor market frictions help explain the credit spread puzzle. \[7\] Entangled risks in incomplete FX markets

作者:Thomas Maurer,Ngoc-Khanh Tran

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002452

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.051

Abstract : We introduce the concept of risk entanglement in a preference-free setting to jointly explain the exchange rate volatility, cyclicality, and currency risk premia in the data. Risk entanglement specifies a subset of incomplete market models, in which nondiffusive or nonlog-normal shocks to exchange rates are not fully spanned by asset returns. When risks are entangled, there exist multiple pricing-consistent exchange rates, but none of them are equal to the ratio of the stochastic discount factors (SDFs) or their projections. \[9\] The impact of arbitrage on market liquidity

标题:套利对市场流动性的影响

作者:Dominik Rösch

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21001641

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.04.034

Abstract : I study how arbitrage affects liquidity by analyzing several billion trades in the American Depositary Receipt (ADR) market from 2001 to 2016. Price deviations persist, on average, for 12 min, and mainly arise because of price pressure. Impulse response functions estimated at 1 min intervals indicate that a positive shock to arbitrage—simultaneous trades of the ADR and the home-market share in the opposite direction—decreases deviations and bid-ask spreads. Abstract : We exploit the 2011 EBA Capital exercise, a quasi-natural experiment that required a number of banks to increase their regulatory capital. This experiment makes secured lending for the affected banks more attractive vis-à-vis unsecured lending, because secured loans require less regulatory capital. Using loan-level data covering the universe of bank loans in Portugal, we identify how banks require collateral on new loans when facing higher capital requirements: relative to the control group, treated banks require loans to be collateralized more often after the shock.
