\[1\] Information Acquisition Experience, Investor Sophistication, and IPO Price Pressure

标题:信息获取经验、投资者成熟度和IPO价格压力

作者:Gerrit Köchling,Peter N. Posch,Philipp Schmidtke

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3793468

Abstract : We propose a simple measure of investor sophistication based on financial statement experience derived from publicly available EDGAR log data about accounting information acquisition activity. This approach allows us to provide unique empirical evidence for the existence of attention induced price pressure effects in the cross-section of initial public offerings, i.e. that pre-IPO-week attention from likely unsophisticated investors is associated with higher initial returns and subsequent, significant price depreciations. These results are robust to various measures of abnormal post-IPO returns, attention, and sophistication. The proposed direct experience-measurement is easily replicable and potentially useful for many other questions in economics.

Keywords : information acquisition, investor sophistication, EDGAR, price pressure, initial public offering, disclosure processing costs

Abstract :我们提出了一个简单的衡量投资者成熟度的方法，该方法基于从公开的EDGAR日志数据中获得的有关会计信息获取活动的财务报表经验。这种方法使我们能够提供独特的经验证据，证明首次公开发行（IPO）的横截面中存在注意力诱导的价格压力效应，即，来自可能不成熟的投资者的IPO前一周的注意力与较高的初始回报率和随后的重大价格贬值有关。这些结果对IPO后异常回报、关注度和成熟度的各种衡量指标都是稳健的。所提出的直接经验度量方法易于复制，并可能对经济学中的许多其他问题有用。

Keywords :信息获取、投资者成熟度、EDGAR、价格压力、首次公开发行、披露处理成本

\[2\] Bond Valuation and Default Risk Reexamined: A Post Reduced Form Model Based on Integer Migration and Portfolio Migration

标题:债券估值与违约风险再审视：基于整数迁移和组合迁移的后简化模型

作者:Brian Barnard

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3812202

Abstract : The study reexamines bond valuation and default risk by considering and relaxing assumptions regarding integer rating migration and portfolio rating migration, and consequently the implied degree of diversification. A reduced form model, based on (a) rating migration (matrix) serves as the basis and starting point. The study subsequently moves towards a post reduced form bond valuation model by considering portfolio migration and integer migration. Portfolio migration refers to actual counts of issues migrating across rating categories between the intervals of the portfolio—the number of issues with a particular rating at a particular point in time. Integer migration considers the fact that only integer counts of issues can migrate. Both portfolio and integer migration imply more sophisticated interpretation of rating migration matrices. Portfolio migration and integer migration result in interval rating category issue count probability distributions and issue interval rating category intensity (intensity as a probability of occurrence) probability distributions, and consequently cause more sophisticated issue interval cash flow distributions to form. The resultant post reduced form model issue price results are encouraging and promising, and are briefly considered.

Keywords : None

Abstract :本研究通过考虑和放宽关于整体评级迁移和投资组合评级迁移的假设，进而考虑隐含的多元化程度，重新审视债券估值和违约风险。基于（A）评级迁移（矩阵）的简化模型是基础和起点。随后，本研究通过考虑投资组合迁移和整数迁移，建立了一个简化后的债券估值模型。投资组合迁移指的是在投资组合的时间间隔之间，跨评级类别迁移的问题的实际计数在特定时间点具有特定评级的问题的数量。整数迁移考虑到只有整数计数的问题才能迁移。投资组合和整数迁移都意味着评级迁移矩阵的解释更加复杂。投资组合迁移和整数迁移导致了区间评级类别发行计数概率分布和发行区间评级类别强度（强度作为发生概率）概率分布，从而形成更复杂的发行区间现金流分布。由此产生的后简化模型发行价格的结果是令人鼓舞的和有希望的，并简要考虑。

Keywords :None

\[3\] Co-opetition and Corporate Disclosure of Narrative R&D Information

标题:合作竞争与企业叙述性R&D信息披露

作者:Yangyang Chen,Gaoliang Tian,Xuan WU,Zilong Zhang

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3793298

Abstract : Firms engage in co-opetition (simultaneously competing and cooperating) behaviors when participating in Technology Standard Setting Organizations (SSOs). SSOs are organizations where competing firms and/or other technological institutions collaborate to develop standards for new technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, SSD, and 5G. Firms share their proprietary R&D information with competitors when cooperating to develop technology standards. We examine whether such co-opetition behaviors at SSOs alter firms’ incentives to publicly disclose narrative R&D information. We find that firms increase quantity and detailedness of narrative R&D disclosure after participating in SSOs. However, we find no evidence that firms adjust their disclosure of other non-R&D proprietary information (customer identity information) after participating in SSOs. The results imply that proprietary information can be multidimensional, and that empirical examinations of firms’ proprietary disclosure require the type of proprietary information to be relevant to the economic factor under examination. The main results of this study remain robust after using alternative SSO participation measures and using an instrumental variable approach.

Keywords : Co-opetition, Standard Setting Organizations, Proprietary Information, R&D Disclosure

Abstract :企业在参与技术标准制定组织（sso）时，存在着合作竞争行为。SSO是竞争公司和/或其他技术机构合作开发新技术标准的组织，如蓝牙、Wi-Fi、SSD和5G。公司在合作开发技术标准时与竞争对手共享其专有研发信息。我们检验SSO的这种合作竞争行为是否改变了企业公开披露叙述性研发信息的动机。研究发现，加入SSOs后，企业叙述性R&D信息披露的数量和细节都有所增加。然而，我们没有发现任何证据表明企业在参与SSO后会调整其对其他非研发专有信息（客户身份信息）的披露。结果表明，专有信息可以是多维的，而对企业专有信息披露的实证检验要求专有信息的类型与被检验的经济因素相关。本研究的主要结果在使用替代SSO参与措施和工具变量方法后仍然稳健。

Keywords :合作竞争、标准制定机构、专有信息、研发披露

\[4\] Adaptive Robust Large Volatility Matrix Estimation Based on High-Frequency Financial Data

标题:基于高频金融数据的自适应鲁棒大波动矩阵估计

作者:Minseok Shin,Donggyu Kim,Jianqing Fan

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3793394

Abstract : Several novel statistical methods have been developed to estimate large integrated volatility matrices based on high-frequency financial data. To investigate their asymptotic behaviors, they require a sub-Gaussian or finite high-order moment assumption for observed log-returns, which cannot account for the heavy tail phenomenon of stock returns. Recently, a robust estimator was developed to handle heavy-tailed distributions with some bounded fourth-moment assumption. However, we often observe that log-returns have heavier tail distribution than the finite fourth-moment and that the degrees of heaviness of tails are heterogeneous over the asset and time period. In this paper, to deal with the heterogeneous heavy-tailed distributions, we develop an adaptive robust integrated volatility estimator that employs pre-averaging and truncation schemes based on jump-diffusion processes. We call this an adaptive robust pre-averaging realized volatility (ARP) estimator. We show that the ARP estimator has a sub-Weibull tail concentration with only finite 2α-th moments for any α > 1. In addition, we establish matching upper and lower bounds to show that the ARP estimation procedure is optimal. To estimate large integrated volatility matrices using the approximate factor model, the ARP estimator is further regularized using the principal orthogonal complement thresholding (POET) method. The numerical study is conducted to check the finite sample performance of the ARP estimator.

Keywords : Heterogeneity, tail index, pre-averaging, minimax lower bound, optimality, POET, factor model

Abstract :基于高频金融数据，已经发展了几种新的统计方法来估计大型综合波动率矩阵。为了研究它们的渐近行为，需要对观测到的对数收益率进行次高斯或有限高阶矩假设，这不能解释股票收益率的重尾现象。近年来，人们发展了一种鲁棒估计来处理具有有界四阶矩假设的重尾分布。然而，我们经常观察到，对数收益率具有比有限四阶矩更重的尾部分布，并且尾部的权重在资产和时间段上是不均匀的。本文针对非均匀重尾分布，提出了一种基于跳扩散过程的自适应鲁棒综合波动率估计方法。我们称之为自适应鲁棒预平均已实现波动率（ARP）估计。我们证明了当α>1时，ARP估计具有一个只有有限2阶矩的次Weibull尾集中。此外，我们建立了匹配的上下界来证明ARP估计过程是最优的。为了利用近似因子模型估计大的综合波动率矩阵，采用主正交补码阈值法（POET）对ARP估计量进行了进一步的正则化。数值研究验证了ARP估计的有限样本性能。

Keywords :异质性，尾部指数，预平均，极大极小下界，最优性，POET，因子模型

\[5\] Optimal Execution with Stochastic Delay

标题:随机时延下的最优执行

作者:Álvaro Cartea,Leandro Sánchez-Betancourt

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3812324

Abstract : We show how traders use marketable limit orders (MLOs) to liquidate a position over a trading window when there is latency in the marketplace. MLOs are liquidity taking orders that specify a price limit and are for immediate execution only; however, if the price limit of the MLO precludes it from being filled, the exchange rejects the trade. We frame our model as an impulse control problem with stochastic latency where the trader controls the times and the price limits of the MLOs sent to the exchange. We show that impatient liquidity takers submit MLOs that may walk the book (capped by the limit price) to increase the probability of filling the trades. On the other hand, patient liquidity takers use speculative MLOs that are only filled if there has been an advantageous move in prices over the latency period. Patient traders who are fast do not use their speed to hit the quotes they observe, nor to finish the execution programme early; they use speed to complete the execution programme with as many speculative MLOs as possible. We use foreign exchange data to implement the random-latency-optimal strategy and to compare it with four benchmarks: execution with deterministic latency, execution with zero latency, time-weighted average price, and execution of the entire order at the best quote in the LOB at the beginning of the trading window. We find that the performances of the random-latency and the deterministic-latency strategies are similar. For patient traders, these two strategies outperform the other three benchmarks by an amount that is greater than the transaction costs paid by liquidity takers in foreign exchange markets. Around news announcements, the value of the outperformance is between two and ten times the value of the transaction costs. The superiority of the latency-optimal strategies is due to both the speculative MLOs that are filled and the price protection of the MLOs.

Keywords : algorithmic trading, high-frequency trading, impulse control, stochastic delay, deterministic delay, latency, fill ratio.

Abstract :我们展示了当市场中存在延迟时，交易者如何使用可交易的限制指令（MLO）来清算交易窗口上的头寸。MLO是一种流动性，接受指定价格限制的指令，并且仅用于立即执行；但是，如果MLO的价格限制使其无法完成，则交易所拒绝交易。我们将我们的模型构建为一个随机延迟的脉冲控制问题，其中交易者控制发送给交易所的MLO的时间和价格限制。我们表明，不耐烦的流动性接受者提交MLO，可能会走账（上限价格），以增加填补交易的概率。另一方面，耐心的流动性接受者使用投机性的MLO，只有在潜伏期内价格出现有利的变动时，这些MLO才会被填补。快速的耐心交易者不会用他们的速度去击中他们观察到的报价，也不会提前完成执行程序；他们用速度来完成执行程序，尽可能多的投机性MLO。我们使用外汇数据来实现随机延迟优化策略，并将其与四个基准进行比较：确定性延迟执行、零延迟执行、时间加权平均价格和交易窗口开始时LOB中最佳报价的整个订单执行。我们发现随机延迟策略和确定性延迟策略的性能是相似的。对于耐心的交易者来说，这两种策略的表现优于其他三种基准，其金额大于外汇市场流动性接受者支付的交易成本。围绕着新闻公告，表现优异的价值是交易成本价值的2到10倍。延迟最优策略的优越性是由于投机性的mlo被填充和mlo的价格保护。

Keywords :算法交易，高频交易，脉冲控制，随机延迟，确定性延迟，延迟，填充率。

\[6\] Sustainability Integration for Sovereign Debt Investors: Engaging with Countries on the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

标题:主权债务投资者的可持续性一体化：与各国就可持续发展目标进行接触

作者:Jan Anton van Zanten,Bhavya Sharma,Malene Christensen

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3812338

Abstract : Investors recently adopted a novel approach to sustainable investing: engaging with countries to advance sustainable development. But engaging with sovereign entities on sustainability challenges – such as those defined by the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) - is a complex endeavor. To guide sovereign debt investors in operationalizing this new sustainable investing strategy, this paper creates a framework that navigates the sovereign engagement process in a systematic manner. The framework answers three questions (i) who to engage with; (ii) what to engage on; and (iii) how to engage. First, relevant countries are identified based on the investor’s (public and private) investment exposure and the country’s progress on the SDGs. Second, using public data, SDGs and sub-targets are identified that face slow progress, thus being priorities to engage on.
