\[1\] Endogenous inverse demand functions

标题 : 内生反需求函数

作者 : Maxim Bichuch, Zachary Feinstein , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08002

说明 : -

Abstract : In this work we present an equilibrium formulation for price impacts. This is motivated by the Buhlmann equilibrium in which assets are sold into a system of market participants and can be viewed as a generalization of the Esscher premium. Existence and uniqueness of clearing prices for the liquidation of a portfolio are studied. We also investigate other desired portfolio properties including monotonicity and concavity. Price per portfolio unit sold is also calculated. In special cases, we study price impacts generated by market participants who follow the exponential utility and power utility.

摘要 : 在这项工作中，我们提出了一个均衡公式的价格影响。这是由Buhlmann均衡所驱动的，在这个均衡中，资产被出售给一个市场参与者系统，可以被看作是对埃舍尔溢价的概括。研究了投资组合清算价格的存在性和唯一性。我们还研究了其他期望的投资组合性质，包括单调性和凹性。每个投资组合单位出售的价格也计算在内。在特殊情况下，我们研究了服从指数效用和幂效用的市场参与者对价格的影响。

\[2\] When does the tail wag the dog Curvature and market making

标题 : 什么时候尾巴摇摆弯曲和做市

作者 : Guillermo Angeris, Alex Evans, Tarun Chitra , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08040

说明 : -

Abstract : Liquidity and trading activity on constant function market makers (CFMMs) such as Uniswap, Curve, and Balancer has grown significantly in the second half of 2020. Much of the growth of these protocols has been driven by incentivized pools or yield farming , which reward participants in crypto assets for providing liquidity to CFMMs. As a result, CFMMs and associated protocols, which were historically very small markets, now constitute the most liquid trading venues for a large number of crypto assets. But what does it mean for a CFMM to be the most liquid market In this paper, we propose a basic definition of price sensitivity and liquidity. We show that this definition is tightly related to the curvature of a CFMM s trading function and can be used to explain a number of heuristic results. For example, we show that low curvature markets are good for coins whose market value is approximately fixed and that high curvature markets are better for liquidity providers when traders have an informational edge. Additionally, the results can also be used to model interacting markets and explain the rise of incentivized liquidity provision, also known as yield farming.

摘要 : Uniswap、Curve和Balancer等固定功能做市商（CFMM）的流动性和交易活动在2020年下半年大幅增长。这些加密协议极大地推动了农产品市场参与者的流动性增长。因此，CFMMs和相关协议（过去是非常小的市场）现在构成了大量加密资产最具流动性的交易场所。但是，对于一个流动性最强的市场来说，这意味着什么呢？在本文中，我们提出了价格敏感性和流动性的基本定义。我们证明了这个定义与CFMM交易函数的曲率密切相关，并且可以用来解释一些启发式结果。例如，我们发现低曲率市场有利于市场价值大致固定的硬币，当交易者具有信息优势时，高曲率市场更有利于流动性提供者。此外，研究结果还可以用来模拟相互作用的市场，并解释激励性流动性供给（也称为产量农业）的兴起。

\[3\] Kicking You When You re Already Down

标题 : 当你已经倒下的时候踢你

作者 : Corrado Giulietti, Brendon McConnell , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08133

说明 : Comments: draft 1.0

Abstract : The UK Welfare Reform Act (2012) imposed a series of welfare cuts, which disproportionately impacted ex ante poorer areas. In this paper we consider the impact of these austerity measures on two different but complementary elements of crime the crime rate and the less studied concentration of crime over the period 2011 2015 in England and Wales, and document four new facts. First, areas more exposed to the welfare reforms experience increased levels of crime, an effect driven by a rise in violent crime. Second, crime becomes more concentrated within an area due to the welfare reforms, both violent and property crime. Third, it is ex ante more deprived neighborhoods that bear the brunt of the crime increases over this period. Fourth, we find no evidence that the welfare reforms increased recidivism, hence the changes in crime we find are likely driven by new criminals. Combining these results, we document unambiguous evidence of a negative spillover of the welfare reforms at the heart of the UK government s austerity program on social welfare, which reinforced the direct inequality enhancing effect of this program. More deprived districts are more exposed to the welfare reforms, and it is these districts that then experience the further negative consequences of the reforms via increased crime. Our findings underscore the importance of considering both multiple dimensions of crime as well as considering different levels of spatial aggregation of crime data. Given that it is violent crime that responds to the (economically based) welfare cuts, our work also highlights the need to develop better economic models of non rational crime.

摘要 : 英国《福利改革法案》（2012年）实施了一系列福利削减措施，这对之前的贫困地区造成了不成比例的影响。在本文中，我们考虑这些紧缩措施对英格兰和威尔士2011年至2015年期间犯罪率和犯罪集中度这两个不同但互补的犯罪要素的影响，并记录了四个新的事实。首先，受福利改革影响较大的地区犯罪率上升，这是由暴力犯罪率上升推动的。其次，由于福利改革，犯罪更加集中在一个地区，包括暴力犯罪和财产犯罪。第三，在这段时间里，犯罪率上升的首当其冲是更加贫困的社区。第四，我们没有发现任何证据表明福利改革增加了累犯，因此我们发现犯罪的变化很可能是由新的罪犯驱动的。结合这些结果，我们记录了明确的证据，表明英国政府紧缩计划的核心福利改革对社会福利产生了负面的外溢效应，强化了该计划的直接不平等加剧效应。贫困程度越高的地区就越容易受到福利改革的影响，而正是这些地区通过增加犯罪率而经历改革的进一步负面后果。我们的研究结果强调了考虑犯罪的多个维度以及考虑犯罪数据的不同空间聚集水平的重要性。鉴于暴力犯罪是对（以经济为基础的）福利削减的回应，我们的工作也强调需要发展更好的非理性犯罪的经济模式。

\[4\] Market consistent pricing with acceptable risk

标题 : 具有可接受风险的市场一致定价

作者 : Maria Arduca, Cosimo Munari , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08351

说明 : Comments: 45 pages

Abstract : We study the range of prices at which a rational agent should contemplate transacting a financial contract outside a given securities market. Trading is subject to nonproportional transaction costs and portfolio constraints and full replication by way of market instruments is not always possible. Rationality is defined in terms of consistency with market prices and acceptable risk thresholds. We obtain a direct and a dual description of market consistent prices with acceptable risk based on superreplication prices and pricing densities. The dual characterization requires an appropriate extension of the classical Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing where the role of arbitrage opportunities is played by acceptable deals, i.e., costless investment opportunities with acceptable risk reward tradeoff. In particular, we highlight the importance of scalable acceptable deals, i.e., investment opportunities that are acceptable deals regardless of their volume. Our results provide a systematic treatment of and new insights into the theory of good deal pricing in a static setting.

摘要 : 我们研究的是一个理性的代理人在给定的证券市场之外进行金融合约交易的价格范围。交易受非比例交易成本和投资组合约束，通过市场工具进行完全复制并不总是可能的。合理性是指与市场价格的一致性和可接受的风险阈值。基于超复制价格和定价密度，我们得到了具有可接受风险的市场一致价格的直接和双重描述。双重特征要求对资产定价的经典基本定理进行适当的扩展，其中套利机会的作用是由可接受的交易来发挥的，即无成本的投资机会和可接受的风险回报权衡。特别是，我们强调可扩展可接受交易的重要性，即无论交易量大小，投资机会都是可接受的交易。我们的研究结果提供了一个系统的处理和新的见解，在一个静态的背景下好交易定价理论。

\[5\] A general framework for a joint calibration of VIX and VXX options

标题 : VIX和VXX选项联合校准的通用框架

作者 : Martino Grasselli, Andrea Mazzoran, Andrea Pallavicini , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08353

说明 : -

Abstract : We analyze the VIX futures market with a focus on the exchange traded notes written on such contracts, in particular, we investigate the VXX notes tracking the short end part of the futures term structure. Inspired by recent developments in commodity smile modeling, we present a multi factor stochastic local volatility model able to jointly calibrate plain vanilla options both on VIX futures and VXX notes. We discuss numerical results on real market data by highlighting the impact of model parameters on implied volatilities.

摘要 : 我们分析波动率指数期货市场，重点关注这些合约上的交易所交易票据，特别是研究跟踪期货期限结构短期部分的VXX债券。受商品微笑模型最新发展的启发，我们提出了一个多因素随机局部波动率模型，能够联合校准波动率指数期货和VXX债券的普通期权。我们通过强调模型参数对隐含波动率的影响来讨论实际市场数据的数值结果。

\[6\] A mathematical model of national level food system sustainability

标题 : 国家级粮食系统可持续发展的数学模型

作者 : Conor Goold, Simone Pfuderer, William H. M. James, Nik Lomax, Fiona Smith, Lisa M. Collins , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08355

说明 : -

Abstract : The global food system faces various endogeneous and exogeneous, biotic and abiotic risk factors, including a rising human population, higher population densities, price volatility and climate change. Quantitative models play an important role in understanding food systems expected responses to shocks and stresses. Here, we present a stylised mathematical model of a national level food system that incorporates domestic supply of a food commodity, international trade, consumer demand, and food commodity price. We derive a critical compound parameter signalling when domestic supply will become unsustainable and the food system entirely dependent on imports, which results in higher commodity prices, lower consumer demand and lower inventory levels. Using Bayesian estimation, we apply the dynamic food systems model to infer the sustainability of the UK pork industry. We find that the UK pork industry is currently sustainable but because the industry is dependent on imports to meet demand, a decrease in self sufficiency below 50 (current levels are 60 65 ) would lead it close to the critical boundary signalling its collapse. Our model provides a theoretical foundation for future work to determine more complex causal drivers of food system vulnerability.

摘要 : 全球粮食系统面临各种内生和外生、生物和非生物风险因素，包括人口不断增加、人口密度增加、价格波动和气候变化。定量模型在了解食物系统对冲击和压力的预期反应方面起着重要作用。在这里，我们提出了一个国家级粮食系统的程式化数学模型，它包括国内食品供应、国际贸易、消费者需求和食品商品价格。我们得出了一个关键的复合参数，即国内供应何时将变得不可持续，粮食系统将完全依赖进口，从而导致商品价格上涨、消费需求下降和库存水平下降。利用贝叶斯估计，我们应用动态食品系统模型来推断英国猪肉产业的可持续性。我们发现，英国猪肉行业目前是可持续的，但由于该行业依赖进口来满足需求，自给率低于50（目前水平为60 65）将导致其接近崩溃的临界边界。我们的模型提供了一个理论基础，为今后的工作，以确定更复杂的因果机制的食品系统脆弱性。

\[7\] Generalized Feynman Kac Formula under volatility uncertainty

标题 : 波动不确定性下的广义Feynman-Kac公式

作者 : Bahar Akthari, Francesca Biagini, Andrea Mazzon, Katharina Oberpriller , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08163

说明 : Comments: 28 pages, 2 figures, 2 tables

Abstract : In this paper we provide a generalization of the Feynmac Kac formula under volatility uncertainty in presence of discounting. We state our result under different hypothesis with respect to the derivation given by Hu, Ji, Peng and Song (Comparison theorem, Feynman Kac formula and Girsanov transformation for BSDEs driven by G Brownian motion, Stochastic Processes and their Application, 124 (2)), where the Lipschitz continuity of some functionals is assumed which is not necessarily satisfied in our setting. In particular, we obtain the G conditional expectation of a discounted payoff as the limit of C 1,2 solutions of some regularized PDEs, for different kinds of convergence. In applications, this permits to approximate such a sublinear expectation in a computationally efficient way.

摘要 : 本文给出了存在折现的波动不确定性下Feynmac-Kac公式的推广。对于Hu，Ji，Peng，Song给出的推导，我们在不同的假设下给出了我们的结果（G布朗运动驱动的BSDEs的比较定理，Feynman-Kac公式和Girsanov变换，随机过程及其应用，124（2）），其中假设了一些泛函的Lipschitz连续性，这在我们的设置中并不一定满足。特别地，对于不同的收敛性，我们得到了一些正则偏微分方程的c1，2解的极限条件期望。在这样一种亚线性的应用中，一种近似的有效的计算方法。
