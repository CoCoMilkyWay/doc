\[1\] How Do Consumers Fare When Dealing with Debt Collectors? Evidence from Out-of-Court Settlements

标题:消费者在与讨债者打交道时表现如何？庭外和解的证据

作者:Ing-Haw Cheng, Felipe Severino, Richard R Townsend

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1617/5878973; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa085

Abstract : Do deals with debt collectors alleviate consumer financial distress? Using new data linking court and credit registry records, we examine civil collection lawsuits where consumers can settle out of court. Random assignment of judges with different styles generates exogenous variation in the likelihood of settlement negotiations. We find that settlements increase financial distress relative to going to court, likely by draining consumers of liquidity. The effect is stronger among less financially literate consumers. Survey evidence suggests that consumers generally overestimate how much they would pay through the court system. Perceived nonpecuniary benefits also motivate some consumers to settle.

Abstract:与债务收集者的交易是否缓解了消费者的财务困境？使用新的数据连接法院和信贷登记记录，我们研究了民事托收诉讼，消费者可以庭外解决。不同风格的法官的随机分配会导致和解谈判可能性的外生变化。我们发现，与诉诸法庭相比，和解增加了财务困境，很可能是因为耗尽了消费者的流动性。这种影响在经济知识水平较低的消费者中更为明显。调查证据表明，消费者通常高估了他们将通过法院系统支付的金额。感知到的非特定利益也促使一些消费者定居下来。

\[2\] Can Strong Creditors Inhibit Entrepreneurial Activity?

标题:强大的债权人能否抑制创业活动？

作者:Nuri Ersahin, Rustom M Irani, Katherine Waldock

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1661/5825294; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa050

Abstract : We examine entrepreneurial activity following the staggered adoption of modern-day fraudulent transfer laws in the United States. These laws strengthen unsecured creditors’ rights and are particularly important for entrepreneurs whose personal assets commingle with the firm’s. Using administrative data from the U.S. Census Bureau, we document declines in startup entry—particularly among riskier entrants—and closures of existing firms after these laws pass. Firm financial data shows that entrepreneurs lower leverage by reducing demand for unsecured credit. Our results suggest that strong creditor protections can limit entrepreneurs’ appetite for risk, which may reduce churning along the extensive margin among the smallest firms in the economy.

Abstract:我们研究创业活动后，交错通过现代欺诈转让法在美国。这些法律加强了无担保债权人的权利，对于个人资产与公司资产相混合的企业家来说尤为重要。利用美国人口普查局的行政数据，我们记录了初创企业进入率的下降，特别是在这些法律通过后，风险较高的进入者和现有公司的关闭。公司财务数据显示，企业家通过减少对无担保信贷的需求来降低杠杆率。我们的研究结果表明，强有力的债权人保护可以限制企业家的风险偏好，这可能会减少经济体中规模最小的企业在广泛利润率上的波动。

\[3\] (Debt) Overhang: Evidence from Resource Extraction

标题:（债务）悬置：来自资源开采的证据

作者:Michael D Wittry

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1699/5856755; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa070

Abstract : I study the empirical importance of debt overhang using a unique data set on resource extraction firms that provides ex ante measures of investment opportunities and important variation in terms of a firm’s obligations. In particular, unsecured reclamation liabilities create overhang that is costly to resolve and induces firms to forgo and postpone positive NPV investments. Traditional debt, in contrast, imposes few overhang-related investment distortions. These results show that (a) the overhang problem is potentially large and more broadly applies to firms’ nondebt liabilities and (b) overhang problems associated with traditional debt can be avoided through contracting and debt composition.

Abstract:我使用一组独特的资源开采企业数据研究了债务过剩的经验重要性，这些数据提供了投资机会的事前衡量标准和企业义务的重要变化。特别是，无担保的回收债务造成了成本高昂的悬置，并促使企业放弃和推迟正净现值投资。相比之下，传统债务很少造成与投资相关的过度扭曲。这些结果表明：（a）悬置问题可能很大，更广泛地适用于企业的非债务；（b）与传统债务相关的悬置问题可以通过契约和债务构成来避免。

\[4\] Sharing the Pain? Credit Supply and Real Effects of Bank Bail-ins

标题:分担痛苦？信贷供给与银行纾困的现实效果

作者:Thorsten Beck, Samuel Da-Rocha-Lopes, André F Silva

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1747/5855685; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa067

Abstract : We analyze the credit supply and real effects of bank bail-ins by exploiting the unexpected failure and subsequent resolution of a major Portuguese bank. Using loan-level data, we show that while firms more exposed to the bail-in suffered a significant contraction of credit at the intensive margin, they were on average able to compensate for the supply-driven shock. However, affected SMEs experienced a binding reduction of funds available through credit lines, and those with lower internal liquidity increased precautionary cash holdings and reduced investment and employment. Our results highlight the trade-off policymakers face when considering this new bank resolution mechanism.

Abstract:我们分析信贷供应和银行纾困的实际效果，利用意外失败和随后解决葡萄牙一家主要银行。利用贷款水平的数据，我们表明，虽然更多地暴露在纾困中的企业在密集利润率下遭受了显著的信贷收缩，但它们平均能够补偿供应驱动的冲击。然而，受影响的中小企业通过信贷额度获得的资金有约束力地减少，而内部流动性较低的中小企业增加了预防性现金持有量，减少了投资和就业。我们的结果突出了政策制定者在考虑这种新的银行处置机制时面临的权衡。

\[5\] Corporate Cash Shortfalls and Financing Decisions

标题:企业现金短缺与融资决策

作者:Rongbing Huang, Jay R Ritter

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1789/5897423; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa099

Abstract : Given their actual revenue and spending, most net equity issuers and an overwhelming majority of net debt issuers would face immediate cash depletion without external financing. Debt issuers tend to have short-lived cash needs, while equity issuers often have persistent cash needs. On average, debt issuers immediately spend almost all of the proceeds, while equity issuers retain much of the proceeds in cash. Anticipated near-future cash needs and fixed costs of financing help explain the fraction of the proceeds being retained. Our findings support a funding-horizon theory in which cash needs and the nature of cash needs motivate financing decisions.

Abstract:考虑到它们的实际收入和支出，大多数净股本发行人和绝大多数净债务发行人在没有外部融资的情况下将面临现金的立即枯竭。债务发行人往往有短暂的现金需求，而股票发行人往往有持久的现金需求。平均而言，债务发行人会立即花掉几乎所有的收益，而股票发行人则以现金形式保留大部分收益。预期的近期现金需求和固定融资成本有助于解释留存收益的比例。我们的研究结果支持了一个融资期限理论，在该理论中，现金需求和现金需求的性质会激励融资决策。

\[6\] Corporate Money Demand

标题:企业资金需求

作者:Xiaodan Gao, Toni M Whited, Na Zhang

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1834/5873590; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa083

Abstract : We document a hump-shaped relation between corporate cash and both real and nominal interest rates in both aggregate and firm-level data. We rationalize this result in a model where firms finance investment with cash and risky debt. The risky rate rises endogenously with the risk-free rate, spurring precautionary cash demand. Simultaneously, foregone interest lowers cash demand. The first mechanism dominates at low interest rates, and the second at high interest rates. The model matches several data moments and reproduces a nonmonotonic cash–interest relation. This nonmonotonicity implies that interest rates are unlikely to be behind the recent rise in corporate cash.

Abstract:我们记录了一个驼峰形的关系，企业现金和实际利率和名义利率在总数据和企业层面的数据。我们在一个模型中合理化了这个结果，在这个模型中，企业用现金和高风险债务为投资融资。风险利率随着无风险利率的内生上升，刺激了预防性的现金需求。同时，放弃的利息降低了现金需求。第一种机制在低利率时占主导地位，第二种机制在高利率时占主导地位。该模型匹配多个数据矩，再现了非单调的现金-利息关系。这种非单调性意味着利率不太可能成为近期企业现金增长的幕后推手。

\[7\] Why Does Equity Capital Flow out of High Tobin’s |$\\boldsymbol{q}$| Industries?

标题:为什么股权资本会流出高托宾的|$\\boldsymbol{q}$|产业？

作者:Dong Wook Lee, Hyun-Han Shin, René M Stulz

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1867/5879290; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa086

Abstract : High Tobin’s |$q$| industries receive more funding from capital markets than low Tobin’s |$q$| industries from 1971 to 1996. Since then, the opposite is true. The key to understanding this shift is that large firms, for which |$q$| is more a proxy for rents than investment opportunities, have become more important within industries. For these firms, repurchases but not capital expenditures increase in the cross-section with |$q$|⁠, so that |$q$| explains the variation of repurchases more than of capital expenditures. Consequently, equity capital flows out of high |$q$| industries because for these industries stock repurchases are high and issuances are low.

Abstract:1971年至1996年，高托宾工业从资本市场获得的资金要多于低托宾工业。从那时起，情况正好相反。理解这一转变的关键在于，大型企业在行业内变得更为重要，对它们而言，q$更能代表租金而非投资机会。对于这些公司，回购而不是资本支出在截面上以|$q$|增加，因此|$q$|比资本支出更能解释回购的变化。因此，股本从高q$行业流出，因为这些行业的股票回购率高，发行量低。

\[8\] Evaluating Firm-Level Expected-Return Proxies: Implications for Estimating Treatment Effects

标题:评估公司层面的预期回报代理：对估计治疗效果的影响

作者:Charles M C Lee, Eric C So, Charles C Y Wang

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1907/5855684; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa066

Abstract : We introduce a parsimonious framework for choosing among alternative expected-return proxies (ERPs) when estimating treatment effects. By comparing ERPs’ measurement error variances in the cross-section and in the time series, we provide new evidence on the relative performance of firm-level ERPs nominated by recent studies. Generally, “implied-costs-of-capital” metrics perform best in the time series, whereas “characteristic-based” proxies perform best in the cross-section. Factor-based ERPs, even the latest renditions, perform poorly. We revisit four prior studies that use ex ante ERPs and illustrate how this framework can potentially alter either the sign or the magnitude of prior inferences.

Abstract:我们介绍了一个节俭的框架选择替代预期收益代理（ERPs）时，估计治疗效果。通过比较企业资源计划在横截面和时间序列上的测量误差方差，我们为最近研究中提出的企业级企业资源计划的相对绩效提供了新的证据。一般来说，“隐含资本成本”指标在时间序列中表现最好，而“基于特征的”指标在横截面上表现最好。基于因素的企业资源规划，即使是最新的版本，表现也很差。我们回顾了四个先前的研究，使用事前ERP，并说明这个框架如何可能改变符号或幅度的事先推断。

\[9\] Does Option Trading Have a Pervasive Impact on Underlying Stock Prices?

标题:期权交易对基础股价有普遍影响吗？

作者:Sophie X Ni, Neil D Pearson, Allen M Poteshman, Joshua White

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1952/5873587; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa082

Abstract : The question of whether and to what extent option trading affects underlying stock prices has been of interest to researchers since exchange-based options trading began in 1973. Recent research presents evidence of an informational channel through which option trading affects stock prices by showing that option market makers’ stock trades to hedge new options positions cause the information reflected in option trading to be impounded into underlying equity prices. This paper provides evidence of a noninformational channel through which option market maker hedge rebalancing affects stock return volatility and the probability of large stock price moves.

Abstract:自1973年交易所期权交易开始以来，期权交易是否以及在多大程度上影响基础股票价格一直是研究者感兴趣的问题。最近的研究表明，期权交易通过一个信息渠道影响股票价格，即期权做市商对冲新期权头寸的股票交易导致期权交易中反映的信息被存储到基础股票价格中。本文提供了一个非信息渠道的证据，通过这个渠道，期权市场做市商对冲再平衡影响股票收益率波动性和股价大幅波动的概率。

\[10\] Who Is Afraid of BlackRock?

标题:谁害怕贝莱德？

作者:Massimo Massa, David Schumacher, Yan Wang

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/1987/5873592; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa081

Abstract : We exploit the merger between BlackRock and Barclays Global Investors to study how changes in expected ownership concentration affect the investment behavior of funds and the cross-section of stocks worldwide. We find that funds with open-end structures and large exposure to commonly held stocks begin avoiding these stocks following the merger announcement. This leads to a permanent change in the composition of institutional ownership and a negative price and liquidity impact. We confirm these results in a large sample of global asset management mergers. Our findings suggest that market participants behave strategically in response to changes in expected financial fragility.

Abstract:我们利用贝莱德（BlackRock）和巴克莱全球投资者（Barclays Global Investors）的合并，研究预期股权集中度的变化如何影响基金的投资行为和全球股票的横截面。我们发现，具有开放式结构和大量普通股敞口的基金在合并公告后开始回避这些股票。这将导致机构所有权构成的永久性变化，并对价格和流动性产生负面影响。我们在全球资产管理合并的大样本中证实了这些结果。我们的研究结果表明，市场参与者在应对预期金融脆弱性变化时具有战略性的行为。

\[11\] Break Risk

标题:违约风险

作者:Simon C Smith, Allan Timmermann

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/2045/5892874; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa084

Abstract : We develop a new approach to modeling and predicting stock returns in the presence of breaks that simultaneously affect a large cross-section of stocks. Exploiting information in the cross-section enables us to detect breaks in return prediction models with little delay and to generate out-of-sample return forecasts that are significantly more accurate than those from existing approaches. To identify the economic sources of breaks, we explore the asset pricing restrictions implied by a present value model which links breaks in return predictability to breaks in the cash flow growth and discount rate processes.

Abstract:我们开发了一种新的方法来建模和预测股票收益率的存在中断，同时影响了一个大截面的股票。利用横截面中的信息，我们可以在很少延迟的情况下检测收益预测模型中的中断，并生成比现有方法更精确的样本外收益预测。为了确定中断的经济来源，我们探讨了现值模型隐含的资产定价限制，该模型将收益可预测性中断与现金流增长和折现率过程中断联系起来。

\[12\] Illiquidity and Stock Returns II: Cross-section and Time-series Effects

标题:流动性不足与股票收益II：横截面与时间序列效应

作者:Yakov Amihud, Joonki Noh

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/4/2101/5869447; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa080

Abstract : Lou and Shu decompose Amihud’s illiquidity measure (ILLIQ) proposing that its component, the average of inverse dollar trading volume (IDVOL), is sufficient to explain the pricing of illiquidity. Their decomposition misses a component of ILLIQ that is related to illiquidity. We find that this component affects stock returns significantly, both in the cross-section and in time-series. We show that the ILLIQ premium is significantly positive after controlling for mispricing, sentiment, and seasonality. In addition, the aggregate market ILLIQ outperforms market IDVOL in estimating the effect of market illiquidity shocks on realized stock returns.

Abstract:Lou和Shu对Amihud的非流动性度量（ILLIQ）进行了分解，提出其组成部分，即逆美元交易量（IDVOL）的平均值，足以解释非流动性的定价。他们的分解遗漏了一个与流动性不足有关的部分。我们发现，无论是在横截面上还是在时间序列上，这一因素都会显著影响股票收益率。我们发现，在控制了错误定价、情绪和季节性因素后，ILIQ溢价显著为正。此外，在估计市场流动性不足冲击对已实现股票收益的影响方面，市场ILLIQ总量优于市场IDVOL。
