\[1\] The Increasing Usefulness of Annual Earnings Announcements: An Examination of Changes in Disagreement Using Analyst Forecasts

标题:年度盈利公告的日益有用性：利用分析师预测对分歧变化的检验

作者:Orie E. Barron,Richard A. Schneible Jr.,Douglas E. Stevens

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3841772

Abstract : Researchers in accounting have recently provided evidence of a striking increase in the usefulness of earnings announcements based on stock market price and volume reactions (Beaver et al., 2018; Barron et al., 2018). Price reactions, however, are unable to capture investor disagreement and volume reactions capture both the resolution of prior disagreement and newfound disagreement generated by earnings announcements. Thus, it remains to be determined if earnings announcements have become increasingly useful in leveling the informational playing field, a key public policy objective of financial reporting. To address this possibility, we examine changes in disagreement around annual earnings announcements over the last forty years using analyst forecast measures found in the literature. First, we show that forecast dispersion is reduced around earnings announcements and this reduction has increased over time. Next, we use a forecast measure of informedness from Barron et al. (1998) to show that analysts as a group are more informed by earnings announcements in recent time periods. Finally, we use Barron et al.’s forecast measure of consensus to show that the ability of earnings announcements to make analysts more commonly informed has increased over time.

Keywords : Earnings announcements, Analysts forecasts, Disagreement, Leveling the Playing Field

Abstract :会计研究人员最近提供了证据，证明基于股市价格和成交量反应的盈利公告的有用性显著提高（Beaver等人，2018；Barron等人，2018年）。然而，价格反应无法捕捉到投资者的分歧，成交量反应则捕捉到先前分歧的解决和盈利公告产生的新分歧。因此，盈利公告是否越来越有助于平衡信息竞争环境，这是财务报告的一个关键公共政策目标，还有待确定。为了解决这种可能性，我们使用文献中发现的分析师预测指标，研究了过去40年中围绕年度收益公告的分歧变化。首先，我们表明，预测分散减少周围的收益公告，这种减少已随着时间的推移而增加。接下来，我们使用Barron et al.（1998）的一个信息量预测指标来表明，分析师作为一个群体，在最近一段时间内对收益公告的信息量更大。最后，我们使用Barron等人的预测一致性度量来表明，随着时间的推移，盈利公告让分析师更为普遍地了解情况的能力有所提高。

Keywords :盈利公告、分析师预测、分歧、公平竞争

\[3\] Why and How Systematic Strategies Decay

标题:为什么以及如何系统化策略衰退

作者:Antoine Falck,Adam Rej,David Thesmar

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3845928

Abstract : In this paper, we propose ex-ante characteristics that predict the drop in risk-adjusted performance out-of-sample for a large set of stock anomalies published in finance and accounting academic journals. Our set of predictors is generated by hypotheses of OOS decay put forward by McLean and Pontiff (2016): arbitrage capital flowing into newly published strategies and in-sample overfitting linked to multiple hypothesis testing. The year of publication alone—compatible with both hypotheses—explains 30% of the variance of Sharpe decay across factors: Every year, the Sharpe decay of newly-published factors increases by 5ppt. The other important variables are directly related to overfitting: the number of operations required to calculate the signal and two measures of sensitivity of in-sample Sharpe to outliers together add another 15% of explanatory power. Some arbitrage-related variables are statistically significant, but their predictive power is marginal.

Keywords : factor returns, arbitrage, overfitting

Abstract :在本文中，我们提出了事前特征，预测风险调整后的业绩下降的样本外的一大套股票异常发表在财务和会计学术期刊。我们的一组预测因子是由McLean和Pontiff（2016）提出的OOS衰退假设产生的：套利资本流入新公布的策略，以及与多重假设检验相关的样本过度拟合。与这两个假设相一致的发表年份解释了各因子夏普衰退方差的30%：每年，新发表因子的夏普衰退增加5个百分点。其他重要变量与过度拟合直接相关：计算信号所需的运算次数和样本内夏普对异常值的敏感性的两个度量加在一起又增加了15%的解释力。一些套利相关变量在统计上是显著的，但它们的预测能力是微乎其微的。

Keywords :因子收益、套利、过度拟合

\[4\] Excess financial volatility explained by endogenous excitations revealed by EM calibrations of a generalized Hawkes point process

标题:广义Hawkes点过程EM校正揭示的内生激励解释的过度金融波动

作者:Alexander Wehrli,Didier Sornette

出处:Swiss Finance Institute Research Paper No. 21-35

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3848661

Abstract : Puzzling deviations from the predictions of rational finance theory have been extensively documented empirically. In this paper, we offer an explanation for one of these anomalies, the “excess volatility puzzle”, i.e. the observation that prices fluctuate more than fundamentally justified. Based on Expectation Maximization (EM) calibrations of a generalized Hawkes point process model to price changes of major currency pairs and equity futures, we construct a decomposition of the variance of high frequency price changes into an exogenous (and thus efficient) component and an endogenous (and thus inefficient) excess component. The endogenously induced excess volatility is found to be substantial, largely stable at longer time scales and thus provides a plausible ex-planation for the excess volatility puzzle. Furthermore, strong endogenous variations at shorter scales are found to lead to major temporary inefficiencies. For example, during the “flash crash” in the GBP/USD exchange rate on October 7, 2016, we document a significant breakdown of market efficiency and an excessive burst in volatility, almost entirely explained by endogenous feedback. Conversely, the shock to EUR/ USD volatility in response to the 2016 Brexit referendum was not accompanied by such a deterioration in market efficiency. These results underline that a more solid understanding of the microstructural origins of financial fluctuations also bears important lessons for neo-classical concepts, like market efficiency, which are fundamental to financial and economic theory.

Keywords : market efficiency, excess volatility, endogeneity, Hawkes process, high frequency data

Abstract :与理性金融理论预测的令人费解的偏差已经被大量的经验证明。在本文中，我们对其中一个异常现象“过度波动之谜”进行了解释，即观察到价格波动超出了基本合理的范围。基于广义Hawkes点过程模型对主要货币对和股票期货价格变化的期望最大化（EM）校正，我们构造了一个将高频价格变化方差分解为外生（因而有效）成分和内生（因而无效）过剩成分的分解模型。内生诱发的过度波动是实质性的，在较长的时间尺度上基本稳定，从而为过度波动之谜提供了一个合理的解释。此外，在较短的范围内，强烈的内生变化会导致主要的暂时无效。例如，在2016年10月7日英镑/美元汇率的“闪电崩盘”期间，我们记录了市场效率的严重崩溃和波动性的过度爆发，几乎完全由内生反馈解释。相反，2016年英国脱欧公投对欧元/美元波动的冲击并没有伴随着市场效率的恶化。这些结果强调，对金融波动微观结构根源的更深入理解，也为新古典主义概念（如市场效率）提供了重要的经验教训，而市场效率是金融和经济理论的基础。

Keywords :市场效率，过度波动，内生性，霍克斯过程，高频数据

出处:Orlando, G.; Bufalo, M. Empirical Evidences on the Interconnectedness between Sampling and Asset Returns’ Distributions. Risks 2021, 9, 88. https://doi.org/10.3390/risks9050088

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3847152

Abstract : The aim of this work was to test how returns are distributed across multiple asset classes, markets and sampling frequency.
