\[3\] New Perspective on Investment Models

标题:投资模式新视角

作者:Kees Koedijk and Alfred Slager

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/5/15;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.224

Abstract : What is the secret ingredient to securing better long-term investment outcomes? Investors seldom consider institutional investors from an organizational and strategic perspective. Thinking in terms of investment models highlights a severely under-researched area. The authors propose that this different lens is required to make sense of the dynamics in the investment industry and has a lot to offer to boards that are open to this. Five models dominate the investment industry, and the authors identify an emergent one that has its roots in the existing models but takes a strong societal perspective, which they denote the prudent society investment model. Thinking in terms of investment models, boards and decision makers will be able, far better than today, to determine why, how, and what is needed for investment success.

TOPICS: Long-term/retirement investing, pension funds, portfolio theory, financial crises and financial market history

Key Finding

\- ▪ Every investor works with an investment model: a unique combination of goals, risk appetite, investment beliefs, and governance of investment implementation.

\- ▪ Making the underlying assumptions and consequences explicit is key

\- otherwise, achieving long-term success is a struggle.

\- ▪ Investment models evolve slowly. The increased focus on long-term, private assets and the incorporation of societal goals are new elements but do not (yet) form an entirely new model. Thus, we have to keep learning from and adapting the existing ones.

Abstract:获得更好的长期投资成果的秘诀是什么？投资者很少从组织和战略的角度考虑机构投资者。从投资模型的角度思考，突出了一个严重缺乏研究的领域。作者提出，这种不同的视角是理解投资行业动态所必需的，对那些对此持开放态度的董事会有很大的帮助。五种模式主导了投资行业，作者确定了一种新兴的模式，它植根于现有的模式，但采取了强烈的社会视角，他们表示谨慎的社会投资模式。从投资模式的角度考虑，董事会和决策者将能够比现在更好地确定投资成功的原因、方式和需要什么。

主题：长期/退休投资，养老基金，投资组合理论，金融危机和金融市场历史

Key Finding

\- ▪ 每个投资者都使用一种投资模式：目标、风险偏好、投资信念和投资实施治理的独特组合。;▪ 明确基本假设和结果是关键

\- 否则，实现长期成功是一场斗争。;▪ 投资模式发展缓慢。对长期私人资产和社会目标的日益重视是新的因素，但还没有形成一个全新的模式。因此，我们必须不断学习和适应现有的。

\[4\] Mean–Variance Optimization for Asset Allocation

标题:资产配置的均值-方差优化

作者:Jang Ho Kim, Yongjae Lee, Woo Chang Kim and Frank J. Fabozzi

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/5/24;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.219

Abstract : The mean–variance model is widely acknowledged as the foundation of portfolio allocation because it provides a framework for analyzing the trade-off between risk and return for gaining diversification benefits. Despite the well-known shortcomings of the model, it is often the starting point for making asset allocation decisions. In this article, the authors briefly review mean–variance optimization and approaches for resolving its limitations by demonstrating backtest results on asset allocation. Feedback from asset managers is also included to explain how optimization methods are applied in practice.

TOPICS: Quantitative methods, statistical methods, portfolio construction, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ Mean–variance optimization provides a framework for analyzing risk–return trade-offs when making asset allocation decisions.

\- ▪ Sensitivity of the mean–variance model can be addressed with robust models, and the model generates candidate asset allocations that are informative in practice.

\- ▪ Feedback from asset managers suggests that mean–variance optimization along with simulation and various robust methods are being applied in practice for asset allocation.

Abstract:均值-方差模型被广泛认为是资产组合分配的基础，因为它提供了一个分析风险和收益之间的权衡以获得多元化收益的框架。尽管该模型存在众所周知的缺点，但它通常是做出资产配置决策的起点。在这篇文章中，作者简要回顾了均值-方差优化和解决其局限性的方法，展示了资产配置的后验结果。资产经理的反馈也被包括进来，以解释优化方法是如何在实践中应用的。

主题：定量方法，统计方法，投资组合构建，绩效评估

Key Finding

\- ▪ 均值-方差优化为在进行资产配置决策时分析风险-收益权衡提供了一个框架。

\- ▪ 均值-方差模型的敏感性可以用稳健的模型来解决，并且该模型生成的候选资产分配在实践中是有用的。

\- ▪ 来自资产管理公司的反馈表明，均值-方差优化以及模拟和各种稳健的方法正在资产配置的实践中得到应用。

\[5\] Risk Parity: The Democratization of Risk in Asset Allocation

标题:风险平价：资产配置中风险的民主化

作者:Francesco A. Fabozzi, Joseph Simonian and Frank J. Fabozzi

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/5/41;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.228

Abstract : The risk parity investment model for asset allocation offers an alternative to the mean–variance framework. The fundamental idea is that the allocation to different asset classes should not be based on an optimization that targets a specific return with a minimal level of risk but, rather, should generate a portfolio in which the contribution to portfolio risk of each asset class is equal, regardless of its expected returns. In this article, the authors explain the fundamentals of the risk parity investment model and the variants in risk parity strategies due to the selection of the asset classes to be included in the portfolio, the choice of the risk metric, the portfolio risk target, how to obtain leverage, associated leverage, whether the selection of the specific investments within an asset class is made using an active or passive approach, and the tactical risk allocation strategy. In addition to describing the practical aspects of implementing risk parity strategies, the authors identify the various shortcomings of the model and some extensions of the basic risk parity model that attempt to address some of the issues identified by the model’s critics.

TOPICS: Portfolio theory, portfolio construction, risk management, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ Unlike mean–variance optimization, a risk parity strategy allocates across asset classes such that each asset class contributes equally to portfolio risk, regardless of its expected returns.

\- ▪ In practice, there are variants of the risk parity strategy due to choices made by the portfolio manager, such as selection of the asset classes, risk measure, targeted volatility, degree of leverage, asset selection using active or passive approach, and tactical risk allocation strategy.

\- ▪ The performance of the risk parity strategy has varied, with critics of the strategy identifying theoretical and practical implementation issues.

Abstract:资产配置的风险平价投资模型提供了均值-方差框架的另一种选择。其基本思想是，对不同资产类别的配置不应基于以最低风险水平的特定回报为目标的优化，而应生成一个投资组合，其中每个资产类别对投资组合风险的贡献是相等的，而不管其预期回报如何。在本文中，作者解释了风险平价投资模型的基本原理和风险平价策略中的变量，这些变量是由于选择了要包含在投资组合中的资产类别、选择了风险度量、投资组合的风险目标、如何获得杠杆率、关联杠杆率、是否选择了具体的资产类别内的投资采用主动或被动方法以及战术风险分配策略。除了描述实施风险平价策略的实际方面外，作者还指出了该模型的各种缺点，并对基本风险平价模型进行了一些扩展，试图解决该模型批评者指出的一些问题。

主题：投资组合理论，投资组合构建，风险管理，绩效评估

Key Finding

\- ▪ 与均值-方差优化不同，风险平价策略跨资产类别进行分配，使得每个资产类别对投资组合风险的贡献相等，而不管其预期收益如何。

\- ▪ 在实践中，由于投资组合经理的选择，风险平价策略有多种变体，如资产类别的选择、风险度量、目标波动率、杠杆程度、使用主动或被动方法的资产选择以及战术风险分配策略。

\- ▪ 风险平价策略的表现各不相同，批评该策略的人士指出了理论和实际实施问题。

However, in recent years investors increasingly have considered factors as an alternative to asset classes. \[8\] Factor Allocation as Reverse Attribution

标题:反向归因的要素配置

作者:Joseph Simonian

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/5/65;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.225

Abstract : Multifactor models and the construction of factor portfolios are by now pervasive in investment management. Active factor allocation can be challenging, however, given the difficulty in generating accurate point estimates for ex ante factor alphas. To overcome this challenge, the present article describes a technique that gives managers the ability to generate expected factor alphas from factor volatilities. An important component of the framework is the way it characterizes factor allocation as reverse attribution. Starting with a portfolio alpha target and risk budget, the methodology proceeds to derive factor weights by finding the benchmark-relative factor loadings that satisfy the alpha and risk objectives. Furthermore, because effective factor allocation is forward looking, the framework described in the article also allows managers to adjust factor volatility values in a manner that is consistent with their subjective beliefs regarding a factor’s likely marginal alpha contribution. The framework presented thus serves as a quantitatively grounded yet practical framework for building actively managed factor portfolios.

TOPICS: Factor-based models, portfolio construction, risk management, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ Performance attribution can be applied in reverse to inform forward-looking factor allocation.

\- ▪ It is possible to derive expected factor alphas from factor volatilities and a target portfolio alpha value and risk budget.

\- ▪ A simple, yet statistically driven, approach to calibrating ex ante factor volatilities can be implemented by coupling a manager’s subjective views with an assumed distribution for factor variances.

Abstract:目前，多因素模型和要素组合的构建已广泛应用于投资管理中。然而，考虑到难以为预先因子α生成准确的点估计，主动因子分配可能具有挑战性。为了克服这一挑战，本文描述了一种技术，使管理者能够从因子波动性中产生预期的因子α。该框架的一个重要组成部分是它将要素分配描述为逆向归因的方式。从投资组合α目标和风险预算开始，该方法通过找到满足α和风险目标的基准相对因子负荷，进而得出因子权重。此外，由于有效的要素配置是前瞻性的，本文所描述的框架还允许管理者调整要素波动率值的方式与其对要素可能边际α贡献的主观信念一致。因此，本文提出的框架可作为构建积极管理的因子投资组合的一个定量基础但又实用的框架。

主题：基于因素的模型，投资组合构建，风险管理，绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 绩效归因可以反向应用，为前瞻性因素分配提供信息。

\- ▪ 可以从因子波动率和目标投资组合α值以及风险预算中得出预期因子α。

\- ▪ 通过将管理者的主观观点与假设的因子方差分布相结合，可以实现一种简单的、但由统计数据驱动的校正事前因子波动性的方法。

\[9\] Macro Factor Model: Application to Liquid Private Portfolios

标题:宏观因素模型在流动性私人投资组合中的应用

作者:Scott Gladstone, Ananth Madhavan, Anita Rana and Andrew Ang

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/5/72;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.231

Abstract : We show how macro factors—economic growth, inflation, real rates, credit, emerging markets, and liquidity—can be used to model private market returns. The framework helps to identify the different components of private returns: those that are common to public market returns, return components common across private markets, specific return components to a particular illiquid market, and leverage. We apply the framework with factor models of private real estate, infrastructure, and private equity, which can be implemented in liquid instruments. Having modeled both public and private market assets with the same risk factors, we then use the framework to create an optimal combined public–private multi-asset portfolio.

TOPICS: Private equity, real estate, other real assets, portfolio construction

Key Finding

\- ▪ Macro factors such as growth and inflation can be used to model private market returns.

\- ▪ We show how factor models of private real estate, infrastructure, and private equity can be implemented in a liquid private portfolio and ultimately in an optimal combined public–private multi-asset portfolio.

\- ▪ Liquid private portfolios are not a replication solution, but rather an all-in alternatives solution in which we can use exchange-traded funds as a liquid interim vehicle.

Abstract:我们展示了如何利用宏观因素经济增长、通货膨胀、实际利率、信贷、新兴市场和流动性来模拟私人市场回报。该框架有助于确定私人回报的不同组成部分：公共市场回报的共同组成部分、私人市场的共同回报组成部分、特定非流动性市场的具体回报组成部分以及杠杆作用。我们应用了私人房地产、基础设施和私人股本的因子模型框架，这些模型可以在流动性工具中实现。在对具有相同风险因素的公共和私人市场资产进行建模之后，我们使用该框架来创建一个最优的公共-私人多资产组合。

主题：私募股权、房地产、其他房地产资产、投资组合构建

Key Finding

\- ▪ 经济增长和通货膨胀等宏观因素可以用来模拟私人市场的回报。

\- ▪ 我们展示了私人房地产、基础设施和私人股本的因子模型如何在流动性私人投资组合中实施，并最终在最优的公私结合多资产投资组合中实施。

\- ▪ 流动性私人投资组合不是一种复制解决方案，而是一种全方位的替代解决方案，在这种解决方案中，我们可以使用交易所交易基金作为流动性临时工具。

\[10\] Black–Litterman and Beyond: The Bayesian Paradigm in Investment Management

标题:布莱克-利特曼与超越：投资管理中的贝叶斯范式

作者:Petter N. Kolm, Gordon Ritter and Joseph Simonian

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/5/91;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.222

Abstract : The Black–Litterman model is one of the most popular models in quantitative finance, with numerous theoretical and practical achievements. From the standpoint of investment theory, the Black–Litterman model allows seamless incorporation of Bayesian statistics into the portfolio optimization process. From a practical standpoint, it provides portfolio managers with a structured approach to express subjective views, thereby freeing their investment processes from a total reliance on backward-looking historical data. In this article, the authors provide an overview of the original Black–Litterman model and its various extensions and enhancements addressing issues in real-world trading and investment management.

TOPICS: Quantitative methods, statistical methods, portfolio construction, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors provide an overview of the original Black–Litterman model and its various extensions and enhancements, addressing issues in real-world trading and investment management.

\- ▪ Many important practical considerations in implementing the Black-Litterman model are discussed including choice of priors, view generation, and transaction costs.

\- ▪ Particular emphasis is given to the extensions of the Black-Litterman model of significant practical relevance to today’s investors such as factor models, model misspecification, non-normality, and multiperiod portfolio optimization.

Abstract:Black-Litterman模型是数量金融中最流行的模型之一，在理论和实践上都取得了许多成果。从投资理论的角度来看，Black-Litterman模型允许将贝叶斯统计无缝地结合到投资组合优化过程中。从实践的角度来看，它为投资组合经理提供了一种结构化的方法来表达主观观点，从而使他们的投资过程完全摆脱对回顾性历史数据的依赖。在这篇文章中，作者提供了一个原始的布莱克-利特曼模型及其各种扩展和增强解决现实世界中的交易和投资管理问题的概述。

主题：定量方法，统计方法，投资组合构建，绩效评估

Key Finding
