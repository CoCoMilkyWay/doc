\[1\] Online Visits and Forecasting Errors: Evidence from Chinese Financial Analysts

标题:在线访问和预测错误：来自中国金融分析师的证据

作者:Jun Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3888702

Abstract : We investigate whether the widespread adoption of online visits after the COVID-19 shock has been associated with higher analysts' forecast errors. Using a difference-in-differences methodology, we find a positive association between online mode and analysts' forecast errors. We further discuss possible underlying mechanisms by analyzing the changes of online mode on managers' attention, meeting tone, and buy-side pressure, and show that there is a residual effect after controlling for these mechanisms. Furthermore, the increase in forecast error is more pronounced for firms with low Internet visibility.

Keywords : Analyst visits; forecasting bias; information acquisition activities; COVID-19

Abstract :我们调查了在新冠病毒-19冲击后，在线访问的广泛采用是否与更高的分析师预测误差有关。使用差异中的差异方法，我们发现在线模式和分析师的预测误差之间存在正相关性。通过分析在线模式对管理者注意力、会议语气和买方压力的影响，我们进一步讨论了可能的潜在机制，并表明在控制这些机制后存在残余效应。此外，对于互联网知名度较低的公司，预测误差的增加更为明显。

Keywords :分析师访问；预测偏差；信息获取活动；2019冠状病毒疾病

\[2\] Auctioning Corporate Bonds: A Uniform-Price under Investment Mandates

标题:公司债券拍卖：投资委托下的统一价格

作者:Lamprini Zarpala

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3888636

Abstract : There has been a rapid growth in the use of investment mandates for the management of fixed-income assets. In this paper, we examine how the limits set in investment mandates can affect the bidding strategy during the issuance of a corporate bond. We apply the uniform-price auction and prove the existence of symmetric Bayesian Nash equilibrium. Under the presence of an exogenous secondary market, the bidding is reduced when there is an expectation for higher yields on resale. Also, we result that the number of participating investors always affects the bidding strategy inversely, while the issue rate not at all. We provide the necessary comparative statics.

Keywords : Bond Market, Auctions, Investment Mandates, Market Design

Abstract :使用投资委托书管理固定收益资产的情况迅速增长。在本文中，我们研究了投资委托书中设定的限制如何影响公司债券发行过程中的投标策略。我们应用统一价格拍卖，证明了对称贝叶斯纳什均衡的存在性。在存在外生二级市场的情况下，当预期转售收益率较高时，竞价会降低。此外，我们还发现，参与投资者的数量总是与竞价策略成反比，而发行率则与之无关。我们提供了必要的比较静力学。

Keywords :债券市场、拍卖、投资委托书、市场设计

\[3\] Competing with Inventory in Dealership Markets

标题:与经销商市场的库存竞争

作者:Yu An

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3284836

Abstract : Existing literature on dealer liquidity provision and inventory management has focused on dealer inventory costs. I uncover a new inventory benefit channel: dealers strategically build inventory in order to compete for market share. I assess the relative impact of the inventory benefit and costs on the pricing of dealer liquidity provision and inventory management. Using the TRACE data for the U.S. corporate bonds, I show that, excluding the crisis, the inventory benefit per trade is on average 8.4 basis points larger than the inventory costs. Abstract :关于经销商流动性供应和库存管理的现有文献主要关注经销商库存成本。我发现了一个新的库存收益渠道：经销商战略性地建立库存以争夺市场份额。我评估了存货收益和成本对经销商流动性供应定价和存货管理的相对影响。使用美国公司债券的跟踪数据，我发现，除去危机，每笔交易的库存收益平均比库存成本高8.4个基点。这一结果在危机期间发生逆转，经销商的库存成本大幅上升。

\[4\] Rationally Turbulent Learning

标题:理性学习

作者:Kent Osband

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3903671

Abstract : In standard models of rational learning from experience, prior uncertainties and disagreements recede smoothly as common evidence accumulates. However, this presumes that the underlying risks are relatively stable. Otherwise, rational learners need to sift random noise for signs that the trend has changed. When the evidence is sufficiently confusing, uncertainties will surge and core disagreements widen. In general, rational learning tends to bifurcate into calm and turbulent phases, with some similarities to the flows of rivers or winds. The bifurcation is rooted in an endless cumulant hierarchy, where the volatility of each cumulant varies with the cumulant one order higher.

Keywords : uncertainty, probabilities, Bayesian, belief, surprise, ignorance, Markov chain, imperfect information, financial forecast, learning, turbulence

Abstract :在从经验中理性学习的标准模型中，随着共同证据的积累，先前的不确定性和分歧逐渐消失。然而，这假定潜在风险相对稳定。否则，理性的学习者需要筛选随机噪音，寻找趋势已经改变的迹象。当证据充分令人困惑时，不确定性将激增，核心分歧将扩大。一般来说，理性学习倾向于分成平静和动荡两个阶段，与河流或风的流动有一些相似之处。分岔的根源在于一个无限的累积量层次结构，其中每个累积量的波动性随累积量高一个阶而变化。

Keywords :不确定性、概率、贝叶斯、信念、意外、无知、马尔可夫链、不完全信息、财务预测、学习、动荡

\[6\] The Macroeconomics of Financial Speculation

标题:金融投机的宏观经济学

作者:Alp Simsek

出处:Annual Review of Economics, Vol. 13, pp. 335-369, 2021, Vol. 13, pp. 335-369

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3903112

Abstract : I review the literature on financial speculation driven by belief disagreements from a macroeconomics perspective. To highlight unifying themes, I develop a stylized macroeconomic model that embeds several mechanisms. With short-selling constraints, speculation can generate overvaluation and speculative bubbles. Leverage can substantially inflate speculative bubbles, and leverage limits depend on perceived downside risks. Shifts in beliefs about downside tail scenarios can explain the emergence and the collapse of leveraged speculative bubbles. Speculative bubbles are related to rational bubbles, but they match better the empirical evidence on the predictability of asset returns. Even without short-selling constraints, speculation induces procyclical asset valuation. When speculation affects the price of aggregate assets, it also influences macroeconomic outcomes such as aggregate consumption, investment, and output. Speculation in the boom years reduces asset prices, aggregate demand, and output in the subsequent recession. Macroprudential policies that restrict speculation in the boom can improve macroeconomic stability and social welfare.

Keywords : None

Abstract :我从宏观经济学的角度回顾了由信念分歧驱动的金融投机的文献。为了突出统一的主题，我开发了一个嵌入了多种机制的风格化宏观经济模型。在卖空限制条件下，投机可能导致估值过高和投机泡沫。杠杆可以极大地膨胀投机泡沫，杠杆限制取决于感知的下行风险。关于下行尾部情景的信念转变可以解释杠杆投机泡沫的出现和崩溃。投机泡沫与理性泡沫相关，但它们更符合资产回报可预测性的经验证据。即使没有卖空约束，投机也会导致资产估值的顺周期性。当投机影响总资产价格时，它也会影响宏观经济结果，如总消费、投资和产出。繁荣时期的投机会在随后的衰退中降低资产价格、总需求和产出。在繁荣时期限制投机的宏观审慎政策可以改善宏观经济稳定和社会福利。

\[7\] Materiality and the Efficient Capital Market Model: A Recipe for the Total Mix

标题:实质性和有效资本市场模型：总组合的配方

作者:Roger J. Dennis

出处:William & Mary Law Review, Vol. 25, No. 3, 1984

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3899400

Abstract : During the past thirty years, prodigious empirical and theoretical research and commentary has provided an economic perspective on the operation of capital markets. The literature has focused on the efficient capital market model, which posits that security prices fully reflect all available, relevant information. A corollary of this thesis posits that security prices react promptly and in an unbiased manner to any new information. Although researchers have debated the parameters of the efficient market model, they agree that the model accurately represents the market's actual operation.Not surprisingly, the operation of capital markets also has been a central concern in securities litigation. In many cases, the determinative issues involve the importance of particular information to the investor and the processing of such information by the market. These issues often are decided under a legal standard of "materiality." Materiality may be important in several types of rule 10b-5 litigation, tender offer cases, and proxy litigation, among other areas.The legal standard of materiality, however, has developed largely without reference to the findings of the efficient capital market researchers. This Article will compare legal notions of materiality with the treatment of materiality in efficient capital market theory. The Article presents an overview of the current state of research on the efficient capital market model, a discussion of the legal tests of materiality, and a comparison of the legal standard and the economic model in different contexts. This Article's premise is that recognition and use of the economic model will lead to more accurate, predictable determinations of materiality in the context of securities litigation. Courts that consider the results of efficient capital market research in analyzing the materiality issue will have a better understanding of when information becomes public, how the market processes information as an aggregate, and how different estimates of the significance. of new information quickly combine to create an unbiased equilibrium market price.

Keywords : None

Abstract :在过去三十年中，大量的实证和理论研究以及评论为资本市场的运作提供了一个经济学视角。文献集中于有效资本市场模型，该模型假定证券价格充分反映所有可用的相关信息。本文的一个推论是，证券价格对任何新信息都能迅速、公正地作出反应。尽管研究人员对有效市场模型的参数进行了辩论，但他们一致认为该模型准确地反映了市场的实际运作。毫不奇怪，资本市场的运作也一直是证券诉讼的核心问题。在许多情况下，决定性问题涉及特定信息对投资者的重要性以及市场对此类信息的处理。这些问题通常是根据“重要性”的法律标准来决定的。重要性在规则10b-5诉讼、要约收购案件和代理诉讼等领域中可能很重要。然而，重要性的法律标准在很大程度上是在没有参考高效资本市场研究人员的发现的情况下发展起来的。本文将对重要性的法律概念与有效资本市场理论中对重要性的处理进行比较。本文概述了有效资本市场模型的研究现状，讨论了重要性的法律检验，并比较了不同背景下的法律标准和经济模型。本文的前提是，在证券诉讼中，承认和使用经济模型将导致更准确、可预测的重要性确定。考虑有效资本市场研究结果的法院在分析信息重要性时会更好地理解信息何时成为公众，市场如何处理信息作为一个集合，以及如何对不同的重要性进行估计。各种新信息迅速结合起来，形成一个无偏的均衡市场价格。

Keywords :None

\[8\] Common Risk Factors in the Cross Section of Catastrophe Bond Returns

标题:巨灾债券收益横截面中的常见风险因素

作者:Alexander Braun,Markus Herrmann,Martin Thomas Hibbeln

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3901695

Abstract : Historically, cat bonds have provided high single-digit average annual returns, paired with a low volatility and little correlation to other asset classes. While there is an extensive literature that explains (ex-ante) cat bonds spreads, there is no factor model in the academic literature that explains this (ex-post) realized return puzzle. Based on monthly quoted prices for the complete cat bond market from 2001 to 2020, we provide insights into relevant risk factors in the cross-section of cat bond returns. After investigating a battery of possible cat bond return factors in bivariate and multivariate portfolio sorts as well as Fama-MacBeth regressions, we propose a four-factor cat bond model. Its factors are the seasonality adjusted probability of first loss, a separate seasonality adjustment factor and the two corporate bond factors TERM and DEF from Fama & French. This novel four-factor model predicts 60% of the time series variation of the historical cat bond market returns - as opposed to 4% for the Fama & French three- or five-factor model - and substantially reduces the observable alpha of the cat bond market.

Keywords : Catastrophe Bonds, Asset Pricing, Factor Model

Abstract :从历史上看，cat债券提供了高个位数的平均年回报率，同时波动性较低，与其他资产类别的相关性很小。虽然有大量文献解释了（事前）cat债券的利差，但学术文献中没有因子模型解释这一（事后）已实现回报难题。根据2001年至2020年整个cat债券市场的月度报价，我们提供了cat债券收益横截面中相关风险因素的见解。在研究了一系列可能的二元和多元投资组合类别的cat债券收益因素以及Fama-MacBeth回归后，我们提出了一个四因素cat债券模型。其因素包括经季节性调整的首次损失概率、一个单独的季节性调整因素以及Fama&French提供的两个公司债券因素期限和DEF。这一新颖的四因素模型预测了历史cat债券市场收益率时间序列变化的60%——而Fama&French三因素或五因素模型的预测值为4%——并大幅降低了cat债券市场的可观测阿尔法。

Keywords :巨灾债券、资产定价、因子模型

\[9\] Diversification and the Distribution of Portfolio Variance, Part 3: Polynomial optimisation for asset allocation

标题:多样化和投资组合方差分布，第3部分：资产配置的多项式优化

作者:Brian Fleming

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3902755

Abstract : Diversification is a fundamental topic for all investors but there remains little agreement on how to measure it. Often it is defined ambiguously through risk-based portfolio construction techniques. Recently it has been suggested to connect maximising diversification with minimising risk instability, via kurtosis, which presents practical optimisation challenges. In particular, minimising kurtosis is a non-convex problem that is typically solved using deterministic Branch-and-Bound methods, that do not scale well, or stochastic methods that provide limited guarantees on finding minima. We thus apply a deterministic hierarchical polynomial optimization framework that allows realistic asset allocation problems to be readily solved and also provides a numerical certificate of optimality.

Keywords :投资组合多样化、高阶矩、方差方差、峰度、维度、最小方差、多样化比率、风险平价、多项式优化

\[10\] MiFID II: The Impact of Double Volume Cap Mechanism on Market Quality

标题:MiFID II：双容量上限机制对市场质量的影响

作者:Zhenkai Ran

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3891160

Abstract : I provide a comprehensive evaluation of the Double Volume Cap mechanism, a regulation that regularly triggers dark trading suspension based on a stock's historical dark trading activity. By analysing the impact of each suspension wave occurring between 2018 and 2020, I show that, during the pre-COVID-19 pandemic period, the dark trading suspension improves market liquidity, worsens informational efficiency, and reduces return volatility, whereas, during the post-COVID-19 pandemic period, the suspension imposes exactly opposite effects on market quality. I also find that lifting the dark trading suspension induces exactly opposite impacts, with a larger effect size, compared to triggering suspension, providing more statistically powerful evidence of how dark trading affects market quality. These results imply that the Double Volume Cap mechanism may have brought about many unintended consequences to the market when the market needs liquidity and stability the most and when the suspension is relaxed. Nevertheless, I do identify evidence that the market gradually learns and adapts to the new trading environment as market participants reduce their reliance on dark pools over time, a result consistent with the policymaker's original objective.

Keywords : MiFID II, COVID-19, Coronavirus, Double Volume Cap, Dark Pool

Abstract :我对双量上限机制（Double Volume Cap mechanism）进行了全面评估，该机制根据股票的历史黑暗交易活动定期触发黑暗交易暂停。通过分析2018年至2020年期间发生的每一次停牌浪潮的影响，我表明，在新冠疫情爆发前，暗交易停牌提高了市场流动性，恶化了信息效率，降低了回报波动性，而在新冠疫情爆发后，暂停对市场质量产生了完全相反的影响。我还发现，与触发暂停相比，取消暗交易暂停会产生完全相反的影响，影响规模更大，为暗交易如何影响市场质量提供了更有力的统计证据。这些结果表明，当市场最需要流动性和稳定性时，以及当暂停措施放松时，双量上限机制可能会给市场带来许多意想不到的后果。然而，我确实发现了证据表明，随着市场参与者逐渐减少对暗池的依赖，市场逐渐学习并适应新的交易环境，这一结果与决策者的原始目标一致。

Keywords :MiFID II，新冠病毒-19，冠状病毒，双容量帽，暗池
