\[1\] Call of Duty: Designated Market Maker Participation in Call Auctions

标题:使命召唤：指定做市商参与召唤拍卖

作者:Erik Theissen,Christian Westheide

出处:SAFE Working Paper No. 319

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3908001

Abstract : Many equity markets combine continuous trading and call auctions. Oftentimes designated market makers (DMMs) supply additional liquidity. Whereas prior research has focused on their role in continuous trading, we provide a detailed analysis of their activity in call auctions. Using data from Germany’s Xetra system, we find that DMMs are most active when they can provide the greatest benefits to the market, i.e., in relatively illiquid stocks and at times of elevated volatility. Their trades stabilize prices and they trade profitably.

Keywords : Designated market makers, Call auctions

Abstract :许多股票市场结合了连续交易和叫价拍卖。通常，指定做市商（DMM）提供额外的流动性。之前的研究主要集中在他们在连续交易中的作用，我们对他们在看涨期权拍卖中的活动进行了详细的分析。使用德国Xetra系统的数据，我们发现，当DMM能够为市场提供最大收益时，即在流动性相对较低的股票和波动性较高的时候，DMM最为活跃。他们的交易稳定了价格，而且交易盈利。

Keywords :指定做市商，电话拍卖

\[2\] Selective Default Expectations

标题:选择性违约预期

作者:Olivier Accominotti,Thilo Albers,Kim Oosterlinck

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3907792

Abstract : Sovereign governments often discriminate between creditors during debt default episodes. This paper explores how expectations of selective default affect sovereign bond trading and sovereign risk premia based on a historical laboratory: the German external default of the 1930s. We exploit a unique feature of the interwar sovereign bond market: identical German government bonds were traded on different creditor countries’ secondary debt markets but investors expected creditors from various countries to be treated differently in case of default. We show that, when creditor countries’ secondary debt markets are integrated, selective default expectations are not reflected in bond yields but affect the volume of bonds traded across markets. By contrast, when creditors’ debt markets are geographically segmented, a large selective risk premium can be priced in sovereign bonds. This premium accounted for up to half of the total risk premium on German external bonds during the 1930s. We establish that creditor countries’ seniority ranks can be explained by their economic power over the debtor government.

Keywords : sovereign risk, debt default, secondary markets, creditor discrimination

Abstract :在债务违约期间，主权政府常常对债权人有所区别。本文基于一个历史实验室：20世纪30年代的德国外部违约，探讨选择性违约预期如何影响主权债券交易和主权风险溢价。我们利用了两次大战之间主权债券市场的一个独特特征：相同的德国政府债券在不同债权国的二级债务市场上进行交易，但投资者预计，如果发生违约，来自不同国家的债权人将受到不同的对待。我们表明，当债权国的二级债务市场一体化时，选择性违约预期不会反映在债券收益率中，而是会影响整个市场的债券交易量。相比之下，当债权人的债务市场在地理上被分割时，主权债券可以定价一个较大的选择性风险溢价。这一溢价占20世纪30年代德国对外债券总风险溢价的一半。我们认为债权国的资历等级可以用它们对债务国政府的经济权力来解释。

Keywords :主权风险、债务违约、二级市场、债权人歧视

\[3\] Short Sellers in the Realm of Social Media: Arbitrageurs or Manipulators?

标题:社交媒体领域的卖空者：套利者还是操纵者？

作者:Guilong Cai,R. David McLean,Tianyu Zhang,Mengxin Zhao

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3907480

Abstract : We study the interaction of short sellers and social media and the effect on stock prices. We use 75.1 million investment-related social media posts for 3,683 unique Chinese firms. Prior to high short interest, social media tone is abnormally positive. Once highly shorted, the tone flips and is abnormally negative. No such pattern exists with traditional media. Compared to firms that are just highly shorted, highly shorted firms with pump-and-dump patterns in social media tone have abnormal returns that are 2.7x higher before, and 3x lower after, the initiation of high short interest. Evidence from natural experiments involving China’s introduction and subsequent suspension of shorting also suggest social media manipulation. Manipulation is more likely in firms located in provinces with weaker legal environments. Our findings show that in the realm of social media, short sellers may profit more by creating mispricing than by correcting it.

Keywords : short selling, social media, manipulation, arbitrage

Abstract :我们研究卖空者和社交媒体的互动以及对股票价格的影响。我们为3683家独特的中国公司使用了7510万条与投资相关的社交媒体帖子。在高卖空利率之前，社交媒体的基调异常积极。一旦高度短路，音调就会翻转，异常为负。传统媒体不存在这样的模式。与只进行高度卖空的公司相比，在社交媒体基调中采用“推送”和“抛售”模式的高度卖空公司的异常回报率在发起高卖空兴趣之前高2.7倍，之后低3倍。来自中国引入和随后暂停做空的自然实验的证据也表明社交媒体被操纵。位于法律环境较弱省份的公司更容易受到操纵。我们的研究结果表明，在社交媒体领域，卖空者可能通过制造错误定价而不是纠正错误定价来获得更多利润。

Keywords :卖空、社交媒体、操纵、套利

\[4\] Carbon Negative Leveraged Investment Strategies

标题:碳负杠杆投资策略

作者:Ulf Erlandsson

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3906531

Abstract : Measuring the impact of portfolio allocations in terms of non-pecuniary effects is becoming more mainstream in the financial industry. A logical extension to methodologies such as portfolio carbon footprinting is that more or less capital provisioning through leverage or short positions shouldhave increased/negative non-financial effects as well. Focusing on climate impact, this paper develops a stylized model where investor capital allocations drive total economy carbon emissions, and derive the carbon footprint attributable to the investor. In an empirical application using the iTraxx Main non-financial index, we demonstrate how a generic corporate bond exposure can achieve a zero or negative carbon footprint by using a leveraged long-short overlay. The findings should be useful in terms of repositioning traditional non-impact portfolios by using overlays as well as to validate and leverage already operational ESGstrategies.

Keywords : Climate change; portfolio management

Abstract :从非金钱效应的角度衡量投资组合配置的影响正在成为金融业的主流。投资组合碳足迹等方法的逻辑延伸是，通过杠杆或空头头寸提供的资本或多或少也会增加/负面非财务影响。以气候影响为重点，本文发展了一个风格化模型，其中投资者资本分配驱动经济体的碳排放总量，并推导出可归因于投资者的碳足迹。在该模型中，使用长短策略形式的杠杆，当投资者的投资分配推动（转移）资本成本和全面经济减排时，投资者可以减少甚至使其足迹为负。在使用iTraxx主要非金融指数的实证应用中，我们展示了普通公司债券敞口如何通过杠杆式长短叠加实现零碳足迹或负碳足迹。这些发现对于通过使用覆盖重新定位传统的无影响投资组合以及验证和利用已经运行的ESGstrategies来说应该是有用的。

Keywords :气候变化；投资组合管理

\[5\] Carbon Risk Exposure in the Mutual Fund Industry

标题:共同基金行业的碳风险敞口

作者:Huan Kuang,Bing Liang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3750244

Abstract : We construct a novel carbon risk measure for mutual funds based on stock holding data and explore how carbon risk affects mutual funds’ performance, risk, and flows. We find that funds with higher carbon risk exposure have lower performance and higher unexplained risk in factor models. Furthermore, carbon risk is not fully captured in traditional Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) measures. We also find that institutional investors take carbon risk more seriously than retail investors do. Institutional funds with higher carbon risk exposure experience a negative flow shock in the subsequent period and their flow–performance relation is more sensitive. Moreover, when there is more news coverage on climate change, carbon risk exposure affects fund flows more negatively. These findings do not apply to retail funds.

Keywords : Climate change; Carbon risk; Mutual fund performance; Mutual fund flow; Socially responsible investing

Abstract :我们基于持股数据构建了一个新的共同基金碳风险测度，并探讨了碳风险如何影响共同基金的绩效、风险和流量。我们发现，在因子模型中，碳风险敞口较高的基金表现较低，且未解释风险较高。此外，传统的环境、社会和治理（ESG）措施并未充分捕捉碳风险。我们还发现，机构投资者比散户投资者更重视碳风险。具有较高碳风险敞口的机构基金在随后一段时间内会经历负流量冲击，其流量-绩效关系更为敏感。此外，当有更多关于气候变化的新闻报道时，碳风险敞口对资金流动的影响更为负面。这些发现不适用于零售基金。

Keywords :气候变化；碳风险；共同基金业绩；共同基金流动；社会责任投资

\[7\] Option Pricing under Stochastic Volatility Models with Latent Volatility

标题:具有潜在波动率的随机波动率模型下的期权定价

作者:Jean-François Bégin,Frédéric Godin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3907701

Abstract : An important challenge regarding the pricing of derivatives is related to the latent nature of volatility. Most studies disregard the uncertain nature of volatility when pricing options; the few authors who account for it typically consider the risk-neutral posterior distribution of the latent volatility. As the latter distribution differs from its physical measure counterpart, this leads to at least two issues: (1) it generates some unwanted path dependence, and (2) it oftentimes requires tracking simultaneously the physical and risk-neutral distributions of the latent volatility. This article presents pricing approaches purging such a path-dependence issue. This is achieved by modifying conventional pricing approaches (e.g., the Girsanov transform) to formally recognize the uncertainty about the latent volatility during the pricing procedure. The two proposed risk-neutral measures circumventing the aforementioned undesired path-dependence feature are based on either the extended Girsanov principle or the Esscher transform. We also show that such pricing approaches are feasible, and we provide numerical implementation schemes.

Keywords : Option pricing, Stochastic volatility, Particle filters, Path dependence

Abstract :衍生品定价方面的一个重要挑战与波动性的潜在性质有关。大多数研究忽略了期权定价时波动性的不确定性；少数作者通常考虑潜在中性波动的风险中性后验分布。由于后一种分布与其物理度量对应分布不同，这至少会导致两个问题：（1）它会产生一些不必要的路径依赖性，（2）它通常需要同时跟踪潜在波动率的物理分布和风险中性分布。本文介绍了清除这种路径依赖问题的定价方法。这是通过修改传统的定价方法（如Girsanov变换）来实现的，以正式识别定价过程中潜在波动的不确定性。提出的两种规避上述不希望的路径依赖特征的风险中性措施基于扩展的Girsanov原理或Esscher变换。我们还证明了这种定价方法是可行的，并给出了数值实现方案。

Keywords :期权定价，随机波动率，粒子滤波，路径依赖

\[9\] The Strategic Use of 13F Restatement by Hedge Funds

标题:对冲基金对13F重述的战略运用

作者:Sean Cao,Zhi Da,Daniel Jiang,Baozhong Yang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3907560

Abstract : Hedge funds can amend their originally reported 13F holdings using restatements subsequently. We conduct the first systematic analysis of such filings, which are as common as the confidential filings but affect three times more stocks. We find the restated holdings are associated with significant abnormal returns, suggesting that restatements are often used strategically to hide funds’ trading intention (for both buys and sells). We examine fund- and stock-characteristics that are related to this strategic choice. We also construct a return gap measure to gauge the value-added from such restatements and find it to predict future fund performance. Finally, we show that commonly used databases such as Thomson Reuters do not fully adjust for restatements. While the resulting discrepancy is small in aggregate, it can be large for many funds.

Keywords : Strategic Disclosure, Hedge Funds, Ownership Disclosure, 13F Holdings, Restatement, Fund Skill

Abstract :对冲基金可以通过随后的重述修改其最初报告的13F持有量。我们对此类文件进行了首次系统分析，这些文件与机密文件一样常见，但影响的股票数量是机密文件的三倍。我们发现重述持股与显著的异常回报率相关，这表明重述经常被战略性地用于隐藏基金的交易意图（无论是买入还是卖出）。我们研究了与这一战略选择相关的基金和股票特征。我们还构建了一个回报缺口测度，以衡量此类重述的附加值，并发现其可预测未来基金业绩。最后，我们发现汤森路透等常用数据库并没有完全调整重述。虽然由此产生的差异在总体上很小，但对许多基金来说可能很大。

Keywords :战略披露、对冲基金、所有权披露、13F持股、重述、基金技能

\[10\] The currency that came in from the cold: Capital controls and the information content of order flow

标题:来自寒冷的货币：资本控制和订单流的信息内容

作者:Francis Breedon,Thórarinn G. Pétursson,Paolo Vitale

出处:Central Bank of Iceland Working Paper 2021/86

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3907289

Abstract : We analyse how capital controls affect FX microstructure, using as a case study the introduction and subsequent removal of controls in Iceland. We use a VAR of private order flow, Central Bank order flow and EURISK that allows for contemporaneous feedback effects to analyse the impact and information content of trades and find that controls have profound effects. When controls were introduced, volume plummeted, the information content of trading activity declined and became less responsive to macro news. While there was no recovery of trading volume after controls were abolished, the information content and responsiveness of trading activity increased sharply.

Keywords : Exchange rates, Order flow, Market microstructure, Capital controls

Abstract :我们分析了资本管制如何影响外汇微观结构，并以冰岛引入和随后取消管制作为案例研究。我们使用私人订单流、中央银行订单流和EURISK的VAR（允许同时反馈效应）来分析交易的影响和信息内容，并发现控制具有深远的影响。当实行管制时，交易量急剧下降，交易活动的信息含量下降，对宏观新闻的反应变差。虽然取消管制后交易量没有恢复，但交易活动的信息含量和反应能力急剧增加。
