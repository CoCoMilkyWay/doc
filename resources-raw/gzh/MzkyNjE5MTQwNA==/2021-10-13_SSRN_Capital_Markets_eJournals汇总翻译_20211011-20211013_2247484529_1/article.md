\[1\] ESG Confusion and Stock Returns: Tackling the Problem of Noise

标题:ESG混乱和股票回报：解决噪音问题

作者:Florian Berg,Julian F Kölbel,Anna Pavlova,Roberto Rigobon

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3941514

Abstract : How strongly does ESG (environmental, social and governance) performance affect stock returns? Answering this question is difficult because existing measures of performance, ESG ratings, are noisy. To tackle the bias, we propose a noise-correction procedure, in which we instrument ESG ratings with ratings of other ESG rating agencies, as in the classical errors-in-variables problem. The corrected estimates demonstrate that the effect of ESG performance on stock returns is stronger than previously estimated; the standard regression estimates of ESG ratings' impact on stock returns are biased downward by about 60%. Our dataset includes scores of eight ESG rating agencies for firms located in North America, Europe, and Japan. We determine which agencies’ scores are valid instruments (not all of them are) and estimate the noise-to-signal ratio for each ESG rating agency (some of which are very large). Overall, our results suggest that it is advantageous to rely on several complementary ratings. In our sample, stocks with higher ESG performance have higher expected returns. Our model provides several explanations for this finding.

Keywords : ESG, errors-in-variables, attenuation bias, ratings, stock returns, noise

Abstract :ESG（环境、社会和治理）绩效对股票回报的影响有多大？回答这个问题是困难的，因为现有的绩效衡量标准，ESG评级，是嘈杂的。为了解决这一偏差，我们提出了一个噪音校正程序，在该程序中，我们将ESG评级与其他ESG评级机构的评级进行了比较，就像经典的变量误差问题一样。修正后的估计表明，ESG绩效对股票回报的影响比先前估计的更大；ESG评级对股票回报影响的标准回归估计值向下偏移了约60%。我们的数据集包括位于北美、欧洲和日本的八家ESG评级机构的得分。我们确定哪些机构的分数是有效的工具（并非所有机构都是），并估计每个ESG评级机构的噪声信号比（其中一些非常大）。总的来说，我们的结果表明，依赖几个互补评级是有利的。在我们的样本中，ESG表现较高的股票具有较高的预期回报。我们的模型为这一发现提供了多种解释。

Keywords :ESG、变量误差、衰减偏差、评级、股票回报、噪音

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3941220

Abstract : This paper examines the relationship between sentiment beta and stock returns in China’s stock market. Stocks with low (negative) investor sentiment beta significantly outperform those with high investor sentiment beta (positive), and an against minus catering sentiment (AMC) pricing factor is identified in Chinese stock market. \[4\] Benchmarking Individual Corporate Bonds

标题:个人公司债券基准

作者:Xin He,Guanhao Feng,Junbo Wang,Chunchi Wu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3940817

Abstract : We propose an alternative approach to the linear factor model to estimate and decompose asset risk premia in empirical asset pricing. To resolve the high-dimensional sort difficulty in forming characteristic-based benchmark portfolios, we introduce a benchmark combination model (BCM) that combines multiple basis portfolios as the pricing kernel. With a non-arbitrage objective, our approach minimizes cross-sectional pricing errors and identifies the sources of risk through the combination of basis portfolios. For a 40-year sample for U.S. corporate bonds, we find that BCM outperforms standard factor models in pricing corporate bonds. Second, our study shows that credit ratings, downside risk, and short-term reversal are primary sources of bond risk premia. Finally, incorporating machine learning forecasts into the BCM, we find strong evidence of return predictability.

Keywords : Characteristic-based portfolios, high-dimensional sort, corporate bond risk premia, forecast combination, machine learning, return predictability

Abstract :我们提出了一种替代线性因子模型的方法来估计和分解经验资产定价中的资产风险溢价。为了解决基于特征的基准组合形成中的高维排序困难，我们引入了一个基准组合模型（BCM），该模型将多个基准组合组合作为定价核心。在无套利的目标下，我们的方法最大限度地减少了横截面定价错误，并通过组合基准投资组合来识别风险源。对于美国公司债券的40年样本，我们发现BCM在公司债券定价方面优于标准因子模型。其次，我们的研究表明，信用评级、下行风险和短期反转是债券风险溢价的主要来源。最后，将机器学习预测纳入BCM，我们发现了回报可预测性的有力证据。

Keywords :基于特征的投资组合、高维排序、公司债券风险溢价、预测组合、机器学习、收益可预测性

\[5\] Intangible investments, scaling, and the trend in the accrual-cash flow association

标题:无形投资、规模和应计现金流关联趋势

作者:Jeremiah Green,Henock Louis,Jalal Sani

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3940735

Abstract : We provide evidence that the documented weakening of the accrual-cash flow association results not from a loss of accrual accounting usefulness per se, but from the deviation from accrual accounting as it relates to intangible investments. More specifically, the weakening of the negative association is driven by the combined effects of (1) increasing intangible investments, (2) the practice of expensing rather than capitalizing intangible investments, and (3) scaling accruals and cash flows by book value of assets, which are understated for intangible-intensive firms. Treating intangible expenditures as capitalized investments and scaling accruals and cash flows by market value of equity, which reflects the value of intangible investments, (1) substantially strengthens the negative association between accruals and cash flows and (2) practically eliminates the apparent weakening trend in the association.

Keywords : Accruals, Accrual accounting, Accrual quality, Cash flows, Capitalization, Earnings quality, Intangible capital, Intangible investments, Scaling by book value of assets

Abstract :我们提供的证据表明，应计现金流关联的弱化并非由于应计会计有用性本身的损失，而是由于与无形投资相关的应计会计的偏差。更具体地说，负关联的减弱是由以下综合效应驱动的：（1）无形投资增加，（2）无形投资成本化而非资本化的做法，（3）按资产账面价值调整应计项目和现金流，这对于无形密集型公司来说是低估的。将无形支出视为资本化投资，并根据反映无形投资价值的股权市值调整应计项目和现金流，（1）大幅加强应计项目和现金流之间的负关联，（2）实际上消除了关联中明显的弱化趋势。

Keywords :权责发生制、权责发生制会计、权责发生制质量、现金流量、资本化、收益质量、无形资本、无形投资、资产账面价值比例

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3927410

Abstract : This paper examines the cross-section of delta-hedged equity option returns, which are found to be predictable by some characteristics of options and those of their underlying stocks. Using the LASSO method to evaluate the forecasting ability of a large set of predictor candidates, we identify the dominant characteristics that provide incremental information for delta-hedged option returns. 出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3939971

Abstract : We introduce a heterogeneous agent model which features extrapolative beliefs and time-varying risk aversion. The model leads to an empirical framework which we estimate with stock prices, survey data and risk aversion measures. We find that extrapolative beliefs and risk aversion are important drivers of stock prices explaining together 86% of movements in the S&P500 index: extrapolative cash flow expectations explain 34%, extrapolative return expectations explain 23% and time-variation in risk aversion explains 29%. Keywords :异质投资者、外推预期、股市估值、资产定价、调查预期、价格分解

\[10\] An Analytic Solution to the CAPM Equilibrium

作者:Pharos Abad

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3939960

Abstract : We determine the one-dimensional general solution to the semi-equilibrium prices of primitive securities in the equation for the capital asset pricing model (CAPM). Abstract : Why do firms engage in costly, voluntary disclosure of information which is eventually subsumed by a later announcement? We consider a model in which the firm’s manager can choose to disclose short-term information which becomes redundant later.
