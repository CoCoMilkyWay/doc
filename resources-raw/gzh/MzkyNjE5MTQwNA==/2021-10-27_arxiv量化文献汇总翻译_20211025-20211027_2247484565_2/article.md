\[1\] Efficient ISDA Initial Margin Calculations Using Least Squares Monte Carlo

标题 : 使用最小二乘蒙特卡罗法计算有效的ISDA初始保证金

作者 : Asif Lakhany, Amber Zhang , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13296

说明 : Comments: 14 pages

Abstract : Non cleared bilateral OTC derivatives between two financial firms or systemically important non financial entities are subject to regulations that require the posting of initial and variation margin. The ISDA standard approach (SIMM) provides a way for computing the initial margin. It involves computing sensitivities of the contracts with respect to several market factors. In this paper, the authors extend the well known LSMC technique to efficiently estimate the sensitivities required in the ISDA SIMM methodology.

摘要 : 两个金融公司或具有系统重要性的非金融实体之间的未清算双边场外衍生品受要求过账初始保证金和变动保证金的监管。ISDA标准方法（SIMM）提供了一种计算初始保证金的方法。它涉及计算合同对几个市场因素的敏感性。在本文中，作者扩展了众所周知的LSMC技术，以有效地估计ISDA SIMM方法中所需的灵敏度。

\[2\] Wealth heterogeneity in a closed pooled annuity fund

标题 : 封闭式集合年金基金的财富异质性

作者 : Thomas Bernhardt, Ge Qu , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13467

说明 : Comments: 22 pages, 7 figures

Abstract : The income stability of a closed pooled annuity fund is studied. The focus is on quantifying the impact of inhomogeneous initial savings amounts on idiosyncratic longevity risk. Besides wealth inhomogeneity, the members of the pool are independent and identical copies of each other. We ignore systematic investment risk or mortality risk and define income stability as keeping the income within a specified tolerance of each member s initial income payment in a fixed proportion of future scenarios. For a given group of people with different savings amounts, we find an analytical expression that closely approximates the time for which the fund provides a stable income. Our main result uses this expression to determine if everyone benefits from pooling their funds with the whole group or how to split a cohort into different pooled annuity funds.

摘要 : 研究了封闭式集合年金基金的收益稳定性。重点是量化不均匀初始储蓄金额对特质长寿风险的影响。除了财富的不均一性，池中的成员是相互独立、完全相同的副本。我们忽略了系统性投资风险或死亡风险，并将收入稳定性定义为在未来情景的固定比例下，将收入保持在每个成员的初始收入付款的规定容差内。对于具有不同储蓄金额的给定人群，我们发现一个解析表达式，该表达式非常接近基金提供稳定收入的时间。我们的主要结果是使用这个表达式来确定是否每个人都能从将他们的资金与整个群体集中起来中受益，或者如何将一个群体分成不同的集中年金基金。

\[3\] Zero Liquidation Loans

作者 : Aetienne Sardon , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13533

说明 : -

Abstract : Zero Liquidation loans allow DeFi users to borrow USDC against their ETH holdings, but without the risk of being liquidated in case of LTV shortfalls. This is achieved by giving users the option to repay their loans, either in USDC or through their previously pledged ETH (the concept can be generalized to other currency pairs as well). Liquidity providers, on the other hand side, are compensated with a higher yield for bearing the ETH downside risk. \[4\] Large Platonic Markets with Delays

标题 : 具有延迟的大型柏拉图式市场

作者 : Yannick Limmer, Thilo Meyer-Brandis , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13678

说明 : Comments: 12 pages

Abstract : The objective is to develop a general stochastic approach to delays on financial markets. We suggest such a concept in the context of large platonic markets, which allow infinitely many assets and incorporate a restricted information setting. The discussion is divided into information delays and order execution delays. The former enables modelling of markets where the observed information is delayed, while the latter provides the opportunity to defer the indexed time of a received asset price. Both delays may be designed randomly and inhomogeneously over time. We show that delayed markets are equipped with a fundamental theorem of asset pricing and our main result is inheritance of the no asymptotic Lp free lunch condition under both delay types. Eventually, we suggest an approach to verify absence of Lp free lunch on markets with multiple brokers endowed with deviating trading speeds.

摘要 : 我们的目标是开发一种通用的随机方法来解决金融市场上的延迟问题。我们在大型柏拉图式市场的背景下提出这样一个概念，该市场允许无限多的资产，并包含有限的信息设置。讨论分为信息延迟和订单执行延迟。前者能够对观察到的信息延迟的市场进行建模，而后者提供了延迟已收到资产价格的指数时间的机会。这两种延迟可以随时间随机且不均匀地设计。我们证明了时滞市场具有资产定价的一个基本定理，并且我们的主要结果是在这两种时滞类型下无渐近Lp自由午餐条件的继承性。最后，我们提出了一种方法来验证在多个经纪人交易速度不同的市场上是否存在Lp免费午餐。

\[5\] Heterogenous criticality in high frequency finance

标题 : 高频金融中的异质临界性

作者 : Jeremy Turiel, Tomaso Aste , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13701

说明 : Comments: 5 pages, 3 figures

Abstract : Flash crashes in financial markets have become increasingly important attracting attention from financial regulators, market makers as well as from the media and the broader audience. Systemic risk and propagation of shocks in financial markets is also a topic or great relevance who has attracted increasing attention in recent years. In the present work we bridge the gap between these two topics with an in depth investigation of the systemic risk structure of co crashes in high frequency trading. We find that large co crashes are systemic in their nature and differ from small crashes. We demonstrate that there is a phase transition between co crashes of small and large sizes, where the former involves mostly illiquid stocks while large and liquid stocks are the most represented and central in the latter. This suggest that systemic effects and shock propagation might be triggered by simultaneous withdrawn or movement of liquidity by HFTs and market makers having cross asset.

摘要 : 金融市场中的闪电崩盘已变得越来越重要，吸引了金融监管机构、做市商以及媒体和广大受众的关注。金融市场中的系统性风险和冲击传播也是近年来越来越受到关注的一个话题或非常相关的话题。在目前的工作中，我们通过深入调查高频交易中共同崩溃的系统性风险结构，弥合了这两个主题之间的差距。我们发现，大型共同碰撞在本质上是系统性的，不同于小型碰撞。我们证明了小型股和大型股的共同崩盘之间存在相变，前者主要涉及非流动性股票，而大型股和流动性股票在后者中最具代表性和核心性。这表明HFT和具有交叉资产的做市商同时撤回或移动流动性可能会触发系统效应和冲击传播。

\[6\] HIST

标题 : 历史的

作者 : Wentao Xu, Weiqing Liu, Lewen Wang, Yingce Xia, Jiang Bian, Jian Yin, Tie-Yan Liu , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13716

说明 : -

Abstract : Stock trend forecasting, which forecasts stock prices future trends, plays an essential role in investment. The stocks in a market can share information so that their stock prices are highly correlated. Several methods were recently proposed to mine the shared information through stock concepts (e.g., technology, Internet Retail) extracted from the Web to improve the forecasting results. However, previous work assumes the connections between stocks and concepts are stationary, and neglects the dynamic relevance between stocks and concepts, limiting the forecasting results. Moreover, existing methods overlook the invaluable shared information carried by hidden concepts, which measure stocks commonness beyond the manually defined stock concepts. To overcome the shortcomings of previous work, we proposed a novel stock trend forecasting framework that can adequately mine the concept oriented shared information from predefined concepts and hidden concepts. The proposed framework simultaneously utilize the stock s shared information and individual information to improve the stock trend forecasting performance. Experimental results on the real world tasks demonstrate the efficiency of our framework on stock trend forecasting. The investment simulation shows that our framework can achieve a higher investment return than the baselines.

摘要 : 股票走势预测是预测股票价格未来走势的一种方法，在投资中起着至关重要的作用。市场中的股票可以共享信息，因此它们的股价高度相关。最近提出了几种方法，通过从Web中提取的股票概念（如技术、互联网零售）挖掘共享信息，以改进预测结果。然而，以前的工作假设股票和概念之间的联系是静态的，忽略了股票和概念之间的动态相关性，限制了预测结果。此外，现有的方法忽略了隐藏概念所携带的宝贵共享信息，这些隐藏概念衡量的股票共性超出了手动定义的股票概念。为了克服以往工作的不足，我们提出了一种新的股票趋势预测框架，该框架能够从预定义的概念和隐藏的概念中充分挖掘面向概念的共享信息。该框架同时利用股票的共享信息和个人信息来提高股票趋势预测性能。通过对实际任务的实验结果，验证了该框架在股票走势预测中的有效性。投资模拟表明，我们的框架可以实现比基线更高的投资回报。

\[7\] Self organised criticality in high frequency finance

标题 : 高频金融中的自组织临界性

作者 : Jeremy D. Turiel, Tomaso Aste , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13718

说明 : Comments: 4 pages, 2 figures

Abstract : With the rise of computing and artificial intelligence, advanced modeling and forecasting has been applied to High Frequency markets. A crucial element of solid production modeling though relies on the investigation of data distributions and how they relate to modeling assumptions. In this work we investigate volume distributions during anomalous price events and show how their tail exponents 2 indicate a diverging second moment of the distribution, i.e. variance. We then tie the dynamics of flash crashes to self organised criticality. The findings are of great relevance for regulators and market makers as they advocate for rigorous heavy tailed modeling of risk and changes in regulation to avoid simultaneous liquidity withdrawals and hard risk constraints which lead to synchronisation and critical events.

摘要 : 随着计算和人工智能的兴起，先进的建模和预测技术已应用于高频市场。然而，实体生产建模的一个关键要素依赖于对数据分布的调查以及它们与建模假设的关系。在这项工作中，我们研究了异常价格事件期间的成交量分布，并展示了它们的尾部指数2如何表示分布的发散二阶矩，即方差。然后，我们将闪存崩溃的动力学与自组织临界性联系起来。这些发现对监管者和做市商非常重要，因为他们主张对风险和监管变化进行严格的重尾建模，以避免同时出现流动性撤回和硬风险约束，从而导致同步和关键事件。

\[8\] Learning New Auction Format by Bidders in Internet Display Ad Auctions

标题 : 网上展示广告拍卖中竞买人学习新的拍卖方式

作者 : Shumpei Goke, Gabriel Y. Weintraub, Ralph Mastromonaco, Sam Seljan , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13814

说明 : Comments: 31 pages, 34 figures

Abstract : We study actual bidding behavior when a new auction format gets introduced into the marketplace. More specifically, we investigate this question using a novel data set on internet display ad auctions that exploits a staggered adoption by different publishers (sellers) of first price auctions (FPAs), in place for the traditional second price auctions (SPAs). Event study regression estimates indicate a significant jump, immediately after the auction format change, in revenue per sold impression (price) of the treated publishers relative to that of control publishers, ranging from 35 to 75 of pre treatment price levels of the treated group. Further, we observe that in later auction format changes the lift in price relative to SPAs dissipates over time, reminiscent of the celebrated revenue equivalence theorem. We take this as evidence of initially insufficient bid shading after the format change rather than an immediate shift to a new Bayesian Nash equilibrium. Prices then went down as bidders learned to shade their bids. We also show that bidders sophistication impacted their response to the auction format change. Our work constitutes one of the first field studies on bidders responses to auction format changes, providing an important complement to theoretical model predictions. As such, it provides valuable information to auction designers when considering the implementation of different formats.

摘要 : 我们研究当一种新的拍卖形式被引入市场时的实际竞价行为。更具体地说，我们使用互联网显示广告拍卖上的一个新数据集来研究这个问题，该数据集利用了不同出版商（卖家）交错采用的第一价格拍卖（FPA），这是传统的第二价格拍卖（SPA）。事件研究回归估计表明，在拍卖形式改变后，与对照出版商相比，接受治疗的出版商的每售出印象（价格）收入显著增加，范围为接受治疗组治疗前价格水平的35到75。此外，我们观察到，在后来的拍卖形式变化中，相对于水疗的价格提升会随着时间的推移而消散，这让人想起著名的收入等价定理。我们将此视为格式更改后初始投标底纹不足的证据，而不是立即转向新的贝叶斯纳什均衡。价格随后下跌，因为竞购者学会了掩盖他们的出价。我们还表明，投标人的复杂程度影响了他们对拍卖格式变化的反应。我们的工作是关于投标人对拍卖形式变化反应的第一批实地研究之一，为理论模型预测提供了重要补充。因此，在考虑不同格式的实现时，它为拍卖设计师提供了有价值的信息。

\[10\] Towards Realistic Market Simulations

标题 : 走向现实的市场模拟

作者 : Andrea Coletta, Matteo Prata, Michele Conti, Emanuele Mercanti, Novella Bartolini, Aymeric Moulin, Svitlana Vyetrenko, Tucker Balch , 类别 ：Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.13287

说明 : Comments: 8 pages, 9 figures, ICAIF'21 - 2nd ACM International Conference on AI in Finance

Abstract : Simulated environments are increasingly used by trading firms and investment banks to evaluate trading strategies before approaching real markets. Backtesting, a widely used approach, consists of simulating experimental strategies while replaying historical market scenarios. Unfortunately, this approach does not capture the market response to the experimental agents actions. In contrast, multi agent simulation presents a natural bottom up approach to emulating agent interaction in financial markets. It allows to set up pools of traders with diverse strategies to mimic the financial market trader population, and test the performance of new experimental strategies. Since individual agent level historical data is typically proprietary and not available for public use, it is difficult to calibrate multiple market agents to obtain the realism required for testing trading strategies. To addresses this challenge we propose a synthetic market generator based on Conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (CGANs) trained on real aggregate level historical data. A CGAN based world agent can generate meaningful orders in response to an experimental agent. We integrate our synthetic market generator into ABIDES, an open source simulator of financial markets. By means of extensive simulations we show that our proposal outperforms previous work in terms of stylized facts reflecting market responsiveness and realism.

摘要 : 交易公司和投资银行越来越多地使用模拟环境在接近真实市场之前评估交易策略。回溯测试是一种广泛使用的方法，它包括模拟实验策略，同时回放历史市场情景。不幸的是，这种方法没有捕捉到市场对实验性代理行为的反应。相比之下，多代理模拟提供了一种自然的自底向上的方法来模拟金融市场中的代理交互。它允许建立具有不同策略的交易员池，以模拟金融市场交易员群体，并测试新实验策略的性能。由于单个代理级别的历史数据通常是专有的，不可供公众使用，因此很难校准多个市场代理以获得测试交易策略所需的真实性。为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了一种基于条件生成对抗网络（CGAN）的合成市场生成器，该网络基于真实聚合级历史数据进行训练。基于CGAN的世界代理可以响应实验代理生成有意义的订单。我们将合成市场生成器集成到ABIDES中，这是一个开放源码的金融市场模拟器。通过广泛的模拟，我们表明，我们的建议在反映市场反应性和现实性的程式化事实方面优于以前的工作。
