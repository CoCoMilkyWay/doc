\[1\] Do Activities of Foreign Investors Affect Main Stock Exchange Indices? Evidence from Turkey before and in Time of COVID-19 Pandemic

标题:外国投资者的活动是否影响主要股票交易指数？在COVID-19大流行之前和期间来自土耳其的证据

作者:Mustafa Tevfik Kartal(Borsa Istanbul - Strategic Planning and Investor Relations Directorate)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3753605

Abstract : The net selling amount and foreign investors’ retention share have been criticized due to the effects on stock exchange indices. The study focuses on the development of the main index in Turkey (XU100 index) by examining two sub-parts (i.e. before and in COVID-19 pandemic). Daily data between 01.02.2020 and 06.26.2020 are analyzed by applying the Engle-Granger cointegration test, Toda-Yamamoto causality test, and multivariate adaptive regression splines (MARS). The findings reveal that (i) net selling amount of foreign investors has an effect on the index in COVID-19 pandemic times; (ii) retention share of foreign investors is influential on the index for both period; (iii) there is cointegration between the index and independent variables but this relationship is not at causality level.

Keywords : COVID-19 pandemic; foreign investors; main stock exchange index; net selling amount; retention share; Engle-Granger cointegration test; Toda-Yamamoto causality test; MARS

Abstract :由于对股票指数的影响，净卖出额和外国投资者的保留份额一直受到批评。本研究以土耳其主要指数（XU100指数）的发展为重点，考察了COVID-19大流行前和大流行期间的两个子部分。应用Engle-Granger协整检验、Toda-Yamamoto因果关系检验和多元自适应回归样条（MARS）对2020年2月1日至6月26日的日数据进行分析。研究结果表明：（i）在COVID-19大流行时期，外国投资者净卖出量对指数有影响；（ii）外国投资者的保留份额在两个时期都对指数有影响；（iii）指数与自变量之间存在协整关系，但这种关系不在因果关系水平上。

Keywords :COVID-19流行病；外国投资者；主要股票交易所指数；净卖出额；保留份额；恩格尔-格兰杰协整检验；山本托达因果检验；MARS
