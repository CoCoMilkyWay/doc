\[1\] Picking funds with confidence

标题:信心十足地挑选基金

作者:Niels S. Grønborg,Asger Lunde,Allan Timmermann,Russ Wermers

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20301896

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.003

Abstract : We present a new approach to selecting actively managed mutual funds that uses both portfolio holdings and fund return information to eliminate funds with predicted inferior performance through a sequence of pairwise fund comparisons. Our methodology determines both the number of skilled funds and their identities, and locates funds with substantially higher risk-adjusted returns than those identified by conventional alpha-ranking methods. We find strong evidence of time-series variation in both the number of funds identified as superior using our approach, as well as in their performance across different economic states.

Keywords : Fund confidence set;Equity mutual funds;Risk-adjusted performance

Abstract :我们提出了一种新的方法来选择积极管理的共同基金，利用投资组合持有量和基金收益信息，通过一系列成对基金比较来剔除预期业绩较差的基金。我们的方法确定了熟练基金的数量及其身份，并确定了比传统的α排序方法具有更高风险调整收益的基金。我们发现，无论是使用我们的方法确定的优势基金数量，还是它们在不同经济状态下的表现，都存在明显的时间序列差异。

Keywords :基金信心集；股票型共同基金；风险调整绩效

\[2\] Reputation and investor activism: A structural approach

标题:声誉与投资者行动主义：一种结构性的研究方法

作者:Travis L. Johnson,Nathan Swem

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20301914

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.005

Abstract : We measure the impact of reputation for proxy fighting on investor activism by estimating a dynamic model in which activists engage a sequence of target firms. Our estimation produces an evolving reputation measure for each activist and quantifies its impact on campaign frequency and outcomes. We find that high reputation activists initiate 3.5 times as many campaigns and extract 85% more settlements from targets, and that reputation-building incentives explain 20% of campaign initiations and 19% of proxy fights. Our estimates indicate these reputation effects combine to nearly double the value that activism adds for target shareholders.

Keywords : Investor activism;Reputation;Governance;Hedge funds;Structural estimation

Abstract :我们通过估计一个动态模型来衡量代理人争夺声誉对投资者积极性的影响，在这个模型中，积极分子参与了一系列目标公司。并对每一次活动的结果进行评估和量化。我们发现高声望的激进分子发起的活动是3.5倍，从目标中获得85%的和解，声誉建设激励解释了20%的活动发起和19%的代理战斗。我们的估计表明，这些声誉效应加起来几乎是激进主义为目标股东带来的价值的两倍。

Keywords :投资者行动主义；声誉；治理；对冲基金；结构估计

\[3\] Windfall gains and stock market participation

标题:暴利与股票市场参与

作者:Joseph Briggs,David Cesarini,Erik Lindqvist,Robert Östling

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302245

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.014

Abstract : We exploit the randomized assignment of lottery prizes in a large administrative Swedish data set to estimate the causal effect of wealth on stock market participation. A $150,000 windfall gain increases the stock market participation probability by 12 percentage points among prelottery nonparticipants but has no discernible effect on prelottery stock owners. A structural life cycle model significantly overpredicts entry rates even for very high entry costs (up to $31,000). Additional analyses implicate pessimistic beliefs regarding equity returns as a major source of this overprediction and suggest that both recent and early-life return realizations affect beliefs.

Keywords : Household saving and personal finance;Intertemporal consumer choice;Portfolio choice and investment decisions

Abstract :我们利用瑞典一个大型行政数据集中彩票奖金的随机分配来估计财富对股票市场参与的因果影响。15万美元的意外收益会使未参与股票市场的投资者的股票市场参与概率提高12个百分点，但对预备投资者没有明显的影响。即使是非常高的进入成本（高达31000美元），结构生命周期模型也大大高估了入学率。额外的分析暗示了悲观的看法，认为股票收益是这种过度预测的主要来源，并表明近期和早期的收益实现都会影响这种信念。

Keywords :家庭储蓄与个人理财；跨期消费者选择；投资组合选择与投资决策

\[4\] Mutual fund flows and fluctuations in credit and business cycles

标题:共同基金流动与信贷与商业周期波动

作者:Azi Ben-Rephael,Jaewon Choi,Itay Goldstein

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20301902

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.004

Abstract : Several measures of credit-market booms are known to precede downturns in real economic activity. We offer an early indicator for all known measures of credit booms. Our measure is based on intra-family flow shifts towards high-yield bond mutual funds. It predicts indicators such as growth in financial intermediary balance sheets, increase in shares of high-yield bond issuers, and downturns of various measures of credit spreads. It also directly predicts the business cycle by positively predicting GDP growth and negatively predicting unemployment. Our results provide support for the investor demand-based narrative of credit cycles and can be useful for policymakers.

Keywords : Credit cycle;Business cycle;Mutual fund flows;High-yield bonds;Investor demand;Leading indicator

Abstract :众所周知，信贷市场繁荣的几个指标先于实际经济活动的衰退。我们为所有已知的衡量信贷繁荣的指标提供了一个早期指标。我们的衡量标准是基于家族内部资金流向高收益债券共同基金。它预测了金融中介资产负债表的增长、高收益债券发行人的股票增加以及各种信用利差衡量指标的下降等指标。它还通过正面预测GDP增长和负面预测失业率来直接预测商业周期。我们的研究结果为基于投资者需求的信贷周期叙述提供了支持，并可对决策者有用。

Keywords :信贷周期；商业周期；共同基金流动；高收益债券；投资者需求；领先指标

\[5\] Common ownership and competition in product markets

标题:共同所有权与产品市场竞争

作者:Andrew Koch,Marios Panayides,Shawn Thomas

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20301938

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.007

Abstract : We investigate the relation between common institutional ownership of the firms in an industry and product market competition. We find that common ownership is neither robustly positively related with industry profitability or output prices nor is it robustly negatively related with measures of nonprice competition, as would be expected if common ownership reduces competition. This conclusion holds regardless of industry classification choice, common ownership measure, profitability measure, nonprice competition proxy, or model specification. Our point estimates are close to zero with tight bounds, rejecting even modestly sized economic effects. We conclude that antitrust restrictions seeking to limit intra-industry common ownership are not currently warranted.

Keywords : Common ownership;Governance;Competition;Horizontal merger

Abstract :我们研究了一个行业中企业的共同制度所有权与产品市场竞争的关系。我们发现，共同所有制与行业盈利能力或产出价格既没有显著的正相关关系，也没有与非价格竞争措施显著负相关，如果共同所有制减少了竞争的话。无论是产业分类选择、共同所有制测度、盈利能力测度、非价格竞争代理，还是模型规范，这一结论都成立。我们的点估计值接近于零，界限很紧，甚至还排除了适度规模的经济效应。我们的结论是，旨在限制行业内共同所有权的反垄断限制目前没有得到保证。

Keywords :共有制；治理；竞争；横向兼并

\[6\] Are return seasonalities due to risk or mispricing?

标题:回报季节性因素是由于风险还是定价错误？

作者:Matti Keloharju,Juhani T. Linnainmaa,Peter Nyberg

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20301951

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.009

Abstract : Stocks tend to earn high or low returns relative to other stocks every year in the same month (Heston and Sadka, 2008). We show these seasonalities are balanced out by seasonal reversals: a stock that has a high expected return relative to other stocks in one month has a low expected return relative to other stocks in the other months. The seasonalities and seasonal reversals add up to zero over the calendar year, which is consistent with seasonalities being driven by temporary mispricing. Seasonal reversals are economically large and statistically highly significant, and they resemble, but are distinct from, long-term reversals.

Keywords : Cross-sectional seasonalities;Reversals;Risk;Mispricing

Abstract :在同一个月里，股票相对于其他股票的回报率往往高或低（Heston和Sadka，2008）。我们发现这些季节性因素是通过季节性反转来平衡的：一只股票在一个月内相对于其他股票具有较高的预期回报，而在其他月份相对于其他股票的预期回报率较低。季节性和季节性反转在一个日历年内加起来为零，这与由临时性错误定价所驱动的季节性一致。季节性反转在经济上是巨大的，在统计上是非常显著的，它们与长期反转相似，但又不同。

Keywords :横截面季节性；逆转；风险；错误定价

\[7\] Impact investing

标题:投资影响

作者:Brad M. Barber,Adair Morse,Ayako Yasuda

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X2030194X

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.008

Abstract : We show that investors derive nonpecuniary utility from investing in dual-objective Venture Capital (VC) funds, thus sacrificing returns. Impact funds earn 4.7 percentage points (ppts) lower internal rates of return (IRRs) ex-post than traditional VC funds. In random utility/willingness-to-pay (WTP) models investors accept 2.5–3.7 ppts lower IRRs ex ante for impact funds. The positive WTP result is robust to fund access rationing and investor heterogeneity in fund expected returns. Development organizations, foundations, financial institutions, public pensions, Europeans, and United Nations Principles of Responsible Investment signatories have high WTP. Investors with mission objectives and/or facing political pressure exhibit high WTP; those subject to legal restrictions (e.g., Employee Retirement Income Security Act) exhibit low WTP.

Keywords : Impact investing;Venture capital;Private equity;Socially responsible investment;United nations principles of responsible investment (UNPRI);Sustainable investing;Public pension funds;Willingness to pay;Random utility discrete choice models

Abstract :我们发现，投资者通过投资双目标风险投资基金获得非证券效用，从而牺牲了回报。冲击基金的内部收益率（IRR）比传统风险投资基金低4.7个百分点（PPT）。在随机效用/支付意愿（WTP）模型中，投资者接受影响基金的事前内部收益率降低2.5-3.7个百分点。正的WTP结果对基金准入配给和投资者在基金预期收益方面的异质性是稳健的。发展组织、基金会、金融机构、公共养老金、欧洲和联合国负责投资原则的签署国具有较高的WTP。具有使命目标和/或面临政治压力的投资者表现出较高的WTP；受到法律限制（如《员工退休收入保障法》）的投资者表现出较低的WTP。

Keywords :影响投资；风险资本；私人股本；社会责任投资；联合国责任投资原则；可持续投资；公共养恤基金；支付意愿；随机效用离散选择模型

\[8\] Creditor control rights and resource allocation within firms

标题:债权人控制权与企业内部资源配置

作者:Nuri Ersahin,Rustom M. Irani,Hanh Le

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20301926

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.006

Abstract : We examine the within-firm resource allocation and restructuring outcomes at firms violating debt covenants. Using establishment-level data from the US Census Bureau, we find that covenant violations are followed by reductions in employment, investment, and more frequent establishment closures among violating firms’ noncore business lines and less productive establishments. These changes are concentrated among establishments at which manager-shareholder agency costs are pronounced and when key lenders have industry experience. Our findings suggest that enhanced creditor control reduces managerial agency costs and encourages a more efficient allocation of resources within the boundaries of firms in technical default.

Keywords : Control rights;Restructuring;Corporate governance;Creditors;Covenant violations

Abstract :我们研究了违反债务契约的公司内部资源配置和重组结果。利用美国人口普查局（uscensusbureau）的企业层面数据，我们发现，违反契约之后，就业、投资减少，违规企业的非核心业务线和生产率较低的企业更频繁地关闭企业。这些变化主要集中在管理者-股东代理成本显著且主要贷款人具有行业经验的机构中。我们的研究结果表明，加强债权人控制降低了管理代理成本，并鼓励在技术违约的公司边界内更有效地分配资源。

Keywords :控制权；重组；公司治理；债权人；违反契约

\[9\] Flying under the radar: The effects of short-sale disclosure rules on investor behavior and stock prices

标题:在雷达下飞行：卖空信息披露规则对投资者行为和股价的影响

作者:Stephan Jank,Christoph Roling,Esad Smajlbegovic

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302075

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.010

Abstract : We study how disclosure requirements for large short positions affect investor behavior and security prices. Short positions accumulate just below the applicable disclosure threshold as certain investors never disclose any of their positions. Further tests suggest that this secrecy is part of investors’ general policy of avoiding disclosure to protect their unique, profitable investment strategies against reverse engineering by competitors. No evidence supports the notion that short sellers avoid disclosure because of potential adverse effects on securities' lending fees, risk of recall, or short squeezes. Finally, the evasive behavior by short sellers in response to transparency regulations hampers price discovery.

Keywords : Information disclosure;Short selling;Investor behavior;Limits to arbitrage;Price discovery

Abstract :我们研究大量空头头寸的披露要求如何影响投资者行为和证券价格。由于某些投资者从不披露任何头寸，空头头寸的累积量略低于适用的披露门槛。进一步的测试表明，这种保密性是投资者避免信息披露的一般政策的一部分，以保护他们独特的、有利可图的投资策略不受竞争对手逆向工程的影响。没有证据支持这样一种观点，即卖空者会因为对证券借贷费用、召回风险或卖空挤压产生潜在的不利影响而避免披露信息。最后，卖空者对透明度法规的回避行为阻碍了价格发现。

Keywords :信息披露；卖空；投资者行为；套利限制；价格发现

\[10\] Global market inefficiencies

标题:全球市场效率低下

作者:Söhnke M. Bartram,Mark Grinblatt

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302087

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.011

Abstract : Using point-in-time accounting data, we estimate monthly fair values of 25,000+ stocks from 36 countries. A trading strategy based on deviations from fair value earns significant risk-adjusted returns (“alpha”) in most regions, especially Asia-Pacific, that are unrelated to known anomalies. The strategy's 40–70 basis point per month alpha difference between emerging and developed markets contrast with prior research findings. A country's pre-transaction cost alpha is positively related to its trading costs, but exceeds country-specific institutional trading costs. Thus, global equity markets are inefficient, particularly in countries with quantifiable market frictions, like trading costs, that deter arbitrageurs.

Keywords : International finance;Valuation;Asset pricing;Market efficiency;Fundamental analysis;Point-in-time;Transaction costs;Principal components;Instrumented principal components analysis

Abstract :利用时间点会计数据，我们估计了36个国家25000多只股票的月公允价值。在大多数地区，尤其是亚太地区，基于偏离公允价值的交易策略可获得显著的风险调整收益（“α”），这些回报与已知异常情况无关。新兴市场和发达市场的月度阿尔法差异为40-70个基点，与之前的研究结果形成对比。一个国家的交易前成本α与其交易成本呈正相关，但超过了特定国家的机构交易成本。因此，全球股票市场效率低下，尤其是在那些存在可量化市场摩擦（如交易成本）的国家，这会阻止套利者。

Keywords :国际金融；估值；资产定价；市场效率；基本面分析；时间点；交易成本；主成分；工具主成分分析

\[11\] Identifying and boosting “Gazelles”: Evidence from business accelerators

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302208

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.012

Abstract : Why is high-growth entrepreneurship scarce in developing countries? Does this scarcity reflect firm capabilities constraints? We explore these questions using as a laboratory an accelerator in Colombia that selects participants using scores from randomly assigned judges and offers them training, advice, and visibility but no cash. Exploiting exogenous differences in judges’ scoring generosity, we show that alleviating constraints to firm capabilities unlocks innovative entrepreneurs’ potential but does not transform subpar ideas into high-growth firms. The results demonstrate that some high-potential entrepreneurs in developing economies face firm capabilities constraints and accelerators can help identify these entrepreneurs and boost their growth.

\[12\] Procyclicality of the comovement between dividend growth and consumption growth

标题:股利增长与消费增长协动的顺周期性

作者:Nancy R. Xu

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X20302233

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.07.013

Abstract : Duffee (2005) shows that the amount of consumption risk (i.e., the conditional covariance between market returns and consumption growth) is procyclical. In light of this “Duffee Puzzle,” I empirically demonstrate that the conditional covariance between dividend growth (i.e., the immediate cash flow part of market returns) and consumption growth is (1) procyclical and (2) a consistent source of procyclicality in the puzzle. Moreover, I solve an external habit formation model that incorporates realistic joint dynamics of dividend growth and consumption growth. The procyclical dividend-consumption comovement entails two new procyclical terms in the amount of consumption risk via cash flow and valuation channels, respectively. These two procyclical terms play an important role in generating a realistic magnitude of consumption risk. In contrast to extant habit formation models, the conditional equity premium no longer increases monotonically when a negative consumption shock arrives because it might lower the amount of risk while increasing the price of risk.

Keywords : Duffee Puzzle;Procyclical amount of consumption risk;Dividend-consumption comovement;Habit formation;Equity premium
