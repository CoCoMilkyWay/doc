\[1\] A Tale of Two Premiums Revisited

标题:重温两件事的故事

作者:Loïc Maréchal

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3881922

Abstract : This paper investigates the effect of the “financialization” of commodity markets in terms of pricing. I explore whether the emergence of commodity index traders affects weekly returns and turnover during the roll periods. I split the sample (1994–2017) into the pre-financialization (1994–2003) and the post-financialization (2004–2017). I directly test whether the market share of index traders contributes to commodity returns and whether risk adjustments (based on momentum, basis, basis-momentum, open interest, crowding, and average factors) alter liquidity and insurance premiums documented in Kang, Rouwenhorst, and Tang (2020). I also examine how the financialization affects liquidity and insurance premiums. Finally, since previous results are obtained with Fama-MacBeth regressions, I use an alternative method totest how liquidity and insurance premiums determine commodity returns.

Keywords : commodity futures, risk premium, liquidity premium, hedging pressure, financialization

Abstract :本文从定价角度考察了商品市场“金融化”的效应。我探讨了商品指数交易者的出现是否会影响滚动期间的每周收益和交易量。我将样本（1994-2017年）分为金融化前（1994-2003年）和金融化后（2004-2017年）。我直接测试指数交易者的市场份额是否有助于商品回报，以及风险调整（基于动量、基差、基差动量、未平仓利率、拥挤和平均因素）是否会改变Kang、Rouwenhorst和Tang（2020）中记录的流动性和保险费。我还研究了金融化如何影响流动性和保险费。最后，由于之前的结果是通过Fama-MacBeth回归得到的，因此我使用另一种方法来测试流动性和保险费如何决定商品回报。

Keywords :商品期货、风险溢价、流动性溢价、对冲压力、金融化

\[2\] Social Capital and Insider Trading

标题:社会资本与内幕交易

作者:Xuan Mi,Ronghong Huang,Kelvin Jui Keng Tan

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3881867

Abstract : Using a large U.S. dataset on insider purchases and variations in the social capital of U.S. counties, we find that the intensity (profitability) of insider trading is positively (negatively) associated with social capital. These results are more pronounced when we restrict our sample to include only opportunistic (rather than routine) insider purchases. Our results indicate that insiders affiliated with firms headquartered in counties with high levels of social capital are relatively more likely to foster market information efficiency by making additional insider purchases. However, they are less likely than those affiliated with firms outside such counties to time the market to increase the profitability of their insider purchases. Furthermore, we address endogeneity issues and establish that these relationships are causal by applying an instrumental variable approach. Finally, we show that the main driver of the positive (negative) relationship between the intensity (profitability) of insider purchases and social capital is the density of social networks (the strength of civic norms).

Keywords : Social capital, Insider trading, Insider trading profitability, Information asymmetry

Abstract :利用一个关于内幕交易和美国各州社会资本变化的大型美国数据集，我们发现内幕交易的强度（盈利能力）与社会资本正（负）相关。当我们限制样本仅包括机会主义（而非常规）内幕交易时，这些结果更加明显。我们的研究结果表明，与总部位于社会资本水平较高的县的公司有关联的内部人员相对更有可能通过进行额外的内部人员购买来提高市场信息效率。然而，与这些国家以外的公司的附属公司相比，他们不太可能通过调整市场时间来增加内幕交易的盈利能力。此外，我们解决内生性问题，并通过应用工具变量方法确定这些关系是因果关系。最后，我们证明了内幕交易强度（盈利能力）与社会资本之间正（负）关系的主要驱动力是社会网络的密度（公民规范的强度）。

Keywords :社会资本、内幕交易、内幕交易盈利能力、信息不对称

\[3\] When the Panic Broke Out: Covid-19 and Investment Funds' Portfolio Rebalancing Around the World

标题:恐慌爆发时：新冠肺炎和全球投资基金的投资组合再平衡

作者:Massimiliano Affinito,Raffaele Santioni

出处:Bank of Italy Temi di Discussione (Working Paper) No. 1342

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3896291

Abstract : To contribute to the understanding of investment funds' (IFs) behaviour, the paper exploits the exogenous shock of the COVID-19 pandemic and analyses more than 12 million security sales and purchases during the first four months of 2020 by over 20,000 IFs from more than 40 national jurisdictions and investing in more than 100 economies and 20 industries. Our estimates reveal that, when the emergency strikes, IFs do not sell indiscriminately but divest from assets considered the most vulnerable at the moment, that is, those issued by more COVID-affected countries and industries. Our results also show several dimensions of heterogeneity according to the pandemic outbreak phase, asset type, IF category and performance, extent of unitholders' outflows, and nationality of IFs. Our results, on the one hand, provide new evidence on the intrinsic fragility of IFs and the connection between their choices and fire sales, but, on the other, they also show that IF industry includes heterogeneous institutions that behave very differently. Finally, our results document that monetary policy measures have a reassuring effect also for IFs, which corroborates recent evidence on a non-bank financial institution channel of unconventional monetary policies.

Keywords : coronavirus, Covid-19, investment funds, Morningstar holdings, pandemic, portfolio rebalancing, resilience

Abstract :为了有助于理解投资基金（IFs）的行为，本文利用了新冠病毒-19大流行的外源性冲击，分析了2020年前四个月超过20年的1200万证券买卖，来自40多个国家管辖区的1000家国际单项体育联合会，投资于100多个经济体和20多个行业。我们的估计表明，当紧急情况发生时，国际单项体育联合会不会不加区别地出售资产，而是从目前被认为最脆弱的资产中剥离出来，即受新冠病毒影响较大的国家和行业发行的资产。我们的研究结果还根据大流行爆发阶段、资产类型、IF类别和绩效、单位持有人流出的程度以及IFs的国籍显示了异质性的几个维度。一方面，我们的研究结果为国际单项体育联合会的内在脆弱性及其选择与甩卖之间的联系提供了新的证据，但另一方面，我们的研究结果也表明，国际单项体育联合会行业包括行为迥异的异质机构。最后，我们的结果证明，货币政策措施对国际单项体育联合会也有令人放心的效果，这证实了最近关于非银行金融机构非常规货币政策渠道的证据。

Keywords :冠状病毒、新冠病毒-19、投资基金、晨星控股、流行病、投资组合再平衡、恢复力

\[4\] The Impact of Complex Financial Instruments on Banks’ Vulnerability: Empirical Evidence on SSM Banks

标题:复杂金融工具对银行脆弱性的影响：基于SSM银行的经验证据

作者:Perez Tommaso,Francesco Potente,Andrea Carboni,Alberto Di Iorio,Jacopo Raponi

出处:Bank of Italy Occasional Paper No. 633

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3896326

Abstract : Level 2 (L2) and Level 3 (L3) assets and liabilities represent a substantial portion of European banks’ balance sheets, and valuing them is extremely difficult, since no liquid market prices are available. This paper relies on a large panel of euro-area banks between 2014 and 2019, and two different econometric frameworks, in order to estimate the relationship between the holdings of selected instruments (L2, L3 and Non-Performing Loans, NPLs) and banks’ key performance and risk profile metrics, namely Credit Default Swaps (CDSs), Price-to-Book (PtB) ratios and Z-scores. It finds that larger holdings of L2 tend to be associated with higher CDSs, at least in the short run, while larger amounts of NPLs and L3 tend to characterize banks with higher CDSs, lower PtB ratios and worse Z-scores, other things being equal.

Keywords : fair value accounting, level 2 instruments, level 3 instruments, non-performing loans, prudential regulation, panel data models

Abstract :二级（L2）和三级（L3）资产和负债占欧洲银行资产负债表的很大一部分，对它们进行估值非常困难，因为没有流动市场价格。本文依赖于2014年至2019年期间的一个大型欧元区银行小组，以及两个不同的计量经济学框架，以估计所选工具（L2、L3和不良贷款，NPL）的持有量与银行关键绩效和风险状况指标，即信用违约互换（CDS）之间的关系，价格与账面（PtB）比率和Z分数。研究发现，至少在短期内，二级贷款持有量越大，信用违约率越高；而不良贷款和三级贷款持有量越大，信用违约率越高，PtB比率越低，Z分数越差，其他条件相同，银行的信用违约率越高。

Keywords :公允价值会计、2级工具、3级工具、不良贷款、审慎监管、面板数据模型

\[5\] Using, Taming or Avoiding the Factor Zoo? A Double-Shrinkage Estimator for Covariance Matrices

标题:使用、驯服或避免因子动物园？协方差矩阵的双收缩估计

作者:Gianluca De Nard,Zhao Zhao

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3883957

Abstract : Existing factor models struggle to model the covariance matrix for a large number of stocks and factors. Therefore, we introduce a new covariance matrix estimator that first shrinks the factor model coefficients and then applies nonlinear shrinkage to the residuals and factors. The estimator blends a regularized factor structure with conditional heteroskedasticity of residuals and factors and displays superior all-around performance against various competitors. We show that for the proposed double- shrinkage estimator, it is enough to use only the market factor or the most important latent factor(s). Thus there is no need for laboriously taking into account the factor zoo. Supplementary material for this article is available online.

Keywords : Double-shrinkage, Factor models, Markowitz portfolio selection, Multivariate GARCH, Nonlinear shrinkage, Regularization.

Abstract :现有的因子模型难以对大量股票和因子的协方差矩阵建模。因此，我们引入了一种新的协方差矩阵估计器，该估计器首先收缩因子模型系数，然后对残差和因子应用非线性收缩。该估计器将正则化因子结构与残差和因子的条件异方差相结合，在与各种竞争者的竞争中显示出优越的综合性能。我们证明，对于所提出的双收缩估计，仅使用市场因素或最重要的潜在因素就足够了。因此，没有必要费力地考虑动物园的因素。这篇文章的补充材料可以在线获得。

Keywords :双收缩，因子模型，Markowitz投资组合选择，多元GARCH，非线性收缩，正则化。

Abstract : This paper subjects the global fear index (GFI) for the COVID-19 pandemic to an empirical investigation to determine its relationship with realized volatility, continuous volatility, and jump volatility for seven international indices. \[7\] Realised Volatility Forecasting: Machine Learning via Financial Word Embedding

标题:已实现波动率预测：基于金融词嵌入的机器学习

作者:Eghbal Rahimikia,Stefan Zohren,Ser-Huang Poon

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3895272

Abstract : We develop FinText, a novel, state-of-the-art, financial word embedding from Dow Jones Newswires Text News Feed Database. Incorporating this word embedding in a machine learning model produces a substantial increase in volatility forecasting performance on days with volatility jumps for 23 NASDAQ stocks from 27 July 2007 to 18 November 2016. \[8\] Investors’ Perception of Business Group Membership during An Economic Crisis: Evidence from the COVID-19 Pandemic

标题:经济危机期间投资者对企业集团成员身份的看法：来自新冠肺炎大流行的证据

作者:Romain Ducret

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3889505

Abstract : This paper examines how investors perceive business group membership in Korea during the COVID-19 pandemic. Evidence of a time-varying and heterogeneous value of affiliation emerges from stock price performance analysis. I find that investors discount business group affiliation during a market collapse, but are willing to pay a premium for affiliation during market recovery. Overall, this pattern is more pronounced for financially weak affiliates and large business groups. Results also show that business group membership alleviates investors’ concerns regarding financial flexibility highlighting the role of internal capital markets as a substitute to external finance.

Keywords : Business groups, financial crisis, COVID-19, internal capital market, Korea

Abstract :本文考察了在新冠肺炎流行期间，投资者如何看待韩国的企业集团成员资格。从股票价格表现分析中可以看出，关联价值具有时变性和异质性。我发现，投资者在市场崩溃时会对企业集团的关联性打折扣，但在市场复苏时，他们愿意为关联性支付溢价。总的来说，这种模式对于财务状况不佳的附属公司和大型企业集团来说更为明显。结果还表明，企业集团成员资格缓解了投资者对财务灵活性的担忧，突出了内部资本市场作为外部融资替代品的作用。

Keywords :企业集团，金融危机，新冠肺炎-19，内部资本市场，韩国

\[9\] Lighting Up the Dark: Liquidity in the German Corporate Bond Market

标题:照亮黑暗：德国公司债券市场的流动性

作者:Yalin Gündüz,Michael Schneider,Loriana Pelizzon,Marti Subrahmanyam

出处:Deutsche Bundesbank Discussion Paper No. 21/2021

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3895495

Abstract : We study the impact of transparency on liquidity in OTC markets. We do so by providing an analysis of liquidity in a corporate bond market without trade transparency (Germany), and comparing our findings to a market with full posttrade disclosure (the U.S.). We employ a unique regulatory dataset of transactions of German financial institutions from 2008 until 2014 to find that: First, overall trading activity is much lower in the German market than in the U.S. Second, similar to the U.S., the determinants of German corporate bond liquidity are in line with search theories of OTC markets. Third, surprisingly, frequently traded German bonds have transaction costs that are 39-61 bp lower than a matched sample of bonds in the U.S. Our results support the notion that, while market liquidity is generally higher in transparent markets, a subset of bonds could be more liquid in more opaque markets because of investors "crowding" their demand into a small number of more actively traded securities.

Keywords : None

Abstract :我们研究透明度对场外交易市场流动性的影响。为此，我们对没有交易透明度的公司债券市场（德国）的流动性进行了分析，并将我们的研究结果与交易后完全披露的市场（美国）进行了比较。我们利用2008年至2014年德国金融机构交易的独特监管数据集发现：第一，德国市场的整体交易活动远低于美国。第二，与美国类似，德国公司债券流动性的决定因素符合场外交易市场的搜索理论。第三，令人惊讶的是，频繁交易的德国债券的交易成本比匹配的美国债券样本低39-61个基点。我们的结果支持这样一种观点，即虽然透明市场的市场流动性通常较高，在更不透明的市场中，债券的一部分可能会更具流动性，因为投资者将其需求“挤入”到少量交易更活跃的证券中。

Keywords :None

\[10\] Clustering Structure of Microstructure Measures

标题:微观结构测度的聚类结构

作者:Liao Zhu,Ningning Sun,Martin T. Wells

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3880751

Abstract : This paper builds the clustering model of measures of market microstructure features which are popular in predicting the stock returns. In a 10-second time frequency, we study the clustering structure of different measures to find out the best ones for predicting. In this way, we can predict more accurately with a limited number of predictors, which removes the noise and makes the model more interpretable.

Keywords : market microstructure, interpretable machine learning, artificial intelligence in finance, prototype clustering, high-dimensional statistics, dimension reduction

Abstract :本文建立了市场微观结构特征测度的聚类模型，该模型是预测股票收益率的常用方法。在10秒的时间频率内，我们研究了不同度量的聚类结构，以找出预测的最佳度量。通过这种方式，我们可以使用数量有限的预测器进行更准确的预测，从而消除噪声，使模型更易于解释。

Keywords :市场微观结构、可解释机器学习、金融人工智能、原型聚类、高维统计、降维
