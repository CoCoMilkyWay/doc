\[1\] The Global Market Portfolio

标题:全球市场投资组合

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management August 2021, jpm.2021.1.259

作者:Gregory Gadzinski, Markus Schuller and Andrea Vacchino

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/05/31/jpm.2021.1.259;

Abstract : Two years after the global capital stock of Gadzinski, Schuller, and Vacchino (2018) and 36 years after the world market wealth portfolio of Ibbotson, Siegel, and Love (1985), investors still lack a global composite portfolio benchmark that includes a broad spectrum of assets with weights in line with a fair representation of the stock of capital for each asset class. Despite ample evidence of the substantial use of alternatives and real assets in institutional portfolios, the authors argue that existing academic and practitioner attempts are still unable to provide a satisfactory approximation of the “true” global market portfolio. The authors fill this gap and provide two benchmarks: one for retail investors constrained by liquidity needs and one for institutional investors who have access to illiquid assets.

TOPICS: Portfolio construction, real assets/alternative investments/private equity, global markets, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors provide two global market portfolio benchmarks, for retail and institutional investors, based on a measurable global capital stock, which include 87 existing indices within 11 asset classes.

\- ▪ Their investable global market portfolio is built with ETFs only, therefore transparent and systematic. The total return index realizes a compounded annual average return of 4.7%, with a standard deviation of 10.1% during the period 2005 Q1–2020 Q2.

\- ▪ Their non-investable global market portfolio realizes a compounded average return of 5.9%, with a standard deviation of 6.3% and a maximum cumulative drawdown of 20%. Thanks to a better allocation of resources toward real assets, diversification is then achieved and makes their total return index an efficient long-term portfolio benchmark.

Abstract:Gadzinski、Schuller和Vacchino的全球资本存量（2018年）两年后，以及Ibbotson、Siegel和Love的世界市场财富组合（1985年）36年后，投资者仍然缺乏一个全球综合投资组合基准，该基准包括各种资产，其权重与各资产类别资本存量的公平代表性一致。尽管有大量证据表明，机构投资组合中大量使用了替代品和实际资产，但作者认为，现有的学术界和实践者尝试仍然无法提供令人满意的“真实”全球市场投资组合的近似值。作者填补了这一空白，并提供了两个基准：一个是针对受流动性需求约束的散户投资者，另一个是针对能够获得非流动资产的机构投资者。

主题：投资组合构建、实物资产/另类投资/私募股权、全球市场、绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者基于可测量的全球资本存量（包括11个资产类别内的87个现有指数），为散户和机构投资者提供了两个全球市场投资组合基准。

\- ▪ 他们可投资的全球市场投资组合仅由ETF构建，因此透明且系统。2005年第一季度至2020年第二季度，总回报指数实现了4.7%的复合年平均回报率，标准差为10.1%。

\- ▪ 其不可投资的全球市场投资组合实现了5.9%的复合平均回报率，标准差为6.3%，最大累计提款为20%。由于将资源更好地配置到实物资产上，多样化就得以实现，并使其总回报指数成为有效的长期投资组合基准。

\[2\] Volatility Targeting: It’s Complicated!

标题:波动率目标：这很复杂！

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management August 2021, jpm.2021.1.258

作者:George Mylnikov

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/05/31/jpm.2021.1.258;

Abstract : This article examines whether volatility targeting can improve returns, decrease tail loss, and deliver a more stable risk profile for risk assets using the example of US equities. The author identifies biases in the methodology used to assess the viability of the strategy in several recently published studies on the subject. After correcting for these biases, he finds that the volatility targeting strategy results in a more stable risk profile and delivers marginally better returns but fails to reduce tail loss. Furthermore, the author shows that a better volatility forecasting model could significantly improve returns but not tail loss.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, quantitative methods, statistical methods, tail risks, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ This article examines whether volatility targeting can improve returns, decrease tail loss, and deliver a more stable risk profile for risk assets using the example of US equities.

\- ▪ There are two key related biases in several recently published studies on the subject: the use of future information in defining the strategy benchmark and the use of risk-adjusted return measures to gauge the profitability of the strategy. This article corrects these biases.

\- ▪ Volatility targeting results in a more stable risk profile and delivers marginally better returns but fails to reduce tail loss. A better volatility forecasting model could significantly improve returns but not tail loss.

Abstract:本文以美国股票为例，考察了波动率目标能否提高收益率，减少尾部损失，并为风险资产提供更稳定的风险预测。作者在最近发表的几项关于这一主题的研究中指出了评估战略可行性的方法中的偏差。在纠正了这些偏差之后，他发现波动率目标策略可以带来更稳定的风险状况，并提供略好的回报，但未能减少尾部损失。此外，作者还发现，一个更好的波动率预测模型可以显著提高收益率，但不会导致尾部损失。

主题：证券分析与评估，定量方法，统计方法，尾部风险，绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 本文以美国股票为例，探讨了波动率目标能否提高收益率，减少尾部损失，并为风险资产提供更稳定的风险预测。

\- ▪ 在最近发表的几项关于这一主题的研究中，存在两个关键的相关偏见：在确定战略基准时使用未来信息和使用风险调整回报衡量战略的盈利能力。这篇文章纠正了这些偏见。

\- ▪ 波动率目标导致更稳定的风险状况，并提供略好的回报，但未能减少尾部损失。一个更好的波动率预测模型可以显著提高收益率，但不会导致尾部损失。

\[3\] American Exceptionalism: The Long-Term Evidence

标题:美国例外论：长期证据

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Non-US Financial Markets 2021, jpm.2021.1.257

作者:Elroy Dimson, Paul Marsh and Mike Staunton

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/05/30/jpm.2021.1.257;

Abstract : In this article, the authors examine the evolving composition of the global equity market over the last 121 years. Using the DMS database from Morningstar, they study the long-term returns on all the main asset classes. The authors present investment returns based on 90 countries, 23 of which have histories starting in 1900. Over the long run, equity returns have systematically exceeded the investment performance of government bonds and Treasury bills. For the world as a whole, stocks beat bills by 4.4% per year and bonds by 3.1% per year. In the United States, stocks outperformed bills by 5.8% per year, compared with 3.7% per year in the world ex-US. Contrary to many equity investors’ expectations at the dawn of the 21st century, the United States continued to outperform non-US stock markets by a significant margin.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, global markets, financial crises and financial market history, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The long-term growth of US financial assets has been exceptional. The US stock market now represents 56% of the market capitalization of worldwide equities.

\- ▪ Over the 1900–2020 period, the annualized real (inflation-adjusted) return on US equities was 6.6%, that on long government bonds was 2.1%, and that on Treasury bills was 0.8%. The US experience is not a guide to the typical non-US market.

\- ▪ The 6.6% real USD return on US equities contrasts with 4.5% real USD return on a 90-country index of international, non-US equities over the same 121-year period. The US was an outlier.

\- ▪ US stock market returns were superior not only in the 20th century. In the 21st century, real US returns (4.6%) have exceeded the world ex-US (2.8%) and Europe (2.5%).

\- ▪ US markets have beaten non-US markets with the help of expansionary monetary policy and a high weighting in technology companies. Whether US exceptionalism will persist is an open question.

Abstract:在这篇文章中，作者考察了过去121年来全球股票市场不断演变的构成。他们利用晨星公司的DMS数据库，研究所有主要资产类别的长期回报。作者根据90个国家的投资回报率，其中23个国家的历史始于1900年。从长期来看，股票回报率系统地超过了政府债券和国库券的投资绩效。就整个世界而言，股票每年比票据高出4.4%，比债券高出3.1%。在美国，股票的年收益率比票据高出5.8%，而在除美国以外的世界，这一数字为3.7%。与许多股票投资者在21世纪初的预期相反，美国股市的表现继续大大超过非美国股市。

主题：证券分析和估值，全球市场，金融危机和金融市场历史，业绩衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 美国金融资产的长期增长是异常的。美国股市目前占全球股票市值的56%

\- ▪ 在1900-2020年间，美国股票的年化实际（通胀调整后）回报率为6.6%，长期政府债券的年化实际（通胀调整后）回报率为2.1%，美国国债的年化实际（通胀调整后）回报率为0.8%。美国的经验并不是典型的非美国市场的指南。

\- ▪ 美国股市6.6%的实际美元回报率与同期90个国家的国际非美国股市指数4.5%的实际美元回报率形成对比。美国是个异类。

\- ▪ 美国股市的回报率不仅在20世纪更高。在21世纪，美国的实际回报率（4.6%）已经超过了除美国以外的世界（2.8%）和欧洲（2.5%）。

\- ▪ 在扩张性货币政策和科技企业高权重的帮助下，美国市场击败了非美国市场。美国的例外主义是否会继续存在是一个悬而未决的问题。

\[4\] Tax-Smart Portfolio Valuation and Performance Measurement

标题:税务智能投资组合评估与绩效评估

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management August 2021, jpm.2021.1.256

作者:Andrew Kalotay

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/05/27/jpm.2021.1.256;

Abstract : The reported performance of a portfolio is consequential for both investors and managers. One component of the return calculation is the beginning and ending value of the portfolio. The standard in the industry is to calculate portfolio values from the market prices of the constituent securities. Assuming the portfolio can be liquidated at these prices, in the absence of tax considerations the market value accurately represents the true value of the portfolio, and therefore the resulting measure of performance is uncontroversial. But what if the portfolio is taxable? Neither the market value nor the liquidation value accurately represents the true worth of a taxable portfolio. However, accurate values are essential to calculating the performance of mutual funds, exchange-traded funds, and separately managed accounts. This is especially true for tax-exempt municipal bond portfolios—interest is tax-free, but capital gains are taxable. The author will explore three alternatives to pretax value: liquidation value, hold value, and the larger of these two, defined as tax-smart value. The author recommends the tax-smart value to measure the performance of an actively managed portfolio.

TOPICS: Portfolio construction, legal/regulatory/public policy, mutual funds/passive investing/indexing, fixed income and structured finance

Key Finding

\- ▪ Pretax portfolio value is inappropriate for measuring the true performance of a taxable portfolio.

\- ▪ For actively managed portfolios, we recommend using tax-smart value, which is the greater of after-tax liquidation value and hold value.

\- ▪ For buy-and-hold portfolios, hold value is recommended.

\- ▪ The calculation of the hold value of a tax-exempt bond requires tax-neutral option-adjusted spread analytics.

Abstract:投资组合的业绩报告对投资者和管理者都具有重要意义。回报计算的一个组成部分是投资组合的开始值和结束值。该行业的标准是根据组成证券的市场价格计算投资组合价值。假设投资组合可以按照这些价格进行清算，在没有税务考虑的情况下，市场价值准确地代表了投资组合的真实价值，因此，由此产生的绩效衡量是无争议的。但如果投资组合是应税的呢？市场价值和清算价值都不能准确地代表应税投资组合的真实价值。然而，准确的价值对于计算共同基金、交易所买卖基金和独立管理账户的业绩至关重要。对于免税市政债券投资组合尤其如此，利息免税，但资本利得应纳税。笔者将探讨税前价值的三种替代方法：清算价值、持有价值，以及这两种价值中的较大者，即税敏价值。作者建议用税务智能价值来衡量一个积极管理的投资组合的绩效。

主题：投资组合构建、法律/监管/公共政策、共同基金/被动投资/指数化、固定收益和结构性融资

Key Finding

\- ▪ 税前投资组合价值不适合衡量应税投资组合的真实表现。

\- ▪ 对于主动管理的投资组合，我们建议使用税务智能价值，即税后清算价值和持有价值中的较大者。

\- ▪ 对于买入并持有的投资组合，建议持有价值。

\- ▪ 计算免税债券的持有价值需要税收中性期权调整利差分析。

\[6\] Who Gains from Place-Based Tax Incentives? Exploring Apartment Sales Prices in Qualified Opportunity Zones

标题:谁从地方税收优惠中获益？探索合格机会区的公寓销售价格

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Real Estate 2021, jpm.2021.1.255

作者:Edward F. Pierzak

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/05/25/jpm.2021.1.255;

Abstract : Qualified opportunity zones, or o-zones, were established by the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017. This new place-based community development program encourages the investment of private capital into economically depressed areas through the use of tax incentives. With significant tax-induced demand, a small “investment box,” and a closing (now closed) window of opportunity for full tax benefits, conditions have been ripe for accelerating o-zone property prices. This study examines the policy’s effect on transaction prices for existing market-rate apartment properties located in census tracts that were designated as o-zones. It finds that certain segments of the apartment investment pool located in o-zones experienced significant price increases between 2017 and 2019. With material sales price increases, the benefits related to tax savings, at least in part, have effectively been transferred to the sellers of these properties. This finding acts as a cautionary reminder to taxable investors. While tax benefits are alluring, investment decisions should not rely on tax incentives alone.

TOPICS: Real estate, legal/regulatory/public policy, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ Qualified opportunity zones, or o-zones, were established by the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017. This new place-based community development program encourages the investment of private capital into economically depressed areas through the use of tax incentives.
