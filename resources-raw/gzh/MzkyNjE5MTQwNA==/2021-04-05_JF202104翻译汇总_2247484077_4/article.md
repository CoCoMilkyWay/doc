\[1\] The Private Production of Safe Assets

标题:安全资产的私人生产

作者:MARCIN KACPERCZYK,CHRISTOPHE PÉRIGNON,GUILLAUME VUILLEMEY

首次发表时间:07 December 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12997

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12997

Abstract : Using high‐frequency, granular panel data on short‐term debt securities issued in Europe, we study the existence, empirical boundaries, and fragility of private assets' safety. We show that only securities with the shortest maturities, issued by banks (certificates of deposit, or CDs), benefit from a safety premium. The supply of such CDs responds positively to excess safety demand. During periods of stress, this relation vanishes for all issuers of private securities, even though their aggregate volumes do not collapse. Other dimensions of heterogeneity, including issuers' balance sheets or their domicile countries' fiscal capacity, are less relevant for private safety.

Abstract :利用欧洲发行的短期债务证券的高频、精细的面板数据，我们研究了私人资产安全的存在性、经验边界和脆弱性。我们表明，只有银行发行的期限最短的证券（存单或CDs）才能从安全溢价中受益。这种CDs的供应对过度的安全需求作出了积极的反应。在压力时期，所有私人证券发行人的这种关系都消失了，即使它们的总量没有崩溃。异质性的其他方面，包括发行人的资产负债表或其所在国的财政能力，与私人安全的关系不大。

\[2\] Mutual Fund Holdings of Credit Default Swaps: Liquidity, Yield, and Risk

标题:共同基金持有的信用违约互换：流动性、收益率和风险

作者:WEI JIANG,JITAO OU,ZHONGYAN ZHU

首次发表时间:04 December 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12996

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12996

Abstract : This study analyzes the motivations for and consequences of funds' credit default swap (CDS) investments using mutual funds' quarterly holdings from pre‐ to postfinancial crisis. Funds invest in CDS when facing unpredictable liquidity needs. Funds sell more in reference entities when the CDS is liquid relative to the underlying bonds and buy more when the CDS‐bond basis is more negative. To enhance yield, funds engage in negative basis trading and sell CDS with the highest spreads within rating categories, and with spreads higher than those of their bond portfolios. Funds with superior portfolio returns also demonstrate more skill in CDS trading.

Abstract :本研究分析了从金融危机前到金融危机后，基金利用共同基金的季度持股进行信用违约互换（CDS）投资的动机和后果。当面临不可预测的流动性需求时，基金会投资于CDS。当CDS相对于标的债券具有流动性时，基金在参考实体中卖出更多，当CDS-债券基础更为负时，基金买入更多。为了提高收益率，基金进行负基差交易，并出售评级类别中息差最高、且息差高于其债券投资组合的CDS。投资组合回报率较高的基金在CDS交易方面也表现出更高的技能。

\[3\] The Misguided Beliefs of Financial Advisors

标题:财务顾问的错误信念

作者:JUHANI T. LINNAINMAA,BRIAN T. MELZER,ALESSANDRO PREVITERO

首次发表时间:28 November 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12995

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12995

Abstract : A common view of retail finance is that conflicts of interest contribute to the high cost of advice. Within a large sample of Canadian financial advisors and their clients, however, we show that advisors typically invest personally just as they advise their clients. Advisors trade frequently, chase returns, prefer expensive and actively managed funds, and underdiversify. Advisors' net returns of −3% per year are similar to their clients' net returns. Advisors do not strategically hold expensive portfolios only to convince clients to do the same; they continue to do so after they leave the industry.

Abstract :零售金融的一个普遍观点是，利益冲突导致咨询成本过高。然而，在加拿大金融顾问及其客户的大量样本中，我们发现，顾问通常会像向客户提供建议一样进行个人投资。顾问们频繁交易，追逐回报，偏好昂贵且管理积极的基金，而且投资组合不够多样化。顾问每年-3%的净回报与客户的净回报相似。顾问在战略上持有昂贵的投资组合并不是为了说服客户也这么做；他们离开这个行业后会继续这样做。

\[4\] The Impact of Repossession Risk on Mortgage Default

标题:收回风险对抵押贷款违约的影响

作者:TERRY O'MALLEY

首次发表时间:12 November 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12990

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12990

Abstract : I study the effect of removing repossession risk on a mortgagor's decision to default. Reducing default costs may result in strategic default, particularly during crises when homeowners can be substantially underwater. I analyze difference‐in‐differences variation in repossession risk generated by an unexpected legal ruling in Ireland that prohibited collateral enforcement on delinquent residential mortgages originated before a particular date. I estimate that borrowers defaulted by 0.3 percentage points more each quarter after the ruling, a relative increase of approximately one‐half. High loan‐to‐value ratios and low liquidity are associated with a larger treatment effect, suggesting both equity and consumption‐based motivations.

Abstract :我研究了消除收回风险对抵押人违约决策的影响。降低违约成本可能会导致战略性违约，尤其是在危机期间，当房主可能会大量陷入困境时。我分析了爱尔兰一项意外的法律裁决所产生的收回风险差异，该裁决禁止在特定日期之前对拖欠的住房抵押贷款执行抵押品。我估计，裁决后，借款人违约率每季度增加0.3个百分点，相对增加约一半。高贷款价值比和低流动性与更大的治疗效果相关，表明股权和消费动机。

\[5\] Financial Fragility with SAM?

标题:山姆的财务脆弱？

作者:DANIEL L. GREENWALD,TIM LANDVOIGT,STIJN VAN NIEUWERBURGH

首次发表时间:03 December 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12992

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12992

Abstract : Shared appreciation mortgages (SAMs) feature mortgage payments that adjust with house prices. They are designed to stave off borrower default by providing payment relief when house prices fall. Some argue that SAMs may help prevent the next foreclosure crisis. However, home owners' gains from payment relief are mortgage lenders' losses. A general equilibrium model in which financial intermediaries channel savings from saver to borrower households shows that indexation of mortgage payments to aggregate house prices increases financial fragility, reduces risk‐sharing, and leads to expensive financial sector bailouts. In contrast, indexation to local house prices reduces financial fragility and improves risk‐sharing.

Abstract :共享增值抵押贷款（SAMs）的特点是抵押贷款付款随房价调整。它们的目的是在房价下跌时提供付款减免，从而避免借款人违约。一些人认为，SAMs可能有助于防止下一次止赎危机。然而，房主从付款减免中获得的收益是抵押贷款贷款人的损失。金融中介机构将储蓄从储蓄者转移到借款人家庭的一般均衡模型表明，将抵押贷款支付与总房价挂钩会增加金融脆弱性，减少风险分担，并导致昂贵的金融部门救助。相比之下，当地房价指数化降低了金融脆弱性，改善了风险分担。

\[6\] Anonymous Trading in Equities

标题:匿名股票交易

作者:TOM GRIMSTVEDT MELING

首次发表时间:12 November 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12988

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12988

Abstract : In this paper, I explore a reform at the Oslo Stock Exchange to assess the causal effect of posttrade trader anonymity on stock liquidity and trading volume. Using a regression discontinuity approach, I find that anonymity leads to a reduction in bid‐ask spreads of 40% and an increase in trading volume of more than 50%. The increase in trading volume is accounted for largely by increased trading activity by institutional investors, while retail investors do not adjust their trading behavior in response to anonymity. The results suggest that posttrade anonymity positively affects standard measures of market quality.

Abstract :在本文中，我探讨了奥斯陆证券交易所的一项改革，以评估交易后交易者匿名对股票流动性和交易量的因果影响。使用回归不连续方法，我发现匿名会导致买卖价差减少40%，交易量增加50%以上。交易量的增加主要是由于机构投资者交易活动的增加，而散户投资者不会因匿名而调整交易行为。研究结果表明，交易后匿名性对市场质量的标准测度有正向影响。

\[7\] Liquidity Supply in the Corporate Bond Market

标题:公司债券市场的流动性供给

作者:JONATHAN GOLDBERG,YOSHIO NOZAWA

首次发表时间:10 November 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12991

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12991

Abstract : This paper examines dealer inventory capacity, or liquidity supply, as a driver of liquidity and expected returns in the corporate bond market. We identify shocks to aggregate liquidity supply using data on corporate bond yields and dealer positions. Liquidity supply shocks lead to persistent changes in market liquidity, are correlated with proxies for dealer financial constraints, and have significant explanatory power for cross‐sectional and time‐series variation in expected returns, beyond standard risk factors. Our findings point to liquidity supply by financially constrained intermediaries as a main driver of market liquidity and asset prices.

Abstract :本文考察了在公司债券市场中，作为流动性和预期收益的驱动因素的交易商库存能力，即流动性供给。我们使用公司债券收益率和交易商头寸的数据来确定对总流动性供应的冲击。流动性供给冲击导致市场流动性的持续变化，与交易商财务约束的替代指标相关，并对预期收益的横截面和时间序列变化具有显著的解释力，超过标准风险因素。我们的研究结果指出，金融约束中介机构的流动性供给是市场流动性和资产价格的主要驱动力。

\[8\] The Perception of Dependence, Investment Decisions, and Stock Prices

标题:对依赖、投资决策和股票价格的看法

作者:MICHAEL UNGEHEUER,MARTIN WEBER

首次发表时间:24 November 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12993

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12993

Abstract : How do investors perceive dependence between stock returns; and how does their perception of dependence affect investments and stock prices? We show experimentally that investors understand differences in dependence, but not in terms of correlation. Participants invest as if applying a simple counting heuristic for the frequency of comovement. They diversify more when the frequency of comovement is lower even if correlation is higher due to dependence in the tails. Building on our experimental findings, we empirically analyze U.S. stock returns. We identify a robust return premium for stocks with high frequencies of comovement with the market return.

Abstract :投资者如何看待股票收益之间的依赖关系；他们对依赖关系的看法如何影响投资和股票价格？我们通过实验证明，投资者理解依赖性的差异，但不理解相关性的差异。参与者的投资就好像对共同运动的频率应用一个简单的计数启发式。当共动频率较低时，即使尾部的相关性较高，它们也会更加多样化。基于我们的实验结果，我们对美国股票收益率进行了实证分析。我们确定了一个稳健的回报溢价的股票与高频率的共同运动的市场回报。

\[9\] Public Thrift, Private Perks: Signaling Board Independence with Executive Pay

标题:公款节俭，私人津贴：用高管薪酬彰显董事会独立性

作者:PABLO RUIZ‐VERDÚ,RAVI SINGH

首次发表时间:12 November 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12989

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12989

Abstract : We analyze how boards' reputational concerns influence executive compensation and the use of hidden pay. Independent boards reduce disclosed pay to signal their independence, but are more likely than manager‐friendly boards to use hidden pay or to distort incentive contracts. Stronger reputational pressures lead to lower disclosed pay, weaker managerial incentives, and higher hidden pay, whereas greater transparency of executive compensation has the opposite effects. Although reputational concerns can induce boards to choose compensation contracts more favorable to shareholders, we show that there is a threshold beyond which stronger reputational concerns harm shareholders. Similarly, excessive pay transparency can harm shareholders.

Abstract :我们分析了董事会的声誉问题如何影响高管薪酬和隐性薪酬的使用。独立董事会降低披露的薪酬以表明其独立性，但比管理者友好的董事会更有可能使用隐性薪酬或扭曲激励合同。声誉压力越大，公开薪酬越低，管理激励越弱，隐性薪酬越高，而高管薪酬的透明度越高，效果就相反。尽管声誉担忧会促使董事会选择对股东更有利的薪酬合同，但我们发现，声誉担忧越强，对股东的伤害就越大，存在一个门槛。同样，过高的薪酬透明度也会损害股东利益。

\[10\] Limited Risk Sharing and International Equity Returns

标题:有限的风险分担和国际股本回报

作者:SHAOJUN ZHANG

首次发表时间:24 November 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12994

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12994

Abstract : Limited stock market participation can potentially explain the disconnect between international asset prices and macro quantities. An incomplete markets model in which risk sharing for stockholders is high generates highly correlated equity returns and relatively smooth exchange rates. Risk sharing for nonstockholders is limited because of their nonparticipation in stock markets and borrowing constraints, reducing the aggregate consumption correlation and the correlation between aggregate consumption differentials and exchange rates. Financial integration widens the disconnect by benefiting stockholders but hurting nonstockholders. Survey data indicate that international risk sharing for stockholders is better than that for nonstockholders, consistent with the predictions.

Abstract :有限的股市参与有可能解释国际资产价格与宏观数量之间的脱节。在一个不完全市场模型中，股东的风险分担率很高，产生了高度相关的股票收益和相对平稳的汇率。由于非股票持有者不参与股票市场和借贷约束，降低了总消费相关性和总消费差异与汇率之间的相关性，非股票持有者的风险分担受到限制。金融一体化扩大了这种脱节，使股东受益，但伤害了非股东。调查数据表明，股东国际风险分担优于非股东国际风险分担，与预测一致。

\[11\] Model‐Free International Stochastic Discount Factors

标题:无模型国际随机贴现因子

作者:MIRELA SANDULESCU,FABIO TROJANI,ANDREA VEDOLIN

首次发表时间:31 July 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12970

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12970

Abstract : We provide a theoretical framework to uncover in a model‐free way the relationships among international stochastic discount factors (SDFs), stochastic wedges, and financial market structures. Exchange rates are in general different from the ratio of international SDFs in incomplete markets, as captured by a stochastic wedge. We show theoretically that this wedge can be zero in incomplete and integrated markets. Market segmentation breaks the strong link between exchange rates and international SDFs, which helps address salient features of international asset returns while keeping the volatility and cross‐country correlation of SDFs at moderate levels.

Abstract :我们提供了一个理论框架，以无模型的方式揭示国际随机贴现因子（SDF）、随机楔子和金融市场结构之间的关系。一般来说，汇率不同于不完全市场中国际特殊目的基金的比率，这是由一个随机楔子捕捉到的。我们从理论上证明了在不完全市场和整合市场中，这种楔形可以为零。市场细分打破了汇率与国际特殊目的基金之间的紧密联系，这有助于解决国际资产回报的显著特征，同时将特殊目的基金的波动性和跨国相关性保持在适度水平。

\[12\] Equilibrium Asset Pricing with Leverage and Default

标题:带杠杆和违约的均衡资产定价

作者:JOÃO F. GOMES,LUKAS SCHMID

首次发表时间:09 November 2020

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12987

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12987

Abstract : We develop a general equilibrium model linking the pricing of stocks and corporate bonds to endogenous movements in corporate leverage and aggregate volatility. The model features heterogeneous firms making optimal investment and financing decisions and connects fluctuations in macroeconomic quantities and asset prices to movements in the cross section of firms. Empirically plausible movements in leverage produce realistic asset return dynamics. Countercyclical leverage drives predictable variation in risk premia, and debt‐financed growth generates a high value premium. Endogenous default produces countercyclical aggregate volatility and credit spread movements that are propagated to the real economy through their effects on investment and output.

Abstract :我们建立了一个一般均衡模型，将股票和公司债券的定价与公司杠杆率和总波动率的内生变动联系起来。该模型的特点是异质企业做出最优的投融资决策，并将宏观经济数量和资产价格的波动与企业横截面的变动联系起来。从经验上讲，杠杆率的合理变动会产生现实的资产回报动态。逆周期杠杆推动风险溢价的可预测变化，债务融资增长产生高价值溢价。内生违约产生逆周期的总波动率和信贷利差运动，通过其对投资和产出的影响传播到实体经济。
