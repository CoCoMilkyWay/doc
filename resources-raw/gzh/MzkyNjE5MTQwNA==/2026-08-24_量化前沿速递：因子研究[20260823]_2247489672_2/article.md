\[1\] Dynamic Physical Hedging amid Jump Losses, Reconstruction Price Uncertainty, Population Interactions

标题:跳跃损失、重建价格不确定性、人口互动中的动态实物套期保值

作者:Paramahansa Pramanik, Michael Bowdin

来源:ARXIV\_20260817

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13745

Abstract : We study dynamic physical hedging for insurers exposed jointly to catastrophe losses and stochastic reconstruction costs. Surplus evolves as a controlled jump diffusion whose loss amplitude combines marked catastrophe severity, an exogenous mean reverting cost factor, and endogenous mitigation. We establish well posedness, moment and stability estimates, and a stopping time dynamic programming principle, and prove that the value function is the unique viscosity solution of the resulting nonlocal Hamilton Jacobi Bellman (HJB) equation Strategic interaction is introduced through a mean field game (MFG) with reduced form vulnerability costs, yielding a coupled backward forward HJB Kolmogorov system. We establish relaxed equilibrium existence, Markovian realization, and uniqueness under appropriate compactness and monotonicity conditions. Numerical experiments show that reconstruction costs and capitalization materially affect optimal hedging and that cross sectional vulnerability alters equilibrium costs. Tail family robustness calculations further assess the sensitivity of these conclusions to alternative catastrophe severity specifications.

\[2\] Structured Payment in Pawnshop Borrowing

标题:典当行借贷中的结构化支付

作者:Francis J. DiTraglia, Craig McIntosh, Isaac Meza, Joyce Sadka, Enrique Seira

来源:ARXIV\_20260817

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13775

Abstract : Pawn loans offer borrowers a substantial degree of repayment flexibility in exchange for a harsh penalty in case of default  forfeit of collateral worth more than the loan amount along with any payments made toward recovery. Using a large RCT conducted in Mexico City, we document key stylized facts about pawn lending and explore the merits of replacing flexibility with structured repayment contracts in this important but understudied form of credit. Our experimental design includes a mandatory frequent payments arm, a (status quo) flexible payments arm, and a choice between the two. This design point identifies not only the average treatment effect, but also the effects of treatment on the treated and the untreated along with the average selection on gains, allowing a rigorous study of mandates versus choice. Although the average treatment effect of assigning borrowers to structured payments is a 19  decrease in their financial cost and a 17.5  decrease in the probability of default, only 11  of borrowers choose structured repayment contracts voluntarily. We show that structured repayment reduces financial costs for nearly all borrowers, including those who would not freely choose it, and find no evidence of selection on gains in cost savings.

Keywords : 

Abstract :典当贷款为借款人提供了很大程度的还款灵活性，以换取在违约的情况下对价值超过贷款金额的抵押品以及为追回而支付的任何款项进行严厉处罚。我们使用在墨西哥城进行的大型循环贷款，记录了典当贷款的关键程式化事实，并探讨了在这种重要但研究不足的信贷形式中用结构化还款合同取代灵活性的优点。我们的实验设计包括一个强制性的频繁支付部门、一个（现状）灵活的支付部门，以及在两者之间进行选择。这一设计点不仅确定了平均治疗效果，还确定了治疗对治疗者和未治疗者的影响，以及平均选择对收益的影响，从而可以对强制与选择进行严格的研究。尽管将借款人分配到结构化支付的平均治疗效果是财务成本降低了19，违约概率降低了17.5，但只有11名借款人自愿选择结构化还款合同。我们发现，结构化还款降低了几乎所有借款人的财务成本，包括那些不会自由选择还款的借款人，并且没有发现选择成本节约收益的证据。

\[3\] Financial Technologies, Labor Markets, and Wage Inequality

标题:金融技术、劳动力市场和工资不平等

作者:Carlos Burga, Jacelly Cespedes, Carlos Parra, Bernardo Ricca

来源:ARXIV\_20260817

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13871

Abstract : While technological innovations typically increase wage inequality by favoring skilled workers, we show that instant payment systems instead reduce it. We study the labor market effects of instant payment systems in the context of Brazil s Pix rollout. Using matched employer employee data, we implement a triple difference design that exploits pre Pix mobile penetration across municipalities, the differential benefits of Pix for small versus large establishments, and the timing of Pix. We find that wages in small establishments rise significantly relative to large establishments after Pix. These gains are concentrated in cash intensive sectors such as retail and services, with no effects in wholesale or manufacturing. Crucially, wage inequality declines, driven by wage gains in the lower half of the distribution, with no effect at the top. Our evidence points to increased small firm labor demand, consistent with lower payment frictions. These effects are amplified where low skill labor is scarce. A calibrated monopsony model implies that uniform Pix adoption would reduce both the within  and between municipality components of wage dispersion, amplifying the aggregate inequality reduction.

Abstract :虽然技术创新通常会通过有利于技术工人来增加工资不平等，但我们发现即时支付系统反而会减少工资不平等。我们在巴西推出Pix的背景下研究了即时支付系统对劳动力市场的影响。使用匹配的雇主-雇员数据，我们实施了一种三重差异设计，该设计利用了Pix之前在各市的移动普及率、Pix对小型和大型企业的不同好处以及Pix的时间。我们发现，在Pix之后，小型企业的工资相对于大型企业大幅上涨。这些收益集中在零售和服务业等现金密集型行业，对批发或制造业没有影响。至关重要的是，在分配的下半部分工资增长的推动下，工资不平等现象有所下降，对顶层没有影响。我们的证据表明，小企业的劳动力需求增加，与较低的薪酬摩擦相一致。在低技能劳动力稀缺的地方，这些影响会被放大。一个经过校准的买方垄断模型意味着，统一的Pix采用将减少工资分散的市政内部和市政之间的组成部分，从而放大总体不平等的减少。

\[5\] Science under Threat  A Natural Experiment in Economics

标题:受威胁的科学：经济学中的自然实验

作者:Dominic Rohner, Oliver Vanden Eynde, Philine Widmer

来源:ARXIV\_20260817

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14164

Abstract : Academic freedom has come under growing strain worldwide. To study whether and how academics respond to political pressure, we exploit a natural experiment  the publication in early 2025 of a  blacklist  of words flagged by the U.S. government. We find that the release of this list led to a sharp reduction in the use of these flagged words among economists at universities that rely heavily on federal funding, relative to scholars from institutions that are less dependent on federal funding or based in the UK. The drop is driven by content related to gender, race, and environment. Our findings are consistent with the idea that scholars respond strongly to political pressure.

\[6\] An ergodic theorem for multi period mutual insurance

标题:多期相互保险的遍历定理

作者:John Armstrong

来源:ARXIV\_20260817

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14256

Abstract : Suppose there are  N  heterogeneous agents in a market with idiosyncratic risks but no uninsurable systematic risk factors. These agents may agree arbitrary financial contracts with one another, subject to the condition that contracts are self enforcing under coalitions of agents in a common state. We show that, under mild conditions, this uniquely determines the limiting utility of every agent as  N  tends to infinity. The result is an ergodic theorem  as the population grows, the number of degrees of freedom in the problem collapses, so that agents in the same state are treated identically in the limit. We exhibit an explicit, practically realisable mechanism achieving this limit using only short dated contracts. The model can be applied either to an economy of heterogeneous agents pooling idiosyncratic risk through self enforcing contracts or to the design of optimal insurance products such as pensions.

Keywords : 

Abstract :假设在一个具有特殊风险但没有不可保险的系统性风险因素的市场中有N个异质代理。这些代理人可以相互达成任意的金融合同，但条件是合同在共同国家的代理人联盟下是自我执行的。我们证明，在温和条件下，当N趋于无穷大时，这唯一地决定了每个代理的极限效用。结果是一个遍历定理，随着人口的增长，问题中的自由度数量会崩溃，因此处于相同状态的代理在极限内被同等对待。我们展示了一种明确的、实际可行的机制，仅使用短期合同来实现这一限制。该模型既可以应用于异构代理通过自我执行合同汇集特殊风险的经济，也可以应用于养老金等最优保险产品的设计。

\[7\] Dependence Informed Sparse Neural Architecture for Stock Return Prediction

标题:基于依赖信息的稀疏神经结构用于股票收益预测

作者:Hongyu Lin, Yulin Chen, Yuanrong Wang, Antonio Briola, Tomaso Aste

来源:ARXIV\_20260817

链接:https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14323

Abstract : Using neural networks for stock return prediction typically requires choices about depth and hidden layer width that are difficult to connect to financial interpretation. We study an alternative  estimate dependence among firm characteristics with a Maximally Filtered Clique Forest (MFCF), then map its clique structure to a Homological Neural Network (HNN). The MFCF maximum clique size K is the only parameter controlling architectural complexity, and it has a clear graphical meaning  it bounds the number of characteristics in each maximal clique and hence the highest interaction order the network can represent. The filtered graph then fixes the neural network s depth, layer widths, and sparse connections before training, in place of a separately chosen depth and width sequence. We apply two HNN variants to annual out of sample forecasts of U.S. stock excess returns from 1987 to 2016 using 94 firm characteristics. The HNN models match a three hidden layer benchmark on pooled predictive accuracy, rank the cross section more accurately, and use roughly 80 times fewer parameters than a fully connected network with the same induced layer widths. Two structural ablations indicate that both the sparse connectivity and the estimated grouping of characteristics contribute to the ranking advantage, and both effects remain significant after correcting for multiple testing.
