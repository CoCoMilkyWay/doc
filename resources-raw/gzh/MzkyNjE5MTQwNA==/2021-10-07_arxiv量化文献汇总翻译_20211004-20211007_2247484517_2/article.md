\[1\] Value at Risk forecasting model based on normal inverse Gaussian distribution driven by dynamic conditional score

标题 : 基于动态条件分数驱动的正态逆高斯分布的风险价值预测模型

作者 : Shijia Song, Handong Li , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.02492

说明 : Comments: 35 pages

Abstract : Under the framework of dynamic conditional score, we propose a parametric forecasting model for Value at Risk based on the normal inverse Gaussian distribution (Hereinafter NIG DCS VaR), which creatively incorporates intraday information into daily VaR forecast. NIG specifies an appropriate distribution to return and the semi additivity of the NIG parameters makes it feasible to improve the estimation of daily return in light of intraday return, and thus the VaR can be explicitly obtained by calculating the quantile of the re estimated distribution of daily return. We conducted an empirical analysis using two main indexes of the Chinese stock market, and a variety of backtesting approaches as well as the model confidence set approach prove that the VaR forecasts of NIG DCS model generally gain an advantage over those of realized GARCH (RGARCH) models. Especially when the risk level is relatively high, NIG DCS VaR beats RGARCH VaR in terms of coverage ability and independence.

摘要 : 在动态条件得分的框架下，我们提出了一种基于正态逆高斯分布的风险价值参数预测模型（以下简称NIG-DCS-VaR），该模型创造性地将日内信息融入每日VaR预测中。NIG指定了一个适当的收益分布，NIG参数的半加性使得根据日内收益改进日收益估计是可行的，因此可以通过计算重新估计的日收益分布的分位数来明确获得VaR。我们使用中国股市的两个主要指标进行了实证分析，各种回溯测试方法以及模型置信集方法证明，NIG DCS模型的VaR预测通常优于已实现GARCH（RGARCH）模型。特别是当风险水平较高时，NIG DCS VaR在覆盖能力和独立性方面优于RGARCH VaR。

\[2\] Reactive Power Markets for the Future Grid

标题 : 未来电网的无功功率市场

作者 : Adam Potter, Rabab Haider, Anuradha M. Annaswamy , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.02337

说明 : Comments: 9 pages, 7 figures

Abstract : As pressures to decarbonize the electricity grid increase, the grid edge is witnessing a rapid adoption of distributed and renewable generation. As a result, traditional methods for reactive power management and compensation may become ineffective. Current state of art for reactive power compensation, which rely primarily on capacity payments, exclude distributed generation (DG). We propose an alternative a reactive power market at the distribution level. The proposed market uses variable payments to compensate DGs equipped with smart inverters, at an increased spatial and temporal granularity, through a distribution level Locational Marginal Price (d LMP). We validate our proposed market with a case study of the New England grid on a modified IEEE 123 bus, while varying DG penetration from 5 to 160 . Results show that our market can accommodate such a large penetration, with stable reactive power revenue streams. The market can leverage the considerable flexibility afforded by inverter based resources to meet over 40 of reactive power load when operating in a power factor range of 0.6 to 0.95. DGs participating in the market can earn up to 11 of their total revenue from reactive power payments. Finally, the corresponding daily d LMPs determined from the proposed market were observed to exhibit limited volatility.

摘要 : 随着电网脱碳压力的增加，电网边缘正在见证分布式和可再生能源发电的迅速采用。因此，传统的无功功率管理和补偿方法可能会变得无效。无功功率补偿目前主要依靠容量支付，不包括分布式发电（DG）。我们提出了配电级无功市场的替代方案。提议的市场使用可变支付，通过分销级位置边际价格（d LMP），以更高的空间和时间粒度补偿配备智能逆变器的DG。我们以改进的IEEE 123母线上的新英格兰电网为例，验证了我们提出的市场，同时将DG渗透率从5变为160。结果表明，我们的市场能够容纳如此大的渗透率，具有稳定的无功功率收入流。在功率因数范围为0.6到0.95时，市场可以利用基于逆变器的资源提供的相当大的灵活性来满足40%以上的无功负载。参与市场的DG可从无功支付中获得多达11%的总收入。最后，根据拟定市场确定的相应每日d LMP显示出有限的波动性。

\[3\] A Hierarchical Local Electricity Market for a DER rich Grid Edge

标题 : 一个层次化的地方电力市场，用于丰富的电网边缘

作者 : Vineet Jagadeesan Nair, Venkatesh Venkataramanan, Rabab Haider, Anuradha Annaswamy , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.02358

说明 : Comments: 10 pages, 13 figures, 1 table. This work has been submitted to the IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid for possible publication. Copyright may be transferred without notice, after which this version may no longer be accessible

Abstract : With increasing penetration of DERs in the distribution system, it is critical to design market structures that enable smooth integration of DERs. A hierarchical local electricity market (LEM) structure is proposed in this paper with a secondary market (SM) at the lower level representing secondary feeders and a primary market (PM) at the upper level, representing primary feeders, in order to effectively use DERs to increase grid efficiency and resilience. The lower level SM enforces budget, power balance and flexibility constraints and accounts for costs related to consumers, such as their disutility, flexibility limits, and commitment reliability, while the upper level PM enforces power physics constraints such as power balance and capacity limits, and also minimizes line losses. The hierarchical LEM is extensively evaluated using a modified IEEE 123 bus with high DER penetration, with each primary feeder consisting of at least three secondary feeders. Data from a GridLAB D model is used to emulate realistic power injections and load profiles over the course of 24 hours. The performance of the LEM is illustrated by delineating the family of power injection profiles across the primary and secondary feeders as well as corresponding local electricity tariffs that vary across the distribution grid. Together, it represents an overall framework for a Distribution System Operator (DSO) who can provide the oversight for the entire LEM.

摘要 : 随着配电系统中DER的日益普及，设计能够顺利整合DER的市场结构至关重要。本文提出了一种分层的本地电力市场（LEM）结构，其中下层的二级市场（SM）代表二次馈线，上层的一级市场（PM）代表一次馈线，以便有效地利用二级市场来提高电网效率和恢复力。较低级别的SM强制执行预算、功率平衡和灵活性约束，并考虑与用户相关的成本，如其无用性、灵活性限制和承诺可靠性，而较高级别的PM强制执行功率物理约束，如功率平衡和容量限制，并将线路损耗降至最低。分层LEM使用改进的IEEE 123总线进行广泛评估，该总线具有高DER穿透，每个一次馈线至少由三个二次馈线组成。来自GridLAB D模型的数据用于模拟24小时内的真实功率注入和负载曲线。LEM的性能通过描绘一次和二次馈线上的功率注入曲线族以及配电网中变化的相应本地电价来说明。总之，它代表了配电系统运营商（DSO）的总体框架，该运营商可以对整个LEM进行监督。

\[4\] Optimal pricing for electricity retailers

标题 : 电力零售商的最优定价

作者 : Román Pérez-Santalla, Miguel Carrión, Carlos Ruiz , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.02735

说明 : -

Abstract : In the present work we tackle the problem of finding the optimal price tariff to be set by a risk averse electric retailer participating in the pool and whose customers are price sensitive. We assume that the retailer has access to a sufficiently large smart meter dataset from which it can statistically characterize the relationship between the tariff price and the demand load of its clients. Three different models are analyzed to predict the aggregated load as a function of the electricity prices and other parameters, as humidity or temperature. In particular, we train linear regression (predictive) models to forecast the resulting demand load as a function of the retail price. Then we will insert this model in a quadratic optimization problem which evaluates the optimal price to be offered. This optimization problem accounts for different sources of uncertainty including consumer s response and renewable source availability, and relies on a stochastic and risk averse formulation. Moreover, we consider both standard forward based contracts and the recently introduced power purchase agreement contracts as risk hedging tools for the retailer. The results are promising as profits are found for the retailer with highly competitive prices and some possible improvements are shown if a better dataset could be produced. A realistic case study and multiple sensitivity analyses have been performed to characterize the risk aversion behavior of the retailer considering price sensitive consumers. It has been assumed that the energy procurement of the retailer can be satisfied from the pool and different types of contracts. The obtained results reveal that the risk aversion degree of the retailer strongly influences contracting decisions, whereas the price sensitiveness of consumers has a higher impact on the selling price offered.

摘要 : 在目前的工作中，我们要解决的问题是找到一个最优电价，该电价由一个风险厌恶的电力零售商来设定，该电力零售商参与该电价池，并且其客户是价格敏感的。我们假设零售商可以访问足够大的智能电表数据集，从中可以统计地描述电价与其客户需求负荷之间的关系。分析了三种不同的模型，以预测作为电价和其他参数（如湿度或温度）函数的总负荷。特别是，我们训练线性回归（预测）模型，以预测作为零售价格函数的最终需求负荷。然后，我们将把这个模型插入到一个二次优化问题中，该问题评估将要提供的最优价格。该优化问题考虑了不同的不确定性来源，包括消费者的反应和可再生能源的可用性，并依赖于随机和风险规避公式。此外，我们考虑标准远期合同和最近推出的电力购买协议合同作为风险对冲工具的零售商。结果是有希望的，因为零售商的利润是具有高度竞争力的价格，并且如果可以生成更好的数据集，则可以显示一些可能的改进。一个现实的案例研究和多重敏感性分析已经被用来描述零售商考虑价格敏感消费者的风险规避行为。假设零售商的能源采购可以通过联营和不同类型的合同来满足。研究结果表明，零售商的风险规避程度对契约决策有很大影响，而消费者的价格敏感性对所提供的销售价格有较大影响。

\[6\] Predicting Credit Risk for Unsecured Lending

标题 : 无担保贷款的信用风险预测

作者 : K.S. Naik , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.02206

说明 : Comments: 9 pages, 4 figures, 3 tables

Abstract : Since the 1990s, there have been significant advances in the technology space and the e Commerce area, leading to an exponential increase in demand for cashless payment solutions. This has led to increased demand for credit cards, bringing along with it the possibility of higher credit defaults and hence higher delinquency rates, over a period of time. The purpose of this research paper is to build a contemporary credit scoring model to forecast credit defaults for unsecured lending (credit cards), by employing machine learning techniques. As much of the customer payments data available to lenders, for forecasting Credit defaults, is imbalanced (skewed), on account of a limited subset of default instances, this poses a challenge for predictive modelling. In this research, this challenge is addressed by deploying Synthetic Minority Oversampling Technique (SMOTE), a proven technique to iron out such imbalances, from a given dataset. On running the research dataset through seven different machine learning models, the results indicate that the Light Gradient Boosting Machine (LGBM) Classifier model outperforms the other six classification techniques. Thus, our research indicates that the LGBM classifier model is better equipped to deliver higher learning speeds, better efficiencies and manage larger data volumes. We expect that deployment of this model will enable better and timely prediction of credit defaults for decision makers in commercial lending institutions and banks.

摘要 : 自20世纪90年代以来，技术领域和电子商务领域取得了重大进展，导致对无现金支付解决方案的需求呈指数级增长。这导致了对信用卡需求的增加，同时也带来了在一段时间内更高的信用违约率和更高的拖欠率。本研究的目的是利用机器学习技术建立一个当代信用评分模型来预测无担保贷款（信用卡）的信用违约。由于贷款机构可用于预测信用违约的大部分客户付款数据是不平衡的（倾斜的），由于违约实例的子集有限，这对预测建模提出了挑战。在这项研究中，这一挑战通过部署合成少数过采样技术（SMOTE）来解决，SMOTE是一种从给定数据集中消除这种不平衡的经验证的技术。通过七种不同的机器学习模型运行研究数据集，结果表明，轻梯度推进机（LGBM）分类器模型优于其他六种分类技术。因此，我们的研究表明，LGBM分类器模型能够更好地提供更高的学习速度、更好的效率和管理更大的数据量。我们预计，该模型的部署将使商业贷款机构和银行的决策者能够更好、及时地预测信贷违约。

\[7\] Zipf s law in nonlinear self excited Hawkes processes

标题 : 非线性自激Hawkes过程中的Zipf定律

作者 : Kiyoshi Kanazawa, Didier Sornette , 类别 ：Statistical Mechanics (cond-mat.stat-mech)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00242

说明 : Comments: 5 pages, 2 figures + Appendices

Abstract : The origin(s) of the ubiquity of Zipf s law (an inverse power law form for the probability density function (PDF) with exponent 1 1 ) is still a matter of fascination and investigation in many scientific fields from linguistic, social, economic, computer sciences to essentially all natural sciences. In parallel, self excited dynamics is a prevalent characteristic of many systems, from seismicity, financial volatility and financial defaults, to sociology, consumer behaviors, computer sciences, The Internet, neuronal discharges and spike trains in biological neuron networks, gene expression and even criminology. Motivated by financial and seismic modelling, we bring the two threads together by introducing a general class of nonlinear self excited point processes with fast accelerating intensities as a function of tension . Solving the corresponding master equations, we find that a wide class of such nonlinear Hawkes processes have the PDF of their intensities described by Zipf s law on the condition that (i) the intensity is a fast accelerating function of tension and (ii) the distribution of the point fertilities is symmetric. This unearths a novel mechanism for Zipf s law, providing a new understanding of its ubiquity.

摘要 : 齐普夫定律（指数为1 1的概率密度函数（PDF）的逆幂律形式）的普遍性的起源仍然是从语言学、社会学、经济学、计算机科学到基本上所有自然科学的许多科学领域中令人着迷和研究的问题。同时，自激动力学是许多系统的普遍特征，从地震活动、金融波动和金融违约，到社会学、消费者行为、计算机科学、互联网、生物神经元网络中的神经元放电和尖峰序列、基因表达甚至犯罪学。在金融和地震建模的推动下，我们通过引入一类具有快速加速强度的非线性自激点过程（作为张力的函数），将这两条线索结合在一起。通过求解相应的主方程，我们发现，在（i）强度是张力的快速加速函数和（ii）点肥力分布对称的条件下，一大类此类非线性Hawkes过程的强度具有Zipf定律描述的PDF。这揭示了齐普夫定律的一种新机制，为其普遍性提供了新的理解。

\[9\] Joint optimization of sales mix and generation plan for a large electricity producer

标题 : 大型发电商销售组合和发电计划的联合优化

作者 : Paolo Falbo, Carlos Ruiz , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.02016

说明 : -

Abstract : The paper develops a typical management problem of a large power producer (i.e., he can partly influence the market price). In particular, he routinely needs to decide how much of his generation it is preferable to commit to fixed price bilateral contracts (e.g., futures) or to the spot market. However, he also needs to plan how to distribute the production across the different plants under his control. The two decisions, namely the sales mix and the generation plan, naturally interact, since the opportunity to influence the spot price depends, among other things, by the amount of the energy that the producer directs on the spot market.
