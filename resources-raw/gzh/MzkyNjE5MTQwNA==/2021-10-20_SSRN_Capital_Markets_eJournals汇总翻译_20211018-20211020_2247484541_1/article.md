\[1\] Dodge v. Ford: What Happened and Why?

标题:道奇v。福特：发生了什么，为什么？

作者:Mark J. Roe

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3943559

Abstract : Behind Henry Ford’s business decisions that led to the widely taught, famous-in-law-school Dodge v. Ford shareholder primacy decision were three relevant industrial organization structures that put Ford in a difficult business position. First, Ford Motor had a highly profitable monopoly. Second, to stymie union organizers and to motivate his new assembly line workers, Henry Ford raised worker pay greatly; Ford could not maintain his monopoly without sufficient worker acquiescence. And, third, if Ford pursued monopoly profit in an obvious and explicit way, the Ford brand would have been damaged with both his workforce and the company’s consumers. The transactions underlying Dodge v. Ford should be reconceptualized as Ford Motor Company and its auto workers splitting the “monopoly rectangle” that Ford Motor’s assembly-line produced, with Ford’s business plans requiring tremendous cash expenditures to keep and expand that monopoly. Hence, a common interpretation of the litigation setting—namely that Ford let slip his charitable purpose when he could have won with a business judgment defense—should be reversed. Ford had a true business purpose—spending on labor and a vertically-integrated factory to solidify his monopoly profit and splitting that profit with labor—but he would have jeopardized the strategy’s effectiveness by articulating it.The existing main interpretations of the corporate law decision and its structural background remain relevant—such as Ford seeking to squeeze out the Dodge brothers to deny the Dodge brothers cash for their own car company. But they must take a second-tier, as none fully encompasses the industrial setting—of monopoly, incipient union-organizing, and a restless workforce. Without accounting for Ford Motor’s monopoly and the related labor tensions, we cannot fully understand the Dodge v. Ford controversy. Stakeholder pressure can more readily succeed in a firm having significant economic rents, a setting that seems common today and was true for Ford Motor Company in the 1910s.

Keywords : corporate governance, corporate purpose, stakeholders, hedge funds, shareholder activism, rents, agency costs, political economy, corporate interests

Abstract :亨利·福特的商业决定导致了法学院广受教育、著名的道奇诉福特一案。福特的股东优先权决定是三个相关的产业组织结构，使福特处于艰难的商业地位。首先，福特汽车拥有高利润的垄断地位。其次，为了阻挠工会组织者和激励他的新装配线工人，亨利·福特大幅提高了工人工资；如果没有足够的工人默许，福特无法维持他的垄断地位。第三，如果福特以一种明显而明确的方式追求垄断利润，那么他的员工和公司的消费者都会对福特品牌造成损害。道奇v。福特应该被重新定义为福特汽车公司，其汽车工人将福特汽车装配线生产的“垄断矩形”分割开来，而福特的商业计划需要巨大的现金支出来保持和扩大这种垄断。因此，对诉讼背景的一种常见解释是，福特在本可以通过商业判断辩护获胜的情况下，却泄露了他的慈善目的，这种解释应该被推翻。福特有一个真正的商业目的——在劳动力上的支出和一个垂直整合的工厂，以巩固他的垄断利润，并将利润与劳动力分割，但如果他明确阐述这一战略，就会危及该战略的有效性。对公司法决定及其结构背景的现有主要解释仍然存在相关的，如福特寻求挤出道奇兄弟，以拒绝道奇兄弟现金为自己的汽车公司。但它们必须走第二层，因为没有一个完全涵盖垄断的行业环境、初期的工会组织和不安的劳动力。如果不考虑福特汽车的垄断和相关的劳资关系紧张，我们就无法完全理解道奇v。福特的争议。利益相关者的压力更容易在一家拥有巨额经济租金的公司取得成功，这种情况在今天似乎很普遍，在1910年代福特汽车公司也是如此。

Keywords :公司治理、公司目标、利益相关者、对冲基金、股东积极主义、租金、代理成本、政治经济学、公司利益

\[2\] The Effect of Skilled Labor Intensity on Corporate Dividend Payouts

标题:技术劳动强度对公司股利支付的影响

作者:Justin Hung Nguyen,Buhui Qiu

出处:Journal of Business Finance and Accounting, Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3945133

Abstract : This study shows that skilled-labor-intensive firms have lower dividend payouts. A one-standard-deviation increase in skilled labor intensity on average results in a 0.74 percentage point decline in the propensity to pay dividends and a 0.72 percentage point decrease in dividend payout ratio. Our evidence suggests that greater labor adjustment costs (LACs) of skilled labor increase future cash flow uncertainty, which discourages managers from paying dividends. Consistently, we find that an exogenous increase (decrease) in LACs following the shock of 2017 H-1B policy changes (the shock of 2005 Hurricane Katrina) leads treated firms to lower (heighten) their dividend payouts. Collectively, these findings indicate a strong dampening effect of LACs on corporate dividend payouts.

Keywords : Skilled Labor Intensity; Labor Adjustment Costs; Dividend; Corporate Payouts; Future Cash Flow Uncertainty

Abstract :这项研究表明，熟练的劳动密集型企业的股息支付较低。平均而言，熟练劳动强度的一个标准差增加会导致股息支付倾向下降0.74个百分点，股息支付率下降0.72个百分点。我们的证据表明，高技能劳动力的劳动力调整成本（LAC）增加了未来现金流的不确定性，这阻碍了管理者支付股息。我们一致发现，2017年H-1B政策变化冲击（2005年卡特里娜飓风冲击）后LAC的外源性增加（减少）导致接受治疗的公司降低（提高）其股息支付。总的来说，这些发现表明拉丁美洲和加勒比海地区对公司股息支付有强烈的抑制作用。

Keywords :熟练劳动强度；劳动力调整成本；股息公司支出；未来现金流不确定性

\[3\] Financing Infrastructure with Inattentive Investors: The Case of US Municipal Governments

标题:与漫不经心的投资者一起为基础设施融资：美国市政政府的案例

作者:Ehsan Azarmsa

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3945106

Abstract : In the United States, building infrastructure (e.g., schools, roads, hospitals) is primarily the responsibility of state and local governments and financed by borrowing in the municipal bond market. Despite the governments' low historical default rate and statutory taxing power, prior empirical evidence suggests they are borrowing constrained. This paper provides new evidence and theory that link the constraint to the dominance of retail investors in the market, who might not be perfectly attentive to the financial needs of their municipal governments. It makes the capital available to more specialized investors, such as municipal mutual funds, a crucial determinant for the borrowing capacity of those governments. Supporting this hypothesis, I find that the mutual funds are among the first investors of municipal bond issues, and they gradually resell their bonds to other investors. Furthermore, in response to a 1% inflow to the mutual funds that are among the major investors of a county government, the government borrows more by 0.2% in the subsequent quarter, conditional on issuing new bonds, while the inflow reduces the interest rate by 0.002%. To rationalize these observations, I develop a model of the municipal bond market in which mutual funds act as the \`\`attentive intermediaries.'' By calibrating the model with the empirical estimates, I find that the elasticity of bond supply by the municipal governments is about 100, and is at least one order of magnitude larger than the short-term elasticity of the bond demand. The results support market interventions by the federal government in times of crisis if they accompany massive outflows from the mutual funds.

Keywords : Municipal governments, Municipal bond market, Infrastructure, Investor inattention, Mutual funds

Abstract :在美国，基础设施建设（如学校、道路、医院）主要由州和地方政府负责，并通过市政债券市场的借款进行融资。尽管政府的历史违约率和法定征税权较低，但先前的经验证据表明，它们的借贷受到限制。本文提供了新的证据和理论，将这一约束与散户投资者在市场中的主导地位联系起来，散户投资者可能没有完全关注其市政府的金融需求。它使资本可用于更专业的投资者，如市政共同基金，这是这些政府借贷能力的一个关键决定因素。支持这一假设，我发现共同基金是市政债券发行的首批投资者之一，它们逐渐将债券转售给其他投资者。此外，为了应对作为县政府主要投资者之一的共同基金1%的流入，政府在随后的一个季度增加了0.2%的借款，条件是发行新债券，而流入降低了0.002%的利率。为了使这些观察结果合理化，我开发了一个市政债券市场模型，在该模型中，共同基金充当“专注的中介机构”。通过使用实证估计对该模型进行校准，我发现市政政府的债券供应弹性约为100，至少比债券需求的短期弹性大一个数量级。这一结果支持了联邦政府在危机时期的市场干预，如果它们伴随着共同基金的大规模外流。

Keywords :市政政府、市政债券市场、基础设施、投资者疏忽、共同基金

\[4\] Benchmark Currency Stochastic Discount Factors

标题:基准货币随机贴现因子

作者:Piotr Orłowski,Valeri Sokolovski,Erik Sverdrup

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3945075

Abstract : We examine the pricing performance of out-of-sample pricing factors in the broad cross-section of currency returns. To this end, we develop a methodology for estimating empirical minimum-entropy stochastic discount factors (SDFs) under economically-motivated constraints on position leverage. Our empirical SDFs deliver superior out-of-sample fit and smaller pricing errors than existing factor models in the cross-section of currency portfolio returns, and are priced in individual currency and hedge fund cross-sections. After transaction costs, an investable SDF portfolio delivers a Sharpe ratio of around 0.8 and positively skewed returns. These empirical SDFs offer tractable benchmarks for candidate currency pricing models.

Keywords : FX risk premium, FX hedge funds, leverage, kernels, machine learning, SDF

\[5\] Global trade network and stock market returns: International evidence

标题:全球贸易网络与股票市场收益：国际证据

作者:Tong Fang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3944781

Abstract : We investigate the relationship between global trade network centrality and international stock market returns. We empirically find that trade network centrality negatively and significantly predicts international stock market returns, indicating that central (peripheral) economies have lower (higher) stock returns. The predictive ability remains significant after controlling for traditional predictors. A centrality-based long-short trading strategy generates a significant centrality premium, which cannot be fully explained by the international risk factors of Fama and French (2012). We combine global consumption risk exposure with economy size (GDP) and reveal that the risk exposure per GDP effectively explains the negative relationship between trade network centrality and stock returns. Conflict risk exposure also explains our results, as central economies have higher costs of conflicts and lower conflict risk exposure. Central (peripheral) economies have low (high) risk exposure per GDP and thus have low (high) stock returns.

Keywords : Trade network, Centrality, Stock returns, Risk exposure, Economy size

Abstract :我们研究了全球贸易网络中心性与国际股票市场收益之间的关系。我们通过实证发现，贸易网络中心性对国际股票市场收益率具有显著的负向预测作用，表明中心（外围）经济体的股票收益率较低（较高）。控制传统预测因子后，预测能力仍然显著。基于中心性的多空交易策略产生了显著的中心性溢价，这不能完全用Fama和French（2012）的国际风险因素来解释。我们将全球消费风险敞口与经济规模（GDP）相结合，发现每GDP的风险敞口有效地解释了贸易网络中心性与股票收益之间的负相关关系。冲突风险敞口也解释了我们的结果，因为中央经济体的冲突成本较高，冲突风险敞口较低。中央（外围）经济体的人均GDP风险敞口较低（较高），因此股票回报率较低（较高）。

Keywords :交易网络、中心性、股票收益、风险敞口、经济规模

\[6\] Predictable Forward Performance Processes: Infrequent Evaluation and Robo-Advising Applications

标题:可预测的未来绩效流程：不频繁的评估和机器人建议应用

作者:Gechun Liang,Moris Simon Strub,Yuwei Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3944223

Abstract : We study discrete-time predictable forward processes when trading times do not coincide with performance evaluation times in the binomial tree model for the financial market. The key step in the construction of these processes is to solve a linear functional equation of higher order associated with the inverse problem driving the evolution of the predictable forward process. We provide sufficient conditions for the existence and uniqueness and an explicit construction of the predictable forward process under these conditions. Furthermore, we show that these processes are time-monotone in the evaluation period. Finally, we argue that predictable forward preferences are a viable framework to model preferences for robo-advising applications and determine an optimal interaction schedule between client and robo-advisor that balances a tradeoff between increasing uncertainty about the client's beliefs on the financial market and an interaction cost.

Keywords : forward performance processes, robo-advising, binomial tree model, portfolio selection, functional equation

Abstract :在金融市场的二叉树模型中，我们研究了当交易时间与绩效评估时间不一致时的离散时间可预测远期过程。构造这些过程的关键步骤是求解与驱动可预测正向过程演化的反问题相关的高阶线性函数方程。在这些条件下，我们给出了可预测正向过程存在唯一性的充分条件，并给出了可预测正向过程的显式构造。此外，我们还证明了这些过程在评价期内是时间单调的。最后，我们认为，可预测的远期偏好是一个可行的框架，可以对机器人咨询应用程序的偏好进行建模，并确定客户和机器人顾问之间的最佳交互计划，以平衡客户对金融市场的信念日益增加的不确定性和交互成本之间的权衡。

Keywords :远期绩效流程，机器人咨询，二叉树模型，投资组合选择，函数方程

\[7\] Momentum strategies, statistical arbitrage and the market efficiency (The case of Tehran Stock Exchange)

标题:动量策略、统计套利与市场效率（德黑兰证券交易所案例）

作者:Sina Abdi,kamran pakizeh

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3943891

Abstract : The basic goal of this paper is to investigate the efficacy of a financial notion called Statistical Arbitrage for evaluating the efficiency of the Tehran Stock Exchange. According to this notion, a profit can be gained while its risk decreases over time and tends toward zero. In order to implement this test and construct its trading profits function, a buy-and-hold strategy is employed. Using the Momentum Strategy, nine sub-strategies were defined based on the chronology of the formation and holding periods (short-term, mid-term, and long-term). Results show that two out of nine strategies succeeded in detecting statistical arbitrage opportunities. The successful strategies then were compared via two different methods to identify the best strategy. In other words, these comparisons distinguished the quality of statistical arbitrage opportunities. While, using statistical arbitrage model we demonstrate that the existence of statistical arbitrage opportunities contradicts the Efficient Market Hypothesis in two strategies in the short-run, results of remaining momentum strategies further support the idea of dynamic efficiency, where there is a move from inefficiency toward efficiency in the long-run in Tehran Stock Exchange.

Keywords : efficient market hypothesis, statistical arbitrage, momentum strategy

Abstract :本文的基本目标是调查一种称为统计套利的金融概念在评估德黑兰证券交易所效率方面的有效性。根据这个概念，当风险随时间降低并趋于零时，可以获得利润。为了实现这一测试并构建其交易利润函数，采用了买入并持有策略。利用动量策略，根据形成和持有期（短期、中期和长期）的时间顺序定义了九个子策略。结果表明，九分之二的策略成功地发现了统计套利机会。然后通过两种不同的方法对成功的策略进行比较，以确定最佳策略。换句话说，这些比较区分了统计套利机会的质量。同时，利用统计套利模型，我们证明了统计套利机会的存在与短期内两种策略中的有效市场假说相矛盾，剩余动量策略的结果进一步支持了动态效率的观点，德黑兰证券交易所从长期来看从低效走向高效。

Keywords :有效市场假说、统计套利、动量策略

\[9\] Corporate Real Estate Usage and Firm Valuation: Evidence from a Dynamic Partial Adjustment Model

标题:企业房地产使用与企业估值：来自动态部分调整模型的证据

作者:Qing Li,David C. Ling,Qie Ellie Yin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3942997

Abstract : The trade-off between the potential benefits and costs of using corporate real estate (CorRE) in the production process creates an optimal level of CorRE that varies over time and across firms. We document the importance of conditioning on a firm’s optimal CorRE usage when analyzing the influences of CorRE on firm valuations and stock returns. Using a dynamic partial adjustment model, we estimate differences in firms’ actual CorRE usage from optimal levels and the speed at which firms move toward their optimal CorRE usage. We find that investors tend to punish the valuation of companies that deviate from optimal CorRE usage, probably through the channel that large deviations from optimal CorRE usage hurt firm profitability.
