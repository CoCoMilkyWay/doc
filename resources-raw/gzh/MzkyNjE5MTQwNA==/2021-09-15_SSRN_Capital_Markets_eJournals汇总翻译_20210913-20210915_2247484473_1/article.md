\[1\] The Influence of Individual Investors on Ex-Dividend Day Returns

标题:个人投资者对除息日收益率的影响

作者:Andrew Ainsworth,Adrian D. Lee

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3922410

Abstract : Taxes create distortions in financial markets. A tax credit attached to dividend payments in Australia creates a wedge in valuations as it can be utilized only by certain investors. Individual investors, who benefit most from the credit, buy aggressively cum-dividend and sell aggressively ex-dividend, demanding liquidity from institutional investors. Stocks with higher net purchases by individual investors operating through discount brokers in the cum-dividend period have ex-day returns that are up to 42 basis points lower. The tax distortion allows individual investors to capture the tax credit and institutional investors to increase trading profits. Individual investor trading influences ex-dividend pricing.

Keywords : dividend clienteles, individual investors, ex-dividend day premium, order choice

Abstract :税收造成金融市场的扭曲。在澳大利亚，股息支付附带的税收抵免造成了估值的楔子，因为它只能由某些投资者使用。从信贷中受益最多的个人投资者，积极买入并派息，积极卖出除息，要求机构投资者提供流动性。在股息期内，通过折扣经纪人运营的个人投资者净买入量较高的股票，其除权日收益率最高可低42个基点。税收扭曲使得个人投资者可以获得税收抵免，而机构投资者可以增加交易利润。个人投资者交易影响除息定价。

Keywords :分红客户、个人投资者、除息日溢价、订单选择

\[2\] Information, Market Power and Welfare

标题:信息、市场力量和福利

作者:Youcheng Lou,Rohit Rahi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3922215

Abstract : We study a financial market in which agents with interdependent values bid for a risky asset. Some agents are privately informed of their own value for the asset while others seek to infer it from the equilibrium price. Due to adverse selection, uninformed agents are less willing than the informed to provide liquidity, and engage in greater bid shading when prices are more informative. While increased participation by informed agents leads to perfect competition in the limit, the market remains illiquid to some degree even with free entry of uninformed traders. The incentive to produce information is increasing in market size and is maximal in a perfectly competitive economy. Price informativeness, on the other hand, is independent of market size. Curtailing information production by one group can reduce adverse selection, and improve liquidity and welfare for all agents.

Keywords : Double auction, interdependent values, market power, adverse selection, information acquisition, welfare.

Abstract :我们研究一个金融市场，在这个市场中，具有相互依赖价值的代理竞购风险资产。一些代理私下被告知自己的资产价值，而另一些代理则试图从均衡价格中推断资产价值。由于逆向选择，不知情的代理比知情的代理更不愿意提供流动性，并且当价格信息更丰富时，参与更大的投标阴影。虽然知情代理人的参与度增加导致了限额的完美竞争，但市场在某种程度上仍然缺乏流动性，即使没有知情的交易员自由进入。市场规模越来越大，在完全竞争的经济中，产生信息的动机是最大的。另一方面，价格信息性与市场规模无关。减少一个群体的信息产出可以减少逆向选择，提高所有代理人的流动性和福利。

Keywords :双重拍卖、相互依存价值、市场力量、逆向选择、信息获取、福利。

\[3\] What Are Benefits of Attracting Gambling Investors? Evidence from Stock Splits in China

标题:吸引赌博投资者的好处是什么？中国股票分割的证据

作者:Conghui Hu,Ji-Chai Lin,Yu-Jane Liu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3922178

Abstract : Analyzing a sample of Chinese firms splitting their stocks via stock dividends and using proprietary trading data to measure investors’ gambling preference, we find that stock splits raise the stocks’ lottery characteristics, making them attractive to gambling investors, who willingly pay higher prices for skewed securities. Split firms also become more risk-taking. Furthermore, their cost of equity declines, largely due to increased gambling investors’ pricing influence. Our findings suggest that firms with weak lottery characteristics and those with inefficient risk sharing, can use stock splits to attract gambling investors to improve risk sharing and to lower their cost of equity.

Keywords : Gambling Preference, Stock Splits, Risk Sharing, Cost of Equity

Abstract :通过分析中国公司通过股票分红拆分股票的样本，并使用自营交易数据衡量投资者的赌博偏好，我们发现，股票拆分提高了股票的彩票特征，使其对愿意为扭曲证券支付更高价格的赌博投资者具有吸引力。分裂的公司也变得更加冒险。此外，他们的股本成本下降，这主要是由于赌博投资者的定价影响力增加。我们的研究结果表明，具有弱彩票特征和风险分担效率低下的公司可以利用股票分割来吸引博彩投资者，以改善风险分担并降低其股本成本。

Keywords :赌博偏好、股票分割、风险分担、股权成本

\[4\] Neglected Peers in Merger Valuations

标题:并购估值中被忽视的同行

作者:Feng Guo,Tingting Liu,Danni Tu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3921440

Abstract : Using merger documents filed with the SEC from 1994 to 2018, we show that being selected by investment banks as “comparable peers” are more than twice as likely to become a target themselves in the future compared to control firms matched for industry and size. They also experience an increase in analyst coverage and institutional ownership. Investment banks’ informational advantage as well as their ability to facilitate future takeover transactions, appear to contribute to peers’ significantly higher probability of becoming targets. Investors and equity analysts largely ignore the rich information contained in merger filings. A portfolio that longs peers and shorts non-peers matched for industry and size earns up to 15.6% alpha in the subsequent year. Alphas come entirely from the long-leg, which cannot be explained by short-sale constraints.

Keywords : M&A valuations, Predicting takeover targets, Comparable peers, Investor attention, Portfolio returns, Market inefficiency

Abstract :利用1994年至2018年向美国证券交易委员会提交的合并文件，我们发现，与行业和规模相匹配的控制公司相比，被投资银行选为“可比同行”的目标公司未来成为目标公司的可能性要高出一倍多。他们还经历了分析师覆盖率和机构所有权的增加。投资银行的信息优势以及促进未来收购交易的能力，似乎有助于同行成为目标的概率显著提高。投资者和股票分析师基本上忽视了合并文件中包含的丰富信息。根据行业和规模匹配，做多同行和做空非同行的投资组合在随后一年的阿尔法收益率高达15.6%。Alpha完全来自长腿，这不能用卖空限制来解释。

Keywords :并购估值、预测收购目标、可比同行、投资者关注度、投资组合回报、市场效率低下

\[5\] Understanding the Pricing of Currency Risk in Global Equity Markets

标题:了解全球股票市场中货币风险的定价

作者:George Andrew Karolyi,Ying Wu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3921466

Abstract : This paper explores potential economic mechanisms through which fluctuations in exchange rates are priced in international stock returns. Our investigation focuses on two currency risk factors – a dollar-risk factor and a carry-trade-risk factor – and their explanatory power for a variety of test assets comprised of monthly returns for over 47,000 stocks from 46 countries and over four decades. We find that currency risk is more likely to be priced among firms producing tradeable goods, and especially during periods of sudden exchange rate fluctuations. The divergent evolution is robust with respect to the evaluating criteria on firm internationalization, the benchmark factor models chosen, the sub-periods examined, and the test asset portfolios assessed.

Keywords : International asset pricing, currency risk, exchange rates

Abstract :本文探讨了汇率波动在国际股票收益中定价的潜在经济机制。我们的调查重点关注两个货币风险因素——美元风险因素和套利交易风险因素——以及它们对各种测试资产的解释力，这些测试资产包括来自46个国家40多年的47000多只股票的月度回报。我们发现，货币风险更可能在生产可贸易商品的公司之间定价，特别是在汇率突然波动期间。就企业国际化的评估标准、选择的基准因子模型、检查的子周期和评估的测试资产组合而言，差异演化是稳健的。

Keywords :国际资产定价、货币风险、汇率

\[6\] Sustainable Systematic Credit

标题:可持续系统信用

作者:Peter Diep,Lukasz Pomorski,Scott A. Richardson

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3920988

Abstract : Interest in sustainable investing has exploded in recent years, initially focused on public equity markets, but now evolving into fixed income. We assess various aspects of sustainable investing for public corporate bond markets (both Investment Grade and High Yield). Using a representative set of sustainability measures spanning environment, societal and governance (ESG) constructs we find: (i) credit spreads are only marginally associated with ESG measures, (ii) ESG measures are only marginally associated with standard return forecasting measures for corporate bonds, and (iii) ESG measures are not reliably associated with future credit excess returns. While the direct investment impact of sustainability may be unclear/modest, there is still considerable interest from asset owners to ensure credit allocations are sustainable. We find that it is possible to incorporate (i) both static and dynamic exclusion screens, (ii) positive tilts toward more sustainable issuers, and (iii) economically meaningful reduction in carbon intensity, with minimal portfolio distortions. Thus, a well-implemented systematic approach has the potential to offer attractive risk-adjusted returns in a sustainable manner.

Keywords : ESG, credit, fixed income, portfolio choice, socially responsible investing, CSR

Abstract :近年来，人们对可持续投资的兴趣激增，最初集中在公共股票市场，但现在已演变为固定收益。我们评估了上市公司债券市场可持续投资的各个方面（投资级和高收益）。通过使用一组跨越环境、社会和治理（ESG）结构的具有代表性的可持续性措施，我们发现：（i）信用利差仅与ESG措施略微相关，（ii）ESG措施仅与公司债券的标准回报预测措施略微相关，以及（iii）ESG措施与未来信用超额收益没有可靠关联。虽然可持续性对直接投资的影响可能不明确/不大，但资产所有者仍对确保信贷分配的可持续性感兴趣。我们发现有可能纳入（i）静态和动态排除筛选，（ii）向更可持续的发行人倾斜，以及（iii）在经济上有意义的碳强度降低，且投资组合扭曲最小。因此，实施良好的系统方法有可能以可持续的方式提供具有吸引力的风险调整回报。

Keywords :ESG、信贷、固定收益、投资组合选择、社会责任投资、企业社会责任

\[7\] An Equilibrium Model of Career Concerns, Investment Horizons, and Mutual Fund Value Added

标题:职业关注、投资期限和共同基金增值的均衡模型

作者:Jules H. van Binsbergen,Jungsuk Han,Hongxun Ruan,Ran Xing

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3920863

Abstract : We study a dynamic equilibrium model of mutual fund investing under career concerns that features investment opportunities at different horizons. Equilibrium returns are endogenously determined by competition. Short-term investment strategies can benefit fund managers by accelerating skill revelation, while the downside risk is managed by manager exit. In the steady state, a large number of new and unskilled managers exploit the value of this call option, driving down short-term excess returns. A small number of experienced and skilled managers exploit scalable long-term investment opportunities, adding substantial value. We empirically confirm our theoretical predictions using US mutual fund data.

Keywords : career concern, investment turnover, fund manager skill, fund size, optionality

Abstract :我们研究了一个基于职业关注的共同基金投资动态均衡模型，该模型以不同视野下的投资机会为特征。均衡收益是由竞争内在决定的。短期投资策略可以通过加速技能揭示而使基金经理受益，而下行风险则由基金经理退出来管理。在稳定状态下，大量新手和非熟练经理人利用看涨期权的价值，压低短期超额回报。少数经验丰富、技术娴熟的经理人利用可扩展的长期投资机会，增加了大量价值。我们使用美国共同基金数据实证证实了我们的理论预测。

Keywords :职业关注、投资周转率、基金经理技能、基金规模、可选性

\[8\] SPACs and Forward-Looking Disclosure: Hype or Information?

标题:SPAC和前瞻性披露：炒作还是信息？

作者:Kimball Chapman,Richard M. Frankel,Xiumin Martin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3920714

Abstract : Regulators and legal scholars express concern that SPAC growth results from a disclosure-regulation loophole and that forward-looking-disclosures by SPACs can mislead investors. In this study, we examine voluntary disclosure in these transactions and associated outcomes. SPACs offer a means for firms to become public, but, unlike IPOs, we find SPAC firms frequently provide forward-looking information (e.g., earnings forecasts). Measuring disclosure by tone, the existence of a forecast, and forecasting intensity, our results suggest that forward-looking disclosure in SPAC merger announcements on average does not mislead small investors. Disclosures containing forecasts are associated with positive returns at the announcement. They are not associated with subsequent return reversals or redemption rates. More forecasts are related to positive subsequent return drift, lower redemption rates and bid-ask spread, and less (more) retail (institutional) buying. Moreover, correlations between outcomes and disclosure tone are not significant, suggesting that hype, if present, does not sway investors. Our findings do not support the concern that SPAC firms take advantage of apparent exemption from prosecution under the 1933 Act to hype forward-looking disclosure to deceive shareholders.

Keywords : Forward-looking disclosure, SPAC, Information Asymmetry; Adverse Selection; Liquidity; Disclosure; IPO

Abstract :监管机构和法律学者表示担心，SPAC的增长源于披露监管漏洞，SPAC的前瞻性披露可能误导投资者。在本研究中，我们考察了这些交易中的自愿披露以及相关结果。SPAC为公司上市提供了一种手段，但与IPO不同，我们发现SPAC公司经常提供前瞻性信息（如盈利预测）。通过音调、预测的存在和预测强度衡量披露，我们的结果表明，SPAC合并公告中的前瞻性披露平均不会误导小投资者。包含预测的披露与公告时的正回报相关。它们与随后的回报逆转或赎回率无关。更多的预测与正的后续回报漂移、更低的赎回率和买卖价差以及更少（更多）的零售（机构）购买有关。此外，结果和披露基调之间的相关性并不显著，这表明炒作（如果存在）不会影响投资者。我们的研究结果不支持SPAC公司利用1933年法案规定的明显免予起诉来炒作前瞻性披露以欺骗股东的担忧。

Keywords :前瞻性披露、SPAC、信息不对称；逆向选择；资产变现能力披露首次公开募股
