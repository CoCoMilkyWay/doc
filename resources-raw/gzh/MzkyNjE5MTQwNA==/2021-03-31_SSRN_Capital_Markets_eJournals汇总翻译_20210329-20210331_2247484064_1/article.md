\[1\] Bond Convenience Yields in the Eurozone Currency Union

标题:欧元区货币联盟的债券收益率

作者:Zhengyang Jiang,Hanno N. Lustig,Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh,Mindy Z. Xiaolan

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3797321

Abstract : We develop a theoretical framework to study bond convenience yields in a currency union. The intertemporal government budget condition requires bond convenience yields to adjust in response to shocks to government surpluses or seigniorage revenues. Empirically, we find convenience yields explain a larger fraction of the variation in Eurozone bond yields than default spreads, and higher convenience yields are correlated with stronger fiscal conditions both in the cross-section and in the time series. Our estimates suggest the convenience yields impose large fiscal costs for the peripheral countries. If all Eurozone countries could have issued sovereign bonds at the same convenience yields as Germany, they would have raised an extra 350 billion Euros in cumulative revenues from bond issuance between 2003 and 2019, representing 3% of 2019 Eurozone GDP.

Keywords : None

Abstract :我们发展了一个理论框架来研究货币联盟中的债券便利收益率。跨期政府预算条件要求债券便利收益率进行调整，以应对政府盈余或铸币税收入的冲击。从经验上看，我们发现便利收益率比违约利差更能解释欧元区债券收益率变化的一部分，而且更高的便利收益率在横截面和时间序列上都与更强的财政状况相关。我们的估计表明，便利收益率给外围国家带来了巨大的财政成本。如果所有欧元区国家都能以与德国相同的便利收益率发行主权债券，它们将在2003年至2019年间从债券发行中额外筹集3500亿欧元的累计收入，占2019年欧元区GDP的3%。

Keywords :None

\[2\] Dynamic Mean-Downside Risk Portfolio Selection Problem with Stochastic Interest Rate in Continuous-Time

标题:连续时间随机利率下的动态平均下行风险投资组合选择问题

作者:Weiping Wu,Ke Zhou,Zhicheng Li,zhenpeng tang

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3764540

Abstract : Even though a consensus has been reached a long time ago that the interest rate is a stochastic process, most of the existing works on dynamic mean-downside risk portfolio selection still focus on a deterministic interest rate. This work studies a dynamic mean-downside risk portfolio selection problem with a stochastic interest rate in a continuous-time financial market. Specifically, we choose the lower-partial moments(LPM), value-at-risk (VaR), and conditional value-at-risk(CVaR) to model our downside risk measures in the Vasicek interest rate model. By using the martingale method and the inverse Fourier Transformation, we successfully derive the semi-analytical optimal portfolio policies and the optimal wealth processes for the mean-downside risk measures with a stochastic interest rate. The results suggest that the higher the degree of negative correlation, the better the performance of the portfolio valued by mean-downside risk framework when the other market opportunity set is the same.

Keywords : Dynamic Portfolio Selection, Stochastic Interest Rate, Vasicek Model

Abstract :尽管利率是一个随机过程早已成为共识，但现有的动态平均下行风险投资组合选择的研究大多集中在确定性利率上。研究了连续时间金融市场中随机利率下的动态平均下行风险投资组合选择问题。具体来说，我们选择低偏矩（LPM）、风险价值（VaR）和条件风险价值（CVaR）来模拟Vasicek利率模型中的下行风险度量。利用鞅方法和傅立叶逆变换，成功地导出了随机利率下平均下行风险测度的半解析最优投资组合策略和最优财富过程。结果表明，在其他市场机会集相同的情况下，负相关程度越高，平均下行风险框架下的投资组合绩效越好。

Keywords :动态投资组合选择，随机利率，Vasicek模型

\[3\] Uncertain Talking at Central Bank’s Press Conference: News or Noise?

标题:央行新闻发布会上的不确定谈话：新闻还是噪音？

作者:Nan Hu,Zexi Sun

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3796816

Abstract : We show that the uncertain talking at the European Central Bank’s press conference raises stock returns of the Eurozone. By decomposing the uncertain talking into two types, we find that the positive returns are driven by one type, the average meaning of which is represented by words "risk”, "uncertainty”, "volatility”, and "turbulence”. This type is related to uncertain and unfavorable environments, which is negative news that lowers returns. However, an increase in this type also signals expansionary monetary policies in the future, which is positive news that raises returns. We find further evidence to imply that the latter channel dominates.

Keywords : None

Abstract :我们表明，欧洲央行（ecb）新闻发布会上的不确定言论提高了欧元区的股票回报率。通过将不确定性话语分解为两种类型，我们发现正收益是由一种类型驱动的，其平均含义是“风险”、“不确定性”、“波动性”和“动荡性”。这种类型与不确定和不利的环境有关，这是降低回报的负面消息。不过，这类货币的增加也预示着未来的扩张性货币政策，这是提高回报的利好消息。我们发现进一步的证据表明，后一种渠道占主导地位。

Keywords :None

\[4\] Sustainability in a Risky World

作者:John Y. Campbell,Ian Martin

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3815370

Abstract : This paper studies the restrictions on consumption, portfolio choice, and social discounting implied by a sustainability constraint, that utility should not be expected to decline over time, in an economy with risky investment opportunities. The sustainability constraint does not distort portfolio choice and implies a consumption-wealth ratio and social discount rate that can be considerably higher than the riskless interest rate.

\[5\] Dual Holding and Bank Risk

标题:双重持有与银行风险

作者:Stefano Bonini,Ali Taatian

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3815293

Abstract : We show that a bank with investors holding simultaneously both its equity and bond (dual-holders) exhibits lower risk and superior performance. Using the 2007-9 financial crisis as a quasi-natural experiment, we find that the presence of dual-holders reduces risky investment and risky business portfolio choices. Dual-holders' influence is higher in more opaque banks, indicating that the mechanism of transmission is through a decrease in information asymmetry and a reduction in debtholder-shareholder conflict. This effect translates into higher unconditional and risk-adjusted stock returns. Our results have important normative implications in improving the stability of financial systems.

Keywords : bank risk, dual holdings, financial systems

Abstract :我们发现，同时持有股票和债券（双重持有人）的银行具有较低的风险和较高的绩效。以2007-9年金融危机为准自然实验，我们发现双重持有人的存在降低了风险投资和风险业务组合的选择。在不透明程度较高的银行中，双重持有人的影响更大，表明这种传导机制是通过减少信息不对称和减少债务持有人与股东之间的冲突来实现的。这种效应转化为更高的无条件和风险调整股票回报。我们的研究结果对改善金融体系的稳定性具有重要的规范意义。

Keywords :银行风险，双重持股，金融体系

\[6\] Is non-fungible token pricing driven by cryptocurrencies?

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3815093

Abstract : In early 2021, non-fungible tokens (NFT) became the first application of blockchain technology to achieve clear public prominence. NFTs are tradeable rights to digital assets (images, music, videos, virtual creations) where ownership is recorded in smart contracts on a blockchain. Given the NFT market emerged out of cryptocurrencies, we explore if NFT pricing is related to cryptocurrency pricing. A spillover index shows only limited volatility transmission effects between cryptocurrencies and NFTs. But wavelet coherence analysis indicates co-movement between the two sets of markets. \[7\] Liquidity in the German Corporate Bond Market: Has the CSPP Made a Difference?

标题:德国企业债券市场的流动性：CSPP有什么不同吗？

作者:Lena Boneva,Mevlud Islami,Kathi Schlepper

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3813661

Abstract : The Eurosystem purchased €178 billion of corporate bonds between June 2016 and December 2018 under the Corporate Sector Purchase Programme (CSPP). Did these purchases lead to a deterioration of liquidity conditions in the corporate bond market, thus raising concerns about unintended consequences of large-scale asset purchases? To answer this question, we combine the Bundesbank’s detailed CSPP purchase records with a range of liquidity indicators for both purchased and nonpurchased bonds. We find that while the flow of purchases supported secondary market liquidity, liquidity conditions deteriorated in the long-run as the Bundesbank reduced the stock of corporate bonds available for trading in the secondary market.

Keywords : None

Abstract :2016年6月至2018年12月，欧元体系根据企业部门购买计划（CSPP）购买了1780亿欧元的企业债券。这些购买是否导致公司债券市场流动性状况恶化，从而引发对大规模资产购买意外后果的担忧？为了回答这个问题，我们将德国央行详细的CSPP购买记录与一系列已购买和未购买债券的流动性指标结合起来。我们发现，虽然购买流支持了二级市场的流动性，但从长期来看，随着德国央行减少了可在二级市场交易的公司债券的存量，流动性状况恶化。

\[8\] High-Dimensional Estimation of Quadratic Variation Based on Penalized Realized Variance

标题:基于惩罚实现方差的二次方差高维估计

作者:Kim Christensen,Mikkel Slot Nielsen,Mark Podolskij

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3797963

Abstract : In this paper, we develop a penalized realized variance (PRV) estimator of the quadratic variation (QV) of a high-dimensional continuous Itô semimartingale. We adapt the principle idea of regularization from linear regression to covariance estimation in a continuous-time high-frequency setting. We show that under a nuclear norm penalization, the PRV is computed by soft-thresholding the eigenvalues of realized variance (RV). It therefore encourages sparsity of singular values or, equivalently, low rank of the solution. We prove our estimator is minimax optimal up to a logarithmic factor. We derive a concentration inequality, which reveals that the rank of PRV is-with a high probability-the number of non-negligible eigenvalues of the QV. Moreover, we also provide the associated non-asymptotic analysis for the spot variance. We suggest an intuitive data-driven bootstrap procedure to select the shrinkage parameter. Our theory is supplemented by a simulation study and an empirical application. The PRV detects about three-five factors in the equity market, with a notable rank decrease during times of distress in financial markets. This is consistent with most standard asset pricing models, where a limited amount of systematic factors driving the cross-section of stock returns are perturbed by idiosyncratic errors, rendering the QV-and also RV-of full rank.

Keywords : Bernstein's inequality, LASSO estimation, low rank estimation, quadratic variation, rank recovery, realized variance, shrinkage estimator.

Abstract :本文给出了一个高维连续半鞅二次变差的惩罚实现方差（PRV）估计。我们将线性回归正则化的基本思想应用于连续时间高频环境下的协方差估计。我们证明了在核范数惩罚下，PRV是通过软阈值化实现方差（RV）的特征值来计算的。因此，它鼓励了奇异值的稀疏性，或者等价地，解决方案的低秩。我们证明了我们的估计在对数因子上是minimax最优的。我们导出了一个集中不等式，它揭示了PRV的秩很可能是QV的不可忽略特征值的个数。此外，我们还提供了相关的非渐近分析现货方差。我们建议一个直观的数据驱动引导程序来选择收缩参数。我们的理论得到了模拟研究和实证应用的补充。PRV在股票市场中检测到大约三到五个因素，在金融市场陷入困境时排名显著下降。这与大多数标准的资产定价模型是一致的，在这种模型中，驱动股票收益横截面的有限数量的系统因素受到特质错误的干扰，从而使QV和RV达到满秩。

Keywords :伯恩斯坦不等式，拉索估计，低秩估计，二次变差，秩恢复，实现方差，收缩估计。

\[9\] The Effect of Exogenous Variables on P/E Determinants in the Context of Expected Post COVID-19 Crisis Recovery - The Case of Balkan Capital Markets

标题:后危机复苏背景下外生变量对市盈率决定因素的影响——以巴尔干资本市场为例

作者:Venelin Terziev,Andrey Zahariev,Tsvetan Pavlov,Kaloyan Petkov,Dimitar Kostov

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3814860

Abstract : The paper aims to evaluate the effect of exogenous variables and phenomena on the relation between P/E ratio and its determinants by researching stock market data from the period of recovery after the last big economic crisis from 2008-2010. This is done in an attempt to generate useful insights and lessons about post-crisis market behaviour which will be much needed by investors in the context of the current COVID-19 crisis and expected medium-term recovery. The theoretical methodology of the research is to establish why and how many applications of P/E ratio have increased the importance of studying its determinants. That problem needs the paper to be focused on examining the effect of exogenous factors (macroeconomic variables) on the connection between P/E and its determinants. Our results show that there are significant implications if the effect of these variables is removed. As major findings, even more, there is evidence that exogenous factors are artificially increasing the connection between P/E and the determinants confirmed by in debt analysis with data of emerging Balkan capital markets: Slovenia, Romania, Bulgaria and Croatia, with the addition of Slovakia. The results of the developed methodology and subsequent empirical analysis allow confirming that in the current state of the Balkan capital markets the growing influence of exogenous (macroeconomic) factors change the dependence between the fundamental variables. After clearing the effect of exogenous variables on the determinants (ROE and Net margin), their influence on P/E actually weakens. The reason for this can be determined the high correlation of exogenous factors with the two determinants and with the P/E coefficient. The comparison between the two main tested indicators clearly shows that for the five stock exchanges ROE is qualified as highly correlated and susceptible to the influence of exogenous factors. The Net margin has a higher level of independence.
