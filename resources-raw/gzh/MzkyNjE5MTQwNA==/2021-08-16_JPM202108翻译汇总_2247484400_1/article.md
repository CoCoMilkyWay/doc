\[1\] The Best Strategies for Inflationary Times

标题:应对通胀时代的最佳策略

作者:Henry Neville, Teun Draaisma, Ben Funnell, Campbell R. Harvey and Otto Van Hemert

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/8/8;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.274

Abstract : Over the past three decades, a sustained surge in inflation has been absent in developed markets. As a result, investors face the challenge of having limited experience and no recent data to guide the repositioning of their portfolios in the face of heightened inflation risk. In this article, the authors provide some insight by analyzing both passive and active strategies across a variety of asset classes for the United States, the United Kingdom, and Japan over the past 95 years. Unexpected inflation is bad news for traditional assets, such as bonds and equities, with local inflation having the greatest effect. Commodities have positive returns during inflation surges, but there is considerable variation within the commodity complex. Among the active strategies, the authors find that trend-following provides the most reliable protection during important inflation shocks. Active equity factor strategies also provide some degree of hedging ability. The authors also provide an analysis of alternative asset classes such as fine art and discuss the economic rationale for including cryptocurrencies as part of a strategy to protect against inflation.

TOPICS: Developed markets, financial crises and financial market history, risk management, portfolio management/multi-asset allocation

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors examine a range of passive and active strategies using a century of data in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Japan and find that passive equity and fixed-income strategies fare poorly during inflation surges.

\- ▪ Commodities provide historical inflation protection, as do collectibles such as wine and fine art.

\- ▪ Active strategies such as trend-following fare well during historical inflation surges, and equity factor strategies such as quality provide some protection.

Abstract:在过去三十年中，发达市场没有出现持续的通胀飙升。因此，面对通胀风险加剧，投资者面临的挑战是经验有限，而且没有最新数据来指导其投资组合的重新定位。在这篇文章中，作者通过分析美国、英国和日本在过去95年中各种资产类别的被动和主动策略，提供了一些见解。对于债券和股票等传统资产而言，意外通胀是个坏消息，而地方通胀的影响最大。在通胀飙升期间，大宗商品的回报率为正，但大宗商品综合体内部存在着相当大的差异。在积极的策略中，作者发现趋势跟踪在重要的通货膨胀冲击期间提供了最可靠的保护。积极的权益因素策略也提供了一定程度的对冲能力。作者还对美术等另类资产类别进行了分析，并讨论了将加密货币纳入通胀防范战略的经济原理。

主题：发达市场、金融危机和金融市场历史、风险管理、投资组合管理/多资产配置

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者利用一个世纪以来美国、英国和日本的数据研究了一系列被动和主动策略，发现被动公平和固定收益策略在通胀飙升期间表现不佳。

\- ▪ 商品提供了历史通胀保护，葡萄酒和艺术品等收藏品也是如此。

\- ▪ 在历史通胀飙升期间，趋势跟踪等积极策略表现良好，而质量等权益因素策略提供了一些保护。

In this article, the authors consider the salient challenges associated with ESG investing and how quantitative approaches may address them. \[3\] Tax-Smart Portfolio Valuation and Performance Measurement

标题:税务智能投资组合评估和绩效衡量

作者:Andrew Kalotay

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/8/50;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.256

Abstract : The reported performance of a portfolio is consequential for both investors and managers. One component of the return calculation is the beginning and ending value of the portfolio. The standard in the industry is to calculate portfolio values from the market prices of the constituent securities. Assuming the portfolio can be liquidated at these prices, in the absence of tax considerations, the market value accurately represents the true value of the portfolio, and therefore, the resulting measure of performance is uncontroversial. But what if the portfolio is taxable? Neither the market value nor the liquidation value accurately represents the true worth of a taxable portfolio. However, accurate values are essential to calculating the performance of mutual funds, exchange-traded funds, and separately managed accounts. This is especially true for tax-exempt municipal bond portfolios—interest is tax-free, but capital gains are taxable. The author explores three alternatives to pretax value: liquidation value, hold value, and the larger of these two, defined as tax-smart value. The author recommends the tax-smart value to measure the performance of an actively managed portfolio.

TOPICS: Portfolio construction, legal/regulatory/public policy, mutual funds/passive investing/indexing, fixed income and structured finance

Key Finding

\- ▪ Pretax portfolio value is inappropriate for measuring the true performance of a taxable portfolio.

\- ▪ For actively managed portfolios, we recommend using tax-smart value, which is the greater of after-tax liquidation value and hold value.

\- ▪ For buy-and-hold portfolios, hold value is recommended.

\- ▪ The calculation of the hold value of a tax-exempt bond requires tax-neutral option-adjusted spread analytics.

Abstract:投资组合的业绩报告对投资者和经理人都有重要影响。回报计算的一个组成部分是投资组合的起始值和结束值。行业标准是根据成分证券的市场价格计算投资组合价值。假设投资组合可以按照这些价格进行清算，在没有税务考虑的情况下，市场价值准确地代表了投资组合的真实价值，因此，由此产生的绩效衡量是无争议的。但如果投资组合是应税的呢？市场价值和清算价值都不能准确地代表应税投资组合的真实价值。然而，准确的价值对于计算共同基金、交易所买卖基金和独立管理账户的业绩至关重要。对于免税市政债券投资组合来说尤其如此，利息是免税的，但资本收益是应纳税的。作者探讨了税前价值的三种替代方案：清算价值、持有价值，以及这两种价值中较大的一种，定义为税收智能价值。作者建议使用税务智能价值来衡量积极管理的投资组合的绩效。

主题：投资组合构建、法律/监管/公共政策、共同基金/被动投资/指数化、固定收益和结构性金融

Key Finding

\- ▪ 税前投资组合价值不适用于衡量应税投资组合的真实绩效。

\- ▪ 对于积极管理的投资组合，我们建议使用税务智能价值，即税后清算价值和持有价值中的较大者。

\- ▪ 对于买入并持有投资组合，建议持有价值。

\- ▪ 计算免税债券的持有价值需要税收中性期权调整利差分析。

\[5\] A Market Microstructure View of the Informational Efficiency of Security Prices

标题:证券价格信息效率的市场微观结构分析

作者:Robert A. Schwartz

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/8/75;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.268

Abstract : Do equity prices efficiently reflect fundamental information, as the efficient market hypothesis (EMH) suggests? The author challenges financial academicians’ widely held acceptance of the EMH. In a frictionless environment, information acquisition and trading would be costless, transaction prices would reflect information perfectly, prices would follow random walks, and the EMH would be validated. But markets are not frictionless. Academic research commonly invokes an assumption that informed investors have homogeneous expectations. But, given the enormity and complexity of information sets, investor expectations differ. Taking an intraday, microstructure focus, the author explains that price discovery in a nonfrictionless, divergent-expectations environment is a protracted, dynamic process that accentuates intraday price volatility and introduces return autocorrelations. This counters the EMH. He stresses the importance of instituting a market structure that further enhances the operational, and therefore informational, efficiency of a security market.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, portfolio theory, statistical methods, exchanges/markets/clearinghouses

Key Finding

\- ▪ Given the enormous size and complexity of information sets, equally informed investors form different expectations of stocks’ risks and returns. Consequently, shares do not have unique fundamental values that can be known by research analysts. Rather, prices must be discovered in a marketplace, and the efficiency with which this task is performed depends on how orders are handled and turned into trades (i.e., market structure matters).

\- ▪ In a frictionless, zero-trading-cost market, security prices depend on two factors: risk and return. In a nonfrictionless market, three factors matter: risk, return, and liquidity. Illiquidity is closely associated with price discovery being a noisy, dynamic process.

\- ▪ High-frequency, intraday data show that intraday price volatility is markedly accentuated and that stock returns are autocorrelated (i.e., share prices do not follow random walks). These properties of intraday price formation are manifestations of price discovery being a noisy process, and they explain why share prices can be informationally inefficient.

Abstract:股票价格是否如有效市场假说（EMH）所示有效地反映了基本面信息？作者质疑金融学者对有效市场假说的广泛接受。在无摩擦的环境中，信息获取和交易将是无成本的，交易价格将完美地反映信息，价格将遵循随机游动，有效市场假说将得到验证。但市场并非没有摩擦。学术研究通常援引一种假设，即知情投资者的预期是一致的。但是，考虑到信息集的巨大性和复杂性，投资者的预期有所不同。以日内微观结构焦点为例，作者解释了在无价格、不同预期环境中的价格发现是一个长期的动态过程，它加剧了日内价格波动并引入了收益自相关。这与有效市场假说相反。他强调建立一个市场结构的重要性，该结构可以进一步提高证券市场的运作效率，从而提高信息效率。

主题：证券分析和估值、投资组合理论、统计方法、交易所/市场/清算所

Key Finding

\- ▪ 鉴于信息集的巨大规模和复杂性，同样知情的投资者对股票的风险和回报形成不同的预期。因此，股票没有研究分析师能够知道的独特的基本价值。相反，价格必须在市场中发现，执行这项任务的效率取决于如何处理订单并将其转化为交易（即市场结构问题）。

\- ▪ 在无摩擦、零交易成本的市场中，证券价格取决于两个因素：风险和回报。在非价格市场中，有三个因素很重要：风险、回报和流动性。流动性不足与价格发现密切相关，价格发现是一个嘈杂、动态的过程。

\- ▪ 高频日内数据表明，日内价格波动明显加剧，股票收益率是自相关的（即股价不遵循随机游动）。日内价格形成的这些特性是价格发现是一个嘈杂过程的表现，它们解释了为什么股价在信息上是低效的。

\[6\] Long-Term Investing and the Frequency of Investment Decisions

标题:长期投资和投资决策的频率

作者:Ronald J. M. van Loon

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/8/86;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.262

Abstract : This article analyzes the impact of the frequency of investment decisions on long-term investment results. Long-term investment returns are shown to be a nonlinear function of skill, transaction costs, volatility, and frequency. The author uses compound returns to show the minimum skill level that is required to result in a positive expected return after costs and shows the skill level required to outperform the buy-and-hold investor. These can be high for markets that have high transaction costs per unit of volatility. The optimum frequency of investment decisions is derived as that frequency that maximizes the expected terminal value of wealth. Empirical estimates from the US equity and fixed-income markets show that this optimum frequency is considerably different between these markets, even when the investor has an equal amount of skill in each. The author also shows how long-horizon value-at-risk varies with the frequency of investment decision-making.

TOPICS: Wealth management, equity portfolio management, fixed-income portfolio management, risk management, portfolio construction, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The author derives the success rate that is required to result in a positive expected return after cost and applies it to directional strategies in the US equity and fixed-income markets. For fixed income, the investor requires a success rate of more than 51%, regardless of the frequency of those decisions. For directional strategies in the US equity market, this minimum success rate varies more with frequency, but it is higher than 53%.

\- ▪ The optimum frequency of investment decisions is derived as that frequency that maximizes expected terminal wealth. The article shows that this frequency is equal to skill times volatility over costs, squared. Because neither of these parameters is likely to be constant through time, the optimum frequency of investment decision-making is also likely to change through time, which has important implications for how an investment process is to be structured. Empirical estimates show very different results for different markets.

\- ▪ Even for highly skilled investment processes, downside risk is meaningful when frequency is low. Downside risks are meaningfully lower only for high-skill investment processes at high frequency. This is an important consideration for investors who place high importance on downside risks at long investment horizons.

Abstract:本文分析了投资决策频率对长期投资结果的影响。长期投资回报率是技能、交易成本、波动性和频率的非线性函数。作者使用复合回报率来显示在成本后产生正预期回报所需的最低技能水平，并显示超越买入并持有投资者所需的技能水平。对于每单位波动率具有较高交易成本的市场而言，这可能很高。投资决策的最佳频率是指财富预期终值最大化的频率。来自美国股票和固定收益市场的经验估计表明，这些市场之间的最佳频率差异很大，即使投资者在每一个市场上拥有同等的技能。作者还展示了长期风险价值随投资决策频率的变化。

主题：财富管理、股权投资组合管理、固定收益投资组合管理、风险管理、投资组合构建、绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者推导出了在成本后获得正预期回报所需的成功率，并将其应用于美国股票和固定收益市场的定向策略。对于固定收益，投资者要求成功率超过51%，无论这些决策的频率如何。对于美国股票市场的定向策略，该最低成功率随频率变化较大，但高于53%。

\- ▪ 投资决策的最佳频率是指最大化预期终端财富的频率。文章表明，该频率等于技能乘以成本波动率的平方。由于这两个参数都不可能随时间而保持不变，因此投资决策的最佳频率也可能随时间而变化，这对投资过程的结构具有重要影响。对于不同的市场，经验估计显示出非常不同的结果。

\- ▪ 即使对于高技能的投资流程，当频率较低时，下行风险也是有意义的。下行风险只有在高技能、高频率的投资过程中才会显著降低。对于那些在长期投资期高度重视下行风险的投资者来说，这是一个重要的考虑因素。

\[7\] Hedge Funds and Their Prime Brokers: Favorable IPO Allocations

标题:对冲基金及其主要经纪人：有利的IPO配置

作者:Xiaohui Yang, Hossein B. Kazemi and Mila Getmansky Sherman

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/8/105;https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2021.1.261

Abstract : Do hedge funds receive special treatment from their prime brokers in initial public offering (IPO) allocations?
