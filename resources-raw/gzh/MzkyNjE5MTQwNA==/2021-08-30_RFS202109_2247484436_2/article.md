\[1\] The Economics of the Fed Put

标题:美联储的经济学

作者:Anna Cieslak, Annette Vissing-Jorgensen

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4045/5917640; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa116

Abstract : Since the mid-1990s, negative stock returns comove with downgrades to the Fed’s growth expectations and predict policy accommodations. Textual analysis of FOMC documents reveals that policy makers pay attention to the stock market. The primary mechanism is their concern with the consumption wealth effect, with a secondary role for the market predicting the economy. We find little evidence of the Fed overreacting to the market in an ex post sense (reacting beyond the market’s effect on growth expectations). Although policy makers are aware that the Fed put could induce risk-taking, moral hazard considerations appear not to significantly affect their decision-making ex ante.

Abstract:自20世纪90年代中期以来，负股票回报率伴随着美联储增长预期的下调和政策调整的预测。对联邦公开市场委员会文件的文本分析表明，决策者关注股市。主要机制是他们对消费财富效应的关注，其次是市场对经济的预测作用。我们没有发现美联储事后对市场反应过度的证据（反应超出了市场对增长预期的影响）。尽管决策者意识到美联储的看跌期权可能会引发风险承担，但道德风险考虑似乎不会对他们事前的决策产生重大影响。

\[2\] Kicking the Can Down the Road: Government Interventions in the European Banking Sector

标题:一脚踢开：政府对欧洲银行业的干预

作者:Viral V Acharya, Lea Borchert, Maximilian Jager, Sascha Steffen

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4090/6124371; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab002

Abstract : We analyze government interventions in the eurozone banking sector during the 2008–2009 financial crisis. Using a novel data set, we document that fiscally constrained governments “kicked the can down the road” by providing banks with guarantees instead of fully-fledged recapitalizations. We econometrically address the endogeneity associated with bailout decisions in identifying their consequences. We find that forbearance prompted undercapitalized banks to shift their assets from loans to risky sovereign debt and engage in zombie lending, resulting in weaker credit supply, elevated risk in the banking sector, and, eventually, a greater reliance on liquidity support from the European Central Bank.

Abstract:我们分析了2008-2009年金融危机期间政府对欧元区银行业的干预。我们使用一个新的数据集记录了财政拮据的政府通过向银行提供担保而不是全面的资本重组来“扫地出门”。我们在确定救助决策的后果时，从计量经济学角度探讨了与救助决策相关的内生性。我们发现，忍耐促使资本不足的银行将其资产从贷款转移到高风险主权债务，并参与僵尸贷款，导致信贷供应减弱，银行业风险升高，最终更依赖欧洲央行的流动性支持。

\[3\] Bank Cleanups, Capitalization, and Lending: Evidence from India

标题:银行清理、资本化和贷款：来自印度的证据

作者:Yakshup Chopra, Krishnamurthy Subramanian, Prasanna L Tantri

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4132/5924381; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa119

Abstract : We examine the Indian bank asset quality review, which doubled the declared loan delinquency rate. Relative economic stability during the exercise and the absence of a capital backstop together make it unique. We find that the expected reduction in information asymmetry does not automatically lead to the recapitalization of banks by markets. The consequent undercapitalization leads to underinvestment and risk-shifting through zombie lending. The impact flows to the real economy through borrowers, including shadow banks, and adversely impacts growth. These findings show that bank cleanup exercises not accompanied by policies aimed at recapitalization may be insufficient even during normal times.

Abstract:我们审查了印度银行资产质量审查，该审查将宣布的贷款拖欠率提高了一倍。演习期间经济相对稳定，加上缺乏资金支持，使其独一无二。我们发现，信息不对称的预期减少不会自动导致市场对银行进行资本重组。随之而来的资本不足导致投资不足，并通过僵尸贷款转移风险。影响通过借款人（包括影子银行）流向实体经济，并对增长产生不利影响。这些发现表明，即使在正常时期，没有旨在资本重组的政策配合的银行清理工作也可能是不够的。

\[4\] Demand Effects in the FX Forward Market: Micro Evidence from Banks’ Dollar Hedging

标题:外汇远期市场的需求效应：来自银行美元对冲的微观证据

作者:Abbassi Puriya, Falk Bräuning

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4177/5941473; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa123

Abstract : Using contract-level supervisory data, we show that dollar forward sales by non-U.S. banks that are initiated at the end of a quarter and mature shortly after it concludes trade at higher prices and higher volumes. These effects are driven by banks with large net on-balance-sheet dollar assets that they can hedge around quarter ends by selling dollars forward (increasing off-balance-sheet short positions), which suggests regulatory arbitrage to reduce capital charges for open foreign exchange (FX) exposure. Our results indicate that demand effects related to banks’ management of FX exposure are an important driver of deviations from covered interest rate parity.

Abstract:利用合同层面的监管数据，我们发现非美国银行在季度末开始的美元远期销售，在以更高价格和更高交易量结束交易后不久到期。这些影响是由拥有大量资产负债表内净美元资产的银行推动的，它们可以在季度末左右通过出售美元远期（增加资产负债表外空头头寸）进行对冲，这意味着监管套利可以降低开放式外汇（FX）敞口的资本费用。我们的结果表明，与银行外汇风险管理相关的需求效应是偏离担保利率平价的重要驱动因素。

\[5\] How is Liquidity Priced in Global Markets?

标题:全球市场的流动性如何定价？

作者:Ines Chaieb, Vihang Errunza, Hugues Langlois

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4216/5956731; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa125

Abstract : We develop a new global asset pricing model to study how illiquidity interacts with market segmentation and investability constraints in 42 markets. Noninvestable stocks that can only be held by foreign investors earn higher expected returns compared to freely investable stocks due to limited risk sharing and higher illiquidity. In addition to the world market premium, on average, developed and emerging market noninvestables earn an annual unspanned local market risk premium of |$1.17\\%$| and |$9.04\\%$|⁠, and a liquidity level premium of |$1.06\\%$| and |$2.39\\%$|⁠, respectively. These results obtained in a conditional setup are robust to the choice of liquidity measure.

Abstract:我们开发了一个新的全球资产定价模型，以研究流动性不足如何与42个市场的市场细分和可投资性约束相互作用。由于有限的风险分担和较高的流动性，只能由外国投资者持有的不可投资股票比自由投资股票获得更高的预期回报。除世界市场溢价外，平均而言，发达市场和新兴市场的非投资性资产每年可获得1.17美元和9.04美元的未经规划的本地市场风险溢价$|⁠, 流动性水平溢价为|$1.06\\%$和|$2.39\\%$|⁠, 分别地在条件设置中获得的这些结果对于流动性度量的选择是稳健的。

\[6\] Funding Constraints and Informational Efficiency

标题:资金约束与信息效率

作者:Sergei Glebkin, Naveen Gondhi, John Chi-Fong Kuong

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4269/5981139; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa124

Abstract : We analyze a tractable rational expectations equilibrium model with margin constraints. We argue that constraints affect and are affected by informational efficiency, leading to a novel amplification mechanism. A decline in wealth tightens constraints and reduces investors’ incentive to acquire information, lowering price informativeness. Lower informativeness, in turn, increases the risk borne by financiers who fund trades, leading them to further tighten constraints faced by investors. This information spiral leads to (a) significant increases in risk premium and return volatility in crises, when investors wealth declines, (b) complementarities in information acquisition in crises, and (c) complementarities in margin requirements.

Abstract:我们分析了一个具有边际约束的可处理理性预期均衡模型。我们认为，约束影响并受信息效率的影响，导致了一种新的放大机制。财富的减少收紧了约束，降低了投资者获取信息的动机，降低了价格的信息性。反过来，较低的信息量增加了为交易提供资金的金融家所承担的风险，导致他们进一步收紧投资者面临的约束。这种信息螺旋导致（a）当投资者财富下降时，危机中的风险溢价和回报波动性显著增加，（b）危机中信息获取的互补性，以及（c）保证金要求的互补性。

\[7\] Learning about the Neighborhood

标题:了解邻居

作者:Zhenyu Gao, Michael Sockin, Wei Xiong

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4323/6012372; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa130

Abstract : We develop a model to analyze information aggregation and learning in housing markets. Households enter a neighborhood by buying houses and consuming each other’s final goods. In the presence of pervasive informational frictions, housing prices serve as important signals to households and capital producers about the neighborhood’s economic strength. Our model provides a novel amplification mechanism in which noise from housing markets propagates throughout the local economy via learning because of the complementarity in households’ decisions, distorting migration into the neighborhood and the supply of capital and labor. We provide consistent evidence based on the recent U.S. housing cycle.

Abstract:我们开发了一个模型来分析住房市场中的信息聚合和学习。家庭通过购买房屋和消费彼此的最终产品进入社区。在普遍存在信息摩擦的情况下，房价成为家庭和资本生产者了解社区经济实力的重要信号。我们的模型提供了一种新的放大机制，其中来自住房市场的噪音通过学习在整个地方经济中传播，因为家庭决策的互补性，扭曲了向社区的迁移以及资本和劳动力的供应。我们根据最近的美国住房周期提供了一致的证据。

\[8\] Does Collateral Value Affect Asset Prices? Evidence from a Natural Experiment in Texas

标题:抵押品价值会影响资产价格吗？德克萨斯州自然实验的证据

作者:Albert Alex Zevelev

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4373/5920333; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa117

Abstract : Does the ability to pledge an asset as collateral, after purchase, affect its price? This paper identifies the impact of collateral service flows on house prices, exploiting a plausibly exogenous constitutional amendment in Texas that legalized home equity loans in 1998. The law change increased Texas house prices 4 |$\\%$|⁠; this is price-based evidence that households are credit-constrained and value home equity loans to facilitate consumption smoothing. Prices rose more in locations with inelastic supply, higher prelaw house prices, higher income, and lower unemployment. These estimates reveal that richer households value the option to pledge their home as collateral more strongly.

Abstract:在购买后将资产作为抵押品的能力是否会影响其价格？本文利用德克萨斯州一项看似外生的宪法修正案，确定了抵押服务流对房价的影响，该修正案于1998年将房屋净值贷款合法化。法律变更使德克萨斯州房价上涨了4 |$\\%$|⁠; 这是基于价格的证据，表明家庭受到信贷约束，重视住房净值贷款以促进消费平滑。在供应缺乏弹性、法前房价较高、收入较高和失业率较低的地区，价格上涨幅度更大。这些估计表明，富裕家庭更看重将自己的房子作为抵押品的选择。

\[9\] How Much Information Is Incorporated into Financial Asset Prices? Experimental Evidence

标题:金融资产价格中包含了多少信息？实验证据

作者:Lionel Page, Christoph Siemroth

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4412/6050887; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa143

Abstract : We investigate the informational content of prices in financial asset markets. To do so, we use a large number of market experiments in which the amount of information held by traders is precisely observed. We derive a new method to estimate how much of this information is incorporated into market prices. We find that public information is almost completely reflected in prices but that surprisingly little private information—less than 50%—is incorporated into prices. Our estimates therefore suggest that, while semistrong informational efficiency is consistent with the data, financial market prices may be very far from strong-form efficiency.

Abstract:我们研究了金融资产市场中价格的信息含量。为了做到这一点，我们使用了大量的市场实验，在这些实验中，交易者所持有的信息量得到了精确的观察。我们推导了一种新的方法来估计这些信息中有多少被纳入了市场价格。我们发现，公共信息几乎完全反映在价格中，但令人惊讶的是，只有不到50%的私人信息被纳入价格中。因此，我们的估计表明，虽然半强信息效率与数据一致，但金融市场价格可能远远不是强形式效率。

\[10\] Experience Does Not Eliminate Bubbles: Experimental Evidence

标题:经验不能消除泡沫：实验证据

作者:Anita Kopányi-Peuker, Matthias Weber

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4450/5926134; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa121

Abstract : We study the role of investor experience in the formation of asset price bubbles. We conduct a call market experiment in which participants trade assets with each other and a learning-to-forecast experiment in which participants only forecast future prices (while trade based on these forecasts is computerized). Each experiment comprises three treatments varying the information that participants receive about the fundamental value. Each experimental market is repeated three times. Throughout, we observe sizable bubbles that persist despite participant experience. Our findings in the call market experiment contrast with those in the literature. Our findings in the learning-to-forecast experiment are novel.

Abstract:我们研究了投资者经验在资产价格泡沫形成中的作用。我们进行了一个叫价市场实验，参与者相互交易资产，以及一个学习预测实验，参与者只预测未来价格（而基于这些预测的交易是计算机化的）。每个实验包括三种不同的处理方法，以改变参与者收到的关于基本值的信息。每个实验市场重复三次。自始至终，我们观察到尽管有参与者的经验，但仍存在相当大的泡沫。我们在电话市场实验中的发现与文献中的结果形成了对比。我们在学习预测实验中的发现是新颖的。

\[11\] User Interface and Firsthand Experience in Retail Investing

标题:零售投资的用户界面和第一手经验

作者:Li Liao, Zhengwei Wang, Jia Xiang, Hongjun Yan, Jun Yang

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4486/5962040; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa127

Abstract : Using data from a major online peer-to-peer lending platform, we document that, due to time pressure, investors appear to focus on interest rates and only partially account for credit ratings in their decisions. The effect is stronger for mobile-based investors than for PC-based ones. Our evidence suggests that this variation is caused by the difference in information content on the interfaces rather than differences in the devices’ physical attributes per se. Investors improve their decisions by slowing down and paying more attention to credit ratings after experiencing a loan default firsthand, but not after observing others experiencing defaults.

Abstract:利用一个主要的在线点对点贷款平台的数据，我们证明，由于时间压力，投资者似乎将注意力集中在利率上，并且在他们的决策中仅部分考虑信用评级。对于基于移动设备的投资者来说，这种影响比基于个人电脑的投资者更大。我们的证据表明，这种变化是由界面上信息内容的差异而不是设备物理属性本身的差异造成的。在亲身经历贷款违约后，而不是在观察其他人违约后，投资者会放慢脚步，更加关注信用评级，从而改善他们的决策。

\[12\] Household Portfolio Underdiversification and Probability Weighting: Evidence from the Field

标题:家庭投资组合多样化不足和概率加权：来自实地的证据

作者:Stephen G Dimmock, Roy Kouwenberg, Olivia S Mitchell, Kim Peijnenburg

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/9/4524/6012373; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa131

Abstract : We test whether probability weighting affects household portfolio choice in a representative survey. On average, people display inverse-S-shaped probability weighting, overweighting low probability events. As theory predicts, probability weighting is positively associated with portfolio underdiversification and significant Sharpe ratio losses. Analyzing respondents’ individual stock holdings, we find higher probability weighting is associated with owning lottery-type stocks and positively skewed equity portfolios. People with higher probability weighting are less likely to own mutual funds and more likely to either avoid equities or hold individual stocks. We are the first to empirically link individuals’ elicited probability weighting and real-world decisions under risk.

Abstract:我们在代表性调查中检验概率权重是否影响家庭投资组合选择。平均而言，人们表现出倒S型概率权重，对低概率事件的权重过高。正如理论预测的那样，概率权重与投资组合多样化不足和显著的夏普比率损失正相关。通过分析受访者的个人持股情况，我们发现更高的概率权重与拥有彩票类股票和正偏斜的股票投资组合相关。概率权重越高的人持有共同基金的可能性越小，而更可能回避股票或持有个人股票。我们是第一个根据经验将个人的概率权重与现实世界中的风险决策联系起来的人。
