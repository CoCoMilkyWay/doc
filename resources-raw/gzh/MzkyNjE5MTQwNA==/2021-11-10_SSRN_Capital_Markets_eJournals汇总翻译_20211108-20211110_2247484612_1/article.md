\[2\] Unjust and Unreasonable: Misuse of the Dividend Growth Model in Public Utility Rate Setting

标题:不公正与不合理：公用事业费率设定中股利增长模型的误用

作者:J.B. Heaton

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3947360

Abstract : The dividend (Gordon) growth model (DGM) is a simple stock valuation equation in four variables: (1) price; (2) next-period cash flow; (3) a perpetual and constant rate of growth in the periodic cash flow; and (4) a perpetually-constant discount rate. The DGM can be expressed to equate the perpetually-constant discount rate to the cash flow yield on the current price plus the perpetual growth rate in the periodic cash flow. But that expression would provide a reliable estimate of a real-world discount rate only if both the cash flow yield and the growth rate used as inputs to the model were perpetually constant. In rate-regulation proceedings, however, utility expert witnesses use the DGM with current (not perpetual) dividend yields and analysts' 3-5 year (not perpetual) earnings growth rates. This practice contributes to approved equity cost of capital awards above plausible cost of capital estimates, shifting wealth from rate payers to utilities.

Keywords : cost of capital, rate regulation, regulatory capture

Abstract :股利（Gordon）增长模型（DGM）是一个包含四个变量的简单股票估值方程：（1）价格；（2） 下一期现金流量；（3） 周期性现金流的永久不变增长率；以及（4）永久不变的贴现率。DGM可以表示为永久不变贴现率等于当前价格的现金流收益率加上周期现金流的永久增长率。但是，只有当作为模型输入的现金流收益率和增长率永远不变时，该表达式才能提供真实世界贴现率的可靠估计。然而，在利率监管程序中，公用事业专家证人将DGM与当前（非永久）股息收益率和分析师的3-5年（非永久）收益增长率一起使用。这种做法有助于批准资本奖励的股本成本高于合理的资本估算成本，从而将财富从费率支付者转移到公用事业。

Keywords :资本成本、利率监管、监管捕获

\[3\] Do Images Provide Relevant Information to Investors? An Exploratory Study

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3954219

Abstract : We develop novel machine learning algorithms to construct metrics of visual readability in firms' annual reports. Firms increase their use of imagery content when there is more news coverage and greater asset growth during the year. Consistent with improvements in the information environment, an increase in the use of imagery content is associated with lower risk, lower cost of capital, and higher bond ratings during the subsequent year. \[4\] Payment Risk and Bank Lending

标题:支付风险与银行贷款

作者:Ye Li,Yi Li

出处:Fisher College of Business Working Paper No. 2021-03-017Charles A. Dice Center Working Paper No. 2021-17

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3959110

Abstract : Deposits finance bank lending and serve as means of payment for bank customers. Under uncertain payment flows, deposits are debts with random maturities. Payment outflows drain reserves, and the risk is most prominent when funding markets are under stress and banks are unable to smooth out payment shocks. We provide the first evidence on the negative impact of payment risk on bank lending, bridging the literatures on payment systems and credit supply. An interquartile increase in payment risk is associated with a decline in loan growth rate that is 10% of standard deviation. Our findings are stronger in times of funding stress and robust across banks of different sizes and loans of long and short maturities. Banks with higher payment risk raise deposit rates to expand customer base and internalize payment flows. Finally, we show that payment risk dampens the bank lending channel of monetary policy transmission.

Keywords : Credit supply, deposits, payment, inside money, monetary policy transmission

Abstract :存款为银行贷款提供资金，并作为银行客户的支付手段。在支付流不确定的情况下，存款是随机到期的债务。支付外流耗尽了准备金，当融资市场处于压力之下，银行无法消除支付冲击时，风险最为突出。我们提供了关于支付风险对银行贷款的负面影响的第一个证据，填补了有关支付系统和信贷供应的文献。支付风险的四分位间增加与贷款增长率下降相关，贷款增长率为标准差的10%。我们的研究结果在资金紧张时期更为有力，在不同规模的银行和长短期贷款中表现强劲。支付风险较高的银行提高存款利率，以扩大客户基础并将支付流内部化。最后，我们证明了支付风险抑制了货币政策传导的银行贷款渠道。

Keywords :信贷供给、存款、支付、内部货币、货币政策传导

\[6\] Audit Partner Individualism and Financial Reporting Comparability: Evidence from the U.S.

标题:审计合伙人个人主义与财务报告可比性：来自美国的证据。

作者:Young Hoon Kim,Yinghua Li,Dechun Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3956856

Abstract : In this study, we examine whether audit partner individualism affects financial reporting comparability in the U.S. Drawing upon theories in psychology and economics, we argue that individualistic audit partners are more inclined to deviate from peers in interpreting and applying their firms’ audit methodologies, thereby resulting in lower financial reporting comparability of their clients. Using uncommonness of first names to measure partner-specific individualism and a company-pair design, our empirical analyses provide supportive evidence. Specifically, total accruals, current accruals, and abnormal accruals are less comparable between a company audited by a partner with an uncommon name and a company audited by a partner with a common name, relative to a pair of companies that are both audited by partners with common names. Results are robust to the use of an alternative measure of audit partner individualism, a company-level analysis, and a battery of other sensitivity tests. Our study provides novel company-level evidence on the role of audit partner individualism in corporate financial comparability.

Keywords : Earnings comparability, Individualist culture, Audit partner uncommon names, Audit partner individualism

Abstract :在本研究中，我们考察了审计合伙人个人主义是否影响美国财务报告的可比性。根据心理学和经济学的理论，我们认为，个人主义审计合伙人在解释和应用其公司的审计方法时更倾向于偏离同行，从而导致其客户的财务报告可比性降低。使用名字的不寻常性来衡量合作伙伴特有的个人主义和公司配对设计，我们的实证分析提供了支持性证据。具体而言，与一对同时由同名合伙人审计的公司相比，由同名合伙人审计的公司与由同名合伙人审计的公司之间的总应计项目、当前应计项目和异常应计项目的可比性较差。结果对于审计合作伙伴个人主义的替代措施、公司层面的分析和一系列其他敏感性测试的使用是可靠的。我们的研究为审计伙伴个人主义在公司财务可比性中的作用提供了新的公司层面证据。

\[7\] Effect of stock basket trading on the liquidity of underlying stocks: Evidence from a market with short-sale constraints

标题:股票篮子交易对标的股票流动性的影响：来自卖空约束市场的证据

作者:Son Duy Pham,Hung Xuan Do,Thao T.T. Nguyen

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3958457

Abstract : The “adverse selection” hypothesis expects that the introduction of index futures trading will decrease the liquidity of index component stocks as liquidity-motivated traders migrate to futures trading. Conversely, the “index arbitrage” hypothesis predicts that index arbitrage activity could enhance the liquidity of component stocks. Using the difference-in-difference approach with carefully matched stocks, we find both the bid-ask spread and the Amihud illiquidity of the VN30’s component stocks increase after the advent of index futures trading. The probability of informed trading of these stocks jumps in the post-futures period, consistent with the “adverse selection” hypothesis. The prevalence of futures under-pricing indicates a lack of index arbitrage, which could be explained by the short-sale prohibition in the Vietnamese stock market.

Keywords : Liquidity, Future, Mispricing, PIN, Short-sale constraints

Abstract :“逆向选择”假设预计，随着流动性驱动的交易者转向期货交易，指数期货交易的引入将降低指数成份股的流动性。相反，“指数套利”假说预测，指数套利活动可以提高组成股票的流动性。通过对仔细匹配的股票使用差分法，我们发现在指数期货交易出现后，VN30成份股的买卖价差和Amihud流动性不足都有所增加。这些股票的知情交易概率在后期货期内大幅上升，符合“逆向选择”假设。期货价格低估的盛行表明缺乏指数套利，这可以用越南股市禁止卖空来解释。

Keywords :流动性、未来、错误定价、PIN、卖空约束

出处:-

Abstract : Even though a multi-factor linear asset pricing model can be equivalently represented in a Beta or in a stochastic discount factor (SDF) form, its inferential efficiency and pricing accuracy features may differ when estimated by the generalized method of moments (GMM), both in small and in large samples. Abstract : To enforce insider trading laws, financial regulators require top executives in listed companies to make their own-company trades public. \[11\] Measuring the dynamic lead-lag relationship between the cash market and stock index futures market

标题:现货市场与股指期货市场动态超前滞后关系的测度

作者:Chaoqun Ma,Ru Xiao,Xianhua Mi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3956936

Abstract : From the view of high frequency, this paper develops three new nonparametric and nonlinear measurements for the lead-lag relationship between the stock index future and its spot index based on dynamic time warping algorithm: a point measurement and two interval measurements. As the name implies, point measurement can estimate lead-lag times for the data point at high frequency (e.g, one-minute), while interval measurement is used to estimate the average lead-lag times for the interval (e.g, a day). The simulation experiment shows great performance of our measurements.
