\[1\] Reaction of The Indian Stock Market to Corona Pandemic and Lockdown

标题:印度股市对电晕大流行和封锁的反应

作者:Dhananjay Ashri,Bibhu Prasad Sahoo

出处:JIMS8M: The Journal of Indian Management & Strategy, 25 (4), 13-22. https://doi.org/10.5958/0973-9343.2020.00028.9

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3864430

Abstract : Purpose: The present paper tries to probe the COVID-19 breakout effect on the stock markets by drawing shreds of evidence from India.Design/methodology/approach: Secondary data has been used and retrieved from the BSE and NSE portal in this study. The study covered the period July 1, 2019, to March 11, 2020, as "pre-COVID period" and March 12, 2020, to July 9, 2020, as "post-COVID period." Welch's t-test, popularly known as unequal variance t-test, and non-parametric Mann-Whitney U test areused to compare the opening, highest, lowest, and closing prices across the two periods of the major stock indices; Nifty 50, Nifty 500, Nifty 100, Nifty Midcap 150, S&P BSE Sensex, S&P BSE 500, S&P BSE Midcap, and S&P BSE All Cap. We further extended our study by employing a multiple regression model for the post-COVID period to identify the repercussions of thespread of the virus and lockdown on the stock indices' prices.Findings: Both Welch's t-test and Mann-Whitney U-test indicated a significant (1% significance level) negative impact of the Covid-19 pandemic on the Indian stock market. Multiple regression model confirmed a significant (significant at 1%) positive effect of a rise in the COVID-19 cases on the stock market, and COVID-19 related deaths do not significantly impact even at 10% level. Furthermore, the results revealed a significant (at 1% level of significance) fall in the prices due to an increase in the lockdown days.Originality/value: These outcomes contribute to the literature by looking at Coronavirus's economic consequences by drawing evidence from the Indian stock market. This study provides insights into the policymakers and corporates into the economic repercussions of COVID-19.

Keywords : COVID-19, Stock market, pandemic

Abstract :目的：本研究试图从印度获取大量证据，探讨COVID-19突围效应对股市的影响。设计/方法/途径：本研究采用BSE和NSE门户网站的二级数据。这项研究涵盖了2019年7月1日至2020年3月11日这段时间，称为“前COVID期”，2020年3月12日至2020年7月9日这段时间，称为“后COVID期”。Welch的t检验（俗称不等方差t检验）和非参数Mann-Whitney U检验用于比较期初、最高、最低、，主要股指两个时期的收盘价；Nifty 50、Nifty 500、Nifty 100、Nifty Midcap 150、标准普尔BSE Sensex、标准普尔BSE 500、标准普尔BSE Midcap和标准普尔BSE All Cap。我们进一步扩展了我们的研究，采用一个多重回归模型来确定病毒传播和股票指数价格锁定的影响印度股市。多元回归模型证实，COVID-19病例的增加对股市有显著的正效应（显著性为1%），COVID-19相关死亡即使在10%的水平上也没有显著影响。此外，研究结果显示，由于禁售日的增加，价格出现了显著（1%的显著性水平）下跌。原创性/价值：这些结果通过从印度股市中提取证据来研究冠状病毒的经济后果，为文献提供了依据。这项研究提供了政策制定者和企业对COVID-19的经济影响的见解。

\[2\] A Note on Tweeting and Equity Markets before and during the COVID-19 Pandemic

标题:关于COVID-19大流行前后tweet和股票市场的说明

作者:Ujjal Chatterjee,Joseph J. French

出处:Finance Research Letters, Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3863347

Abstract : We investigate the differential effects of a new index of Twitter-based market uncertainty (TMU) and variables for the US equity market before and during the COVID-19 pandemic. We find that markets are significantly more sensitive to the uncertainty contained in tweets during the pandemic, the TMU is a leading indicator of returns only during the pandemic, and the effect of the TMU on the volatility and liquidity of equity markets is greater during the pandemic compared to the pre-pandemic period. Our results show that the information contained tweets are having a much larger effect on equity markets during the pandemic.

Keywords : COVID-19, Twitter, Tweets, Pandemic, Uncertainty, Equity Return Predictability

Abstract :我们研究了一个新的基于Twitter的市场不确定性指数（TMU）和变量在COVID-19大流行之前和期间对美国股市的不同影响。我们发现，在流感大流行期间，市场对tweet中包含的不确定性更为敏感，TMU只是流感大流行期间回报的领先指标，流感大流行期间TMU对股市波动性和流动性的影响比流感大流行前更大。我们的研究结果显示，在流感大流行期间，tweet所包含的信息对股市的影响要大得多。

Keywords :COVID-19、Twitter、Tweets、流行病、不确定性、股票回报可预测性

\[3\] Planning in Financial Markets in Presence of Spikes: Using Machine Learning GBDT

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3862428

Abstract : Planning in financial markets is a difficult task as the method needs to dramatically change its behavior when facing very rare black swan events like crises that shift market regime. In order to address this challenge, we present a gradient boosting decision trees (GBDT) approach to predict large price drops in equity indexes from a set of 150 technical, fundamental and macroeconomic features. We report an improved accu-racy of GBDT over other machine learning (ML) methods on the S&P 500 futures prices. We show that retaining fewer and carefully selected features provides improvements across all ML approaches. We show that this model has a strong predic-tive power. \[4\] Time is Money: The Equilibrium Trading Horizon and Optimal Arrival Price

标题:时间就是金钱：均衡交易视界与最优到达价格

作者:Kevin Darby

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3862181

Abstract : Executing even moderately large derivatives orders can be expensive and risky; it’s hard to balance the uncertainty of working an order over time versus paying a liquidity premium for immediate execution. Here, we introduce the Time Is Money model, which calculates the Equilibrium Trading Horizon over which to execute an order within the adversarial forces of variance risk and liquidity premium. We construct a hypothetical at-the-money option within Arithmetic Brownian Motion and invert the Bachelier model to compute an inflection point between implied variance and liquidity cost as governed by a central limit order book, each in real time as they evolve. As a result, we demonstrate a novel, continuous-time Arrival Price framework. Further, we argue that traders should be indifferent to choosing between variance risk and liquidity cost, unless they have a predetermined bias or an exogenous position with a convex payoff. We, therefore, introduce half-life factor asymptotics to the model based on a convexity factor and compare results to existing models. We also describe a specialization of the model for trading a basket of correlated instruments, as exemplified by a futures calendar spread. Finally, we establish groundwork for microstructure optimizations as well as explore short term drift and conditional expected slippage within the Equilibrium Horizon framework.

Keywords : derivatives, execution, equilibrium, arrival price, volatility

Abstract :执行即使是中等规模的衍生品订单也可能是昂贵和有风险的；很难在一段时间内处理订单的不确定性与为立即执行支付流动性溢价之间取得平衡。在这里，我们引入时间即货币模型，它计算了在方差风险和流动性溢价的对抗力量中执行订单的均衡交易期限。我们在算术布朗运动中构造了一个假设的货币期权，并反转Bachelier模型来计算隐含方差和流动性成本之间的拐点，这两个拐点由一个中央限额指令簿控制，每一个都随着它们的发展而实时变化。因此，我们展示了一个新颖的，连续时间到达价格框架。此外，我们认为交易者应该对方差风险和流动性成本之间的选择漠不关心，除非他们有一个预先确定的偏差或一个具有凸收益的外生头寸。因此，我们将半衰期因子渐近性引入到基于凸性因子的模型中，并将结果与现有模型进行比较。我们还描述了一篮子相关工具交易模型的特殊化，以期货日历价差为例。最后，我们建立了微观结构优化的基础，并探讨了平衡层框架内的短期漂移和条件预期滑移。

Keywords :衍生品，执行，均衡，到达价格，波动性

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3861980

Abstract : We study the valuation of callable barrier reverse convertible contracts written on one or two underlying asset prices. We assume the issuer of the contract can call early redemption at any time during a pre-specified time interval. We identify the optimal redemption policy and show, in the single underlying asset case, it is characterized by a time-dependent boundary. \[6\] Order Flow Fragmentation and Flight-To-Transparency during Stressed Market Conditions: Evidence from COVID-19

标题:压力市场条件下的订单流碎片化与透明化：来自COVID-19的证据

作者:Giulio Anselmi,Giovanni Petrella,Mahendrarajah Nimalendran

出处:Finance Research Letters, Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3864104

Abstract : The proliferation of trading venues has resulted in a fragmented secondary markets landscape both in the US and Europe. Different factors drive the fragmentation of order flow during normal market conditions. This paper studies the composition of order flow during stressed market conditions based on the COVID-19 outbreak. We specifically investigate two related questions. Firstly, we assess whether stressed market conditions alter the level of fragmentation and, secondly, we study the choice of lit vs. dark trading when markets are under stress. As for the first question, we construct a measure to capture order flow fragmentation and find that fragmentation strongly decreases when markets are under stress (i.e., traders concentrate their order flow in fewer venues in times of market stress). This is especially the case for stocks experiencing a deeper shock in volatility. As for the second question, we find a migration of order flow from dark to lit venues when markets are under stress. Our interpretation of this finding is that traders tend to move to lit venues, where the probability of trading with an informed trader is less severe, during times of hyper-volatility. We call this evidence ’flight-to-transparency’.

Keywords : COVID-19, Fragmentation, Dark trading, Stressed market conditions

Abstract :交易场所的激增导致美国和欧洲的二级市场格局支离破碎。在正常的市场条件下，不同的因素会导致订单流的分散。本文以COVID-19爆发为例，研究了市场压力条件下订单流的构成。我们专门研究了两个相关的问题。首先，我们评估了压力市场条件是否会改变市场的分散程度，其次，我们研究了当市场处于压力之下时，亮交易和暗交易的选择。对于第一个问题，我们构造了一个测度来捕捉订单流的碎片化，并发现当市场处于压力下时（即，在市场压力时期，交易员将订单流集中在较少的场所），碎片化显著降低。这对于波动性冲击更大的股票尤其如此。至于第二个问题，我们发现，当市场处于压力之下时，订单流会从黑暗场所转移到光明场所。我们对这一发现的解释是，交易者倾向于在剧烈波动的时候，转移到lit场所，在那里，与知情交易者进行交易的概率较低。我们称这一证据为“逃避透明度”。

Keywords :COVID-19，分散，黑暗交易，压力市场条件

\[7\] Everywhere Differentiable Continuums, Mathematical Spaces, and Axioms: Modeling of Financial Phenomena

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3861264

Abstract : Formal theoretical proofs show the statement, \`modeling of stock returns on everywhere differentiable continuums, or using twice continuously differentiable functions, is inappropriate to modeling of rational expectations' always is true, as such, has characterization as an Axiom. Using same mathematical property, the statement, \`modeling of stock returns in discrete time is robust to modeling of each of rational expectations, or feasibility of deviations from rational expectations' also is shown always to be true. Formal proofs show bane of modeling in continuous time is non-dichotomization of connectedness property of stock prices from evolution of stock prices, a structure at variance with the mathematical norm that connectedness properties for topologies be independent of specific elements that are located in topological spaces. Consistent with its robustness, changes to information sets (concept, not realizations) are connectedness properties of discrete time modeling and there is arrival at dichotomous sufficiency conditions for conformance with, or deviation of priors from rational expectations equilibriums. In aggregate, while assumptions of \`everywhere differentiability' or \`twice continuously differentiable functions' induce positive relations between risk (volatility) and returns, contrary to rational expectations, it is relatively low realizations for volatility that, rather unavoidably, are associated with either of positive or negative returns.

Abstract :形式化的理论证明表明，“在处处可微连续统上建立股票收益模型，或用两次连续可微函数建立股票收益模型，不适用于理性预期的建模”这一命题始终是正确的，因而具有公理化的特征。利用同样的数学性质，“离散时间内股票收益的建模对每个理性预期的建模是稳健的，或偏离理性预期的可行性”这句话也总是正确的。形式证明表明，连续时间建模的祸根是股票价格演化过程中股票价格连通性的非二分法，这种结构与拓扑的连通性独立于拓扑空间中特定元素的数学规范不一致。与它的鲁棒性相一致，信息集的变化（概念，而不是实现）是离散时间建模的连通性性质，并且存在符合或偏离理性期望均衡的二分充分条件。总的来说，虽然“处处可微”或“两次连续可微函数”的假设在风险（波动性）和收益之间产生了正的关系，但与理性预期相反，波动性的实现相对较低，不可避免地与正收益或负收益相关联。

Keywords :关联性，理性预期，一般均衡，股票收益，股票价格，公理

\[9\] Behavioral Biases in the NFL Gambling Market: Overreaction to News and the Recency Bias

标题:NFL赌博市场中的行为偏差：对新闻的过度反应和近期偏差

作者:Robert B. B. Durand,Fernando Patterson,Corey A. Shank

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3861231

Abstract : This paper examines the recency bias and overreaction in the NFL betting market from 2003 to 2017. Consistent with the recency bias, bettors are more likely to bet on teams who have won previous outcomes. We add to the literature and find that the magnitude of prior wins and losses in the previous weeks' plays a greater importance than the sole outcome of wins and losses in betting behavior. Additionally, our results show that bettors wager 2.1% less on the home team when their first-string quarterback does not play, and 3.1% more on the home team when the visitor's first-string quarterback does not play, which is consistent with overreaction.
