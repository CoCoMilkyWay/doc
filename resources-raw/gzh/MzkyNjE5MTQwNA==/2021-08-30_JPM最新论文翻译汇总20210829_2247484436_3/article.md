\[1\] Measuring and Managing ESG Risks in Sovereign Bond Portfolios and Implications for Sovereign Debt Investing

标题:衡量和管理主权债券投资组合中的ESG风险以及对主权债务投资的影响

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Novel Risks 2021, jpm.2021.1.290

作者:Lionel Martellini and Lou-Salomé Vallée

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/08/24/jpm.2021.1.290;

Abstract : This article shows that implementation choices matter with respect to how environment, social, and governance (ESG) constraints are incorporated in sovereign bond portfolio construction. In particular, the authors confirm that negative screening leads to more diversified portfolios and lower levels of tracking error, whereas positive screening leads to higher levels of improvement of ESG scores, at the cost of an increase in absolute and relative risk budgets. The authors also find that a dedicated focus on absolute or relative risk reduction at the selection stage allows investors to reduce the opportunity costs along the dimension that is most important to them. Overall, the results suggest that sound risk management practices are critically important in allowing investors to incorporate ESG constraints in investment decisions at an acceptable cost in terms of dollar or risk budgets.

TOPICS: ESG investing, fixed income and structured finance, global markets, portfolio construction

Key Finding

\- ▪ Higher environmental scores for developed countries and higher social scores for emerging countries are associated with lower costs of borrowing for issuers and consequently with lower yields for investors.

\- ▪ A minimum variance optimization approach leads in general to better performance compared to a negative screening strategy for the same level of E, S, and G score improvements.

\- ▪ ESG momentum strategies generate additional value, suggesting the presence of some form of underreaction to news related to changes in ESG scores.

Abstract:本文表明，实施选择与环境、社会和治理（ESG）约束如何纳入主权债券投资组合构建有关。特别是，作者确认，负面筛查导致投资组合更加多样化，跟踪误差水平更低，而正面筛查导致ESG分数更高水平的改善，但代价是绝对和相对风险预算的增加。作者还发现，在选择阶段专注于绝对或相对风险降低，可以让投资者在对他们最重要的维度上降低机会成本。总体而言，结果表明，健全的风险管理实践对于允许投资者以可接受的美元或风险预算成本将ESG约束纳入投资决策至关重要。

主题：ESG投资、固定收益和结构性金融、全球市场、投资组合构建

Key Finding

\- ▪ 发达国家的环境分数越高，新兴国家的社会分数越高，发行人的借贷成本越低，因此投资者的收益率越低。

\- ▪ 对于相同水平的E、S和G分数改进，与消极筛选策略相比，最小方差优化方法通常会带来更好的性能

\- ▪ ESG动量策略产生附加值，表明存在某种形式的对ESG分数变化相关新闻的反应不足。

\[2\] Investment Performance of US High-Yield Real Estate Debt: An Empirical Analysis

标题:美国高收益房地产债务投资绩效的实证分析

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Real Estate 2021, jpm.2021.1.269

作者:Michael Giliberto

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/08/23/jpm.2021.1.269;

Abstract : This article uses a multimanager sample of loan-level data to assess the performance of commercial real estate (CRE) subordinate debt from 2010 through 2020. The author compares its performance to various real estate alternatives, including senior mortgage loans and real estate equity. Risk-return trade-offs within CRE subordinate debt are examined using a subsample of loans that originated and were fully realized during the sample period.

TOPICS: CMBS and commercial mortgage loans, performance measurement, risk management, financial crises and financial market history

Key Finding

\- ▪ Over the sample period, average returns from subordinate CRE debt were higher than those produced by senior loans and lower than what real estate equity generated. Income was the main driver of return achieved by subordinate debt.

\- ▪ Investors were, on average, compensated for taking on both greater financial risk (higher position within a property’s capital stack) and greater real estate risk (e.g., value-add vs. stabilized properties).

\- ▪ During the sample period, loan defaults were infrequent. However, high loss severity caused meaningful effects on composite or portfolio outcomes.

Abstract:本文使用贷款水平数据的多经理人样本来评估商业房地产（CRE）次级债务从2010年到2020年的表现。作者将其表现与各种房地产替代方案进行比较，包括高级抵押贷款和房地产权益。CRE次级债务中的风险回报权衡使用在样本期内产生并完全实现的贷款的子样本进行检查。

主题：CMBS和商业抵押贷款、绩效衡量、风险管理、金融危机和金融市场历史

Key Finding

\- ▪ 在样本期间，次级CRE债务的平均回报高于高级贷款，低于房地产权益产生的回报。收入是次级债务回报的主要驱动力。

\- ▪ 平均而言，投资者因承担更大的金融风险（在房地产资本堆栈中的地位更高）和更大的房地产风险（例如增值房地产与稳定房地产）而获得补偿。

\- ▪ 在样本期内，贷款违约很少发生。然而，高损失严重性对组合或投资组合结果产生了有意义的影响。

\[4\] Private Equity Real Estate Fund Performance: A Comparison to REITs and Open-End Core Funds

标题:私募股权房地产基金业绩：与REITs和开放式核心基金的比较

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Real Estate 2021, jpm.2021.1.276

作者:Thomas R. Arnold, David C. Ling and Andy Naranjo

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/08/23/jpm.2021.1.276;

Abstract : We provide a comprehensive examination of the return performance of closed-end, private equity real estate (PERE) funds relative to the performance of listed real estate stocks (real estate investment trusts \[REITs\]) and the NCREIF ODCE fund index. We first match each PERE fund in our sample and its realized internal rate of return and equity multiple with the return that would have been earned by an LP investor on an investment in the designated benchmark over each fund’s investment horizon. Overall, we find that closed-end PERE funds have underperformed listed REITs. In contrast, we find similar overall performance between PERE and the NCREIF ODCE fund index. We also examine the determinants of the relative performance spread between the PERE funds and the equity REIT index and find that the spread widens with interest rate environment variables (Treasury yields and default spreads) and narrows with broad macroeconomic performance indicators (growth rate of GDP).

TOPICS: Real assets/alternative investments/private equity, real estate, private equity, exchange-traded funds and applications, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ Closed-end PERE funds underperform listed REITs—both on average and by the percentages of individual funds.

\- ▪ The performance spread widens with interest rate environment variables (Treasury yields and default spreads) and narrows with broad macroeconomic performance indicators (growth rate of GDP).

\- ▪ The overall performance between PERE and the NCREIF ODCE fund index is similar.

Abstract:我们对封闭式私募股权房地产（PERE）基金相对于上市房地产股票（房地产投资信托基金\[REIT\]）和NCREIF ODCE基金指数的回报表现进行了全面检查。我们首先将样本中的每只PERE基金及其实现的内部收益率和股权倍数与LP投资者在每只基金的投资期限内对指定基准进行投资所获得的回报进行匹配。总体而言，我们发现封闭式PERE基金的表现低于上市REIT。相比之下，我们发现PERE和NCREIF ODCE基金指数的总体表现相似。我们还研究了PERE基金和股票REIT指数之间相对绩效差的决定因素，发现随着利率环境变量（国债收益率和违约差）的增加，相对绩效差扩大，随着宏观经济绩效指标（GDP增长率）的增加，相对绩效差缩小。

主题：房地产/另类投资/私募股权、房地产、私募股权、交易所买卖基金和应用、绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 封闭式PERE基金的平均业绩和单个基金的百分比均低于上市REIT。

\- ▪ 绩效利差随利率环境变量（国债收益率和违约利差）而扩大，随宏观经济绩效指标（GDP增长率）而缩小。

\- ▪ PERE和NCREIF ODCE基金指数之间的总体表现相似。

\[5\] Firm-Level Cybersecurity Risk and Idiosyncratic Volatility

标题:企业层面的网络安全风险和特质波动

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Novel Risks 2021, jpm.2021.1.286

作者:Nazli Sila Alan, Ahmet K. Karagozoglu and Tianpeng Zhou

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/08/21/jpm.2021.1.286;

Abstract : The authors propose a measure of firm-level cybersecurity risk developed by employing pattern-based sequence-classification method from computational linguistics to determine the proportion of time devoted to issues related to cybersecurity risk during earnings conference calls. Using their measure, they investigate the effect of cybersecurity risk on firm-level return volatility; they examine both idiosyncratic volatility and implied volatility and find that firm-level cybersecurity risk is positively correlated to idiosyncratic volatility on the days on which earnings calls are held. This suggests that the discussion of issues related to cybersecurity risk during earnings calls is related to an increase in the component of the volatility that responds only to firm-specific news. That positive relationship is robust to alternative measurements of the language in earnings call discussions and to industry classifications.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, risk management, big data/machine learning

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors propose a new measure of firm-level cybersecurity risk that applies textual analysis to earnings conference call transcripts

\- they use that measure to investigate the effect of cybersecurity risk on firm-level return volatility.

\- ▪ They find that discussion of issues related to cybersecurity risk during earnings calls is associated with an increase in the component of volatility that responds only to firm-specific news.

\- ▪ The authors show that the impact of cybersecurity risk on firm-level volatility is robust to alternative measurements of the language in earnings call discussions and to different industry classifications.

Abstract:作者提出了一种企业层面网络安全风险的度量方法，该方法采用计算语言学中基于模式的序列分类方法来确定收益电话会议期间用于网络安全风险相关问题的时间比例。利用他们的测量方法，他们调查了网络安全风险对公司层面回报波动性的影响；他们对特质波动率和隐含波动率进行了研究，发现公司层面的网络安全风险与持有收益通知日的特质波动率正相关。这表明，盈利电话会议期间对网络安全风险相关问题的讨论与仅对公司特定新闻作出反应的波动性成分的增加有关。这种积极的关系对财报电话会议和行业分类中的语言替代测量具有很强的鲁棒性。

主题：安全分析和评估、风险管理、大数据/机器学习

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者提出了一种新的企业级网络安全风险度量方法，将文本分析应用于收益电话会议记录

\- 他们利用这一指标来调查网络安全风险对公司层面回报波动性的影响。

\- ▪ 他们发现，在盈利电话会议期间对网络安全风险相关问题的讨论与仅对公司特定新闻作出反应的波动性成分的增加有关。

\- ▪ 作者表明，网络安全风险对企业级波动的影响是稳健的替代测量的财报电话会议和不同行业分类。

\[6\] Novel Risks: A Research and Policy Overview

标题:新风险：研究和政策概述

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Novel Risks 2021, jpm.2021.1.287

作者:Ahmet K. Karagozoglu

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/08/20/jpm.2021.1.287;

Abstract : In a broad sense, novel risks arise from environmental-, governance-, healthcare-, social responsibility–, sustainability-, and technology-related shortcomings of or challenges faced by firms, as well as the uncertainty caused by potential domestic and global regulatory policy responses. Recent academic literature suggests that there are parallels among environmental, social, and governance (ESG) risk, climate change risk, cybersecurity risk, and geopolitical risk in terms of measurement challenges, including but not limited to emerging data and measurement methods; the similarities in terms of their insufficient, noncomparable, less-specific, and non–decision-useful disclosures; and the potential interaction between these risks. Establishment of consistent disclosure policy and reporting requirements as well as improvement in measuring the impact of these novel risks on asset prices, volatility, and global financial stability is at the forefront of contemporary financial economics and portfolio management.

TOPICS: Risk management, tail risks, ESG investing, legal/regulatory/public policy

Key Finding

\- ▪ A broad characterization of novel risks is presented, and environmental, social, and governance (ESG) risk, climate change risk, cybersecurity risk, and geopolitical risk specifically are defined.

\- ▪ An overview of the recent academic literature suggests that there are parallels among these novel risks in terms of measurement challenges and disclosure regulations.

\- ▪ Measures based on novel applications of text and news analytics are identified as proxies for novel risks as investigated in the recent academic literature.

Abstract:从广义上讲，新的风险来自企业面临的环境、治理、医疗、社会责任、可持续性和技术相关的缺陷或挑战，以及潜在的国内和全球监管政策响应所造成的不确定性。最近的学术文献表明，环境、社会和治理（ESG）风险、气候变化风险、网络安全风险和地缘政治风险在计量挑战方面存在相似之处，包括但不限于新兴数据和计量方法；相似之处在于披露的信息不充分、不可比喻、不太具体和非决策有用；以及这些风险之间的潜在相互作用。建立一致的披露政策和报告要求，以及改进衡量这些新风险对资产价格、波动性和全球金融稳定的影响，是当代金融经济学和投资组合管理的前沿。

主题：风险管理、尾部风险、ESG投资、法律/监管/公共政策

Key Finding

\- ▪ 提出了新风险的广泛特征，并具体定义了环境、社会和治理（ESG）风险、气候变化风险、网络安全风险和地缘政治风险。

\- ▪ 对近期学术文献的综述表明，这些新风险在计量挑战和披露法规方面存在相似之处。

\- ▪ 基于文本和新闻分析新应用的措施被确定为最新学术文献中调查的新风险的代理。

\[7\] The Market Measure of Carbon Risk and its Impact on the Minimum Variance Portfolio

标题:碳风险的市场测度及其对最小方差投资组合的影响

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Novel Risks 2021, jpm.2021.1.285

作者:Théo Roncalli, Théo Le Guenedal, Frédéric Lepetit, Thierry Roncalli and Takaya Sekine

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/08/20/jpm.2021.1.285;

Abstract : Like environment, social, and governance investing, climate change is an important concern for asset managers and owners and a new challenge for portfolio construction. Until now, investors have mainly measured carbon risk using fundamental approaches, such as with carbon intensity metrics. Nevertheless, it has not been proven that asset prices are directly affected by these fundamental-based measures. In this article, the authors focus on another approach, which consists of measuring the sensitivity of stock prices with respect to a carbon risk factor. In the authors’ opinion, carbon betas are market-based measures that are complementary to carbon intensities or fundamental-based measures when managing investment portfolios; carbon betas may be viewed as an extension or forward-looking measure of the current carbon footprint. In particular, they show how this new metric can be used to build minimum variance strategies and how it affects portfolio construction.

TOPICS: ESG investing, portfolio construction, tail risks, fundamental equity analysis

Key Finding

\- ▪ Measuring carbon risk is different if we consider a fundamental-based approach by using carbon intensity metrics or a market-based approach by using carbon betas.

\- ▪ Managing relative carbon risk implies overweighting of green firms, whereas managing absolute carbon risk implies having zero exposure to the carbon risk factor. The first approach is an active management bet, whereas the second is an immunization investment strategy.

\- ▪ Both specific and systematic carbon risks are important when building a minimum variance portfolio and justify combining fundamental and market approaches to carbon risk.

Abstract:与环境、社会和治理投资一样，气候变化也是资产管理者和所有者关注的一个重要问题，也是投资组合构建的一个新挑战。到目前为止，投资者主要使用基本方法（如碳强度指标）来衡量碳风险。然而，尚未证明资产价格直接受到这些基本面指标的影响。在这篇文章中，作者关注的是另一种方法，包括测量股票价格对碳风险因素的敏感性。作者认为，在管理投资组合时，碳beta是与碳强度或基本面指标互补的基于市场的指标；碳beta可被视为当前碳足迹的延伸或前瞻性度量。特别是，他们展示了如何使用这个新指标来构建最小方差策略，以及它如何影响投资组合的构建。

主题：ESG投资、投资组合构建、尾部风险、基本权益分析

Key Finding

\- ▪ 测量碳风险是不同的，如果我们考虑一个基本的方法，通过使用碳强度度量或基于市场的方法，使用碳贝塔。▪ 管理相对碳风险意味着绿色企业的权重过高，而管理绝对碳风险意味着零碳风险因素。第一种方法是积极的管理赌注，而第二种方法是免疫投资策略

\- ▪ 在构建最小方差投资组合时，具体和系统的碳风险都很重要，并证明将基本方法和市场方法结合起来应对碳风险是合理的。
