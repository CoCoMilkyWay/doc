\[1\] Duration-Based Valuation of Corporate Bonds

标题:基于期限的公司债券估值

作者:Jules H. van Binsbergen,Michael Schwert

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3914422

Abstract : We decompose firm-level corporate bond and equity index returns into (1) duration-matched government bond returns and (2) the excess return over and above this duration-matched counterfactual, what we term duration-adjusted returns. Our decomposition provides markedly different return patterns and asset pricing model implications compared to previously employed excess return definitions (i.e., returns in excess of a short-duration fixed income instrument).

Keywords : corporate bonds, duration, capital asset pricing model

Abstract :我们将公司层面的公司债券和股票指数收益分解为（1）期限匹配的政府债券收益和（2）超过该期限的超额收益匹配的反事实收益，我们称之为期限调整收益。与之前采用的超额收益定义（即短期固定收益工具的超额收益）相比，我们的分解提供了明显不同的回报模式和资产定价模型含义。

Keywords :公司债券、期限、资本资产定价模型

\[2\] Boosting Cryptocurrency Return Prediction

标题:提高加密货币收益率预测

作者:Ilias Filippou,David Rapach,Christoffer Thimsen

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3914414

Abstract : We use boosted decision trees to generate daily out-of-sample forecasts of excess returns for Bitcoin and Ethereum, the two best-known and largest cryptocurrencies. The decision trees incorporate information from 39 predictors, including variables relating to cryptocurrency fundamentals, technical indicators, Google Trends searches, Reddit comments, and articles from Factiva. We use the XGBoost algorithm to boost trees and find that excess return forecasts based on boosted trees produce statistically and economically significant out-of-sample gains. We explore the importance of individual predictors and nonlinearities in the fitted boosted trees. We find that a broad array of predictors are relevant for forecasting daily cryptocurrency returns and that strong nonlinearities characterize the predictive relationships.

Keywords : Bitcoin, Ethereum, Out-of-sample return prediction, Machine learning, XGBoost, SHAP values

Abstract :我们使用增强的决策树来生成比特币和以太坊这两种最著名、规模最大的加密货币的每日超额收益预测。决策树包含来自39个预测因子的信息，包括与加密货币基础、技术指标、谷歌趋势搜索、Reddit评论和Factiva文章相关的变量。我们使用XGBoost算法来提升树，发现基于提升树的超额收益预测会产生统计上和经济上显著的样本外收益。我们探讨了个体预测因子和非线性在拟合树中的重要性。我们发现，广泛的预测因子与预测每日加密货币收益率相关，并且强非线性表征了预测关系。

Keywords :比特币、以太坊、样本外收益预测、机器学习、XGBoost、SHAP值

\[3\] Sales & Marketing Workforce Growth and Firm Performance

标题:销售和营销人员增长与公司绩效

作者:Lorenzo Garlappi,Zhongzhi Song

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3914408

Abstract : Using a unique labor dataset of publicly-listed Chinese companies, we document that workforce growth in the sales and marketing function is a strong predictor of firms' revenue growth, profitability, and stock returns. Firms in the highest quintile of growth rate in the sales and marketing workforce earn an annual future return 7.7% higher than firms in the lowest quintile. This return spread cannot be explained by (i) existing return factors, (ii) cash-flow risk, (iii) earnings surprises, or (iv) idiosyncratic risk. Our results suggest that heterogeneity in the professional composition of firms' workforce is important in determining the relationship between labor growth and future performance.

Keywords : Workforce growth; Sales and marketing; Stock returns

Abstract :利用一个独特的中国上市公司劳动力数据集，我们证明了销售和营销功能中的劳动力增长是公司收入增长、盈利能力和股票回报的有力预测因素。在销售和营销人员中，增长率最高的五分之一公司的年未来回报率比最低的五分之一公司高7.7%。这种收益差不能用（i）现有收益因素、（ii）现金流风险、（iii）收益意外或（iv）特殊风险来解释。我们的研究结果表明，企业劳动力专业构成的异质性对于决定劳动力增长与未来绩效之间的关系非常重要。

Keywords :劳动力增长；销售和营销；股票收益

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3914356

Abstract : A positive stock market variance-return relation is the key building block of extant rational-expectations asset pricing models. Empirical evidence is elusive despite forty years of intensive academic research. The conjecture is also resoundingly refuted by the late 1990s’ dotcom bubble featured by sharp increases in both market prices and variance. \[6\] Alternative investments in the Fintech era: The risk and return of Non-Fungible Token (NFT)

标题:金融科技时代的另类投资：不可替代代币（NFT）的风险与回报

作者:De-Rong Kong,Tse-Chun Lin

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3914085

Abstract : This paper utilizes one of the earliest and largest NFT collections to investigate the pricing and the risk-return profile of NFTs. In general, we find that NFTs outperform traditional financial assets. Yet, investing in NFTs usually comes along with extremely high risks. The average monthly returns on NFTs range from 6.10% to 44.11%, and the standard deviations fluctuate between 44.35% and 74.57%, leading to a Sharpe ratio comparable to the NASDAQ index. NFT prices surge when there is a drastic increase in demand for alternative investments and a search for yield, especially in a low interest rate environment. We also find that the pricing of NFT largely depends on a token’s scarceness and an investor’s aesthetic preference. Hence, conventional asset-pricing models are unlikely to explain NFT returns. Overall, we provide the first evidence that NFTs serve as a novel investment vessel in this Fintech era.

Keywords : Non-Fungible Tokens, NFT, Fintech, Ethereum, Blockchain, Alternative investments, Risk and return

Abstract :本文利用最早和最大的NFT集合之一来研究NFT的定价和风险回报情况。总体而言，我们发现非金融资产的表现优于传统金融资产。然而，投资非金融交易通常伴随着极高的风险。非金融交易的平均月回报率在6.10%至44.11%之间，标准差在44.35%至74.57%之间波动，导致夏普比率与纳斯达克指数相当。非金融债券的价格在对另类投资的需求急剧增加和对收益率的追求时飙升，特别是在低利率环境下。我们还发现，NFT的定价在很大程度上取决于代币的稀缺性和投资者的审美偏好。因此，传统的资产定价模型不太可能解释NFT回报。总的来说，我们提供了第一个证据，证明NFT在金融科技时代是一种新型的投资工具。

Keywords :不可替代代币、NFT、Fintech、以太坊、区块链、替代投资、风险和回报

\[7\] How Important Are Semi-Annual Earnings Announcements? An Information Event Perspective

标题:半年收益公告有多重要？信息事件透视图

作者:Stephen L. Taylor,Alex Tong

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3904463

Abstract : Using a method that avoids the need to specify earnings expectations (Ball and Shivakumar 2008), we demonstrate that the period surrounding the semi-annual announcement of Australian firms’ earnings is, on average, an important source of information. Although there is substantial year-to-year variation, we observe no evidence of any significant time trend, and also conclude that a shift from Australian domestic GAAP to IFRS did not impact the association between earnings announcement windows and stock returns. We also find no evidence that the informativeness of earnings announcements varies systematically with firm size, analyst following or economic news (i.e., positive versus negative stock returns, profits versus losses), although we do observe significant variation across industries. Our conclusion is further supported by contrasting the earnings release date with the days immediately prior to release, or high information days other than earnings announcement windows. Using a more precise event window relative to prior studies (i.e., three hours versus three days), we confirm that earnings announcements contain significant new information about fundamentals.

Keywords : Earnings announcements, information content, fundamentals

Abstract :使用一种避免指定收益预期的方法（Ball和Shivakumar，2008年），我们证明，平均而言，围绕澳大利亚公司半年度收益公告的时期是一个重要的信息来源。虽然存在较大的年度变化，但我们没有观察到任何明显的时间趋势，并且还得出结论，从澳大利亚国内公认会计准则到国际财务报告准则的转变并未影响收益公告窗口和股票回报之间的关联。我们也没有发现任何证据表明，盈利公告的信息性会随着公司规模、分析师关注或经济新闻（即股票收益率的正与负、利润与亏损）的不同而系统地变化，尽管我们确实观察到不同行业之间存在显著差异。我们的结论通过将收益发布日期与发布前几天或收益公告窗口以外的高信息日进行对比得到进一步支持。使用与先前研究相比更精确的事件窗口（即三小时对三天），我们确认收益公告包含重要的基本面新信息。

Keywords :盈利公告、信息内容、基本面

\[11\] Most claimed statistical findings in cross-sectional return predictability are likely true

标题:大多数声称的横截面收益可预测性的统计结果可能是真实的

作者:Andrew Y. Chen

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3912915

Abstract : Harvey, Liu, and Zhu (2016) “argue that most claimed research findings in financial economics are likely false.” Surprisingly, their false discovery rate (FDR) estimates suggest most are true. I revisit their results by developing non- and semi-parametric FDR estimators that account for publication bias and empirical correlations. These estimators provide simple closed-form expressions and reliably produce an upper bound on the FDR in simulations that cluster-bootstrap from empirical predictor returns. Applying these estimators to the Chen-Zimmermann dataset of 205 predictors, I find that most claimed statistical findings in the cross-sectional predictability literature are likely true.

Keywords : stock market predictability, stock market anomalies, p-hacking, multiple testing

Abstract :Harvey、Liu和Zhu（2016）“认为大多数声称的金融经济学研究结果可能是错误的。”令人惊讶的是，他们的错误发现率（FDR）估计表明大多数是正确的。我通过发展非参数和半参数FDR估计量来重新审视他们的结果，这些估计量可以解释发表偏倚和经验相关性。这些估计器提供了简单的闭式表达式，并在模拟中可靠地生成FDR的上界，该上界由经验预测值返回进行聚类引导。将这些估计器应用于包含205个预测因子的Chen-Zimmermann数据集，我发现横断面可预测性文献中大多数声称的统计结果可能是正确的。

Keywords :股票市场的可预测性、股票市场异常、p-hacking、多重测试

\[12\] Financially Constrained Index Futures Arbitrage

标题:金融约束指数期货套利

作者:Kristoffer J. Glover,Hardy Hulley

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3895655

Abstract : We develop two models for index futures arbitrage that take the financing constraints faced by real-world arbitrageurs into account. Our models predict that the price of an index futures contract and the value of its underlying index should deviate further from their theoretical cost-of-carry relationship when (a) the contract has a longtime to go before expiry, and (b) volatility is high. The fact that these predictions enjoy considerable empirical support highlights the importance of financing constraints for explaining index futures mispricing.

Keywords : Index futures arbitrage, limits to arbitrage, financing constraints, optimal stopping

Abstract :我们开发了两个指数期货套利模型，将现实世界套利者面临的融资约束考虑在内。我们的模型预测，当（a）合约在到期前还有很长一段时间，以及（b）波动性较高时，指数期货合约的价格及其标的指数的价值应该进一步偏离其理论结转成本关系。这些预测得到了相当多的实证支持，这一事实突出了融资约束对于解释指数期货错误定价的重要性。

Keywords :指数期货套利、套利限制、融资约束、最优止损
