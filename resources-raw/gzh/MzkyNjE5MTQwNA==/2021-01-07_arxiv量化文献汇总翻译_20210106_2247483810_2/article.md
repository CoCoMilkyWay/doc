\[1\] Optimal Hedging with Margin Constraints and Default Aversion and its Application to Bitcoin Perpetual Futures

标题 : 带保证金约束和违约厌恶的最优套期保值及其在比特币永续期货中的应用

作者 : Carol Alexander, Jun Deng, Bin Zou , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.01261

说明 : Comments: 41 pages

Abstract : We consider a futures hedging problem subject to a budget constraint that limits the ability of a hedger with default aversion to meet margin requirements. We derive a semi closed form for an optimal hedging strategy with dual objectives to minimize both the variance of the hedged portfolio and the probability of forced liquidations due to margin calls. An empirical analysis of bitcoin shows that the optimal strategy not only achieves superior hedge effectiveness, but also reduces the probability of forced liquidations to an acceptable level. We also compare how the hedger s default aversion impacts the performance of optimal hedging based on minute level data across major bitcoin spot and perpetual futures markets.

摘要 : 我们考虑一个受预算约束的期货套期保值问题，预算约束限制了具有违约厌恶的套期保值者满足保证金要求的能力。我们推导了一个具有双重目标的最优套期保值策略的半封闭形式，以最小化被套期投资组合的方差和因追加保证金而强制清算的概率。对比特币的实证分析表明，该最优策略不仅取得了较好的套期保值效果，而且将被迫变现的概率降低到了可接受的水平。我们还比较了基于分时期货的最佳套期保值水平以及基于分时期货的最佳套期保值水平对各主要市场的影响。

\[2\] A Model of Market Making and Price Impact

标题 : 做市商模型与价格影响

作者 : Angad Singh , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.01388

说明 : -

Abstract : Traders constantly consider the price impact associated with changing their positions. This paper seeks to understand how price impact emerges from the quoting strategies of market makers. To this end, market making is modeled as a dynamic auction using the mathematical framework of Stochastic Differential Games. In Nash Equilibrium, the market makers quoting strategies generate a price impact function that is of the same form as the celebrated Almgren Chriss model. The key insight is that price impact is the mechanism through which market makers earn profits while matching their books. As such, price impact is an essential feature of markets where flow is intermediated by market makers.

摘要 : 交易者经常考虑与改变仓位相关的价格影响。本文旨在了解做市商的报价策略是如何产生价格影响的。为此，利用随机微分对策的数学框架，将做市行为建模为动态拍卖。在纳什均衡中，做市商的报价策略产生了与著名的Almgren-Chriss模型相同的价格影响函数。关键在于，价格影响是做市商在匹配账面价值的同时赚取利润的机制。因此，价格影响是市场的一个基本特征，在这种市场中，资金流动由做市商进行中介。

\[3\] Recurrent Neural Networks for Stochastic Control Problems with Delay

标题 : 时滞随机控制问题的递归神经网络

作者 : Jiequn Han, Ruimeng Hu , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.01385

说明 : -

Abstract : Stochastic control problems with delay are challenging due to the path dependent feature of the system and thus its intrinsic high dimensions. In this paper, we propose and systematically study deep neural networks based algorithms to solve stochastic control problems with delay features. Specifically, we employ neural networks for sequence modeling ( emph e.g. , recurrent neural networks such as long short term memory) to parameterize the policy and optimize the objective function. The proposed algorithms are tested on three benchmark examples a linear quadratic problem, optimal consumption with fixed finite delay, and portfolio optimization with complete memory. Particularly, we notice that the architecture of recurrent neural networks naturally captures the path dependent feature with much flexibility and yields better performance with more efficient and stable training of the network compared to feedforward networks. The superiority is even evident in the case of portfolio optimization with complete memory, which features infinite delay.

摘要 : 具有时滞的随机控制问题是一个具有挑战性的问题，这是由于系统的路径依赖性和内在的高维性。本文提出并系统地研究了基于神经网络的具有时滞特性的随机控制问题求解算法。具体来说，我们使用神经网络进行序列建模（emph，例如长-短期记忆等递归神经网络）来参数化策略和优化目标函数。本文提出的算法分别在线性二次问题、固定有限时滞最优消费和完全记忆投资组合优化三个算例上进行了验证。特别地，我们注意到，与前馈网络相比，递归神经网络的结构自然地以更大的灵活性捕获了路径相关的特征，并且通过更有效和稳定的网络训练产生了更好的性能。这种优越性在具有无限时滞的完全记忆投资组合优化问题中更为明显。
