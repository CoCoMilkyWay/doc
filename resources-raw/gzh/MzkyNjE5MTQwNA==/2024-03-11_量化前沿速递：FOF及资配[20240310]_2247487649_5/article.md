标题:证券借贷与信息获取

作者:Stefan Greppmair,Stephan Jank,Pedro Saffi,Jason Sturgess

来源:SSRN\_20240310

Abstract : Using microdata on stock-level lending positions from German mutual funds, we show that active funds use the equity lending market to obtain information about short sale demand. Funds reduce long positions in response to these demand signals, which allows fund managers to front-run public disclosure of big short positions and avoid future losses.
