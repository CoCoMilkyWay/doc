\[1\] Liquidity and Price Impact at the 52 Week High

标题:52周高点的流动性和价格影响

作者:Joshua Della Vedova,Andrew R. Grant,P. Joakim Westerholm

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3925756

Abstract : A stock’s 52 week high price represents an extremely salient anchor in an investor’s tradingdecision. Using trade and quote data, we document that stocks near the 52 week high priceexhibit significantly higher levels of liquidity than stocks far from the 52 week high. Thisincrease in liquidity is concentrated on the supply side, with depth on the ask side essentiallydoubling relative to a normal day. Price impact declines by as much as two-thirds on the52 week high day. We argue that the abnormal increase in liquidity is driven by dispositionand anchoring effects. These findings help explain the role of the 52 week high as a barrierto information discovery and a potential driver of long run momentum.

Keywords : Liquidity, Individual Investors, Limit Orders

Abstract :一只股票52周的最高价格代表着投资者交易决策中一个极其突出的锚。利用交易和报价数据，我们发现接近52周高点的股票的流动性水平明显高于远离52周高点的股票。流动性的增加主要集中在供给端，而需求端的深度与正常日相比基本上是双倍的。在52周的高点，价格影响下降了三分之二。我们认为，流动性的异常增加是由处置和锚定效应驱动的。这些发现有助于解释52周高点作为信息发现障碍和长期动力潜在驱动力的作用。

Keywords :流动性、个人投资者、限额指令

\[2\] Another Arbitrage-Free Affine Model of TIPS Yields with Embedded Liquidity Risk

标题:另一种具有隐含流动性风险的TIPS收益率无套利仿射模型

作者:J. Benson Durham

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3927298

Abstract : This study outlines an affine term structure model (ATSM) of TIPS that decomposes yields into real expected rates, real term premiums, and liquidity premiums. The estimation incorporates an observable liquidity factor that more comprehensively captures limits to arbitrage implied by yield curve fitting errors, strictly eschews non-market information to provide more relevant estimates for investors, and addresses factor persistence and small-sample bias, yet prices TIPS with linear regressions. The results imply counter-cyclical TIPS liquidity premiums as well as a steady secular decline in equilibrium real rates from July 1999 to August 2021, including a meaningful drop in the aftermath of Covid-19. Real required returns follow a similar downward path, and although mean real term premiums are positive, they do not appear to be counter-cyclical even across frequency bands, broadly consistent with a hedge.

Keywords : None

Abstract :本研究概述了TIPS的仿射期限结构模型（ATSM），该模型将收益率分解为实际预期利率、实际期限溢价和流动性溢价。该估计纳入了一个可观察的流动性因素，该因素更全面地捕捉了收益率曲线拟合误差所隐含的套利限制，严格避开非市场信息，为投资者提供更相关的估计，并解决了因素持续性和小样本偏差，但价格与线性回归有关。结果表明，从1999年7月到2021年8月，反周期TIPS流动性溢价以及均衡实际利率的长期稳定下降，包括新冠疫情后的显著下降。实际所需回报遵循类似的下降路径，尽管平均实际定期溢价为正，但即使在不同频段，它们似乎也不是反周期的，与对冲基本一致。

Keywords :None

\[3\] Systemic Risk in Gulf Cooperation Council Countries’ Equity Markets and Banking Sectors: A Dynamic Conditional Value-at-Risk Approach

标题:海湾合作委员会国家股票市场和银行部门的系统性风险：动态条件风险价值法

作者:Aktham Issa Maghyereh,Nader Virk,Basel Awartani

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3926447

Abstract : We employ dynamic conditional Value at Risk (CoVaR) technique of Adrian and Brunnermeir (2016) in examining the systemic risk and its spillovers for the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries during the period of January 2004 to June 2020. To do so, we identify 11 large banks in the region that are systemically important given their size and construct country specific banking indices excluding the systemically important banks (SIBs) listed in that market. We examine the systemic spillovers from SIBs to the broad-based GCC market indices and value-weighted banking indices. Our results show that exclusion of the SIB is crucial in isolating the incremental CoVaR spillovers, otherwise it is simply function of SIB’s conditional VaR. We document that incremental tail spillovers are large just not for the market in which SIB is listed but they also increase the tail risk of other GCC markets. The only country that appear insulated from systemic spillovers is Kuwait, otherwise a systemic shock has the strength and size to increases losses in the GCC market and banking sector that goes unaccounted for using bank-specific VaR.

Keywords : Systemic risk;ΔCoVaR; GCC countries; Banking sectors

Abstract :我们采用Adrian和Brunnermeir（2016）的动态条件风险价值（CoVaR）技术，研究了2004年1月至2020年6月期间海湾合作委员会（GCC）国家的系统性风险及其溢出效应。为此，我们确定了该地区11家具有系统重要性的大型银行的规模，并构建了特定国家的银行指数，不包括该市场上市的系统重要性银行（SIB）。我们研究了SIB对基础广泛的GCC市场指数和价值加权银行指数的系统溢出效应。我们的结果表明，排除SIB对于隔离增量CoVaR溢出至关重要，否则它只是SIB条件VaR的函数。我们证明，增量尾部溢出非常大，只是不适用于SIB上市的市场，但也增加了其他GCC市场的尾部风险。科威特似乎是唯一一个没有受到系统性溢出影响的国家，否则，系统性冲击的强度和规模足以增加GCC市场和银行部门的损失，而使用银行特定VaR无法解释这些损失。

Keywords :系统性风险；ΔCoVaR；海湾合作委员会国家；银行部门

\[4\] Constrained Dealers and Market Efficiency

标题:约束经销商与市场效率

作者:Wenqian Huang,Angelo Ranaldo,Andreas Schrimpf,Fabricius Somogyi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3956582

Abstract : We analyse how constraints on dealers’ risk bearing capacity affect market efficiency in the foreign exchange (FX) market. Dealers support market efficiency by accommodating their customers’ trading demands through elastic liquidity provision in normal times but when they face constraints their elasticity of liquidity provision weakens. Episodes of tight dealer constraints – for instance, due to high leverage, Value-at-Risk, and funding costs – in turn go hand in hand with price inefficiencies due to law of one price deviations and elevated trading costs. We rationalise our novel empirical findings with a tractable model that sheds light on the key mechanisms of how market efficiency can deteriorate when dealers are more constrained.

Keywords : Market Efficiency, Dealer Constraints, Foreign Exchange, Liquidity Provision.

Abstract :我们分析了外汇市场中对交易商风险承受能力的约束如何影响市场效率。交易商在正常情况下通过弹性流动性供给来满足客户的交易需求，从而支持市场效率，但当他们面临约束时，其流动性供给的弹性会减弱。紧绷的交易商约束事件——例如，由于高杠杆、风险价值和融资成本——反过来又伴随着价格低效，这是由于价格偏差定律和交易成本上升。我们用一个易于处理的模型对我们的新实证结果进行了合理化，该模型揭示了当经销商受到更多约束时，市场效率如何恶化的关键机制。

Keywords :市场效率、交易商约束、外汇、流动性供应。

\[5\] Optimal Trading: A Model Predictive Control Approach

标题:最优交易：一种模型预测控制方法

作者:Simon Clinet,Jean-François Perreton,Serge Reydellet

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3947001

Abstract : We develop a dynamic trading strategy in the Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) framework. By including a price mean-reversion signal into the optimization program, in a trading environment where market impact is linear and stage costs are quadratic, we obtain an optimal trading curve that reacts opportunistically to price changes while retaining its ability to satisfy smooth or hard completion constraints. The optimal allocation is affine in the spot price and in the number of outstanding shares at any time, and it can be fully derived iteratively. It is also aggressive in the money, meaning that it accelerates whenever the price is favorable, with an intensity that can be calibrated by the practitioner. Since the LQR may yield locally negative participation rates (i.e round trip trades) which are often undesirable, we show that the aforementioned optimization problem can be improved and solved under positivity constraints following a Model Predictive Control (MPC) approach. In particular, it is smoother and more consistent with the completion constraint than putting a hard floor on the participation rate. We finally examine how the LQR can be simplified in the continuous trading context, which allows us to derive a closed formula for the trading curve under further assumptions, and we document a two-step strategy for the case where trades can also occur in an additional dark pool.

Keywords : None

Abstract :我们在线性二次型调节器（LQR）框架下开发了一个动态交易策略。通过将价格均值回归信号纳入优化程序，在市场影响为线性且阶段成本为二次的交易环境中，我们获得了一条最优交易曲线，该曲线可机会主义地对价格变化作出反应，同时保持其满足平滑或硬完成约束的能力。最优配置在现货价格和任何时候的流通股数量上都是仿射的，并且可以完全迭代推导。它在金钱上也具有侵略性，这意味着只要价格有利，它就会加速，其强度可以由从业者校准。由于LQR可能产生局部负参与率（即往返交易），这通常是不可取的，因此我们表明，上述优化问题可以在模型预测控制（MPC）方法的正约束下得到改进和解决。特别是，它比在参与率上设置硬地板更平滑，更符合完成约束。最后，我们研究了LQR如何在连续交易环境中简化，这使我们能够在进一步假设下推导出交易曲线的闭合公式，并记录了交易也可以在额外暗池中发生的情况下的两步策略。

Keywords :None

\[8\] Are Firms Biasing Stakeholder Expectations by Attributing Prior Poor Performance to COVID-19?

标题:公司是否通过将先前的不良表现归因于新冠病毒19而偏离了利益相关者的期望？

作者:Hrishikesh Desai

出处:Desai, H. (2021). Are firms biasing stakeholder expectations by attributing prior poor performance to COVID-19?. IIM Kozhikode Society & Management Review (Forthcoming).

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3955445

Abstract : Item 503(c) of the United States Securities and Exchange Commission’s (SEC’s) Regulation S-K requires firms to disclose the ‘most significant’ factors that affect them in their Item 1A risk factor disclosures made in their 10-K (annual) or 10-Q (quarterly) SEC filings. Prior to COVID-19, firms discussed risk factors such as Liquidity, Competition, etc. as part of their Item 1A disclosures. The current pandemic has resulted in the COVID-19 risk factor being widely discussed as part of firms’ Item 1A risk factor disclosures. A ‘firm-specific’ discussion on this transient risk factor is unique in the sense that it can affect the salience of other already disclosed less transient but significant risk factors to investors and other stakeholders. Using a sample of 68 firms hard hit by COVID-19 with prior poor performance, I find that market reactions to their Item 1A risk factor disclosures were significantly more positive if they disclosed the COVID-19 risk factor in a certain firm-specific manner compared to those that didn’t. These results suggest that stakeholder perceptions of firms’ risk profiles are being biased to some extent as the less transient but other significant risk factors that were already affecting these firms seem to be underweighted by them in evaluating the firms’ risk profiles. I explain this bias further using the meta-theoretical framework of the elaborate likelihood model. I also propose a solution to this problem that involves making these disclosures in the form of risk matrices.

Keywords : COVID-19, financial disclosures, firm performance, judgement bias, risk factor disclosures, risk analysis, SEC regulations, elaboration likelihood model

Abstract :美国证券交易委员会（SEC）s-K条例第503（c）项要求公司在其10-K（年度）或10-Q（季度）SEC文件中的第1A项风险因素披露中披露影响其的“最重要”因素。在新冠病毒-19之前，公司将流动性、竞争等风险因素作为第1A项披露的一部分进行了讨论。目前的大流行导致新冠病毒-19风险因素被广泛讨论，作为公司第1A项风险因素披露的一部分。关于这一暂时性风险因素的“特定于公司”的讨论是独特的，因为它可以影响投资者和其他利益相关者已经披露的其他不太暂时但重要的风险因素的显著性。通过对68家之前业绩不佳的受新冠肺炎重创的公司进行抽样调查，我发现，与未披露新冠肺炎风险因素的公司相比，如果他们以特定于公司的方式披露新冠肺炎风险因素，市场对其1A项风险因素披露的反应明显更积极。这些结果表明，利益相关者对公司风险状况的看法在一定程度上存在偏差，因为在评估公司风险状况时，已经影响到这些公司的不太短暂但其他重要的风险因素似乎被低估了。我使用精心设计的似然模型的元理论框架进一步解释了这种偏见。我还提出了一个解决方案，涉及以风险矩阵的形式进行披露。

Keywords :新冠病毒-19，财务披露，公司绩效，判断偏差，风险因素披露，风险分析，美国证券交易委员会法规，精化可能性模型

\[9\] Lifetime Earnings

标题:终身收入

作者:Sanjeev Bhojraj,Ashish Ochani,Shivaram Rajgopal

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3951530

Abstract : Using realized earnings over long periods of time, we investigate errors in earnings expectations implied by stock prices of firms. We compute lifetime earnings for 16,386 (3,711) domestic firms that started trading after (existed as of) January 1, 1975 for the years 1975-2019 and compare such earnings to the stock price prevailing at the beginning of every year starting 1975. Of the 16,386 firms examined, only 17% survived till 2019, 42% merged with other firms and the rest were delisted for other reasons. Finally, we predict lifetime earnings using extant information and find economically and statistically significant abnormal future stock returns based on such predictions.

Keywords :寿命、收益、股票回报、财富创造、合并、IPO

\[10\] Returns to Firm Globalization: Risk Premium or Mispricing?

标题:企业全球化的回报：风险溢价还是错误定价？

作者:Jared DeLisle,Mengying Wang,H. Zafer Yuksel,Gulnara R. Zaynutdinova, Ph.D.

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3928270

Abstract : This paper investigates mispricing (specifically limits to arbitrage) as an alternative to the risk-based explanation of the globalization premium documented by Barrot et al. (2019). We document that the globalization premium is positively correlated with measures of limits to arbitrage. We further show that both displacement risk related to foreign competition and limits to arbitrage exhibit a joint effect on the globalization premium. Interestingly, displacement risk plays an explanatory role only in the presence of limits to arbitrage. Furthermore, when globalization is measured at the firm level, mispricing independently impacts the globalization premium while risk does not. Overall, our evidence is not consistent with a purely risk-based explanation of the globalization premium.

Keywords : Globalization, risk premium, limits-to-arbitrage, mispricing, displacement risk

Abstract :本文研究了错误定价（特别是对套利的限制），作为Barrot等人（2019）记录的基于风险的全球化溢价解释的替代方法。我们证明全球化溢价与套利限制措施正相关。我们进一步表明，与国外竞争相关的置换风险和套利限制对全球化溢价具有共同影响。有趣的是，置换风险仅在存在套利限制的情况下起解释作用。此外，当在企业层面衡量全球化时，错误定价会独立影响全球化溢价，而风险不会。总体而言，我们的证据与纯粹基于风险的全球化溢价解释不一致。

Keywords :全球化、风险溢价、套利限制、错误定价、置换风险
