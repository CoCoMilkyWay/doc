\[1\] Drawdown Measures: Are They All the Same?

标题:缩编措施：都一样吗？

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management April 2022, jpm.2022.1.346

作者:Olaf Korn, Philipp M. Möller and Christian Schwehm

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/02/26/jpm.2022.1.346;

Abstract : Over the years, a diverse range of drawdown measures has evolved to guide asset management. The authors show that almost all of these measures fit into a unified framework. This new framework simplifies the implementation of drawdown measures and improves an understanding of their similarities and differences. Conceptual differences between drawdown measures translate into different rankings of portfolios, which the authors document in a simulation study. Their research also shows that all drawdown measures can (to some degree) discriminate between skillful and unskillful portfolio managers, but differ in terms of accuracy. However, the ability to detect skill does not easily improve performance ratios where drawdown measures serve as the denominator. In conclusion, this article shows that the choice of an adequate drawdown measure is vital to the assessment of investments because different measures emphasize different aspects of risk.

Key Finding

\-

Abstract:多年来，各种各样的支取措施已经演变为指导资产管理的手段。作者指出，几乎所有这些措施都符合一个统一的框架。这一新框架简化了缩编措施的实施，并增进了对其异同的理解。提取指标之间的概念差异转化为投资组合的不同排名，作者在一项模拟研究中记录了这一点。他们的研究还表明，所有的缩减指标（在一定程度上）都可以区分熟练和不熟练的投资组合经理，但在准确性方面有所不同。然而，在缩编指标作为分母的情况下，检测技能的能力并不容易提高绩效比率。总之，本文表明，选择适当的提款措施对投资评估至关重要，因为不同的措施强调风险的不同方面。

\[2\] Tail Risk Hedging Performance: Measuring What Counts

标题:尾部风险对冲绩效：衡量什么是重要的

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management April 2022, jpm.2022.1.345

作者:Linda Chang, Jeremie Holdom and Vineer Bhansali

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/02/24/jpm.2022.1.345;

Abstract : The authors discuss the importance of using proper metrics for measuring the historical performance of tail risk hedging portfolios in particular and for any strategy with levered payoffs in general. It is their view that simply using historical compounded returns when the payoffs may be multiples of the investment and ignoring the timing and magnitude of cash flows can potentially paint an inaccurate picture, sometimes grossly so, of the economic value of such strategies. To obtain a more accurate picture that is consistent with the objectives of such strategies, the timing and magnitude of cash flows should be included when analyzing their impact on portfolio construction. Although the correct quantitative metrics are obviously critical in measuring the efficacy and reliability of tail hedging strategies, the importance of subjective metrics, ease of implementation, flexibility, and the relevance to underlying objectives of investors is equally important.

Key Finding

\-

Abstract:作者讨论了使用适当的指标来衡量尾部风险对冲投资组合的历史表现的重要性，尤其是对于任何具有杠杆回报的策略。他们认为，当回报可能是投资的倍数时，简单地使用历史复合回报，而忽略现金流的时间和规模，可能会对此类策略的经济价值描绘出一幅不准确的图景，有时甚至非常严重。为了获得与此类策略目标一致的更准确的情况，在分析现金流对投资组合构建的影响时，应包括现金流的时间和规模。虽然正确的量化指标在衡量尾部套期保值策略的有效性和可靠性方面显然至关重要，但主观指标、易实施性、灵活性以及与投资者基本目标的相关性的重要性同样重要。

\[3\] Systematic ESG Risk and Passive ESG Investing

标题:系统性ESG风险与被动ESG投资

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management April 2022, jpm.2022.1.344

作者:Ick Jin

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/02/24/jpm.2022.1.344;

Abstract : Investors can regard passive environment, social, and governance (ESG) investing as a cost-effective strategy to manage systematic ESG risk. This analysis aims to explain the expected performance of passively following an ESG-screened index within the risk–return paradigm. The author formulates the ESG factor surprise by subtracting returns on ESG-screened indexes from returns on their parent indexes. This approach reveals many intriguing aspects of passive ESG investing. The empirical result of the index model for the sample period from 2011 to 2020 shows that the daily returns of ESG-screened indexes (constructed through negative screening without portfolio skews) have a beta coefficient lower than 1. The EGARCH analysis confirms that ESG factor surprise has a lower long-run level, higher persistence, and minor asymmetry compared with market factor surprise. These empirical findings show that passive ESG investing may be suitable for value-based investors. If investors want to enhance their risk-adjusted return through passive ESG investing, they may need to seek alternative indexes with more ESG exposure deviation.

Key Finding

\-

Abstract:投资者可以将被动环境、社会和治理（ESG）投资视为管理系统性ESG风险的成本效益策略。该分析旨在解释在风险回报范式下被动遵循ESG筛选指数的预期绩效。作者通过从其父指数的回报中减去ESG筛选指数的回报来计算ESG因子。这种方法揭示了被动ESG投资的许多有趣方面。2011年至2020年样本期指数模型的实证结果表明，ESG筛选指数（通过无投资组合偏斜的负筛选构建）的日收益率的贝塔系数低于1。EGARCH分析证实，与市场因素意外相比，ESG因素意外具有较低的长期水平、较高的持续性和较小的不对称性。这些实证结果表明，被动ESG投资可能适合基于价值的投资者。如果投资者希望通过被动ESG投资提高其风险调整后的回报率，他们可能需要寻找ESG风险敞口偏差更大的替代指数。

\[4\] Carbon-Tax-Adjusted Value

标题:碳税调整值

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management April 2022, jpm.2022.1.343

作者:David Blitz and Tobias Hoogteijling

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/02/15/jpm.2022.1.343;

Abstract : The authors examine the effects of incorporating a potential tax on carbon emissions into a value investment strategy. They show that in a portfolio optimization problem, a carbon tax at the stock level is mathematically equivalent to a carbon constraint at the portfolio level. Using this insight, the authors derive a value–carbon efficient frontier that reflects the trade-off between a high value exposure and a low carbon footprint. Empirically, they find that carbon taxes up to $100, corresponding to a portfolio carbon footprint reduction of about 50%, have little effect on the characteristics and performance of the long side of a value strategy. More aggressive footprint reduction targets require progressively higher, less realistic carbon tax levels that do erode the magnitude of the value premium.

Key Finding

\-

Abstract:作者研究了将潜在的碳排放税纳入价值投资战略的影响。他们表明，在投资组合优化问题中，股票层面的碳税在数学上等同于投资组合层面的碳约束。利用这一观点，作者得出了一个价值-碳效率边界，反映了高价值暴露和低碳足迹之间的权衡。从经验上看，他们发现高达100美元的碳税（相当于投资组合碳足迹减少约50%）对价值策略的长期特征和绩效几乎没有影响。更积极的碳足迹削减目标要求逐步提高、更不现实的碳税水平，这会削弱价值溢价的幅度。

\[5\] Workhorse or Trojan Horse? The Alternative Risk Premium Conundrum in Multi-Asset Portfolios

标题:Workhorse还是特洛伊木马？多资产组合中的另类风险溢价难题

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Multi-Asset Special Issue 2022, jpm.2022.1.342

作者:Stephen A. Gorman and Frank J. Fabozzi

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2022/02/12/jpm.2022.1.342;

Abstract : Diversified alternative risk premium (ARP) portfolios seek to generate absolute returns using a broad range of systematic trading strategies incorporating multiple investment styles covering all the major asset classes. Against a backdrop of low developed market bond yields and fully valued equities, ARP offered a reasonably priced combination of low correlation with traditional asset classes, attractive expected Sharpe ratio, and reasonable liquidity. Following a period of rapid adoption, disappointing performance over the 2018–2020 period has produced considerable soul searching regarding the role of ARP in institutional portfolios. Should these strategies remain a candidate for multi-asset portfolios, or is the experience of the past years a death knell regarding their usefulness? To examine this very topical issue, in this article, the authors use a unique array of benchmarks leveraging a proprietary database of 2,000 tradable bank indexes. They evaluate whether recent returns are consistent with long-term expectations. In the process, they consider the extent to which unique environmental headwinds and a lack of true breadth across ARP strategies contributed to this outcome.

Key Finding

\-

Abstract:多元化另类风险溢价（ARP）投资组合寻求使用广泛的系统交易策略产生绝对回报，包括涵盖所有主要资产类别的多种投资风格。在低发达市场债券收益率和完全估值股票的背景下，ARP提供了与传统资产类别低相关性、有吸引力的预期夏普比率和合理流动性的合理定价组合。经过一段时间的快速采用，2018-2020年间令人失望的表现让人们对ARP在机构投资组合中的作用进行了深刻的反思。这些策略应该继续作为多资产投资组合的候选者，还是过去几年的经验为它们的有用性敲响了丧钟？为了研究这个非常热门的问题，在本文中，作者使用了一系列独特的基准，利用了2000个可交易银行指数的专有数据库。他们评估最近的回报是否与长期预期一致。在这个过程中，他们考虑到独特的环境逆风和缺乏真正的广度在ARP战略的贡献这一结果的程度。
