\[2\] Calibrating an adaptive Farmer Joshi agent based model for financial markets

标题 : 基于agent的金融市场自适应模型的标定

作者 : Ivan Jericevich, Murray McKechnie, Tim Gebbie , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.09863

说明 : Comments: 9 pages, 13 figures

Abstract : We replicate the contested calibration of the Farmer and Joshi agent based model of financial markets using a genetic algorithm and a Nelder Mead with threshold accepting algorithm following Fabretti. The novelty of the Farmer Joshi model is that the dynamics are driven by trade entry and exit thresholds alone. We recover the known claim that some important stylized facts observed in financial markets cannot be easily found under calibration in particular those relating to the auto correlations in the absolute values of the price fluctuations, and sufficient kurtosis. However, rather than concerns relating to the calibration method, what is novel here is that we extended the Farmer Joshi model to include agent adaptation using an Brock and Hommes approach to strategy fitness based on trading strategy profitability. We call this an adaptive Farmer Joshi model the model allows trading agents to switch between strategies by favouring strategies that have been more profitable over some period of time determined by a free parameter fixing the profit monitoring time horizon. In the adaptive model we are able to calibrate and recover additional stylized facts, despite apparent degeneracy s. This is achieved by combining the interactions of trade entry levels with trade strategy switching. We use this to argue that for low frequency trading across days, as calibrated to daily sampled data, feed backs can be accounted for by strategy die out based on intermediate term profitability we find that the average trade monitoring horizon is approximately two to three months (or 40 to 60 days) of trading.

摘要 : 我们复制了基于Farmer和Joshi代理的金融市场模型的有争议的校准，使用遗传算法和Nelder-Mead阈值接受算法跟踪Fabretti。Farmer-Joshi模型的新颖之处在于，这种动态仅受贸易进入和出口门槛的驱动。我们恢复了一个已知的说法，即在金融市场中观察到的一些重要的程式化事实，特别是那些与价格波动绝对值的自相关性和足够的峰度有关的事实，在校准下不容易找到。然而，与校准方法相关的问题不同，这里的新颖之处在于，我们扩展了Farmer-Joshi模型，使用Brock和Hommes方法将代理适应纳入基于交易策略盈利能力的策略适应。我们称之为适应性的Farmer-Joshi模型，该模型允许交易代理在策略之间进行切换，通过一个固定利润监控时间范围的自由参数来确定在一段时间内更有利的策略。在自适应模型中，我们能够校正和恢复额外的程式化事实，尽管存在明显的退化。这是通过将贸易进入水平与贸易策略转换的相互作用结合起来实现的。我们利用这一点来论证，对于跨日低频交易，根据每日抽样数据进行校准，反馈可以通过基于中期盈利能力的策略消亡来解释。我们发现，平均交易监控期限约为交易的两到三个月（或40到60天）。

\[4\] GARCH UGH

标题 : 加希啊

作者 : Hibiki Kaibuchi, Yoshinori Kawasaki, Gilles Stupfler , 类别 ：Applications (stat.AP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.09879

说明 : -

Abstract : The Value at Risk (VaR) is a widely used instrument in financial risk management. The question of estimating the VaR of loss return distributions at extreme levels is an important question in financial applications, both from operational and regulatory perspectives in particular, the dynamic estimation of extreme VaR given the recent past has received substantial attention. We propose here a two step bias reduced estimation methodology called GARCH UGH (Unbiased Gomes de Haan), whereby financial returns are first filtered using an AR GARCH model, and then a bias reduced estimator of extreme quantiles is applied to the standardized residuals to estimate one step ahead dynamic extreme VaR. Our results indicate that the GARCH UGH estimates are more accurate than those obtained by combining conventional AR GARCH filtering and extreme value estimates from the perspective of in sample and out of sample backtestings of historical daily returns on several financial time series.

摘要 : 风险价值（VaR）是金融风险管理中广泛使用的工具。极端水平下损失收益分布的VaR估计问题是金融应用中的一个重要问题，特别是从操作和监管的角度来看，极端VaR的动态估计近年来受到了广泛的关注。本文提出了一种两步减偏估计方法GARCH-UGH（Unbiased Gomes-de-Haan），即首先利用AR-GARCH模型对财务收益进行滤波，然后对标准化残差应用极端分位数的减偏估计来估计一步超前的动态极端VaRGARCH-UGH估计比传统的AR-GARCH滤波和极值估计相结合，从样本内和样本外对多个金融时间序列的历史日收益率进行回溯检验得到的估计更为准确。

Abstract : This paper proposes a framework to investigate the value of sharing privacy protected smart meter data between domestic consumers and load serving entities. \[7\] The American put with finite time maturity and stochastic interest rate

标题 : 有限期限随机利率美式看跌期权

作者 : Cheng Cai, Tiziano De Angelis, Jan Palczewski , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.08502

说明 : Comments: 41 pages, 5 figures

Abstract : In this paper we study pricing of American put options on the Black and Scholes market with a stochastic interest rate and finite time maturity. We prove that the option value is a C 1 function of the initial time, interest rate and stock price. By means of Ito calculus we rigorously derive the option value s early exercise premium formula and the associated hedging portfolio. We prove the existence of an optimal exercise boundary splitting the state space into continuation and stopping region. The boundary has a parametrisation as a jointly continuous function of time and stock price, and it is the unique solution to an integral equation which we compute numerically. Our results hold for a large class of interest rate models including CIR and Vasicek models. We show a numerical study of the option price and the optimal exercise boundary for Vasicek model.

摘要 : 本文研究了利率随机、期限有限的Black-Scholes市场上美式看跌期权的定价问题。我们证明了期权价值是初始时间、利率和股价的c1函数。利用Ito演算严格推导了期权价值的早期行使溢价公式及相应的套期保值组合。证明了将状态空间分为连续区域和停止区域的最优运动边界的存在性。边界是时间和股价的联合连续函数，是数值计算的积分方程的唯一解。我们的结果适用于一大类利率模型，包括CIR和Vasicek模型。本文对Vasicek模型的期权价格和最优行权边界进行了数值研究。

\[10\] On the Investment Strategies in Occupational Pension Plans

标题 : 论企业年金计划的投资策略

作者 : Frank Bosserhoff, An Chen, Nils Sorensen, Mitja Stadje , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.08956

说明 : -

Abstract : Demographic changes increase the necessity to base the pension system more and more on the second and the third pillar, namely the occupational and private pension plans this paper deals with Target Date Funds (TDFs), which are a typical investment opportunity for occupational pension planners. TDFs are usually identified with a decreasing fraction of wealth invested in equity (a so called glide path) as retirement comes closer, i.e., wealth is invested more risky the younger the saver is. We investigate whether this is actually optimal in the presence of non tradable income risk in a stochastic volatility environment. The retirement planning procedure is formulated as a stochastic optimization problem. We find it is the (random) contributions that induce the optimal path exhibiting a glide path structure, both in the constant and stochastic volatility environment. Moreover, the initial wealth and the initial contribution made to a retirement account strongly influence the fractional amount of wealth to be invested in risky assets. The risk aversion of an individual mainly determines the steepness of the glide path.

摘要 : 人口结构的变化使得养老金制度越来越依赖于第二和第三支柱，即企业和私人养老金计划。本文讨论的是目标日期基金（TDFs），这是企业养老金规划者的典型投资机会。TDF通常被认为是随着退休时间的临近，投资于股票的财富比例逐渐减少（即所谓的下滑路径），也就是说，储蓄者越年轻，投资财富的风险就越大。我们研究了在随机波动环境中，当存在非交易收益风险时，这是否是最优的。退休计划过程是一个随机优化问题。我们发现，正是（随机）贡献导致了最优路径呈现下滑路径结构，无论是在常数和随机波动的环境。此外，初始财富和对退休账户的初始供款对投资于风险资产的部分财富有很大影响。个人的风险厌恶主要决定了下滑路径的陡度。

Abstract : This paper promotes the application of a path independent decomposition scheme. \[12\] A public micro pension programme in Brazil

标题 : 巴西的公共小额养老金计划

作者 : Renata Gomes Alcoforado, Alfredo D. Egídio dos Reis , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.09210

说明 : -

Abstract : Brazil is the 5th largest country in the world, despite of having a High Human Development it is the 9th most unequal country. The existing Brazilian micro pension programme is one of the safety nets for poor people. To become eligible for this benefit, each person must have an income that is less than a quarter of the Brazilian minimum monthly wage and be either over 65 or considered disabled. That minimum income corresponds to approximately 2 dollars per day. This paper analyses quantitatively some aspects of this programme in the Public Pension System of Brazil. We look for the impact of some particular economic variables on the number of people receiving the benefit, and seek if that impact significantly differs among the 27 Brazilian Federal Units. We search for heterogeneity. We perform regression and spatial cluster analysis for detection of geographical grouping.
