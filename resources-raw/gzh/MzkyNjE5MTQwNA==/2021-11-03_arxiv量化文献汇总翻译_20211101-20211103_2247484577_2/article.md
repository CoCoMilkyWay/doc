\[1\] Stock Price Prediction Using Time Series, Econometric, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning Models

标题 : 利用时间序列、计量经济学、机器学习和深度学习模型进行股票价格预测

作者 : Ananda Chatterjee, Hrisav Bhowmick, Jaydip Sen , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01137

说明 : Comments: This is the accepted version of our paper in the international conference, IEEE Mysurucon'21, which was organized in Hassan, Karnataka, India from October 24, 2021 to October 25, 2021. The paper is 8 pages long, and it contains 20 figures and 22 tables. This is the preprint of the conference paper

Abstract : For a long time, researchers have been developing a reliable and accurate predictive model for stock price prediction. According to the literature, if predictive models are correctly designed and refined, they can painstakingly and faithfully estimate future stock values. This paper demonstrates a set of time series, econometric, and various learning based models for stock price prediction. The data of Infosys, ICICI, and SUN PHARMA from the period of January 2004 to December 2019 was used here for training and testing the models to know which model performs best in which sector. One time series model (Holt Winters Exponential Smoothing), one econometric model (ARIMA), two machine Learning models (Random Forest and MARS), and two deep learning based models (simple RNN and LSTM) have been included in this paper. MARS has been proved to be the best performing machine learning model, while LSTM has proved to be the best performing deep learning model. But overall, for all three sectors IT (on Infosys data), Banking (on ICICI data), and Health (on SUN PHARMA data), MARS has proved to be the best performing model in sales forecasting.

摘要 : 长期以来，研究人员一直在开发一种可靠、准确的股票价格预测模型。根据文献，如果预测模型被正确地设计和完善，它们可以仔细而忠实地估计未来的股票价值。本文展示了一套用于股票价格预测的时间序列、计量经济学和各种基于学习的模型。这里使用Infosys、ICICI和SUN PHARMA 2004年1月至2019年12月期间的数据对模型进行培训和测试，以了解哪种模型在哪个行业表现最好。本文包括一个时间序列模型（Holt Winters指数平滑）、一个计量经济学模型（ARIMA）、两个机器学习模型（随机森林和MARS）和两个基于深度学习的模型（简单RNN和LSTM）。MARS已被证明是性能最好的机器学习模型，而LSTM已被证明是性能最好的深度学习模型。但总体而言，对于IT（基于Infosys数据）、银行（基于ICICI数据）和健康（基于SUN PHARMA数据）这三个行业，事实证明，MARS是销售预测中表现最好的模型。

\[2\] Optimal allocation to deferred income annuities

标题 : 递延收益年金的最优配置

作者 : F. Habib, H. Huang, A. Mauskopf, B. Nikolic, T.S. Salisbury , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01234

说明 : -

Abstract : In this paper we employ a lifecycle model that uses utility of consumption and bequest to determine an optimal Deferred Income Annuity (DIA) purchase policy. We lay out a mathematical framework to formalize the optimization process. The method and implementation of the optimization is explained, and the results are then analyzed. We extend our model to control for asset allocation and show how the purchase policy changes when one is allowed to vary asset allocation. Our results indicate that (i.) refundable DIAs are less appealing than non refundable DIAs because of the loss of mortality credits (ii.) the DIA allocation region is larger under the fixed asset allocation strategy due to it becoming a proxy for fixed income allocation and (iii.) when the investor is allowed to change asset allocation, DIA allocation becomes less appealing. However, a case for higher DIA allocation can be made for those individuals who perceive their longevity to be higher than the population.

摘要 : 在本文中，我们使用一个生命周期模型，该模型使用消费和遗产的效用来确定一个最优的递延收益年金（DIA）购买策略。我们提出了一个数学框架来形式化优化过程。阐述了优化的方法和实现，并对优化结果进行了分析。我们扩展了我们的模型来控制资产配置，并展示了当允许改变资产配置时，购买策略是如何变化的。我们的结果表明：（i.）由于死亡积分的损失，可退还的DIA的吸引力低于不可退还的DIA；（ii.）固定资产分配策略下的DIA分配区域更大，因为它成为固定收益分配的代理；（iii.）当允许投资者改变资产分配时，DIA分配变得不那么吸引人。然而，对于那些认为自己的寿命高于人口的个体，可以提出更高的DIA分配的理由。

\[3\] Refundable income annuities

标题 : 可退还收入年金

作者 : Moshe A. Milevsky, Thomas S. Salisbury , 类别 ：Pricing of Securities (q-fin.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01239

说明 : -

Abstract : Refundable income annuities (IA), such as cash refund and instalment refund, differ in material ways from the life only version beloved by economists. In addition to lifetime income they guarantee the annuitant or beneficiary will receive their money back albeit slowly over time. We document that refundable IAs now represent the majority of sales in the U.S., yet they are mostly ignored by insurance and pension economists. And, although their pricing, duration, and money s worth ratio is complicated by recursivity which will be explained, we offer a path forward to make refundable IAs tractable. A key result concerns the market price of cash refund IAs, when the actuarial present value is grossed up by an insurance loading. We prove that price is counterintuitively no longer a declining function of age and older buyers might pay more than younger ones. Moreover, there exists a threshold valuation rate below which no price is viable. This may also explain why inflation adjusted IAs have all but disappeared.

摘要 : 可退还的收入年金（IA），如现金退款和分期付款退款，与经济学家所钟爱的纯人寿年金在物质上有所不同。除了终身收入外，他们还保证年金受益人或受益人将收到他们的钱，尽管随着时间的推移会很慢。我们证明，可退还的国际会计准则目前占美国销售额的大部分，但保险和养老金经济学家大多忽视了这些准则。而且，尽管它们的定价、期限和货币价值比会因递归性而变得复杂，我们将对此进行解释，但我们提供了一条使可退还的国际会计准则易于处理的途径。一个关键结果涉及现金退款IAs的市场价格，当精算现值被保险负载加总时。我们证明了价格不再是年龄的递减函数，而且年纪大的买家可能比年纪小的买家支付更多。此外，存在一个阈值估值率，低于该阈值，任何价格都不可行。这也可以解释为什么经通胀调整的国际会计准则几乎消失了。

\[4\] A General Revealed Preference Test for Quasilinear Preferences

标题 : 拟线性偏好的一般揭示偏好检验

作者 : Mikhail Freer, Marco Castillo , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01248

说明 : -

Abstract : We provide a generalized revealed preference test for quasilinear preferences. The test applies to nonlinear budget sets and non convex preferences as those found in taxation and nonlinear pricing contexts. We study the prevalence of quasilinear preferences in a laboratory real effort task experiment with nonlinear wages. The experiment demonstrates the empirical relevance of our test. We find support for either convex (non separable) preferences or quasilinear preferences but weak support for the hypothesis of both quasilinear and convex preferences.

摘要 : 我们为拟线性偏好提供了一个广义的揭示偏好测试。该测试适用于非线性预算集和非凸偏好，如在税收和非线性定价环境中发现的。我们研究了准线性偏好在具有非线性工资的实验室真实努力任务实验中的普遍性。实验证明了我们测试的经验相关性。我们发现对凸（不可分离）偏好或拟线性偏好的支持，但对拟线性和凸偏好假设的支持较弱。

\[5\] Integrated Assessment Modeling of Korea 2050 Carbon Neutrality Technology Pathways

标题 : 韩国2050碳中和技术路径综合评价模型

作者 : Hanwoong Kim, Haewon McJeon, Dawoon Jung, Hanju Lee, Candelaria Bergero, Jiyong Eom , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01598

说明 : -

Abstract : This integrated assessment modeling research analyzes what Korea s 2050 carbon neutrality would require for the national energy system and the role of the power sector concerning the availability of critical mitigation technologies. Our scenario based assessments show that Korea s current policy falls short of what the nation s carbon neutrality ambition would require. Across all technology scenarios examined in this study, extensive and rapid energy system transition is imperative, requiring the large scale deployment of renewables and carbon capture & storage (CCS) early on and negative emission technologies (NETs) by the mid century. Importantly, rapid decarbonization of the power sector that goes with rapid electrification of end uses seems to be a robust national decarbonization strategy. Furthermore, we contextualize our net zero scenario results using policy costs, requirements for natural resources, and the expansion rate of zero carbon technologies. We find that the availability of nuclear power lowers the required expansion rate of renewables and CCS, alleviating any stress on terrestrial and geological systems. By contrast, the limited availability of CCS without nuclear power necessarily demands a very high penetration of renewables and significantly high policy compliance costs, which would decrease the feasibility of achieving the carbon neutrality target.

摘要 : 这种综合评估模型研究分析了韩国2050碳中和将需要什么国家能源系统和电力部门的作用，关键缓解技术的可用性。我们的基于情景的评估显示韩国目前的政策与国家碳中和野心所需的政策相去甚远。在本研究所考察的所有技术方案中，广泛而快速的能源系统转型势在必行，需要在本世纪初大规模部署可再生能源和碳捕获与储存（CCS）技术，并在本世纪中叶之前部署负排放技术（NET）。重要的是，随着终端用途的快速电气化，电力部门的快速脱碳似乎是一项强有力的国家脱碳战略。此外，我们利用政策成本、对自然资源的需求和零碳技术的扩张率，对我们的净零情景结果进行上下文分析。我们发现，核能的可用性降低了可再生能源和CCS所需的扩展速度，减轻了对陆地和地质系统的任何压力。相比之下，没有核电的CCS的有限可用性必然要求可再生能源的渗透性和政策依从性的显著提高，这将降低实现碳中和目标的可行性。

\[6\] Central Limit Theory for Models of Strategic Network Formation

标题 : 战略网络形成模型的中心极限理论

作者 : Konrad Menzel , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01678

说明 : -

Abstract : We provide asymptotic approximations to the distribution of statistics that are obtained from network data for limiting sequences that let the number of nodes (agents) in the network grow large. Network formation is permitted to be strategic in that agents incentives for link formation may depend on the ego and alter s positions in that endogenous network. Our framework does not limit the strength of these interaction effects, but assumes that the network is sparse. We show that the model can be approximated by a sampling experiment in which subnetworks are generated independently from a common equilibrium distribution, and any dependence across subnetworks is captured by state variables at the level of the entire network. Under many player asymptotics, the leading term of the approximation error to the limiting model established in Menzel (2015b) is shown to be Gaussian, with an asymptotic bias and variance that can be estimated consistently from a single network.

摘要 : 对于允许网络中节点（代理）数量增长较大的限制序列，我们提供了从网络数据获得的统计分布的渐近近似。网络形成被允许是战略性的，因为代理人对链接形成的激励可能取决于自我，并改变其在该内生网络中的位置。我们的框架并不限制这些交互效应的强度，而是假设网络是稀疏的。我们证明了该模型可以通过抽样实验来近似，在抽样实验中，子网络独立于公共平衡分布生成，并且子网络之间的任何依赖性都由整个网络级别的状态变量捕获。在多参与者渐近下，Menzel（2015b）中建立的极限模型的近似误差的前导项为高斯项，其渐近偏差和方差可从单个网络中一致估计。

\[7\] Contagious McKean Vlasov systems with heterogeneous impact and exposure

标题 : 具有异质影响和暴露的传染McKean-Vlasov系统

作者 : Zachary Feinstein, Andreas Sojmark , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.06776

说明 : Comments: 39 pages. Entirely new version of the preprint, replacing v1 which was an early draft

Abstract : We introduce a heterogeneous formulation of a contagious McKean Vlasov system, whose inherent heterogeneity comes from asymmetric interactions with a natural and highly tractable structure. It is shown that this formulation characterises the limit points of a finite particle system, deriving from a balance sheet based model of solvency contagion in interbank markets, where banks have heterogeneous exposure to and impact on the distress within the system. We also provide a simple result on global uniqueness for the full problem with common noise under a smallness condition on the strength of interactions, and we show that, in the problem without common noise, there is a unique differentiable solution up to an explosion time. Finally, we identify an intuitive and consistent way of specifying how the system should jump to resolve an instability when the contagious pressures become too large. This is known to happen even in the homogeneous version of the problem, where jumps are specified by a physical notion of solution, but no such notion currently exists for a heterogeneous formulation of the system.

摘要 : 我们介绍了一个具有传染性的McKean-Vlasov系统的异质性公式，其固有的异质性来自于具有自然和高度可处理结构的不对称相互作用。结果表明，该公式描述了有限粒子系统的极限点，该极限点源自基于资产负债表的银行间市场偿付能力传染模型，银行在该模型中对系统内的困境具有不同的风险敞口和影响。在相互作用强度很小的条件下，我们还提供了一个关于带公共噪声的完整问题全局唯一性的简单结果，并且我们证明了在没有公共噪声的问题中，在爆炸时间内存在唯一的可微解。最后，我们确定了一种直观且一致的方法，指定当传染压力变得太大时，系统应如何跳转以解决不稳定性。这是已知的，即使在问题的同质版本中也会发生，在同质版本中，跳跃是由解的物理概念指定的，但对于系统的异质公式，目前还不存在这样的概念。

\[8\] Anticipative information in a Brownian Poissonmarket

标题 : 布朗泊松市场中的预期信息

作者 : Bernardo D'Auria, José A. Salmerón , 类别 ：Probability (math.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01529

说明 : Comments: 18 pages

Abstract : The binary information collects all those events that may or may not occur. With this kind of variables, a large amount of information can be captured, in particular, about financial assets and their future trends. In our paper, we assume the existence of some anticipative information of this type in a market whose risky asset dynamics evolve according to a Brownian motion and a Poisson process. Using Malliavin calculus and filtration enlargement techniques, we compute the semimartingale decomposition of the mentioned processes and, in the pure jump case, we give the exact value of the information. Many examples are shown, where the anticipative information is related to some conditions that the constituent processes or their running maximum may or may not verify.

摘要 : 二进制信息收集所有可能发生或不发生的事件。通过这类变量，可以获取大量信息，特别是关于金融资产及其未来趋势的信息。在本文中，我们假设在一个风险资产动态按照布朗运动和泊松过程演化的市场中存在这种类型的预期信息。利用Malliavin演算和滤波放大技术，我们计算了上述过程的半鞅分解，在纯跳跃情况下，我们给出了信息的精确值。给出了许多示例，其中预期信息与组成过程或其运行最大值可能验证或可能无法验证的某些条件有关。

\[9\] AIRCC Clim

标题 : AIRCCLIM

作者 : Francisco Estrada, Oscar Calderón-Bustamante, Wouter Botzen, Julián A. Velasco, Richard S.J. Tol , 类别 ：Atmospheric and Oceanic Physics (physics.ao-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01762

说明 : -

Abstract : Complex physical models are the most advanced tools available for producing realistic simulations of the climate system. However, such levels of realism imply high computational cost and restrictions on their use for policymaking and risk assessment. Two central characteristics of climate change are uncertainty and that it is a dynamic problem in which international actions can significantly alter climate projections and information needs, including partial and full compliance of global climate goals. Here we present AIRCC Clim, a simple climate model emulator that produces regional probabilistic climate change projections of monthly and annual temperature and precipitation, as well as risk measures, based both on standard and user defined emissions scenarios for six greenhouse gases. AIRCC Clim emulates 37 atmosphere ocean coupled general circulation models with low computational and technical requirements for the user. This standalone, user friendly software is designed for a variety of applications including impact assessments, climate policy evaluation and integrated assessment modelling.

摘要 : 复杂物理模型是制作气候系统真实模拟的最先进工具。然而，这种现实水平意味着高计算成本，并限制了其用于决策和风险评估。气候变化的两个核心特征是不确定性，这是一个动态问题，国际行动可以大大改变气候预测和信息需求，包括部分和完全遵守全球气候目标。这里我们介绍了AIRCCLIM，一个简单的气候模型模拟器，它根据六种温室气体的标准和用户定义的排放情景，生成月度和年度温度和降水的区域概率气候变化预测，以及风险度量。AIRCCLIM模拟了37个大气-海洋耦合的大气环流模型，对用户的计算和技术要求较低。这一独立的、用户友好的软件设计用于各种应用，包括影响评估、气候政策评估和综合评估建模。

\[10\] Location inference on social media data for agile monitoring of public health crises

标题 : 基于社交媒体数据的公共卫生危机敏捷监测位置推断

作者 : Angela E. Kilby, Charlie Denhart , 类别 ：Computers and Society (cs.CY)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01778

说明 : Comments: 10 pages, 7 figures, 5 tables

Abstract : The Covid 19 pandemic has intersected with the opioid epidemic to create a unique public health crisis, with the health and economic consequences of the virus and associated lockdowns compounding pre existing social and economic stressors associated with rising opioid and heroin use and abuse. In order to better understand these interlocking crises, we use social media data to extract qualitative and quantitative insights on the experiences of opioid users during the Covid 19 pandemic. In particular, we use an unsupervised learning approach to create a rich geolocated data source for public health surveillance and analysis. To do this we first infer the location of 26,000 Reddit users that participate in opiate related sub communities (subreddits) by combining named entity recognition, geocoding, density based clustering, and heuristic methods. Our strategy achieves 63 percent accuracy at state level location inference on a manually annotated reference dataset. We then leverage the geospatial nature of our user cohort to answer policy relevant questions about the impact of varying state level policy approaches that balance economic versus health concerns during Covid 19.
