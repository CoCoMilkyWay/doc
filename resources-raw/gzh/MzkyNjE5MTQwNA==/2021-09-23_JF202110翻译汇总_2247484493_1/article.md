\[1\] The Cross Section of MBS Returns

标题:MBS返回的横截面

作者:PETER DIEP,ANDREA L. EISFELDT,SCOTT RICHARDSON

首次发表时间:15 June 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13055

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13055

Abstract : We present a simple, linear asset pricing model of the cross section of Mortgage-Backed Security (MBS) returns. MBS earn risk premia as compensation for their exposure to prepayment risk. We measure prepayment risk and estimate risk loadings using prepayment forecasts versus realizations. Estimated loadings on prepayment risk decrease monotonically in securities' coupons relative to the par coupon, consistent with the predicted effect of prepayment on bond value. Prepayment risk appears to be priced by specialized MBS investors. The price of prepayment risk changes sign over time with the sign of a representative MBS investor's exposure to prepayment shocks.

Abstract :我们提出了一个简单的，线性资产定价模型的横截面抵押担保证券（MBS）的回报。MBS赚取风险溢价，作为其提前还款风险敞口的补偿。我们衡量提前还款风险，并使用提前还款预测与实现来估计风险负荷。预付风险的估计负荷相对于票面价值在证券券中单调递减，与预付债券对债券价值的预测效果一致。提前还款风险似乎由专业MBS投资者定价。随着代表性MBS投资者暴露于提前还款冲击的迹象，提前还款风险的价格随着时间的推移而变化。

\[2\] Reinvestment Risk and the Equity Term Structure

标题:再投资风险与股权期限结构

作者:ANDREI S. GONÇALVES

首次发表时间:27 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13035

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13035

Abstract : The equity term structure is downward sloping at long maturities. I estimate an Intertemporal Capital Asset Pricing Model (ICAPM) to show that the trade-off between market and reinvestment risk explains this pattern. Intuitively, while long-term dividend claims are highly exposed to market risk, they are good hedges for reinvestment risk because dividend prices rise as expected returns decline, and longer-term claims are more sensitive to discount rates. In the estimated ICAPM, reinvestment risk dominates at long maturities, inducing relatively low risk premia on long-term dividend claims. The model is also consistent with the equity term structure cyclicality and the upward-sloping bond term structure.

Abstract :长期债券的股权期限结构向下倾斜。我估计了一个跨期资本资产定价模型（ICAPM），以表明市场风险和再投资风险之间的权衡解释了这种模式。从直觉上看，虽然长期股息债权高度暴露于市场风险，但它们是再投资风险的良好对冲工具，因为股息价格随着预期回报的下降而上升，而长期债权对贴现率更为敏感。在估计的ICAPM中，再投资风险在长期债券中占主导地位，导致长期股息债权的风险溢价相对较低。该模型也符合股权期限结构的周期性和向上倾斜的债券期限结构。

\[3\] Inventory Management, Dealers' Connections, and Prices in Over-the-Counter Markets

标题:库存管理、经销商关系和场外市场价格

作者:JEAN-EDOUARD COLLIARD,THIERRY FOUCAULT,PETER HOFFMANN

首次发表时间:28 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13034

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13034

Abstract : We propose a new model of trading in over-the-counter markets. Dealers accumulate inventories by trading with end-investors and trade among each other to reduce their inventory holding costs. Core dealers use a more efficient trading technology than peripheral dealers, who are heterogeneously connected to core dealers and trade with each other bilaterally. Connectedness affects prices and allocations if and only if the peripheral dealers' aggregate inventory position differs from zero. Price dispersion increases in the size of this position. The model generates new predictions about the effects of dealers' connectedness and dealers' aggregate inventories on prices.

Abstract :我们提出了一种新的场外交易模式。交易商通过与最终投资者交易积累存货，并相互交易以降低存货持有成本。核心交易商使用比外围交易商更高效的交易技术，外围交易商与核心交易商之间存在异质联系，并通过双边方式进行交易。当且仅当外围经销商的总库存头寸不同于零时，连通性才会影响价格和分配。价格分散增加了该头寸的规模。该模型对经销商的连通性和经销商的总库存对价格的影响做出了新的预测。

\[4\] Tracking Retail Investor Activity

标题:追踪散户投资者活动

作者:EKKEHART BOEHMER,CHARLES M. JONES,XIAOYAN ZHANG,XINRAN ZHANG

首次发表时间:28 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13033

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13033

Abstract : We provide an easy method to identify marketable retail purchases and sales using recent, publicly available U.S. equity transactions data. Individual stocks with net buying by retail investors outperform stocks with negative imbalances by approximately 10 bps over the following week. Less than half of the predictive power of marketable retail order imbalance is attributable to order flow persistence, while the rest cannot be explained by contrarian trading (proxy for liquidity provision) or public news sentiment. There is suggestive, but only suggestive, evidence that retail marketable orders might contain firm-level information that is not yet incorporated into prices.

Abstract :我们提供了一种简单的方法，使用最近公开的美国股票交易数据来识别适销对路的零售购买和销售。散户投资者净买入的个股在随后一周的表现优于负失衡的个股约10个基点。可销售零售订单不平衡的预测能力不到一半可归因于订单流的持续性，而其余部分无法用反向交易（流动性提供的代理）或公共新闻情绪来解释。有暗示性的证据，但只是暗示性的证据表明，零售可销售订单可能包含尚未纳入价格的公司级信息。

\[5\] Can the Market Multiply and Divide? Non-Proportional Thinking in Financial Markets

标题:市场能繁衍生息吗？金融市场中的非比例思维

作者:KELLY SHUE,RICHARD R. TOWNSEND

首次发表时间:28 May 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13059

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13059

Abstract : We hypothesize that investors partially think about stock price changes in dollar rather than percentage units, leading to more extreme return responses to news for lower-priced stocks. Consistent with such non-proportional thinking, we find a doubling in price is associated with a 20% to 30% decline in volatility and beta (controlling for size/liquidity). To identify a causal price effect, we show that volatility jumps following stock splits and drops following reverse splits. Lower-priced stocks also respond more strongly to firm-specific news. Non-proportional thinking helps explain asset pricing patterns such as the size-volatility/beta relation, the leverage effect puzzle, and return drift and reversals.

Abstract :我们假设投资者部分地以美元而非百分比单位来考虑股票价格的变化，从而导致对价格较低的股票的消息做出更极端的回报反应。与这种非比例思维一致，我们发现价格翻倍与波动性和贝塔值（控制规模/流动性）下降20%至30%有关。为了确定一种因果价格效应，我们证明了股票分割后波动率上升，反向分割后波动率下降。低价股对公司特定消息的反应也更强烈。非比例思维有助于解释资产定价模式，如规模波动率/贝塔系数关系、杠杆效应难题以及收益漂移和反转。

\[6\] The Misallocation of Finance

标题:金融配置不当

作者:TONI M. WHITED,JAKE ZHAO

首次发表时间:28 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13031

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13031

Abstract : We estimate real losses arising from the cross-sectional misallocation of financial liabilities. Extending a production-based framework of misallocation measurement to the liabilities side of the balance sheet and using manufacturing firm data from the United States and China, we find significant misallocation of debt and equity in China but not the United States. Reallocating liabilities of firms in China to mimic U.S. efficiency would produce gains of 51% to 69% in real value-added, with only 17% to 21% stemming from inefficient debt-equity combinations. For Chinese firms that are large or in developed cities, we estimate lower distortionary financing costs.

Abstract :我们估计因金融负债的横向错配而产生的实际损失。将基于生产的错配计量框架扩展到资产负债表的负债方面，并使用来自美国和中国的制造业企业数据，我们发现中国（而非美国）存在严重的债务和股权错配。将中国企业的负债重新配置以模仿美国的效率将产生51%到69%的实际增加值收益，只有17%到21%来自于效率低下的债务-股权组合。对于大型或发达城市的中国企业，我们估计扭曲性融资成本较低。

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13058

Abstract : We study the effect of a change in property rights on employee behavior in the financial advice industry. Our identification comes from staggered firm-level entry into the Protocol for Broker Recruiting, which waived nonsolicitation clauses for advisor transitions among member firms, effectively transferring ownership of client relationships from the firm to the advisor. \[9\] Do Physiological and Spiritual Factors Affect Economic Decisions?

标题:生理和精神因素会影响经济决策吗？

作者:CEM DEMIROGLU,OGUZHAN OZBAS,RUI C. SILVA,MEHMET FATİH ULU

首次发表时间:28 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13032

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13032

Abstract : We examine the effects of physiology and spiritual sentiment on economic decision-making in the context of Ramadan, an entire lunar month of daily fasting and increased spiritual reflection in the Muslim faith. Using an administrative data set of bank loans originated in Turkey during 2003 to 2013, we find that small business loans originated during Ramadan are 15% more likely to default within two years of origination. Loans originated in hot Ramadans, when adverse physiological effects of fasting are greatest, and those approved by the busiest bank branches perform worse. Despite their worse performance, Ramadan loans have lower credit spreads.

Abstract :我们在斋月的背景下研究生理和精神情绪对经济决策的影响，斋月是整个农历月的每日禁食，穆斯林信仰中的精神反映增加。使用2003年至2013年期间源自土耳其的银行贷款管理数据集，我们发现源自斋月的小企业贷款在发放后两年内违约的可能性高出15%。贷款起源于炎热的斋月，当时禁食对生理的不利影响最大，而最繁忙的银行分行批准的贷款表现更差。尽管斋月贷款表现较差，但信用利差较低。

\[10\] Structuring Mortgages for Macroeconomic Stability

标题:为宏观经济稳定构建抵押贷款

作者:JOHN Y. CAMPBELL,NUNO CLARA,JOÃO F. COCCO

首次发表时间:24 May 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13056

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13056

Abstract : We study mortgage design features aimed at stabilizing the macroeconomy. We model overlapping generations of borrowers and an infinitely lived risk-averse representative lender. Mortgages are priced using an equilibrium pricing kernel derived from the lender's endogenous consumption. We consider an adjustable-rate mortgage with an option that during recessions allows borrowers to pay only interest on their loan and extend its maturity. The option stabilizes consumption growth over the business cycle, shifts defaults to expansions, and enhances welfare. The cyclical properties of the contract are attractive to a risk-averse lender so that the mortgage can be provided at a relatively low cost.

Abstract :我们研究旨在稳定宏观经济的抵押贷款设计特征。我们为重叠的几代借款人和无限生存的风险规避代表贷款人建模。抵押贷款的定价使用了从贷款人的内生消费中衍生出来的均衡定价核心。我们考虑可调整利率抵押贷款的选择，在衰退期间允许借款人只支付其贷款利息，并延长其到期日。这一选择在整个商业周期内稳定消费增长，将默认值转变为扩张，并提高福利。合同的周期性特征对规避风险的贷款人具有吸引力，因此可以以相对较低的成本提供抵押。

\[11\] Out-of-Town Home Buyers and City Welfare

标题:外地购房者与城市福利

作者:JACK FAVILUKIS,STIJN VAN NIEUWERBURGH

首次发表时间:22 May 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13057

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13057

Abstract : Many cities have attracted a flurry of out-of-town (OOT) home buyers. Such capital inflows affect house prices, rents, construction, labor income, wealth, and ultimately welfare. We develop an equilibrium model to quantify the welfare effects of OOT home buyers for the typical U.S. metropolitan area. When OOT investors buy 10% of the housing in the city center and 5% in the suburbs, welfare among residents falls by 0.61% in consumption-equivalent units. House prices and rents rise substantially, resulting in welfare gains for owners and losses for renters. Policies that tax OOT buyers or mandate renting out vacant property mitigate welfare losses.

Abstract :许多城市吸引了大批外地购房者。这些资本流入影响房价、租金、建筑、劳动收入、财富，最终影响福利。我们开发了一个均衡模型来量化典型美国大都市地区购房者的福利效应。当投资者在市中心购买10%的住房，在郊区购买5%的住房时，居民的福利在消费当量单位中下降了0.61%。房价和租金大幅上涨，导致业主的福利收益和租客的损失。对购房者征税或强制出租空置房产的政策可以减轻福利损失。

\[12\] Prospect Theory and Stock Market Anomalies

标题:前景理论与股市异常

作者:NICHOLAS BARBERIS,LAWRENCE J. JIN,BAOLIAN WANG

首次发表时间:05 June 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13061

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13061

Abstract : We present a new model of asset prices in which investors evaluate risk according to prospect theory and examine its ability to explain 23 prominent stock market anomalies. The model incorporates all of the elements of prospect theory, accounts for investors' prior gains and losses, and makes quantitative predictions about an asset's average return based on empirical estimates of the asset's return volatility, return skewness, and past capital gain. We find that the model can help explain a majority of the 23 anomalies.

Abstract :我们提出了一个新的资产价格模型，在该模型中，投资者根据前景理论评估风险，并检验其解释23个突出的股市异常的能力。该模型综合了前景理论的所有要素，考虑了投资者之前的收益和损失，并基于对资产回报波动性、回报偏斜和过去资本收益的经验估计，对资产的平均回报进行了定量预测。我们发现该模型有助于解释23个异常中的大部分。
