\[1\] Analyst Information Contagion

标题:分析师信息传染

作者:Zilong Niu

出处:-

Abstract : This paper provides empirical evidence of information contagion in equity analysts’ stock recommendations. The information contagion hypothesis predicts that agents consider idiosyncratic bad news on one asset as potential systematic news and update beliefs on other assets accordingly. We create an analyst-level measure of idiosyncratic shock by combining stock returns with analyst recommendations. We show that analysts that are exposed to an idiosyncratic shock are more likely to downgrade other stocks. Firm fundamentals do not explain the results as we compare analysts covering the same stock in the same month. The results hold similarly for analysts and firms with different characteristics. The market only partially adjusts to the information contagion effect on recommendation revisions, causing prices to move away from fundamentals.

Keywords : None

\[2\] Trades, Quotes, and Information Shares

标题:交易、报价和信息共享

作者:Björn Hagströmer,Albert J. Menkveld

出处:-

Abstract : Information arrives at securities markets through price quotes and trades. Informed traders impose adverse-selection costs on quote suppliers. This creates incentives for the latter to identify relatively uninformed groups and trade with them off-exchange. The marketplace turns hybrid, at the cost of thinner, highly informed (toxic) volume at the center. This pattern has largely eluded econometricians, because the standard approach to measuring information shares is biased against finding it. We show why this is the case, and design a bias-free approach. The novel approach shows that, indeed, the conjectured pattern is strongly present in the data.

Keywords : price quotes, trades, information share

\[4\] Are Uncertain Firms Riskier?

标题:不确定的公司风险更大吗？

作者:Fahiz Baba Yara,Carter Davis,Fotis Grigoris,Preetesh Kantak

出处:-

Abstract : We use novel data on firm-level attention to characterize firms’ exposures to uncertainty. Our data captures a daily cross-section of firm-employee reading across thousands of topics. We use natural language processing tools to assess the types of topics firms are reading and show that firms that allocate relatively more attention to uncertainty versus other business-related news have nearly 15% higher CAPM betas. Consistent with their higher discount rates, these uncertain firms have relatively lower investment rates: higher attention to uncertainty relates to more than 10% lower investment and 6% lower hiring on an annual basis.

Keywords : Economic Uncertainty, Equity Premium, Risk, Hedging, Investment, Employment

\[5\] The Evolving Academic Field of Climate Finance

标题:气候金融学术领域的演变

作者:Matteo Gasparini,Peter Tufano

出处:-

Abstract : The urgency and the magnitude of climate change will affect every aspect of our economies, societies, and planet. The academic finance research has begun to study the financial implications of global warming, although this body of literature is small, has distinct geographic tilts, draws young researchers, and much remains outside of the traditional finance domain. We explore, quantitatively and qualitatively, the emerging field of climate finance. We discuss its relevance for finance research and teaching and provide implications for financial economist and practitioners - in particular the need to incorporate this massive externality in valuation and risk analyses.

\[6\] Data Sales, Asset Prices and Competition in Product Market

标题:数据销售、资产价格与产品市场竞争

作者:Zhigang Qiu,Luyao Shen,Ziyue Wang

出处:-

Abstract : We develop a data sales model with feedback effect to study how alternative data affects interaction between the financial and product markets. The asset price in the financial market aggregates information from speculators who purchase data from a monopolistic data vendor and feeds back to the product market. The information obtained by young firm in the product market both from the asset prices in the financial market and the data purchased from the data vendor determines the competition in the product market. In addition to the positive effect by more information from alternative data, data-sales in financial market also has negative indirect effect of information leakage through asset price which affects the data-sales in product market under the feedback effect. When the size of data endowment or noise trading increases, we observe non-monotonic changes in the market quality variables of the financial market, market competition and consumer welfare of the product market, influenced by vendor’s endogenous supply of data.

Keywords : Alternative data, Data sales, Learning from prices, Price informativeness, Real efficiency

\[7\] Risk Aggregation, Tail Risk, Correlation: Capital Allocation Efficiency and Regulator-Set Standard Models for Bank Capital

标题:风险聚合、尾部风险、相关性：资本配置效率和监管机构设定的银行资本标准模型

作者:Roberto Violi

出处:-

Abstract : In modern frameworks for financial regulation such as Basel III, IV as well as Solvency II, financial institutions are regulated to maintain a certain level of capital to prepare for potential future losses. In this paper, we take the perspective of a regulator who designs regulatory capital principles to calculate the amount of capital required for financial institutions bearing risks.We review the implications for regulatory capital requirement resulting from the risk model and risk classes aggregation approaches in the Standardised Approach (SA) and Simplified Standard Approach (SSA) frameworks of the FRTB. We highlight a number of significant issues for the efficiency of regulatory capital allocation in the banking sector, as a result of the risk measure implementation framework and adopted risk aggregation methods in the SSA. In particular, we show that risk aggregation turns out to be inconsistent with a coherent-type of risk measure, which would be a necessary condition for a “coherent” capital allocation rule implementation (such as the Euler rule). Moreover, we reckon that aggregating risk classes by adding up stand-alone risk measures (e.g., assuming perfect correlation across risk classes) can induce large overestimates of capital requirements within the SSA framework. According to the Basel Committee of Banking Supervision estimates banks that are likely to adopt the SA approaches are expected to reckon an average increase in regulatory capital of 30 (SA banks) and 58 percent (SSA banks), while IMA banks' average capital requirement increase is limited to 20 percent.We argue, based on some recent academic studies and modeling evidence on financial risk, that the implied excess capital as a result of assuming perfect correlation across risk classes in the SSA framework cannot be convincingly justified by appealing to a generic quest for prudent regulatory capital fixing rule, (perhaps) anchored into some (vaguely specified) concept of “model risk”, whose technical apparatus needs some effective expedient to be pragmatically implemented in a (simplified) risk model approach. Sure enough, model uncertainty is a vital component of the current challenges in risk measurement, and therefore the regulator should design risk measures encouraging well-understood prudent decisions over (less understood) risky ones. From this perspective robust regulation should be a desirable goal. To achieve such an objective, simple – but not simpler – rules are needed.

Keywords : Risk Models; Banks; Economic Capital; Regulatory Capital Requirements; Portfolio Choice; Investment Decisions

\[8\] Do Credit Rating Agencies Learn from the Options Market?

标题:信用评级机构从期权市场中学习吗？

作者:Paul Brockman,Musa Subasi,Jundong (Jeff) Wang,Eliza Xia Zhang

出处:Management Science, Forthcoming

Abstract : Do credit rating agencies (CRAs) learn from the options market? We examine this question by exploring the relation between options trading activity and credit rating accuracy. We find that as options trading volume increases, credit ratings become more responsive to expected credit risk and exhibit greater ability to predict future defaults. We also find that CRAs rely more on the options market as a source of ratings-related information when firm default risk is higher, options trading is more informative, manager-provided information is of lower quality, and firm uncertainty is higher. Our results are robust to a number of sensitivity tests, including alternative measures of options trading and credit rating accuracy. We reach similar inferences using various approaches to address endogeneity issues, including difference-in-difference analyses and an instrumental variables approach. Overall, our findings are consistent with the view that CRAs incorporate unique information from the options market into their rating decisions which, in turn, improves credit rating accuracy.

Keywords : Credit Rating; Credit Risk; Default Probability; Credit Rating Accuracy; Options Market

\[9\] Feedback Between the Financial Market and the Product Market

标题:金融市场和产品市场之间的反馈

作者:Ken Deng,Joel D. Shapiro

出处:-

Abstract : Financial markets aggregate information about firm profitability. However, consumers, a key driver of profitability, may react to information coming from markets. This creates a feedback effect. We examine a model in which information from financial markets may push consumers to act uniformly, eliminating gains from speculation and pushing the stock price away from fundamentals. Firm disclosure may reduce the firm's power to set product prices - thus creating incentives for misreporting. Speculators may benefit from firm disclosure, providing incentives for them to engage in activism.

Keywords : Feedback, Real Effects of Financial Markets

\[10\] Cracking Down on Fake State-Owned Enterprises in China

标题:中国打击假冒国有企业

作者:Kam C. Chan,Chao Yan,Xinran Zhang,Huijie Zhong

出处:-

Abstract : Using a unique list of 528 fake state-owned enterprises (SOEs) exposed in China, we examine whether and how investors react to the government’s actions of property rights protection. Our results show that real SOEs with more subsidiaries, more pyramid layers, and higher popularity are more likely to be targeted by wrongdoers. We find that the exposure of fake SOEs led to a significant increase in stock prices of the listed central SOEs controlled by the State Council. Further analysis shows that the stock price rise is driven by both the cash flow effect and the risk effect. We also find that the value impact of the crackdown is more pronounced for the listed central SOEs with less media coverage, located in weaker legal protection regions, and facing more competition. Overall, our findings provide support for the effectiveness of property rights protection through exposure, in a non-litigation pathway, in enhancing firm value.

Keywords : SOEs; Property rights protection; Stock returns; Event study
