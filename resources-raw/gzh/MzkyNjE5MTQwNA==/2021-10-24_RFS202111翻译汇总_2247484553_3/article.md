\[1\] COVID-19 and Its Impact on Financial Markets and the Real Economy

标题:新冠病毒-19及其对金融市场和实体经济的影响

作者:Itay Goldstein, Ralph S J Koijen, Holger M Mueller

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5135/6337991; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab085

Abstract : The COVID-19 pandemic severely disrupted financial markets and the real economy worldwide. These extraordinary events prompted large monetary and fiscal policy interventions. Recognizing the unusual nature of the shock, the academic community has produced an impressive amount of research during the last year. Macro-finance models have been extended to analyze the impact of epidemics. Empirical papers study the origins and consequences of the disruptions and the impact of policy interventions. New research evaluates the ongoing financial fragility and its relation to previous episodes and regulations. This special issue contains early contributions to this important and rapidly developing literature.1

Abstract:新冠疫情严重扰乱了全球金融市场和实体经济。这些非同寻常的事件引发了大规模的货币和财政政策干预。认识到这次冲击的不寻常性质，学术界在去年进行了大量的研究。宏观金融模型已经扩展到分析流行病的影响。实证论文研究了中断的起源和后果以及政策干预的影响。新的研究评估了当前的金融脆弱性及其与以往事件和监管的关系。本期特刊包含对这一重要且迅速发展的文献的早期贡献。1

\[2\] The Macroeconomics of Epidemics

标题:流行病的宏观经济学

作者:Martin S Eichenbaum, Sergio Rebelo, Mathias Trabandt

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5149/6213914; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab040

Abstract : We extend the canonical epidemiology model to study the interaction between economic decisions and epidemics. Our model implies that people cut back on consumption and work to reduce the chances of being infected. These decisions reduce the severity of the epidemic but exacerbate the size of the associated recession. The competitive equilibrium is not socially optimal because infected people do not fully internalize the effect of their economic decisions on the spread of the virus. In our benchmark model, the best simple containment policy increases the severity of the recession but saves roughly half a million lives in the United States.

Abstract:我们扩展了经典流行病学模型来研究经济决策和流行病之间的相互作用。我们的模型意味着人们减少消费和工作以减少感染的机会。这些决定降低了疫情的严重性，但加剧了相关衰退的规模。竞争均衡不是社会最优的，因为受感染者没有充分将其经济决策对病毒传播的影响内在化。在我们的基准模型中，最好的简单遏制政策增加了衰退的严重性，但在美国挽救了大约50万人的生命。

\[3\] Optimal Mitigation Policies in a Pandemic: Social Distancing and Working from Home

标题:流行病中的最佳缓解政策：社会距离和在家工作

作者:Callum Jones, Thomas Philippon, Venky Venkateswaran

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5188/6364911; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab076

Abstract : We study an economy’s response to an unexpected epidemic. The spread of the disease can be mitigated by reducing consumption and hours worked in the office. Working from home is subject to learning-by-doing. Private agents’ rational incentives are relatively weak and fatalistic. The planner recognizes infection and congestion externalities and implements front-loaded mitigation. Under our calibration, the planner reduces cumulative fatalities by 48|$\\%$| compared to 24|$\\%$| by private agents, although with a sharper drop in consumption. Our model can replicate key industry and/or occupational-level patterns and explain how large variations in outcomes across regions can stem from small initial differences.

Abstract:我们研究经济体对突发疫情的反应。通过减少消耗和在办公室工作的时间，可以减轻疾病的传播。在家工作需要边干边学。私人代理人的理性激励相对较弱，且具有宿命性。规划者认识到感染和拥挤的外部性，并实施前置缓解措施。在我们的校准下，与私人代理的24 |$\\%$相比，计划者将累计死亡人数减少48$\\%$，尽管消耗量下降更大。我们的模型可以复制关键的行业和/或职业水平模式，并解释不同地区的结果有多大的差异可能源于微小的初始差异。

\[4\] Implications of Stochastic Transmission Rates for Managing Pandemic Risks

标题:随机传播率对管理大流行风险的影响

作者:Harrison Hong, Neng Wang, Jinqiang Yang

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5224/6131442; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa132

Abstract : We introduce aggregate transmission shocks to an epidemic model and link firm valuations to infections via an asset pricing framework with vaccines. Infections lower earnings growth but firms can mitigate damages. We estimate a large reproduction number |${\\mathcal R}\_0$| and transmission volatility for COVID-19. Using these estimates, we quantify the bias of deterministic approximations based on |${\\mathcal R}\_0$|⁠. Our model generates predictions consistent with the data: unexpected infection resurgence, nonmonotonic mitigation policies, and higher price-to-earnings ratios during a pandemic. Valuations would be significantly lower absent mitigation and a high vaccine arrival rate.

Abstract:我们将总传播冲击引入流行病模型，并通过疫苗资产定价框架将公司估值与感染联系起来。感染降低了收益增长，但企业可以减轻损失。我们估计了新冠病毒-19的大量繁殖数${\\mathcal R}u 0$|和传播波动性。利用这些估计，我们量化了基于${\\mathcal R}u 0的确定性近似的偏差$|⁠. 我们的模型产生了与数据一致的预测：意外的感染死灰复燃，非单调的缓解政策，以及大流行期间较高的市盈率。如果没有缓解措施和较高的疫苗接种率，估值将显著降低。

\[5\] Business Restrictions and COVID-19 Fatalities

标题:业务限制和新冠病毒-19死亡

作者:Matthew Spiegel, Heather Tookes

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5266/6304878; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab069

Abstract : We collect a time-series database of business and related restrictions for every county in the United States from March through December 2020. We find strong evidence consistent with the idea that employee mask policies, mask mandates for the general population, restaurant and bar closures, gym closures, and high-risk business closures reduce future fatality growth. Other business restrictions, such as second-round closures of low- to medium-risk businesses and personal care/spa services, did not generate consistent evidence of lowered fatality growth and may have been counterproductive.

Abstract:从2020年3月到12月，我们收集了美国每个县的商业和相关限制的时间序列数据库。我们发现强有力的证据与以下观点一致：员工口罩政策、普通人群口罩要求、餐馆和酒吧关闭、健身房关闭和高风险企业关闭可降低未来死亡率增长。其他业务限制，如第二轮低风险至中等风险业务和个人护理/水疗服务的关闭，没有产生降低死亡率增长的一致证据，可能会产生反作用。

\[6\] When Selling Becomes Viral: Disruptions in Debt Markets in the COVID-19 Crisis and the Fed’s Response

标题:当抛售成为病毒式传播：新冠肺炎危机中债务市场的混乱和美联储的反应

作者:Valentin Haddad, Alan Moreira, Tyler Muir

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5309/6123759; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa145

Abstract : We document extreme disruption in debt markets during the COVID-19 crisis: a severe price crash accompanied by significant dislocations at the safer end of the credit spectrum. Investment-grade corporate bonds traded at a discount to credit default swaps; exchange-traded funds traded at a discount to net asset value, more so for safer bonds. The Federal Reserve’s announcement of corporate bond purchases caused these dislocations to disappear and prices to recover. These facts inform potential theories of the disruption. The best explanation is an acute liquidity need for specific bond investors, such as mutual funds, leading them to liquidate large positions.

Abstract:我们记录了新冠肺炎危机期间债务市场的极度混乱：严重的价格崩溃，伴随着信贷领域更安全一端的严重混乱。以信用违约掉期折价交易的投资级公司债券；交易所交易基金的交易价格低于资产净值，而更安全的债券更是如此。美联储（Federal Reserve）宣布购买公司债券，导致这些混乱局面消失，价格回升。这些事实为潜在的破坏理论提供了依据。最好的解释是，共同基金等特定债券投资者迫切需要流动性，这导致他们清算大量头寸。

\[7\] Corporate Bond Liquidity during the COVID-19 Crisis

标题:新冠疫情期间的公司债券流动性

作者:Mahyar Kargar, Benjamin Lester, David Lindsay, Shuo Liu, Pierre-Olivier Weill ...

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5352/6279757; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab063

Abstract : We study liquidity conditions in the corporate bond market during the COVID-19 pandemic. We document that the cost of trading immediately via risky-principal trades dramatically increased at the height of the sell-off, forcing customers to shift toward slower agency trades. Exploiting eligibility requirements, we show that the Federal Reserve’s corporate credit facilities have had a positive effect on market liquidity. A structural estimation reveals that customers’ willingness to pay for immediacy increased by about 200 bps per dollar of transaction, but quickly subsided after the Fed announced its interventions. Dealers’ marginal cost also increased substantially but did not fully subside.

Abstract:我们研究了新冠疫情期间公司债券市场的流动性状况。我们记录了通过高风险本金交易立即进行交易的成本在抛售高峰期急剧增加，迫使客户转向较慢的代理交易。利用资格要求，我们表明，美联储的企业信贷工具对市场流动性产生了积极影响。一项结构性估计显示，每一美元交易中，客户的即时支付意愿增加了约200个基点，但在美联储宣布干预措施后，这种意愿迅速消退。经销商的边际成本也大幅上升，但并未完全下降。

\[8\] Liquidity Restrictions, Runs, and Central Bank Interventions: Evidence from Money Market Funds

标题:流动性限制、挤兑和央行干预：来自货币市场基金的证据

作者:Lei Li, Yi Li, Marco Macchiavelli, Xing (Alex) Zhou

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5402/6289999; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab065

Abstract : Liquidity restrictions on investors, like the redemption gates and liquidity fees introduced in the 2016 money market fund (MMF) reform, are meant to improve financial stability. However, we find evidence that such liquidity restrictions exacerbated the run on prime MMFs during the COVID-19 crisis. Our results indicate that gates and fees could generate strategic complementarities among investors in crisis times. Severe outflows from prime MMFs led the Federal Reserve to intervene with the Money Market Mutual Fund Liquidity Facility (MMLF). Using MMLF microdata, we show how the provision of “liquidity of last resort” stabilized prime funds.

Abstract:对投资者的流动性限制，如2016年货币市场基金（MMF）改革中引入的赎回门和流动性费用，旨在改善金融稳定。然而，我们发现有证据表明，这种流动性限制加剧了新冠疫情期间主要货币市场基金的挤兑。我们的研究结果表明，在危机时期，盖茨和费能在投资者之间产生战略互补性。主要货币市场基金的严重外流导致美联储干预货币市场共同基金流动性工具（MMLF）。利用MMLF微观数据，我们展示了“最后的流动性”是如何稳定优质基金的。

\[9\] How Did Depositors Respond to COVID-19?

标题:储户对新冠病毒-19有何反应？

作者:Ross Levine, Chen Lin, Mingzhu Tai, Wensi Xie

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5438/6279756; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab062

Abstract : Why did banks experience massive deposit inflows during the pandemic? We discover that deposit interest rates at bank branches in counties with higher COVID-19 infection rates fell by more than rates at branches—even branches of the same bank—in counties with lower infection rates. Credit drawdowns, national policies, such as the Payment Protection Program, and a flight-to-safety do not account for these cross-branch changes in deposit rates. Evidence suggests that higher local COVID-19 infection rates are associated with households’ greater anxiety about future job and income losses, anxiety that induces households to reduce spending and increase deposits.

Abstract:为什么银行在流感大流行期间经历了大规模存款流入？我们发现，在新冠病毒感染率较高的县，银行分行的存款利率比感染率较低的县，甚至同一家银行的分行的存款利率下降的幅度都要大。信贷支取、国家政策（如支付保护计划）和逃往安全地带并不考虑存款利率的这些跨分行变化。有证据表明，较高的本地新冠病毒-19感染率与家庭对未来工作和收入损失的更大焦虑有关，这种焦虑导致家庭减少支出和增加存款。

\[10\] How Valuable Is Financial Flexibility when Revenue Stops? Evidence from the COVID-19 Crisis

标题:当收入停止时，财务灵活性有多大价值？新冠肺炎危机的证据

作者:Rüdiger Fahlenbrach, Kevin Rageth, René M Stulz

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5474/6046943; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa134

Abstract : Firms with greater financial flexibility should be better able to fund a revenue shortfall resulting from the COVID-19 shock and benefit less from policy responses. We find that firms with high financial flexibility within an industry experience a stock price drop that is 26|$\\%$|⁠, or 9.7 percentage points, lower than those with low financial flexibility. This differential return persists as stock prices rebound. Firms more exposed to the COVID-19 shock benefit more from cash holdings. No evidence suggests that recent payouts worsened the average firm’s drop in stock price. Our results cannot be explained by a leverage effect.

Abstract:具有更大财务灵活性的公司应该能够更好地为新冠疫情冲击造成的收入短缺提供资金，并从政策应对中获益较少。我们发现，在一个行业内具有高度财务灵活性的公司经历了26 |$\\%的股价下跌$|⁠, 或9.7个百分点，低于财务灵活性低的国家。随着股价反弹，这种差异回报持续存在。更容易受到新冠病毒-19冲击的公司从现金持有中获益更多。没有证据表明，最近的支出加剧了平均公司股价的下跌。我们的结果不能用杠杆效应来解释。

\[11\] A Model of Endogenous Risk Intolerance and LSAPs: Asset Prices and Aggregate Demand in a “COVID-19” Shock

标题:内生风险不容忍和LSAP模型：“新冠病毒-19”冲击中的资产价格和总需求

作者:Ricardo J Caballero, Alp Simsek

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/34/11/5522/6210659; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab036

Abstract : We theoretically investigate the interaction of endogenous risk intolerance and monetary policy following a large recessionary shock. As asset prices dip, risk-tolerant agents’ wealth share declines. This decline reduces the market’s risk tolerance and triggers a downward loop in asset prices and aggregate demand when the interest rate policy is constrained. In this context, large-scale asset purchases are effective because they transfer unwanted risk to the government’s balance sheet. These effects are sizable when the model is calibrated to match the estimates of aggregate asset demand inelasticity. The COVID-19 shock illustrates the environment we seek to capture.

Abstract:我们从理论上研究了大规模衰退冲击后内生风险不容忍与货币政策的相互作用。随着资产价格的下跌，风险容忍机构的财富份额下降。这种下降降低了市场的风险承受能力，并在利率政策受到约束时触发了资产价格和总需求的下降循环。在这种情况下，大规模资产购买之所以有效，是因为它们将不必要的风险转移到了政府的资产负债表上。当模型被校准以匹配总资产需求非弹性的估计时，这些影响是相当大的。新冠病毒-19休克说明了我们试图捕捉的环境。
