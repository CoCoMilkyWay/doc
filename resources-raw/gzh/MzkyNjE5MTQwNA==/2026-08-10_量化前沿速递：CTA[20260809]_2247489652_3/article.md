Abstract : Long term transmission rights (LTTRs) are designed to support hedging in interconnected European electricity markets. LTTR auction prices have historically fallen short of forward market prices, signaling limited arbitrage. This paper studies the interaction of transmission rights and forward markets. Option pricing theory predicts that LTTR holders take short forward positions in importing markets and long forward positions in exporting markets to lock in arbitrage profits.
