\[1\] Network centric indicators for fragility in global financial indices

标题 : 全球金融指数脆弱性的网络中心指标

作者 : Areejit Samal, Sunil Kumar, Yasharth Yadav, Anirban Chakraborti , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00070

说明 : Comments: 32 pages, 18 figures, including supplementary material

Abstract : Over the last two decades, financial systems have been studied and analysed from the perspective of complex networks, where the nodes and edges in the network represent the various financial components and the strengths of correlations between them. Here, we adopt a similar network based approach to analyse the daily closing prices of 69 global financial market indices across 65 countries over a period of 2000 2014. We study the correlations among the indices by constructing threshold networks superimposed over minimum spanning trees at different time frames. We investigate the effect of critical events in financial markets (crashes and bubbles) on the interactions among the indices by performing both static and dynamic analyses of the correlations. We compare and contrast the structures of these networks during periods of crashes and bubbles, with respect to the normal periods in the market. In addition, we study the temporal evolution of traditional market indicators, various global network measures and the recently developed edge based curvature measures. We show that network centric measures can be extremely useful in monitoring the fragility in the global financial market indices.

摘要 : 在过去的二十年中，人们从复杂网络的角度对金融系统进行了研究和分析，其中网络中的节点和边代表了各种金融组成部分以及它们之间的关联强度。在这里，我们采用类似的基于网络的方法来分析2000年至2014年期间65个国家69个全球金融市场指数的每日收盘价。我们通过构造在不同时间帧上叠加在最小生成树上的阈值网络来研究索引之间的相关性。我们通过对相关性进行静态和动态分析，研究了金融市场中的关键事件（崩溃和泡沫）对指数之间相互作用的影响。我们将这些网络在崩溃和泡沫时期的结构与市场正常时期的结构进行比较和对比。此外，我们还研究了传统市场指标的时间演化、各种全球网络测度以及最近发展起来的基于边缘的曲率测度。我们表明，以网络为中心的措施可以非常有用的监测脆弱性在全球金融市场指数。

\[2\] An evolutionary view on the emergence of Artificial Intelligence

标题 : 人工智能出现的进化观

作者 : Matheus E. Leusin, Bjoern Jindra, Daniel S. Hain , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00233

说明 : Comments: Keywords: Artificial Intelligence; technological space; evolutionary economic geography; technological relatedness; knowledge complexity

Abstract : This paper draws upon the evolutionary concepts of technological relatedness and knowledge complexity to enhance our understanding of the long term evolution of Artificial Intelligence (AI). We reveal corresponding patterns in the emergence of AI globally and in the context of specific geographies of the US, Japan, South Korea, and China. We argue that AI emergence is associated with increasing related variety due to knowledge commonalities as well as increasing complexity. We use patent based indicators for the period between 1974 2018 to analyse the evolution of AI s global technological space, to identify its technological core as well as changes to its overall relatedness and knowledge complexity. At the national level, we also measure countries overall specialisations against AI specific ones. At the global level, we find increasing overall relatedness and complexity of AI. However, for the technological core of AI, which has been stable over time, we find decreasing related variety and increasing complexity. This evidence points out that AI innovations related to core technologies are becoming increasingly distinct from each other. At the country level, we find that the US and Japan have been increasing the overall relatedness of their innovations. The opposite is the case for China and South Korea, which we associate with the fact that these countries are overall less technologically developed than the US and Japan. Finally, we observe a stable increasing overall complexity for all countries apart from China, which we explain by the focus of this country in technologies not strongly linked to AI.

摘要 : 本文利用技术关联性和知识复杂性的演化概念来加深我们对人工智能长期演化的理解。我们揭示了全球人工智能出现的相应模式，以及美国、日本、韩国和中国的特定地理环境。我们认为，由于知识的共性以及复杂性的增加，人工智能的出现与相关种类的增加有关。我们使用1974-2018年间基于专利的指标来分析人工智能全球技术空间的演变，以确定其技术核心以及其整体关联性和知识复杂性的变化。在国家层面上，我们还根据人工智能的具体情况来衡量国家的总体专业化水平。在全球层面上，我们发现人工智能的整体关联性和复杂性不断增加。然而，对于人工智能的技术核心，随着时间的推移，它已经趋于稳定，我们发现相关的种类越来越少，复杂性越来越高。这一证据表明，与核心技术相关的人工智能创新正变得越来越不同。在国家层面，我们发现美国和日本一直在提高其创新的整体关联性。中国和韩国的情况正好相反，我们认为这两个国家的技术总体上不如美国和日本发达。最后，我们观察到除中国以外的所有国家的总体复杂性都在稳步增加，这一点我们可以通过这个国家关注与人工智能没有紧密联系的技术来解释。

\[3\] Global health science leverages established collaboration network to fight COVID 19

标题 : 全球健康科学利用已建立的合作网络对抗COVID 19

作者 : Stefano Bianchini, Moritz Müller, Pierre Pelletier, Kevin Wirtz , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00298

说明 : -

Abstract : How has the science system reacted to the early stages of the COVID 19 pandemic Here we compare the (growing) international network for coronavirus research with the broader international health science network. Our findings show that, before the outbreak, coronavirus research realized a relatively small and rather peculiar niche within the global health sciences. As a response to the pandemic, the international network for coronavirus research expanded rapidly along the hierarchical structure laid out by the global health science network. Thus, in face of the crisis, the global health science system proved to be structurally stable yet versatile in research. The observed versatility supports optimistic views on the role of science in meeting future challenges. However, the stability of the global core periphery structure may be worrying, because it reduces learning opportunities and social capital of scientifically peripheral countries not only during this pandemic but also in its normal mode of operation.

摘要 : 科学系统对冠状病毒19大流行的早期阶段有何反应这里我们将（正在增长的）国际冠状病毒研究网络与更广泛的国际卫生科学网络进行比较。我们的研究结果表明，在疫情爆发之前，冠状病毒研究在全球卫生科学中实现了一个相对较小且相当独特的生态位。作为对大流行的一种回应，国际冠状病毒研究网络沿着全球卫生科学网络所规定的层次结构迅速扩展。因此，在危机面前，全球卫生科学体系在结构上是稳定的，但在研究上却是多才多艺的。观察到的多才多艺支持对科学在应对未来挑战中的作用的乐观看法。然而，全球核心-外围结构的稳定性可能令人担忧，因为它不仅在这场大流行期间，而且在其正常运作模式下，减少了科学外围国家的学习机会和社会资本。

\[4\] Recruitment, effort, and retention effects of performance contracts for civil servants

标题 : 公务员绩效契约之招募、努力与保留效果

作者 : Clare Leaver, Owen Ozier, Pieter Serneels, Andrew Zeitlin , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00444

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper reports on a two tiered experiment designed to separately identify the selection and effort margins of pay for performance (P4P). At the recruitment stage, teacher labor markets were randomly assigned to a pay for percentile or fixed wage contract. Once recruits were placed, an unexpected, incentive compatible, school level re randomization was performed, so that some teachers who applied for a fixed wage contract ended up being paid by P4P, and vice versa. By the second year of the study, the within year effort effect of P4P was 0.16 standard deviations of pupil learning, with the total effect rising to 0.20 standard deviations after allowing for selection.

摘要 : 本文报告了一个两级实验，旨在分别确定绩效工资（P4P）的选择和努力边际。在招聘阶段，教师劳动力市场被随机分配到百分位工资或固定工资合同。一旦新兵被安置，一个意想不到的，激励相容的，学校层面的重新随机进行，因此，一些教师谁申请了固定工资合同，最终由P4P支付，反之亦然。到研究第二年，P4P的年内努力效应为0.16个标准差，在允许选择后，总效应上升到0.20个标准差。

\[5\] Social Mobility in India

标题 : 印度的社会流动

作者 : A. Singh, A. Forcina, K. Muniyoor , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00447

说明 : Comments: 11 pages, 3 figures

Abstract : Rapid rise in income inequality in India is a serious concern. While the emphasis is on inclusive growth, it seems difficult to tackle the problem without looking at the intricacies of the problem. Social mobility is one such important tool which helps in reaching the cause of the problem and focuses on bringing long term equality in the country. The purpose of this study is to examine the role of social background and education attainment in generating occupation mobility in the country. By applying an extended version of the RC association model to 68th round (2011 12) of the Employment and Unemployment Survey by the National Sample Survey Office of India, we found that the role of education is not important in generating occupation mobility in India, while social background plays a critical role in determining one s occupation. This study successfully highlights the strong intergenerational occupation immobility in the country and also the need to focus on education. In this regard, further studies are needed to uncover other crucial factors limiting the growth of individuals in the country.

摘要 : 印度收入不平等的迅速加剧是一个令人严重关切的问题。虽然重点是包容性增长，但如果不考虑问题的复杂性，似乎很难解决这个问题。社会流动是这样一个重要的工具，它有助于找到问题的根源，并侧重于实现国家的长期平等。本研究的目的是探讨社会背景和教育程度对职业流动的影响。通过对印度国家抽样调查办公室第68轮（2011年12月）的就业和失业调查应用RC协会模型的扩展版本，我们发现教育在印度产生职业流动中的作用并不重要，而社会背景在决定一个人的职业方面起着关键作用。这项研究成功地突出了该国强烈的代际职业固定性以及重视教育的必要性。在这方面，还需要进一步研究，以找出限制该国个人成长的其他关键因素。

\[6\] Nonstationary Portfolios

标题 : 非平稳投资组合

作者 : Bruno Scalzo, Alvaro Arroyo, Ljubisa Stankovic, Danilo P. Mandic , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00477

说明 : Comments: 5 pages, 3 figures, 1 table. arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:2007.13855

Abstract : Classical portfolio optimization methods typically determine an optimal capital allocation through the implicit, yet critical, assumption of statistical time invariance. Such models are inadequate for real world markets as they employ standard time averaging based estimators which suffer significant information loss if the market observables are non stationary. To this end, we reformulate the portfolio optimization problem in the spectral domain to cater for the nonstationarity inherent to asset price movements and, in this way, allow for optimal capital allocations to be time varying. Unlike existing spectral portfolio techniques, the proposed framework employs augmented complex statistics in order to exploit the interactions between the real and imaginary parts of the complex spectral variables, which in turn allows for the modelling of both harmonics and cyclostationarity in the time domain. The advantages of the proposed framework over traditional methods are demonstrated through numerical simulations using real world price data.

摘要 : 经典的投资组合优化方法通常通过隐含的，但关键的，统计时间不变性的假设来确定最优的资本配置。这类模型不适用于现实世界的市场，因为它们采用基于标准时间平均的估计器，如果市场可观测数据是非平稳的，那么这些估计器将遭受重大的信息损失。为此，我们在谱域中重新构造了投资组合优化问题，以满足资产价格波动固有的非平稳性，并以这种方式允许最优资本配置是时变的。与现有的谱组合技术不同，该框架采用了增广复统计量，以利用复谱变量实部和虚部之间的相互作用，进而在时域中对谐波和循环平稳性进行建模。通过实际价格数据的数值模拟，证明了该框架相对于传统方法的优越性。

\[7\] The Impact of the COVID 19 Pandemic on Scientific Research in the Life Sciences

标题 : covid19大流行对生命科学研究的影响

作者 : Massimo Riccaboni, Luca Verginer , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00497

说明 : -

Abstract : The COVID 19 outbreak has posed an unprecedented challenge to humanity and science. On the one side, public and private incentives have been put in place to promptly allocate resources toward research areas strictly related to the COVID 19 emergency. But on the flip side, research in many fields not directly related to the pandemic has lagged behind. In this paper, we assess the impact of COVID 19 on world scientific production in the life sciences. We investigate how the usage of medical subject headings (MeSH) has changed following the outbreak. We estimate through a difference in differences approach the impact of COVID 19 on scientific production through PubMed. We find that COVID related research topics have risen to prominence, displaced clinical publications, diverted funds away from research areas not directly related to COVID 19 and that the number of publications on clinical trials in unrelated fields has contracted. Our results call for urgent targeted policy interventions to reactivate biomedical research in areas that have been neglected by the COVID 19 emergency.

摘要 : covid19的爆发给人类和科学带来了前所未有的挑战。一方面，公共和私人的激励措施已经到位，以便迅速将资源分配给与covid19紧急事件密切相关的研究领域。但另一方面，许多与流感大流行没有直接关系的领域的研究却落在了后面。在本文中，我们评估了covid19对世界生命科学生产的影响。我们调查了疾病爆发后医学主题词（MeSH）的使用发生了怎样的变化。我们通过差异中的差异方法，通过PubMed评估COVID 19对科学生产的影响。我们发现，与COVID相关的研究主题已经变得突出，取代了临床出版物，将资金从与covid19不直接相关的研究领域转移出去，并且在不相关领域的临床试验出版物的数量已经减少。我们的研究结果呼吁采取紧急的有针对性的政策干预措施，重新启动那些被covid19紧急事件忽视的领域的生物医学研究。

\[8\] Controlling volatility of wind solar power

标题 : 控制风能和太阳能的波动

作者 : Hans Lustfeld , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00587

说明 : Comments: 12 pages, 4 figures

Abstract : The main advantage of wind and solar power plants is the power production free of CO2. Their main disadvantage is the volatility of the generated power. According to the estimates of H. W. Sinn 1 , suppressing this volatility requires pumped storage plants with a huge capacity, several orders of magnitude larger than the present available capacity in Germany 2 . Sinn concluded that wind solar power can be used only together with conventional power plants as backups. However, based on German power data 3 of 2019 we show that the required storage capacity can significantly be reduced, provided i) a surplus of wind solar power plants is supplied, ii) smart meters are installed, iii) partly a different kind of wind turbines and solar panels are used in Germany. Our calculations suggest that all the electric energy, presently produced in Germany, can be obtained from wind solar power alone. And our results let us predict that wind solar power can be used to produce in addition the energy for transportation, warm water, space heating and in part for process heating, meaning an increase of the present electric energy production by a factor of about 5 1 . Of course, to put such a prediction on firm ground the present calculations have to be confirmed for a period of many years. And it should be kept in mind, that in any case a huge number of wind turbines and solar panels is required.

摘要 : 风能和太阳能发电厂的主要优点是发电不含二氧化碳。它们的主要缺点是发电量的波动性。根据H.W.sinn1的估计，抑制这种波动需要容量巨大的抽水蓄能电站，比德国目前的可用容量大几个数量级。辛恩总结说，风能和太阳能只能与传统发电厂一起作为备用。然而，根据2019年第3期德国电力数据，我们表明，只要i）提供了过剩的风能太阳能发电厂，ii）安装了智能电表，iii）德国使用了部分不同类型的风力涡轮机和太阳能电池板，所需的储能能力可以显著降低。我们的计算表明，目前德国生产的所有电能都可以单独从风能和太阳能中获得。我们的结果让我们预测，风能太阳能除了可以用于生产交通、热水、空间供暖和部分用于工艺供暖的能源外，还可以用于生产其他能源，这意味着目前的电能生产将增加约51倍。当然，要把这样的预测建立在坚实的基础上，目前的计算必须在多年的时间内得到证实。而且应该记住，在任何情况下，都需要大量的风力涡轮机和太阳能电池板。

\[9\] Flashot

标题 : 闪络

作者 : Yixin Cao, Chuanwei Zou, Xianfeng Cheng , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00626

说明 : -

Abstract : Flash Loan attack can grab millions of dollars from decentralized vaults in one single transaction, drawing increasing attention from the Decentralized Finance (DeFi) players. It has also demonstrated an exciting opportunity that a huge wealth could be created by composing DeFi s building blocks and exploring the arbitrage change. However, a fundamental framework to study the field of DeFi has not yet reached a consensus and there s a lack of standard tools or languages to help better describe, design and improve the running processes of the infant DeFi systems, which naturally makes it harder to understand the basic principles behind the complexity of Flash Loan attacks. In this paper, we are the first to propose Flashot, a prototype that is able to transparently illustrate the precise asset flows intertwined with smart contracts in a standardized diagram for each Flash Loan event. Some use cases are shown and specifically, based on Flashot, we study a typical Pump and Arbitrage case and present in depth economic explanations to the attacker s behaviors. Finally, we conclude the development trends of Flash Loan attacks and discuss the great impact on DeFi ecosystem brought by Flash Loan. We envision a brand new quantitative financial industry powered by highly efficient automatic risk and profit detection systems based on the blockchain.

摘要 : Flash贷款攻击可以在一次交易中从分散的金库中攫取数百万美元，引起分散金融（DeFi）玩家越来越多的关注。它还展示了一个令人兴奋的机会，即通过构建DeFi的构件和探索套利变化，可以创造出巨大的财富。然而，研究DeFi领域的基本框架尚未达成共识，而且缺乏标准工具或语言来帮助更好地描述、设计和改进婴儿DeFi系统的运行过程，这自然使人们更难理解Flash贷款攻击复杂性背后的基本原理。在本文中，我们首先提出了Flashot，这是一个原型，能够透明地说明精确的资产流与智能合约交织在一个标准化的图表，为每个Flash贷款事件。本文展示了一些用例，特别是基于Flashot，我们研究了一个典型的泵和套利案例，并对攻击者的行为给出了深入的经济解释。最后，总结了Flash-Loan攻击的发展趋势，讨论了Flash-Loan对def生态系统的巨大影响。我们设想一个全新的定量金融行业，由基于区块链的高效自动风险和利润检测系统提供动力。

\[10\] Quantum crypto economics

标题 : 量子密码经济学

作者 : Peter P. Rohde, Vijay Mohan, Sinclair Davidson, Chris Berg, Darcy Allen, Gavin K. Brennen, Jason Potts , 类别 ：Pricing of Securities (q-fin.PR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00659

说明 : Comments: 12 pages, 1 figure

Abstract : Two of the most important technological advancements currently underway are the advent of quantum technologies, and the transitioning of global financial systems towards cryptographic assets, notably blockchain based cryptocurrencies and smart contracts. There is, however, an important interplay between the two, given that, in due course, quantum technology will have the ability to directly compromise the cryptographic foundations of blockchain. We explore this complex interplay by building financial models for quantum failure in various scenarios, including pricing quantum risk premiums. We call this quantum crypto economics.

摘要 : 目前正在进行的两项最重要的技术进步是量子技术的出现，以及全球金融系统向加密资产的过渡，特别是基于区块链的加密货币和智能合约。然而，这两者之间存在着重要的相互作用，因为在适当的时候，量子技术将有能力直接损害区块链的密码基础。我们通过建立各种情况下量子失败的金融模型，包括量子风险溢价定价，来探索这种复杂的相互作用。我们称之为量子密码经济学。

\[11\] The Impacts of the Gender Imbalance on Marriage and Birth

标题 : 性别失衡对婚姻和生育的影响

作者 : Kota Ogasawara, Erika Igarashi , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00687

说明 : -

Abstract : This study uses the unprecedented changes in the sex ratio due to the losses of men during World War II to identify the impacts of the gender imbalance on marriage market and birth outcomes in Japan. Using newly digitized census based historical statistics, we find evidence that men had a stronger bargaining position in the marriage market and intra household fertility decisions than women. Under relative male scarcity, while people, especially younger people, were more likely to marry and divorce, widowed women were less likely to remarry than widowed men. We also find that women s bargaining position in the marriage market might not have improved throughout the 1950s. Given the institutional changes in the abortion law after the war, marital fertility and stillbirth rates increased in the areas that suffered relative male scarcity. Our result on out of wedlock births indicates that the theoretical prediction of intra household bargaining is considered to be robust in an economy in which marital fertility is dominant.

摘要 : 本研究利用二战期间男性流失所造成的性别比的空前变化，来探讨性别失衡对日本婚姻市场和生育结果的影响。利用最新的基于数字化人口普查的历史统计数据，我们发现有证据表明，男性在婚姻市场和家庭内部生育决策中的议价地位要高于女性。在男性相对稀缺的情况下，虽然人们，尤其是年轻人，更容易结婚和离婚，但丧偶女性再婚的可能性低于丧偶男性。我们还发现，妇女在婚姻市场上的讨价还价地位可能在整个1950年代都没有改善，考虑到战后堕胎法的制度变化，在男性相对匮乏的地区，婚姻生育率和死胎率有所上升。我们对非婚生育的研究结果表明，在婚姻生育率占主导地位的经济中，家庭内部谈判的理论预测被认为是稳健的。

\[12\] Efficient Modeling and Forecasting of the Electricity Spot Price

标题 : 电力现货价格的有效建模与预测

作者 : Florian Ziel, Rick Steinert, Sven Husmann , 类别 ：Applications (stat.AP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.7027

说明 : -

Abstract : The increasing importance of renewable energy, especially solar and wind power, has led to new forces in the formation of electricity prices. Hence, this paper introduces an econometric model for the hourly time series of electricity prices of the European Power Exchange (EPEX) which incorporates specific features like renewable energy. The model consists of several sophisticated and established approaches and can be regarded as a periodic VAR TARCH with wind power, solar power, and load as influences on the time series. It is able to map the distinct and well known features of electricity prices in Germany. An efficient iteratively reweighted lasso approach is used for the estimation. Moreover, it is shown that several existing models are outperformed by the procedure developed in this paper.

摘要 : 可再生能源，特别是太阳能和风能的日益重要，导致了电价形成的新力量。因此，本文引入了一个计量经济学模型，用于计算欧洲电力交易所（EPEX）的每小时电价时间序列，该模型结合了可再生能源等特殊特征。该模型由多种成熟的方法组成，可以看作是一个以风电、太阳能和负荷为时间序列影响因素的周期无功补偿模型。它能够绘制出德国电价的独特和众所周知的特征。采用一种有效的迭代加权lasso方法进行估计。此外，文中所提出的方法也比现有的几种模型有更好的性能。

\[13\] A Class of Explicit optimal contracts in the face of shutdown

标题 : 一类面临停机的显式最优契约

作者 : Jessica Martin (INSA Toulouse), Stéphane Villeneuve (TSE) , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00001

说明 : -

Abstract : What type of delegation contract should be offered when facing a risk of the magnitude of the pandemic we are currently experiencing and how does the likelihood of an exogenous early termination of the relationship modify the terms of a full commitment contract We study these questions by considering a dynamic principal agent model that naturally extends the classical Holmstr o m Milgrom setting to include a risk of default whose origin is independent of the inherent agency problem. We obtain an explicit characterization of the optimal wage along with the optimal action provided by the agent. The optimal contract is linear by offering both a fixed share of the output which is similar to the standard shutdown free Holmstr o m Milgrom model and a linear prevention mechanism that is proportional to the random lifetime of the contract. We then tweak the model to add a possibility for risk mitigation through investment and study its optimality.

摘要 : 当面临我们目前正在经历的流行病的严重程度的风险时，应该提供什么类型的委托合同，以及外生提前终止关系的可能性如何修改完全承诺合同的条款我们通过考虑一个动态委托代理模型来研究这些问题扩展了经典的Holmstr-o-m-Milgrom设置，以包括违约风险，其来源独立于固有的代理问题。我们得到了最优工资的一个显式刻画，以及代理人提供的最优行为。通过提供与标准无停堆holmstrom-Milgrom模型相似的固定输出份额和与契约的随机寿命成比例的线性预防机制，最优契约是线性的。然后，我们调整模型，增加通过投资降低风险的可能性，并研究其最优性。

\[14\] Rapid detection of fast innovation under the pressure of COVID 19

标题 : covid19压力下快速创新的快速检测

作者 : Nicola Melluso, Andrea Bonaccorsi, Filippo Chiarello, Gualtiero Fantoni , 类别 ：Information Retrieval (cs.IR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00197

说明 : Comments: Published in PlOs One in 12/31/2020

Abstract : Covid 19 has rapidly redefined the agenda of technological research and development both for academics and practitioners. If the medical scientific publication system has promptly reacted to this new situation, other domains, particularly in new technologies, struggle to map what is happening in their contexts. The pandemic has created the need for a rapid detection of technological convergence phenomena, but at the same time it has made clear that this task is impossible on the basis of traditional patent and publication indicators. This paper presents a novel methodology to perform a rapid detection of the fast technological convergence phenomenon that is occurring under the pressure of the Covid 19 pandemic. The fast detection has been performed thanks to the use of a novel source the online blogging platform Medium. We demonstrate that the hybrid structure of this social journalism platform allows a rapid detection of innovation phenomena, unlike other traditional sources. The technological convergence phenomenon has been modelled through a network based approach, analysing the differences of networks computed during two time periods (pre and post COVID 19). The results led us to discuss the repurposing of technologies regarding Remote Control , Remote Working , Health and Remote Learning .

摘要 : covid19迅速重新定义了学术界和实践者的技术研发议程。如果医学科学出版系统对这一新形势作出了迅速反应，那么其他领域，特别是新技术领域，就很难描绘出它们所处的环境。这一流行病使人们需要迅速发现技术趋同现象，但同时也表明，根据传统的专利和出版指标，这项任务是不可能完成的。本文提出了一种新的方法来执行快速检测快速技术收敛现象，这是发生在压力下的冠状病毒19流行。由于使用了一种新的来源，即在线博客平台媒体，因此实现了快速检测。我们证明，与其他传统来源不同，这个社会新闻平台的混合结构允许快速检测创新现象。通过基于网络的方法对技术趋同现象进行了建模，分析了在两个时间段（COVID 19之前和之后）计算的网络差异。研究结果使我们讨论了远程控制、远程工作、健康和远程学习等技术的再利用。

\[15\] CRPS Learning

标题 : CRPS学习

作者 : Jonathan Berrisch, Florian Ziel , 类别 ：Machine Learning (stat.ML)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00968

说明 : -

Abstract : Combination and aggregation techniques can improve forecast accuracy substantially. This also holds for probabilistic forecasting methods where full predictive distributions are combined. There are several time varying and adaptive weighting schemes like Bayesian model averaging (BMA). However, the performance of different forecasters may vary not only over time but also in parts of the distribution. So one may be more accurate in the center of the distributions, and other ones perform better in predicting the distribution s tails. Consequently, we introduce a new weighting procedure that considers both varying performance across time and the distribution. We discuss pointwise online aggregation algorithms that optimize with respect to the continuous ranked probability score (CRPS). After analyzing the theoretical properties of a fully adaptive Bernstein online aggregation (BOA) method, we introduce smoothing procedures for pointwise CRPS learning. The properties are confirmed and discussed using simulation studies. Additionally, we illustrate the performance in a forecasting study for carbon markets. In detail, we predict the distribution of European emission allowance prices.

摘要 : 组合和聚合技术可以大幅度提高预测精度。这也适用于概率预测方法，其中完全预测分布是组合的。有几种时变和自适应加权方案，如贝叶斯模型平均（BMA）。然而，不同预报员的表现可能不仅随着时间的推移而有所不同，而且在部分分布上也有所不同。因此，一种方法可能在分布的中心更准确，而另一种方法在预测分布的尾部方面表现更好。因此，我们引入了一个新的加权过程，它既考虑了随时间变化的性能，也考虑了分布。我们讨论了点式在线聚合算法的优化方面的连续排名概率得分（CRPS）。在分析了完全自适应Bernstein在线聚合（BOA）方法的理论性质之后，我们引入了点式CRPS学习的平滑过程。通过仿真研究，对其性能进行了验证和讨论。此外，我们还对碳市场的预测研究进行了说明。详细地，我们预测了欧洲排放配额价格的分布。
