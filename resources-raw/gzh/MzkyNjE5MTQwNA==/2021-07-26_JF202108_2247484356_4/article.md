Abstract : Beliefs of professional forecasters are benchmarked against those of a Bayesian econometrician BE who is learning about the unknown dynamics of the bond risk factors. Consistent with rational Bayesian learning, the forecast errors of individual professionals and BE are comparably predictable over the business cycle. Inconsistent with many models with belief dispersion, the relationship between professionals' yield disagreement and their matched disagreements about macroeconomic fundamentals is very weak.

\[2\] What Explains Differences in Finance Research Productivity during the Pandemic?

标题:是什么解释了流感大流行期间金融研究生产力的差异？

作者:BRAD M. BARBER,WEI JIANG,ADAIR MORSE,MANJU PURI,HEATHER TOOKES,INGRID M. WERNER

首次发表时间:13 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13028

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13028

Abstract : Based on a survey of American Finance Association members, we analyze how demographics, time allocation, production mechanisms, and institutional factors affect research production during the pandemic. Consistent with the literature, research productivity falls more for women and faculty with young children. Independently, and novel, extra time spent on teaching (much more likely for women) negatively affects research productivity. Also novel, concerns about feedback, isolation, and health have large negative research effects, which disproportionately affect junior faculty and PhD students. Finally, faculty who express greater concerns about employers’ finances report larger negative research effects and more concerns about feedback, isolation, and health.

Abstract :基于对美国金融协会成员的调查，我们分析了人口统计学、时间分配、生产机制和制度因素如何影响流感大流行期间的研究成果。与文献一致，研究生产力下降更多的妇女和教师与幼儿。独立的、新颖的、用于教学的额外时间（对女性来说更可能）会对研究生产力产生负面影响。同样新颖的是，对反馈、隔离和健康的担忧会对研究产生巨大的负面影响，对初级教师和博士生的影响不成比例。最后，那些对雇主的财务状况表现出更大担忧的教师报告了更大的负面研究效果，并对反馈、隔离和健康表现出更多的担忧。

作者:HUAIZHI CHEN,LAUREN COHEN,UMIT G. GURUN

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13023

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13023

Abstract : We provide evidence that bond fund managers misclassify their holdings, and that these misclassifications have a real and significant impact on investor capital flows. The problem is widespread, resulting in up to 31.4% of funds being misclassified with safer profiles, compared to their true, publicly reported holdings. “Misclassified funds”—those that hold risky bonds but claim to hold safer bonds—appear to on-average outperform lower risk funds in their peer groups. \[4\] Weathering Cash Flow Shocks

标题:抵御现金流冲击

作者:JAMES R. BROWN,MATTHEW T. GUSTAFSON,IVAN T. IVANOV

首次发表时间:15 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13024

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13024

Abstract : Unexpectedly severe winter weather, which is arguably exogenous to firm and bank fundamentals, represents a significant cash flow shock for bank-borrowing firms. Firms respond to these shocks by drawing on and increasing the size of their credit lines. Banks charge borrowers for this liquidity via increased interest rates and less borrower-friendly loan provisions. Credit line adjustments occur within one calendar quarter of the shock and persist for at least nine months. Overall, we provide evidence that bank credit lines are an important tool for managing the nonfundamental component of cash flow volatility, especially for solvent, small bank borrowers.

Abstract :出人意料的严冬天气，可以说是企业和银行基本面的外生因素，对银行借贷公司来说是一个重大的现金流冲击。企业对这些冲击的反应是利用和增加信贷额度。银行通过提高利率和减少对借款人有利的贷款准备金向借款人收取流动性费用。信贷额度调整发生在冲击发生的一个日历季度内，并持续至少九个月。总的来说，我们提供的证据表明，银行信贷额度是管理现金流波动的非基础组成部分的一个重要工具，特别是对于有偿付能力的小型银行借款人。

\[5\] Are CEOs Different?

标题:首席执行官们有什么不同吗？

作者:STEVEN N. KAPLAN,MORTEN SORENSEN

首次发表时间:09 March 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13019

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13019

Abstract : Using 2,603 executive assessments, we study how CEO candidates differ from candidates for other top management positions, particularly CFOs. More than half of the variation in the 30 assessed characteristics is explained by four factors that we interpret as general ability, execution (vs. interpersonal), charisma (vs. analytical), and strategic (vs. managerial). CEO candidates have more extreme factor scores that differ significantly from those of CFO candidates. Conditional on being considered, candidates with greater general ability and interpersonal skills are more likely to be hired. These and our previous results on CEO success suggest that boards overweight interpersonal skills in hiring CEOs.

Abstract :利用2603份高管评估报告，我们研究了首席执行官候选人与其他高管职位候选人（尤其是首席财务官）的差异。在这30个被评估的特征中，有一半以上的差异是由四个因素解释的，我们将这些因素解释为一般能力、执行力（与人际关系）、魅力（与分析能力）和战略（与管理能力）。首席执行官候选人的因子得分更为极端，与首席财务官候选人的因子得分存在显著差异。在被考虑的条件下，综合能力和人际交往能力较强的候选人更有可能被录用。这些以及我们之前关于CEO成功的研究结果表明，董事会在招聘CEO时过于看重人际交往能力。

\[6\] A Theory of Zombie Lending

标题:僵尸借贷理论

作者:YUNZHI HU,FELIPE VARAS

首次发表时间:06 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13022

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13022

Abstract : An entrepreneur borrows from a relationship bank or the market. The bank has a higher cost of capital but produces private information over time. While the entrepreneur accumulates reputation as the lending relationship continues, asymmetric information is also developed between the bank/entrepreneur and the market. In this setting, zombie lending is inevitable: Once the entrepreneur becomes sufficiently reputable, the bank will roll over loans even after learning bad news, for the prospect of future market financing. Zombie lending is mitigated when the entrepreneur faces financial constraints. Finally, the bank stops producing information too early if information production is costly.

Abstract :企业家从关系银行或市场借款。该银行的资本成本较高，但随着时间的推移会产生私人信息。随着借贷关系的持续，企业家积累了声誉，银行/企业家与市场之间也形成了信息不对称。在这种情况下，僵尸贷款是不可避免的：一旦企业家的声誉变得足够好，银行甚至在得知坏消息后也会展期发放贷款，以备未来市场融资之需。当企业家面临财务约束时，僵尸贷款会得到缓解。最后，如果信息生产成本高昂，银行会过早停止生产信息。

\[7\] Rent Extraction with Securities Plus Cash

标题:有价证券加现金提取租金

作者:TINGJUN LIU,DAN BERNHARDT

首次发表时间:16 March 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13018

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13018

Abstract : In our target-initiated theory of takeovers, a target approaches potential acquirers that privately know their standalone values and merger synergies, where higher synergy acquirers tend to have larger standalone values. Despite their information disadvantage, targets can extract all surplus when synergies and standalone values are concavely related by offering payment choices that are combinations of cash and equity. Targets exploit the reluctance of high-valuation acquirers to cede equity claims, inducing them to bid more cash. When synergies and standalone values are not concavely related, sellers can gain by combining cash with securities that are more information sensitive than equities.

Abstract :在我们的目标公司发起的收购理论中，目标公司接近那些私下知道其独立价值和合并协同效应的潜在收购者，其中协同效应越高的收购者往往拥有更大的独立价值。尽管目标公司存在信息劣势，但当协同效应和独立价值通过提供现金和股权组合的支付选择而密切相关时，它们可以提取所有盈余。目标公司利用高估值收购者不愿放弃股权要求的心理，诱导他们出价更多现金。当协同效应和独立价值没有内在联系时，卖方可以通过将现金与比股票更敏感的信息的证券结合起来获得收益。

\[8\] How Debit Cards Enable the Poor to Save More

标题:借记卡如何让穷人存得更多

作者:PIERRE BACHAS,PAUL GERTLER,SEAN HIGGINS,ENRIQUE SEIRA

首次发表时间:29 March 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13021

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13021

Abstract : We study an at-scale natural experiment in which debit cards were given to cash transfer recipients who already had a bank account. Using administrative account data and household surveys, we find that beneficiaries accumulated a savings stock equal to 2% of annual income after two years with the card. The increase in formal savings represents an increase in overall savings, financed by a reduction in current consumption. There are two mechanisms. First, debit cards reduce transaction costs of accessing money. Second, they reduce monitoring costs, which led beneficiaries to check their account balances frequently and build trust in the bank.

Abstract :我们研究了一个大规模的自然实验，在这个实验中，借记卡被提供给已经有银行账户的现金转账接受者。通过使用行政账户数据和家庭调查，我们发现受益人在使用信用卡两年后积累了相当于年收入2%的储蓄存量。正规储蓄的增加意味着总储蓄的增加，其资金来源是当前消费的减少。有两种机制。首先，借记卡降低了存取资金的交易成本。其次，它们降低了监控成本，这导致受益人经常检查账户余额，并建立对银行的信任。

\[9\] Time Variation of the Equity Term Structure

标题:股权期限结构的时间变化

作者:NIELS JOACHIM GORMSEN

首次发表时间:30 March 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13020

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13020

Abstract : I study the term structure of one-period expected returns on dividend claims with different maturity. I find that the slope of the term structure is countercyclical. The countercyclical variation is consistent with theories of long-run risk and habit, but these theories cannot explain the average downward slope. At the same time, the cyclical variation is inconsistent with recent models constructed to match the average downward slope. More generally, the average and cyclicality of the slope are hard to reconcile with models with a single risk factor. I introduce a model with two priced factors to solve the puzzle.

Abstract :研究了不同期限股利债权的一期预期收益的期限结构。我发现期限结构的斜率是反周期的。逆周期变化与长期风险和习惯理论是一致的，但这些理论不能解释平均下降斜率。同时，周期性变化与最近为匹配平均向下斜率而构建的模型不一致。一般来说，坡度的平均值和周期性很难与单一风险因素的模型相一致。我引入一个包含两个定价因素的模型来解决这个难题。

\[10\] Sentiment Trading and Hedge Fund Returns

标题:情绪交易与对冲基金回报

作者:YONG CHEN,BING HAN,JING PAN

首次发表时间:08 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13025

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13025

Abstract : In the presence of sentiment fluctuations, arbitrageurs may engage in different strategies leading to dispersed sentiment exposures. We find that hedge funds in the top decile ranked by sentiment beta outperform those in the bottom decile by 0.59% per month on a risk-adjusted basis, with the spread being larger among skilled funds. We also find that about 10% of hedge funds have sentiment timing skill that positively correlates with fund sentiment beta and contributes to fund performance. Our findings show that skilled hedge funds can earn high returns by predicting and exploiting sentiment changes rather than betting against mispricing.

Abstract :在情绪波动的情况下，套利者可能会采取不同的策略，导致情绪敞口分散。我们发现，在风险调整的基础上，情绪贝塔指数排名前十分位的对冲基金每月的表现要比排名后十分位的对冲基金高出0.59%，在技术型基金中的利差更大。我们还发现，约10%的对冲基金具有情绪计时技能，该技能与基金情绪贝塔正相关，并有助于基金业绩。我们的研究结果表明，熟练的对冲基金可以通过预测和利用情绪变化而不是押注于错误定价来获得高回报。

\[11\] Asset Managers: Institutional Performance and Factor Exposures

标题:资产管理公司：机构绩效和因素敞口

作者:JOSEPH GERAKOS,JUHANI T. LINNAINMAA,ADAIR MORSE

首次发表时间:15 April 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13026

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13026

Abstract : Using data on $18 trillion of assets under management, we show that actively managed institutional accounts outperformed strategy benchmarks by 75 (31) bps on a gross (net) basis during the period 2000 to 2012. Estimates from a Sharpe model imply that asset managers' outperformance came from factor exposures. If institutions had instead implemented mean-variance efficient portfolios using index and institutional mutual funds available during the sample period, they would not have earned higher Sharpe ratios. Our results are consistent with the average asset manager having skill, managers competing for institutional capital, and institutions engaging in costly search to identify skilled managers.

Abstract :利用管理的18万亿美元资产的数据，我们表明，在2000年至2012年期间，积极管理的机构账户在总（净）基础上的表现超过了战略基准75（31）个基点。夏普模型的估计表明，资产管理者的表现来自要素风险敞口。如果机构在样本期内使用指数和机构共同基金实施均值-方差有效的投资组合，它们就不会获得更高的夏普比率。我们的结果与拥有技能的平均资产管理人、竞争机构资本的管理人以及从事高成本搜索以确定技能管理人的机构一致。
