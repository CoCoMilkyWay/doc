**【本期导读】**展示论文：动量因子与因子动量Factor Momentum and The Momentum Factor复刻的全部结果。

  

**【论文摘要】**Momentum in individual stock returns emanates from momentum in factor returns. Most factors are positively autocorrelated: the average factor earns a monthly return of 1 basis point following a year of losses and 53 basis points following a positive year. Factor momentum explains all forms of individual stock momentum. Stock momentum strategies indirectly time factors: they profit when the factors remain autocorrelated, and crash when these autocorrelations break down. Our key result is that momentum is not a distinct risk factor; it aggregates the autocorrelations found in all other factors. 

个股收益的动量来源于要素收益的动量。大多数因素都是正自相关的:亏损一年的平均月回报率为1个基点，盈利一年的平均月回报率为53个基点。因素动量解释了所有形式的个股动量。股票动量策略间接地影响了时间因素:当这些因素保持自相关时，它们就会获利，而当这些自相关被打破时，它们就会崩溃。我们的关键结论是，动量并不是一个明显的风险因素;它汇集了在所有其他因素中发现的自相关性。
