\[1\] Portfolio Selection with Mental Accounts: An Equilibrium Model with Endogenous Risk Aversion

标题:心理账户下的投资组合选择：一个内生风险厌恶均衡模型

作者:Gordon J. Alexander,Alexandre M. Baptista,Shu Yan

出处:Journal of Banking and Finance, 2020, Vol. 110, 105599

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3954563

Abstract : In Das et al. (2010), an agent divides his or her wealth among mental accounts that have different goals and optimal portfolios. While the moments of the distribution of asset returns are exogenous in their normative model, they are endogenous in our corresponding positive model. We obtain the following results. First, there are multiple equilibria that we parameterize by the implied risk aversion coefficient of the agent’s aggregate portfolio. Second, equilibrium asset prices and the composition of optimal portfolios within accounts depend on this coefficient. Third, altering the goal of any given account affects the composition of each portfolio.

Keywords : Portfolio selection, Mental accounts, Equilibrium, Endogenous risk aversion, Behavioral finance, Mean-variance model

Abstract :在Das等人（2010年）的研究中，代理人将其财富划分为具有不同目标和最优投资组合的智力账户。虽然资产收益分布的时刻在其规范模型中是外生的，但在我们相应的正模型中是内生的。我们得到以下结果。首先，我们通过代理人总投资组合的隐含风险规避系数来参数化多重均衡。第二，平衡资产价格和账户内最优投资组合的构成取决于该系数。第三，改变任何给定账户的目标会影响每个投资组合的构成。

Keywords :投资组合选择，心理账户，均衡，内生风险规避，行为金融学，均值-方差模型

\[2\] Noise and Deficiency of the Implied Cost of Capital as an Expected Return Proxy

标题:作为预期收益代理的隐含资本成本的噪音和不足

作者:C.S. Agnes Cheng,Jing Fang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3951890

Abstract : Because stock price generally deviates from the intrinsic value, stock price is a noisy indicator of the intrinsic value. As an expected return proxy, the implied cost of capital (ICC)—the internal rate of return that equates the noisy stock price to discounted expected future dividends—thus includes a mispricing-driven component. The mispricing-driven component is positive and increases with the degree of undervaluation for undervalued firms and is negative and decreases with the degree of overvaluation for overvalued firms. Our findings suggest that the mispricing-driven component plays a dominant role. Due to the dominant role of the mispricing-driven component, ICC is a deficient expected return proxy for examining the effect of a factor on expected return. Moreover, existing methods do not seem able to address this deficiency.

Keywords : Implied cost of capital, Expected return, Noise, Bias

Abstract :由于股票价格通常偏离内在价值，因此股票价格是内在价值的一个嘈杂指标。作为一种预期回报代理，隐含资本成本（ICC）——将嘈杂的股票价格等同于贴现的预期未来股息的内部回报率，因此包括一个由错误定价驱动的部分。错误定价驱动的成分是正的，并且随着低估公司的低估程度而增加；对于高估公司，错误定价驱动的成分是负的，并且随着高估公司的低估程度而减少。我们的研究结果表明，定价失误驱动的因素起着主导作用。由于错误定价驱动因素的主导作用，ICC是一个缺陷预期回报代理，用于检查因素对预期回报的影响。此外，现有方法似乎无法解决这一缺陷。

Keywords :隐含资本成本、预期收益、噪音、偏差

\[3\] Incentivizing Effort and Informing Investment: The Dual Role of Stock Prices

标题:激励努力和告知投资：股票价格的双重作用

作者:Snehal Banerjee,Jesse Davis,Naveen Gondhi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3955129

Abstract : Stock prices reflect firm performance and aggregate investor information about investment opportunities. We show that these dual roles are in tension: when prices are more informative about future investment, they are less effective at incentivizing managerial effort. Overall firm value need not increase with price informativeness; both decreasing transparency and allowing for ex-post inefficient investment can increase firm value. We show that standard empirical measures of price efficiency are incomplete and derive testable predictions for the price-sensitivity and composition of managerial compensation.

Keywords : contracting, feedback effect, optimal compensation

Abstract :股票价格反映了公司业绩和投资者关于投资机会的总体信息。我们表明，这些双重角色处于紧张状态：当价格对未来投资的信息更丰富时，它们在激励管理努力方面的效果就较差。企业总价值不需要随着价格信息量的增加而增加；降低透明度和允许事后低效投资都可以增加公司价值。我们证明了价格效率的标准经验度量是不完整的，并得出了关于价格敏感性和管理层薪酬构成的可测试预测。

Keywords :契约、反馈效应、最优补偿

\[4\] The Influence of the Board of Directors and the Auditor in the Disclosure of Derivative Financial Instruments: Evidence on Portuguese Capital Market

标题:董事会和审计师在衍生金融工具披露中的影响：葡萄牙资本市场的证据

作者:Sara Serra,Kátia Matos Lemos,Maria Sofia Matins,International Journal of Professional Business Review

出处:Serra, S., Lemos, K. M., & Matins, M. S. (2022). The Influence of the Board of Directors and the Auditor in the Disclosure of Derivative Financial Instruments: Evidence on Portuguese Capital Market. International Journal of Professional Business Review, 7(1), e0240.

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3954650

Abstract : Purpose: This article aims to analyze the influence of certain characteristics of the board of directors and the auditor on the disclosure of derivative financial instruments.Theoretical framework: Operations with derivative instruments are associated with high risks, so the disclosure of these operations and their results helps to reduce information asymmetries. On the other hand, acccording to agency theory, the characteristics of the board of directors and of the auditor can affect the quality and the level of financial information disclosed.Design/methodology/approach: The data were obtained through content analysis, considering the 2017 reports and accounts of 37 companies listed on Euronext Lisbon, through a disclosure index. Subsequently, a multiple linear regression model was developed to identify the determinants of the level of disclosure about derivative instruments.Findings: The results revealed that only the number of independent members on the board of directors, the auditor's gender, and the size of the entity influence the level of disclosure about derivative instruments.Research, Practical & Social implications: This study may be useful for regulators in setting disclosure requirements for derivative instruments and useful in companies' decision making in defining the members of the board and the auditor.Originality/value: It contributes to the literature on compliance with IFRS and contributes to the perception of the influence of certain characteristics associated with the board of directors and the auditor in the disclosure of derivative instruments.

Keywords : Auditor, Board of Directors, Listed Companies, Disclosure Index, Derivative financial instruments

Abstract :目的：本文旨在分析董事会和审计师的某些特征对衍生金融工具披露的影响。理论框架：使用衍生工具的操作与高风险相关，因此，披露这些操作及其结果有助于减少信息不对称。另一方面，根据代理理论，董事会和审计师的特征会影响所披露财务信息的质量和水平。设计/方法/途径：数据通过内容分析获得，考虑到2017年在Euronext Lisbon上市的37家公司的报告和账目，通过披露指数。随后，开发了一个多元线性回归模型，以确定衍生工具披露水平的决定因素。结果表明，只有董事会独立成员的数量、审计师的性别、，而实体的规模会影响衍生工具的披露水平，实践和社会影响：本研究可能有助于监管机构制定衍生工具的披露要求，并有助于公司在确定董事会成员和审计师的决策。独创性/价值：它有助于有关遵守国际财务报告准则的文献，并有助于对与董事会和审计师相关的某些特征对衍生工具披露的影响。

Keywords :审计师、董事会、上市公司、披露指数、衍生金融工具

Abstract : This note considers the questions of: i) what exactly is an "edge" or advantage, ii) what is the value of an edge, and iii) what are the values of competing edges, when each could potentially tip the results either way in a competitive strategic game?These questions are considered in the context of and against the backdrop of the recent UK Supreme Court judgment in Ivey v Genting Casinos \[2017\] UKSC 67 (25 October 2017), which concerned whether professional gambler Phil Ivey could contractually enforce payment of c.£8m potential winnings on the card game “Punto Banco” which he had achieved using a cryptic "edge-sorting" technique (with similarities both to "card-counting" and to a financial derivatives trading strategy) played against counterparty Crockfords casino.

Keywords :艾维、云顶、Crockfords、Borgata、边缘排序、优势、博彩、Punto Banco、百家乐、衍生品、交易

Abstract : We survey extant literature on the economics of blockchain fundamentals with particular focus on Bitcoin, Proof-of-Work and Proof-of-Stake. \[7\] The Short-Termism Trap: Competition for Informed Investors Under Stock-Based CEO Compensation

标题:短期主义陷阱：基于股票的CEO薪酬下的知情投资者竞争

作者:James Dow,Jungsuk Han,Francesco Sangiorgi

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3953799

Abstract : We show that stock-based CEO compensation can create a "race to the bottom" among firms that escalates short-termist pressure. More informative stock prices reduce the agency cost of using stock-based compensation to incentivize managers. Also, shortening a firm's project maturity improves stock price informativeness by attracting informed investors, who prefer to invest in short-term assets. However, when informed trading is a scarce resource, this is a prisoners' dilemma game: competition for informed investors creates excessive short-termism that destroys shareholder value, while in equilibrium, price informativeness stays the same. More intense competition between firms sharpens incentives to shorten project maturity, deepening the "short-termism trap."

Keywords : informed trading, managerial compensation, short-termism, optimal contracting, race to the bottom

Abstract :我们的研究表明，基于股票的CEO薪酬会在公司中造成“竞相触底”，从而加剧短期主义者的压力。信息量更大的股票价格降低了使用股票薪酬激励经理人的代理成本。此外，缩短公司的项目成熟度可以通过吸引愿意投资短期资产的知情投资者来提高股票价格的信息性。然而，当知情交易是一种稀缺资源时，这是一种囚徒困境博弈：对知情投资者的竞争产生了过度的短期主义，破坏了股东价值，而在均衡状态下，价格信息性保持不变。企业之间更激烈的竞争加剧了缩短项目成熟度的动机，加深了“短期主义陷阱”

Keywords :知情交易、管理层薪酬、短期主义、最优合约、竞逐底线

\[8\] Accounting for Cryptocurrency Value

标题:加密货币价值的会计处理

作者:Yukun Liu,Aleh Tsyvinski,Xi Wu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3951514

Abstract : Information pertaining to new addresses is highly value-relevant in the cryptocurrency market. Innovations to the number of new addresses explain eight percent of the variations in cryptocurrency returns. Unlike traditional markets, we do not find evidence of pre- or post-drift around the release of new address information. The presence of strong market reactions at the release of new address information and the absence of drifts around the release highlight the distinct features of cryptocurrencies and provide a benchmark case where information is released publicly and continuously. Lastly, we show that the price-to-new address ratios negatively predict future cryptocurrency returns.

Keywords : Cryptocurrency, New Address, Value-Relevance, Return Predictability

Abstract :与新地址相关的信息在加密货币市场中具有高度的价值相关性。新地址数量的创新解释了加密货币回报率8%的变化。与传统市场不同，我们没有发现新地址信息发布前后的漂移迹象。在发布新地址信息时，市场反应强烈，并且在发布过程中没有出现漂移，这突出了加密货币的独特特征，并提供了一个公开和持续发布信息的基准案例。最后，我们证明了新地址价格比率对未来加密货币回报率的负面预测。

Keywords :加密货币、新地址、值相关性、返回可预测性

\[9\] Synthetic Options and Implied Volatility for the Corporate Bond Market

标题:公司债券市场的综合期权与隐含波动率

作者:Steven Shu-Hsiu Chen,Hitesh Doshi,Sang Byung Seo

出处:Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis, Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3286470

Abstract : We synthetically create option contracts on a corporate bond index using CDX swaptions, overcoming the limitations that stem from the lack of traded corporate bond options. Our approach allows us to estimate forward-looking moments concerning the corporate bond market in a model-free manner. By constructing an aggregate volatility measure and the associated variance risk premium, we examine the role of volatility risk in the corporate bond market. We highlight that the ex ante conditional second and higher moments we estimate from synthetic corporate bond options carry important implications for credit risk models, providing an extra basis for testing their validity.

Keywords : synthetic options, corporate bond index, option-implied moments, credit risk models

Abstract :我们使用CDX互换期权在公司债券指数上综合创建期权合约，克服了因缺乏可交易公司债券期权而产生的限制。我们的方法允许我们以无模型的方式估计有关公司债券市场的前瞻性时刻。通过构造一个总波动率测度和相关的方差风险溢价，我们检验了波动率风险在公司债券市场中的作用。我们强调，我们从合成公司债券期权中估算的事前条件二阶矩和更高阶矩对信用风险模型具有重要意义，为测试其有效性提供了额外的基础。

Keywords :综合期权、公司债券指数、期权隐含时刻、信用风险模型
