\[2\] Gender Differences in Motivated Reasoning

标题 : 动机推理中的性别差异

作者 : Michael Thaler , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.01538

说明 : -

Abstract : Men and women systematically differ in their beliefs about their performance relative to others in particular, men tend to be more overconfident. This paper provides support for one explanation for gender differences in overconfidence, performance motivated reasoning, in which people distort how they process new information in ways that make them believe they outperformed others. Using a large online experiment, I find that male subjects distort information processing to favor their performance, while female subjects do not systematically distort information processing in either direction. These statistically significant gender differences in performance motivated reasoning mimic gender differences in overconfidence beliefs of male subjects are systematically overconfident, while beliefs of female subjects are well calibrated on average. The experiment also includes political questions, and finds that politically motivated reasoning is similar for both men and women. These results suggest that, while men and women are both susceptible to motivated reasoning in general, men find it particularly attractive to believe that they outperformed others.

摘要 : 男人和女人对自己的表现有着系统性的不同，尤其是男人更容易过于自信。这篇论文支持了一种解释过度自信和绩效动机推理中的性别差异，即人们扭曲了他们处理新信息的方式，使他们相信自己的表现优于其他人。通过一个大型的在线实验，我发现男性受试者扭曲信息处理以利于他们的表现，而女性受试者不会系统地扭曲信息处理的任何一个方向。这些统计上显著的性别差异在表现动机推理中模仿了男性被试的过度自信信念的性别差异，而女性受试者的信念在平均水平上得到了很好的校准。该实验还包括政治问题，并发现出于政治动机的推理对男性和女性都是相似的。这些结果表明，虽然男性和女性都容易受到动机推理的影响，但男性认为自己的表现优于其他人尤其有吸引力。

\[3\] Do People Engage in Motivated Reasoning to Think the World Is a Good Place for Others

标题 : 人们是否有动机去思考这个世界对其他人来说是个好地方

作者 : Michael Thaler , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.01548

说明 : -

Abstract : Motivated reasoning is a bias in inference in which people distort their updating process in the direction of more attractive beliefs. Prior work has shown how motivated reasoning leads people to form overly positive beliefs that also serve to bolster one s self image in domains such as intelligence, prosociality, and politics. In this paper, I study whether positivity motivated reasoning persists in domains where self image does not play a role. In particular, I analyze whether individuals motivatedly reason to think that the world is a better place for others. Building off of the design from Thaler (2020), I conduct a large online experiment to test for positivity motivated reasoning on issues such as cancer survival rates, others happiness, and infant mortality. I find no systematic evidence for positivity motivated or negativity motivated reasoning, and can rule out modest effects. Positivity is not a sufficient condition for motivated reasoning.

摘要 : 动机推理是一种在推理中的偏差，人们扭曲他们的更新过程，朝着更有吸引力的信念的方向发展。先前的研究表明，动机推理是如何引导人们形成过度积极的信念的，这些信念也有助于在智力、亲社会性和政治等领域提升自我形象。在这篇论文中，我研究在自我形象不起作用的领域，积极性动机推理是否持续存在。特别是，我分析个人是否有动机地认为世界对他人来说是一个更好的地方。在泰勒（2020）的设计基础上，我进行了一个大型在线实验，以测试癌症存活率、他人幸福感和婴儿死亡率等问题上的积极性推理。我没有找到系统的证据来证明积极或消极动机的推理，并且可以排除适度的影响。积极性不是动机推理的充分条件。

\[4\] The Fake News Effect

标题 : 假新闻效应

作者 : Michael Thaler , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.01663

说明 : -

Abstract : Motivated reasoning posits that people distort how they process information in the direction of beliefs they find attractive. This paper creates a novel experimental design to identify motivated reasoning from Bayesian updating when people enter into the experiment with endogenously different beliefs. It analyzes how subjects assess the veracity of information sources that tell them the median of their belief distribution is too high or too low. A Bayesian would infer nothing about the source veracity from this message, but a motivated reasoner would believe the source were more truthful when it reports the direction that he is more motivated to believe. Experimental results show evidence for politically motivated reasoning about immigration, income mobility, crime, racial discrimination, gender, climate change, and gun laws. Motivated reasoning from these messages leads people s beliefs to become more polarized and less accurate, even though the messages are uninformative.

摘要 : 动机推理假设人们扭曲了他们处理信息的方式，使之朝着他们认为有吸引力的信念的方向发展。本文提出了一种新的实验设计，当人们进入实验时，从贝叶斯更新中识别出动机推理。它分析了受试者如何评估信息来源的准确性，这些信息来源告诉他们，他们的信念分布中位数过高或过低。贝叶斯不会从这条信息中推断出任何关于信息源真实性的信息，但一个有动机的推理者会相信，当信息源报告了他更有动力相信的方向时，该来源会更加真实。实验结果显示了关于移民、收入流动、犯罪、种族歧视、性别、气候变化和枪支法律的政治动机推理的证据。从这些信息中进行有动机的推理会导致人们的信念变得更加两极分化和不那么准确，即使这些信息没有信息。

\[5\] Estimating the Blood Supply Elasticity

标题 : 估计血液供应弹性

作者 : Sara R. Machado , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.01814

说明 : -

Abstract : I estimate the semi elasticity of blood donations with respect to a monetary benefit, namely the waiver of user fees when using the National Health Service, in Portugal. Using within county variation over time in the value of the benefitI estimate both the unconditional elasticity, which captures overall response of the market, and the conditional elasticity, which holds constant the number of blood drives. This amounts to fixing a measure of the cost of donation to the blood donor. I instrument for the number of blood drives, which is endogenous, using a variable based on the number of weekend days and the proportion of blood drives on weekends. A one euro increase in the subsidy leads 1.8 more donations per 10000 inhabitants, conditional on the number of blood drives. The unconditional effect is smaller. The benefit does not attract new donors, instead it fosters repeated donation. Furthermore, the discontinuation of the benefit lead to a predicted decrease in donations of around 18 , on average. However, I show that blood drives have the potential to effectively substitute monetary incentives in solving market imbalances.

摘要 : 我估计，在葡萄牙，献血相对于金钱利益的半弹性，即在使用国家卫生服务时免除使用费。利用县域内随时间变化的收益值来估计无条件弹性（捕获市场的总体响应）和条件弹性（保持血液驱动的数量不变）。这相当于确定了献血者的献血成本。我使用一个基于周末天数和周末血液驱动比例的变量来测量血液驱动的次数，这是内源性的。补贴每增加1欧元，每10000名居民就可多捐赠1.8欧元，这取决于献血次数。无条件的影响较小。这种福利并不会吸引新的捐赠者，反而会促进重复捐赠。此外，停止这项福利将导致捐款平均减少约18项。然而，我指出，在解决市场失衡方面，血腥驱动有可能有效替代货币激励。

\[6\] Bayesian Quantile Based Portfolio Selection

标题 : 基于贝叶斯分位数的投资组合选择

作者 : Taras Bodnar, Mathias Lindholm, Vilhelm Niklasson, Erik Thorsén , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.01819

说明 : -

Abstract : We study the optimal portfolio allocation problem from a Bayesian perspective using value at risk (VaR) and conditional value at risk (CVaR) as risk measures. By applying the posterior predictive distribution for the future portfolio return, we derive relevant quantiles needed in the computations of VaR and CVaR, and express the optimal portfolio weights in terms of observed data only. This is in contrast to the conventional method where the optimal solution is based on unobserved quantities which are estimated, leading to suboptimality. We also obtain the expressions for the weights of the global minimum VaR and CVaR portfolios, and specify conditions for their existence. It is shown that these portfolios may not exist if the confidence level used for the VaR or CVaR computation are too low. Moreover, analytical expressions for the mean VaR and mean CVaR efficient frontiers are presented and the extension of theoretical results to general coherent risk measures is provided. One of the main advantages of the suggested Bayesian approach is that the theoretical results are derived in the finite sample case and thus they are exact and can be applied to large dimensional portfolios. By using simulation and real market data, we compare the new Bayesian approach to the conventional method by studying the performance and existence of the global minimum VaR portfolio and by analysing the estimated efficient frontiers. It is concluded that the Bayesian approach outperforms the conventional one, in particular at predicting the out of sample VaR.

摘要 : 本文以风险价值（VaR）和条件风险价值（CVaR）作为风险度量，从贝叶斯的角度研究了最优投资组合分配问题。通过对未来投资组合收益率的后验预测分布，我们得到了VaR和CVaR计算所需的相关分位数，并仅用观测数据表示最优投资组合权重。这与传统的方法不同，传统方法的最优解是基于未观测量的估计，从而导致了次优性。我们还得到了全局最小VaR和CVaR投资组合权重的表达式，并给出了它们存在的条件。结果表明，如果用于VaR或CVaR计算的置信水平过低，这些投资组合可能不存在。此外，给出了平均VaR和平均CVaR有效前沿的解析表达式，并将理论结果推广到一般相干风险度量。贝叶斯方法的一个主要优点是理论结果是在有限样本情况下得到的，因此它们是精确的，可以应用于大维投资组合。通过模拟和实际市场数据，我们通过研究全局最小VaR投资组合的性能和存在性，并通过分析估计的有效前沿，比较了新贝叶斯方法与传统方法。结果表明，贝叶斯方法在预测样本外VaR方面优于传统方法。

(2019).This approach allows us quantifying the proportion of disagreement in business and consumer expectations of 32 countries.We have used information from all monthly forward looking questions contained in Joint Harmonised Programme of Business and Consumer Surveys conducted by the European Commission (the industry survey, the service survey, the retail trade survey, the building survey and the consumer survey).First, we have calculated a discrepancy indicator for each of the 17 survey questions analysed, which allows us to approximate the proportion of uncertainty about different aspects of economic activity, both form the demand and the supply sides of the economy.We then use these indicators to calculate disagreement indices at the sector level.We graphic the evolution of the degree of uncertainty in the main economic sectors of the analysed economies up to June 2020.We observe marked differences, both across variables, sectors and countries since the inception of the COVID 19 crisis.Finally, by adding the sectoral indicators, an indicator of business uncertainty is calculated and compared with that of consumers.Again, we find substantial differences in the evolution of uncertainty between managers and consumers.This analysis seeks to offer a global overview of the degree of economic uncertainty in the midst of the coronavirus crisis at the sectoral level.

\[9\] Competition analysis on the over the counter credit default swap market

标题 : 场外信用违约掉期市场的竞争分析

作者 : Louis Abraham , 类别 ：Machine Learning (cs.LG)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.01883

说明 : -

Abstract : We study two questions related to competition on the OTC CDS market using data collected as part of the EMIR regulation. First, we study the competition between central counterparties through collateral requirements. We present models that successfully estimate the initial margin requirements. However, our estimations are not precise enough to use them as input to a predictive model for CCP choice by counterparties in the OTC market. Second, we model counterpart choice on the interdealer market using a novel semi supervised predictive task. We present our methodology as part of the literature on model interpretability before arguing for the use of conditional entropy as the metric of interest to derive knowledge from data through a model agnostic approach. In particular, we justify the use of deep neural networks to measure conditional entropy on real world datasets. We create the textit Razor entropy using the framework of algorithmic information theory and derive an explicit formula that is identical to our semi supervised training objective. Finally, we borrow concepts from game theory to define textit top k Shapley values . This novel method of payoff distribution satisfies most of the properties of Shapley values, and is of particular interest when the value function is monotone submodular. Unlike classical Shapley values, top k Shapley values can be computed in quadratic time of the number of features instead of exponential. We implement our methodology and report the results on our particular task of counterpart choice. Finally, we present an improvement to the textit node2vec algorithm that could for example be used to further study intermediation. We show that the neighbor sampling used in the generation of biased walks can be performed in logarithmic time with a quasilinear time pre computation, unlike the current implementations that do not scale well.

摘要 : 我们研究了两个与场外CDS市场竞争有关的问题，这些问题是根据EMIR法规收集的数据。首先，我们通过抵押品要求来研究中央对手之间的竞争。我们提出了成功估计初始保证金要求的模型。然而，我们的估计不够精确，无法将其作为场外交易市场交易对手选择CCP的预测模型的输入。第二，我们使用一个新的半监督预测任务来模拟经销商间市场上的对手选择。在主张使用条件熵作为兴趣度量，通过模型不可知论方法从数据中获取知识之前，我们将我们的方法作为模型可解释性文献的一部分。特别是，我们证明了使用深度神经网络来测量真实世界数据集的条件熵是正确的。在算法信息论的框架下，我们建立了文本剃刀熵，并推导出了一个与我们的半监督训练目标一致的显式公式。最后，我们借用博弈论的概念来定义textit top k Shapley值。这种新的支付分配方法满足Shapley值的大部分性质，当值函数是单调子模时，它是一个特别有趣的方法。与经典的Shapley值不同，top k Shapley值可以用特征数的二次时间来计算，而不是指数。我们实施了我们的方法，并报告了相应选择的特定任务的结果。最后，我们提出了一个改进的texitnode2vec算法，可以用来进一步研究中介。我们证明了在生成有偏游动中使用的邻域采样可以在对数时间内执行，并且具有一个准线性时间预计算，这与当前的实现方法不具有很好的伸缩性不同。

\[10\] A Concern Analysis of FOMC Statements Comparing The Great Recession and The COVID 19 Pandemic

标题 : 美国联邦公开市场委员会（FOMC）声明比较大萧条和covid19大流行的关注度分析

作者 : Luis Felipe Gutiérrez, Sima Siami-Namini, Neda Tavakoli, Akbar Siami Namin , 类别 ：Computational Engineering, Finance, and Science (cs.CE)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.02098

说明 : Comments: 10 pages

Abstract : It is important and informative to compare and contrast major economic crises in order to confront novel and unknown cases such as the COVID 19 pandemic. The 2006 Great Recession and then the 2019 pandemic have a lot to share in terms of unemployment rate, consumption expenditures, and interest rates set by Federal Reserve. In addition to quantitative historical data, it is also interesting to compare the contents of Federal Reserve statements for the period of these two crises and find out whether Federal Reserve cares about similar concerns or there are some other issues that demand separate and unique monetary policies. This paper conducts an analysis to explore the Federal Reserve concerns as expressed in their statements for the period of 2005 to 2020. The concern analysis is performed using natural language processing (NLP) algorithms and a trend analysis of concern is also presented. We observe that there are some similarities between the Federal Reserve statements issued during the Great Recession with those issued for the 2019 COVID 19 pandemic.
