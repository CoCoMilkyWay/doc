\[1\] Optimal Design of Limited Partnership Agreements

标题 : 有限合伙协议的优化设计

作者 : Mohammad Abbas Rezaei , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.07049

说明 : Comments: 49 pages

Abstract : General partners (GP) are sometimes paid on a deal by deal basis and other times on a whole portfolio basis. When is one method of payment better than the other I show that when assets (projects or firms) are highly correlated or when GPs have low reputation, whole portfolio contracting is superior to deal by deal contracting. In this case, by bundling payouts together, whole portfolio contracting enhances incentives for GPs to exert effort. Therefore, it is better suited to alleviate the moral hazard problem which is stronger than the adverse selection problem in the case of high correlation of assets or low reputation of GPs. In contrast, for low correlation of assets or high reputation of GPs, information asymmetry concerns dominate and deal by deal contracts become optimal, as they can efficiently weed out bad projects one by one. These results shed light on recent empirical findings on the relationship between investors and venture capitalists.

摘要 : 普通合伙人（GP）有时按交易支付，有时按整体投资组合支付。当一种支付方式优于另一种时，我表明，当资产（项目或公司）高度相关或全球定位系统声誉较低时，整体投资组合契约优于逐笔交易契约。在这种情况下，通过将支出捆绑在一起，整个投资组合契约增强了全科医生努力的动力。因此，在资产相关性较高或GPs声誉较低的情况下，它更适合于缓解比逆向选择问题更强的道德风险问题。相比之下，由于资产相关性较低或全球定位系统声誉较高，信息不对称问题占主导地位，逐笔交易契约能够有效地逐项剔除不良项目，从而成为最优契约。这些结果揭示了最近关于投资者和风险资本家之间关系的实证研究结果。

\[2\] Quantifying firm level economic systemic risk from nation wide supply networks

标题 : 从全国供应网络量化企业级经济系统性风险

作者 : Christian Diem, András Borsos, Tobias Reisch, János Kertész, Stefan Thurner , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.07260

说明 : -

Abstract : Crises like COVID 19 or the Japanese earthquake in 2011 exposed the fragility of corporate supply networks. The production of goods and services is a highly interdependent process and can be severely impacted by the default of critical suppliers or customers. While knowing the impact of individual companies on national economies is a prerequisite for efficient risk management, the quantitative assessment of the involved economic systemic risks (ESR) is hitherto practically non existent, mainly because of a lack of fine grained data in combination with coherent methods. Based on a unique value added tax dataset we derive the detailed production network of an entire country and present a novel approach for computing the ESR of all individual firms. We demonstrate that a tiny fraction (0.035 ) of companies has extraordinarily high systemic risk impacting about 23 of the national economic production should any of them default. Firm size alone cannot explain the ESR of individual companies their position in the production networks does matter substantially. If companies are ranked according to their economic systemic risk index (ESRI), firms with a rank above a characteristic value have very similar ESRI values, while for the rest the rank distribution of ESRI decays slowly as a power law 99.8 of all companies have an impact on less than 1 of the economy. We show that the assessment of ESR is impossible with aggregate data as used in traditional Input Output Economics. We discuss how simple policies of introducing supply chain redundancies can reduce ESR of some extremely risky companies.

摘要 : 像covid19或2011年日本地震这样的危机暴露了企业供应网络的脆弱性。商品和服务的生产是一个高度相互依赖的过程，可能会受到关键供应商或客户违约的严重影响。虽然了解单个公司对国民经济的影响是有效风险管理的先决条件，但迄今为止，对所涉及的经济系统性风险（ESR）的定量评估几乎不存在，主要原因是缺乏结合连贯方法的细粒度数据。基于一个独特的增值税数据集，我们得出了一个国家的详细生产网络，并提出了一种新的方法来计算所有单个企业的ESR。我们证明了一小部分（0.035）的公司具有非常高的系统性风险，如果其中任何一家公司违约，将影响大约23%的国民经济生产。单靠企业规模无法解释单个企业的ESR——它们在生产网络中的地位确实非常重要。如果根据经济系统风险指数（ESRI）对公司进行排名，排名高于特征值的公司具有非常相似的ESRI值，而对于其他公司，ESRI的排名分布衰减缓慢，因为所有公司中99.8%的公司对经济的影响不到1%。我们表明，在传统的投入产出经济学中，用总数据来评估ESR是不可能的。我们讨论了引入供应链冗余的简单政策如何降低一些风险极高的公司的ESR。

\[3\] Exporters reaction to positive foreign demand shocks

标题 : 出口商对国外需求正面冲击的反应

作者 : Asier Minondo , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.07319

说明 : -

Abstract : I use the quasi natural experiment of the 2018 African swine fever (ASF) outbreak in China to analyze swine exporters reaction to a foreign market s positive demand shock. I use the universe of Spanish firms export transactions to China and other countries, and compare the performance of swine and other exporters before and after the ASF. The ASF increased Spanish swine exporters sales to China three times. Swine exporters did not increase exported product portfolio or export revenue concentration in their best performing products in China after the ASF. The increase in exports to China positively impacted export revenue and survival in third markets. This positive impact was especially intense for small swine exporters.

摘要 : 我使用2018年非洲猪瘟（ASF）在中国爆发的准自然实验，分析生猪出口商对国外市场积极需求冲击的反应。我利用西班牙公司对中国和其他国家的出口交易，比较了ASF前后猪和其他出口商的表现。ASF将西班牙生猪出口商对中国的销量提高了三倍。在ASF之后，生猪出口商没有增加出口产品组合，也没有增加出口收入集中在中国表现最好的产品上。对中国出口的增加对出口收入和第三方市场的生存产生了积极影响。这一积极影响对小型生猪出口商尤其强烈。

\[5\] Mission Statements in Universities

标题 : 大学使命宣言

作者 : Julian D. Cortes, Liliana Rivera, Katerina Bohle Carbonell , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.07438

说明 : -

Abstract : The mission statement(s) (MS) is one of the most used tools for planning and management. Universities worldwide have implemented MS in their knowledge planning and management processes since the 1980s. Research studies have extensively explored the content and readability of MS and its effect on performance in firms, but their effect on public or nonprofit institutions such as universities has not been scrutinized with the same intensity. This study used Gunning s Fog Index score to determine the readability of a sample of worldwide universities MS and two rankings, i.e., Quacquarelli Symonds World University Ranking and SCImago Institutions Rankings, to determine their effect on performance. 摘要 : 任务说明是规划和管理中最常用的工具之一。自20世纪80年代以来，世界各地的大学都在其知识规划和管理过程中实施了管理学硕士。研究广泛探讨了管理学硕士的内容和可读性及其对企业绩效的影响，但它们对公立或非营利机构（如大学）的影响并没有受到同样的严格审查。本研究采用Gunning的Fog指数评分法，对全球大学MS和Quacquarelli-Symonds世界大学排名和SCImago大学排名两个样本的可读性进行了研究，以确定它们对绩效的影响。在区域、规模、研究重点、研究类型、年龄段或地位方面没有发现显著的可读性差异。Logistic回归（累积链接模型）结果显示，大学年龄、重点和规模等变量对学习成绩的解释力大于阅读能力。

\[7\] Mission Statement Effect on Research and Innovation Performance

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.07476

说明 : -

Abstract : The mission statement (MS) is the most used organizational strategic planning tool worldwide. The relationship between an MS and an organizations financial performance has been shown to be significantly positive, albeit small. However, an MSs relationship to the macroeconomic environment and to organizational innovation has not been investigated. We implemented a Structural Equation Modeling using the SCImago Institutional Ranking (SIR) as a global baseline sample and assessment of organizational research and innovation (RandI), an automated MS content analysis, and the Economic Complexity Index (ECI) as a comprehensive macroeconomic environment measure. \[8\] Lessons Learned from Photovoltaic Auctions in Germany

标题 : 德国光伏拍卖的经验教训

作者 : Taimyra Batz Liñeiro, Felix Müsgens , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.07536

说明 : -

Abstract : Auctions have become the primary instrument for promoting renewable energy around the world. However, the data published on such auctions are typically limited to aggregated information (e.g., total awarded capacity, average payments). These data constraints hinder the evaluation of realisation rates and other relevant auction dynamics. In this study, we present an algorithm to overcome these data limitations in German renewable energy auction programme by combining publicly available information from four different databases. We apply it to the German solar auction programme and evaluate auctions using quantitative methods. We calculate realisation rates and using correlation and regression analysis explore the impact of PV module prices, competition, and project and developer characteristics on project realisation and bid values. Our results confirm that the German auctions were effective. We also found that project realisation took, on average, 1.5 years (with 28 of projects finished late and incurring a financial penalty), nearly half of projects changed location before completion (again, incurring a financial penalty) and small and inexperienced developers could successfully participate in auctions.

摘要 : 拍卖已成为世界各地推广可再生能源的主要手段。然而，此类拍卖上公布的数据通常仅限于汇总信息（例如，总授予容量、平均付款）。这些数据限制阻碍了对变现率和其他相关拍卖动态的评估。在这项研究中，我们提出了一个算法，以克服这些数据的限制，在德国可再生能源拍卖计划相结合的公开资料，从四个不同的数据库。我们将其应用于德国太阳能拍卖计划，并使用定量方法评估拍卖。我们计算实现率，并使用相关和回归分析探讨光伏组件价格、竞争、项目和开发商特征对项目实现和投标价值的影响。我们的结果证实了德国的拍卖是有效的。我们还发现，项目实现平均需要1.5年时间（其中28个项目完工晚，并受到经济处罚），近一半的项目在完工前改变了位置（同样，受到经济处罚），小型和缺乏经验的开发商可以成功参与拍卖。

\[11\] Unprecedented decarbonization of China s power system in the post COVID era

标题 : 后科维德时代中国电力系统前所未有的脱碳

作者 : Biqing Zhu, Rui Guo, Zhu Deng, Wenli Zhao, Piyu Ke, Xinyu Dou, Steven J. Davis, Philippe Ciais, Pierre Gentine, Zhu Liu , 类别 ：Physics and Society (physics.soc-ph)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.06904

Abstract : In October of 2020, China announced that it aims to start reducing its carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions before 2030 and achieve carbon neutrality before 20601. The surprise announcement came in the midst of the COVID 19 pandemic which caused a transient drop in China s emissions in the first half of 2020. Here, we show an unprecedented de carbonization of China s power system in late 2020 although China s power related carbon emissions were 0.5 higher in 2020 than 2019, the majority (92.9 ) of the increased power demand was met by increases in low carbon (renewables and nuclear) generation (increased by 9.3 ), as compared to only 0.4 increase for fossil fuels. China s low carbon generation in the country grew in the second half of 2020, supplying a record high of 36.7 (increased by 1.9 compared to 2019) of total electricity in 2020, when the fossil production dropped to a historical low of 63.3 . Combined, the carbon intensity of China s power sector decreased to an historical low of 519.9 tCO2 GWh in 2020. If the fast decarbonization and slowed down power demand growth from 2019 to 2020 were to continue, by 2030, over half (50.8 ) of China s power demand could be provided by low carbon sources. Our results thus reveal that China made progress towards its carbon neutrality target during the pandemic, and suggest the potential for substantial further decarbonization in the next few years if the latest trends persist.

摘要 : 2020年10月，中国宣布计划在2030年前开始减少二氧化碳排放量，并在20601年前实现碳中和。这一令人惊讶的消息是在covid19流感大流行期间发布的，该流感大流行导致中国排放量在2020年上半年短暂下降。在这里，我们展示了中国电力系统在2020年末前所未有的去碳化，尽管中国2020年与电力相关的碳排放量比2019年高0.5，但大部分（92.9）增加的电力需求是通过低碳（可再生能源和核能）发电的增加（增加9.3）来满足的，相比之下，化石燃料只增加了0.4%。2020年下半年，中国的低碳发电量有所增长，2020年发电量占总发电量的36.7%（比2019年增加1.9），创历史新高，化石发电量降至63.3，创历史新低。加在一起，中国电力行业的碳强度在2020年降至519.9 tCO2 GWh的历史低点。如果2019年至2020年的快速脱碳和电力需求增长放缓继续下去，到2030年，中国一半以上（50.8%）的电力需求可以由低碳能源提供。因此，我们的结果表明，中国在这场大流行期间朝着碳中和目标取得了进展，并表明，如果最新趋势持续下去，中国在未来几年内有可能进一步大幅脱碳。
