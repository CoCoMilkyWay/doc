\[1\] Dynamic Portfolio Allocation in High Dimensions using Sparse Risk Factors

标题 : 基于稀疏风险因子的高维动态投资组合配置

作者 : Bruno P. C. Levy, Hedibert F. Lopes , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.06584

说明 : -

Abstract : We propose a fast and flexible method to scale multivariate return volatility predictions up to high dimensions using a dynamic risk factor model. Our approach increases parsimony via time varying sparsity on factor loadings and is able to sequentially learn the use of constant or time varying parameters and volatilities. We show in a dynamic portfolio allocation problem with 455 stocks from the S&P 500 index that our dynamic risk factor model is able to produce more stable and sparse predictions, achieving not just considerable portfolio performance improvements but also higher utility gains for the mean variance investor compared to the traditional Wishart benchmark and the passive investment on the market index.

摘要 : 我们提出了一种快速灵活的方法，利用动态风险因子模型将多元收益率波动率预测扩展到高维。我们的方法通过因子负荷的时变稀疏性增加了简约性，并且能够依次学习常数或时变参数和波动率的使用。我们在标准普尔500指数455只股票的动态投资组合分配问题中表明，我们的动态风险因素模型能够产生更稳定和稀疏的预测，与传统的Wishart基准和市场指数的被动投资相比，均值方差投资者不仅获得了可观的投资组合绩效改善，而且获得了更高的效用收益。

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.06607

说明 : Comments: 27 pages, 5 figures

Abstract : This paper studies time inconsistent stopping control problems under general multi dimensional controlled diffusion model. We first formulate the time inconsistent stopping control problems and propose a formal definition of their equilibriums. This definition for equilibriums is consistent with the ones in pure control stopping problems and thus can be seen as nontrivial extension to the existing theory. We show that an admissible pair ( hat u ,C) of control stopping policy is equilibrium if and only if the axillary function associated to it solves the extended Hamiltonian system. \[6\] Do City Borders Constrain Ethnic Diversity

标题 : 城市边界限制了种族多样性吗

作者 : Scott W. Hegerty , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.06017

说明 : -

Abstract : U.S. metropolitan areas, particularly in the industrial Midwest and Northeast, are well known for high levels of racial segregation. This is especially true where core cities end and suburbs begin often crossing the street can lead to physically similar, but much less ethnically diverse, suburban neighborhood. While these differences are often visually or intuitively apparent, this study seeks to quantify them using Geographic Information Systems and a variety of statistical methods. 2016 Census block group data are used to calculate an ethnic Herfindahl index for a set of two dozen large U.S. cities and their contiguous suburbs. Then, a mathematical method is developed to calculate a block group level Border Disparity Index (BDI), which is shown to vary by MSA and by specific suburbs. Its values can be compared across the sample to examine which cities are more likely to have borders that separate more diverse block groups from less diverse ones. The index can also be used to see which core cities are relatively more or less diverse than their suburbs, and which individual suburbs have the largest disparities vis a vis their core city. Atlanta and Detroit have particularly diverse suburbs, while Milwaukee s are not. Regression analysis shows that income differences and suburban shares of Black residents play significant roles in explaining variation across suburbs.

摘要 : 美国大都会地区，特别是在工业中西部和东北部，以高度种族隔离而闻名。尤其是在核心城市结束而郊区开始的地方，经常横穿街道可能会导致在物理上相似，但在种族上不那么多样化的郊区社区。虽然这些差异通常在视觉上或直观上很明显，但本研究试图利用地理信息系统和各种统计方法对其进行量化。2016年人口普查区组数据用于计算一组由二十多个美国大城市及其毗邻郊区组成的种族赫芬达尔指数。然后，提出了一种计算块组边界视差指数（BDI）的数学方法，该指数随MSA和特定郊区而变化。它的值可以在整个样本中进行比较，以检验哪些城市更有可能有边界，将更多样化的街区群与更少样化的街区群分开。该指数还可以用来查看哪些核心城市的多样性相对高于或低于郊区，哪些郊区与核心城市的差异最大。亚特兰大和底特律的郊区特别多样化，而密尔沃基的郊区则不然。回归分析表明，黑人居民的收入差异和郊区份额在解释郊区差异中起着重要作用。

作者 : Scott W. Hegerty , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.06021

说明 : -

Abstract : Milwaukee s 53206 ZIP code, located on the city s near North Side, has drawn considerable attention for its poverty and incarceration rates, as well as for its large proportion of vacant properties. As a result, it has benefited from targeted policies at the city level. Keeping in mind that ZIP codes are often not the most effective unit of geographic analysis, this study investigates Milwaukee s socioeconomic conditions at the block group level. These smaller areas statistics are then compared with those of their corresponding ZIP codes. The 53206 ZIP code is compared against others in Milwaukee for eight socioeconomic variables and is found to be near the extreme end of most rankings. This ZIP code would also be among Chicago s most extreme areas, but would lie near the middle of the rankings if located in Detroit. \[8\] Revisiting the Implied Remaining Variance framework of Carr and Sun (2014)

标题 : 重新审视Carr和Sun（2014）的隐含剩余方差框架

作者 : Claude Martini, Iacopo Raffaelli , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.06390

说明 : -

Abstract : Implied volatility is at the very core of modern finance, notwithstanding standard option pricing models continue to derive option prices starting from the joint dynamics of the underlying asset price and the spot volatility. These models often cause difficulties no closed formulas for prices, demanding calibration techniques, unclear maps between spot and implied volatility. Inspired by the practice of using implied volatility as quoting system for option prices, models for the joint dynamics of the underlying asset price and the implied volatility have been proposed to replace standard option pricing models. Starting from Carr and Sun (2014), we develop a framework based on the Implied Remaining Variance where minimal conditions for absence of arbitrage are identified, and smile bubbles are dealt with. The key concepts arising from the new IRV framework are those of locally consistent dynamics and sandwiched martingale. Within the new IRV framework, the results of Schweizer and Wissel (2008b) are reformulated, while those of El Amrani, Jacquier and Martini (2021) are independently derived.

摘要 : 隐含波动率是现代金融的核心，尽管标准期权定价模型继续从标的资产价格和现货波动率的联合动力学出发推导期权价格。这些模型往往造成困难：没有封闭的价格公式，需要校准技术，现货和隐含波动率之间的映射不清楚。受隐含波动率作为期权价格报价系统的实践启发，人们提出了标的资产价格和隐含波动率的联合动态模型来代替标准的期权定价模型。从Carr和Sun（2014）开始，我们开发了一个基于隐含剩余方差的框架，其中确定了不存在套利的最小条件，并处理了微笑泡沫。新IRV框架的核心概念是局部一致动力学和夹层鞅。在新的IRV框架下，Schweizer和Wissel（2008b）的结果被重新表述，而El-Amrani、Jacquier和Martini（2021）的结果被独立推导。

\[9\] Inflation who cares Monetary Policy in Times of Low Attention

标题 : 通胀在关注度不高的时候谁会在乎货币政策

作者 : Oliver Pf uti , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05297

说明 : -

Abstract : Based on a model of optimal information acquisition, I propose an approach to measure attention to inflation in the data. Applying this approach to US consumers and professional forecasters provides substantial evidence that attention to inflation in the US decreased significantly over the last five decades. Consistent with the theoretical model, attention is higher in times of volatile inflation. To examine the consequences of limited attention for monetary policy, I augment the standard New Keynesian model with a lower bound constraint on the nominal interest rate and inflation expectations that are characterized by limited attention. Accounting for the lower bound fundamentally alters the normative implications of low attention. While lower attention raises welfare absent the lower bound constraint, it decreases welfare when accounting for the lower bound. In the presence of the lower bound, limited attention can lead to inflation attention traps prolonged periods of a binding lower bound and low inflation due to slowly adjusting inflation expectations. To prevent these traps, it is optimal to increase the inflation target as attention declines.

摘要 : 基于最优信息获取模型，本文提出了一种度量数据中通货膨胀注意程度的方法。将这一方法应用于美国消费者和专业预测机构，提供了大量证据，表明过去50年来，人们对美国通胀的关注度显著下降。与理论模型一致的是，通胀波动时期的关注度更高。为了检验对货币政策关注度有限的后果，我增加了标准的新凯恩斯模型，对以关注度有限为特征的名义利率和通胀预期进行了下限约束。对下限的解释从根本上改变了低关注的规范含义。在没有下限约束的情况下，较低的注意力会提高福利，但在考虑下限约束时，它会降低福利。在存在下限的情况下，有限的关注可能会导致通胀注意力陷阱长期处于约束性下限，并由于通胀预期的缓慢调整而导致低通胀。为了防止这些陷阱，最好是在注意力下降时提高通胀目标。

\[12\] Emerging Platform Work in the Context of the Regulatory Loophole (The Uber Fiasco in Hungary)

标题 : 监管漏洞背景下的新兴平台工作（Uber在匈牙利的惨败）

作者 : Csaba Mako, Miklos Illessy, Jozsef Pap, Saeed Nosratabadi , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05651

说明 : -

Abstract : The study examines the essential features of the so called platform based work, which is rapidly evolving into a major, potentially game changing force in the labor market. From low skilled, low paid services (such as passenger transport) to highly skilled and high paying project based work (such as the development of artificial intelligence algorithms), a broad range of tasks can be carried out through a variety of digital platforms. Our paper discusses the platform based content, working conditions, employment status, and advocacy problems. Terminological and methodological problems are dealt with in depth in the course of the literature review, together with the gray areas of work and employment regulation. To examine some of the complex dynamics of this fast evolving arena, we focus on the unsuccessful market entry of the digital platform company Uber in Hungary 2016 and the relationship to institutional regulatory platform based work standards. Dilemmas relevant to the enforcement of labor law regarding platform based work are also paid special attention to the study. Employing a digital workforce is a significant challenge not only for labor law regulation but also for stakeholder advocacy.

摘要 : 这项研究考察了所谓的基于平台的工作的基本特征，这种工作正迅速演变为劳动力市场中一种可能改变游戏规则的主要力量。从低技能、低报酬的服务（如客运）到高技能、高报酬的项目性工作（如人工智能算法的开发），通过各种数字平台可以完成广泛的任务。本文从平台内容、工作环境、就业状况、宣传问题等方面进行了探讨。在文献综述的过程中，对术语和方法学问题以及工作和就业监管的灰色地带进行了深入的探讨。为了研究这一快速发展领域的一些复杂动态，我们重点关注2016年匈牙利数字平台公司优步（Uber）的市场进入失败，以及与基于平台的机构监管工作标准的关系。本研究也特别关注与平台工作相关的劳动法实施困境。雇佣数字劳动力不仅是劳动法监管的一个重大挑战，也是利益相关者倡导的一个重大挑战。

\[13\] Carbon Leakage in a European Power System with Inhomogeneous Carbon Prices

标题 : 碳价格不均匀的欧洲电力系统中的碳泄漏

作者 : Markus Schlott, Omar El Sayed, Mariia Bilousova, Fabian Hofmann, Alexander Kies, Horst Stöcker , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05669

说明 : Comments: 19 pages, 10 figures

Abstract : Global warming is one of the main threats to the future of humanity and extensive emissions of greenhouse gases are found to be the main cause of global temperature rise as well as climate change. During the last decades international attention has focused on this issue, as well as on searching for viable solutions to mitigate global warming. In this context, the pricing of greenhouse gas emissions turned out to be the most prominent mechanism First, to lower the emissions, and second, to capture their external costs. By now, various carbon dioxide taxes have been adopted by several countries in Europe and around the world moreover, the list of these countries is growing. However, there is no standardized approach and the price for carbon varies significantly from one country to another. Regionally diversified carbon prices in turn lead to carbon leakage, which will offset the climate protection goals. In this paper, a simplified European power system with flexible carbon prices regarding the Gross Domestic Product (GDP) is investigated. A distribution parameter that quantifies carbon leakage is defined and varied together with the base carbon price, where the combination of both parameters describes the spatially resolved price distribution, i.e. the effective carbon pricing among the European regions. It is shown that inhomogeneous carbon prices will indeed lead to significant carbon leakage across the continent, and that coal fired electricity generation will remain a cheap and therefore major source of power in Eastern and South Eastern Europe representing a potential risk for the long term decarbonization targets within the European Union.

摘要 : 全球变暖是人类未来面临的主要威胁之一，温室气体的大量排放是全球气温升高和气候变化的主要原因。在过去的几十年里，国际社会的注意力集中在这个问题上，以及寻找可行的解决办法来减轻全球变暖。在这种背景下，温室气体排放定价成为最突出的机制，一是降低排放量，二是捕获其外部成本。到目前为止，欧洲和世界上已有多个国家采用了各种各样的二氧化碳税，而且这些国家的名单还在不断增加。然而，没有标准化的方法，各国的碳价格差异很大。区域多元化的碳价格反过来导致碳泄漏，这将抵消气候保护目标。本文研究了一个简化的欧洲电力系统，该系统考虑了国内生产总值（GDP）的弹性碳价格。量化碳泄漏的分布参数与基础碳价格一起定义和变化，其中两个参数的组合描述了空间解析的价格分布，即欧洲地区之间的有效碳定价。结果表明，不均匀的碳价格确实会导致整个欧洲大陆的碳泄漏，燃煤发电仍然是东欧和东南欧廉价的主要电力来源，这对欧盟内部的长期脱碳目标构成了潜在风险。

\[14\] Using social network analysis to prevent money laundering

标题 : 利用社会网络分析防止洗钱

作者 : A. Fronzetti Colladon, E. Remondi , 类别 ：Social and Information Networks (cs.SI)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05793

说明 : -

Abstract : This research explores the opportunities for the application of network analytic techniques to prevent money laundering. We worked on real world data by analyzing the central database of a factoring company, mainly operating in Italy, over a period of 19 months. This database contained the financial operations linked to the factoring business, together with other useful information about the company clients. We propose a new approach to sort and map relational data and present predictive models, based on network metrics, to assess risk profiles of clients involved in the factoring business. We find that risk profiles can be predicted by using social network metrics. In our dataset, the most dangerous social actors deal with bigger or more frequent financial operations they are more peripheral in the transactions network they mediate transactions across different economic sectors and operate in riskier countries or Italian regions. Finally, to spot potential clusters of criminals, we propose a visual analysis of the tacit links existing among different companies who share the same owner or representative. Our findings show the importance of using a network based approach when looking for suspicious financial operations and potential criminals.

摘要 : 本研究探讨应用网路分析技术预防洗钱的机会。我们通过分析一家主要在意大利运营的保理公司的中央数据库，研究了19个月的真实数据。该数据库包含与保理业务相关的金融业务，以及有关公司客户的其他有用信息。我们提出了一种新的方法来排序和映射关系数据，并提出了基于网络度量的预测模型，以评估参与保理业务的客户的风险状况。我们发现，风险概况可以预测使用社会网络指标。在我们的数据集中，最危险的社会行为体处理更大或更频繁的金融业务，他们在交易网络中更边缘化，他们调解不同经济部门的交易，并在风险更高的国家或意大利地区开展业务。最后，为了找出潜在的犯罪集团，我们提出了一个视觉分析的默契联系存在于不同的公司谁共享相同的所有者或代表。我们的研究结果表明，在寻找可疑金融活动和潜在犯罪分子时，使用基于网络的方法非常重要。
