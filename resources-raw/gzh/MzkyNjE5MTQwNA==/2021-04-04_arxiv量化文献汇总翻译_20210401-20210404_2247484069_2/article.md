链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00129

说明 : -

Abstract : Tis paper is a literature review focusing on human capital, skills of employees, demographic change, management, training and their impact on productivity growth. Intrafirm behaviour has been recognized as a potentially important driver for productivity. Results from surveys show that management practices have become more structured, in the sense of involving more data collection and analysis. Furthermore, a strong positive correlation between the measured management quality and firm performance can be observed. Studies suggest that there is a positive association between management score and productivity growth. 摘要 : 本文是一篇关于人力资本、员工技能、人口变化、管理、培训及其对生产率增长影响的文献综述。企业内部行为已被公认为生产力的潜在重要驱动力。调查结果显示，管理实践已变得更加结构化，涉及更多的数据收集和分析。此外，管理品质与企业绩效呈现显著正相关。研究表明，管理得分与生产率增长之间存在正相关关系。缺乏或低水平的员工技能和资格可能从不同的角度解释了所观察到的生产率增长放缓。熟练劳动力减少的主要原因是人口结构的变化。建筑业日益受到人口发展的影响。建筑业的劳动力储备基本耗尽。合格劳动力的短缺正在影响项目成本、进度和质量。

\[2\] Limit Theorems for Default Contagion and Systemic Risk

标题 : 违约传染与系统风险的极限定理

作者 : Hamed Amini, Zhongyuan Cao, Agnes Sulem , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00248

说明 : Comments: 54 pages

Abstract : We consider a general tractable model for default contagion and systemic risk in a heterogeneous financial network, subject to an exogenous macroeconomic shock. We show that, under some regularity assumptions, the default cascade model could be transferred to a death process problem represented by balls and bins model. We also reduce the dimension of the problem by classifying banks according to different types, in an appropriate type space. These types may be calibrated to real world data by using machine learning techniques. We then state various limit theorems regarding the final size of default cascade over different types. In particular, under suitable assumptions on the degree and threshold distributions, we show that the final size of default cascade has asymptotically Gaussian fluctuations. We next state limit theorems for different system wide wealth aggregation functions and show how the systemic risk measure, in a given stress test scenario, could be related to the structure and heterogeneity of financial networks. We finally show how these results could be used by a social planner to optimally target interventions during a financial crisis, with a budget constraint and under partial information of the financial network.

摘要 : 我们考虑了一个受外部宏观经济冲击影响的异质金融网络中违约传染和系统性风险的一般可处理模型。我们证明了在一定的正则性假设下，缺省级联模型可以转化为一个由球和桶模型表示的死亡过程问题。我们还通过在适当的类型空间中按不同类型对银行进行分类来降低问题的维数。这些类型可以通过使用机器学习技术来校准到真实世界的数据。然后我们陈述了关于不同类型上缺省级联的最终大小的各种极限定理。特别地，在适当的度分布和阈值分布假设下，我们证明了缺省级联的最终大小具有渐近的高斯涨落。接下来，我们给出了不同系统范围内财富聚集函数的状态极限定理，并说明了在给定的压力测试情景下，系统风险测度如何与金融网络的结构和异质性相关。最后，我们展示了社会规划者如何利用这些结果，在有预算限制和金融网络部分信息的情况下，在金融危机期间，以最佳的目标为干预措施。

\[3\] Optimal Fees for Geometric Mean Market Makers

标题 : 几何平均做市商的最优费用

作者 : Alex Evans, Guillermo Angeris, Tarun Chitra , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00446

说明 : -

Abstract : Constant Function Market Makers (CFMMs) are a family of automated market makers that enable censorship resistant decentralized exchange on public blockchains. Arbitrage trades have been shown to align the prices reported by CFMMs with those of external markets. These trades impose costs on Liquidity Providers (LPs) who supply reserves to CFMMs. Trading fees have been proposed as a mechanism for compensating LPs for arbitrage losses. However, large fees reduce the accuracy of the prices reported by CFMMs and can cause reserves to deviate from desirable asset compositions. CFMM designers are therefore faced with the problem of how to optimally select fees to attract liquidity. We develop a framework for determining the value to LPs of supplying liquidity to a CFMM with fees when the underlying process follows a general diffusion. Focusing on a popular class of CFMMs which we call Geometric Mean Market Makers (G3Ms), our approach also allows one to select optimal fees for maximizing LP value. We illustrate our methodology by showing that an LP with mean variance utility will prefer a G3M over all alternative trading strategies as fees approach zero.

摘要 : 固定功能做市商（cfmm）是一个自动化做市商家族，能够在公共区块链上实现抗审查的分散式交易所。套利交易已被证明能使CFMMs报告的价格与外部市场的价格保持一致。这些交易对向CFMM提供准备金的流动性提供者（LP）施加了成本。有人提议将交易费用作为补偿有限合伙人套利损失的机制。然而，大额费用会降低CFMMs报告价格的准确性，并可能导致储量偏离理想的资产构成。因此，CFMM设计人员面临着如何优化选择费用以吸引流动性的问题。我们开发了一个框架，用于确定当基础流程遵循一般扩散时，向CFMM提供流动性并收取费用对LP的价值。针对一类流行的cfmm，我们称之为几何平均做市商（G3Ms），我们的方法也允许人们选择最优的费用来最大化LP价值。我们说明了我们的方法，显示了一个平均方差效用的有限合伙人将倾向于G3M的所有替代交易策略的费用接近零。

\[4\] On the Perception of Plagiarism in Academia

标题 : 学术界对剽窃的认识

作者 : Aaron Gregory, Joshua Leeman , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00574

说明 : -

Abstract : Plagiarism is the representation of another author s language, thoughts, ideas, or expressions as one s own original work. In educational contexts, there are differing definitions of plagiarism depending on the institution. Prominent scholars of plagiarism include Rebecca Moore Howard, Susan Blum, Tracey Bretag, and Sarah Elaine Eaton, among others. Plagiarism is considered a violation of academic integrity and a breach of journalistic ethics. It is subject to sanctions such as penalties, suspension, expulsion from school or work, substantial fines and even incarceration. Recently, cases of extreme plagiarism have been identified in academia. The modern concept of plagiarism as immoral and originality as an ideal emerged in Europe in the 18th century, particularly with the Romantic movement. Generally, plagiarism is not in itself a crime, but like counterfeiting fraud can be punished in a court for prejudices caused by copyright infringement, violation of moral rights, or torts. In academia and industry, it is a serious ethical offense. Plagiarism and copyright infringement overlap to a considerable extent, but they are not equivalent concepts, and many types of plagiarism do not constitute copyright infringement, which is defined by copyright law and may be adjudicated by courts. Plagiarism might not be the same in all countries. Some countries, such as India and Poland, consider plagiarism to be a crime, and there have been cases of people being imprisoned for plagiarizing. In other instances plagiarism might be the complete opposite of academic dishonesty, in fact some countries find the act of plagiarizing a professional s work flattering. Students who move to the United States and other Western countries from countries where plagiarism is not frowned upon often find the transition difficult.

摘要 : 剽窃是将另一个作者的语言、思想、想法或表达方式表现为自己的原创作品。在教育背景下，对剽窃的定义因学校而异。著名的剽窃学者包括丽贝卡·摩尔·霍华德（Rebecca Moore Howard）、苏珊·布鲁姆（Susan Blum）、特雷西·布雷塔格（Tracey Bretag）和莎拉·伊莲·伊顿（Sarah Elaine Eaton）等。抄袭被认为是对学术诚信的侵犯，是对新闻职业道德的违背。它受到处罚、停学、开除学籍或工作、巨额罚款甚至监禁等制裁。最近，学术界发现了一些极端抄袭的案例。现代观念认为剽窃是不道德的，原创是一种理想，这种观念在18世纪的欧洲出现，尤其是在浪漫主义运动中。一般来说，剽窃本身并不是一种犯罪，但与假冒一样，欺诈也可以因侵犯版权、侵犯道德权利或侵权行为造成的偏见而在法庭上受到惩罚。在学术界和工业界，这是一种严重的道德犯罪。剽窃与著作权侵权有相当程度的重叠，但并非等同的概念，许多类型的剽窃并不构成著作权侵权，著作权法对此进行了界定，法院可能会作出判决。剽窃在所有国家可能都不一样。一些国家，如印度和波兰，认为抄袭是一种犯罪行为，并且有人因为抄袭而被监禁的案例。在其他情况下，剽窃可能是学术欺诈的完全对立面，事实上，有些国家认为剽窃专业人士的作品会让人受宠若惊。从不反对剽窃的国家移居美国和其他西方国家的学生，往往会发现这种转变很困难。

\[5\] TradeR

标题 : 交易员

作者 : Karush Suri, Xiao Qi Shi, Konstantinos Plataniotis, Yuri Lawryshyn , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00620

说明 : -

Abstract : Advances in Reinforcement Learning (RL) span a wide variety of applications which motivate development in this area. While application tasks serve as suitable benchmarks for real world problems, RL is seldomly used in practical scenarios consisting of abrupt dynamics. This allows one to rethink the problem setup in light of practical challenges. We present Trade Execution using Reinforcement Learning (TradeR) which aims to address two such practical challenges of catastrophy and surprise minimization by formulating trading as a real world hierarchical RL problem. Through this lens, TradeR makes use of hierarchical RL to execute trade bids on high frequency real market experiences comprising of abrupt price variations during the 2019 fiscal year COVID19 stock market crash. The framework utilizes an energy based scheme in conjunction with surprise value function for estimating and minimizing surprise. In a large scale study of 35 stock symbols from the S&P500 index, TradeR demonstrates robustness to abrupt price changes and catastrophic losses while maintaining profitable outcomes. We hope that our work serves as a motivating example for application of RL to practical problems.

摘要 : 强化学习（RL）的进展跨越了促进这一领域发展的各种各样的应用。当应用程序任务作为现实世界问题的合适基准时，RL很少用于由突然动态组成的实际场景。这使人们能够根据实际挑战重新思考问题的设置。我们提出了利用强化学习（TradeR）的交易执行，旨在通过将交易描述为现实世界的层次RL问题来解决灾难性和意外最小化这两个实际挑战。通过这一视角，交易员利用层级RL在2019财年COVID19股市崩盘期间的高频率真实市场经验（包括突然的价格变化）上执行交易出价。该框架采用基于能量的方法，结合出其不意值函数来估计和最小化出其不意。在对标准普尔500指数35个股票符号的大规模研究中，交易员在保持盈利的同时，表现出了对价格突变和灾难性损失的鲁棒性。我们希望我们的工作能为RL在实际问题中的应用提供一个激励性的例子。

\[7\] Statistical significance revisited

标题 : 统计学意义再探讨

作者 : Maike Torm hlen, Galiya Klinkova, Michael Grabinski , 类别 ：Methodology (stat.ME)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00262

说明 : Comments: 14 pages, 3 figures

Abstract : Statistical significance measures the reliability of a result obtained from a random experiment. We investigate the number of repetitions needed for a statistical result to have a certain significance. In the first step, we consider binomially distributed variables in the example of medication testing with fixed placebo efficacy, asking how many experiments are needed in order to achieve a significance of 95 . In the next step, we take the probability distribution of the placebo efficacy into account, which to the best of our knowledge has not been done so far. Depending on the specifics, we show that in order to obtain identical significance, it may be necessary to perform twice as many experiments than in a setting where the placebo distribution is neglected. We proceed by considering more general probability distributions and close with comments on some erroneous assumptions on probability distributions which lead, for instance, to a trivial explanation of the fat tail.

摘要 : 统计显著性衡量随机实验结果的可靠性。我们调查了统计结果具有一定意义所需的重复次数。在第一步中，我们以安慰剂疗效固定的药物试验为例考虑二项分布变量，询问需要多少实验才能达到95的显著性。在下一步中，我们考虑安慰剂疗效的概率分布，据我们所知，迄今为止还没有这样做。根据具体情况，我们发现为了获得相同的显著性，可能需要进行两倍于忽略安慰剂分布的实验。我们继续考虑更一般的概率分布，并以一些错误的概率分布假设的评论结束，例如，导致对厚尾的一个微不足道的解释。

\[8\] Normalizations and misspecification in skill formation models

标题 : 技能形成模型中的规范化与误判

作者 : Joachim Freyberger , 类别 ：Econometrics (econ.EM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00473

说明 : -

Abstract : An important class of structural models investigates the determinants of skill formation and the optimal timing of interventions. To achieve point identification of the parameters, researcher typically normalize the scale and location of the unobserved skills. This paper shows that these seemingly innocuous restrictions can severely impact the interpretation of the parameters and counterfactual predictions. For example, simply changing the units of measurements of observed variables might yield ineffective investment strategies and misleading policy recommendations. To tackle these problems, this paper provides a new identification analysis, which pools all restrictions of the model, characterizes the identified set of all parameters without normalizations, illustrates which features depend on these normalizations, and introduces a new set of important policy relevant parameters that are identified under weak assumptions and yield robust conclusions. As a byproduct, this paper also presents a general and formal definition of when restrictions are truly normalizations.

摘要 : 一类重要的结构模型研究技能形成的决定因素和干预的最佳时机。为了实现参数的点识别，研究人员通常对未观察到的技能的规模和位置进行规范化。本文表明，这些看似无害的限制会严重影响参数的解释和反事实预测。例如，仅仅改变观察变量的计量单位可能会产生无效的投资策略和误导性的政策建议。为了解决这些问题，本文提出了一种新的辨识分析方法，该方法综合了模型的所有约束条件，刻画了未经规范化处理的所有参数的辨识集，说明了哪些特征依赖于这些规范化处理，并引入了一组新的弱条件下辨识的重要政策相关参数假设并得出可靠的结论。作为一个副产品，本文还提出了一个一般和正式的定义时，限制是真正的规范化。

\[9\] A new spin on optimal portfolios and ecological equilibria

标题 : 关于最优投资组合与生态平衡的一个新观点

作者 : Jerome Garnier-Brun, Michael Benzaquen, Stefano Ciliberti, Jean-Philippe Bouchaud , 类别 ：Statistical Mechanics (cond-mat.stat-mech)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00668

说明 : Comments: 37 pages, 7 figures

Abstract : We consider the classical problem of optimal portfolio construction with the constraint that no short position is allowed, or equivalently the valid equilibria of multispecies Lotka Volterra equations, in the special case where the interaction matrix is of unit rank, corresponding to a single resource MacArthur model. We compute the average number of solutions and show that its logarithm grows as N alpha , where N is the number of assets or species and alpha leq 2 3 depends on the interaction matrix distribution. We conjecture that the most likely number of solutions is much smaller and related to the typical sparsity m(N) of the solutions, which we compute explicitly. We also find that the solution landscape is similar to that of spin glasses, i.e. very different configurations are quasi degenerate. Correspondingly, disorder chaos is also present in our problem. We discuss the consequence of such a property for portfolio construction and ecologies, and question the meaning of rational decisions when there is a very large number satisficing solutions.

摘要 : 在相互作用矩阵为单位秩的特殊情况下，我们考虑了不允许空头头寸的经典最优投资组合构造问题，或等价于多种群Lotka-Volterra方程的有效平衡点，对应于单资源MacArthur模型。我们计算了解的平均数，并证明其对数随着Nα增长，其中N是资产或物种的数量，αleq 2 3取决于相互作用矩阵的分布。我们推测最可能的解的数目要小得多，并且与解的典型稀疏性m（N）有关，这是我们显式计算的。我们还发现，溶液景观类似于自旋玻璃，即非常不同的配置是准退化。相应地，我们的问题中也存在无序-混沌现象。我们讨论了这样一个性质对投资组合构建和生态的影响，并在有大量满意解的情况下质疑理性决策的意义。

\[10\] Optimal Retirement Time and Consumption with the Variation in Habitual Persistence

标题 : 习惯持续性变化下的最优退休时间与消费

作者 : Lin He, Zongxia Liang, Yilun Song, Qi Ye , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.16800

说明 : -

Abstract : In this paper,we study the individual s optimal retirement time and optimal consumption under habitual persistence. Because the individual feels equally satisfied with a lower habitual level and is more reluctant to change the habitual level after retirement, we assume that both the level and the sensitivity of the habitual consumption decline at the time of retirement. We establish the concise form of the habitual evolutions, and obtain the optimal retirement time and consumption policy based on martingale and duality methods. The optimal consumption experiences a sharp decline at retirement, but the excess consumption raises because of the reduced sensitivity of the habitual level. This result contributes to explain the retirement consumption puzzle . Particularly, the optimal retirement and consumption policies are balanced between the wealth effect and the habitual effect. Larger wealth increases consumption, and larger growth inertia (sensitivity) of the habitual level decreases consumption and brings forward the retirement time.

摘要 : 本文研究了个人在习惯性坚持下的最优退休时间和最优消费。由于个人对较低的习惯性消费水平感到同样满意，并且更不愿意在退休后改变习惯性消费水平，我们假设退休时习惯性消费水平和敏感性都会下降。建立了习惯演化的简明形式，并基于鞅和对偶方法得到了最优的退休时间和消费政策。最佳消费在退休时急剧下降，但由于习惯水平的敏感性降低，过度消费增加。这一结果有助于解释退休消费之谜。特别是最优的退休和消费政策在财富效应和习惯效应之间取得了平衡。财富越大，消费越高；习惯水平增长惯性（敏感性）越大，消费越低，退休时间提前。

\[11\] A survey of electricity spot and futures price models for risk management applications

标题 : 用于风险管理的电力现货和期货价格模型综述

作者 : Thomas Deschatre, Olivier Féron, Pierre Gruet , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.16918

说明 : -

Abstract : This review presents the set of electricity price models proposed in the literature since the opening of power markets. We focus on price models applied to financial pricing and risk management. We classify these models according to their ability to represent the random behavior of prices and some of their characteristics. In particular, this classification helps users to choose among the most suitable models for their risk management problems.

摘要 : 本文综述了电力市场开放以来文献中提出的一套电价模型。我们关注应用于金融定价和风险管理的价格模型。我们根据这些模型代表价格随机行为的能力和它们的一些特征对它们进行分类。特别是，这种分类有助于用户为他们的风险管理问题选择最合适的模型。

\[12\] Solving Heterogeneous General Equilibrium Economic Models with Deep Reinforcement Learning

标题 : 用深度强化学习求解异质一般均衡经济模型

作者 : Edward Hill, Marco Bardoscia, Arthur Turrell , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.16977

说明 : Comments: 11 pages, 5 figures

Abstract : General equilibrium macroeconomic models are a core tool used by policymakers to understand a nation s economy. They represent the economy as a collection of forward looking actors whose behaviours combine, possibly with stochastic effects, to determine global variables (such as prices) in a dynamic equilibrium. However, standard semi analytical techniques for solving these models make it difficult to include the important effects of heterogeneous economic actors. The COVID 19 pandemic has further highlighted the importance of heterogeneity, for example in age and sector of employment, in macroeconomic outcomes and the need for models that can more easily incorporate it. We use techniques from reinforcement learning to solve such models incorporating heterogeneous agents in a way that is simple, extensible, and computationally efficient. We demonstrate the method s accuracy and stability on a toy problem for which there is a known analytical solution, its versatility by solving a general equilibrium problem that includes global stochasticity, and its flexibility by solving a combined macroeconomic and epidemiological model to explore the economic and health implications of a pandemic. The latter successfully captures plausible economic behaviours induced by differential health risks by age.

摘要 : 一般均衡宏观经济模型是决策者用来理解一国经济的核心工具。它们将经济表现为前瞻性行为体的集合，其行为可能与随机效应相结合，以确定动态均衡中的全球变量（如价格）。然而，解决这些模型的标准半分析技术使得很难包含异质经济行为体的重要影响。COVID 19流行病进一步突出了异质性的重要性，例如年龄和就业部门的异质性，宏观经济结果的异质性，以及更容易将其纳入模型的必要性。我们使用强化学习的技术，以一种简单、可扩展和计算效率高的方式来解决包含异构代理的模型。我们通过求解一个包含全局随机性的一般均衡问题，证明了该方法对一个已知解析解的玩具问题的精确性和稳定性，以及它的通用性，其灵活性是通过解决宏观经济和流行病学相结合的模型来探讨大流行对经济和健康的影响。后者成功地捕捉到了由年龄不同的健康风险引起的看似合理的经济行为。

\[13\] Subsidising Inclusive Insurance to Reduce Poverty

标题 : 资助普惠保险以减贫

作者 : José Miguel Flores Contró, Kira Henshaw, Sooie-Hoe Loke, Séverine Arnold, Corina Constantinescu , 类别 ：Applications (stat.AP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.17255

说明 : -

Abstract : In this article, we consider a compound Poisson type model for households capital. Using risk theory techniques, we determine the probability of a household falling under the poverty line. Microinsurance is then introduced to analyse its impact as an insurance solution for the lower income class. Our results validate those previously obtained with this type of model, showing that microinsurance alone is not sufficient to reduce the probability of falling into the area of poverty for specific groups of people, since premium payments constrain households capital growth. This indicates the need for additional aid particularly from the government. As such, we propose several premium subsidy strategies and discuss the role of government in subsidising microinsurance to help reduce poverty.

摘要 : 在本文中，我们考虑家庭资本的复合泊松模型。利用风险理论技术，我们确定了一个家庭陷入贫困线以下的概率。然后介绍小额保险，分析其作为低收入阶层保险解决方案的影响。我们的研究结果验证了以前用这类模型得到的结果，表明小额保险本身不足以降低特定人群陷入贫困的可能性，因为保费支付限制了家庭资本的增长。这表明需要额外的援助，特别是政府的援助。因此，我们提出了若干保费补贴策略，并讨论了政府在补贴小额保险以帮助减贫方面的作用。
