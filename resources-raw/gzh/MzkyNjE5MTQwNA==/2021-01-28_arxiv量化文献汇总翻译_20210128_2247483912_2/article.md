\[1\] Sample path generation of the stochastic volatility CGMY process and its application to path dependent option pricing

标题 : 随机波动率CGMY过程的样本路径生成及其在路径相关期权定价中的应用

作者 : Young Shin Kim , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11001

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper proposes the sample path generation method for the stochastic volatility version of CGMY process. We present the Monte Carlo method for European and American option pricing with the sample path generation and calibrate model parameters to the American style S &P 100 index options market, using the least square regression method. Moreover, we discuss path dependent options such as Asian and Barrier options.

摘要 : 针对CGMY过程的随机波动性，提出了样本路径生成方法。本文提出了欧美期权定价的montecarlo方法和样本路径生成方法，并用最小二乘回归方法对美式标普100指数期权市场的模型参数进行了校正。此外，我们还讨论了路径依赖型期权，如亚式期权和障碍期权。

\[2\] Understanding the uneven spread of COVID 19 in the context of the global interconnected economy

标题 : 在全球互联经济的背景下理解covid19的不均匀传播

作者 : Dimitrios Tsiotas, Vassilis Tselios , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11036

说明 : -

Abstract : Using network analysis, this paper develops a multidimensional methodological framework for understanding the uneven (cross country) spread of COVID 19 in the context of the global interconnected economy. The globally interconnected system of tourism mobility is modeled as a complex network, where two main stages in the temporal spread of COVID 19 are revealed and defined by the cutting point of the 44th day from Wuhan. The first stage describes the outbreak in Asia and North America, the second one in Europe, South America, and Africa, while the outbreak in Oceania is spread along both stages. The analysis shows that highly connected nodes in the global tourism network (GTN) are infected early by the pandemic, while nodes of lower connectivity are late infected. Moreover, countries with the same network centrality as China were early infected on average by COVID 19. The paper also finds that network interconnectedness, economic openness, and transport integration are key determinants in the early global spread of the pandemic, and it reveals that the spatio temporal patterns of the worldwide spread of COVID 19 are more a matter of network interconnectivity than of spatial proximity.

摘要 : 利用网络分析，本文发展了一个多维的方法论框架来理解全球互联经济背景下covid19的不均匀（跨国）传播。全球互联的旅游机动性系统被建模为一个复杂的网络，其中covid19的时间传播的两个主要阶段被武汉第44天的切点揭示和定义。第一阶段描述了亚洲和北美的疫情，第二阶段描述了欧洲、南美和非洲的疫情，而大洋洲的疫情是沿着这两个阶段传播的。分析表明，全球旅游网络（GTN）中高度连通的节点在大流行初期受到感染，而连通性较低的节点在大流行后期受到感染。此外，与中国具有相同网络中心地位的国家平均在CoVID19早期受到感染。本文还发现，网络互联性、经济开放性和运输一体化是该流行病早期全球传播的关键决定因素，并揭示了covid19全球传播的时空模式与其说是空间邻近性，不如说是网络互联性。

\[3\] Four Ways to Scale Up

标题 : 四种扩大规模的方法

作者 : Bent Flyvbjerg , 类别 ：General Finance (q-fin.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11104

说明 : -

Abstract : Scale up is the process of growing a venture in size. The paper identifies modularity and speed as keys to successful scale up. On that basis four types of scale up are identified Smart, dumb, forced, and fumbled. Smart scale up combines modularity and speed. Dumb scale up is bespoke and slow, and very common. The paper presents examples of each type of scale up, explaining why they were successful or not. Whether you are a small startup or Elon Musk trying to grow Tesla and SpaceX or Jeff Bezos scaling up Amazon or you are the US, UK, Chinese, or other government trying to increase power production, expand your infrastructure, or make your health, education, and social services work better modularity and speed are the answer to effective delivery, or so the paper argues. How well you deal with modularity and speed decides whether your efforts succeed or fail. Most ventures, existing or planned, are neither fully smart nor fully dumb, but have elements of both. Successful organizations work to tip the balance towards smart by (a) introducing elements of smart scale up into existing ventures and (b) starting new, fully smart scaled ventures, to make themselves less dumb and ever smarter.

摘要 : 扩大规模是扩大企业规模的过程。本文认为模块化和速度是成功扩展的关键。在此基础上确定了四种扩大规模的方式：聪明的、愚蠢的、被迫的和笨拙的。智能扩展结合了模块化和速度。Dumb-scale-up是定制的、缓慢的，而且非常常见。本文列举了每种规模扩大的例子，解释了它们成功与否的原因。无论你是一家小型初创企业，还是伊隆•马斯克（Elon Musk）试图发展特斯拉（Tesla）和SpaceX，或杰夫•贝佐斯（Jeff Bezos）扩大亚马逊的规模，或是美国、英国、中国或其他国家的政府试图增加电力生产、扩大基础设施，或是让你的健康、教育得到改善，而社会服务工作更好的模块化和速度是有效交付的答案，或者说，论文认为如此。你处理模块化和速度的好坏决定了你的努力是成功还是失败。大多数企业，无论是现有的还是计划中的，既不是完全聪明的，也不是完全愚蠢的，而是两者兼备的。成功的组织致力于通过（a）在现有的企业中引入智能扩展的元素，以及（b）开始新的、完全智能化的规模化企业，使自己不那么愚蠢，变得更聪明，从而将天平转向智能。

\[4\] The Impact of Privacy Laws on Online User Behavior

标题 : 隐私法对网络用户行为的影响

作者 : Julia Schmitt, Klaus M. Miller, Bernd Skiera , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11366

说明 : -

Abstract : Policy makers worldwide draft privacy laws that require trading off between safeguarding consumer privacy and preventing economic damage to companies that use consumer data. However, little empirical knowledge exists as to how privacy laws affect companies performance. Accordingly, this paper empirically quantifies the effects of the enforcement of the EU s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) on online user behavior over time, analyzing data from 6,286 websites spanning 24 industries, during the 10 months before and 18 months after the GDPR s enforcement in 2018. A difference in differences analysis, with a synthetic control group approach, enables the short and long term effects of the GDPR on user behavior to be reliably isolated. The results show that, on average, the GDPR s effects on user quantity and usage intensity were negative e.g., 3 months (18 months) post GDPR, the numbers of unique visitors and total visits to a website decreased by 0.8 (6.6 ) and 4.9 (10 ), respectively. These effects could translate into average revenue losses of 7 million USD for e commerce websites and almost 2.5 million USD for ad based websites 18 months after GDPR. The GDPR s effects vary across websites, with some industries even benefiting from it moreover, more popular websites suffered less damage, suggesting that the GDPR increased market concentration.

摘要 : 世界各地的政策制定者起草隐私法，要求在保护消费者隐私和防止对使用消费者数据的公司造成经济损害之间进行权衡。然而，关于隐私法如何影响公司绩效的实证知识却很少。因此，本文通过分析2018年欧盟数据保护条例（GDPR）实施前10个月和实施后18个月来自24个行业6286家网站的数据，实证量化了欧盟数据保护条例（GDPR）实施对在线用户行为的影响。采用综合控制组方法进行差异分析，可以可靠地隔离GDPR对用户行为的短期和长期影响。结果表明，平均而言，GDPR对用户数量和使用强度的影响是负的，例如，GDPR后3个月（18个月），网站的唯一访问量和总访问量分别下降了0.8（6.6）和4.9（10）。这些影响可能转化为平均收入损失700万美元的电子商务网站和近250万美元的广告为基础的网站18个月后GDPR。GDPR的影响因网站而异，一些行业甚至从中受益。此外，更受欢迎的网站受到的损害较小，这表明GDPR提高了市场集中度。

\[5\] Optimal Bundling

标题 : 最佳捆绑

作者 : Soheil Ghili , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11532

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper studies pure bundling. Specifically, I show that, under some conditions, a firm optimally chooses to sell only the full bundle of a given set of products if and only if the optimal sales volume of the full bundle is weakly larger than the optimal sales volume for any smaller bundle. I argue that this characterization can be interpreted as follows pure bundling is sub optimal when there is considerable variation across consumers in how complementary they find disjoint sub bundles, and or when this variation correlates negatively with their price sensitivity. I then demonstrate using simulated data and a random coefficient discrete choice demand model that capturing these two variations is indeed crucial for model selection in the empirical analysis of bundling decisions.

摘要 : 本文研究纯捆绑。具体地说，我证明了在某些条件下，一个企业最优地选择只销售一组给定产品的全部捆绑，当且仅当全部捆绑的最优销售量弱于任何较小捆绑的最优销售量。我认为，这种特征可以解释为：当消费者发现不相交子捆绑的互补性存在很大差异时，或者当这种差异与其价格敏感性负相关时，纯捆绑是次优的。然后，我使用模拟数据和随机系数离散选择需求模型证明，在捆绑决策的实证分析中，捕捉这两种变化对于模型选择确实至关重要。

\[6\] Current and Emergent Economic Impacts of Covid 19 and Brexit on UK Fresh Produce and Horticultural Businesses

标题 : Covid 19和英国脱欧对英国新鲜农产品和园艺业的当前和紧急经济影响

作者 : Lilian Korir, Archie Drake, Martin Collison, Tania Carolina Camacho-Villa, Elizabeth Sklar, Simon Pearson , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11551

说明 : Comments: 15pages, 1 figures

Abstract : This paper describes a study designed to investigate the current and emergent impacts of Covid 19 and Brexit on UK horticultural businesses. Various characteristics of UK horticultural production, notably labour reliance and import dependence, make it an important sector for policymakers concerned to understand the effects of these disruptive events as we move from 2020 into 2021. The study design prioritised timeliness, using a rapid survey to gather information from a relatively small (n 19) but indicative group of producers. The main novelty of the results is to suggest that a very substantial majority of producers either plan to scale back production in 2021 (47 ) or have been unable to make plans for 2021 because of uncertainty (37 ). The results also add to broader evidence that the sector has experienced profound labour supply challenges, with implications for labour cost and quality. The study discusses the implications of these insights from producers in terms of productivity and automation, as well as in terms of broader economic implications. Although automation is generally recognised as the long term future for the industry (89 ), it appeared in the study as the second most referred short term option (32 ) only after changes to labour schemes and policies (58 ). Currently, automation plays a limited role in contributing to the UK s horticultural workforce shortage due to economic and socio political uncertainties. The conclusion highlights policy recommendations and future investigative intentions, as well as suggesting methodological and other discussion points for the research community.

摘要 : 本文描述了一项旨在调查covid19和英国脱欧对英国园艺企业的当前和紧急影响的研究。英国园艺生产的各种特点，特别是对劳动力的依赖和对进口的依赖，使相关政策制定者在我们从2020年进入2021年的过程中了解这些破坏性事件的影响成为一个重要的部门。研究设计优先考虑及时性，使用快速调查从相对较小（n 19）但具有指示性的生产者群体收集信息。结果的主要新颖之处在于，绝大多数生产商要么计划在2021年缩减产量（47），要么由于不确定性而无法制定2021年的计划（37）。研究结果还进一步证明，该行业经历了深刻的劳动力供应挑战，并对劳动力成本和质量产生了影响。这项研究从生产率和自动化以及更广泛的经济影响方面讨论了生产者的这些见解的含义。尽管自动化通常被认为是该行业的长期未来（89），但在研究中，自动化似乎是仅次于劳动力计划和政策变化（58）的第二大短期选择（32）。目前，由于经济和社会政治的不确定性，自动化在造成英国园艺劳动力短缺方面所起的作用有限。结论强调了政策建议和未来的调查意图，并为研究界提出了方法论和其他讨论点。

\[7\] Modeling surrender risk in life insurance

标题 : 寿险退保风险建模

作者 : Mark Kiermayer , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11590

说明 : -

Abstract : Surrender poses one of the major risks to life insurance and a sound modeling of its true probability has direct implication on the risk capital demanded by the Solvency II directive. We add to the existing literature by performing extensive experiments that present highly practical results for various modeling approaches, including XGBoost and neural networks. Further, we detect shortcomings of prevalent model assessments, which are in essence based on a confusion matrix. Our results indicate that accurate label predictions and a sound modeling of the true probability can be opposing objectives. We illustrate this with the example of resampling. While resampling is capable of improving label prediction in rare event settings, such as surrender, and thus is commonly applied, we show theoretically and numerically that models trained on resampled data predict significantly biased event probabilities. Following a probabilistic perspective on surrender, we further propose time dependent confidence bands on predicted mean surrender rates as a complementary assessment and demonstrate its benefit. This evaluation takes a very practical, going concern perspective, which respects that the composition of a portfolio might change over time.

摘要 : 退保是人寿保险的主要风险之一，其真实概率的合理建模直接影响到偿付能力II指令要求的风险资本。我们在现有文献的基础上进行了大量的实验，为各种建模方法（包括XGBoost和神经网络）提供了非常实用的结果。此外，我们还发现了流行模型评估的缺点，这些缺点本质上是基于混淆矩阵的。我们的结果表明，准确的标签预测和真实概率的合理建模可能是相反的目标。我们用重采样的例子来说明这一点。虽然重采样能够改善稀有事件环境（如投降）中的标记预测，因此得到普遍应用，但我们从理论和数值上证明了在重采样数据上训练的模型可以预测显著偏倚的事件概率。从自首的概率角度出发，我们进一步提出了预测平均自首率的时间相关置信带作为补充评估，并证明了其优势。这种评估采用了一种非常实用的持续经营的观点，即投资组合的组成可能会随着时间的推移而变化。

\[8\] Hiding Behind Machines

标题 : 躲在机器后面

作者 : Till Feier, Jan Gogoll, Matthias Uhl , 类别 ：Computers and Society (cs.CY)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11465

说明 : -

Abstract : The transfer of tasks with sometimes far reaching moral implications to autonomous systems raises a number of ethical questions. In addition to fundamental questions about the moral agency of these systems, behavioral issues arise. This article focuses on the responsibility of agents who decide on our behalf. We investigate the empirically accessible question of whether the production of moral outcomes by an agent is systematically judged differently when the agent is artificial and not human. The results of a laboratory experiment suggest that decision makers can actually rid themselves of guilt more easily by delegating to machines than by delegating to other people. Our results imply that the availability of artificial agents could provide stronger incentives for decision makers to delegate morally sensitive decisions.

摘要 : 将有时具有深远道德意义的任务转移到自治系统中，引发了许多伦理问题。除了关于这些系统的道德代理的基本问题之外，行为问题也出现了。这篇文章的重点是代理人的责任谁决定代表我们。我们研究了经验性的问题，即当代理人是人为的而不是人时，是否系统地判断代理人产生的道德结果是不同的。一项实验室实验的结果表明，决策者实际上更容易通过授权给机器而不是授权给其他人来摆脱罪恶感。我们的研究结果表明，人工智能的可用性可以为决策者提供更强大的激励，使他们做出道德敏感的决策。

\[9\] A Balance for Fairness

标题 : 公平的平衡

作者 : Song-Ju Kim, Taiki Takahashi, Kazuo Sano , 类别 ：Computer Science and Game Theory (cs.GT)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11496

说明 : Comments: 13 pages, 5 figures

Abstract : In chaotic modern society, there is an increasing demand for the realization of true fairness . In Greek mythology, Themis, the goddess of justice , has a sword in her right hand to protect society from vices, and a balance of judgment in her left hand that measures good and evil. In this study, we propose a fair distribution method utilising physics for the profit in games of characteristic function form. Specifically, we show that the linear programming problem for calculating nucleolus can be efficiently solved by considering it as a physical system in which gravity works. In addition to being able to significantly reduce computational complexity thereby, we believe that this system could have flexibility necessary to respond to real time changes in the parameter.

摘要 : 在混乱的现代社会，人们对实现真正公平的要求越来越高。在希腊神话中，正义女神忒弥斯右手拿着一把剑，保护社会免遭恶习，左手拿着衡量善恶的判断天平。在这项研究中，我们提出了一个公平分配方法，利用物理学的利润在游戏的特征函数形式。具体地说，我们把计算核仁的线性规划问题看作是一个引力作用的物理系统，可以有效地解决这个问题。除了能够显著降低计算复杂度之外，我们认为该系统还具有响应参数实时变化所需的灵活性。
