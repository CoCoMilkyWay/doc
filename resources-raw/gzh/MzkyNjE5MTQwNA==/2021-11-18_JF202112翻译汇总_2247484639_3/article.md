\[1\] Do Intermediaries Matter for Aggregate Asset Prices?

标题:中介机构对总资产价格有影响吗？

作者:VALENTIN HADDAD,TYLER MUIR

首次发表时间:04 October 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13086

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13086

Abstract : Poor financial health of intermediaries coincides with low asset prices and high risk premiums. Is this because intermediaries matter for asset prices, or because their health correlates with economy-wide risk aversion? In the first case, return predictability should be more pronounced for asset classes in which households are less active. We provide evidence supporting this prediction, suggesting that a quantitatively sizable fraction of risk premium variation in several large asset classes such as credit or mortgage-backed securities (MBS) is due to intermediaries. Movements in economy-wide risk aversion create the opposite pattern, and we find this channel also matters.

Abstract :中介机构财务状况不佳与低资产价格和高风险溢价同时发生。这是因为中介机构关系到资产价格，还是因为他们的健康状况与整个经济体的风险厌恶相关？在第一种情况下，对于家庭不太活跃的资产类别，回报的可预测性应该更加明显。我们提供了支持这一预测的证据，表明信贷或抵押贷款支持证券（MBS）等几大资产类别的风险溢价变化在数量上相当大的一部分是由中介机构造成的。整个经济体风险厌恶情绪的变化产生了相反的模式，我们发现这个渠道也很重要。

\[2\] Currency Mispricing and Dealer Balance Sheets

标题:货币错误定价和经销商资产负债表

作者:GINO CENEDESE,PASQUALE DELLA CORTE,TIANYU WANG

首次发表时间:14 September 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13079

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13079

Abstract : We find dealer-level evidence that recent regulation on the leverage ratio requirement causes deviations from covered interest parity. Our analysis uses a unique data set of currency derivatives with disclosed counterparty identities together with exogenous variation introduced by the U.K. leverage ratio framework. Dealers who are affected by the regulatory shock charge an additional premium of about 20 basis points per annum for synthetic dollar funding relative to unaffected dealers. This finding holds even after controlling for changes in clients' demand. Also, some clients increase their trading activity with unaffected dealers with whom they already had a preexisting relationship.

Abstract :我们发现交易商层面的证据表明，最近对杠杆率要求的监管导致了与担保利率平价的偏差。我们的分析使用一个独特的数据衍生工具，具有公开的交易对手身份，以及由英国杠杆比率框架引入的外生变量。受监管冲击影响的交易商，相对于未受影响的交易商，每年对合成美元融资收取约20个基点的额外溢价。即使在控制了客户需求的变化后，这一发现仍然成立。此外，一些客户增加了与未受影响的经销商的交易活动，他们已经与这些经销商建立了关系。

\[3\] Partisan Professionals: Evidence from Credit Rating Analysts

标题:党派专业人士：来自信用评级分析师的证据

作者:ELISABETH KEMPF,MARGARITA TSOUTSOURA

首次发表时间:24 September 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13083

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13083

Abstract : Partisan perception affects the actions of professionals in the financial sector. Linking credit rating analysts to party affiliations from voter records, we show that analysts not affiliated with the U.S. president's party downward-adjust corporate credit ratings more frequently. Since we compare analysts with different party affiliations covering the same firm in the same quarter, differences in firm fundamentals cannot explain the results. We also find a sharp divergence in the rating actions of Democratic and Republican analysts around the 2016 presidential election. Our results show that analysts' partisan perception has price effects and may influence firms' investment policies.

Abstract :党派观念影响着金融部门专业人士的行动。将信用评级分析师与选民记录中的党派关联联系起来，我们发现，与美国总统所在党派无关的分析师下调公司信用评级的频率更高。由于我们比较了同一季度同一公司不同党派的分析师，公司基本面的差异无法解释结果。我们还发现，2016年总统大选前后，民主党和共和党分析师的评级行为存在明显分歧。我们的研究结果表明，分析师的党派观念具有价格效应，并可能影响公司的投资政策。

\[4\] Inequality Aversion, Populism, and the Backlash against Globalization

标题:不平等厌恶、民粹主义和对全球化的抵制

作者:ĽUBOŠ PÁSTOR,PIETRO VERONESI

首次发表时间:14 September 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13081

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13081

Abstract : Motivated by the recent rise of populism in Western democracies, we develop a tractable equilibrium model in which a populist backlash emerges endogenously in a strong economy. In the model, voters dislike inequality, especially the high consumption of “elites.” Economic growth exacerbates inequality due to heterogeneity in preferences , which leads to heterogeneity in returns on capital. In response to rising inequality, voters optimally elect a populist promising to end globalization. Equality is a luxury good. Countries with more inequality, higher financial development, and trade deficits are more vulnerable to populism, both in the model and in the data.

Abstract :受最近西方民主国家民粹主义兴起的推动，我们发展了一个易于驾驭的均衡模型，在这个模型中，民粹主义的反弹在一个强大的经济体中内生性地出现。在该模型中，选民不喜欢不平等，特别是“精英”的高消费。经济增长由于偏好的异质性而加剧不平等，从而导致资本回报的异质性。为了应对日益加剧的不平等，选民们最好选出一位承诺结束全球化的民粹主义者。平等是一种奢侈品。无论从模型还是从数据上看，不平等性更大、金融发展更高、贸易赤字更大的国家更容易受到民粹主义的影响。

\[5\] Talent in Distressed Firms: Investigating the Labor Costs of Financial Distress

标题:困境企业中的人才：财务困境的劳动力成本调查

作者:RAMIN P. BAGHAI,RUI C. SILVA,VIKTOR THELL,VIKRANT VIG

首次发表时间:13 September 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13077

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13077

Abstract : The importance of skilled labor and the inalienability of human capital expose firms to the risk of losing talent at critical times. Using Swedish microdata, we document that firms lose workers with the highest cognitive and noncognitive skills as they approach bankruptcy. In a quasi-experiment, we confirm that financial distress drives these results: following a negative export shock caused by exogenous currency movements, talent abandons the firm, but only if the exporter is highly leveraged. Consistent with talent dependence being associated with higher labor costs of financial distress, firms that rely more on talent have more conservative capital structures.

Abstract :熟练劳动力的重要性和人力资本的不可剥夺性使企业在关键时刻面临人才流失的风险。利用瑞典的微观数据，我们记录了当企业濒临破产时，失去了具有最高认知和非认知技能的员工。在一个准实验中，我们证实了财务困境驱动了这些结果：在外生货币流动造成的负面出口冲击之后，人才放弃了公司，但前提是出口商的杠杆率很高。与人才依赖与更高的财务困境劳动力成本相关联，更依赖人才的公司拥有更保守的资本结构。

\[6\] Negative Home Equity and Household Labor Supply

标题:负家庭资产与家庭劳动力供给

作者:ASAF BERNSTEIN

首次发表时间:02 August 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13070

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13070

Abstract : Using U.S. household-level data and plausibly exogenous variation in the location-timing of home purchases with a single lender, I find that negative home equity causes a 2% to 6% reduction in household labor supply. Supporting causality, households are observationally equivalent at origination and equally sensitive to local housing shocks that do not cause negative equity. Results also hold comparing purchases within the same year-metropolitan statistical area that differ by only a few months. Though multiple channels are likely at work, evidence of nonlinear effects is broadly consistent with costs associated with housing lock and financial distress.

Abstract :利用美国家庭层面的数据和与单一贷款人购房地点时间的外生变化，我发现负家庭资产导致家庭劳动力供应减少2%至6%。支持因果关系的是，家庭在开始时在观察上是等价的，并且对不会导致负资产的当地住房冲击同样敏感。结果还表明，在同一年的大都会统计区内，购买量仅相差几个月。尽管多种渠道可能起作用，但非线性效应的证据与住房锁定和财务困境相关的成本大体一致。

\[7\] Leverage Regulation and Market Structure: A Structural Model of the U.K. Mortgage Market

标题:杠杆调节与市场结构：英国抵押贷款市场的结构模型

作者:MATTEO BENETTON

首次发表时间:04 August 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13072

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13072

Abstract : I develop a structural model of mortgage demand and lender competition to study how leverage regulation affects the U.K. mortgage market. Using variation in risk-weighted capital requirements across lenders and mortgages with different loan-to-values (LTVs), I show that a 1-percentage-point increase in risk-weighted capital requirements increases lenders' marginal cost of originating mortgages by about 26 basis points (11%) on average. I use the estimated model to study proposed leverage regulations. Counterfactual analyses show that large lenders exploit a regulatory cost advantage, which increases concentration by about 20%, and suggest that banning high-LTV mortgages may reduce large lenders' equity buffer.

Abstract :本文建立了抵押贷款需求与贷款人竞争的结构模型，研究杠杆调控对英国抵押贷款市场的影响。利用不同贷款价值比（LTV）的贷款人和抵押贷款的风险加权资本要求变化，我表明风险加权资本要求增加1个百分点，贷款人发起抵押贷款的边际成本平均增加约26个基点（11%）。我使用估计模型研究拟议的杠杆监管。反事实分析表明，大型贷款人利用监管成本优势，将集中度提高约20%，并表明禁止高LTV抵押贷款可能会减少大型贷款人的股权缓冲。

\[8\] Fire-Sale Spillovers in Debt Markets

标题:债务市场的甩卖溢出效应

作者:ANTONIO FALATO,ALI HORTAÇSU,DAN LI,CHAEHEE SHIN

首次发表时间:14 September 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13078

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13078

Abstract : Fire sales induced by investor redemptions have powerful spillover effects among funds that hold the same assets, hurting peer funds' performance and flows, and leading to further asset sales with negative bond price impact. A one-standard-deviation increase in our fire-sale spillover measure leads to a 45 (90) bp decrease in peer fund returns (flows) and a two percentage point increase in the likelihood of a large bond price drop. The results hold in a regression-discontinuity design addressing identification concerns. Timing, heterogeneity, instrumental-variable, and placebo tests further support the price-impact mechanism. Model-based counterfactual and stress-test analyses quantify the financial stability implications.

Abstract :投资者赎回引发的抛售在持有相同资产的基金之间具有强大的溢出效应，损害了同行基金的业绩和流量，并导致进一步的资产抛售，从而产生负面的债券价格影响。我们的甩卖溢出措施中的一个标准差增加导致同行基金回报（流量）减少45（90）个基点，债券价格大幅下跌的可能性增加两个百分点。结果适用于解决识别问题的回归不连续设计。时间、异质性、工具变量和安慰剂测试进一步支持价格影响机制。基于模型的反事实和压力测试分析量化了金融稳定的影响。

\[9\] Intermediation Variety

标题:中间品种

作者:JASON RODERICK DONALDSON,GIORGIA PIACENTINO,ANJAN THAKOR

首次发表时间:02 October 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13084

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13084

Abstract : We explain why banks and nonbank intermediaries coexist in a model based only on differences in their funding costs. Banks enjoy a low cost of capital due to safety nets and money-like liabilities. We show that this can actually be a disadvantage: it generates a soft-budget-constraint problem that makes it difficult for banks to credibly threaten to withhold additional funding to failed projects. Nonbanks emerge to solve this problem. Their high cost of capital is an advantage: it allows them to commit to terminate funding. Still, nonbanks never take over the entire market, but other coexist with banks in equilibrium.

Abstract :我们解释了为什么银行和非银行中介机构在一个仅基于其融资成本差异的模型中共存。由于安全网和类似货币的负债，银行享有较低的资本成本。我们表明，这实际上可能是一个缺点：它产生了一个软预算约束问题，使得银行难以可信地威胁扣留对失败项目的额外资金。非银行组织的出现解决了这个问题。他们的高资本成本是一个优势：它允许他们承诺终止融资。尽管如此，非银行从未接管整个市场，但其他银行与银行在均衡状态下共存。

\[10\] Asset Pricing and Sports Betting

标题:资产定价与体育博彩

作者:TOBIAS J. MOSKOWITZ

首次发表时间:21 September 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13082

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13082

Abstract : Sports betting markets offer a novel laboratory to test theories of cross-sectional asset pricing anomalies. Two features of this market—no systematic risk and terminal values exogenous to betting activity—evade the joint hypothesis problem, allowing mispricing to be detected. Examining a large and diverse set of liquid betting contracts, I find strong evidence of momentum, consistent with delayed overreaction and inconsistent with underreaction and rational pricing. Returns are a fraction of those in financial markets and fail to overcome transactions costs, preventing arbitrage from eliminating them. An insight from betting also predicts value and momentum returns in U.S. equities.

Abstract :体育博彩市场提供了一个新的实验室来测试跨部门资产定价异常的理论。该市场的两个特征——无系统性风险和投注活动外生的终值——规避了联合假设问题，允许发现错误定价。通过检查大量不同的流动性博彩合同，我发现了强劲的势头证据，与延迟过度反应一致，与反应不足和理性定价不一致。回报率只是金融市场回报率的一小部分，无法克服交易成本，阻碍套利消除交易成本。博彩的洞察还可以预测美国股市的价值和动量回报。

\[11\] Volatility, Valuation Ratios, and Bubbles: An Empirical Measure of Market Sentiment

标题:波动性、估值比率和泡沫：市场情绪的实证衡量

作者:CAN GAO,IAN W. R. MARTIN

首次发表时间:13 July 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13068

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13068

Abstract : We define a sentiment indicator based on option prices, valuation ratios, and interest rates. The indicator can be interpreted as a lower bound on the expected growth in fundamentals that a rational investor would have to perceive to be happy to hold the market. The bound was unusually high in the late 1990s, reflecting dividend growth expectations that in our view were unreasonably optimistic. Our approach exploits two key ingredients. First, we derive a new valuation ratio decomposition that is related to the Campbell–Shiller loglinearization but that resembles the Gordon growth model more closely and has certain other advantages. Second, we introduce a volatility index that provides a lower bound on the market's expected log return.

Abstract :我们根据期权价格、估值比率和利率定义情绪指标。该指标可以被解释为基本面预期增长的下限，理性投资者必须意识到这一点才能乐于持有市场。上世纪90年代末，该界限异常高，反映了我们认为过于乐观的股息增长预期。我们的方法利用了两个关键要素。首先，我们推导了一个新的估值比率分解，该分解与Campbell–Shiller对数线性化相关，但与Gordon增长模型更为相似，并具有某些其他优势。其次，我们引入了波动率指数，该指数提供了市场预期对数回报的下限。

\[12\] Valuing Private Equity Investments Strip by Strip

标题:逐条评估私人股本投资的价值

作者:ARPIT GUPTA,STIJN VAN NIEUWERBURGH

首次发表时间:09 August 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13073

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13073

Abstract : We propose a new valuation method for private equity (PE) investments. It constructs a replicating portfolio using cash flows on listed equity and fixed-income instruments (strips). It then values the strips using an asset pricing model that captures the risk in the cross-section of bonds and equity factors. The method delivers a risk-adjusted profit on each PE investment and a time series for the expected return on each fund category. We find negative risk-adjusted profits for the average PE fund, with substantial heterogeneity and some persistence in the performance. Expected returns and risk-adjusted profit decline in the later part of the sample.

Abstract :我们提出了一种新的私募股权（PE）投资估值方法。它利用上市股票和固定收益工具（strips）的现金流构建了一个复制投资组合。然后，它使用资产定价模型对这些条带进行估值，该模型捕捉债券和股票因素横截面中的风险。该方法为每个私募股权投资提供风险调整后的利润，并为每个基金类别的预期回报提供时间序列。我们发现，普通私募股权基金的风险调整后利润为负，表现具有显著的异质性和一定的持续性。样本后期的预期收益和风险调整后的利润下降。

\[13\] Real Estate Shocks and Financial Advisor Misconduct

标题:房地产冲击与财务顾问不当行为

作者:STEPHEN G. DIMMOCK,WILLIAM C. GERKEN,TYSON VAN ALFEN

首次发表时间:13 July 2021

链接:https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13067

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13067

Abstract : We test whether personal real estate shocks affect professional misconduct by financial advisors. We use a panel of advisors' home addresses and examine within-advisor variation relative to other advisors who work at the same firm and live in the same ZIP code. We find a negative relation between housing returns and misconduct. We show that advisors' housing returns explain misconduct against out-of-state customers, breaking the link between customer and advisor housing shocks. Furthermore, the results are stronger for advisors with lower career risk from committing misconduct, and for advisors with greater borrowing constraints.

Abstract :我们测试个人房地产冲击是否会影响财务顾问的专业不当行为。我们使用一组顾问的家庭地址，并检查顾问内部相对于在同一家公司工作、居住在同一邮编的其他顾问的差异。我们发现住房回报与不当行为之间存在负相关。我们发现，顾问的住房回报解释了针对州外客户的不当行为，打破了客户和顾问住房冲击之间的联系。此外，对于职业失当风险较低的顾问，以及借贷限制较大的顾问，结果更为明显。

\[14\] Economic Stimulus at the Expense of Routine-Task Jobs

标题:以牺牲日常工作为代价的经济刺激

作者:SELALE TUZEL,MIAO BEN ZHANG

首次发表时间:14 September 2021

Abstract : Do investment tax incentives improve job prospects for workers? Abstract : I examine the effects of information asymmetry–driven mispricing on security issuance. Using predisclosure changes in purchase obligations as a proxy for information asymmetry–driven mispricing, I find that managers avoid (prefer) issuing securities when they perceive their firms to be undervalued (overvalued). The effects of information asymmetry–driven mispricing are stronger on equity issuance than debt issuance. Consequently, undervaluation (overvaluation) causes an increase (decrease) in leverage. These effects are more pronounced for firms, periods, and securities associated with greater information asymmetry. The stock-trading patterns that managers follow suggest that their perceived mispricing is an important factor in both private and firm-level decisions.
