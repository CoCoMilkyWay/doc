\[1\] Happy Analysts

标题:快乐的分析师

作者:Ole-Kristian Hope(University of Toronto - Rotman School of Management),Congcong Li(Duquesne University - A.J. Palumbo School of Business Administration),An-Ping Lin(Singapore Management University - School of Accountancy),MaryJane Rabier(Washington University in St. Louis)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722133

Abstract : This paper is the first to investigate the role of work-life balance in financial analysts’ performance and career advancement. Using a large sample of Glassdoor reviews by financial analysts, we find a significant non-linear relation between work-life balance and analyst performance and analyst career advancement. Specifically, when perceived work-life balance is relatively low, an increase in work-life balance is associated with better analyst performance and career advancement; however, when perceived work-life balance is already high, a further increase in work-life balance is associated with worse analyst performance and career advancement.

Keywords : Analysts, Work-Life Balance, Job Satisfaction, Performance, Promotion, Labor Market, Social Media, Glassdoor, All-Star

Abstract :本文首次探讨了工作-生活平衡在财务分析师绩效和职业发展中的作用。通过一个大型样本，我们发现分析员的工作和职业发展之间的非线性关系。具体来说，当感知的工作-生活平衡相对较低时，工作-生活平衡的增加与更好的分析师绩效和职业发展相关；然而，当感知的工作-生活平衡已经很高时，工作-生活平衡的进一步增加与更差的分析师绩效和职业发展相关。

Keywords :分析师、工作生活平衡、工作满意度、绩效、晋升、劳动力市场、社交媒体、Glassdoor、全明星

\[2\] Asset Price Bubbles: Invariance Theorems\*

标题:资产价格泡沫：不变性定理\*

作者:Robert Jarrow(Cornell SC Johnson College of Business),Philip Protter(Columbia University),Jaime San Martin(University of Chile),Johnson School Working Paper Series(Cornell University - Johnson Graduate School of Management)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3722111

Abstract : This paper provides invariance theorems that facilitate testing for the existence of an asset price bubble in a market where the price evolves as a Markov diffusion process. The test involves only the properties of the price process’ quadratic variation under the statistical probability. It does not require an estimate of either the equivalent local martingale measure or the asset’s drift. To augment its use, a new family of stochastic volatility price processes is also provided where the processes’ strict local martingale behavior can be characterized.

Keywords : None

Abstract :本文提供了不变性定理，有助于检验资产价格泡沫的存在性，在一个市场的价格演变为马尔可夫扩散过程。检验只涉及统计概率下价格过程二次变化的性质。它不需要估计等价的局部鞅测度或资产的漂移。为了扩展它的应用，本文还提出了一类新的随机波动价格过程，其中可以刻画过程的严格局部鞅行为。

Keywords :None

\[3\] Convolutional Neural Network for Stock Price Prediction Using Transfer Learning

标题:基于转移学习的卷积神经网络股价预测

作者:Yohei Komori(WorldQuant LLC - WorldQuant University; Mizuho Securities Co. Ltd)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3756702

Abstract : The goal of this paper is to build a trading algorithm by applying image recognition neural network - Convolutional Neural Network(CNN) - to the 2D technical candle stick charts. First, this paper shows a research survey of the previous paper. Second, this paper explains the basic theory of CNN model and how it can works on chart images. Next, this project performs an experimental study of CNN on S&P 500 index from January 1, 1985 to June 30, 2020. The CNN model structure used in this paper is transferred from inception v3 with three additional layers, and the technical indicators used in the input chart image are simple moving average (25 days). The label data used in the model are categorical - either up, flat, or down. The model has 50% accuracy on the test set when conducting three-days ahead forecast, which is higher than the simple momentum strategy and contrarian strategy, indicating its high alpha generating potential. One-day ahead forecast and five-days ahead forecast have lower accuracy than the three-days forecast. This means you might have the best performance when you close your position at T + 3.

Keywords : trading algorithm, convolutional neural network, candle sticks, transfer learning

Abstract :本文的目的是将图像识别神经网络-卷积神经网络（CNN）应用于二维技术蜡烛图，建立一种交易算法。首先，本文对前人的研究进行了综述。其次，本文阐述了CNN模型的基本理论以及CNN模型在图表图像上的应用。接下来，本计画将于1985年1月1日至2020年6月30日期间，对CNN在标普500指数上的表现进行实验研究。本文使用的CNN模型结构是从inception v3转换而来的，增加了三层，输入图表图像中使用的技术指标是简单的移动平均值（25天）。模型中使用的标签数据是分类的-上、平或下。该模型在进行3天提前预测时，在测试集上的准确率为50%，高于简单的动量策略和反转策略，表明其具有较高的α生成潜力。一日预报和五日预报的准确率低于三日预报。这意味着当你在T+3结束你的位置时，你可能会有最好的表现。

Keywords :交易算法，卷积神经网络，蜡烛棒，转移学习

\[6\] Congress Characteristics, Political Diversity and Stock Market Outcomes

标题:国会特征、政治多样性与股市结果

作者:Yosef Bonaparte(University of Colorado at Denver - Department of Finance)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3756190

Abstract : We analyze panel data on 24 congress’ characteristics, since the 80th congress (1945), and show its overall impact on stock market return (negatively) and volatility (positively). Our key hypotheses is that congress background influence bills passed, and hence affects policies and budgeting. Our study is critical as we observe key trends in congresses demographic; Congress becomes more of: African Americans, experienced, educated, women; and less of: White, Christian Protestants and military background. Abstract :我们分析了自第80届国会（1945年）以来24个国会的面板数据，并显示其对股市回报率（负）和波动率（正）的总体影响。我们的主要假设是，国会背景影响通过的法案，从而影响政策和预算。我们的研究是至关重要的，因为我们观察到国会人口的主要趋势；国会变得更多的是：非洲裔美国人，有经验的，受过教育的，妇女；和较少的：白人，基督教新教徒和军事背景。然后，我们表明，这些趋势改变了关键的国会生产率指标，例如，法案通过的数量，并从此对股市结果产生影响。然后，我们创建了一个反映国会多样性水平的指数，显示第116届（2019年）的多样性水平为55%，股市正是国会多样性的价格。总的来说，当我们分析政治和金融之间的联系时，立法部门的人口统计是需要考虑的重要决定因素。

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3757876

Abstract : The aim of this paper is to develop a multiple-case study design (Yin, 2003) to consider those companies for the case studies which have the highest return on their shares over the time period of twenty years. The study provides criteria for the selection of companies that can go through multiple stages before its final selection. The study involves various steps that are used for shortlisting of companies that are most suitable for the purpose of the study of sustainable companies. The research finds that out of ET500 companies, there was data availability of 269 companies from January 1998 which was first cut in the selection criteria.
