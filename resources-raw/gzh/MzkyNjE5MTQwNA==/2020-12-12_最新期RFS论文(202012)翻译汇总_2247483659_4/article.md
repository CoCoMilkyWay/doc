\[1\] The Deregulation of the Private Equity Markets and the Decline in IPOs

标题:私人股本市场的放松与ipo的减少

作者:Michael Ewens, Joan Farre-Mensa

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5463/5835291; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa053

Abstract : The deregulation of securities laws—in particular the National Securities Markets Improvement Act (NSMIA) of 1996—has increased the supply of private capital to late-stage private startups, which are now able to grow to a size that few private firms used to reach. NSMIA is one of a number of factors that have changed the going-public versus staying-private trade-off, helping bring about a new equilibrium where fewer startups go public, and those that do are older. This new equilibrium does not reflect an initial public offering (IPO) market failure. Rather, founders are using their increased bargaining power vis-à-vis investors to stay private longer.

Abstract:证券法的放松管制，特别是1996年的《国家证券市场改善法》（NSMIA）增加了对后期私营初创企业的私人资本供应，这些初创企业现在能够发展到过去很少有私营企业达到的规模。NSMIA是一系列改变了上市与保留私有的权衡的因素之一，有助于实现一种新的平衡，即上市的初创公司更少，而上市的公司则更老。这种新的均衡并不反映首次公开发行（IPO）市场的失败。相反，创业者们正利用他们对投资者日益增强的讨价还价能力，以保持更长时间的私有化。

\[2\] Information Revealed through the Regulatory Process: Interactions between the SEC and Companies ahead of Their IPO

标题:通过监管程序披露的信息：美国证券交易委员会（SEC）与上市公司之间的互动

作者:Michelle Lowry, Roni Michaely, Ekaterina Volkova

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5510/5716337; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa007

Abstract : We analyze communications between the SEC and firms prior to IPOs using LDA analysis and KL divergence. The SEC’s concerns closely map onto the regulator’s stated mandate: companies increase prospectus disclosures on precise topics of SEC concern. Revenue recognition is the dominant topic of SEC concern, and it is not independently discovered by investors. Increased SEC concern about it is associated with greater secondary sales, lower post-IPO liquidity, lower post-IPO returns, and a higher probability of withdrawal. The regulator’s role during the capital raising process results in increased transparency but contributes to delays in the going public process.

Abstract:我们利用LDA分析和KL散度分析法分析了SEC与公司在ipo前的沟通。SEC的担忧与监管机构声明的任务密切相关：公司增加招股书披露，涉及SEC关注的确切主题。收入确认是证券交易委员会关注的主要话题，并不是投资者独立发现的。美国证交会（SEC）对它的担忧增加，与二级销售增加、IPO后流动性降低、IPO后回报率降低以及更高的退市概率有关。监管者在融资过程中的作用提高了透明度，但也导致了上市过程的延迟。

\[3\] The Role of Government in Firm Outcomes

标题:政府在企业绩效中的作用

作者:Ran Duchin, Zhenyu Gao, Haibing Shu

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5555/5713524; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa005

Abstract : Using a unique setting in China, where the geographic distance between collective firms and local governments is highly persistent because of legal restrictions on land ownership and mobility, we investigate the role of government involvement in small firms. In our analysis of survey responses, we find that weaker government involvement, measured by greater distance from government, is associated with higher firm autonomy and reduced taxes, protectionism, and anticompetitive behavior. In our analysis of firm-level financial data, we find that distant firms have better operating performance, higher growth, and higher entry rates. We find similar results around exogenous government office relocations.

Abstract:在中国，由于法律对土地所有权和流动性的限制，集体企业与地方政府之间的地理距离持续存在，因此我们研究了政府参与在小企业中的作用。在我们对调查结果的分析中，我们发现，以与政府的距离越远衡量，政府参与程度越弱，与企业自主性越高、税收减少、保护主义和反竞争行为就越大。在我们对企业层面财务数据的分析中，我们发现距离较远的公司有更好的经营业绩、更高的增长率和更高的进入率。我们在外生政府办公室搬迁方面也发现了类似的结果。

\[4\] Rules versus Discretion in Bank Resolution

标题:银行处置中的规则与自由裁量权

作者:Ansgar Walther, Lucy White

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5594/5803658; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa032

Abstract : Recent reforms have given regulators broad powers to “bail-in” bank creditors during financial crises. We analyze efficient bail-ins and their implementation. To preserve liquidity, regulators must avoid signaling negative private information to creditors. Therefore, optimal bail-ins in bad times only depend on public information. As a result, the optimal policy cannot be implemented if regulators have wide discretion, due to an informational time-inconsistency problem. Rules mandating tough bail-ins after bad public signals, or contingent convertible (co-co) bonds, improve welfare. We further show that bail-in and bailout policies are complementary: if bailouts are possible, then discretionary bail-ins are more effective.

Abstract:最近的改革赋予了监管者在金融危机期间“救助”银行债权人的广泛权力。我们分析了有效的自救机制及其实施。为了保持流动性，监管机构必须避免向债权人传递负面的私人信息。因此，在经济不景气的情况下，最佳的纾困方案只能依赖于公共信息。因此，由于信息时间不一致问题，如果监管机构有很大的自由裁量权，最优政策将无法实施。在公共信号不好或有条件的可转换债券（co-co）后，强制要求采取严厉的纾困措施，改善福利。我们进一步表明，自救和救助政策是互补的：如果救助是可能的，那么自由裁量的纾困就更有效。

\[5\] Overcoming Discount Window Stigma: An Experimental Investigation

标题:克服折扣窗口污名的实验研究

作者:Olivier Armantier, Charles A Holt

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5630/5835290; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa055

Abstract : A core responsibility of the Federal Reserve is to ensure financial stability by acting as the “lender of last resort” through its discount window (DW). Historically, however, the DW has not been effective because its usage is stigmatized. In this paper, we develop a coordination game with adverse selection, and we test in the lab policies that have been proposed to mitigate DW stigma. We find that lowering the DW cost and making DW borrowing difficult to detect are ineffective, but regular random DW borrowing can overcome DW stigma. Implications for other forms of stigma in finance are discussed.

Abstract:美联储的核心职责是通过贴现窗口（DW）充当“最后贷款人”，确保金融稳定。然而，从历史上看，DW并不有效，因为它的使用受到了污名化。在本文中，我们发展了一个具有逆向选择的协调博弈，并在实验室中测试了为减轻DW污名而提出的策略。我们发现降低数据仓库成本和使数据仓库借用难以检测是无效的，但定期随机数据仓库借用可以克服数据仓库的污名。还讨论了金融业其他形式的耻辱感的含义。

\[6\] Pipeline Risk in Leveraged Loan Syndication

标题:杠杆贷款银团的管道风险

作者:Max Bruche, Frederic Malherbe, Ralf R Meisenzahl

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5660/5788549; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa029

Abstract : What is the economic role played by arrangers of leveraged loans, and what are the risks they face? We provide evidence that arrangers solve a demand discovery problem. Investors have incentives to feign little interest in the loan to obtain better terms. To deter such behavior, arrangers underprice hot deals and ration investors on cold deals. The risk associated with demand discovery is often shared between borrowers and arrangers. One implication is that to ration investors on cold deals, arrangers retain larger loan shares. This motive for retention is different from the monitoring incentive motive previously considered in the literature.

Abstract:杠杆贷款的安排人在经济上扮演什么角色？他们面临的风险是什么？我们提供证据证明，安排解决了需求发现问题。投资者有动机假装对贷款不感兴趣以获得更好的条件。为了阻止这种行为，安排机构压低了热盘交易的价格，并对冷盘交易的投资者进行了配给。与需求发现相关的风险通常由借款人和安排人分担。其中一个暗示是，为了让投资者在冷淡的交易中获得配给，安排机构将保留更大的贷款份额。这种留用动机不同于以往文献中所考虑的监督激励动机。

\[7\] Credit and Punishment: Are Corporate Bankers Disciplined for Risk-Taking?

标题:信用与惩罚：企业银行家是否因冒险而受到惩罚？

作者:Janet Gao, Kristoph Kleiner, Joseph Pacelli

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5706/5820527; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa046

Abstract : We examine whether bankers face disciplining consequences for structuring poorly performing corporate loans. We construct a novel data set containing the employment histories and loan portfolios of a large sample of corporate bankers and find that corporate credit events (i.e., downgrades, defaults, bankruptcies) increase banker turnover. The effect is pronounced when bankers issue loans with loose terms or experience severe losses. Credit events prompt bankers to adopt stricter future risk management practices, such as offering restrictive covenant packages. Overall, our findings are consistent with banks disciplining employees as a means to manage their own risk exposure.

Abstract:我们研究银行业者是否因构建表现不佳的公司贷款而面临惩戒性后果。我们构建了一个新的数据集，包含了大量公司银行家的就业历史和贷款组合，发现公司信贷事件（如降级、违约、破产）会增加银行家的营业额。当银行家发行宽松条款的贷款或遭受严重损失时，这种影响就很明显了。信贷事件促使银行业者采取更严格的未来风险管理做法，例如提供限制性的契约包。总的来说，我们的调查结果与银行对员工进行纪律处分作为管理自身风险敞口的一种手段是一致的。

\[8\] Communication within Banking Organizations and Small Business Lending

标题:银行机构内部沟通与小企业贷款

作者:Ross Levine, Chen Lin, Qilin Peng, Wensi Xie

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5750/5810505; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa036

Abstract : We investigate how communication within banks affects small business lending. Using travel times between a bank’s headquarters and its branches to proxy for the costs of communicating soft information, we exploit shocks to these travel times—the introduction of new airline routes—to evaluate the impact of within-bank communication costs on small business loans. We find that reducing headquarters-branch travel time boosts small business lending in the branch’s county. Several extensions suggest that new airline routes facilitate in-person communications that boost small-firm lending.

Abstract:我们调查银行内部沟通如何影响小企业贷款。利用银行总部与分支机构之间的旅行时间来代表软信息沟通的成本，我们利用对这些旅行时间的冲击来评估银行内部沟通成本对小企业贷款的影响。我们发现，减少总部分行的旅行时间会促进分行所在县的小企业贷款。一些扩展表明，新的航线有助于促进小型企业的借贷。

\[9\] Creditor Rights, Technology Adoption, and Productivity: Plant-Level Evidence

标题:债权、技术采用和生产率：工厂层面的证据

作者:Nuri Ersahin

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5784/5811766; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa038

Abstract : I use U.S. Census microdata to analyze the effect of stronger creditor rights on productivity. Following the adoption of antirecharacterization laws that give lenders greater access to the collateral of firms in financial distress, treated plants’ total factor productivity increases by 2.6%. This effect is concentrated among plants belonging to financially constrained firms. I explore the underlying mechanism and find that treated plants change the composition of their investments and their workforce toward newer capital and skilled labor. My results suggest that stronger creditor rights relax borrowing constraints and help firms adopt more efficient production technologies.

Abstract:我用美国人口普查的微观数据来分析更强大的债权对生产率的影响。随着反腐败法的通过，放款人有更多机会获得陷入财务困境的公司的抵押品，经过处理的工厂的全要素生产率提高了2.6%。这种影响集中在属于财务拮据企业的工厂中。我探究了潜在的机制，发现经过处理的工厂改变了他们的投资和劳动力的构成，转向了更新的资本和熟练的劳动力。我的研究结果表明，更强大的债权可以放松借贷限制，帮助企业采用更高效的生产技术。

\[10\] The Collateralizability Premium

标题:抵押溢价

作者:Hengjie Ai, Jun E Li, Kai Li, Christian Schlag

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5821/5850873; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa063

Abstract : A common prediction of macroeconomic models of credit market frictions is that the tightness of financial constraints is countercyclical. Theory suggests a negative collateralizability premium; that is, capital that can be used as collateral to relax financial constraints insures against aggregate shocks and commands a lower risk compensation compared with noncollateralizable assets. We show that a long-short portfolio constructed using a novel measure of asset collateralizability generates an average excess return of around 8% per year. We develop a general equilibrium model with heterogeneous firms and financial constraints to quantitatively account for the collateralizability premium.

Abstract:信贷市场摩擦的宏观经济模型的一个常见预测是，金融约束的紧缩是反周期的。理论认为，负抵押溢价；也就是说，可以作为抵押品来放松金融约束的资本，可以确保抵御总体冲击，并且与非可抵押资产相比，需要更低的风险补偿。我们发现，使用一种新的资产抵押率衡量方法构建的长短期投资组合每年产生大约8%的超额收益。我们建立了一个具有异质性企业和财务约束的一般均衡模型，以定量地解释可抵押溢价。

\[11\] Production Networks and Stock Returns: The Role of Vertical Creative Destruction

标题:生产网络与股票收益：纵向创造性破坏的作用

作者:Michael Gofman, Gill Segal, Youchang Wu

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5856/5807619; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa034

Abstract : We examine empirically and theoretically the relation between firms’ risk and distance to consumers in a production network. We document two novel facts: firms farther away from consumers have higher risk premiums and higher exposure to aggregate productivity. We quantitatively explain these findings using a general equilibrium model featuring a multilayer production process. The economic force is “vertical creative destruction,” that is, positive productivity shocks to suppliers devalue customers’ assets-in-place, thereby lowering the cyclicality of downstream firms’ values. We show that vertical creative destruction varies with competition and firm characteristics and generates sizable cross-sectional differences in risk premiums.

Abstract:本文从实证和理论上考察了生产网络中企业风险与消费者距离的关系。我们记录了两个新的事实：离消费者较远的公司有更高的风险溢价和更高的总生产率敞口。我们用具有多层生产过程的一般均衡模型定量地解释了这些发现。经济力量是“纵向创造性破坏”，也就是说，对供应商的正向生产力冲击会使客户资产贬值，从而降低下游企业价值的周期性。我们发现，纵向创造性破坏随竞争和公司特征的不同而变化，并在风险溢价方面产生相当大的横截面差异。

\[12\] Competition, Markups, and Predictable Returns

标题:竞争、加价和可预测的回报

作者:Alexandre Corhay, Howard Kung, Lukas Schmid

链接:https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/12/5906/5835289; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa054

Abstract : This paper jointly examines the link between competition and expected returns in the time series and in the cross-section. To this end, we build a general equilibrium model where markups vary because of firm entry with oligopolistic competition. When concentration is high, markups are more sensitive to entry risk. We find that higher markups are associated with higher expected returns over time and across industries, in line with the data. The model can also quantitatively account for the persistent rise in aggregate risk premiums and macroeconomic volatility associated with the secular increase trend industry concentration since the mid-1980s.

Abstract:本文在时间序列和横截面上共同考察了竞争与预期收益之间的联系。为此，我们建立了一个一般均衡模型，在这个模型中，由于寡头垄断竞争的企业进入，加价会发生变化。当集中度较高时，加价对进入风险更为敏感。我们发现，随着时间的推移，在不同行业，加价越高，预期回报越高，这与数据相符。该模型还可以定量地解释自20世纪80年代中期以来，与行业集中度长期增长趋势相关的总风险溢价和宏观经济波动的持续上升。
