\[1\] Testing Models of Strategic Uncertainty

标题 : 战略不确定性的检验模型

作者 : Emanuel Vespa, Taylor Weidman, Alistair J. Wilson , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05900

说明 : -

Abstract : In repeated game applications where both the collusive and non collusive outcomes can be supported as equilibria, researchers must resolve underlying selection questions if theory will be used to understand counterfactual policies. One guide to selection, based on clear theoretical underpinnings, has shown promise in predicting when collusive outcomes will emerge in controlled repeated game experiments. In this paper we both expand upon and experimentally test this model of selection, and its underlying mechanism strategic uncertainty. Adding an additional source of strategic uncertainty (the number of players) to the more standard payoff sources, we stress test the model. Our results affirm the model as a tool for predicting when tacit collusion is likely unlikely to be successful. Extending the analysis, we corroborate the mechanism of the model. When we remove strategic uncertainty through an explicit coordination device, the model no longer predicts the selected equilibrium.

摘要 : 在重复博弈的应用中，共谋和非共谋的结果都可以作为均衡来支持，研究人员必须解决潜在的选择问题，如果理论将被用来理解反事实的政策。一个基于清晰理论基础的选择指南，在预测受控重复博弈实验中何时会出现共谋结果方面显示出了希望。在本文中，我们扩展并实验验证了这种选择模型，以及其潜在的机制——战略不确定性。在更标准的回报来源中增加一个额外的战略不确定性来源（参与者的数量），我们对模型进行压力测试。我们的研究结果证实了该模型是一种预测默契合谋何时不可能成功的工具。扩展分析，我们证实了模型的机理。当我们通过一个显式的协调装置消除战略不确定性时，模型不再预测所选择的均衡。

\[2\] Migration, Information Gap, and Visible Consumption

标题 : 迁移、信息差距和有形消费

作者 : Shihas Abdul Razak, Upasak Das , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06149

说明 : -

Abstract : Using representative migration survey data from the Indian state of Kerala, this paper assesses the impact of transnational migration on social signaling through the consumption of visible goods. Using the plausibly exogenous variation in migration networks in the neighborhood and religious communities to account for the potential endogeneity, we find significant and positive effects on conspicuous consumption. In terms of the mechanisms, we put forward three possible channels. While we are unable to rule out the associated changes in preferences driving up the spending on status goods, we observe only modest effects of peer group spending due to higher status competition. A key channel that we propose through a theoretical framework is a potential information gap among permanent residents about the income levels of a migrant. This we argue can be leveraged by migrants to increase the visible consumption to gain higher status in the society.

摘要 : 本文利用印度喀拉拉邦具有代表性的移民调查数据，评估了跨国移民通过有形商品消费对社会信号传递的影响。利用邻近地区和宗教社区移民网络中看似外生的变异来解释潜在的内生性，我们发现显著和积极的影响炫耀性消费。在机制方面，我们提出了三种可能的途径。虽然我们无法排除相关的偏好变化推高地位商品支出的可能性，但我们观察到，由于地位竞争加剧，同龄人群体支出的影响微乎其微。我们通过一个理论框架提出的一个关键渠道是永久居民之间关于移民收入水平的潜在信息差距。我们认为，移民可以利用这一点来增加有形消费，从而获得更高的社会地位。

\[3\] The adequacy of time series reduction for renewable energy systems

标题 : 可再生能源系统时间序列缩减的充分性

作者 : Leonard Göke, Mario Kendziorski , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06221

说明 : -

Abstract : To manage computational complexity, models of macro energy systems commonly deploy reduced sets of time series data. This paper evaluates the adequacy of time series reduction when modelling energy systems with fully renewable generation and a consequent dependency on storage. Analysis includes various methods to derive reduced time series and to implement them into models, either as time slices, also referred to as representative days, or continuous time steps. All methods are tested with regard to unmet demand and accuracy of estimated system costs using a simple capacity expansion model of the power sector within a renewable energy system. Some methods achieve little unmet demand, but instead their results regarding storage are biased and favour seasonal at the expense of short term storage. We conclude that renewable energy systems limit the adequacy of time series reduction and future research should focus on alternative methods to reduce computational complexity.

摘要 : 为了管理计算的复杂性，宏观能量系统的模型通常部署时间序列数据的缩减集。本文评估了在对完全可再生能源系统建模时时间序列缩减的充分性，以及由此产生的对存储的依赖性。分析包括各种方法来推导简化的时间序列，并将其实现为模型，或者作为时间片（也称为代表日）或连续时间步。利用可再生能源系统内电力部门的简单扩容模型，就未满足的需求和估计系统成本的准确性对所有方法进行了测试。有些方法几乎不能满足未满足的需求，但相反，它们关于储存的结果是有偏见的，以牺牲短期储存为代价，有利于季节性的。我们得出结论，可再生能源系统限制了时间序列缩减的充分性，未来的研究应集中在替代方法，以降低计算复杂度。

\[4\] Modelling Universal Order Book Dynamics in Bitcoin Market

标题 : 比特币市场的通用订单动态模型

作者 : Fabin Shi, Nathan Aden, Shengda Huang, Neil Johnson, Xiaoqian Sun, Jinhua Gao, Li Xu, Huawei Shen, Xueqi Cheng, Chaoming Song , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06236

说明 : -

Abstract : Understanding the emergence of universal features such as the stylized facts in markets is a long standing challenge that has drawn much attention from economists and physicists. Most existing models, such as stochastic volatility models, focus mainly on price changes, neglecting the complex trading dynamics. Recently, there are increasing studies on order books, thanks to the availability of large scale trading datasets, aiming to understand the underlying mechanisms governing the market dynamics. In this paper, we collect order book datasets of Bitcoin platforms across three countries over millions of users and billions of daily turnovers. We find a 1 1D field theory, govern by a set of KPZ like stochastic equations, predicts precisely the order book dynamics observed in empirical data. Despite the microscopic difference of markets, we argue the proposed effective field theory captures the correct universality class of market dynamics. We also show that the model agrees with the existing stochastic volatility models at the long wavelength limit.

摘要 : 理解诸如市场中的程式化事实等普遍特征的出现是一个长期的挑战，引起了经济学家和物理学家的极大关注。现有的大多数模型，如随机波动率模型，主要关注价格变化，忽略了复杂的交易动态。最近，由于大量交易数据集的可用性，有越来越多的关于订单簿的研究，旨在了解控制市场动态的潜在机制。在本文中，我们收集了三个国家超过数百万用户和数十亿日营业额的比特币平台订单数据集。我们发现，由一组类似KPZ的随机方程控制的一维场论精确地预测了经验数据中观察到的有序本动力学。尽管市场的微观差异，我们认为提出的有效场理论抓住了正确的普遍性类市场动力学。我们还证明了该模型在长波极限下与现有的随机波动率模型是一致的。

\[5\] Using Monotonicity Restrictions to Identify Models with Partially Latent Covariates

标题 : 利用单调性约束识别部分潜在协变量模型

作者 : Minji Bang, Wayne Yuan Gao, Andrew Postlewaite, Holger Sieg , 类别 ：Econometrics (econ.EM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.05847

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper develops a new method for identifying econometric models with partially latent covariates. Such data structures arise naturally in industrial organization and labor economics settings where data are collected using an input based sampling strategy, e.g., if the sampling unit is one of multiple labor input factors. We show that the latent covariates can be nonparametrically identified, if they are functions of a common shock satisfying some plausible monotonicity assumptions. With the latent covariates identified, semiparametric estimation of the outcome equation proceeds within a standard IV framework that accounts for the endogeneity of the covariates. We illustrate the usefulness of our method using two applications. The first focuses on pharmacies we find that production function differences between chains and independent pharmacies may partially explain the observed transformation of the industry structure. Our second application investigates education achievement functions and illustrates important differences in child investments between married and divorced couples.

摘要 : 本文提出了一种识别具有部分潜在协变量的计量经济模型的新方法。这种数据结构自然出现在工业组织和劳动经济学环境中，在这些环境中，数据是使用基于投入的抽样策略收集的，例如，如果抽样单位是多个劳动投入因素中的一个。我们证明，如果潜在的协变量是一个共同冲击的函数，并且满足一些合理的单调性假设，那么这些协变量是可以非参数识别的。确定潜在协变量后，结果方程的半参数估计在解释协变量内生性的标准IV框架内进行。我们使用两个应用程序来说明我们的方法的有用性。第一部分以药店为研究对象，我们发现连锁药店与独立药店的生产函数差异可以部分解释产业结构的变化。我们的第二个应用程序调查了教育成就函数，并说明了已婚和离婚夫妇在子女投资方面的重要差异。

\[6\] Estimation of future discretionary benefits in traditional life insurance

标题 : 传统寿险未来可自由支配收益的估计

作者 : Florian Gach, Simon Hochgerner , 类别 ：Methodology (stat.ME)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06077

说明 : -

Abstract : In the context of traditional life insurance, the future discretionary benefits ( FDB ), which are a central item for Solvency II reporting, are generally calculated by computationally expensive Monte Carlo algorithms. We derive analytic formulas for lower and upper bounds for the FDB . This yields an estimation interval for the FDB , and the average of lower and upper bound is a simple estimator. These formulae are designed for real world applications, and we compare the results to publicly available reporting data.

摘要 : 在传统人寿保险的背景下，未来可自由支配福利（FDB）是Solvency II报告的核心项目，通常采用计算成本高昂的蒙特卡罗算法进行计算。我们推导了FDB的上下界的解析公式。这就产生了FDB的估计区间，上界和下界的平均值是一个简单的估计量。这些公式是为实际应用而设计的，我们将结果与公开的报告数据进行比较。
