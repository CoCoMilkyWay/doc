\[1\] Man vs. Machine: Quantitative and Discretionary Equity Management

标题:人与机器：数量与自由裁量股权管理

作者:Simona Abis(Columbia University - Columbia Business School)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3717371

Abstract : In modern asset markets, man and machine compete for profits. How does each fare? I build a learning model in which quantitative investors (reliant on computer models) have more learning capacity but less flexibility to adapt to market conditions than discretionary investors (reliant on human judgment). I use machine learning to categorize US active equity mutual funds as quantitative or discretionary. Consistent with the model's predictions, I find that quantitative funds hold more stocks, specialize in stock picking, and engage in more overcrowded trades. Discretionary funds hold lesser known stocks, switch between picking and timing and outperform in recessions.

Keywords : Investment Management, Quantitative Mutual Funds, Machine Learning, Rational Inattention

Abstract :在现代资产市场上，人和机器为了利润而竞争。每种票价如何？我建立了一个学习模型，在这个模型中，定量投资者（依赖计算机模型）比全权投资者（依赖人类判断）有更多的学习能力，但适应市场条件的灵活性较低。我用机器学习将美国活跃股票型共同基金分为数量型和自由型。与模型的预测相一致，我发现数量型基金持有更多的股票，专门从事选股，从事更为拥挤的交易。可自由支配的基金持有不太知名的股票，在选股和择时之间切换，在衰退中表现出色。

Keywords :投资管理，定量共同基金，机器学习，理性忽视

\[2\] Adverse Selection Dynamics in Privately-Produced Safe Debt Markets

标题:私人生产的安全债务市场中的逆向选择动态

作者:Nathan Foley-Fisher(Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System),Gary B. Gorton(Yale School of Management; National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER); Yale University - Yale Program on Financial Stability),Stephane Verani(Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3717489

Abstract : Privately-produced safe debt is designed so that there is no adverse selection in trade. This is because no agent finds it profitable to produce private information about the debt's backing and all agents know this (i.e., it is information-insensitive). But in some macro states, it becomes profitable for some agents to produce private information, and then the debt faces adverse selection when traded (i.e., it becomes information-sensitive). We empirically study these adverse selection dynamics in a very important asset class, collateralized loan obligations, a large symbiotic appendage of the regulated banking system, which finances loans to below investment-grade firms.

Keywords : safe debt, adverse selection, information sensitivity, collateralized loan obligations

Abstract :私人生产的安全债券的设计是为了在交易中没有逆向选择。这是因为没有一个代理发现提供有关债务支持的私人信息是有利可图的，而且所有代理都知道这一点（即，它对信息不敏感）。但在一些宏观国家，一些代理人提供私人信息变得有利可图，然后债务在交易时面临逆向选择（即变得信息敏感）。我们在一个非常重要的资产类别，抵押贷款债务，一个巨大的共生附属物，为低于投资级别的公司提供贷款的受监管银行系统中，我们对这些逆向选择动态进行了实证研究。

Keywords :安全债务、逆向选择、信息敏感性、抵押贷款债务

\[3\] Local Political-Turnover-Induced Uncertainty and Bond Market Pricing

标题:地方政治更替引发的不确定性与债券市场定价

作者:Zhuo Chen(Tsinghua University - PBC School of Finance),Andrea Lu(The University of Melbourne - Department of Finance; Financial Research Network (FIRN)),Huili Xiao(Tsinghua University - PBC School of Finance),Xiaoquan Zhu(Tsinghua University, PBC School of Finance, Students)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3741300

Abstract : Using political turnovers in mayoral appointments at the prefecture-city level in China, we show that investors in the municipal corporate bond market price their concerns for rising local political uncertainty into bonds and relocate capital toward other corporate bonds issued by local firms. Municipal/non-municipal corporate bond issue spreads increase (decrease) by 8.9 (14) basis points before the expected political turnover of mayors and reverse afterwards. The effect is more prominent for bonds issued in cities with investors who have a strong local preference, suggesting investors switch from MCBs to local non-MCBs in their bond holdings. The pricing effect is also stronger for bonds issued in regions with more developed financial markets and bonds with lower credit ratings. Lastly, bond market participants only price in the political risk induced by the turnovers of politician with direct involvement in local economic activities.

Keywords : Political uncertainty, Implicit government guarantee, Municipal corporate bonds, Local investors

Abstract :利用中国地市级市长任命中的政治失误，我们发现市政公司债券市场的投资者将他们对地方政治不确定性上升的担忧定价为债券，并将资本转移到当地公司发行的其他公司债券上。市政/非市政公司债券发行利差在市长预期政治更替之前增加（减少）8.9（14）个基点，之后逆转。在投资者对本地偏好较强的城市发行的债券，这种影响更为突出，这表明投资者在持有债券时从小型商业银行转向本地非小型商业银行。在金融市场较为发达的地区发行的债券和信用评级较低的债券，其定价效应也更强。最后，债券市场参与者只对直接参与当地经济活动的政治家的转变所引发的政治风险进行定价。

Keywords :政治不确定性，隐性政府担保，市政公司债券，当地投资者

\[4\] Cohort Trading and Security Design: Theory and Evidence from Agency MBS Markets

标题:群体交易与证券设计：来自机构MBS市场的理论与证据

作者:Yu An(Johns Hopkins Carey Business School),Wei Li(Johns Hopkins University - Carey Business School),Zhaogang Song(Johns Hopkins University - Carey Business School)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3674660

Abstract : Agency mortgage-backed securities (MBSs) are traded via both individual-asset-based specified pool (SP) contracts and cohort-based to-be-announced (TBA) contracts, which allow for the delivery of heterogeneous MBSs at a uniform price. We theoretically derive and empirically document the economic effects of cohort trading on issuers' security design: (1) Low-value and high-value loans are securitized into separate groups of MBSs sold in TBA and SP markets respectively; (2) issuers pool low-value loans together into TBA MBSs, consistent with arbitrary packaging practices based on the uniform TBA pricing; (3) issuers separate distinct high-value loans into multiple SP MBSs, suggesting that SP selling revenue exhibits a certain degree of convexity in MBS value; and (4) large issuers take more advantage of security design than small issuers do. Finally, we quantify the economic benefits of strategic security design to issuers to be 3 basis points in price, about 36% of the transaction costs for SP MBSs.

Keywords : Cohort Trading, MBS, Security Design, Specified Pool, TBA

Abstract :机构抵押贷款支持证券（MBS）通过基于个人资产的特定池（SP）合同和基于队列的待宣布（TBA）合同进行交易，这两种合同允许以统一的价格交付异构MBS。我们从理论上推导并实证记录了队列交易对发行人证券设计的经济影响：（1）低值和高值贷款分别证券化为在TBA和SP市场出售的MBS；（2）发行人将低价值贷款集中到TBA MBS中，这与基于统一的TBA定价；（3）发行人将不同的高价值贷款分成多个标的MBS，表明标的销售收入在MBS价值上表现出一定程度的凸性；（4）大型发行人比小型发行人更多地利用证券设计。最后，我们将策略性证券设计对发行人的经济效益量化为价格上的3个基点，约占标普mbs交易成本的36%。

Keywords :队列交易，MBS，安全设计，指定池，TBA

\[5\] Does the Readability of Target Firm’ Annual Reports Matter to Bidders?

标题:目标公司年报的可读性对竞买人是否重要？

作者:Balasingham Balachandran(Dept of Economics, Finance and Marketing, La Trobe Business School, La Trobe University; Financial Research Network (FIRN)),Huu Nhan Duong(Monash University - Department of Banking and Finance; Financial Research Network (FIRN)),Robert W. Faff(University of Queensland),Syed Shams(University of Southern Queensland)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3730737

Abstract : This study investigates value-relevant implications of target-firm annual report readability for acquiring firms. We document robust evidence that readability has a positive association with bidder market reaction. This finding is more pronounced among firms with higher levels of opacity, lower analyst following; bidders with lower managerial ability; and less reputed M&A advisors. Further, in terms of other M&A outcomes, readability has a positive association with the bid premium and long-term bidder performance, and a negative association with the time to complete the M&A. Overall, these findings indicate that markets do value the readability of target 10-K filings – readability does matter to bidding firms.

Keywords : 10-K Filing Readability, Acquisition Performance, Mergers and Acquisitions, Information Asymmetry

Abstract :本研究探讨目标公司年报可读性对收购公司价值相关之影响。我们提供了强有力的证据，证明可读性与投标人的市场反应有着积极的联系。这一发现在不透明程度较高、分析师关注度较低的公司、管理能力较低的竞购者以及知名度较低的并购顾问公司中更为明显。此外，就其他并购结果而言，可读性与投标溢价和长期投标人业绩呈正相关，与完成并购的时间呈负相关。总体而言，这些研究结果表明，市场确实看重目标10-K申报的可读性，可读性对投标公司来说很重要。

Keywords :10-K备案可读性、收购绩效、并购、信息不对称

\[6\] Rivals’ Returns

标题:竞争对手的回报

作者:Eric de Bodt(NHH-Caltech),Jean-Gabriel Cousin(Univ. Lille, ULR 4112 - LSMRC),Richard Roll(California Institute of Technology)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3717082

Abstract : Roll R² (1988) concludes that our ability to explain stock returns is modest: close to 80% of the daily stock returns variance (65% for monthly stock returns) remain unexplained by a combination of priced factors and a portfolio of industry peers, even controlling for firm specific news reported in the financial press. Using Hoberg and Phillips (2010) similarity scores to identify a firm’s rivals, we show that direct interactions between firms matter. Five firms among the ten product market closest firm rivals explain firm returns by an order of magnitude close to the Fama and French (1993) size and value factors taken together and more than the Fama and French (2015) profitability and investment factors and an industry index. These conclusions appear robust to many alternative specifications, are not driven by low liquid stocks and pertain to all industries. Firms active in more heterogenous industries, larger, more profitable and with more growth opportunities are more reactive to rivals’ idiosyncratic returns. Firms more leveraged and a higher market share are less so. R&D and competition appears to play a more ambiguous role, depending on the set of control variables included in our regressions. Using the Jegadeesh et al. (2019) test of asset pricing models, rivals’ idiosyncratic returns appear not to be a priced factor.

Keywords : Stock Return, Information, Rivals’ Interactions

Abstract :Roll R²（1988）得出的结论是，我们解释股票收益的能力是有限的：接近80%的每日股票收益方差（每月股票收益为65%）仍然无法解释价格因素和行业同行的投资组合，甚至控制了金融报刊上报道的公司特定新闻。利用Hoberg和Phillips（2010）相似性得分来识别公司的竞争对手，我们表明企业之间的直接互动很重要。十大产品市场中最接近竞争对手的五家公司解释了公司回报的数量级接近Fama和French（1993）的规模和价值因素，并超过了Fama和French（2015）的盈利能力和投资因素以及一个行业指数。这些结论似乎对许多替代规范是可靠的，不是由低流动性股票驱动的，适用于所有行业。活跃于更为异质的行业、规模更大、利润更高、增长机会更多的企业对竞争对手的特殊回报反应更大。杠杆率越高、市场份额越高的公司，情况就越差。研发和竞争似乎扮演了一个更加模糊的角色，这取决于回归中包含的一组控制变量。利用Jegadeesh等人（2019）对资产定价模型的检验，竞争对手的特殊回报似乎不是定价因素。

Keywords :股票回报，信息，竞争对手的互动
