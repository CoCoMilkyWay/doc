\[1\] Corporate Governance in Determining the Stock Price in the Colombo Stock Exchange

标题:科伦坡证券交易所股票价格确定中的公司治理

作者:P.K.C.L. Fernando,Piyal Hareendra Dissa Bandara

出处:Dissabandara, H. and Fernando, P.K.C.L. (2018). Corporate Governance in Determining the Stock Price in Colombo Stock Exchange. International Research Conference of the Kotelawala Defense University | KDU-IRC, 11(1), pp.31–39. Available at: http://ir.kdu.ac.lk/handle/345/2629?show=full.

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3924840

Abstract : The impact of corporate governance on firm performance has become a topic of conversation. However there is less attention on how it impacts the share price. The purpose of the study is to determine the impact of corporate governance on the share price of companies listed in the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE). Descriptive analysis, regression analysis and correlation analysis, were used in this study and it the period of financial year of 2015-16. A sample of 64 companies was chosen as representative of the population out of 296 companies listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE), excluding banking and finance sector and data was collected using secondary method. Compliance level of corporate governance have been measured by utilizing self-constructed Corporate Governance Index (CG Index) with five sub-indexes namely Board Structure (BS), CEO and Management(CEO), Transparency and Disclosure (TR), Investor Relation(IV) and CSR Disclosure(CSR). The index contains 40 criteria under the above Sub-Indexes. The study found that there is no significant relationship between overall CG index and the share price. However, transparency demonstrated a significant negative impact on the share price. When the overall sample was clustered in to sub samples as high, medium and low according to firm size, the medium sample demonstrated a significant positive relationship between CG index and the share price. The important key finding that was supported with enough evidence to show lowering the firm size the Board Structure (BS) and Investor Relation (IV) is significant to increase the share price. Unexpectedly Transparency (TR) demonstrated a significant negative impact for smaller firms. Further, it was found that the corporate governance practices generated a long term impact on share price since the same impact can be found in lead share price also.

Keywords : Corporate Governance, CG Index, Share Price

Abstract :公司治理对公司绩效的影响已经成为一个话题。然而，人们很少关注它对股价的影响。本研究旨在确定公司治理对科伦坡证券交易所（CSE）上市公司股价的影响。本研究采用描述性分析、回归分析和相关分析，并对2015-16财年进行了分析。在科伦坡证券交易所（CSE）上市的296家公司中，选择了64家公司作为代表，不包括银行和金融部门，并采用二次方法收集数据。利用自建的公司治理指数（CG指数）和五个子指数，即董事会结构（BS）、首席执行官和管理层（CEO）、透明度和披露（TR）、投资者关系（IV）和企业社会责任披露（CSR），测量了公司治理的合规水平。该指数包含上述子指数下的40项标准。研究发现，总体CG指数与股价之间没有显著关系。然而，透明度对股价产生了显著的负面影响。当总体样本根据公司规模分为高、中、低三个子样本时，中等样本在CG指数和股价之间表现出显著的正相关关系。有足够证据支持的重要关键发现表明，降低公司规模、董事会结构（BS）和投资者关系（IV）对提高股价具有重要意义。出乎意料的是，透明度（TR）对较小的公司产生了显著的负面影响。此外，研究发现，公司治理实践对股价产生了长期影响，因为铅股价格也会产生同样的影响。

Keywords :公司治理、CG指数、股价

\[2\] Price Transmission in Cotton Futures Market: Evidence from Three Countries

标题:棉花期货市场的价格传导：来自三个国家的证据

作者:Amrinder Singh,Tarun Kumar Soni

出处:Singh, Amrinder, and Tarun K. Soni 2021. "Price Transmission in Cotton Futures Market: Evidence from Three Countries" Journal of Risk and Financial Management 14, no. 9: 444. https://doi.org/10.3390/jrfm14090444

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3924808

Abstract : This study examines the price transmission between cotton prices in U.S., Indian, and Chinese futures markets. We focus on studying the long-run price movements using cointegration and alternate causality tests. The empirical results indicate the following: (a) the U.S. cotton futures market continues to be the most dominant market, and it leads price changes in India and China; (b) the cotton prices in India also impacts the cotton prices in China as we report a unidirectional relationship flowing from India to China; (c) there is duality of direction of price transmission for U.S. and Chinese commodity markets as we document bi-directional causality between U.S. to Chinese cotton futures for the entire period and uni-directional causality from U.S. to Chinese markets for the two sub-periods; (d) the long-term relationship between the three markets has seen a significant shift as documented by the absence of cointegration which may be due to changes in government policy, especially in India and China specifically after 2014. Overall, results provide support for further reforms especially for Indian and Chinese commodity exchanges so that they can play a vital role in the price discovery process especially for commodities that are largely produced or consumed in these economies.

Keywords : India; U.S.; China; cotton; commodity futures; price transmission

Abstract :本研究考察了美国、印度和中国期货市场棉花价格之间的价格传导。我们专注于使用协整和替代因果检验研究长期价格运动。实证结果表明：（a）美国棉花期货市场仍然是最主要的市场，它引领着印度和中国的价格变化；（b） 印度的棉花价格也影响中国的棉花价格，因为我们报告了从印度流向中国的单向关系；（c） 美国和中国商品市场的价格传导方向具有双重性，因为我们记录了整个时期美国和中国棉花期货之间的双向因果关系，以及两个子时期美国和中国市场之间的单向因果关系；（d） 这三个市场之间的长期关系已经发生了重大变化，正如协整的缺失所证明的那样，这可能是由于政府政策的变化，特别是在2014年之后的印度和中国。总的来说，研究结果为进一步改革提供了支持，特别是对印度和中国的商品交易所，以便它们能够在价格发现过程中发挥重要作用，特别是对这些经济体主要生产或消费的商品而言。

Keywords :印度美国。；中国棉商品期货；价格传递

\[3\] The Anna Karenina Principle and Stock Prices

标题:安娜·卡列尼娜原理与股票价格

作者:Dirk G. Baur

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3912558

Abstract : The Anna Karenina principle (AKP) based on the opening line of Leo Tolstoy’s Anna Karenina, “All happy families are all alike; each unhappy family is unhappy in its own way” is applied to financial markets. We test the AKP by defining happy firms as positive return firms and unhappy firms as negative return firms and analyze whether happy firms are more “alike” than unhappy firms. We use return correlations, cross-sectional return and cross-sectional volatility dispersion as measures and find for different stock index constituent samples totalling more than 8,000 stocks that the AKP does not hold in general. In contrast, all samples exhibit cross-sectional volatility asymmetry where the average return volatility of unhappy firms is larger than that of happy firms.

Keywords : Anna Karenina Principle, volatility asymmetry, return dispersion

Abstract :安娜·卡列尼娜原则（AKP）基于列夫·托尔斯泰的安娜·卡列尼娜（Anna Karenina）的开场白，“所有幸福的家庭都是一样的；每个不幸的家庭都有自己的不幸”，适用于金融市场。我们通过将快乐企业定义为正回报企业，将不快乐企业定义为负回报企业来检验AKP，并分析快乐企业是否比不快乐企业更“相似”。我们使用收益率相关性、横截面收益率和横截面波动率离散度作为衡量指标，并发现AKP通常不持有8000多只股票的不同股票指数成分样本。相反，所有样本都表现出横截面波动不对称性，其中不快乐企业的平均收益波动率大于快乐企业的平均收益波动率。

Keywords :安娜·卡列尼娜原理、波动不对称性、收益分散性

\[5\] Does Annual Report Readability Affect Labor Investment Efficiency?

标题:年报可读性是否影响劳动力投资效率？

作者:Hasibul Chowdhury,Kelvin Jui Keng Tan,Junyi Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3925362

Abstract : This study examines whether the complexity of textual narratives captured by annual report readability affects a firm’s labor investment efficiency. Using a large sample of US firms from 1994 to 2017, we find that linguistic simplicity through better annual report readability has a strong positive effect on a firm’s labor investment efficiency. Furthermore, we find that this positive association is more pronounced for firms suffering from weak monitoring. Our results are robust to additional control variables, endogeneity concerns, and alternative measures of annual report readability and labor investment efficiency. Using a difference-in-differences framework with the Plain Writing Act (PWA) 2010 as an exogenous variation in readability, we also document a causal relation between readability and labor investment efficiency. Overall, our core findings are consistent with the idea that readable annual reports positively affect firms’ financial flexibility, which, in turn, enhances the efficiency of firms’ labor investments.

Keywords : Labor Investment Efficiency, Accounting Readability

Abstract :本研究考察了年报可读性所体现的文本叙述的复杂性是否会影响企业的劳动力投资效率。通过使用1994年至2017年的大量美国企业样本，我们发现，通过更好的年度报告可读性，语言简洁性对企业的劳动力投资效率有着强烈的积极影响。此外，我们发现，这种积极的联系更为明显的公司遭受薄弱的监测。我们的结果对额外的控制变量、内生性问题以及年度报告可读性和劳动力投资效率的替代措施具有鲁棒性。使用2010年《普通书写法》（PWA）的差异框架作为可读性的外生变量，我们还记录了可读性和劳动力投资效率之间的因果关系。总体而言，我们的核心发现与可读年度报告积极影响企业财务灵活性的观点一致，而财务灵活性反过来又提高了企业劳动力投资的效率。

Keywords :劳动投资效率、会计可读性

\[6\] Analyst Coverage Overlaps and Interfirm Information Spillovers

标题:分析师覆盖范围重叠和公司间信息溢出

作者:Tim Martens,Christoph J. Sextroh

出处:Journal of Accounting Research, Volume 59, Issue 4, 2021

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3925782

Abstract : We offer a novel perspective on the role of sell-side analysts as information intermediaries in capital markets by documenting a flow of information in a new direction, namely, from analysts to the firms they cover. We use analyst coverage overlaps and patent citations to examine analyst-induced information spillovers about technology and industry trends. Consistent with analyst coverage–related information flows, firms are more likely to cite another firm's patent if that firm is covered by the same analyst. The effect varies with analysts' specialization, experience, and level of activity. Firms with more analyst-based connections to peers also show greater corporate innovation. Collectively, our evidence indicates that financial analysts not only reduce information asymmetries between firms and capital market participants but also facilitate the production of business intelligence through feedback and interfirm information spillovers.

Keywords : analysts; coverage overlap; feedback effect; knowledge ﬂow; in-formation spillover; patent citations; corporate innovation

Abstract :通过记录新方向的信息流，即从分析师到他们所涵盖的公司，我们提供了一个全新的视角，说明卖方分析师作为资本市场信息中介的作用。我们使用分析师覆盖率重叠和专利引用来检验分析师诱导的关于技术和行业趋势的信息溢出。与分析师覆盖率相关的信息流一致，如果另一家公司的专利被同一位分析师覆盖，那么该公司更有可能引用该公司的专利。这种影响因分析师的专业化、经验和活动水平而异。与同行有更多分析师关系的公司也表现出更大的企业创新。总体而言，我们的证据表明，金融分析师不仅减少了企业与资本市场参与者之间的信息不对称，而且通过反馈和企业间信息溢出促进了商业智能的产生。

\[8\] Labor Market Networks and Asset Returns

标题:劳动力市场网络与资产回报

作者:Turan G. Bali,Joon Woo Bae,Ali Sharifkhani,Xiaofei Zhao

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3951333

Abstract : This paper proposes a measure of labor market connectivity based on the similarity in the composition of occupational knowledge characteristics across industries and provides evidence of return predictability in the cross-section of industries that are connected through the labor market. In long-short portfolios, an industry's return is strongly predicted by the past return of its labor-market-connected neighboring industries with an annualized return of up to 9%, which is not explained by established asset pricing models. The return predictability remains significant after controlling for the supply chain momentum as well as the industry lead-lag effect, and is concentrated in stocks with higher arbitrage costs and higher ownership of uninformed investors. We find similar predictive relations for the labor productivity, wages, employment, and profitability of labor connected industries. Our findings are consistent with positive spillover of productivity shocks among industries that are connected through the labor market. Informational frictions, costly arbitrage, and investors' limited attention magnify the delayed response of stock prices to the spillover of labor productivity shocks, which results in the observed return predictability.

Keywords : Labor network, labor productivity, cross section of stock returns.

Abstract :本文提出了一种基于跨行业职业知识特征构成相似性的劳动力市场连通性度量方法，并提供了通过劳动力市场连通的跨行业回报可预测性的证据。在长短期投资组合中，一个行业的回报由其劳动力市场相关的相邻行业的过去回报强烈预测，年化回报率高达9%，这无法用现有的资产定价模型来解释。在控制了供应链动量和行业领先-滞后效应后，回报的可预测性仍然显著，且集中在套利成本较高、未知情投资者持股比例较高的股票上。我们发现劳动生产率、工资、就业和与劳动相关行业的盈利能力存在类似的预测关系。我们的研究结果与通过劳动力市场连接的行业之间生产率冲击的正向溢出一致。信息摩擦、昂贵的套利和投资者有限的注意力放大了股价对劳动生产率冲击溢出的延迟反应，从而导致观察到的回报可预测性。

Keywords :劳动网络、劳动生产率、股票收益横截面。

\[9\] Long Run Risks in FX Markets: Are They There?

标题:外汇市场的长期风险：存在吗？

作者:Sun Yong Kim,Konark Saxena

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3950981

Abstract : This paper constitutes an empirical evaluation of the international long run risks (LRRs) model. To empirically identify the relationship between exchange rates and LRRs we estimate country level LRRs using the ICAPM-VAR framework of Campbell et al (2017), a tractable framework that only requires the use of asset market data. With this identification strategy in hand, we find that the key testable predictions of the international LRR model for currency excess returns, currency risk premia and the carry trade are supported by exchange rate data. We also find a tight connection between US relative LRRs and the global exchange rate factor structure, a fact that supports an international LRR model where US LRRs drive global shocks to the world economy.

Keywords : International Finance, Asset Pricing and Recursive Preferences

Abstract :本文对国际长期风险（LRRs）模型进行了实证评估。为了从经验上确定汇率与LRR之间的关系，我们使用Campbell等人（2017）的ICAPM-VAR框架估算了国家层面的LRR，该框架是一个易于操作的框架，只需要使用资产市场数据。有了这种识别策略，我们发现国际LRR模型对货币超额收益、货币风险溢价和套利交易的关键可测试预测得到了汇率数据的支持。我们还发现美国相对LRR与全球汇率因素结构之间存在紧密联系，这一事实支持了一个国际LRR模型，即美国LRR对世界经济造成全球冲击。

Keywords :国际金融、资产定价和递归偏好

\[10\] Do Speculators Exacerbate Managerial Myopia? Evidence from Margin Traders in China

标题:投机者会加剧管理近视吗？来自中国保证金交易商的证据

作者:Jun Chen

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3950960

Abstract : This paper exploits a regulatory experiment that lifts the margin trading ban in China to test whether speculative retail investors impact stock prices and corporate decisions. Using a regression discontinuity design, I find that the margin trading eligibility increases stock share turnover and prices, and that marginable firms cater to investor short-termism by manipulating earnings and reducing long-term investment. Consistent with managerial myopia, marginable firms experience a decline in operating profit and equity valuation in the long run. My results suggest that margin traders, as short-term speculators, pressure the manager to focus on current earnings and sacrifice long-term growth.

Keywords : Investor Short-Termism, Managerial Myopia, Margin Trading, Retail Speculators, Earnings Management, Long-Term Investment

Abstract :本文利用一项监管实验，解除了中国的保证金交易禁令，以测试投机性散户投资者是否会影响股票价格和公司决策。使用回归不连续性设计，我发现保证金交易资格增加了股票交易量和价格，可保证金公司通过操纵收益和减少长期投资来迎合投资者的短期主义。与管理层的短视相一致，从长期来看，可盈利公司的营业利润和股权估值都会下降。我的研究结果表明，作为短期投机者，保证金交易者会迫使经理专注于当前收益，牺牲长期增长。

Keywords :投资者短期主义、管理近视、保证金交易、散户投机者、盈余管理、长期投资

\[11\] Asset Pricing and Machine Learning: A Critical Review of Empirical Findings

标题:资产定价和机器学习：对实证结果的批判性评论

作者:Matteo Bagnara

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3950568

Abstract : The latest development in Empirical Asset Pricing is the employment of Machine Learning methods to address the problem of the factor zoo. These techniques offer great flexibility and prediction accuracy but require special care as they strongly depart from traditional Econometrics. I review and critically assess the most recent and relevant contributions in the literature with special attention to the interpretation of non-standard statistical tools applied to this field and I summarize the empirical findings in detail into hints for further developments.

Keywords : Empirical Asset Pricing, Risk Premium, Machine Learning

Abstract :经验资产定价的最新发展是使用机器学习方法来解决要素定价问题。这些技术具有很大的灵活性和预测准确性，但需要特别注意，因为它们与传统的计量经济学有很大的不同。我回顾并批判性地评估了文献中最新和相关的贡献，特别关注应用于该领域的非标准统计工具的解释，并将实证结果详细总结为进一步发展的提示。

Keywords :经验资产定价、风险溢价、机器学习

\[12\] Monetary Policy and Stock Market Valuation

标题:货币政策与股票市场估值

作者:Olli-Matti Laine

出处:International Journal of Central Banking, forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3950239

Abstract : This paper estimates the effect of monetary policy on the term structure of stock market riskpremia. The implied stock market risk premia are obtained using analysts’ dividend forecastsand dividend future prices. The effect of monetary policy on risk premia is analysed using localprojections and VAR models. According to the results, monetary policy easing raises theaverage risk premium. The effect is driven by a rise in long-horizon risk premia.

Keywords : Monetary policy, Stock market, Equity premium

Abstract :本文估计了货币政策对股票市场风险溢价期限结构的影响。隐含的股票市场风险溢价是使用分析师的股息预测和股息未来价格获得的。使用本地预测和VAR模型分析了货币政策对风险溢价的影响。根据结果，货币政策宽松提高了平均风险溢价。这种影响是由长期风险溢价的上升所驱动的。

Keywords :货币政策、股票市场、股票溢价
