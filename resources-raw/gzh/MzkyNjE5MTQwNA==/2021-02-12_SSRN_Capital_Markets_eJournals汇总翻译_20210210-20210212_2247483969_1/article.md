\[1\] Impact of Manufacturing PMI on Stock Market Index: A Study on Turkey

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3743277

Abstract : Purchasing Managers’ Index (PMI) is an indicator to measure the health of an economy. PMI is considered by policymakers and related bodies as it is an influential indicator for gauging the general tendency of the economy, especially GDP growth and Industrial Added Value. This study examines whether the manufacturing PMI has any influence on the stock market of Turkey or vice versa. We use the secondary sources of information collected from the official website of BIST, Turkey for Stock Index data and “investing.com” for Manufacturing PMI data. The study covers monthly data ranging from April 2015 to February 2019. We test the causality between Manufacturing PMI and BIST index through employing the Granger Causality Test. Our analysis reveals that manufacturing PMI does not granger cause the Turkish Stock Index but the Turkish Stock index or the stock market does granger cause manufacturing PMI.

\[3\] The Effect of Hidden Liquidity: Evidence from an Exogenous Shock

标题:隐性流动性效应：来自外生冲击的证据

作者:Amy K. Edwards,Paul Hughes,John Ritter,Patti Vegella,Hao Zhang

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3766512

Abstract : While existing studies find mixed results on the effect of hidden liquidity, this study uses the Tick Size Pilot (“the Pilot”) as an instrument to study how hidden liquidity, both on and off-exchange, affect the informativeness of quotations. We document that an increase in tick size results in a reduction in pre-trade transparency, but with opposing effects on on- and off-exchange hidden liquidity. We find that an increase in either hidden liquidity reduces price efficiency, the contribution of quotes to price discovery, and the ability to manage the order execution risk and cost of exchange order submission. In addition, an increase in either hidden liquidity reduces the ability to manage transaction costs off-exchange. These results hold for both on- and off-exchange hidden liquidity. However, several results differ when pre-trade transparency is measured using trade-based measures of hidden liquidity rather than order-based measures.

Keywords : Hidden Liquidity, Off-Exchange Routing Pre-Trade Transparency, Price Efficiency, Price Discovery, Information Shares, Execution Risk, Execution Cost, Tick Size

Abstract :虽然现有的研究对隐性流动性的影响结果不一，但本研究使用了指数规模试点（“试点”）作为工具来研究隐性流动性如何影响报价的信息性。我们发现，交易前交易的透明度随着交易量的增加而降低，但对交易内外隐藏的流动性产生相反的影响。我们发现，隐藏流动性的增加会降低价格效率、报价对价格发现的贡献以及管理订单执行风险和提交交易订单成本的能力。此外，任何一种隐性流动性的增加都会降低管理场外交易成本的能力。这些结果适用于交易所内外的隐性流动性。然而，当使用基于贸易的隐性流动性衡量标准而不是基于订单的衡量标准来衡量贸易前透明度时，有几个结果有所不同。

Keywords :隐藏流动性、场外交易路径、交易前透明度、价格效率、价格发现、信息共享、执行风险、执行成本、交易量

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3762175

Abstract : How does an idiosyncratic shock to the liquidity of a stock affect the liquidity and prices of its related stocks? Utilizing the unique features of two-step spinoffs, we document strong evidence that the increased liquidity of spun-off firms spills over to their industry peers after the spinoffs. \[5\] On the RND under Heston's Stochastic Volatility Model

作者:Ben Boukai

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3763494

Abstract : We consider Heston's (1993) stochastic volatility model for valuation of European options to which (semi) closed form solutions are available and are given in terms of characteristic functions. We prove that the class of scale-parameter distributions with mean being the forward spot price satisfies Heston's solution. Thus, we show that any member of this class could be used for the direct risk-neutral valuation of the option price under Heston's SV model. In fact, we also show that any RND with mean being the forward spot price that satisfies Hestons' option valuation solution, must be a member of a scale-family of distributions in that mean. As particular examples, we show that one-parameter versions of the {\\it Log-Normal, Inverse-Gaussian, Gamma, Weibull} and the {\\it Inverse-Weibull} distributions are all members of this class and thus provide explicit risk-neutral densities (RND) for Heston's pricing model. We demonstrate, via exact calculations and Monte-Carlo simulations, the applicability and suitability of these explicit RNDs using already published Index data with a calibrated Heston model (S&P500, Bakshi, Cao and Chen (1997), and ODAX, Mrázek and Pospíšil (2017)), as well as current option market data (AMD).

Keywords : Heston model, option pricing, risk-neutral valuation, calibration

Abstract :我们考虑了Heston（1993）的随机波动率模型，该模型的（半）闭式解可用，并以特征函数的形式给出。我们证明了一类平均值为远期现货价格的标度参数分布满足Heston解。因此，我们证明了在Heston的SV模型下，这个类别的任何成员都可以用于期权价格的直接风险中性估值。事实上，我们还表明，任何平均值为远期现货价格的RND满足Hestons的期权估值解，都必须是该平均值下的标度分布族的一员。作为特殊的例子，我们证明了{it Log Normal，Inverse Gaussian，Gamma，Weibull}和{it Inverse Weibull}分布的单参数版本都是这类分布的成员，因此为Heston的定价模型提供了显式的风险中性密度（RND）。我们通过精确计算和蒙特卡罗模拟，使用已经公布的指数数据和校准的赫斯顿模型（S&P500、Bakshi、Cao和Chen（1997）以及ODAX、Mrázek和Pospíšil（2017））以及当前期权市场数据（AMD），证明了这些显式RND的适用性和适用性。

Keywords :赫斯顿模型，期权定价，风险中性估值，修正

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3765216

Abstract : Chordia and Miao (2020) provide evidence that low-latency trading (LLT) improves the long-term informational efficiency of stock prices. This discussion raises two primary concerns with their analysis. First, the mechanism through which LLT enhances long-term efficiency is unclear. 链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3759035

Abstract : We provide empirical evidence that optimism bias increases with the forecasting horizon. We label this empirical regularity the horizon bias.
