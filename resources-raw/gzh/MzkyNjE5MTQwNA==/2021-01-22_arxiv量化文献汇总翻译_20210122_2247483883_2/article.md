\[1\] Impact of closing schools on mental health during the COVID 19 pandemic

标题 : COVID 19大流行期间关闭学校对心理健康的影响

作者 : Eiji Yamamura, Yoshiro Tsutsui , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08476

说明 : -

Abstract : The spread of the novel coronavirus disease caused schools in Japan to close to cope with the pandemic. In response to this, parents of students were obliged to care for their children during the daytime when they were usually at school. Does the increase in burden of childcare influence parents mental health Based on short panel data from mid March to mid April 2020, we explored how school closures influenced the mental health of parents with school aged children. Using the fixed effects model, we found that school closures lead to students mothers suffering from worse mental health than other females, while the fathers mental health did not differ from other males. This tendency was only observed for less educated mothers who had children attending primary school, but not those attending junior high school. The contribution of this paper is to show that school closures increased the inequality of mental health between genders and the educational background of parents.

摘要 : 新型冠状病毒病的传播导致日本的学校关闭，以应对这场大流行。对此，学生家长有义务在孩子平时上学的白天照顾孩子。育儿负担的增加是否影响家长的心理健康基于2020年3月中旬至4月中旬的短面板数据，我们探讨了学校关闭对学龄儿童家长心理健康的影响。采用固定效应模型，我们发现学校关闭导致学生母亲的心理健康状况比其他女性差，而父亲的心理健康状况与其他男性没有差异。这一趋势只在教育程度较低的母亲中观察到，这些母亲的孩子上小学，但没有上初中。本文的贡献在于说明学校的关闭增加了性别和父母教育背景之间的心理健康不平等。

\[2\] Changing views about remote working during the COVID 19 pandemic

标题 : 在covid19流感大流行期间改变了对远程工作的看法

作者 : Eiji Yamamura, Yoshiro Tsutsui , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08480

说明 : -

Abstract : COVID 19 has led to school closures in Japan to cope with the pandemic. Under the state of emergency, in addition to school closure, after school care has not been sufficiently supplied. We independently collected individual level data through internet surveys to construct short panel data from mid March to mid June 2020, which covered before and after the state of emergency. We analyze how the presence of school aged children influences their parents views about working from home. After controlling for various factors using a fixed effects model, we find that in cases where parents were workers, and the children are (1) in primary school, parents are willing to promote working from home. If children are (2) in junior high school, the parents view is hardly affected. (3) Surprisingly, workers whose children are primary school pupils are most likely to support promotion of working from home after schools reopen. Due to school closure and a lack of after school care, parents need to work from home, and this experience motivated workers with small children to continue doing so to improve work life balance even after schools reopen.

摘要 : covid19导致日本学校关闭，以应对流感大流行。在紧急状态下，除了学校关闭外，课后护理也没有得到充分的供应。我们通过互联网调查独立收集了个人层面的数据，构建了2020年3月中旬至6月中旬的短面板数据，涵盖了紧急状态前后的数据。我们分析学龄儿童的存在如何影响他们的父母对在家工作的看法。在运用固定效应模型对各种因素进行控制后，我们发现，在父母是工人，子女是（1）小学的情况下，父母愿意促进在家工作。如果孩子在初中（2），父母的观点几乎不受影响。（3） 令人惊讶的是，孩子是小学生的工人最有可能在学校重新开学后支持促进在家工作。由于学校关闭和缺乏课后照顾，家长需要在家工作，这种经历促使有小孩的工人继续这样做，以改善工作与生活的平衡，即使在学校重新开放之后。

\[3\] Female teachers effect on male pupils voting behavior and preference formation

标题 : 女教师对男生投票行为及偏好形成的影响

作者 : Eiji Yamamura , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08487

说明 : -

Abstract : This study examines the influence of learning in a female teacher homeroom class in elementary school on pupils voting behavior later in life, using independently collected individual level data. Further, we evaluate its effect on preference for women s participation in the workplace in adulthood. Our study found that having a female teacher in the first year of school makes individuals more likely to vote for female candidates, and to prefer policy for female labor participation in adulthood. However, the effect is only observed among males, and not female pupils. These findings offer new evidence for the female socialization hypothesis.

摘要 : 本研究以一位小学女教师为研究对象，利用独立收集的个人资料，探讨学习对学生日后投票行为的影响。此外，我们还评估了其对成年期妇女参与工作场所偏好的影响。我们的研究发现，在学校的第一年有一名女教师，使得个人更有可能投票给女性候选人，并且在成年后更喜欢女性参与劳动的政策。然而，这种效应只在男性学生中观察到，而在女性学生中没有观察到。这些发现为女性社会化假说提供了新的证据。

\[4\] Long term effects of female teacher on her pupils smoking behaviour later in life

标题 : 女教师对学生晚年吸烟行为的长期影响

作者 : Eiji Yamamura , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08488

说明 : -

Abstract : In Japan, teacher and student is randomly matched in the first year of elementary school. Under the quasi natural experimental setting, we examine how learning in female teacher homeroom class in the elementary school influence pupils smoking behavior after they become adult. We found that pupils are unlikely to smoke later in life if they belonged to female teacher homeroom class in pupil s first year of school.

摘要 : 在日本，老师和学生在小学一年级随机配对。在准自然实验条件下，我们考察了小学女教师课堂学习对小学生成年后吸烟行为的影响。我们发现，如果学生在一年级时属于女教师班主任班，他们在以后的生活中不太可能吸烟。

\[5\] To VaR, or Not to VaR, That is the Question

标题 : 对VaR，还是不对VaR，这是个问题

作者 : Victor Olkhov , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08559

说明 : Comments: 13 pages

Abstract : This paper discusses the value at risk (VaR) concept and assesses the financial adequacy of the price probability determined by frequency of trades at price p. We take the price definition as the ratio of executed trade value to volume and show that it leads to price statistical moments, which differ from those, generated by frequency price probability. We derive the price n th statistical moments as ratio of n th statistical moments of the value and the volume of executed transactions. We state that the price probability determined by frequency of trades at price p does not describe probability of executed trade prices and VaR based on frequency price probability may be origin for unexpected and excessive losses. We explain the need to replace frequency price probability by frequency probabilities of the value and the volume of executed transactions and derive price characteristic function. After 50 years of the VaR usage main problems of the VaR concept are still open. We believe that VaR commitment to forecast the price probability for the time horizon T seems to be one of the most tough and expensive puzzle of modern finance.

摘要 : 本文讨论了风险价值（VaR）的概念，并对由p价交易频率决定的价格概率的财务充分性进行了评估。我们将价格定义为执行交易价值与交易量的比率，并表明它导致价格统计矩，这不同于，由频率价格概率产生。我们推导了价格的第n个统计矩作为价值和交易量的第n个统计矩的比值。我们指出，以p价交易频率决定的价格概率并不描述执行交易价格的概率，基于频率价格概率的VaR可能是意外和过度损失的根源。我们解释了用价值和交易量的频率概率代替频率价格概率的必要性，并导出了价格特征函数。经过50年的使用，VaR概念的主要问题仍然没有解决。我们认为，VaR承诺预测时间范围T内的价格概率似乎是现代金融学中最困难和最昂贵的难题之一。

\[6\] Climate Change Adaptation under Heterogeneous Beliefs

标题 : 异质信念下的气候变化适应

作者 : Marcel Nutz, Florian Stebegg , 类别 ：Optimization and Control (math.OC)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08424

说明 : -

Abstract : We study strategic interactions between firms with heterogeneous beliefs about future climate impacts. To that end, we propose a Cournot type equilibrium model where firms choose mitigation efforts and production quantities such as to maximize the expected profits under their subjective beliefs. It is shown that optimal mitigation efforts are increased by the presence of uncertainty and act as substitutes i.e., one firm s lack of mitigation incentivizes others to act more decidedly, and vice versa.

摘要 : 我们研究对未来气候影响有不同看法的公司之间的战略互动。为此，我们提出了一个古诺型均衡模型，在该模型中，企业选择缓解措施和生产数量，以便在主观信念下使预期利润最大化。研究表明，不确定性的存在会增加最优缓解努力，并起到替代作用，即一个企业缺乏缓解激励其他企业更果断地采取行动，反之亦然。
