\[1\] Show Me the Money! Dividend Policy in Countries with Weak Institutions

标题:把钱给我看！制度薄弱国家的股利政策

作者:Atif Ellahie,Zachary Kaplan

出处:Journal of Accounting Research, Volume 59, Issue 2

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3850362

Abstract : We hypothesize that, in weak-institution countries, firms adjust the ‘timing’ of dividend payments by committing to distribute a percentage of current earnings as dividends, revealing the extent of firm-level agency conflicts to future investors and facilitating the raising of external capital. Consistent with this hypothesis, we find that, on average, firms in weak-institution countries have a higher speed of adjustment (SOA) to their target payout ratio, pay dividends earlier in the life cycle, and are more likely to disclose a dividend policy committing to pay a minimum percentage of earnings. Within-country tests show that, in weak-institution countries, the firms with the highest SOA dividend policies have fewer agency problems and an increased ability to raise external capital. Finally, returns tests around earnings announcements show that high-SOA dividend policies are associated with larger market reactions to earnings in weak-institution countries. Collectively, our findings suggest that dividend policy helps to alleviate agency conflicts in weak-institution countries between firms and (future) investors.

Keywords : dividend policy; payout policy; earnings; institutional quality

Abstract :我们假设，在机构薄弱的国家，企业通过承诺将当前收益的一定比例作为股息进行分配，从而调整股息支付的“时间”，向未来投资者揭示企业层面代理冲突的程度，并促进外部资本的筹集。与这一假设相一致，我们发现，平均而言，弱制度国家的企业对其目标支付率的调整速度（SOA）较高，在生命周期中较早支付股息，并且更有可能披露承诺支付最低收益百分比的股息政策。国内测试表明，在制度薄弱的国家，拥有最高SOA红利政策的公司代理问题较少，筹集外部资本的能力增强。最后，围绕收益公告的回报测试表明，在机构薄弱的国家，高SOA股利政策与市场对收益的较大反应有关。总的来说，我们的研究结果表明，股利政策有助于缓解机构薄弱国家企业与（未来）投资者之间的代理冲突。

Keywords :股利政策；支付政策；收益；制度素质

\[2\] Uncovering the Iceberg from Its Tip: A Model of Publication Bias and p-Hacking

标题:从冰山一角揭开冰山：出版偏见和p-Hacking的模型

作者:Campbell R. Harvey,Yan Liu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3865813

Abstract : Harvey, Liu, and Zhu (2016) argue that a large proportion of published asset-pricing factors are likely false. Researchers may try many variables and report only the significant ones, so-called p-hacking. Some recent work challenges the prevalence of p-hacking and argues that the amount of shrinkage necessary for reported results is trivial. We present a model where there are true anomalies and false anomalies. Our model does a good job of fitting the observed population. Our evidence is consistent with the idea that a large proportion of anomalies are false and reinforces the need to raise the thresholds for statistical significance.

Keywords : p-hacking, Data mining, Anomalies, Simulations, Publication bias, Multiple testing

Abstract :Harvey、Liu和Zhu（2016）认为，大部分公布的资产定价因素可能是错误的。研究人员可能会尝试许多变量，只报告重要的变量，即所谓的p-hacking。最近的一些工作对p-hacking的流行提出了挑战，并认为报告结果所需的收缩量是微不足道的。我们提出了一个有真异常和假异常的模型。我们的模型很好地拟合了观察到的人口。我们的证据与很大一部分异常是假的这一观点是一致的，并加强了提高统计显著性阈值的必要性。

Keywords :黑客攻击，数据挖掘，异常，模拟，出版偏见，多重测试

\[3\] Climate Change Vulnerability and Currency Returns

标题:气候变化脆弱性与货币回报

作者:Alexander Cheema-Fox,George Serafeim,Hui (Stacie) Wang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3864393

Abstract : Using measures of physical risk from climate change, we develop a methodology to allocate currency pairs according to a country’s vulnerability to climate change and construct portfolios with decreasing vulnerability to physical risk. We show that non-G10 currencies are more vulnerable to physical risk, have become less vulnerable over time, and that the vulnerability measure is correlated with higher losses from natural disasters. Portfolios exposed to currencies with decreasing vulnerability have exhibited positive abnormal returns, with the abnormal return coming from currencies that have relatively high levels of vulnerability. These results exist in non-G10 currencies, while no relation to returns exist within G10 currencies.

Keywords : climate change, physical risk, climate vulnerability, foreign exchange, currency returns, emerging market; ESG

Abstract :利用气候变化的物理风险度量，我们开发了一种方法，根据一个国家对气候变化的脆弱性来分配货币对，并构建物理风险脆弱性降低的投资组合。我们表明，非G10国家的货币更容易受到物理风险的影响，随着时间的推移，其脆弱性已变得不那么脆弱，而且脆弱性指标与自然灾害造成的更高损失相关。暴露于脆弱性降低货币的投资组合表现出正的异常回报，异常回报来自脆弱性相对较高的货币。这些结果存在于非10国集团货币中，而与10国集团货币中的回报率无关。

Keywords :气候变化、物理风险、气候脆弱性、外汇、货币回报、新兴市场；静电陀螺仪

\[4\] Leverage and Cash Dynamics

标题:杠杆和现金动态

作者:Harry DeAngelo,Andrei Gonçalves,René M. Stulz

出处:Fisher College of Business Working Paper No. 2021-03-010Charles A. Dice Working Paper No. 2021-10

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3871170

Abstract : This paper documents new and empirically important interactions between cash-balance and leverage dynamics. Cash ratios typically vary widely over extended horizons, with dynamics remarkably similar to (and complementary with) those of capital structure. Leverage and cash dynamics interact approximately as predicted by the internal-versus-external funding regimes in Myers and Majluf (1984). Leverage is quite volatile when cash ratios are stable and vice-versa, while net-debt ratios are almost always volatile. Most firms increase leverage sharply as cash balances (internal funds) become scarce. Capital structure models that extend Hennessy and Whited (2005) to include cash-balance dynamics explain some, but not all, aspects of the observed relation between cash squeezes and leverage increases.

Keywords : capital structure, cash balances, leverage dynamics, financial flexibility, payout policy

Abstract :本文记录了新的和重要的经验之间的相互作用现金平衡和杠杆动力学。现金比率通常在长期内变化很大，其动态性与资本结构的动态性非常相似（并与之互补）。杠杆和现金动态的相互作用大致如Myers和Majluf（1984）所预测的那样。当现金比率稳定时，杠杆率相当不稳定，反之亦然，而净负债率几乎总是不稳定的。随着现金余额（内部资金）变得稀缺，大多数公司都会大幅提高杠杆率。资本结构模型将Hennessy和Whited（2005）扩展到包括现金余额动态，解释了现金挤压和杠杆率增加之间观察到的关系的一些方面，但不是所有方面。

Keywords :资本结构、现金余额、杠杆动态、财务灵活性、支付政策

\[5\] Efficient Detection in Large-Scale Testing

标题:大规模测试中的有效检测

作者:Min Zhu

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3866076

Abstract : Large-scale inference has become increasingly popular in financial economics nowadays. I explore an empirical Bayes approach for large-scale multiple testing. The proposed approach bases its inferences on the posterior probability that the null is true given the observed data. It establishes a data-driven t-statistic hurdle that can be associated with any pre-specified false discovery rate, or weighted combination of false discovery rate and false nondiscovery rate. Applying the method to meta-analysis of market anomalies, the factor zoo which is characterized by both multiple testing and p-hacking, and fund performance evaluation, I find that t-hurdle needs to be raised substantially in all cases.

Keywords : Multiple testing, False discovery rate, False nondiscovery rate, Empirical Bayes, Anomalies, Factor zoo, Performance evaluation, t-hurdle, p-hacking

Abstract :大规模推理在金融经济学中日益流行。我探索了一种用于大规模多重测试的经验贝叶斯方法。该方法基于观测数据为真的后验概率进行推理。它建立了一个数据驱动的t统计量障碍，可以与任何预先指定的错误发现率或错误发现率和错误不发现率的加权组合相关联。将该方法应用于市场异常的meta分析、多重检验和p-hacking的因子分析以及基金绩效评价，发现在所有情况下都需要大幅度提高t-buller。

Keywords :多重测试，错误发现率，错误不发现率，经验贝叶斯，异常，因子动物园，性能评估，t-障碍，p-黑客

\[6\] Regulation and Stock Market Quality: The Impact of MiFID II Provision on Research Unbundling

标题:监管与股市质量：MiFIDⅡ条款对研究分拆的影响

作者:Giulio Anselmi,Giovanni Petrella

出处:International Review of Financial Analysis, Forthcoming

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3864060

Abstract : This paper investigates the effects produced by the unbundling of analyst research costs required by MiFID II on market quality, as measured by stock liquidity and price efficiency. We find that the payment of an explicit price for research is associated with a reduction in analyst coverage in the EU. Unexpectedly, the reduction is stronger for large-cap stocks. For mid- and large-cap stocks analyst coverage in the EU is still greater than in the US. The reduction in analyst coverage observed in the EU is part of a downward trend that initiated prior to MiFID II and contributes to close the gap between the two regions. We also find no change in the bid-ask spread for small-, mid- and large-cap stocks, and a slight increase for micro-cap stocks. We observe no significant change in price efficiency. Taken together our findings seem to suggest that there was an overproduction of research in Europe with the previous regulatory regime. However, the growth of passive management and index funds may also explain the observed decrease in coverage.

Keywords : MiFID II, Analyst coverage, Sell-side research

Abstract :本文以股票流动性和价格效率为衡量指标，考察了MiFIDⅡ所要求的分拆分析师研究成本对市场质量的影响。我们发现，支付明确的研究价格与欧盟分析师覆盖率的降低有关。出乎意料的是，大盘股的减持力度更大。对于中大盘股，欧盟的分析师覆盖率仍高于美国。在欧盟观察到的分析师覆盖率下降是MiFID II之前开始的下降趋势的一部分，有助于缩小这两个地区之间的差距。我们还发现，中小盘股和大盘股的买卖价差没有变化，微盘股的买卖价差略有增加。我们观察到价格效率没有明显变化。综合来看，我们的研究结果似乎表明，欧洲在以前的监管制度下，研究成果过剩。然而，被动管理和指数基金的增长也可能解释了观察到的覆盖率下降。

Keywords :MiFID II，分析师报道，卖方研究

\[7\] Global Financial Crisis, COVID-19, Lockdown, and Herd Behavior in the US ESG Leader Stocks

标题:全球金融危机、COVID-19、锁定与美国ESG龙头股的羊群行为

作者:Ghulame Rubbaniy,Shoaib Ali,Costas Siriopoulos,Aristeidis Samitas

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3868114

Abstract : Using data of the constituents of the MSCI USA ESG leader index, this study investigates the herding behavior in the US ESG stocks over the period from January 03, 2007 to September 30, 2020. Our results reveal a significant herding behavior in the US ESG leader stocks. Our findings also show that herd effect is present in the US ESG stocks during both bear and bull market conditions. Our study documents the evidence of market-wide herding during the global financial crisis, COVID-19, lockdown, and post lockdown episodes. However, in all of these cases, herding is mainly characterized by intentional motives rather than fundamental factors. Finally, the outcome of our study has important implications for the investors, portfolio managers, and policy makers as herding can result in asset mispricing, adversely affecting portfolio diversification and adding to the market inefficiency.

Keywords : ESG stocks, COVID-19, Global financial crisis, Herding behavior, Capital asset pricing model

Abstract :本研究利用摩根士丹利资本国际（MSCI）美国ESG领先者指数的成分股数据，考察了2007年1月3日至2020年9月30日期间美国ESG股票的羊群行为，结果表明美国ESG领先者股票存在显著的羊群行为。我们的研究结果还表明，在熊市和牛市条件下，美国ESG股票都存在羊群效应。我们的研究记录了全球金融危机期间市场范围内羊群行为的证据，COVID-19，锁定和锁定后事件。然而，在所有这些情况下，羊群行为的主要特征是故意动机，而不是根本因素。最后，我们的研究结果对投资者、投资组合经理和政策制定者都有重要的启示，因为羊群效应会导致资产定价错误，对投资组合多样化产生不利影响，并增加市场的低效率。

Keywords :ESG股票，COVID-19，全球金融危机，羊群行为，资本资产定价模型
