\[1\] Technological Leapfrogging and Manufacturing Value added in sub Saharan African (1990 2018)

标题 : 撒哈拉以南非洲地区的技术跨越和制造业增加值（1990-2018）

作者 : Segun Michael Ojo, Edward Oladipo Ogunleye , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.16049

说明 : -

Abstract : This study examines the impact of technological leapfrogging on manufacturing value added in SSA. The study utilizes secondary data spanning 1990 to 2018. The data is analyzed using cross sectional autoregressive distributed lags (CS ARDL) and cross sectional distributed lags (CS DL) techniques. The study found that technological leapfrogging is a positive driver of manufacturing value added in SSA. This implies that SSA can copy the foreign technologies and adapt them for domestic uses, rather than going through the evolutionary process of the old technologies that are relatively less efficient. If the governments of SSA could reinforce their absorptive capacity and beef up productivity through proper utilization of the existing technology. The productive activities of the domestic firms will stir new innovations and discoveries that will eventually translate into indigenous technology

摘要 : 本研究探讨技术跨越对台湾制造业增加值的影响。这项研究利用了1990年至2018年的二级数据。数据分析采用截面自回归分布滞后（CS-ARDL）和截面分布滞后（CS-DL）技术。研究发现，技术跨越是SSA制造业增加值的正驱动力。这意味着SSA可以复制国外的技术并将其应用于国内，而不是经历效率相对较低的旧技术的进化过程。如果SSA的政府能够通过适当利用现有技术来增强其吸收能力和提高生产力。国内企业的生产活动将激发新的创新和发现，最终转化为本土技术

\[2\] On Capital Allocation for a Risk Measure Derived from Ruin Theory

标题 : 一类破产风险测度的资本配置

作者 : Guusje Delsing, Michel Mandjes, Peter Spreij, Erik Winands , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.16264

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper addresses allocation methodologies for a risk measure inherited from ruin theory. Specifically, we consider a dynamic value at risk (VaR) measure defined as the smallest initial capital needed to ensure that the ultimate ruin probability is less than a given threshold. We introduce an intuitively appealing, novel allocation method, with a focus on its application to capital reserves which are determined through the dynamic value at risk (VaR) measure. Various desirable properties of the presented approach are derived including a limit result when considering a large time horizon and the comparison with the frequently used gradient allocation method. In passing, we introduce a second allocation method and discuss its relation to the other allocation approaches. A number of examples illustrate the applicability and performance of the allocation approaches.

摘要 : 本文讨论了从破产理论中继承的风险度量的分配方法。具体来说，我们考虑一个动态风险价值（VaR）度量，定义为确保最终破产概率小于给定阈值所需的最小初始资本。本文介绍了一种直观的、新颖的资产配置方法，重点讨论了该方法在通过动态风险价值（VaR）测度确定资本公积中的应用。推导了该方法的各种理想性质，包括考虑大时间范围时的极限结果以及与常用的梯度分配方法的比较。在此基础上，我们介绍了第二种分配方法，并讨论了它与其他分配方法的关系。许多例子说明了分配方法的适用性和性能。

说明 : -

Abstract : Stock price prediction can be made more efficient by considering the price fluctuations and understanding the sentiments of people. A limited number of models understand financial jargon or have labelled datasets concerning stock price change. To overcome this challenge, we introduced FinALBERT, an ALBERT based model trained to handle financial domain text classification tasks by labelling Stocktwits text data based on stock price change. We collected Stocktwits data for over ten years for 25 different companies, including the major five FAANG (Facebook, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, Google). \[4\] Deep Hedging of Derivatives Using Reinforcement Learning

标题 : 基于强化学习的衍生品深度套期保值

作者 : Jay Cao, Jacky Chen, John Hull, Zissis Poulos , 类别 ：Computational Finance (q-fin.CP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.16409

说明 : Comments: 21 pages, 3 figures, 4 tables

Abstract : This paper shows how reinforcement learning can be used to derive optimal hedging strategies for derivatives when there are transaction costs. The paper illustrates the approach by showing the difference between using delta hedging and optimal hedging for a short position in a call option when the objective is to minimize a function equal to the mean hedging cost plus a constant times the standard deviation of the hedging cost. Two situations are considered. In the first, the asset price follows a geometric Brownian motion. In the second, the asset price follows a stochastic volatility process. The paper extends the basic reinforcement learning approach in a number of ways. First, it uses two different Q functions so that both the expected value of the cost and the expected value of the square of the cost are tracked for different state action combinations. This approach increases the range of objective functions that can be used. Second, it uses a learning algorithm that allows for continuous state and action space. Third, it compares the accounting P&L approach (where the hedged position is valued at each step) and the cash flow approach (where cash inflows and outflows are used). We find that a hybrid approach involving the use of an accounting P&L approach that incorporates a relatively simple valuation model works well. The valuation model does not have to correspond to the process assumed for the underlying asset price.

摘要 : 本文研究了在存在交易成本的情况下，如何利用强化学习来推导衍生品的最优套期保值策略。本文说明了在看涨期权中，当目标是最小化一个等于平均套期保值成本加上一个常数乘以套期保值成本标准差的函数时，对空头头寸使用delta套期保值和最优套期保值的区别。考虑两种情况。首先，资产价格遵循几何布朗运动。第二，资产价格遵循随机波动过程。本文从很多方面扩展了基本的强化学习方法。首先，它使用两个不同的Q函数，以便对不同的状态-动作组合跟踪代价的期望值和代价平方的期望值。这种方法增加了可以使用的目标函数的范围。其次，它使用了一种允许连续状态和动作空间的学习算法。第三，它比较了会计损益法（套期保值头寸在每一步进行估值）和现金流量法（使用现金流入和流出）。我们发现，一个混合的方法，包括使用会计损益法，结合了一个相对简单的估值模型运作良好。估值模型不必与基础资产价格的假设过程相对应。

\[5\] Robustifying Conditional Portfolio Decisions via Optimal Transport

标题 : 基于最优传输的条件投资组合决策的稳健性分析

作者 : Viet Anh Nguyen, Fan Zhang, Jose Blanchet, Erick Delage, Yinyu Ye , 类别 ：Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.16451

说明 : -

Abstract : We propose a data driven portfolio selection model that integrates side information, conditional estimation and robustness using the framework of distributionally robust optimization. Conditioning on the observed side information, the portfolio manager solves an allocation problem that minimizes the worst case conditional risk return trade off, subject to all possible perturbations of the covariate return probability distribution in an optimal transport ambiguity set. Despite the non linearity of the objective function in the probability measure, we show that the distributionally robust portfolio allocation with side information problem can be reformulated as a finite dimensional optimization problem. If portfolio decisions are made based on either the mean variance or the mean Conditional Value at Risk criterion, the resulting reformulation can be further simplified to second order or semi definite cone programs. Empirical studies in the US and Chinese equity markets demonstrate the advantage of our integrative framework against other benchmarks.

摘要 : 我们提出了一个数据驱动的投资组合选择模型，该模型综合了边信息、条件估计和鲁棒性，采用了分布鲁棒优化的框架。在观察到的边信息的条件下，投资组合经理解决了一个分配问题，该问题使最坏情况下的条件风险收益权衡最小化，受最优传输模糊集中协变量收益概率分布的所有可能扰动的影响。尽管目标函数在概率测度上是非线性的，但是我们证明了带边信息的分布稳健投资组合分配问题可以转化为一个有限维优化问题。如果投资组合的决策是基于均值-方差或均值-条件风险值准则，则所得到的重新表述可以进一步简化为二阶或半定锥规划。在美国和中国股市的实证研究表明，与其他基准相比，我们的综合框架具有优势。

\[6\] Public transport users versus private vehicle users

标题 : 公共交通用户与私人车辆用户

作者 : Juan de Oña, Esperanza Estévez, Rocio de Oña , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14762

说明 : Comments: 23 pages, 1 figure, 6 tables

Abstract : This paper aims to further understand the main factors influencing the behavioural intentions (BI) of private vehicle users towards public transport to provide policymakers and public transport operators with the tools they need to attract more private vehicle users. As service quality, satisfaction and attitudes towards public transport are considered the main motivational forces behind the BI of public transport users, this research analyses 26 indicators frequently associated with these constructs for both public transport users and private vehicle users. Non parametric tests and ordinal logit models have been applied to an online survey asked in Madrid s metropolitan area with a sample size of 1,025 respondents (525 regular public transport users and 500 regular private vehicle users). In order to achieve a comprehensive analysis and to deal with heterogeneity in perceptions, 338 models have been developed for the entire sample and for 12 users segments. The results led to the identification of indicators with no significant differences between public transport and private vehicle users in any of the segments being considered (punctuality, information and low income), as well as those that did show significant differences in all the segments (proximity, intermodality, save time and money, and lifestyle). The main differences between public transport and private vehicle users were found in the attitudes towards public transport and for certain user segments (residents in the city centre, males, young, with university qualification and with incomes above 2,700EUR month). Findings from this study can be used to develop policies and recommendations for persuading more private vehicle users to use the public transport services.

摘要 : 本文旨在进一步了解影响私人车辆使用者对公共交通行为意向的主要因素，为政策制定者和公共交通运营商提供吸引更多私人车辆使用者所需的工具。由于服务质量、满意度和对公共交通的态度被认为是公共交通用户BI背后的主要驱动力，本研究分析了公共交通用户和私人车辆用户经常与这些结构相关的26个指标。非参数检验和有序logit模型已应用于马德里市区的一项在线调查，样本量为1025名受访者（525名常规公共交通用户和500名常规私人车辆用户）。为了实现综合分析和处理感知的异质性，为整个样本和12个用户群体开发了338个模型。结果表明，公共交通和私人车辆使用者在所考虑的任何部分（正点率、信息量和低收入）以及在所有部分（接近性、联运性、节省时间和金钱、，生活方式）。公共交通和私人车辆使用者之间的主要差异在于对公共交通的态度以及某些使用者群体（市中心居民、男性、年轻人、大学学历、月收入超过2700欧元）。这项研究的结果可以用来制定政策和建议，说服更多的私人车辆使用者使用公共交通服务。

\[7\] Replicating Market Makers

标题 : 复制做市商

作者 : Guillermo Angeris, Alex Evans, Tarun Chitra , 类别 ：Mathematical Finance (q-fin.MF)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14769

说明 : -

Abstract : We present a method for constructing Constant Function Market Makers (CFMMs) whose portfolio value functions match a desired payoff. More specifically, we show that the space of concave, nonnegative, nondecreasing, 1 homogeneous payoff functions and the space of convex CFMMs are equivalent in other words, every CFMM has a concave, nonnegative, nondecreasing, 1 homogeneous payoff function, and every payoff function with these properties has a corresponding convex CFMM. We demonstrate a simple method for recovering a CFMM trading function that produces this desired payoff. This method uses only basic tools from convex analysis and is intimately related to Fenchel conjugacy. We demonstrate our result by constructing trading functions corresponding to basic payoffs, as well as standard financial derivatives such as options and swaps.

摘要 : 本文提出了一种构造常数函数做市商的方法，该做市商的投资组合价值函数与期望收益相匹配。更具体地说，我们证明了凹、非负、非减、1齐次支付函数空间和凸CFMM空间是等价的，即每个CFMM都有一个凹、非负、非减、1齐次支付函数，并且每个具有这些性质的支付函数都有一个相应的凸CFMM。我们演示了一种简单的方法来恢复CFMM交易函数，该函数产生所需的收益。该方法仅使用凸分析的基本工具，与Fenchel共轭密切相关。我们通过构造与基本收益相对应的交易函数以及期权和掉期等标准金融衍生工具来证明我们的结果。

\[9\] Drivers of academic engagement in public private research collaboration

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14859

说明 : Comments: arXiv admin note: text overlap with arXiv:2102.05364

Abstract : University industry research collaboration is one of the major research policy priorities of advanced economies. In this study, we try to identify the main drivers that could influence the propensity of academics to engage in research collaborations with the private sector, in order to better inform policies and initiatives to foster such collaborations. At this purpose, we apply an inferential model to a dataset of 32,792 Italian professors in order to analyze the relative impact of individual and contextual factors affecting the propensity of academics to engage in collaboration with industry, at overall level and across disciplines. \[10\] Accurate Stock Price Forecasting Using Robust and Optimized Deep Learning Models

标题 : 基于稳健优化深度学习模型的精确股价预测

作者 : Jaydip Sen, Sidra Mehtab , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.15096

说明 : Comments: This paper is an accepted version of our paper in the IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Technologies (IEEE CONIT), which will be organized in Hubli, Karnataka, INDIA, from June 25 to June 27, 2021. The paper is 8 pages long and it contains eleven tables and seventeen figures

Abstract : Designing robust frameworks for precise prediction of future prices of stocks has always been considered a very challenging research problem. The advocates of the classical efficient market hypothesis affirm that it is impossible to accurately predict the future prices in an efficiently operating market due to the stochastic nature of the stock price variables. However, numerous propositions exist in the literature with varying degrees of sophistication and complexity that illustrate how algorithms and models can be designed for making efficient, accurate, and robust predictions of stock prices. We present a gamut of ten deep learning models of regression for precise and robust prediction of the future prices of the stock of a critical company in the auto sector of India. Using a very granular stock price collected at 5 minutes intervals, we train the models based on the records from 31st Dec, 2012 to 27th Dec, 2013. 标题 : 大赦政策与战后南方精英的坚守

作者 : Jason Poulos , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14220

说明 : -

Abstract : This paper investigates the impact of Reconstruction era amnesty policy on the officeholding and wealth of planter elites in the postbellum U.S. South. Amnesty policy restricted the political and economic rights of the planter class for nearly three years during Reconstruction. The paper estimates the effects of being excepted from amnesty on elites future wealth and political power using a regression discontinuity design. Results on a sample of delegates to Reconstruction conventions show that exclusion from amnesty substantially decreases the likelihood of holding political office. I find no evidence that exclusion from amnesty impacted later census wealth for Reconstruction delegates or for a larger sample of known slaveholders who lived in the South in 1860. These findings are in line with previous studies evidencing changes to the identity of the political elite and continuity of economic mobility for the planter elite across the Civil War and Reconstruction.

摘要 : 本文探讨重建时期的特赦政策对战后美国南方种植业精英的任职和财富的影响。在重建期间，大赦政策限制了种植阶级近三年的政治和经济权利。本文采用回归不连续设计，估计了特赦例外对精英未来财富和政治权力的影响。对参加重建大会的代表的抽样调查结果表明，被排除在大赦之外大大降低了担任政治职务的可能性。我没有发现任何证据表明，特赦的排除会影响到重建代表或1860年居住在南方的已知奴隶主的更大样本后来的人口普查财富。这些发现与先前的研究一致，这些研究证明了政治精英身份的变化以及种植业精英在内战和重建期间经济流动的持续性。

\[23\] Superiority of mild interventions against COVID 19 on public health and economic measures

标题 : 轻度抗covid19干预对公共卫生和经济措施的优越性

作者 : Makoto Niwa, Yasushi Hara, Yusuke Matsuo, Hodaka Narita, Lim Yeongjoo, Shintaro Sengoku, Kota Kodama , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14298

说明 : Comments: 44 pages, 6 figures, 1 table

Abstract : During the global spread of COVID 19, Japan has been among the top countries to maintain a relatively low number of infections, despite implementing limited institutional interventions. Using a Tokyo Metropolitan dataset, this study investigated how these limited intervention policies have affected public health and economic conditions in the COVID 19 context. A causal loop analysis suggested that there were risks to prematurely terminating such interventions. On the basis of this result and subsequent quantitative modelling, we found that the short term effectiveness of a short term pre emptive stay at home request caused a resurgence in the number of positive cases, whereas an additional request provided a limited negative add on effect for economic measures (e.g. the number of electronic word of mouth (eWOM) communications and restaurant visits). These findings suggest the superiority of a mild and continuous intervention as a long term countermeasure under epidemic pressures when compared to strong intermittent interventions.

摘要 : 在covid19全球传播期间，尽管实施了有限的机构干预措施，但日本一直是保持相对较低感染率的主要国家之一。本研究利用东京都的数据集，调查了这些有限的干预政策如何影响covid19背景下的公共卫生和经济状况。因果循环分析表明，过早终止此类干预措施存在风险。根据这一结果和随后的定量建模，我们发现短期先发制人的居家请求的短期有效性导致了阳性病例数量的回升，而另一项请求对经济措施（如电子口碑通信和餐馆访问的数量）的负面附加效应有限。这些发现表明，在流行病压力下，作为一项长期对策，温和和持续的干预措施优于强有力的间歇干预措施。

\[24\] Evolutionary Strategies with Analogy Partitions in p guessing Games

标题 : p猜测博弈中的类比划分进化策略

作者 : Aymeric Vie , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14379

说明 : -

Abstract : In Keynesian Beauty Contests notably modeled by p guessing games, players try to guess the average of guesses multiplied by p. Convergence of plays to Nash equilibrium has often been justified by agents learning. However, interrogations remain on the origin of reasoning types and equilibrium behavior when learning takes place in unstable environments. When successive values of p can take values above and below 1, bounded rational agents may learn about their environment through simplified representations of the game, reasoning with analogies and constructing expectations about the behavior of other players. We introduce an evolutionary process of learning to investigate the dynamics of learning and the resulting optimal strategies in unstable p guessing games environments with analogy partitions. As a validation of the approach, we first show that our genetic algorithm behaves consistently with previous results in persistent environments, converging to the Nash equilibrium. We characterize strategic behavior in mixed regimes with unstable values of p. Varying the number of iterations given to the genetic algorithm to learn about the game replicates the behavior of agents with different levels of reasoning of the level k approach. This evolutionary process hence proposes a learning foundation for endogenizing existence and transitions between levels of reasoning in cognitive hierarchy models.

摘要 : 在凯恩斯主义的选美比赛中，尤其是以p猜游戏为模型的选美比赛中，参赛者试图猜出平均猜数乘以p的结果。博弈收敛到纳什均衡常常被代理学习所证明。然而，当学习发生在不稳定的环境中时，审问仍然停留在推理类型和均衡行为的起源上。当p的连续值可以取大于或小于1时，有界理性主体可以通过游戏的简化表示、类比推理和构建对其他参与者行为的期望来了解他们的环境。我们引入一个学习的进化过程来研究在不稳定的p猜测博弈环境中的学习动态以及由此产生的最优策略。作为对该方法的验证，我们首先证明了我们的遗传算法在持久性环境中的行为与以前的结果一致，收敛到纳什均衡。我们刻画了p值不稳定的混合区域中的策略行为。通过改变遗传算法的迭代次数来了解博弈，复制了k级方法中不同推理水平的代理行为。因此，这一进化过程为认知层次模型中的推理水平的内生存在和转换提供了一个学习基础。

\[26\] Isolating the impact of trading on grid frequency fluctuations

标题 : 隔离交易对电网频率波动的影响

作者 : Benjamin Sch fer, Marc Timme, Dirk Witthaut , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14592

说明 : -

Abstract : To ensure reliable operation of power grids, their frequency shall stay within strict bounds. Multiple sources of disturbances cause fluctuations of the grid frequency, ranging from changing demand over volatile feed in to energy trading. Here, we analyze frequency time series from the continental European grid in 2011 and 2017 as a case study to isolate the impact of trading. We find that trading at typical trading intervals such as full hours modifies the frequency fluctuation statistics. While particularly large frequency deviations in 2017 are not as frequent as in 2011, large deviations are more likely to occur shortly after the trading instances. A comparison between the two years indicates that trading at shorter intervals might be beneficial for frequency quality and grid stability, because particularly large fluctuations are substantially diminished. Furthermore, we observe that the statistics of the frequency fluctuations do not follow Gaussian distributions but are better described using heavy tailed and asymmetric distributions, for example L evy stable distributions. Comparing intervals without trading to those with trading instances indicates that frequency deviations near the trading times are distributed more widely and thus extreme deviations are orders of magnitude more likely. Finally, we briefly review a stochastic analysis that allows a quantitative description of power grid frequency fluctuations.

摘要 : 为保证电网可靠运行，电网频率应严格控制。多个干扰源会导致电网频率波动，从波动性上网引起的需求变化到能源交易。在这里，我们分析了欧洲大陆电网2011年和2017年的频率时间序列作为一个案例研究，以隔离交易的影响。我们发现，在典型的交易间隔，如全小时交易修改频率波动统计。虽然2017年特别大的频率偏差不像2011年那么频繁，但大的偏差更可能发生在交易实例之后不久。两年之间的比较表明，较短时间间隔的交易可能有利于频率质量和电网稳定性，因为特别大的波动会大大减少。此外，我们观察到频率波动的统计不遵循高斯分布，而是使用重尾和不对称分布来更好地描述，例如evy稳定分布。将无交易区间与有交易实例的区间进行比较表明，交易时间附近的频率偏差分布更广，因此极端偏差的可能性更大。最后，我们简要回顾了一个随机分析，可以定量描述电网频率波动。

作者 : V.A. Kalyagin, A.P. Koldanov, P.A. Koldanov , 类别 ：Statistical Finance (q-fin.ST)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14593

说明 : -

Abstract : Maximum spanning tree (MST) is a popular tool in market network analysis. Large number of publications are devoted to the MST calculation and it s interpretation for particular stock markets. However, much less attention is payed in the literature to the analysis of uncertainty of obtained results. In the present paper we suggest a general framework to measure uncertainty of MST identification. We study uncertainty in the framework of the concept of random variable network (RVN). We consider different correlation based networks in the large class of elliptical distributions. We show that true MST is the same in three networks Pearson correlation network, Fechner correlation network, and Kendall correlation network. We argue that among different measures of uncertainty the FDR (False Discovery Rate) is the most appropriated for MST identification. We investigate FDR of Kruskal algorithm for MST identification and show that reliability of MST identification is different in these three networks. In particular, for Pearson correlation network the FDR essentially depends on distribution of stock returns. We prove that for market network with Fechner correlation the FDR is non sensitive to the assumption on stock s return distribution. Some interesting phenomena are discovered for Kendall correlation network. Our experiments show that FDR of Kruskal algorithm for MST identification in Kendall correlation network weakly depend on distribution and at the same time the value of FDR is almost the best in comparison with MST identification in other networks. These facts are important in practical applications.

\[28\] Inequality, Identity, and Partisanship

标题 : 不平等、身份认同和党派之争

作者 : Alexander J. Stewart, Joshua B. Plotkin, Nolan McCarty , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14619

说明 : -

Abstract : The form of political polarization where citizens develop strongly negative attitudes towards out party policies and members has become increasingly prominent across many democracies. Economic hardship and social inequality, as well as inter group and racial conflict, have been identified as important contributing factors to this phenomenon known as affective polarization. Such partisan animosities are exacerbated when these interests and identities become aligned with existing party cleavages. In this paper we use a model of cultural evolution to study how these forces combine to generate and maintain affective political polarization. We show that economic events can drive both affective polarization and sorting of group identities along party lines, which in turn can magnify the effects of underlying inequality between those groups. But on a more optimistic note, we show that sufficiently high levels of wealth redistribution through the provision of public goods can counteract this feedback and limit the rise of polarization. We test some of our key theoretical predictions using survey data on inter group polarization, sorting of racial groups and affective polarization in the United States over the past 50 years.

摘要 : 在许多民主国家，公民对党外政策和党员产生强烈消极态度的政治两极分化形式日益突出。经济困难和社会不平等以及群体间和种族冲突被认为是造成这种被称为情感两极分化现象的重要因素。当这些利益和身份与现有的党派分裂相一致时，这种党派仇恨就会加剧。在本文中，我们使用一个文化进化模型来研究这些力量是如何结合起来产生和维持情感政治两极分化的。我们的研究表明，经济事件既可以推动情感分化，也可以推动政党路线上的群体认同排序，这反过来又可以放大这些群体之间潜在不平等的影响。但从更乐观的角度来看，我们表明，通过提供公共产品进行足够高水平的财富再分配，可以抵消这种反馈，限制两极分化的上升。我们测试了我们的一些关键的理论预测使用调查数据的群体间的极化，种族群体的分类和情感极化在美国过去50年。

\[29\] A Genetic Algorithm approach to Asymmetrical Blotto Games with Heterogeneous Valuations

标题 : 一种求解异质估值非对称Blotto对策的遗传算法

作者 : Aymeric Vie , 类别 ：Neural and Evolutionary Computing (cs.NE)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14372

说明 : -

Abstract : Blotto Games are a popular model of multi dimensional strategic resource allocation. Two players allocate resources in different battlefields in an auction setting. While competition with equal budgets is well understood, little is known about strategic behavior under asymmetry of resources. We introduce a genetic algorithm, a search heuristic inspired from biological evolution, interpreted as social learning, to solve this problem. Most performant strategies are combined to create more performant strategies. Mutations allow the algorithm to efficiently scan the space of possible strategies, and consider a wide diversity of deviations. We show that our genetic algorithm converges to the analytical Nash equilibrium of the symmetric Blotto game. We present the solution concept it provides for asymmetrical Blotto games. It notably sees the emergence of guerilla warfare strategies, consistent with empirical and experimental findings. The player with less resources learns to concentrate its resources to compensate for the asymmetry of competition. When players value battlefields heterogeneously, counter strategies and bidding focus is obtained in equilibrium. These features are consistent with empirical and experimental findings, and provide a learning foundation for their existence.

摘要 : Blotto博弈是一种流行的多维战略资源配置模型。两个玩家在拍卖场景中分配不同战场的资源。虽然预算相等的竞争是众所周知的，但对于资源不对称下的战略行为却知之甚少。我们引入一种遗传算法来解决这个问题，这是一种源于生物进化的启发式搜索算法，被解释为社会学习。大多数绩效策略被结合起来创造出更多的绩效策略。变异使算法能够有效地扫描可能策略的空间，并考虑各种各样的偏差。我们证明了我们的遗传算法收敛于对称Blotto对策的解析Nash均衡。我们提出了解决方案的概念，它提供了不对称Blotto游戏。它特别注意到游击战战略的出现，与经验和实验结果相一致。资源较少的玩家学会集中资源来弥补竞争的不对称性。当博弈者对战场进行异质性评价时，均衡地获得了反策略和竞价焦点。这些特征与实验和实验结果一致，为它们的存在提供了学习基础。
