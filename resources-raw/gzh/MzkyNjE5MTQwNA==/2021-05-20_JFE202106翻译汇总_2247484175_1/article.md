\[1\] Why are firms with more managerial ownership worth less?

标题:为什么拥有更多管理所有权的公司价值更低？

作者:Kornelia Fabisik,Rüdiger Fahlenbrach,René M. Stulz,Jérôme P. Taillard

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000416

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.02.008

Abstract : Using more than 50,000 firm-years from 1988 to 2015, we show that the empirical relation between a firm's Tobin's q and managerial ownership is systematically negative. When we restrict our sample to larger firms, as in the prior literature, we confirm earlier findings of an increasing and concave relation between q and managerial ownership. We show that cumulative past performance and liquidity can explain these seemingly contradictory results. Better performing firms have more liquid equity, which enables firms and insiders to more easily sell shares after the IPO, and they also have a higher Tobin's q.

Keywords : Managerial ownership;Tobin's q;Firm value;Liquidity

Abstract :从1988年到2015年的5万多个企业年中，我们发现企业的托宾q与管理层持股之间的经验关系是系统性的负相关。当我们将样本限制在大型企业时，如先前文献中所述，我们证实了先前的发现，即q与管理层所有权之间存在一种增加和凹进的关系。我们发现，累积过去的表现和流动性可以解释这些看似矛盾的结果。表现较好的公司拥有更多的流动性股票，这使得公司和内部人士在IPO后更容易出售股票，而且他们的托宾q值也更高。

Keywords :管理所有权；托宾q；企业价值；流动性

\[2\] Understanding momentum and reversal

标题:理解动量和反转

作者:Bryan T. Kelly,Tobias J. Moskowitz,Seth Pruitt

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000878

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.06.024

Abstract : Stock momentum, long-term reversal, and other past return characteristics that predict future returns also predict future realized betas, suggesting these characteristics capture time-varying risk compensation. We formalize this argument with a conditional factor pricing model. Using instrumented principal components analysis, we estimate latent factors with time-varying factor loadings that depend on observable firm characteristics. We show that factor loadings vary significantly over time, even at short horizons over which the momentum phenomenon operates (one year), and this variation captures reliable conditional risk premia missed by other factor models commonly used in the literature. Our estimates of conditional risk exposure can explain a sizable fraction of momentum and long-term reversal returns and can be used to generate even stronger return predictions.

Keywords : Momentum;Reversal;Factor model;Conditional betas;Conditional expected returns;IPCA

Abstract :股票动量、长期反转和其他预测未来收益的过去收益特征也预测未来实现的beta，这表明这些特征捕获了时变风险补偿。我们用条件因素定价模型形式化了这个论点。使用工具主成分分析，我们估计潜在因素随时间变化的因素负荷，取决于可观察到的企业特征。我们发现，即使在动量现象运行的短期内（一年），因子负荷也会随时间显著变化，这种变化捕捉到了文献中常用的其他因子模型遗漏的可靠条件风险溢价。我们对条件风险敞口的估计可以解释动量和长期反转收益的相当一部分，并可用于生成更强大的收益预测。

Keywords :动量；倒转；因子模型；条件beta；有条件预期收益；IPCA

\[3\] Do limits to arbitrage explain the benefits of volatility-managed portfolios?

标题:套利限制是否解释了波动性管理投资组合的好处？

作者:Pedro Barroso,Andrew Detzel

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000775

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.02.009

Abstract : We investigate whether transaction costs, arbitrage risk, and short-sale impediments explain the abnormal returns of volatility-managed equity portfolios. Even using six cost-mitigation strategies, after transaction costs, volatility management of asset-pricing factors besides the market return generally produces zero abnormal returns and significantly reduces Sharpe ratios. In contrast, abnormal returns of the volatility-managed market portfolio are robust to transaction costs and concentrated in the most easily arbitraged stocks, those with low arbitrage risk and impediments to short selling. Moreover, the managed market strategy only provides superior performance when sentiment is high, consistent with prior theory that sentiment traders underreact to volatility.

Keywords : Transaction costs;Short-sale constraints;Arbitrage risk;Factor timing;Sentiment

Abstract :我们研究了交易成本、套利风险和卖空障碍是否解释了波动性管理股票投资组合的异常收益。即使使用六种成本缓解策略，在交易成本之后，资产定价因素的波动性管理除了市场收益一般产生零异常收益，并显著降低夏普比率。相比之下，波动率管理的市场投资组合的异常回报对交易成本具有鲁棒性，并集中在最容易套利的股票，即套利风险低和卖空障碍的股票。此外，管理型市场策略仅在情绪高涨时提供优异的表现，这与之前情绪交易者对波动反应不足的理论一致。

Keywords :交易成本；卖空约束；套利风险；因素时序；情绪

\[4\] Geographic diversification and bank lending during crises

标题:地理多样化与危机期间的银行贷款

作者:Sebastian Doerr,Philipp Schaz

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000374

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.02.004

Abstract : We classify a large sample of banks according to the geographic diversification of their international syndicated loan portfolio. We show that diversified banks maintain higher loan supply during banking crises in borrower countries. Positive loan supply effects lead to higher firm investment and employment growth. Diversified banks are stabilizing due to their ability to raise additional funding during times of distress. Distinguishing banks by nationality reveals that diversified domestic banks are a stable source of funding, while foreign banks with little diversification are fickle. Findings suggest that declining financial integration makes countries more vulnerable to local financial shocks.

Keywords :全球银行；多元化；银团贷款；金融危机

\[5\] Monetary policy at work: Security and credit application registers evidence

标题:货币政策在起作用：安全和信贷应用注册证据

作者:José-Luis Peydró,Andrea Polo,Enrico Sette

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000313

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.01.008

Abstract : Monetary policy transmission may be impaired if banks rebalance their portfolios toward securities. We identify the bank lending and risk-taking channels of monetary policy by exploiting—Italy's unique—credit and security registers. In crisis times, with higher central bank liquidity, less capitalized banks react by increasing securities over credit supply, inducing worse firm-level real effects. However, they buy securities with lower yields and haircuts. Unlike in crisis times, in precrisis times, securities do not crowd out credit supply. \[6\] Negative peer disclosure

作者:Sean Shun Cao,Vivian W. Fang,Lijun (Gillian) Lei

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000404

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.02.007

Abstract : This paper provides first evidence of negative peer disclosure (NPD), an emerging corporate strategy to publicize adverse news of industry peers on social media. Consistent with NPDs being implicit positive self-disclosures, disclosing firms experience a two-day abnormal return of 1.6–1.7% over the market and industry. Further exploring the benefits and costs of such disclosures, we find that NPD propensity increases with the degree of product market rivalry and technology proximity and disclosing firms outperform nondisclosing peers in the product markets in the year following NPDs. Abstract : Better access to reproductive healthcare increases women’s propensity to become entrepreneurs. Access correlates positively with female entrepreneurial activity and negatively with female entrepreneurial age. \[8\] Calendar rotations: A new approach for studying the impact of timing using earnings announcements

标题:日历轮换：利用收益公告研究时间安排影响的新方法

作者:Suzie Noh,Eric C. So,Rodrigo S. Verdi

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000325

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.01.009

Abstract : We develop a novel methodology for studying the causal impact of announcement timing. Our methodology uses firms’ earnings announcements and leverages quasi-exogenous variation attributable to the specific day-of-week on which a calendar month begins. We refer to the resulting variation in announcement timing as “calendar rotations,” which are uncorrelated with proxies for announcement content. In applying our methodology, we show announcements moved forward by calendar rotations receive heightened media and investor attention, and experience greater earnings announcement premia. Taken together, our study details a method for studying how the timing of information flows impacts outcomes of interest to financial economists.

Keywords : Earnings announcements;Exogenous shock;Mispricing;Attention;Earnings announcement premium

Keywords :收益公告；外源性休克；错误定价；注意；收益公告溢价

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000349

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.02.001

Abstract : This paper studies the disciplinary spillover effects of proxy contests on companies that share directors with target firms, that is, interlocked firms. In difference-in-differences tests, I find that interlocked firms reduce excess cash holdings, increase shareholder payouts, cut CEO compensation, and engage in less earnings management in the year after proxy contests. \[10\] Information shocks, disagreement, and drift

标题:信息冲击、分歧和漂移

作者:Will J. Armstrong,Laura Cardella,Nasim Sabah

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000350

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.02.002

Abstract : We examine the effects of investor disagreement on price discovery using a recurring public information event in the highly liquid crude oil futures market, a market free of short sale constraints. We show that prices reflect positive news within one-half second of trading but continue to drift for five minutes when news is negative. Evidence suggests the drift arises from a systematic surge in buying pressure that impedes the price discovery process when news is negative. Our results are consistent with price drift arising from differences in trading horizons, where traders taking long positions condition trades on information beyond the news.

Keywords : Asymmetric price drift;Disagreement;Intraday news;High frequency trading

Keywords :不对称价格漂移；分歧；当天新闻；高频交易

\[12\] Learning from noise: Evidence from India’s IPO lotteries

标题:从噪音中学习：来自印度IPO彩票的证据

作者:Santosh Anagol,Vimal Balasubramaniam,Tarun Ramadorai

链接:https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21000362

DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.02.003

Abstract : We study a natural experiment in which 1.5 million investors participate in allocation lotteries for Indian IPO stocks. Investors who win the lottery and obtain IPO stocks that rise in value increase portfolio trading volume in non-IPO stocks relative to lottery losers; the effects are negative for lottery winners obtaining IPO stocks that fall in value. A model in which agents learn from random experience about their ability to operate in the market environment best explains the results. Investors who have received multiple past IPO allocations show smaller responses, suggesting that learning/selection moderates these responses to noise shocks.
