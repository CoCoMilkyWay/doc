\[1\] The Five-Factor Asset Pricing Model Tests and Profitability and Investment Premiums: Evidence from Pakistan

标题:五因素资产定价模型检验与盈利能力和投资溢价：来自巴基斯坦的证据

作者:Fahad Ali(Zhejiang University - College of Economics),Muhammad Usman Khurram(College of economics, Zhejiang university),Yuexiang Jiang(Zhejiang University - College of Economics)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3718569

Abstract : Using an extensive sample of the Pakistani stock market over the 2003–2016 period, this paper is the first to evaluate and compare the performance of four most popular factor pricing models: the Fama and French three-factor model, Carhart’s four-factor model, the five-factor model proposed by Fama and French, and the six-factor model that adds momentum to the five-factor model. We also test different nested models and find that the five-factor model best explains the returns of anomaly portfolios and outperforms the other models. We note that the profitability factor significantly improves the description of average returns, whereas factor spanning tests show that the value and momentum factors are redundant for the Pakistani stock market. Our results are robust to alternative factor definitions, formation of test assets, and across sub-periods.

Keywords : asset pricing, factor models, profitability and investment, momentum, emerging markets, Pakistani stock market

Abstract :本文以2003-2016年巴基斯坦股市为样本，首次对四种最流行的因素定价模型进行了评价和比较：Fama和French的三因素模型、Carhart的四因素模型、Fama和French提出的五因素模型以及添加了五因素模型的动量。我们还对不同的嵌套模型进行了检验，发现五因素模型能够很好地解释异常投资组合的收益，并且优于其他模型。我们注意到，盈利能力因素显著改善了对平均回报的描述，而因素跨越测试表明，价值和动量因素对巴基斯坦股市是多余的。我们的结果是稳健的替代因素定义，形成测试资产，并跨子时期。

Keywords :资产定价，要素模型，盈利能力和投资，动量，新兴市场，巴基斯坦股市

\[2\] Amazon Is Coming to Town: Private Information and Housing Market Efficiency

标题:亚马逊即将进城：私人信息与房地产市场效率

作者:Yifan Chen(Pennsylvania State University, Mary Jean and Frank P. Smeal College of Business Administration),Sean Wilkoff(University of Nevada, Reno),Jiro Yoshida(Pennsylvania State University - Smeal College of Business; The University of Tokyo - Graduate School of Economics)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3762672

Abstract : This study provides evidence of strong-form efficiency in the housing market, in which private information is incorporated into prices. We use Amazon’s progressive disclosure of its new headquarters locations in Virginia and New York to distinguish changes in the public’s knowledge. Using a spatial difference-in-differences approach, we test whether housing prices increase before Amazon’s public announcements. The quality-adjusted housing prices consistently exhibit 4.3−4.9% premia near the Virginia headquarters before Amazon’s decision. Price premia for New York reach 17.5% before the decision but disappear once Amazon cancels the headquarters. Other finalist cities exhibit no price premia, precluding the possibility of speculation.

Keywords : market efficiency, private information, insiders, office locations, housing demand

Abstract :这项研究提供了强有力的证据，在住房市场的形式效率，其中私人信息纳入价格。我们利用亚马逊逐步披露其在弗吉尼亚州和纽约的新总部地点来区分公众知识的变化。利用空间差异的方法，我们测试了亚马逊发布公告前房价是否上涨。在亚马逊做出决定之前，弗吉尼亚总部附近经质量调整后的房价一直表现出4.3-4.9%的溢价。纽约的价格溢价在决定前达到17.5%，但一旦亚马逊取消总部，价格溢价就会消失。其他入围城市没有价格溢价，排除了投机的可能性。

Keywords :市场效率、私人信息、内部人员、办公地点、住房需求

\[3\] Implementing Lead Qualification Model Using ICP for Saas Products

标题:利用ICP实现Saas产品的铅认证模型

作者:Laxmi Priya V(SSN School of Management),Hariharanath K(SSN School of Management Chennai)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3736569

Abstract : Lead qualification is a critical task for the marketing team as it brings the effectiveness to the campaigns run by sales teams. A well-qualified lead will assist the sales team to increase the conversion rate. There are few other factors like optimization of time, targeting the right kind of leads, and making the leads management process a more meaningful exercise with the support of the Marketing Qualified Leads (MQL). This paper discusses a lead qualification model for the business-to-business market. As the companies face stiff competition in the market place, it is common that they expand the search for potential leads which results in an increased number of leads entering the customer relationship management module. It is difficult for companies to track and engage potential leads without assessing the quality of such leads in the absence of a well-structured MQL process. It is more common now a day that most of the customer acquisitions are done through a well-targeted email campaigns. Therefore, the determined parameters have been chosen based on the earlier research done in this area and the nature of data to evaluate MQLs. Findings suggest that implicit data like the company’s size and explicit data like email opens, email clicks, and signups, could predict the probability of conversion. The research also throws light at less studied factors like the type of designation of the target executive contributing to the lead success. The paper proposes a lead scoring method which is a simplified version, yet effective, of the existing complex models available in this domain.

Keywords : Behavioral data, Characteristic data, Hot leads, Ideal Customer Profile, Lead qualification model, Lead scoring, Marketing Qualified Leads, Sales qualified leads, Sales conversions, Warm leads

Abstract :领导资格认证是营销团队的一项关键任务，因为它为销售团队开展的活动带来了有效性。一个合格的领导将协助销售团队提高转化率。很少有其他因素，如优化时间、确定正确的潜在客户类型、在市场营销合格潜在客户（MQL）的支持下使潜在客户管理过程成为更有意义的活动。本文讨论了一个企业对企业市场的领先者资格模型。由于公司在市场上面临激烈的竞争，他们通常会扩大对潜在客户的搜索，这导致进入客户关系管理模块的客户数量增加。在缺乏结构良好的MQL流程的情况下，如果不评估潜在客户的质量，公司很难跟踪和参与潜在客户。如今，大多数客户收购都是通过目标明确的电子邮件活动完成的，这一点更为常见。因此，所确定的参数是根据这一领域的早期研究和评价mql的数据性质选择的。研究结果表明，公司规模等隐性数据和电子邮件打开、电子邮件点击和注册等显性数据可以预测转换的可能性。这项研究还揭示了一些研究较少的因素，如目标高管的指定类型对领导成功的贡献。本文提出了一种铅评分方法，这是一个简化版本，但有效的，现有的复杂模型在这一领域。

Keywords :行为数据、特征数据、热门潜在客户、理想客户概况、潜在客户资格模型、潜在客户评分、营销合格潜在客户、销售合格潜在客户、销售转换、热情潜在客户

\[4\] Cryptocurrencies are Not Immune to Coronavirus: Evidence from Investor Fear

标题:加密货币不能免疫冠状病毒：来自投资者恐惧的证据

作者:Lai T. Hoang(The University of Western Australia),Dirk G. Baur(University of Western Australia - Business School; Financial Research Network (FIRN))

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3778988

Abstract : This paper examines the predictability of fear of coronavirus on future returns and volatility of five major cryptocurrencies during the COVID-19 outbreak. Adopting Google search volume on a comprehensive list of coronavirus-related terms to construct a gauge of fear, we show that daily innovation in fear of coronavirus is associated with next-day negative returns and positive volatility. The results indicate that (i) cryptocurrencies (particularly bitcoin) are not a safe haven for investors against the COVID-19 pandemic, and (ii) Google search contains important information that might be useful to predict cryptocurrency market movements during times of crisis.

Keywords : cryptocurrencies, bitcoin, coronavirus, pandemic, Google Trends

Abstract :本文考察了在COVID-19爆发期间，对冠状病毒的恐惧对五种主要加密货币未来收益和波动性的可预测性。采用Google搜索量对冠状病毒相关术语的综合列表构建恐惧量表，我们发现，每天对冠状病毒恐惧的创新与次日的负收益和正波动相关。结果表明：（i）加密货币（尤其是比特币）不是投资者抵御COVID-19流行的避风港；（ii）Google搜索包含的重要信息可能有助于预测危机期间加密货币市场的走势。

Keywords :加密货币、比特币、冠状病毒、流行病、谷歌趋势

\[5\] The Impact of Assets Impairment on Earnings: A Special Reference to Listed Manufacturing Companies of Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) in Sri Lanka

标题:资产减值对收益的影响：以斯里兰卡科伦坡证券交易所（CSE）上市制造业公司为例

作者:Logeswary Sooriyakumaran(Advanced Technological Institute, Jaffna),Periyathamby Elangkumaran(Independent)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3736528

Abstract : Abstract. The impact of assets impairment on earnings has been a topic of international research since decades because of sever global economic crises. In this paper we derive and test the impact of assets impairment on earnings of listed manufacturing companies on Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) in Sri Lanka. The application of LKAS36 measures the impairment of assets and accordingly it is necessary to disclose the information in the financial statements. The Impairment Loss (IL ) is used as independent variables while Operating Profit Margin (OPR), Net Profit Margin (NPM), Earning per Share (EPS), Return on Asset (ROA) and Return on Equity (ROE) which are the de-pendent variables. The analysis are performed using data derived from the financial statements of sixteen manufacturing companies listed on CSE during the period from 2007/2008 to 2011/2012. It is found that there is a significant negative relationship between impairment loss and OPM, NPM and ROA. Further regression results also confirmed that impairment loss is significantly impact on OPR, NPM as well as ROA of listed manufacturing companies on CSE in Sri Lanka. Hence the objective of this study is to provide insights that how far firms’ earnings are impacted by implementing the impairment of assets.

Keywords : Impairment of Assets, Net Profit Margin, Return on Assets

Abstract :抽象的。由于全球经济危机的不断加剧，资产减值对收益的影响一直是国际上研究的热点。本文推导并检验了斯里兰卡科伦坡证券交易所（CSE）制造业上市公司资产减值对盈利的影响。应用LKAS36衡量资产减值，因此有必要在财务报表中披露相关信息。以减值损失为自变量，营业利润率、净利润率、每股收益、资产收益率和净资产收益率为自变量。该分析使用了来自于中国证券交易所上市的16家制造业公司2007/2008年至2011/2012年期间财务报表的数据。研究发现，减值损失与OPM、NPM和ROA之间存在显著的负相关关系。进一步的回归结果也证实了减值损失对斯里兰卡制造业上市公司的营业利润率、净现值以及资产收益率有显著影响。因此，本研究的目的是提供见解，企业的收益在多大程度上受到影响实施资产减值。

Keywords :资产减值、净利润率、资产收益率
