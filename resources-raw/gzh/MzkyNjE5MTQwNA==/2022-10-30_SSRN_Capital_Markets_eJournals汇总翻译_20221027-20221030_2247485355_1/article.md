\[1\] Local Fintech Development and Stock Price Crash Risk

标题:本地金融科技发展与股价崩溃风险

作者:Xinyue Wang,Yuqiang Cao,Zhuoan Feng,Meiting Lu,Yaowen Shan

出处:-

Abstract : This study investigates the effect of financial technology (FinTech) development on stock price crash risk. We show that the development of FinTech can inhibit management from deliberately hiding bad news and alleviate information asymmetry, thereby reducing stock price crash risk. This effect is more pronounced among non-state-owned enterprises, firms with poor information environments and low-quality internal controls, and those in competitive industries and regions with high marketization. Overall, these findings suggest that the development of FinTech can mitigate the deliberate concealment of bad news by management and improve the timeliness of disclosure, leading to lower risks faced by investors.

Keywords : Local FinTech development, Stock price crash risk, China

\[2\] Regret Aversion and Stock Returns in Ccapm: Evidence from China

标题:Ccapm中的后悔厌恶与股票收益：来自中国的证据

作者:yajie wang,jiayu yang

出处:-

Abstract : we develop a Consumption Capital Asset Pricing Model with Regret Aversion (R-CCAPM) for stock returns. A representative investor determines allocation of wealth between consumption and investment by perceiving the "expected rate of return" and "regret effect" of the stock to maximize utility. Under the choice of intertemporal investment and consumption, the expected rate of return shows a downward convex shape with the change in regret aversion. This mechanism is confirmed using data from China's A-share market and different signals of the bull and bear market. Our findings improve empirical results of the CCAPM by smaller pricing error and equity premium.

\[3\] Financial Market Reactions to the Russian Invasion of Ukraine

标题:金融市场对俄罗斯入侵乌克兰的反应

作者:Christopher J. Neely

出处:-

Abstract : This article analyzes financial market reactions to the Russia-Ukraine war with a focus on the opening weeks. Markets did not completely anticipate the war, and asset price reactions strengthened from the first week—when there were hopes for a quick resolution—to the second week, when prices generally peaked and began to partially revert to prewar values. Exposure to commodity trade and trade with Russia and Ukraine determined market perceptions of the riskiness of equity and foreign exchange assets. Credit default swap prices on sovereign debt and breakeven inflation rates indicate that markets saw the war as a measurable fiscal risk even for nonbelligerents.

\[4\] Granular Betas and Risk Premium Functions

标题:粒度贝塔和风险溢价函数

作者:Tim Bollerslev,Andrew J. Patton,Rogier Quaedvlieg

出处:-

Abstract : We propose new refined measures of the local covariation between the return on an asset and a risk factor. Our proposed "granular betas" generalize the notion of up- and down-side betas to multi-factor functional measures of covariation. We then show how the resulting granular beta functions may be used in the estimation of new "risk premium functions." Implementing the proposed new methods with a large cross-section of U.S. equity returns, we find significant evidence against the traditional (non-granular) CAPM, the Fama-French three and five-factor models, and the Fama-French-Carhart model in favor of the new granular versions of these models. Our empirical results also provide new insights into where in the "factor space" the compensation for exposures to systematic risks is mostly earned.

Keywords : Cross section of expected returns, factor models, downside risk

\[5\] The Cryptocurrency Participation Puzzle

标题:加密货币参与难题

作者:Ran Duchin,David H. Solomon,Jun Tu,Xi Wang

出处:-

Abstract : We show that ongoing zero portfolio weights in cryptocurrency are surprisingly difficult to generate in a standard Bayesian portfolio theory framework. With ten years of prior data, equity market investors would need very pessimistic  priors on mean returns to justify never having bought cryptocurrency: -10.6% per month for Bitcoin, and -19.6% per month for a diversified portfolio of cryptocurrencies. Moreover, most priors that involve never purchasing cryptocurrency imply that investors should short cryptocurrency. Optimal absolute weights are generally small but non-trivial (1-5%), frequently positive, and fairly smooth despite returns being volatile. Under a wide range of priors, the certainty equivalent gains from cryptocurrency are comparable to international diversification and exceed the size anomaly. Trading costs (ambiguity aversion, storage, fees) would need to be enormous to justify non-investment, over 21% per year for Bitcoin and 39% for a diversified cryptocurrency portfolio.

Keywords : Cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, Bayesian Portfolio Theory, Portfolio Choice, Non-Participation, Beliefs, Investment Frictions

\[6\] Are Investors Aware of Relative Operational Inefficiency?

标题:投资者是否意识到相对运营效率低下？

作者:Joshua Livnat,Dan Segal,Duo Selina Pei

出处:-

Abstract : Theory suggests that relatively inefficient firms should have lower and more uncertain future cash flows, which should lead to lower current equity values and higher future equity returns. However, the literature provides contradictory evidence on the relationship between operational efficiency and future stock performance. We provide evidence that investors are not fully aware of firms’ operational inefficiencies and are negatively surprised when future negative earnings are announced, resulting in a significant negative drift in returns for these firms. Furthermore, we provide evidence that analysts do not properly incorporate information about operational inefficiency into their earnings forecasts and target prices, and do not seem to care about the issue during conference calls.

\[8\] Endogenous Offer Arrival in the Market for Lemons

标题:柠檬市场内生报价

作者:Cyrus Aghamolla,Tadashi Hashimoto

出处:-

Abstract : We allow buyers to choose the timing of offers in a dynamic adverse selection model with a single seller. Buyers have private information and can delay their one-time private offers to learn from other buyers by observing trading behavior. We find that the equilibrium exhibits maximum delay in the sense that all buyers delay their offers until the last period. In the infinite horizon limit, buyers do not make offers in finite time. Moreover, the presence of many buyers exacerbates delay, leading to a high probability of delay in trade  even under optimistic initial beliefs. The results suggest that endogenizing the timing of offer arrival in adverse selection markets critically affects equilibrium behavior.

Keywords : Dynamic adverse selection, endogenous timing, social learning, lemons market,  winner's curse, bargaining

\[9\] Derivatives WMD, Liability Driven Investment Strategies LDI, the UK Gilt Market Crisis and Pension Fund Collapse Risk

标题:衍生工具大规模违约、负债驱动投资策略本地设计院、英国金边市场危机和养老基金崩溃风险

作者:Rupert Macey-Dare

出处:-

Abstract : In his Berkshire Hathaway annual newsletter to investors c.20 years ago, Warren Buffett while discussing the Long Term Capital Management LTCM and Enron collapses, famously called derivatives: "financial weapons of mass destruction, carrying dangers that, while now latent, are potentially lethal."  Buffett’s wise and perceptive dictum and cautious approach to derivatives have been very well illustrated once again by recent events unfolding  in UK gilts government bond and related financial markets, where a total market meltdown and collapse has- for now- just been avoided.This note considers in particular how UK Chancellor Kwarteng’s now infamous mini-budget on the morning of Friday 23rd September 2022, prompted a cascade of events through UK financial markets, starting with a precipitate fall in the value of GBP v major currencies  and a rise in UK interest rate yields. This rise in yields had a dramatic effect on UK pension fund valuations both directly through a significant fall in the revaluation prices of their long dated UK treasury gilt bond portfolios and indirectly and just as significantly through at least 3 mutually-reinforcing and risk-amplifying derivatives channels. The first channel was bond “repo” transactions, where the value of the bonds that pension funds owned and could lend out in bond sale and repurchase derivative transactions fell, and so the amount of short term money that pension funds could borrow via this secure collateralized lending channel, also fell. The second channel was OTC derivatives used by pension funds under so-called LDI or Liability Driven Investment strategies, purportedly to reduce their interest rate risks, but which in this case, and arguably totally predictably, actually amplified them. The culprit transactions are likely to have been very and ultra large scale and long term interest rate swap transactions, potentially mis-sold to or mis-bought by pension funds from banks, where banks were providing fixed rate interest to pension funds and where pension funds in return were providing floating and now rising rate interest to banks. Problems then occurred when GBP interest rates spiked and the swap net present values NPVs or mark to market values MTMs suddenly became much more highly positive for the banks and correspondingly, much more highly negative for the affected pension funds.The third channel was the collateral insurance contracts, the CSAs or collateral support annexes typically incorporated into the ISDA Master Agreements between the parties to the swap transactions, to help hedge the credit risk of those transactions. These agreements would have mandated the handing over of increasingly large amounts of cash collateral from the pension funds to the banks to protect the banks, from the risk of losing their derivatives transaction gains, in the case of sudden pension fund failure. However to provide this cash collateral, affected pension funds both would have had to hand over their limited cash reserves, to sell stocks, and primarily to sell some of their gilt holdings, thus depressing gilt prices further through excess sales pressure, thereby raising yields and worsening their swap positions and collateral liabilities in a doom-loop, collapse spiral.The situation was only stabilized by a week of concerted intervention by the Bank of England, acting as a gilts market maker of last resort, buying up long dated gilts being sold into the market so as to stabilize supply and demand and hold up their prices. However this short term remedy conflicted with the Bank’s longer term anti-inflationary mandate and existing policy of slowly increasing GBP interest rates so as to squeeze inflation and inflationary expectations out of the UK economy. This duly led to Bank of England Governor Bailey publicly limiting the emergency support programme to just one week and effectively forcing the government’s hand to change policy. This in turn led after days of financial chaos to the firing of Chancellor Kwarteng, soon followed by Prime Minister Truss, and their replacement by the more fiscally prudent, competent and experienced Chancellor Hunt and Prime Minister Sunak, both more trusted by global financial markets.It is unknown how the forthcoming mandatory inclusion of UK pension funds in CCP, Collateral Central Party membership, scheduled for 2023, would have attenuated or exacerbated this chaotic situation further and how a suspension or collapse of one or more large UK pension funds under such circumstances would have propagated through the financial markets and beyond- this is clearly an important question left for financial markets systemic risk scenario analysis programmes to explore, as is the regulatory question of how better to control and limit the grave risks and dangers, that are now apparent, of derivatives transactions in pension fund markets.

Keywords : Warren Buffett, LTCM, Enron, WMD, Liability Driven Investment, LDI, Caps, Floors, Collars, Interest Rate Swap, Inflation Swap, Longevity Swap, collateral, ISDA Master Agreement, CSA, Collateral Support Agreement, Repo, Bank of England, lender of last resort, fragility, instability, meltdown, risk

\[10\] Unraveling the Dividend Puzzle: A Field Experiment

标题:解开红利难题：一项实地实验

作者:Xiaoqiao Wang,Jing Xie,Bohui Zhang,Xiaofeng Zhao

出处:-

Abstract : We conduct a field experiment to explore why firms pay dividends. We inform publicly listed firms of concerns or queries that can be resolved by paying or increasing dividends according to the predictions of four dividend theories (agency, bird-in-hand, signaling, and tax clientele theories) and examine whether firms receiving the treatments change their dividend policy. Using the agency theory treatment, we find that the treatment group of past payers (firms that paid dividends in the previous year) increase their dividends relative to the control group. In contrast, firms receiving the treatments designed based on bird-in-hand, signaling, and tax clientele theories do not experience changes in their dividend policy. The treatment effect of agency theory in past payers is more prominent for firms with weaker governance and robust to various model specifications. Our work provides causal evidence that the agency cost motive is the main determinant of a firm’s dividend policy.

Keywords : Dividend policy, Field experiment, Agency costs, Investor Relations

\[11\] Accounting Conservatism and the Agency Problems of Overvalued Equity

标题:会计保守主义与高估股权的代理问题

作者:Juan M. García Lara,Beatriz Garcia Osma,Akram Khalilov

出处:-

Abstract : We examine the role of accounting conservatism in alleviating the agency problems of overvalued equity. We predict that by imposing asymmetric timeliness of good versus bad news reporting, conservatism limits the incidence of earnings expectation games. According to Jensen (2005), these games are the main driving force behind overvaluation. Consistent with our prediction, we find that conservatism reduces the extent and duration of equity overvaluation. We document lower penalties for conservative firms when they miss earnings forecasts, and that conservatism reduces short selling. We corroborate our findings exploiting the passage of SFAS 121 as a plausible exogenous shock to conservatism.

出处:-

Abstract : We propose a dynamic measure of stock market sentiment, extending heavily the widely adopted Baker and Wurgler (2006) market sentiment index construct on the novel basis that (1) the aggregate market sentiment must cover as many views from different investors or investment activities as possible; (2) such aggregation must adapt to changes in how different sentiment indicators mutually express sentiment, i.e. their co-movements, which evolve dramatically over time; and (3) the aggregation must not be informed by future information, so that a resultant sentiment index is usable in empirical analyses without forward looking bias. \[20\] Media Abnormal Tone and Cross-Section of Stock Returns: Evidence from China

标题:媒体反常音调与股票收益率横截面：来自中国的证据

作者:Lu Yan,Yunyuan Wang,Chang-Shuai Li,Guohao Tang

出处:-

Abstract : This paper measures the media abnormal tone based on novel textual data in China and investigates its predictive power in cross-sectional stock returns. We observe that firms with high media abnormal tone generate lower future returns than those with low media abnormal tone. This negative relation is robust when controlling for common risk factors. These results are more pronounced among firms with low investment, high short-term reversal and value firms. We find that the negative premium induced by media abnormal tone is related to mispricing. Abstract : We assess whether the global decline in inflation expectations together with increased fear of recession explain the fall in nominal government bond yields and the stock price dynamics of six major economies from 1988 to 2019. Fear of recession is the economic mechanism that drives asset prices in the Campbell and Cochrane (1999) consumption habit model. We calibrate the habit model for each sample country and generate artificial time series of nominal government bond yields and price-dividend ratios from consumption and inflation data. The model-generated series tend to follow the long-run time series patterns of their counterparts in the data but temporarily deviate substantially due to country-specific reasons.
