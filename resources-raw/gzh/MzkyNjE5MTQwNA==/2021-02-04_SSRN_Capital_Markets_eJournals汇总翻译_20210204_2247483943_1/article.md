\[1\] Relation Between Open Interest and Volatility in Futures Markets

标题:期货市场未平仓利率与波动性的关系

作者:Dr. Jayesh Desai(Veer Narmad South Gujarat University (VNSGU) - B.R.C.M. College of Business Administration),Dr. Nisarg A Joshi(Nirma University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3735992

Abstract : This paper emphasizes on investigation of the relation of the open interest for the Nifty Index futures. The purpose of this paper is to examine the consequence of open interest on volatility of futures markets. An effort was made to capture the size and change in speculative behavior in futures markets by examining the role of open interest in the behavior of futures prices. The findings show that depth of the market has an effect on the futures market’s volatility but the direction of this effect depends on the type of contract.

Keywords : Future, Nifty Index, Indices, E-Garch, Volatility and Open Interest

Abstract :本文着重研究了漂亮指数期货的未平仓利率之间的关系。本文旨在探讨未平仓利率对期货市场波动性的影响。通过研究未平仓利率在期货价格行为中的作用，试图捕捉期货市场投机行为的规模和变化。研究结果表明，市场深度对期货市场的波动性有影响，但这种影响的方向取决于合约类型。

Keywords :期货，漂亮指数，指数，E-Garch，波动性和开放利率

\[2\] Does Social Media Sentiment Matter in the Pricing of U.S. Stocks?

标题:社交媒体情绪对美国股市定价有影响吗？

作者:Christian Koeppel(University of St. Gallen)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3771788

Abstract : This paper applies a recently developed social media-based sentiment proxy for the construction of a new risk factor for sentiment-augmented asset pricing models on U.S. equities. Accounting for endogeneity, autocorrelation and heteroskedasticity in a GMM framework, we ﬁnd that the inclusion of sentiment signiﬁcantly improves the performance of the ﬁve-factor model from Fama and French (2015, 2017) for di ﬀ erent industry and style portfolios like size, value, proﬁtability, investment. The sentiment risk premium provides the missing component in the behavioral asset pricing theory of Shefrin and Belotti (2008) and (partially) resolves the pricing puzzles of small extreme growth, small extreme investment stocks and small stocks that invest heavily despite low proﬁtability.

Keywords : Asset pricing, behavioral ﬁnance, ﬁnancial markets, investor sentiment, sentiment risk premium, GMM

Abstract :本文应用最近开发的基于社交媒体的情绪代理构建了一个新的风险因子，用于美国股票情绪增强资产定价模型。考虑到GMM框架中的内生性、自相关性和异方差性，我们发现，对于不同行业和风格的投资组合（如规模、价值、可投资性和投资），包含情绪显著提高了Fama和French（2015、2017）的五因素模型的性能。情绪风险溢价提供了Shefrin和Belotti（2008）行为资产定价理论中缺失的部分，并（部分）解决了小极端增长、小极端投资股票和尽管低盈利能力但仍大量投资的小股票的定价难题。

Keywords :资产定价、行为金融、金融市场、投资者情绪、情绪风险溢价、GMM

\[3\] National Culture of Secrecy and Stock Price Synchronicity: Cross-Country Evidence

标题:国家保密文化与股价同步性：跨国证据

作者:Chrysovalantis Gaganis(University of Crete - Faculty of Social Sciences - Department of Economics),George N. Leledakis(Athens University of Economics and Business - Department of Accounting and Finance),Fotios Pasiouras(GSCM-Montpellier Business School),Emmanouil G. Pyrgiotakis(University of Essex - Essex Business School)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3769565

Abstract : Stock price synchronicity has been associated with various market outcomes like the return-sentiment relations, stock liquidity, and asset pricing models. Therefore, researchers have devoted a lot of time in revealing the underlying factors that drive stock price synchronicity. Using a sample of 49 countries over the period 1990 to 2019 we find a robust association between higher cultural secretiveness and stock price synchronicity. Our results suggest that a deep-rooted country characteristic like the culture of secrecy can diminish the information environment of stock markets. The results are robust to the use of various control variables suggested in earlier studies and alternative regression techniques, including ones that address endogeneity concerns.

Keywords : Culture, Information and market efficiency, Stock price synchronicity

Abstract :股票价格的同步性与各种市场结果相关，如回报情绪关系、股票流动性和资产定价模型。因此，研究者们花费了大量的时间来揭示驱动股价同步性的内在因素。利用1990年至2019年期间49个国家的样本，我们发现较高的文化保密性和股价同步性之间存在强大的关联。我们的研究结果表明，像保密文化这样根深蒂固的国家特征会削弱股市的信息环境。结果是稳健的使用各种控制变量在早期的研究和替代回归技术，包括那些解决内生性的关注。

Keywords :文化、信息与市场效率、股价同步性
