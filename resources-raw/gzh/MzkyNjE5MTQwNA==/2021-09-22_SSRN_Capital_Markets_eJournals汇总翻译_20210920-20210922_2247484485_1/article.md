\[1\] The Role of Leveraged ETFs and Option Market Imbalances on End-of-Day Price Dynamics

标题:杠杆ETF和期权市场失衡对日终价格动态的作用

作者:Andrea Barbon,Heiner Beckmeyer,Andrea Buraschi,Mathis Moerke

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3925725

Abstract : Leveraged ETFs and market makers who are active in option markets must ad-just imbalances arising from market movements. Establishing delta-neutrality may cause either return momentum or reversal depending on the sign and size of the imbalance vis-a-vis market prevailing liquidity. We ﬁnd that a large and negative (positive) aggregated gamma imbalance, relative to the average dollar volume, gives rise to an economically and statistically signiﬁcant end-of-day momentum (rever-sal). We compare this channel to the rebalancing of leveraged ETFs and ﬁnd that the eﬀect generated by leveraged ETFs is economically larger. Consistent with the notion of temporary price pressure, the documented eﬀects quickly revert at the next day’s open. Information-based explanations are unlikely to cause the results, suggesting a non-informational channel through which leveraged ETFs and option markets aﬀect underlying stocks towards the market close.

Keywords : Intraday Momentum, Cross-Sectional Momentum, Gamma Exposure, Option Market Maker, Leveraged ETF

Abstract :活跃于期权市场的杠杆式ETF和做市商必须消除市场波动引起的失衡。建立三角洲中性可能会导致回报势头或逆转，这取决于相对于市场流动性的不平衡的迹象和规模。我们发现，相对于平均美元交易量而言，一个巨大的负（正）总伽马不平衡导致了经济上和统计上显著的日终动量（Reversal）。我们将该渠道与杠杆式ETF的再平衡进行比较，发现杠杆式ETF产生的影响在经济上更大。与临时价格压力的概念一致，记录的影响在第二天开盘时迅速恢复。基于信息的解释不太可能导致结果，这表明杠杆ETF和期权市场通过非信息渠道影响基础股票走向市场收盘。

Keywords :日内动量、横截面动量、伽马风险敞口、期权做市商、杠杆ETF

\[2\] COVID-19 Pandemic and the Dependence Structure of Global Stock Markets

标题:新冠病毒-19大流行与全球股市的依赖结构

作者:Faheem Aslam,Khurrum Mughal,Saqib Aziz,Muhammad Farooq Ahmad,Dhoha Trabelsi

出处:Applied Economics incorporating Applied Financial Economics 2021

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3927104

Abstract : In this paper, we examine the changes in the dependence structure of global stock markets amid the outbreak of COVID-19. We divide 56 stock markets into developed, emerging, and frontier markets and study their daily price data from October 15, 2019 to August 17, 2020 using the canonical vine (C-vine) copula approach. We observed significant changes in the dependence structure, the selection of the pair copula families, and the associated parameter estimates in the tree. In developed markets, during the COVID-19, the dependence of markets shifted from the Netherlands to France while the root node position of Hungary replaced the market of Poland. The frontier markets showed strong dependence signs with Mauritius before COVID-19 and Slovenia during the outbreak. Our findings are of interest to regulators and practitioners, particularly in monitoring the value at risk of portfolios and adopting appropriate strategies in light of the varying dynamics of stock markets during extreme events, such as COVID-19.

Keywords : COVID-19, vine copulas, dependence structures, stock markets

Abstract :在本文中，我们考察了新冠肺炎爆发后全球股市依赖结构的变化。我们将56个股票市场分为发达市场、新兴市场和前沿市场，并使用经典vine（C-vine）copula方法研究2019年10月15日至2020年8月17日的每日价格数据。我们观察到树中的依赖结构、成对copula家族的选择和相关参数估计的显著变化。在发达市场，在新冠肺炎期间，市场的依赖性从荷兰转移到了法国，而匈牙利的根节点位置取代了波兰的市场。在新冠疫情爆发之前，边境市场与毛里求斯和斯洛文尼亚呈现出强烈的依赖迹象。监管机构和从业者对我们的研究结果感兴趣，特别是在监测投资组合的风险价值以及根据极端事件（如新冠病毒-19）期间股票市场的变化动态采取适当的策略方面。

Keywords :新冠病毒-19，葡萄连接蛋白，依赖结构，股票市场

\[3\] The Unintended Cost of Data Breach Notification Laws: Evidence from Managerial Bad News Hoarding

标题:数据泄露通知法的意外成本：来自管理层囤积坏消息的证据

作者:Ivan Obaydin,Limin Xu,Ralf Zurbruegg

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3926962

Abstract : We investigate how a consumer protection law can exacerbate firms’ agency issues. Our analysis focuses on the staggered adoption of state-level data breach notification laws that require firms to disclose data breaches. We find that the enactment of these laws leads to a rise in stock price crash risk by incentivizing managers to stockpile negative financial news. The cross-sectional analysis also reveals that the impact is stronger where managers have either a greater incentive or ability to hoard information. Our results highlight that an improved operational disclosure regime can have unintended capital market consequences.

Keywords : data breach notification laws; cybersecurity risk; stock price crash risk; bad news hoarding; managerial incentives

Abstract :我们调查消费者保护法如何加剧公司的代理问题。我们的分析侧重于州一级数据泄露通知法的交错采用，该法律要求公司披露数据泄露。我们发现，这些法律的颁布通过激励经理人囤积负面金融消息，导致股价暴跌风险上升。横截面分析还显示，如果管理者有更大的动机或能力囤积信息，则影响更大。我们的结果表明，改进的运营披露制度可能会产生意外的资本市场后果。

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3908248

Abstract : This study reviews and summarizes the relevant findings of nearly two decades of accounting and finance research papers that focus on the links between the Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX) and the quality of reported earnings. \[6\] The Determinants of Insider Trading in the Credit Default Swap Market -A Network Perspective

标题:信用违约掉期市场内幕交易的决定因素——网络视角

作者:Nan Hu,Xingnan Xue,Peng Liang,Ling Liu

出处:Hu, Nan and Xue, Xingnan and Liang, Peng and Liu, Ling, The Determinants of Insider Trading in the Credit Default Swap Market -A Network Perspective (August 18, 2020). Available at SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3925911

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3925911

Abstract : This study investigates whether and how insider trading in the credit default swap (CDS) market is influenced by the corporate board network, formed by interlocking boards. We find strong evidence that firms with a more centralized position in the board network experience a higher degree of insider trading in the CDS market. Our results suggest that the board network facilitates private information leakage to investors, resulting in more active informed trading in the CDS market. Moreover, such an association is more pronounced for firms with negative earnings news and for firms with weak corporate governance. In addition, using the enactment of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act (Dodd-Frank Act) as a quasi-natural experiment, we document that insider trading becomes less active after the passage of the Dodd-Frank Act. Our results still hold when we run our analysis using four centrality measures separately or when controlling for the number of banking relationships.

Abstract :本研究调查了信用违约互换（CDS）市场的内幕交易是否以及如何受到由连锁董事会形成的公司董事会网络的影响。我们发现强有力的证据表明，在董事会网络中拥有更集中地位的公司在CDS市场中经历了更高程度的内幕交易。我们的结果表明，董事会网络有助于向投资者泄露私人信息，从而在CDS市场上进行更积极的知情交易。此外，这种关联对于有负面盈利消息的公司和公司治理薄弱的公司更为明显。此外，以《多德-弗兰克华尔街改革和消费者保护法案》（多德-弗兰克法案）的颁布为准自然实验，我们记录了《多德-弗兰克法案》通过后，内幕交易变得不那么活跃。当我们分别使用四个中心性指标进行分析或控制银行关系数量时，我们的结果仍然成立。

Keywords :内幕交易、董事会网络、信用违约互换（CDS）、多德-弗兰克法案

\[8\] Copycat Skills and Disclosure Costs: Evidence from Peer Companies’ Digital Footprints

标题:模仿技能和披露成本：来自同行公司数字足迹的证据

作者:Sean Cao,Kai Du,Baozhong Yang,Alan L. Zhang

出处:Journal of Accounting Research, Volume 59, Issue 4

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3925634

Abstract : We examine whether firms that imitate peer companies’ strategies (copycats) profit from such behavior and how their success may cause competitive harm to disclosing companies. We identify copycat companies by tracking the digital footprints of investment companies that view disclosures on the SEC EDGAR Web site. We find that copycat companies are able to identify profitable trades that outperform other trades disclosed by the copycatted companies by 5.5% annually. Such stock-screening skills are related to investment sophistication and research intensity. Furthermore, copycats inflict greater damage on the performance of disclosing companies when they possess superior copycat skills, when disclosed trading strategies take longer to complete, and when disclosed stock holdings are characterized by high information asymmetry.

\[9\] Economic Downturns and the Informativeness of Management Earnings Forecasts

标题:经济衰退与管理层盈利预测的信息性

作者:David A. Maslar,Matthew Serfling,Sarah Shaikh

出处:Journal of Accounting Research, Volume 59, Issue 4

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3925791

Abstract : Economic downturns create uncertainty about a firm's operations and make it disproportionately harder for outside market participants to assess the firm's prospects. We posit that in this environment, management earnings forecasts will be more informative to investors and analysts. Consistent with this prediction, we find larger stock price reactions and analyst forecast revisions to news in management forecasts during downturns. Holding the amount of news in forecasts constant, stock price reactions to management forecasts are also greater than those to analyst forecasts. We also find that relative to analyst forecasts, management forecast accuracy increases during downturns, suggesting that investors justifiably assess management forecasts as more informative. \[10\] Non-Answers During Conference Calls

标题:电话会议期间无人接听

作者:Ian D. Gow,David F. Larcker,Anastasia A. Zakolyukina

出处:Journal of Accounting Research, Volume 59, Issue 4

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3925693

Abstract : We construct a novel measure of disclosure choice by firms. Our measure is computed using linguistic analysis of conference calls to identify whether a manager's response to an analyst question is a “non-answer.” Using our measure, about 11% of analyst questions elicit non-answers from managers, a rate that is stable over time and similar across industries. A useful feature of our measure is that it enables an examination of disclosure choice within a call. Analyst questions with a negative tone, greater uncertainty, greater complexity, or requests for greater detail are more likely to trigger non-answers.
