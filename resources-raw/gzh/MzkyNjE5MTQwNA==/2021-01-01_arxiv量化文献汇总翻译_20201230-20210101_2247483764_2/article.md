\[1\] Growth, development, and structural change at the firm level

标题 : 企业层面的增长、发展和结构变化

作者 : Torsten Heinrich, Jangho Yang, Shuanping Dai , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.14503

说明 : -

Abstract : Understanding the microeconomic details of technological catch up processes offers great potential for informing both innovation economics and development policy. We study the economic transition of the PR China from an agrarian country to a high tech economy as one example for such a case. It is clear from past literature that rapidly rising productivity levels played a crucial role. However, the distribution of labor productivity in Chinese firms has not been comprehensively investigated and it remains an open question if this can be used to guide economic development. We analyze labor productivity and the dynamic change of labor productivity in firm level data for the years 1998 2013 from the Chinese Industrial Enterprise Database. We demonstrate that both variables are conveniently modeled as L evy alpha stable distributions, provide parameter estimates and analyze dynamic changes to this distribution. We find that the productivity gains were not due to super star firms, but due to a systematic shift of the entire distribution with otherwise mostly unchanged characteristics. We also found an emerging right skew in the distribution of labor productivity change. While there are significant differences between the 31 provinces and autonomous regions of the P.R. China, we also show that there are systematic relations between micro level and province level variables. We conclude with some implications of these findings for development policy.

摘要 : 了解技术追赶过程的微观经济细节为创新经济学和发展政策提供了巨大的信息潜力。本文以中国从农业国向高科技经济体的经济转型为例进行研究。从过去的文献中可以清楚地看出，生产力水平的迅速提高起到了至关重要的作用。然而，我国企业劳动生产率的分布还没有得到全面的研究，能否用劳动生产率来指导经济发展仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。我们分析了中国工业企业数据库1998-2013年企业层面数据中的劳动生产率和劳动生产率的动态变化。我们证明了这两个变量可以方便地建模为L evyα稳定分布，提供了参数估计并分析了这个分布的动态变化。我们发现，生产率的提高并不是由于超级明星公司，而是由于整个分布系统的变化，否则大部分特征不变。我们还发现劳动生产率变化的分布出现了右偏。我国31个省、自治区之间虽然存在显著差异，但微观层面与省域层面变量之间也存在系统性关系。最后，我们总结了这些发现对发展政策的一些启示。

\[2\] Self sustained price bubbles driven by Bitcoin innovations and adaptive behavior

标题 : 比特币创新和适应性行为驱动的自我维持价格泡沫

作者 : Misha Perepelitsa, Ilya Timofeyev , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.14860

说明 : -

Abstract : We show that infinite divisibility of a trading commodity leads to a self sustained price bubble when traders use adaptive investment strategies. The adaptive strategy can be viewed as a psychological response of a trader to the situation when the trader s estimation of future prices does not match the actual, realized price. We use a multi agent model to illustrate the price bubble formation and to quantify its main statistical properties such as the return, the volatility, and the systematic risk of the price bubble to crash. We discuss the plausibility for bubbles to drive prices of digital currencies.

摘要 : 我们证明了当交易者使用适应性投资策略时，交易商品的无限可分性会导致一个自我维持的价格泡沫。适应性策略可以看作是交易者对未来价格的估计与实际实现价格不符时的心理反应。我们使用多主体模型来说明价格泡沫的形成，并量化其主要统计特性，如回报率、波动率和价格泡沫崩溃的系统风险。我们讨论了泡沫推动数字货币价格的可能性。

\[3\] The wealth of nations and the health of populations

标题 : 国家的财富和人民的健康

作者 : Adel Daoud , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.14941

说明 : -

Abstract : The wealth of nations and the health of populations are intimately strongly associated, yet the extent to which economic prosperity (GDP per capita) causes improved health remains disputed. The purpose of this article is to analyze the impact of sovereign debt crises (SDC) on child mortality, using a sample of 57 low and middle income countries surveyed by the Demographic and Health Survey between the years 1990 and 2015. These surveys supply 229 household data and containing about 3 million childbirth history records. This focus on SDC instead of GDP provides a quasi experimental moment in which the influence of unobserved confounding is less than a moment analyzing the normal fluctuations of GDP. This study measures child mortality at six thresholds neonatal, under one (infant), under two, under three, under four, and under five mortality. Using a machine learning (ML) model for causal inference, this study finds that while an SDC causes an adverse yet statistically insignificant effect on neonatal mortality, all other child mortality group samples are adversely affected between a probability of 0.12 to 0.14 (all statistically significant at the 95 percent threshold). Through this ML, this study also finds that the most important treatment heterogeneity moderator, in the entire adjustment set, is whether a child is born in a low income country.

摘要 : 国家的财富和人民的健康密切相关，但经济繁荣（人均国内生产总值）在多大程度上改善了健康状况仍有争议。本文的目的是分析主权债务危机（SDC）对儿童死亡率的影响，以1990年至2015年期间人口与健康调查所调查的57个中低收入国家为样本。这些调查提供了229个家庭数据，包括大约300万个分娩史记录。这种对SDC而不是GDP的关注提供了一个准实验时刻，在这个时刻中，未观察到的混淆的影响小于分析GDP正常波动的时刻。这项研究测量了六个阈值下的儿童死亡率新生儿、1岁以下（婴儿）、2岁以下、3岁以下、4岁以下和5岁以下的死亡率。使用机器学习（ML）模型进行因果推断，本研究发现，虽然SDC对新生儿死亡率产生不利影响，但在统计学上不显著，但所有其他儿童死亡率组样本受到不利影响的概率在0.12到0.14之间（在95%阈值下均具有统计学显著性）。通过这项研究，本研究还发现，在整个调整集中，最重要的治疗异质性调节因素是孩子是否出生在低收入国家。

\[4\] The Impact of Corona Populism

标题 : 民粹主义的影响

作者 : Patrick Mellacher , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.14962

说明 : -

Abstract : I study the impact of corona populism politics aimed at denying or downplaying the danger posed by COVID 19 for strategic reasons on the evolution of the pandemic using regional data from Austria. The right wing FPOE first vocalized strong support for strict lockdown measures, but made a corona populist turn at the end of the first wave of infections. Using regression analysis, I show that the vote share of the FPOE at the last national parliamentary elections is a strong predictor for the number of COVID 19 deaths after the FPOE switched their policy stance, while there is no or even a negative correlation before the policy switch. These results are robust under simple as well as sophisticated specifications of the model controlling for demographic and socioeconomic conditions. Interestingly, I do not find a statistically significant correlation between the FPOE vote share and the reported number of infections. I hypothesize that this can be traced back to a self selection bias in testing. To explore this hypothesis, I extend the classical SIRD model to incorporate conditional quarantine and two groups of agents the majority and the corona sceptics, where the latter are less inclined to get tested and engage in social distancing. Such a model can explain the nontrivial empirics if mixing is sufficiently homophilic, an increase in the share of corona sceptics can cause an increase in the number of deaths without increasing the number of reported infections. I finally discuss the implications for both groups.

摘要 : 我研究了科罗纳民粹主义政治的影响，旨在否认或轻描淡写的危险所构成的冠状病毒19号的战略原因对演变的流行病使用区域数据从奥地利。右翼的FPOE首先表示强烈支持严格的封锁措施，但在第一波感染结束时，却出现了电晕民粹主义的转变。通过回归分析，我发现在上次全国议会选举中，FPOE的得票率是FPOE转变政策立场后COVID 19死亡人数的一个强有力的预测因子，而在政策转变前没有甚至是负相关。这些结果是稳健的简单和复杂的规范下的模型控制人口和社会经济条件。有趣的是，我没有发现FPOE投票份额和报告的感染人数之间有统计上的显著相关性。我假设这可以追溯到测试中的自我选择偏差。为了探索这一假设，我扩展了经典的SIRD模型，将条件隔离和两组代理（多数代理和corona怀疑者）结合起来，后者不太倾向于接受测试和进行社会疏远。这样一个模型可以解释非平凡的经验，如果混合是足够的同质化，增加电晕怀疑者的比例可以导致死亡人数的增加而不增加报告的感染人数。我最后讨论了对这两个群体的影响。

\[8\] Testing the effectiveness of unconventional monetary policy in Japan and the United States

标题 : 检验日本和美国非常规货币政策的有效性

作者 : Daisuke Ikeda, Shangshang Li, Sophocles Mavroeidis, Francesco Zanetti , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.15158

说明 : -

Abstract : The effective lower bound on a short term interest rate may not constrain a central bank s capacity to achieve its objectives if unconventional monetary policy (UMP) is powerful enough. We formalize this irrelevance hypothesis using a dynamic stochastic general equilibrium model with UMP and test it empirically for the United States and Japan using a structural vector autoregressive model that includes variables subject to occasionally binding constraints. The hypothesis is strongly rejected for both countries. However, a comparison of the impulse responses to a monetary policy shock across regimes shows that UMP has had strong delayed effects in each country.

摘要 : 如果非常规货币政策（UMP）足够强大，短期利率的有效下限可能不会限制央行实现目标的能力。我们用一个带有UMP的动态随机一般均衡模型形式化了这个不相关假设，并用一个结构向量自回归模型对美国和日本进行了实证检验，该模型包含了偶尔受到约束的变量。这两个国家都强烈反对这一假设。然而，通过比较不同制度下货币政策冲击的脉冲响应，可以看出货币政策联盟在各国都有很强的延迟效应。

\[9\] Sequential Deep Learning for Credit Risk Monitoring with Tabular Financial Data

标题 : 基于表格式金融数据的信用风险监测序贯深度学习

作者 : Jillian M. Clements, Di Xu, Nooshin Yousefi, Dmitry Efimov , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.15330

说明 : -

Abstract : Machine learning plays an essential role in preventing financial losses in the banking industry. Perhaps the most pertinent prediction task that can result in billions of dollars in losses each year is the assessment of credit risk (i.e., the risk of default on debt). Today, much of the gains from machine learning to predict credit risk are driven by gradient boosted decision tree models. However, these gains begin to plateau without the addition of expensive new data sources or highly engineered features. In this paper, we present our attempts to create a novel approach to assessing credit risk using deep learning that does not rely on new model inputs. We propose a new credit card transaction sampling technique to use with deep recurrent and causal convolution based neural networks that exploits long historical sequences of financial data without costly resource requirements. We show that our sequential deep learning approach using a temporal convolutional network outperformed the benchmark non sequential tree based model, achieving significant financial savings and earlier detection of credit risk. We also demonstrate the potential for our approach to be used in a production environment, where our sampling technique allows for sequences to be stored efficiently in memory and used for fast online learning and inference.

摘要 : 机器学习在防止银行业的财务损失方面起着至关重要的作用。也许每年可能造成数十亿美元损失的最相关的预测任务是评估信用风险（即债务违约风险）。如今，机器学习预测信用风险的大部分收益都是由梯度增强的决策树模型驱动的。然而，如果不添加昂贵的新数据源或高度工程化的特性，这些收益就开始趋于平稳。在本文中，我们尝试创建一种新的方法来评估信用风险使用深度学习，不依赖于新的模型输入。我们提出了一种新的信用卡交易抽样技术，用于基于深度递归和因果卷积的神经网络，该网络利用金融数据的长历史序列，而无需昂贵的资源需求。结果表明，我们使用时间卷积网络的序贯深度学习方法优于基于非序贯树的基准模型，实现了显著的财务节约和早期的信用风险检测。我们还展示了我们的方法在生产环境中使用的潜力，在生产环境中，我们的采样技术允许序列有效地存储在内存中，并用于快速在线学习和推理。

Abstract : The transformation of the electricity sector is a main element of the transition to a decarbonized economy. Conventional generators powered by fossil fuels have to be replaced by variable renewable energy (VRE) sources in combination with electricity storage and other options for providing temporal flexibility. We discuss the market dynamics of increasing VRE penetration and their integration in the electricity system. \[12\] Life insurance policies with cash flows subject to random interest rate changes

标题 : 具有随机利率变动现金流的寿险保单

作者 : David R. Baños , 类别 ：Risk Management (q-fin.RM)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.15541

说明 : Comments: 23 pages, 8 figures

Abstract : The main purpose of this work is to derive a partial differential equation for the reserves of life insurance liabilities subject to stochastic interest rates where the benefits and premiums depend directly on changes in the interest rate curve. In particular, we allow the payment streams to depend on the performance of an overnight technical interest rate, making them stochastic as well.
