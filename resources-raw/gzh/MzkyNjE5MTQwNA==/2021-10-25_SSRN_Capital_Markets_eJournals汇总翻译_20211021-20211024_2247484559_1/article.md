\[1\] Impact of Awareness of the Financing Choices on Firm Profitability (A Study on Banking Sector of Pakistan)

标题:融资选择意识对企业盈利能力的影响（巴基斯坦银行业研究）

作者:Rauf Kashif,Shumaila Bibi,Muhammad Imran Farooq,Anum Shafique,Zaib un Nissa

出处:INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL OF SCIENTIFIC & TECHNOLOGY RESEARCH VOLUME 10, ISSUE 03, MARCH 2021

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3921610

Abstract : Since the development of the Pakistan stock market on 10 March 1949, corporations have been able to substitute equity for debt more freely. This research was conducted to know the effect of the debt and equity mix, as measured by financial choices, on a firm’s financial performance. It focuses on profitability, specifically in the Pakistan capital market. This study was developed to extend understandings in the literature that how financial choices operate in a stressed based financial system, and how it may affect financial performance. This research examined 15 publicly trading banks listed in the Pakistan stock market between 2015 and 2020. This study is centered on the data about firm financial choices that have been composed of the banking sector. We involved all firms of the banking sector, predominantly 15 banks listed at Pakistan stock exchange (PSX), in our analysis for the period of five years (2015-2020). This research explores that the Pakistani banks do have the possibility for the increment of their profitability while managing their behaviors about debt and equity.

Keywords : Financing Awareness, Banking Sector, Firm Profitability, Pakistan

Abstract :自1949年3月10日巴基斯坦股票市场发展以来，公司能够更自由地以股权代替债务。本研究旨在了解债务和股权组合（通过财务选择衡量）对公司财务绩效的影响。它侧重于盈利能力，特别是在巴基斯坦资本市场。本研究旨在扩展文献中的理解，即金融选择如何在基于压力的金融系统中运作，以及它如何影响财务绩效。本研究调查了2015年至2020年间在巴基斯坦股市上市的15家公开交易银行。本研究以银行业企业财务选择数据为中心。我们在五年（2015-2020年）的分析中涉及了银行业的所有公司，主要是在巴基斯坦证券交易所（PSX）上市的15家银行。本研究探讨了巴基斯坦银行在管理其债务和股权行为的同时，确实有可能增加其盈利能力。

Keywords :融资意识、银行业、公司盈利能力、巴基斯坦

\[2\] Dependence Structure between Stock Returns of Developing and Developed Countries

标题:发展中国家与发达国家股票收益率的依赖结构

作者:Anum Shafique,Madia Javed,Kamran Iftikhar,Muhammad Usama Ishaque,Zulqarnain Masood

出处:International Journal of Management (IJM)Volume 12, Issue 6, June 2021, pp. 57-74, Article ID: IJM\_12\_06\_005Available online at https://iaeme.com/Home/issue/IJM?Volume=12&Issue=6ISSN Print: 0976-6502 and ISSN Online: 0976-6510DOI: 10.34218/IJM.12.6.2021.005

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3921596

Abstract : This study aims to examine the dependence pattern of stock returns prevailing in developing and developed countries. To check the dependence pattern, this study choose sample dataset of stock returns from time span of 2009 to 2019 from stock exchange institutes of Pakistan, Indonesia, Germany and United Kingdom. On the basis of gross domestic product, imports and exports, Pakistan and Indonesia are choosen as developing countries while United Kingdom and Germany as developed countries. In this study, descriptive statistics, copulas approach, spearman’s correlation, kendall’s tau and akaike information criterion (AIC) are the techniques used for testing the hypothesis. Findings show that tail dependence is identical between stock returns of developing and developed countries with the movement in same directions whether increasing or decreasing. Furthermore, results are showing that t-student copula is best-fitted for measuring the dependence pattern among stock institutions of developing and developed countries. This study proves helpful for investors, policy makers and financial representatives for risk management.

Keywords : Stock returns, dependence pattern, Pakistan, Germany, Indonesia, United Kingdom

Abstract :本研究旨在考察发展中国家和发达国家普遍存在的股票收益依赖模式。为了检验相关性模式，本研究选取了巴基斯坦、印度尼西亚、德国和英国的证券交易所研究所2009年至2019年的股票收益率样本数据集。根据国内生产总值和进出口，巴基斯坦和印度尼西亚被选为发展中国家，而英国和德国被选为发达国家。在本研究中，描述性统计、copulas方法、spearman相关、kendall的tau和akaike信息标准（AIC）是检验假设的技术。研究结果表明，发展中国家和发达国家的股票收益率之间的尾部依赖性是相同的，无论是增加还是减少，其变动方向都是相同的。此外，研究结果表明，t-student copula最适合于衡量发展中国家和发达国家股票制度之间的依赖模式。本研究为投资者、决策者和财务代表进行风险管理提供了有益的参考。

Keywords :股票回报，依赖模式，巴基斯坦，德国，印度尼西亚，英国

\[3\] Does Election Impact on Stock Market Returns in Bangladesh?

标题:选举对孟加拉国股市回报率有影响吗？

作者:Gourav Roy,Md Atiqullah Khan

出处:Roy, G., & Khan, M. A. (2021). DOES ELECTION IMPACT ON STOCK MARKET RETURNS IN BANGLADESH?. Asian Finance & Banking Review, 5(1), 1-18. https://doi.org/10.46281/asfbr.v5i1.1256

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3898536

Abstract : This paper investigates the impact of parliamentary general election on the stock market returns by considering the previous fifteen days and the after fifteen days of each of six elections in Bangladesh held between 1991 and 2018. The study analyzed the election effect on stock returns through considering both abnormal returns by choosing 20 stocks as a proxy of portfolio motive of the investors and the broad index returns as a measurement of whole market scenario. The study employed descriptive statistics, t-tests, and F-tests to understand the impact of election by gauging the changes in return series. Descriptive statistics showed very high differences in means, standard deviations, and volatilities. Paired t-tests showed significant differences between the means and F-tests showed significant differences between the variances of the returns during before and after days of these elections. The results were the same for abnormal returns and broad index returns. The impacts of individual election on the returns were also found as the same in most cases. The study has found some very useful insights part of which can benefit the policymakers to reform the policies. The common investors and the financial market participants can also make better investment plan.

Keywords : Election, Event study, Stock returns, DSE, Bangladesh

Abstract :本文通过考虑1991年至2018年期间孟加拉国举行的六次选举的前十五天和后十五天，研究了议会大选对股市回报的影响。本研究通过选择20只股票作为投资者投资组合动机的代表，同时考虑异常收益和作为整个市场情景衡量的广义指数收益，分析了选举对股票收益的影响。该研究采用描述性统计、t检验和F检验，通过衡量回报率序列的变化来了解选举的影响。描述性统计显示，均值、标准差和波动性存在很大差异。配对t检验显示平均数之间存在显著差异，F检验显示在这些选举之前和之后的几天内，回报率的方差之间存在显著差异。异常收益率和广义指数收益率的结果相同。在大多数情况下，个人选举对选举结果的影响也相同。这项研究发现了一些非常有用的见解，其中一部分可以帮助决策者改革政策。普通投资者和金融市场参与者也可以制定更好的投资计划。

Keywords :选举、事件研究、股票回报、DSE、孟加拉国

\[4\] Square It Off: Trading Strategy

标题:平衡：交易策略

作者:Dr Preet Deep Singh

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3920557

Abstract : In this paper we propose a strategy to short stocks intraday and beat the market over a period of time. Our strategy for BSE Sensex gives returns of more than 40,000 absolute points while the market climbed less than 25,000 points in the same period. We use 20 years data from 1995 to 2014. We expand our sample to multiple indices across the globe. There is a similar trend on most of the other indices. We slice the data on the basis of trend to provide granularity to our results. We also provide a survey of literature on trading strategies, trading during non-trading hours and overnight information content.

Keywords : Trading Strategy, Stocks, Profit, Trends

Abstract :在本文中，我们提出了一个策略，以短期内的股票和击败市场在一段时间内。我们对BSE Sensex的策略给出了超过40000个绝对点的回报，而同期市场上涨不到25000个点。我们使用1995年至2014年的20年数据。我们将样本扩展到全球多个指数。其他大多数指数也有类似的趋势。我们根据趋势对数据进行切片，以提供结果的粒度。我们还提供了有关交易策略、非交易时间交易和隔夜信息内容的文献调查。

Keywords :交易策略、股票、利润、趋势

\[5\] The Demand-Driven Information Market

标题:需求驱动的信息市场

作者:Shiyang Huang,Dan Li,Bohui Zhang

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3920444

Abstract : We hypothesize a demand-driven information market where information production is tailored by investors’ investment constraints. Using a comprehensive data set of news releases and institutional equity holdings during the 2000–2016 period, we show that more negative (positive) news are produced for stocks overweighed (underweighted) by institutions. A natural experiment based on the 2003 mutual funds scandal confirms the negative relation between institutional investment constraints and news sentiment. The effect is more pronounced when the cost of information production is higher, especially when the distance between the information producer and a firm headquarter is larger. The asymmetry in information production causes stock returns to display negative skewness, increasing the probability for overweighed stocks to experience large negative price movement in the future.

Keywords : News; Institutional investors; Investment constraints; Market efficiency; Skewness

Abstract :我们假设一个需求驱动的信息市场，其中信息生产是由投资者的投资约束定制的。通过使用2000-2016年期间新闻发布和机构股权持有的综合数据集，我们发现机构增持（减持）股票会产生更多负面（正面）消息。一项基于2003年共同基金丑闻的自然实验证实了机构投资约束与新闻情绪之间的负相关关系。当信息生产成本较高时，尤其是当信息生产者与企业总部之间的距离较大时，这种影响更为显著。信息生产的不对称导致股票收益呈现负偏斜，增加了权重过高的股票在未来经历大幅负价格波动的可能性。

Keywords :消息机构投资者；投资限制；市场效率；偏斜

\[6\] Flow-Driven ESG Returns

标题:流驱动ESG返回

作者:Philippe van der Beck

出处:-

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3929359

Abstract : I show that the performance of ESG investments is strongly driven by price-pressure arising from flows towards sustainable funds, causing high realized returns that do not reflect high expected returns. The coefficient linking ESG flows and realized returns is the product of two factors: The deviation of green funds' portfolios from the market portfolio and a flow multiplier matrix that is the inverse of the market's demand elasticity of substitution between stocks. Empirically, withdrawing 1 dollar from the market portfolio and investing it in the representative ESG fund increases the aggregate value of high ESG-taste stocks by 2-2.5 dollars. Under the absence of flow-driven price pressure, the aggregate ESG industry would have strongly underperformed the market from 2016 to 2021. Furthermore, the positive alpha of a long-short ESG taste portfolio becomes significantly negative.

Keywords : sustainable investing, ESG, price pressure, flows, demand elasticity

Abstract :我表明，ESG投资的表现受到流向可持续基金的价格压力的强烈推动，导致实现的高回报率并不反映高预期回报率。将ESG资金流与已实现收益联系起来的系数是两个因素的乘积：绿色基金投资组合与市场投资组合的偏差，以及一个流量乘数矩阵，该矩阵是股票之间替代的市场需求弹性的倒数。从经验上看，从市场投资组合中提取1美元并将其投资于代表性ESG基金会增加高ESG品味股票的总价值2-2.5美元。在没有流量驱动的价格压力的情况下，从2016年到2021年，ESG行业的总体表现将远远落后于市场。此外，长短ESG口味组合的正阿尔法显著变为负。

Keywords :可持续投资、ESG、价格压力、流量、需求弹性

\[7\] Evaluation the Performances of Portfolios Created with TOPSIS and Grey Relational Analysis Methods during COVID-19 Pandemic

标题:用TOPSIS和灰色关联分析方法创建的投资组合在新冠疫情期间的绩效评估

作者:Bilgehan Tekin,Burak Keskin

出处:Tekin, B. & Keskin, B. (2021). TOPSIS VE GRİ İLİŞKİSEL ANALİZ YÖNTEMLERİ İLE OLUŞTURULAN PORTFÖYLERİN PERFORMANSLARININ PANDEMİ SÜRECİNDE DEĞERLENDİRİLMESİ . Çankırı Karatekin Üniversitesi İktisadi ve İdari Bilimler Fakültesi Dergisi , 11 (1) , 377-407 . DOI: 10.18074/ckuiibfd.889178

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3945925

Abstract : In this study, portfolio diversification was tried to be made based on stock market performance ratios with TOPSIS and Grey Relational Analysis (GRA) methods using quarterly data of companies operating in Borsa Istanbul Banks (XBANK), Wholesale & Retail Trade (XTCRT) and Textile & Leather (XTEKS) between 2015-2019. In the next stage, the performances of the created portfolios during the pandemic period were tried to be determined by comparing various indicators. As result, it was seen that the portfolio formed by the companies with the lowest performance in terms of stock market performance ratios reflects the highest average percentage change in positive sense. It has been determined that the return of the created portfolios is higher than the yield of BIST100 (XU100), BIST Banks XBANK, Gram Gold and US Dollar alternatives. This situation can be interpreted as TOPSIS, and GRA methods can be used as an alternative method in creating profitable portfolios.

Keywords : None

Abstract :在本研究中，利用2015-2019年期间在Borsa伊斯坦布尔银行（XBANK）、批发和零售贸易（XTCRT）和纺织和皮革（XTEKS）运营的公司的季度数据，利用TOPSIS和灰色关联分析（GRA）方法，尝试基于股市绩效比率进行投资组合多元化。在下一阶段，通过比较各种指标，试图确定大流行期间创建的投资组合的绩效。因此，可以看出，股票市场绩效比率最低的公司形成的投资组合反映了正意义上的最高平均百分比变化。已确定，所创建投资组合的回报率高于BIST 100（XU100）、BIST银行XBANK、Gram Gold和美元替代品的收益率。这种情况可以解释为TOPSIS，GRA方法可以作为创建盈利投资组合的替代方法。

Keywords :None
