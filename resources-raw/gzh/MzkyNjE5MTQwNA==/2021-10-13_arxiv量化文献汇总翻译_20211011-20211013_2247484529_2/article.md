\[1\] Towards Robust Representation of Limit Orders Books for Deep Learning Models

标题 : 面向深度学习模型的极限订单鲁棒表示

作者 : Yufei Wu, Mahmoud Mahfouz, Daniele Magazzeni, Manuela Veloso , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.05479

说明 : Comments: arXiv admin note: substantial text overlap with arXiv:2110.04752

Abstract : The success of machine learning models is highly reliant on the quality and robustness of representations. The lack of attention on the robustness of representations may boost risks when using data driven machine learning models for trading in the financial markets. In this paper, we focus on representations of the limit order book (LOB) data and discuss the opportunities and challenges of representing such data in an effective and robust manner. We analyse the issues associated with the commonly used LOB representation for machine learning models from both theoretical and experimental perspectives. Based on this, we propose new LOB representation schemes to improve the performance and robustness of machine learning models and present a guideline for future research in this area.

摘要 : 机器学习模型的成功高度依赖于表示的质量和鲁棒性。在使用数据驱动的机器学习模型进行金融市场交易时，对表示的健壮性缺乏关注可能会增加风险。在本文中，我们重点讨论了限额订单簿（LOB）数据的表示，并讨论了以有效和稳健的方式表示此类数据的机会和挑战。我们从理论和实验两个角度分析了机器学习模型中常用LOB表示的相关问题。基于此，我们提出了新的LOB表示方案，以提高机器学习模型的性能和鲁棒性，并为该领域的未来研究提供了指导。

\[2\] Indian urban workers labour market transitions

标题 : 印度城市工人劳动力市场转型

作者 : Jyotirmoy Bhattacharya , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.05482

说明 : Comments: 25 pages, 6 figures

Abstract : This paper constructs an individual level panel dataset of urban Indian workers for the period 2017 18 to 2019 20 using all the available micro data from the Periodic Labour Force Survey. To do so I propose and validate a matching procedure that overcomes the inconsistency in block identifiers in the 2017 18 and 2018 19 data releases of the PLFS. I use the constructed data set to document a number of stylised facts about gross worker flows and to estimate the effects of different individual characteristics on probabilities of job gain and loss. I document gross flows much larger than net flows and large differences in labour market conditions between men and women, between different industries, and between the different states of India.

摘要 : 本文利用定期劳动力调查的所有可用微观数据，构建了2017年18月至2019年20年间印度城市工人的个人层面面板数据集。为此，我提出并验证了一个匹配程序，该程序克服了PLFS 2017 18和2018 19数据发布中块标识符的不一致性。我使用构建的数据集记录了大量关于总劳动力流动的典型事实，并估计了不同个人特征对就业机会增加和减少概率的影响。我记录了总流动远远大于净流动，以及男女之间、不同行业之间以及印度不同州之间劳动力市场状况的巨大差异。

\[3\] Online Segregation

标题 : 在线隔离

作者 : John Lynham, Philip R Neary , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.05608

说明 : -

Abstract : A large number of agents from two groups prefer to interact with their own types online and also have preferences over two online platforms. We find that an online platform can be tipped from integrated to segregated without any change in the ratio of the two groups interacting on the platform. Instead, segregation can be triggered by changes in the absolute numbers of both groups, holding the Schelling ratio fixed. In extreme cases, the flight of one group from a platform can be triggered by a change in the group ratio in favor of the group that ends up leaving.

摘要 : 来自两个群体的大量代理更喜欢与他们自己的类型进行在线交互，并且对两个在线平台也有偏好。我们发现，在线平台可以从集成到分离，而不改变平台上交互的两个组的比率。相反，分离可以由两组的绝对数的变化触发，保持谢林比率不变。在极端情况下，一个团队从平台上的飞行可能会因团队比例的变化而触发，该变化有利于最终离开的团队。

\[4\] The Effects of Temperature on Economic Preferences

标题 : 温度对经济偏好的影响

作者 : Michelle Escobar Carias, David Johnston, Rachel Knott, Rohan Sweeney , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.05611

说明 : Comments: 50 pages, 5 figures, 5 Appendix Figures

Abstract : The empirical evidence suggests that key accumulation decisions and risky choices associated with economic development depend, at least in part, on economic preferences such as willingness to take risk and patience. This paper studies whether temperature could be one of the potential channels that influences such economic preferences. Using data from the Indonesia Family Life Survey and NASAs Modern Era Retrospective Analysis for Research and Applications data we exploit quasi exogenous variations in outdoor temperatures caused by the random allocation of survey dates. This approach allows us to estimate the effects of temperature on elicited measures of risk aversion, rational choice violations, and impatience. We then explore three possible mechanisms behind this relationship, cognition, sleep, and mood. Our findings show that higher temperatures lead to significantly increased rational choice violations and impatience, but do not significantly increase risk aversion. These effects are mainly driven by night time temperatures on the day prior to the survey and less so by temperatures on the day of the survey. This impact is quasi linear and increasing when midnight outdoor temperatures are above 22C. The evidence shows that night time temperatures significantly deplete cognitive functioning, mathematical skills in particular. Based on these findings we posit that heat induced night time disturbances cause stress on critical parts of the brain, which then manifest in significantly lower cognitive functions that are critical for individuals to perform economically rational decision making.

摘要 : 经验证据表明，与经济发展相关的关键积累决策和风险选择至少部分取决于经济偏好，如冒险意愿和耐心。本文研究了温度是否可能是影响这种经济偏好的潜在渠道之一。利用印度尼西亚家庭生活调查数据和NASAs现代回顾性分析研究和应用数据，我们利用调查日期随机分配导致的室外温度准外生变化。这种方法使我们能够估计温度对风险规避、违反理性选择和不耐烦的诱导措施的影响。然后，我们探讨了这种关系背后的三种可能机制：认知、睡眠和情绪。我们的研究结果表明，较高的温度会显著增加违反理性选择的行为和不耐烦，但不会显著增加风险厌恶。这些影响主要受调查前一天夜间温度的影响，而受调查当天温度的影响较小。这种影响是准线性的，并且在午夜室外温度高于22℃时会增加。有证据表明，夜间温度会显著削弱认知功能，尤其是数学技能。基于这些发现，我们假设夜间热干扰会对大脑的关键部位造成压力，然后表现为认知功能显著降低，这对个人进行经济理性决策至关重要。

\[5\] Inferring supply networks from mobile phone data to estimate the resilience of a national economy

标题 : 从手机数据推断供应网络以估计国民经济的弹性

作者 : Tobias Reisch, Georg Heiler, Christian Diem, Stefan Thurner , 类别 ：General Economics (econ.GN)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.05625

说明 : Comments: 19 pages, 11 figures

Abstract : National economies rest on networks of millions of customer supplier relations. Some companies in the case of their default can trigger significant cascades of shock in the supply chain network and are thus systemically risky. Up to now, systemic risk of individual companies was practically not quantifiable, due to the unavailability of firm level transaction data. So far, economic shocks are typically studied in the framework of input output analysis on the industry level that can t relate risk to individual firms. Exact firm level supply networks based on tax or payment data exist only for very few countries. Here we explore to what extent telecommunication data can be used as an inexpensive, easily available, and real time alternative to reconstruct national supply networks on the firm level. We find that the conditional probability of correctly identifying a true customer supplier link given a communication link exists is about 90 . This quality level allows us to reliably estimate a systemic risk profile of an entire country that serves as a proxy for the resilience of its economy. In particular, we are able to identify the high systemic risk companies. We find that 65 firms have the potential to trigger large cascades of disruption in production chains that could cause severe damages in the economy. We verify that the topological features of the inter firm communication network are highly similar to national production networks with exact firm level interactions.

摘要 : 国家经济依赖于数以百万计的客户-供应商关系网络。一些公司在违约的情况下可能会在供应链网络中引发严重的连锁冲击，因此具有系统性风险。到目前为止，由于无法获得公司层面的交易数据，单个公司的系统性风险实际上无法量化。到目前为止，经济冲击通常是在行业层面的投入产出分析框架内研究的，不能将风险与单个企业联系起来。基于税收或支付数据的精确企业级供应网络只存在于极少数国家。在这里，我们探讨了电信数据在多大程度上可以作为一种廉价、易获得和实时的替代方案，用于在企业层面重建国家供应网络。我们发现，如果存在通信链路，正确识别真实客户-供应商链路的条件概率约为90。这一质量水平使我们能够可靠地估计整个国家的系统性风险状况，作为其经济恢复力的代表。特别是，我们能够识别系统性风险较高的公司。我们发现，65家企业有可能引发生产链的大规模连锁中断，从而对经济造成严重损害。我们验证了企业间通信网络的拓扑特征与具有精确企业级交互的国家生产网络高度相似。

Abstract : Topline hotels are now shifting into the digital way in how they understand their customers to maintain and ensuring satisfaction. Rather than the conventional way which uses written reviews or interviews, the hotel is now heavily investing in Artificial Intelligence particularly Machine Learning solutions. Analysis of online customer reviews changes the way companies make decisions in a more effective way than using conventional analysis. The purpose of this research is to measure hotel service quality. \[8\] Exploring the Endogenous Nature of Meme Stocks Using the Log Periodic Power Law Model and Confidence Indicator

标题 : 利用对数周期幂律模型和置信度指标探讨模因股票的内生性

作者 : Hideyuki Takagi , 类别 ：Applications (stat.AP)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.06190

说明 : Comments: 9 pages, 6 figures

Abstract : This study examined the endogenous nature of negative bubbles forming in meme stocks with the Log Periodic Power Law (LPPL) Confidence Indicator (CI). A meme stock is a stock that has gained a significant amount of attention on a large social media platform such as Yahoo! or Reddit. This study examined four meme stocks including Tesla, Inc. (TSLA), GameStop Corp. (GME), Koss Corporation (KOSS), and AMC Entertainment Holdings Inc (AMC). The CI was able to detect numerous bubbles forming in meme stocks, but had difficulty in significantly predicting social media induced exogenous rallies. This may have been due to price movements affected by external causes such as short squeezes. This study outlines the real unpredictability of many black swan events, and further studies could be done examining exogenous bubbles.

摘要 : 本研究用对数周期幂律（LPPL）置信指数（CI）检验了模因股票中形成负泡沫的内生性质。meme股票是在大型社交媒体平台（如雅虎）上获得大量关注的股票！或者Reddit。这项研究考察了四种meme股票，包括特斯拉公司（TSLA）、GameStop公司（GME）、Koss公司（Koss）和AMC娱乐控股公司（AMC）。CI能够检测到模因股票中形成的大量泡沫，但很难显著预测社交媒体引发的外生反弹。这可能是由于受到短期挤压等外部原因影响的价格波动。然而，该模型确实为之前泡沫的形成提供了证据，而泡沫可能是meme股票反弹的催化剂。这项研究概述了许多黑天鹅事件的真实不可预测性，可以对外源性泡沫进行进一步研究。

\[9\] Reinforcement Learning for Systematic FX Trading

标题 : 系统外汇交易的强化学习

作者 : Gabriel Borrageiro, Nick Firoozye, Paolo Barucca , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.04745

说明 : -

Abstract : We conduct a detailed experiment on major cash fx pairs, accurately accounting for transaction and funding costs. These sources of profit and loss, including the price trends that occur in the currency markets, are made available to our recurrent reinforcement learner via a quadratic utility, which learns to target a position directly. We improve upon earlier work, by casting the problem of learning to target a risk position, in an online learning context. This online learning occurs sequentially in time, but also in the form of transfer learning. We transfer the output of radial basis function hidden processing units, whose means, covariances and overall size are determined by Gaussian mixture models, to the recurrent reinforcement learner and baseline momentum trader. Thus the intrinsic nature of the feature space is learnt and made available to the upstream models. The recurrent reinforcement learning trader achieves an annualised portfolio information ratio of 0.52 with compound return of 9.3 , net of execution and funding cost, over a 7 year test set. This is despite forcing the model to trade at the close of the trading day 5pm EST, when trading costs are statistically the most expensive. These results are comparable with the momentum baseline trader, reflecting the low interest differential environment since the the 2008 financial crisis, and very obvious currency trends since then. The recurrent reinforcement learner does nevertheless maintain an important advantage, in that the model s weights can be adapted to reflect the different sources of profit and loss variation. This is demonstrated visually by a USDRUB trading agent, who learns to target different positions, that reflect trading in the absence or presence of cost.

摘要 : 我们对主要的现金外汇对进行了详细的实验，准确地计算了交易和融资成本。这些损益来源，包括货币市场中出现的价格趋势，通过二次效用提供给我们的经常性强化学习者，二次效用学习直接瞄准头寸。我们在早期工作的基础上进行了改进，在在线学习环境中解决了学习如何定位风险头寸的问题。这种在线学习是按时间顺序进行的，但也是以迁移学习的形式进行的。我们将径向基函数隐藏处理单元（其均值、协方差和总体大小由高斯混合模型确定）的输出传递给递归强化学习器和基线动量交易者。因此，特征空间的内在本质被学习并提供给上游模型。在为期7年的测试集中，经常性强化学习交易者的年化投资组合信息比率为0.52，复合回报率为9.3，扣除执行和融资成本。尽管如此，该模型还是被迫在美国东部时间下午5点交易日结束时进行交易，从统计上看，交易成本是最昂贵的。这些结果与动量基准交易者相当，反映了自2008年金融危机以来的低利率差异环境，以及自那时以来非常明显的货币趋势。尽管如此，反复强化学习者仍然保持着一个重要的优势，即模型的权重可以调整以反映不同的损益变化来源。USDRUB交易代理直观地证明了这一点，该代理学习瞄准不同的头寸，以反映在没有或存在成本的情况下的交易。

\[10\] How Robust are Limit Order Book Representations under Data Perturbation

标题 : 在数据扰动下，极限订单表示的鲁棒性如何

作者 : Yufei Wu, Mahmoud Mahfouz, Daniele Magazzeni, Manuela Veloso , 类别 ：Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

链接 : https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.04752

说明 : -

Abstract : The success of machine learning models in the financial domain is highly reliant on the quality of the data representation.
