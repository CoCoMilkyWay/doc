\[1\] Passive Exit

标题:被动出口

作者:Joshua Mitts(Columbia Law School)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3716249

Abstract : Share lending allows passive investors to generate revenue from a decline in portfolio value. When an active mutual fund exits a portfolio firm, passive index funds belonging to the same fund family raise the cost of borrowing the firm’s shares for short selling. To identify supply-side shifts, I exploit changes in the identity of active managers exogenous to within-portfolio variation in the informational sensitivity of share lending costs. The exercise of market power is pronounced in value lending programs targeting hard-to-borrow securities. Share lenders with market power capture most of the surplus arising from the price decline.

Keywords : share lending,passive investing,short selling,corporate governance,law,finance

Abstract :股票借贷允许被动投资者从投资组合价值下降中获得收入。当一只主动型共同基金退出一家投资组合公司时，属于同一基金家族的被动指数基金会提高借入该公司股票进行卖空的成本。为了确定供给侧的变化，我利用了主动经理人身份的变化，这种变化源于股票借贷成本的信息敏感性的投资组合内变化。市场力量的行使在针对难以借贷的证券的价值借贷计划中表现得尤为明显。拥有市场力量的股份出借人占据了价格下跌带来的大部分盈余。

Keywords :股票借贷，被动投资，卖空，公司治理，法律，金融

\[2\] Estimating Real-world Probabilities: a Forward-looking Behavioral Framework

标题:估计现实世界的概率：一个前瞻性的行为框架

作者:Ricardo Crisóstomo(Comisión Nacional del Mercado de Valores (CNMV); Universidad Nacional de Educacion a Distancia (UNED))

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3732350

Abstract : We show that disentangling sentiment-induced biases from fundamental expectations significantly improves the accuracy and consistency of probabilistic forecasts. Using data from 1994 to 2017, we analyze 15 stochastic models and risk-preference combinations and in all possible cases a simple behavioral transformation delivers substantial forecast gains. Our results are robust across different evaluation methods, risk-preference hypotheses and sentiment calibrations, demonstrating that behavioral effects can be effectively used to forecast asset prices. Further analyses confirm that our real-world densities outperform densities recalibrated to avoid past mistakes and improve predictive models where risk aversion is dynamically estimated from option prices.

Keywords : Sentiment, density forecasts, pricing kernel, options data, behavioral finance

Abstract :我们的研究表明，将情绪引发的偏差与基本面预期分离，可以显著提高概率预测的准确性和一致性。利用1994年至2017年的数据，我们分析了15个随机模型和风险偏好组合，在所有可能的情况下，一个简单的行为转换就能带来可观的预测收益。我们的结果在不同的评估方法、风险偏好假设和情绪校正上都是稳健的，证明行为效应可以有效地用于预测资产价格。进一步的分析证实，我们的现实世界密度优于重新校准的密度，以避免过去的错误，并改进预测模型，其中风险规避是根据期权价格动态估计的。

\[3\] Climate Change Concerns and the Performance of Green Versus Brown Stocks

标题:气候变化担忧和绿色股票与棕色股票的表现

作者:David Ardia(HEC Montreal - Department of Decision Sciences),Keven Bluteau(HEC Montreal - Department of Decision Sciences; Ghent University - Department of Economics),Kris Boudt(Ghent University; Vrije Universiteit Brussel; Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam),Koen Inghelbrecht(Ghent University - Department of Economics)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3717722

Abstract : We empirically test the prediction of Pastor, Stambaugh, and Taylor 2020 that green firms can outperform brown firms when climate change concerns strengthen unexpectedly for S&P 500 companies over the period January 2010 - June 2018. To capture unexpected increases in climate change concerns, we construct a Media Climate Change Concern index using climate change-related news published by major U.S. newspapers. We find a negative relationship between the firms’ exposure to the Media Climate Change Concerns index and the level of the firm’s greenhouse gas emission per unit of revenue. This result implies that when concerns about climate change rise unexpectedly, green firms’ stock price increases, while brown firms’ stock price decreases. Further, using topic modeling, we analyze which type of climate change news drives this relationship. We identify five themes that have an effect on green vs. brown stock returns. Some of those themes can be related to change in investors’ expectations about the future cash-flow of green vs. brown firms, while others cannot. This result implies that the relationship between concern and green vs. brown stock returns arises from both investors updating their expectations about the future cash-flows of green and brown firms and changes in investors’ sustainability taste.

Keywords : Asset Pricing, Climate Change, Sustainable Investing, ESG, Greenhouse Gas Emission, Sentometrics, Textual Analysis

Abstract :我们实证检验了Pastor、Stambaugh和taylor2020的预测，即在2010年1月至2018年6月期间，当标准普尔500指数成份股公司对气候变化的担忧意外增强时，绿色公司的表现可能优于棕色公司。为了捕捉气候变化担忧的意外增加，我们利用美国主要报纸发布的气候变化相关新闻构建了媒体气候变化关注指数。我们发现，企业对媒体气候变化关注指数的敞口与企业单位收入的温室气体排放水平呈负相关。这一结果表明，当对气候变化的担忧意外上升时，绿色企业的股票价格上涨，而棕色公司的股票价格下降。此外，通过话题建模，我们分析了哪种类型的气候变化新闻推动了这种关系。我们确定了五个对绿股和棕股收益有影响的主题。其中一些主题可能与投资者对绿色企业和棕色企业未来现金流预期的变化有关，而另一些则与此无关。这一结果表明，关注度与绿色与棕色股票收益之间的关系来自于投资者更新其对绿色和棕色公司未来现金流的预期以及投资者可持续性品味的变化。

Keywords :资产定价，气候变化，可持续投资，ESG，温室气体排放，计量学，文本分析

\[4\] Relative Valuation with Machine Learning

标题:机器学习的相对评价

作者:Paul Geertsema(University of Auckland - Department of Accounting and Finance),Helen Lu(University of Auckland - University of Auckland, Business School; University of Auckland - Department of Accounting and Finance)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3740270

Abstract : We use a decision-tree-based machine learning approach to perform relative valuation. Stocks are valued using market-to-book, enterprise-value-to-assets and enterprise-value-to-sales multiples. Our machine learning models reduce median absolute valuation errors by a minimum of 5.6 to 31.4 percentage points relative to traditional models, depending on the multiple used. The identified valuation drivers are consistent with theoretical predictions derived from a discounted cash flow approach. Accounting variables related to profitability, growth, efficiency and financial soundness are important valuation drivers. The valuations produced by machine learning models behave like fundamental values. A value-weighted strategy that buys (sells) undervalued (overvalued) stocks generates highly significant abnormal returns. When we use models trained on listed firms to value IPOs, machine learning models outperform traditional models in valuation accuracy and are better at identifying overpriced IPOs.

Keywords : Relative Valuation, Fundamental Analysis, Comparable Companies, IPOs, Machine Learning, Gradient Boosting Machines

Abstract :我们使用一个基于决策树的机器学习方法来进行相对评估。股票的估值采用市场对帐面、企业价值对资产和企业价值与销售额的倍数。与传统模型相比，我们的机器学习模型将绝对估值误差中位数至少降低5.6至31.4个百分点，具体取决于使用的倍数。确定的估值驱动因素与现金流折现法得出的理论预测一致。与盈利能力、增长、效率和财务稳健相关的会计变量是重要的估值驱动因素。机器学习模型产生的估值表现为基本价值。买入（卖出）低估（高估）股票的价值加权策略会产生非常显著的异常回报。当我们使用经过上市公司培训的模型对ipo进行估值时，机器学习模型在估值精度方面优于传统模型，并且更善于识别定价过高的ipo。

Keywords :相对估值、基本面分析、可比公司、IPO、机器学习、梯度提升机器

\[5\] When Bad News is Good News: Geopolitical Risk and the Cross-Section of Emerging Market Stock Returns

标题:当坏消息是好消息时：地缘政治风险和新兴市场股票收益的横截面

作者:Adam Zaremba(Poznań University of Economics and Business; Montpellier Business School),Nusret Cakici(Fordham University),Ender Demir(Istanbul Medeniyet University)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3728341

Abstract : Using a news-based gauge of geopolitical risk, we study its role for asset pricing in global emerging markets. We find that changes in risk positively predict future stock returns. The countries with the highest increase in geopolitical uncertainty outperform their counterparts with the lowest change by up to 1% per month. The anomaly is not explained by other established asset pricing effects and remains robust to many considerations. We link the observed phenomenon with investor overreaction to geopolitical news driven by the availability bias.

Abstract :利用基于新闻的地缘政治风险衡量标准，我们研究了其在全球新兴市场资产定价中的作用。我们发现风险的变化对未来股票收益率有正向预测作用。地缘政治不确定性增加最多的国家比变化最小的国家每月高出1%。这一异常现象无法用其他既定的资产定价效应来解释，而且对于许多因素来说，它仍然是稳健的。我们将观察到的现象与投资者过度反应联系起来，并将其与可用性偏差驱动的地缘政治新闻联系起来。

Keywords :地缘政治风险指数，股票收益横截面，新兴市场，股票异常，资产定价，收益可预测性，过度反应，可用性启发式，显著性

\[6\] Misery on Main Street, Victory on Wall Street: Economic Discomfort and the Cross-Section of Global Stock Returns

标题:华尔街的苦难，华尔街的胜利：经济不适与全球股票收益的横截面

作者:Nusret Cakici(Fordham University),Adam Zaremba(Poznań University of Economics and Business; Montpellier Business School)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3730645

Abstract : This study argues that local economic discomfort influences investors’ risk-aversion, leading to a cross-sectional variation of equity risk premia around the world. To test this assertion, we use the popular Misery Index, which aggregates unemployment and inflation rates, as a gauge of macroeconomic welfare. Using data from 65 stock markets for the years 1960–2019, we demonstrate that economic discomfort reliably predicts future equity returns in the cross-section. A quartile of countries with the highest Misery Index outperform the countries with the lowest Misery Index by 0.65% per month. The phenomenon is not subsumed by a battery of established return predictors and proves robust to many additional checks. The effect is stronger in countries where prices are set primarily by local investors and where uncertainty avoidance is high. Finally, this economic discomfort premium can be successfully harvested with liquid exchange-traded funds.

Keywords : Equity Indices, International Markets, Asset Pricing, Equity Anomalies, Return Predictability, the Cross-Section of Stock Returns, the Misery Index, Economic Discomfort Index, Unemployment, Inflation

Abstract :本研究认为，当地经济不景气影响投资者的风险规避，导致全球股票风险溢价的横截面变化。为了检验这一论断，我们使用大众痛苦指数（populardiscress Index）来衡量宏观经济福利，该指数将失业率和通胀率综合起来。利用1960年至2019年65个股市的数据，我们证明经济不景气可靠地预测了未来股票收益的横截面。痛苦指数最高的四分之一国家每月的表现比痛苦指数最低的国家高0.65%。这一现象并没有被一组已建立的收益预测因子所包含，并且被证明对许多额外的检查是鲁棒的。在价格主要由本地投资者定价、不确定性规避率较高的国家，这种影响更为强烈。最后，这种经济不适溢价可以通过流动性交易所交易基金成功收获。

Keywords :股票指数，国际市场，资产定价，股票异常，收益可预测性，股票收益的横截面，痛苦指数，经济不适指数，失业率，通货膨胀

\[7\] Salience Theory and the Cross-Section of Stock Returns: International and Further Evidence

标题:显著性理论与股票收益横截面：国际和进一步的证据

作者:Nusret Cakici(Fordham University),Adam Zaremba(Poznań University of Economics and Business; Montpellier Business School)

链接:https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract\_id=3750815

Abstract : The salience theory perspective on asset prices implies that investors overvalue stocks with salient upsides while undervaluing firms with salient downsides. The resulting mispricing is subsequently reverted — producing a predictable pattern in the cross-section of returns. This study is the first to perform an international examination of this phenomenon. Having scrutinized data from 49 countries, we demonstrate that the salience effect prevails globally. However, it is priced only among the smallest firms, that account for a minuscule fraction of total market capitalization. Outside this microcap universe, the salience anomaly does not exist.

Keywords : salience theory, probability weighting, microcaps, asset pricing, firm size, return predictability, equity anomalies, international markets, replication

Abstract :显著性理论对资产价格的观点表明，投资者高估了具有显著优势的股票，而低估了具有显著劣势的公司。由此产生的错误定价随后又被还原——在收益的横截面上产生一种可预测的模式。这项研究是第一次对这一现象进行国际考察。我们仔细研究了49个国家的数据，证明了显著性效应在全球普遍存在。然而，它只在最小的公司中定价，这些公司只占总市值的很小一部分。在这个微宇宙之外，显著性异常并不存在。

Keywords :显著性理论，概率加权，微观资本，资产定价，公司规模，收益可预测性，股票异常，国际市场，复制
