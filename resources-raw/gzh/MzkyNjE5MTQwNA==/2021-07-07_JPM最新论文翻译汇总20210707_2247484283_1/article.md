\[1\] The Myth of Diversification Reconsidered

标题:对多元化神话的再思考

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management August 2021, jpm.2021.1.273

作者:William Kinlaw, Mark Kritzman, Sébastien Page and David Turkington

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/07/02/jpm.2021.1.273;

Abstract : That investors should diversify their portfolios is a core principle of modern finance. Yet there are some periods in which diversification is undesirable. When the portfolio’s main growth engine performs well, investors prefer the opposite of diversification. An ideal complement to the growth engine would provide diversification when it performs poorly and unification when it performs well. Numerous studies have presented evidence of asymmetric correlations between assets. Unfortunately, this asymmetry is often of the undesirable variety: It is characterized by downside unification and upside diversification. In other words, diversification often disappears when it is most needed. In this article, the authors highlight a fundamental flaw in the way some prior studies have measured correlation asymmetry. Because they estimate downside correlations from subsamples in which both assets perform poorly, they ignore instances of successful diversification (i.e., periods in which one asset’s gains offset the other’s losses). The authors propose instead that investors measure what matters: the degree to which a given asset diversifies the main growth engine when it underperforms. This approach yields starkly different conclusions, particularly for asset pairs with low full-sample correlation. In this article, the authors review correlation mathematics, highlight the flaw in prior studies, motivate the correct approach, and present an empirical analysis of correlation asymmetry across major asset classes.

TOPICS: Portfolio theory, portfolio construction, quantitative methods, statistical methods, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ There is strong empirical evidence that asset class correlations are asymmetric, which poses complications in portfolio construction.

\- ▪ Investors prefer diversification when a portfolio’s main growth engine performs poorly and unification when it performs well.

\- ▪ To measure correlation asymmetry caused by nonnormality, investors must adjust for changes in correlation that arise mathematically when part of a sample is excluded.

\- ▪ Unlike prior research, investors should condition correlations on the performance of a single asset, not two assets.

Abstract:投资者应该使投资组合多样化，这是现代金融的核心原则。然而，在某些时期，多样化是不可取的。当投资组合的主要增长引擎表现良好时，投资者倾向于多元化的相反方向。对增长引擎的理想补充是，当它表现不佳时，提供多样化，当它表现良好时，提供统一。大量研究表明，资产之间存在不对称相关性。不幸的是，这种不对称往往是不受欢迎的变化：它的特点是下行统一和上行多样化。换句话说，多样化往往在最需要的时候消失。在这篇文章中，作者强调了一个基本的缺陷，在一些以前的研究测量相关不对称。因为他们从两种资产表现不佳的子样本中估计下行相关性，所以他们忽略了成功多样化的例子（即一种资产的收益抵消另一种资产的损失的时期）。相反，作者建议投资者衡量什么是重要的：当某一特定资产表现不佳时，它在多大程度上使主要增长引擎多样化。这种方法得出了截然不同的结论，特别是对于全样本相关性较低的资产对。在这篇文章中，作者回顾了相关数学，突出了以往研究中的缺陷，激励了正确的方法，并提出了一个实证分析的相关性不对称跨主要资产类别。

主题：投资组合理论，投资组合构建，定量方法，统计方法，绩效评估

Key Finding

\- ▪ 有强有力的经验证据表明，资产类别的相关性是不对称的，这给投资组合的构建带来了复杂性。

\- ▪ 当一个投资组合的主要增长引擎表现不佳时，投资者更喜欢多元化，当它表现良好时，投资者更喜欢统一。

\- ▪ 为了衡量由非正态性引起的相关性不对称性，投资者必须根据排除部分样本后在数学上产生的相关性变化进行调整。

\- ▪ 与以往的研究不同，投资者应将相关性的条件设定为单个资产的表现，而不是两个资产的表现。

\[2\] Reexamining the Real Estate Quadrants

标题:重新审视房地产象限

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Real Estate 2021, jpm.2021.1.271

作者:Kieran Farrelly and Alex Moss

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/07/01/jpm.2021.1.271;

Abstract : The real estate quadrant approach for categorizing the real estate investment universe is now 20 years old. These investment conduits have developed significantly over this time, and real estate allocations are shaped by both market and regulatory forces, which are leading investors to reexamine the broadening of blended strategies across these quadrants. In this article, the performance and interrelationships between the quadrants and the way that these have evolved over time are examined. The results show that significant diversification benefits are available to investors using the quadrants. The commercial mortgage-backed securities market is found to be the main transmitter of shocks among the quadrants and private real estate the most significant receiver, with a meaningful shift in their relationships before and after the global financial crisis. Finally, blended real estate portfolios are found to reduce estimated risk and enhance the risk-adjusted performance of a purely private real estate exposure.

TOPICS: Real estate, CMBS and commercial mortgage loans, financial crises and financial market history, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ Significant diversification benefits are available to investors using all forms of real estate exposure, given our finding that less than one-third of the system is influenced by itself in isolation.

\- ▪ Using the dynamic connectedness methodology, the study finds that public real estate debt is the main transmitter of shocks among the quadrants and that private real estate equity is the most significant receiver of shocks among the real estate quadrants.

\- ▪ Further evidence of blended real estate portfolios enhance the risk-adjusted performance of a purely private real estate exposure.

Abstract:房地产象限法用于房地产投资领域的分类已经有20年的历史了。这些投资渠道在这段时间内得到了显著发展，房地产配置受到市场和监管力量的双重影响，这导致投资者重新审视在这些象限扩大混合策略的问题。在这篇文章中，性能和象限之间的相互关系，以及它们随着时间的推移而演变的方式进行了研究。结果表明，使用象限法的投资者可以获得显著的多元化收益。研究发现，商业抵押贷款支持证券市场是象限之间冲击的主要传递者，而私人房地产是最重要的接受者，它们在全球金融危机前后的关系发生了有意义的转变。最后，混合房地产投资组合被发现，以减少估计的风险，并提高风险调整后的业绩，一个纯粹的私人房地产风险敞口。

主题：房地产，招商银行和商业抵押贷款，金融危机和金融市场的历史，业绩衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 考虑到我们的研究发现，只有不到三分之一的房地产投资体系独立地受到自身的影响，使用各种形式的房地产风险敞口的投资者可以获得显著的多元化收益。

\- ▪ 运用动态关联性方法，研究发现公共房地产债务是房地产四分之一冲击的主要传导者，私人房地产权益是房地产四分之一冲击的最主要接受者。

\- ▪ 混合房地产投资组合的进一步证据增强了纯私人房地产风险敞口的风险调整绩效。

\[3\] Finding Value Using Momentum

标题:利用动量求值

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management QES Special Issue 2022, jpm.2021.1.272

作者:Bijon Pani and Frank J. Fabozzi

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/early/2021/07/01/jpm.2021.1.272;

Abstract : Value investing has been one of the cornerstones of equity investment strategies. This article provides a new methodology to select value stocks using the concept of relative value across firms. Results show that using multiple value ratios as foundation blocks provides better risk-adjusted returns than using a single ratio, as used by the Fama–French HML factor. The use of trends in fundamental ratios helps capture value in a stock portfolio in a new way. The trend in value metrics ranks firms that are not just cheap compared to the cross section but also those that have become cheaper over time. The results show that this relative value method proves effective for individual value parameters and when combined to create a composite value model. Trends carry incremental information not captured by common factor models and control variables. The authors construct a composite value model that uses trends in six value ratios. Momentum when combined with value generates enhanced return performance, as documented in various academic literatures. The authors extend the discussion on the combination of momentum with value using three different methods.

TOPICS: Fundamental equity analysis, analysis of individual factors/risk premia, statistical methods, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ The authors use trends in value ratios to generate high risk-adjusted returns.

\- ▪ Incremental information in trends in fundamentals is difficult to capture using common factor models.

\- ▪ Combining a value model with momentum enhances the returns of the strategy.

Abstract:价值投资一直是股权投资策略的基石之一。本文利用企业间相对价值的概念，提出了一种新的价值股票选择方法。结果表明，使用多值比率作为基础块比使用单一比率提供更好的风险调整回报，如FAMA-法国HML因子所使用的。基本面比率趋势的使用有助于以一种新的方式捕捉股票投资组合的价值。价值计量的趋势将那些不仅比横截面便宜，而且随着时间的推移变得更便宜的公司进行了排名。结果表明，这种相对值方法对单个价值参数和组合建立复合价值模型都是有效的。趋势携带着公共因子模型和控制变量所没有的增量信息。作者构建了一个综合价值模型，使用六个价值比率的趋势。如各种学术文献所记载的，动量与价值相结合会产生更高的回报率。作者用三种不同的方法扩展了动量与值结合的讨论。

主题：基本权益分析，个体因素/风险溢价分析，统计方法，绩效衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ 作者利用价值比率的趋势来产生高风险调整后的回报。

\- ▪ 基本面趋势中的增量信息很难用公因子模型来捕捉。

\- ▪ 将价值模型与动量相结合可以提高策略的回报。

\[4\] What Portfolio in Europe Makes Sense?

标题:在欧洲什么样的投资组合才有意义？

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Non-US Financial Markets 2021, 47

作者:Gueorgui S. Konstantinov

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/7/79;

Abstract : This article focuses on European bond and equity portfolios, and specifically the relevance of balanced portfolios. European equity and bond markets have undergone tremendous changes since the launch of the European Monetary Union (EMU) in 2000. Describing the risk–return profile of these markets provides valuable information about past structure and historical returns, and shifts the focus onto future expectations for institutional portfolios. The great volatility and low returns involved in European equity markets are a possible explanation for why investors prefer bonds that show Sharpe ratios higher than one. Taking into account five-year realized inflation, as well as strong bond market performance in the past 20 years with yields in the negative territory, the evidence indicates that the window for expected returns on balanced portfolios with large bond exposure is firmly shut. There are two challenging issues for European portfolio allocation. The first reflects the future of bond exposure in a balanced mandate. The second involves meaningful allocation to equity markets in a balanced portfolio.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, fixed income and structured finance, developed markets, portfolio construction

Key Finding

\- ▪ European bond markets have performed excellently in the last 20 years because of investor demand, capital inflows, and growth. The European bond market dominates in institutional portfolio allocations, and equity markets have been less attractive, given their risk–return characteristics.

\- ▪ In a negative yield environment and where real equity returns are expected to be positive, it is questionable whether balanced allocation is capable of generating the desired financial outcomes. Foreign exchange, emerging market bonds, and global equities might provide desired returns for investors.

\- ▪ Two challenging issues are involved in European portfolio allocation. The first reflects the future of bond exposure in a balanced mandate. The second involves meaningful allocation to equity markets in a balanced portfolio. Diversification has not disappeared, but finding expected returns might be challenging.

Abstract:本文关注欧洲债券和股票投资组合，特别是平衡投资组合的相关性。自2000年欧洲货币联盟（EMU）成立以来，欧洲股票和债券市场经历了巨大的变化。描述这些市场的风险-回报概况提供了有关过去结构和历史回报的宝贵信息，并将重点转移到机构投资组合的未来预期上。欧洲股市的巨大波动性和低回报率可能解释了为什么投资者更喜欢夏普比率高于1的债券。考虑到五年期已实现的通胀，以及过去20年债券市场表现强劲，收益率处于负值区域，证据表明，具有大量债券敞口的平衡投资组合的预期收益窗口已经关闭。欧洲投资组合配置面临两个具有挑战性的问题。第一个指标反映了债券敞口在均衡授权下的未来。第二种是在均衡的投资组合中对股票市场进行有意义的配置。

主题：证券分析与估值，固定收益与结构金融，发达市场，投资组合构建

Key Finding

\- ▪ 由于投资者需求、资本流入和经济增长，欧洲债券市场在过去20年中表现出色。欧洲债券市场在机构投资组合配置中占据主导地位，而鉴于其风险回报特征，股票市场的吸引力一直较低。

\- ▪ 在收益率为负的环境下，在实际股本回报预期为正的情况下，均衡配置是否能够产生预期的财务结果值得怀疑。外汇、新兴市场债券和全球股票可能会为投资者提供理想的回报。

\- ▪ 欧洲投资组合配置涉及两个具有挑战性的问题。第一个指标反映了债券敞口在均衡授权下的未来。第二种是在均衡的投资组合中对股票市场进行有意义的配置。多样化并没有消失，但要找到预期回报可能是一个挑战。

\[5\] The European ETF Market: Growth, Trends, and Impact on Underlying Instruments

标题:欧洲ETF市场：增长、趋势及对基础工具的影响

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Non-US Financial Markets 2021, 47

作者:Véronique Le Sourd and Shahyar Safaee

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/7/95;

Abstract : This article presents some key figures about the European market for exchange-traded funds (ETFs), with a focus on growth, key trends, and impact on underlying instruments. One particular important area of growth is the environmental, social, and governance (ESG) ETF segment, with the market size in Europe exceeding that in the United States both in terms of assets under management (AUM) and number of products, although the gap has tended to decrease slightly over time. Turning to ETF ownership, the authors find several reasons to expect the impact of ETFs on European equity markets to be more modest than that documented in the academic literature for the US equity markets, namely a materially lower level of average ETF ownership and a primarily international ownership (i.e., located in the United States). These figures are put in perspective with the results of a survey carried out during the first quarter of 2021, which provides insights into European professional investor perceptions of ETFs. The survey also reveals a particularly strong appetite for ESG-themed funds, and ETFs appear to be a useful instrument for integrating an ESG component into investment decisions.

TOPICS: ESG investing, exchange-traded funds and applications, developed markets, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ ETF ownership of European stocks is about half that of US stocks

\- the European ESG ETF market size exceeds that in the United States, both in terms of AUM and the number of products, but incremental AUM are now almost on par.

\- ▪ European investors show great interest in SRI/ESG investment, with 55% of ETF users already investing in SRI/ESG in 2021 and 68% of those who have not yet done so considering integrating ESG into their portfolios in the near future.

\- ▪ ETFs appear to be a useful instrument for integrating an SRI/ESG component into investment, with 67% of respondents using them to do so and 60% wishing to see further developments in SRI/ESG-based ETFs and/or low-carbon ETFs.

Abstract:本文介绍了欧洲交易所交易基金（etf）市场的一些关键数据，重点是增长、主要趋势以及对基础工具的影响。一个特别重要的增长领域是环境、社会和治理（ESG）ETF部门，欧洲的市场规模在管理资产（AUM）和产品数量方面都超过了美国，尽管随着时间的推移，差距有略微缩小的趋势。在谈到ETF所有权时，作者发现了几个理由，认为ETF对欧洲股市的影响比美国股市学术文献中记载的要小，即ETF的平均所有权水平大大降低，主要是国际所有权（即位于美国）。这些数据与2021年第一季度进行的一项调查结果相吻合，该调查提供了对欧洲专业投资者对etf看法的见解。调查还显示，投资者对ESG主题基金的兴趣尤其强烈，ETF似乎是将ESG组成部分纳入投资决策的有用工具。

主题：ESG投资，交易所交易基金和应用，发达市场，业绩衡量

Key Finding

\- ▪ ETF持有的欧洲股票约为美国股票的一半

\- 欧洲ESG ETF市场规模超过美国，无论是在AUM和产品数量上，但增量AUM现在几乎是平价。▪ 欧洲投资者对SRI/ESG投资表现出极大的兴趣，2021年，55%的ETF用户已经在投资SRI/ESG，68%尚未投资的ETF用户考虑在不久的将来将ESG纳入其投资组合中。

\- ▪ etf似乎是将SRI/ESG成分纳入投资的有用工具，67%的受访者使用etf，60%的受访者希望看到基于SRI/ESG的etf和/或低碳etf的进一步发展。

\[6\] Analyzing Markets with a Large Public Company: The Case of South Korea

标题:分析大型上市公司的市场：以韩国为例

来源:The Journal of Portfolio Management Non-US Financial Markets 2021, 47

作者:Jang Ho Kim, Taehyeon Kang, Jaeyong Yu and Frank J. Fabozzi

链接:https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/47/7/162;

Abstract : This article observes the impact of the largest public firm in a country, especially focusing on the case when the largest firm is disproportionately large. Although the size factor of stock returns is widely recognized, the largest firm may provide further value in analyzing stock markets. The authors perform various factor-based analyses to compare the effect of the largest firm with other statistical and fundamental factors of the market. The findings based on the Korean market show that a market with a massive firm, in terms of market capitalization, has a distinct structure, and distinguishing the effect of the largest firm is effective in modeling the market.

TOPICS: Security analysis and valuation, emerging markets, analysis of individual factors/risk premia, performance measurement

Key Finding

\- ▪ This study observes the impact of the largest public firm in a country, with a focus on the case of the Korean market, where the largest firm is an outlier in terms of market capitalization.
